# DDEX3-16 *Assault on Maerimydra* — Structural Reference

GM reference, not prep. Written from the module text via the 5etools MCP (`source_id: DDEX316A`, with cross-refs to `DDEX315S` and `DDEX34IT`). **Section IDs are given throughout** — pull full text with `get_section(section_ids=["NNN"], source_id="DDEX316A")`.

Assumes **DDEX3-15 ends well for the party**: Szith Morcane taken, Dengor dead, the coalition intact.

---

## The one thing that un-confuses it

**This is two different games glued together, and the first one is scripted to lose.**

**Parts 1–5** are a mass-battle frame — allocate mercenaries, pick objectives off a menu, resolve the rest with offscreen d20s. **Part 5 ends, no matter how well the party performed, with Elanil saying *"I think we have failed"*** (§255). The army cannot take the city. That is not a fail state; it is the script.

**Part 6** is the actual adventure: a dungeon crawl into the Undying Temple to fight Graz'zt, and it is the only part that changes the ending (§382).

Everything before Part 6 exists to buy the party the time to do Part 6.

**Consequence for prep:** prep Part 6 properly. Run Parts 1–5 as theatre. And do **not** telegraph the scripted retreat as a defeat — frame it as *"we bought you the time"* or it lands badly at the table.

## Part 1 — the muster (§063, §066, §074)

Sporedome's fungal amphitheatre. **Elanil Elassidil**, the Moonsilver Herald of Elventree — bard, public agent of the Harpers — briefs the assembled army and hands the party their choice of objectives. Confident, opinionated, backs everything with historical detail, defuses arguments with a song.

Her framing: *"Our allies from Szith Morcane have provided us with the location and avenues of approach to the city... Hledh Hellspawn is present in the city preparing for their invasion of the sunlit realms. This leaves their command structure vulnerable."*

### The allied contingents (§067–073)

| Contingent | Led by | Pre-assault leaning |
|---|---|---|
| Government of New Hillsfar (Red Plumes) | **First Lord Vuhm Yestral** | Northern Forward Base |
| Hillsfar Rebellion | **Elanil Elassidil** | Western Forward Base |
| **Drow of Szith Morcane** *(+ a squad of nycaloth mercenaries)* | **Solom Ned'razak** | **Eastern Forward Base** |
| Faithful of Eilistraee | Saradreza Oussmtor | Convincing the Friendly Dead |
| Myconids of Sporedome | Sovereign Agaricus | Reinforcing the bases; **braincap supply** |
| Mind flayers of Ryxyg | Huum | Mapping the Underdoor |
| **Elisande and her blind goat** | — | **No plans.** "Assumes she will find something that entertains her." Nobody knows who she is. |

> Elisande is the module's planted mystery and the Conclusion's closing line in both the win and the loss text. She is waiting on the bridge in Part 6, covered head to toe in blood, speaking in broken pidgin. Play her as unexplained.

### Faction contacts & secret missions (§074)

| Faction | Contact | Their objective |
|---|---|---|
| Order of the Gauntlet | Zern Xerkstil (male half-orc) | The Friendly Dead |
| Harpers | Olisara Lightsong (female elf) | Western Forward Base |

Secret missions are **not mutually exclusive** and factions do **not** require members to follow their NPC.

### Supplies issued (§074, §077)

One +1 short bow with nonmagical arrows, one +1 quarterstaff, one *scroll of lesser restoration*, one *potion of greater healing* **per character**, one **enhanced braincap mushroom per character** (acts as *greater restoration*; advantage on the next madness save within the hour; **more than one per 24 hours = 1d4 madness levels, no save**).

## Part 2 — pick two of six (§080)

**The party attempts exactly two. The module strongly discourages splitting, and difficulty does not scale down.**

| Objective | Section | Constraint | Sets up in Part 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northern Forward Base | §083 | — | Fungus Farms |
| Eastern Forward Base | §096 | — | Slave District |
| Western Forward Base | §110 | — | Six-Fingered Courtyard |
| **Fortify Gains** | §122 | **Must be second** (a base must exist) | Affects *all* Part 3 encounters |
| **The Underdoor** | §130 | **Must be second** (ends inside the city). **Emerald Enclave mission** | Coliseum |
| **The Friendly Dead** | §148 | **Must be first** (far from the city). **Order of the Gauntlet mission** | Slave District |

Handouts: **Two** = pre-assault objectives (§462), **Three** = breach objectives (§470).

**Rest rules:** short rest permitted between objectives, **but it removes the allied help** for the second (diverted elsewhere). A long rest *replaces* the second objective. No long rest after the second.

**Allied help:** each objective comes with a small allied detachment that hangs back, stabilises the fallen, uses Help, and pulls people out. Explicitly **not** trap detectors or fodder.

### The offscreen subsystem (§162)

For each of the four objectives the party skipped: **d20 + renown points of troops the party assigned there, vs the objective's DC.**

- Northern / Eastern / Western Forward Base — **DC 20**
- Fortify Gains — **DC 15** (17 if the alarm was sounded)
- The Underdoor — **DC 25**
- The Friendly Dead — **DC 25**

> **"If the first three checks are successful, the last one is automatically a failure. The overall attack should be marred by some failure."** No faction leader dies regardless of casualties.

Failures translate into Part 3 as raised alarms (Grumble starts with an extra quaggoth, wave two arrives on round 5 instead of 6), enemy buffs (*aid* + *bless* if Fortify Gains failed), or the Coliseum being closed off. **Friendly Dead success** puts spectral dwarves on the field who hit **every enemy *and* every drow character** for 4d6+3 necrotic.

### Standing environmental rules (§080)

**Regional effects of Graz'zt's lair:**
- Flat stone/metal within 1 mile becomes mirror-bright — **applies throughout Maerimydra**.
- Wild beasts within 6 miles break into constant conflict and coupling — every encounter except the Friendly Dead.
- A humanoid spending 1 hour within 1 mile: **DC 23 Wisdom save or descend into a madness from the Madness of Graz'zt table.** Success grants 24-hour immunity. **Rolled when the party long rests before Part 6.**

**All Maerimydra fire giants are fiendborne**, infused with Hledh's blood: immune to poison and the poisoned condition, *darkness* 1/day, blindsight 60 ft., Abyssal, plus cosmetic horns/wings/tails/extra eyes.

Madness track (§058): three levels — short-term (1d10 min) / long-term (1d10×10 min) / indefinite. *Greater restoration* or better resets to 0. *Remove curse* and *dispel evil* also work, given the demonic source.

## Part 3 — one fight, four skins (§171)

Whichever gate the party came through determines the read-aloud and terrain — **A. Fungus Farms** (§171), **B. Slave District** (§189), **C. Six-Fingered Courtyard** (§195), **D. The Coliseum** (§202) — but **the encounter is mechanically identical**:

- **Wave one:** the shadow demon **Verix** + 2 fiendborne fire giant whelps + **Grumble** (quaggoth thonot overseer) + 2 quaggoths. *Grumble rallies one more quaggoth every round he isn't engaged, up to 8.*
- **Wave two, round 6:** a quasit + 4 ogres + 6 orcs arriving to reinforce the gate.

**The slaves don't want to be there.** Quaggoths, orcs and ogres can be flipped with a **DC 20 Persuasion** — but only if bribed with something *hedonistic* (food, drink, gambling) on top of freedom; without that, disadvantage. They fight for the current combat, then desert to loot the city, which is still useful chaos.

**Interrogation intel (this is where the plot turns):**
- **Hledh Hellspawn and Graz'zt himself are in the castle courtyard performing a ritual.**
- **A gaunt drow woman was recently seen in the city with a half-drow, half-spider creature.** Squallocks the Vrock, Graz'zt's advisor, has ordered her killed on sight.
- All non-warrior drow slaves were rounded up and taken somewhere two days ago. Nobody has seen them since.

## Part 4 — the fork (§209)

The derro of Ravagestone hit the alliance's rear with **Mother**, a Graz'zt spawn. **No rest between Parts 3 and 4.**

**Rally the Lines** (§209 → §216) — for every party. **Coralynn Winsail**, keeper of hounds for the First Lord, arrives bloodied and begging. The North Gate is being overrun by hundreds of derro and things hatching out of thrown pods.

**Rescue** (§213 → §228) — **only presented if the party contains a Harper.** Elanil herself comes: **Solom Ned'razak has been abducted.** A gaunt drow woman appeared in a swirl of mist behind him at the Eastern Gate, wrapped him in sickly blue light, and vanished. She wore a holy symbol of Kiaransalee and **the piwafwi of House T'sarran**. She was heard saying Solom would suffer in the ruins of the **Shattered Tower**.

> Without Solom, Elanil warns, **the Szith Morcane drow may desert and the alliance will crumble.**

Coralynn is appalled at the Harpers choosing one drow over all the soldiers of Hillsfar. Elanil doesn't command — she argues the long game and the eventual retaking of Phlan. Whichever the party abandons, the other messenger goes to mitigate it.

**If the party refuses to move at all and insists on resting:** Rescue is a total failure, and everything from Rally the Lines is added to Part 5, plus Mother spawns an extra demon.

## Part 5 — Mommy Dearest (§242)

**No rest between Parts 4 and 5.** Mother sits on a pile of chitinous eggs surrounded by ~55 prostrate, chanting derro, birthing a vrock as the party arrives.

- **Witnessing it: DC 20 Wisdom (Madness) save or gain a madness level.**
- **Mother's legendary actions each consume a living derro.** No derro left, no legendary actions. That is the fight's lever.
- Infected myconid corpses detonate into tentacled swarms (4d10 piercing + 2d10 acid) when approached.
- Caustic ooze puddles: DC 15 Con or 4d10 acid and slowed.
- The module explicitly says **do not track derro individually** — every attack auto-kills one, and it gives AoE body counts (20-ft radius = 10 derro, etc.). "Feel free to hand wave and be generous."

**Then the scripted retreat** (§255). *"We're going to have to retreat. I think we have failed."* Long rest permitted before Part 6 — **which triggers the DC 23 Graz'zt madness save.**

*"Thus Ends Round One."*

## Part 6 — the real adventure (§038, §261, §262)

The leaders conclude the city cannot be taken militarily. Yestral offers to **hold the line long enough for heroes to do something else.** Elanil: *"Now we just need the right heroes."*

The party is teleported to a bridge between Castle Maerimydra's highest tower and a hanging stalactite. **Elisande and her blind goat are sitting on the railing, covered head to toe in blood**, bloody footprints leading from the tower door.

### Graz'zt's plan (§038)

The **Undying Temple** is a former temple to **Kiaransalee**, built for research into undeath and planar travel, half of it magically shifted into the **Ethereal Plane**. Graz'zt revived it and **regrew its heart — not with necrotic energy, but with the raw chaos of the Abyss.**

He knows Vizeran DeVir intends to summon all the demon lords together so they destroy each other and banish themselves (*Out of the Abyss*). The Temple makes Graz'zt **immune to being forcibly moved between planes** — and gives him permanent access to both the Material Plane and the Abyss through the Ethereal overlap, so he can convert the Material into another layer of his realm.

### Temple mechanics
- **Dimensional Lock** (§266): no teleporting *through* the walls; teleporting *within* works.
- **Ethereal Solids** (§276): half the structure is ethereal stone.
- **Matliedun** (§424), male cambion, blood of Graz'zt, master of temple security.
- Two mapped levels (§451, §453).

### Conclusion (§382)

**Win:** Graz'zt is forced back to the Abyss; without their demonic allies the giants break; Maerimydra is liberated. **The drow of Szith Morcane join the faithful of Eilistraee to occupy the city.** Ryxyg, Sporedome and all factions send people to rebuild and set up trading enclaves. On the surface, New Hillsfar and Elventree commit to supplying Mulmaster and marching on Phlan.

**Loss:** the alliance dissolves. Szith Morcane's drow and Eilistraee's faithful part ways permanently. Ryxyg and Sporedome withdraw and distrust the surface. Hillsfar drifts back toward xenophobia.

Both endings close on: *"you wonder what became of young Elisande and her goat?"*

# What our DDEX3-15 hands into this

## 1. Velasta's aunt is in Maerimydra, and she takes Solom

This is the single most important carry-over and the module built it for us.

- **DDEX3-15 §143** — Velasta T'sarran "wishes to see Solom dead and Szith Morcane under the rule of Kiaransalee's priestesses **as her aunt attempted to do over a hundred years ago.**"
- **DDEX3-16 §458 / §213 / §228** — **Dorina T'sarran**, "the former ruler of Szith Morcane and a **vampire** drow cleric of Kiaransalee," is in the area. She abducts Solom in Part 4 and takes him to the Shattered Tower, **"furious that Solom has betrayed her and taken over the outpost."** Worth 13,000 XP (§388).

So: **Solom is abducted in public, at the Eastern Gate, in front of the army — including in front of the Szith Morcane drow he commands, and in front of Velasta.**

That is how she learns he is alive. It costs us nothing to stage. Her aunt beats her to a hundred-year vendetta by about ninety seconds, and the party's lie about who killed Solom comes apart in the middle of a battle.

And **the endgame is a temple to Velasta's own goddess**, desecrated by Graz'zt and re-hearted with Abyssal chaos. She has a reason to be in Part 6 that has nothing to do with the party.

> The "gaunt drow woman with a half-drow half-spider creature" the Part 3 prisoners describe (§171) is Dorina, and the drider is her escort. **That intel arrives one full part before the abduction** — the party can be warned and still fail to stop it.

## 2. The Harper gate is a problem to solve in advance

**The Rescue branch only appears "if the party contains a member of the Harpers."** Our roster: Akritas is Emerald Enclave; Daein, Felkur and Bramgrim are unaffiliated. **As written, this table never learns Solom was taken.**

Given how much this campaign has invested in Solom, open the branch deliberately. **Alder is the obvious key** — senior Harper, runs Underdark operations, already signed Bramgrim's sword, and already called this party her contingency. A field commission costs her nothing.

## 3. Akritas's faction objective is The Underdoor

Emerald Enclave contact **Seranolla the Whisperer**; the Enclave's special mission sits inside the Underdoor objective (§130), which **must be attempted second** because it ends with the party inside the city. It sets up the Coliseum in Part 3.

Note the collision: the Zhentarim's objective is the **Eastern** Forward Base — the one **Solom's drow are assigned to**, and the gate where Dorina takes him.

## 4. Who holds Szith Morcane decides who commands a faction block

The module assumes **Solom Ned'razak leads the Drow of Szith Morcane** as one of seven allied contingents, reinforced with nycaloth mercenaries, assigned the Eastern Forward Base. **If our party gives the outpost to Rilira, Hamad or Velasta instead, that contingent changes hands** — and so does who gets abducted in Part 4, and whose desertion threatens the alliance.

## 5. Vuhm Yestral has been promoted

The module has him as **First Lord of New Hillsfar**. Our docs still have him as a Red Plumes commander in Hillsfar. He commands the Red Plumes contingent here and gets the "we could hold — time enough for heroes" line in Part 6.

## 6. The completed blood-purge ritual is an anti-Hledh weapon

**Every fire giant in Maerimydra is fiendborne, infused with Hledh's blood** (§080). DDEX3-04 §139: the ritual removes **demon taint from blood** — "of the fire giants, or really anyone" — and needs living fiendborne blood as reagent.

Our ritual **completed**. Nobody at the table has connected those two facts. The module does not provide a use for it; it is ours to invent if we want one.

## 7. Braincap continuity

DDEX3-16 §071 — the myconids of Sporedome are "key to resisting the madness inducing energies **emanating from Maerimydra**," and "the operation would fail without their provisions." Our party's braincap relationship with Sporedome is already deep. Good.

# HOUSE RULE — The Coalition as the Renown Pool

## What RAW actually does

**Gathering Forces (§041), before play:**
- **Gold:** 500 gp = 1 renown, **max 5**. 2,500 gp+ also earns the *Shared Valor* favor.
- **Or downtime:** 20 DT = 1 renown, **max 5**. 100 DT+ also earns *Shared Valor*.
- **Not both.** Players *"should not know the conversion rate to renown."* DM told to cap deliberation at five minutes.

**Assignment (§078):** each character gets a mercenary unit equal to their renown, splittable, assigned to the four objectives they are not personally attempting.

**Resolution (§162):** each of those four rolls **d20 + renown assigned there** vs DC — **20 / 20 / 20** for the Forward Bases, **15** for Fortify Gains, **25** for the Underdoor and the Friendly Dead. Plus the override: **if the first three succeed, the fourth auto-fails.**

## Why it breaks at our table

Maxing all four characters costs **10,000 gp or 400 downtime days.** This party is not sitting on 10,000 gp — they have ~200 gp in uncut gems, a circlet, and a thousand gold of warhammers earmarked as Rilira's army. Felkur is incorporating a business, which is a gold sink pointing the other way.

Realistically they field **+1 to +2 per objective against DC 20–25** — a 5–15% success rate on nearly everything. **The offscreen war becomes a scripted rout that has nothing to do with anything they did in DDEX3-15.**

That is the actual defect. Not that the subsystem is fiddly — that it is *disconnected*. They spent a module recruiting five factions and the module asks how much gold they brought.

## The replacement

**The party's five coalition factions ARE the renown pool.** They personally attempt two objectives, leaving **four offscreen** — so with five factions there is always either a double-up or one faction held in reserve. A real choice, every time.

**Values:**
- Well-matched assignment: **+5**
- Neutral: **+3**
- Badly matched: **+1, and something breaks** (see political costs)
- A faction may be **split across two objectives at half value** (+2/+2 matched, +1/+1 otherwise)
- **Keep the module's "if three succeed the fourth auto-fails."** The attack should still be marred.

### Affinity table

| Faction | Natural fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Decha's stone giants** | Any Forward Base, esp. **Western** | The only thing in the coalition that trades blows with fire giants. The Western tunnels ate every scout sent into them. |
| **The Hidden** (Hamad) | **The Underdoor** | Their entire audition was a covert heist behind a Faerzress-warded lock. Scouting an unknown river for a saboteurs' route is the same job. |
| **The Nerevenans** (Velasta) | **The Friendly Dead** | Kiaransalee's cult negotiating with haunted dead. Nobody else in the army can open that conversation at all. |
| **Rilira's slaves + Grundar's duergar** | **Eastern Forward Base** or **Fortify Gains** | Numbers and labour — and the Eastern approach is closest to Maerimydra's Slave District, where they can talk to their own. Duergar know fortification. |
| **Quil'bryn's drow** | **Northern Forward Base** | The main approach passes near Szith Morcane. It is their ground. |

> **The trap worth steering toward.** The Friendly Dead are dwarves slaughtered *by the drow of Szith Morcane*, now haunting the Cave of Black Whispers. Sending Quil'bryn's drow there is a disaster. Sending Velasta's death cult is the **best-matched assignment on the board** — and §162 says a Friendly Dead *success* puts spectral dwarves on the field who attack every enemy **and every drow character** for 4d6+3 necrotic. The optimal play actively maims their own allies.

### The political layer — the reason to do this at all

All five factions were promised Szith Morcane. **Assigning them is assigning risk, and where they get sent says who the party is willing to spend.** Give each assignment a cost line, not just a modifier:

- **Rilira's people sent somewhere hard and mauled** → she was promised a city and used as a battering ram. That is the shield doctrine, executed by the party's own hand, in public.
- **The Nerevenans sent in first** → Quil'bryn already predicted they would be first to fall. If they are mauled, he was right, and he will say so where Velasta can hear it.
- **A faction held back** → the other four notice which one got protected.

That is the conversion: from *"how much gold did you donate"* to *"who are you willing to spend."*

### Modifiers earned in DDEX3-15

- **The Forge of Surtr arsenal saved** → Rilira's people are armed → **+2 wherever they are assigned.** Arsenal lost → they are two hundred bodies with farming tools, **+1 anywhere, matched or not.**
- **Velasta laid to rest** → **the Nerevenan block does not exist.** The party loses the only faction that fits the DC 25 Friendly Dead objective. *(This is a concrete price for tonight's Scene 1½ decision — add it to that scene's cost list.)*
- **Messanth freed** (unspent as of Ch22) → a bronze shadow dragon is a one-objective swing worth **+5 on its own**, wherever pointed.
- **Who holds Szith Morcane** determines whether Solom commands the module's own Szith Morcane contingent (§070) or someone else does.

### What to keep from RAW

- **A small gold/downtime overlay for personal contributions**, routed through the campaign rather than an abstract donation box: Felkur buying Red Plume mercenaries through **Isthana Rao**, or Charles calling in Stargate's Hillsfar connections. Cap it at one or two points so it flavours rather than replaces — and note that it entangles them further, which is the point.
- **Invert the blind-auction rule.** RAW hides the conversion rate to prevent optimisation. Here the drama is entirely in the choosing, so show them the numbers and let them agonise.

# HOUSE RULE — The Harper Gate: Alder's Commission

**Ruling: Alder field-commissions one of the party into the Harpers.** The gate opens; the module's Harper content is available to this table.

## What is actually gated (§456, §209, §213)

Two things, and the second is the smaller loss.

**1. Part 1 foreknowledge.** Handout One's Harper entry, given at the muster:

> *"Solom Ned'razak, the archmage of Szith Morcane is the key to keeping our alliance together with his fellow drow... We must protect him at all cost so we can keep the drow in the alliance and keep them from potentially becoming another foe. Unfortunately we have heard that **Dorina T'sarran**, the former ruler of Szith Morcane and a **vampire drow cleric of Kiaransalee**, has been seen in the area and **vowed revenge upon Solom.** We cannot afford her meddling."*
>
> **Faction Special Mission: Preserve the drow alliance and protect Solom Ned'razak at all costs.**

**2. Part 4's Rescue branch** — *"The second alternative is not present if no Harper characters are in the party."*

A non-Harper party never learns Dorina exists, then Solom vanishes and nobody comes to tell them. They fight at the North Gate and discover afterwards that their patron is gone and the drow contingent is disintegrating. Legitimate, but it wastes the best-loaded thread in the campaign.

## How Alder does it

She is a senior Harper who runs Underdark operations, already called this party her *"backup plan to the backup plan,"* and already **signed Bramgrim's sword with a quill, under protest.** A field commission costs her one sentence.

**Play it as paperwork.** Maximum deflation — this is an administrator processing a form, not a knighting. The callback writes itself: she produces **the same quill.**

> "You've been doing the work for a year. Someone in Everlund keeps asking me who authorised it." She does not look up. "So now someone did. Congratulations, there's no ceremony and no stipend."

**Where to spend it — two stagings:**

- **Preferred: Alder appears briefly at the Sporedome muster** and does it in the middle of everything else, then leaves. Keeps it cheap and in character.
- **Fallback: Elanil Elassidil delivers it on Alder's behalf.** Elanil is *already* "a public agent of the Harpers" and is already running the briefing scene. This keeps Alder in reserve — worth considering, since the standing note in `planning.md` is that **she is a pressure valve, not a solution**, and her ability to teleport and reframe dissolves tension if she appears often.

## Who gets it — still open, with a lean

**Not Bramgrim.** He is GM-run, and the Part 4 fork should sit with a player.

| Candidate | Case for | What it costs him |
|---|---|---|
| **Felkur** *(lean)* | The Harpers recruiting a career infiltrator with a false identity is entirely on-brand, and he is already the coalition's political centre — Quil'bryn is sworn to him personally | **An organisation that keeps records now has him on a list.** He has spent the whole campaign not being on lists, while Mulmaster's Brotherhood of the Cloak hunts the Phantom Thief |
| **Daein** | He is the face, he carries the Solom lie, and he is the one who would be handed the "protect Solom at all costs" brief | He declined a political post in Hillsfar and is chasing FU money. **Joining a faction is against his stated character** — which makes accepting it a real choice |

Both have a genuine price, which is the point. Pick at the table or let the player volunteer.

## The commission's real payload

It is not just the Part 4 fork. It is **Handout One**, in Part 1, with Dorina T'sarran's name on it — and that is where the risk lives.

**The party will be told, at the muster, that a vampire cleric of Kiaransalee has vowed revenge on Solom Ned'razak.** Velasta is in that army. If that name or that vendetta reaches her:

> **Dorina hunting Solom is proof Solom is alive.** Velasta does not need the party to slip. Her aunt's existence unravels the lie on its own.

*(House T'sarran connection: DDEX3-15 §143 — Velasta wants Szith Morcane under Kiaransalee's priestesses "as her aunt attempted to do over a hundred years ago." Dorina is the aunt.)*

**Decide deliberately when that fires.** Three options:

1. **The warning without the name** — "a vampire priestess of Kiaransalee." Velasta learns nothing actionable. Safest, and slightly cowardly.
2. **The name without the vendetta** — she hears her aunt is in Maerimydra, which is family news, not evidence.
3. **The full handout, in Part 1** — and run the whole module with the lie already burning. Highest heat, and DDEX3-16 will hand you this by default if you don't intervene.
