# Charles — "Dumb Karl," leader of La Résistance, Felkur's business partner

> **Status:** Staging (notes/, not canon). Origin: **DDEX3-05 *Bane of the Tradeways*** (source `DDEX35BA`, §065–066, §157), played in the Tier 1 Hillsfar arc. **Started as a joke character and became one of the party's most loved NPCs.** Links: `notes/npcs/glenna_moonsmith.md` *(his girlfriend — to write)*, `notes/session_prep/20260830_nobody_came_looking.md` (Scene 1, the Cave), `docs/party.md` (Felkur's business).

## What the module actually says

- **Karl.** Male **hill dwarf**. Grain farmer — **provides much of the grain sold in Hillsfar** — and leader of the outlaw attacks on Red Plume caravans on the Tradeway just north of **The Stop**.
- **"Dumb Karl" is an ironic nickname given to him by the locals.** §066, verbatim:

> *"A homely male hill dwarf that provides much of the grain sold in Hillsfar. His moniker is actually an ironic nickname given to him by locals. **Despite his common way of speaking, he's actually quite bright** — something that becomes quickly apparent as he prattles off on obscure bits of information regarding whatever topic he finds particularly engaging."*

- **His canonical quote, and his tic:**

> **"Funny thing you mentioned that. Let me tell you more about it."**

> **The module tells you the joke is not a joke.** He talks like a farmhand and is the smartest person in most rooms, and the locals named him for the first thing and never revised it. **That is why he was always going to become the leader of something.**

## What radicalised him (§065)

He explained it to the party while running through the woods, out of breath, during the first fight they ever fought alongside him:

- Farming in the region used to be good business. **The merchant representatives at The Stop stopped paying what they used to.**
- **Human farmers get better prices.**
- **Non-human farmers are banned from entering Hillsfar to sell their own crops.** The representatives are therefore the only buyer.
- The guild **offers loans against the value of the farm** so they can afford to plant next season.
- **The Red Plumes have been in the merchants' pocket for a while now.**

> **He is not an idealist. He is a supplier who was cheated on price by a monopsony backed by soldiers, worked out exactly how the mechanism functioned, and then dismantled it.** Everything he has become follows from a grain contract.

## ⭐ How Karl became Charles — the centre of the character

**The madness took him.** Graz'zt's Abyssal madness, spreading through the Hillsfar region, the same corruption the party spent two tiers chasing to a marilith-shaped samovar in Zortagra and finally broke at the Obsidian Keep.

**And what came out the other side was Charles** — a French Resistance leader out of *Casablanca*. The accent, the defiance, the toasts, the absolute romantic conviction that the cause is worth everything. **"La Résistance"** is not the party's nickname for it. **It is what he called it**, and he meant it, and it worked.

**Then the party cured him — of the madness.**

> **Say it precisely, because it matters: they cured the madness. They did not cure Charles.**

The madness went, and it went completely. And when it went, **Charles went with it** — the accent stopped, the declarations stopped, and **Karl was standing there**, sane and mild and entirely himself. **The party was devastated.**

**So Karl decided he liked being Charles, and brought him back.**

> **That is the character.** A grain farmer who was driven mad, and while mad became someone braver and larger and more loved than he had ever been — and who, **handed his own mind back and in full possession of it**, looked at the person he had been and chose the other one.

### ⚠ Charles is not madness. Charles is sane.

**This is the thing to be unambiguous about, at the table and in every future doc.**

- **The madness is gone.** Cured, cleanly, permanently. Graz'zt has no hold on him.
- **Charles is a persona a sane man maintains on purpose**, because he prefers being him and because the people he loves prefer him too.
- **Nothing detects anything.** *Detect evil and good*, *dispel evil and good*, *greater restoration*, a braincap, the Madness of Graz'zt table — **none of it touches him, because there is nothing there to touch.** If a player ever tests him, the result is *clean*, and that should be quietly devastating rather than reassuring.
- **He is not relapsing and he is not at risk.** Do not run him as a man one bad day from slipping. **He is not afflicted. He made a decision.**

> The party does not need fixing him again as a plot. **They need to notice, at some point, that he never asked to be fixed the first time.**

### Why it lands as hard as it does

**He has never in his life been called what he wanted to be called.** "Dumb Karl" was an ironic nickname **the locals gave him** and never revised — the module says so outright. He was named by other people, for the wrong reason, for decades.

> **"Charles" is the first name he ever chose for himself.** He found it while out of his mind, and he kept it on purpose.

### The irony you should never say out loud

**The madness was Graz'zt's.** The party is currently marching to kill Graz'zt.

> **Charles is the only good thing the Dark Prince ever made, and he made it by accident, and the man kept it.**
>
> Do not have anyone articulate this. **If a player arrives at it on their own — ideally somewhere near U29 — let it sit and move on.**

## What he became — this campaign

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Met robbing a Red Plume caravan. The party joined in. Together they **exposed the Red Plume slave ring selling locals to duergar** *(the Boon of Bitterwood — check whether any PC carries it)*. |
| **The madness** | Became **Charles**, and became the leader of **La Résistance**. |
| **The cure** | The party cured him. **He chose to stay Charles.** |
| **Now** | **A member of the constabulary at The Stop** — and **Felkur's business partner**, running **Stargate** through **Isthana Rao**, with Felkur's money, while Felkur is in the Underdark. |
| **Personally** | **Glenna Moonsmith fell in love with Charles.** She keeps the Plodding Plow; he keeps the peace outside it. |

### Why Glenna, and why it was inevitable

**Karl farms the Tradeway just north of The Stop. Glenna runs the inn at The Stop.** DDEX3-05 and DDEX3-10 are set in the same two miles of road. **They are neighbours**, and they have been for the party's entire acquaintance with both of them.

> **And note who she actually fell for.** Not the grain farmer she had lived up the road from for years. **She fell in love with Charles** — the man he became while out of his mind, and then chose to stay. She has never known him as anything else and she is not owed an explanation.

## Where he is now — the fixture of The Stop

**He is the law at The Stop.** A member of the constabulary, on the same stretch of the Tradeway where he used to ambush Red Plume caravans and strip the bodies.

> **He robbed soldiers on that road. Now he is the one you report a robbery to.** Nobody at The Stop finds this strange, because everyone there knows exactly why he did it and exactly who he did it to — and because the alternative was the Red Plumes.

**And he is the hub.** Glenna runs the inn; Charles walks the road. **Whenever the party comes back to The Stop and needs pointing at something, Charles is the one who points them.** He has become the reason The Stop is a place adventures start.

> **Play the arrival, not the errand.** He sees them from a distance, he is delighted, there is a toast, and the job comes third. **He is pleased to see them before he is useful to them, every single time.**

## ⚠ The collision Felkur has not thought about

At the Sporedome muster, **Felkur accepted a Zhentarim proposition** — a smuggling partnership, sealed with a bottle of rare wine, in exchange for retrieving newborn children of Graz'zt out of Castle Maerimydra.

**Felkur's business partner is a policeman.**

> Stargate — **still being built, not yet running** — will be a network of doors that bypass every gate and checkpoint in the region. **It is the single most valuable smuggling asset anyone in the Moonsea could own**, it is half Charles's, and Charles is a constable who took that job because of what caravan monopolies and bought soldiers did to his neighbours.
>
> **And it is not operational yet, which is why the Zhentarim are interested now.** They are not buying cargo space. **They are buying in early, before the man who polices The Stop has any say in what it carries.**
>
> **The Black Network is not offering Felkur a favour. It is offering him his partner's company.**
>
> Nobody has raised this. **Do not raise it either** — but know that when the Zhentarim come to collect, the person Felkur has to lie to is the one he has never had to lie to.

## ⭐ The thing nobody has said out loud

**Karl's entire grievance was that he was not allowed to bring his goods into the city.**

Non-human farmers banned at the gate. One buyer. One price. Soldiers on the buyer's payroll.

**He now co-owns a company that builds doors which make gates irrelevant.**

> **Stargate is Dumb Karl's revenge on the Hillsfar tollgate, and neither he nor Felkur has ever framed it that way.** Felkur pitched it as a business. Charles said yes for a reason he has probably never articulated even to himself.
>
> **And it is a Resistance man's project too** — a network that makes borders and checkpoints meaningless, sold as commerce. **Both halves of him want it.** Spend this when Felkur next talks to him, ideally after the Cave, when Felkur owes him something.

## Playing him — two registers, one man

**Underneath:** a homely hill dwarf who drops his g's, says "ain't," and is the smartest person in most rooms. Prattles happily about obscure detail on any subject he finds engaging — crop rotation, the flaws in a guild loan instrument, how to spot a load-bearing arch.

**On top:** *Casablanca.* The Resistance leader. Declarations, toasts, "mon ami," romantic fatalism about the cause, the total absence of irony about any of it.

> **The join is the whole performance.** He will begin a sentence as a defiant Free French patriot and finish it with the yield-per-acre figures that prove his point. **Neither half is a put-on any more.** He has been both for long enough that they have fused.

- **The tic survives from before the madness — and that is how the party knows he is still in there:**

> **"Funny thing you mentioned that. Let me tell you more about it."**

  Use it once a scene. **In the accent.** It should be endearing the first time and slightly alarming when he says it about something the party wishes he did not understand.

- **Nobody at the table mentions that Charles is a choice.** Not the party, not Glenna, not him. **It is the most obvious unspoken thing in his life and everyone protects it.**
- **He is not deferential and not impressed.** He knew Felkur before Felkur was a political figure in the Underdark and he is not adjusting.
- **He never asks for anything.** Load-bearing for the Cave: he did not ask. Glenna asked the Order of the Gauntlet. The party found out sideways.
- **He is a hill dwarf, and so is Bramgrim.** They should get on immediately and at ruinous length, about grain.

## The Cave of Black Whispers — his connection

**Vragni Moonsmith is Glenna's ancestor.** Glenna begged the Order of the Gauntlet to lay him to rest; Zern Xerkstil brought it to the party at the muster; **the party recognised her name and accepted on the spot.**

> **They did not accept it as a job. They accepted it because of Karl**, and Daein and Felkur both have a relationship with him that made it a non-decision.
>
> **So the Cave is not a question of whether. It is a question of whether they can do it right** — and "right" means Vragni **at rest**, permanently, which is a narrower target than "dead" and much narrower than "the dwarves are freed." See the Part 2 prep, Scene 1.

**What he does not know:** that the party's coalition is built out of the drow of Szith Morcane, and **the drow of Szith Morcane are the ones who massacred Vragni's company and desecrated his body.** Glenna's ancestor is undead because of the people Felkur is currently commanding.

> **Nobody has to raise this. But if a player does, it is the scene.**

## ⭐ He comes to the Cave of Black Whispers

**Ruled in.** **He walked.** Stargate is not operational — there was no shortcut. He put on the beret, attached himself to the coalition's baggage train, and came down into the Underdark on foot behind seven contingents, pushing a farm cart with a hand-built pine coffin roped into the bed. The party finds him sitting on the tailgate outside a haunted cave, eating.

**He came to bring Vragni home.** Glenna asked the Order of the Gauntlet to lay her ancestor *to rest*; Charles heard that and understood it the way a man who farms and buries people understands it — **home, in the ground, with his family** — and worked out that somebody has to carry him. So he built a box to a dwarf's measure and came to collect.

> **It is the most insane thing he has ever done and he did it for L'amour, and he will tell you so before you can ask.**

**His opening line, and the whole point of the scene:**

> **"Non — non, do not look at me like that. This time it is personal. It is not La Résistance!"**
>
> *(and then, drawn up to his full and considerable lack of height, with total sincerity)*
>
> **"It is for L'AMOUR."**

> **He is not diminishing it. He is promoting it.**
>
> Charles has spent his whole invented life believing that some things are worth everything. **He has not stopped believing that. He has found another one.** La Résistance was a cause; **L'amour is a cause**, and he says the word with exactly the conviction he used to spend on freedom, because to him it is the same category of thing and possibly the higher one.
>
> **Nobody gets to hear this as small.** He did not come down here for a woman. **He came down here for L'amour**, and the fact that it is also Glenna is, to Charles, a detail of implementation.

**Full staging, dialogue and branches: `notes/session_prep/20260830_nobody_came_looking.md`, Scene 0½ — the cart.**

Three things it does:

1. **It makes "at rest" an object.** Not a phrasing the Order gave them. A box.
2. **It puts a beloved NPC forty feet from a CR 15 encounter**, under his own power, having asked nobody. **Be honest with yourself that this is a hostage**, and hold to the rules in the prep doc: he does not go in unless they bring him, he is never useful in a fight, he dies only if the party's own choices kill him, and never as a stinger.
3. **It is the one legitimate place to spend the accent slipping.** If they order him home twice, Karl answers: *"She asked the paladins because she didn't want to ask me. So I ain't waitin' on bein' asked."*

## Open

1. **Does anyone carry the Boon of Bitterwood** from DDEX3-05? Tavern recognition on a d20 roll of 15+, anywhere in the Moonsea outside Hillsfar proper — a free drink and their heroics loudly proclaimed. **Worth firing once at The Stop.**
2. **Does he ever drop it?** Is there a moment — bad news, real fear, Glenna hurt — where the accent goes and **Karl** is standing there? **Recommend holding that in reserve and spending it once, ever.** It is worth more unspent than spent, and if you use it, use it on Felkur.
3. **Does Charles know what Felkur actually is?** The Phantom Thief of Mulmaster, the voided Maram contract, the devil owed his location. **He is bright enough to have worked out more than Felkur thinks.**
4. **What happens to Stargate if Felkur doesn't come back?** Karl is running it, in his name, with his money, and has a partner in the Underdark facing a demon lord.
5. **Write Glenna properly.** She is dating Charles, she asked the Order of the Gauntlet to lay her ancestor to rest, and she has now driven a session's opening scene from two hundred miles away — and she has no dossier.
