> **Status:** Staging (notes/, not canon). ## Identity (module canon, §212 / §412)

- **Race/role:** Female human. **Master of hounds for Vuhm Yestral — from before he ascended to First Lord.** She kept his kennels when he was nobody.
- **Description:** "Brutish." Blunt, socially graceless, and entirely uninterested in performing otherwise.
- **Disposition:** **Uncomfortable around humans and devoted to her dogs. Dislikes soldiers.** Does not care for politics or the recent rebellion. Mostly wants to be left alone with her animals.
- **The one thing that moves her:** she is **only roused to confrontation when her pets are threatened.** Everything else she lets go.

## The thing to understand about her

**She is the wrong person to be making the argument she makes, and that is why it works.**

In Part 4 she is the advocate for **hundreds of soldiers of Hillsfar dying at the North Gate** — and she does not like soldiers, has never liked soldiers, and thinks the entire rebellion was other people's business. She is not making a political case or a strategic one. **She is a kennel-master who watched people get torn apart and ran to find the only people she could think of who might be able to stop it.**

Elanil, opposite her, is the better strategist, the better speaker, and the more senior figure. **Coralynn beats her anyway**, at most tables, because Elanil argues the long game and the eventual retaking of Phlan, and Coralynn argues that people are dying right now and she does not know what else to do.

> Neither of them is wrong. **Do not tilt it.**

## The Yestral connection — free, and this campaign's own

She kept Vuhm Yestral's kennels **before he was anyone.** This party met him as a common guard on Hillsfar's gate and has watched him become First Lord — he stood up at the Sporedome muster to publicly praise Daein and the party as heroes and defenders of Hillsfar (Ch24).

**So Coralynn and the party are the only people in this war who knew Yestral when he was nothing.** The difference is that the party got promoted alongside him and she did not, and she is visibly unimpressed by what he became.

- She will not call him "First Lord" unless forced.
- She is at this war because his household is at this war, not because she believes in it.
- **If a player asks her what he was like before — that is the warmest she gets all scene.**

## Her arrival (§209)

She reaches the party's forward position wearing Hillsfar's colours, **accompanied by a dog in barding.**

> Blood streams from her brow. Her left arm hangs limply at her side. Her face is contorted in fear.
>
> "You have to come quickly! It's horrible. **They are everywhere**, attacking from behind, appearing in the middle of us, we're being overrun. **I didn't know what else to do.** Please come back. We need you."

**What she can tell them:**

- The rear lines were **lightly defended** — the alliance trusted the cavern walls and tunnels to block assaults from the city.
- Moments ago at the **North Gate**, hundreds of small grey dwarves with huge white eyes swarmed up from behind, crazed, throwing themselves heedlessly at the defenders. **They would have been rebuffed easily** —
- — except that **pods or eggs were flung from deeper in the tunnel**, exploding into noxious gas, and **things that defy comprehension crawled out of the goo** and began massacring the allied forces.
- With the alliance split between three gates, **the North Gate force is in danger of being wiped out.**

## In the fork

She is horrified at the idea of **the Harpers choosing one drow over all the soldiers of Hillsfar** (§213 Development). She says so. She does not have the vocabulary to say it diplomatically and she does not try.

> **Since Ch24, Bramgrim is a Harper.** Coralynn does not know that, and if she finds out mid-scene it costs the party something real — **she is describing his organisation's choice, to his face, without knowing he is in it.**
>
> And nobody tells her that the drow the Harpers are choosing over her soldiers **is a clone, and Bramgrim knows it.**

She does not command and cannot compel. **Once the party chooses, she and Elanil both disperse to mitigate the damage of whichever choice was not made.**

## Playing her

- **Never articulate.** She restarts sentences. She says the same thing twice because she does not trust that it landed.
- **She addresses the dog more comfortably than she addresses the party.** If the scene stalls, she talks to it.
- **She does not thank anyone and does not apologise.** If they go with her, she just turns and starts walking.
- **The Owlbear.** Bramgrim rides it. She is a master of animals meeting the only party in the coalition with a beast in it — **she will notice the Owlbear before she notices any of the PCs**, and it is the only moment she relaxes. Play it once, briefly, in the middle of a catastrophe.
- **If her dog dies**, she stops being frightened and becomes something considerably worse. It is the one thing that rouses her.

## Their history — **resolved**

**They know her and she knows them.** Not a debt, not a grudge — **recognition, both ways.** She met them during the Hillsfar arc, when they were adventurers and she was somebody's kennel-master, and then she watched them **free the city.**

That is what she knows them as: **the people who broke Graz'zt's hold on Hillsfar.** Storming the Obsidian Keep, Malkyn Grenefeld, the Abyssal link shattered, the Dark Prince screaming back into the Abyss.

> **Which makes her the only person at the North Gate who has watched this party do this exact thing before — and Graz'zt is in this city.**
>
> She does not make the connection out loud. **She does not have to.** It is why she ran to them instead of to anyone else, and it is sitting underneath every word of her plea.

**How that changes the scene:**

- The module says *"she knows the characters are great heroes."* At this table that is **not reputation — it is testimony.** She was there. She is not repeating what she heard.
- **She is not impressed by heroes.** She dislikes soldiers, dislikes politics, and has watched an entire rebellion happen around her without caring. **She is impressed by these four**, specifically, because she saw them before they were anything and she saw what they did.
- Which means her plea is close to unanswerable, and it should be. **"I didn't know what else to do"** from a stranger is a stranger panicking. From her it means *I have seen you do the impossible once and I am asking you to do it again, and I know exactly what I am asking.*

### The Yestral contrast — play it, it's free

**Both of them were nobodies when the party freed Hillsfar. Only one of them got promoted for it.**

Yestral stood up at the Sporedome muster and publicly praised Daein and the party as heroes and defenders of Hillsfar — in front of seven contingents, attaching himself to their credit. **Coralynn was also there for the actual thing, got nothing, wants nothing, and is still keeping his dogs.**

> She is the un-Yestral, and she is in this story to make him look smaller without ever saying a word about him. **If a player notices, do not confirm it. Let her change the subject to the dog.**

### The first thirty seconds

Give her one line of recognition before the panic — **she should know their names and use the wrong one first**, because she is bad at people and it has been years:

> "You — " *(she points at Bramgrim, then corrects to Daein, then gives up)* "**You lot.** You're the ones from the Keep."
>
> *(and then, because she has no idea how to be graceful about it, straight into it)*
>
> "**They're everywhere.** I didn't know what else to do."
