# Part One — The recap, for players

## The problem this solves

At the Ch24 muster you said: *"She was somebody cryptic you met very early on. She keeps reappearing at critical junctures. If you give me a second, I will tell you when—"* and then couldn't find it, and didn't have the picture either.

Dimos said: **"I remember the GOAT, I don't remember her."**

> **That is not a failure. That is the best thing that could have happened, and you should build the recap on it.** The players remember a blind black goat and cannot remember the child holding it. **The goat is the one that is actually a person.** Their misfiled memory is correct and they don't know why yet.

## The recap — read this

> "Before we start. The little girl with the goat. **Some of you remember the goat.**"
>
> *(let them answer)*
>
> "You met her at **Elventree**, at the festival, before any of this. She was sitting up a tree eating a whole plum pie with the filling running down her chin, in a filthy dress, watching the stage. She was excited about the fiddler. She said — and I'm quoting her —"
>
> **"Goat say she extra good. Goat sayin' I gotta ta hear the song before I meet da Dark Man."**
>
> "You asked what that meant. She said she couldn't tell you. **The goat wouldn't say.** And when the fiddler actually came on, she was gone."
>
> "You saw her again out past **The Stop**, near the Lumbus place, digging in the mud with a stick. Her dress was torn and it had blood on it, and it wasn't hers. She ran over and hugged you. She gave the stick to whoever was wearing the most armour — she said it was a special stick, you could hit things with it and it wouldn't fall apart in the rain."
>
> *(beat)*
>
> "And then she said: **'You beter hurry. Fancy man git bored and that bad for personens. If you'se groeien nuff, I see you in a few tendays, deep under the mud.'**"
>
> "Which is where you saw her next. **Sporedome.** Sitting on a mushroom in the middle of a war council with seven armies in the room, with her arm around a blind black goat, and she was the only one there with no plans and nothing to be frightened of."
>
> "Nobody has ever been able to tell you who she is. **She has never once told you her name** — you know it because other people say it."
>
> "She has not gotten any older."

> **Then stop.** Do not take questions, do not confirm theories, do not let it become a discussion. **The recap's only job is to make them notice they stopped noticing her.**

## If you have five minutes and want it to land harder

Ask the table, before you recap: **"What do you remember about the girl with the goat?"** Let them answer wrong. Then give the recap above. **Their own faulty memory is the horror**, and it costs you nothing to use it.

# Part Two — The appearance log, sourced

**Searched every DDEX3 module JSON in the rpg-library.** She appears in **DDEX3-08**, **DDEX3-10** and **DDEX3-16** — and **not** in DDEX3-14 *Death on the Wall*, DDEX3-04 or DDEX3-15. Her origin is **DDEX1-03 *Shadows over the Moonsea***, with a return in DDEX1-10 *Tyranny in Phlan*.

## Who she is on the record (DDEX3-10 §075, DDEX1-03 §086)

- **Elisande** (ELL-ih-sand). Human female. **Chaotic Neutral.** **Thirteen years old** by DDEX3-10 — twelve when first met. **Years of malnourishment make her smaller than other children her age and she is frequently mistaken for younger.**
- **An orphan and a village outcast** from "a disturbing, isolated island village in the Stormy Bay." Wild dirty hair, pockmarked face. **Her parents died in a sickness that tormented the village.**
- **The village had turned away from the gods and prayed to fiends from the Hells — fiends who granted them very real power.** Generations of it. She is the child who got out.
- **She is fascinated with outsiders.** She calls people ***"Outsides."*** It is the first word she ever said to an adventuring party.
- **"She has few morals and, due to her upbringing, no true concept of right or wrong."** A troublemaker.
- She left the island two years ago and travelled south and then west with the Phlan refugees. **"Traveling where she will, she continues to show up at times of great importance, frequently crossing paths with the characters."**

## The goat on the record (DDEX3-10 §076)

- Found **on the same island**, at the same time.
- **"Appears to be totally blind but for some inexplicable reason seems able to see certain characters, which it tends to follow"** — completely unbothered by things that would frighten a normal animal.
- **"It seems especially attracted to those who are 'pure of heart.'"**
- Nonthreatening. **Most people find it creepy.**
- DDEX3-08's NPC summary lists it as *"Goat (GOTE). Male goat. Goat eat food. Food goat food. Food."* — **which contradicts §379, where the goat is Ausuriel, a woman.** Elisande's own word for it settles the argument. See below.

## ⚠ Three things the party was told years ago and did not hear

**This is the whole reason to run the recap. All three are verbatim module text, already spoken at your table.**

### 1. She told them she was going to meet Graz'zt.

> *"Goat sayin' I gotta ta hear the song before I meet **da Dark Man**."*

**Da Dark Man is the Dark Prince.** At a party in Elventree, years ago, a teenage girl eating pie in a tree told this party that she had been instructed to hear one particular song **before meeting Graz'zt** — and she is about to meet him in U29. **She has been on her way to this room the entire campaign.**

### 2. She told them where and when she'd see them next.

> *"I see you in a few tendays, **deep under the mud**."*

**And she did — at Sporedome.** She also told them to hurry, because **"Fancy man git bored and that bad for personens."**

### 3. **She has been calling the goat her sister the entire time.**

> *"**Schweschder** goat says soon eyes have to do."*

**"Schweschder" is dialect German for *sister*.** Her broken pidgin is full of German — *hebben*, *vergaten*, *groeien*, *personens* — and the word she uses for the goat is **sister**.

> **The §379 reveal is that the goat is a woman. Elisande told them that years ago and nobody was listening.**
>
> **This is the single best thing in this dossier.** Put *"Schweschder goat"* in the recap, in her voice, without translating it. If a player with any German asks, you say nothing. Let it sit until Ausuriel's legs come out from under her.

*(Note the gender contradiction: DDEX3-08's NPC summary calls the goat male. **"Schweschder" and §379 both say female. Go with female** — the male entry is a throwaway joke line in an NPC list.)*

## The goat and "pure of heart" — free, and this campaign should use it

DDEX3-10 §076: the goat **seems able to see certain characters, which it tends to follow, and is especially attracted to those who are "pure of heart."**

> **Which one of them does it look at?** In a party containing a career thief with a false identity, a deceiver chasing FU money, and a ranger who concealed an order for an arc — **the answer is Bramgrim**, and it costs you nothing to have established it retroactively.
>
> Play it once in Part 2 or on the bridge: the blind goat tracking Bramgrim across a room. **Never remark on it.** After §379 it means a Herald of Shiallia has been watching him specifically since Elventree.

# Part Three — What she actually is (GM-only)

**Do not read this section aloud, and do not let it leak before §379.**

## The reveal (§379, verbatim in effect)

The first time Elisande acts in U29, she skips across the room toward one of the paralysed demons, the goat walking calmly behind. **Graz'zt notices them for the first time:**

> **"Do not think I do not see you, Ausuriel. Show us your pretty smile."**
>
> Making a cruel gesture in the air, **the goat falls to the ground in agony and her body melts away** until you see a screaming gnome-like woman with extremely long hair and goat legs.
>
> **"You should have stayed in your woods. You have no power here, Herald of Shiallia."**
>
> Through pain-gritted teeth, the goat-legged woman whispers: **"That is why I brought these heroes and the daughter of the Dark Sun."**
>
> A smile across her blood-splattered face, Elisande **plunges her dagger into the chest of the unmoving demon and pulls out its heart.** With a swirl of scintillating light, the demon disappears and the symbol goes dark. Graz'zt screams in pain. **"What have you done!?!?!?"**
>
> Elisande giggles.

## So:

| | |
|---|---|
| **The goat** | **Ausuriel, a Herald of Shiallia** — a fey, goat-legged, gnome-like, with extremely long hair. **The blindness was the disguise.** She has been escorting a child, in an animal's shape, for the entire campaign. |
| **Elisande** | **"The daughter of the Dark Sun."** The module never explains the phrase, and neither should you. |
| **Why they're here** | **Ausuriel brought them** — the party *and* Elisande — deliberately. *"That is why I brought these heroes."* **The party has been escorted to this fight by a goat.** |
| **What Elisande does** | **Destroys one symbol per round** by stabbing a paralysed demon and pulling out its heart. **She is the fight's damage.** When she runs out of symbols she stabs the nearest foe for 30 magical piercing damage per round. |
| **Ausuriel's mercy (§380)** | Once per fight, if the party is being overwhelmed, she may **sing one clear note: heal 40 HP and free the target from any curse, charm, domination, disease, poison, blindness, deafness, or madness.** Save it for someone who would otherwise be out of the fight. |

## The bridge, before all that (§262)

Their first sight of her in Part 6 is her **sitting on the railing, covered head to toe in blood, with the goat** — and **bloody footprints leading from the castle tower door to the rail.**

> "Willkommenm Outsides! Eyes wishin' to be on greet avontuur wit youse! Wese hab greet fun."

**The broken pidgin is the module's. Keep it exactly.** The blood is not hers, and the footprints lead *out* of the tower the party is about to enter — she has already been in there and come back.

## Both endings (§382)

Win or lose, the module closes on the same line:

> *"…you wonder what became of young Elisande and her goat?"*

**Read it either way. Do not change it.**

# Part Four — Running her before the reveal

- **She has no plans** (§067–073). That is her official, on-the-record contribution to a war council: *she assumes she will find something that entertains her.* **It is true.**
- **She is never anywhere she should be, and never with anyone.**
- **She does not age.** Never say this. Let a player who has been paying attention say it.
- **She is not frightening in the moment.** She is a small girl in a dirty dress who is having a nicer day than anyone else present. **The wrongness is entirely retrospective.**
- **If a player pushes for an answer, she offers the goat.** Holds it out. Waits. That is the complete response and you should not improve on it. *(Muster doc, stage directions.)*
- **The goat never makes a sound.** Establish this by omission, repeatedly, and never remark on it.
- **She waves at exactly one party member** and it should be a different one each time.

> **The single best thing you can do between now and Part 6 is put her somewhere with no scene attached.** Appendix C1 #20 and C4 #10 in the Part 2 prep are both this: on a rock beside the marching column, gone on the way back; or **just the goat, alone in a passage, blind, facing them, not moving until they walk around it.**
>
> **Never explain. Never follow up. Never give her a scene she is the point of** — until §379, when she becomes the point of everything.
