# Grundar Ashmantle — Duergar Slave-Boss, Szith Morcane

> **Status:** Staging (notes/, not canon). Introduced mid-session Jun 28. Link to `notes/session_prep/20260628_szith_morcane_recruitment_tour.md` (Door 4 — the Slaves) and `notes/npcs/rylzyn_veth.md` (his grudge target).

## Identity

- **Race/class:** Duergar — gray dwarf, middleman operator. No combat class needed; his value is information and access.
- **Role in Szith Morcane:** Slave-pen inventory boss. Not a giant, not a guard — the man the giants trust to run logistics on the slave pens. He's been here longer than the fire giants.
- **Visibility:** Invisible to the garrison (duergar are background furniture to a fire giant). Knows the slave pens, the patrol rotation, and who owes what to whom.

## The Grudge (vs. Rylzyn Veth)

Three years ago Rylzyn was a prisoner at Szith Morcane. He bought his escape by giving the giants a list — duergar names, mostly. **Grundar lost two cousins to that list.**

Grundar doesn't know Rylzyn is back yet. When he sees him, he won't shout. He'll go blank-faced, start following at a distance, and send a runner. He's patient. He's been patient for three years.

**Voice — flat, transactional, no heat:**
> *"I wondered if you'd come back. People who run usually do. Something they left behind."*

## Link to Door 4 — The Rilira Gate

Grundar is **already inside the gate** the party is trying to crack. He runs the pen logistics — which means:

- He knows **which pens Rilira is held in** and can route the party straight there, bypassing a patrol.
- He knows **Korkan's schedule** (the hobgoblin slave-boss the party might want to use or remove).
- He is **not loyal to the giants** — he's loyal to his margin. He's a transaction waiting to happen.

**The leverage question:** Rylzyn will not volunteer what he did. The party is standing there when Grundar surfaces. They can:
1. **Buy him off** — he understands transactions; name a price.
2. **Weaponize him** — his pen access plus patrol knowledge makes Door 4 significantly cleaner. Korkan's position becomes irrelevant if Grundar routes the party around him.
3. **Let the grudge play** — Grundar rats out Rylzyn to the garrison if the party doesn't offer him a better deal. Rylzyn's problem becomes the whole party's problem.

**Bramgrim / Akritas angle:** Grundar will mention, once, that the list wasn't only duergar — there were other names on it too. He doesn't say what kind. He leaves it there.

## What he wants

His cousins are dead. He wants Rylzyn to know that he knows, and that the debt isn't forgiven — just priced. His actual ask is probably small (safe passage out, or Rylzyn handed to him after the giants are dead). The interesting version: he wants to walk out of Szith Morcane when this is over, and he'll trade everything he knows for that guarantee.
