# Sorn — Drow Warrior, Quil'bryn's 20

> **Status:** Staging (notes/, not canon). Named mid-session Jun 28. Felkur recruited him to create a distraction on the infiltration path to the drow prisoners.

## Identity

- **Race/class:** Drow warrior (not elite — just good, and angry enough to be reliable).
- **Affiliation:** Quil'bryn's original 20; stayed after the cure.

## What he did

Felkur sent him ahead to clear a corridor of duergar overseers. He killed three. Two survived. He catches up with the party shortly after — calm, blades clean, saying nothing about what's behind him.

**Voice:**
> *"Three. Took less time than your plan."*
> *(when the tail comes out)* *"I said three. I meant three dead. The other two are a different count."*

## The Tail

The two surviving duergar are following him through the fungus farms. They did **not** call the giants — duergar don't admit they got beaten by one drow, and calling a patrol means explaining why they let it happen. This is personal. They want satisfaction, not a report.

They're staying in the terrace rows, waiting for a moment Sorn is briefly alone. **Sorn knows they're back there.** He's been keeping the party between himself and them. He hasn't mentioned it.

## Arrival at Door 4

Sorn reaches the slave pen with the party — and his tail arrives with him. The two duergar overseers are now in the slave dwellings, furious and cornered: they can't call for help without exposing their failure, but they can't back off either. They are an armed problem that has to be resolved quietly before or during the Door 4 beat.

**The Grundar collision:** if Grundar Ashmantle sees two of his duergar crew trailing a drow with murder-face into the slave dwellings, he has leverage over everyone. The two who didn't report are now his to squeeze. His price just went up. See `notes/npcs/grundar_ashmantle.md`.

## Character note

He's offended that no one came to confirm the path was clear. By the time the party reaches him he's been doing math in the dark about whether they trust him. The math has conclusions.

- If **Felkur** arrives: fine. Felkur asked him. That's the relationship.
- If **Rylzyn** arrives: different conversation. Sorn has opinions about Rylzyn that predate this mission. He won't say them unprompted. He will say them if asked.
