# Rylzyn Veth — Drow Elite Warrior, Quil'bryn Loyalist

> **Status:** Staging (notes/, not canon). Named mid-session Jun 28. From Quil'bryn's gang of 20; attached to the party as guide for the Szith Morcane infiltration.

## Identity

- **Race/class:** Drow elite warrior (male).
- **House:** Veth — dissolved two generations ago. He still uses the name. Vanity, or something to prove.
- **Affiliation:** One of Quil'bryn's original 20; stayed after the cure; now guilt-adjacent-loyal.

## Character note

He was a prisoner inside Szith Morcane before — he escaped by giving the giants a list of names (duergar mostly; possibly others — see Grundar). He's been back in the field since and doesn't volunteer this.

**Committed to drow freedom. Not to slave liberation broadly.** At Door 4, he finds a wall to watch. He's not cruel about it — just focused. His math: twenty drow, two hundred slaves, these are not the same problem.

**Voice:**
> *"There are two hundred of them and twenty of us. We are not the same kind of problem."*

## The Grudge

**Grundar Ashmantle** (duergar slave-boss, Szith Morcane) lost two cousins to Rylzyn's list. Grundar is already inside the outpost. When he sees Rylzyn, he won't shout — he'll go blank and send a runner. See `notes/npcs/grundar_ashmantle.md` for the full beat and Door 4 leverage options.

Rylzyn knows Grundar is in there. He hasn't mentioned it.
