# MEMORANDUM OF GRIEVANCE

**Bureau of Infernal Accounts — Maerimydra Branch**

> **TO:** Graz'zt, the Dark Prince, Demon Lord of the 45th Layer, &c., &c.
> **FROM:** Vraxen, Arcanaloth, Host of the Spire of Inevitable Reckoning, Keeper of the Lexicon of Oaths, Auditor of the Maerimydra Account
> **RE:** The catastrophic and ongoing fiscal hemorrhage occasioned by your vendetta against four (4) mortals
> **CLASSIFICATION:** Private. Sealed. Not to be entered into the Lexicon.
> **CURRENCY OF RECORD:** soulcoins (sc). Every figure herein has been audited twice. They are correct. They are *always* correct.

---

My Prince,

I have served this account with perfect ledgers for longer than you have held this layer's lieutenancy in the field. In all that time I have never once submitted a grievance. I submit one now.

I write to inform you, in figures you cannot wave a six-fingered hand at, that your obsession with these surface vermin — this *ranger*, this *cleric*, this *liar with a mandolin*, and the trembling **gnome** who has somehow become the load-bearing column of every disaster in two tiers of the cosmos — has become an **abject, total, and unrecoverable drain upon the resources of this dominion.**

You scream of vengeance. You scream beautifully; no one disputes it. But screaming does not *balance*. And I am the one who must balance it.

## Let us be precise, since you will not be.

You sent these four against Hillsfar and they did not merely defeat your agent there — they **assassinated the First Lord of the city in his own camp**, collapsed your political instrument into a grief-maddened ruin, and when that ruin finally cracked your link to the Material Plane, *you were thrown screaming back into the Abyss by a dwarf and an archer.* That was levels one through ten, my Prince. That was the **warm-up.** And every soulcoin of it is itemized below, because someone in this organization keeps the books, and it is not you.

Then — *then* — having lost the surface, you reached down here, into **my** city, into **my** Spire, and invited them into **my** accounts. And what did your vendetta purchase?

- They turned my own **dinner** into a three-way brawl. My linens. My dishware. My **dignity**, Lord, which does not appear on a balance sheet but which I am billing you for regardless.
- They **defaced the Lexicon of Oaths** — the *medium of contract itself* — striking one name and forging another, an act of fiscal heresy so profound I had to sit down.
- They stole my **private journal.** (We will not discuss the journal. The journal is not relevant. I trust the Prince's discretion as I trust my own, and I will be *watching* to confirm it.)
- They extracted my personal **scent** from a domesticated **owlbear** and used it to defeat my own golem. I cannot adequately convey to you the indignity of being burgled by your own smell.

And the centerpiece of your grand campaign of retribution — the **Rogue Thought**, your treasured pet avenger, your co-signatory in vengeance — *severed his own tentacles and fled to another dimension* the moment they cornered him. That is the caliber of ally your hatred attracts, my Prince. Self-amputating cowards who plane-shift the instant the invoice comes due.

## The drain, stated plainly.

The Maerimydra account is presently in arrears **one hundred fifty-eight thousand, two hundred ninety-nine soulcoins** against these four mortals, **not counting** the personal claim you have reserved for yourself — which I remind you remains *uncollected*, because every agent you dispatch to collect it either dies, flees, or, in the case of your fire giants, gets **shrunk, stuffed into a portable hole, and drained for ritual fuel by the very drow we are meant to be exterminating.**

This is not a war, my Prince. A war has a balance sheet that occasionally tilts the other way. This is a **subscription.** You are paying, in perpetuity, for the privilege of being humiliated by people who do not even know your full title.

I have honored every contract you have ever handed me. I will honor this one too — I will keep the books, I will dress the golems, I will smile my fox's smile at your business dinners. But I will not pretend, in a sealed memorandum to my own master, that this vendetta is anything other than what the figures say it is.

**It is a loss. It has always been a loss. And I am tired of being the only entity in Maerimydra honest enough to write it down.**

The exhibits follow. Read them. Or do not, and scream — but understand that when you scream, *I hear a number.*

— **V.**
*Arcanaloth, and the only thing in this city that still adds up.*
