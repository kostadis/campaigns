# Chapter 19 Drow Allies and Dangerous Decisions

Date: 2026-06-29

## Summary
The battle at the outpost came to a swift and brutal close. The party and their Drow allies moved with lethal precision, cutting down the Azure Sentinels one by one before turning their full attention to the fire giant, who lay helpless on the ground — poisoned, prone, and outlined in shimmering fairy fire. Bramgrim called forth a spectral weapon that hovered and struck at the massive creature while Akritas closed the distance to loose her arrows at point-blank range. Felkur unleashed a torrent of freezing energy, and the Drow warriors piled on with their own devastating strikes. When the dust settled, the giant had been brought down without a single member of the party falling, and the group took a moment to appreciate just how fearsome their Drow allies truly were.

With the fighting done, the party turned their attention to the task ahead. Maps of Szith Morcane were studied — a sprawling Drow outpost carved into a massive chasm, layered with spider webs, fungus farms, barracks, and fortified noble houses. They had three days before the missing patrol would be noticed, and they intended to use every hour. After debating whether to smash through the heavily guarded main gate or find a quieter way in, the group agreed to sneak into the main cavern through the old bazaar. When one of their Drow guides questioned why they were bothering to free the slaves at all, Daein explained plainly that they weren't truly freeing anyone — they were giving the slaves hope and telling them just enough of the truth to get them moving. The Drow guide, unimpressed with this approach, suggested that killing five slaves in excruciating fashion and threatening the next five would be far more efficient, a proposal the party dismissed on purely practical grounds.

The infiltration began carefully. Akritas borrowed Felkur's thieves' tools and, with the quiet blessing of Bramgrim's divine guidance and a surge of confidence from Daein's bardic inspiration, picked the lock on the first gate leading into the tunnels beneath the bazaar. Inside, a massive Behir lay sleeping in the middle of the chamber, its coiled body surrounded by the scattered bones of those the giants had discarded. Akritas wrapped the group in a supernatural veil of silence and shadow, then crept ahead alone to unlock the second gate, slipping past the slumbering creature without so much as disturbing the air around it. She returned to the group, and for a moment it seemed the whole party might pass through undetected — until Bramgrim and one of the Drow guides stumbled, and the sound of their misstep echoed through the chamber like a thunderclap.

The Behir's eyes snapped open. It roared and lunged, crashing toward the Owlbear with terrifying speed, but the great beast twisted free of its grasp. The party didn't wait to see what came next — they ran, pouring through the door and slamming it shut behind them. As they caught their breath on the other side, one of the Drow guides remarked, almost casually, that they might have noticed the bones scattered across the floor — that chamber was where the giants disposed of slaves they no longer wanted to deal with. The party filed that information away and pressed on into the fungus farms.

The farms stretched across three terraced levels, where slaves of a dozen different races — Drow, Duergar, and others — toiled under the watchful eyes of Drow and Duergar overseers. The party needed to speak with the laborers, but the overseers stood in the way. One of their Drow allies stepped forward, puffed himself up, and declared himself a noble of House Blob, berating the overseers for their incompetence until the flustered guards followed him away from their posts. With the path clear, the party approached the slaves, who expressed a fierce willingness to revolt — but only if Szith Morcane itself would be returned to them. They were directed to find Rilira, a Drow woman organizing the resistance from within the slave pens, whose word alone could set the entire uprising in motion. The party also learned that the Fire Giant ruler was rumored to consort with demons and was forcing three enslaved stone giants to build him a palace — potential allies waiting in chains. As the group slipped away, Akritas leaned close to Bramgrim and Daein and growled that one false step from their Drow guides would be all the excuse she needed, a warning that hung in the air long after she had moved on.

## Locations
### Szith Morcane
A Drow outpost built into a massive chasm, featuring layers of spider webs, fungus farms, barracks, and fortified houses. The party studied maps of its layout and infiltrated its upper levels during this session.

### The Old Bazaar
A chamber within Szith Morcane containing two locked gates and a sleeping Behir. The party used it as a stealthy entry point into the main cavern, though Bramgrim and a Drow guide woke the creature while passing through.

### Fungus Farms
Three terraced levels within Szith Morcane where slaves of various races, including Drow, Duergar, and other humanoids, tend fields of fungus under the watch of Drow and Duergar overseers. The party made contact with the slaves here.

### Slave Pens
The area where the laborers of Szith Morcane are housed. It is the base of operations for the slave leader Rilira and home to three stone giant slaves when they are not working on the Fire Giant's palace.

## NPCs
### Drow Elite Warriors
Skilled Underdark combatants allied with the party, wielding hand crossbows loaded with sleep poison and short swords. Two accompany the party into Szith Morcane. They are pragmatic and ruthless, suggesting brutal methods of slave control and expressing confusion over the party's use of political manipulation. One successfully bluffs the fungus farm overseers by posing as a high-ranking noble from 'House Blob.'

### Azure Sentinels
Guards at the outpost who were targeted and eliminated by the party and their Drow allies during the opening combat.

### Fire Giant
A massive foe found already poisoned, prone, and glowing with fairy fire. The party coordinated a devastating ambush against him, bringing him down without suffering any casualties.

### Behir
A massive, multi-legged serpentine creature found sleeping in the tunnels of the old bazaar. It woke after Bramgrim and a Drow guide made noise while sneaking past, and lunged at the Owlbear before the party fled through the door.

### Rilira
A Drow slave leader who is organizing the laborers within the slave pens. A slave spokesperson in the fungus farms directed the party to find her, saying that if she gives the order, all the slaves will rise up.

### Stone Giants
Three enslaved giants who reside in the slave pens when they are not forced to work on the Fire Giant's new palace. Mentioned by a slave spokesperson in the fungus farms as a potential asset for the revolt.
