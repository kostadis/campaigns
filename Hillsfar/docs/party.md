# Party Overview

**Current location:** **inside Szith Morcane**, having taken the slave pens (end of Chapter 21). Their Underdark refuge is Sporedome; their surface base is Hillsfar, where Charles is building Felkur's teleportation-circle guild in their absence.

**Standing force:** Quil'bryn's twenty drow (commanded by a man sworn to Felkur personally), the slave uprising under Rilira, Grundar Ashmantle's duergar, and the Hidden under Hamad — the last two conditional. Attached drow guides: **Rylzyn Veth** and **Sorn**.

**Active quests:**
- **Kill Denagor**, the fire giant king of Szith Morcane. Hamad's precondition for the Hidden's support and the practical requirement for the uprising.
- **Arm the slaves.** Rilira has asked how the drow intend to arm her followers and has not been answered. The forge in the **House Morcane ruins** is the standing option — seize it or destroy it.
- **Recruit the Nerevenans**, the fourth faction and the only one untouched. Complicated: they worship a goddess of death whose altar the party previously destroyed, and Bramgrim and Akritas were ruled out as envoys.
- **Salvage or write off the Lodge of Faces.** Felkur shattered the stone giants' sacred crystal and the party fled. Three of their giants are still enslaved building Denagor's palace.
- **March on Maerimydra** (strategic objective), staged from Sporedome — now urgent for a new reason: per Alder, **Maerimydra is Graz'zt's fallback** if the Menzoberranzan operation succeeds.
- **The Arcanaloth's ledger.** The Emerald Enclave's High Council wants access; the party agreed to attempt it only if the opportunity is safe.
- **Open threats:** Solom Ned'razak is dead and his blood-purge ritual is orphaned; Felkur's voided Maram contract and the devil owed his location; the Rogue Thought (+ Graz'zt), the Githyanki Knight, and a loose Mezzaloth all sworn to revenge; Quil'bryn's plan to spend the recruited factions as a shield, which the party agreed to conceal.

**Collective resources:** Wand of the War Mage +2 (Glor's reward), Shard of Crystallized Faerzress, stolen runic key (Spire vaults), Vraxen's betrayal journal, the Soulcoin Ledger, blank infernal contracts, three bottles of rare wine, the weapon-cache treasure taken for the Hidden's trial, Bramgrim's sword signed by the Harper Alder, Potion of Fire Giant Strength, Potion of Flying, assorted potions/scrolls. Owlbear companion active. Glor's siege-weapon sabotage intel still unused.

**Group reputation:** Underground, they are now a political force rather than a contract crew — Felkur's speech saved the drow resistance and a cured assassin swore his sword to him personally. Above ground, Felkur remains a figure of Mulmaster legend as the "Phantom Thief" while his true identity stays hidden. The Githyanki Knight has named the party a "permanent enemy," a Rogue Thought identified Bramgrim as their "greatest magical threat," and the stone giants of the Lodge of Faces have every reason to name them desecrators.

**Standing resolution (Ch21):** never split the party again. Splitting for the Hidden's trial cost them a silent entry — Felkur's Faerzress shard and tools would have opened the cache lock without the alarm.

## Characters

### Akritas
**High Elf Ranger 11** — player: kostadis1

A focused hunter who prefers action over words, specializing in ranged archery with her oath-sworn Oathbow, and increasingly the party's lockpick as well as its artillery — she opened both bazaar gates and the Faerzress-worded weapon cache. A loyal but reluctant operative of the Emerald Enclave, she **concealed a High Council order to obtain an Arcanaloth's ledger for an entire arc**, surrendering it only to an insight check in Ch17. Her distrust of the drow allies is open and unhidden: she has told Bramgrim and Daein that one false step is all the excuse she needs.

**Notable relationships:** Emerald Enclave (ranked operative; access to scouts and supplies — and now a disclosed private brief). Travels closely with Daein, Felkur, and the Owlbear. Held the weapon cache alone with Bramgrim during the Hidden's trial, killing the hellhound and both azers. Openly hostile toward Rylzyn Veth and Sorn.

**Items of significance:** Oathbow (attuned), Bracers of Archery, +1 Studded Leather.

*(Pronoun note resolved: she/her. Chapters 17, 19 and 21 are uniform; the earlier source inconsistency is closed.)*

### Bramgrim Stoutale
**Hill Dwarf Life Cleric 11** — player: kostadis1

A polite, respectful dwarf who serves as the party's primary healer, area-controller, and resident expert on Underdark lore and illusions. Once a strict "healing-only" cleric, he was shamed by Daein into embracing offensive necrotic and radiant magic, and now finishes enemies himself while still anchoring the party's survival. His *Spirit Guardians* consumed the hobgoblin Corkin outright when Corkin turned on him in spite — the party's verdict was that he "died of entirely natural causes." Took two flaming-sword hits from the fire giant overseer in the slave pens. Also fumbled the sneak past the sleeping Behir, and asked the legendary Harper Alder to autograph his sword.

**Notable relationships:** Close combat partner of Daein; protects Felkur. Paired with Akritas for the entire Hidden negotiation and trial. Cousins to the Rockseeker brothers (Gundren, Tharden, Nundo) — his strongest personal bonds. Immediately suspicious of Glor. Identified by the Rogue Thought as the party's "greatest magical threat."

**Items of significance:** Mithral Plate, Javelin of Lightning, Ring of Evasion (attuned), Solom's Pendant (its authority now posthumous), his sword — signed by Alder. Rides the Owlbear when the water runs deep.

### Daein
**Human Fighter 9 / Bard 2** — player: kostadis1

A mysterious warrior-poet from "Far Far Away" whose origins are unclear even to him, blending martial power, psionic/telekinetic tricks, and bardic deception. He is the party's face and master deceiver, and this arc he used it twice at decisive scale: he forced **Quil'bryn's braincap ultimatum** in front of the assembled drow — the single act that restored the resistance's leadership — and he talked **Corkin** into a suicidal charge, then held the party back and let the giant's azers destroy the hobgoblins before striking. He states the party's ethics without decoration: *"We're not going to release the slaves. We just have to give them hope and tell them half the truth."* He declined a political post in Hillsfar's new order and is chasing "FU money."

**Notable relationships:** Frequent combat partner and banter-rival of Bramgrim (whom he shamed into fighting). Collaborator with Felkur on illusion and deception. Agreed with Quil'bryn to keep the shield doctrine from the recruited factions. One of only two viable envoys to the Nerevenans.

**Items of significance:** +1 Plate Armor, +1 Longsword, Belt of Frost Giant Strength (Str 23), Canaith Mandolin (casts *Invisibility*; spell charges), Goggles of Night, stolen runic key.

### Felkur Oldenwood
**Rock Gnome Artillerist Artificer 11** — player: kostadis1

A guild artisan with a complicated past who maintains a "simple gnome artificer" cover while privately owning his secret legend as Mulmaster's "Phantom Thief." This arc turned him into a political figure by accident: his rallying speech from atop a table saved the collapsing drow resistance, and the cured assassin **Quil'bryn swore his sword to Felkur personally** rather than to the party. He proposed **"Stargate,"** a commercial network of teleportation circles, and handed the business to Charles. He read Grundar Ashmantle's hunger for freedom under a blood feud and bought a duergar contingent with a personal guarantee of escape, and bypassed a cell's guards to negotiate terms directly with Rilira. He also **shattered the stone giants' flawless crystal Skoraeus Stonebones with a thrown beer bottle** while trying only to chip it — *"I think I broke him."*

**Notable relationships:** Quil'bryn (sworn to him personally). Grundar Ashmantle (personal guarantee of escape). Charles (business partner, building Stargate through Isthana Rao). Maram il Vass (Yugoloth contract-master, now technically double-crossed — detection unknown). Daein (illusion/deception partner). Bramgrim (protector). Vraxen (arcanaloth enemy). Isthana Rao (gifted him rare wine).

**Items of significance:** Wand of the War Mage +1 (sheet; note the party's Ch16 reward was a Wand of the War Mage +2), All-Purpose Tool +2, +1 Studded Leather (all attuned), Spell-Storing Item, eldritch cannon (cold/flamethrower infusions, ice bullets), Shard of Crystallized Faerzress, thieves' tools, pocket bottles of beer.

## Party Dynamics

Four veteran adventurers who function as a tight, complementary unit despite divergent agendas. Bramgrim is the moral anchor and healer; Akritas the reconnaissance, ranged-damage core and — increasingly — the lockpick; Daein the social and deception engine; Felkur the magical-engineering specialist who has become, without particularly intending to, the resistance's figurehead. Daein and Bramgrim share the strongest in-combat partnership; Felkur and Daein run the party's con games; Bramgrim and Akritas were paired for the whole Hidden arc and fought a patrol to a standstill together.

Shared goal: break Szith Morcane, then Maerimydra and Graz'zt. But the arc has loaded three tensions that are no longer merely latent. **Akritas** concealed an Emerald Enclave order for an entire arc and has openly told two of her companions she will kill the drow allies at the first excuse. **The party has agreed to spend its own recruits** — Quil'bryn's shield doctrine, kept from the slaves, the Hidden, and everyone else who signed on — while Rilira has promised there is nowhere in the Underdark or on the surface they would be safe if they betrayed her. And **Felkur now holds a personal oath that the rest of the party does not**, alongside a criminal past, a devil owed his location, and a business incorporating above ground in his absence.
