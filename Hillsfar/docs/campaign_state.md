## Completed Encounters & Quests (DDEX-04)

*Definitive list of finished content. Do NOT replay or re-offer. Most recent last.*

- **The Enclave's summons (Ch16 close, Hillsfar).** A telepathic message from the Emerald Enclave reached Akritas: the fire giants holding Szith Morcane are near completion and have turned toward the Dalelands and Hillsfar. *Consequence:* the party is tasked to reach Szith Morcane and link up with the drow resistance under Murssvayas Dhuurniv. **This is where DDEX-04 starts.**
- **Charles's briefing & the Stargate pitch (Ch17, Hillsfar).** Charles — now in full French-resistance persona, beret and affected accent — briefed the party in a tavern he insisted on calling a café, reminding them of Graz'zt's six-fingered symbol. *Consequence:* Felkur pitched **"Stargate,"** a commercial teleportation-circle network; Charles dropped the persona entirely and took over guild paperwork, tax structure, and land acquisition through Isthana Rao while the party is underground.
- **Through the Waydown (Ch17).** An Emerald Enclave contact vouched for the party past the Red Plume guards.
- **Alder's interception (Ch17, Underdark).** The Harper **Alder** — halfling, head of Harper operations across the Underdark — materialized on the march. *Consequence:* she explained that **Gromph Baenre's** spell, cast to free Lolth, tore the Abyss open and flooded the world with demon lords; revealed the five adventurers she sent at Menzoberranzan (Celia, Mor, Spots, Draco, Connor); named the party her **"backup plan to the backup plan"** against Graz'zt's line of retreat to Maerimydra; told them Solom was dead. She signed Bramgrim's sword, then teleported the whole party days deep into the Underdark.
- **The weeping Galeb Duhr (Ch17).** Met **Grek**, a Galeb Duhr with a half-carved stone giant face and a shattered nose, exiled from the Lodge of Faces by the elder **Decha** for imperfection. *Consequence:* the party talked him out of his despair; he gave directions to the Lodge and later served as guide and translator among the stone giants.
- **The forge escapees (Ch17).** A mixed band of drow, quaggoths and duergar picking over dead slavers turned out to be escaped forge slaves. *Consequence:* they pointed the party to the resistance camp.
- **The fractured resistance & Quil'bryn's braincap trial (Ch17).** Found the camp in shambles: **Solom Ned'razak had been murdered eight days earlier** by the drow warrior **Quil'bryn**, with half the drow standing by. Felkur's speech from atop a table (nat 20) rallied them; Daein forced a public ultimatum — eat the braincap mushroom or face the consequences. *Consequence:* the mushroom **cured Quil'bryn's Graz'zt-induced madness**; he pledged his sword to **Felkur personally**. Twenty drow committed. Four recruitment targets named: the Hidden, the Lodge of Faces, the Nerevenans, the escaped slaves. Akritas revealed a concealed Emerald Enclave order to obtain an Arcanaloth's ledger.
- **Outpost ambush finished (Ch19).** Party and drow allies wiped out the azer sentinels *(rendered "Azure Sentinels" in the export)*, a hellhound, and a fire giant caught poisoned, prone and lit by fairy fire. *Consequence:* no casualties; a **three-day clock** before the missing patrol is noticed; two drow elite warriors (**Rylzyn Veth** and **Sorn**) attached as guides.
- **Infiltration through the old bazaar (Ch19).** Akritas picked both gates with Felkur's tools under Guidance and Bardic Inspiration, and slipped past a sleeping **Behir** under Pass Without Trace. *Consequence:* Bramgrim and a drow guide both fumbled the group crossing and woke it; the party fled and sealed the door. The chamber is where the giants dispose of unwanted slaves.
- **Fungus farms contact (Ch19).** A drow ally bluffed the overseers away posing as a noble of **"House Blob."** *Consequence:* the slaves will revolt, but only if Szith Morcane is returned to them; party directed to **Rilira**; learned the fire giant ruler consorts with demons and has three enslaved stone giants building his palace.
- **Liberation of the slave pens (Ch21).** Sorn cleared the path; **Grundar Ashmantle** recognized Rylzyn Veth as the man who bought his freedom by killing Grundar's cousins, but Felkur read his hunger for freedom under the feud and bought his cooperation with a personal guarantee of escape — at which point a large concealed body of armed duergar stood down. The hobgoblin **Corkin** tried to claim the uprising; Felkur bypassed the guards straight into Rilira's cell to take her terms. Daein sold Corkin a joint charge, then the party held back and let the giant and azers shred the hobgoblins before striking. *Consequence:* **Corkin dead** (consumed by Bramgrim's *Spirit Guardians* after turning on him in spite), **the fire giant overseer dead** (it wounded Bramgrim twice with a flaming sword first), and **Rilira's alliance sealed.**
- **The Hidden's trial (Ch21).** Bramgrim and Akritas made themselves conspicuous on the Webs until a drow scout routed them to the gnome ruins and **Hamad**. He demanded proof by cunning: rob a giant-held weapon cache behind a Faerzress-worded lock. Akritas picked it but tripped a silent alarm; the pair stood their ground and destroyed a hellhound and two azers. *Consequence:* treasure secured, **Hamad pledged the Hidden's support — conditional on Dengor's death.**
- **The Cathedral of Faces (Ch21).** Daein and Felkur reached the stone giants' gallery of deliberately flawed faces to find **Decha** catatonic before a flawless crystal carving of Skoraeus Stonebones. Grek explained the doctrine: to carve a perfect face is to carve a tomb. The giants could not deface their own god's image. Felkur threw a pocket bottle of beer at it (nat 20) and **shattered the sacred statue.** *Consequence:* Decha collapsed, the giants froze, and the party fled believing they had destroyed the alliance.
- **The Lodge of Faces alliance — SECURED (Ch22).** Grek rose out of the rock not in rage but in **relief**: no stone giant could raise a hand to the artifact ("It is doctrine. It is the *first* doctrine"), and Felkur's reckless act ended **seven years** of the elder's madness. *Consequence:* Decha is lucid, humiliated among the carvings he defaced during the madness, and **pledged his giants to the party's cause** in exchange for one thing — free his enslaved kin from Dengor's palace. He paid a bag of uncut gems (~200 gp) and made a formal stone giant apology (a lowered head, which Grek had to translate). Felkur's advice on the ruined carvings — put them in "a cave for things you don't want anymore" — the king found profound.
- **The coalition morality debate (Ch22).** Daein and Felkur argued for allying with an evil death cult; Bramgrim resisted, then relented on a dwarven myth about gods of good and evil sharing a meal rather than starve, rationalizing the cultists as a meat shield to be dealt with after Graz'zt. Akritas agreed flatly: her duty is the Realms, not purging the Underdark. *Consequence:* the party is on record, internally, as willing to spend the Nerevenans.
- **The Nerevenans recruited on a lie (Ch22).** Quil'bryn sent **Rylzyn Veth** as escort only to the edge of cult territory. In the bone-littered **crypts of the drow governors**, the drow **Asala** brought the party before **Velasta T'sarran**, priestess of **Kiaransalee** and a **revenant** — undead, unkillable until her vengeance is satisfied. She demanded Solom Ned'razak, who betrayed and murdered her mother, her sister, and herself; sensing he was already dead, she demanded to know who killed him. **Daein told her the fire giants did it.** *Consequence:* Velasta swore to destroy every fire giant she finds. **The party's most powerful ally is bound to them by a lie that Quil'bryn's own men could break.**
- **The Inverted Tower (Ch22).** Murssvayas suggested memory-altering magic might be in Solom's private quarters and handed over **Solom's arcane mark** (a signet ring set with a black jewel) and the password **"Kural"** to pass the wards. On the fourth level the party found a weak, pale drow on a throne: **Solom Ned'razak's magical clone**, a contingency laid long before Quil'bryn's knife. *Consequence:* he confirmed **memory magic almost certainly will not work on a revenant** (identity bound too tightly to purpose); laughed himself breathless when Daein casually admitted promising Szith Morcane to every faction leader — *"You would have made a wonderful [drow]"* — and gifted the party **a spellbook usable as scrolls** (including *Modify Memory*).
- **Scouting the Forge of Surtr (Ch22).** Murssvayas's mages turned the party to vapor. *Consequence:* they drifted through the forge — a cavern the size of a keep's great hall, ceiling lost in smoke, channels of molten rock worked by azer, salamanders and a helmed horror — and found rows of **Hellfire projectors**: serpent-jawed ballistae of steel and darkwood, **three crated for shipment and four half-assembled.**
- **The fall of King Dengor (Ch22).** Felkur dropped gaseous form to cast *Arcane Eye*, found Dengor asleep below with fire giant guards, helmed horrors and shackled stone giants, then set the ambush: *Evard's Black Tentacles* on the sleeping king, the **Owlbear** biting through the stone giants' shackles, Akritas opening with a critical *Lightning Arrow*, Bramgrim anchoring with *Guardian of Faith*. The freed stone giants turned on their captors and began to lose; Daein and Felkur briefly debated abandoning them before remembering Decha's price, at which point Felkur cast *Passwall* to open a shortcut and Bramgrim came through with *Mass Healing Word*. **Daein killed Dengor with *Vicious Mockery* — mocked the fire giant king to death.** *Consequence:* Dengor dead, the palace garrison destroyed, the enslaved stone giants freed and allied, the Owlbear severely wounded.
