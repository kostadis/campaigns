## CAMPAIGN SNAPSHOT — Where Things Stand Now

**Szith Morcane has been decapitated.** The party (now **12th level**) killed **King Dengor** in his own bedchamber beneath the **Forge of Surtr**, and every one of the four recruitment targets named back at the resistance camp is now committed. The coalition is complete. It is also held together by two lies and a promise made four times over.

The Ch21 disaster turned out not to be one. When Felkur shattered the flawless crystal Skoraeus Stonebones with a thrown beer bottle and the party fled, they believed they had destroyed the stone giant alliance. **Grek followed them out of the rock to tell them the opposite:** no stone giant could raise a hand to a sacred image of their own god — *"It is doctrine. It is the first doctrine"* — and Felkur's recklessness had ended **seven years** of the elder's madness in one throw. **Decha is lucid**, grateful, humiliated by the carvings he defaced during those years, and has pledged his giants. His price — free his enslaved kin from Dengor's palace — was paid in the same session.

The fourth faction cost more. The **Nerevenans** are the Kiaransalee cult in the crypts of the drow governors, and their priestess **Velasta T'sarran** is a **revenant**: undead, unkillable until her vengeance is satisfied, and hunting **Solom Ned'razak** for the murders of her mother, her sister, and herself. Sensing Solom already dead, she demanded his killer. **Daein told her the fire giants did it.** She swore to destroy every fire giant she meets. The truth — that **Quil'bryn** killed Solom — is now a secret the drow are actively suppressing, and Solom's own clone says *Modify Memory* will not work on a revenant.

Because **Solom Ned'razak is not gone.** In the **Inverted Tower**, on the fourth level past wards keyed to his signet ring and the password *"Kural,"* the party found a pale, weak drow on a throne: a **magical clone**, contingency laid long before the knife. Same memories, same pragmatism. He gave them a spellbook to burn as scrolls, and laughed himself breathless when Daein admitted he had promised Szith Morcane to every faction leader in the coalition.

The decapitation strike itself was clean. Murssvayas's mages turned the party to vapor; they drifted through the forge — azer at the molten channels, salamanders, rows of serpent-jawed **Hellfire projectors** crated for the surface — and down into the palace. *Evard's Black Tentacles* pinned the sleeping king, the **Owlbear** bit through the stone giants' shackles, Akritas opened with a critical *Lightning Arrow*, and **Daein finished Dengor with *Vicious Mockery*** — mocked a fire giant king to death.

**Then Velasta walked in.** She came into the tail of the fight screaming Dengor's name and ***banished* a fire giant still standing** — her oath, kept, in front of witnesses. Then she turned and demanded to know who killed the king, because a stolen kill is also a debt. The party's most powerful ally is standing in a room full of bodies asking a question they cannot answer honestly, and the giant she took off the board is coming back.

**Immediate next beat:** survive the room. Then decide who actually gets Szith Morcane, whether the Forge of Surtr burns or gets seized to arm the slaves, and how long a lie survives inside a coalition with a revenant in it.

## PLAYER CHARACTERS & COMPANIONS

Tactical support, infiltration, and the party's engineer — and increasingly its accidental head of state. His table-top speech saved the drow resistance (nat 20) and **Quil'bryn swore to him personally**, not to the party.

## ALLIES & PATRONS

### Solom Ned'razak — Drow Archwizard (the clone)
Deposed ruler of Szith Morcane and the party's original patron-in-chief. **The man was assassinated by Quil'bryn** eight days before the Ch17 arrival, while half the resistance watched. **A magical clone of him is alive on the fourth level of the Inverted Tower**, carrying his memories, personality and ruthless pragmatism. He confirmed *Modify Memory* is unlikely to touch a revenant, gifted the party a spellbook to use as scrolls, and laughed until he could barely breathe at Daein's promise-everything diplomacy — *"You would have made a wonderful [drow]."* **His ten-day blood-purge ritual completed** at Sporedome, on Brandyr's fiendborne blood, while the party was on the road to the Rogue Thought — his life's work finished before Quil'bryn's knife found him.

### Velasta T'sarran — Revenant priestess of Kiaransalee, leader of the Nerevenans
Pallid skin that does not look living, eyes burning with fierce intelligence. **A revenant** — bound to the world by an unfinished vengeance and unkillable until it is satisfied. Solom Ned'razak betrayed and murdered her mother, her sister, and her. She sensed he was already dead and demanded his killer; **Daein told her the fire giants did it**, and she swore to destroy every fire giant she finds. She entered the tail of the battle at Dengor's palace screaming his name and **cast *Banishment* on a fire giant still fighting** — proof, in front of the party, that her oath is not rhetoric. Then she demanded to know who killed the king, **because a kill she claimed was taken from her.** Prep material has her wanting Szith Morcane itself as a death-cult holding. The most powerful and least controllable ally the party has ever taken.

### Asala — Drow inquisitor, Nerevenan intermediary
Intercepted the party in the crypts and brought them before Velasta. Prep material gives her a grief of her own — a dead lover.

### Decha — Stone Giant Elder & King, Lodge of Faces
**Lucid, allied, and ashamed.** For seven years he sat before a flawless crystal Skoraeus Stonebones the size of a war shield — a perfect face, and therefore a tomb — while his giants stood in the gallery unable to help, because the first doctrine forbids raising a hand to a sacred image. He exiled **Grek** for imperfection during that madness and defaced his own carvings. **Felkur's thrown beer bottle broke the trance.** He has pledged his giants to the party's cause; his one condition — free his enslaved kin from Dengor's palace — was met the same session. He paid a bag of uncut gems (~200 gp) and offered a formal stone giant apology, which is a lowered head and needs a translator.

### Grek — Galeb Duhr, exile of the Lodge of Faces
Sentient rock whose right half is carved into a stone giant's face with a shattered nose. Exiled by Decha for imperfection and weeping about it when the party found him. **He is the reason the stone giant alliance exists**: he came out of the rock after the Cathedral to explain that the shattering was a mercy and not a desecration, and he translates giant custom for the party.

### Quil'bryn — Drow elite warrior, resistance commander
**Killed Solom Ned'razak** under Graz'zt's madness, then insisted publicly he was sane. The braincap mushroom cured him mid-boast in front of the assembled drow; he drew his sword and **pledged to Felkur personally**. Now the drow's field commander: cold, competent, and openly willing to spend the recruited factions as a shield — he told the party the Nerevenans will probably be the first to fall. **He is also the secret at the center of the Velasta lie**, and has ordered his troops to say nothing about how Solom really died. Rilira sneers at his name.

### Murssvayas Dhuurniv — Drow wizard, resistance leader
Solom's former representative, holding the resistance together by will. Near-purple skin, silver hair, thick sideburns, emerald eyes; carries Solom's Pendant. Explained the Hidden's Vhaeraunite hostility to Lolth-followers. Supplied **Solom's arcane mark** and the password *"Kural"* for the Inverted Tower, and had his mages cast **gaseous form** on the whole party for the forge infiltration.

### Rilira — Drow organizer of the slave uprising
Imprisoned in the slave pens; haughty, imperious, every inch the noblewoman she claims to be. Her word alone triggers a full revolt, and her price is **Szith Morcane returned to the slaves**. Her terms for the alliance — **Corkin's head and the fire giant overseer dead** — were met in Ch21, and she smiled coldly as her oppressors fell. On Corkin: *"Corkan was useful the way a good dog is useful."* She warned there is nowhere in the Underdark or on the surface the party would be safe if they betrayed her. **Her practical question is still unanswered: how are the drow going to arm her people?**

### Hamad — Leader of the Hidden (Vhaeraunite rebels)
Cautious, deliberate; meets at gnome ruins reached through drow scouts on the Webs. Satisfied himself that Bramgrim and Akritas had made **no binding commitments to any other drow faction** before talking terms — they had already committed to Rilira. Set a trial of cunning rather than force: rob a giant-held weapon cache behind a Faerzress-worded lock. **Pledged the Hidden's full support conditional on Dengor's death — a condition now satisfied.**

### Grundar Ashmantle — Duergar slave-boss
Recognized **Rylzyn Veth** on sight as the man who bought his own freedom by killing several of Grundar's cousins. Felkur read the desperation under the feud and offered a personal guarantee of escape; a startling number of concealed armed duergar lowered their weapons on his nod. The feud is suspended, not settled.

### Rylzyn Veth & Sorn — Quil'bryn's attached guides
**Rylzyn Veth** (export variants "Rilzin"/"Rizlyn"): drow elite warrior, pragmatic to the point of atrocity — recommended killing five slaves in excruciating ways to motivate the next five, and noted the Behir's chamber is the giants' disposal pit. Escorted the party to the edge of Nerevenan territory and went no further, doubting aloud that they would come back. **Sorn**: not elite, just good and angry enough to be reliable; reports body counts as arithmetic.

### Alder — Senior Harper, head of Underdark operations
Halfling; blunt, world-weary, enormously powerful. Explained that **Gromph Baenre** tore a passage between the Abyss and the Material Plane with a reckless spell meant to free Lolth, flooding the world with demon lords. Dispatched five adventurers (**Celia, Mor, Spots** a tabaxi paladin, **Draco** a warlock, **Connor**) toward Menzoberranzan; of the heroes Daein had read about, *"Most of them are dead now."* Warned that **Graz'zt's backup plan is retreat to Maerimydra** — making this party her *backup plan to the backup plan*. Signed Bramgrim's sword under protest, then teleported the party days deeper into the Underdark.

### Charles — Emerald Enclave contact, Hillsfar
The party's Ch1 messenger, now a former revolutionary performing a full French-resistance aesthetic: beret, affected accent, a tavern he insists on calling a café. Briefed the party on the Szith Morcane giants, then **dropped the persona entirely** when Felkur pitched **"Stargate"** and became a man of business — guild paperwork, tax structure, land through **Isthana Rao** — promising to handle all of it while the party was underground. *(The Ch17 auto-generated NPC block calls him a drow; the prose does not, and prior canon has him as the Enclave's Hillsfar messenger. Treated as an extraction artifact.)*

### Isthana Rao — Criminal Matriarch of Hillsfar
Head of the thieves' guild; pragmatic and opportunistic. Offered patronage and protection for any business the party starts, with a velvet threat that ventures without her backing fail. Gifted Felkur three bottles of rare wine. **Charles is routing Stargate's land acquisition through her**, which entangles the party's business with the guild whether or not they sign anything.

### Emerald Enclave — Faction patron
Contacts the party through Akritas by messenger and telepathy; vouched for them at the Waydown. **The High Council wants access to an Arcanaloth's ledger** — an order Akritas concealed until Ch17.

### Glor — Mind Flayer of the Ryxyg Colony
Cowled illithid, telepathic; claims his colony has chosen peaceful coexistence. Paid the party for the Rogue Thought and provided intel on disabling Maerimydra's siege weapons "without the perpetrators being identified" — **still unused.**

### Xaven Tanor'this — Drow alchemist
Former chief alchemist of Maerimydra; holds the alchemy to purge fiendish blood and knows the secret route into Maerimydra via the Shattered Tower. At Sporedome as of Ch16; unconfirmed since.

## ANTAGONISTS & THREATS

### Graz'zt — Demon Lord, "The Dark Prince" (off-screen prime mover)
Source of the region's corruption and madness; infused the Maerimydra fire giants with fiendish blood and brought in **Vraxen** to enforce his contracts. Worshipped at the **Six-Fingered Courtyard**. **Per Alder: Maerimydra is his fallback** — if the Menzoberranzan operation succeeds, he retreats there, which makes the party's regional campaign his line of escape. His madness drove Quil'bryn to murder Solom; the braincap mushroom is the known cure.

### Maram il Vass — Ultroloth, Broker of the Den of the Whispered Word
Seven-foot yugoloth from Gehenna behind an illusion of a "rakishly handsome half-elf." **Engineered a double-cross:** sold Felkur's location to a devil, then contracted the party to retrieve the Lexicon of Oaths so they would steal it for him. Felkur voided his own obligation by forging the Lexicon — replacing his name with **Veris of Mulmaster** — and it has never been established whether Maram knows. **He has not appeared anywhere in DDEX-04**; the Ch22 export's claim that he arrived at Dengor's palace is a transcription error for Velasta.

### Hledh — Fiendborne Fire Giant Queen (not yet encountered)
Fiendish ruler of Maerimydra; bloodline source of all fiendborne giants; dwells in **Castle Maerimydra**. Pipyap warned she is to be avoided at all costs. **With Dengor dead, she is the occupation's remaining authority.**

### Vraxen — Arcanaloth, Host of the Spire of Inevitable Reckoning
Fox-faced yugoloth; enforcer of Graz'zt's contracts and manager of the giant pact; obsessed with financial accuracy. Held the **Lexicon of Oaths**; his scent keys the vault's stone golem. **His private journal details plans to betray his yugoloth superiors.** Presumptively the Arcanaloth whose **ledger the Emerald Enclave wants.** Last seen alive.

### Veperyd — Fiendborne Fire Giant, Coliseum Master
Demon-blooded; skin weeps oily black smoke. Runs Maerimydra's **Bloody Coliseum** under Vraxen. Caught skimming the betting pool — the lever the party used to ignite the dinner brawl. Vowed to "repay a debt in blood." A viable future capture/blood target.

### Rogue Thought — Mad Mind Flayer (ESCAPED)
Telepathic; plane-shifts; manipulates Faerzress; claims a blessing from Graz'zt. Identified Bramgrim as the party's "greatest magical threat." Reduced to near-death, he **severed his own tentacles and plane-shifted away**, vowing that he and Graz'zt would have revenge. Glor's contract is technically unfulfilled.

### Githyanki Knight (at large)
*Plane Shift*-ed away in the lair, declaring the party a "permanent enemy." Returns at will.

### Brandyr — Fiendborne Fire Giant (fate unestablished)
Led Graz'zt worship at the Six-Fingered Courtyard; ambushed, *Sleep*'d, shrunk and delivered to Solom at Sporedome as the blood source for the purge ritual (Ch13). **The ritual completed.** Whether Brandyr survived it, was consumed by it, or is still shrunk in a chamber has never been established at the table.

## KEY OBJECTS & ARTIFACTS

- **Solom's Spellbook** — Gifted by the clone; the party can burn its contents as scrolls in the coming fights. Includes ***Modify Memory*** — which Solom himself says will not work on a revenant.
- **Solom's Arcane Mark** — A signet ring set with a black jewel that glows with its own inner light. With the password **"Kural,"** it bypasses the Inverted Tower's wards and opens the archmage's chambers.
- **Hellfire Projectors** — Oversized ballistae shaped like serpent jaws, steel and darkwood, mass-produced at the Forge of Surtr for the assault on the surface. **Three crated for shipment, four half-assembled on the floor.**
- **Braincap Mushroom** — Sporedome fungus that pushes back Graz'zt's madness. Cured Arrizz and, publicly, **Quil'bryn**. Also brewed into a special alcohol by the party.
- **Crystal Statue of Skoraeus Stonebones** — A flawless, perfectly symmetrical carving the size of a war shield that held Decha in a seven-year trance. **Destroyed by Felkur's thrown beer bottle (Ch21) — which proved to be the cure, not the crime.**
