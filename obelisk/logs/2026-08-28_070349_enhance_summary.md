## Locations

### Sporedome

A luminous fungal cavern-city and Drow refugee settlement serving as the staging ground for the coalition forces. The war council is held in the great fungal amphitheater at its heart, where tall mushrooms function as both scenery and seating. The cavern is filled with Drow, Myconids, dwarves, humans in Hillsfar's red-plumed helmets, mind flayers, cultists, undead worshippers, and representatives of the party's accumulated allies. All of the session's planning, negotiations, private warnings, and faction assignments take place here before the army mobilizes.

### Szith Morcane

A Drow outpost where political tensions recently boiled over, resulting in the disintegration of Asala by a clone of Solom Ned'razak. Felkur's ballista shot and commanding speech united the fractious coalition behind the more urgent campaign against Maerimydra. The outpost's former slaves, elite Drow, Vhaeraunites, Nerevenans, and other factions now form uneasy components of the army gathered at Sporedome.

### Maerimydra

The target of the upcoming military operation, a city deep in the Underdark currently occupied by fire giants and demonic forces under the command of the fire giant queen Hledh Hellspawn. The coalition intends to establish advanced bases, penetrate the city, and kill Hledh so the demonically empowered giants turn upon one another. Unknown to most of the army, Graz'zt is expected to manifest there as part of his escape from Alder's larger trap.

### Hillsfar

A city in the sunlight realms whose newly appointed First Lord attends the war council. The party recognizes Vuhm Yestral as the guard it met when first entering the city; amid Hillsfar's chaos, he somehow rose to its highest office. He eagerly praises Daein and the party as protectors, heroes, and defenders of Hillsfar.

### Cave of Black Whispers

A haunted cavern where a large group of dwarves was surprised and slaughtered by the Drow of Szith Morcane. Their leader, Vragni Moonsmith, has risen as a powerful mummy, and evil forces compel the other dead to languish in undeath. Glenna Moonsmith has begged the Order of the Gauntlet to lay her ancestor permanently to rest. The party plans to attempt this mission before proceeding to the Western Forward Base.

### Castle Maerimydra

The seat of power within occupied Maerimydra. The Zhentarim have heard rumors that newborn children of Graz'zt can be found somewhere in or near the castle and want Felkur to extract them so they can be raised as Black Network operatives.

## NPCs

### Elanil Elassidil

Known as the Moonsilver Herald, Elanil is an elven woman and the coalition's overall military commander. She can magically silence the entire amphitheater with a raised hand and speaks with blunt authority about the army's limitations. She admits the coalition cannot truly seize Maerimydra; it can only distract the enemy long enough to reach and kill Hledh.

Elanil initially dismisses Daein's vague warning about Graz'zt because leaders hear countless hypothetical disasters before a campaign. She demands verifiable information before reconsidering an operation minutes before mobilization. Bramgrim's presentation of Alder's signature shocks her into taking the warning seriously and provokes bitter anger toward the legendary Harper. Elanil ultimately accepts that the immediate plan cannot change and understands that her objectives and the party's will diverge once Hledh falls. Despite her grim expectation that death would be preferable to capture by Graz'zt, Daein's confidence and Felkur's peculiar reassurance encourage her before she puts on her helmet and leaves to command her forces.

### Solom Ned'razak

*(This is the clone; the original was murdered by Quil'bryn and the alliance does not know.)* A powerful Drow Archmage whose presence keeps the fractious Drow factions in line through fear. He has successfully developed a method to remove demonic taint from fire giants — demonstrated on Brandyr, who stands purged and shrunk at Sporedome — though it requires the subject to be subdued for ten days and is not a military solution.

Solom's intentions remain opaque. The party previously found evidence in his Maerimydra laboratory that he had attempted to negotiate with the fire giants. At the council, the Drow instinctively look toward him after his lethal display in Szith Morcane. He watches the party's decision to keep Quil'bryn's Drow in reserve with a cryptic smile and is currently the target of Dorina T'sarran's vendetta.

### Sovereign Agaricus

The leader of Sporedome's Myconids, who communicates through connecting spores. He speaks sparingly, opening with only, ��Much has changed since you last came,” and waits for the party to request further explanation. Because Myconids find change especially disturbing, this understated warning carries considerable weight.

Agaricus reveals that Zuggtmoy and Juiblex made war on **Araumycos**, the vast sentient fungal colony of the deep Underdark, and that both demon lords were defeated by five adventurers. He knows a great deal about events within Sporedome but pays virtually no attention to matters outside it.

### Elisande

A mysterious little girl who appears at critical junctures in the party's journey. At Sporedome she sits alone atop a tall mushroom, dressed in a dirty garment with one arm around her blind black goat. The goat has black fur and milky white eyes. The party remembers the goat more readily than Elisande herself.

### First Lord Vuhm Yestral

Formerly the first guard the party encountered when entering Hillsfar, Vuhm somehow rose to become the city's First Lord amid its chaos. He greets the party warmly, invokes Isthana Rao's praise, and eagerly associates himself with their deeds. During the council, he stands to extol “my good friend, Daein” as a protector and hero of Hillsfar, granting Daein a title he did not know he possessed.

Vuhm thinks of the conflict principally as a war against fire giants. The party suspects that learning a demon lord may appear could convince him that the situation is above his pay grade.

### Quil'bryn

The Drow warrior who killed the original Solom Ned'razak; the archmage at the muster is the clone. Quil'bryn is characterized as a direct fighter concerned with targets that can be killed using the point of an arrow or stick. His faction invokes its standing arrangement that everyone else will enter first and soften the enemy. The party keeps his Drow in reserve, intending them to follow the Stone Giants once a forward position is captured or reinforce another threatened objective.

### Hledh Hellspawn

The fire giant queen of Maerimydra and leader of its fire giant and demonic forces. Graz'zt's half-fire-giant granddaughter, she is the force holding the demonically infused giants together. Killing her is the coalition's primary objective because her death is expected to disorganize the giants and provoke them into fighting one another.

### Alder

A legendary Harper hero famous throughout the North. Her adventuring party trapped Lolth in a dimensional prison through an arrangement involving Vhaeraun, and she believes the madness in the Underdark was engineered by Lolth to facilitate an escape. She is also associated with legendary victories against Drow incursions, and her son helped save the world from dragons.

Alder dispatched five adventurers as “Plan A” to collapse the demonic incursion by drawing demon lords together. After Graz'zt developed an escape route through Maerimydra, she made the party her “Plan B.” Bramgrim was so excited to meet her that he asked her to autograph his sword.

At Sporedome, Alder appears in person as a short halfling with the look of a middle-aged rogue. She discreetly pulls Bramgrim aside and warns him that Dorina T'sarran is hunting Solom and that Solom's survival is essential to keeping the Drow alliance together.

### Zern Xerkstil

A member of the Order of the Gauntlet who approaches the party during preparations. He acknowledges that they have not sworn fealty to the Order but believes their focus on doing good makes them suitable for a mission. He asks them to enter the Cave of Black Whispers and lay the dwarven mummy Vragni Moonsmith permanently to rest at Glenna Moonsmith's request. Refusing would earn his obvious and considerable disapproval.

### Dorina T'sarran

A vampire Drow cleric of Kiaransalee and former ruler of Szith Morcane. She is Velasta T'sarran's aunt and has been seen nearby after vowing revenge against Solom Ned'razak. If she discovers that Solom is alive and informs Velasta, she could redirect the revenant's vengeance and destabilize the entire Drow alliance.

### Rilira

Leader of the former Drow slaves. She vociferously objects when the party proposes keeping Quil'bryn's Drow knights in reserve. Daein persuades her that a reserve is not an evasion of battle but a mobile force that can reinforce any position in trouble. She accepts the strategic reasoning after his exceptionally strong persuasion.

### Glenna Moonsmith

Owner of the Plodding Plow and The Stop and a familiar contact of the party. She has approached the Order of the Gauntlet and begged it to lay her ancestor, Vragni Moonsmith, permanently to rest. The party remembers her well and accepts the Cave of Black Whispers mission on her behalf.

### Huum

The mind flayer who speaks for the illithids at the Sporedome war council. Huum acknowledges the party's past help against a rogue thought but initially insists that the colony's ledger is clean because the party already received two powerful magical items.

When warned of Graz'zt, Huum considers withdrawing from the conflict, arguing that the best place to survive a demonic incursion is somewhere far away from it. Felkur successfully appeals to his pride. Huum responds by calling Felkur's brain “tasty,” prompting Akritas to prepare her bow, then agrees to provide a list of Graz'zt's abilities. He promises no further commitment until the situation unfolds.

## Items

### Hellfire Projectors

Powerful ballistic weapons extracted from the fire giant forge after the death of King Dengor. The party managed to secure three finished projectors and components for four more before escaping the crumbling forge and its rampaging fire salamanders. Felkur's fascination with ballistic components was so complete that he continued tinkering with them during the political confrontation in Szith Morcane.

### Rare Metals

Valuable materials worth roughly one thousand gold pieces, salvaged from the crumbling fire giant forge while Akritas and Daein magically concealed the party. The group escaped just as the fire salamanders gave chase.

### Alder's Autograph

The signature of the legendary Harper hero Alder, written on Bramgrim's sword after he excitedly asked her for an autograph. Bramgrim later realizes that carrying the signature of the hero who imprisoned Lolth may be diplomatically awkward around Lolth's worshippers.

At Sporedome, the autograph becomes proof of the party's otherwise extraordinary claims. Elanil laughs when Alder is first named, then recognizes the signature and asks, “Alder? You spoke to Alder?” The evidence transforms a vague warning into credible intelligence about Graz'zt.

### Vraxen's Betrayal Journal

The document Felkur intends to steal from the arcanaloth's tower during the invasion of Maerimydra. His plan is to enter the tower, get blackout drunk, steal the journal, and emerge without remembering that he took it. He will know only that he possesses it, frustrating attempts to obtain the truth from his memory.

Felkur also intends to remove other objects from the tower and hand those to the Zhentarim. If anyone traces the theft, the visible trail will lead to the Black Network while Felkur secretly retains the journal.

### Rare Bottle of Wine

An expensive and rare vintage offered to Felkur by a Zhentarim operative during a private negotiation. The bottle is part of an effort to wine and dine Felkur, flatter his skill at moving goods, and cement a discreet smuggling partnership with the Black Network.

## Spells

### Magical Silence

Elanil Elassidil raises her arms toward the cavern roof and a magical silence descends over the rowdy fungal amphitheater. The effect immediately ends the many furious conversations and directs the attention of every Drow, dwarf, Myconid, human, and faction representative toward the Moonsilver Herald before her address.

### Connecting Spores

A Myconid ability used by Sovereign Agaricus after the party asks what has changed in the Underdark. Agaricus pauses, sways, moves his hands, and releases the spores, establishing a shared fungal link through which the party learns about Zuggtmoy and Juiblex's attack on Araumycos and their defeat by five adventurers.
