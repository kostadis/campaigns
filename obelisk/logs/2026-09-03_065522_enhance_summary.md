## Locations

### Tresendar Manor

A ruined estate in Phandalin containing a secret Redbrand hideout, prison cells, skeletal guardians, and a dangerous crevasse inhabited by the Nothic Ssarnak. The party rescued the Dendrar family and recovered hidden treasure here before escaping from the bugbear barracks. The surviving bugbears remain an unresolved threat behind the manor's locked door.

### Stonehill Inn

A local tavern and inn in Phandalin where the party recovered from its wounds, received a free night's stay for its heroics, and paid ten silver pieces for food sent to the room. Toblen later welcomed the adventurers as the toast of the town, Elsa served them beer, and Sildar shared breakfast with them before their departure.

### Shrine of Luck

A temple in Phandalin where Sister Garaele praised the new heroes of Phandalin and requested their help approaching the banshee Agatha. She provided a jeweled silver comb to win Agatha's favor and offered three potions of healing for information about Bowgentle's spellbook.

### Miner's Exchange

The headquarters for the local guild in Phandalin, where Halia Thornton manages trade and attempted to purchase the party's recovered correspondence for 100 gold pieces. The party also reviewed its open quests here and deliberately chose Old Owl Well over Cragmaw Castle and the unresolved threat beneath Tresendar Manor.

### Edermath Orchard

A cottage and orchard on the edge of Phandalin, home to the retired adventurer Daran Edermath. The party found Daran clearing underbrush, received intelligence about the eastern wilderness and Netherese history, and endured ten minutes of loosely relevant adventuring advice.

### Triboar Trail

A road running east from Phandalin, currently showing signs of caravan attacks. Although still used, it is quieter than the north-south High Road; the party encountered no travelers during its day upon the trail and found the wreckage of an attacked caravan.

### Conyberry

A ruined town abandoned after the Spellplague devastated it and Uthgardt raiders killed those who remained. Toblen warned that its ruins might attract people dangerous to the unwary, though an Insight check revealed he had never visited it. The route to Agatha's lair passes through the settlement.

### Old Owl Well

The crumbling ruins of an ancient Netherese watchtower in the rugged hills northeast of Phandalin, currently serving as an archaeological dig site for the necromancer Hamun Kost. A colorful tent stands within its rubble-enclosed courtyard. Twelve zombies — the Kost Wardens — serve as his laborers and defensive cordon; among them are Warden Dreth, a sun-peeled former sentinel, and Warden Holl, a dead acolyte still wearing a singed robe.

### Wyvern Tor

A rugged ridge on the lower slopes of the hills featuring a hidden ravine and the camp of the Axe-Biter Marauders. The full module roster is the orc chief Brughor Axe-Biter, four bugbears, and the ogre Gog. Smoke rises faintly from the vicinity, and a lone bugbear sentry guards the cave entrance from behind a boulder twenty yards away.

## NPCs

### Myrna Dendrar

A human woman rescued from the Redbrand cells with her thirteen-year-old son Nars and eighteen-year-old daughter Nilsa. The Redbrands murdered her husband, Thel, and she delayed her family's execution by inventing wealthy relatives in Neverwinter who would pay a ransom. In gratitude for the rescue, she revealed the location of a hidden emerald necklace in her family's former alchemy shop in Thundertree.

### Ssarnak

A Nothic living in the caverns beneath Tresendar Manor that feeds on corpses and recognized the sound of Glasstaff. As the party fled, it whispered to Veyra that her teacher was not dead and was “not in a place where dead is easy,” causing her to stop and attempt to return.

### Nosk

The lead bugbear of the Scarlet Fist, four tall, furry shock troops sent by the Black Spider to reinforce the Redbrands and intimidate Phandalin. Nosk mocked the idea that a Redbrand could command him and accidentally revealed that the Black Spider was at Cragmaw Castle after Zenvon provoked him. He then dealt Zenvon a crushing 14-damage morning-star blow during the ensuing fight.

### Sildar Hallwinter

A member of the Lords' Alliance who paid the party the remaining 150 gold pieces for exposing Iarno Albrek's betrayal. He prefers Iarno returned alive for trial but admitted he would not be upset if the traitor died. Sildar later offered 500 gold pieces for clearing Cragmaw Castle and implied that the Lords' Alliance would grant favors to whoever accomplished the task. His admiration of Pip's “fine, noble sword” carried a subtle suggestion of possible recruitment.

### Sister Garaele

A scholarly acolyte at the Shrine of Luck who previously approached the banshee Agatha and barely escaped alive. She asked the party to present Agatha with a jeweled silver comb and learn the location of the legendary spellbook of the mage Bowgentle, offering three potions of healing as payment.

### Halia Thornton

The guildmaster of the Miner's Exchange who congratulated the party on defeating the Redbrands but grew cold upon learning Glasstaff escaped. She offered 100 gold pieces for correspondence recovered from his quarters, supposedly to safeguard local trade. Zenvon rebuffed her, and the party left convinced she knew more than she admitted.

### Daran Edermath

A retired adventurer who tends an orchard on the edge of Phandalin. He warned the party about the ravaged eastern wilderness, the dangers of the hills, and caravan attacks near Wyvern Tor. He declined to join the expedition because an old adventurer survives by choosing to stop adventuring. Daran also shared stories of Netherese flying ships, twelfth-level magic, Karsus breaking the Weave, and powerful artifacts scattered across the North.

### Harbin Wester

The Townmaster of Phandalin, mentioned by Daran as having reported attacks on caravans near Wyvern Tor. Daran admitted he had not followed every detail of the reports.

### Toblen Stonehill

The friendly proprietor of the Stonehill Inn who welcomed the party as heroes, ordered Elsa to serve Zenvon a beer, and shared warnings about Conyberry. He knew the town's history but had never visited it, so his fears about its present dangers were speculative. He wished the party well upon its departure.

### Elsa

A barmaid at the Stonehill Inn. At Toblen's instruction, she poured Zenvon a beer when the heroes returned to announce their coming journey.

### Pip Thistlewick

A companion who refers to Zenvon as “Boss.” Pip warned against fighting all twelve zombies at Old Owl Well and became visibly excited about Sildar's 500-gold bounty for Cragmaw Castle. After Zenvon reminded him the reward would be split four ways, Pip moderated his response and said the party would keep the offer under advisement. His magical longsword caught Sildar's eye and prompted hints of future opportunities in the Lords' Alliance.

### Veyra

A scholarly companion whose blue crystal resonates with Zenvon's black stone. She recalled Orryn Voss's theory about a device controlling people and identified Hamun Kost's head tattoos as necromantic symbols using her notebook. At Old Owl Well, she correctly inferred that the zombies' controller was likely in the tent. She later doubted Zenvon's navigation after an unsuccessful hour but conceded “Well done” when he found a shortcut to Wyvern Tor.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge

A companion who helped keep Zenvon alive during the fight beneath Tresendar Manor and physically prevented Veyra from turning back toward Ssarnak. At Old Owl Well, she explained that zombies are mindless, identified the work of a powerful necromancer, and characterized necromancy as evil, dangerous magic. She strongly preferred fighting bugbears and an ogre to risking a conversation with Agatha.

### Hamun Kost

A Red Wizard of Thay and necromancer with distinctive tattoos across his shaved head who commands the twelve Kost Wardens at Old Owl Well. The zombies are laborers and set-dressing around the real danger: Hamun himself is powerful enough that the encounter is a conversation, not a fair fight. He is conducting an archaeological dig and refuses to explain the full purpose of his interest in the watchtower. Although he claims no desire to threaten nearby communities, he casually tells Zenvon that he would kill the party if that were necessary to protect his secrets. He offers Cragmaw Castle's location in exchange for clearing the Wyvern Tor marauders or learning the tower builder's name from Agatha.

### Axe-Biter Bugbear Sentry

A tall, furry bugbear with large ears keeping watch outside the marauder cave at Wyvern Tor, positioned behind a boulder about twenty yards from the cave entrance. It is one of four bugbear rank-and-file serving the orc chief Brughor Axe-Biter alongside the ogre Gog. It has not yet noticed the party on the ridge.
