## Locations

### Tresendar Manor

A ruined estate in Phandalin containing a secret Redbrand hideout, prison cells, skeletal guardians, an apothecary, and a dangerous crevasse inhabited by the Nothic Ssarnak. The party rescued the Dendrar family here, discovered a hidden treasure chest, and escaped after a brutal fight with Nosk's bugbears. The entrance was locked behind them while the surviving bugbears hammered against the other side.

### Stonehill Inn

A local tavern and inn in Phandalin where the party recovered from its wounds, received a free night's stay for its heroics, gathered local lore from Toblen, and shared a final breakfast with Sildar Hallwinter. Elsa served Zenvon a beer when Toblen welcomed the group back as the heroes of Phandalin.

### Shrine of Luck

A temple in Phandalin where Sister Garaele requested the party's help in approaching the banshee Agatha. Garaele supplied a jeweled silver comb intended to win the banshee's favor and offered three potions of healing in exchange for information about Bowgentle's spellbook.

### Miner's Exchange

The headquarters of the local guild in Phandalin, where Halia Thornton manages trade and attempted to purchase the party's recovered correspondence for 100 gold pieces. The party also reviewed its quest log and available destinations here before selecting Old Owl Well.

### Edermath Orchard

A cottage and orchard on the edge of Phandalin, home to the retired adventurer Daran Edermath. Daran was clearing underbrush when the party arrived. Here he shared warnings about the eastern wilderness, delivered a lengthy lecture of general adventuring advice, and recounted the legendary history of Netheril.

### Triboar Trail

A road running east from Phandalin, currently plagued by attacks and showing signs of a recently abandoned caravan. Although still used, it was empty during the party's journey. It is less important for commerce than the north-south High Road and extends into a region where settled civilization has badly receded.

### Conyberry

A ruined town abandoned years ago following destruction during the Spellplague and subsequent killings by Uthgardt raiders. Toblen warned that roadside ruins can attract people dangerous to the unwary, although an insight check revealed that he had never visited Conyberry himself. The route to Agatha's lair also passes through the ruins.

### Old Owl Well

The crumbling ruins of an ancient Netherese watchtower in the rugged hills northeast of Phandalin, currently serving as an archaeological dig site for the necromancer Hamun Kost. Mounds of rubble enclose a courtyard where Hamun has erected a colorful tent. Twelve zombies—the Kost Wardens—serve as his laborers and defensive cordon; among them are Warden Dreth, a sun-peeled former sentinel, and Warden Holl, a dead acolyte still wearing a singed robe.

### Wyvern Tor

A rugged ridge on the lower slopes of the hills featuring a hidden ravine and the camp of the Axe-Biter Marauders. The full module roster is the orc chief Brughor Axe-Biter, four bugbears, and the ogre Gog. Smoke from the camp can be smelled from the ridge, and a cave mouth lies roughly fifty yards from the party's initial vantage point. A lone bugbear sentry guards the entrance from behind a boulder about twenty yards outside the cave.

## NPCs

### Mirna Dendrar

A human woman rescued from the Redbrand cells along with her two teenage children, thirteen-year-old Nars and eighteen-year-old Nilsa. The Redbrands murdered her husband, Thel, and she delayed her family's execution by inventing wealthy relatives in Neverwinter who would pay a ransom. She revealed the location of a hidden emerald necklace in her family's former alchemy shop in Thundertree and asked the party to recover Thel's body if possible.

### Ssarnak

A Nothic living in the caverns beneath Tresendar Manor that feeds on corpses. Zenvon distracted it by imitating Glasstaff's activity with Minor Illusion. As the party fled, Ssarnak whispered to Veyra that her teacher was not dead and was “not in a place where dead is easy,” causing her to stop and attempt to return.

### Nosk

The leader of the Scarlet Fist, the Black Spider's four-bugbear reinforcement group sent to support the Redbrands and intimidate Phandalin. He serves the Black Spider directly rather than answering to Glasstaff. Zenvon baited him by questioning what the Black Spider would think of his behavior, causing Nosk to reveal that the Black Spider was at Cragmaw Castle before initiating a brutal fight.

### Sildar Hallwinter

A member of the Lords' Alliance who paid the party 150 gold pieces after receiving evidence of Iarno Albrek's betrayal. He prefers Iarno captured alive for trial but would not be upset if the traitor returned dead. Sildar later offered 500 gold pieces for locating Cragmaw Castle and driving out its leaders, then subtly suggested that the Lords' Alliance might have future opportunities for Pip.

### Sister Garaele

A scholarly acolyte at the Shrine of Luck who asked the party to bring a jeweled silver comb to the banshee Agatha in exchange for information on the legendary spellbook of the mage Bowgentle. She previously attempted to approach Agatha herself and barely escaped alive. She offers three potions of healing as payment.

### Halia Thornton

The guildmaster of the Miner's Exchange who congratulated the party on defeating the Redbrands but grew cold upon learning Glasstaff escaped. She offered 100 gold pieces for correspondence recovered from his quarters, claiming she wanted to protect local trade. Zenvon rebuffed her, and the party left convinced that she knows more than she admits.

### Daran Edermath

A retired adventurer who tends an orchard on the edge of Phandalin. He warned the party about the devastated eastern wilderness, bandit activity near Wyvern Tor, and the dangers of leaving the road. He shared stories about Netheril and its magical artifacts, declined to join the expedition because he had chosen to stop adventuring, and rewarded Zenvon's patience with a one-time bonus after delivering ten minutes of rambling advice.

### Harbin Wester

The Townmaster of Phandalin, mentioned by Daran as having reported attacks on caravans near Wyvern Tor. Daran admitted that he had not followed all the details of Harbin's reports.

### Toblen Stonehill

The friendly proprietor of the Stonehill Inn who repeatedly welcomed the party as the heroes of Phandalin. He shared warnings about Conyberry, its destruction during the Spellplague, and its later fall to the Uthgardt. An insight check showed that his fear of the ruins was not based on firsthand experience. He wished the party luck when it departed.

### Elsa

A barmaid at the Stonehill Inn. At Toblen's request, she poured Zenvon a beer when the party returned to announce its forthcoming expedition.

### Pip Thistlewick

A companion of the party and wielder of the magical longsword recovered beneath Tresendar Manor. Pip became visibly excited by Sildar's 500-gold bounty and asked Zenvon whether the party should pursue it. After Zenvon reminded him of the reward's four-way division and the risks involved, Pip moderated his response. Sildar's comments about Pip's “fine, noble sword” hinted at possible membership in the Lords' Alliance.

### Veyra

A scholarly companion whose blue crystal resonates with Zenvon's black stone. She recalled Orryn Voss's theory about a device controlling people and helped connect the stones to the altered goblins and Ssarnak's revelation. At Old Owl Well, she consulted her notebook and identified Hamun Kost's head tattoos as necromantic symbols. She questioned Zenvon's navigation after his first failed search for Wyvern Tor but praised him after he found a shortcut.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge

A companion who channeled divine energy to keep Zenvon alive during the bugbear battle and physically restrained Veyra during the escape from Tresendar Manor. At Old Owl Well, she recognized that the zombies implied the presence of a powerful necromancer and explained the evil magic used to reanimate corpses. She strongly advocated fighting the bugbears and ogre at Wyvern Tor rather than approaching Agatha.

### Hamun Kost

A Red Wizard of Thay and necromancer with distinctive tattoos on his shaved head who commands the twelve Kost Wardens at Old Owl Well. The zombies are laborers and set-dressing around the real danger: Hamun himself is powerful enough that the encounter is a conversation, not a fair fight. He is conducting an archaeological dig and claims no interest in harming neighboring settlements. He offered to trade the location of Cragmaw Castle for either clearing the Wyvern Tor marauders or learning the original tower-builder's name from Agatha. He promised a meal when the party returned and instructed them to announce themselves before approaching his protective zombies.

### Axe-Biter Bugbear Sentry

A tall, furry bugbear with large ears keeping watch outside the marauder cave at Wyvern Tor. It is positioned behind a boulder about twenty yards from the cave entrance and roughly fifty yards from the party's initial vantage point. It is one of four bugbear rank-and-file serving the orc chief Brughor Axe-Biter alongside the ogre Gog.

## Items

### Unidentified Magical Longsword

A finely crafted magical longsword discovered in a hidden chest beneath the crevasse in Tresendar Manor. The party has not identified its name or provenance and agreed it belonged in the hands of its most capable fighter, Pip. Its true name is Talon, and it once belonged to the long-dead knight Aldith Tresendar, though the characters did not learn this during play. Sildar later noticed the blade and used its noble appearance to hint at possible future opportunities for Pip within the Lords' Alliance.

A gift provided by Sister Garaele to be presented to the banshee Agatha in order to win her favor and obtain information about the location of the mage Bowgentle's legendary spellbook. Hamun Kost later offered the party another question to ask Agatha: the name of the wizard who built the tower at Old Owl Well.

### Black Stone

A mysterious object carried secretly by Zenvon since the road. It resonates with Veyra's blue crystal when brought near it, causing her crystal to flare more brightly and suggesting a connection to a larger magical device or conspiracy. Zenvon revealed it only after the escape from Tresendar Manor, increasing his trust in Veyra.
