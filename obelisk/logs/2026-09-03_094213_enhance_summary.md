## Locations

### Tresendar Manor

A ruined estate in Phandalin containing a secret Redbrand hideout, prison cells, skeletal guardians, an apothecary, and a dangerous crevasse inhabited by the Nothic Ssarnak. The party rescued the Dendrar family, recovered treasure from the crevasse, and escaped through the manor after a brutal fight with Nosk's bugbears. The entrance was locked while the surviving bugbears hammered against the other side, leaving the hideout unresolved.

### Stonehill Inn

A local tavern and inn in Phandalin where the party recovered from its wounds, received a free night's stay for its heroics, gathered local lore from Toblen, and shared a final breakfast with Sildar Hallwinter. Toblen and Elsa welcomed the adventurers as the heroes of Phandalin, and the party completed a long rest here before leaving town.

### Shrine of Luck

A temple in Phandalin where Sister Garaele requested the party's help in approaching the banshee Agatha. Garaele provided a jeweled silver comb to win the banshee's favor and offered three potions of healing if the party could learn the location of Bowgentle's legendary spellbook.

### Miner's Exchange

The headquarters for the local guild in Phandalin where Halia Thornton manages trade and attempted to purchase the party's recovered correspondence for 100 gold pieces. The party also reviewed its quest log here, considering Cragmaw Castle, Old Owl Well, Wyvern Tor, Agatha's Lair, and Thundertree before selecting Old Owl Well.

### Edermath Orchard

A tidy cottage and orchard on the edge of Phandalin, home to the retired adventurer Daran Edermath. Daran was clearing underbrush when the party arrived. The adventurers gathered intelligence about eastern travel, Wyvern Tor, and Netheril here, while Zenvon's patient attention to Daran's lengthy advice earned a one-use Investigation or Survival bonus.

### Triboar Trail

A road running east from Phandalin, currently plagued by attacks on caravans. The party traveled roughly thirty miles along it and found an abandoned caravan bearing signs of violence, though no travelers passed that day. It remains in use but is less important to regional trade than the north-south High Road.

### Conyberry

A ruined town abandoned years ago following the Spellplague and subsequent Uthgardt attacks. Toblen believes roadside ruins attract dangerous people, though he has apparently never visited the town himself. Conyberry lies along the route to Agatha's lair, and the party considered passing through it while planning the journey to Old Owl Well.

### Old Owl Well

The crumbling ruins of an ancient Netherese watchtower in the rugged hills northeast of Phandalin. Mounds of rubble enclose a courtyard where Hamun Kost has pitched a colorful tent and established an archaeological dig. Twelve zombies—the Kost Wardens—serve as his laborers and defensive cordon; among them are Warden Dreth, a sun-peeled former sentinel, and Warden Holl, a dead acolyte still wearing a singed robe. Hamun claims no hostile interest in nearby settlements, but keeps the purpose and object of his excavation private.

### Wyvern Tor

A rugged ridge on the lower slopes of the hills featuring a hidden ravine and the camp of the Axe-Biter Marauders. The full module roster is the orc chief Brughor Axe-Biter, four bugbears, and the ogre Gog. Smoke rises faintly from a cave at the bottom of the ravine, while a lone bugbear sentry guards the entrance from behind a boulder twenty yards away.

## NPCs

### Myrna Dendrar

A human woman rescued from the Redbrand cells along with her teenage children, thirteen-year-old Nars and eighteen-year-old Nilsa. Her husband, Thel, was murdered by the Redbrands. Myrna protected her family by inventing wealthy relatives in Neverwinter who would pay a ransom. In gratitude for the rescue, she revealed the location of an emerald necklace hidden behind storage shelves in her family's former alchemy shop in Thundertree and asked the party to recover Thel's remains if possible.

### Ssarnak

A Nothic living in the caverns beneath Tresendar Manor that feeds on corpses. Zenvon distracted it by imitating Glasstaff's work with Minor Illusion. During the party's escape, it whispered to Veyra that her teacher was not dead and was “not in a place where dead is easy,” causing her to stop and attempt to return to it.

### Nosk

The lead bugbear of the Scarlet Fist, four tall, furry shock troops sent by the Black Spider to reinforce the Redbrands and intimidate Phandalin. Nosk mocked Zenvon's attempted authority, accidentally revealed that the Black Spider was at Cragmaw Castle, and then initiated a brutal fight. His morning-star strike dealt fourteen points of damage and nearly incapacitated Zenvon.

### Sildar Hallwinter

A member of the Lords' Alliance who paid the party the outstanding 150 gold pieces for exposing Iarno Albrek's betrayal. He prefers Iarno returned alive for trial but would not be distressed by his death. Sildar later offered 500 gold pieces for locating Cragmaw Castle and defeating or driving off its band leaders, along with the implied favor of the Lords' Alliance. His comments about Pip's “fine, noble sword” subtly suggested that successful service could lead to a place for Pip within the organization.

### Sister Garaele

A scholarly acolyte at the Shrine of Luck who previously tried to approach Agatha but barely escaped alive. She asked the party to present the banshee with a jeweled silver comb and learn the location of the legendary spellbook of the mage Bowgentle, offering three potions of healing in return.

### Halia Thornton

The guildmaster of the Miner's Exchange who congratulated the party on defeating the Redbrands but grew cold upon learning Glasstaff escaped. She claimed an interest in protecting local trade and offered 100 gold pieces for correspondence recovered from Glasstaff. Zenvon rebuffed the offer as too low, and the party left convinced that Halia knows more than she lets on.

### Daran Edermath

A retired adventurer who tends his orchard on the edge of Phandalin. He warned the party about the devastated eastern wilderness, safer and riskier routes to Old Owl Well, and caravan attacks near Wyvern Tor. Daran declined to join the expedition because surviving long enough to become an old adventurer meant knowing when to stop. He shared stories of Netheril, Karsus, the breaking of the Weave, and the artifacts scattered after the empire's fall, while repeatedly cautioning that he was not a scholar.

### Harbin Wester

The Townmaster of Phandalin, mentioned by Daran as the source of reports that caravans had been attacked near Wyvern Tor. Daran admitted he had not closely followed the details.

### Toblen Stonehill

The friendly proprietor of the Stonehill Inn who welcomed the party as heroes, provided lodging, and shared warnings about Conyberry. Although he knows the town's history, Zenvon concluded that Toblen has never visited it and was expressing personal fear rather than firsthand intelligence. Toblen wished the party luck upon its departure.

### Elsa

A barmaid at the Stonehill Inn. At Toblen's request, she poured Zenvon a beer when the party returned to discuss its travel plans.

### Pip Thistlewick

A companion of the party who received the magical longsword recovered beneath Tresendar Manor. He was wary of fighting Hamun's twelve zombies and became visibly excited about Sildar's 500-gold bounty for Cragmaw Castle. After Zenvon reminded him that his share would be only 125 gold pieces, Pip moderated his enthusiasm and diplomatically told Sildar the offer would be kept under advisement. Sildar's interest in Pip's “fine, noble sword” may indicate a future opening in the Lords' Alliance.

### Veyra

A scholarly companion whose blue crystal resonates with Zenvon's black stone. The effect strengthened her concerns about her advisor Orryn Voss's theories concerning a device that controls people. At Old Owl Well, she consulted her notebook and identified Hamun Kost's tattoos as necromantic symbols. She expressed skepticism after Zenvon's initial failure to navigate Wyvern Tor, then grudgingly congratulated him when he discovered a shortcut.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge

A companion who used divine power to keep Zenvon standing during the fight with Nosk and physically dragged Veyra away after Ssarnak's revelation. At Old Owl Well, she recognized the work of a necromancer, explained that zombies are mindless reanimated corpses, and warned that controlling twelve of them required dangerous magic. She strongly advocated fighting bugbears and an ogre over visiting Agatha.

### Hamun Kost

A Red Wizard of Thay and necromancer with distinctive tattoos on his shaved head who commands the twelve Kost Wardens at Old Owl Well. The zombies are laborers, guards, and a deliberate show of force around the real danger: Hamun himself is powerful enough that the encounter is a conversation, not a fair fight. He is conducting an archaeological dig but refuses to explain his precise interest in the tower. Hamun claims no intention of harming neighboring settlements and offered to trade the location of Cragmaw Castle for either clearing out the Wyvern Tor marauders or obtaining the tower builder's name from Agatha. Calmly menacing and dryly hospitable, he threatened to kill the party if secrecy required it, then promised them a meal if they completed his task.

### Axe-Biter Bugbear Sentry

A tall, furry bugbear with large ears keeping watch outside the marauder cave at Wyvern Tor. It is hunkered behind a boulder about twenty yards from the cave entrance and has not yet detected the party. It is one of four bugbear rank-and-file serving the orc chief Brughor Axe-Biter alongside the ogre Gog.

Cast by Zenvon during the brutal fight in the bugbear barracks, blasting into two bugbears. The creatures endured the attack and answered with crushing morning-star blows, including a fourteen-point strike from Nosk that left Zenvon barely standing.
