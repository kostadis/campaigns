### Maerimydra

The target of the upcoming military operation, a city deep in the Underdark occupied by fire giants and demonic forces under Hledh Hellspawn's command. The coalition does not intend to hold the city; it means to penetrate its defenses, kill Hledh, and provoke chaos. Graz'zt is expected to manifest there, though only the party knows this. The city also contains the arcanaloth's tower Felkur intends to raid.

### Hillsfar

A city in the sunlight realms whose newly appointed First Lord, formerly a simple gate guard the party met, attends the war council. Vuhm Yestral publicly praises Daein and the party as Hillsfar's heroes and protectors while eagerly attaching himself to their reputation.

### Cave of Black Whispers

A haunted cavern where a large group of dwarves was surprised and slaughtered by Drow from Szith Morcane. Their leader, Vragni Moonsmith, rose as a powerful mummy whose evil keeps the dead languishing in undeath. Glenna Moonsmith has begged the Order of the Gauntlet to lay her ancestor permanently to rest. The party plans to visit the cave before moving against the Western Forward Base.

### Castle Maerimydra

The seat of power within the occupied city. The Zhentarim have heard that newborn children of Graz'zt may be found somewhere in or near the castle and want them recovered so they can be raised as Black Network operatives.

## NPCs

### Elanil Elassidil

Known as the Moonsilver Herald, Elanil is the elven overall commander of the coalition. She magically silences the amphitheater and gives the order to mobilize within an hour. Pragmatic and weary, she admits that the army cannot truly take Maerimydra; it can only distract the defenders long enough to reach Hledh.

Elanil initially dismisses Daein's vague warning about Graz'zt as one more hypothetical disaster presented before battle. She demands verifiable intelligence before changing plans involving an entire army. Bramgrim's possession of Alder's autograph transforms her disbelief into bitter recognition, and she accuses Alder of sending others to clean up the Harpers' mess. Elanil nevertheless determines that the operation must continue because the party cannot reach Graz'zt unless the coalition first breaches Maerimydra. She is displeased that the party's priorities will diverge after Hledh falls but accepts the necessity.

### Solom Ned'razak

*(This is the clone; the original was murdered by Quil'bryn and the alliance does not know.)* A powerful Drow Archmage whose demonstrated power and lethality keep the fractious Drow factions in line through fear. He previously attempted to negotiate with the fire giants, leaving the party uncertain about his real agenda.

Solom has successfully developed a method to remove demonic taint from a captured fire giant—demonstrated on Brandyr, who stands purged and shrunk at Sporedome���but the procedure requires the subject to be subdued for ten days. It is useful for individual prisoners but not as a military solution. Solom watches the battle preparations and the decision to retain Quil'bryn's Drow reserve with a cryptic smile. Dorina T'sarran has sworn revenge against him, forcing the party to become his reluctant protectors.

### Sovereign Agaricus

The leader of Sporedome's Myconids, who communicates through connecting spores. Agaricus speaks sparingly and offers no elaboration unless specifically asked. His opening declaration that “Much has changed since you last came” is significant because Myconids find change deeply disturbing.

Through his spores, he reveals that Zuggtmoy and Juiblex made war upon **Araumycos**, the vast sentient fungal colony of the deep Underdark, and were defeated by five outside adventurers. Agaricus knows much about events inside Sporedome but pays almost no attention to affairs beyond it.

### Elisande

A mysterious girl who appears at critical junctures in the party's journey. At the council she sits alone atop a tall mushroom in a dirty dress, one arm around a black goat with milky white eyes. The party remembers her blind goat more readily than it remembers her.

### First Lord Vuhm Yestral

Formerly the first guard the party encountered when entering Hillsfar, Vuhm has somehow risen to become First Lord amid the city's chaos. He claims Isthana Rao has spoken glowingly about the party and openly flatters them. During the council he interrupts to praise “my friend Daein, protector of Hillsfar,” casting the party as Hillsfar's heroes and defenders. His concern remains the recognizable threat of fire giants; fighting a demon lord would likely be above his preferred pay grade.

### Quil'bryn

The Drow warrior who killed the original Solom Ned'razak; the archmage present at the muster is the clone. Quil'bryn is a direct fighter who prefers a clear enemy at the end of a weapon. He invokes his standing arrangement that everyone else should enter first and soften the enemy. His Drow are therefore held in reserve to move behind the Stone Giants and reinforce captured gains.

### Hledh Hellspawn

The fire giant ruler of Maerimydra and leader of its fire giant and demonic forces, described as a fiendishly empowered fire giant and Graz'zt's half-fire-giant granddaughter. Hledh's presence holds the chaotic, demonic fire giants together. The coalition's primary objective is to kill Hledh, expecting that the leaderless giants will become disorganized and begin fighting among themselves.

### Alder

A legendary Harper hero known throughout the North. Her adventuring party trapped Lolth in a dimensional prison with Vhaeraun's assistance, and she devised the broader response to the demonic incursion. Her Plan A sent five adventurers into the Underdark to gather the demon lords and induce them to destroy one another; the current party became Plan B after Graz'zt learned of that scheme and prepared to appear in Maerimydra.

Alder previously appeared without warning, delivered her assignment, and left—her characteristic habit as an extraordinarily powerful rogue. Bramgrim was so excited to meet her that he asked her to sign his sword. At Sporedome she appears in person as a short halfling with the look of a middle-aged rogue, pulls Bramgrim aside, and warns him about Dorina T'sarran and the need to protect Solom.

### Zern Xerkstil

A member of the Order of the Gauntlet who approaches the party despite its members not yet having sworn fealty to the Order. He brings the Cave of Black Whispers mission: lay the mummy Vragni Moonsmith to rest at the request of his descendant, Glenna Moonsmith. The request typifies the Order's priorities—saving souls and opposing evil even in the middle of a war. Refusal would earn his considerable disapproval.

### Dorina T'sarran

A vampire Drow cleric of Kiaransalee and former ruler of Szith Morcane. She is Velasta T'sarran's aunt and has been seen in the area after vowing revenge upon Solom Ned'razak. If she discovers that Solom lives and informs Velasta, she may overturn the lie directing the revenant's vengeance toward the fire giants and destabilize the entire coalition.

### Rilira

Leader of the former Drow slaves. She vociferously objects when Quil'bryn's Drow are retained as a reserve, arguing that the Drow knights should be fighting at the front. Daein persuades her that a mobile reserve can reinforce whichever objective falters, and she accepts the assignment plan.

### Glenna Moonsmith

Owner of the Plodding Plow and The Stop and a familiar contact of the party. She has begged the Order of the Gauntlet to lay her ancestor Vragni Moonsmith—now a powerful mummy in the Cave of Black Whispers—permanently to rest.

### Huum

The mind flayer who speaks for the illithids at Sporedome. Huum remembers the party's help against a rogue thought but initially insists the colony's ledger with them is clean because it already provided two powerful magical items.

He argues that the best response to a demonic incursion is to be elsewhere, but Bramgrim and Felkur persuade him to assist. After calling Bramgrim's brain “tasty,” Huum agrees to provide a list of Graz'zt's known abilities. He makes no promise of direct aid and will reconsider further involvement only as circumstances unfold.

## Items

### Hellfire Projectors

Powerful ballistic weapons extracted from the fire giant forge after King Dengor's death. The party secured three complete projectors and components for four more while the chamber collapsed and fire salamanders rampaged around them. Felkur later tinkered calmly with their ballistic components during the political confrontation in Szith Morcane.

### Rare Metals

Valuable materials worth roughly one thousand gold pieces, salvaged from the crumbling fire giant forge during the party's narrow escape from the fire salamanders.

### Alder's Autograph

Alder's signature, written on Bramgrim's sword after he excitedly asked the legendary Harper for an autograph. It carries diplomatic risk when displayed before worshippers of Lolth, whom Alder helped imprison. At Sporedome it proves the source of the party's intelligence to Elanil, turning an apparently wild warning about Graz'zt into credible information.

### Vraxen's Betrayal Journal

The document Felkur intends to steal from the arcanaloth's tower during the invasion of Maerimydra. His plan is to become blackout drunk before taking it, ensuring he has no memory of the theft and only knows that he later possesses the journal. He also intends to give other objects from the tower to the Zhentarim, using them as decoys so investigators pursue the Black Network rather than him.

### Rare Bottle of Wine

An expensive and rare bottle offered to Felkur by a Zhentarim operative during their private negotiations. The wine is part of an attempt to flatter and “wine and dine” him into a smuggling partnership and the recovery of Graz'zt's newborn children.

## Spells

### Magical Silence

Elanil Elassidil raises her arms toward the cavern ceiling and a magical silence descends over the rowdy fungal amphitheater. The effect immediately ends the competing conversations and draws the entire coalition's attention to the Moonsilver Herald for her briefing.

### Connecting Spores

A Myconid ability used by Sovereign Agaricus after the party asks what has changed. He sways, gestures, and releases spores that create a shared link, conveying the recent conflict in which Zuggtmoy and Juiblex attacked Araumycos and were defeated by five adventurers.
