# Rescue and Pursuit

The last Redbrand had seen enough. He burned his action to disengage and counted out every step of his retreat, boots slapping against the stone. I watched him go.

Pip turned to me. *"Are we chasing him, boss, or not?"*

I hesitated. Part of me wanted to say no — we had the prisoners, we had won, let him run. But the part that had been doing this long enough to know better spoke first.

"Wait, where did he run away? Like, which way?"

"Okay."

"You gonna chase him?"

"Not yet," I said.

*"Do we want to chase him in case he calls any help or he's only one guy?"*

"Quite possible. Okay, yeah, yeah. Two of us will chase them, and two of us…"

"Free these people."

"So, me and Veyra will go chase them, you guys free this," I started.

But Pip was already shaking his head. He turned to me, and I could see him weighing the options the way he does — tactical, efficient, no sentiment in it.

*"You're the guy with the fingers who knows how to open the locks. I'm the guy with the sword that knows how to."*

He was right. I knew it the moment he said it.

"Yeah, okay."

"So, you and Veyra can go behind the Redbrands ruffian, and Sister Maela will help."

I heard Pip's boots hammering down the corridor before I heard anything else. The sound of steel meeting flesh — he'd caught one of them. Nine points of damage, solid work, I thought. Then the sounds kept going, farther away, and I turned back to the cells.

"Alright, Zenvon, rush in to open the locks."

"Interesting."
