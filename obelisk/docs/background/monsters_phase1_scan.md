**Source:** `_scan_A.md` (ch1–2, lines 1–1145), `_scan_B.md` (ch3–5, lines 1146–2918), `_scan_C.md` (ch6–8, lines 2919–6078), cross-checked against `name_glossary.md` and `monsters_cr_reference.md`.
**Method:** Every generic/unnamed creature stat-block reference in `obelisk.md` was collected and merged into ONE entry per **(tribe / faction, monster type)**. Cross-chapter appearances were combined (e.g. grells appear in A/B as Cragmaw + as J-cave/Feeder Trenches; Sawplee goblins in B and C). Counts and die expressions (`1d4`, `2d4`, `1d6`, etc.) are preserved verbatim from the scans. Named individuals per `name_glossary.md` are excluded and listed separately.

**This file is FAITHFUL to the scans** — it does not invent monsters or change counts. For the creative naming deliverable, see `monsters_phase2_descriptions.md`.
