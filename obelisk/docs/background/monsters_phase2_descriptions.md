# Phase 2 — Named Monster Descriptions (Creative Deliverable)

**Companion to** `monsters_phase1_scan.md`. For every unnamed/generic monster group, this file supplies a **tribe name**, **named individuals** (with distinguishing features), **CR** (from `monsters_cr_reference.md`), and **group flavor**. Module-supplied tribe labels (Cragmaw, Sawplee, Redbrands, Cult of the Obelisk, Spawn Hollow slaadi, Naruv's Feeder Trenches grells, Nellik's rebel mezzoloths, Qunbraxel's grimlocks, Brughor's Wyvern Tor raiders, Thundertree Dragon Cult, Old Owl Well undead, Wave Echo Cave undead) are KEPT with a flavorful full name added. Truly untribed monsters get an invented D&D-style tribe/warren/lair/pack name. **No already-named NPC/creature is renamed** — the glossary's named leaders stay exactly as written and only their unnamed subordinates receive names.

## 1.1 Goblins — Cragmaw band ("the Jagged-Tooth Tribe")  (chapters 1–4)
**CR:** 1/4 (goblin); 1 (goblin boss)
**Tribe name (full):** Cragmaw band — the Jagged-Tooth Tribe (named for their filed teeth and the goblin god they invented, the Jagged Tooth).
**Named leaders already in module:** King Grol (supreme chief); Klarg (Hideout bugbear); Yeemik & Errk (Hideout goblin bosses); Yegg (Castle cook-boss); Lhupo (Castle shrine-boss). ALL stay exactly as named.
**Named individuals (this group's members):**
- **Zark** — filed his four front teeth into broken shards; left ear missing (lost to a wolf bite); carries a notched javelin. The loudest of the H2 guards, he counts kills by carving notches into his thigh-guards.
- **Mog** — pock-marked face smeared with red clay war-paint; one eye clouded white from a childhood fungus. Skulks in H6 and hoards shiny pebbles he thinks are dragon eggs.
- **Snik** — smallest of the den, with a hairless tail of scar-tissue down his back from a whipping by Klarg; squeaks when he lies. Watches the H5 rope-bridge and is proud he's never been spotted.
- **Grish** — broad-nosed with a single tusk grown long through a split lip; obsessed with the "long-shiny-road" (the Triboar Trail) and the caravans that cross it. Leads the H7 pool guards.
- **Vex** — three parallel claw-scars across her scalp (a Cragmaw rite of passage); missing the pinky of her left hand. Foul-tempered, trains the H3 kennel wolves and bites anyone who gets close.
- **Krib** — soot-stained from the C7 banquet hall heaves; a lazy eye and a permanent grin from a cracked jaw. Believes King Grol can hear his thoughts and whispers reports to empty corners.
- **Thok** — scarred knuckles from fist-fighting the other C4 goblins; a crude tattoo of a wolf (Ripper) scratched into his shoulder. Distrusts bugbears and spits when they pass.
- **Bilge** — born with six fingers on his right hand, hidden inside a mitten; superstitious, carries a "lucky" rat skull on a string. The Castle's unofficial alarm-runner.
**Group flavor:** The Cragmaws are a miserable, snapping goblinoid band whose teeth are filed to jagged points "to make them look fiercer." They fight to the death until only one remains, then flee — a survival reflex beaten into them by bugbear overlords and a bullying chief. Under King Grol they are paid by the Spider, and they resent Klarg's cruel favoritism even as they cower before it.

## 1.2 Goblin Boss — Cragmaw ("the Arrow-Slit Watch")  (chapters 1, 3)

**Tribe name (full):** Cragmaw band — the Arrow-Slit Watch (the shortbow-armed elite posted at Cragmaw Castle's arrow slits).

**Named individuals (this group's members):**
