- **Redbrand barracks & workshop (Ch. 6)** — Four Redbrands surprised and killed; the bugbear door barricaded with ale barrels; the wizard's workshop looted. *Consequence:* scarlet cloaks acquired (disguise + skeleton pacifier); three 25-gp reagents; **Urmon's journal** (Lost Mine, Forge of Spells, **Lightbringer**).
- **The pursuit of Iarno Albrek (Ch. 6)** — Caught fleeing through his own secret door, wounded by *Firebolt* and javelin. *Consequence:* **he teleported away and escaped**, leaving behind the Spider's letter, his treasure chest, and his cistern escape kit.
- **The Tresendar crypts (Ch. 6–7)** — Six skeletons; Maela turned two, the other four destroyed. *Consequence:* jeweled gold bracelet (140 gp); two surviving skeletons now stand down for red-cloak wearers.
- **The slave pens (Ch. 7)** — Guard ambush defeated; locks picked. *Consequence:* **the Dendrar family freed** — Mirna and her two children.
- **The cellar deception (Ch. 7)** — Zenvon, in a red cloak, convinced a room of Redbrands their own man was the traitor. *Consequence:* the remaining upper-cellar Redbrands dead including **Dosa Rook**; **Wick** captured, interrogated, and executed; gold/silver/electrum and two garnets recovered; potion of healing and **potion of invisibility** recovered from the cistern.
- **Freeing and escorting the Dendrars (Ch. 8)** — The captives had barricaded themselves in; the crypt skeletons stood aside for the red cloaks. Zenvon fed them, then failed a Deception roll about Thel's fate and handed the telling to Maela, who broke it gently. *Consequence:* **Mirna, Nars and Nilsa delivered safely to the outskirts of Phandalin**; Mirna gave up the **Thundertree emerald necklace** and asked the party to recover Thel's body — a promise now outstanding.
- **The crevasse chest (Ch. 8)** — Zenvon used *Minor Illusion* to fake the sound of Glasstaff at work; Ssarnak went to investigate and left the crevasse unguarded. The chest was opened at range with *Mage Hand*. *Consequence:* 160 sp, 120 gp, five malachite gems (12 gp each), potions of **healing, climbing and growth**, and **Talon** — a +1 longsword, unidentified at the table, given to Pip. **Thel Dendrar's remains and his unicorn pendant were never searched for and are still down there.**
- **The bugbear barracks — the party's first defeat (Ch. 8)** — In red cloaks, Zenvon rage-baited the bugbear leader **Nosk** into naming **Cragmaw Castle** as the Black Spider's location, then lost the fight that followed. *Consequence:* one bugbear dead in an oil fire; **three survive, wounded, and are loose**; **Droop unaccounted for**; the party escaped through a door they believe they locked behind them. First time this party has run from a fight.
- **Reporting to Sildar (Ch. 8)** — The Spider's letter presented; Iarno's treason proved. *Consequence:* **250 gp paid in full** (150 on the night, 100 previously); a new bounty on Iarno — **200 gp alive, 100 dead, 50 if he cannot be raised**; Sildar conceded that the Black Spider is the more important prize.
- **The Shrine of Luck and the Miner's Exchange (Ch. 8)** — Sister Garaele, recovered, commissioned the party to carry a **jeweled silver comb** to the banshee **Agatha** near Conyberry in exchange for three potions of healing. Halia Thornton offered **100 gp for Glasstaff's correspondence** and was refused. *Consequence:* a new quest accepted; Halia rebuffed and now certain the party is effective and not cheaply bought.
