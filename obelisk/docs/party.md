## Party Overview

**Current location:** **Phandalin**, long-rested — a free night at the Stonehill Inn from Toblen after the Redbrands fell. First proper rest since the crypts. Business in town is done: Sildar paid, Garaele commissioned, Halia rebuffed.

**What they just did:** Freed the Dendrars and walked them home; robbed the crevasse without looking for the body they'd promised to find; **lost their first fight** to four bugbears; and escaped by pouring seven pounds of oil across a hallway and lighting it. On the way out a nothic named itself and told Veyra her missing teacher is alive. Then Zenvon, four sessions into lying professionally, told the truth about the black stone in his pocket.

**Active quests / threads:**
- **Sildar Hallwinter's contract** — **delivered.** The Spider's letter was presented in Ch. 8, Iarno's treason proved, and the full **250 gp** paid. New standing bounty on Iarno: **200 gp alive, 100 dead, 50 if he cannot be raised.** Sildar has also offered **500 gp to locate Cragmaw Castle** — a number the party has not yet heard, because nobody asked.
- **Cragmaw Castle** — **named** by the bugbear Nosk in Ch. 8 as the Black Spider's location. The party believes it. They have no route there and nobody in Phandalin knows the way.
- **Agatha and the comb (Sister Garaele)** — carry a jeweled silver comb to a banshee near Conyberry and ask after Bowgentle's spellbook. Payment: three potions of healing. Accepted, not started. Garaele: "don't take too much time."
- **Thel Dendrar's body** — Mirna was told the truth and asked for his remains. They are at the bottom of the crevasse the party looted without searching. **This is now a promise, not an errand.**
- **The three loose bugbears** — wounded, at large, and the only creatures within reach who know the way to Cragmaw Castle. The party thinks they are still behind a locked door.
- **Wyvern Tor** — 120 gp from Harbin Wester, negotiated up by Pip. Accepted, not started.
- **Old Owl Well** — Boots of Striding and Springing from Daran Edermath. Accepted, not started.
- **Return the Lionshield Coster goods** to Linene Graywind. Carried since Ch. 3, still undelivered.
- **Gundren Rockseeker** — still missing, presumed at Cragmaw Castle, location unknown.
- **Halia Thornton** — visited in Ch. 8. She offered **100 gp** for Glasstaff's correspondence and Zenvon refused, telling her the information was worth far more. The party left convinced she knows more than she says. *(They are right, and wrong about why.)*
- **Mirna Dendrar's heirloom** — an emerald necklace behind the storage shelves of the family shop in the **southeast parcel of Thundertree**. Offered freely. Pip wants it for armour.
- **The obelisk** — **half-assembled out loud in Ch. 8.** Veyra asked Pip what his shard felt like to hold; Zenvon produced his own stone and her crystal flared brighter; she named Orryn Voss's theory that a device was working on people, and the party connected it to the elongated-skull goblins. Three of the four are now in the conversation. **Maela is not**, and wore a look nobody could read.

**Collective resources:** The Spider's sealed letter (**still held — Halia's 100 gp refused**); **Urmon's journal** (Dwarvish — the Lost Mine, the Forge of Spells, and *Lightbringer*); **Talon**, a +1 longsword carried by Pip and unidentified at the table; **scarlet Redbrand cloaks** worn by the whole party, which both disguise them and pacify the crypt skeletons; **potion of invisibility**; potions of **climbing** and **growth**; potions of healing (Klarg's two plus the crevasse chest's, less the one Zenvon drank mid-fight); a **jeweled silver comb** for Agatha; jeweled gold bracelet (140 gp); five malachite gems (12 gp each); three reagents at 25 gp each (Mercury, Dragon Bile, Powdered Nightshade); carnelians, peridots, two garnets (10 gp each); the unappraised jade frog statuette; 1,700 cp; **250 gp from Sildar in total**, plus 160 sp and 120 gp from the crevasse chest and looted coin from five separate fights. *(No running coin total appears in any session record — worth reconciling at the table.)*

**Standing constraint:** Zenvon and Pip still have no darkvision; Maela and Veyra carry the light.

**Group reputation:** They are the heroes of Phandalin and everyone says so — Toblen gave them a free night, Garaele called them "the new heroes of Phandalin," Halia congratulated them, Sildar praised them backhandedly. The town has decided the Redbrand problem is over. **It is not**: three wounded bugbears are loose out of the manor at the east edge of town, and the party does not know it.

## Characters

### Zenvon Forepot
- **Class/Level:** **Rogue 3 — Arcane Trickster** | **Species:** Halfling | **Player:** Nikhil Reddy
- **New in Ch. 8:** the Arcane Trickster archetype — *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, and *Thunderwave* (save DC 12). All three carried the session: *Minor Illusion* faked Glasstaff at work and drew the nothic off the crevasse; *Mage Hand* opened the chest at range, eased the barracks door, and lit the oil; *Thunderwave* opened the bugbear fight while deliberately sparing Droop.
- **Traits & motivation:** Started as the scout. Has become the party's face. Two of the last three sessions were won by his mouth rather than his blades — talking down a Redbrand ambush he'd walked into alone, mimicking a bandit's voice through a door to send a bugbear away, and, in Ch. 7, striding into a room of hostile bandits in a red cloak and convincing them their own man was the traitor. He is also the party's executioner: he read Wick as a man who'd take gold and vanish, and killed him rather than let him go.
- **Relationships:** Leads. Pip backs his plays with violence when the talking stops (the knife to Rondar's throat); Maela pushes back when he'd rather not stop for the helpless, and he relents. Pulled Pip out of a twenty-foot pit on his whip.
- **Items of significance:** **Ornate short sword** from the Redbrand armory (semi-precious stones — he traded up on looks, and Maela rolled her eyes); Scimitar (Nick), Dagger, Javelin, **handaxe**; thieves' tools, used to free the captives; a **whip**, pressed into service as a rope and used to haul Pip out of the pit trap (the GM offered rope; Zenvon's player corrected him — Zenvon carries no rope). **The black stone he pocketed at the cart, where they first saw the black obelisk — which in Ch. 8, after four sessions of silence, he finally produced and showed to Veyra.**
- **Recent notable actions:** Negotiated Sildar up to 100 gp upfront after refusing an appeal to loyalty; tailed the Redbrand spotter into the ambush and opened with *"Are you not going to introduce me to your friends?"*; wounded Iarno in the escape passage; dropped to **4 HP** against a skeleton and **was never healed** — he withdrew and finished the crypt fight there; ran the barracks deception; executed Wick. **In Ch. 8:** talked the barricaded Dendrars out from behind their own door; failed a Deception roll to Mirna about Thel and handed the telling to Maela rather than lie again; rage-baited Nosk into naming Cragmaw Castle; took 14 from a morning star and dropped to 6; drank a potion mid-fight; and, when the party was beaten, poured seven pounds of oil across a hallway and lit it with *Mage Hand*. **Told Veyra about the stone.**

### Veyra of the Blue Candle
- **Tiefling mage sidekick, Level 3** (the overdue bump was paid in Ch. 8; parity with Zenvon held).
- **Traits & motivation:** The party's artillery and its only scholar. She is the one who knows what things are — she identified the nothic on sight and named Vecna as the source of its curse, and she priced Iarno's reagents from across the room. She keeps a notebook and reads it by candlelight when everyone else sleeps.
- **The thread she carries:** her **blue crystal flared** the night she wrote down the name **"Ruxithid."** She announced it at breakfast; Maela rolled her eyes and Pip went quiet. Her mentor **Orryn Voss** is still missing, and still the man who said the obelisk fragments must never be assembled.
- **Relationships:** Closest to Zenvon, who follows her lead on anything arcane and who ordered her to wound Iarno rather than kill him. Tolerated with affection by Maela.
- **Items of significance:** The **blue candle / crystal** — an obelisk detector she doesn't fully understand; darkvision 60 ft.
- **Recent notable actions:** *Magic Missile* killed a Redbrand in the street ambush; cleared the barracks with a final burst; struck the fleeing Iarno with *Firebolt*; **ended Ch. 7 completely out of first-level slots**. Also the one who rolled the Investigation that found the **dead goblins with elongated skulls** in the caverns. **In Ch. 8** she firebolted bugbears, and then had the session's two biggest moments handed to her: Ssarnak stopped her as she fled past the crevasse to tell her **her teacher is not dead — "he is not in a place where dead is easy"** — and she broke back toward the creature screaming "I've got to go talk to him" until **Maela grappled her**. Later she asked Pip what his shard felt like to hold, and Zenvon answered by producing his own.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge

- **Traits & motivation:** Healer, front-line hammer, and the party's stated conscience — *"Boss, I think we should help the helpless. We're the good guys, remember?"* She is also the one who executed a bound, begging prisoner with a mace and said *"It's nothing personal, you see."* Both of those are her. Nobody at the table has picked at the seam yet.
- **Relationships:** Overrules Zenvon on moral questions and gets away with it. Heals everyone. Reads people — her Insight caught Rondar lying before Pip's knife got the truth out.

- **Mechanical note:** **Turn Undead was added to her stat block by DM ruling mid-Ch. 7** ("clerics that can't turn undead are kind of pointless"). She used it immediately, routing two of the six crypt skeletons. **This should be written into `characters/maela.md`.**
- **Recent notable actions:** Turned the crypt skeletons; healed **Pip twice** — 10 HP in the crypts and 6 HP out of the pit (she never healed Zenvon, who ended the crypt fight at 4 HP); incinerated a ruffian with *Sacred Flame*; found **Urmon's journal** on the workshop shelves; killed Rondar. **In Ch. 8:** took the job of telling Mirna how her husband died and softened it rather than describing what the party actually saw; pushed Zenvon aside to listen at the barracks door; called "Medic!" and healed Zenvon for seven mid-brawl; failed to talk Veyra down and **physically grappled her** to stop her running back to the nothic.
- **The thread she carries now:** after the obelisk conversation she wore a **sour expression nobody could read** — insight and investigation both came back with nothing. She is the only party member who has not bought into Veyra's theory, and nobody has asked her why.

### Pip Thistlewick
- **Human fighter sidekick, Level 3** (the overdue bump was paid in Ch. 8).
- **Traits & motivation:** The muscle who turns out to be the negotiator. He talked Harbin Wester up from 100 gp to 120 and impressed a banker doing it; he nudged Zenvon not to work for free in front of a Lords' Alliance agent — *"heroes, you know, we also need to eat."* In a fight he leads with a one-liner (*"Never did like the crooked kind"*) and he is the one who put a blade to Rondar's throat when Zenvon's intimidation failed.
- **The thread he carries:** the nothic pulled his secret out of his head and told the whole party — **he won a blackstone shard in a dice game near Neverwinter's docks and sold it to a peddler heading for Phandalin, and has dreamed of broken black stone beneath the earth and heard whispers saying his name ever since.** He went pale and told the party to kill the thing. **Nobody has raised it since.**
- **Relationships:** Zenvon's enforcer and his financial adviser. Supports Maela in the line. Sees the tactical picture — he's the one who warned about being caught between the bugbears and the barracks, and who suggested the ale-barrel barricade.
- **Items of significance:** **Talon** — the +1 longsword from the crevasse chest, handed to him as the party's primary fighter, which nobody has identified and which he missed with on his first swing; **ornate crossbow** from the armory; javelins (one of them hit Iarno mid-flight).
- **Recent notable actions:** Negotiated the Wyvern Tor fee; wounded the fleeing Iarno; killed Redbrands in both the slave pens and the upper cellar; **fell twenty feet into a pit trap** chasing a runner and was hauled out on Zenvon's whip. **In Ch. 8:** rolled a natural 20 on the approach stealth; lobbied hard for the Thundertree necklace — *"I'm all for heroism… but I do want to get better armor"* — and slammed the barracks door shut to buy the seconds the oil trap needed.

## Party Dynamics

The division of labor from Ch. 3 still holds — Maela and Veyra carry light and firepower, Zenvon scouts and finishes, Pip sets up and absorbs — but the last four sessions rewired how the party actually wins. **They now win by lying.** The red cloaks, the mimicked voice, the false accusation in the barracks: the fights they chose were short because the setup was good, and Zenvon has grown into a face rather than just a knife.

**Chapter 8 is where that stopped working.** The same red cloaks that fooled a room of bandits got laughed at by a bugbear — *"leave it to a Redbrand to think he's in charge"* — and the con that followed worked *too* well, extracting Cragmaw Castle and a fight the party could not win in the same breath. They ran. They got out on improvisation rather than deception: a door held shut, seven pounds of oil, and a cantrip used as a match. It is the first evidence this party has that talking is not a universal solvent, and nobody has said that out loud either.

The moral center has moved too, and not in one direction. Maela is the one who insists on saving captives *and* the one who executed a prisoner. Zenvon freed a boy from a cage with his own tools in the same hour he killed a man who had surrendered. Pip made the tactical case for feeding Rondar to a monster. This is a party that has decided it is good and has started doing things that don't match, and nobody has said so.

Both secrets came out in Ch. 8. Veyra asked Pip what his shard felt like to hold, and Zenvon — unprompted, and after four sessions of saying nothing — produced his own stone and let her point the crystal at it. It flared brighter. That conversation was the one thing the party had been too busy to have, and having it moved the obelisk from Veyra's private theory to a thing three of them are now looking at together. Zenvon came out of it trusting her more than he did, and kept that to himself.

**The new unaddressed thing is Maela.** She said nothing during the obelisk conversation and wore a face nobody could read, and the party rolled to read it and got nothing. She is also the one who has now been asked twice to be the party's conscience and twice supplied violence instead — the mace to Rondar, the grapple on Veyra. There is a version of her sour expression that is about the stones, and a version that is about a fainted goblin the party stepped over in a room they then set on fire. Neither has been picked at.

Zenvon is an **Arcane Trickster** (*Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Thunderwave* at DC 12). The party is **long-rested** for the first time since the crypts.
