## Party Overview

**Current location:** Inside the Redbrand hideout beneath **Tresendar Manor**, outside the holding cells, with three freed captives — Mirna Dendrar and her two children — at the end of a very long day. No long rest has been taken since the session opened in the crypts.

**What they just did:** Broke the Redbrands as a power in Phandalin over four sessions; unmasked **Glasstaff as Iarno Albrek** and lost him to a teleport by a single round; fed a bound prisoner to a nothic to buy passage; and walked out of a dungeon with a wizard's correspondence, his gold, and his escape kit.

**Active quests / threads:**
- **Sildar Hallwinter's contract** — find Iarno Albrek, identify the Black Spider. 150 gp paid so far, more promised. **Sildar has not been told what they found.**
- **Wyvern Tor** — 120 gp from Harbin Wester, negotiated up by Pip. Accepted, not started.
- **Old Owl Well** — Boots of Striding and Springing from Daran Edermath. Accepted, not started.
- **Return the Lionshield Coster goods** to Linene Graywind. Carried since Ch. 3, still undelivered.
- **Gundren Rockseeker** — still missing, presumed at Cragmaw Castle, location unknown.
- **Halia Thornton's invitation** — a standing offer to Zenvon to visit the Miner's Exchange for "business."
- **Mirna Dendrar's question** — she will ask what happened to her husband. The answer is at the bottom of the crevasse.
- **The obelisk** — Pip's shard, Zenvon's black stone, Veyra's candle, Orryn Voss, the dead elongated-skull goblins. The party has every piece and has not assembled them out loud.

**Collective resources:** The Spider's sealed letter; **Urmon's journal** (Dwarvish — the Lost Mine, the Forge of Spells, and *Lightbringer*); **scarlet Redbrand cloaks** worn by the whole party, which both disguise them and pacify the crypt skeletons; **potion of invisibility**; three potions of healing; jeweled gold bracelet (140 gp); three reagents at 25 gp each (Mercury, Dragon Bile, Powdered Nightshade); carnelians, peridots, two garnets (10 gp each); the unappraised jade frog statuette; 1,700 cp; 150 gp from Sildar plus looted gold, silver, electrum, and copper from four separate fights. *(No running coin total appears in any session record — worth reconciling at the table.)*

**Standing constraint:** Zenvon and Pip still have no darkvision; Maela and Veyra carry the light.

**Group reputation:** Changed sharply. They fought four Redbrands in the open street in front of the Townmaster's Hall and won; Harbin Wester came out and hired them; the whole town knows the gang's grip is broken. They are also currently **wearing the dead gang's cloaks**, which is going to read badly to anyone who sees them coming up out of that tunnel.

## Characters

### Zenvon Foreput
- **Class/Level:** Rogue 2 | **Species:** Halfling | **Player:** Nikhil Reddy
- **Traits & motivation:** Started as the scout. Has become the party's face. Two of the last three sessions were won by his mouth rather than his blades — talking down a Redbrand ambush he'd walked into alone, mimicking a bandit's voice through a door to send a bugbear away, and, in Ch. 7, striding into a room of hostile bandits in a red cloak and convincing them their own man was the traitor. He is also the party's executioner: he read Wick as a man who'd take gold and vanish, and killed him rather than let him go.
- **Relationships:** Leads. Pip backs his plays with violence when the talking stops (the knife to Rondar's throat); Maela pushes back when he'd rather not stop for the helpless, and he relents. Pulled Pip out of a twenty-foot pit on his whip.
- **Items of significance:** **Ornate short sword** from the Redbrand armory (semi-precious stones — he traded up on looks, and Maela rolled her eyes); Scimitar (Nick), Dagger, Javelin, **handaxe**; thieves' tools, used to free the captives; a **whip**, pressed into service as a rope and used to haul Pip out of the pit trap (the GM offered rope; Zenvon's player corrected him — Zenvon carries no rope). **And the black stone he pocketed on the road — which he still has not told anyone about.**
- **Recent notable actions:** Negotiated Sildar up to 100 gp upfront after refusing an appeal to loyalty; tailed the Redbrand spotter into the ambush and opened with *"Are you not going to introduce me to your friends?"*; wounded Iarno in the escape passage; dropped to **4 HP** against a skeleton and **was never healed** — he withdrew and finished the crypt fight there; ran the barracks deception; executed Wick.

## Party Dynamics

The division of labor from Ch. 3 still holds — Maela and Veyra carry light and firepower, Zenvon scouts and finishes, Pip sets up and absorbs — but the last four sessions rewired how the party actually wins. **They now win by lying.** The red cloaks, the mimicked voice, the false accusation in the barracks: the fights they chose were short because the setup was good, and Zenvon has grown into a face rather than just a knife.

The moral center has moved too, and not in one direction. Maela is the one who insists on saving captives *and* the one who executed a prisoner. Zenvon freed a boy from a cage with his own tools in the same hour he killed a man who had surrendered. Pip made the tactical case for feeding Rondar to a monster. This is a party that has decided it is good and has started doing things that don't match, and nobody has said so.

Two secrets sit in the group unaddressed. **Pip's** is public — the nothic broadcast it and then a fight started and it was dropped. **Zenvon's** is not: he has a black stone in his pocket, taken on the road, and he hasn't mentioned it to the woman whose crystal lights up around such things. Veyra is one conversation away from putting the whole obelisk plot together, and the only reason she hasn't is that nobody has stopped moving long enough to talk.
