## NPC Dossiers

### Iarno Albrek — "Glasstaff" *(primary antagonist, now personal)*
- **Location/Status:** **At large.** Teleported out of his own escape passage in Ch. 6 after taking a *Firebolt* and a javelin. Wounded when he left.
- **What he lost:** his quarters, his chest of gold and gems, his workshop and reagents, **the Spider's letter**, the cistern escape kit (potion of invisibility, potion of healing) — and eight men. He is running on whatever he had on his person and his glass staff.
- **Goals:** survive; explain himself to the Spider; not be found by the Lords' Alliance. *(Claude-proposed: he needs money and a bolt-hole, which makes him vulnerable to whoever in Phandalin has been giving him orders.)*
- **Party knows:** his name, his face, his treason, his handwriting, and that he answers to the Spider. **Hidden:** where he went and whether he'll run or retaliate. *("Who in town handles him" is a question with no answer — see the header ruling.)*
- **Bounty as of Ch. 8:** Sildar pays **200 gp alive, 100 dead, 50 if he cannot be raised.** He'd prefer a trial in Neverwinter and admits he "would not be upset if he came back dead."
- **Playing him** (from `notes/redbrand_hideout.md`): airs and courtesy. "My good fellows." Murders are "those unfortunate events." He values his own life above everything and will negotiate the instant he can't escape.
- **Truths he'll give up in captivity:** the Black Spider is a **drow**; the Spider sent the bugbears; the bugbears know the way to Wave Echo Cave and he does not; the Spider wants the **Forge of Spells**; no one in the Lords' Alliance knows he turned.
- **Leverage:** Sildar. Also: the Spider does not tolerate failure, and Iarno knows it.

### Sildar Hallwinter
- **Location/Status:** Alive in Phandalin. No longer travels with the party.
- **Goals:** find Iarno; identify the Black Spider; secure Wave Echo Cave for Lord Neverember and the Lords' Alliance.
- **Delivered in Ch. 8:** he was shown the letter, was appalled, paid the balance (**250 gp total** — the table's negotiated figure, above the module's 200), posted the Iarno bounty, and conceded that the Spider matters more: *"if Iarno fled, the most likely place for him to have fled was to his boss. So perhaps we will get two birds with one stone."*
- **Still on the table and unspoken:** the module has him offering **500 gp to locate Cragmaw Castle and defeat or drive off its leader**, plus the method — search the lands around the Triboar Trail for raiding parties. **Nobody has asked him.** This is the single largest unclaimed thing in Phandalin.
- **Also unlocked:** the module gates the Lords' Alliance recruitment on *eliminating the Cragmaw threat **or** uncovering Iarno's treachery.* The party has done the second. Sildar can offer a character the title **"Cloak"** and a pin whenever you want it, and he offers it to those who protect vulnerable folk — which points at Maela before Zenvon, and Sildar will notice that.
- **Voice note:** the class condescension is load-bearing and he cannot help it — *"for someone of your background, you have done well,"* delivered while visibly not saying "wrong side of the tracks."

### Halia Thornton
- **Location/Status:** Alive; Phandalin Miner's Exchange. **Standing invitation to Zenvon** for "future business arrangements."
- **What changed:** she was found at a table with the Redbrand lieutenant Rondar, publicly feigned shock at his affiliation while he wore the cloak, was detained by the party through the interrogation, and heard the **Glasstaff = Iarno** reveal at the same moment they did.
- **Goals:** unconfirmed. She wants leverage, contacts, and information — and she now knows the party is effective.
- **What happened in Ch. 8:** she congratulated them, **stopped smiling the instant she learned Glasstaff had escaped rather than died**, and asked directly for "any correspondence among his personal effects," claiming she wanted to know whether deeper plots threatened local trade. She offered **100 gp**. Zenvon refused and told her the information was worth far more. She replied *"You like to negotiate?"* and let them go.
- **The live question is settled and the party doesn't know it.** She is **not** Iarno's handler — there is no handler. She is a **Zhentarim** agent who wanted Glasstaff removed so she could take the Redbrands over herself. Every instinct the party has about her is right except the one they are testing, and that is the useful shape: their suspicion is correct in spirit and wrong in fact.
- **What she does next** *(Claude-proposed)*: she does not ask a third time. She establishes herself as a good counterparty — buys the reagents, the malachite, appraises the jade frog fairly — and she works out **when the party will be out of town.** She also keeps assessing whether Zenvon is Zhentarim material; the module's title for that is **"Fang."** Sildar's Lords' Alliance offer is live in the same week, which means Zenvon can be recruited twice in one morning by people who don't know about each other.

### The nothic (Ssarnak)
- **Location/Status:** Alive, in the crevasse cave, **fed three times** (Rondar, the Redbrand dead, then the gambling-room dead). **It named itself in Ch. 8.**
- **Goals:** meat. That's the whole of it.
- **Standing arrangement:** it let the party pass in exchange for one body. Iarno's last order to it was to **guard the entrance** — an instruction it has no particular reason to honor now that Iarno isn't paying.
- **What it can still trade for food:** the full hideout layout and every secret door; the skeleton password **"Illefarn"**; that there are bugbears it finds annoying; that Glasstaff had a glass staff and a little invisible demon watching his workshop; and what it knows about the two dead elongated-skull goblins — they came up the south tunnel about two weeks ago, moved like they were searching for something, ignored it until it attacked, and **tasted wrong**.
- **Weird Insight** (once per rest, one secret from one creature it can see): used on Pip in Ch. 5, and **the Veyra line was spent in Ch. 8** — *"Your teacher is not dead. He is not in a place where dead is easy."* She broke back toward the crevasse to ask more and Maela had to grapple her; **the follow-up conversation has not happened and Ssarnak is still down there.** Still unused: *"You took something on the road. It knows it was moved"* (Zenvon's black stone) — though Zenvon has since produced the stone voluntarily, so this now lands differently.
- **New leverage it has that the party would want:** it watched the three surviving bugbears leave. *(Claude-proposed: it will trade what it saw — how many, how hurt, which way they went — for a meal.)*
- **Leverage:** Maela will have opinions about any ongoing feeding arrangement. That tension is the interesting part.

### Nosk and the bugbears — *now the campaign's loose end*
- **Location/Status:** **Loose.** The party opened the door in Ch. 8, was mocked, extracted Cragmaw Castle from Nosk by rage-baiting him, lost the fight, and ran behind burning oil. **One bugbear died in the fire. Three survive, wounded.** Where they went is unanswered and is the biggest single staging decision for Ch. 9.
- **The key.** `(module)` Nosk carries an iron key that **locks and unlocks every door in the Redbrands' hideout.** The party locked the manor door behind them and was told it would take the bugbears three rounds to break through. If Nosk lived, it took none — they have been out since the party turned their backs, and were out for the whole long rest at the Stonehill. **Decide whether Nosk was the one who burned.**
- **Allegiance:** the **Spider's**, not Glasstaff's — Nosk said so out loud: *"Black Spider. We work for Black Spider… Glasstaff works for Black Spider. And don't you forget it."*
- **What they know:** the locations of **Cragmaw Castle and Wave Echo Cave**. They will not share it willingly. They are now also the party's **most accessible route north**, which makes the creatures that beat them the map they are missing.
- **Droop:** Scraptops goblin, press-ganged, wants to go home. **Fainted at the party's feet and was still on the floor when the hallway caught fire — unaccounted for.** He knows the hideout layout, its secret doors and traps, and that Cragmaw Castle is "up north, in the forest," no better than that. *(Claude-proposed: alive and hiding near the tunnel mouth is the better option — he is worthless as a guide and valuable as a receipt for the party's habit of stepping over people.)*

### Wick (deceased) — what he left behind
Three pieces of intelligence outlive him. **Two of them are false**, and that is now the point:
1. **"Glasstaff answers to someone in Phandalin."** *There is no such person.* Iarno reported to the Spider directly. A thug at the bottom of the food chain saw sealed letters arrive off the Triboar road and assumed a local boss, because thugs assume the world is small. **The party is chasing a conspiracy a frightened man invented in the ninety seconds before Zenvon killed him.** Do not resolve this out loud; both the party's suspicion and Halia's genuine guilt survive indefinitely, and the moment an NPC issues a clean denial the board collapses.
2. **"The dungeon has more levels."** *It does not.* He was describing the natural caverns, the crevasse, and a nothic in his head. Route any search here and let them be satisfied.
3. **"Black Spider"** made him flinch and go pale while he insisted he knew nothing. **This one is true.**

## Faction States

### The Redbrands
- **Status: destroyed as a street power.** Body count across Ch. 4–7: three in the street ambush plus one prisoner, four in the barracks, the cell guards, the upper-cellar crew, Rondar, Dosa Rook, Wick. Glasstaff fled.
- **What's left:** Glasstaff's ability to rebuild from outside. The bugbear contingent never really belonged to them and, as of Ch. 8, is **out of the hideout and unaccounted for**.
- **The unanswered question:** the **armory**. Racks of spears, swords, crossbows, bolts, and a dozen spare cloaks — far more than a dozen thugs needed. They were arming up for something. Nobody has asked what.

### The Spider's network
- **Confirmed structure:** the Spider → Iarno (by sealed letter) → the Redbrands; the Spider → the bugbears directly (**Nosk said so out loud in Ch. 8**); the Spider → the Cragmaw goblins (the Triboar ambush). Wick's "unidentified handler in Phandalin" is **not part of this structure and never was** — see his dossier above.
- **Objective:** Wave Echo Cave and the **Forge of Spells**, via Gundren's map.
- **Standing order regarding the party:** capture if possible, kill if necessary, take any dwarven maps. In writing. The party is holding the letter.
- **Per GM prep:** the Black Spider is a **drow** — Glasstaff will confirm this if taken alive, and knows the title but not a name.
- **The Ch. 8 intel and its useful flaw:** Nosk named **Cragmaw Castle** as where the Spider is, and the party believes it. Module canon puts Nezznar at **Wave Echo Cave**; what is actually at the castle is **Vyerith**, the Spider's doppelganger messenger, negotiating with King Grol for Gundren. **Nosk was not lying — he was wrong in a way that will look right when they arrive.** The party goes hunting the Black Spider and finds something wearing a face. Protect that reveal; do not correct the intel in advance.
- **The bounty is physical now.** `(module)` The Triboar Trail carries a **hobgoblin squad** hunting the party for the Spider, one of them holding a crude sketch of a single party member marked *"25 gold pieces for this one"* under a spider symbol. Decide whose face is on it before you run the road.

### The psionic goblins / the obelisk incursion
- **Status: active and unexamined.** Two dead goblins with elongated skulls and filthy rags in the hideout caverns *(Ch. 6 VTT)*. Same morphology as the goblin who said *"You're not what Ruxithid wants"* on the Triboar Trail.
- **Per GM prep:** they came up the south tunnel about two weeks before the party, searching for something; the nothic killed them; they were here **before the Redbrands mattered**. The ones who sent them don't yet know they're dead.
- **This is a second, unrelated conspiracy**, and it is **not under Tresendar Manor** — the hideout has no lower level and never did. Where it surfaces next, per the module, is **Wyvern Tor**, where elongated-skull goblins have been joining Brughor Axe-Biter's orc raiders on the Triboar Trail. That is a job **the party already accepted from Harbin and has not started.**
- **In Ch. 8 the party half-assembled the thread on their own** — Pip's shard, Zenvon's stone, Veyra's crystal flaring at both, Orryn's device theory, the mutated goblins, and Sildar's description of his interrogators, all named in one conversation outside the manor. Three of the four are now in it. **Maela is not.**

### Lords' Alliance
- Sildar; funding and directing the party. **As of Ch. 8 it has been told about its traitor** and has put a price on him. Recruitment is unlocked (title: **"Cloak"**), and Sildar's 500-gp castle contract is unclaimed because nobody asked.

## DM Notes

- **The session's shape, if you want one:** the party thinks they finished the dungeon. They finished *most* of it, lost the last fight in it, and left three witnesses alive with a key. Chapter 9 opens on a grateful town and an unlocked door, and the party gets to choose whether to leave before finding out. Staging for both branches is in `notes/the_door_they_locked.md`.

- **The hinge is Nosk's key, and it fires either way.** `(module)` Nosk carries an iron key that opens every door in the hideout. If he lived, the bugbears have been loose since the moment the party turned their backs — through the entire long rest. **Decide whether Nosk was the one who burned before you run the session**; it determines whether the party's best lead north is a leaderless trio or an enemy with a grudge and a name for them.

- **Glasstaff escaping is still better than Glasstaff captured.** Unchanged. A recurring humiliated wizard with a patron who punishes failure is worth more than a prisoner. *(But if they catch him, the truth list in his dossier is what he gives up — don't withhold it.)*

- **Wick's line has resolved into paranoia, not investigation.** "Glasstaff answers to somebody in town" has no referent. Do **not** let any NPC issue a clean denial — a convincing denial reads as elimination and collapses the board. Let everyone be evasive about something unrelated instead: Halia about her Zhentarim business, Harbin about the locked door he hid behind, Toblen about how much he knew and never said.

- **The red cloaks stopped being a skeleton key in Ch. 8 and nobody noticed.** They worked on the crypt skeletons and the terrified Dendrars; they got *laughed at* by a bugbear. The party has won four sessions by lying and has now met the first thing that wasn't buying it. That is a lesson worth letting them draw for themselves rather than stating.

- **Maela is the loaded gun.** She said nothing during the obelisk conversation, wore a sour expression, and the players rolled to read it and got nothing. Pick what it was before the next session — the stones, the execution of Wick, or the fainted goblin the party stepped over in a room they then set on fire. **The last one is free and detonates later.** A sidekick with an unexplained mood that the table has already noticed does not stay neutral.

- **Feeding the nothic is still a character question, not a tactical one** — and it has more leverage now. Ssarnak has no employer, has been fed three times, watched the bugbears leave, and knows something about Orryn Voss it has only half said. Veyra wants to go back. Maela grappled her to stop her. That is the seam.

- **Two faction pins are live in the same week.** Sildar can offer **"Cloak"** (the Iarno-treachery condition is met); Halia is assessing Zenvon for **"Fang"**. Offering both in one morning is the strongest version and also the one most likely to get answered carelessly — decide whether you want them spaced.

- **Sildar's 500 gp is the largest unclaimed thing in Phandalin** and it exists only if somebody asks him about Cragmaw Castle. If the party is drifting, that's the nudge: he raises it himself.

- **Overdue mechanics:** the level-ups were paid at the top of Ch. 8 — **all four at 3rd, parity held.** Still outstanding: writing **Turn Undead** into `characters/maela.md` per the Ch. 7 ruling, and giving Zenvon's sheet the **Arcane Trickster** additions (*Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Thunderwave*).

- **Loose hooks still ready to reconnect:** the jade frog (never appraised, never explained); **Talon**, carried as a generic magic sword by a party that looted the Tresendar crypt and never made the connection; the armory's over-supply; Elsa's scholar sister in Neverwinter; the manor above, which belongs to no one living and now stands open; **Zeond the quasit**, Glasstaff's invisible familiar, unaccounted for since Ch. 6 and appearing in no summary since.
