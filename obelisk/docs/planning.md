## NPC Dossiers

### Iarno Albrek — "Glasstaff" *(primary antagonist, now personal)*
- **Location/Status:** **At large.** Teleported out of his own escape passage in Ch. 6 after taking a *Firebolt* and a javelin. Wounded when he left.
- **What he lost:** his quarters, his chest of gold and gems, his workshop and reagents, **the Spider's letter**, the cistern escape kit (potion of invisibility, potion of healing) — and eight men. He is running on whatever he had on his person and his glass staff.
- **Goals:** survive; explain himself to the Spider; not be found by the Lords' Alliance. *(Claude-proposed: he needs money and a bolt-hole, which makes him vulnerable to whoever in Phandalin has been giving him orders.)*
- **Party knows:** his name, his face, his treason, his handwriting, and that he answers to the Spider. **Hidden:** where he went, who in town handles him, whether he'll run or retaliate.
- **Playing him** (from `notes/redbrand_hideout.md`): airs and courtesy. "My good fellows." Murders are "those unfortunate events." He values his own life above everything and will negotiate the instant he can't escape.
- **Truths he'll give up in captivity:** the Black Spider is a **drow**; the Spider sent the bugbears; the bugbears know the way to Wave Echo Cave and he does not; the Spider wants the **Forge of Spells**; no one in the Lords' Alliance knows he turned.
- **Leverage:** Sildar. Also: the Spider does not tolerate failure, and Iarno knows it.

### Sildar Hallwinter
- **Location/Status:** Alive and recovering in Phandalin. No longer travels with the party.
- **Goals:** find Iarno; identify the Black Spider; secure Wave Echo Cave for Lord Neverember and the Lords' Alliance.
- **Party knows:** the Phandelver Pact history; that Gundren was taken for the map; that Sildar pays (150 gp so far, more promised).
- **What he does not know:** that Iarno is Glasstaff. **This is the campaign's next emotional beat.**
- **Staging note** (from `notes/redbrand_hideout.md`): don't describe his face. Let him say Iarno's name once, quietly, then "Lord's Alliance business. I'll arrange the transport." He does not shout or weep. Don't rush it.
- **Module reward:** 200 gp for eliminating the Redbrand threat. *(Claude-proposed: pay it in full — the gang is destroyed — but let Sildar be visibly unsatisfied that Iarno walked.)*

### Halia Thornton
- **Location/Status:** Alive; Phandalin Miner's Exchange. **Standing invitation to Zenvon** for "future business arrangements."
- **What changed:** she was found at a table with the Redbrand lieutenant Rondar, publicly feigned shock at his affiliation while he wore the cloak, was detained by the party through the interrogation, and heard the **Glasstaff = Iarno** reveal at the same moment they did.
- **Goals:** unconfirmed. She wants leverage, contacts, and information — and she now knows the party is effective.
- **Wants the Spider's letter.** Per GM prep she'll pay **100 gp** for it and anything else from Iarno's chest, and she will file the name "Iarno Albrek" for later. She becomes more useful and more dangerous the more they give her.
- **The live question:** Wick's line — Glasstaff "answers to somebody… orders from town" — makes her the obvious candidate for Glasstaff's Phandalin handler. *(Claude-proposed: whether that's true is a GM decision that hasn't been made yet; either answer is playable, and she is a strong enough scene partner to survive being innocent.)*

### The nothic (Ssarnak)
- **Location/Status:** Alive, in the crevasse cave, fed twice by the party (Rondar, then the Redbrand dead).
- **Goals:** meat. That's the whole of it.
- **Standing arrangement:** it let the party pass in exchange for one body. Iarno's last order to it was to **guard the entrance** — an instruction it has no particular reason to honor now that Iarno isn't paying.
- **What it can still trade for food:** the full hideout layout and every secret door; the skeleton password **"Illefarn"**; that there are bugbears it finds annoying; that Glasstaff had a glass staff and a little invisible demon watching his workshop; and what it knows about the two dead elongated-skull goblins — they came up the south tunnel about two weeks ago, moved like they were searching for something, ignored it until it attacked, and **tasted wrong**.
- **Weird Insight** (once per rest, one secret from one creature it can see): already used on Pip. Unused prompts from prep — *"Your teacher is not dead. He is not in a place where dead is easy"* (Veyra); *"You took something on the road. It knows it was moved"* (Zenvon's black stone).
- **Leverage:** Maela will have opinions about any ongoing feeding arrangement. That tension is the interesting part.

### The bugbears (R9) + Droop
- **Location/Status:** Four bugbears and the goblin servant **Droop**, sealed behind a door the party barricaded with ale barrels in Ch. 6. Alive. One of them knocked, was talked away by Zenvon's voice trick, and went back to sleep.
- **Allegiance:** the **Spider's**, not Glasstaff's. Contemptuous of the Redbrands. If the party is wearing scarlet cloaks, they read as Redbrands and get dismissiveness rather than immediate violence.
- **What they know:** the locations of **Cragmaw Castle and Wave Echo Cave**. Fear of the Spider beats any pressure the party can bring — except *charm person*, which the party does not have. *(Claude-proposed: Veyra could learn it on her overdue level-up if you want that door open.)*
- **Droop:** a Scraptop goblin press-ganged into service; faints when strangers enter; wants to go home; knows the hideout layout and that Cragmaw Castle is north in the forest, general area only. A small beat, not an ally — don't oversell him.
- **Why this matters now:** they are the party's only in-hideout lead to Gundren, and they are behind a stack of barrels the party is currently walking away from.

## Faction States

### The Redbrands
- **Status: destroyed as a street power.** Body count across Ch. 4–7: three in the street ambush plus one prisoner, four in the barracks, the cell guards, the upper-cellar crew, Rondar, Desa Rook, Wick. Glasstaff fled.
- **What's left:** the bugbear contingent (who never really belonged to them), and Glasstaff's ability to rebuild from outside.
- **The unanswered question:** the **armory**. Racks of spears, swords, crossbows, bolts, and a dozen spare cloaks — far more than a dozen thugs needed. They were arming up for something. Nobody has asked what.

### The Spider's network
- **Confirmed structure:** the Spider → Iarno (by sealed letter) → the Redbrands; the Spider → the bugbears directly; the Spider → the Cragmaw goblins (the Triboar ambush). Plus, per Wick, **an unidentified handler in Phandalin** between the Spider and Glasstaff.
- **Objective:** Wave Echo Cave and the **Forge of Spells**, via Gundren's map.
- **Standing order regarding the party:** capture if possible, kill if necessary, take any dwarven maps. In writing. The party is holding the letter.
- **Per GM prep:** the Black Spider is a **drow** — Glasstaff will confirm this if taken alive, and knows the title but not a name.

### The psionic goblins / the obelisk incursion
- **Status: active and unexamined.** Two dead goblins with elongated skulls and filthy rags in the hideout caverns *(Ch. 6 VTT)*. Same morphology as the goblin who said *"You're not what Ruxithid wants"* on the Triboar Trail.
- **Per GM prep:** they came up the south tunnel about two weeks before the party, searching for something; the nothic killed them; they were here **before the Redbrands mattered**. The ones who sent them don't yet know they're dead.
- **This is a second, unrelated conspiracy operating in the same basement**, and Wick's "the subterranean part messes with your head" is its calling card.

### Lords' Alliance
- Sildar; funding and directing the party; carrying a traitor it hasn't been told about.

## Active Plots
*Ordered by urgency.*

1. **Tell Sildar that Glasstaff is Iarno.** Free, immediate, and the biggest character beat available. Unlocks: the 200-gp Redbrand bounty, Lords' Alliance resources for the manhunt, and Sildar as a changed man.
2. **Hunt Iarno Albrek.** Wounded, unfunded, and cut off from his own escape kit. Every hour he has is an hour to reach whoever handles him. *Intersection:* the Phandalin handler; Halia; the Spider.
3. **Open the bugbear door — or decide not to.** Four hostile soldiers behind barrels, holding the only in-building lead to **Cragmaw Castle and Wave Echo Cave**. *Intersection:* Gundren; Droop; the red cloaks the party is already wearing.
4. **Go down into the crevasse.** **Talon**, the nothic's hoard, three potions, **Thel Dendrar's remains and pendant**, and the two dead psionic goblins — all twenty feet below two bridges the party has crossed twice. *Intersection:* Mirna's question; the obelisk thread; the party's next magic weapon.
5. **Find the lower levels.** "Something really wrong… messes with your head." *Intersection:* the psionic goblins, Ruxithid, Pip's dreams, Veyra's candle. This is the obelisk plot's front door and it is inside a dungeon the party currently believes it has finished.
6. **Identify Glasstaff's Phandalin handler.** *Intersection:* Halia's invitation is a free scene the party has already been offered.
7. **Rescue Gundren / find Cragmaw Castle.** The spine. Blocked on a location. *Intersection:* the bugbears, Droop, Qelline Alderleaf → Reidoth, Garaele's banshee.
8. **Deliver the Dendrars, and answer Mirna.** *Intersection:* the crevasse; Thundertree; the party's self-image as "the good guys," which Maela has now said out loud twice.
9. **The obelisk convergence.** Pip's shard (public), Zenvon's black stone (private), Veyra's candle, Orryn's warning, the dead goblins. *Intersection:* everything, eventually.
10. **Paid work in the queue:** Wyvern Tor (120 gp, accepted), Old Owl Well (boots, accepted), the Lionshield goods (owed since Ch. 3), Thundertree's emerald necklace, the Shrine of Luck's held quest.

## DM Notes

- **The session's shape, if you want one:** the party is standing in a cleared dungeon with three civilians, believing they're done. They are not done. Three things they walked past — the barrels, the crevasse, the stairs down — are all still there, and the town above has four scenes waiting (Sildar, Halia, Harbin, Mirna). *(Claude-proposed: let them try to leave. Make the leaving be the choice.)*

- **The Sildar scene is the emotional peak, and it's free.** No dice, no map. Protect it: don't let it get compressed into a line of loot-sorting. If the party heads up to town, put Sildar in their path before anyone else.

- **Glasstaff escaping is better than Glasstaff captured.** The party nearly had him, he took two wounds and ran, and he left every possession he owns in their hands. A recurring humiliated wizard with a patron who punishes failure is worth more than a prisoner. *(But if they catch him, the truth list in his dossier above is what he gives up — don't withhold it.)*

- **Wick's line is the pivot.** "Glasstaff answers to somebody in town." It reframes Phandalin from *a town with a gang problem the party solved* into *a town with something underneath it*. Let the party repeat that line back to an NPC and watch the reaction — Halia's, Harbin's, Sildar's. Pick one and make it count.

- **The red cloaks are load-bearing.** They pacify the crypt skeletons, they fool bugbears, and they fooled a room of bandits. They will also get the party mistaken for Redbrands by frightened townsfolk the moment they walk into Phandalin wearing them. *(Claude-proposed: play that beat once, early, with someone harmless — Tuck, Toblen, or one of the freed Dendrars flinching.)*

- **Pip's secret is out and nobody has talked about it.** The nothic told the whole party, in their heads, that Pip won a black shard and has been dreaming of broken stone under the earth and hearing his name whispered. Then combat started and it was never mentioned again. Someone should bring it up — probably Veyra, whose candle lights up for exactly this. **Zenvon still has a black stone in his pocket and hasn't said so.**

- **Feeding the nothic is a character question, not a tactical one.** The party murdered a bound prisoner to buy passage, and Maela — who says "we're the good guys" out loud — swung the killing blow and called it nothing personal. That's not a contradiction the campaign has cashed in yet.

- **Overdue mechanics, before the next session:** three sidekick level-ups (owed since Ch. 3), a decision on whether the hideout clear takes the party to 3, and writing **Turn Undead** into `characters/maela.md` per the Ch. 7 ruling.

- **Loose hooks still ready to reconnect:** the jade frog statuette (never appraised, never explained); the armory's over-supply; Elsa's scholar sister in Neverwinter; Sister Garaele's silence; the manor above, which belongs to no one living and sits empty over a dungeon the party now owns.
