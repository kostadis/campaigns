## OPEN THREADS & MYSTERIES

**Glasstaff is loose (urgent).** Iarno Albrek teleported out wounded and knows the party's faces. He left his money, his correspondence, and his escape kit behind. He is desperate, humiliated, and still a wizard.

**Who in Phandalin does Glasstaff answer to?** Wick, under duress: Glasstaff "answers to somebody… orders from town… the boss's business… I don't think Glasstaff's the big boss." A Phandalin-resident handler sits between Glasstaff and the Spider. **Halia Thornton is the obvious candidate and the party already has an open invitation to her office.**

**Sildar doesn't know yet.** The party is carrying the news that the Lords' Alliance agent Sildar vouched for is the man who has been terrorizing this town. That conversation hasn't happened.

**The bugbears behind the barrels.** Four of the Spider's own soldiers and a goblin servant are still sealed in a room in a dungeon the party thinks it has cleared. They know where Cragmaw Castle and Wave Echo Cave are.

**The deeper levels.** "Something really wrong over there that messes with your head." Given the dead elongated-skull goblins upstairs, this is the obelisk plot's front door.

**The elongated-skull goblins.** Two dead ones under the manor, one alive on the Triboar Trail saying *"You're not what Ruxithid wants."* They are not Cragmaw. They were here first. Something is looking for something under Phandalin.

**"Ruxithid."** The name that makes Veyra's blue crystal light up.

**The obelisk, converging.** Four separate hooks now point the same way: Pip's blackstone shard (won in Neverwinter, sold to a peddler bound for Phandalin, dreams of broken black stone under the earth and whispers of his name); Zenvon's pocketed black stone; Veyra's blue candle and Orryn Voss's warning that the fragments must never be assembled; the psionic goblins. **Pip's secret is now public** — the nothic told everyone.

**Gundren.** Still missing, still the reason any of this started. Cragmaw Castle is the next stop and the party has no directions.

**Mirna's question.** She will ask what happened to her husband. The answer is twenty feet down a crevasse the party walked past twice.

**Orryn Voss.** Still missing. Veyra's mentor.

**Sister Garaele's injuries.** Still unexplained.

**Old Owl Well, Wyvern Tor, Thundertree, the Lionshield goods.** Four accepted or available jobs, none started.
