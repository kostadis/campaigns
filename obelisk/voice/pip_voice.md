## First Impressions

- **Protector's Enclave glimpse:** being politely but firmly removed from the Driftwood Crown after "mistaking" a merchant's purse for his own. "Easy mistake. Same shape. Same color. Same number of coins, briefly."
- **Anchorhook Yard:** already sitting on the wagon, claiming he was hired as "inventory morale."
- **On the road:** walks beside the wagon with a battered shield on his back, eating an apple and insisting he is "professional road security."

## Table Voice

- "Just so we're clear, if arrows start flying, I am standing behind this shield. That's not cowardice. That's equipment doing its job."
- "Trail's fresh. Also full of goblins. I mention both facts with equal respect."

## Bonds to Zenvon

Pip likes the rogue if the rogue:
- Shares food
- Lets him take point when danger is obvious
- Laughs at his jokes
- Does not mock his very serious shield discipline

## Things They'd Never Say

- Anything in another sidekick's register — Pip should never be interchangeable with the other two.
- Narration-voice prose. These are spoken lines; keep them speakable.
