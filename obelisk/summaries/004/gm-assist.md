# Ambush in Phandalin: The Redbrand Reckoning

Date: Jun 17th, 2026

## Summary
The party rode into Phandalin from the north as the last light of evening faded, weary but triumphant, with Sildar Hallwinter safely in tow. Toblen Stonehill burst from the doors of the inn the moment he spotted them, barely able to contain his relief, and Tuck — Toblen's young son — sprinted across the room to throw his arms around Zenvon in a tearful embrace. The locals raised their eyebrows and their cups, offering grudging respect for the party's success against the goblin bandits and the bugbear that had been terrorizing the road. Sildar, exhausted to his bones, promised the party a proper conversation, a proper drink, and their promised coin come morning, then disappeared upstairs to sleep. Elsa the barkeep poured out celebratory ale, and the party settled in for a well-earned night of rest.

Come morning, Sildar Hallwinter was already seated at a table large enough for all of them when the party came downstairs, and he waved them over with the air of a man who had been waiting. He offered what he clearly intended as a compliment to Zenvon — remarking that he hadn't expected a rogue from the more disreputable corners of Neverwinter to be a man of his word — though it landed with all the grace of a thrown brick. Over breakfast, Sildar laid out the full weight of what was at stake: Gundren Rockseeker had not been taken by chance, but had been specifically targeted for his map to the legendary Wave Echo Cave, the long-lost site of the Phandelver Pact. Five hundred years ago, dwarves, gnomes, and human spellcasters had shared a wondrous mine and a magical Forge of Spells, until a devastating bandit assault and a catastrophic spell battle buried the cavern's location beneath centuries of ruin and rumor. Sildar made clear that Lord Neverember and the Lords' Alliance considered any treasure found there to be rightfully theirs, and he tasked the party with finding his missing agent, Iarno Albrek — a wizard sent to Phandalin weeks ago to establish a constabulary who had since gone silent — as well as uncovering the identity of a shadowy villain known only as the Black Spider, believed to be pulling the strings behind the goblin raids and bandit gangs. Meanwhile, Veyra had spent the entire previous night poring over her notes by candlelight, and at the breakfast table she announced with wide eyes that her blue crystal had flared to life the moment she wrote down the name "Ruxithid" — the same cryptic name a strange, elongated-headed goblin had spoken — a revelation that made Sister Maela roll her eyes and Pip Thistlewick go very quiet and thoughtful. When Sildar appealed to Zenvon's sense of personal loyalty to justify the danger ahead, Zenvon pushed back without flinching, telling the pompous Lords' Alliance man plainly that he could find someone else if the terms didn't suit him. Pip was barely able to hide his delight as Sildar sighed and counted out one hundred gold pieces onto the table — on top of the fifty he had already paid for delivering him safely to Phandalin.

Zenvon's sharp instincts had also flagged a man eating breakfast across the room — dressed like a laborer but watching the party with a little too much interest, while Toblen Stonehill kept as much distance from the stranger as the inn's walls would allow. When the man eventually made to leave, Zenvon slipped out after him, tailing him through the streets of Phandalin. Rounding a corner, Zenvon found exactly what he had feared: four Redbrand ruffians in their distinctive red cloaks, arranged in a careful ambush, with the laborer standing in the middle looking around for the rest of the party to walk into the trap. Rather than retreat, Zenvon squared his shoulders and called out to the man with a cool smirk, asking if he wasn't going to introduce him to his friends. The bravado bought a moment's hesitation, but when Zenvon pressed further, the Redbrand leader snarled and ordered his men to attack.

The brawl that followed was brutal and chaotic. Sister Maela took the worst of it early, absorbing blow after blow from the ruffians as her own strike failed to find its mark. Pip waded into the fight with fierce determination, trading blows with the nearest ruffian, while Zenvon darted through the melee with practiced precision, landing a series of devastating strikes before repositioning out of reach. Veyra, calm amid the chaos, unleashed a torrent of magical energy that tore through multiple ruffians at once, dropping one of them dead in the street and leaving the others staggered. With Sister Maela badly wounded and the remaining Redbrands still standing, the fight hung in the balance — and the streets of Phandalin held their breath.

## Locations
### Phandalin
A rough-and-tumble frontier town where the party returns after their adventure in the wilds. The party arrives from the north in the late evening.

### Stonehill Inn
A local tavern and inn in Phandalin run by Toblen Stonehill. The party rests, celebrates, shares breakfast, and negotiates their next contract here. A suspicious laborer believed to be connected to the Redbrands is also present during breakfast, and Toblen visibly keeps his distance from the man.

### Wave Echo Cave
A mythical cavern and site of the ancient Phandelver Pact, revealed to the party by Sildar Hallwinter. It once housed the Forge of Spells before its location was lost to history following a great magical battle 500 years ago.

### Phandalin Streets
The narrow alleys and corners just outside the Stonehill Inn where the Redbrands lay in wait to ambush the party.

## NPCs
### Sildar Hallwinter
A Lords' Alliance agent who was rescued by the party. He pays the party their promised 50 gold pieces, reveals the history of the Wave Echo Cave and the Phandelver Pact, and hires the party to locate his missing agent Iarno Albrek and uncover the identity of the Black Spider. He is somewhat pompous and class-conscious, and his 'compliment' to Zenvon about being a man of honor came across as a backhanded remark.

### Toblen Stonehill
The proprietor of the Stonehill Inn who welcomes the party back with relief and celebration. During breakfast, he visibly keeps his distance from a suspicious patron believed to be associated with the Redbrands.

### Tuck
Toblen Stonehill's young son, overjoyed to see that the party successfully rescued Sildar Hallwinter, running up and giving Zenvon a hug.

### Elsa
The barkeep at the Stonehill Inn who serves the party celebratory ale for their heroics in rescuing Sildar and clearing out the goblin bandits.

### Pip Thistlewick
A companion of the party who insists on sharing credit for the rescue with the whole team. He nudges Zenvon to negotiate proper payment from Sildar rather than volunteering for free, and is visibly pleased when Zenvon stands his ground.

### Veyra
A companion who meticulously documents the party's journey in a notebook. She discovers that her blue crystal flared with light when she wrote down the name 'Ruxithid,' connecting it to a strange, elongated-headed goblin previously encountered, and uses magical bolts in combat against the Redbrand ruffians.

### Iarno Albrek
A wizard and agent of the Lords' Alliance who was sent to Phandalin weeks ago to establish a constabulary on Sildar Hallwinter's recommendation, but has since stopped sending reports and gone missing.

### The Black Spider
A mysterious villain mentioned by Sildar Hallwinter, believed to be controlling the network of bandit gangs and goblins operating in the area around Phandalin.

### Redbrand Ruffians
Members of a local gang wearing distinctive red cloaks who attempt to ambush the party in the streets of Phandalin. They act as bullies in the town and are connected to the broader criminal network in the region.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge
A companion of the party who participates in the street brawl against the Redbrand ruffians, taking significant damage from their attacks and failing to land a blow of her own during the skirmish.
