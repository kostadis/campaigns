# Third Level

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Before heading back down to the cells, the whole party levels up and Zenvon picks a subclass.
- The party reaches 3rd level, clearing a level-up that had been owed since Chapter 3. No long rest has been taken.
- Zenvon takes the **Arcane Trickster** roguish archetype, gaining *Mage Hand* and *Minor Illusion* — both of which he uses repeatedly over the rest of the session.
- Veyra, Sister Maela and Pip Thistlewick advance to 3rd level alongside him. Zenvon also advanced to 3rd, so **sidekick parity was maintained** and the anticipated level inversion never occurred.
- The DM lays out the open loose threads before the party proceeds: the bugbears (who "can, you know, bring back up or worse, tell people"), the Nothic in the crevasse and its unknown treasure, and the need to escort and talk to the Dendrar family.
- The party requests, and the DM agrees to generate, a running list of open quests — later realized as "Veyra's notes."
