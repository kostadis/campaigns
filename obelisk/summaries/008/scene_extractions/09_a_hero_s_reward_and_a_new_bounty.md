# A Hero's Reward and a New Bounty

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party meets with Sildar Hallwinter to report their success at Tresendar Manor and negotiate a new bounty.
- Zenvon presents Sildar Hallwinter with the Black Spider's sealed letter (recovered from Glasstaff's desk in Chapter 6) proving that Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek was working for the Black Spider.
- Sildar is appalled: "Well, this was not the reward I was hoping for when I signed it, but I did offer a reward, did I not?"
- Sildar pays the party the remaining 150 gold pieces (250 total for eliminating the Redbrand threat, with 100 paid up front). *The module sets this reward at 200 gp; 250 is the table's negotiated figure and stands as canon for this campaign.*
- Sildar praises them backhandedly — "for someone of your background, you have done well" — visibly trying not to say Zenvon was born "on the wrong side of the tracks without the right bloodlines."
- Sildar offers a new bounty for the capture of Iarno Albrek, ultimately settling on 200 gold if brought back alive, 100 if dead, and 50 if he cannot be raised from the dead.
- Zenvon argues that capturing the Black Spider is more important than capturing Iarno himself, framing the Lords' Alliance's real interest as "order and pulling things together."
- Sildar concedes: "defeating whoever is behind Iarno is more important than capturing Iarno," but notes that "if Iarno fled, as you said, the most likely place for him to have fled was to his boss. So perhaps we will get two birds with one stone."
