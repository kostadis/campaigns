# The Bugbear Barracks Brawl

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The confrontation in the bugbear barracks erupts into a desperate fight for survival as the party is overwhelmed by the powerful brutes.
- Zenvon opens with *Thunderwave* (spell save DC 12), targeting two bugbears while sparing the goblin; one saves, one fails, and one takes 12 points of damage.
- Veyra follows up with a firebolt, striking a bugbear for eight points of damage.
- A bugbear retaliates against Zenvon with a heavy morning star for 14 points, nearly knocking him unconscious (down to 6).
- Sister Maela channels divine energy — "Medic!" — casting cure wounds to heal Zenvon for seven, then a bugbear hits him again for 11, snarling "you think you smarter than bug bears."
- The bugbears focus their aggression on Zenvon; the DM twice reminds him he can disengage and run rather than fight to the end.
- Pip Thistlewick attempts to strike back with his new sword but misses in the chaotic melee.
- Zenvon drinks a potion of healing (2d4+2) during the fight, recovering seven points, and disengages further back to use his arrows.
- The party realizes they are outmatched and begins to plan a retreat, floating options including closing the door and burning the room.
