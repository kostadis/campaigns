# Escape from Tresendar Manor

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party escorts the Dendrar family to the surface and then returns to the cellars to deal with remaining threats.
- Zenvon leads the Dendrar family through a door leading up and out the front of the manor — described as "more a castle than a house," standing at the east edge of town among woods and thickets.
- The party debates the sequencing of threats — whether to fight the bugbears first or escort the family, given the risk the family could be caught in the crossfire — and elects to get the family out first.
- The party positions "two in the front, two in the back, and all three family members" for the escort.
- The party successfully reaches the outskirts of Phandalin, leaving the family in safety.
- Zenvon and his companions decide to head back into the cellars to confront the bugbears and investigate the crevasse.
- The group re-enters the hideout through the apothecary, moving stealthily toward the workshop area while hearing the sounds of the Nothic feeding on the bodies of the ruffians it dragged from the gambling room.
