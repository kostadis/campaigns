# Business at the Miner's Exchange

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Zenvon visits Halia Thornton to discuss the aftermath of the Redbrand defeat and is met with suspicious interest in Glasstaff's correspondence.
- The Miner's Exchange is described as "a trading post where local miners have their valuable finds weighed, measured, and paid out," also serving as an unofficial records office.
- Halia Thornton, the Guildmaster, greets them warmly: "A little bird told me that the Redbrands are a thing of the past."
- Halia stops smiling the moment she learns Glasstaff escaped rather than died, then presses: "did you, by any chance, find any correspondence among his personal effects?"
- When asked why she cares, Halia claims: "I am a local authority here. I would love to know if there were any deeper plots involved."
- Halia offers 100 gold pieces for any letters the party recovered.
- Zenvon rebuffs her: "The information I got from the Tresendar Manor is far more valuable than the 100 gold pieces you're offering." Halia responds, "You like to negotiate?" — Zenvon: "I like to help."
- The party leaves feeling suspicious of Halia's true motives, considering her "worth investigating later. In private."
