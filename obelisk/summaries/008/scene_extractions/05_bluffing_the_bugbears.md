# Bluffing the Bugbears

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Dressed in Redbrand cloaks, the party attempts to deceive a group of bugbears lounging in their barracks, leading to a tense interrogation.
- The party approaches the barracks stealthily; Sister Maela pushes Zenvon aside to listen at the door and hears "several gruff voices issuing commands in the goblin tongue."
- Zenvon uses *Mage Hand* to open the door and walks in, attempting to pass the party off as Redbrand reinforcements.
- The barracks is described: "four roughly built wooden bunks with heaped blankets and dirty dishes scattered about. A strong smell of unwashed bodies and rotten meat fills the air. Four tall, furry humanoids are lounging in the mess, barking orders at a goblin."
- A goblin named Droop faints in terror upon seeing the adventurers enter.
- The lead bugbear mocks Zenvon: "leave it to a Redbrand to think he's in charge."
- Zenvon realizes the bugbears serve the Black Spider directly rather than Glasstaff: "Black Spider sent us here to make sure people like you report to Glasstaff."
- Nosk brags his allegiance: "Black Spider. We work for Black Spider... Glasstaff works for Black Spider. And don't you forget it."
- The bugbear explains Droop: "Droop here is a member of the Scraptops, a Goblin clan. He walked into the hideout looking for parts. The Cragmaw want him to join the band." *(Transcript per module canon; the DM said "Sawplee" at the table — PaBTSO R9 has Droop as "a member of the Scraptops, a goblin clan known for its scavenging and tinkering," and the Sawplee are Ruxithid's separate psionic goblins.)*
- Zenvon rage-baits Nosk, then flips the leverage: "how do I know that you're just using... not using his name" — offering to be led to the Black Spider to prove Nosk's authority. A deception/persuasion roll with Sister Maela's help lands.
- Nosk names **Cragmaw Castle** as the Black Spider's location before realizing his mistake.
- Nosk declares: "it don't matter that now you know, because now I kill you," and combat begins.
