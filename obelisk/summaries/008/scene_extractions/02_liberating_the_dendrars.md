# Liberating the Dendrars

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party returns to the prison cells to free the Dendrar family and learns of a potential treasure in Thundertree.
- Zenvon uses his red cloak to bypass skeletal guardians standing watch outside the prison cells, calling out: "Hey guys, it's me. I came back for you. It's all safe now. You can let go of the door. Let me help you."
- The party reassures the terrified captives, who had barricaded themselves in, and releases Mirna Dendrar and her two children, Nars and Nilsa.
- Zenvon, uncertain how to roleplay a rescue, offers the starved family food and water first; Mirna finds this "very thoughtful."
- Mirna reveals: "My husband, Thel... they were going to kill us too, but I promised them that we had wealthy relatives in Neverwinter who would pay a substantial sum for us and the children's safety."
- Zenvon fails a Deception roll and Mirna sees through his lie about the location of Thel's body.
- Rather than describe what the party saw, Zenvon delegates the news to Sister Maela, who breaks it gently instead of saying Thel was "probably being torn to stretch and eaten alive by a monster."
- Satisfied and relieved by the honesty, Mirna admits her Neverwinter ransom story was a bluff: "I was lying about the folks in Neverwinter. I was just trying to buy time... I was hoping somebody would come rescue me."
- Mirna reveals a valuable emerald necklace hidden behind a section of storage shelves in the southeast parcel of Thundertree, in her family's old herb and alchemy shop.
- Pip lobbies hard to retrieve it: "Boss, I'm all for heroism, doing a good thing, and periodically serving evil bandits to unspeakable horrors and monsters, but I do want to get better armor." He notes full plate costs "several thousand gold pieces," which they don't have.
- Zenvon commits to retrieving the necklace "someday in the future" — Pip presses to confirm it is "on the radar."
- Mirna leaves the party with a request: recover Thel's body if they ever can.
- The party agrees to escort the Dendrars out of the manor and back to the safety of Phandalin.
