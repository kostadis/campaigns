# A Fiery Escape

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party executes a tactical retreat, using oil and fire to block their pursuers before fleeing through the manor's tunnels.
- Pip slams the barracks door shut to buy time; a bugbear hacks at the wood with a hand axe for 15 points, then another smashes it into pieces.
- Zenvon pours seven pounds of oil under the door and across the hallway, preparing a fiery trap.
- Using *Mage Hand* and his tinderbox, Zenvon ignites the oil just as the bugbears burst through the shattered door.
- The hallway erupts in flames, dealing five points of damage per round and killing one bugbear outright; the slick floor becomes difficult terrain, slowing the bugbears to half speed.
- The oil burns for two rounds before running out — Sister Maela screams "the fire went out."
- As they flee past the crevasse, the Nothic watches them and whispers a chilling message to Veyra.
- The Nothic, calling itself Ssarnak, reveals to a pale Veyra: "Your teacher is not dead. He is not in a place where dead is easy."
- Veyra breaks toward the creature; a persuasion attempt by Sister Maela fails to talk her down, so Sister Maela grapples her — Veyra screaming "I've got to go talk to him."
- The bugbears take repeated five-point burns from the burning terrain and one screams "bloody cursing help" at the top of its lungs.
- The party dashes toward the manor entrance; the DM notes it will take the bugbears three rounds to break the locked door — more than enough time for the party to escape.
