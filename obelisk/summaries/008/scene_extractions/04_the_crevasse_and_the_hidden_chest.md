# The Crevasse and the Hidden Chest

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party sneaks through the Tresendar Manor caverns to investigate a deep crevasse while a monstrous creature is distracted.
- The party debates splitting up; the group unanimously rejects it. Sister Maela: "I don't know about you, guys, but I've been told that the easiest way for the party to die is for it to split up." Pip: "never split up the party." Veyra warns Zenvon that going solo "could probably result in your untimely demise."
- Pip rolls a natural 20 on stealth (with a +7 modifier, for a 27+); the creature does not notice the party.
- The cavern is described: "A cool breeze sighs softly in this natural cavern, carrying the faint sign of decaying flesh... a faint crevasse with sloped walls divides the cavern and is flanked by two rough stone columns that support the 20-foot high ceiling. Two arched wooden bridges span the crevasse."
- Zenvon uses *Minor Illusion* to mimic the sound of Glasstaff working in his workshop; the creature — the Nothic being Glasstaff's, in effect — "suddenly becomes silent" and creeps off to investigate. Sister Maela is "very impressed."
- Zenvon descends into the fissure and discovers a hidden treasure chest.
- **Not recovered:** the party neither searched for nor found **Thel Dendrar's remains** or his carved unicorn-head pendant, which lie at the bottom of this same crevasse. Mirna's request for his body is still open.
- Using *Mage Hand*, Zenvon safely opens the chest to avoid potential traps — the DM's rule of thumb: "you don't use your active knife to open things... there's all sorts of monsters whose only purpose in life is to teach you don't touch."
- The party recovers 160 silver pieces, 120 gold pieces, five malachite gems (12 gp each), potions of healing, climbing and growth, and a +1 longsword from the chest.
