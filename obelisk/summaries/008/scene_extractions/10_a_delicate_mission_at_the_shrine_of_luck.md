# A Delicate Mission at the Shrine of Luck

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party visits Sister Garaele at the Shrine of Luck, where she requests their help negotiating with a dangerous banshee.
- The shrine is described as "made of stones taken from nearby ruins... dedicated to Tymora, the god of luck and good fortune."
- Sister Garaele greets them: "Well, the new heroes of Phandalin... I see that you cleared out the Redbrand, and everybody is walking with a happier step."
- Garaele reveals she attempted to approach the banshee Agatha to ask about a spellbook, "but I fled for my life... I barely recovered after several days."
- Garaele explains a banshee is "an undead creature of considerable power," and that Agatha "spoke wistfully about the beautiful gems, baubles she had in her life."
- The party is tasked with bringing a jeweled silver comb to Agatha to persuade her to reveal the location of the spellbook belonging to the legendary mage Bowgentle.
- Veyra checks her notes and confirms Bowgentle is there — the DM notes this "means that he's real."
- Zenvon accepts the quest in exchange for three potions of healing, but notes other high-priority business first: "I'll prioritize this right away after we connect some other high-priority things." (Framed in the players' shorthand as "a P0, but not everything.")
- Garaele warns: "the good news is the banshee isn't going anywhere. The bad news is we do really need that information. So don't take too much time."
