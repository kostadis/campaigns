# Revelations and Rest in Phandalin

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### After escaping the manor, the party discusses strange magical connections before returning to the Stonehill Inn to recover.
- Veyra turns to Pip first, asking him to describe the black stone he had once sold — "what it felt like to hold."
- Zenvon volunteers the black stone he has carried undisclosed since the very beginning at the cart, where they saw the black obelisk; when he produces it, Veyra points her blue crystal at it and "her crystal lights even brighter."
- Veyra recalls her advisor Orryn Voss and his theory of "some kind of device that was doing something really bizarre and controlled in people" — theories that once "sounded like... the kind of thing people believe, but isn't."
- The party connects the second glowing rock, the mutated goblins in the crevasse, and the two dead goblins with strange faces matching Sildar's account of his goblin interrogators.
- Zenvon decides he believes Veyra's theories more than before, but keeps that to himself.
- Sister Maela wears a sour, "cheesed" expression the party cannot read (insight/investigation rolls reveal nothing).
- Toblen Stonehill offers the party a free night's stay: "since you guys did us a good deed with the Redbrand, tonight's free." Zenvon pays ten silver for food sent up.
- At the inn, Sildar is at his table; Elsa, Tuck, Toblen, Freda and Narth are present. The party settles in for a long rest.
