# Chapter 03 Cave Crawl, Goblin Brawl, and a Serpent's Fall

Date: 2026-06-08

## Summary
The party crept deeper into the goblin cave, the darkness pressing in around them like a living thing. Zenvon gathered the group and relayed what he had seen — a massive, coiled serpent lurking in the rubble ahead. Those among them blessed with the ability to see in the dark took the lead, pairing off with those who were blind in the blackness, and together they slipped silently past a pack of wolves without so much as a growl to betray them. Moving with careful precision, Zenvon and Sister Maela edged toward the snake, and before the creature could react, the party unleashed a torrent of fire and steel upon it. Varya's arcane flame scorched the beast, and Zenvon's arrow found its mark, dropping the serpent in a heap of scales and silence.

Emboldened by their victory, the group pressed further into the cave, but fortune was not entirely on their side. Sister Maela's heavy footfalls echoed off the stone walls, and a sharp-eyed goblin spotted her in the gloom. She turned to Zenvon with wide eyes and muttered a single, blunt word — "Shit." Thinking quickly, Zenvon stepped forward and spun a tale for the goblins, claiming the party had traveled all this way out of admiration for the legendary Glasstaff and wished only to meet him. The goblins were not convinced, and one of them barked something in their guttural tongue that none of the party could understand — but the meaning was clear enough when they raised their weapons.

The fight that followed was brutal and chaotic. Arrows rained down from goblins perched on a ledge above, and Pip Thistlewick bore the brunt of the assault, absorbing blow after blow as the party scrambled to respond. Varya hurled bolts of magical fire into the fray, and Sister Maela called upon her divine gifts to keep Pip on his feet, mending his wounds even as new ones were opened. Then, from somewhere deeper in the cave, a voice rang out through the darkness — the unmistakable voice of Sildar Hallwinter, alive and calling out to them. The revelation steeled the party's resolve, and they fought on with renewed purpose.

The goblin boss, Yeemik, proved to be a tenacious and vicious opponent. He screeched at the party the moment Zenvon produced a light source, shrieking about "bright lighters" before hurling himself at Pip with reckless fury. Pip was driven to his knees, barely clinging to consciousness, but he dug deep and found the strength to rise again. In a moment of pure, blazing triumph, Pip landed a devastating blow on Yeemik that left the goblin reeling — and Sister Maela stepped in to finish the job, her mace coming down with righteous finality. The remaining goblins fell shortly after, and the cave grew quiet.

With the fighting done, the party moved swiftly to find Sildar Hallwinter, who was bound and badly wounded on a ledge within the cave. He was alive, though barely, and relief flooded his face when he saw them. He asked urgently whether they had dealt with Klarg, the bugbear who commanded the cave — and Sister Maela confirmed they had not, meaning the beast still lurked somewhere in the depths. Sildar urged them not to linger, and the party agreed, pausing only long enough to search Yeemik's remains, where they found three glittering gems and a vial of healing potion.

The group retreated from the cave and found a small clearing some distance away, well hidden from the trail. Pip, drawing on his years of experience surviving in the wild, worked alongside Zenvon to obscure every trace of their passage, covering tracks and concealing their camp with practiced skill. Sildar, leaning heavily on whoever would support him, made one final request before they settled in for the night — no fires. Somewhere back in that cave, Klarg was still breathing, and none of them wanted to give him a reason to come looking.

## NPCs
### Varya
A frail but capable magic user known as 'Varya of the Blue Candle' who assists the party with arcane firebolts and magical missiles. She possesses darkvision, making her valuable in the darkness of the cave.

### Pip Thistlewick
A fighter who accompanies the party. He cannot see in the dark without a light source, making him vulnerable to the goblins' attacks, but he proves his worth by shoving enemies and landing a devastating critical blow on Yeemik.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge
A cleric who provides healing and darkvision to the party. Her heavy footfalls make her less than stealthy, and she is the one spotted by the goblins, triggering the ambush. She ultimately delivers the killing blow to Yeemik.

### Yeemik
A goblin boss who led the group guarding the ledge where Sildar was held. He screeched at the party as 'bright lighters' when Zenvon lit a light source, and was ultimately slain by Sister Maela's mace.

### Sildar Hallwinter
A human warrior and associate of Gundren Rockseeker who was found bound and badly wounded on a ledge inside the cave. He warned the party that the bugbear leader Klarg was still at large and advised against lighting fires while hiding.

### Klarg
A bugbear leader mentioned by Sildar Hallwinter as a dangerous threat still lurking somewhere deeper in the cave, whom the party did not encounter during this session.
