# A Nothic to Worry About

Date: 2026-07-03

## Summary
The streets of Phandalin rang with the clash of steel as the party clashed with a band of Redbrand ruffians. Sister Maela swung her warhammer with devastating precision, landing a punishing blow on one of the thugs, while Veyra hurled a brilliant streak of fire through the air that lit up the street but sailed wide of its mark. Pip Thistlewick launched a javelin at a fleeing ruffian, but the throw went wide, leaving Zenvon Forepot to step in and deliver the killing blow himself. The last remaining Redbrand, cornered by a relentless Sister Maela, threw up his hands and surrendered on the spot. The party stripped the fallen enemies of their meager coin — a handful of gold, silver, and copper pieces — and took their prisoner in hand.

Before the dust had even settled, the door to the Townmaster's Hall creaked open and a nervous, round-faced man peered out from behind it. Harbin Wester, the mayor and townmaster of Phandalin, crept forward and introduced himself with the cautious energy of a man who had spent the battle hiding behind a locked door. He praised the party's work against the Redbrands, whom he called a terrible plague on the town, and wasted little time before dangling a new opportunity in front of them — a contract to investigate a string of caravan attacks near a place called Wyvern Tor, far to the east along the Triboar Trail. He opened with an offer of one hundred gold pieces, but Pip Thistlewick, channeling the spirit of a seasoned merchant, pushed back with a confident counter. After a spirited back-and-forth that left even the banker-mayor visibly impressed, the party walked away with a promise of one hundred and twenty gold pieces. Before handing their prisoner over to Harbin's custody, Zenvon pressed the man for information, and the ruffian gave up two things freely: the gang's hideout was Tresendar Manor, and their usual haunt was a rundown taproom called the Sleeping Giant.

After a brief rest to tend to their wounds, the party made their way to the Sleeping Giant, a ramshackle establishment at the eastern edge of town. Zenvon attempted to slip inside unnoticed, but his effort at subtlety was so thoroughly unsuccessful that the surly orc bartender called him out the moment he crossed the threshold — less a stealthy infiltration and more a cartoon character tiptoeing in plain sight. He recovered quickly, ordering an overpriced ale and scanning the room, where he spotted two figures seated at a table: Halia Thornton, the sharp-eyed owner of Phandalin's Miner's Exchange, and a broad-shouldered man in a red cloak who introduced himself as Rondar. Rondar wasted no time acknowledging that he knew exactly who Zenvon was and what the party had done to four of his men, and he offered what he called a professional courtesy — a truce, with each side staying out of the other's business.

Zenvon called his companions inside, and the interrogation began in earnest. Halia, for her part, performed a masterclass in feigned innocence, declaring herself shocked — shocked — to discover that her dear friend Rondar was a member of the Redbrand Ruffians, all while he sat across from her in his unmistakable red cloak. Sister Maela quietly noted that Rondar was holding something back, and when Zenvon's attempts at intimidation failed to move him, Pip Thistlewick stepped forward and pressed a blade to the man's throat. The color drained from Rondar's face, and the truth spilled out: Glasstaff, the shadowy leader of the Redbrands, was none other than Iarno Albrek — a former agent of the Lord's Alliance who had gone rogue. Halia, ever the opportunist, used the commotion to quietly invite Zenvon to visit her exchange for future business arrangements, though Zenvon made sure she wasn't going anywhere just yet.

With Rondar now bound and serving as an unwilling guide, the party pressed him for more. He led them deep into the forest to a concealed tunnel roughly a hundred feet from Tresendar Manor, warning them as they went of something monstrous that lurked in the caverns below — a creature with eyes that peered from the darkness and an unsettling, malevolent intelligence. Veyra's eyes lit up at the description; she identified it as a Nothic, a wretched being cursed by the dark legacy of Vecna, a god of secrets, and reduced from a seeker of forbidden knowledge to a tormented, creeping monster. Rondar added grimly that Iarno had struck some kind of deal with the thing, keeping it fed in exchange for its service as a guardian, and that one of his own men had gone down into the cellar chasing a rat and come back speaking things he had no way of knowing.

The party descended into the cavern, where the cold air carried the faint, unmistakable scent of decay. The space opened into a vast natural chamber split by a deep crevasse, its sloped walls flanked by rough stone columns that rose to a ceiling twenty feet above, with two arched wooden bridges spanning the dark gap below. Then the voice came — not through the air, but directly into their minds. The Nothic spoke, and it did not introduce itself with pleasantries. It turned its terrible gaze on Pip Thistlewick and laid bare his most guarded secret: that he had once won a strange stone shard in a dice game near the docks of Neverwinter, sold it to a peddler bound for Phandalin, and had been haunted ever since by dreams of broken black stone beneath the earth and whispers that called his name. Pip went pale, his voice barely steady as he urged the party to kill the creature, while the Nothic's singular eye drifted hungrily toward Rondar and it made its terms plain — it simply wanted to be fed.

## Locations
### Phandalin
A rough-and-tumble frontier town where the party is currently dealing with the local Redbrand ruffians and meeting with the town leadership.

### Townmaster's Hall
The administrative center of Phandalin where Harbin Wester resides and conducts town business; the party negotiated their quest reward here and left their prisoner in custody.

### Wyvern Tor
A location to the east on the Triboar Trail where caravans have been frequently attacked by marauders. Harbin Wester offered the party 120 gold pieces to investigate.

### The Sleeping Giant
A ramshackle taproom at the east end of Phandalin, known as a frequent hangout for the Redbrand ruffians. Zenvon was spotted immediately upon entering, leading to a tense confrontation with Rondar and Halia Thornton.

### Phandalin Miner's Exchange
A local establishment owned by Halia Thornton; notably one of the few places in town the Redbrands do not target.

### Tresendar Manor
An old manor house that serves as the primary hideout for the Redbrand ruffians and their leader, Glasstaff. The party learned of a secret back entrance via a forest tunnel.

### Tresendar Manor Secret Tunnel
A natural cavern hidden in the forest roughly 100 feet from Tresendar Manor, reeking of decaying flesh. It features a deep crevasse, two arched wooden bridges, and stone columns — and is guarded by a telepathic Nothic.

## NPCs
### Harbin Wester
The townmaster and mayor of Phandalin, a banker by trade who is visibly terrified of the Redbrands but quick to offer rewards to adventurers willing to handle the town's problems. He negotiated a reward of 120 gold pieces with Pip Thistlewick for the Wyvern Tor quest and agreed to hold the captured Redbrand as a prisoner.

### Redbrand Ruffian
A captured member of the local gang who, under pressure, reveals the location of their hideout at Tresendar Manor and mentions their leader, Glasstaff, and the gang's hangout at the Sleeping Giant.

### Rondar
A Redbrand lieutenant who wears the signature red cloak of his gang. He is initially confrontational but becomes cooperative and fearful when threatened by Pip Thistlewick. He reveals that Glasstaff is Iarno Albrek, warns the party about the monster in the manor's tunnel, and is ultimately tied up and used as a guide and potential lure.

### Halia Thornton
The owner of the Phandalin Miner's Exchange. She is a shrewd and calculating woman who was found meeting with Rondar at the Sleeping Giant. She feigns shock at his Redbrand affiliation and acts as a neutral observer during the interrogation, while quietly inviting Zenvon Forepot to visit her establishment for future business.

### Orc Bartender
A surly woman managing the Sleeping Giant who charges a gold piece for ale and immediately spotted Zenvon's failed attempt at stealth.

### Iarno Albrek (Glasstaff)
Revealed by Rondar to be one and the same person — a former agent of the Lord's Alliance who has gone rogue and now leads the Redbrand ruffians from Tresendar Manor.

### The Nothic
A malevolent, telepathic monster with a singular large eye residing in the caverns beneath Tresendar Manor. It guards the entrance under a deal made with Iarno Albrek in exchange for food. It possesses the ability to unearth secrets from people's minds, revealing Pip Thistlewick's haunted past with a mysterious stone shard, and demands food while eyeing Rondar hungrily.

### Pip Thistlewick
A companion of Zenvon Forepot whose secret past is telepathically exposed by the Nothic — he once won a mysterious stone shard in a dice game near Neverwinter's docks, sold it to a peddler heading toward Phandalin, and has since been haunted by dreams of broken black stone and whispers of his name. He is visibly shaken by the revelation.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge
A companion of Zenvon Forepot who fights with a warhammer, corners the surrendering Redbrand, and uses her insight to detect that Rondar is being deceptive during the interrogation.

### Veyra
A magically inclined companion who casts a firebolt during the skirmish and later identifies the creature in the tunnel as a Nothic, noting its connection to Vecna and its potential for powerful magic.
