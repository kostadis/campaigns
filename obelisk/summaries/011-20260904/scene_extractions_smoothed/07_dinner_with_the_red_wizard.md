# Dinner with the Red Wizard

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party returns to Old Owl Well to report their success to Hamun Kost and shares a lavish meal served by the undead.
- The party returns to Old Owl Well to report success and collect information about Cragmaw Castle.
- Harbin Wester’s 120 gp bounty on the Wyvern Tor marauders, accepted in Chapter 5, is now collectible on their return to Phandalin.
- Zenvon calls for Hamun while trying to avoid attracting the zombies’ attention.
- The party distinguishes several undead servants: one resembles a Red Wizard, another a former guard, and the rest look like peasants.
- Hamun delightedly receives the news that the boss and ogre are dead and the bugbears have fled.
- Zenvon reminds him of the promised information, but Hamun insists on a meal first.
- Dinner is well cooked and served around a table beside a fire, demonstrating Hamun’s taste for comfort and fine living.
- Warden Holl, Hamun’s undead former apprentice, serves as waiter. Hamun remarks that the apprentice serves him better dead than alive.
- Zenvon remains suspicious but treats the arrangement as business.
- He describes the sentry ambush, the attack on the leader, and the charcoal drawing.
- Hamun’s cheerful manner stops immediately. His hands drop, and he demands an exact account of the image.
- Zenvon invites Veyra to supply the details. Hamun expresses particular interest in her having encountered similar signs before.
- Hamun says he does not know what the image means. Zenvon’s Insight result of 12 cannot establish whether he is truthful.
- Hamun asks what they know about crystals and black stones. Zenvon prevents Veyra from disclosing everything, limiting the account to a black crystal encountered on the road to Phandalin that disappeared.
- Zenvon again fails to read Hamun, but Pip catches a moment of excitement at the black-obelisk information before the wizard suppresses it.
- Hamun presents himself as an expert archaeologist specializing in the Netheril Empire and its language.
- He identifies Talon as Aldith Tresendar’s sword. The connection to Tresendar Manor prompts roleplay that earns a point of inspiration.
- Asked whether the Black Hawk’s name relates to black stones, Hamun says it derives from the knight’s black armor and cape.
- Pip expresses the hope of living up to the sword’s former bearer, and Hamun wishes him well.
- Hamun offers his expertise for future Netherese artifacts or questions.
- Zenvon keeps Veyra’s blue crystal secret. The table explicitly confirms that it has not been disclosed to Hamun.
- Veyra visibly struggles to contain her questions but respects the decision.
- Hamun has not supplied the promised route to Cragmaw Castle when the session ends. The debt remains owed, and the party has the castle’s name without directions.
- The party remains suspicious of Hamun’s motives and intends to pursue answers at Cragmaw Castle.
- The DM awards advancement to level 4. Zenvon must choose a feat or two ability-score points and an additional prepared spell, bringing his available prepared choices to four.
- In the closing discussion, the DM emphasizes the danger of engaging large groups with the party’s current capabilities. Zenvon recognizes why trying to summon more bugbears outside would have been risky.
