# The Ambush Inside the Cave

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party uses a magical illusion to sneak into position and launch a surprise assault on the orc leader.
- Zenvon proposes creating an illusion of the dead sentry and having all four companions walk behind it toward the orc.
- The table checks Minor Illusion. The DM rules that the image must remain stationary and discusses its five-foot-cube limit.
- They adapt the plan: the image will conceal their staging position before the rush, granting advantage on the approach.
- Zenvon and Maela succeed in gaining surprise; Pip and Veyra must wait for normal initiative.
- Zenvon uses his shortsword and off-hand attack, discussing Vex and the advantage it grants on a subsequent attack.
- His opening hits deal 7 damage each, leaving the orc at 2 hit points after the DM recalculates.
- Maela misses her opening attack.
- Normal initiative begins with Zenvon acting again. His successful assault culminates in a shortsword strike that unbalances Brughor, followed by a scimitar slash across his throat.
- Brughor Axe-Biter dies, and the bugbears begin fleeing.
- Zenvon’s Insight attempt fails, but Perception makes the division clear: the bugbears want out, while Gog wants to kill them.
- Zenvon permits the bugbears to escape rather than attack them and prolong the fight.
- Veyra nearly attacks a fleeing bugbear, but Pip reminds her of the plan.
- Gog remains, furious at the others’ cowardice and devastated by Brughor’s death.
