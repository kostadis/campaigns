# The Battle Against Gog

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party engages in a fierce fight against the enraged ogre Gog, who is devastated by the loss of his companion.
- Maela warns the bugbears to leave and casts Sacred Flame on Gog. He fails his save and takes 10 damage.
- Gog howls, invokes Brughor’s name, and threatens to eat the party alive.
- The remaining bugbears flee as their turns arrive. The DM notes that their retreat is unusually complete given their apparent strength, leaving something about the group unexplained.
- Pip moves inside and strikes Gog with Talon for 6 damage. He uses Action Surge, but his additional attack misses.
- Gog swings his great club at Pip and misses.
- Having already cast Magic Missile twice against the sentry, Veyra uses Fire Bolt and deals 1 damage.
- Zenvon tries True Strike for the first time. The DM helps him locate his spellcasting modifier and resolve the weapon damage in the character sheet.
- His attack roll of 23 hits; the shortsword attack resolves for 12 damage.
- Zenvon follows with a natural-20 scimitar attack for 10 damage. The DM explains that Sneak Attack has already been used that turn and cannot be added again.
- Gog is left at 20 hit points. He saves against Maela’s next Sacred Flame, and both Pip and Gog miss subsequent attacks.
- Veyra asks whether to use her last spell. With Zenvon’s approval, she deals 15 damage with Magic Missile.
- Zenvon pauses to question Gog, explaining their work for Hamun Kost and asking why the raiders had been troubling him.
- A natural 20 produces a Persuasion total of 22. Gog breaks down over Brughor, and the DM establishes that their relationship was romantic.
- Gog explains that Brughor ordered attacks. He does not recognize Hamun Kost’s name; identifying the wizard would require a description.
- Zenvon concludes that Gog has nothing useful to add and calls for the fight to end.
- Maela attempts another spell, which Gog resists. Pip then hits and kills him.
- Zenvon applauds the finishing blow, initially crediting Maela before being reminded that Pip struck it.
- Maela calls Zenvon cold for attempting negotiation and then ordering Gog killed. Zenvon cites his earlier sympathy for the sentry; Maela acknowledges his kindness but maintains the distinction.
- Veyra is pleased with the outcome.
