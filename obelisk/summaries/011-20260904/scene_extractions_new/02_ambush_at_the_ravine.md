# Ambush at the Ravine

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party coordinates a distraction to take down the bugbear sentry guarding the cave entrance.
- Zenvon proposes using Veyra as bait because she resembles a student. After considering calling out for help, they choose a silent performance to avoid alerting the cave.
- Veyra picks up her notebook, judges that she has room to dodge, and steps into view looking confused.
- The sentry growls and advances, muttering, “Just a tourist!”
- The party rolls for the ambush with advantage, but only Veyra gains a surprise attack. Zenvon remains hidden and eligible for a ranged Sneak Attack if he hits.
- Veyra opens with Magic Missile for 6 damage.
- The bugbear advances and sees Pip, Maela, and Veyra.
- Zenvon rolls a 7 with his shortbow and misses. He moves along the ridge toward his companions and uses his bonus action to hide.
- Maela rushes the sentry to prevent an alarm and hits him with her mace for 6 damage.
- Veyra’s next Magic Missile deals 12 damage.
- Pip closes with his +1 sword and delivers the killing blow.
- Zenvon expresses sadness over the sentry’s blind eye. Maela praises his compassion while treating the fight as necessary; Veyra points out that she was the intended meal.
- Maela warns that leaving the corpse visible could waste their efforts at surprise. The party hides it in nearby bushes.
