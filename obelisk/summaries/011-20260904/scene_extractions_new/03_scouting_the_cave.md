# Scouting the Cave

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Zenvon and his companions peer into the dark cave to assess the threats waiting inside.
- Before Zenvon looks inside, the DM reminds him that he has a potion of invisibility. No use of the potion is recorded.
- The cookfire provides enough illumination to inspect the interior.
- Zenvon sees four bugbears, an ogre, and one orc.
- Zenvon’s investigation yields no further insight. Maela also looks in without identifying anything useful.
- Pip studies the occupants through the lens of his military experience. The orc sits by the fire while everyone listens to him.
- Pip explains that the largest creature would ordinarily dominate such a group; the orc’s authority therefore marks him as its organizer.
- He suggests that killing the gang boss could make the others unwilling to fight.
- Pip recalls that the party previously had to flee from four bugbears, reinforcing the danger of attacking everyone at once.
