# Secrets in the Charcoal and Looting the Cave

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### After the battle, the party searches the cave, discovers a disturbing drawing, and divides the modest loot.
- All four companions spread out to search.
- Zenvon examines a strange charcoal drawing on the wall. It resembles a goblin with a crystal protruding from its skull.
- The figure’s proportions look unusual, though Zenvon cannot tell whether this reflects the subject or simply poor drawing.
- The DM clarifies that the crystal is also drawn in charcoal; neither a real crystal nor its actual color can be established from the image.
- Zenvon calls Veyra over and asks whether she recognizes it from her reading.
- Veyra’s crystal flares faintly. She identifies this as their third strange-goblin clue and retrieves her notes about Sildar’s account.
- The recalled account concerns a goblin with an elongated head encountered during the Cragmaw ambush. The name Ruxithid is revisited, along with an earlier reaction from Veyra’s crystal when she wrote it.
- Pip finds an unlocked treasure chest and complains about the disappointing contents.
- The chest holds 180 silver pieces, 15 electrum pieces, and three bottles of perfume worth 5 gp each. The DM later says 160 silver pieces while recording the treasure; the final ledger uses the module's 180-sp total.
- Pip remarks that Harbin Wester’s payment is especially welcome given the poor haul.
- Zenvon questions why raiders would have so little. Veyra notes the area’s lack of traffic, and he points out that this could merely be their hideout.
- Veyra enthusiastically connects the bugbears, strange goblins, crystals, and her mentor in a diagram.
- Maela’s missing-arrow joke sends Veyra searching for a gap in the theory.
- Zenvon’s Insight result of 5 leaves him unable to judge whether Veyra sees real connections or imagined patterns.
- When asked for a conclusion, Veyra suspects someone in charge is doing something awful.
- Zenvon proposes seeking answers from Hamun and at Cragmaw Castle. Veyra adds a castle to the diagram and directs the arrows toward it, proudly treating it as the missing connection.
- Maela closes her eyes and says a small prayer to avoid responding unkindly.
- Zenvon proposes giving Pip half the silver and a perfume vial for killing Gog. Veyra objects to changing their equal division into kill bonuses.
- Zenvon resolves the dispute by giving Pip his own share while the others receive theirs. Everyone accepts the arrangement.
