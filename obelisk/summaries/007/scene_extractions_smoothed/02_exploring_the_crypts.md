# Exploring the Crypts

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party searches the sarcophagi and discovers that their red cloaks prevent the skeletal guardians from attacking them.
- The party discovers a jeweled gold bracelet worth 140 gold pieces within a sarcophagus containing human female remains within the Tresendar Crypts.
- The two skeletons that had fled return but, noticing the party's red cloaks, choose not to attack; the DM establishes they will hold their position as long as the party does not threaten them.
- As the party finishes searching the sarcophagi, they hear noise from the northeastern door. Zenvon rolls investigation, confirming the sound as: "Please, help us! Please, help us!" — voices that are clearly those of captives in distress.
- Sister Maela appeals to Zenvon's better nature: "I think we should help the helpless, you know, we're the good guys, remember?"
- Zenvon relents — "I'll do this for you, Sister M" — and the party moves toward the cells.
