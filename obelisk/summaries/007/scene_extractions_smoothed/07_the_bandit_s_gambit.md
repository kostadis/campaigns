# The Bandit's Gambit

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Zenvon attempts to interrogate and recruit a surviving bandit named Wick, only to realize the man's loyalty cannot be trusted.
- Wick opens with a self-serving plea: "So look, I helped you, right? And, you know, I didn't — Glasstaff? Man, he's like some scary shit, and I don't want to be here anymore, and I don't really want to die for him, so I did you a solid, and you're gonna do me a solid, and let me leave, and pretend you never saw me."
- Zenvon counter-offers gold and a share of the party's accumulated treasure in exchange for Wick's loyalty and information.
- Wick shares what he knows about Glasstaff: "He's a wizard, not just a muscle with a title. I've seen him drop a man with a spell, no weapon drawn, nobody messes with him twice."
- Wick notes that Glasstaff answers to someone and has overheard the wizard complaining about orders from town and references to "the boss's business" — someone in Phandalin, he believes, though he trails off before finishing the thought.
- Wick reveals the dungeon has multiple levels; he was sent to the subterranean part only once to carry supplies: "I don't want to go back. Don't know what's there, but it's kinda wrong. There's something really wrong over there that messes with your head, I'm told." He briefly slips from third to first person mid-sentence, catching himself.
- When Zenvon speaks the name "Black Spider," Wick visibly flinches. Pressed further, he retreats: "Look, dude, I just carry crates, alright? You start asking about the boss's boss, that's how people end up carrying less."
- Zenvon offers to recruit Wick formally; Wick agrees — "Of course I'm with you, boss" — but a high insight roll reveals the truth: Wick intends to take the money and disappear at the first opportunity. The DM clarifies he won't actively betray the party, but his "yes" is a lie of convenience.
- Under further pressure, Wick reveals that the Redbrands previously "handled" a family called the Dendrars — the same family, the DM confirms, whose members are currently locked in the cells below.
- Zenvon decides Wick cannot be trusted or released, and executes him. The DM waives a combat roll for the helpless prisoner.
- Only now does the party search the cistern, finding a waterproof satchel hanging from a submerged rope tied to the south wall, about two feet below the water's surface. It contains a potion of healing and a potion of invisibility.
- The party loots the three fallen bandits: 16 silver pieces and 7 gold pieces on the first; 12 silver pieces and 5 gold pieces on the second; 20 copper pieces, 11 electrum pieces, and two garnets worth 10 gold pieces each on the third.
