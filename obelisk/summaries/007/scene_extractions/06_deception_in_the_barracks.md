# Deception in the Barracks

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Zenvon uses his Redbrand cloak to bluff a group of bandits, leading to a chaotic betrayal.
- Zenvon opens the door and strides in wearing his red cloak. The bandits pause and address him as an ally: "Wait! You're one of us!" They then confront the exhausted, wounded ruffian: "What do you mean they attacked you, man? Are you sure these are the guys that attacked you?"
- The ruffian fires back: "Do I look like I'm the kind of person who would lie? That is a killing machine! Kill him!"
- Zenvon delivers his deception: the cloaked party are loyal Redbrands, the ruffian is a traitor who was caught letting enemies into the hideout, and he had to be stopped. The DMcalls for a deception roll with advantage, declaring the argument "very clever — extremely clever."
- Dosa Rook immediately believes the story and declares: "About time somebody cleaned house — I'm going to cut this rat into several pieces."
- A second, more skeptical bandit is not fully convinced: "Hmm, traitor. Funny. Nobody sent word down, but it's not very convincing when he's covered in blood and you're covered in blood." He announces he needs to go verify the story in person.
- Zenvon offers to accompany the skeptical bandit toward the stairs, luring him within reach of the waiting party.
- Pip strikes the bandit the moment he is in position, dealing 6 points of damage and blowing the ruse open.
- The remaining bandits realize they have been deceived and combat erupts. Pip finishes the bandit he had struck; Zenvon drives his dagger home for 12 points of damage, killing another.
- When only Wick remains beside the injured original ruffian, Wick draws his blade and cuts down the wounded ruffian himself before throwing up his hands and begging for mercy.
