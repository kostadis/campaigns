# Rescue and Pursuit

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### While some party members free the prisoners, others chase the fleeing bandit into a trap.
- Zenvon uses his lockpicking skills (dexterity check) to pick the lock on the iron bars of the boy's cell; the child is overjoyed upon being freed.
- Sister Maela unlocks the remaining cell, freeing the captive women. The DM notes that the freed captives cannot simply leave the area, as the patrolling skeletons outside — who do not recognize non-cloaked figures as allies — would attack them on sight.
- Pip sprints after the fleeing Redbrand through the corridors.
- Pip inadvertently triggers a hidden pit trap concealed in the hallway floor, plummeting twenty feet into the darkness below. The DM rolls poorly for fall damage; Pip takes only 3 points of damage but is now twenty feet down and out of the chase.
- Veyra attempts to catch the fleeing ruffian with a Firebolt from range but misses entirely, scorching the wall.

- Pip asks Zenvon whether to pursue; Zenvon hesitates, then decides to split the party.
- Pip argues Zenvon's lockpicking hands are more valuable on the cells; the switch is accepted.
- Final split: Pip and Veyra give chase; Zenvon handles the locks; Sister Maela assists.
