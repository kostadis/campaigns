# The Pit Trap and the Cellar

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party navigates a dangerous hallway and enters a large cellar while chasing a fleeing Redbrand ruffian.
- Zenvon improvises a recovery tool — pressing his whip into service as a makeshift rope — and hauls Pip back up out of the twenty-foot pit.
- Sister Maela heals Pip's injuries with another Cure Wounds casting, restoring 6 hit points.
- Now that the pit's location is known, the party carefully walks around the exposed trap rather than triggering it again.
- The party enters a large cellar matching a read-aloud description: a five-foot landing fifteen feet above the cellar floor, stone steps descending in two short flights, a door beneath the stairs to the north, a stone cistern dominating the western wall, and walls lined with kegs and barrels.
- The party notes the stone cistern in passing but does not search it at this point.
- An investigation roll of 19 while listening at the door on the upper landing reveals murmuring voices — multiple people speaking on the other side. The fleeing ruffian has clearly raised the alarm.
