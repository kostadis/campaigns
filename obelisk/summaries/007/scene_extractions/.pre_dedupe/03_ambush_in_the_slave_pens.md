# Ambush in the Slave Pens

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party enters a prison area and is immediately engaged by Redbrand ruffians guarding captives.
- The DM reads a room description: a long room partitioned into three areas by iron bars, filthy straw lining the cell floors, hinge doors secured by chains and padlocks, two human women held in the southern cell and a human boy confined to the northern cell.
- Redbrand ruffians attempt to ambush the party but fail to catch them by surprise, rolling so poorly that neither side gains a surprise round.
- Zenvon darts in, drives his dagger into the nearest ruffian (armor class 11) for 5 points of damage, then uses his Cunning Action to disengage and retreat.
- A Redbrand ruffian attacks Pip with a shortsword but misses; he repositions to allow a second ruffian a clear angle.
- Sister Maela strikes a ruffian with her hammer for 6 points of damage.
- Pip kills one of the Redbrands with a 6-point decisive sword strike.
- Veyra unleashes a Firebolt that scores 8 points of damage, dropping a ruffian to 1 hit point. The DM remarks that this time the damage did sound the way it looked.
- Sister Maela calls down Sacred Flame on the critically wounded ruffian; he fails his saving throw and takes 5 points of radiant damage, dying outright.
- The final Redbrand ruffian, seeing his companions fall, disengages and flees the room.
