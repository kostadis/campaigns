# The Return to the Cells

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party loots the barracks and returns to free the captives guarded by skeletons.
- The party returns to the cells, passing the two skeletal guardians who remain passive in the presence of the red cloaks.
- The freed Dendrar family members have barricaded themselves inside the cell, pressing themselves against the door in terror of the skeletons loitering just outside — they cannot leave without red cloaks to identify themselves as allies to the mindless undead.
- Zenvon calls through the door: "Hey guys, it's me. I came back for you. It's all safe now. You can let go of the door. Let me help you."
- The sound of furniture scraping across stone is heard, and the door swings open.
- The skeletons, seeing Zenvon step inside in his red cloak, retreat to their resting positions without incident.
- The Dendrar family is escorted to safety, and the Tresendar hideout is cleared of Redbrands — at least on this level.
