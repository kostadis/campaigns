# Battle in the Tresendar Crypts

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party faces off against a group of skeletons within the ancient crypts of Tresendar Manor.
- A skeleton initiates combat by firing a shortbow at the party — the DM describes the shot as missing "spectacularly."
- Zenvon Forepot attacks a skeleton with his blade; his first swing misses, but his second roll is a natural twenty, dealing 8 points of damage — which the DM notes is "not very good, but better than nothing," as the skeleton stays up.
- Sister Maela steps forward, takes a position in front of the monsters, and invokes the power of her dwarven god to turn the undead, commanding: "BE GONE! In the name of my dwarven god!" Two of the six skeletons fail their wisdom saving throws (DC 12, against their +2 wisdom modifier) and are frightened; the remaining four fight on and are destroyed over the course of the battle.
- The two frightened skeletons immediately flee through a door and vanish into the darkness beyond.
- A remaining skeleton strikes Zenvon with its short sword for 11 points of damage, leaving him with only 4 hit points.
- Pip kills the skeleton he had already wounded and, using his Action Surge, attacks a second for 6 additional points of damage.
- Veyra has already cast Magic Missile twice earlier in the delve; she expends her last remaining slot here, sending three darts of force into the remaining undead.
- Zenvon switches to his scimitar to use Nick, misses his first swing with a 10, then lands the second for 12 points of damage and destroys another skeleton — leaving one active.
- Zenvon withdraws rather than hold the line at 4 hit points, so the last skeleton attacks Pip instead, hitting him for 6 points of damage.
- Sister Maela casts Cure Wounds (2d8+2), restoring 10 hit points to **Pip**. Zenvon is not healed and finishes the crypt fight at 4 hit points.
- Pip strikes the last skeleton for 5 points; Veyra, now out of Magic Missile slots, hits it with Firebolt for 2 points — prompting the DM's aside that it "does a lot less damage than the sound effects."
- Zenvon delivers the killing blow to the final active skeleton, rolling 24 to hit and dealing 8 points of damage with his dagger.
- The two fled skeletons return partway through the subsequent search, but upon seeing the party's red cloaks, they take a passive guarding stance rather than attacking. The DM acknowledges the red cloaks should have prevented the original attack entirely and applies the rule retroactively.
- The party begins to investigate the stone sarcophagi in the room as the frightened skeletons linger nearby.
