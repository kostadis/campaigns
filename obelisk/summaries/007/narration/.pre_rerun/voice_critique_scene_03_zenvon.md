## Flags

### [1] Register-wrong vocabulary — mathematical

> Both of them were inside the room now, so the geometry favored me.

**Why:** Mechanical scan B hit. *Geometry* is the analytical default the scan exists to catch — nobody in this party thinks in coordinate terms. Zenvon tracks **reach and exits**, which is the same information in his vocabulary. The very next sentence gets it right (*"Reach is a resource. You do not give it away for free"*), which makes the abstraction redundant as well as off-register.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Both of them were inside the room now, which put my exit behind me and their backs to each other.`

### [2] Register-wrong vocabulary — mathematical

> he stepped aside, deliberately, making a clean angle for the man behind him

**Why:** Second scan B hit. *Angle* is the same failure in miniature. What he is admiring is that the ruffian **made room for his colleague** — a trained habit, priced approvingly — not a geometric fact.
**Suggested rewrite:** `he stepped aside, deliberately, opening the lane for the man behind him`

### [3] Convergence with the examples file — second of four (and the same beat as scene 04)

> The boy in the north cell had his hands on the bars. For a moment I was not counting anything.
>
> Then I was again.

**Why:** The formula from `examples/zenvon.md`, fired again — and this is the *same boy, the same cell, the same emotion* that scene 04 renders with the identical construction eleven lines in (*"His face when the bars moved was — I was not counting anything, for a moment. / Then I was again."*). Two adjacent scenes, one emotional beat, one borrowed sentence used twice. Whichever one survives, the other must change.
**Suggested rewrite:** Since scene 04 is where the cell actually opens, let that scene own the beat and make this one pure observation: `The boy in the north cell had his hands on the bars. I made myself finish the count.`

## Verdict

Two mathematical abstractions (*geometry*, *angle*) in a scene whose whole thesis is that Zenvon thinks in reach and exits — the narration states his method correctly one sentence later, then reaches for the analytical word anyway. The reused cage-boy beat is the more consequential fix, because it is shared with scene 04. Spot-edit.
