## Flags

### [1] Verbatim quotation of the examples file — now the only surviving instance

> The boy had his hands on the bars.
>
> For a moment I was not counting anything.
>
> Then I was again.

**Why:** This is `examples/zenvon.md` §*"Feeling, arriving as an interruption to the arithmetic"* reproduced word for word. The examples file opens with *"only the **manner** transfers, never the incidents."* In the previous run this construction fired in four scenes; it now fires in one, which is a large improvement — but it is still a direct quotation of the sample rather than a use of the technique it demonstrates. Scene 04 now opens on the same image (*"The boy still had his hands on the bars"*) and handles the emotion differently and better, which shows the manner is available without the borrowed sentence.
**Suggested rewrite:** `The boy had his hands on the bars. / I lost the count and had to start it again.`

### [2] Register-wrong vocabulary

> In, cut, out, back through the doorway before his friend could get an angle on me.

**Why:** Mechanical scan B hit. *Angle* is geometric abstraction; Zenvon tracks **reach**, and the paragraph says so two sentences later (*"You do not linger in a room like that if you are my size. You visit."*). Note the run has already fixed the previous version's *"the geometry favored me"* — line 39 now reads *"which meant the room was mine to enter."* This is the last survivor of that family.
**Suggested rewrite:** `…back through the doorway before his friend could get inside my reach.`

## Verified, not flagged

- **Every quoted line in this scene checks out verbatim** against the smoothed source — as was true in the first run.
- `"Makes sense, yes."` and `"minimum back, yes"` — the ESL sentence-final `", yes"` marker protected by the 2026-08-01 ruling, carried into narration intact and, in the second case, woven into the prose without being normalised.
- `"Go on, baby."` — verbatim.
- *"Radiant, they call that. I call it the last item on his bill."* — invented, and the best line in the scene. Squarely on-voice.
- *"I watched him go and did not spend anything stopping him. Some debts you write off."* — the fleeing ruffian priced rather than moralised about. Correct per spec.
