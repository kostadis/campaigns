Five destinations. One map. More obligations than roads.

Old Owl Well sat east along the Triboar Trail. Wyvern Tor was another paid job in the same broad direction: one hundred and twenty gold from Harbin Wester, because Pip had improved the original offer. I respected the improvement. An accepted contract with an agreed price is simpler than most things.

Agatha’s Lair meant carrying Sister Garaele’s silver comb toward Conyberry and asking a banshee about Bowgentle’s spellbook. The payment was healing potions. Useful, but not coin.

Thundertree had Mirna Dendrar’s necklace hidden in the ruins. That one was not exactly a contract. She had offered it freely after we returned her family home without her husband. His body was still beneath Tresendar Manor, where we had left it.

I kept my attention on the map.

Then there was Cragmaw Castle. Gundren Rockseeker was supposed to be there. The Spider might be there. Nosk had given us the name during the fight, which was useful information obtained at a very high price.

There were two Cragmaw marks. For a moment that suggested two places we had not found. It was only one. Cragmaw Hideout was the goblin cave we had already cleared on the road into Phandalin. Finished work, still sitting on the map as if it wanted to be paid twice.

It did not.

Cragmaw Castle was different. It was the important one. The direct line through all our business led there: Gundren, the Spider, the missing mine. Side work could make us stronger before we reached it. It could provide magic, information, and perhaps enough experience to avoid another retreat through burning oil.

That was the reasonable argument for doing side work.

The other argument was that we did not know where the castle was.

Somewhere in the forest is not a location. It is a category. We could walk beneath trees for days and remain exactly as informed as when we began. Someone nearby might know more, but nobody had yet placed a useful mark on the map.

The map itself also made one thing clear. We would have to travel the road between these places. No convenient jump from Phandalin to the next mark. Each stretch of trail had distance, weather, and whatever decided to meet us there. We could cover roughly thirty miles in a day if the road allowed it. That made the Triboar Trail the sensible first line.

There were local matters still open.

Halia Thornton wanted Glasstaff’s correspondence and had offered one hundred gold for it. I had refused because the information was worth more. Her interest remained unexplained. So did the price she had chosen. I did not believe either was accidental.

And there were the bugbears.

We had last accounted for them behind a door, with fire between us and them. They had survived as far as we knew. After that, nothing. They might have fled the manor, remained beneath it, or found another exit into Phandalin. They also knew the route to Cragmaw Castle.

That should have moved Tresendar Manor to the top of the list.

It did not.

I wanted to see what they would do.

A surviving enemy becomes information if given room to act. Perhaps they would run toward the castle. Perhaps they would find their Redbrand friends. Perhaps they would come looking for us. Any of those choices would tell us something. Going back beneath the manor immediately would tell us only whether we could survive the same place twice.

Old Owl Well offered a different return. Daran Edermath had promised boots with useful magic if we investigated it. More importantly, I had magic of my own now.

A hand I could send where mine could not reach. A false image made from nothing. Force held inside me until I released it and the room moved away.

I had used all of them. I did not yet understand any of them.

Old Owl Well was a place with old trouble and reports that did not fit ordinary explanations. If there was something arcane there, Veyra might identify it. I might learn what my new abilities were good for beyond opening doors, setting oil alight, and throwing bugbears away from me.

Those were already good uses. I was not complaining.

But new power changes the value of old work. A dangerous place that offered only boots before now offered information as well. That made the price better.

So I chose Old Owl Well.

We would take the Triboar Trail east and make the distance normally, about thirty miles each day. Wyvern Tor remained on the account. Agatha remained waiting. Thundertree remained where ruined towns remain. Cragmaw Castle remained the main objective, somewhere beneath an unhelpfully large number of trees.

Tresendar Manor remained unfinished.

For the moment, I was willing to leave it that way.
