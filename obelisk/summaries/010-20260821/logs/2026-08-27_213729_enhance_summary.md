# Chapter 24 The Council at Sporedome

Date: 2026-08-23

## Summary
The party arrived at Sporedome, the luminous fungal cavern-city that served as the staging ground for the coalition's march on Maerimydra, and made their way to the great amphitheater at its heart. The cavern buzzed with a myriad of voices — secretive Drow mingling with tall mushroom men, stout dwarves conversing with humans in the red-plumed helms of Hillsfar's soldiery, and faction leaders locked in furious debate. Among the assembled faces were Solom Ned'razak, the cryptic Drow Archmage — in truth the clone, though no one at the muster knew it; Sovereign Agaricus of the Myconids, who communicated through drifting spores; Huum, speaking for the mind flayers, watching from the shadows; and the newly appointed First Lord of Hillsfar, a man the party recognized as the very guard they had once met at the city's gates. Perched atop a tall mushroom, a little girl in a dirty dress sat alone with her arm around a blind black goat — the mysterious Elisande, who had a habit of appearing at the most critical junctures of their journey.

Sovereign Agaricus conveyed through his connecting spores that a fierce battle had recently taken place between **Araumycos** — the vast sentient fungal colony of the deep Underdark, not Agaricus's own colony — and the demon queen of fungi, Zuggtmoy, alongside the demon lord Juiblex — a conflict resolved only when five outside adventurers defeated them both. The revelation reminded the party of Alder's grand design — her "Plan A" had been those same five — the adventurers she dispatched toward Menzoberranzan — working to collapse the demonic incursion from within, while the party themselves were her "Plan B," tasked with intercepting the demon lord Graz'zt when he inevitably manifested in Maerimydra. Solom Ned'razak, for his part, announced that his experiment to purge fiendish taint from a fire giant had succeeded — the subject, Brandyr, was alive at Sporedome, purged and shrunk, though the process required the subject to be subdued for ten full days, making it useless as a battlefield solution. The news sparked excited debate among the assembled factions, but the hard truth was clear: there was no shortcut. The city would have to be taken by force.

Elanil Elassidil, known as the Moonsilver Herald, raised her arms and a magical hush fell over the amphitheater. She addressed the crowd with calm authority, outlining the plan: three coalition forces would deploy to advanced positions and establish bases for a coordinated assault on Maerimydra, while two smaller teams would scout alternate approaches and gather local allies. The primary objective, she made clear, was not to seize the city but to kill Hledh Hellspawn, the fire giant queen of Maerimydra and Graz'zt's half-fire-giant granddaughter, who commanded the demonic forces. Without her, the fire giants would descend into chaos and begin tearing each other apart. When Akritas asked bluntly whether they could actually take the city, Elanil answered with equal bluntness — they had enough to make the enemy look the wrong way for a while, and that had been enough before.

Knowing what the rest of the coalition did not, the party pulled Elanil aside to warn her about Graz'zt. At first, she dismissed the warning as one of many dire predictions she heard before every campaign. But when Bramgrim produced Alder's autograph — written on his sword during their legendary encounter with the Harper hero — Elanil's expression shifted from dismissal to bitter recognition. She accused Alder of engineering the entire situation — a Harper herself, turning on a colleague's operation — sending them to die cleaning up a mess her own people had made. After a tense exchange, however, she acknowledged that nothing about the plan could change: the fire giants had to be defeated regardless, as it was the only path to Graz'zt. She told the party plainly that once they were inside Maerimydra, they would be on their own — then paused and asked, half to herself, whether they had not always been.

The party then turned to Huum, who spoke for the mind flayers, hoping to gain intelligence on Graz'zt before the battle. Huum initially claimed that all debts between the colony and the party were settled, the party having already been given two magical items of significant power. Bramgrim and Felkur appealed to the creature's pride, arguing that the demonic incursion was a threat worth finishing, and Huum — eyeing Bramgrim's brain with unsettling interest — eventually agreed to provide a detailed accounting of Graz'zt's known abilities. Akritas kept her bow quietly at the ready throughout the entire negotiation, just in case Huum's interest in Bramgrim's brain became more than academic.

No sooner had Huum departed than a short halfling with the look of a middle-aged rogue pulled Bramgrim aside and delivered a grim warning — Alder herself, come in person. The Drow alliance, she explained, was held together by a single thread: fear of Solom Ned'razak. Drow society operated on a brutal principle — if any faction attempted to kill another and failed to eliminate every last member, the remaining factions were honor-bound to destroy the attacker. This was what kept the coalition from collapsing into civil war. The danger, however, was that Dorina T'sarran — a vampire Drow cleric and the former ruler of Szith Morcane — had been spotted in the area and had sworn revenge against Solom. Daein recognized the name immediately: Dorina shared a surname with Velasta T'sarran, and both were clerics of the same dark goddess. Dorina was Velasta's aunt, and if she found Solom alive and told her niece, the lie that had kept Velasta focused on Maerimydra would unravel, and the entire coalition could come apart before a single blow was struck.

With the political landscape mapped and the dangers catalogued, the party turned to the practical work of assigning their allied factions to the battle's objectives. The Stone Giants, well-matched against fire giants, were sent to the Northern Forward Base. The Hidden, a faction of skilled thieves, were sent to scout the Underdoor and determine whether it was one of the passages feeding the Lake of Blood. The leaderless Nerevenans were paired with the friendly undead forces, while Rilira's former slaves and Grundar's Duergar were sent to the Eastern Base. Quil'bryn's Drow invoked their standing arrangement — everyone else goes in first and softens the targets — and were held in reserve, ordered to move up behind the Stone Giants once a forward position fell. Rilira objected loudly until Daein's persuasive reasoning won her over. Solom Ned'razak watched the entire arrangement with a quiet, cryptic smile. For themselves the party took two objectives: the Western Forward Base, and the Cave of Black Whispers.

As the factions were organized, two more parties approached with private requests. Zern Xerkstil of the Order of the Gauntlet told the party of the Cave of Black Whispers, where a dwarven leader — Vragni Moonsmith — had risen as a powerful mummy and held his kin in undeath; his descendant Glenna Moonsmith had begged the Order to lay him to rest. The party accepted it as their second objective. Then, in a far darker corner of the preparations, a Zhentarim operative approached Felkur with an expensive bottle of rare wine and a proposition: in exchange for cementing a smuggling partnership, the Black Network wanted the party to retrieve newborn children of Graz'zt from Castle Maerimydra, to be raised as demonic operatives. Felkur listened with apparent interest, privately planning to give the Zhentarim minor items as a decoy while keeping the real prize — Vraxen's betrayal journal — for himself, ensuring that any magical investigation would lead to the Black Network rather than him. As the armies began to march out of Sporedome to the sound of cheering, Elanil shared a final somber moment with Daein, confessing her fear of what awaited them. Felkur cut in to reassure her, mentioning the extremely strong alcohol he had been brewing — enough to knock someone out if they were captured. Elanil smiled despite herself, put on her helmet, and headed off to lead her forces into the dark.

When Daein became more direct, she demanded both the complete intelligence and its source before changing a military plan on unverifiable information. You spoke to Alder?” She accused Alder of sending everyone to clean up the disaster that Alder and her companions had created.

Despite the revelation, Elanil concluded that nothing could change. If the coalition did not breach Maerimydra and defeat the fire giants, the party would never reach Graz'zt. Their objectives would diverge only after Hledh fell: Elanil needed the party's full assistance entering the city, while the party needed to preserve enough strength to confront the demon lord. When Daein warned that the coalition would be on its own after the party turned toward Graz'zt, Elanil answered, “Weren't we always alone?” She ended the strained conversation by declaring that she had a war to win and the party had assignments to make.

The party then turned to Huum, hoping to gain intelligence on Graz'zt before the battle. Huum initially claimed that all debts between the colony and the party were settled, since they had already received two magical items of significant power and value. He also offered the illithids' hard-won strategic wisdom: “The best solution to a demonic incursion is to be anywhere but near the demonic incursion.” Bramgrim and Felkur nevertheless appealed to his pride and urged him to help finish the incursion the mind flayers had endured since the Blink. Huum—eyeing Bramgrim's brain with unsettling interest—eventually agreed to provide a detailed accounting of Graz'zt's known abilities. Any further support would be reconsidered only as events unfolded. Akritas kept her bow quietly ready in case Huum's culinary interest became more than academic.

No sooner had Huum departed than a short halfling with the look of a middle-aged rogue pulled Bramgrim aside: Alder herself, appearing in person. The Drow alliance, she explained, was held together by one thread—fear of Solom Ned'razak. Drow society operated on a brutal principle: if a faction attempted to destroy another and failed to kill every last member, all remaining factions were honor-bound to exterminate the failed attacker. Bramgrim illustrated the custom with a story from Menzoberranzan, where a noble house failed to kill one surviving child and was consequently destroyed by the other houses.

The immediate danger was Dorina T'sarran, a vampire Drow cleric of Kiaransalee and former ruler of Szith Morcane, who had been seen nearby and had sworn revenge upon Solom. Daein recognized the shared surname and religious affiliation: Dorina was Velasta T'sarran's aunt. Velasta's vengeance had depended on Dorina losing power, and Velasta still believed the fire giants responsible for Solom's death. If Dorina discovered that Solom lived and informed her niece, the party's lie would unravel, Velasta could redirect her vengeance, and the coalition might collapse. The party therefore found itself assigned the unwelcome task of secretly protecting Solom.

The party next assigned its allies to the assault objectives. The Stone Giants, well-matched against fire giants, were sent to the Northern Forward Base. The Hidden, a faction of skilled thieves, were sent to scout the Underdoor and determine whether it was one of the passages feeding the Lake of Blood. The leaderless Nerevenans were paired with the friendly undead forces, while Rilira's former slaves and Grundar's Duergar were sent to the Eastern Base. Quil'bryn's Drow invoked their standing arrangement—everyone else would enter first and soften the targets—and were held in reserve to move behind the Stone Giants after a forward position fell. Rilira objected vociferously until Daein argued that a reserve could reinforce whichever position needed help; his persuasive case won her over. Solom watched the arrangement with a quiet, cryptic smile. The party chose the Western Forward Base and Fortify Gains as its two coalition objectives.

Two additional factions delivered private requests. Zern Xerkstil of the Order of the Gauntlet told the party about the Cave of Black Whispers, where Drow from Szith Morcane had slaughtered a group of dwarves. Their leader, Vragni Moonsmith, had risen as a powerful mummy whose evil kept the dead from finding rest. His descendant Glenna Moonsmith, owner of the Plodding Plow and The Stop, had begged the Order to lay him to rest permanently. The party planned to visit the cave before proceeding to the Western Forward Base.

In a far darker corner of the preparations, a Zhentarim operative approached Felkur—addressed under the name “Oldenwood”—with an expensive bottle of rare wine. The Black Network praised his team's demonstrated ability to move goods discreetly and alluded to rumors that an arcanaloth's tower had been breached after the party's last visit to Maerimydra. They offered to move any future acquisitions out of the Underdark to suitable buyers for a small shipping fee. As a token cementing the partnership, they asked Felkur to recover newborn children of Graz'zt from near Castle Maerimydra so the Black Network could raise them as operatives possessing the powers of a demon lord.

Felkur accepted with apparent enthusiasm, but privately planned to use the Zhentarim as a decoy. He would give them minor items from the tower while keeping the real prize—Vraxen's betrayal journal—for himself, ensuring that anyone tracing stolen goods would pursue the Black Network. His separate plan for defeating magical scrutiny was to become blackout drunk before the theft, leave the tower with the journal, and genuinely have no memory that he had stolen it.

As the armies marched from Sporedome amid cries of “Huzzah,” Solom watched with his cryptic smile. Elanil warned the party that if Graz'zt appeared, the assembled soldiers might all die and told Daein she hoped he had a plan. Daein assured her that everything was under control. Remembering their first meeting, Elanil reflected that she had never imagined they would end up rushing toward death or glory. When Daein insisted they would not die, she grimly observed that if a demon lord captured them, death might be preferable. Felkur reassured her by mentioning the extremely strong alcohol he had been brewing—enough to knock someone unconscious if captured. Encouraged despite herself, Elanil smiled, donned her helmet, and went to command her position.

## Scenes

After Felkur's slow-clap moment in Szith Morcane, the factions had accepted that rulership of the outpost was the second problem; defeating the fire giants was the first.

Political tensions erupted immediately between Rilira, Asala, and Hamadh, representative of the cult of Vhaeraun, while Felkur serenely ignored the standoff and tinkered with ballistic components.

He had asked her to autograph his sword, though carrying the signature of the hero who imprisoned Lolth while negotiating with Lolth's worshippers presented an obvious diplomatic risk.

#### Elanil Elassidil, the Moonsilver Herald, magically silences the assembled factions and delivers a rousing address outlining the military operation against Maerimydra.

- Around Sporedome's fungal amphitheater, “excitement wars with panic.” Those not seated paced back and forth as a dozen furious conversations competed with one another.

- Elanil Elassidil raised her arms toward the cavern ceiling. A magical silence descended, and the entire assemblage turned toward the Moonsilver Herald. Akritas silently cheered her arrival.

Intelligence supplied by the allies from Szith Morcane had revealed the city's approaches and established that Hledh Hellspawn was present, preparing an invasion of the sunlight realms.

- Two smaller teams would scout alternate approaches, gather local allies, and provide the intelligence needed to drive coalition forces into the city.

A city in the sunlight realms whose newly appointed First Lord, formerly a simple gate guard the party met, attends the war council. Vuhm Yestral publicly praises Daein and the party as Hillsfar's heroes and protectors while eagerly attaching himself to their reputation.

A haunted cavern where a large group of dwarves was surprised and slaughtered by Drow from Szith Morcane. The seat of power within the occupied city. The Zhentarim have heard that newborn children of Graz'zt may be found somewhere in or near the castle and want them recovered so they can be raised as Black Network operatives.

A legendary Harper hero known throughout the North. Her adventuring party trapped Lolth in a dimensional prison with Vhaeraun's assistance, and she devised the broader response to the demonic incursion. Her Plan A sent five adventurers into the Underdark to gather the demon lords and induce them to destroy one another; the current party became Plan B after Graz'zt learned of that scheme and prepared to appear in Maerimydra.

Alder previously appeared without warning, delivered her assignment, and left—her characteristic habit as an extraordinarily powerful rogue. Bramgrim was so excited to meet her that he asked her to sign his sword. At Sporedome she appears in person as a short halfling with the look of a middle-aged rogue, pulls Bramgrim aside, and warns him about Dorina T'sarran and the need to protect Solom.

### Zern Xerkstil

A member of the Order of the Gauntlet who approaches the party despite its members not yet having sworn fealty to the Order. He brings the Cave of Black Whispers mission: lay the mummy Vragni Moonsmith to rest at the request of his descendant, Glenna Moonsmith. The request typifies the Order's priorities—saving souls and opposing evil even in the middle of a war. Refusal would earn his considerable disapproval.

### Dorina T'sarran

A vampire Drow cleric of Kiaransalee and former ruler of Szith Morcane. She is Velasta T'sarran's aunt and has been seen in the area after vowing revenge upon Solom Ned'razak. If she discovers that Solom lives and informs Velasta, she may overturn the lie directing the revenant's vengeance toward the fire giants and destabilize the entire coalition.

### Rilira

Leader of the former Drow slaves. She vociferously objects when Quil'bryn's Drow are retained as a reserve, arguing that the Drow knights should be fighting at the front. Daein persuades her that a mobile reserve can reinforce whichever objective falters, and she accepts the assignment plan.

### Glenna Moonsmith

Owner of the Plodding Plow and The Stop and a familiar contact of the party. She has begged the Order of the Gauntlet to lay her ancestor Vragni Moonsmith—now a powerful mummy in the Cave of Black Whispers—permanently to rest.

### Huum

The mind flayer who speaks for the illithids at Sporedome. Huum remembers the party's help against a rogue thought but initially insists the colony's ledger with them is clean because it already provided two powerful magical items.

He argues that the best response to a demonic incursion is to be elsewhere, but Bramgrim and Felkur persuade him to assist. After calling Bramgrim's brain “tasty,” Huum agrees to provide a list of Graz'zt's known abilities. He makes no promise of direct aid and will reconsider further involvement only as circumstances unfold.

## Items

### Hellfire Projectors

Powerful ballistic weapons extracted from the fire giant forge after King Dengor's death. The party secured three complete projectors and components for four more while the chamber collapsed and fire salamanders rampaged around them. Felkur later tinkered calmly with their ballistic components during the political confrontation in Szith Morcane.

### Rare Metals

Valuable materials worth roughly one thousand gold pieces, salvaged from the crumbling fire giant forge during the party's narrow escape from the fire salamanders.

### Alder's Autograph

Alder's signature, written on Bramgrim's sword after he excitedly asked the legendary Harper for an autograph. It carries diplomatic risk when displayed before worshippers of Lolth, whom Alder helped imprison. At Sporedome it proves the source of the party's intelligence to Elanil, turning an apparently wild warning about Graz'zt into credible information.

### Vraxen's Betrayal Journal

The document Felkur intends to steal from the arcanaloth's tower during the invasion of Maerimydra. His plan is to become blackout drunk before taking it, ensuring he has no memory of the theft and only knows that he later possesses the journal. He also intends to give other objects from the tower to the Zhentarim, using them as decoys so investigators pursue the Black Network rather than him.

### Rare Bottle of Wine

An expensive and rare bottle offered to Felkur by a Zhentarim operative during their private negotiations. The wine is part of an attempt to flatter and “wine and dine” him into a smuggling partnership and the recovery of Graz'zt's newborn children.

## Spells

### Magical Silence

Elanil Elassidil raises her arms toward the cavern ceiling and a magical silence descends over the rowdy fungal amphitheater. The effect immediately ends the competing conversations and draws the entire coalition's attention to the Moonsilver Herald for her briefing.

### Connecting Spores

A Myconid ability used by Sovereign Agaricus after the party asks what has changed. He sways, gestures, and releases spores that create a shared link, conveying the recent conflict in which Zuggtmoy and Juiblex attacked Araumycos and were defeated by five adventurers.
