# Planning at the Miner's Exchange

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Zenvon and the party review their current quests and decide to head toward the Old Owl Well.

- The party reviews its open quests on the Sword Coast map and in a newly assembled quest log, including Old Owl Well, Wyvern Tor (already accepted — 120 gp from Townmaster Harbin Wester, negotiated up by Pip), Agatha's Lair, Thundertree, and Cragmaw Castle.
- The Cragmaw Hideout marker is identified as the goblin cave the party already cleared, not a new quest.
- The DM explains that travel occurs normally rather than by teleportation and that encounters can happen along the way.
- Cragmaw Castle is the main-line objective, while side quests may provide experience, power, magic items, and useful information.
- The party is reminded of local loose ends: Halia's unexplained agenda and the surviving bugbears, whose whereabouts after the fire are unknown.
- The party knows Cragmaw Castle lies somewhere in the forest but not its exact location; local information may help.
- Zenvon chooses Old Owl Well, citing his recently gained magical powers.
- Zenvon knowingly leaves the Tresendar Manor business unresolved, curious what the surviving bugbears will do.
- The party plans to follow the Triboar Trail, covering roughly thirty miles per day.
- Before departing, Zenvon decides to visit Daran Edermath at his orchard.
