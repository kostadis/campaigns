# A Bounty for Cragmaw Castle

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Before departing Phandalin, the party shares a final meal with Sildar Hallwinter, who offers a significant reward to deal with Cragmaw Castle.

- Sildar Hallwinter meets the party as they come downstairs the following morning and invites them to join him for breakfast.
- Sildar says that the Lords' Alliance wants Cragmaw Castle found and its band leaders defeated and driven off.
- He distinguishes this assignment from the separate bounty already discussed for Iarno Albrek.
- Sildar hints at a large reward, prompting Pip to nudge Zenvon and ask, "can we at least find out how much?"
- Pip rises and addresses him as "Mr. Sildar," openly attempting to ingratiate himself. Sildar tells him that "Sildar" is sufficient.
- Sildar offers 500 gold pieces if the party handles everything at Cragmaw Castle.
- Pip turns to Zenvon and eagerly asks, "boss, are we gonna go do that?"
- Zenvon pulls Pip aside and points out that 500 gold divided four ways is only 125 gold each, asking whether that is enough for the danger involved.
- Pip accepts the advice and tempers his enthusiasm: "Well, I mean, you know, it's a chunk of change, but we're… you know, we'll keep that under advisement, Sildar."
- Sildar adds that the Lords' Alliance will "look very kindly" upon whoever clears Cragmaw Castle, implying future favors or opportunities.
- Zenvon keeps his word to Daran and maintains that Old Owl Well remains the party's immediate destination.
- Before they leave, Sildar notices the magical sword recovered from Tresendar Manor and says, "my, my, my, that is a fine, noble sword, Pip."
- Zenvon rolls a 20 on Insight and realizes that Sildar is hinting at a possible opening in the Lords' Alliance for Pip. *(The tape records the result as "20" on an unmodified `/r d20`; the GM never calls it a natural 20.)*
- Zenvon is unsure whether Pip understands the implication and chooses to keep the observation to himself for the moment.
- The party is reminded of Mirna's treasure in Thundertree before setting out.
- Toblen wishes the adventurers well as they depart Phandalin.
