# Arrival at the Old Owl Well

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party reaches the Old Owl Well, meets the Red Wizard Hamun Kost, and accepts a bargain rather than a battle.

- The party reaches Old Owl Well around noon after crossing the surrounding hills.
- As the party enters, twelve zombies shamble out of the tower. *(GM-side prep, not established at the table: among them a sun-peeled former sentinel and an acolyte in a singed Red Wizard's robe. The party observed only "a lot of zombies".)*
- Sister Maela's Arcana check establishes that zombies are mindless and do not speak, and that someone must be controlling them.
- She concludes a necromancer is probably in the tent, and warns that raising this many corpses requires evil, dangerous and powerful magic.
- Pip cautions against fighting the horde outright.
- Rather than draw steel, Zenvon taunts the unseen controller as "Zombie Lord" to bait him into appearing.
- Hamun Kost emerges from the tent. Veyra identifies the tattoos on his shaved head as necromantic symbols.
- Hamun names himself a Red Wizard of Thay and makes clear he could kill the party if his secrecy required it, but insists he has no interest in harming the region.
- He offers the location of Cragmaw Castle in exchange for one of two favours.
- The first option is to deal with marauders at Wyvern Tor who have scouted Hamun's camp and appear inclined to cause trouble. Hamun tells the party only that the group includes several bugbears and an ogre; he does not name Brughor Axe-Biter or the ogre Gog. *(GM-side: the full band is Brughor Axe-Biter, four bugbears and Gog.)*
- The second option is to ask the banshee Agatha for the name of the wizard who originally built the tower.
- Asked why he does not question Agatha himself, Hamun admits he does not want to be killed by a banshee.
- The party debates. Zenvon considers Agatha the more suicidal errand; Sister Maela agrees emphatically, preferring ogres and bugbears to a conversation with a banshee.
- The party accepts the Wyvern Tor task. Hamun promises the information — and possibly a fine meal — on their return, asking only that they announce themselves before approaching his camp.
- Zenvon reflects out of character that he had expected a fight; the GM confirms that avoiding one was a real choice.
