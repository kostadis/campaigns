# Consulting Daran Edermath

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party visits Daran Edermath at his orchard to gather intelligence and warnings before departing.

- Before choosing the final route, Zenvon decides to ask Daran Edermath what he knows.
- The party finds Daran, a retired adventurer, clearing underbrush beside his cottage and orchard.
- Daran greets them warmly as the heroes of Phandalin.
- Daran expresses his pride in the party for dealing with the Redbrands.
- Daran asks whether they have handled the Old Owl Well problem and is delighted to hear they are setting out.
- Daran warns that the farther east one travels, the more the land has been ravaged by wars, demons, gods, and elemental catastrophes, becoming progressively less settled.
- He advises that the road is likely safer than the hills, where territorial creatures may claim the land.
- He reports that Townmaster Harbin Wester has spoken of several caravan attacks near Wyvern Tor, though Daran has not followed all the details.
- Invited to join the expedition, Daran declines, explaining that an old adventurer is old because he chose to stop adventuring.
- Asked for anything useful, Daran instead offers general advice: keep your eyes up, keep your sword sharpened, keep your back straight.
- Zenvon begins taking notes, which delights Daran and prompts roughly ten minutes of reminiscence while Pip struggles not to laugh.
- For his patience Zenvon receives a one-time +1 bonus to an Investigation or Survival check.
- Zenvon asks about Netheril, which Daran had previously connected to the Old Owl Well watchtower.
- Daran describes the Netherese Empire: flying ships, magic beyond modern reach, and spells to the twelfth level where modern wizardry reaches the ninth.
- He recounts that a leader named Karsus did something that broke the Weave, nearly destroying magic and bringing the empire down.
- Daran cautions that a great empire destroyed by hubris sounds suspiciously like a morality play; Zenvon counters that stories usually rest on something real, and Daran concedes.
- Netheril's fall scattered powerful magical trinkets across the north, and rumours of one attract magic-users, scavengers and the power-hungry.
- Daran dismisses tales of cursed Netherese artifacts as old wives' tales, while allowing that fear keeps the incapable away from dangerous things.
- He warns that with any powerful magical object, no one can be certain who made it, why, or to what purpose — and that disaster can follow in hands not capable of handling it.
- Ultimately, what he wants from the party is an answer: whether a threat to Phandalin is brewing at Old Owl Well.
