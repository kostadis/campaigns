# Preparations at Stonehill Inn

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party returns to the Stonehill Inn for the night, gathers what Toblen knows about Conyberry, and takes a long rest.

- Toblen Stonehill is delighted to see the party, who are still "the toast of the town."
- Elsa pours Zenvon a beer at Toblen's invitation.
- Zenvon tells Toblen the party leaves in the morning, travelling by way of Conyberry toward the Old Owl Well.
- Toblen questions why anyone would deliberately enter an abandoned town.
- Toblen warns that Conyberry has lain in ruins for years and that roadside ruins attract people dangerous to the unwary.
- An Insight check reveals that Toblen has never visited Conyberry and is expressing fears rather than firsthand knowledge.
- The Conyberry history the party comes away with — devastated during the Spellplague, its remaining inhabitants later killed by Uthgardt raiders — is accurate, though Toblen has never been there himself and is not a firsthand source.
- Toblen reminds the party that the route to Agatha, should they undertake Sister Garaele's errand, also passes through Conyberry.
- The party takes a long rest at the inn and wakes the next morning.
