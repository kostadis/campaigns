# Arrival at the Old Owl Well

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party discovers a mysterious camp at the ruins of an old watchtower, only to be greeted by the undead.

- Cresting a low ridge, the party sees a crumbling watchtower and mounds of rubble enclosing a courtyard amid rugged hills.
- A colorful tent stands in the middle of the courtyard, but no one is visible.
- Zenvon interprets the tent as evidence that someone is present and senses “Some eeriness in the air.”
- He advises the party to tiptoe and advance slowly through the main entrance toward the well.
- As the party enters, twelve zombies shamble out of the tower, including a sun-peeled former sentinel and an acolyte in a singed Red Wizard's robe.
- Sister Maela explains that zombies do not talk and are essentially mindless monsters.
- Looking toward the tent, she concludes, “there's probably a necromancer.”
- She explains that a necromancer is a magic-user who reanimates dead corpses and calls the practice “very evil magic.”
- Sister Maela warns that whoever can control so many zombies must command dangerous and powerful magic.
- Pip tells Zenvon, “Boss, I don't know if we want to fight all these zombies.”
- Zenvon hopes the undead have a central controller and that finding the necromancer could prevent a direct battle.
- He attempts to provoke the controller into showing himself: “Hey, Zombie Lord, are you afraid to face us? And that's why�� is that why you're sending the zombies?”
- The taunt succeeds. Hamun Kost steps out of the tent and demands, “WHAT IS THE MEANING OF THIS?”
- Veyra studies the tattoos on his shaved head, consults her notebook, and identifies them as the symbols of a necromancer.
- The party's historical knowledge yields nothing further about the markings.
- Zenvon remembers that Daran's task is to determine whether a threat to Phandalin is brewing at Old Owl Well, not necessarily to kill whoever is present.
- Zenvon says, “we are just here to talk to you, nothing else. We don't want any… fight unnecessarily.”
- He introduces himself and names Pip, Sister Maela, and Veyra before asking the wizard's name.
- The wizard introduces himself as Hamun Kost.
- Hamun says, “I'm a wizard. And it's not polite to ask what a wizard is doing here,” but admits that he is interested in an archaeological dig at the tower.
- Zenvon presses him to explain what makes the site special. Hamun refuses, saying that a wizard's interests are his own concern.
- Zenvon argues that Hamun's presence and undead entourage are alarming the neighboring lands and that he wants to keep the local population safe.
- Hamun laughs and replies, “My, my dear boy! Zenvon, is that your name again?” before assuring him that he has no intention of harming anyone nearby.
- When Zenvon promises to keep his secret, Hamun answers, “My dear boy, if I wanted my secrets to be safe, I would kill you.”
- Hamun reveals that he is a Red Wizard of Thay, an order whose members are not to be treated lightly.
- He says that he wants to be a positive member of the community and can offer information as proof.
- As the conversation continues, the zombies gather around Hamun in a deliberate show of force.
- After an awkward silence in which Hamun waits for a question, he asks, ���Now… Zenvon. What is it that you would like to know?”
- Zenvon asks for the location of Cragmaw Castle. Hamun immediately claims to know it.
- Hamun offers the castle's location in exchange for a small service.
- The first option is to deal with marauders at Wyvern Tor who have scouted Hamun's camp and appear inclined to cause trouble.
- Hamun describes the group as several bugbears and an ogre, explaining that ogres are even tougher than bugbears.
- He claims he could deal with them himself but does not want to raise more zombies simply to handle his business.
- The second option is to ask the banshee Agatha for the name of the wizard who built the tower at Old Owl Well.
- Sister Maela asks why Hamun does not question Agatha himself. Hamun candidly replies that he does not wish to be killed by a banshee.
- The party discusses the choice. Zenvon considers Agatha the greater risk and favors fighting enemies they understand.
- Sister Maela argues passionately for Wyvern Tor, preferring ogres and bugbears to a conversation with a banshee every day and “twice on the 10th day.”
- The party agrees to deal with the Wyvern Tor marauders.
- Hamun responds, “wonderful, wonderful,” promises to prepare the information about Cragmaw Castle, and suggests there may be a fine meal upon their return.
- Zenvon asks Hamun to ensure that the zombies do not attack them when they return.
- Hamun advises the party not to enter someone's home without shouting first.
- Zenvon counters, “Well, technically your house is the tent, and we haven't walked into it yet.”
- Hamun replies that his zombies are protective and asks the party to call out so he can emerge and receive them.
- Zenvon later admits that he expected a fight, but judged Hamun too powerful to attack. The DM confirms that choosing conversation was a meaningful and wise choice.
