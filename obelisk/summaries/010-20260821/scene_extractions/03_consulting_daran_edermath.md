# Consulting Daran Edermath

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party visits Daran Edermath at his orchard to gather intelligence and discuss the history of the Netheril Empire before their journey.

- The party finds Daran, a retired adventurer, clearing underbrush beside his cottage and orchard.
- Daran greets them enthusiastically as the heroes of Phandalin: “well, well, well, well, well. I'm… I… the heroes of… the heroes of Phandalin.”
- Daran expresses his pride in the party for dealing with the Redbrands.
- Zenvon answers modestly, “we didn't intend to, but it was, I guess, God's plan.”
- Daran asks whether they have handled the Old Owl Well problem and is delighted to learn they intend to leave the following morning.
- Daran warns that the farther east one travels, the more the land has been ravaged by wars, demons, gods, and elemental catastrophes.
- He explains that Conyberry has been a ruin for years.
- Daran describes two possible routes: the road, which should be safer and may have occasional travelers, or the hills, where creatures may regard the land as their territory.
- He reports that Townmaster Harbin Wester has spoken of several caravan attacks near Wyvern Tor, though Daran has not followed all the details.
- Zenvon invites Daran to join the expedition. After briefly considering it, Daran declines: “you know, one of the things about being an old adventurer is you're an old adventurer because you choose to stop adventuring.”
- Asked for equipment or other practical help, Daran advises the party to keep their eyes up, sharpen their swords, keep their backs straight, and maintain a “head on a swivel.”
- Zenvon starts writing the advice down, encouraging Daran to continue for roughly ten minutes with old stories of uncertain relevance.
- Pip watches Zenvon and tries not to laugh while Daran is delighted by his attentive student.
- For patiently enduring the lecture, Zenvon gains a one-time +1 bonus to an Investigation or Survival check.
- Zenvon asks about Netheril after consulting his notes and reminding Daran that he previously called Old Owl Well an ancient Netherese watchtower.
- Daran describes the Netherese Empire as an ancient civilization with flying ships and magic beyond modern capability.
- Modern wizard spells reach ninth level, while Netherese magic was said to reach the twelfth level of power.
- Daran explains that Karsus, one of the empire's leaders, did something that caused the Weave itself to break, nearly destroying magic and precipitating Netheril's collapse.
- Daran cautions that he is not a scholar and finds the familiar story of an empire destroyed by its own hubris suspiciously similar to a morality play.
- Zenvon argues that stories are usually founded upon real incidents. Daran agrees: “There's… there's always a grain of truth.”
- Netheril's fall scattered powerful magical trinkets across the north. Rumors of one attract magic-users, scavengers, and power-hungry seekers.
- Daran hopes artifacts found by good people are used for society's benefit; if they fall into worse hands, that is why the world needs adventurers.
- Zenvon asks whether Netherese relics might be cursed or bring bad luck to anyone who picks them up.
- Daran dismisses curses and corrupting influence as old wives' tales, but admits that no one necessarily knows who created an artifact, why it was made, or what purpose it serves.
- He warns that powerful objects can cause disaster in the wrong hands — or in the hands of people incapable of controlling them.
- Daran concedes that frightening stories may help keep unprepared people away from dangerous magic and tells Zenvon that recovering whatever lies at Old Owl Well would be valuable.
