# Quest Log

*Kept by Veyra of the Blue Candle, because somebody has to and I am the one who brought the books.*
*Current as of the morning we left the Miner's Exchange, having told Halia Thornton that her hundred gold was not enough.*

---

## The Wagon

### We never delivered the wagon
**Who's asking:** Gundren Rockseeker, technically. Elmina Barthen, of Barthen's Provisions, is the one who owes us for it.
**What we know:** Zenvon was hired to escort one wagon of mining supplies from Neverwinter to Barthen's Provisions in Phandalin. Picks, shovels, lantern oil, rope. **Ten gold pieces, payable on receipt.** Then there was an ambush on the road, and Gundren was taken, and I do not believe any of us has walked into Barthen's since.
**Status:** Open, and faintly embarrassing. It is the only contract any of us actually signed.

*Note in the margin: Gundren wrote at the bottom of it, in his own hand — "If I'm late, wait for me at the Stonehill Inn. If I'm very late, start worrying." He is very late.*

---

## The Spine

### Find Gundren Rockseeker
**Who's asking:** Nobody had to ask.
**What we know:** He was taken on the Triboar Trail and dragged off separately from Sildar, which means it was not a robbery — somebody told the goblins he was coming. He was taken **for his map to Wave Echo Cave**. Sildar says Gundren and his brothers were close to finding the lost mine of the Phandelver Pact, and that the Forge of Spells is down there.
**Where/why:** A bugbear named Nosk told us, without meaning to, that the Black Spider is at **Cragmaw Castle**. That is where Gundren will be.
**Status:** Urgent, and we have no idea how to get there. Nobody in Phandalin has been able to tell us where Cragmaw Castle is.

*What worries me: Sildar said his captors are working on him for the map. Once they have it, they have no reason to keep him.*

---

## Paid Work

### Find Iarno Albrek — and the Black Spider
**Who's asking:** Sildar Hallwinter, Lords' Alliance.
**Terms:** Fifty gold for getting him to Phandalin. One hundred up front, which Zenvon got by telling a Lords' Alliance man to find someone else if he didn't like it. **One hundred and fifty more paid when we brought him the letter — two hundred and fifty in all.**
**Where it stands:** **Half done and half undone.** We found Iarno. Iarno is Glasstaff. We chased him down his own escape passage, I put a firebolt in him, Pip put a javelin in him, and he said something to the creature in the crevasse and vanished. He is alive and he knows our faces.
**The new offer:** Sildar will pay **200 gold for Iarno alive**, 100 if he's dead, and 50 if he's dead in a way that can't be undone. He would rather have a trial in Neverwinter. He also said, quietly, that he would not be upset by the other outcome.
**The Spider:** Still no name and no face. The letter proves he exists, gives orders, and wants us captured or killed and any dwarven maps delivered. Zenvon argued that the Spider matters more than Iarno, and Sildar agreed — he thinks Iarno has run straight to his master, so we may get both at once.

### Wyvern Tor — the caravan raiders
**Who's asking:** Harbin Wester, Townmaster.
**Terms:** **120 gold pieces**, negotiated up from 100 by Pip, who impressed a banker doing it.
**What we know:** Marauders have been hitting caravans east along the Triboar Trail.
**Status:** Accepted. Not started. Nobody has mentioned it since.

### Old Owl Well — who's disturbing the ruins
**Who's asking:** Daran Edermath, at the orchard.
**Terms:** **Boots of striding and springing.**
**What we know:** Prospectors in the hills northeast of town have been driven out of the ruins by undead. Daran called it an old watchtower of Netheril, which was an empire of magic, and he was more worried about that than about the undead. He also told us to rescue Gundren first.
**Status:** Accepted. Not started.

### Agatha, and the silver comb
**Who's asking:** Sister Garaele, at the Shrine of Luck.
**Terms:** **Three potions of healing.**
**What we know:** Garaele went to a banshee named Agatha to ask about a spellbook that belonged to a mage called Bowgentle, and barely got out alive — that is what the limp was, all this time. Agatha spoke wistfully about beautiful things she owned when she was living. Garaele has given us a **jeweled silver comb** to offer her as a gift, and wants us to ask the question in her place.
**I checked:** Bowgentle is in my notes. He was real. That is a good sign and I said so.
**Status:** Accepted. Zenvon told her it was a priority but not the priority. Her answer: *the banshee isn't going anywhere, but don't take too much time.*

## Unfinished Business Under Tresendar Manor

*We told ourselves we cleared it. We did not clear it.*

### The bugbears we ran from
Four of them, and a miserable goblin. They served the Black Spider directly, not Glasstaff, and they laughed at our red cloaks. Zenvon got Cragmaw Castle out of Nosk by making him angry, and then Nosk tried to kill us for it, and very nearly did.

We burned the hallway behind us. **One of them died in the fire. Three did not.** We shut the door at the top and locked it and heard them hitting it as we walked away.

**They know the way to Cragmaw Castle.** They are also the only ones left down there who do.

### Droop
The goblin. He fainted the moment we came through the door and I do not know what happened to him after that. He was still on the floor when the oil went up. Nobody has said his name since and I am writing it here so that at least it is written.

### Thel Dendrar
We promised Mirna we would bring her husband's body back if we ever could. **We did not look for him.** We went down into the crevasse the same afternoon, took the chest, and came back up. He is still down there.

I want it noted that we had the time.

### The creature in the crevasse
It calls itself **Ssarnak**. We have fed it three times now — a bound prisoner, and then the dead. It let us pass each time, and it has kept every bargain it made, which is more than can be said for most people we have met in this town.

It is still down there. Whoever comes up that tunnel next will meet it.

### The deeper levels — if they exist
Wick, before he died, said the dungeon had more levels, that he'd been sent down once with supplies and would not go again, and that there was *"something really wrong over there that messes with your head."*

He was a frightened man saying anything he thought would keep him alive. I am recording it because it might be true, not because I believe it.

## Errands and Offers

- **The Lionshield goods.** Crates and sacks marked with a blue lion, taken back from the goblins in the hideout. They belong to the Lionshield Coster here in town. They have been in our packs since before we came back to Phandalin. We have not returned them.
- **The jade frog.** A statuette with tiny gold eyes, from the bugbear's cave. Nobody has ever told us what it's worth. The Miner's Exchange weighs and prices things, which is an honest reason to go back through Halia's door.
- **Halia Thornton.** She congratulated us on the Redbrands and stopped smiling the instant she learned Glasstaff had got away rather than died. Then she asked whether we'd found any *correspondence* among his effects, and offered a hundred gold for it, and said it was about protecting local trade.

  Zenvon said the information was worth more than a hundred gold. She said, *"You like to negotiate?"* We left.

  She was sitting at a table with a Redbrand lieutenant when we first met her, and then acted astonished to learn he was a Redbrand while he was wearing the cloak. Freda told us, back at the start, that the Redbrands bothered everyone in Phandalin except Halia, and that we should ask ourselves whether they feared her or owed her.

  **She is not what she presents herself as. We should find out what she is, and not in her own building.**
- **Carp Alderleaf's tunnel.** The boy found the way in. We used it. He should probably be told it worked, and told never to go back.

## Our Own Business

### Zenvon
He has been carrying a piece of black stone since the very beginning — since the cart, where we saw the shadow of the obelisk — and told none of us. He told **me**, on the road outside the manor, and put it in my hand.

My crystal burned brighter than I have ever seen it.

He has not told the others. That is his to tell.

### Pip
He won a shard of black stone in a dice game near the Neverwinter docks and sold it to a peddler who was coming here — **to Phandalin.** Since then he has dreamed of broken black stone under the earth and heard something whispering his name.

He did not tell us. The creature in the crevasse pulled it out of his head and told all of us at once, and then a fight started, and nobody has spoken to him about it properly since. **Somebody should.**

He also wants better armour, has mentioned full plate, has costed it at several thousand gold, and would like the Thundertree necklace pursued at the earliest opportunity. He has raised this more than once.

### Mine
**Professor Orryn Voss is alive.**

The creature said it to me as we ran past — *"Your teacher is not dead. He is not in a place where dead is easy."* I tried to go back and ask it what that meant and Maela put her arms around me and would not let go.

I have not stopped thinking about the phrasing. *Not in a place where dead is easy.* That is not a comfort. That is a location.

He came to Phandalin because he believed there were fragments of black stone here, from something larger. He said the fragments must never be assembled. My candle answers to them — it flared when I wrote the name **"Ruxithid,"** and it flared again beside Zenvon's stone.

### Maela
I do not have a thread to write for her, and that may be the point. When we stood outside the manor and finally said all of this out loud, she said nothing at all, and her face did something none of us could read. We looked. We could not read it.

I am putting it here so that when it matters, we can say we noticed.

---

## Things Nobody Has Hired Us For, Which We Notice Anyway

- **"Ruxithid."** A goblin with a long, wrong-shaped skull said it to Sildar during the ambush — *"You're not what Ruxithid wants."* Neither he nor we know what it means. My candle knows.
- **The goblins with the elongated skulls.** One at the ambush. **Two more dead in the caverns under the manor**, in filthy rags, killed by something before we ever got there. They are not the goblins we have been fighting. They were here first.
- **Wick said Glasstaff answers to somebody in town.** *"Orders from town." "The boss's business." "I don't think Glasstaff's the big boss."* Then he went pale at the words *Black Spider* and stopped talking. He may have been lying to buy his life. He did not buy it.
- **The armoury.** Racks of spears, swords, crossbows, bolts, and a dozen spare cloaks — far more than a dozen thugs needed. They were arming for something bigger than shaking down a frontier town. Nobody has asked what.
- **Elsa's sister** is a scholar in Neverwinter and the pride of her family. Professor Voss may have known her. I have been meaning to write.
- **Toblen said the Redbrands killed Thel Dendrar in broad daylight** and the family vanished, and that was the first thing anyone in this town told us. We found the family. Nine days late, or thereabouts.

---

*Four things now point the same direction: a shard Pip sold to a peddler bound for this town, a stone Zenvon carried here without knowing why, a candle that answers to both, and a teacher who vanished saying the fragments must never be assembled. I do not think we came to Phandalin. I think we were brought.*

*Professor Voss used to say a ruin is only dead if nothing remembers it. I thought that was beautiful. Lately I have started to think it was a warning.*
