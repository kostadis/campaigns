# Quest Log

*Kept by Veyra of the Blue Candle, in the notebook she never puts down.*
*Current as of the clearing of the Tresendar hideout — the upper level, anyway.*

---

## The Job That Started It

### Find Gundren Rockseeker
**Who's asking:** Nobody had to ask. He hired us, and they took him.
**What we know:** Gundren was pulled off the Triboar Trail in the ambush and dragged away on his own — he was never in the Cragmaw cave with Sildar. He was targeted on purpose. Someone told the goblins exactly which wagon to hit.
**Why they wanted him:** His map to Wave Echo Cave, the lost mine of the Phandelver Pact. Sildar says the Forge of Spells is down there, buried five hundred years.
**Where he is:** Unknown. This is the one that keeps us up at night.
**Status:** Open. Urgent.

---

## Paid Work

### Find Iarno Albrek
**Who's asking:** Sildar Hallwinter, Lords' Alliance.
**Terms:** 100 gp paid up front, more on delivery. (He also settled the 50 gp for walking him home.)
**What we know now:** We found him. Iarno Albrek *is* Glasstaff — Sildar's own missing agent, running the Redbrands out of Tresendar Manor. Rondar gave up the name with a knife at his throat.
**What happened:** We cornered him in a secret passage. Veyra burned him, Pip put a javelin in him, and he shouted an order at the Nothic and vanished — teleported clean out.
**Status:** Open. He's alive, he's hurt, and he knows our faces.

### Identify the Black Spider
**Who's asking:** Sildar Hallwinter, same contract.
**What we know:** A name the Cragmaw goblins used. A wax spider seal on a letter in Iarno's desk, ordering him to *capture us if possible, kill us if necessary,* and hand over any dwarven maps without delay. So: whoever this is, they run the bandit gangs, they wanted Gundren's map, and now they want us.
**And one more thing:** Say "Black Spider" to a Redbrand and watch the colour leave his face. Wick swore he knew nothing. Wick was lying about how frightened he wasn't.
**Status:** Open. We have a seal and a title. No face, no name.

### The Wyvern Tor Marauders
**Who's asking:** Harbin Wester, Townmaster of Phandalin.
**Terms:** 120 gold pieces. *(Offered 100. Pip happened.)*
**The job:** Something has been hitting caravans near Wyvern Tor, east along the Triboar Trail. Find out what, and stop it.
**Status:** Accepted. Not started.

### Old Owl Well
**Who's asking:** Daran Edermath, the orchard keeper.
**Terms:** Boots of Striding and Springing — he's holding them for us.
**The job:** Undead have taken an old ruined watchtower in the hills northwest of town and run the prospectors off.
**Status:** Accepted. Not started.

## Unfinished Business Under Tresendar Manor

We cleared the upper level. We did not clear the manor.

- **The bugbears.** Zenvon talked one of them off through a door, and then we stacked ale barrels against it and walked away. Those barrels are still the only thing between us and whatever is behind that door.
- **The lower levels.** Wick said the dungeon goes down further, and that the deep part is *wrong* in a way he didn't have words for. He said it like a man who'd rather not have known.
- **The Nothic.** Still down there. Still hungry. We have fed it twice now. It knows things about us that we never said out loud.
- **Glasstaff answers to someone.** Wick's words: there's a higher power in Phandalin that Iarno reports to. Someone in this town is above him.

---

## Errands and Offers

### Return the Lionshield goods
Crates we pulled off the goblins belong to **Linene Graywind** at the Lionshield Coster. Still in our packs. Still not delivered.

### Halia Thornton's invitation
The owner of the Miner's Exchange sat drinking with a Redbrand lieutenant and claimed to be *shocked — shocked* to learn what he was. She then invited Zenvon to call on her at the Exchange about "future business."
She is not what she pretends to be. The invitation stands anyway.
*(We're also carrying a jade frog with gold eyes that nobody has put a price on. She would know a price.)*

### The Shrine of Luck
The acolyte of Tymora said she has work for us — after Gundren is home. Not before.

### The Dendar family
We found them in the cells: two women and a boy. Wick admitted the Redbrands "handled" them, which is what a man says instead of admitting what happened to the father. They're out. They're free. We should see them somewhere safe, and we should hear what they know.

## Our Own Business

### Veyra — Professor Orryn Voss
My mentor. He vanished here in Phandalin, chasing fragments of a black stone obelisk — something to do with minds, and memory, and *changing* people. The last thing he told me was that the pieces must never be put back together.
And my blue crystal flared when I wrote down the name **Ruxithid**.

### Pip — the shard
The Nothic said it out loud in front of everyone, so it may as well go in the book. Pip won a piece of black stone in a dice game by the Neverwinter docks and sold it to a peddler headed here. Since then: dreams of broken black stone under the ground, and a voice in them that knows his name.
He sold it to a peddler *coming to Phandalin.* It is somewhere near us.

### Maela — the cold
Her holy symbol went cold once, with no enemy in sight and nothing to explain it. She hasn't mentioned it since. I wrote it down anyway.

## Things Nobody Has Hired Us For, Which We Notice Anyway

- **Sister Garaele** is hurt, limping, and will not say by whom. Narth thinks she's keeping quiet to keep the town out of it.
- **Thel Dendar** was killed in the street in daylight and nobody stopped it.
- **"You're not what Ruxithid wants."** Whispered at the ambush by a goblin whose head was the wrong shape. Sildar has never heard the name. Neither has anyone else we've asked.
- **Pip heard a voice say "returned."** There was nobody there.
