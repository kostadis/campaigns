# Quest Log

*Kept by Pip Thistlewick, because Veyra keeps hers in a way I can't read back at speed and somebody ought to have the numbers written plain.*
*Current as of lying on our bellies in the rocks above Wyvern Tor, looking down at one bored bugbear and a cave mouth.*

---

*A word on how I keep this. Veyra writes down what things **mean**. I write down what we **agreed to**, what we **get paid**, and what we **still owe**. Between the two of us it ought to come out about right.*

---

## The Wagon

### We still have not delivered the wagon
**Who's owed:** Elmina Barthen, at Barthen's Provisions. Right here in town. Two minutes' walk.
**Terms:** **Ten gold on receipt.** Picks, shovels, lantern oil, rope.
**Where it stands:** We have been in and out of Phandalin four or five times now. We have fought a wizard, a nothic, and four bugbears. Nobody has walked the wagon down the street.
**Status:** Open. Ten gold is ten gold.

*I am aware this is the smallest thing in this book. I am writing it first on purpose. It is the only piece of paper any of us actually signed, and we have not honored it. That matters more than the ten gold does.*

---

## The Spine

### Find Gundren Rockseeker
**Who's asking:** Nobody had to ask.
**What we know:** Taken on the Triboar Trail, hauled off separate from Sildar. That is not robbery, that is a job. They want his **map to Wave Echo Cave**. Sildar says the Rockseekers were close to the lost mine and the Forge of Spells.
**Where:** **Cragmaw Castle.** Nosk the bugbear let it slip when Zenvon got him angry enough to brag.
**The change:** For a long while we had the name and no road. **Hamun Kost knows the way, and has agreed to tell us.** Terms below.
**Status:** Urgent. First real progress we have made on this since the ambush.

*Sildar said they're working on Gundren for the map. I keep coming back to what that means. The day he gives it up is the day he stops being worth feeding.*

---

## Paid Work

*Running total on Sildar's account: **250 gold, paid in full.** He has been good for every coin. Whatever else is true about the Lords' Alliance, the man pays.*

### Iarno Albrek — and the Black Spider
**Who's asking:** Sildar Hallwinter, Lords' Alliance.
**Paid so far:** 100 up front, **150 on delivery of the letter. Settled.**
**Standing bounty:** **200 gold for Iarno alive.** 100 dead. 50 if he's dead in a way that can't be walked back. Sildar wants a trial in Neverwinter. He also said, plain, that he wouldn't lose sleep over the other way.
**Where it stands:** **Found, named, wounded, lost.** Iarno is Glasstaff. We ran him down his own bolt-hole, Veyra put a firebolt in him and I put a javelin in him, and he said something to the thing in the crevasse and was gone. He is alive and he has seen all four of our faces.
**The Spider:** No name, no face. The letter proves he's real, gives orders, and wants us taken or killed and any dwarf maps handed over. Zenvon made the case that the Spider is the bigger fish and Sildar agreed — figures Iarno ran straight home to him. Two birds, one stone, if we're lucky and quick.

### Cragmaw Castle — Sildar's new offer
**Who's asking:** Sildar, over breakfast, the morning we left.
**Terms:** **500 gold** to locate the castle and drive out the band leaders in it.
**Where it stands:** **Under advisement.** That was my word and I stand by it.

*Straight arithmetic, because it's why I didn't jump at it: five hundred split four ways is a hundred and twenty-five apiece. That is real money and I am not going to pretend otherwise. It is also not full-plate money, and it is a fortified castle full of goblins that has already beaten us once in a basement. Zenvon pulled me aside and did the sum out loud before I could embarrass myself. He was right to.*

*Also worth setting down: we were going to Cragmaw Castle regardless, for Gundren, for free. Sildar is paying us five hundred gold to do the thing we already decided to do. I don't think he's being cheated — he wants it done and doesn't care who does it — but I'd be a fool not to notice which side of that trade we're on.*

*He said something nice about my sword on the way out. "A fine, noble sword, Pip." Not much to write down, but I'm writing it down.*

### Wyvern Tor — the caravan raiders
**Who's asking:** Two people, for the same job.
**Terms:** **120 gold from Townmaster Harbin Wester.** Talked up from a hundred. He did not enjoy it.
**And also:** **Hamun Kost will give us the road to Cragmaw Castle** for clearing the same camp — see below.
**What we know:** They have been hitting caravans east along the Triboar Trail. We saw the proof of it on the road: a wagon done over and left, goods scattered, nobody about. Might be these boys, might be goblins, might be Redbrands with nowhere left to sleep. The wreck doesn't say.
**Status:** **Started.** We are lying in the rocks above their camp as I write this. One bugbear sentry behind a boulder, twenty yards out from a cave mouth, and we are about fifty yards up. We have not seen inside the cave and we do not know what's in it.

*Two men are paying us for one walk. I want to be clear with myself that this is honest — Harbin wants the road safe and Hamun wants his dig left alone, and both of those get bought with the same afternoon's work. Neither of them is being told a story. It is just the first time since Neverwinter that a thing has broken our way, and I've learned to write those down and look at them twice.*

### Old Owl Well — who's disturbing the ruins
**Who's asking:** Daran Edermath, at his orchard.
**Terms:** **Boots of striding and springing.**
**The job, in his words:** Find out **whether there's a threat to Phandalin brewing out there.** Not to go kill it. He was clear on that and I want it recorded clear, because we nearly forgot it standing in front of twelve zombies.
**Where it stands:** **Done, near enough — we just haven't been back to say so.** There's a Red Wizard of Thay digging in the ruin with a dozen zombies for labor. He says he has no interest in the towns nearby and I half believe him. That is an answer. Daran is owed it.

*Daran also gave me about ten minutes on keeping your back straight and your sword sharp and your eyes up. Zenvon took notes on every word of it and the old man about glowed. I had to look at the trees. But I'll say this — the man is seventy-odd and still standing, and I have met a lot of confident young men who are not.*

### Agatha, and the silver comb
**Who's asking:** Sister Garaele, Shrine of Luck.
**Terms:** **Three potions of healing.**
**What we know:** She went to a banshee called Agatha to ask about a spellbook belonging to a mage named Bowgentle, and barely walked away — that's the limp, and she never said so till now. Agatha talks about pretty things she owned when she was alive. Garaele's given us a **jeweled silver comb** as a gift and wants us to ask her question for us.
**Where it stands:** Accepted. Not started. Garaele's word: *the banshee isn't going anywhere, but don't take too much time.*

*Two things about this one. Veyra checked her books and Bowgentle was a real man, which is more than we usually get. And Hamun offered us Agatha as his other option — he wants the name of whoever built the tower at Old Owl Well. He won't go himself. Asked why, he said straight out he doesn't want to be killed by a banshee.*

*A wizard with twelve corpses on a string is scared of her. **Whenever we do go to Conyberry, we are going with that in mind.** The road to her lair runs through those ruins anyway, so it's one trip when we take it.*

## Unfinished Business Under Tresendar Manor

*We told the town we cleared it. We cleared about half of it.*

### The three bugbears
Four of them and one sorry goblin, serving the Black Spider straight, not Glasstaff. They laughed at our red cloaks. Zenvon needled Cragmaw Castle out of Nosk and then Nosk tried to kill us for it and came near enough.

We poured seven pounds of oil down the hall and lit it. **One died in the fire. Three did not.** We shut the door up top and locked it and heard them hammering on it while we walked off.

**Where they are now: no idea.** We never went back. Nobody has laid eyes on that manor since. I'd not care to bet they're still politely waiting behind that door — and Nosk carries keys, which is the sort of thing you think of too late.

**They know the road to Cragmaw Castle.** They will not sell it to us.

### Droop
The goblin. Went over in a dead faint the second we came through the door and I never saw him after. He was still down when the oil went up.

Nobody's said his name since. I'm putting it here so it's at least written somewhere.

### Thel Dendrar
We told Mirna we'd bring her husband's body up if ever we could. **We did not look.** We went down that crevasse the same afternoon for the chest and came back up with it and never went twenty feet further.

We had the time. I'm not going to write it prettier than that.

### The thing in the crevasse
Calls itself **Ssarnak**. We have fed it three times — a tied-up man, and then the dead. It let us by every time and it kept every bargain it made, which puts it ahead of a fair number of people we've met in this town.

Still down there. Whoever comes up that tunnel next is going to meet it.

### Whatever's below
Wick said, before he died, that the place had more levels. Said he was sent down once with supplies and wouldn't go again. Said there was *"something really wrong over there that messes with your head."*

He was a scared man saying whatever he thought would keep him breathing. Doesn't make it false. Doesn't make it true either.

---

## Errands, Goods, and Standing Offers

- **The emerald necklace at Thundertree.** Mirna Dendrar told us where it is — **behind the storage shelves in her family's old herb and alchemy shop, southeast side of the ruins.** She said take it, it's ours. Nobody has to be talked into this one. I have raised it more than once and I'll raise it again.
- **The Lionshield goods.** Crates and sacks with a blue lion on them, taken back off the goblins. They belong to the Lionshield Coster, here, in this town. They have been riding in our packs for weeks. **This is somebody's stock and we are walking around with it.**
- **The jade frog.** Little statuette, gold chips for eyes, out of the bugbear cave. Nobody's ever priced it. The Miner's Exchange weighs and prices things, which is an honest reason to walk back through Halia's door.
- **Halia Thornton.** Congratulated us over the Redbrands and quit smiling the moment she heard Glasstaff got out alive instead of dead. Then asked, careful, whether we'd turned up any *correspondence* in his things. Offered a hundred gold. Said it was about protecting local trade.

  Zenvon told her the information was worth more than that. She said, *"You like to negotiate?"* and we left.

  Here's what I keep next to that. First time we saw her she was sat at a table with a Redbrand lieutenant, and then acted amazed to hear he was a Redbrand while the man was wearing the cloak. And Freda told us at the very start that the Redbrands leaned on everybody in Phandalin except Halia, and that we might ask ourselves whether they were scared of her or working for her.

  **She's not what she puts about. Worth finding out what she is — somewhere that isn't her own building.**
- **Toblen Stonehill** put us up free for a night for the Redbrands business and stood me a beer besides. Good man. He warned us off Conyberry hard, though it came out he's never set foot in it. His history was sound enough — Spellplague took it, Uthgardt raiders finished it — he's just never been.
- **Carp Alderleaf.** The boy found the tunnel that got us in. It worked. Somebody ought to tell him so, and tell him plain never to go back down it.

---

## Our Own Business

### Zenvon
He'd been carrying a piece of black stone since the cart, since the start, and told none of us. Brought it out on the road outside the manor with all of us stood there — Veyra had just got done asking me about mine. Her crystal came up brighter than I've seen it.

He still hasn't said what he thinks it all adds to. That's his to say when he's ready and I'm not going to pick at it. He's been square with me every time it counted, including over Sildar's five hundred.

### Me
I won a shard of black stone off a dice game down by the Neverwinter docks and sold it on to a peddler headed **here. To Phandalin.** After that I started dreaming about broken black stone down under the ground and hearing something say my name.

I didn't tell anybody. Didn't get the choice in the end — the thing in the crevasse reached in and pulled it out and told all four of us at once, and then a fight started, and nobody's sat down with me about it since.

I'm not sore about it. I'd just as soon somebody asked.

**Also, and separately: I want better armour.** Full plate runs into the thousands. I've said so more than once and I'll keep saying it, and the necklace at Thundertree is the nearest thing to a plan any of us has got.

### Veyra
Her Professor Orryn Voss is alive, or something near it. The crevasse thing said it to her while we were running — *"not in a place where dead is easy."* She tried to go back and ask it what that meant and Maela got both arms round her and would not turn loose.

Voss came to Phandalin reckoning there were fragments of black stone here, off something bigger, and said they must never be put back together. Her candle answers to them. It answered to Zenvon's, right in front of us.

### Maela
She hauled Veyra out of that tunnel bodily and she has kept me on my feet in a fight more than once. I owe her for both.

When we all stood outside the manor and finally said the black stone business out loud, she didn't say a word, and her face did something none of us could read. We all had a look. None of us got it.

Not writing that as an accusation. Writing it because Veyra noticed too, and two of us noticing is worth a line.

---

## Things Nobody Is Paying Us For, Which I Notice Anyway

- **"Ruxithid."** A goblin with a long wrong-shaped head said it to Sildar in the ambush — *"You're not what Ruxithid wants."* Sildar doesn't know it. We don't know it. Veyra's candle does.
- **The goblins with the long skulls.** One at the ambush. **Two more dead under the manor**, in rags, killed by something before we ever got down there. Those are not the goblins we've been fighting. Those were here first.
- **Wick said Glasstaff answered to somebody in town.** *"Orders from town." "The boss's business." "I don't think Glasstaff's the big boss."* Then he went grey at the words Black Spider and shut his mouth. Might have been buying his life with it. Didn't buy it.
- **The armoury under the manor.** Spears, swords, crossbows, bolts, a dozen spare cloaks. Far more kit than a dozen bullies need. Somebody was outfitting for something a good deal bigger than squeezing a mining town. Nobody has asked what for. I'm asking.
- **The Triboar Trail was empty.** All day, thirty miles, not one traveler, and the trail's still in use. Everything moves north and south on the High Road instead. East of Neverwinter is about the end of settled country and you can feel where the edge is.
- **Elsa's sister** is a scholar in Neverwinter and the pride of that family. Voss may have known her. Veyra's been meaning to write.

---

*Here's my arithmetic, for whatever it's worth. We came out here on a ten-gold delivery job. Since then a wizard of Thay has traded us the location of a castle for an afternoon's work, and the Lords' Alliance has offered five hundred gold for a place we were already going. I have been on the wrong end of enough easy deals to know how they generally sit.*

*I don't think we're being played. I do think that of the four of us, one sold a piece of black stone to a man riding to this town, one carried a piece of it here in his pocket, and one has a candle that lights up next to both. That's three out of four, and none of us planned it.*

*Something wanted us in Phandalin. Nobody has told us what for, and nobody is paying us to find out. I'd still like to know.*
