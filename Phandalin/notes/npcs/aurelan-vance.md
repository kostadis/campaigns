# Aurelan Vance — guest, the Moonstone Mask (RETIRED, DO NOT USE)

> **RETIRED 2026-08-18.** This draft NPC shares a name with the banker who actually appeared and signed Brewbarry's loan at the Counting House in ch47. That's an accidental collision, not the same character — the GM's ruling was to retire this concept rather than rename it. Left in place for history only; nothing below should be run at the table.

**Role:** Regular guest — retired Enclave trade magistrate
**Location:** The Moonstone Mask, Neverwinter (corner table, three nights a week)
**Faction:** None active; former Commission-adjacent
**First seen:** Not yet — drafted ahead of play

## Look
Older, precise, dressed one notch below what he could afford — money that doesn't need to announce itself. Turns his signet ring on his finger rather than pressing it into anything.

## Tic
Insists he's not conducting business, only "happening to have a conversation nearby."

## Anchor
The ring-turning — never seals anything with it, never lets it become evidence of anything.

## What he wants from any interaction
To be owed a favor rather than paid a coin — currency he trusts more than gold.

## What he would never do
Put anything in writing, or let anyone see him negotiate.

## Voice
- *"I don't do business at the Mask. I merely happen to be having a conversation near it."*
- *"Coin spends once. A favor spends whenever you like."*

## What the party knows about them
- [GM-ONLY] Not yet encountered.

- Useful lever if the party needs Commission-adjacent intel (Harbor authority, salvage writs, Margaster's paper trail) without going through Mira Tollund or an official channel.
