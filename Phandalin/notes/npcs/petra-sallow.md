# Petra Sallow — guest, the Moonstone Mask (DRAFT)

**Role:** Regular guest — minor Bluelake heiress
**Location:** The Moonstone Mask, Neverwinter
**Faction:** None; old Bluelake money, faded
**First seen:** Not yet — drafted ahead of play

## Look
Dressed extremely well for a fortune that's mostly memory now. Laughs a half-beat too loud, a half-beat too late.

## Tic
Genuinely here for the fun of it — Feywine, courtesans, the works — and doesn't notice the room has an undercurrent at all.

## Anchor
The over-loud laugh; it's how you know she's in a room before you see her.

## What she wants from any interaction
Company, gossip, a good night — nothing deeper, and that's not an act.

## What she would never do
Notice she's being used, unless someone spells it out for her directly.

## Voice
- *"Isn't this place simply the most fun?"*
- *"Oh, don't be so serious — nobody comes here to be serious."*

## What the party knows about them
- [GM-ONLY] Not yet encountered.

## Notes for the GM
- Good cover or distraction NPC — anyone using her as an unwitting decoy (Margaster, a Nasher operative, whoever) gets to hide in plain sight next to actual sincerity.
- Contrast piece against Aurelan Vance: he performs not-noticing; she genuinely doesn't.
