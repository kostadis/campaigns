# Ninsel — server, the Moonstone Mask (DRAFT)

**Role:** Front-of-house server
**Location:** The Moonstone Mask, Protector's Enclave earthmote, Neverwinter
**Faction:** House staff (Liset Cheldar's establishment)
**First seen:** Not yet — drafted ahead of play

## Look
Sheer black gown, moonstone-trimmed mask, the house amulet at her throat (mind-reading/mind-control immunity, per house canon — see `notes/neverwinter/floating_earthmotes.md`). Never removes the mask; carries herself like she already knows what you came here to forget.

## Tic
Delivers "Utmost Discretion" (the house drink, per *Puncheons and Flagons*) before a guest has said a word — greeting-as-threat, courtesy-as-control.

## Anchor
The drink is always already in your hand by the time you notice her approach — she never breaks stride to pour it in front of you.

## What she wants from any interaction
To establish, in the first ten seconds, that she already has the upper hand.

## What she would never do
Break the house's anonymity convention — even under bribery, even to save a tip.

## Voice
- *"You'll find we already know what you came here to forget."*
- *"Discretion is complimentary here. Everything else, we bill for."*

## What the party knows about them
- [GM-ONLY] Not yet encountered.

## Notes for the GM
- Good first contact for any scene staged at the Mask (Cassian/statue delivery, Cullen Sharpe's pitch) — she can seat the party, set tone, and vanish before anything sensitive is said.
- Pairs against Ondrae — Ninsel is house-loyal front-facing control; Ondrae is the crack in that control.
- Per canon (the "Beauty Mark" cocktail bit), guests are *not supposed to* be able to tell servers apart by appearance — play her distinctness through behavior only, never physical description.

## Open decisions (for the user)
- Whether Ninsel answers to Liset directly or is just senior staff.
- Whether she ever breaks the anonymity rule under extreme pressure (recommend: no, ever — that's the point of her).
