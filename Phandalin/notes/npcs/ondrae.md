# Ondrae — server, the Moonstone Mask (DRAFT)

**Role:** Front-of-house server (newer hire)
**Location:** The Moonstone Mask, Protector's Enclave earthmote, Neverwinter
**Faction:** House staff, nominally — runs a private side ledger
**First seen:** Not yet — drafted ahead of play

## Look
Same sheer black gown and moonstone mask as every server — the point is she can't be told apart from any other, until she hums.

## Tic
Hums four bars of the same tune whenever she's actually listening hard — a tell she doesn't know she has.

## Anchor
The humming. If a player ever "recognizes" a server by behavior rather than appearance, it's her.

## What she wants from any interaction
To learn something she can trade later — she treats every conversation as inventory.

## What she would never do
Sell to a buyer who'd hurt the house itself — her ledger runs on Liset's rivals, not Liset.

## Voice
- *"I only pour. What you say over the glass is yours to regret."*
- *"Everyone here already knows everyone's secret. The trick is knowing whose is worth something."*

## What the party knows about them
- [GM-ONLY] Not yet encountered.

## Notes for the GM
- Potential information broker if the party ever needs a lead inside the Mask that Liset wouldn't give them directly.
- The house amulet blocks mind-reading, not eavesdropping — she's a listener by trade, not by magic.
- Same anonymity-by-canon caveat as Ninsel — distinguish her only through behavior, not appearance.

## Open decisions (for the user)
- Who buys her information, if anyone — left unnamed (Harpers? a rival house? open).
- Whether Liset knows about the side ledger (recommend: suspects, tolerates it as long as it stays outside her own business).
