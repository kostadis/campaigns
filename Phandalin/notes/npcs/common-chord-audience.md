# The Common Chord Audience — Lathander's Death, Opening Night

**Role:** Crowd NPCs for the Common Chord concert (Protector's Enclave, Docks-facing edge)
**Location:** The Common Chord, Neverwinter
**Faction:** None. Paying customers.
**First seen:** not yet — queued for the gig billed in Chapter 48 ("Lathander's Death, Performing, Two days from now")

> Four faces for a forty-person room. Use one, use all four. None of them are load-bearing;
> each one is a handle on a payoff the chapter already set up. Throwaways — promote only if
> a player adopts one.

---

## 1. Ondrelle Kass — "the woman with the coin"

**Look.** Sixty-ish human, front table she paid too much for, sitting very straight the way people
sit when they have rehearsed something. A single copper on a bootlace around her neck, worn shiny.

**Tic.** She took Vukradin's refund. Eleven years she has felt bad about it, and she has come to
give the coin back — but only after the show, and only if it was good.

**Wants:** absolution, in the form of being allowed to pay.
**Never:** lies about the rating. If pressed she will say, kindly and ruinously, "It was a seven."

- *"You gave me my money back. I let you."*
- *"I've been carrying it. Play well and I'll put it down."*

---

## 2. Wick Ambersole — "the reservation"

**Look.** Young, brutally well-dressed, holding a slip of paper from the man with the
reservations book like it is a writ. Sweating through a very good coat.

**Tic.** He does not know who Lathander's Death are. He booked the table six weeks ago because
the Common Chord is the place to be, and he is terrified of being caught not knowing.

**Wants:** to be seen at the correct table, agreeing with the correct opinion.
**Never:** admits he came for the room and not the band.

- *"Oh, I've followed them for ages. The— the early material, obviously."*
- *"Is it— am I supposed to be standing?"*

---

## 3. Sabbatha Ren — "the Exile"

**Look.** Half-elf, thirty, dressed in deliberate ruin: good cloth cut to look confiscated,
one boot laced wrong on purpose. She is a devotee of the Exile Aesthetic and she is delighted
to be in a room with a goliath who "gets it."

**Tic.** Compliments Brewbarry on his *commitment to the look.* Means it as the highest praise
available to her.

**Wants:** to be told the exile was real, by someone it was real for.
**Never:** connects that a lifestyle for her was a sentence for him. She is not cruel. She is
just downstream of a fashion and has never once looked upstream.

- *"You wear it so honestly. Most people overdo the ruin."*
- *"Where do you have it made?"*

> GM: this is the Brewbarry ache the prep doc flags. Let it land flat. Do not let her apologize;
> she has no idea there is anything to apologize for. That's the whole knife.

---

## 4. Old Hesp — "the count"

**Look.** Ancient dwarf, back corner, same stool for two decades, one hand flat on the table
counting the house by touch. Smells of pipe and wet rope. Doesn't clap.

**Tic.** Announces the attendance figure to nobody, out loud, twice a night. He is always right,
and his number is always lower than Petra's.

**Wants:** the room to hold what the room holds.
**Never:** buys the good beer. Knows what it costs to make.

- *"Thirty-one. She'll say sixty."*
- *"I saw the boy the first time. Room was empty and he played it like it wasn't. That was the trick. Let's see if he still has it."*

---

## Notes for the GM

- **The plaque is the pressure.** Ondrelle and Hesp are the only two people in the room who
  remember the actual Common Chord. Everyone else is buying Petra's version. If Vukradin signs
  the plaque, play the signing to Hesp's corner, not to the crowd.
- **If the party works the room:** Wick will repeat, verbatim and to the wrong person, whatever
  the party tells him. He is a free rumor-injection vector with no filter and no memory of who
  said it. Very cheap, very fast, entirely deniable.
- **If the show goes badly:** Ondrelle keeps the coin. That's the only mechanical stake here —
  let it be visible, don't explain it.
- **If a player tries to refund the whole house** (Vukradin absolutely might): Petra has already
  shaken hands on the net. Hesp gives the real headcount. The math is now Petra's problem in
  front of witnesses.

## Open decisions

- Whether Ondrelle's "seven" is the true historical rating or a softened one. Chapter 48 says
  patrons who couldn't give a ten got refunded — it does not say what they actually scored it.
- Whether Sabbatha knows Brewbarry by name or only by silhouette. Name recognition makes her
  a fan; silhouette-only makes her worse, and better.
