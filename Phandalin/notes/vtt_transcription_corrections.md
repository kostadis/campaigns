## Notes for future passes — landmine rows

Rows here are applied `\bwrong\b` with `re.IGNORECASE` to *every* future
transcript. A wrong-form that is also an ordinary English word, or a common
given name, will corrupt correct text. Grep the tape for the lowercase form
before trusting any of these.

**Removed 2026-08-13 (ch48 pass) — all three had already corrupted the tape:**

| Removed row | What it did |
|---|---|
| `Bony` → **Boney** | rewrote *"I was a **bony** creature"* (cue 3725 raw). Sibling transcription confirms the adjective. `Bony Euro` and the other multi-word forms were kept. |
| `Lara` → **Lyra** | rewrote *"**Lara** Seesong Milamne returns statue from Lighthouse…"* into *Lyra* — i.e. turned the murdered noblewoman **Elara Meliamne** into a different NPC. Sibling reads the span as "Elara Seasong Meliamne." |
