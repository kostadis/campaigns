## Scene 4 — Rsolk  `[TABLE]` — **it was Rsolk himself**

Ran **early, solo, and as a fan encounter** rather than a mid-tunnel negotiation with the whole party.

**Rsolk** — leader of the Dead Rats, not a lieutenant — intercepted Vukradin, introduced himself as the tunnel
manager for that section, recognised him as the famous musician, and flipped from suspicion to fandom. Vukradin
sold **"I'm just lost"** on a **Deception 23**, signed autographs, and left with goodwill intact.

**The deal:** the party clears the spider infestation; Rsolk gets **Common Chord tickets**. Location information
already handed over.

> **That bill is still due, and it is the only real loss from this scene.** The party is walking toward Sridar

**Zeleen Varnaster / Ruling 11:** never fired — Vukradin didn't name her. **Fully unspent**, which is where the
prep wanted it (it belongs to the Commission arc at the dinner).

### Two liabilities created

## The dead drop and the Harper station  `[TABLE]`

Party followed Denvar to a **stone wall**, where he retrieved a payment bag from a **concealed two-way
compartment**, smiled, and left. Fee collected.

The wall **backs into the House of a Thousand Faces**, Bluelake District.

**The combined play:** cast **Knock** on the interior side of the compartment, then sent the rat familiar through.

**What the rat saw:** a windowless **back office** — desk, papers, and **a symbol of the Harpers**.

**DM ruling, at table:** in this campaign the Harpers are **not good people** — morally ambiguous to antagonist,
distinct from standard lore.

> **This ruling has canon support and needs no retrofit.** `notes/neverwinter/bluelake_district.md` already has
> the Bluelake chapter with *"a complicated reputation: a high-ranking Harper was caught acting as a double-agent
> between Lord Neverember and the Sons of Alagondar."*

**The tavern itself** `[MODULE]`: high-end — mirrors, mannequins, antiquated fashions, lush couches, a
well-drawing fire — and conspicuously out of place in low-end Bluelake. The party flagged it as suspicious.

**Brewbarry entered immediately and independently**, having wanted to go since his early Neverwinter days when he
couldn't afford it. **That is where the session ended.**

> **Note the geography.** The module places the Harper chapter's meeting room in the **basement**. The rat scouted
> a ground-floor back office. **The basement is unseen.**

## Lim is a Harper  `[TABLE]` — GM ruling, 2026-09-01

**This is the biggest single change in this file and it reaches backwards.**

### It contradicts the 0825 doc, and that doc was played

`20260825_bluelake_and_the_price_that_did_not_change.md` states the opposite, twice and emphatically:

> line 197 — *"**She is not the mystery's author and she genuinely does not care about it.** Pressing makes her
> less useful, which is the correct penalty for treating a friend like a source."*
>
> line 626 — *"Why doesn't Lim just tell us? → Because she does not know. … **Six years of not asking a question
> is not a conspiracy. It is a small business.**"*

**The ruling wins; the doc is what was got wrong.** Flagging it only because those lines shaped a session that has
already happened, and anyone reading the 0825 doc later will be misled by them. Worth a one-line correction at the
top of that file.

### Nothing needs retconning — every played beat re-reads cleanly

This is the good news, and it is unusually clean. Everything the 0825 doc staged as *genuine ignorance* plays
better as *tradecraft*:

| Played as | Now reads as |
|---|---|
| "She does not answer questions about herself" | Standard tradecraft |
| Lim "gives the wall" on price, supplier, owner | Not deflection from disinterest — a trained refusal |
| *"Mysteriously cheap ownerless restaurant"* (entity registry) | **Ownerless because the chapter owns it.** Subsidised front |
| Prices wrong for six years, nobody asks | A front does not need to make money |
| Her supplier was never on the Manifold and isn't Margaster's | Harper logistics, off every legible channel |
| **Soma pushes, and is "handled like a customer for the first time in six years"** | **Not indifference — a Harper refusing to compromise a friend by answering.** Far better, and much worse for Soma |

**Geography confirms it:** the Board Laid Bare and the House of a Thousand Faces are both in Bluelake. The
restaurant is a Harper front a few streets from the chapter house, and the wall drop sits between them.

### What actually changes — the ninth crate

*"She ordered eight crates and raised the ninth twice"* was, under the old reading, honest confusion.

**It cannot be that any more.** A Harper who runs the account knows what is on it. Raising the ninth twice, in
front of guests, is a deliberate act — either building deniability in front of witnesses, or **pointing Soma at
it on purpose.** Decide which; they lead to very different Lim.

### Open Decision 2 — Lim is now the obvious answer  `[OVERLAY]`

Not "the Harpers" as an abstraction. **Lim.** She runs a food-delivery account through a legitimate restaurant,
eight go up to her kitchen and one goes down, and the chit carries a client reference with no name — **which is
her account number.** Nobody questions a restaurant ordering food.

**If the party ever matches that client reference to the Board Laid Bare's supplier account, they identify her
themselves**, off evidence already in their hands.

> **The payload:** Soma's friend of six years has been keeping a man alive in a sewer, nightly, without a missed
> week, and never said a word about it. That is a much better answer than a faction, and it is Soma's scene.

### What the operation is actually for  `[TABLE]` — GM ruling, 2026-09-01

**The smuggling is how Lim gets her goods, and the Harpers run it to fight rising food prices in Neverwinter.
They see cheap food as a way to subvert Margaster and the Zhentarim.**

This is not an espionage operation with a restaurant attached. It is a **price war fought logistically**, and the
restaurant is its public argument.

- **Lim's "wrong" prices were never a mystery — they are the demonstration.** The Board Laid Bare is a Harper
  proof-of-concept: *this is what food costs when Margaster is not taking a cut.* It is propaganda you can eat,
  and the party has been eating it for six years.
- **No Commission Harbor mark** means this bypasses the Harbor authority — a Commission sub-body. So the operation
  is anti-Margaster, anti-Zhentarim **and** anti-Commission, which puts it on the campaign's main line rather than
  beside it.
- **The moral frame is exactly this campaign's register.** Per the GM's ruling the Harpers are not good people —
  and their project here is feeding the poor cheaply by breaking the law to hurt worse people. The party is
  currently working to expose it.

### Three collisions this creates, all of them already planted

**1. Brewbarry is on the wrong side of his own problem.** His import business has capital but is being crushed by
high costs on **Margaster-controlled shipping lanes** — the exact toll the Harpers exist to break. He is either
their natural recruit or their competitor, and he walked into their tavern alone at the end of the session.

**2. Vukradin is the foil the planning doc already promised.** `docs/planning.md:400` has the Harpers as
*"structurally the closest faction to Vukradin's actual politics, and the one that would most object to his
methods — a Harper who admires UBT and despises how he got it is a ready-made foil."* **They now have an engine
for it.** He fixes prices by giving money away in public; they fix prices by moving turnips in the dark. Same
goal, and they will find his method vulgar.

**3. The irony is already on the table and nobody has picked it up.** Scene 1 landed the planted line that
**the Common Chord is unaffordable now and that this is Vukradin's doing.** He is personally inflating prices at
his old venue in the same week he meets a faction whose entire project is affordability — and he has a 500 gp
performance there in two days.

> That collision cost nothing to build and is sitting fully loaded. **Do not explain it.** Let a Harper say it to
> him.

### The ninth crate — SETTLED  `[TABLE]` — GM ruling, 2026-09-01

**The Harpers are keeping Bimble alive deliberately, and it is sanctioned. They want to understand what is going
on with the Commission.**

Open Decision 2 is **closed**, exactly as the prep predicted it would go — *"gives them their debut as a rival
claimant to the witness."*

**Bimble is an asset, not a charity case.** The ninth crate is an investment in the only living expert on the
Manifold. One supply line, two purposes: eight crates fight the price war, the ninth preserves a witness. Lim's
account carries both, which is why the count was never clean and why she had to say something about it in front
of guests.

### Why they have never debriefed him — and this is the load-bearing piece

**They cannot reach him.** Six Rift Weavers sit at the junction. The Dead Rats pulled out of the lower tunnels for
exactly that reason and the Harpers are no better equipped for it.

So for seven tendays the chapter has been able to do precisely one thing: **put a crate on a ledge and let his
homunculus come up for it.** They are keeping an asset alive that they have no way of talking to, and waiting for
a way through.

> This explains the entire operation's shape — the nightly cadence, the unbroken settlement record, the fact that
> nobody has ever gone down after him, and why a faction that wants information has spent seven tendays buying
> vegetables instead of getting any.

### What that does to the spider job

**The party is about to clear the only obstacle between three separate factions and the man they all want.**

| Faction | What clearing the junction gives them | Do the party know? |
|---|---|---|
| **Dead Rats** | Their territory back — and a Harper smuggling route they've hunted for six years | They know they're clearing for Rsolk |
| **Harpers** | Their witness, finally reachable | **No** |
| **Sridar's cell** | A clear run at the man they're hunting | **No** |

**One act, three unblocks, two of them invisible to the party.** They think they are doing a favour for a wererat
in exchange for concert tickets.

### The race this creates — the strongest pressure available next session

Once the weavers are down, the tunnel is open **to everyone who has been waiting on it.** The party's only
advantage is that they will be standing in it.

> **If they clear the junction and withdraw** — to report to Rsolk, to rest, because someone is hurt — **they can
> come back to an empty room.** That is a fair, earned, entirely non-arbitrary version of "Bimble is gone," and
> unlike the dawn-sighting trigger it is a consequence of a choice rather than of the clock.
>
> **Press on and they get him.** This is the tide window doing its job a second time.

### One decision left: does Bimble know who feeds him?

**Recommendation: no.** Keep the prep as written — he does not know, and the Harpers deliberately never made
contact, because an anonymous asset cannot bargain and cannot be traced back to the chapter.

It also sharpens his existing line. *"You're not them"* becomes literal: seven tendays of crates arriving from
people who have never once spoken to him, and no way to tell whether the food means rescue or fattening. **The
Harpers kept him alive without ever comforting him**, which is precisely the reading of them the ruling asks for.

### The rhyme worth protecting

**The Harpers are wrong in exactly the way the party is wrong.** They are investigating the Commission believing
the Manifold failure is a conspiracy at the heart of the city. It is a supplier who was told no and switched the
engine off on her way out.

Both parties are hunting a villain and will find a contractor. Both are hunting a conspiracy and will find an
invoice. **Do not let a Harper be right about this** — they have better tradecraft than the party and exactly the
same wrong answer.

### Two costs this creates

- **The party may burn her.** They Knocked her drop open and put a familiar in her back office. If the chapter
  runs internal security, the leak traces to the account and the account traces to Lim. **They will have done it
  by being good at this**, which is the same shape as the Grundar arc already committed to.
- **Bellows is now a question.** The 0825 doc's best advice was *"ask Bellows, not Lim — he answers precisely what
  he is asked."* If Lim is a Harper, that is a standing vulnerability in her own operation, and the party has
  already been taught to use it. Decide whether Bellows is read in, or is the hole in her tradecraft. He
  reportedly did not know the name Bimble.

## The collision the spider job creates  `[OVERLAY]` — emergent, nobody designed this

Worth writing down before it gets lost, because it fell out of the session rather than the prep.

**The Harper smuggling route works *because* the Dead Rats abandoned the lower tunnels.** Rsolk pulled his people
out after the weavers took them — that is why nine crates a night have crossed his ground for years and he has
never been able to work out who protects them.

**The party has now been hired by Rsolk to remove the weavers.**

If they succeed, Rsolk takes his tunnels back — and lands directly on top of a Harper smuggling operation he has
spent six years failing to identify. **The party has been contracted to hand the Dead Rats the Harpers**, and
neither the party nor Rsolk knows that is what the job is.

The party is also, separately, about to be the reason the Harpers learn their drop is blown. **They are pulling
both ends of the same rope.**

> No decision needed now. But when the weavers die, decide whether Rsolk works it out — and how fast.
