# Quest Log

*Kept by Soma, because somebody has to track what we owe and who's still angry at us, and it was never going to be Vukradin.*
*Current as of the evening at the Eastern Quay, Neverwinter — the day the Counting House signed Brewbarry's loan and Cullen Sharpe cornered us on the docks.*

---

## Where We Started

Job board outside Harbin Wester's hall, first week in Phandalin. Warn some dwarves about a dragon. Bother some gnomes for anti-dragon tools. Walk a midwife home. Fifty gold here, twenty-five there. All three paid out a lifetime ago.

Vukradin took the dwarf job because it paid the most and he'd already decided plunder was theft. That argument is still going. It just has a lot more zeroes on it now.

---

## The Manifold and the Dead Noblewoman

**Who's asking:** Lord Cassian Meliamne — grieving, sincere, and not brave enough to press this himself.
**What we know:** Neverwinter runs a magic device at the docks that moves goods between here and everywhere else — the Manifold. It went dark about seven weeks back and nobody official will say why. A noblewoman named Elara Meliamne was digging into it when she died at her own festival, eight days before we reached the city. The Crown calls it an accident. Her brother doesn't believe that for one breath, and he can't say so out loud without House Margaster hearing him.
**Where/why:** The only real lead is a gnome. Older, precise, the kind who wants to know *why* something works rather than whether it does. Elara met him twice at the docks and wrote down what she noticed. Nobody's seen him since the Manifold stopped.
**Status:** Open. This is the job now, whatever else is sitting on top of it.

---

## Paid Work

**Brewbarry's loan — Counting House, Neverwinter.** Linene's letter of introduction got him in the door. A banker named Aurelan Vance signed off on the bathrobe business, Linene standing as shipping agent. Vance also promised to "make inquiries" into House Margaster by the next day. We haven't heard back.

**Mine dividend — collected.** Vukradin picked up a Neverwinter Commission letter of credit at the Counting House. Two hundred gold. Turns out it isn't our mine paying us — the Commission owns Mountain's Toe outright and we hold shares, same as anyone. Vukradin took that news the way you'd expect a man to take news about his own house.

**Mountain's Toe — still owed, not collected.** We haven't been back since the alliance with the dwarves and the wererats was struck. Zeleen still owes us that Earthstone Gem she swore was "completely taken care of." I didn't believe her the first time either.

**Dragon scale mail — paid for, not carried home.** Three hundred gold, cut down from a much bigger number because the armorer took one look at Valphine and decided the drow business had been the right call all along. Lathander's mark on the back, made for Brewbarry. He walked off with Valphine's elven chainmail instead and left the scale mail on the rack. I don't know why. Nobody's asked him twice.

---

## Unfinished Business in Neverwinter

**A forged writ, sitting in our pocket, unused.** A House Margaster man named Cullen Sharpe produced a Harbor Authority writ at the Moonstone Mask claiming salvage rights on Lord Cassian's returned statue. Both the bard and Valphine clocked it as false the moment it hit the table. We said nothing. Cassian paid the two hundred gold anyway and told himself he hadn't been swindled. We're holding a card we haven't played, and I don't love that we're the ones holding it.

**Perrin Alagondar's necklace.** We're carrying the Necklace of Fireballs for a claimant we haven't met yet — a dynasty man, wants it back for his family. He's promised three witnesses, none of them tied to House Margaster, to meet us at the Spire after the concert. Boney, of all creatures, is the one who can vouch for the lineage — spent a few hundred years shut in a tomb with nothing to do but read gravestones. Useful, in the end.

**Cullen Sharpe's gnome.** He as good as admitted a gnome was hired to "fix, or improve" the Manifold, and that it broke worse right after. He said he'd give us a name "the following day." That day came and went at the docks with no name attached. I intend to collect.

---

## Errands and Offers

**The benefit concert, the Spire of the Morninglord.** No date fixed. We're announcing the band name there — Lathander's Death, if it survives the room. Valphine means to corner Brother Aldric Sunmantle about her eyes while we're at it. Cullen Sharpe has invited himself as Margaster's man.

**Dinner with the Lord Protector.** Arranged through his steward Aldus, who is a nicer man than his employer deserves. Neverember wants to talk about how much gold we've been giving away and what we did to Phandalin's government without asking him first. Vukradin's already decided to pitch him a charity gala. I'd bring a second plan.

**Tea at Lord Cassian's residence.** His own idea — the Moonstone Mask floor was no place to speak freely about Margaster or the Manifold. Nothing scheduled yet.

**The Woodland Manse brewery.** Brewbarry promised Falcon he'd help get it running again. Future beer. He hasn't forgotten; he just hasn't gone.

---

## Our Own Business

**Brewbarry** is building something. Bathrobes — cotton out of Phandalin, blessed by Lathander's priests, his logo on the collar, a discount for anyone who buys the bard's record. He talks about thread count the way he used to talk about kills. Linene calls it his personal quest and then calls it hers too. I believe her; I've watched her mean things before.

**Valphine's** eyes went gold sometime around Icespire Hold and haven't gone back. Strangers in this city take her for a walking blessing. Ser Kaelen didn't look happy about it, which told me more than the gold did — sent her off to a priest here to get it explained. Something new showed up in her hands at the docks, too — she caught Cullen Sharpe in a lie before he finished the sentence. Nobody's put a name to that yet. Her sister is still out there somewhere, blinded, and I doubt she's forgiven the blinding.

**Vukradin** still hasn't found a name for this bale of ours. He gave up his share of a dragon's hoard on principle and is somehow poorer for it — twenty-nine gold to his name and a studio fund with five hundred and fifty in it he can't touch for himself. He's holding a necklace that isn't his and a black sword that used to be a monster, both waiting on owners nobody's found yet.

**Me.** I still don't know if my hatchlings made it. I still don't know if Meril is really gone or just somewhere I haven't looked. The staff's grown another leaf since the mountain — it does that now, when I earn it, never the other way. I said as much out loud back when I felt it in that fused dragon gold: it isn't just leftover dragon magic, it's the same shape as that stag, the same shape as those gnome-things nobody else seemed to clock. Nobody followed up. I intend to.

---

## Things Nobody Has Hired Us For, Which We Notice Anyway

- Sister Kaella walked off on her own two feet a long while back and hasn't been seen since. Gone to the wind, as the saying goes. Vukradin keeps saying "as long as she's still breathing" like it's a plan.
- There's a third one of the Carver's people we never accounted for. Xanth named him Zax. "Walks without rest, without breath, without soul." Nobody's seen him, dead or otherwise.
- Prutha took five converts and walked off to preach at Vorga's tribe, unsupervised, at the fortress where we killed a dragon. Vorga's still a friend. I'd like that to stay true.
- Jenna Roscoe beat us to this city and isn't speaking to us anymore. She got here early enough to talk to people before we did. I don't know what she told them.
- Cullen Sharpe let it slip that Ser Kaelen reached out to House Margaster on our behalf, through one of their agents. Could be the Order doing ordinary business through channels I don't love. Could be something else. Kaelen's also the one who told Vukradin, more than once, to stay clear of Margaster. Worth sitting with.
- Those two strange performers, Rimardo and the quiet one, keep turning up where they shouldn't and leaving behind more questions than tricks. Something about a sealed door under a stage. Nobody's found the stage yet.
- Boney taught himself to read noble bloodlines off headstones during however many years he spent dead in a tomb. He's better at genealogy than anyone else in this bale, which says more about us than him.

---

*The stag, the gnome-things, whatever's inside that fused gold — same shape, and I've said so once already. Next time I see it, I want to have a name for it before Vukradin turns it into a speech.*
