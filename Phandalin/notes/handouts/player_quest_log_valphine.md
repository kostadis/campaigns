# Quest Log

*Kept by Valphine Sotorra, because the others record what they felt and I record what is owed. Only one of those can be collected.*
*Current as of the morning at the Spire of the Morninglord: the day we gave the necklace away, put a name to the band, and learned who owns the Commission.*

---

## Where We Started

A notice board outside Harbin Wester's hall. Warn the dwarves about a dragon. Extract something useful from the gnomes. Walk a midwife home before something ate her. Fifty gold, twenty-five, fifty. All three paid, all three a lifetime ago.

The dragon is dead. The work did not stop; it changed hands. We no longer fight things that announce themselves by breathing on us. We negotiate with men who can ruin a town with a signature and never once raise their voice. Vukradin still speaks as though the second kind can be reasoned with. I have not yet decided whether that is naivete or the most disciplined performance I have ever watched.

---

## The Manifold, and the Woman Who Died Asking About It

**Who is asking:** Lord Cassian Meliamne. Grieving, sincere, and not brave enough to press it himself, which is why he pressed it onto us.

**What we know:** Neverwinter runs a device at the docks that moves goods across planes. It went dark about seven weeks ago. The Commission calls it a maintenance failure. Elara Meliamne was investigating when she died at her own festival, eight days before we reached the city, and the Crown ruled it an accident and instructed the city to grieve efficiently and move on.

**What we learned this morning, which is a great deal:**

- The gnome has a name. **Bimble Nackle.** Cullen Sharpe produced it, described him as fastidious, and denied knowing where he is or who employed him. He was not lying about that part, which is not the same as being honest.
- The Commission publicly declared the failure a maintenance issue and said their technician found nothing. They have never named him. The banker's phrasing was *"for all we know, he never did exist."*
- The Commission's membership is a guarded secret. **Lord Neverember is its majority holder.** We are dining with him. Nobody has scheduled it.
- **House Margaster is not a member of the Commission.** The banker was certain, and said the exclusion is a standing grievance in this city. Yet Margaster profits most from the shutdown, and says so aloud, at parties.

**My read:** a house that harvests a failure inside a body it does not belong to has a hand in that body that is not its own. Find whose arm it is wearing. Brewbarry arrived at the same place by a slower road and calls it a yarn board.

**Status:** Open, and now the only thread that matters. Everything else in this ledger is a way of paying for it.

---

## Paid Work

**Brewbarry's loan. Signed.** Linene's letter opened the door; the banker Aurelan Vance signed the paper, with Linene standing as shipping agent. The bathrobe concern has capital.

**Vance's inquiries. Partly delivered.** He promised to look into House Margaster by the next day. What he produced was the Commission's story and its membership, which is more than he meant to give and less than we asked for. He turns visibly grey at the suggestion of approaching the Commission directly. Brewbarry has since bound him into a relationship of reciprocal favours, which is the first genuinely drow thing that man has ever done.

**The mine dividend. Collected.** Two hundred gold, as a Commission letter of credit. It is worth restating what that document is: we do not own Mountain's Toe. The Commission owns it outright and we hold a share, like anyone else with money. Vukradin had been telling the whole of the North about *our mine*.

**Mountain's Toe. Still owed, still uncollected.** We have not returned since the alliance with the dwarves and the wererats. Zeleen still owes the Earthstone Gem she swore was "completely taken care of," and I did not believe her when she said it.

**Brewbarry's dragon scale mail. Paid for, still on the rack.** Three hundred gold, reduced from one thousand six hundred and seventy-five, because the armourer looked at me and concluded his prejudices had been resolved in his favour. Lathander's emblem on the back. Made for Brewbarry, who has not gone to fetch it and is wearing my elven chain instead.

**My own armour. Delivered.** Ostren Vayle of Dragon Scales R Us worked through the night and fitted the white dragon plate onto me at the temple threshold in front of the entire waiting congregation. He asked for a blessing rather than coin. I blessed him afterward, publicly, at length, where his customers could see it. He believes he was paid in grace. He was paid in advertising, and it was the better rate.

---

## Settled at the Spire

**The Alagondar necklace. Given away.** Perrin brought his three witnesses as required: a revolutionary who is sincerely thrilled about the symbol and nothing else, a genealogist who had cross-examined Boney and would stake his reputation that Perrin is the true and only claimant, and a notary clerk who is frightened of something. Soma established the clerk is not frightened of *Perrin*. The Margaster notary's genealogy had already survived my own examination the day before; the paperwork is authentic, which makes it worse rather than better. A man can be the rightful heir and still be the instrument.

Vukradin handed it over on principle and then refused to speak another word to him. Brewbarry logged his dissent for the record: *"mock my words, this is gonna bite us in the ass."* We have given seven beads of fireball to a man with allies we do not like. I record this without comment because the comment is obvious.

**The band has a name.** *Lathander's Death.* I ended the sermon with the phrase and Vukradin took it off the air in front of the congregation. Whatever else that room was, it was efficient.

**The sermon was delivered, and it carried.** Brother Aldric and a scarred colleague set a theological trap in the receiving chamber before I was permitted the pulpit. I threaded it. He welcomed me forward, made the sign of the Morninglord, and warned me away from the path of pain, which he described as not Lathander's way. A congregation of that size does not stay to hear a warning. Eight of them stayed to take the sacrament.

**Not settled, and I want it noted:** Ser Kaelen sent me to Aldric to ask what the eyes are. Aldric asked *me* what the blessing means, I answered him, and I never put my own question. I had the man in a private chamber and I spent the audience winning an argument. That was a poor allocation.

---

## Unfinished Business in Neverwinter

**Dinner with the Lord Protector.** Arranged through Aldus, who confirmed nervously that it will happen. Still no date. Neverember wishes to discuss how much gold we have been circulating and what we did to Phandalin's government without consulting him. He also approached me privately to admire my focus on eliminating enemies of the light, proposed that we could work wonders together, and walked away muttering about power and the necessity of exercising it. He is the Commission's majority holder. He is offering me a partnership. Both of these are true at once and I intend to arrive prepared for both.

**A forged writ, still unplayed.** The Harbor Authority document Cullen used to take two hundred gold from Cassian. Vukradin and I both made it as false the moment it was set down, and we said nothing. It is a knife we are holding by the blade until we choose the moment.

**Cullen Sharpe's purse.** Brewbarry took the whole of it off him in front of the congregation and dropped it in the collection basket, and Cullen was obliged to smile and call it generous. Brewbarry now intends to return it seeded with a sending stone disguised as a coin and a second coin carrying Magic Mouth, to make it a listening post. We lack the magic to disguise it and require a shop that can. Cullen assured Vukradin that *"we want the same things."*

**We have not counted the basket.** Cullen's purse, the takings from two gossips who were persuaded that their theology obliged them, and the rest of the congregation's tithe. Nobody has established the total.

**Tea at Lord Cassian's residence.** My arrangement. The Moonstone Mask floor was no place for the real conversation. Still unscheduled.

**Loviatar.** I blinded a man in their service for interrupting the sermon and framed it as the sacrament, which it was. A second promised consequences on his way out of the building. The congregation looked at them with contempt, which is pleasant and is not protection. Their goddess has a longer memory than their agents do.

---

## Errands and Offers

**The Woodland Manse brewery.** Brewbarry promised Falcon he would help restore it. He has not forgotten and has not gone.

**The frozen treasure at Icespire Hold.** The Crown's claim on it is unresolved and nobody has pressed it.

**A merchant wishes to license Soma's appearance** for a clothing line he called the SOBA line. Brewbarry immediately proposed merging the production with the bathrobes on the grounds that he completely trusts his partners. I record this so that when it goes badly the record shows how quickly it was agreed.

**Phandalin, in our absence.** Linene chairs the subcommittee drafting the rules for the treasure distribution. Eligibility was never settled. Our five percent was negotiated and has never been tested. Nothing has been paid to anyone, because the seed of the whole fund is a lump of dragon gold fused into a single mass that no one can separate. Ser Kaelen holds it. Soma read something in it that was not residual dragon magic.

---

## Our Own Business

**Vukradin** gave away a dragon's hoard and is worth twenty-nine gold. He holds a studio fund of five hundred and fifty that he has arranged so he cannot spend it on himself, a black sword that used to be a living thing and is waiting on an owner nobody has found, and a conviction that the world settles its accounts in the same currency it is paid in. This morning he handed a fireball necklace to a man he believes is evil, because the man was owed it. I have stopped calling this naivete. Naivete is not this consistent.

**Brewbarry** has become suspicious in a manner I find genuinely useful. He designed a listening device out of two coins, identified the Margaster contradiction before any of us said it aloud, and has begun purchasing bankers with favours instead of threats. He also wants a headband of intellect and says it will enhance his spycraft. Both of those men are in there.

**Soma** still does not know whether her hatchlings survived, or whether Meril is dead or merely somewhere she has not looked. The staff has grown another leaf. She blessed a stranger's garden this morning with a rite she admits she invented on the spot, and it satisfied the woman entirely, which tells you what most people want from a religion. She read the fused gold and named the shape in it. Nobody followed her. She intends to raise it again.

**Boney** taught himself the succession of the noble houses by reading gravestones during the century he spent interred, and a professional genealogist this morning described his knowledge as almost equal to his own. He is the most useful witness we have and the only one of us who is not trying to become something.

**Myself.** My eyes are gold and no one has explained them, and this morning I let the one man qualified to try spend the audience defending his own orthodoxy instead. There is something in my hands now that catches a lie while it is still being spoken; it arrived without announcement and without a name, and I have not established its limits, which is careless. My sister is alive, blind, and unavenged in her own accounting rather than mine. I have eight followers in this city, five orcs preaching my doctrine at Icespire Hold without me, and a temple in Phandalin. That is a church. Churches attract exactly two things, and I have already met the first of them today.

---

## Things Nobody Has Hired Us For, Which We Notice Anyway

- Cullen Sharpe says Ser Kaelen approached House Margaster through one of their agents on our behalf. Kaelen is also the man who has told Vukradin twice to stay clear of that house. One of those is a lie and I cannot yet tell which, because both of them are useful to somebody.
- Neverember was, by my read, particularly appalled with Cullen Sharpe. A man who owns the majority of a commission and despises the house profiting from its failure is either a victim or building a case.
- The notary clerk let slip that the Alagondar records were purged when Neverember became the first Lord of Neverwinter. Nobody in that room wanted that sentence said aloud, including him.
- Prutha took five converts to Vorga's tribe at Icespire Hold and has no supervision, no governance, and my doctrine. Vorga is still an ally.
- Sister Kaella walked away on her own feet and has not been seen. Vukradin says "as long as she is still breathing" as though it were a plan.
- The third of the Carver's lieutenants was never accounted for. Xanthopoulos named him Zax and said he walks without rest, without breath, without soul.
- Jenna Roscoe reached this city before we did and is no longer speaking to us. She had days to say whatever she wished to whomever she liked.
- Rimardo and the silent one keep appearing where they were not booked and leaving invoices. A sealed door beneath a stage. No one has found the stage.
- The banshee at the lighthouse warned of a storm gathering in the depths below Leilon. The performers said we would be a better audience in Leilon. Two unconnected sources, one destination.
- A map on a public broadsheet already draws the Lord's Alliance jurisdiction over Neverwinter Wood, Leilon, and Phandalin. Our town has been annexed on paper and has not been told.
- Aletra is somewhere with a box of poison and a grudge that predates the blinding.

---

*Every thread in this ledger terminates at a table I have been invited to sit at. The Commission's majority holder is hosting us at dinner. The house that profits from his failure is not permitted in his commission and attends our concerts anyway. The gnome has a name and no body. I do not believe in coincidence of that density; I believe in seating charts, and I intend to see this one before I am shown to my place.*
