# Quest Log

*Kept by Soma, because a long life teaches you to write down what people promise before the promise changes shape.*
*Current as of early morning at the House of a Thousand Faces, after the ninth crate walked into the sewers and Denvar collected his pay from a Harper dead drop.*

---

## We Are Here

**The House of a Thousand Faces, Bluelake District, Neverwinter.** Brewbarry has just walked into the expensive front room. My rat has already been through the wall and found the Harper office in the basement.

**Why we're here:** Denvar collected payment from this building after delivering eight crates for Lim and setting a ninth aside for a brass-and-leather construct. We need to learn who pays him, who gets that ninth crate, and whether any of it leads to Bimble.

**What we left running:** Lim's restaurant still depends on the other eight crates. She asked us not to frighten her suppliers away. Vukradin also promised Rsolk that we would deal with the wrong-colored spiders taking people in the floodline tunnels.

**Our present position:** As far as we can tell, the Harpers do not know we opened their dead drop or sent a rat into their office. We know what the room is. We do not know which side of the ninth-crate business they occupy. Those are different facts. Keep them separate.

---

## Find Bimble, and Find Who Killed Elara

### The job that brought us into this

**Who's asking:** Lord Cassian Meliamne. His sister Elara investigated the displacement manifold before she died. The Crown called her death an accident. Cassian does not believe it.

**What we know:** Bimble Nackle maintained the Commission's manifold for eleven years. His payments stopped seven tendays ago, on the day, with no severance, final accounting, or forwarding instructions. He has not banked, borrowed, or left Neverwinter since. Somebody knows where he went or has kept him alive without letting him touch his own money.

The manifold stopped at about the same time. Lionshield lost its supply line. House Margaster became the city's expensive answer to moving goods. Lord Neverember owns most of the Commission. House Margaster is not allowed into it. Everyone profits from a different part of the same failure and nobody admits to knowing why it happened.

**What the ninth crate changed:** Lim orders eight crates. The couriers bring nine. The extra one is collected by a small brass-and-leather thing and carried below. Denvar's chits show the same order for at least seven to ten days: *9 crates, 3rd hour, 8 up, 1 down.* His pay comes through a Harper dead drop. This may lead to Bimble. It may lead somewhere else. We have not earned the right to join those facts yet.

**Status:** Open. We are inside the first place that may have records.

---

## The Work Immediately in Front of Us

### The Harper office behind the wall

The basement room is windowless. Desk, papers, supplies, Harper mark. Denvar used the outside mechanism to collect his weekly settlement, then left smiling. We can enter by the front, keep scouting, or ask questions. Whatever we do, the first useful answer is who authorized his payments.

### Rsolk's floodline tunnels

Vukradin wandered into Rsolk's territory after something in the sewers convinced him that the route he wanted was too boring to follow. Rsolk's people have lost several men near a floodline tunnel over the last two months. A survivor saw something like a spider, except the colors and the webs were wrong.

Vukradin offered our help. Rsolk accepted and asked for tickets to the Common Chord. No price was settled. We have directions and a promise to keep.

### Lim's suppliers

Lim asked us to watch without ambushing anyone and without frightening away the people keeping her kitchen supplied. We did that. Eight unmarked crates still reached the restaurant.

The dead drop now gives us a way to investigate beyond her suppliers. That matters. She has a business to keep alive, and I have already pressed hard enough on an old friendship for one visit.

---

## Appointments, Debts, and Other Things With Clocks

**The Common Chord performance.** We owe Petra one exclusive show two days after our surprise visit. We take half the house, gross. Her cover musician may keep performing under the band's name so long as she tells people he is not Vukradin. The twenty-five gold for signing the plaque was paid. We raised another twenty gold for the studio while waiting for the stakeout.

**Dinner with Lord Neverember.** Aldus arranged it. No hour has been fixed. Neverember wants to discuss all the gold we gave away and the government we helped rearrange in Phandalin. We need to ask why he closed Elara's death as an accident, why his Commission's manifold failed, and how much he truly knows about the people profiting from it.

**Tea with Lord Cassian.** Still unscheduled. He offered a private room where he could speak more freely about Elara, Margaster, and the manifold.

**Cullen Sharpe's purse.** Brewbarry put the whole thing in the Spire's collection basket, then planned to return it with disguised listening magic among the coins. We still need a shop capable of making a sending stone and a *Magic Mouth* coin look ordinary. We also never counted the basket.

**Brewbarry's armor.** The dragon scale mail with Lathander's mark is paid for and still sitting at the armorer's. Three hundred gold. He is wearing Valphine's elven chain instead. Apparently this is easier than walking back to the shop.

**Linene's shipments.** Lionshield still is not receiving goods. Brewbarry carries Linene's written request for help and has refused to move the business to House Margaster. Finding out what happened to the manifold is the useful form of helping her.

**The Woodland Manse brewery.** Brewbarry told Falcon he would help restore it. Future beer remains persuasive. The brewery remains broken.

---

## Money That Is Ours, Owed, or Stuck to Something

**Mountain's Toe.** The Commission owns the mine. We own shares. That distinction arrived after Vukradin had already told most of the North it was our mine. We collected a two-hundred-gold dividend in Neverwinter, but we have not returned to the mine. Zeleen still owes us the Earthstone Gem she said was completely handled.

**The studio.** The restricted fund had five hundred and fifty gold before tonight. Vukradin raised twenty more from patrons at the Common Chord. Petra's concert share has not been earned yet.

**Phandalin's treasure fund.** Linene's committee is still deciding who qualifies. Our five percent has never been tested. Nobody has been paid because Cryovain's gold is fused into one lump under Ser Kaelen's care, and the Crown's claim on it was never settled. I read something planar in it—the same sort of wrongness as the stag and the little gnome-shaped things. I have said this before. I am writing it again because saying a useful thing near three loud people is not the same as being heard.

---

## Our Own Business

### Lim and me

Lim and I used to cook together. She still knew what food to bring before I ordered it. Then Brewbarry pushed until she told us about the sewer route, and I told her she could have raised her prices rather than trust people who might demand more later.

She said I had changed. She remembered a different Soma.

I told her the only constant in life is change. True things are not always kind things. I have lived a full tortle life. I know friends can grow in different directions and still remember the same kitchen. Knowing that has not made the walk from her restaurant any lighter.

We kept our word during the stakeout. Her eight crates reached her. The next part is finding an answer that does not cost her the place she built. That is what I can do now.

### The fame

Old Hesp remembers Vukradin playing to empty rooms. Now the Common Chord has a six-week wait, charges enough to keep its old patrons outside, and has put a plaque on Brewbarry's table. Strangers dress like Vukradin. People give us money when he names donation tiers. Men guarding a sewer tunnel went from deciding what to do with an intruder to asking for autographs and escorting him home.

Our faces, armor, horse, music, and my staff announce us before we speak. We used that to enter places that once kept Brewbarry outside. It also means everyone sees us coming. Vukradin has said aloud what the rest of us have started to notice: somebody is making us more famous, too quickly and too thoroughly, and we do not know who or why.

Healthy things take time. This did not.

### The rest of my bale

**Vukradin** has a studio fund, twenty-nine gold for himself, and a city full of people who know his songs. He still carries the black sword that used to be the Carver. We have not found its rightful owner. Rimardo promised that if Vukradin kept at the music, they would perform together at the very end. I do not know what *the very end* means, and I dislike promises with horizons instead of dates.

**Valphine's** eyes are still gold. She went to Brother Aldric to ask what that meant, won a theological argument instead, and left without asking. She can now catch some lies as they are spoken. Eight people in Neverwinter follow her teaching. Prutha and five more are preaching it to Vorga's tribe at Icespire Hold without her. Her sister Aletra remains alive, blind, and somewhere ahead of us.

**Brewbarry** has a loan, a shipping partner, a bathrobe business, a possible clothing arrangement using my face, no collected armor, and a headband of intellect on his shopping list because he believes it will improve the spycraft. He also frightened Lim. He noticed. That is at least a beginning.

**Boney** proved Perrin's bloodline from the gravestones he read while shut in a tomb. The Necklace of Fireballs has been handed to Perrin in front of three witnesses. That business is closed, even if Brewbarry is probably right that it will bite us later.

**Me.** I still do not know whether my hatchlings survived. I still do not know whether Meril is dead or simply somewhere I have not looked. His staff has gained another leaf. Power is borrowed. Friends are not possessions. Both can still become heavy in the hand.

## Things Nobody Hired Us to Notice

- The Harpers paid Denvar. We do not yet know whether they ordered the ninth crate, received it, or merely moved money for whoever did.
- Three weeks ago, people dressed as Many-Starred Cloaks associates entered the Cloak Tower by the basement and came out with nothing. Lim said the Tower District had become quieter. Quieter is different from safer.
- Something in the sewers can make a chosen road seem too dull to continue. It stopped Vukradin from following the construct.
- House Margaster is excluded from the Commission and profits most from the Commission's failure. Lord Neverember owns most of the Commission and seemed to dislike Cullen Sharpe. Those facts want an explanation.
- Ser Kaelen warned Vukradin away from House Margaster. Cullen says Kaelen contacted the house through one of its agents on our behalf.
- The Harbor Authority writ Cullen used against Cassian was forged. We recognized it and said nothing. We still hold that knowledge.
- Neverember's records of the Alagondars were purged when he became the first Lord of Neverwinter. The notary clerk who told us was afraid, but not of Perrin.
- A public map already places Phandalin, Leilon, and Neverwinter Wood under Lord's Alliance authority. Phandalin has not been told it was annexed on paper.
- Jenna Roscoe reached Neverwinter before us and stopped speaking to us. She had time to tell the city whatever version of us suited her.
- Sister Kaella is still gone. Zax, the Carver's third lieutenant, is still unaccounted for.
- The banshee at the lighthouse warned of a storm below Leilon. Rimardo and Corrin said we would be a better audience there. They also keep mentioning a sealed door beneath a stage.
- A woman in Bluelake locked a door she had already locked. Small things count. Large things are usually made of them.

---

*I have been old, obscure, and mostly left alone. Now strangers know my staff and merchants want my face on clothes. Lim knew me before any of that. Perhaps that is why her saying I changed landed where all the cheering did not.*
