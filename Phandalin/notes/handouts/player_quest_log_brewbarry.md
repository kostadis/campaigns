# Quest Log

*Kept by Brewbarry, because I run a business now and a business is a list of who owes who. Soma writes what she feels. Valphine writes what is owed. I write what we do next.*
*Current as of the night at the Common Chord, with the stakeout still ahead of us at the third hour after midnight.*

---

## Where We Started

A board outside Harbin Wester's hall. Warn the dwarves. Get tools off the gnomes. Walk the midwife home. Fifty gold, twenty-five, fifty. All paid, long ago.

Before that I was one of eleven people in a room at the Common Chord listening to Vukradin play. He refunded the other ten because they would not rate the show a ten out of ten. He kept my money. That is how all of this started.

We went back to that room tonight. There is a plaque on the table now saying he created his music there. It was my table. I sat there. The plaque does not mention that and I do not need it to.

---

## The Gnome

**Who wants it:** Lord Cassian Meliamne. His sister Elara asked questions about the manifold and then died at her own festival. He is not brave enough to press it himself, so he pressed it onto us.

**What we know:**

- His name is Bimble. Ori wrote it on paper and showed it to us. I keep calling him Bimbo. So does everyone.
- Eleven years on the Commission's maintenance roll. Payments stopped seven tendays ago. On the day, not the tenday. No severance. No final reconciliation. No forwarding instructions.
- Ori says that is not how a contract ends. That is how a contract is abandoned. He was genuinely angry about it. Bankers get angry about paperwork.
- Bimble has not banked, has not borrowed, and has not left the city. So somebody is keeping him.
- Cullen Sharpe gave us the name and said he does not know where the gnome is or who hired him. Valphine says that part was not a lie. Not lying is not the same as honest.

**What we do next:** Find who feeds him. Ori said find who feeds a man and you have found the man. I said he sounds like he is feeding worms. I would like to be wrong.

**Status:** This is the job. Everything else in here pays for it.

---

## Tonight

The Board Laid Bare takes nine crates a night. It uses eight. Bellows raised it twice and nobody ever answered him, so he stopped asking.

The crates come at the third hour after midnight, sometimes the fourth. Rear door, service lane, aqueduct steps. Two men, sometimes three, never the same two. Six or seven of them rotating over the last thirty days. Wooden, rope-bound, wet on the underside.

The ninth crate is set down outside. It is gone by morning. It is not billed to the restaurant and Lim never ordered it.

Somebody is eating the ninth crate. Vukradin said it out loud and I think he is right.

**The plan:** Soma turns into something small, or sends a rat. We do not get seen. We do not grab anybody. If tonight gives us nothing we do it again tomorrow night.

**What I promised:** Lim asked us not to scare them off and not to ambush them. She has a business to run. We watch. That is all we do.

Dinner with Neverember can wait a night. So can tea with Cassian.

---

## Money

**The loan. Signed.** Linene's letter got me in the door. Ori signed the paper with Linene standing as shipping agent. The bathrobes have capital.

**Ori.** Aurelan Vance, of the Counting House. He is my friend now. The others say I recruited him with an intimidation check. I was making friends. He sat up the whole night after Valphine's sermon reading a missing man's paperwork for us, and then ran across the city out of breath to hand it over. Nobody paid him to do that. He calls me Brew. I call him Ori.

**Lionshield.** Ori told me to move my shipping to House Margaster for the better margin. I said no. Sorry for being loyal. They're only loyal to money. I'm not. Linene's store still is not receiving, and I am still carrying her written request for help with supplies, and I have not helped her yet. That one is mine and I have not done it.

**The mine dividend. Collected.** Two hundred gold as a Commission letter of credit. We do not own Mountain's Toe. The Commission owns it outright and we hold a share like anyone with coin. Vukradin had been calling it our mine to the entire North.

**Mountain's Toe. Still owed.** We have not been back since the deal with the dwarves and the wererats. Zeleen still owes us the Earthstone Gem she swore was completely taken care of.

**My scale mail. Paid for, not fetched.** Three hundred gold, cut down from sixteen hundred and seventy-five. Lathander's mark on the back. Made for me. It is still on the rack at the armorer in the Protector's Enclave. I wore Valphine's elven chain into Margaster's warehouse instead. I keep not going to get it.

**The Common Chord. Settled tonight.** Petra was booking a man to play Vukradin's songs under our band name every night. She always disclosed it was not him and she made money off him anyway. Five hundred gold was named as the value of what she took. She refused it twice and never paid a copper of it. It is squared instead by one exclusive show in two nights, and she keeps the cover act forever so long as she keeps saying out loud it is not him.
On top of that we take half the house on the night of the concert. Gross. Never net. She shook on it before anyone could think about it again.
Then she paid twenty-five gold on the spot for Vukradin to sign his own plaque. She asked me what I had done to him. I did not answer that.

**The basket at the Spire. Uncounted.** Cullen's whole purse, the two gossips who were persuaded that their theology obliged them, and the congregation's tithe. Nobody has added it up.

---

## People

**Lim.** Tortle. Runs the Board Laid Bare. Cooks better than anyone I have eaten from. Soma's friend for years. Her prices never moved while the rest of the city doubled, and that is because her supplier was never on the manifold in the first place.
I went into her kitchen with a halberd in one arm and the dragon sword in the other and a story about needing a delivery partner. The story did not work. Then I pushed. She broke because she likes Soma, not because of me.
She was frightened of me by the end. Soma said watching it made her uncomfortable. I said I was probing. And yet.
Lim says the suppliers found her at the market, she pays them regular, and they believe in fair trade and organic goods and not being trapped by Neverember and the Commission. That does not sound like Margaster. That sounds like people with a cause.
She says she orders eight crates and knows nothing about a ninth. I believe her.

**Bellows.** Warforged. Works the door. Counts everything. Told Soma she had been gone seventy-three days without being asked. Valphine looked at him and said he cannot lie.
He offered to answer anything if we asked properly. When they arrive, how they arrive, what the crates look like, are they stamped, how many, what is in them. Nobody in this city answers a question that way. He is the best witness we have and he does not know he is one.

**Cullen Sharpe.** Margaster's man in the green suit. Took two hundred gold off Lord Cassian with a Harbor Authority writ that Vukradin and Valphine both read as forged the moment it hit the table. We said nothing. We still have it. He told Vukradin we want the same things.
I took his whole purse off him in front of the congregation and dropped it in the collection basket, and he had to smile and call it generous. I mean to hand it back with a sending stone in it made to look like a coin, and a second coin carrying Magic Mouth. We do not have the magic to disguise them. **I need to find a shop that can.**

**Petra.** Gave Vukradin his first gig when nobody else in the city would put him on a stage. She reminded us of that tonight and Soma agreed she had earned the reminder. It counted.

**Ser Kaelen.** Vukradin says Kaelen told him to stay away from House Margaster. Cullen says Kaelen went to House Margaster through one of their agents about connecting our finds to their real owners. Both of those cannot be the same man doing the same thing. Kaelen also sent Valphine to a priest here to find out what her eyes are, and she never got round to asking him.

**Lord Neverember.** Majority holder of the Commission. Dinner arranged through Aldus, still no date. Valphine says he took her aside at the Spire, told her they could work wonders together, and walked off talking about power and the need to use it. There is now a rumor in the street that he has taken a drow advisor. Valphine went quiet at that.
I wanted the band to sing about him being evil. Vukradin said he is our ally against Margaster and the enemy of our enemy is our friend. Fine. Margaster first.

---

## The Band

**Lathander's Death.** Valphine ended a sermon with the phrase and Vukradin took it off the air in front of the whole congregation.

**A show in two nights at the Common Chord.** Exclusive. We found out we were booked by reading a bill nailed to a wall.

**Tonight's teaser.** One song, at Petra's insistence. Valphine rolled badly and it came out screeching and off-cue, and the room went up, because that is exactly what Vukradin's early stuff sounded like. Very avant-garde.

**The second song.** About House Margaster, and the trade routes, and who is paying for it. The whole room picked it up and hummed it out the door. Everyone who left that tavern tonight thinks House Margaster is the most evil organization in this city. That was the entire point and it worked.

**The soup song.** I wrote a ballad about Lim's turtle soup on a napkin at lunch. Vukradin said it is not our style. Valphine ruled it a marketing jingle. Soma told them to move on without me. I still think it is a good song.

**Merchandise.** I have none. The only merchandise I own is what I am wearing. I took pre-orders anyway.

---

## Our Own Business

**Vukradin.** Twenty-nine gold to his name. Gave away a dragon's hoard on principle. Gave a necklace of seven fireballs to a man he thinks is evil because the man was owed it, and then would not speak to him again. I said at the time it was going to bite us and I am writing it down here so it is on the record twice. He is holding a black sword that used to be alive with no owner found for it.
I like his music. I like him. That is all of it.

**Valphine.** Her eyes went gold at Icespire Hold and never went back and nobody has explained them. She had white dragon plate fitted at the temple door in front of the whole waiting congregation and paid the armorer in a public blessing instead of coin, which he thought was the better deal and was wrong. She blinded a Loviatar man for interrupting her sermon and a second one promised consequences on his way out of the building. Eight followers here, five orcs preaching at Icespire Hold with nobody watching them, a temple in Phandalin. Her sister Aletra is alive, blind, and out there somewhere.

**Soma.** Still does not know whether her hatchlings lived. Still does not know if Meril is dead or somewhere she has not looked yet. The staff has grown another leaf. She read the fused dragon gold and said what is in it is not leftover dragon magic, and that it is the same shape as the stag and the same shape as those gnome-things nobody else clocked. Nobody followed her on it. She means to raise it again and somebody should listen this time.
Tonight her oldest friend told her she has changed. She said the only constant in life is change, and then she did not eat much.

**Boney.** Learned the succession of every noble house in the North off gravestones during the century he was shut in a tomb. A professional genealogist said Boney's knowledge is almost equal to his own. He is still in Lady Alagondar's tackle. A man stopped us in the Enclave today certain he had seen him before. He had. That morning. Outside the sermon.

**Me.** Bathrobes. Phandalin cotton, blessed by Lathander's priests, my mark on the collar, discount for anyone who bought the record. Linene calls it my personal quest and then calls it hers as well.
There is a merchant who wants to license Soma's face for a clothing line he calls the SOBA line. I said we should merge the production with the bathrobes because I completely trust my partners.
I want a headband of intellect. It will help the spycraft.
I owe Falcon a working brewery at the Woodland Manse. I have not forgotten it. I have not gone.
I still have not collected my armor.

## Things On The Board With No String Yet

- Three weeks ago, people dressed as Many-Starred Cloaks associates went into the Cloak Tower by the basement stairs, spent the whole day in there, and came out with nothing. Lim says the Tower District has gone quiet. Not safer. Quieter is different.
- A woman in the Blue Lake District locked a door she had already locked.
- Rents have doubled. People are shouting about it in the street and they are correct.
- A rumor that Lord Neverember has taken a drow advisor.
- The Alagondar records were purged when Neverember became the first Lord of Neverwinter. The notary clerk let that out and did not want to have said it. He was frightened of something, and Soma established it was not Perrin.
- House Margaster is not permitted in the Commission, makes the most money out of the Commission failing, and says so out loud at parties.
- Where there are sewers there are rats. Where there are rats there are wererats. Wererats know each other, the ones at Mountain's Toe may have come from here, and Zeleen already owes us. That is a door nobody has knocked on.
- The broadsheet map already draws Lord's Alliance jurisdiction over Neverwinter Wood, Leilon, and Phandalin. Our town has been annexed on paper and has not been told.
- The banshee at the lighthouse warned of a storm gathering below Leilon. Rimardo and the quiet one said we would be a better audience in Leilon. Two things pointing the same direction.
- Rimardo and the quiet one keep turning up where they were not booked. Something about a sealed door under a stage. Nobody has found the stage.
- Sister Kaella walked off on her own two feet and has not been seen since. Vukradin keeps saying as long as she is still breathing like it is a plan.
- The third of the Carver's people was never accounted for. Xanth called him Zax. Walks without rest, without breath, without soul.
- Prutha took five converts to Vorga's tribe at Icespire Hold with no supervision at all. Vorga is still a friend and I want that to stay true.
- Jenna Roscoe reached this city before we did and has stopped speaking to us. She had days to say whatever she liked to whoever she liked.
- The frozen treasure at Icespire Hold. The Crown's claim is unresolved and nobody has pushed it.
- Phandalin's treasure fund is a lump of dragon gold fused into one mass that nobody can separate. Linene chairs the committee. Eligibility was never settled. Our five percent has never been tested. Nothing has been paid to anybody.

*Nine crates go down that lane. Eight go inside. One walks away on its own. Somebody stopped paying that gnome on purpose and somebody is feeding him on purpose, and either that is the same person or it is two, and I find out which one tonight. I am a barbarian, not a thief. I do not work in the shadows and I let my enemy know that I am there. And yet tonight I go quiet, because Lim asked me to and because Soma is already unhappy with me.*
