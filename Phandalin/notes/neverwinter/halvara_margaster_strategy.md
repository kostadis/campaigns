# Halvara Margaster — Strategy Doc

GM-only working doc. Created 2026-08-19. Sits above Cullen Sharpe; **she is the one person in House Margaster who knows there are illithids inside the Neverwinter Commission** (`the_commission.md` Ruling 9).

> **Knowledge boundary — read before running her.** Per `docs/Margaster.md`, the cambion connection to House Margaster is **player knowledge that no character has**, and the rule extends to *scanning for it in the negative*. **Halvara is not fiend-blooded.** She is a mortal who bought a fact. Nothing in this doc requires the cambion reveal, and running her does not spend it.

---

## The Frame (Halvara from inside her own head)

She is not the villain of this. She is the only person in Neverwinter who can see, and the cost of seeing was a contract she has not finished paying.

The Commission is occupied. Not corrupt — occupied. She worked it out the way anyone sensible would: personnel who cannot be bribed, which is impossible; documents in a single trained hand, which is merely odd; and decisions that no living person will admit to making, which is the tell. Every institution has someone who decided. The Commission has an empty chair where that person should be, and Halvara has spent four years staring into it. Then she stopped guessing and **bought the answer** from something that would sell it, and now she knows, and she cannot say it aloud in Waterdeep without ending her house.

So she is doing the only thing a Margaster can do about a tenant: **she is evicting it.** Not burning the building — you do not exorcise a cathedral by burning it, you establish that the occupant has no lease. Every licence, salvage right, harbour claim and hinterland charter that moves from Commission paper to Margaster paper is one more room the thing at the centre no longer holds. Starve it of function and it has nothing left to sit inside. That this also makes House Margaster the richest concern on the Sword Coast is not the point, but she is not embarrassed by it either. Asmodeus does not ask you to be poor.

What she fears, at three in the morning: **that she paid for a lie.** A bought fact cannot be checked, because the only source is the seller. She has never seen an illithid. She has seen an absence and built a certainty on it. If she is wrong, she has spent four years, a great deal of money and one noblewoman's life dismantling an ordinary bank.

---

## Her Current Position

- **In Neverwinter, in the Margaster compound.** Not hiding, not public. She receives.
- **Cullen Sharpe reports to her and knows none of it.** He believes he runs a distribution takeover against a stubborn bureaucracy, because that is what she told him, and he is very good at it. She intends him to go on believing it. His ignorance is not an oversight; it is the reason he survives interrogation.
- **Her window is the dark Manifold, and it is the only reason any of this is possible.** Before it failed, the Commission could not be attacked — you cannot bribe a Factor, blackmail a thrall, or seduce an office. The vacuum gave her a function the Commission could no longer perform, and function is the only surface that takes a lien.
- **The window closes the moment planar routing comes back.**
- **She ordered Elara Meliamne killed.** Elara had pulled the original Manifold contract out of the Harbor authority records and was weeks from publishing the existence of the thing Halvara is evicting. Cullen did not order it, does not know why it happened, and his denial to the party was **honest** — which is exactly why it did not sound like one.
- **Live, from ch48:** Aurelan Vance promised inquiries into House Margaster *by the next day*. Halvara will hear about that within hours and will not mind. And **the party has resolved to come to her house and intimidate her.** She is expecting someone. She does not yet know it is them.

## Her Secret

Two layers, and the second is the one that matters.

**One — the knowledge is bought, not discovered.** She did not deduce the illithid presence. She purchased it, under contract, from something infernal that was willing to sell.

**Two — the price was not money. She owes a name.** The contract obliges her to deliver **the name of the thing at the centre of the Commission**, once she has it. She does not know what the buyer wants it for and has been careful not to ask.

This is the lever, and it turns the party's own investigation into a weapon against them. **If they identify Xeltharuun and Halvara learns it, her debt discharges itself** — and something in the Nine Hells acquires the true name of a mind flayer running a city. She has begun quietly *avoiding knowing*, which is an extraordinary thing for a woman in her position and is the first crack in her.

## The Optimal Play (For Her)

1. **Meet the party as the honest one in the room.** She is the only person in Neverwinter who will tell them the truth about the Commission, and she can afford to, because it is true. Everything she says checks out. Let them verify it. Verified truth from an enemy is worth more than reassurance from a friend.
2. **Do not defend House Margaster. Concede it.** The forged writ, the shakedown, the racket — admit all of it in the first five minutes, flatly, and watch the party lose their prepared attack. She is not there to win the argument about the writ. She is there to change the subject to the building.
3. **Point them.** They are violent, well-connected and already aimed at her. Redirect: the Commission killed Elara's investigation, the Commission owns their mine, the Commission has been taking a cut of everything they earn since Phandalin. All true. **Let them do the demolition.**
4. **Accelerate the transfer while the Manifold stays dark.** Priority order: the **Harbor authority** (the choke point, and the last thing still earning), then licensing, then the hinterland charters. Salvage adjudication is already hers.
5. **Use Soman Galt for anything needing an administrative signature.** He is the one lever in the Commission that takes money.
6. **Kill the restoration.** If anyone can bring planar routing back, her window shuts and the eviction stalls with the thing still tenanted. **This makes the vanished gnome technician the single most dangerous loose object in the city** — and the party is looking for him.
7. **Pay the debt as late as possible, and avoid learning the name.**

---

## The Optimal Play (Against Her)

1. **Find Bimble Nackle and restore the Manifold.** It ends the distribution monopoly, closes her window, and costs her more than any violence could. The party is already on this thread and has no idea it is the kill shot.
2. **Make her prove it.** She has no evidence a mortal can check. Force the claim into public and she is a noblewoman announcing that monsters run the bank.
3. **Find the contract.** She signed something, and Asmodean instruments are physical, filed and enforceable. The party has now identified two forgeries on sight; reading a contract is a thing they demonstrably do. **The contract names the debt**, and the debt is the only thing she is actually afraid of.
4. **Do not kill her.** Her death does not void the contract, it transfers it — and the collector arrives on schedule regardless, now with nobody in Neverwinter who understands what is being collected.
5. **Take Cullen alive and keep him talking.** He is a truthful and cooperative witness to everything commercial, and he cannot be broken on the rest because he does not know it. He is the best evidence they will get that the house has a second floor.

---

## Negotiation Stances

- **Concedes:** money, the forged writs, the distribution business, Cullen himself. **And Elara** — she will admit the killing, coldly and without excuse, if admitting it buys her the larger argument. She regards the concealment as the crime, not the death.
- **Trades:** everything she knows about the Commission, in full and in detail, **for the party's violence pointed at it.** She will fund them. She will open her books. She does not require them to like her.
- **Never gives up:** **the eviction**, and **who she owes.** Push on the second and the conversation ends — politely, immediately, and she will never grant another audience.

---

## Contingencies

- **If the party comes to intimidate (the ch48 resolution):** she meets them personally and at once, unarmed, and offers tea. Being unafraid *is* her counter to intimidation; there is nothing for Brewbarry to lean on. She will have read them beforehand — she knows about the mine, the dividend, and Vukradin's proclamation.
- **If Vance's inquiries land:** she welcomes the audit. An audit of House Margaster finds a distribution business with excellent margins.
- **If the party finds Bimble Nackle:** she moves to have him killed and lets it look like the Commission — **and she will be believed**, because it is precisely what the Commission would do. This is her cruellest available move and the one most likely to break the party's read of the city.
- **If they produce the contract:** genuine fear, for the first and only time. She will bargain hard, and this is where she becomes an asset rather than an antagonist.
- **If cornered with no exit:** she tells the truth. All of it, to anyone present, in daylight. It is the most dangerous thing she can do to everyone else in the room and she knows it.

## Voice samples

- *"I did not discover it. I bought it. There is a difference, and the difference is the price."*
- *"You came here about a forged writ. Yes. We forged it. Sit down — I would like to talk to you about something that matters."*
- *"Cullen believes he sells shipping. He is very good at selling shipping. I would prefer he go on believing that, and so would you, because a man who knows nothing cannot be made to say anything."*
- *"You are hunting a murderer. I am evicting a tenant. I think we are further along the same corridor than you would like."*
- *(cold, on Elara)* *"She was going to publish. I have never pretended otherwise to myself, and I will not start for you."*

**Tic:** she talks about property. Tenancy, leases, occupancy, premises, vacating. Never "demon," never "devil," never "possession" — those are words for people who have not read the paperwork. Under real pressure her speech goes contractual: *the party of the second part*, *absent performance*, *on the instrument as written*.

---

## What she would never say

She would never say *"trust me."* Trust is the thing people reach for when they have no instrument, and she regards it roughly the way a surgeon regards prayer — not contemptible, simply not load-bearing. She would never claim to be good, never apologise for Elara, and never speak a word about her own blood or her house's; the first two because she does not feel them, the third because she genuinely has nothing to say. And she will not say the word *Asmodeus* in front of strangers. It is not a secret. It is a sacrament, and you do not discuss it with the tradesmen.
