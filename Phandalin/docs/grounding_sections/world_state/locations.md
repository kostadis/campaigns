## Location Dossiers

### Phandalin *(current base of operations)*
Frontier town in the Sword Mountains foothills; four to five dozen log buildings among older stone ruins (broken walls beneath sheds/gardens indicate the settlement was once larger). **Wholly undefended** — no gate, ditch, palisade, wall, garrison, river, or natural water source; open approaches from the road. No reliable deliveries. Residents go armed. Led by a **Townmaster**. Lord Neverember has turned his attention here, wanting the area cleared of monsters, cults, and evildoers to encourage settlement.

### Stonehill Inn
A large log roadhouse near the center of Phandalin — common room below, guest rooms above; meals and lodging. Innkeeper is **Toblen**, who has warned the party of **orc tribes coming from the north**. Soma last headed here to spend the night. *(Referred to inconsistently as "Stonehill Tavern" in ch06 — treat as the same establishment.)*

### Townmaster's Hall
Seat of local government, a couple of doors from the Stonehill Inn. Currently **boarded shut and locked** — anomalous for a government seat. Associated with **Harbin Wester** ("Harbin Wester's hall"). A **quest/job board** outside lists work for armed strangers and describes the town's problems.

### Lionshield Coster
Mercantile store in Phandalin, easily spotted sign, across the street from the shops. **Operating but struggling** — gaps on shelves, crates not arriving; inventory falls short of its name. Once a large business.

### Umbrage Hill
Hill site with a windmill, cairns, and a ruined building. Home to **Adabra** (midwife). Posted as a quest-board notice. The party travelled there to **rescue Adabra**.

### Gnomengarde
Enclave of **rock gnomes**; intact and reachable (journey there was uneventful). Posted on the Phandalin quest board and offered by **Harbin** as a rock-gnome quest; earlier favored by Valphine. No interior events witnessed yet.

### River District
A river that serves as the **route to the gnomish hold**; located/found by Soma.

### Triboar
Settlement that hosts plays — a performance there featured an actor always dressed as a demon named **Bob**.

### Menzoberranzan
Culturally rigid drow-associated city-state. Customs: negotiating **without a visible blade is an insult** (showing a blade is ritual etiquette); **ogres are normally kept as slaves**. Valphine has firsthand knowledge of these customs.

### Neverwinter
Major, resilient city of the North (survived dragons, giants, Uthgardt wars). **South gate stands open**; the party departed through it.

### High Road

### Triboar Trail

### Sword Coast
