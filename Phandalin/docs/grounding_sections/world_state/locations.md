## 2. LOCATIONS

### Phandalin
- **Type:** Mining town / burgeoning settlement
- **Location:** Nestled in the snow-capped Sword Mountains, in hilly terrain south of the expedition route.
- **Current State:** Active, growing settlement. The party arrived here.
- **Layout & Defenses:**
  - Forty to fifty simple log buildings; crumbling stone ruins surround newer houses and shops.
  - No walls or garrison; most adults keep weapons within easy reach.
- **Significance / Patronage:** Lord Neverember takes particular interest in Phandalin, wanting the area cleared of monsters, cults, and evildoers to encourage settlement and growth.

### Stonehill Inn
- A modest inn/tavern at the center of Phandalin, offering lodging and meals.
- Operational and in use as of Chapter 4.
- Soma was last seen heading there to spend the night.
- Townmaster Harbin referred to it as the "Stonehill Tavern" and mentioned a musician who played there.

### Townmaster's Hall
- Sturdy stone building with pitched wooden roof and bell tower at the back.
- Front door is locked.
- A job board for adventurers is posted outside, a couple of doors down from the inn.
- Vukradin banged on the locked front door (Chapter 2).

### Sword Mountains
- Snow-capped, rugged mountain range.
- Phandalin is nestled at its foot; Gnomengarde's subterranean halls are located deep within its peaks.

### Gnomengarde
- **Type:** Small network of caves / subterranean halls; gnomish outpost (hold).
- **Location:** In the Sword Mountains, southeast of Phandalin, reached through broken hilly terrain.
- **Access route:** A river leads from the mountains and ends at a waterfall whose erosion created a natural concavity by the gnomish hold.
- **Known population:** Reclusive rock gnome clan resides there (Chapter 2). Current status unconfirmed.

### Umbrage Hill
- The party traveled here to rescue Adabra (Chapter 4).
- Current condition and state unconfirmed.

### Triboar
- **Current state:** Razed.
- **Notable event:** A play was held featuring a main actor who was always dressed in character as a Demon, with the ridiculous name "Bob."
- A tavern keeper suggested Triboar was as good a place as any to find "the script" (a specific script sought by the party).

### Calling Horn
- **Current status:** Destroyed — obliterated.
- No longer exists as a location.
- Cause and manner of destruction unspecified.

### Mirabar
- A city in the North.
- **Current state:** Several villages on its outskirts have been pillaged. The city itself has not been reported as attacked, captured, or destroyed.
- Perpetrator and timeframe of pillaging unconfirmed.

### Silverymoon
- A major settlement in the region.
- **Current state:** At least several villages in its outskirts were pillaged.
- Perpetrator and timeframe of pillaging unconfirmed; current recovery status unknown.
