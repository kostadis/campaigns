# WORLD STATE — Phandalin Campaign

*Living canon reference. Reconciled across all chapters; recent chapters override earlier. Where the source material could not confirm a claim, it is flagged as an open thread rather than asserted as fact.*

---

## FACTIONS

### Lord's Alliance
- **Status:** Active; growing in strength.
- **Notes:** No leadership, members, or specific agenda established in current material. The party has a documented broader association with the Alliance (via the War of the Giants), but present-chapter facts confirm only its rising power.

### Neverwinter
- **Type:** City-state / civilized power.
- **Status:** Highly lawful and orderly — no open slave markets, no torture racks, no notable assassinations. Has survived dragons, giants, and Uthgardtian tribal wars.
- **Standing custom / law:** Anyone who performs a service for the kingdom may keep whatever gold and treasure the kingdom has not specifically paid them to recover. (Relevant to party reward expectations.)

### Phandalin
- **Type:** Town; party base of operations.
- **Status:** Recovered and inhabited. Was overrun by orcs many years ago; that occupation is long past.
- **Current problem:** A "small dragon problem" affecting the town. (Species/threat level unconfirmed — see Open Threads. Consistent with the campaign's standing Cryovain threat.)

### Lionshield Coster / Lionshields
*Treat as one merchant faction — "Lionshield Coster" and "Lionshields" refer to the same trading concern.*
- **Type:** Far-reaching merchant guild / trading company; stores in many locations, including at least one in Phandalin.
- **Reputation:** Stores purport to sell "nearly everything."
- **Current condition:** Trade disrupted by **supply chain issues and tariffs**. The Phandalin store cannot currently live up to its "nearly everything" reputation — inventory breadth has failed.
- **Unknowns:** Leadership, whether the Phandalin store is a branch or franchise, cause/duration of the supply disruption.

### Emerald Enclave
- **Type:** Naturalist faction.
- **Doctrine:** The meta-verse is part of the natural order; divine interventions should be accepted as part of that natural order. Members are called "naturalists."
- **Key associate:** **Adabra Gwynn** speaks zealously on the Enclave's behalf and identifies with its worldview ("like us"). (Note: Adabra is also tracked campaign-wide on a path toward a Tiamat alliance — see Open Threads. Her Enclave sympathies and that trajectory are both live.)
- **Unknowns:** Leadership, membership, broader goals or missions.

### Cult of the Dragon
- **Status:** Presumed active — no fact indicates it was destroyed or disbanded.
- **Revealed purpose:** Under **Severin**, the cult was **duped by Tiamat** into attempting to summon/bring her into the world. The cult is effectively Tiamat's pawn and likely unaware it was deceived.
- **Key member:** Severin — central figure / apparent leader (exact title unconfirmed).
- **Relationship:** Tiamat (resident of Avernus alongside the cult's ambitions) manipulated the cult to serve her entry into the material world.
- **Unknowns:** Whether the summoning effort has advanced, failed, or changed; whether the cult knows it was duped.

## OPEN THREADS & MYSTERIES

**The Dragon Threat (Phandalin)**
- Phandalin has a "small dragon problem." Species, size, and immediacy are unconfirmed in current material. Reconcile with the standing campaign threat of the white dragon Cryovain and the Carver's plan to bind it.

**The Cult of the Dragon & Tiamat**
- The Cult, under Severin, was duped by Tiamat into trying to bring her into the world. Unresolved: current progress of the summoning, whether the cult realizes it was deceived, and its present standing.
- Adabra Gwynn — Enclave-aligned naturalist — is separately tracked toward a Tiamat alliance. Whether these two Tiamat threads connect is an open question worth watching.

**Lionshield Supply Crisis**
- Supply chain disruption and tariffs have crippled the Lionshields' inventory. Cause, extent, and whether it signals a larger economic or political disturbance are unknown.

**Lord's Alliance Ascendancy**
- The Alliance is "growing in strength" with no stated cause or agenda. Flag for future political developments.
