# Scan: Storm Lord's Wrath (Storm Lord's Wrath, D&D Essentials Kit)

Audit trail of every generic/unnamed **monster stat-block reference** (MM/Appendix A creatures), grouped by the module's own tribe/faction labels. Named individuals are excluded and listed at the end. Counts and die expressions are quoted **verbatim** — never totalled or re-rolled.

Conventions used below:
- "PC" = player character. "Characters" per the module usually means "including sidekicks" unless the text says "excluding sidekicks" (quoted verbatim each time).
- Line numbers refer to `Essentials Kit_ Storm Lord's Wrath.md`.

# TRIBE 1 — Cult of Myrkul (Ularan Mortus's Undead Army)

> Module context: "Ularan Mortus, a priest of the god of death Myrkul, and his followers are raising an army of undead to lay siege to the city of Neverwinter" (L120). The undead bear the skull brand of Myrkul (L342, L650).

## 1. Zombies
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "Attack on the Wayside Inn" → Undead Attack (L327–345). Count: **"1 **zombie** per player character"** (L338).
- **Module descriptors:** "Both sets of doors are under attack by humanoid figures... The zombies are the only ones attacking the building" (L333–340). "Each bears a brand on its forehead in the shape of a skull... similar to the symbol of the death god Myrkul" (L342).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE (leaderless horde; Ularan Mortus excluded/unnamed here).

## 2. Wraiths
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "Attack on the Wayside Inn" → Undead Attack (L327–345). Count: **"1 **wraith** per every two characters (rounded down), excluding sidekicks"** (L338).
- **Module descriptors:** "The wraiths come out of the darkness after the party approaches the zombies" (L338–340).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE.

## 3. Ghouls
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "Aid from Phandalin" → The Attack (L623–653). Count: **"two **ghouls**, plus one ghoul per character, including sidekicks"** (L627).
- **Module descriptors:** "Argus Skeel (male **vampire spawn**) and two ghouls, plus one ghoul per character... approach" (L627). "The ghouls working for Skeel bear the same skull marks as the ones who attacked the Wayside Inn" (L650).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE (field commander Argus Skeel is an excluded named individual; see EXCLUDED list).

## 4. Allips
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "Thunder Cliffs" → Ghost Ship (L1229–1238). Count: **"One **allip** per three adventurers (rounded down)"** (L1236).
- **Module descriptors:** "Spectral figures emerge from the mist, swooping up onto the decks" (L1233–1236). The Sea Less Traveled sidebar frames these as "undead creatures from the army of Ularan Mortus" (L1244); their babble references "the Ebondeath rises" (L1238).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE.

## 5. Specters
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "Thunder Cliffs" → Ghost Ship (L1229–1238). Count: **"two **specters** per character, excluding sidekicks"** (L1236).
- **Module descriptors:** Same ghost-ship assault as the allips (L1236).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE.

# TRIBE 2 — Cult of Talos (Fheralai Stormsworn's Cult)

> Module context: A cult of Talos, god of storms, led by priestess Fheralai Stormsworn; HQ is the death-knight-dreadnaught beached near the Tower of Storms (L118). The dreadnaught/crew itself is named as HQ but **no separate statted crew stat-block appears in this file** — Talos forces below are the encounter-stat'd members.

## 6. Archers
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "A Normal Day in Leilon" → Storm's Moving In (L423–444). Count: **"one **archer** ... for every two characters, including sidekicks, rounded down"** (L427).
- **Module descriptors:** "Strange knights bearing a symbol with three lightning bolts striking ride horses made of water. Accompanied by a wagon full of archers" (L431).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE (roadside wagon assault; no named leader present).

## 7. Dark Tide Knights
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "A Normal Day in Leilon" → Storm's Moving In (L423–444). Count: **"one **dark tide knight** (see *appendix A*) for every two characters, including sidekicks, rounded down. If there is an odd number of characters, add one dark tide knight"** (L427).
- **Module descriptors:** Same wagon assault as the archers (L427–431).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE.

## 8. Berserkers
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "A Normal Day in Leilon" → Something Fishy in Town (L446–479). Count: **"1 **berserker** per character, including sidekicks"** (L468).
- **Module descriptors:** "In front of this building stands a tiefling dressed in blue robes... Before her, at the end of the swampy water, stand a small number of humans, greataxes raised in the air, who chant in response to her" (L458). The tiefling is the kraken priest Nixoxious (named, excluded).
- **Named-leader flag:** YES → Nixoxious (named kraken priest, EXCLUDED individual; L468).

## 9. Water Elemental
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "A Normal Day in Leilon" → Marsh, Unmellow (L480–495). Count: **"at least one **water elemental**"** (L484).
- **Module descriptors:** "The watery marshland bubbles and roils... large watery forms emerge" (L488). Summoned by the kraken priest's ritual.
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE (ritual-summoned; Nixoxious excluded).

## 10. Water Weird
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "A Normal Day in Leilon" → Marsh, Unmellow (L480–495). Count: **"a maximum of one **water weird** for every two characters, including sidekicks"** (L484).
- **Module descriptors:** Same marsh emergence as the water elemental (L484–488).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE.

## 11. Air Elemental Myrmidons
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "Foul Weather at Wayside" → Armor of Talos (L700–722). Count: **"three **air elemental myrmidons** (see *appendix A*)"** (L707).
- **Module descriptors:** "Three figures in plate armor, each swinging a flail at a lone figure in splint armor" (L704–707). "These suits of armor were the same ones crafted by the Wayside Inn's blacksmith, Teega" (L707). "Curse of Talos... a mark resembling three parallel lightning bolts appears on their head" (L709).
- **Named-leader flag:** NONE (animated armor of Talos; the soldier Emmalou is an excluded named veteran).

## 12. Cult Fanatics
- **Chapters / Areas & counts:** "Foul Weather at Wayside" → Inside the Inn / The Ritual (L762–766 sidebar). Count: **"**cult fanatics** (one for every two characters rounded down, excluding sidekicks)"** (L764).
- **Module descriptors:** "Backes (Illuskan human **gladiator**) leads the chanting in the common area as **cult fanatics** ... walk back and forth between the common area and the courtyard, making sure the ritual is progressing" (L764). Ritual to fully activate the statue of Talos.
- **Named-leader flag:** YES → Backes Dunfield (named gladiator/cult leader, EXCLUDED individual; L764).

## 13. Cultists

- **Module descriptors:** "Spread out between the two areas are **cultists** ... with tridents, guarding several bound victims who will be sacrificed" (L764).
- **Named-leader flag:** YES → Backes Dunfield (named gladiator/cult leader, EXCLUDED individual; L764).

## 14. Commoners (victims, noncombatants)

# EXCLUDED NAMED INDIVIDUALS

These clearly-named individuals (with stat-block references) were intentionally excluded per the scan rules. Mandated exclusions first, then others found.
