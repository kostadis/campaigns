# Phandalin Campaign — Module Chronology & Lore Map

Purpose: order every adventure module in `docs/background/` by what happens
*before* and *after*, then explain how each connects to the next. Two tiers:

- **TIER A — Published spine.** The four Essentials Kit books present in this
  folder. This is the canonical, in-publication play order. Factual claims about
  these come straight from each book's own Introduction/Adventure Background.
- **TIER B — Campaign overlay.** Homebrew/hybrid modules the campaign guide
  (`Phandalin/CLAUDE.md`) says are fused into this game (Lost Mine of Phandelver,
  Storm King's Thunder → "War of the Giants", Tyranny of Dragons → "War of the
  Dragons", Neverwinter). These are placed relative to the spine by campaign
  role, not by their standalone publication order.
- **TIER C — Lore references.** Not adventures; the backdrop the spine draws on
  (Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, Heroes of Faerûn, the two inventory files, the
  Factions-of-Phandalin PDF, the Lord's Alliance / Order of the Gauntlet agent
  docs, the KP material, etc.).

## TIER A — Published spine (play order, levels)

### 1. Dragon of Icespire Peak  (levels 1 → 7)
- File: `Essentials Kit_ Dragon of Icespire Peak.md`
- The starter. Set near Neverwinter on the Sword Coast; Phandalin is the home
  base, Icespire Hold (in the Sword Mountains) is the white dragon's seat.
- Core threat: **Cryovain**, a white dragon that has terrorized the region
  (killed six orcs near the Mountain's Toe Gold Mine ~3 days before the party
  arrives). The party takes contracts around Phandalin and ends by slaying
  Cryovain.
- This book explicitly seeds the sequel: later books repeatedly reference
  "Phandalin, which recently had dealings with a white dragon named Cryovain …
  dispatched by a group of adventurers."
- **Ends the moment Cryovain dies.** Everything below assumes that's done.

### 2. Storm Lord's Wrath  (levels 7 → 9)
- File: `Essentials Kit_ Storm Lord's Wrath.md`
- Direct sequel. "If the characters played *Dragon of Icespire Peak*, they have
  already saved the area around Phandalin … and are likely 7th level."
- Shift in base: from Phandalin to **Leilon**, a town on the High Road being
  rebuilt under Dagult Neverember's funding. (Phandalin becomes a supporting
  location / source of aid — see "Aid from Phandalin".)
- Two evil powers rise near Leilon:
  - **Cult of Talos** (god of storms), led by priestess **Fheralai
    Stormsworn**, headquartered in a death-knight-dreadnaught beached by the
    Tower of Storms.
  - **Cult of Myrkul** (god of death), led by **Ularan Mortus**, raising an
    undead army to siege Neverwinter. Aided by the spirit of the dead black
    dragon **Chardansearavitriol**, a.k.a. **Ebondeath**, who wants a living
    dragon body to inhabit.

### 3. Sleeping Dragon's Wake  (levels 9 → 11)
- File: `Essentials Kit_ Sleeping Dragon's Wake.md`
- Middle of the "Beyond the Dragon of Icespire Peak" trilogy (SLW → SDW → DC).
- Leilon continues rebuilding (new locations added). The Myrkul/Talos threat
  escalates.
- Key lore turn: in Kryptgarden Forest, the ancient green dragon
  **Claugiyliamatar** ("Old Gnawbone") is **possessed by the wraith of
  Chardansearavitriol / Ebondeath** — the same black-dragon spirit from Book 2,
  now wearing a living dragon as a host. Also introduces the **Bronze Shrine to
  Bahamut** hidden in the Sword Mountains.

### 4. Divine Contention  (levels 11 → 13)
- File: `Essentials Kit_ Divine Contention.md`
- Concluding book of the trilogy.
- Leilon reconstruction is now *complete* (vs. "in progress" in SLW/SDW).
- Payoff of the Myrkul arc: the **Mere of Dead Men** holds the tomb of
  Chardansearavitriol, now the **HQ of the cult of Myrkul** ("Ebondeath's
  Mausoleum"). The dracolich Ebondeath (still wearing/linked to Claugiyliamatar
  per Book 3) is the climax threat.

**Spine in one line:**
Cryovain dies (DoIP) → Leilon rises, Talos+Myrkul cults appear, Ebondeath seeks
a dragon body (SLW) → Ebondeath possesses green dragon Claugiyliamatar (SDW) →
Myrkul cult entrenches in the Mere of Dead Men, dracolich showdown (DC).

## TIER B — Campaign overlay modules (homebrew hybrids)

Per `Phandalin/CLAUDE.md`, this is a *Dragon of Icespire Peak / Lost Mine of
Phandelver hybrid* with three modified published arcs layered on top. Their
placement relative to the spine:

- **Lost Mine of Phandelver (LMoP)** — early-tier parallel/merged with DoIP.
  The Redbrands, Cragmaw, Glassstaff, the Black Spider, Agatha the banshee,
  Hamun Kost, and the Wave Echo Cave thread run *alongside* the Cryovain
  contract board rather than after it. The Carver (homebrew sorcerer-warlord who
  marched on Icespire Hold to bind Cryovain, then burned Phandalin) is the
  campaign's signature villain built on top of this tier.
- **Storm King's Thunder → "War of the Giants"** — giant-conflict arc, modified
  so **Hekaton (Storm Giant King) allied with the Lord's Alliance**, ending the
  war. Surfaces in the giant/storm material and the Counterforce doctrine
  (`docs/CounterForce.md`).
- **Tyranny of Dragons → "War of the Dragons"** — Cult of the Dragon / Tiamat
  arc. **Severin** (Cult leader), **Zariel** (archdevil who used the cult as a
  loophole to expel Tiamat from Avernus), and **Iymrith** ("Desert Wind,"
  blue dragon, true architect of the giant war; escaped to Avernus) all belong
  here. Connects to the dragon-throughline of the spine (Cryovain → Ebondeath →
  Tiamat-endgame).
- **Neverwinter** — city-politics layer (per `neverwinter-inventory.md`):
  Dagult Neverember / Lord Protector, the Nashers (Sons of Alagondar), the
  Ashmadai, the Dead Rats, and the **KP / planar** thread (Kazneporium
  Ketternopappux, the Counterforce of Rimardo+Corrin, Savras). Neverember is the
  through-line linking DoIP's Phandalin region to the Leilon rebuild in the
  spine.

**Overlay in one line:**
LMoP+Carver (early, parallel to DoIP) → War of the Giants (Hekaton+Lords
Alliance) and War of the Dragons (Tiamat/Zariel/Iymrith) braid into the
mid-to-late game on top of SLW/SDW/DC → Neverwinter/KP planar thread runs
underneath the whole thing and collides at the Iymrith endgame.

  (Tier B overlay). Marked "not raw source material."
