## Campaign State

The Icespire crisis is over — Cryovain dead, the Carver dissipated, the Woodland Manse cleared. What followed was not a rest but a change of arena. In Phandalin (ch45) Vukradin turned the victory into policy, proclaiming **Universal Basic Treasure** from the town green: monster-hoarded gold belongs to the people it was taken from. The town cheered, Harbin claimed credit, Linene took the drafting, and Jenna Roscoe walked out and rode for Neverwinter to tell the Lord's Alliance what had happened before the party could arrive to explain it.

The party is now **in Neverwinter** (ch46) and the campaign has shifted from monsters to institutions. Elara Meliamne, the woman they came to see, was murdered eight days before they arrived; Lord Neverember has declared it an accident and told the city to move on; her brother Cassian names House Margaster and can prove nothing. A Margaster fixer ran a forged-writ shakedown at the table in front of them and they let it pass. The **Planar Manifold** — the interplanar shipping engine that supplies the whole region — has been dark for seven weeks, its technician was a nameless gnome who seemed to be *mapping* it rather than fixing it, and Elara died investigating why. The Lord Protector has summoned them to ask, essentially, whether they intend to keep redistributing his kingdom's gold.

The older threats have not gone anywhere: a hidden "headliner" behind six circling gods of fate, an older lightning-pale dragon beneath the green dragon card, Aletra at large with her poison cache, and Prutha unsupervised at Icespire Hold with a warning attached to him.

## The Party
