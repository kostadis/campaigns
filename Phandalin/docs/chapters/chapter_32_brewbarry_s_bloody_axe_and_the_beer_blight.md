# Chapter 32 Brewbarry's Bloody Axe and the Beer Blight

Date: Feb 18th, 2026

## **Scenes**

### **Recap and Dwarven Etymology**

#### **The session begins with a recap of the party's previous adventures, including their encounter with orcs and ogres, the discovery of Don-Jon's death, and their meeting with the Stonetallow dwarves.**

* The GM recaps the party's return to the mine after defeating orcs and ogres allied with the Carver.  
* The party learned that Don-Jon was killed by dwarves before being eaten by a carrion crawler.  
* The party met the Stonetallow clan of shield dwarves, who are intensely proud of their mining abilities and trace their ancestry to a disgruntled engineer who knew how to build proper archways.  
* The dwarves view the wererats as turning the mine into a disaster and believe only the Stonetallow clan knows how to mine properly.  
* The party proposed that wererats act as guards while dwarves handle mining, a proposal Horia found valuable as it would allow the dwarves to focus on perfecting mining.  
* Vukradin expresses confusion about the name 'Stonetallow,' questioning the oxymoron of stone and tallow (fat/wax).  
* Horia explains that 'Stonetallow' comes from 'stone to allow,' referring to their founder, a disgraced engineer from Gauntlgrym who was the only one who knew how to build a proper archway, reflecting the clan's fixation on doing things properly.

### **Negotiating with the Wererats**

#### **The party prepares to present their proposal to the wererats, with Vukradin gaining an insight into their true motivations. They then engage in a tense negotiation with Zeleen Varnaster, the wererat leader.**

* Vukradin makes an insight check and realizes the wererats never wanted to mine and give off a gangster vibe, preferring muscle work over hard labor.  
* The party approaches Zeleen Varnaster, who questions if the dwarves are dead.  
* The party explains their plan for the dwarves to do the menial labor while the wererats act as muscle and protect the mine as their territory.  
* The party emphasizes that the dwarves will extract gold for the wererats and they will receive cheese from Petunia.  
* Zeleen is intrigued by the idea and initially demands an upfront payment of 1,000 gold pieces for the first six months.  
* Brewbarry counters Zeleen's demand, reminding her that the wererats were chased from their homes and are now getting shelter, cheese, and protection without being kicked out, questioning why they would demand payment for such a good deal.  
* Brewbarry uses his axe, still covered in orc and ogre blood, as an intimidating visual during the negotiation.  
* Zeleen, impressed by Brewbarry's argument and the implied threat, agrees to protect the mine without an upfront payment, stating they should protect their home and family.  
* Zeleen offers Vukradin a piece of 'Petunia's cheese,' which is described as delicious, unique, and textured, almost like Gouda but better.  
* Zeleen mentions that orcs, ogres, and a dragon are still causing chaos in the area and suggests the party deal with them, noting that information about the dragon might be found at the Shrine of Savras.  
* Vukradin attempts to trick Zeleen by claiming dragons can polymorph into smaller forms to enter caves, but Zeleen rolls a natural 20 and sees through the deception, finding it a good joke.  
* Brewbarry reveals his full name as 'Brewbarry Root Smasher Ogalakadu,' explaining he has a family name, nickname, and clan name.  
* Zeleen warns that the dragon's raids are affecting farming and there will be no brewing of beer this year, which greatly concerns Brewbarry.

### **Planning the Next Adventure and Mine Logistics**

#### **The party discusses their next steps, weighing various quests and ultimately deciding to head towards Dragonbarrow on their way to Neverwinter. They also finalize the mine agreement.**

* The party reviews their outstanding quests: helping Lyra with the Emerald Enclave, returning a statue for Ser Kaelen in Neverwinter, dealing with the Carver, finding the Dragonblade, and investigating the dragon.  
* The party confirms they completed the quest to persuade Petunia about the cheese.  
* Ser Kaelen has information about one of the magical objects the party found.  
* The party needs to return a statue from the lighthouse to an art collector before House Markaster attacks and inform them that the Order of the Gauntlet is to thank.  
* Sister Kaella was supposed to contact the party about the Carver's situation but never has.  
* Vukradin votes to go to Neverwinter to see Elara ‘Seasong’ Meliamne and unlock some of their loot.  
* Brewbarry believes the dragon is a worthy challenge and anything else is beneath them.  
* The party decides to visit Dragonbarrow to obtain the dragon slayer sword, which is on the way to Neverwinter.  
* They take a long rest in the mine, now protected by the wererats and worked by the dwarves.  
* Horia and the wererats shake hands on their agreement, with contracts exchanged.  
* The dwarves look at the wererats' modifications with skepticism, while the wererats view the dwarves as suckers.  
* Vukradin asks about expected weekly earnings from the mine, and Horia mentions needing to figure out overhead and details, suggesting around 100 gold pieces per week, possibly more with investment.

### **Encounter with The Falcon on the Triboar Trail**

#### **While traveling to Dragonbarrow, the party encounters The Falcon on the Triboar Trail, who is distraught because his hunting lodge has been overrun.**

* The party crosses the Triboar Trail and encounters The Falcon, owner of the Falcon's Hunting Lodge.  
* The Falcon is distraught because the party never cleared the Woodland Manse, which had fireballs and lightning that drove them away.  
* The Falcon reveals that orcs and ogres have taken over his Falcon Lodge and are trying to muscle everyone out of Neverwinter Wood.  
* The Falcon is returning to Phandalin to settle debts and plan his next moves, refusing to return to the lodge.  
* The Falcon offers Vukradin a particular wood for his music studio if the party clears the lodge.  
* The Falcon proposes turning the Falcon Lodge into a venue for musical performances by Vukradin and Fina, with ale, to raise rents and attract people.  
* Brewbarry enthusiastically agrees that everything tastes better with ale, including music.  
* The Falcon departs for Phandalin, leaving the party with the option to reclaim his lodge later.

### **Arrival at Dragonbarrow and Centaur Warning**

#### **The party arrives at Dragonbarrow and is approached by a centaur named Xanth who warns them about dangers in the area.**

* The party arrives at Dragonbarrow, a 30-foot-high hill with a flat top and bone-white rocks jutting from it like outstretched talons.  
* A centaur approaches peacefully and introduces himself as Xanth.  
* Vukradin, having never seen a centaur before, is freaked out and considers it an abomination, becoming gun-shy after previous brutal encounters.  
* Soma calms Vukradin, explaining that centaurs are woodland creatures and not a threat.  
* Xanth warns the party about strange will-o'-wisps over Dragonbarrow at night, stating the hill is haunted by restless spirits and he only goes there in daylight.  
* Soma, as a druid, makes a nature check and recognizes the description as potentially will-o'-wisps, evil lights that emerge in natural surroundings, which the druid Meryl had told her about.  
* Xanth mentions that Neverwinter Wood has been overrun by orcs allied with half-orc spellcasters who perform dark rites at a circle of standing stones atop a cave-ridden hill.  
* Xanth offers to take the party to the half-orc spellcasters after they visit Dragonbarrow.  
* Brewbarry makes an insight check and connects the half-orc spellcasters to the orcs causing farmers to flee, which is affecting beer production, making it a priority after obtaining the sword.  
* Xanth confirms these are the Anchorites of Talos, the same group that tried to kill the party.

### **Entering the Dragonbarrow Tomb**

#### **The party discovers the entrance to the Dragonbarrow tomb and enters, beginning their exploration of the burial mound.**

* Valphine, with her high perception, finds a secret entrance at the base of the hill—a rock acting as a stone plug embedded in the earth.  
* Valphine is disappointed because the entrance is too obvious and not truly secret.  
* The party considers using Mold Earth to open the entrance, but Brewbarry insists on using his strength.  
* Soma offers to help with Mold Earth while letting Brewbarry think he's doing it alone, making a deception check.  
* Brewbarry rolls a 22 on his strength check and easily opens the entrance without needing help.  
* Soma's assistance boosts Brewbarry's confidence, and he gains an inspiration point.  
* The party enters the passageway and sees yellow lights coming from different directions within the tomb.  
* The party discusses whether the lights are the will-o'-wisps Xanth warned about.

### **Rat Familiar Scouts the Western Passage**

#### **Soma sends a rat familiar to scout the western passage, where it discovers sarcophagi and encounters a deadly light.**

* Soma summons a rat familiar to scout ahead, establishing a telepathic connection to see through its eyes and hear through its ears.  
* The rat explores the western hallway and discovers four sarcophagi in alcoves.  
* The northwest sarcophagus contains a loot (a musical instrument).  
* The southeast sarcophagus is sealed too tightly for the rat to enter.  
* The other two sarcophagi contain moldy bones of adventurers.  
* As the rat investigates, a light in the area goes off.  
* The rat moves past the light, which turns back on.  
* Soma has the rat dance back and forth, causing the light to turn on and off repeatedly.  
* The light suddenly attacks the rat, dealing 9 points of lightning damage and instantly killing it.  
* The party realizes the lights are dangerous and decides to avoid them.

### **Eastern Passage and Trapped Sarcophagi**

#### **The party proceeds east to avoid the lights and encounters sarcophagi that release corrosive dust when opened.**

* The party decides to explore the eastern passage to avoid the dangerous lights.  
* They arrive at an area with two sealed sarcophagi in alcoves and notice a small, two-foot-wide passageway.  
* Brewbarry prepares to open a sarcophagus, but Valphine suggests checking for traps first.  
* Brewbarry agrees to let Valphine check for traps, and she rolls a 20 perception check.  
* Valphine finds no sign of mechanical traps or anything suspicious on the outside or base of the sarcophagus.  
* Brewbarry opens the first sarcophagus, releasing a cloud of corrosive dust that fills a 10-foot area.  
* Brewbarry fails his dexterity saving throw and takes 14 points of acid damage.  
* Brewbarry uses his Stone Endurance racial ability, rolling 1d12+2 to reduce the damage by 11, taking only 3 points of damage total.  
* The first sarcophagus is found to be completely empty, with no corpse or bones.  
* Valphine recalls something about tombs but gets distracted thinking about burying people alive.  
* Brewbarry uses his halberd to open the second sarcophagus from 10 feet away.  
* Another cloud of dust emerges but falls harmlessly around the party.  
* The second sarcophagus is also found to be empty.  
* The session ends at 10:30 PM with the party having explored two empty, trapped sarcophagi and the GM noting that will-o'-wisps are intelligent creatures.
