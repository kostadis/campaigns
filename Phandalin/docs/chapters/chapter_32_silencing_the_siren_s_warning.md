# Chapter 32 Silencing the Siren's Warning

## Scenes

### Discovery of the Dragon Slayer Sword and Boney's Bonding

#### After defeating will-o-wisps and navigating traps, Valphine discovers the Dragon Slayer Sword resting on a dragon skull. The party also discusses Boney, a bone horse that has bonded with Valphine.

- Valphine navigates past traps in the northern chamber.  
- Valphine finds the Dragon Slayer Sword resting atop a dragon skull.  
- The party discusses Boney, a sentient bone horse, and who it has bonded with.  
- Boney humorously remarks that he no longer has 'irregularity' problems, being partially alive.  
- The party debates who should wield the newly found Dragon Slayer Sword, ultimately deciding Brewbarry should carry it.

### Ambush by an Invisible Stalker

#### As the party prepares to leave with the Dragon Slayer Sword, an invisible stalker ambushes them, leading to a tense combat encounter in the northern chamber.

- An invisible stalker launches a surprise attack on Valphine, striking her twice.  
- Vukradin attempts to move his Cloud of Daggers to the stalker's last known position but cannot see it.  
- Brewbarry attempts a reaction attack against the invisible creature but misses.  
- The stalker attacks Valphine again, dealing further damage.  
- Valphine casts Moonbeam, dealing significant radiant damage to the stalker and making it visible.  
- Brewbarry lands a hit on the now-revealed stalker.  
- Valphine casts Fairy Fire to outline the stalker in light, making it easier to target.  
- Brewbarry is persuaded not to pick up the Dragon Slayer Sword, as the stalker appears to be guarding it.  
- Vukradin moves the Cloud of Daggers onto the stalker, dealing damage.  
- The invisible stalker moves to hover directly over the Dragon Slayer Sword in a 'come and get it' posture.  
- Valphine moves Moonbeam onto the sword, hitting the stalker again with radiant damage.  
- Brewbarry delivers multiple hits on the stalker.  
- Vukradin moves the Cloud of Daggers again, dealing further damage.  
- The invisible stalker attacks Brewbarry, dealing significant damage, which Brewbarry partially absorbs using Stone's Endurance.  
- Soma moves Moonbeam to continue damaging the stalker.  
- The invisible stalker is defeated, and Brewbarry picks up the Dragon Slayer Sword.

### Aftermath, the Ghost, and Sister Kaella's Warning

#### After defeating the stalker and securing the Dragon Slayer Sword, the party takes a short rest, witnesses Lady Alagondar's ghost pointing toward the Ice Spire Mountains, and is then confronted by a wounded Sister Kaella bearing urgent news.

- Brewbarry picks up the Dragon Slayer Sword.  
- The party takes a short rest to recover.  
- Lady Alagondar's ghost appears, sadly staring towards the Ice Spire Mountains, hinting at the dragon's location.  
- Xanth, a wounded and bloodied centaur, is noted to be present outside.  
- Sister Kaella stumbles into the clearing, disheveled and injured, warning the party that the Carver is no longer fully human — a hollow shell speaking in a tongue that makes ears bleed.  
- Sister Kaella warns that the Carver is marching on Icespire Hold to bind the white worm, and that if not stopped, he will wear the dragon like a suit of armor and burn Phandalin to the ground.  
- Vukradin decides to attack Sister Kaella, believing her to be evil and currently weakened.

### Confrontation with Sister Kaella

#### Vukradin surprises and paralyzes Sister Kaella, and the party subdues her with non-lethal damage before she can finish her warning.

- Vukradin successfully surprises Sister Kaella with a stealthy approach.  
- Vukradin casts Hold Person on Sister Kaella, paralyzing her and preventing her from speaking further.  
- Soma and Brewbarry attack the paralyzed Sister Kaella with non-lethal blows.  
- Sister Kaella is knocked unconscious.

### Ambush by Jax and the Carver's Agents

#### Immediately after subduing Sister Kaella, the party is ambushed by Jax — the last of the Carver's agents — along with several thugs and a Rot-Tusk Ogre.

- Jax and his thugs, including a Rot-Tusk Ogre, appear and ambush the party.  
- A thug successfully throws a net, restraining Vukradin.  
- Valphine is briefly blinded by an effect from the attackers.  
- Vukradin casts Hold Person on Jax and the Rot-Tusk Ogre, immobilizing them both.  
- Soma transforms into a bear and attacks the paralyzed Jax.  
- Valphine casts Spirit Guardians, creating a radiant aura that damages Jax and the Ogre.  
- Brewbarry attacks Jax, dealing significant critical damage.  
- Thugs attack the restrained Vukradin, who endures their blows and maintains concentration on his spell.  
- Vukradin uses Array of Cards in a cone, damaging and defeating multiple thugs surrounding him.  
- Vukradin casts Silvery Barbs to force an attacker to re-roll a successful hit.  
- Soma continues to attack Jax, dealing heavy damage.  
- Valphine's Spirit Guardians continue to damage the immobilized Jax and the Ogre.  
- Brewbarry delivers the final blow to the Rot-Tusk Ogre.  
- Jax is defeated by the combined efforts of the party.  
- The last remaining thug attempts to flee; Brewbarry gets an attack of opportunity but the thug escapes.  
- The party stands victorious, with Sister Kaella unconscious and the Carver's agents defeated.
