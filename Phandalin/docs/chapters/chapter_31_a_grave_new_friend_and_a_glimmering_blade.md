# Chapter 31 A Grave New Friend and a Glimmering Blade

## Scenes

### Tunnel Collapse and Mould Earth Rescue

#### The party enters a narrow secret passageway, where Vukradin triggers a pressure plate, causing the tunnel walls to collapse. Soma uses Mould Earth to clear the debris and free the trapped party members.

- The party decides to take a secret passageway to the west, with Vukradin eagerly leading the way toward the sword.  
- Vukradin, running ahead, steps on a five-foot-long pressure plate hidden in the two-foot-wide tunnel.  
- The tunnel walls collapse inward, burying everyone but Soma in dirt and stone.  
- Soma uses the Mould Earth cantrip to extract the party members from the collapsed debris.  
- The party is covered in dirt, and their forward progress in the tunnel is blocked.  
- Soma continues to use Mould Earth to excavate the entire tunnel, allowing the party to proceed.

### The Reanimated Skeletal Horse

#### After clearing the tunnel, Soma discovers the bones of a horse, which reanimate into a skeletal horse. Soma communicates with the undead horse, learning its history and naming it Boney.

- Soma, leading the way through the excavated tunnel, discovers the bones and rotting saddle of a horse in a southern niche.  
- Upon Soma's inspection, the horse's bones writhe together and rise as a skeletal horse.  
- Soma casts Speak with Animals to communicate with the skeletal horse.  
- The horse initially believes Soma to be 'Lady Alagondar', whom it was brought back to help on a quest.  
- The party attempts to deceive the horse into believing Soma is Lady Alagondar through reincarnation, which the horse accepts.  
- The horse expresses confusion about missing parts, particularly its mane and other features, despite being fully skeletal.  
- The horse reveals it was brought to the dungeon to guard something for Lady Alagondar but was killed by Will-o'-wisps.  
- The party debates names for the horse, with suggestions including 'Mr. Ed', 'Butterscotch', and 'Boney'.  
- The horse states its name is 'Barney', but the party convinces it to embrace the name 'Boney', which it accepts happily.  
- Boney vaguely indicates the sword might be buried 'across the way' from the chamber he is in, pointing north.  
- The party discusses having Boney as a potential sidekick, though his stats are low with only 13 hit points.

### Traps and Will-o'-Wisp Encounter

#### Brewbarry, leading the party, falls into a trap, initiating a combat encounter with invisible Will-o'-wisps while Soma attempts to use magic to counter them and Valphine uses Turn Undead.

- Soma casts Detect Magic as a ritual to help locate the sword and avoid magical traps.  
- Brewbarry, leading the way, falls into a non-magical trap, taking 12 points of damage.  
- A Will-o'-wisp, previously invisible, attacks Brewbarry with a shock, dealing 9 points of damage, then becomes visible before flitting through the rock.  
- Boney recalls that Will-o'-wisps attacked and killed him in the past, describing them as evil and not very talkative.  
- Vukradin casts Silence in the corridor as a prophylactic measure against the Will-o'-wisps.  
- Valphine heals Brewbarry for 25 hit points.  
- Valphine readies an action to use Turn Undead when a Will-o'-wisp appears.  
- Valphine successfully uses Turn Undead on the Will-o'-wisp in the room with Boney, causing it to flee far away.  
- The Will-o-Wisp flees in terror.  
- Soma dashes towards Vukradin to provide aid.  
- Vukradin seing the path open, runs to get the sword.

### Vukradin splits the party

#### Vukradin decides to run after the sword, but goes west. Brewbarry sighs and moves north. Valphine follows Brewbarry. Soma follows Vukradin.

- With the Will-o-Wisp turned, Vukradin runs to get the sword.  
- Vukradin reaches the intersection, and turns west.  
- Brewbarry shouts, North, but the silence spell blocks it.

### Vukradin and Soma

#### Vukradin runs into a room with sarcophagi, is attacked by a Will-o-Wisp, kills the creature, and gets some magical treasure. Soma helps with healing and magical moon beams.

- Vukradin rushes past the entrance of the room where Soma's rat died earlier.  
- Vukradin falls into a pit trap, taking 19 points of damage and losing concentration on Silence.  
- A Will-o'-wisp appears and attacks Vukradin, dealing 9 points of damage.  
- Vukradin uses a reaction to attack the fleeing Will-o'-wisp, hitting with a 21 and dealing 6 points of damage.  
- Soma hears something, and realizes the bard is in danger and races after him.  
- The Will-o-Wisp disappears into a sarcophagus.  
- Vukradin opens the sarcophagus, discovers and sees the Will-o'-Wisp  
- As Vukradin moves away from the sarcophagus, the Will-o-Wisp which misses by rolling a 1\.  
- Vukradin casts Cloud of Daggers on the Will-o'-wisp, dealing a meager 4 points of damage after rolling four ones.  
- Vukradin retrieves a Necklace of Fireballs with seven beads from the sarcophagus to the south west.  
- Vukradin then runs to the northwest where he picks up a magical flute of illusions.  
- Soma casts Moonbeam on the same Will-o'-wisp that is caught in Cloud of Daggers, dealing 12 points of damage and destroying it.

### Brewbarry and Valphine

#### Brewbarry runs towards the North to get the sword. Valphine follows him.

- Brewbarry moves north towards the sword, past the intersection and arrives at an archway that leads to a tomb.  
- Brewbarry sees the same  odd light source in the archway leading into a room that killed Soma's rat. f  
- Brewbarry uses Thaumaturgy to brighten the light, discovering it is a magical spell, not a Will-o'-wisp.  
- Brewbarry intentionally triggers a pressure plate trap using his halberd.  
- When Valphine notes Brewbarry's increased intelligence he shouts, "It's the RED EYES\!"  
- Brewbarry approaches the pit, sees the  Will-o'-wisp, rages and attacks.  
- Brewbarry hits the Will-o'-wisp with a 23, dealing 10 points of damage.  
- Brewbarry makes a second attack while raging, destroying the Will-o'-wisp.  
- Valphine rushes towards Brewbarry.

### Discovery of the Dragon Slayer Sword

#### Valphine follows Brewbary to the northern area. There, she discovers the bones of a dragon with the Dragon Slayer Sword resting atop its skull, completing the party's quest.

- Valphine moves to the northern area of the dungeon moving past Brewbarry who has just killed a Will-o-Wisp  
- Valphine jumps over the pit trap, and enters the room which has a pair of sarcophagi.  
- The area contains the bones of a dragon with its skull resting on the floor.  
- A long sword, identified as the Dragon Slayer Sword, is found resting atop the dragon's skull.  
- The session ends with the party having successfully located the Dragon Slayer Sword, along with the Flute of Illusions and Necklace of Fireballs.
