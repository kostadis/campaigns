## World Events

- Following the War of the Dragons, the Northern Territories and the Lord's Alliance forces were recovering from their conclusive battle against the Cult of the Dragon.
- The great sundering completed, ending the era of gods walking the world.
- Lolth was trapped in a demi-plane as part of an elaborate multi-century plan and was thought finished; her plan required magic that could temporarily loosen the bonds holding the planes separate.
- Gromph Baenre of the First House of Menozberanzan, Lolth's pawn, thought his spell would give the Drow control of the Faerzess, allowing them to attack other powers at will; he was wrong — the bonds between the Abyss and Toril weakened for a moment, and the demon lords entered the Underdark.
- Demogorgon wreaked havoc on cities, including the notable city of Menozberranzan; the demon Grazz't instigated widespread insanity and madness; the demon Orcus marshaled armies of undead; the demoness Zugtomy disseminated perilous fungi.
- Rampaging demons leveled the great city of the Drow; many people fled the Dark Realms.
- Valphine fled to the Overbrite due to the madness of the Underdark and stories of Menzoberranzan's final fall, where the sun's evil was supposed to be; when she first saw the sun, it was a religious experience that burned out the darkness.
- Valphine became a devoted follower of Lathander after stumbling on a cleric who told her of the Morning God's power.
- Twice the plans of the gods had been thwarted; Amn's plans made other plans possible, and those plans were thwarted.
- Zariel found the loophole in the gods' rules and had plans of her own; Tiamat, co-resident of Avernus, duped Severin and the Cult of the Dragon to attempt to bring her into the world, but the plan was Zariel's, not hers; Zariel was tired of Tiamat's presence in her kingdom of Avernus.
- The combined strength of the Giants and major cities successfully ended the barbarian invasion; the Ordning was reinstated, keeping the armies intact and preventing a catastrophic giant assault.
- The All-Father, god of the giants, fantasized about a north that the Giants ruled; in the chaos of the All-Father's plans, the Great Ancient Blue Dragon Iymrith almost destroyed the kingdoms of the giants, but Iymrith escaped.
- The Storm Giant King Hekaton created an alliance between giants and the people of the realms.
- The chaos in the Underdark may have provoked the Uthgardt Barbarians to launch a devastating assault on the North, destroying numerous smaller communities; Triboar was razed, Calling Horn was obliterated, and several villages on the outskirts of Silverymoon and Mirabar were pillaged.
- The alliance of the Silvery Marches was revived due to the efforts of several agents from Everlund; the Kraken society was revealed and survived; the Lord's Alliance grew in strength.
- The rules of Aos always have exceptions, which convinced the gods to try again.
- On Vukradin 01-01-Taraskh 1495, the session began; Vukradin was performing at the First Flophouse in the Heapside, earning no coin, as an avant-garde musician whose music was underappreciated; he had a discussion with a Wintershield watchman about going to Phandalin to make money (flashback: on the 8/3 of Ches 1495, the watchman told him he could clear the area of monsters and keep the treasure found).
- On 03.05 Vukradin, 01-02-Taraskh 1495 (the first scene), Vukradin quit performing at the First Flophouse; Brewbarry, the only one listening, followed him out.
- On 03.06 Valphine, 01-02-Taraskh 1495 (the second scene), Valphine saw Vukradin walk out of the bar and decided to follow him.
