### High Road

A major trade road heading south from Neverwinter. Near Neverwinter, it is patrolled frequently enough that travelers feel safe and wagon drivers keep their hands away from their weapons.

**Current state:** The road is open and actively patrolled near Neverwinter; the merchant caravan travelling it has not yet encountered trouble.
