### Townmaster's Hall

- **What it is:** The seat of local government in Phandalin, located a couple of doors away from the Stonehill Inn.
- **Current state:** The hall is **boarded shut** and the **door is locked** — an unusual posture for the seat of local government, suggesting either siege or hiding from the governed. Harbin Wester is associated with the hall (referred to as "Harbin Wester's hall").
- **Notable features:** A job board / quest board outside the hall, listing notices offering work to armed strangers and describing the settlement's problems.
- **Current occupants/control:** Not specified; the hall is sealed.

## Uncertainty

- The relationship between Harbin Wester and the hall is implied ("Harbin Wester's hall") but not explicitly stated as his office or residence; whether he is currently inside or elsewhere is unknown.
- It is unclear whether the job board remains accessible/current given the hall is boarded shut and locked — the board's current condition (still posted, removed, or vandalized) is not stated.
- The reason for the boarding/locking (siege vs. hiding) is offered as speculation in the source quote, not confirmed fact.
