### Dabbledob

**Status:** Alive.

**Current Location:** Gnomengarde.

**Allegiance/Faction:** One of Gnomengarde's foremost inventors.

**Current Role/Assignment:** Co-lead (with coworker Fibblestib) of the project to cure King Korboz's madness. She is skeptical of proposals without evidence, initially arguing against a sanity ray (no evidence it works) and suggesting a straitjacket or music ("Music has helped in some circumstances.") as alternatives.

**Key Relationships:**
- Coworker: Fibblestib (she rolls her eyes at Fibblestib's boasts).
- Interacted with the party members Vukradin and Valphine during their visit.

**Defining Recent Actions:**
- Dismissed the party's dragon problem as unimportant compared to Gnomengarde's calamity (King Korboz's madness).
- Debated the cure with Fibblestib, grousing about lack of evidence for a ray.
- Ultimately proposed building a sanity ray ("A sanity ray!").
- In a later exchange, expressed a preference for more kings so they could have a vote ("I prefer more kings! That way we could have a vote!").

**Revealed Motivations/Secrets:**
- Entirely focused on curing King Korboz.
- Pragmatic, evidence-based approach to invention; skeptical of unproven theories.

## Uncertainty

- **Cure stance and proposal chronology:** The facts are ambiguous on whether her proposal of a sanity ray (a) supersedes her earlier skepticism (i.e., she changed her mind after the party's suggestion) or (b) is a separate, possibly sarcastic or competing proposal. Also unclear if her "prefer more kings" statement is a sincere political view or a joke meant to one-up Fibblestib.
- **Current king situation:** Unclear whether Korboz is still king and mad as of the latest chapter, or if the "prefer more kings" remark implies a change in the monarchy structure.
