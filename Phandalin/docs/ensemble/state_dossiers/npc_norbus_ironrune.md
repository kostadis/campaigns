### Norbus Ironrune

**Current status:** Alive.
**Current location:** At the excavation site (the dwarven temple/ruin southwest of Phandalin), where he is cooking food over the fire alongside the party.

**Allegiance/Faction:** Shield Dwarf prospector, partnered with Dazlyn Grayshard. He describes them as partners with a deal for the site's artifacts/gems.

**Current possessions / notable items:** None confirmed beyond ordinary prospecting gear; he offers two sending stones to the party in exchange for clearing the temple (but has not been confirmed as currently holding them — the offer was made, not verified as kept).

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**
- Prospector and explorer, paired with Dazlyn Grayshard.
- Employing/cooperating with the party to clear the temple; asked them to finish clearing everything out, noting there might be more monsters deeper inside.
- Withholding payment from the party (per Vukradin's accusation in ch03).
- Distrusts Valphine (drow cleric of Lathander) and Brewbarry (drow, Uthgardt-marked) — does not believe a drow can be genuinely faithful to Lathander; he accepts Valphine's magic as useful but not her god as true.
- Also disliked Brewbarry's holy symbol of Lathander, face tightening at the sight.
- Clash over the treasure: Norbus claims the museum only needs half the gems; the other half is "not of any archaeological interest." He asked permission to take the gems.
- Wounded Vukradin by forgetting his name ("Vukradin, right."), genuinely hurting him.

**Defining recent actions:**
- Discovered an ancient dwarven settlement buried by an avalanche in a canyon southwest of Phandalin — it contains a temple of Abbathor, the evil god of greed.
- Fled from an ochre jelly before confronting it (admitted this).
- Hired the party to enter the temple and deal with monsters, offering two sending stones as payment.
- Asked the party to avoid damaging spells to preserve the interior's artifacts.
- Stayed out of combat (claimed he and Dazlyn are "one good hit from finished" and useless in a fight); after the oozes/jellies were dealt with, he entered cautiously, asking if it was safe.
- Upon seeing the altar: "Oh, it is as dazzling as we thought it would be!"
- Discovered the party found the "Hall of Greed."
- Said he and Dazlyn will handle artifacts with utmost care, taking them to a museum and to a wizard to be uncursed.
- Currently cooking food for the party at the excavation camp and tapping rhythm for Vukradin's song.

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**
- His stated objection to excavating the Dwarven Dead is waived because the site is a temple of Abbathor (evil god of greed) — his actual motive appears to be appropriating what survived beneath the avalanche.
- He holds contradictory views of Valphine simultaneously — sees her as both a potential trap (old stories about drow luring people into ambush) and a blessing (a cleric of Lathander), and visibly sweats over the tension.
- Claims ignorance of what makes a dangerous versus easy monster, framing himself and Dazlyn as simple prospectors/explorers.
