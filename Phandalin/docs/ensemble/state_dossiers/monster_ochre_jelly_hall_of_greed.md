### Ochre Jelly (Hall of Greed)

**Current status:** Dead — destroyed by the party in the Hall of Greed.

**Current location:** The Hall of Greed, near the blood-stained altar; now a wet stain on the stone.

**Allegiance/faction:** None; a mindless ooze acting on hunger.

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None applicable — the creature is dead and dissolved. In life, it had no head, eyes, bones, or face; it smelled of vinegar and old meat.

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** No ongoing role or relationships. It was encountered as a hazard guarding/near the altar in the Hall of Greed. It fought the party (Brewbarry, Soma, Valphine, Vukradin, and a bard).

**Defining recent actions (collapsed):** Multiple ochre jellies were encountered and destroyed in the Hall of Greed. One large jelly (ox-sized) was cut by Brewbarry's halberd and split into two living halves; a third dropped from the ceiling. The party killed all of them: Brewbarry burst one with a punch, Soma killed a smaller one with Poison Spray, Valphine destroyed one half of the big jelly with her mace, and Vukradin finished the last (and another small one) with his daggers. A second large jelly (brewing-vat-sized) emerged from behind the altar, split into two smaller jellies after taking damage (Soma threw two ice shards; Valphine's fire failed to burn it), and was ultimately killed — one small half by Brewbarry's punch, the last by Vukradin's daggers. The jellies attacked with pseudopods, striking Brewbarry with acid burns; one pseudopod missed the bard.

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** None — no intelligence or motivation beyond hunger was revealed. The creature's splitting ability (when cut) was demonstrated, and it was hard to burn ("hard to burn a puddle").
