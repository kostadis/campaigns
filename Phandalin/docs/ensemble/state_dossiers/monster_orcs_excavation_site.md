### Orcs (Excavation Site)

**Current status:** Dead — all encountered orcs at the site were killed in the skirmish; the last two fell to Brewbarry's halberd strikes.

**Current location:** The incline at the excavation site where the party encountered them; the bodies remain there.

**Allegiance/faction:** Unknown — no affiliation, leader, or purpose beyond guarding/occupying the excavation site was revealed.

**Current possessions / notable items:**
- Greataxes (carried by several orcs, including those who attacked Vukradin and Valphine)
- At least one javelin (thrown at Brewbarry)
- Hide armor (one orc dragged down the incline was described as wearing hide armor)

**Current assignment / role:** Unknown — the orcs were encountered on an incline in fog; no goal, orders, or objective beyond combat with the party was stated.

**Key relationships:** None established with any named NPC or faction.

**Defining recent actions (collapsed):**
- Ambushed/engaged the party on an incline; only two were initially visible in the fog, with the rest hidden "up here in the fog of war."
- Fought the party in melee with greataxes (felling Vukradin, staggering Valphine) and at range with a javelin (striking Brewbarry).
- One orc was dragged down the incline by Soma's Thorn Whip and killed by Brewbarry's halberd.
- The last two survived the party's Ice Knife explosion (with the party's cleric on the ground beside them) but were both killed by Brewbarry's halberd.

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** None stated.
