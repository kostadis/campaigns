### City Guard

- **Current status:** Alive and on duty.
- **Current location:** Standing beside a notice board in Neverwinter, in view of the adventurers.
- **Allegiance/faction:** City guard of Neverwinter, subordinate to Lord Neverember.
- **Current role:** Recruiting/informing would-be adventurers at the notice board; acts as a spokesperson for how adventuring work is compensated in Neverwinter.
- **Key relationships:** Reports to Lord Neverember; interacting with the adventurers (including one named Vukradin).
- **Notable statements/behaviors:**
  - Told the adventurers Neverwinter does not pay adventurers wages.
  - Explained that adventurers earn money by defeating monsters and securing their trinkets and treasure.
  - Stated Lord Neverember and the guard do not care where the monsters got their money.
  - Warned against bringing treasure to Lord Neverember.
  - Stated that outside Neverwinter's walls, the laws of Neverwinter do not apply; "the gray begins at the wall."
  - Suggested Vukradin might be a paladin.
  - Proposed donating treasure to the city guard retirement fund for safekeeping.
- **Revealed motivations/secrets:** None beyond the guard's official position and dry humor regarding the retirement fund.
