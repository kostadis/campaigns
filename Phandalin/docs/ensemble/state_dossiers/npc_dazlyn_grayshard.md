### Dazlyn Grayshard

**Current status:** Alive; active at the excavation site.

**Current location:** The excavation site — an ancient dwarven settlement in the canyon.

**Allegiance/faction:** Shield Dwarf prospector; amateur archaeologist (hobby is new, "days at the very outside"). Works with Norbus Ironrune.

**Current possessions / notable items:** Axe (kept "close to hand"); otherwise unspecified.

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Prospector at a private excavation with partner Norbus Ironrune. Has claimed the excavation's findings (including gems) are not to be shared; insists on splitting any recovered gems with the party. Recognizes the Hall of Greed as a significant find.

**Defining recent actions:** Confronted the party about the private excavation; negotiated over recovered gems; emerged from hiding wielding his axe, dust-streaked, watching the entrance. First question to the returning party was whether they were there for the gem — not about their wounds. Showed disappointment when Vukradin played an original song rather than a familiar local tune.

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Practiced in dwarven religious lore — confirmed details of Abbathor's worship (blood and gemstone sacrifices during solar eclipses; the earthquake/avalanche as divine punishment) and explicitly distinguished dwarven gods from drow gods. Interested in the archeological/historical value of the finds, though admits he does not know whether the green gem truly has great historical significance. Proposed storytelling around the campfire with the party's bard.
