### Corbin

**Current Status:** Alive. Traumatized former bandit and farmer, last seen at the windmill after a manticore attack.

**Current Location:** At the windmill (arrived looking worse and smelling; was attacked by a manticore).

**Allegiance/Faction:** Company of adventurers (traveling with them, including Brewbarry, Valphine, Vukradin, and Soma); formerly a bandit in a forest crew.

**Appearance:** Gaunt, late twenties, haunted hollowed-out look; matted dirt-brown hair, patchy beard, pale blue eyes; ill-fitting leather armor scavenged from a dead man.

**Current Possessions / Items:** The leather armor (he asked Brewbarry to keep it; status of request not resolved in facts).

**Current Assignment / Role & Key Relationships:** Traveling with the adventuring party; asked Brewbarry (who seems to hold authority over the armor) to keep his armor; terrified of Soma in spider form; suggested Vukradin leave his musical instrument behind.

**Defining Recent Actions:**
- Attacked by a manticore that decided to eat him; survived and reached the windmill.
- Broke down emotionally, recounting his past.
- Revealed he witnessed his fields burn, home collapse, and family fall while fighting barbarians with only a wood-axe and farmer's grit.

**Revealed Motivations & Secrets:**
- Doesn't want to die.
- Was a farmer from Greenmeadow, a small village in the plains east of the High Forest; worked land his father worked; married to Elarah, daughter Lily.
- Family killed in an attack by Black Lion barbarians; survived by being knocked unconscious, left for dead under his neighbor's body.
- Came to town after losing everything; turned away as "another broken man"; joined the forest crew.
- Met Meril during his journey.
