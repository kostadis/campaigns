### Glowing Green Gem (Hall of Greed)

**What it is:** A magical, glowing green gem, humming with magic.

**Current location:** Held in the cupped hands of an intact statue of a horned dwarf (a "greed god") in an alcove to the north of the Hall of Greed.

**Current holder:** The statue of the horned dwarf.

**Current condition:** Intact, still in the statue's hands; not taken.

**Significance:** The horned dwarf statue stares greedily at the gem, which is cupped in its hands. The gem's worth was assessed by Valphine's long squint, and on that basis it was left in place.

## Uncertainty
- None.
