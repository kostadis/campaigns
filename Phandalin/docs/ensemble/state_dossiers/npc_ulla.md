### Ulla

- **Current status:** Alive.
- **Current location:** A gnome settlement in a mountain cave system, positioned near/after a hanging bridge alongside Pog.
- **Allegiance/faction:** A gnome of the settlement; acts as a guard/greeter at the bridge.
- **Current assignment/role:** Guarding the approach to the settlement with Pog; directing visitors to the inventors’ workshop.
- **Key relationships:** Partnered/sitting with Pog; suspicious of Pug (stated he looked "a little bit different" lately).
- **Defining recent actions:**
  - Challenged the party at the bridge ("Who goes there?").
  - Allowed the party to pass, noting a prior standing order to attack Uthgardt on sight was not clearly renewed, and that former Uthgardt member Brewbarry would be let through.
  - Gave directions to the inventors’ workshop: cross the hanging bridge from the cave entrance, or take a long route (north past the first east passageway, past a large room, exit the mountain, cross the river, climb the cliff wall to the sleeping chambers, then south to the workshop) if no one can cast *Mage Hand*.
  - Insisted she and Pog are "real gnomes" when questioned about shapechangers; earlier quipped that the party's denials were "exactly what a shapechanger would say."
- **Revealed motivations/secrets:** Openly suspects shapechangers may be present; concerned about Pug’s recent changed appearance.

## Uncertainty
- **Party composition:** Ulla assumed the party had a "mage who could cast Mage Hand" — it's unclear if this was accurate or a misassumption; the party's actual capabilities at that time are not confirmed in the facts.
- **Pug vs. Pog identity:** Facts refer to both "Pog" (sitting with Ulla) and "Pug" (the one Ulla says looked different). It is unclear whether "Pug" and "Pog" are the same character (name spelling variance) or two distinct gnomes.
