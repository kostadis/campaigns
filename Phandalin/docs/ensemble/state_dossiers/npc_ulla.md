### Ulla

- **Current status:** Alive.
- **Current location:** Gnomengarde, sitting where the bard and his friends arrive after crossing the hanging bridge.
- **Allegiance/faction:** Gnome guard at Gnomengarde.
- **Current role:** Guard; with Pug (another gnome) at the bridge. She directs visitors (Vukradin and the party) through Gnomengarde's routes.
- **Key relationships:** With Pug (a fellow gnome guard); she is suspicious of Pug, noting he "looked a little bit different the past few days." She addresses Vukradin and the party, giving them directions.
- **Current possessions / notable items:** None stated.
- **Notable recent actions:** Gave Vukradin directions to the inventors' workshop, offering two routes — the northern hallway route (long way: past first eastern passageway, past a large room, second eastern passageway out of the mountain, cross the river, climb cliff wall to sleeping chambers, then passageway south to the workshop) and the hanging bridge route (from where they entered the caves). Expressed surprise that the party lacked a mage who could cast Mage Hand. Shouted that she and Pug are real gnomes and that they like music, mostly gnomish music.
- **Revealed motivations/secrets:** None beyond her suspicion of Pug.
