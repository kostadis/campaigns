### Toblen Stonehill

- **Current status:** Alive.
- **Current location:** Stonehill Inn, Phandalin (proprietor).
- **Current role / key relationships:** Innkeeper of the Stonehill Inn in Phandalin; knows and greets the party by sight.
- **Notable actions / knowledge:**
  - Formerly lived in Triboar; fled or received word of the Uthgardt barbarian attack there.
  - Publicly forgives Uthgardt barbarians ("the past is the past"), and apologized to Brewbarry for the attack.
  - Warned the party (via Vukradin) about orc tribes coming down from the north.
  - Warned the party about a small dragon problem near Phandalin.
  - Recommended the party check the quest board for work.
- **Defining traits:** Short, affable, hospitable, urgent in greeting returning party members.
- **Revealed motivations / secrets:** None beyond a personal history with Triboar's destruction.

## Uncertainty
- Whether Toblen currently remains at the Stonehill Inn at the latest chapter is inferred from the chapter 4 greeting at the inn; no later fact places him elsewhere or confirms he left.
- The exact timing/order of the orc-tribe warning versus the dragon warning is unclear — both are attributed to Toblen, but the orc warning is only confirmed through Vukradin's recollection in ch04.
- It is not stated why or how Toblen "managed to get word" about Triboar's disaster — whether he fled, received a message, or something else is unknown.
