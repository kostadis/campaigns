### Valphine Sotorra

**Current status:** Alive. Traveling with Vukradin's party, most recently on the trail to Umbrage Hill.

**Current location:** En route to Umbrage Hill with the party; last seen being hit by a bandit's attack on the trail.

**Allegiance / faction:** Drow cleric of Lathander; former follower of Lolth. Survivor of Drow society (Menzoberranzan).

**Current possessions / notable items:** At least one magical bullet (originally found by Brewbarry on a previous ogre) — used one to knock an ogre prone; appears to have access to at least one more or has used them previously. Carries divine fire (channeled against an ogre) and healing ability (healed Vukradin).

**Spells / abilities observed:** Divine fire (used against ogre, dodged), healing magic, magical bullet use, destructive energy use (displeased Lathander).

**Current assignment / role:** Adventurer with Vukradin's party; cleric. Interested in Gnomengarde Quest and the opportunity for plunder. Recently involved in negotiating with the two kings of Gnomengarde and heading to Umbrage Hill to save a woman for 25 gold (which she finds absurd; Vukradin agreed to ask for more).

**Key relationships:**
- **Vukradin:** Travels with him; initially followed him, joined his party. Finds him gallant but ineffectual, a fool at times, yet wonders if Lathander sent him to show her a better way. Senses power in him she doesn't yet realize. Continues to talk with him, amused by his negotiating and spinning fictional tales.
- **Brewbarry:** Travels with; stood together watching Vukradin and Soma climb; Brewbarry provided the magical bullets Valphine used.
- **Soma:** Travels with; was knocked out by Soma's ice knife in an earlier encounter (ch02); annoyed by Soma's mocking jibes; finds Soma's kinship-with-life claim ironic given the ice knife.

**Defining recent actions (collapsed):**
- Joined Vukradin's party as a self-identified cleric of Lathander wishing to adventure.
- Fought ogres; used magical bullet to knock one prone; was surrounded and knocked out by Soma's ice knife in an earlier battle.
- At Gnomengarde: suspected gnomes Pog and Ulla of being shapeshifters, accused Ulla directly, believed torture purifies the crazed mind, doubted she could cure King Korboz, and convinced the party needed a clear approver structure with the two kings.
- On the trail: dismissed a shadow she spotted (reminding herself she's not in the Dark Realms); was hit by a bandit's attack.

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**
- Motivated by plunder and survival instincts from Drow society; pragmatic and ruthless (willing to kill monsters, considered decapitating the kings).
- Struggles with Lathander's teachings; has been cautioned by Lathander's cleric about greed (e.g., finding 25 gold absurd), and senses Lathander is not pleased with her use of destructive energy.
- Believes monsters are "what we call things we are allowed to kill."
- Former follower of Lolth; initially misread Lathander's clerics as duplicitous, then learned they genuinely condemn murder of enemies.
- Finds torture purifying for a crazed mind; smiles when violence befalls enemies.
- Thinks of ogres as darkness to be obliterated by Lathander's light.
