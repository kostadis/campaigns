### Valphine Sotorra

**Current Status:** Alive. A drow cleric of Lathander, formerly of House Sotorra in Menzoberranzan. Traveling with the party (Vukradin, Soma, Brewbarry) as part of the expedition.

**Current Location:** With the party, most recently in the vicinity of Gnomengarde and en route through the overbright surface. Was near a manticore encounter. They were tasked with bringing a woman (Adabra) back.

**Allegiance / Faction:** Cleric of Lathander the Overbright. Former member of House Sotorra (renounced), formerly a follower of Lolth (renounced). Driven from the Underdark when demons destroyed the drow city. Welcomed by clerics of Lathander in Neverwinter.

**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**
- A mithral holy symbol of the Morninglord (Lathander)
- A mace (used in combat; has missed multiple times recently, once "worse than both of Brewbarry's swings put together")
- A jeweled dagger stolen from a dead Abbathor priest's remains, hidden in her pouch (taken in Gnomengarde ruin, not announced to party)
- A hand-held crossbow with bolts (used recently, missing both shots against the manticore)
- A dagger she sharpens with a whetstone
- Red leather armor (her own; not the Abbathor vestments, which she deemed ruined/worthless)
- No spells remaining as of the end of chapter 3 (spent radiance on ochre jellies, reserve "scraped dry"); has since cast Healing Word and Sacred Flame multiple times in later chapters, so her spell reserves have since partially recovered (chapter 4 had her casting both)

**Current Assignment / Role:**
- Cleric and healer for the party
- Partnered with Vukradin (who she studies, finding his sincerity indistinguishable from coercion)
- Joined the group because a flyer promised hardship and she likes pain
- Was told to bring the woman (Adabra) back — reiterated this to Vukradin
- Offered to teach Adabra about Lathander

**Key Relationships:**
- Travels with Vukradin, Soma, and Brewbarry
- Vukradin: She watches him closely, uncertain whether his decency is "the finest manipulation" or "something rarer." She finds his persuasion "measured, warm, and entirely merciless."
- Soma: She has healed Soma multiple times, but savors Soma feeling bad about her "We had servants" comment regarding Orcish fluency; finds Soma's Ice Knife clumsy and makes sure to stay out of range
- Brewbarry: Shared wavelength on torture purifying the crazed mind; thinks Brewbarry will "get us all killed"
- Norbus and Dazlyn (Gnomengarde kings): She has dealt with them; they doubt her faith despite her holy symbol

**Defining Recent Actions:**
- Fought ochre jellies, orcs, an ogre (killed it with Sacred Flame), a mimic (found it, shouted "We found it!"), and a manticore (missed with crossbow)
- Stole the jeweled dagger from the dead priest's remains while sending Vukradin ahead, considering it "procedure" not theft
- Was taken down in combat by Soma's Ice Knife shards among the orcs but survived
- Wants to travel via the most brightly lit path possible, needing sunlight ("the sun is gloriously painful")
- During the Gnomengarde investigation, was convinced the two gnomes Pog and Ulla were the shapeshifters, but the mimic was later revealed

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**
- Left House Sotorra because her sisters would have knelt to Abbathor's power
- Left before the demon invasion that destroyed the drow city and drove out Underdark denizens
- Finds the sun's pain holy; loves pain generally, enjoys combat pain
- Has stolen from corpses and kept it hidden, composing herself with "mild clerical curiosity" to conceal the theft
- Believes in torture as purification of the crazed mind (aligned with Brewbarry on this)
- Finds surface culture strange but acceptable; considers splitting wealth among strangers normal
- Speaks Orc (learned from House Sotorra's orc labor) and Undercommon
- Knows about dangerous shapeshifters from the Dark Realms
- Has survived House Sotorra, renounced Lolth, offered herself to Lathander
- Thinks Vukradin's reintroduction of Undercommon side-conversations was his "first genuinely drow thought"
