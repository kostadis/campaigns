### Sending Stones (object)

A pair of paired magical stones, each of which speaks to the other across any distance, allowing their holders to stay connected no matter where they scatter.

- **Current holder:** The party (specifically noted as in Valphine’s hands after being given by the gnomes; later simply described as "the party possesses" them).
- **Current location:** With the party, in/around Phandalin (they opted to keep them after no one in Phandalin would buy them).
- **Current condition:** Functional. Each stone can send a single short message to its counterpart across any distance. (Vukradin confirmed they are *not* capable of projecting music continuously or serving as a music relay — one short message only.)
- **Significance / origin:** Agreed payment (fee) from the dwarves (Vukradin's group) for the party clearing ogres/jellies from the ruins. The stones were held by the dwarves until the ruins were clear, then given to Valphine by the gnomes. Estimated value: possibly a couple hundred gold pieces.
- **Current disposition:** Kept, not sold. No buyer was found in Phandalin, so the party decided to keep them for their communication utility.

## Uncertainty
- None stated explicitly, but note a minor attribution inconsistency: [ch03] says the stones were "given to Valphine by the gnomes," while [ch03] also frames them as "the agreed fee" from the dwarves (Vukradin's group). The facts don't clarify the exact relationship between the dwarves and gnomes in the handoff, though this doesn't affect the stones' current state.
