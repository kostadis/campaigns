### Woman in Windmill (NPC)

- **Current status:** Alive, present at the windmill on Umbrage Hill.
- **Current location:** The windmill on Umbrage Hill.
- **Allegiance/faction:** Self-identified as a naturalist in the great schism — she accepts divine interventions as part of the natural order.
- **Current possessions / notable items:** A special potion of healing with extra scent and taste that "makes it go down sweeter," which she offers to the party.
- **Current assignment / role:** She insists she must remain at the windmill to craft her potions, stating this is her role in the natural order.
- **Key relationships:** She converses with a party member named Soma, asking whether Soma is on the naturalist or interventionist side.
- **Defining recent actions:** She refused the party's invitation to come to town, explaining she cannot run because the manticore could attack her. She also noted she had been uncertain whether a thump outside was one of the party dropped by the creature or the beast itself and was determined not to tempt fate. The party discovered she has only been under the manticore's siege for a short time.
- **Physical description:** Past her prime, lean, slightly stooped, with a weather-beaten face set in a grim, unyielding line; piercing, cold gaze; grey or brown hair pulled back simply; plain, functional, possibly stained clothing; calloused hands bearing the faint scent of herbs and antiseptic.

## Uncertainty
- None. All facts are consistent, and the only explicit contradiction (whether the thump was a person or the beast) is presented as the woman's own uncertainty, not a factual conflict.
