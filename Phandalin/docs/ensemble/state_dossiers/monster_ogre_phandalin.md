### Ogre (Phandalin)

**Current status:** Dead — killed in battle with the party in the valley near Phandalin.

**Current location:** The valley (where the party encountered it); corpse left at the battle site.

**Allegiance/faction:** None revealed; a lone, hungry ogre.

**Current possessions / notable items:** None. Brewbarry pulled his halberd from the ogre's corpse after it was slain.

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** No known allegiances or companions. Confronted the party on their march back to Phandalin after Vukradin heard it whistling ahead.

**Defining recent actions (collapsed):**
- Stood in the middle of the valley, shouting that it was hungry.
- Demanded turtle, pointing at Soma, rejecting offered food.
- Threatened to eat Turtle and the party: *"I'm hungry! I eat Turtle! I eat you!"*
- Howled after Soma said the party killed its brother.
- Was covered in poison by Soma.
- Charged at Brewbarry, who dodged; the ogre missed.
- Took a bloody (non-lethal) blow from the party before being killed.

**Revealed motivations / secrets:** It had a brother killed by the party (stated by Soma, which provoked the howl); it was hungry and specifically wanted turtle.
