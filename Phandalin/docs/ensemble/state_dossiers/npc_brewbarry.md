### Brewbarry

**Current Status:** Alive, in the middle of a conflict with fleeing bandits.

**Current Location:** Fighting bandits on and around a hill near the First Flophouse.

**Allegiance/Faction:** Former Uthgardt barbarian — openly says "I used to be one" of the Uthgardt tribe, no longer considers himself part of them. Travels with and fights alongside Vukradin's group.

**Current Possessions / Notable Items, Spells, Feats, Wounds:**
- Wields a formidable **halberd** (currently stuck fast in some unseen impediment just before the bandit fight).
- Carries a **pair of gleaming handaxes** in a hidden pouch — his only ranged option; has **no other ranged weapons**.
- Wears the **distinct tattoos** of one of the barbarian tribes.
- Has a **damaging rage** fueled by his fury; is **covered in blood** after decapitating a bandit with his halberd.

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**
- Fights alongside **Vukradin** (who asked about ranged weapons before the fight) and **Valphine** — he openly opposes Valphine's torture idea, preferring to *"talk"* and *"cure"*.
- Recently, mid-fight, dropped his halberd and shouted **"Friends"** to a fleeing bandit he believes has lost his tribe, offering an apology.

**Defining Recent Actions:**
- Killed an ogre earlier, failed to bring down the fleeing ogre, charged and struck a mimic with his halberd.
- Just decapitated a bandit on a hill; the body fell on him, covering him in blood, then he sprinted to the spider-fearing bandit, dropped his weapon, and apologized for the Uthgardts' cruelty.

**Revealed Motivations or Secrets:**
- Left/gave up his Uthgardt identity — says the Uthgardts were mean to him too.
- Prefers talk/cure over violence or torture, despite his barbaric fury.
- Believes the fleeing man has suffered at the hands of his people (the Uthgardts) and was expecting to die, not receive an apology.

## Uncertainty
