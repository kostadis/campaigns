### Brewbarry

**Current Status:** Alive. Injured by manticore claws (per ch07).

**Current Location:** At/near a windmill, asking to spend the night (per ch07).

**Allegiance/Faction:** Travels with Vukradin's party. Formerly a Uthgardt Barbarian, cast out from his tribe. Claims "I am no shapeshifter."

**Profession/Class:** A drow cleric of Lathander (bears the holy symbol of Lathander) who fights as a melee fighter. Also identifies as a barbarian ("I am a barbarian"), though he "keeps forgetting to rage."

**Current Possessions/Notable Items:**
- A halberd (his primary weapon, repeatedly used and defended: "Nobody keeps my halberd"). Weapon got stuck in a mimic in ch05 but he still had it in ch06, using it to kill a bandit.
- A pair of gleaming handaxes, kept in a hidden pouch, pulled out when he needed a ranged option (ch06).
- Two rations he hasn't eaten (as of ch02).
- No attack spells: "I do not have attack spells. I have a halberd."
- Wears no armor (ch07).

**Current Wounds/Injuries:**
- Injured by manticore claws (ch07).
- Acid burns on his chest from ochre jelly (ch03); chest smelled like vinegar and hot metal.
- Bruise from an orc's javelin (ch04), mostly shrugged off.
- Raw knuckles (ch03).

**Key Relationships:**
- **Vukradin:** His closest companion. Brewbarry rated Vukradin's music 10/10 on the piano bar survey — the only patron ever to do so — and was the only customer who ever paid Vukradin. Follows him because "the talking is the music, and the music is why I follow him." Vukradin calls him "my number one fan." Brewbarry's job: "standing in front of Vukradin and taking the hit."
- **Valphine:** Travels with her. Understands her love of pain; stopped arguing about it long ago. Saw her take a dagger and a holy symbol, said nothing. Noticed she was "worn thin," carrying a mace low all day.
- **Soma:** Travels with. Told Soma a simplified version of his connection to Vukradin.
- **Adabra:** Met at the windmill. She told him he is "part of the natural world," which seemed to ease his hurt at being called barbaric.
- **Corbin:** Pushed him to answer Vukradin's question about armor (ch07).

**Defining Recent Actions:**
- Killed a manticore and waved its dead tail at a woman (ch07).
- Killed a bandit with a decapitating halberd swing, then dropped his halberd and shouted "Friends" to a spider-fearing bandit, apologizing for the Uthgardt's treatment of him: "I am so sorry that the Uthgardt hurt you. They were mean to me, too." Felt sadness, believing the bandit had lost his tribe.
- Killed an ogre (hence "Brewbarry, killer of Ogres!"), fought jellies, a mimic, and orcs.
- Refused to let Valphine torture: "We talk! We cure!"

**Revealed Motivations/Secrets:**
- Cast out from his tribe for failing to guard a sacred thing, which disappeared while he was sober; he only took up drinking afterward.
- Has Uthgardt heritage ("goliath blood, older than the tribe that threw me out") and has heard "worse insults... from Uthgardt mouths."
- Dislikes drums (from his tribe's practices) but loves music.
- Healed/comforted wounds aside, he feels sadness for those who have lost their tribe, reflecting his own exile.

## Uncertainty

- **Class ambiguity:** Facts show he bears a Lathander holy symbol and is called "a drow cleric," but also says "I do not have attack spells. I have a halberd," and repeatedly refers to himself as a barbarian without rage. Whether he is *both* a cleric and a barbarian (or one mislabeled) is unresolved.
- **Halberd status after mimic:** The halberd was stuck in the mimic at one point, then used later to kill a bandit — presumably recovered, but no explicit recovery fact was given.
- **Rage usage:** He "keeps forgetting to rage" in ch04, yet rage is described in the same chapter ("Rage settles into his shoulders"); whether he has reliable access to rage as a class feature is unclear.
- **Current party composition:** He travels with Vukradin, Valphine, and Soma, but no later-chapter fact confirms all are still with him at ch07; only his presence with Vukradin at the windmill is certain.
- **Chronology of ch07 clarifications:** The "only customer who ever paid Vukradin" fact appears in ch07 and contradicts the ch02 framing (rating him 10/10); both may be true and distinct actions, but the exact relationship is left implicit.
