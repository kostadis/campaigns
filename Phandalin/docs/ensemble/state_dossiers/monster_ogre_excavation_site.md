### Ogre (Excavation Site)

**Current status:** Dead (second ogre killed by the party; the first ogre was also killed by Valphine's Sacred Flame).

**Current location:** The second ogre died in the mountain pass where it blocked the party's path. The first ogre died on a hillside.

**Allegiance/faction:** None apparent; both ogres were solitary and wild, not chained, collared, or pursued.

**Current possessions / notable items:**
- Second ogre wielded a greataxe, which it used to take off one of its own toes mid-fight; it also tasted poison from Soma's Poison Spray.
- First ogre carried a heavy club.
- Neither ogre had chains, a collar, or any sign of servitude/escape.

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**
- Second ogre was the brother of the first ogre killed by the party ("You killed my brother!").
- Second ogre wanted to eat Soma specifically, declaring "I like turtle."
- Both ogres regarded the party as prey/threats.

**Defining recent actions (collapsed):**
- First ogre: encountered on a hill crest, whistling cheerfully; took offense at being called a musician/slave ("Me? Musician? Music? No. Me kill you."); survived a Sacred Flame, staggered from a halberd, absorbed a Poison Spray without lasting weakness, then was killed by Valphine's Sacred Flame.
- Second ogre: blocked the mountain pass; refused food offered by Soma ("I like turtle."); was unfazed by intimidation ("Nobody frightens the ogre."); killed its brother; was struck by Poison Spray (coughing, staggering, tasting poison), a walloping bolt (knocked prone), and Vukradin's Command (halted, immobile); its greataxe missed Valphine and severed its own toe; it remained prone, badly hurt, and furious before being killed by the party.

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**
- Second ogre was driven by hunger and sought to eat turtle meat (specifically Soma, whom it called "turtle").
- Second ogre declared "I, eternal elf." after intimidation attempts—an odd, unexplained self-identification.
- The ogres were brothers and appear to have been present near the excavation site region.

## Uncertainty
- **Identity/link between the two ogres:** The first (hillside) ogre and second (road) ogre are clearly both dead, and the second refers to the first as its brother, so that relationship is certain.
- **"Eternal elf" remark:** The meaning/context of "I, eternal elf." is unclear — whether it is a self-claim, a slur, or a mistranslation is not resolvable from the facts.
- **Exact death blow for the second ogre:** The facts state the second ogre was "killed by the party" but do not specify which attack or character delivered the killing blow; the quote only references "Just yesterday we took out one of your brothers," which is also chronologically odd — the first ogre was killed earlier in the same chapter, not necessarily "yesterday." This suggests possible time compression or that the quote refers to a prior encounter not listed here.
