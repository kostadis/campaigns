### Zariel

- **Current status:** Alive (last known state).
- **Current location:** Avernus (her kingdom).
- **Allegiance/faction:** Archdevil ruler of Avernus (implied — "her kingdom").
- **Current assignment / role:** Archdevil of Avernus.
- **Key relationships:** Tiamat — an unwanted presence in Zariel's domain; Zariel seeks to deal with her. Gods — subject to their rules, which contain a loophole Zariel exploited.
- **Defining recent actions:** Zariel discovered a loophole in the gods' rules, which she used to devise a plan to bring Tiamat into the world. However, contrary to simply enabling Tiamat's attempt, Zariel had **other plans of her own** for Tiamat — she is not acting on Tiamat's behalf but pursuing a separate scheme.
- **Revealed motivations / secrets:** Tired of Tiamat's presence in Avernus; motivated to remove or counter her. Her true plan for Tiamat is not yet detailed.

## Uncertainty
- **Attribution of the plan:** The facts state both that Zariel "devised the plan to bring Tiamat into the world" and that she "had other plans for Tiamat's attempt" / "other plans for dealing with Tiamat." These could be a single plan (using Tiamat's arrival as leverage) or two distinct schemes; the facts do not clarify how they relate.
- **Tiamat's current presence:** It is clear Zariel *was* tired of Tiamat's presence in Avernus, but whether Tiamat is currently *in* Avernus or has already been brought into the world is not stated.
