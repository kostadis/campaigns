### Zariel

- **Current status:** Alive; active and in control of events.
- **Current location:** Her kingdom of Avernus (the first layer of the Nine Hells).
- **Allegiance/faction:** Archdevil ruler of Avernus; acting independently of Tiamat.
- **Key current relationship:** At odds with Tiamat. Zariel is tired of Tiamat's presence in Avernus and is working against Tiamat's designs.
- **Revealed motivations / secrets:** Zariel discovered a loophole in Tiamat's plan — a plan Tiamat believes is hers, but is actually Zariel's. Zariel has her own agenda ("other plans") beyond what Tiamat intends, exploiting this loophole to pursue it.

## Uncertainty

- None. All five facts are consistent and unambiguous; the only mild ambiguity is whether "other plans" refers to a single scheme or multiple, and whether the loophole itself *is* the "other plans"—but both readings are compatible with the same current state.
