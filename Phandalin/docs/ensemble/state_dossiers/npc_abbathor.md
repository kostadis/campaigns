### Abbathor

**Type:** Deity (evil dwarf god of greed)
**Current status:** Active deity — no fact indicates death, defeat, or loss of divinity.
**Current location:** Not bound to any single place; associated with the ruined temple in the canyon.
**Allegiance/faction:** Evil-aligned deity of greed; object of worship for a now-defunct order of clerics in the canyon.
**Current possessions / notable items:** None recorded directly. His priests' red leather vestments remain in a stone wardrobe at the ruined temple.
**Current role and key relationships:** Recipient of a former temple cult in the canyon settlement. One of his clerics was killed when Abbathor failed to hold up a collapsing ceiling over the cleric. Valphine, a current speaker, characterizes Abbathor as "the one dwarf god who does not lie about appetite" and frames his keeping the priest dead as "Greed is good."
**Defining recent actions (collapsed):** Caused an earthquake and avalanche that collapsed parts of his own temple and buried the settlement when the priests' sacrifices were found lacking. His cleric died under a ceiling that collapsed. The Hall of Greed, a sacred space at the temple, was once important to his clerics.
**Revealed motivations/secrets:** Demands sacrifices and punishes inadequacy directly with natural catastrophe. Valphine describes him as a "god of hoarding," one who "could not hold up a ceiling for his own cleric," and references "rationed power. Hidden power. Lolth's grammar in a beard," suggesting themes of withheld/deceptive power.

## Uncertainty
- Whether Abbathor's cleric died *in* the same earthquake/avalanche Abbathor caused (both events involve ceiling collapse and burial, but the facts do not explicitly tie them together).
- Whether the Temple of Abbathor and the settlement remain buried/collapsed as of the latest chapter, or whether any of it was later excavated or restored — facts only describe the collapse, not any subsequent state.
- The identity and status of any surviving Abbathor clerics is unstated; only the dead cleric and the former vestments are mentioned.
- Valphine's relationship to Abbathor (worshipper, critic, or other) is not specified — she speaks about him, but her current allegiance to him is not stated.
