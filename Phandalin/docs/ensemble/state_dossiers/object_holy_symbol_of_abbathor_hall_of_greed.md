### Holy Symbol of Abbathor (Hall of Greed)

**Type:** Object — holy symbol (tiny jeweled dagger on a silver chain) of Abbathor, the dwarven god of greed.

**Current holder:** Valphine, who took it from the corpse and now carries it in her pouch.

**Current condition:** Intact; the dwarf priest's skeleton (its original wearer) remains in the Hall of Greed.

**Description:** A tiny jeweled dagger hanging on a silver chain, inscribed with dwarvish runes reading "Greed is good."

**Value:** Approximately fifty gold pieces.

**Origin:** Recovered from around the neck vertebrae of a dwarf priest's skeleton in the Hall of Greed.

## Uncertainty
- None. All facts are consistent and unambiguous regarding the symbol's description, origin, value, and current possession by Valphine.
