### Gnerkli

**Current status:** Alive. Co-King of Gnomengarde alongside King Korboz, in a dual monarchy system. No seniority between the two kings; they argue until a decision is made, and the best argument wins. They would elect another king if one were needed.

**Current location:** Gnomengarde (cave system).

**Allegiance/faction:** Co-ruler of Gnomengarde.

**Current possessions / notable items:** Owns a **hat of wizardry**, which he was seen giving to the adventurers (Vukradin's party) during a negotiation in chapter 6.

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Co-King of Gnomengarde alongside King Korboz. The dual monarchy exists to prevent despotic autocratic decision-making by a single individual who could be rendered unfit by a shapeshifter—a direct institutional response to the shapeshifter threat.

**Defining recent actions:**
- Offered the party a hat of wizardry as payment to kill the shapeshifter terrorizing the cave system (ch05).
- Speculated the creature is not bipedal and would hide among big objects it could shapeshift into, such as the cellar with many barrels (ch05).
- In ch06, during a negotiation with Vukradin, insisted "I am the king," explained the dual monarchy system, and cut off Vukradin to clarify the two-kings rationale; angrily handed over the hat with "Here! And go!" and asked Vukradin where he learned about government.

**Revealed motivations/secrets:** Values collective decision-making over single-rule; the dual-king structure is a deliberate safeguard against shapeshifter infiltration. Willing to part with his hat of wizardry to solve the creature problem.
