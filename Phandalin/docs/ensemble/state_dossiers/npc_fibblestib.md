### Fibblestib

**Current status:** Alive, present in Gnomengarde
**Current location:** Gnomengarde
**Allegiance/faction:** Gnomengarde inventors; works with Dabbledob (coworker)
**Current role/assignment:** Foremost inventor focused on curing King Korboz's madness
**Key relationships:** Coworker of Dabbledob; made proposals to adventurers Valphine and Vukradin
**Current possessions/notable items/inventions:**
- Has a rotating blades invention (claims it's the "better option" for dealing with threats)
- No piano currently, but offers to build one (non-normal, but functional for music)
- Wants to build a sanity ray as a cure approach
- Rejects Dabbledob's straitjacket proposal (calls it "a shirt with no holes for the hands")
**Defining recent actions:**
- Introduced himself and Dabbledob as Gnomengarde's foremost inventors
- Demonstrated/testing inventions against Vukradin, nearly killing him with rotating blades and treating it as proof of superiority
- Argued with Dabbledob over approach: sanity ray vs. straitjacket
- Offered to build a piano for the adventurers, warning it won't be ordinary
**Revealed motivations/beliefs:**
- Believes gnomes "invent their way out of illness" — sees invention as the solution to King Korboz's madness
- Views near-death experiences as valid proof of an invention's worth
