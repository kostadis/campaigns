### Flyer (Phandalin)

**Type:** Printed flyer / recruitment notice

**Current state:** Physical paper flyer circulating in or near Phandalin.

**Contents:** 
- Announces the end of the War of the Giants and the War of the Dragons.
- Declares Neverwinter's hinterland in need of adventurers and heroes.
- Offers peace and prosperity to anyone willing to march south and secure the hinterland.
- Promises easy money near Phandalin.

**Physical details:** Has an ale stain near the bottom, caused by Brewbarry during his time at the piano bar.

**Significance:** Recruitment advertisement aimed at adventurers, urging them to march south to secure Neverwinter's hinterland.

## Uncertainty
- "Easy money" is stated in one fact but not directly quoted in the verbatim source for that specific phrase; the quote provided supports the ale stain detail only. The claim that the flyer promises easy money is taken from the collated fact, not the quote.
- The current holder or specific posting location of the flyer is not indicated in the facts.
