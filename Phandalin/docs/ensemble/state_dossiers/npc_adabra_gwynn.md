### Adabra Gwynn

- **Current status:** Alive, at her windmill on Umbrage Hill.
- **Current location:** The windmill on Umbrage Hill.
- **Allegiance/faction:** Naturalist member of the Emerald Enclave; opposes the interventionist faction within the Enclave.
- **Current possessions / notable items:** Holds a deed to the land on Umbrage Hill (the dwarven house was destroyed before she arrived); tends the dwarf cairns there.
- **Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Townmaster Harbin sent a note to his Lordship asking for help, which was promised; Adabra recognizes the party as that promised help. She has agreed to stay at the windmill, "safely protected by" the party, and will not leave her home (she gave Vukradin a note for Harbin declining to leave). She has offered to help Soma, whom she sees as inadequately trained by her druidic masters.
- **Defining recent actions:** Confirmed she is healing Townmaster Harbin ("Yes."). Fed the party a meal of fresh-baked bread and vegetable/herb stew; offered to make a fantastic meal if they spend the night, asking for help. Refused the word "barbarian" in her house, insisting Brewbarry is a nomad. Claimed the manticore wanted her because she is human (manticores prefer human meat). Declared "barbaric slaughtering of monsters to seize their treasures" a crime. Praised Vukradin's music as blessed, saying Aasimars are touched by the Celestial planes.
- **Revealed motivations / secrets:** Believes the meta-verse is part of the natural order and divine interventions should be accepted. Believes the natural order requires villagers to come to her. Says the interventionists draw lines that make those outside the lines prey to be destroyed. She is hurt by the implication she is a grave robber, insisting she is "quite human." She promised to come support the narrator (a fellow naturalist) if Vukradin is not paid. A woman's voice from the windmill called for help ("Help me!"), though whether this was Adabra is unstated.

## Uncertainty
- Whether the "Help me!" voice from the windmill was Adabra herself is not confirmed in the facts.
- Whether Adabra has already begun healing Townmaster Harbin, or only confirmed she is doing so, is ambiguous (the fact states she "confirms she is healing" him).
- Whether Adabra will actually accompany the party when they leave (she said she will come support the narrator as a fellow naturalist) versus remaining at the windmill is unresolved — she also said she will stay "safely protected by" the party, and refused to leave her home in her note to Harbin.
