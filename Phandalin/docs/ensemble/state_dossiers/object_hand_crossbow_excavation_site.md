### Hand Crossbow (excavation site)

**What it is:** A hand crossbow with a **polished stock** and **compact limbs**, with a trigger positioned under the first joint of the finger. It is currently loaded (or was last used) with **walloping ammunition** taken from the previous ogre.

**Current holder:** Valphine — she acquired it before reaching the Townmaster's Hall in Phandalin and subsequently used it to fire the walloping rounds.

**Current condition:** Functional; in use during the described combat.

**Significance:** Serves as Valphine's personal ranged weapon; her use of it to deliver the ogre-derived walloping ammunition was a notable tactical choice.

## Uncertainty
- The walloping ammunition is described as being loaded and fired in the latest fact — the dossier assumes it was consumed or spent by the shot, but the facts do not state whether any remains.
- Whether Valphine still carries the crossbow after the encounter is not directly stated (only that she acquired and used it in chapter 4).
