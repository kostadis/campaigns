### Lathander

- **Type:** Deity (the sun god), referenced rather than directly present.
- **Current status:** Active deity; worshipped in the present timeline.
- **Allegiance/faction:** The Morninglord; patron of light and the sun.
- **Current relationships/key followers:** Valphine is a cleric of Lathander, bearing his holy symbol. A figure referred to as "Lathander's Cleric" previously cautioned Valphine (about saving a woman's life for 25 gold).
- **Defining recent actions / attributes:**
  - Lathander's light is described as inexhaustible and not bound by small constraints like a ceiling.
  - Referenced as a deity whose light obliterates darkness — Valphine invokes this imagery when vowing to smash an opponent's body.
  - The narrator attributes the quality of Overbrighters' meals to Lathander's glory: the sun-god fuels the ground and the food, making it "just tastier."
- **Revealed motivations/secrets:** None beyond his nature as a radiant, life- and growth-granting sun deity whose light is inexhaustible and world-sustaining.

## Uncertainty
- **Clergy distinction:** "Lathander's Cleric" (ch06) is likely a separate individual from Valphine, but the facts do not explicitly confirm they are different people; only that "Lathander’s Cleric had cautioned me" in Valphine's voice. It is possible Valphine refers to herself in the third person, though context suggests another cleric.
- **Norbus's stance (ch03)** is about Norbus, not Lathander, and reflects a past interpretive struggle; no later fact updates whether Norbus resolved it, so his current attitude toward Lathander's faith is unconfirmed.
