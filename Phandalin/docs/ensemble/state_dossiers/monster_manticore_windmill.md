### Manticore (windmill)

**Current status:** Dead — killed by Brewbarry in the same encounter (ch07).  
**Current location:** The windmill on the hill (specifically, it flew to the second floor, where it was slain).  
**Allegiance/faction:** None — a solitary predator.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None noted.  
**Current assignment / role:** Unassigned; acting on its own predatory instincts.  

**Key relationships / notable interactions:**  
- Targeted the party atop the windmill; offered to spare them in exchange for the human **Corbin** (whom it preferred to eat).  
- Attacked **Valphine** with tail spikes (hit her).  
- Attacked **Brewbarry** with its claws after Brewbarry hit its wing with an axe.  
- Was unaffected by **Vukradin's** spell.  
- Enraged further by the conjured familiar **Soma** offered to it, rather than satisfied.  
- Shouted "Maybe you are tastier" at Brewbarry after being struck by his axe, and later shouted for "meat, blood, and tasty crunch."  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Perched atop the windmill when encountered; fired tail spikes at Valphine.  
- Engaged the party in melee; was injured, flew to the second floor howling in pain, was hit twice more by Brewbarry, then died.  
- Spoke, declaring its preference for human meat (confirmed later by Adabra as a general manticore trait).  

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
- Motivated by hunger; sought a "tasty meal," specifically preferring human flesh.  
- Capable of speech and bargaining (offered to take Corbin in exchange for the party's lives).
