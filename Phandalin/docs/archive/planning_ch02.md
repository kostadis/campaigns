## Active Plots

### 1. White Dragon Rumor (driving thread)
Unconfirmed. Every quest on Phandalin's job board exists because of dragon sightings no PC has personally witnessed. Toblen and the job-board postings are the only sources so far. Species/identity not yet confirmed in play.

### 2. Dwarven Temple — Deeper Interior (open, active objective)
The party fought orcs at the temple's approach but has not gone past the secret doors and partially collapsed rooms described in the ruins. Dazlyn's warning about "something that scared us" (possibly oozes) is unresolved. Sending stones promised as the reward, not yet delivered — the dwarves withhold them until the temple is fully cleared (GM-confirmed 2026-08-17 against the session transcript).

### 3. Gnomengarde Quest (in progress)
Party is en route, currently blocked by a second ogre in a narrow valley pass. Objective: retrieve something from the gnomes usable against the dragon, or sellable to Harbin Wester for 50 gp.

### 4. Umbrage Hill Quest (not yet started)
Adabra Gwynn, an isolated midwife south of town, needs to be urged back to Phandalin given the dragon sightings. Read from the board but not yet picked up.

### 5. Lionshield Coster Supply Disruption (background, unresolved)
Both Soma and Linene independently reference a disrupted supply network — "interdimensional," per Soma's private thought, and "tariffs" plus a Neverwinter Wood magic rumor, per Linene. Explicitly called "a mystery for another day" in the prose itself.
