**Aasimar Bard | Player: David Mendenhall (Dave)**

*Level not stated on the page — presumed 1st (campaign start); do not use the current character sheet's Bard 5 as this snapshot's baseline. Confirm with GM.*

## Party Dynamics

**A brand-new party, barely acquainted.** Vukradin, Brewbarry, Valphine, and Soma have known each other for, at most, a few days of travel plus one shared fight. Soma says it outright after the temple battle: "I just met you guys on the road!" There is no established trust, shorthand, or combat coordination yet — the Ice Knife incident (Soma's AoE spell downing her own cleric) is a first-session coordination failure, not a character flaw being tracked.

**Vukradin already sets the ethical frame, but the others aren't fully bought in.** He refuses to treat adventuring as looting, presses the dwarves on rightful ownership, and tries diplomacy with hostile creatures before violence. Valphine humors this while privately still thinking in terms of advantage ("there may be money here"); Soma is pragmatic rather than aligned or opposed. This is the seed of the dynamic the later, much longer campaign builds on — but at Chapter 2 it reads as one earnest new companion's outlook, not yet a settled party culture.

**Combat is not yet competent.** The temple fight nearly killed Vukradin twice and produced friendly fire. This is a party still finding its footing, not the seasoned unit described in the current (post-Chapter-45) party reference.
