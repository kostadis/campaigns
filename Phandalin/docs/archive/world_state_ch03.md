## Pre-Campaign Background (Chapter 1)

- The Great Sundering is complete, but gods continue to exploit loopholes allowing interference in the world.
- Tiamat used Severin and the Cult of the Dragon in an attempt to enter the world; Zariel had enabled the scheme because she wanted Tiamat out of Avernus.
- The All-Father's plans for giant rule destabilized the North. Iymrith nearly destroyed the giant kingdoms but escaped; Storm Giant King Hekaton ultimately allied giants and the peoples of the Realms. The Kraken Society was exposed but survived, the Lord's Alliance grew stronger, and the sage KP recovered part of an artifact he needed.
- Lolth used Gromph Baenre and altered Faerzress to loosen the boundary between the Abyss and Toril. Graz'zt, Zuggtmoy, Orcus, Demogorgon, and other demon lords entered the Underdark; Menzoberranzan was among the devastated cities.
- The Underdark chaos may have prompted an Uthgardt invasion of the North. Triboar was razed, Calling Horn destroyed, and settlements near Silverymoon and Mirabar pillaged. The restored Ordning, revived Silvery Marches alliance, giants, and major cities ended the invasion.
- Lord Neverember then turned to Phandalin's hinterland. Unable to afford a standing army after the wars, he offered adventurers tax-free, no-questions-asked plunder to clear monsters, cults, and evildoers. Vukradin, an underappreciated avant-garde musician, was one of those adventurers.

## Campaign State

Vukradin, Valphine, Soma, and Brewbarry are an adventuring party. At the opening of the accepted Chapter 3 source they are already exploring an ancient dwarven ruin for prospectors Dazlyn and Norbus under a standing agreement to clear the site in exchange for sending stones. The source set does not establish how the party formed, met the dwarves, or reached the ruin.

During the session the party destroyed multiple ochre jellies, explored rooms and collapsed passages associated with priests of Abbathor, found and divided a cache of gemstones with the dwarves, discovered the Hall of Greed and its magical green gem, and completed its bargain. Dazlyn and Norbus admitted they were recently self-declared amateur archaeologists and handed over the sending stones.

The party left the ruins for town, believing a 50 gp dragon-warning payment awaited them. Four hostile orcs intercepted them on the road. Chapter 3 ends before the first attack, with nobody surprised and the party depleted by the excavation.

## The Party

### Vukradin
*Bard and musician | alive | road from the dwarven ruins*

- Chapter 1 identifies him as an underappreciated avant-garde musician participating in Neverember's adventurer program.
- In Chapter 3 he repeatedly chooses disclosure over private profit: he reports archaeological finds to Dazlyn and Norbus and insists on negotiating a fair division of the fifteen gemstones.
- He remembers that piercing weapons do not divide ochre jellies and becomes unexpectedly effective with paired daggers.
- He wants to parley with the four road orcs despite the party's exhaustion.
- He does not know Valphine took the priest's holy symbol.

### Valphine
*Drow cleric of Lathander | alive | road from the dwarven ruins*

- Keeps companions alive with healing magic and fights with sacred radiance and a mace.
- Secretly pockets Abbathor's jeweled-dagger holy symbol while Vukradin is out of sight. Soma and Brewbarry witness the theft.
- Tries to underreport the gemstone count during negotiations with the dwarves.
- Detects magic on the Hall of Greed's green gem but, after a failed investigation, tells the party it is probably worthless glass.
- Speaks Orcish and is ready to translate at the road encounter, although the summary marks one nearby line's speaker attribution as unconfirmed because of unreliable diarization.

### Soma
*Tortle druid and self-described hired guide | alive | road from the dwarven ruins*

- Uses Earth Tremor to control the altar-room battlefield, survives being knocked unconscious, and later uses Mold Earth to reveal the Hall of Greed.
- Pragmatic about danger and willing to flee when survival demands it.
- Frustrated by Vukradin's determination to disclose treasure, but participates in the negotiated split.
- Has exhausted most or all leveled spell resources by the time the party meets the road orcs.

### Brewbarry
*Barbarian | alive | road from the dwarven ruins*

- Functions as the party's close-combat anchor. His halberd initially worsens the jelly threat by splitting one, after which he switches to bludgeoning attacks.
- Survives heavy acid strikes and helps destroy both groups of jellies.
- Witnesses Valphine take the holy symbol but does not expose her during this session.

## Major NPCs

### Dazlyn
*Alive | dwarven excavation*

A dwarf prospector and recently self-declared amateur archaeologist. Concerned for the party during combat but remained outside the dangerous rooms. Excited by the Hall of Greed, participated in the gemstone split, and delivered the sending stones when the ruins were cleared.

### Norbus
*Alive | dwarven excavation*

Dazlyn's partner, also a prospector and amateur archaeologist. More visibly worried about damage to the site than willing to enter the fight. Offered food, lodging, and stories to persuade the party to finish clearing the complex. Delivered the sending stones with Dazlyn.

### Four road orcs
*Alive at session end | road from the dwarven ruins*

Armed warriors who crest a hill and prepare to attack the party. Their origin, larger affiliation, and reason for attacking are not established before the session ends.

## Locations

### Dwarven excavation / temple of Abbathor

An ancient subterranean complex with a blood-stained altar, concealed masonry, stone bedrooms, a font and wardrobe containing rotted red vestments, collapsed passages, the remains of a dwarf priest, and the Hall of Greed. Dazlyn and Norbus had spent months prospecting there before declaring themselves archaeologists.

### Hall of Greed

A shrine devoted to Abbathor. Its intact northern statue is a horned dwarf staring greedily at a glowing green gem; the southern matching statue is shattered and its gem absent. The intact gem remains in place and radiates magic. The GM identifies it as an emerald, while the party believes it may be glass.

### Road to town

Hilly terrain outside the excavation. Four orcs confront the party here at Chapter 3's end.

## Items and Durable State

- **Sending stones:** Pair now in the party's possession; payment for clearing the ruins.
- **Eight small gemstones:** Party's share of fifteen gems, each worth 10 gp; Dazlyn and Norbus took seven.
- **Holy symbol of Abbathor:** Jeweled dagger on a silver chain, inscribed "greed is good," worth 50 gp; secretly held by Valphine.
- **Glowing green emerald:** Magical, left in the Hall of Greed. Its true identity is GM knowledge, not party knowledge.
- **Rotted priestly vestments and dwarf remains:** Left within the ruins; no durable value established.
