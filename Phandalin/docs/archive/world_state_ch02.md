## Pre-Campaign Background (Chapter 1)

Chapter 1 is a third-person in-world lore excerpt ("from the Great Sage Konstantinious the 12th"), not a scene with player characters. It establishes the world state the campaign opens into:

- The Great Sundering is complete; the gods keep finding loopholes to meddle anyway.
- **Tiamat/Zariel:** Tiamat used Severin and the Cult of the Dragon to try to enter the world; the scheme was secretly Zariel's, who wanted Tiamat out of Avernus.
- **The All-Father (god of giants):** Fantasized about giant rule of the North; the chaos this created nearly let the Great Ancient Blue Dragon (Iymrith) destroy the giant kingdoms. The sage KP recovered a piece of an artifact he needed; the Kraken Society was revealed and survived; the Lord's Alliance grew; Storm Giant King Hekaton allied giants and the peoples of the Realms. Iymrith escaped.
- **Lolth:** Trapped in a demiplane, used Gromph Baenre of House Baenre and a spell twisting the Faerzess to loosen the boundary between the Abyss and Toril. Demon lords (Grazz't, Zuggtmoy, Orcus, Demogorgon) poured into the Underdark, devastating Menzoberranzan among other cities.
- **Uthgardt invasion:** The Underdark chaos likely provoked an Uthgardt Barbarian assault on the North — Triboar razed, Called Horn destroyed, villages near Silverymoon and Mirabar pillaged. The Ordning's reinstatement kept the Giants from joining the assault; the Silvery Marches alliance was revived via agents from Everlund. The combined Giant/city forces ended the invasion.
- **Present day:** With the Barbarian threat over, Lord Neverember has turned to clearing his hinterland — specifically Phandalin — of monsters, cults, and evildoers, using tax-free-plunder adventurers rather than a standing army (which the wars made too costly to field). Vukradin is one such adventurer.

## Campaign State

The party has just formed and arrived in Phandalin. Vukradin (Aasimar bard, disillusioned Neverwinter busker), Brewbarry (Goliath barbarian, exiled from his adopted Uthgardt tribe, devoted fan of Vukradin's music), Valphine Sotorra (drow cleric of Lathander, ex-Lolth follower, drawn to Vukradin out of theological curiosity), and Soma (tortle druid, hired as a wilderness guide en route) traveled together from Neverwinter to Phandalin. They took on two of the three job-board quests posted by Townmaster Harbin Wester — all driven by an unconfirmed rumor of a white dragon in the area. They delivered a dragon warning to two dwarf prospectors (Dazlyn Grayshard and Norbus Ironrune) at a ruined settlement southwest of town, fought off a group of orcs guarding the ruins' temple entrance, collected their reward, sold looted goods at the Lionshield Coster, killed one ogre en route, and are now mid-combat with a second ogre blocking the mountain path to Gnomengarde. No dragon has been seen on the page yet.

## The Party

### Vukradin
*Aasimar Bard | alive | en route to Gnomengarde*

> **Note on level/mechanics:** The character sheet on file (`characters/vukradin.md`) reflects the character's *current, much later* build (Bard 5, College of Eloquence). Chapter 2 is the party's first adventuring session in the fiction — do **not** backdate the level-5 sheet to this snapshot. The prose does not state a starting level; presumed 1st level per standard campaign-start convention, **unconfirmed — GM should confirm before this feeds any regenerated chapter-3+ document.**

- **Drive:** Make honest coin through music; deep discomfort with the "tax-free plunder" premise of adventuring, which he reads as legalized theft/money laundering. Already insists on returning stolen goods to rightful owners (pressed Dazlyn and Norbus on this before agreeing to help them).
- **Established this chapter:** Was a working (unsuccessful) musician at the First Flophouse in Neverwinter's Heapside; quit on the page. Prefers diplomacy first, even with hostile creatures — tried to parley with orcs at the temple and to reason with/intimidate an ogre, both times ineffectively and at cost to himself (dropped to "death's door" twice in the orc fight).
- **Key items:** Clarinet/flute (musical instrument), rapier (per character sheet's baseline kit — not independently confirmed on the page this chapter).
- **Recent:** Recruited Brewbarry and (unintentionally) Valphine at the Flophouse; chose the Dwarven Excavation quest over Gnomengarde despite Valphine's interest in plunder, on ethical/payout grounds; accepted the dwarves' sending-stones offer once satisfied artifacts would go to "rightful owners."
- **Uncertainty:** Starting level/class features not confirmed on the page (see note above).

### Soma
*Tortle Druid | alive | en route to Gnomengarde*

- **Drive:** Not yet strongly characterized on the page beyond guiding the party safely and pragmatically; backstory (`docs/Soma - Backstory.md`) establishes her as a semi-retired wilderness guide, trained late in life by a visiting elven druid named Meril, who left her an ash staff (the future "Meril's Staff") as a parting gift.
- **Established this chapter:** Met the party just outside Neverwinter's city limits and offered to guide them; knows the roads (High Road safe, Triboar Trail riskier but she knows how to avoid trouble); privately keeps a white-dragon rumor to herself early in the chapter rather than alarming the others.
- **Key spells (seen in play):** Thorn Whip, Poison Spray, Ice Knife.
- **Recent:** Guided the party to Phandalin and toward Gnomengarde; killed an orc with Poison Spray at the temple; accidentally downed Valphine with an AoE Ice Knife during the same fight, then healed her; remarked afterward the party is "not a well-oiled machine yet."
- **Uncertainty:** Full personality still forming on the page — currently reads as dry, practical, mildly bemused by her new companions.

### Valphine Sotorra
*Drow Cleric (Lathander) | alive | en route to Gnomengarde*

- **Drive:** Openly a devoted, if theologically unorthodox, cleric of Lathander — drawn to Vukradin as a possible sign ("A symbol of good. A symbol of the celestials.") and resolved to "observe" whether there's something to his worldview she doesn't yet understand.
- **Established this chapter:** Ex-follower of Lolth; fled Menzoberranzan's fall to the surface, was converted by a chance encounter with a cleric of Lathander. Struggles openly with the gap between Drow pragmatism (schemes rewarded, murder acceptable if unpunished) and Lathander's actual teachings (violence/assassination against people is wrong; monsters are fair game). Still fundamentally combat-pragmatic under the theology — privately considered letting Vukradin die in the orc fight before healing him.
- **Key spells (seen in play):** Healing (unspecified spell, used on Vukradin twice), an offensive divine blast ("raw blast of coruscating divine energy").
- **Recent:** Followed Vukradin out of the Flophouse uninvited; suggested the sending-stones pitch that won Vukradin over to helping the dwarves; used a magic bullet looted from Ogre #1 to knock the second ogre prone.
- **Note on naming:** The chapter alternates between "Valphine Sotorra" (scenes 02.02, 02.05, 02.08, 02.11) and plain "Valphine" (elsewhere, including 02.12) — a known inconsistency, not a distinct second character. See `docs/chapter_provenance.md`.

## Major NPCs

### Dazlyn Grayshard
*Alive | ancient dwarven ruins SW of Phandalin*

- Shield dwarf prospector; business partner of Norbus Ironrune. Found the ruined settlement while prospecting for gold; spent months clearing rubble and searching for treasure. Identified the site as an old temple of Abbathor (evil dwarf god of greed), destroyed by an avalanche/earthquake he attributes to the god punishing an insufficient sacrifice. Offered the party sending stones (rather than coin, which they don't have) in exchange for dealing with something deeper in the temple that scared him and Norbus off — possibly oozes, by his own uncertain account.

### Norbus Ironrune
*Alive | ancient dwarven ruins SW of Phandalin*

- Shield dwarf prospector; business partner of Dazlyn Grayshard. More cautious/gruff than Dazlyn on the page. Confirmed to Vukradin that any finds would go to their rightful owners when directly asked.

### Toblen Stonehill
*Alive | Stonehill Inn, Phandalin*

- Proprietor of the Stonehill Inn; native of Triboar; came to Phandalin to prospect but found he was better suited to innkeeping. Paid Vukradin in food/drink for busking. Warned the party of a white dragon rumor as they left for the job board.

### Harbin Wester
*Alive | Townmaster's Hall, Phandalin*

- Townmaster; posts jobs on the board outside his hall. Has not been seen face-to-face on the page — every interaction so far is through a locked door, with him fearfully assuming any knock might be a dragon ("If you're a dragon, know that I'm far too thin and bony to make a good meal!"). Paid the 50 gp Dwarven Excavation reward promptly. Brushed off Vukradin's "grave robbers" characterization of Dazlyn and Norbus by citing Lord Neverember's tax-free-plunder guarantee.

### Linene Graywind
*Alive | Lionshield Coster, Phandalin*

- Master of the Lionshield Coster outpost. Bought the party's looted orc gear. Noted (without elaborating) that her preferred mode of transporting goods has been disrupted, and mentioned a rumor that "strange magic pervades Neverwinter Wood, confounding navigators and obscuring the ancient ruins of bygone kingdoms lost in its depths." Left as an open mystery on the page.
