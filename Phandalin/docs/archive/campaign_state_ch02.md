## Completed Encounters & Quests

- **Party Formation (Neverwinter, the Heapside, First Flophouse):** Vukradin quit busking at the Flophouse in frustration; Brewbarry (a regular, fan of his music) followed him out. Valphine, drinking in the same bar, followed the pair out of curiosity about the Aasimar. Soma (a tortle druid) was encountered just outside the city limits heading south and offered to guide the group to Phandalin.
- **Journey Neverwinter → Phandalin:** Uneventful. High Road safe post-barbarian-incursion; Triboar Trail riskier but Soma navigated it without incident. (Soma privately noted a white-dragon rumor to herself but did not share it with the party at this point.)
- **Arrival in Phandalin / Stonehill Inn:** Party met innkeeper Toblen Stonehill. Vukradin busked for food; Toblen warned the party of a white dragon rumor in the area.
- **Job Board Read (Townmaster's Hall):** Vukradin read all three posted quests aloud (Dwarven Excavation, Gnomengarde, Umbrage Hill). Confirmed with (unseen, voice-only) Townmaster Harbin Wester that the rewards are real. Party chose the Dwarven Excavation quest first (Vukradin: highest payout; Valphine privately wanted Gnomengarde for plunder potential).
- **Dwarven Excavation Quest — Warn Dazlyn Grayshard & Norbus Ironrune of the dragon:** Delivered. The two shield-dwarf prospectors, found at the canyon ruins southwest of Phandalin, offered a follow-up job (clear something deeper in the ruined temple) in exchange for a pair of sending stones rather than coin.
- **Dwarven Excavation — Orc Encounter (temple approach):** Party (initially attempting to parley — Vukradin's idea) fought a group of orcs guarding the passage into the temple interior. Vukradin was dropped twice and healed by Valphine both times; Soma's Ice Knife accidentally caught and downed Valphine, who Soma then healed. Brewbarry raged and finished the fight. All orcs in this group killed; weapons looted. Dazlyn and Norbus cooked the party a meal afterward.
- **Return to Phandalin — Dwarven Quest Reward Collected:** Harbin Wester paid the promised 50 gp. Vukradin also editorialized to Harbin that the dwarves looked like "grave robbers"; Harbin brushed this off ("His lordship guarantees every adventurer tax-free plunder!").
- **Loot Sold at Lionshield Coster:** Orc weapons and hide armor sold. Party met Linene Graywind, master of the outpost, who noted her supply network has been disrupted (see Active Quests & Open Threads).
- **Ogre Encounter #1 (en route, between Phandalin and the dwarven ruins):** A hungry ogre (initially mistaken by Vukradin for a musician/possible escaped slave) attacked. Killed by the party (Brewbarry melee, Soma poison spray, Valphine and Vukradin support). Soma searched the corpse afterward and "picks up something" — the item is not named on the page; Vukradin chose not to ask what it was.
- **Gnomengarde Quest — Departure:** Soma led the party south then up into the Sword Mountains toward Gnomengarde.
- **Ogre Encounter #2 (narrow mountain valley en route to Gnomengarde) — IN PROGRESS, unresolved at chapter's end:** A second, hungry ogre blocked a 5-ft.-wide valley path and demanded to eat Soma ("I want turtle!"). Vukradin's attempt to intimidate it failed and provoked it further. Combat began; Valphine used one of the magic bullets looted from Ogre #1 to knock this ogre prone. **Chapter 2 ends here — the ogre's fate (killed, fled, etc.) is not narrated within this chapter.** (Do not backfill this from later documents when building this archive; the resolution belongs to whatever chapter narrates it next.)

## Active Quests & Open Threads

**Gnomengarde Quest — in progress**
Party is en route; currently mid-combat with the second ogre blocking the mountain path. Objective per job board: get whatever useful anti-dragon item(s) the gnomes of Gnomengarde have; 50 gp from Harbin Wester if the party doesn't want to keep it themselves.

**Umbrage Hill Quest — not yet started**
Posted on the job board (urge the midwife Adabra Gwynn, alone in a windmill south of town, back to Phandalin given dragon sightings; 25 gp from Harbin Wester on confirmation). Read aloud by the party but not picked up yet.

**Dwarven Temple — deeper interior unexplored**
Dazlyn and Norbus asked the party to go deeper into the temple (behind the canyon settlement, carved into the canyon's back wall — an old temple of Abbathor, evil dwarf god of greed) and "deal with" something that scared them off. Dazlyn: "I think we saw some oozes in there, but I could be wrong." The party fought orcs at the temple's approach/entrance but has not yet gone past the secret doors and passageways described (partially collapsed rooms, defensive blocks, an unexplored secret door to the south). Sending stones were offered as the reward for this follow-up job, not the original 50 gp.

**White Dragon — unconfirmed rumor, not yet encountered**
The entire current quest line (all three job-board postings) is driven by dragon sightings that no one in the party has personally witnessed. Toblen warned the party as they left the inn. Source and species of the "white dragon" are unconfirmed on the page.

**Lionshield Coster / interdimensional supply disruption**
Both Soma (privately, ch02.03) and Linene Graywind (ch02.12) reference disrupted trade — Soma calls it an "interdimensional supply chain" issue forcing Lionshield to use ordinary suppliers; Linene mentions unspecified "tariffs" and a rumor of "strange magic" pervading Neverwinter Wood that confounds navigators and obscures ancient ruins. Valphine is curious about what's affecting the Coster's "fabled internal network." Explicitly left as "a mystery for another day" in the prose.

**Sending Stones — promised, not yet delivered**
Offered by Dazlyn and Norbus as the reward for the deeper-temple job, at Valphine's suggestion ("Sending stones would allow you to send your music between two locations" — pitched to win over Vukradin, who was hesitant about the dwarves' ethics). GM-confirmed 2026-08-17 against the session transcript: the dwarves withhold the stones until the temple is fully cleared and hand them over only at the end of that session — see `docs/entity_registry.yaml`'s "reward for clearing the temple" framing, which had this right; this archive's earlier "in party's possession" line was the error.
