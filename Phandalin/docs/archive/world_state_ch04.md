## Pre-Campaign Background (Chapter 1)

- The Great Sundering is complete, but gods continue exploiting loopholes to interfere in the world.
- Tiamat's attempted return, enabled by Zariel and pursued through Severin and the Cult of the Dragon, failed.
- The All-Father's giant crisis nearly allowed Iymrith to destroy the giant kingdoms. Iymrith escaped; Hekaton allied giants and the peoples of the Realms; the Kraken Society survived exposure; the Lord's Alliance strengthened.
- Lolth used Gromph Baenre and altered Faerzress to loosen the Abyss–Toril boundary, allowing demon lords into the Underdark and contributing to Menzoberranzan's devastation.
- An Uthgardt invasion then ravaged the North. Restored alliances, cities, and giants ended it after the Ordning's reinstatement.
- Lord Neverember turned to clearing Phandalin's hinterland with adventurers promised tax-free, no-questions-asked plunder. Vukradin was one such adventurer.

## Campaign State

Vukradin, Valphine, Soma, and Brewbarry have cleared the dwarven excavation, found the Hall of Greed, divided a small-gem cache with Dazlyn and Norbus, and received sending stones. On leaving the site they were attacked by four orcs. They killed the attackers, returned to the excavation to recover, and then killed a hostile whistling ogre on the road to Phandalin.

In Phandalin, Harbin Wester paid 50 gp for the completed dragon-warning task, defended Neverember's permissive plunder policy, and offered two new quests. The party chose the 50 gp Gnomengarde job over a less-defined midwife job. They sold orc equipment, learned of strange magic disrupting navigation in Neverwinter Wood, stayed at the Stonehill Inn, and equipped Valphine with a hand crossbow.

The party is now en route to Gnomengarde. A hungry ogre has attacked in a narrow mountain pass. At Chapter 4's end the creature remains alive but is badly damaged, prone from walloping ammunition, and temporarily halted by Vukradin's Command. Valphine acts next.

## The Party

### Vukradin
*Bard and avant-garde musician | alive | mountain pass to Gnomengarde*

- Continues to prefer dialogue over violence, offering the road orcs food, shelter, and music before they attack and later trying to negotiate with the hungry ogre.
- Is knocked unconscious twice in the orc battle and revived by Valphine.
- Treats the orc axes as probable blood money and declines a share of their sale proceeds.
- Challenges Harbin's claim that Neverember's authority makes unrestricted ruin-plunder ethical; Harbin becomes visibly anxious.
- Performs original music for Dazlyn, Norbus, and later Stonehill Inn patrons. Reactions range from encouragement to indifference.
- Uses Command to halt the hungry ogre after Valphine knocks it prone.

### Valphine
*Drow cleric of Lathander | alive | mountain pass to Gnomengarde*

- Revives Vukradin during the orc battle, then is knocked unconscious by Soma's Ice Knife and stabilized after the fight.
- Notices Harbin's reaction to Vukradin is more anxious than simple annoyance.
- Acquires a hand crossbow in Phandalin and uses it to deliver walloping ammunition, knocking the hungry ogre prone.
- Still secretly holds Abbathor's holy symbol from Chapter 3; Soma and Brewbarry know, Vukradin does not.
- Has the next action when Chapter 4 ends.

### Soma
*Tortle druid and guide | alive | mountain pass to Gnomengarde*

- Uses Thorn Whip to drag an orc down a twenty-foot incline and Poison Spray repeatedly against orcs and ogres.
- Her Ice Knife misses its intended enemies and knocks Valphine unconscious; she recognizes the failure immediately.
- Guides the party toward Gnomengarde and becomes the hungry ogre's chosen target.
- Briefly considers a Mold Earth rockslide in the pass, then abandons it because of the terrain and ally positioning.

### Brewbarry
*Barbarian | alive | mountain pass to Gnomengarde*

- Remains the party's front-line anchor. He kills the final two road orcs, absorbs a critical blow from the whistling ogre, contributes to that ogre's death, and strikes the prone hungry ogre while raging.
- His accepted-source characterization is primarily action, endurance, and combat reliability; long-term backstory remains unestablished because Chapter 2 is excluded.

## Major NPCs

### Dazlyn and Norbus
*Alive | dwarven excavation*

Amateur dwarf archaeologists who honored the sending-stone bargain. When the wounded party returned after the orc fight, the dwarves had been hiding and considering what to do about an unspecified gem they thought the party carried. Once reassured, they offered food and shelter. Dazlyn disliked Vukradin's original song; Norbus encouraged him. Their exact intentions regarding the gem remain ambiguous.

### Harbin Wester
*Alive | Townmaster's Hall, Phandalin*

Fearful townmaster who keeps his door locked against a possible dragon. Recognized Vukradin, paid the party 50 gp for the dragon-warning task, defended Neverember's plunder authorization, and reacted to Vukradin's moral challenge with unexplained anxiety. Offered quests involving Gnomengarde and a midwife.

### Linene
*Alive | Lionshield Coster, Phandalin*

Merchant who bought intact hide armor for 10 gp. Reports disrupted trade routes and repeats a rumor that strange magic in Neverwinter Wood confounds navigators and obscures ancient ruins. She explains the disruption in general market terms but does not establish its cause.

### Toblen Stonehill
*Alive | Stonehill Inn, Phandalin*

Warmly welcomes the party and asks Vukradin to perform on clarinet. The inn's patrons provide no useful new rumor during this visit.

### Hungry ogre
*Alive, prone, and badly damaged | mountain pass to Gnomengarde*

Hostile creature intent on eating Soma. Claims the party killed its "brother and sister." Valphine's walloping shot knocked it prone and Vukradin's Command temporarily halted it. Kinship and the identity of both claimed relatives are not independently confirmed.

## Locations

### Dwarven excavation and Hall of Greed

The ruin is clear of encountered ochre jellies but still contains collapsed passages. The magical emerald remains in the intact Hall of Greed statue. Dazlyn and Norbus remain on site.

### Phandalin

A frontier town with a Townmaster's Hall, smithy, Lionshield Coster, and Stonehill Inn. Harbin administers quests from behind a locked door. The party has now conducted business and stayed overnight here, but the excluded Chapter 2 prevents assuming any other prior relationships.

### Neverwinter Wood

A nearby forest where strange magic is rumored to confound navigators and obscure old ruins. The party has not entered it in the accepted sources.

### Mountain pass to Gnomengarde

A narrow valley on a fifteen-mile route through difficult hilly terrain. The party's current ogre fight occurs here.
