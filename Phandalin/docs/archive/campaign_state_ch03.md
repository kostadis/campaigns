## Completed Encounters & Quests

- **Dwarven excavation — standing bargain:** At the session's opening, the party was already inside an ancient dwarven ruin being excavated by Dazlyn and Norbus. The accepted source establishes a standing bargain: clear the dangerous interior in exchange for a pair of sending stones. How the party met the dwarves and accepted this bargain is outside the accepted source set.
- **Blood-stained altar / first ochre-jelly battle:** An ochre jelly attacked near a blood-stained altar. Brewbarry's halberd split it; additional jellies joined the fight, including one that dropped from the ceiling. Soma used Earth Tremor to impede them, was knocked unconscious and revived by Valphine, and Vukradin learned to use piercing daggers rather than slashing attacks. All jellies in this encounter were killed.
- **Ruins explored:** The party found three stone bed frames, an empty stone font, rotted red priestly vestments of Abbathor, collapsed passages, and the skeleton of a dwarf priest killed by a collapse.
- **Priest's holy symbol found:** Valphine secretly pocketed a jeweled-dagger holy symbol of Abbathor inscribed "greed is good," worth 50 gp. Soma and Brewbarry saw her take it; Vukradin did not.
- **Skull cavity / gemstone settlement:** A hidden pillar cavity released a cascade of dwarf skulls and contained a stone coffer with fifteen small gemstones worth 10 gp each. Vukradin disclosed the find. After negotiation, the party received eight stones and Dazlyn and Norbus received seven.
- **Second ochre-jelly battle:** A large jelly attacked from a narrow channel behind the altar. The party killed it and its split halves. All ochre jellies encountered in the ruins are dead.
- **Hall of Greed discovered:** Soma cleared rubble with Mold Earth, exposing a shrine of Abbathor. An intact horned-dwarf statue held a glowing green gem. The GM identified it out of character as a magical emerald, but the party's failed investigation left them believing it might be worthless glass. They left it in place. A matching southern statue was shattered and had no gem.
- **Dwarven ruins cleared / sending stones delivered:** Dazlyn and Norbus admitted they were amateur archaeologists, accepted the party's report, and handed over the promised sending stones. Their bargain with the party is complete.
- **Departure from the ruins:** The party left for town, framing the trip as an attempt to collect payment for delivering a dragon warning. Four orcs then intercepted them on the road. The session ended as combat was about to begin.
