## Characters

### Vukradin

**Bard and avant-garde musician**

**Personality and motivations:** An ethical idealist who tries conversation before violence and treats provenance as morally meaningful. He offers the road orcs aid, food, shelter, and a choice of instruments; later offers the hungry ogre rations. He labels the looted axes probable blood money, declines their proceeds, and openly challenges Harbin's use of Neverember's authority to justify plunder.

**Behavior under pressure:** His diplomacy repeatedly puts him in danger. The road orcs knock him unconscious twice, but after recovery he continues using ranged magic and control rather than abandoning his principles. He casts Command to halt the hungry ogre.

**Music:** Performs an original flute composition for Dazlyn and Norbus and later an original clarinet piece at the Stonehill Inn. Norbus is encouraging, Dazlyn unimpressed, and the inn performance unremarkable.

**Relationships:**

- *Valphine* — relies on her healing and remains unaware of her concealed holy symbol
- *Soma* — her practical criticism is increasingly familiar, but she still talks about eventually making him wealthy enough to pay her as a guide
- *Brewbarry* — Brewbarry repeatedly anchors fights that Vukradin's diplomacy cannot prevent
- *Harbin* — transactional relationship complicated by Vukradin's moral challenge and Harbin's anxiety

**Capabilities shown:** Thunderwave, Dissonant Whispers, Starry Wisp, Command, paired daggers, musical performance.

### Valphine

**Drow cleric of Lathander**

**Personality and motivations:** Combines pragmatic opportunism with reliable care for endangered companions. She heals Vukradin after he falls, survives being knocked unconscious by friendly fire, notices Harbin's concealed anxiety, and equips herself for ranged fighting.

**Secrets and knowledge:**

- Secretly holds the holy symbol of Abbathor; Soma and Brewbarry know, Vukradin does not.
- Knows the Hall of Greed gem is magical but shares the party's uncertainty about its value.
- Reads Harbin's response to Vukradin as anxiety beyond ordinary annoyance; the cause is unknown.

**Recent equipment:** Acquires a hand crossbow in Phandalin and successfully uses walloping ammunition to knock the hungry ogre prone.

**Capabilities shown:** Healing Word, Sacred Flame, Detect Magic, mace, hand crossbow, Orcish.

**Current:** Has the next action against the prone, halted ogre.

### Soma

**Tortle druid and guide**

**Personality and motivations:** Practical, dry, and willing to improvise with terrain. She sees danger clearly, criticizes failed heroics, and still positions herself as a guide trying to get Vukradin wealthy enough to pay her.

**Recent:**

- Pulls an orc down a twenty-foot incline with Thorn Whip.
- Accidentally knocks Valphine unconscious with Ice Knife while all intended enemies avoid damage.
- Helps kill the whistling ogre with Poison Spray.
- Guides the party toward Gnomengarde and becomes the hungry ogre's chosen prey.
- Considers causing a rockslide with Mold Earth, but abandons the plan when the positioning and likely effect are poor.
- Damages the current ogre twice with Poison Spray.

**Capabilities shown:** Earth Tremor, Ice Knife, Poison Spray, Mold Earth, Thorn Whip, shell defense.

## Party Dynamics

**Vukradin's ethics now shape shared bookkeeping.** The group explicitly tracks whether loot is blood money. The others may not share his refusal, but his concern has become part of how they record and discuss proceeds.

**Valphine is the party's recovery hinge.** Vukradin survives the orc battle because of her healing. Her own friendly-fire collapse shows that the party still lacks safe spell coordination.

**Brewbarry anchors failed diplomacy.** When Vukradin's negotiations become combat, Brewbarry repeatedly absorbs the enemy's strongest attacks and finishes weakened opponents.

**Soma's practical judgment pushes against idealism.** She tries creative terrain use, calls out tactical failures, and recognizes when an idea is not worth the complexity. Her Ice Knife mishap remains a serious coordination failure, not evidence of malice.

**Secrets remain inside the group.** Soma and Brewbarry know Valphine took Abbathor's holy symbol; Vukradin still does not. No accepted source shows this being resolved.
