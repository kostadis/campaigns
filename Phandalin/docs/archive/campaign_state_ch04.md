### Completed during Chapter 4

- **Road-orc battle:** Vukradin attempted diplomacy and offered music; the orcs interpreted his approach as a sacrificial offering and attacked. Vukradin was knocked unconscious twice. Valphine revived him, was later knocked unconscious by Soma's Ice Knife friendly fire, and Brewbarry killed the final two orcs. All four attackers died.
- **Orc loot and blood-money ruling:** The party recovered a broken hatchet, javelin, hide armor, and four battle axes. The axes were marked as probable blood money in the party ledger. Vukradin declined a share of their proceeds.
- **Return to the excavation to recover:** The party backtracked to Dazlyn and Norbus. The dwarves had been hiding and weighing their options concerning an unspecified gem they believed the party carried. They emerged once reassured, cooked a meal, and hosted the party's long rest. Vukradin performed an original flute composition about the orc fight; Norbus was encouraging and Dazlyn unimpressed.
- **Whistling ogre:** On the road toward Phandalin, the party encountered and killed a hostile ogre. Its pouch contained walloping ammunition, which can knock a struck target prone.
- **Dragon-warning payment collected:** Harbin Wester opened the Townmaster's Hall only after recognizing Vukradin and paid the party 50 gp. Vukradin challenged the ethics of plundering ruins; Harbin cited Lord Neverember's authority and appeared unusually anxious.
- **Phandalin resupply:** The party sold four damaged orc battle axes at the smithy for half price and sold intact hide armor to Linene at the Lionshield Coster for 10 gp. Valphine acquired a hand crossbow. The party paid 1 gp to stay at the Stonehill Inn, where Vukradin performed on clarinet. No useful new inn rumors were learned.
- **Next quest chosen:** Harbin offered a better-paying Gnomengarde quest involving rock gnomes and another quest involving a midwife. The party chose Gnomengarde and departed the next morning.
- **Hungry-ogre encounter begun:** A second ogre blocked the narrow mountain pass to Gnomengarde and demanded to eat Soma. Negotiation and intimidation failed. After Vukradin mentioned a prior ogre kill, it claimed the party had killed its "brother and sister." Combat began. Valphine's walloping shot knocked it prone, Vukradin's Command halted it, Brewbarry and Soma damaged it, and the session ended before the ogre was defeated.
