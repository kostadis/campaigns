**Current location:** On the road from the dwarven excavation toward town. Four hostile orcs have appeared; initiative is beginning and nobody is surprised.

## Characters

### Vukradin

**Bard and avant-garde musician**

**Personality and motivations:** Chapter 1 calls him an underappreciated musician who joined Neverember's tax-free-plunder adventurer program. Chapter 3 shows that he is intensely concerned with honest provenance: he reports the priest's body and every treasure find to the dwarves, presents the gemstones as archaeological artifacts rather than free loot, and forces a negotiated division instead of silently taking them.

**Behavior under pressure:** Prefers to talk before fighting and asks to parley with the road orcs. In the jelly battles he retreats to effective spell range when possible, but adapts quickly when he remembers that piercing attacks do not split oozes.

**Notable relationships:**

- *Dazlyn and Norbus* — distrusts their improvised claims of expertise but insists on dealing honestly with them
- *Valphine* — unaware that she secretly kept the priest's holy symbol and tried to underreport the gemstone count
- *Soma* — her frustration with his honesty is open; she still frames herself as his hired guide and jokes that making him wealthy enough to pay her may take the whole campaign
- *Brewbarry* — welcomes the barbarian's direct combat effectiveness; no deeper relationship is established in the accepted sources

**Capabilities shown:** Thunderwave, Dissonant Whispers, Starry Wisp, paired-dagger attacks, musical performance.

**Items of significance:** Musical instruments; paired daggers.

---

### Valphine

**Drow cleric of Lathander**

**Personality and motivations:** Pragmatic, secretive, and materially opportunistic alongside genuine commitment to keeping the party alive. She pockets Abbathor's holy symbol and proposes understating the gemstone count, but repeatedly spends healing magic on companions in immediate danger.

**Knowledge and secrets:**

- Holds the jeweled-dagger holy symbol of Abbathor, worth 50 gp.
- Soma and Brewbarry saw the theft; Vukradin did not.
- Knows the Hall of Greed gem radiates magic, but publicly concluded it was probably worthless glass after a failed investigation.
- Speaks Orcish. One associated line in the source has uncertain diarization, so do not over-attribute surrounding banter.

**Capabilities shown:** Sacred Flame, Healing Word, Detect Magic, mace attacks, sleight of hand, Orcish.

**Items of significance:** Mace; concealed holy symbol of Abbathor.

---

### Soma

**Tortle druid and self-described hired guide**

**Personality and motivations:** Practical and survival-minded. She openly calls for retreat when the jelly battle turns deadly, uses her shell defensively, and becomes exasperated with Vukradin's insistence on reporting treasure. She nevertheless helps negotiate rather than simply stealing from the dwarves.

**Party role:** Battlefield control, terrain manipulation, ranged magic, and pragmatic risk assessment.

**Capabilities shown:** Earth Tremor, Ice Knife, Poison Spray, Mold Earth, shell defense.

**Recent:** Knocked unconscious by jelly pseudopods and revived by Valphine; revealed the Hall of Greed with Mold Earth; enters the orc encounter low on spell resources.

---

### Brewbarry

**Barbarian**

**Personality and motivations:** Direct and action-oriented in the accepted source. The session does not independently establish his backstory or long-term motivation.

**Party role:** Front-line combatant and damage absorber. He learns the hard way that slashing an ochre jelly causes it to divide and switches from his halberd to fists against the oozes.

**Capabilities shown:** Rage, halberd attacks, powerful unarmed/bludgeoning strikes, exceptional endurance.

**Knowledge:** Saw Valphine take the holy symbol and did not expose her during this session.

**Items of significance:** Halberd.

## Party Dynamics

**Honesty versus opportunism is the clearest group tension.** Vukradin insists on disclosing finds and establishing rightful ownership. Valphine tries to conceal the holy symbol and understate the gemstones. Soma complains about Vukradin's moral streak. Brewbarry watches without forcing either side's position.

**The group can improvise tactically but remains vulnerable.** Slashing attacks initially multiply the jellies, Soma is knocked unconscious, and several characters hover near collapse. Once they identify the creatures' weakness, they coordinate piercing, bludgeoning, battlefield control, ranged magic, and healing effectively enough to clear the ruins.

**Knowledge is uneven inside the party.** Valphine's theft is known to Soma and Brewbarry but not Vukradin. The party shares the mistaken belief that the magical emerald may be worthless glass; only the GM-facing source confirms its true nature.
