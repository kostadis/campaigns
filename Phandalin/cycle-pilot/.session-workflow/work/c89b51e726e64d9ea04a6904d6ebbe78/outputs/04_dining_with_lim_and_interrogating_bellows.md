## Scene summary (copied from the approved remove-recap draft)

#### The party enjoys a meal at The Board Laid Bare while Valphine and Brewbarry separately question Bellows about the restaurant's mysterious supply chain.

- Lim, the tortle chef and proprietor, greets Soma warmly while carrying a tray of food she has already decided the party wants.
- Lim asks whether Soma had gone north. Soma explains that Brewbarry and Vukradin have been taking her “all over creation.”
- Lim recognizes Vukradin and bluntly tells him, “I don't like your music.” She nevertheless acknowledges that everyone else seems to love it.
- Lim says the Common Chord is now “the hottest ticket in town” because Vukradin used to perform there.
- Lim mentions that the Tower District has become quieter but not necessarily safer: “Not safer. Quieter is different.”
- Three weeks earlier, people dressed as Many-Starred Cloaks associates entered the Cloak Tower by the basement stairs, spent the day inside, and emerged carrying nothing.
- Lim says something has changed in the neighborhood.
- Valphine notices the restaurant's prices are not merely low but conspicuously unaffected by the city's supply crisis. The food itself remains excellent, especially by tortle standards: raw and steamed dishes prepared simply and well.
- Lim becomes annoyed when repeatedly asked about the restaurant's finances and redirects the conversation toward Soma's transformation from local guide to druid of Mielikki.
- She has heard stories about a druid carrying a staff that sprouts leaves and is astonished to find Soma traveling with Vukradin and “a cosplaying Brewbarry.”
- Soma corrects her: “He's not cosplaying, either.… The real deal.”
- Vukradin praises Lim's cooking and asks for a tour: “Could you show us, like, how you do it? Can you take… can you show us the magic?”
- Lim eagerly leads Soma into the kitchen to exchange recipes and catch up.
- During the kitchen visit, Lim asks whether Soma still tells people the restaurant serves turtle soup. When Soma admits she told her companions, Lim laughs: “I can't believe they believed you.”
- Lim explains that the restaurant once sold turtle soup to a different clientele and that many people still arrive expecting it.
- Valphine realizes that Bellows appears incapable of lying and elects to question him.
- Valphine asks why a restaurant with such good food is not busier. Bellows looks around and answers, “All but two tables are empty. How many more patrons do you want?”
- Bellows explains that Lim refuses advertising and relies entirely on word of mouth: “I've had this conversation with Lim many times.… There will be no advertising.”
- Brewbarry asks how the restaurant keeps its prices so low despite the city-wide trade disruptions. Bellows answers, “We have very good deliveries.”
- Invited to elaborate, Bellows asks which details are wanted: the arrival time, route, crate appearance, markings, number of crates, or their contents.
- Deliveries arrive nightly at the third hour after midnight, occasionally the fourth, through a rear door opening onto a service lane connected to the aqueduct.
- Two men usually make each delivery, sometimes three. Bellows initially describes them as different each night, then precisely corrects himself.
- Over the previous thirty days, six or seven couriers have rotated through the deliveries, with some individuals appearing more than once.
- Bellows does not involve himself in procurement. He receives wooden crates that are rope-bound and wet on the underside.
- Nine crates have arrived each night since midsummer, but the restaurant uses only eight. Bellows has raised the discrepancy twice.
- Asked what happens to the ninth, Bellows answers: “It does not come inside. It is set down, and it is not there in the morning. I have not asked. It is not billed to us.”
- The restaurant also employs several humans for dishwashing, cleaning, and table service, but Bellows identifies Lim as the person responsible for logistics.
