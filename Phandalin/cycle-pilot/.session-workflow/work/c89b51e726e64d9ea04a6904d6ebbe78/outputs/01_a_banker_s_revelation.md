## Scene summary (copied from the approved remove-recap draft)

#### Outside the Spire of the Morninglord, the party meets with Aurelan Vance, who provides critical information regarding a missing gnome named Bimble.

- Aurelan Vance catches up with the party outside the Spire of the Morninglord, out of breath after rushing back from the Counting House.
- He is Brewbarry's banker, previously persuaded through intimidation to act as the goliath's agent. Brewbarry characterizes this more charitably: “I was just making friends with him, you know, he's making me a favor, I'll do him one.”
- Aurelan pats himself down and adjusts his clothing before addressing Brewbarry, uncertain of the proper form of address under goliath customs.
- Brewbarry tells him, “Just call me Brew, that's fine.” Aurelan responds, “And since we're on such friendly terms, my friends call me Ori.”
- Aurelan explains that he had intended to wait for Brewbarry to visit the bank, but Valphine's sermon inspired him to act immediately: “Valphine's sermon was inspiring.… It inspired me to action.”
- Aurelan reveals that he reviewed paperwork concerning the gnome Bimble. The party repeatedly mispronounces the name as “Bimbo,” despite Aurelan displaying its spelling on a piece of paper.
- The banker explains that Bimble was on a commission maintenance roll for eleven years until payments abruptly stopped seven tendays ago.
- There was no severance, final reconciliation, or forwarding instruction. Aurelan emphasizes, “That is not how a contract ends.… That is how a contract is abandoned!”
- Bimble has drawn no funds since then. He has not banked, borrowed, or left the city, suggesting that someone is keeping him locally.
- Aurelan advises, “Find who feeds a man, and you have found the man.”
- The party begins informally naming the investigation “Who's Feeding Bimbo?” while Aurelan continues correcting the gnome's name.
