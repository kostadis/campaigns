## Scene summary (copied from the approved remove-recap draft)

#### Brewbarry confronts Lim in the kitchen about her mysterious suppliers, and she eventually reveals the goods are moved through the city's sewer system.

- Brewbarry enters the small kitchen carrying his halberd, the sword of dragon slaying, and wearing his bathrobe.
- He pretends to be interested in the cooking while waiting for a chance to speak privately with Lim.
- Brewbarry tells Lim he is entering business and wants efficient suppliers capable of moving goods around the city.
- Lim says her supplier has not been affected by recent changes but refuses to identify them, calling the arrangement a trade secret.
- Brewbarry replies, “Just like the ninth crate, huh?” and reveals that Bellows told him nine crates arrive while only eight enter the restaurant.
- Lim insists, “I don't order 9 crates, I order 8. I don't know what Bellows is talking about.”
- She further states that she does not know why the suppliers bring an extra crate or why it disappears.
- Lim describes the suppliers as people interested in keeping local businesses afloat with fair-trade, organic goods outside the economic policies of Lord Neverember and the Neverwinter Commission.
- She met a man through another man after being approached at the market. She says she does not know the organization's name or location and pays them regularly for the deliveries.
- Frustrated by her evasiveness, Brewbarry proposes abandoning the conversation and questioning the delivery crew directly when they arrive.
- Lim finally admits, “There is a sewer system in the Bluelake District. If you go in there, you should be able to find the suppliers.”
- When Vukradin asks whether she serves food obtained from a sewer, Lim corrects him: “No, I said it gets moved through the sewer.”
- Valphine recognizes that trade cartels currently control the conventional routes and identifies Lim's suppliers as smugglers.
- Lim asks Soma and the others not to ambush or frighten them because she depends upon their deliveries to keep her restaurant operating.
- She argues that Neverwinter's rising taxes, disrupted trade, and soaring prices have left local establishments with few alternatives. Her food is good, her prices are fair, and she does not consider the suppliers bad people.
- Soma warns that such people may return with demands in the future and condemns Lim's use of “dubious means” to keep prices low.
- Lim tells Soma, “You've changed. You're different. I remember a different Soma.”
- Soma replies that the Lim she remembers would not have become so deeply involved with criminals and suggests that she could have raised her prices instead.
- Lim's goal was to preserve a community establishment without pricing out or losing her clientele.
- Valphine deduces that the restaurant is being supplied through a smuggling operation bypassing the harbor authority and what the party believes to be House Margaster’s grip on port distribution.
