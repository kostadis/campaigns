# The Skeletal Horse Sighting

*Derived extraction draft: dialogue candidates come from the approved corrected transcript, not verbatim raw recording text. Speaker attribution and any proposed corrections still require human review.*

## Scene summary (copied from the approved remove-recap draft)

#### While walking through the Protector's Enclave, a passerby claims to recognize the party's skeletal horse, Boney.

- A stranger stops the party, saying, “I've seen that horse somewhere!”
- The man initially wonders whether he saw Boney in the fields outside Neverwinter's walls. Boney confirms he has traveled outside the city.
- The stranger considers whether the horse he remembers might instead have been one of Boney's children or siblings, prompting the party to wonder whether Boney has offspring and whether they would also be skeletal.
- The party observes that skeletal horses lack distinctive coloring and wonders whether Lady Alagondar's tackle or Boney's posture caused the recognition.
- The stranger eventually declares, “I remember where I saw this horse.”
- He then explains that Boney was outside the service where “that charming cleric gave a speech” earlier the same day.
- Vukradin responds, “Oh, God, you're wasting our time,” and later asks, “Why don't we talk to strangers? Stop talking to strangers, please.”
- Soma counters, “Stranger's just a friend you haven't met.”
