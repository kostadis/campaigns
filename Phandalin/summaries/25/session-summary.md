# Chapter 25 Where Reality Unravels: Breaching Axeholm

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The party breaches the ancient dwarven fortress of Axeholm, miles south of Phandalin. Their mission: open and secure the long-sealed stronghold as an evacuation point against the dragon threat. Soma's rat familiar scouts ahead, discovering a winch mechanism to lift the portcullis. But the rat also perceives nightmare-inducing spatial distortions — distances that don't align, light that is "mathematically cold" and refuses to flicker.

The party attaches a rope to the winch and raises the gate. Brewbarry smashes through an inner portcullis door with considerable effort. Beyond lies a 40-foot-high pillared hall choked with dust. Grotesque creatures emerge — once dwarven inhabitants, now warped into out-of-phase horrors with parchment skin covered in shifting ink, gold coins embedded in their flesh, mouths sewn shut with golden wire or red tape, and six multiplying limbs that phase through reality. They mutter constantly about "efficiency."

Soma uses her staff to sense the planar anomaly that has corrupted the fortress. Valphine attempts Turn Undead — the creatures flee despite not being truly undead, suggesting some form of holy vulnerability. The party engages in brutal combat.

The dwarves reveal a horrifying ability: when they strike, they paralyze their victims and inflict the sensation of being "packaged in a box." Soma is paralyzed and nearly killed — the swarming dwarves land critical blows on her defenseless form. Valphine heals her, breaking the paralysis. Soma transforms into a bear and tears through her attackers. Brewbarry also resists the paralysis, hearing "incorrect packaging" as he is momentarily knocked into cold storage before breaking free. Soma shakes the ground, knocking dwarves prone.

Just as the party seems to gain the upper hand, another dwarf paralyzes Soma in bear form — she stands frozen, vulnerable, as the session ends on a dramatic cliffhanger.

## Memorable Moments

> "...incorrect packaging."
> — A disembodied voice, as Brewbarry resists paralysis

**Incorrect Packaging.** The dwarves' paralysis ability tries to store Brewbarry like a package, but he fights it off. The voice from "customer service" is an unsettling glimpse into the machine's logic.

> "I CALL UPON THE GLORY OF LATHANDER!"
> — Valphine, using Turn Undead

**Turn (Sort of) Undead.** Valphine's divine power causes the out-of-phase dwarves to flee — despite them not being truly undead. The "sister of the sun" epithet from the Chief Accountant later suggests her divine connection is uniquely potent here.

> "It's like they're... packaged. Like cargo. Like they're being shipped somewhere."
> — Valphine, describing the paralysis sensation

**Packaged Souls.** The dwarves' touch doesn't just paralyze — it makes victims feel like objects being prepared for shipment. A horrifying insight into what the machine was doing to the fortress.

> "That's not normal fire. That light is wrong."
> — Soma, on the fortress lighting

**Mathematically Cold Light.** The unnatural lighting in Axeholm refuses to flicker like normal flames. The spatial distortions make distances unreliable. The fortress is fundamentally broken.

### COMBAT — Out-of-Phase Dwarves (Mustering Hall)
- **Opening conditions:** Party in mustering hall, dwarves emerge from multiple doors. 40-ft ceiling with stone pillars provides cover/concealment. Ballistas present but not manned. Visible count: escalating waves.
- **Opening maneuver:** Valphine's Turn Undead causes multiple dwarves to flee despite not being truly undead. **(DM tactical note):** This suggests their corruption has enough unholy/undead-like qualities for Turn Undead to work. Valphine's divine connection to Lathander may be especially potent against this planar corruption.
- Soma unleashes Moonbeam (radiant) and Thunderwave — damages multiple enemies, sends them crashing against walls.
- Vukradin provides Bardic Inspiration while striking with rapier — one dwarf blinks out of existence as space folds from the psychic/verbal assault.
- Brewbarry rages (Big Brewbarry), cleaves through dwarves with halberd.
- More dwarves materialize from the unnatural light, phasing in and out of reality.
- **HORROR ABILITY:** First dwarf strikes Soma — she becomes paralyzed, experiencing the sensation of being "packaged in a box, sealed, and delivered like an object."
- The other dwarves swarm Soma's paralyzed form, landing critical blows — she nearly dies.
- Valphine channels healing magic — restores Soma, breaks the paralysis.
- Soma transforms into a bear, tears through her attackers.
- Brewbarry is also hit by paralysis — hears "incorrect packaging" voice, resists and breaks free.
- Soma uses Earth Tremor (or similar) — knocks several dwarves prone, creates difficult terrain.
- Party systematically cuts down the out-of-phase creatures.
- **CLIFFHANGER:** Another dwarf successfully paralyzes Soma in bear form.
- **(DM tactical note):** The paralysis ability is the most dangerous threat — it removes a PC from combat without a save-breaking effect. The party's lack of Freedom of Movement or similar protections makes them vulnerable. Soma's habit of frontlining in Wild Shape puts her at highest risk.
- **(DM tactical note):** The "packaging" theme strongly connects to the machine later revealed. These dwarves aren't just corrupted — they're being processed by the planar device.
