# Chapter 11 The Stag, the Brambles, the Wolves, and the Pool

Date: Early Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party enters the Whispering Wood, Soma's awakened staff pulsing. There are no obvious signs of rot, but something is fundamentally wrong — a palpable planar disruption that unsettles even nature-tuned senses.

They first encounter a majestic stag that shimmers and flickers, caught between this plane and another. Its antlers constantly shift shape, and it can teleport. Soma casts Speak with Animals. The stag is territorial and confused: it used to "just walk and prance and eat vegetables," but now everything is wrong. It mentions "other nice people" who also spoke to it and "said they would fix it, but they haven't." The stag is hungry; Soma feeds it Gnomengarde mushrooms, and it directs them toward the forest's center where "it all started."

Deeper in, they encounter a dense thicket of brambles with leaves pulsing unnatural colors — planar leakage. **COMBAT:** Soma approaches within ten feet; the brambles lash out with thorny tendrils, dealing piercing and necrotic damage. Valphine's Sacred Flame merely glances off. Brewbarry, enraged, tears into the branches. Soma transforms into a bear and joins the assault. The thicket appears to regenerate but is eventually rendered inert. The party pushes through.

Next, they face unnatural wolves with glowing eyes. **COMBAT:** The wolves have a howl that magically roots Vukradin in place. The creatures are quickly dispatched. Their pelts are taken — Vukradin considers them "conflict-free gold," and the planar nature may increase their value. Unease lingers.

The party reaches a clearing with a pool of water. Its surface reflects distorted versions of themselves. Valphine asks if she'll "see myself happy and content," but avoids staring — afraid of happiness. Vukradin gazes into the pool and sees a version of himself clutching coins obsessively. He jumps in, seeking answers about his fear of pleasing fans vs. artistic purity. He's assaulted with fragmented visions, including a distorted creature saying "He saw beyond, but only what he was meant to see. The numbers. Always the numbers." Overwhelmed, he stumbles out and collapses unconscious. Soma revives him with a slap.

Brewbarry looks into the pool and asks, "What side of the bread should I butter?" He sees a version of himself saying "butter both sides simultaneously." He flees screaming, terrified by the absurd wisdom. Soma (bear) subdues him.

Valphine wants to ask "more intelligent questions," but Soma advises against it for their sanity. The party moves on, shaken. The vision of "numbers" sticks with Vukradin.

## Memorable Moments

> "I was okay... Until you walked into my territory. Why are you in my territory? Get out of my territory."
> — The Stag, immediately hostile

**Territorial Dispute.** Soma's attempt at diplomatic animal-speak meets immediate hostility. The stag's aggressive territoriality is later revealed to be fear — it's scared by the planar changes it can't understand.

> "I used just to be able to walk and prance and eat the vegetables."
> — The Stag, lamenting its pre-corruption life

**Simpler Times.** The stag's poignant description of its former life — just walking, prancing, eating vegetables — perfectly captures the tragedy of the planar corruption. It didn't ask for this power.

> "He saw beyond, but only what he was meant to see. The numbers. Always the numbers."
> — The vision in the pool, to Vukradin

**The Numbers.** Vukradin's psychic vision crystallizes his deepest fear: that his obsession with "conflict-free gold" and moral accounting has replaced actual artistic expression. The creature's phrase echoes through his mind for the rest of the chapter.

> "What side of the bread should I butter?"
> — Brewbarry, asking the pool of forbidden knowledge

**The Most Brewbarry Question.** While Vukradin confronts existential dread and Valphine contemplates happiness, Brewbarry asks a question about toast. The pool's answer — "butter both sides simultaneously" — drives him to hysterical terror, suggesting even simple questions get universe-breaking answers.

> "Both sides! Both sides!"
> — Brewbarry, fleeing in existential panic

**Butter Trauma.** Brewbarry's flight from the pool, screaming about buttering both sides of bread, is simultaneously hilarious and unsettling. The pool's answer was clearly *too* profound for a mortal mind to bear.

## Scenes

### Entering the Whispering Wood
- Soma's staff feels alive — awakened magic.
- No visible rot or blight, but the party feels something fundamentally wrong.
- The air tastes wrong; planar disturbance is palpable.
- Soma's keen senses detect "planar leakage, bleeding."

**(DM note):** The Whispering Wood's corruption is not necrotic or poison — it's planar mutation. Standard detect magic or nature checks may produce confusing results because the anomaly is extraplanar, not natural.

### The Stag Encounter
- A majestic stag materializes, its fur shimmering — caught between planes.
- Antlers constantly shift shape; it teleports 15 feet away.
- Soma casts Speak with Animals.
- **Opening conditions:** Stag is not immediately hostile but is territorial and frightened.
- Stag demands the party leave its territory; Valphine complies immediately.
- Brewbarry and Vukradin initially don't move; Soma asks them to back up.
- Stag explains: it used to "walk and prance and eat vegetables," now it blinks, its antlers change constantly.
- "The whole forest has gotten weird."
- Asks for food; Soma offers Gnomengarde mushrooms. Stag accepts.
- Reveals "it all started in the center of the forest."
- Mentions "other nice people" who also "spoke to me" and said they would "fix it, but they haven't" — likely the Emerald Enclave agents (Lyra's team).
- The stag provides crucial direction before departing.

**(DM note):** The stag is not corrupted in the same way as the treant — it's been affected by planar energies but not consumed. Its nature is still fundamentally stag-like, just "off." This distinguishes it from the corrupted treant in ch12.

### Combat 1: Planar Brambles
- **Opening conditions:** Dense thicket with bioluminescent, pulsing leaves. Soma approaches within 10 ft, triggering hostile reaction. Single terrain encounter (no separate enemies).
- **Round-by-round:**
  - Brambles lash out: thorny tendrils strike Soma (piercing + necrotic damage).
  - Valphine attempts Sacred Flame — it glances off (planar resistance to radiant).
  - Brewbarry enters rage, moves within 10 ft, brambles miss. He tears into branches with his halberd.
  - Soma transforms into bear form, attacks the thicket.
  - Vukradin provides support with bardic commentary.
  - The thicket appears to regenerate between attacks.
- **Key tactical moment:** The brambles' resistance to Sacred Flame suggests planar creatures nullify or reduce radiant damage — notable given Valphine's reliance on it.
- **Enemy behavior:** Brambles are not intelligent — they lash out at anyone within 10 ft, retract when struck, appear to regenerate slowly.
- **PC resource decisions:** Soma burned a Wild Shape on the bramble fight. Brewbarry raged. These resources may be needed for future encounters.
- **Resolution:** Brewbarry and Soma (bear) render the thicket inert through sustained physical damage. Party passes through before it reanimates.

**(DM tactical note):** The party used significant resources (Wild Shape, rage, spell attempts) on a terrain hazard. This drain should be considered when balancing subsequent encounters — the wolves and pool are meant to challenge an already-depleted party.

### Combat 2: Unnatural Wolves
- **Opening conditions:** Wolves with glowing eyes, moving through the forest. Number unclear (at least 2-3 visible).
- **Round-by-round:**
  - Wolves howl — Vukradin is magically rooted to the spot (paralyzed or restrained by magical fear).
  - Party engages the wolves in melee.
  - Soma (bear) and Brewbarry make short work of the creatures.
  - Vukradin recovers after the wolves are killed.
- **Post-combat:** Party skins the wolves. Vukradin is pleased — wolf pelts are "conflict-free gold," potentially enhanced by planar exposure.

**(DM tactical note):** The wolves' rooting howl targeted only Vukradin. This could be coincidence, pack tactics focusing on the squishiest target, or the wolves sensing he was the party's primary magic user. Vukradin's inability to act for the fight's duration is concerning — if he'd been isolated or the wolves had focused fire on the rooted bard, he could have been downed before the party could intervene.

### The Pool of Reflections
- Clearing with a pool whose surface shows distorted reflections.
- **Valphine:** Asks if she'll "see myself happy and content" — remarkable vulnerability. She avoids staring directly, as if afraid of the answer.
- **Vukradin:** Sees himself clutching coins obsessively. Fears this is the price of pleasing fans vs. artistic purity. Jumps in voluntarily.
  - Vision: "A dizzying cacophony... people, scenes, faces looking at me all at the same time."
  - Sees a distorted creature: "He saw beyond, but only what he was meant to see. The numbers. Always the numbers."
  - Overwhelmed, stumbles out, collapses unconscious.
  - Soma slaps him awake.
- **Brewbarry:** Asks "What side of the bread should I butter?"
  - Vision: "Butter both sides simultaneously."
  - Flees screaming. Soma (bear) subdues him.
  - Keeps muttering "both sides" long after.
- Valphine wants to ask "more intelligent questions"; Soma advises against it.
- Party moves on, shaken. The "numbers" vision sticks with Vukradin.

**(DM tactical note):** Vukradin jumping into the pool was a high-risk choice. He had no way to know what would happen and no safety line. A DM could have ruled psychic damage, permanent madness, or even HP drain. His subsequent unconsciousness left the party without their primary caster for a recovery period. The pool is a planar scar — not a puzzle to be solved, but a wound to be avoided.

**(DM note):** The pool is a planar scar, a tear in reality where multiple near-planes bleed into the Material. Its answers are true but useless or destructive. Valphine's desire for "more intelligent questions" suggests she correctly identified it as a source of information — but without context, the information is poison. This sets up the planar theme for ch12.

### Moving Toward the Center
- The party is now committed to reaching the forest's center.
- Three encounters (stag, brambles, wolves, pool) have established the planar corruption theme.
- Resources are partially depleted.
- Vukradin is haunted by the phrase "the numbers."
- Brewbarry is traumatized by toast philosophy.
- The party presses deeper, toward where "it all started."

## NPCs

- **The Stag** — A once-normal stag now warped by planar energies. Can teleport, has constantly shifting antlers. Confused, territorial, ultimately helpful. Directs the party to the forest's center. Its normal stag-mind means it doesn't understand what's happening to it.

- **The Pool Entity/Vision** — An intelligence or echo within the planar scar pool. Responds to questions with disturbing, half-truthful answers. May be a fragment of whatever caused the corruption, a trapped planar being, or a psychic echo of the forest's distress.

## Items

- **Unnatural Wolf Pelts (x2+)** — Skinned from the planar-corrupted wolves. Their fur may retain planar properties. Linene may find them valuable. Vukradin considers them "conflict-free gold."

- **Soma's Staff (Active)** — Pulses with awareness in the Whispering Wood. Appears to sense planar disturbance. Its exact capabilities are still unknown.

## Spells

- **Speak with Animals** — Soma. Used to communicate with the stag. Essential for gaining information about the forest's center.
- **Sacred Flame** — Valphine. Used against planar brambles; ineffective (glanced off). First indication that radiant damage may be resisted by planar corruption.
- **Wild Shape (Brown Bear)** — Soma. Used in both the bramble fight and later to subdue Brewbarry. Significant resource expenditure.
