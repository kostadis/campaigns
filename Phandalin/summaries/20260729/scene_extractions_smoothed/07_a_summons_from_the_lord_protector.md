# A Summons from the Lord Protector

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### As the party prepares to leave the Moonstone Mask, they are intercepted by a steward delivering a formal invitation from the ruler of Neverwinter.

- A steward named Aldus, in the livery of the Lord Protector, approaches the party with a formal summons from Lord Dagult Neverember.
- Aldus announces: "On behalf of Lord Dagult Neverember, Lord Protector of Neverwinter, I extend a formal welcome to your group. The Lord Protector has followed your recent activities with considerable interest. He wishes to arrange a meeting at your earliest..."
- Brewbarry interrupts: "I asked a question, who's considerable interest? What does he have to do with this business? I'm sorry." He then clarifies: "I asked, whose considerable interest? I know it's Neverember. I mean, what's he got to do with this?"
- Aldus clarifies: "The Lord Protector has an interest in Neverwinter's cultural future, and separately an interest in the economic questions recently raised by the Candlekeep report regarding adventuring parties and the circulation of wealth."
- Aldus hints that Neverember has concerns: "He's particularly curious whether Vukradin is aware of the comparisons some sages have drawn between his financial philosophy and that of a large reptile [hoarding gold]."
- The party resists a morning meeting, citing scheduled sacred rites and performances at the Spire of the Morninglord.
- Vukradin smoothly proposes: "If the Lord would like to arrange, like, a gala for the music studio, you know, like a charitable event where we could discuss our plans and donations, I'd be happy to attend that."
- Vukradin rolls a 22 on Persuasion.
- Aldus responds: "I think that would be a fantastic idea, a superb idea, but it's been my experience that his lordship responds better to a personal plea. My suggestion to you is perhaps a small soiree where you make your pitch to him, he asks his questions, and then you present to him this. I can assure you that I will do what I can to encourage his lordship, who's very much interested in the arts and culture."
- Aldus adds: "And I must say, I am quite the fan."
- When Vukradin offers an autograph, Aldus is too embarrassed to accept for himself and claims: "I don't [want one], but my daughter would." He invents the name "Alducia."
- Vukradin writes: "To Alducia, with love — Vukradin."
- The DM describes Aldus as "looking like he's about to cry" and "She'll be very excited. I can't thank you enough, Vukradin."
- Aldus asks about dietary restrictions. Brewbarry demands: "Meat, lots of it." Vukradin requests: "What is it, the Chateauneuf du... [wine]... barrels of it."
- Aldus promises: "Don't worry, I will make sure that it will be a meal that you will never forget."
- The party discusses the timing of their dawn performance at the Spire of the Morninglord, committing to "tomorrow morning."
- The group plans their next steps: Brewbarry needs to go to the counting house to investigate the Lionshield Coster disruptions — the DM notes the party has a "letter of recommendation" to use there.
