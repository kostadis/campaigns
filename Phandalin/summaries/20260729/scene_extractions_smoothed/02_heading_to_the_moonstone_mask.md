# Heading to the Moonstone Mask

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party prepares to return a long-lost statue and navigates the streets of Neverwinter toward the luxurious Moonstone Mask.

- The party decides to return the heavy statue recovered from a shipwreck before pursuing other business.
- They pass through the Protector's Enclave, noting the contrast between the wealthy neighborhood and the grungier dock area.
- Vukradin and Brewbarry observe the Common Chord, their former low-end hangout, which has surprisingly become one of the most popular and exclusive venues in the city. A guard apparently "would love for Vukradin to get him tickets at the hardest place to get a ticket to go eat," which is now the Common Chord.
- Brewbarry mutters: "All I remember is a shitty al[e]. Good music, but shitty ale."
- The DM clarifies: "Canonically speaking, it's when you arrived in Neverwinter that you discovered that ale could be good." Brewbarry now reflects: "How did I even ever drink that stuff?"
- The party approaches the Moonstone Mask, a luxurious inn situated on a massive earthmote floating above the city docks, and discusses the strategic significance of the city's three floating earthmotes.
- Vukradin speculates about military strategy: "It's a perfect place to, like, launch an attack on the city proper, right? You've got the high ground. You're, like, got a natural defense... You cut the chains and just, yeah, turn it into, like, a death from [above]."
- The DM discusses the depth of the floating islands: they're cone-shaped, not a flat cutoff, so catacombs and basements would "fit naturally into that cone area."
