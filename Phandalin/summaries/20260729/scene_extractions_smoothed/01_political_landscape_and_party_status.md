# Political Landscape and Party Status

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party gathers in Neverwinter and the Dungeon Master recaps the current political factions and the party's recent rise in fame.

- The Dungeon Master explains the current state of the major political factions: the Lords' Alliance (a coalition of city-states, "the plutocrats, the privileged"), the Zhentarim (who "view that market dynamics should not be constrained by regulatory systems" and are "amoral" but "tend to attract people of the evil persuasion"), the Harpers (whose "goal is to just keep everything just the right level of fracture so that nobody can run anything" and are described as "anarcho-syndicalists"), the Emerald Enclave (still "apart, torn apart by the War of the Dragon"), and the Order of the Gauntlet (unambiguously good guys, "the only group that qualifies as unalloyed good guys" — but "nobody trusts them").
- The DM clarifies that a 20th-level character is "essentially a demigod" and that "because of magic, and the power, and because player... the novaing capabilities of it are so outrageous... there are also gods and magical artifacts" — so the Lords' Alliance is just one power faction among many.
- The party discusses their current reputation in Neverwinter, noting that Brewbarry has become a known cultural figure and Vukradin has acquired fame for his early musical work. The DM clarifies that Vukradin is famous "for his early stuff, not for his current stuff."
- The group identifies their most urgent task: returning a recovered statue to the Meliamne family.
- A brief discussion occurs regarding local dining, specifically a tavern known for Tortle home cooking — which the DM clarifies is "not Made of Tortles" but rather "turtle soup... like Girl Scout cookies aren't made of Girl Scouts."
- Boney is with the party as a horse — the DM clarifies that since he "doesn't poop," he's allowed to wander around with them rather than being stabled, because "normal horses get to go through town just fine."
- Prutha is confirmed to have departed on a crusade to convert orcs; the DM notes "that will have consequences."
