# Arrival at the Moonstone Mask

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party enters the luxurious Moonstone Mask to return a long-lost statue, only to learn of a tragic death.

- The party arrives at the Moonstone Mask, a high-end inn floating above Neverwinter on a tethered earthmote.
- Valphine's passive perception of 17 notices that "everybody here is very... all the waitstaff is very pretty."
- The DM describes the staff as dressed in "sheer black," all wearing moonstone-lined half-masks alongside amulets.
- A staff member recognizes Vukradin and says: "Oh my. Vukradin, you've come to our establishment."
- When Vukradin asks for Elara, the DM describes: "Her eyes suddenly grow cold."
- The staff member informs the party that Lady Elara Meliamne, the intended recipient of the statue, was killed eight days prior in a "catastrophic, catastrophic, spectacular and bloody disaster" during a festival.
- The staff member says Elara's death was "beyond the ability to be raised. Even if her brother did want to raise her." *[Quoted verbatim. The table used "brother"/"his sister" throughout this scene; per campaign canon Cassian is Elara's **cousin**. Quotes are left as spoken; narration uses cousin.]*
- The staff member notes that Lord Neverember "has declared that the death of Elara was a tragic accident, and that we were all to mourn her passing and move on."
- The party is directed to Lord Cassian, Elara's cousin, who is currently at the inn.
- The staff member mistakes Brewbarry for someone merely dressed in the popular "Brewbarry aesthetic." She says: "Oh my, I didn't realize that the Brewbarry aesthetic was something that people would wear in the Moonstone Mask... the whole... I always thought the Brewbarry aesthetic was... the Uthgardian aesthetic was anti-Moonstone Mask." She assumes Brewbarry is "somebody who's dressed like you, not actually a Uthgarian Barbarian... Brewbarry."
- Brewbarry doesn't mind and gets a "very large pouring of something that tastes amazing."
- The staff member looks at Valphine and says: "Oh, you're a blessing upon me."

**GM** — *establishing the Moonstone Mask's geography*

> "Yeah, so essentially what happened was that there was some magical effects, and as a result, these rocks are now afloat in the sky, and they're held down by rocks."

**GM** — *reading the earthmote lore aloud*
> "One of the most enchanting city features of the city of Neverwinter is its three floating, large earthmotes."
> "The spell plague of 1385 caused chunks of Neverwinter, as well as other areas all over Toril, to rise up and begin floating away. Industrious residents of Neverwinter were able to tether a few of these earthmotes."
> "and prevent them from ultimately flying off into the Sword Coast."

**GM** — *reading the Moonstone Mask description*
> "The Moonstone Mask is a large inn that sits on a floating earthmote connected to the docks of the Protector's Enclave section of Neverwinter.
