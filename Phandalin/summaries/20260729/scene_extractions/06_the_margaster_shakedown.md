# The Margaster Shakedown

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### A representative of House Margaster interrupts the meeting to assert a legal claim on the recovered statue, resulting in an extortion of Lord Cassian.

- Cullen Sharpe, described as "a gentleman in a well-tailored dark green suit," arrives at the table and introduces himself as "an associate to the House Margaster."
- When Vukradin states: "Oh, you're the ones who slaughtered Elara," Cullen Sharpe quickly responds: "No, we had nothing to do with that."
- Cullen presents a Harbor Authority writ claiming prior salvage rights to the statue: "How House Margaster maintains a privileged claim... a prior salvage claim on the piece. Documented. If you'd like to review the Harbor's authority writ, we're prepared to offer a fair market rate. No need for this to become complicated."
- Vukradin responds: "Well, you know, I turned over the statue to the Lord, and you're free to negotiate with him."
- The party inspects the legal documents. Valphine rolls a 16 perception on spot forgery, Vukradin rolls a natural 20 on perception. The party determines they appear to be "a racket... a shakedown... fraudulent."
- Lord Cassian, clearly intimidated by the influence of House Margaster, says: "Perhaps House Margaster would be willing to discuss a small exchange of funds to cover..." — and pays a 200 gold piece bribe to settle the dispute.
- Vukradin mutters: "Well, good thing you're a lord, and you have lots of money."
- Cullen Sharpe transfers the writ to Lord Cassian, declaring the matter settled.
- Now friendly, Cullen Sharpe attempts to recruit the party: "Vukradin, separately, and I raise this only because I understand your commercial interests in the Phandalin Hinterlands? House Margaster has developed considerable expertise in regional distribution since the unfortunate disruption at the docks. We're in a position to arrange the transfer of goods to rightful owners efficiently and discreetly. If you or any of your colleagues find yourself in need of that kind of arrangement, we'd welcome that conversation."
- Brewbarry skeptically asks: "You claim expertise in returning those goods to their rightful owners. Who can attest to this?" Cullen responds: "All you have to do is ask around, and everyone will say how House Margaster always delivers the goods."
- Lord Cassian, clearly uncomfortable, invites the party to his private residence for a more secure conversation later.
