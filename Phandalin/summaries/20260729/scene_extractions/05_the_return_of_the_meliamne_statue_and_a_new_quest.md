# The Return of the Meliamne Statue and a New Quest

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Lord Cassian gratefully accepts the family heirloom and shares a troubling lead about his cousin's death and her investigation into the Planar Manifold.

- Lord Cassian rewards the party with 50 gold pieces each — Vukradin included — and a donation to Vukradin's music studio as a token of gratitude for returning the statue. The DM clarifies: "He doesn't want to offend you. He's making a donation to the studio, and then he's thanking your traveling companions independently."
- Between Cassian's donation and the money raised from the Moonstone Mask patrons, the DM totals the fund: **"there's now 550 gold pieces in the studio fund."**
- Lord Cassian reveals that Elara was investigating a malfunction in the "displacement manifold," an interplanar shipping device at the docks, before her death.
- The Planar Manifold stopped working about seven weeks ago, disrupting regional distribution for the Lionshield Coster, their primary client. "When it went dark, their store stopped receiving goods."
- Lord Cassian says: "Nobody official will say why. She believed the timing wasn't coincidental."
- "I told her that this wasn't her business, but she was very determined to go look into this."
- The party learns of a mysterious gnome technician who was seen mapping the planar routing of the manifold and asking unusual questions: "Smaller, older, very precise, the kind of person who asks questions that go past the job... he seemed more interested in why the planar routing worked than whether it was functioning, like he was mapping something."
- The gnome "hasn't been seen since" the manifold stopped working. No name is available, only a description and the questions he asked.
- Lord Cassian asks the party: "I would be much appreciative if you were to see if there was any connection between her investigations and her death."
- Vukradin mentions the statue was returned "under the auspices of the Order of the Gauntlet." Cassian smiles and responds: "They are truly one of the few heroes of the realms."
