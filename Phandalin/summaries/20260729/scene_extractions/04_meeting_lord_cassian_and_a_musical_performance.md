# Meeting Lord Cassian and a Musical Performance

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party meets with Lord Cassian to return the family heirloom and ends up performing for the elite crowd.

- The party approaches Lord Cassian, a sea elf nobleman seated at the best table. Vukradin announces: "I am Vukradin, and I came to deliver something to your late sister, Elara. Sorry."
- Lord Cassian responds: "Vukradin. You have the statue. Good. My cousin told me someone was bringing it. She was killed. Eight days ago. House Margaster. I thought you should know before you handed it to anyone."
- When Vukradin asks why he needs to know that, Cassian explains: "I don't want you... before you hand it over to anyone else. Because they would offer you more money."
- Vukradin insists: "Look, this is your statue, and I'm returning it to you, because it's the right thing to do, and here you go."
- Lord Cassian reveals that his cousin Elara was ecstatic to hear the artifact had resurfaced before her untimely death, and that she had been waiting for the party to arrive at a party they never attended because they were off saving the world from a dragon.
- Vukradin insists on returning the statue as a matter of honor rather than for a reward.
- Lord Cassian invites the party to stay for drinks and conversation, clicking his fingers to summon refreshments.
- Brewbarry exclaims: "Let's stay just for a little while, please!" — of which Vukradin quips: "All they have to do is give you alcohol, and you'll do anything."
- Vukradin and the party provide a musical performance for the patrons of the Moonstone Mask.
- Valphine's insight check reveals that while the audience applauds, many are over-intellectualizing the performance or hiding their distaste to remain polite. The DM describes: "You definitely get the whole, like... people who are over-intellectualizing your missed chords." Valphine adds: "You have to listen to the notes they weren't playing."
- Vukradin rolls a subpar performance check, but the DM rules that "all these are posers. They don't know. So they would never admit to a poor performance, anyway... because that would make them look, like, uncivilized and gauche."
- Vukradin uses the opportunity to solicit donations for his upcoming music studio, successfully raising funds from the wealthy patrons.
- Brewbarry jumps in: "We also encourage, strongly encourage, while I seize my axe, donations to Vukradin's music studio upcoming label. Please get out your checkbooks. The rich and wealthy do, you know, throw their money around."
- The party invites the crowd to a future performance and sermon at the Church of Lathander.
