# Chapter 6 Vukradin, Rank Cheval

Date: 04-01 to 05-01 of Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The walk back to Phandalin from Umbrage Hill is dominated by Adabra's relentless philosophical lectures on naturalism vs. interventionism. She reframes monsters as "merely acting as nature intends" and criticizes the interventionists who "want to draw lines." Brewbarry, still grappling with his new "nomad" identity, sighs repeatedly. Vukradin plays music to cut the tension.

In Phandalin, Adabra confronts Townmaster Harbin, demanding he pay Vukradin extra for saving her — a masterful piece of advocacy that gets the party free room and board at the Stonehill Inn as a bonus. Vukradin is delighted; this is "conflict-free, fair-trade gold." Notably, Adabra pointedly ignores Valphine in her advocacy — a tension that Valphine notes.

At the Stonehill Inn, Vukradin and Valphine perform together for the first time, blending Vukradin's discordant experimental music with Valphine's *Choralum Lux Maleficarum* — choral harmonies blending Lathanderian brightness with Lolth-influenced dissonance. The crowd loves it. A musical partnership is born.

Silverleaf (Daran Edermath), a retired adventurer, tells Vukradin about the Tower of Storms — a lighthouse drawing ships to their doom, supposedly filled with treasure. Vukradin's response is immediate rejection: "You don't go and rob from the dead and call it treasure!" The party learns about the lighthouse's cursed reputation.

Ser Kaelen Thorn of the Order of the Gauntlet enters the inn. He seeks out Vukradin personally, having heard of his principled stance on plunder. Despite some awkward moments (he hasn't heard about Vukradin's music studio), Ser Kaelen offers Vukradin associate membership — Rank Cheval — in the Order of the Gauntlet. Vukradin accepts. Valphine is offered but politely demurs. Brewbarry is hostile (the Order fought against his tribe in the war). Ser Kaelen handles it diplomatically. Soma is pointedly excluded: "She's a naturalist, and I don't think that our points of view would align."

The party also encounters a wererat ambush on the road — a brief fight highlighting their lack of silvered weapons. **(Note:** This encounter is referenced in the chapter summary but is not fully detailed in the narrative.) The party resolves to investigate the Tower of Storms, with Ser Kaelen suggesting the curse could be a monster rather than a supernatural affliction.

## Scenes

### The Walk to Phandalin
- Adabra lectures relentlessly about naturalism vs. interventionism: "The manticore, the orcs driven from their homes, they are merely acting as nature intends."
- Soma nods along; the philosophy resonates.
- Adabra warns: "Some believe we must fight every evil that comes from the outer plane. But where do you draw the line? Do we draw the line at a spirit from the ethereal plane? A fiend from the abyss? Or is it a god's design? To fight against these is to deny the very nature of existence itself."
- Brewbarry: "I was a barbarian!" Adabra: "You were a nomad."
- Vukradin plays music to end the lecture. Valphine joins in tentatively — the first hint of their musical partnership.

### Confronting Harbin
- Harbin's familiar door-panic: "If you're a dragon..."
- Adabra cuts him off, demanding payment.
- Vukradin argues for more than 25 gp. Harbin pushes back.
- Adabra delivers a passionate speech about principled heroes vs. mercenaries.
- Harbin offers free room and board at the Stonehill Inn as a compromise.
- Adabra pointedly excludes Valphine from her advocacy. **(DM note):** This slight is clearly based on Valphine's drow heritage. Adabra, despite her naturalist philosophy that rejects "civilized vs. savage" lines, is not immune to prejudice. This hypocrisy may become a point of conflict.

### The Stonehill Inn — First Duet
- Vukradin sets up to play. Toblen reveals his "Spiderman" nickname.
- Valphine asks to join. Vukradin agrees if she supports his lead.
- Vukradin's music: discordant, experimental, brilliant but too complex for the audience.
- Valphine adds *Choralum Lux Maleficarum* — Lathanderian brightness woven with Lolth-influenced dissonance in her contralto voice.
- The crowd is silent, watches, then applauds.
- Vukradin: "Perhaps we should play together." A partnership is born.

### Wererat Ambush
- **(Briefly described in the chapter — minimal detail):** The party encounters wererat-infested bandits on the road. The fight highlights the party's lack of silvered weapons and the difficulty of type change. Brief combat, party prevails. **(DM note):** This encounter deserves more detail if expanded. Wererats present an interesting challenge for parties without magic weapons — it tests resource management and creative problem-solving.

### Silverleaf's Lighthouse Tale
- Daran Edermath ("Silverleaf"), a retired half-elf adventurer turned orchard-keeper, approaches Vukradin.
- He tells the party about the Tower of Storms: an old stone lighthouse west of Phandalin on the coast that draws ships to crash on the rocks.
- The wrecks "must be filled with treasure."
- Vukradin's reaction is immediate and sharp: "No! No! No! That's not treasure. How many times do I have to explain this. You don't go and rob from the dead and call it treasure!"
- Silverleaf is shocked and apologizes.
- **(DM note):** Silverleaf's embarrassment is a missed hook opportunity. A retired adventurer of his experience might know more about the lighthouse than he's letting on — perhaps he's been inside, or knows someone who has.

### Meeting Ser Kaelen Thorn
- Ser Kaelen enters the inn — early 30s, confident, dark hair, clean-shaven, scar above right eyebrow. Chain mail. Emblem of a gauntlet grasping a sword by the blade.
- Vukradin misidentifies the emblem as a musical symbol. Awkward.
- Ser Kaelen has heard of Vukradin's "unique perspective on treasure. It's refreshing in a world that only measures worth in plunder."
- Valphine: "Or dead drow." Ser Kaelen does a double-take at a drow Lathander cleric.
- Brewbarry: "Order is bullies. They bully barbarians."
- Ser Kaelen handles it diplomatically: the war was brutal, people died on both sides, it was "over nonsense."
- Brewbarry accepts a drink.
- Ser Kaelen offers Vukradin associate membership (Rank Cheval). The benefits: influence, reach, a platform for his message.
- Vukradin accepts. Ser Kaelen: "Know this, you are a brother. And when we reach out for help, you must come to us as a brother."
- Valphine declines membership. Soma is pointedly excluded.
- **(DM note):** The Order's exclusion of naturalists is significant. This establishes faction lines that will deepen. Soma's naturalist philosophy is now flagged as incompatible with the Order of the Gauntlet.

### Planning for the Tower of Storms
- Vukradin fixates on destroying the lighthouse. Soma: "The lighthouse is meant to protect the boats, not destroy them."
- Harbin warns against destruction: "Lord Neverember would be appalled!"
- Ser Kaelen suggests: "I think it's the second" — a monster haunting the place, not a supernatural curse.
- The party resolves to investigate the Tower of Storms as their next major quest.

## NPCs
- **Ser Kaelen Thorn** — Early 30s human. Confident, capable. Dark hair, clean-shaven, scar above right eyebrow. Wears chain mail. Emblem of the Order of the Gauntlet (gauntlet grasping a blade). Recruited Vukradin as Rank Cheval. Diplomatic with Brewbarry about the war. Offers influence and platform over wealth. Pointedly excludes Soma from membership based on naturalist alignment. **(DM note):** Ser Kaelen's pointed exclusion of Soma suggests he knows more about the naturalist/interventionist conflict than he's revealing. He may have encountered naturalists before.
- **Daran Edermath "Silverleaf"** — Tall, lean half-elf with silver-streaked hair, green eyes, and a well-trimmed beard. Retired adventurer, now an orchard-keeper. A proud member of the Lord's Alliance or former Order of the Gauntlet himself. Wears simple earth-toned clothing. Friendly and well-meaning. Shares the lighthouse rumor. Was shocked and apologetic when Vukradin called him out on "grave-robbing."
- **Toblen "Spiderman" Stonehill** — Reveals his childhood nickname: fell into a spider nest, emerged covered in baby spiders, walked calmly away. Valphine finds this fascinating.
- **Martisha Vinetalker** — (Brief mention in transitioning to Wayside Inn) Half-elf owner of the Wayside Inn. Cold, calculating, charismatic. (Referenced in chapter 6 but not deeply encountered.)
- **Backes Dunfield** — Human bartender at Wayside Inn. Scarred face, crooked smile.
- **Cooragh Struckt** — Cook at Wayside Inn. Famous for well-spiced stews and meat pies.
