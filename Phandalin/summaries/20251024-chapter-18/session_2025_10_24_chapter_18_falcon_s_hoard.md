# Chapter 18 Falcon's Hoard

Date: 2025-10-24

## Summary
While his companions slept, Vukradin slipped out of the guest house under the cover of night and made his way into the main lodge. He moved through the dining room — a grand trophy gallery lit by iron chandeliers hanging from high rafters — and crept up to the upper floor, scouting the hallway before arriving at the door to Falcon the Hunter's private quarters. Wrapping the room in a sphere of absolute silence, Vukradin stepped inside and began his search.

The bedroom was cozy but untidy, the unmistakable den of a bachelor who had lived alone for many years. On a curio beside the bedstand, Vukradin found a pair of wooden elven ears strung on a golden chain, inscribed with the words "my dearly beloved," and a delicate wooden square carved with a labyrinth maze that he recognized as a representation of the legendary Undercity of Waterdeep. Prying open a locked drawer with his dagger, he discovered a small iron box crafted in the remarkably realistic shape of a dragon's head — so anatomically precise it seemed as though someone had severed a blue dragon's head and shrunk it down as a vindictive trophy. When he wound the key on its base, the jaw creaked open to reveal a brass ring inscribed with the word "lightning" in both Elvish and Sylvan script. Vukradin returned everything as best he could, retreated to the guest house, and spent the remaining hours of the night sketching every detail he could remember by candlelight.

Come morning, Falcon the Hunter stormed into breakfast with the brass ring in hand, convinced that the Cult of Talos had broken into his room during the night — and that they had failed to steal the ring only because they could not figure out the mechanism of the dragon-head box. Vukradin listened with a perfectly composed expression as Falcon unknowingly exonerated him entirely. Seizing the moment, Vukradin feigned wide-eyed wonder at the ring, claiming he had never seen an artifact from a dragon's horde before, and Falcon believed every word of it. He passed the ring around the table, allowing Vukradin to quietly improve his earlier sketches with fresh, daylight detail. Then, with the ring back in his hand, Falcon grew quiet and confessed the truth: the ring had come from the horde of Imrith, a fearsome blue dragon known as the Doom of the Desert. He had once been an adventurer, part of a party that faced Imrith — and he had been the only one to walk away. Years later, upon hearing of her death, he returned to her horde not as a conqueror, but as a grieving survivor seeking closure. The horde had already been plundered, bodies scattered across the fields, but enough scattered coins remained for him to buy land and build his lodge. His reputation as a dragon slayer, he admitted with a sheepish grin, was good for business — and the party agreed to keep his secret. Vukradin, for his part, left the encounter with a head full of threads: a lightning ring from a blue dragon's horde, a labyrinth tied to Waterdeep, a pair of ears belonging to someone dearly beloved, and a growing suspicion that Falcon's grudge against the Cult of Talos ran far deeper and far more personally than he had let on.

## Locations
### Guest House
A small building near the main lodge where the party stays, located near the north yard and the outhouse.

### Main Lodge
A large structure featuring a dining room that doubles as a trophy gallery with iron chandeliers and an oak dining table, and an upper floor containing Falcon the Hunter's private quarters.

### Falcon's Bedroom
A cozy but untidy bachelor pad located on the upper level of the main lodge, containing a walk-in closet and a curio filled with mysterious mementos from Falcon's past.

### Waterdeep
A legendary city mentioned in lore, specifically regarding a famous labyrinth located within its Undercity, which Vukradin recognized from a carving found in Falcon's room.

## NPCs
### Falcon the Hunter
The owner of the lodge and a former adventurer who publicly claims to be a dragon slayer. In truth, he scavenged the remains of the blue dragon Imrith's horde after she was already dead, using the gold as seed capital to build his lodge. He keeps personal mementos from the horde hidden in his room and is deeply suspicious of the Cult of Talos, whom he blames for the break-in.

### Imrith
A blue dragon known as the 'Doom of the Desert,' mentioned by Falcon. She is dead, and her plundered horde provided the wealth Falcon used to build his lodge. A miniaturized version of her head and a ring from her horde were found among Falcon's possessions.

## Items
### Wooden Elven Ears
A pair of wooden ears attached by a golden chain, featuring an inscription that reads 'my dearly beloved.' Found in Falcon's private curio, suggesting a personal and sentimental connection to someone from his past.

### Labyrinth Square
A delicate wooden square carved with a labyrinth maze, identified as a representation of the legendary Undercity of Waterdeep. Found alongside the elven ears in Falcon's curio.

### Miniaturized Dragon Head
A remarkably realistic and anatomically correct iron box shaped like a dragon's head, believed to be a shrunken blue dragon head. It functions as a mechanical box with a wind-up key that opens the jaw to reveal hidden contents. Found in a locked drawer in Falcon's bedroom.

### Lightning Ring
A brass ring inscribed with the word 'lightning' in both Elvish and Sylvan script. It was recovered from the horde of the blue dragon Imrith and kept hidden inside the mechanical dragon-head box. Falcon confirmed it is an artifact from Imrith's horde.

## Spells
### Silence
Cast as a ritual spell to create a 20-foot radius sphere of absolute quiet, covering Falcon's bedroom and the hallway. This allowed the caster to pry open a locked drawer and search the room without making any audible noise that could wake Falcon.
