# Chapter 11

Date: 2025-09-02

## Summary
Before setting out, the party took a moment to reflect on their purpose. Vukradin, the ever-ambitious bard, made no secret of his desire to forge alliances with everyone they encountered — not purely out of altruism, but with an eye toward filling future concert halls. His companion Velfanar, the Drow musician whose art blended the soaring choral traditions of Lathander with the grinding intensity of Drow metal, seemed unbothered by his mercenary motivations. Together with Soma the druid and the towering barbarian Brewberry, the group set off on the one-hour hike from Phandalin toward the Whispering Wood, tasked by the midwife Adabra with rooting out the source of a mysterious magical blight that had taken hold of the forest.

When they arrived at the edge of the Whispering Wood, the party found it deceptively ordinary at first glance. There were no rotting trees, no obvious decay — only a vague, creeping sense of wrongness that settled over them like a cold fog. Soma, attuned to the natural world, felt it most keenly: something was deeply off, though the forest wore its corruption like a mask, hiding it beneath a veneer of unsettling, too-vivid green. It was only as they pressed forward that the true strangeness of the wood began to reveal itself.

Their first encounter came in the form of a majestic stag standing in the path ahead — or rather, a stag that could not quite decide where it existed. Its fur shimmered and flickered, phasing in and out of reality, and its great antlers twisted through shapes that seemed to belong to more dimensions than the world possessed. Soma called upon her gift for speaking with beasts and addressed the creature, which was initially territorial and irritable, demanding the party leave its domain. After the group respectfully backed away from its boundaries and offered it some mushroom rations, the stag grew more forthcoming, explaining that it had once been an ordinary creature of the wood until everything changed. It pointed them toward the center of the forest as the origin of the strangeness, and mentioned that a group of druids had come before them, promising to fix things — but had never succeeded.

Deeper in the wood, the party came upon a dense thicket of brambles whose leaves pulsed with unnatural colors, blooming and withering in the same breath. As Soma moved to examine it more closely, the thicket lashed out with thorny tendrils, piercing her flesh and flooding her veins with a creeping poison. The creature seemed to bleed between planes of existence, phasing just enough to let Velfanar's radiant fire pass harmlessly through it — twice. Brewberry waded in with his halberd, its long reach allowing him to carve into the writhing mass of branches, while Vukradin hurled biting mockery at the plant, rattling whatever passed for its senses. Soma, shaking off the poison, transformed into a great brown bear and tore into the thicket with tooth and claw, and Brewberry finished it with a thunderous critical blow that left the brambles in ruin. Sensing the creature beginning to stir and regenerate, the party pressed on rather than risk setting the entire forest ablaze.

The eerie quiet that followed was broken by howls — distant at first, then overlapping and out of sync with the world around them, as though the sound itself was caught between realities. A dire wolf and its pack emerged from the shadows, and their unified howl struck a primal terror into Vukradin's heart, rooting him in place. The wolves moved with predatory coordination, biting and snapping at the party's flanks, knocking Vukradin to the ground. Soma called down a beam of pale moonlight that incinerated one wolf and scorched the dire wolf, while Velfanar outlined several of the pack in shimmering faerie fire. Brewberry, raging and relentless, cleaved through wolf after wolf with his halberd, cutting them down as they charged, even striking one dead as it tried to close the distance. The dire wolf fell to a final blow, and the last surviving wolf, its pack destroyed, disengaged and fled into the dark trees. The party stripped five regular pelts and one dire wolf hide from the fallen, noting the strange, blighted shimmer that clung to the fur.

As they ventured further into the heart of the wood, they stumbled upon a small, perfectly still pool. Rather than reflecting the canopy above, its surface showed twisted, anguished versions of their own faces staring back at them. Vukradin, bolstering his nerve with a burst of bardic inspiration, leapt into the water. The pool erupted into a kaleidoscope of mirrors, faces, and fragmented scenes, and within the chaos he glimpsed a distorted, pleading figure whispering about numbers — always the numbers — before the vision collapsed and he was expelled from the pool, crumpling unconscious on the bank. Brewberry, drawing on half-remembered legends of his people about such pools being windows to other places, decided to peer into the water himself and ask a question. The question he chose — which side of the bread to butter — was perhaps not the most dignified inquiry, but the pool answered it by driving him into a screaming, wide-eyed panic that sent him sprinting into the dangerous forest.

Soma, still wearing the form of the brown bear, gave chase and brought Brewberry down with a full-body tackle, pinning the terrified barbarian to the forest floor until the madness passed. He came around muttering about both sides, simultaneously, which the party chose to interpret as a cryptic insight rather than the ravings of a man who had asked an eldritch mirror about toast. With Brewberry calmed and Vukradin back on his feet, the group gathered themselves and pressed on toward the center of the Whispering Wood, approaching from the northwest to skirt the worst of the blighted bramble patches.

What they found at the heart of the forest stopped them cold. Caught within the grasping, pulsing brambles were a druid and two rangers, their green cloaks marking them unmistakably as members of the Emerald Enclave. Soma recognized them at once — these were the very people the blink stag had spoken of, the ones who had come before the party with promises to heal the wood and had never returned. They were alive, but ensnared, and whatever had trapped them had clearly proven more than a match for trained wilderness defenders. Vukradin's eyes lit up at the prospect of new allies, and the party moved to free them, steeling themselves for whatever lay waiting at the corrupted core of the Whispering Wood.

## Locations
### Phandalin
The town where the party received their quest from Adabra and from which they departed for the Whispering Wood, located about a one-hour hike from the forest.

### Whispering Wood
A dense wooded area located about a one-hour hike from Phandalin, currently suffering from a mysterious magical blight that causes planar leakage, resulting in shimmering and shifting flora and fauna, unnatural echoes, hostile vegetation, and reality-warping pools.

### The Distorted Pool
A small, still pool found within the Whispering Wood that acts as a planar mirror, showing warped reflections of those who look into it and causing temporary madness to those who peer too deeply or enter the water.

## NPCs
### Adabra
A midwife who previously aided the party and has now tasked them with saving the Whispering Wood from a magical blight caused by unknown interventionists.

### Velfanar
A Drow ally and musician traveling with the party who performs a unique mixture of Lathander choral music and Drow metal.

### Sir Kaelen
A figure of suspicion for Vukradin, mentioned in connection with a letter that has raised concerns within the party.

### The Blink Stag
A majestic stag caught between dimensions, possessing the ability to teleport short distances and leaving after-images behind. Its antlers constantly shift in four-dimensional patterns. It is protective of its territory but willing to share information in exchange for food, revealing that the forest's strangeness began at its center and that a previous group of druids had tried and failed to fix it.

### Emerald Enclave Druid
A member of the Emerald Enclave found trapped in the blighted brambles at the center of the Whispering Wood, part of an earlier expedition sent to heal the wood.

### Emerald Enclave Rangers
Two scouts accompanying the Emerald Enclave druid who have become ensnared by the unnatural, pulsing vegetation at the heart of the Whispering Wood.
