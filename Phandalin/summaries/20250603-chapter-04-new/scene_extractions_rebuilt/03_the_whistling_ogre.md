# The Whistling Ogre

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### While traveling through hilly terrain toward Phandalin, the party encounters a lone ogre with an unexpected habit.

- The party hears an ogre whistling cheerfully as it approaches through the hills; the sound drifts over the terrain before the creature comes into view.
- Vukradin attempts to harmonize with the creature but rolls only a 10 on performance; the attempt does not land as intended.
- A debate breaks out within the party: Valphine, familiar with Underdark ogres used as slave labor, wonders whether this might be a surface escapee from a slave pit; Valphine rolls 11 on insight but the DM rules that is not enough to distinguish a surface ogre from an Underdark ogre by appearance alone.
- Vukradin calls out asking whether the ogre is a musician or a slave; the ogre's reaction is immediate and violent: *"Me? Musician? Music? No. Me kill you. How dare you?"*
- The party rolls initiative; Vukradin rolls a 21 and takes the top of the order, but waves Valphine ahead of him — *"All right, Valphine, you're up."*
- Valphine attacks first with Sacred Flame — the ogre makes its save, taking four points of damage; she retreats to let Brewbarry anchor the front.
- Vukradin strikes with Starry Wisp for eight radiant damage from 60 feet back, staying out of melee range.
- Brewbarry rages and charges in, missing his first swing. When the ogre then leaves a square he threatens, his opportunity attack crits — nineteen points of base damage plus four, taking the creature from forty-seven down to twenty-four.
- The ogre answers with an ordinary hit for ten, halved to five by rage resistance.
- Soma uses Poison Spray from range for seven damage — an attack roll rather than a save, and no secondary condition.
- Soma considers using Mold Earth to loosen the hillside terrain and trigger a rockslide onto the ogre; the DM rules this would deal 1d6 damage but the logistics of measuring it prompt Soma to abandon the idea: *"Screw being creative."*
- The ogre is killed by a combination of Brewbarry's halberd, Soma's poison, Vukradin's radiant blasts, and Valphine's sacred fire before it can seriously threaten the party.
- Searching the ogre's belongings, the party recovers walloping ammunition — a specialized projectile enchanted so that any creature struck by it must make a saving throw or be knocked prone. The ogre, the DM notes, preferred walloping people personally and never figured out how to use it.
