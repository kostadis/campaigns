# Return to Phandalin

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party returns to Phandalin to collect their reward from Harbin Wester, sell the orc loot, and pick up their next commissions.

- After killing the ogre and looting the walloping ammunition, the party arrives in Phandalin.
- During the shopping visit, the table notes that Valphine — as a drow — really ought to have a hand crossbow; the DM agrees that a drow without one is *"less of a drow."* Valphine acquires a hand crossbow in Phandalin before the party moves on.
- The door to the Town Master's Hall is shut again and Harbin will not open it, insisting through the door that he is far too thin and bony to make a good meal for a dragon.
- Vukradin rolls a 20 on persuasion and talks him out; Harbin recognises his voice from the Stonehill Inn, then immediately walks the recognition back.
- Harbin pays the 50 gold pieces for warning the dwarves about the dragon. Vukradin sours the mood by describing the dwarves as grave robbers; Harbin cites Lord Neverember's ruling that adventurers may seize whatever treasure they wish.
- Valphine's insight read: Harbin is not merely irritated but genuinely anxious.
- Harbin offers the next set of commissions, noting a shortage of adventurers on account of the dragon. The party chooses the gnomes of Gnomengarde over the other hooks.
- The party sells the orc weapons to the smithy at half price (damaged) and the hide armor to the Lionshield Coster; Linene is evasive about what has disrupted her supply chains, and passes on a rumor about strange magic in Neverwinter Wood.
- At the Stonehill Inn, Toblen asks for one of Vukradin's own compositions rather than a popular number; the performance roll is average again. Attempts to read the room for further rumors turn up nothing. The party pays one gold for the night and departs for Gnomengarde in the morning.
