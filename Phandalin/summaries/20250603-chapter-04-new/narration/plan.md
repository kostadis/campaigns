## Scene 1
narrator: Brewbarry
chunks: 1
scene: A Clash of Ideals and Orcs
focus: Brewbarry is the only party member conscious and cutting through orcs from start to finish, watching Vukradin's diplomacy collapse, both casters drop — one to Soma's own ice knife — and ending the fight alone with back-to-back halberd strikes.

## Scene 2
narrator: Vukradin
chunks: 2
scene: Return to the Excavation Site
focus: Fresh off failing two death saves, Vukradin processes the day the only way he knows how — an impromptu flute elegy titled "Slaughtered Us" — while missing that his dwarven hosts had been hiding, weighing whether to rob his corpse, and would really have preferred a local ditty.

## Scene 3
narrator: Soma
chunks: 3
scene: The Whistling Ogre
focus: Soma's dry naturalist eye is perfect for the party's earnest debate over whether a whistling ogre is an artist or a slave — and for the anticlimax of grinding it down with reliable poison spray while everyone else philosophizes.

## Scene 4
narrator: Valphine Sotorra
chunks: 4
scene: Return to Phandalin
focus: Valphine is the one who reads past Harbin's irritation to something genuinely anxious behind the locked door, and her quiet acquisition of a hand crossbow — because a drow without one is "less of a drow" — makes the town errands a study in identity.

## Scene 5
narrator: Soma
chunks: 5
scene: Ambush in the Mountain Pass
focus: The ogre wants to eat Soma specifically — "Turtle. I eat turtle" — so the mountain pass standoff, her abandoned Mold Earth rockslide ("Screw being creative"), and the vengeful sibling's fury all land hardest from inside her shell.
