## Summary
The party's troubles began on a steep canyon incline when they spotted a group of orcs descending from the north. Ever the idealist, Vukradin strode forward and offered the warriors a choice between his clarinet and his trumpet, genuinely believing that music might bridge the gap between them. The orcs, interpreting his peaceful advance as a sacrificial offering, wasted little time in answering with the blade of a greataxe. What followed was a brutal and chaotic skirmish: Soma lashed out with a conjured vine of thorns, dragging one orc tumbling down the hillside, while Brewbarry flew into a thunderous rage and carved through the orcish ranks with his halberd. Vukradin was struck down not once but twice during the melee, revived the first time by Valphine's Healing Word and left down the second, stabilised only once the fight was over, a painful lesson in the limits of diplomacy.

The fight reached its most chaotic moment when Soma hurled a shard of conjured ice at the remaining orcs, only for the resulting explosion of freezing shards to catch Valphine in the blast, dropping her unconscious alongside the enemies she had been fighting. With two of their number fallen and the situation looking grim, Brewbarry anchored himself and cleaved through the last of the orcs, ending the threat and leaving the hillside quiet save for the sound of heavy breathing. The party stabilized their wounded, stripped the orcs of their javelins, hide armor, and battle axes, and made their way back to the dwarven excavation site to lick their wounds. Around the campfire that evening, Vukradin performed an impromptu original composition for the dwarves Dazlyn and Norbus, a mournful little piece he had apparently titled "Slaughtered Us." Dazlyn was unimpressed, Norbus was encouraging, and both gently suggested he might consider playing something the locals actually recognized.

Rested and recovered, the party set out toward Phandalin, only to be interrupted mid-journey by the sound of cheerful whistling drifting over the hills. The source turned out to be a wandering ogre, lumbering along without a care in the world. Vukradin attempted to harmonize with the creature, but the ogre made its feelings about music abundantly clear the moment it opened its mouth: "Me? Musician? Music? No. Me kill you. How dare you?". The party drew their weapons, and the ogre was eventually brought down through a combination of toxic blasts, radiant energy, and Brewbarry's relentless halberd work. Among the ogre's belongings, the party discovered a pouch of unusual ammunition enchanted to knock its targets flat, a find that would prove useful sooner than expected.

Arriving in Phandalin, the party made their way to the Townmaster's Hall to collect the reward they had earned for warning the dwarves about the dragon. They were greeted by Harbin Wester's voice shouting through a firmly locked door that he was far too thin to make a satisfying meal for any dragon that might be knocking. Vukradin's silver tongue eventually coaxed the cowardly townmaster into opening up, and the fifty gold pieces were duly handed over, though Harbin's expression soured considerably when Vukradin began questioning the ethics of adventurers plundering ruins. Harbin cited the authority of his lordship as justification for the practice, but Valphine quietly noted that the man seemed deeply unsettled by the bard's moral convictions, anxious in a way that went beyond mere annoyance.

The party made the rounds of Phandalin's merchants, selling the recovered hide armor to Linene at the Lionshield Coster and offloading the damaged orcish weapons at the smithy. Linene mentioned in passing that her trade routes had been badly disrupted, and shared a rumor that strange and confounding magic had begun to pervade Neverwinter Wood, obscuring ancient ruins that had once been navigable. The evening ended at the Stonehill Inn, where Toblen Stonehill welcomed them back with open arms and a round of beer, and Vukradin took to the clarinet for the assembled patrons. The performance was serviceable if unremarkable, and the party turned in for the night with new quests on the horizon, chief among them a visit to the gnome enclave of Gnomengarde.

The road to Gnomengarde wound through a narrow mountain pass, and it was there that the party's luck ran out again. A hulking ogre stood squarely in their path, announcing with great conviction that it was hungry and that its preferred meal was, specifically, turtle — fixing on Soma. The party attempted to reason with the creature, offering food and even trying to stare it down, but nothing landed until Vukradin, growing impatient, boasted that they had already slain one of its kind just the day before. Rather than giving the ogre pause, this declaration sent it into a fury, and the fight was on. Soma opened with a blast of noxious gas while Valphine fired the enchanted walloping ammunition from a hand crossbow, sending the ogre crashing to the ground. Vukradin's Command then froze the creature in place, leaving it prone and helpless as the party pressed their advantage, and the session drew to a close with the ogre incapacitated but not yet defeated, the outcome of the confrontation left to be decided another day.

## Scenes

#### The party encounters a group of orcs on an incline, leading to a disastrous attempt at diplomacy followed by a sudden ambush.
- Vukradin attempts to parlay with the orcs, offering to play music or provide food, which the orcs interpret as a sign of weakness and a sacrificial offering.
- An orc attacks Vukradin with a great axe but narrowly misses.
- Soma retaliates by using a magical whip of thorns to drag an orc down a twenty-foot incline, leaving the creature prone and injured from the fall.
- Vukradin is struck down by a powerful orcish greataxe and falls unconscious.
- Valphine calls upon divine power to heal Vukradin's wounds and strikes at the orcs with sacred flames.
- Brewbarry enters a rage and cleaves through the orcish ranks with a halberd, slaying one attacker as it tries to reposition.
- An orcish warrior hurls a javelin at Brewbarry, who shrugs off the brunt of the impact through sheer barbarian resilience.
- Vukradin is knocked unconscious a second time by a brutal greataxe swing.
- Soma attempts to finish the remaining orcs with a conjured ice knife. The shard misses entirely, but explodes anyway; both surviving orcs make their saves and only Valphine fails hers, knocking her unconscious.
- Soma finishes a wounded orc with a blast of poison spray.
- Brewbarry continues his relentless assault, cutting down the last two orcs with his halberd to end the threat.
- The party stabilizes their fallen comrades and takes a long rest to recover from their near-death experiences.
- The group loots the fallen orcs, finding a broken hand axe, javelins, hide armor, and four battle axes, though Vukradin expresses moral concerns about keeping 'blood money'.

- Dazlyn is unimpressed by Vukradin's original musical compositions while Norbus is more encouraging.
- Both dwarves express disappointment that Vukradin chose to play one of his own personal compositions instead of a more popular local tune.
- The party prepares to leave the excavation site and head back to Phandalin.

- Vukradin attempts to harmonize with the creature, but his performance fails to impress.

- The party wears the ogre down with repeated poison spray, Starry Wisp and Sacred Flame until it falls.

### Ambush in the Mountain Pass
#### On the road to Gnomengarde, the party is confronted by a hungry ogre blocking a mountain pass.
- A hungry ogre blocks the party's path in a mountain pass, demanding to eat and expressing a specific craving for turtle — meaning Soma.
- The party attempts to offer food and reason with the ogre, but it is not interested.
- Vukradin attempts to intimidate the ogre by claiming they killed another ogre recently, but this only enrages the creature further.
- Combat begins with the party struggling to establish a proper marching order in the narrow terrain.
- Soma opens with a blast of toxic mist that deals damage to the ogre.
- The ogre charges the party but swings its axe wildly and misses.
- Valphine moves to strike with her mace but cannot reach the ogre without backtracking through Brewbarry, and never gets the attack off.
- Valphine switches tactics, using a hand crossbow loaded with walloping ammunition that knocks the ogre prone.
- Vukradin's Command halts the ogre, preventing it from moving or taking actions.
- Soma delivers another blast of poison before the session ends with the ogre still alive but incapacitated.

## Items
### Halberd
A long-reaching polearm used by Brewbarry to cleave through multiple orcish attackers during the canyon skirmish, proving highly effective when combined with his barbarian rage.

### Sending Stones
Valuable magical items in the party's possession that allow for long-distance communication. When their provenance is challenged, Vukradin insists he obtained them in a fair and honorable way, then pushes the logic to absurdity — asking which wizard made them and how he came by the spell components. His blood-money objection this session is aimed at the orc loot, not the stones.

### Orc Battle Axes
Four weapons recovered from the orc corpses after the canyon skirmish, considered 'blood money' by Vukradin due to their likely stolen origins.

### Hide Armor
Armor looted from the orcs after the skirmish, found to be in fine shape and later sold to the Lionshield Coster in Phandalin for ten gold pieces.

### Walloping Ammunition
Specialized ammunition recovered from the first defeated ogre on the road. A creature hit by it must succeed on a saving throw or be knocked prone. Used effectively against the ogre in the mountain pass, knocking it to the ground.

### Hand Crossbow
A compact ranged weapon used to deliver the walloping ammunition against the ogre in the mountain pass, knocking the creature prone.

### Clarinet
A reeded woodwind instrument carried by Vukradin, used to perform for the patrons at the Stonehill Inn and offered as a peace offering to the hostile orcs.

## Spells
### Thorn Whip
A magical vine covered in thorns conjured by Soma to snag an orc and drag it down a steep twenty-foot hill, causing it to tumble and land prone. Later used again to attempt to pull the whistling ogre across the battlefield.

### Healing Word
A divine restorative spoken by Valphine to bring Vukradin back to consciousness mid-battle during the orc skirmish, restoring a significant amount of health.

### Sacred Flame
Divine radiance called down by Valphine to burn the orcish attackers during the canyon skirmish, and used twice against the whistling ogre for four points and then five.

### Ice Knife
A shard of ice hurled by Soma during the orc fight. It missed its target outright, but exploded into freezing fragments regardless. While intended for the orcs, the resulting cold burst accidentally struck and knocked out Valphine.

### Poison Spray
A puff of noxious gas projected by Soma, used multiple times throughout the session — first to finish off a wounded orc, then repeatedly against both ogres encountered on the road, dealing consistent damage each time.

### Starry Wisp
A luminous magical projectile launched by Vukradin to strike the whistling ogre with radiant energy, dealing moderate damage from a safe distance.

### Mold Earth
Attempted by Soma during the mountain pass ogre fight with the intent to loosen terrain and cause a rockfall, but the spell was deemed insufficient to cause direct damage and the idea was abandoned in favor of Poison Spray.

### Command
Cast by Vukradin during the mountain pass ogre fight to halt the ogre's movements, leaving it unable to move or take actions until the end of its next turn.
