# Return to the Excavation Site

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party returns to the dwarven excavation to rest and interact with the miners they previously assisted.

- When the party arrives back at the excavation, Dazlyn and Norbus are not immediately visible — they had been hiding, watching to see whether the group survived the orcs and quietly weighing whether to flee or attempt to take the gem the party was carrying.
- Seeing the party alive, both dwarves emerge cautiously. Dazlyn's first words: *"You're not here for the gem, right?"*
- Reassured on that point, the dwarves welcome the group and move to a fire pit where they cook a pleasant meal.
- Vukradin plays flute for the assembled group — an impromptu original composition apparently centered on the phrase "The orc slaughtered us, slaughtered us" — a mournful piece about the day's events.
- Dazlyn is unimpressed by Vukradin's original composition; Norbus is more encouraging, keeping him at it.
- Both dwarves express the same mild disappointment: they would have preferred one of the more popular local ditties over a personal original work.
- The party takes their long rest here before heading back to Phandalin in the morning.
