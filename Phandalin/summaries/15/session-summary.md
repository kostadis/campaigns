# Chapter 15 Freeing Big Al, Petunia, and Watching a Ranch Burn

Date: 4-02 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The parley with the four Carver's brigands in Conyberry fails, as Corbin warned it would. The brigands draw steel, and combat erupts.

**COMBAT (Brigands in Conyberry):** Soma transforms into a brown bear and kills one brigand instantly. Brewbarry, also enlarged, dispatches another. Vukradin puts one to sleep with magic. The fourth flees. The party has a prisoner — Skippy, a terrified Carver foot soldier.

Interrogation reveals critical intelligence: the Carver is using the Butterskull Ranch quest as a trap. Borg the Hammer, a half-orc enforcer, is waiting at the ranch with Rot-Tusk Ogres. The Carver's spy network in Phandalin is run by "Sister Kayla." The Carver knew Harbin would post a quest, knew adventurers would take it, and set an ambush.

Vukradin wants to return Skippy to Phandalin for safety — days of travel. Corbin volunteers to guard the prisoner. Vukradin warns him: "He better not be dead when I return."

The party approaches Butterskull Ranch stealthily from the north. Dead bodies — orcs and humans — litter the grounds, swarming with flies. The orcs' wounds look mangled, suggesting larger, more vicious attackers. Vukradin resists the temptation to loot the corpses: "It is not what I do in front of anyone that sets my moral compass. But what I do when no one is looking."

They find the cellar door, slip in, and discover Big Al Kalazorn tied to a chair with a burlap sack over his head. Vukradin frees him. Big Al explains: orcs raided first, then Borg the Hammer killed the orcs and took over. The whole thing is a trap.

Big Al refuses to leave without his cow, Petunia. He offers mithril chainmail as a reward. Vukradin verifies the chainmail is legitimately owned (bought, not stolen) and accepts the quest. Soma uses Speak with Animals — pigs direct them to Petunia. The cow is recovered and whisked to safety.

The party observes from a nearby hill (250 feet away). Borg the Hammer emerges, finds Skippy's escapee (Bandit #4), and executes him in rage. Borg sets the ranch on fire — farmhouse, cornfields, gardens. Three Rot-Tusk Ogres emerge. Borg splits his forces: the ogres are pointed in one direction while Borg heads another.

Valphine finds the destruction a "glorious, fleeting display." The party decides unanimously: follow Borg.

## Memorable Moments

> "It is not what I do in front of anyone that sets my moral compass. But what I do when no one is looking. I will not scavenge them. That is not who I am and it is not my style."
> — Vukradin, refusing to loot corpses when no one would know

**Integrity in the Dark.** Vukradin's moral code faces its truest test — alone with dead bodies and no witnesses. He passes. This is the most important character moment he's had: his philosophy is real, not performative.

> "He better not be dead when I return."
> — Vukradin, warning Corbin about the prisoner

**The Moral Line Drawn.** Vukradin knows exactly what Corbin wants to do to Skippy. His warning is a clear line: no revenge killings on his watch. It's a rare moment of steel beneath the bardic charm.

> "I really need my cow. She's my prized possession. Without her, you know, the Butterskull Ranch is just another…"
> — Big Al Kalazorn

**Butter Over Blood.** In the middle of a life-or-death rescue, Big Al's priority is his cow. The absurdity is played completely straight, and it works — Petunia becomes an immediate party priority.

> "Can I take his armor? If I take his armor, is it conflict-free armor?"
> — Corbin, eyeing Skippy's gear

**The Vukradin Infection.** Corbin has been corrupted by Vukradin's loot philosophy. His genuine question about whether prisoner armor is "conflict-free" proves Vukradin's influence has spread beyond the party.

> "I think we follow Borg."
> — Vukradin, after Borg burns the ranch

**The Hunter's Choice.** The party could chase the ogres, save the ranch, or pursue Borg. They choose the tactically superior option: go for the leader. It's a rare moment of party-wide strategic alignment.

> "It might affect your chainmail."
> — Vukradin, on the burning farmhouse

**Priorities.** While the party debates whether to save the ranch, Vukradin's primary concern is whether mithril chainmail can survive fire. (It can.) The man has his priorities straight.

## Scenes

### Combat 1: Conyberry Brigands
- **Opening conditions:** Four brigands, Carver's gang members. Parley fails. Bandits draw steel. Open ground in Conyberry ghost town.
- **Round-by-round:**
  - Soma transforms into brown bear, charges — kills first bandit instantly (claws + raw damage).
  - Brewbarry becomes large, kills second bandit instantly (halberd, primal fury).
  - Vukradin casts Sleep on one bandit — neutralized without death.
  - Fourth bandit sees three companions dead or unconscious — flees. Party does not pursue.
- **Key tactical moments:**
  - Soma and Brewbarry's alpha strikes removed two threats in one round, causing the fourth to rout.
  - Vukradin's Sleep brought a non-lethal capture option, enabling interrogation.
  - The party collectively decided not to chase the fleeing bandit — a deliberate choice to avoid unnecessary killing.
- **Enemy behavior:** Brigands were overconfident initially, expecting a 4v4 fight. They had no answer for a bear and an enlarged barbarian. The fleeing bandit was not pursued — the party chose information over revenge.
- **Spell/ability impact:**
  - Sleep (Vukradin): Non-lethal capture. Allowed interrogation.
  - Wild Shape (Soma, bear): Devastating alpha strike.
  - Enlarge (Brewbarry): Enabled the instant kill.
- **PC resource decisions:** Soma burned a Wild Shape on the first fight of the day. Brewbarry may have raged.
- **Post-combat:** Skippy captured. Interrogation yields: Carver's trap at Butterskull, Borg the Hammer leading, Sister Kayla running Phandalin spy network.

**(DM tactical note):** The party's two heavy hitters (Soma in bear form, enlarged Brewbarry) could have overkilled the first bandits, but in a 4v4 they needed to act decisively to prevent the bandits from getting attacks in. If the bandits had acted first and focused fire on Vukradin (the squishy caster who started the parley), the fight could have gone differently. The DM may consider what the bandits' opening action would have been if they'd won initiative.

### Interrogation and Prisoner Disposition
- Skippy wakes up surrounded by bear-Soma, enlarged Brewbarry, and Vukradin.
- Cracks immediately: "I don't know where the Carver is."
- Reveals the Butterskull Ranch is an ambush, led by Borg the Hammer.
- The Carver anticipated Harbin's quest board — it's a standard trap.
- Sister Kayla runs Carver's spy network in Phandalin.
- Vukradin insists on returning Skippy to Phandalin (days of travel).
- Corbin volunteers to guard Skippy. Vukradin: "He better not be dead when I return."
- Corbin asks about taking Skippy's armor — "conflict-free armor?" — Vukradin says no.

**(DM note):** Skippy's intelligence is valuable but time-sensitive. Borg may change plans if his ambush doesn't trigger. Corbin's desire to watch Skippy is partly revenge-driven; Vukradin's warning creates a clear moral boundary.

### Approach to Butterskull Ranch
- Party approaches stealthily from the north, bypassing main trail.
- Dead bodies everywhere — orcs and humans, flies already swarming.
- Vukradin notes the orcs' wounds: mangled, ripped — consistent with ogre attacks.
- **Moral test:** Vukradin alone near unguarded corpses. Temptation to loot for his music studio fund. He refuses: integrity when no one is watching.
- Finds cellar door. Opens it quietly with sleight of hand.
- Descends into cool, earthen cellar. Shelves hold peculiar butter-shaped skulls.
- Big Al Kalazorn: tied to a chair, burlap sack over his head.

**(DM tactical note):** The party's stealth approach was critical. If Borg's sentries had spotted them, the ambush would have triggered with them still outside. Vukradin's decision not to loot the corpses was a deliberate RP choice — had he taken anything, a loot trail or missing items could have alerted Borg.

### Rescuing Big Al
- Vukradin frees him. Big Al is startled but relieved.
- "Is there more of you?"
- Big Al explains: orcs raided first, Borg the Hammer killed the orcs, took over.
- "They kept you alive as the bait, huh?" — Vukradin identifies the trap design.
- Big Al refuses to leave without Petunia — his prized cow.
- Offers mithril chainmail as reward.
- Vukradin: "How did you come by mithril chainmail, Big Al?"
- Big Al explains he bought it — retired sheriff of Triboar, predecessor to Darathra.
- Clean acquisition confirmed. Deal accepted.
- Party escorts Big Al to Corbin's camp.

**(DM note):** Big Al's mithril chainmail is a significant reward. It's light armor, potentially usable by multiple party members. His relationship with Darathra (current Triboar sheriff) may be relevant in future storylines.

### Finding Petunia
- Soma casts Speak with Animals.
- Pigs provide directions to Petunia's location.
- Petunia found with the stamp: "Big Al's Cow."
- Whisked to safety at Corbin's camp.
- Petunia retrieval successful. Mithril chainmail secured.

### Watching the Ranch Burn
- Party retreats to a hill 250 feet away.
- Observation begins.
- Borg the Hammer emerges: half-orc, chainmail, shield.
- Finds Bandit #4 (the fleer from Conyberry).
- Executes him for incompetence — no hesitation.
- Borg screams orders; three Rot-Tusk Ogres emerge from farmhouse.
- Borg burns the farmhouse, cornfields, and gardens — wanton destruction.
- Fire is high and bright. The ranch is lost.
- Borg splits forces: ogres pointed in one direction, Borg heads another.
- Brewbarry asks about saving the ranch; Vukradin worries about mithril chainmail's fire resistance.
- Party decision: follow Borg.

**(DM tactical note):** The party's choice to observe rather than intervene was tactically sound — a frontal assault against Borg + three ogres in open ground would have been dangerous. Following Borg to his base of operations may yield larger strategic gains than a single battle.

**Valphine's perspective:** The fire is "glorious, fleeting display" — she finds aesthetic value in the destruction. Her dark fascination with pain and transformation colors her reaction to violence in a way that's increasingly concerning to the rest of the party.

- **Corbin** — Former Carver gang member, party aide. Volunteers to guard Skippy. Asks about "conflict-free armor." Vukradin warns him not to kill the prisoner. His loyalty is being tested.
