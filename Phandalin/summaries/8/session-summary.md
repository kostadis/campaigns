# Chapter 8 The Tower of Storms: Echoes, Art, and the Serpent's Coil

Date: Taraskh 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The party arrives at the Tower of Storms, a grim lighthouse on a rocky coastal promontory that has been luring ships to their doom with corrupted light and harpy song. Three harpies — a male in grey rags and two females — immediately attack with their charm songs as the party approaches.

Vukradin's quick thinking with a Silence spell neutralizes the harpies' magical singing, breaking the charm on Valphine and Brewbarry before anyone is lured off a cliff. The ensuing battle is protracted and messy — harpies prove surprisingly resilient, and Brewbarry's handaxes are consistently inaccurate. Soma takes spider form to scale walls, delivering venomous bites. Vukradin delivers the killing blow on the male harpy via Vicious Mockery, quipping him "to death." The party rests briefly before exploring deeper.

Within the tower, they discover frescoes depicting stormy seas and a dark god, a stone altar carved with lightning bolts, and a lightning rod — confirming Talos's presence. A harpy nest yields a potion of water breathing, sparking Vukradin's first objection to "blood money."

Upstairs, they confront Moesko, an Orc Anchorite of Talos clad in octopus-hide armor, seated in a barnacle-encrusted chair clutching an opalescent conch — the artifact belonging to Miral the Sea Elf. The fight is brutal: Moesko opens with Call Lightning and Thunderwave, devastating the party. Soma (brown bear) absorbs damage and deals heavy hits. Vukradin traps Moesko in Cloud of Daggers. Brewbarry lands a decisive charge. Soma, forced to revert from bear form in the cramped tower, delivers the final blow.

Valphine, fascinated by pain and Lathander's light, thrives in the combat's intensity. The conch is recovered, and the tower's corrupt beacon is identified: Moesko's own heart, still beating, radiating a sickly green light from the lighthouse top.

## Memorable Moments

> "He is mocked to death. To death."
> — Vukradin, after killing the male harpy with Vicious Mockery

**Mocked to Death.** Vukradin delivers the killing blow to the male harpy not with a blade but with a scathing insult, cementing his bardic style even in lethal combat.

> "Their music is just… They're not joining the Academy, that's for sure."
> — Vukradin, critiquing harpy vocal technique mid-battle

**Harsh Critic.** Even in life-or-death combat, Vukradin cannot resist grading the competition's artistic merit. The harpies fail his audition.

> "I didn't see anything at all; it just seemed like it was light."
> — Valphine, when Vukradin calls the beacon heart "evil"

**Light Cannot Be Evil.** Valphine's devotion to Lathander blinds her — literally and philosophically — to the possibility that light itself could be corrupted. Her argument that the heart serves a "useful purpose" reveals a pragmatic, almost utilitarian morality that clashes with the party's instinct to destroy it.

> "This will not do."
> — Vukradin, casting Silence on the harpies' charm songs

**Silence Falls.** Vukradin's first decisive magical intervention of the campaign saves the party from a potentially lethal charm-fall off the cliffs. The moment establishes him as more than just comic relief — he has tactical instincts.

> "I use my Bardic Inspiration, turning my failure into a success. It feels like failing upward, a perfectly Vukradin maneuver."
> — Vukradin, on breaking his own charm

**Failing Upward.** Even when charmed, Vukradin's self-awareness and resourcefulness turn a failed save into a success via Bardic Inspiration. He frames his entire life philosophy in a single line.

## Scenes

### Approach and Harpy Ambush
- Three harpies (one male, two female) ambush the party on the coastal approach to the lighthouse.
- Harpies use charm songs immediately: Vukradin's Silence spell (20-ft radius) nullifies the magical effect and breaks the charm on Valphine and Brewbarry.
- Combat is described as a "long, slow battle" — harpies have moderate HP and resist several attacks.
- Soma takes wolf spider form, scales the tower walls, and delivers venomous bites to the female harpies.
- Brewbarry struggles with accuracy, missing multiple handaxe throws — his axes land "a few feet away."
- The male harpy in grey rags attempts to resume singing; Vukradin breaks its concentration with a spell.
- Vukradin engages the male harpy with daggers (one hit, one miss), then finishes it with Vicious Mockery.
- The remaining female harpies are brought down by Soma's venom and Valphine's crossbow.

**(DM tactical note):** The harpies' charm mechanics require WIS saves each round. If Brewbarry had been charmed and lured toward the cliff edge, his low INT/WIS could have resulted in a PC death from fall damage. The party had no fallback plan if Vukradin's Silence failed — positioning on the approach was not discussed before engagement.

### Exploring the Tower
- Empty 15-ft-high room with a dirty window overlooking shipwrecks below.
- Frescoes depict stormy seas and a "dark and terrible god" — Talos iconography.
- Stone altar with lightning bolt carvings and a metal lightning rod.
- Harpy nest on a ledge yields a potion of water breathing.
- Vukradin debates the morality of taking "blood money" from the nest; Soma dismisses it as resources found, not stolen.
- Valphine observes the irony of shipwrecks being the result of the tower's curse — power dictates ownership, in her view.

### Confronting Moesko
- Moesko the Orc Anchorite sits in a barnacle-encrusted chair, clad in giant octopus-hide armor, clutching an opalescent conch.
- The conch is identified as Miral's artifact — the party's original objective.
- Battle begins. Soma opens with poison spray, then transforms into brown bear.
- Vukradin casts Cloud of Daggers, trapping Moesko in a 5-ft cube (9 initial damage, ongoing if he stays).
- Moesko retaliates with Call Lightning — most of the party takes heavy damage (halved on successful save; Brewbarry somehow evades entirely).
- Moesko follows with Thunderwave, pushing Soma back and dealing additional damage.
- Brewbarry charges Moesko, landing a hit that brings the anchorite to a single hit point.
- Soma reverts from bear form (cramped corridor made her multiattack impractical) and delivers the final strike.

**(DM tactical note):** The party had no lightning resistance and no counter-spell/dispel magic for Call Lightning. If Moesko had survived another round, the AoE would have likely downed multiple PCs. Brewbarry's charge was the decisive moment — any delay and the fight turns into a TPK. Soma's decision to drop bear form was correct (bear's large size was a liability in tight quarters), but she spent a full round transforming, during which she was vulnerable.

**(DM tactical note):** No one attempted to disrupt Moesko's concentration on Call Lightning before it was cast. A readied action to interrupt the casting could have reduced incoming damage significantly.

### Aftermath
- Moesko's body is searched: the opalescent conch is recovered.
- Party establishes that the tower's corrupted light source is Moesko's own heart — still beating, radiating green light from the beacon.
- The party debates destroying the heart vs. using it as a functional light source.
- The lighthouse's curse is broken with Moesko's death, but the green heart remains as a grotesque puzzle.

## NPCs

- **Moesko the Orc Anchorite** — Cleric/warlock of Talos, corrupted by a "foreign shadow" that twisted his devotion. Wears octopus-hide armor. Possesses the opalescent conch belonging to Miral. Uses Call Lightning and Thunderwave in combat. Has a hole in his chest where his heart was removed — the heart serves as the lighthouse's corrupted beacon. **(DM note):** Moesko was not entirely acting of his own will; something darker was manipulating him, connected to the Kraken Society and the tentacle-eye symbol.

- **Miral the Sea Elf** (mentioned, not present) — An undead sea elf whose conch was stolen by Moesko. The party's quest is to recover the conch and return it to Miral.
