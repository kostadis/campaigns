# Planning the Stakeout

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party pieces together the mystery of the third trade route and the missing gnome, and plans a midnight stakeout.
- The party assembles the timeline: seven weeks ago the displacement manifold went dark and regional distribution collapsed; Lionshield's store stopped receiving (they still carry Linene's written request for help with supplies); House Margaster now controls the port — and yet the Board Laid Bare's prices never moved, because Lim's supplier was never on the manifold.
- They deduce that a third trade route exists — one that "outlived the manifold, pays no harbor authority, and moves at the third hour after midnight, down a service lane onto aqueduct steps" — bypassing Margaster's control of the docks, which is also where Bimble the gnome used to work.
- They suspect that Bimble is surviving by intercepting the mysterious ninth crate from the nightly deliveries — "Who's feeding Bimbo? He's eating the ninth box," Vukradin concludes.
- The danger with a stakeout is scaring off the suppliers, so the plan is built around not being seen: Soma suggests turning into a cat or watching through an animal familiar — cat, lizard, or rat. "Perfect, a rat." The familiar can stay out of sight and hear everything, and the watch can be repeated every night as needed.
- Brewbarry adds his own errand to the list: find a magic shop to buy a listening device to plant on Margaster's goon.
- The DM notes that sewers tend to have rats — and where there are rats, there are probably were-rats, who tend to know each other. The were-rats of Mountain's Toe Gold Mine may have been from Neverwinter, and the party has a standing business relationship with Zeleen — an angle worth keeping in mind.
- Scheduling is debated: the dinner with Lord Neverember does not have to be tonight (it could be tomorrow night), and the party is also due to meet a lord for tea — "Tea and Intrigue." Brewbarry offers to run the stakeout solo while the others dine; Vukradin vetoes splitting the party in case of combat ("Just a stakeout — how would there be a combat?").
- With the delivery not due until the third hour after midnight, the party decides to use the remaining time to visit the Common Chord.
