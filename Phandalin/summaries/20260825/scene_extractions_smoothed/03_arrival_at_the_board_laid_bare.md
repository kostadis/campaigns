# Arrival at The Board Laid Bare

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party arrives at Soma's favorite restaurant in the Blue Lake District and is greeted by a precise warforged host.
- The party enters the Blue Lake District and finds many shops shut, eventually reaching The Board Laid Bare.
- They are met at the door by Bellows, a humorless and precise warforged host, whom Soma remembers from her regular visits as unfailingly humorless and precise.
- Bellows states the table options with precision unusual even for servers: "Three tables available. The table near the window offers the best view of the tower. The corner table is preferred by guests who do not wish to be observed. I recommend the corner table."
- Soma declines the recommendation — "Window, please. I have guests today" — explaining that unlike her old solo visits, she now has company.
- Bellows recognizes Soma, noting she has been absent for exactly 73 days: "I've seen you before. You are the one known as Soma, a friend of Lim's. ... You haven't been here in 73 days." Soma allows that "it's been an interesting journey."
- Brewbarry attempts to push two window tables together to accommodate his size — the goliath takes up a table almost all on his own.
- Vukradin asks the host's name ("Bellows!") and the party inquires about Bimble — "Do you know a gnome named Bimbo?" — but Bellows claims not to know him: "No, I do not."
- Bellows presents a handwritten card: fresh fish, vegetables, potatoes, two sauces — and no alcohol.
- Soma asks for Lim and orders the restaurant's famous soup for the group, trusting the kitchen: "Trust someone to order for you."
- The party notices that the menu prices are unusually low compared to the rest of Neverwinter's inflated economy — pre-disruption prices, which Soma rationalizes as "farm-to-table... It has its own supply chain right within the walls" and Vukradin dubs "Organic Fair Trade!"
- Brewbarry makes a mental note: "We should make a mental note to talk to your friend Lim and find out how."
