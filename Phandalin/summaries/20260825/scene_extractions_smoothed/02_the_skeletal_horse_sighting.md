# The Skeletal Horse Sighting

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### While walking through the Protector's Enclave, a passerby claims to recognize Brewbarry's skeletal horse, Boney.
- A stranger stops the party, claiming to have seen Boney before: "I've seen that horse somewhere!"
- Soma insists the man put his questions to the horse directly; the man asks Boney whether he was ever "fritting around outside of the walls of Neverwinter," and Boney — who once belonged to Lady Alagondar — says yes, he's "been grazing all over the place."
- The man is initially confused, wondering if he saw the horse in the fields outside the city walls — "It might have been the tackle... It might have been the posture of the horse. I'm sure I've never seen a skeletal horse before" — even floating the theory that it was one of Boney's children or a sibling, prompting Soma's alarmed "Boney has kids? ... Wait, are his kids skeletal?"
- Vukradin dryly notes that "most skeletal horses look alike to me. ... It's just bones, right?"
- After some questioning, the man realizes he simply saw Boney earlier that day: "I remember it was the horse that was outside of the service where that charming cleric gave a speech."
- Vukradin is done: "Oh, God, you're wasting our time... Stop talking to strangers, please." Soma remains unrepentant: "Stranger's just a friend you haven't met."
