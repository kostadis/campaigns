# Return to the Common Chord

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party arrives at their former haunt to find it has become the most popular venue in Neverwinter, now boasting a six-week reservation list.
- The Common Chord was once the kind of place that didn't ask too many questions and paid in actual coin: a room that held 40 comfortably, where Vukradin played three consecutive nights to audiences of ten to fifteen — eleven, really. "But always Brewbarry."
- In those days, Vukradin refunded his fee to every audience member who did not rate the show a ten out of ten — keeping only Brewbarry's money — and Petra, though she "really enjoyed the music," never booked him again. That is how the adventuring career began.
- The party arrives to find a six-week reservation list, fans cosplaying as Vukradin, and — after Vukradin's 21 Persuasion declaration, "We're Lathander's Death! We're here to perform!" — chanting barbarians and screaming fans.
- Brewbarry attempts to sell merchandise and take pre-orders from the crowd ("I don't have merchandise. The only merchandise I have is the one I'm wearing"), coached by his bandmates: "A-B-C. Always be closing." Meanwhile Valphine makes an impression in his dragon scale armor, drawing pointed stares from every corner.
- When the door staff balk at letting the band on stage, the question answers itself — "Who's gonna stop Brewbarry?" — and a 17 Strength check settles it.
- Vukradin confronts the proprietor, Petra, about the sign advertising a performance he never agreed to. She denies posting it — "That was more of a cover band idea than the real Lathander's Death" — revealing a man who plays Vukradin's songs on stage every night, always disclosed as not the real thing.
- Vukradin threatens legal action for his likeness — "I should sue you for a percentage of all your profits" — and calls in muscle: "Brewbarry, can you dispute for me, please?" Brewbarry towers over Petra: "Are you trying to rip off my friend? Making profit on his image and reputation?"
- Petra refuses the asking price twice — "I can't afford 500 gold pieces for a one-night performance" — and never agrees to pay it. A 23 Persuasion and the looming goliath close a different deal: 500 gold is named as the *value* of the past infringement and squared by one exclusive performance in two nights, with Petra licensed to keep the cover act in perpetuity so long as she keeps disclosing it. No coin changes hands. "I respect the image of the one, the only, the great Vukradin."
- Along the way Brewbarry floats a revenue share — "We get 50% of whatever earnings she makes while we sing" — prompting Petra's instant "Is that after or before expenses?" and the band's hard-won wisdom: "You always want gross" ("that is where Hollywood…"). Petra takes the deal for the concert, shaking on it before minds can change, drawing Vukradin's protest: "No, on the gross, on the gross!" Her verdict: "You've learned something, Vukradin. You're not the same guy I used to remember."
- Petra pleads her history — "I discovered you, man. I was there at the beginning. I was the one who gave you the gig. Nobody wanted to put you on stage, actually" — a point Soma concedes on her behalf.
- Vukradin's old table — really just where Brewbarry sat — is now polished, set with beautiful couches, and bears a plaque: "Vukradin created his music here." Asked to sign it, Vukradin replies, "Well, that'll be extra," extracting 25 gold pieces paid on the spot, as Petra despairs: "Brewbarry, what did you do to this guy? This guy used to be the nicest guy!"
- The band performs a teaser set — one song only, at Petra's insistence — nearly opening with the soup ballad before Brewbarry redirects: "That's not the one about Lord Neverwinter. There's one called Vault of Silver Lies, that's the one you want." Valphine's natural 1, combined with Vukradin's 14, comes out discordant and off-key — and the lack of harmony and off-cue screeches perfectly echo the original early Vukradin genre. "Very avant-garde."
- Brewbarry then proposes weaponizing their popularity in song. His first target — the evils of Lord Neverember — is vetoed by the band ("Lord Neverember is our ally against Margaster"; "The enemy of our enemy is our friend"), so he pivots: "So let's sing about the evil of House Margaster. And how corrupt they are. Plant that seed into the people's mind. ... Everyone who leaves this tavern tonight needs to think that House Margaster is the most evil organization."
- Vukradin offers to cast — "You want me to cast some spells, too, to, like, really alter their minds?" "Yes, yes, let's do that" — but no enchantment is ever cast; the GM checks ("You're not doing a special magical performance tonight, right?") and the second performance runs on a straight Performance check, boosted only by Bardic Inspiration and Bless (raw rolls of 14 and 7 lifted to 19 and 13). It lands: a witty lyric about Margaster and the trade routes profiting off the people's inflation, which the crowd picks up and hums along to.
- The party settles in to eat, noting the tavern's transformation: in the old days the Common Chord had no food and cheap booze Brewbarry once loved and now remembers as undrinkable swill; tonight the food is fancy and the beer is genuinely good — though when Brewbarry declares "Anything that will get me drunk is good, that's my definition of good," Petra reclaims the good beer, and Brewbarry keeps both anyway. "Pearls before swine," Soma sighs.
