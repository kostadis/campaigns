# Lim's Secret Supplier

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Brewbarry confronts Lim in the kitchen about her mysterious suppliers, and she eventually reveals the goods are moved through the city's sewer system.
- Brewbarry enters the small kitchen under the pretense of interest in cooking — halberd in one arm, dragon-slaying sword in the other, still in his bathrobe ("I'll always keep my weapons on me") — then catches Lim in a quiet moment and questions her about her supply chain, spinning a cover story: "I'm getting into business myself, and I'm looking for good suppliers or delivery guys who could move around some goods."
- Lim is reluctant to reveal her supplier, saying only "my supplier hasn't been affected by the changes" and calling the rest a trade secret — but Brewbarry presses her: "Just like the ninth crate, huh?"
- Lim clarifies that she only orders eight crates and has no knowledge of a ninth crate being delivered: "I don't order 9 crates, I order 8. I don't know what Bellows is talking about."
- Lim admits her supplier was never part of the official trade manifold and has not been affected by the economic disruptions — describing them as a group "keeping local businesses in line" that "believes in fair trade, organic goods, and not being trapped by the economic policies of Lord Neverember and the Neverwinter Commission." The party notes this doesn't sound like Margaster — "Sounds like the revolutionaries, actually."
- Brewbarry escalates as she stalls — "Do me a favor, I'll do you a favor... Or I make you an offer you can't refuse" — while Soma squirms: "I don't want to torture an old friend. ... Watching you shake her down makes me a little uncomfortable." ("I'm not shaking her down," Brewbarry protests. "I'm just being curious. I'm probing.")
- When Brewbarry finally threatens to simply stake out the delivery and interrogate the couriers, Lim breaks down — because she likes Soma: "There is a sewer system in the Bluelake District. If you go in there, you should be able to find the suppliers. ... They have to move the goods somehow."
- Vukradin recoils — "So you're telling me that the fresh food that you serve to your customers comes from the sewer" — and Lim corrects him: "No, I said it gets *moved* through the sewer." Brewbarry smooths it over: "Yeah, it's just transit, you know?"
- Lim explains she made a deal with the suppliers after they approached her once at the market — all she has to do is pay them on a regular basis — and asks Soma really politely not to scare them away or ambush them, because she has a business to run.
- Soma expresses disappointment in Lim for dealing with criminal elements — "You understand that these are not the types of people you should be in business with. ... You could have just raised your prices. ... They make demands in the future" — while Lim defends her actions: the food is good, the prices are good, "they're not bad people," and those inside Neverwinter's walls have had to survive rising taxes ever since something happened with the trade system. "I don't know where you've been, Soma."
- Vukradin needles: "So you're saying the ends justify the means?" Soma mock-gasps, "This is not the Lim I once knew. Roll for initiative" — kidding, mostly.
- Lim, hurt, tells Soma: "You've changed, and you're different. I remember a different Soma." Soma shrugs it off — "The only constant in life is change" — but the wound is real on both sides.
- Valphine, a child of Menzoberranzan who finds nothing strange about underground transport ("It's very natural to transport things in the ground"), immediately puts one and one together: the trade routes are currently controlled by various cartels, and these are smugglers.
