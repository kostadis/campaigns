# Dining with Lim and Interrogating Bellows

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party enjoys a meal at The Board Laid Bare while Valphine and Brewbarry separately question Bellows about the restaurant's mysterious supply chain.
- Lim, the tortle chef and proprietor, greets Soma warmly and serves the party food she has already decided they want — she doesn't wait for an order, sets the tray down, and sits: "You came back? I thought you'd gone somewhere northern."
- Lim sizes up the great Vukradin and delivers her verdict: "I don't like your music. ... It's not even useful. But everybody else seems to like it. Do you know that your place, where you used to play, is now the hottest ticket in town?"
- Lim mentions that the Tower District has become quieter — "not safer. Quieter is different" — and notes that three weeks ago, people dressed as Many-Starred Cloaks associates entered the Cloak Tower by the basement stairs, spent the day, and "came out with nothing." Something has changed in the neighborhood.
- Lim probes Soma about her transformation — last she heard, Soma had gone off to be a guide, and now there are stories of "some druid of Mielikki with some staff that's sprouting leaves," sitting next to "a cosplaying Brewbarry." ("He's not cosplaying," Soma corrects. "The real deal.")
- The food is fabulous — the turtle cuisine, in particular, "raw and steamed" — and Valphine's sharp eye (a 22 Insight) confirms the prices genuinely are below what anyone should be paying; the party is not being charged a special rate.
- Vukradin engineers the split: lavishing praise on Lim's cooking ("Could you show us the magic?"), he gets her to lead a delighted kitchen tour, freeing Valphine and Brewbarry to work on Bellows.
- A second read (25 Insight) tells Valphine that Bellows has the look of someone who could not tell a lie.
- Valphine questions Bellows about why the restaurant is not busier — "with the quality of this food... there should be people lining up outside" — and Bellows replies with literal precision: "All but two tables are empty. How many more patrons do you want?" He then explains that Lim believes only in word of mouth: "I've had this conversation with Lim many times. ... There will be no advertising."
- Brewbarry asks Bellows how the restaurant keeps its prices so low despite city-wide trade disruptions; Bellows answers, "We have very good deliveries," and, asked to elaborate, offers a menu of specifics: "When they arrive, how they arrive? What do the crates look like? Are they stamped? How many crates? What's contained in the crates?"
- Bellows reveals that deliveries arrive nightly at the third hour after midnight, occasionally the fourth, at the rear door — which opens onto a service lane connecting to the aqueduct — brought by a rotating group: "Two men bring the crates, sometimes three, they are not the same two men," some six or seven people oscillating over the last thirty days.
- The crates are "wooden, rope-bound, wet on the underside"; Bellows does not involve himself in how they are procured.
- Bellows notes that the order "has been 9 crates since midsummer. We use 8. I have raised it twice." Asked what happens to the ninth crate: "It does not come inside. It is set down, and it is not there in the morning. I have not asked. It is not billed to us."
- In the kitchen, Lim and Soma share the turtle soup joke: "Do you still tell people that we sell turtle soup? ... I can't believe they believed you. ... We did used to sell it, but it was for a different clientele."
- Vukradin argues for staking out the back door rather than confronting Lim; Brewbarry prefers the direct route — "She's a friend, we don't need to spy on her, we just ask her. ... If she doesn't [want to help], then we'll spy."
