We walk to the Common Chord. I have news for the band.

At lunch the soup was good. Lim's turtle soup, at the Board Laid Bare. Warm all the way down, and warm things should be sung about. So I got quiet over the bowl while the others talked, and I wrote. Somebody looked over at the scratching.

"Lyrics, on the napkin."

Now, on the walk, I tell them I have laid down a new track for the band.

"You laid down a new track?" Vukradin stops walking.

Now we have a couple. I told them I was writing at lunch. Nobody listened at lunch.

"Guys, we're talking about soup."

"Do you— do you have it prepared?"

I hand him the napkin.

"Our next track."

"Yeah, kill some time," Soma says.

Vukradin reads it as we walk. He gives it the full voice. Everything sounds better in his voice. That is why I follow him.

"When the cold winds bite in Neverwinter Town, and weary travelers start to get you down. There's a savory cure that's famed across the land, a bowl of warmth from good ol' Lim's own hand."

*Good.*

"Turtle soup slow simmer just right. It fills your belly with a pure golden light. They serve it hot at the Board Laid Bare. A taste of home that's beyond all compare. Yeah, Brewbarry, Brewbarry,"

Then he stops. I know the stop. It is the stop before a no.

"We're a— we're— this is Lathander's Death."

He hunts for the soft way to put the hard part down.

"Really our style."

"Oh, come on, young man."

"We really appreciate the effort, and thank you, and we really, we love." The sentence runs out of road. "You're our biggest fan, man."

The biggest fan of my own band. I turn that over. It is a strange seat.

"But— we've got our artistic direction."

"It's a marketing jingle," Valphine says. She rules on it and the ruling is final.

"Call him Yoko and move on," Soma says.

"We're not getting married!"

"Yay."

I do not know who Yoko is. Probably somebody else who wrote about soup. The song is still mine. Lim still gets a fan.

The Bluelake District is wrong in a small way. A woman locks a door behind her. Then she locks it again. It was already locked. My neck knows before my head does. My tribe had signs for things that repeat when they should not. I keep walking. I watch her hands.

"You're saying we saw deja vu?" Vukradin says.

"Or— was it the exact same cat?" Soma says.

Then a wizard's apprentice steps into our road, selling authentic many-starred cloak thread.

"Brewbarry? Is this, like, you want to incorporate this into your robes?"

"What, sorry?"

"Thread?" Soma says.

I look at the spool. This is my business now. Linene made it hers, so it is doubly mine.

"Yes. So, are they soft?"

They are not. The apprentice says so himself.

"Softies, huh?"

"It's decorative," Valphine says. "Decorous."

"The thread isn't what makes the fabric soft," Vukradin says.

I love him. He is wrong.

"It's the thread."

"So what makes this fabric soft is the thread, right?"

"It tracks. You can't…" Soma starts, and lets it stand.

"What do you think makes the fabric soft?"

"If the fabric is made of threads."

The argument lands. Nothing gets back up. Fabric is threads. Soft fabric is soft threads. A robe is only as kind as its smallest part. Something in me sits up straighter, like the world itself just handed me the point.

"Was this spray softness on top of the fabric?"

"Yeah, that's how it works. You're right, you're right," Vukradin says.

"It's not pure silk, you know."

We do not buy the thread. Decorative thread is a lie a robe tells.

Near the Common Chord a bill is posted. Lathander's Death, it says. Performing. Two days from now. Nobody agreed to this. I would remember. I remember every deal I make now.

"Hey, I didn't know— when did you guys make this deal?" Soma says.

"Let's add it to the quest list! We're doing a performance!" Vukradin is already glowing. "What is it?"

Two days.

"Okay!"

"Apparently they have," Soma says.

"Sounds like Vukradin already has," Valphine says.

"Yeah, I think he has."

"Where is it? What's the time?"

The bill answers him. Evening. The Common Chord. The same Common Chord that pours piss for ale and has been living off Vukradin's old songs. Now it hangs our name on its wall like it owns us.

"Definitely be there in 2 days. That is awesome."

"They summoned me."

"His naivety says: yep, show up," Soma says.

I will show up too. Somebody has to stand behind him and watch the room.

Through the Protector's Enclave a man argues, loud, that rents have doubled. He is right. Prices are climbing all over this city. I think about cotton. The robe has to stay soft and stay cheap. Both. That is the hard part.

Two rumors follow us through the streets. The first: Lord Neverember has taken a drow advisor.

"No," Valphine says.

"Drow," Soma says.

"Wait a second here…"

"It's you!" Vukradin says.

"Hough."

The second rumor: Lathander's Death is playing the Common Chord.

"We heard that one," Soma says.

Then the Common Chord is in front of us.
