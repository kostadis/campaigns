- **Location:** Summary; Scenes — “Ambush in the Sewers”
  - **Issue:** Brewbarry is said to attack with his halberd while staying clear of the darkness, but the recap also says Freedom of Movement let him automatically resist the Rift Weaver’s web. This conflates two different protections.
  - **Evidence:** Freedom of Movement addresses movement restrictions and restraint; it does not provide visibility through magical darkness. Brewbarry’s extended reach comes from his Path of the Giant combat features and halberd.
  - **Suggested fix:** Keep the claims separate: his halberd and Giant’s Havoc reach let him attack from the hallway, while Freedom of Movement prevented the web from restraining him.
