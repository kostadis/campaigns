# Chapter 52 Combat At Last

Date: 2026-09-08

## Summary
The party arrived at the House of a Thousand Faces, a bustling establishment hiding in plain sight as a secret Harper stronghold. Brewbarry was immediately distracted by a mannequin draped in an exceptionally soft-lined coat, muttering admiringly to himself and demanding to know its origin, while the rest of the group took in the room. A bartender greeted them with a rehearsed line about the tavern's selectively flattering mirrors and its more reliably satisfying drinks, all while the staff quietly evaluated the newcomers with careful, measuring eyes.

Valphine stepped forward and opened the conversation with a pointed remark about House Margaster's grip on distribution out of Neverwinter's harbor and the party's interest in restoring the old order. The bartender tested her carefully, probing whether she was a true cleric of Lathander, and Valphine said that people must understand how to use power. The bartender answered that power should be used only against equally powerful opponents and never for control — a statement of Harper ideology. The tension in the room dissolved almost immediately, and an elderly human wearing a Harper pin came up from downstairs to speak with them directly.

The Harper representative, sharp-eyed and guarded, explained that her organization had been running secret food routes to undercut the economic grip of House Margaster and the Zhentarim. She revealed that the Manifold — a critical piece of the city's infrastructure — had been shut down for weeks, and that the mechanic responsible for its repair had vanished without a trace. She also confirmed what the party had long suspected: that the ninth crate moving through the sewers was almost certainly intended for the missing gnome mechanic, a man the party had taken to calling Bimbo.

The conversation took a more unsettling turn when the representative described the creatures blocking the Harper supply routes — unnatural spiders that blinked in and out of existence, their heads replaced by gaping maws full of teeth, their webs shimmering with impossible colors. Valphine raised the matter of a Drow said to be influencing Lord Neverember, though the Harpers claimed no knowledge of such a figure. Valphine kept her suspicion to herself — that the Drow in question was likely her own sister, Aletra — though she did reveal her house name, Sotorra, which drew a stunned silence from the representative, who had believed the family to be long dead. Valphine replied coolly that it was often convenient to be thought so.

The representative then turned to Soma, encouraging her to give her old friend Lim the benefit of the doubt. She revealed that Lim had been a Harper agent for years and had originally suspected Soma of radical naturalist sympathies, gathering intelligence on her under that assumption. The Harper noted dryly that Soma had clearly changed — her staff and her interventionist outlook were nothing like what Lim had predicted — before remarking that her brief foray into friend therapy was now concluded for the year. The party was then tasked with clearing the sewer junction of the strange spiders so the Harper food routes could be reopened, with the representative offering clean clothes and disguises to ensure the group looked presentable for their dinner engagement with Lord Neverember that same evening.

Descending into the sewers, the party encountered Rsolk, the leader of a local gang who went by the Dead Rats, and negotiated a payment of two hundred and fifty gold pieces to deal with the spider infestation disrupting his smuggling routes. They sent a familiar ahead through the narrow passageway to scout, and it returned with a grim picture: a large, partially flooded junction choked with thick, shimmering webs, dominated by a massive boss spider clinging to the ceiling far above. Valphine wove a protective enchantment over Brewbarry before the fighting began, ensuring he could move freely through the water and webbing without being slowed or snared.

The battle erupted with Soma conjuring a rolling ball of fire that scorched through the webs and burned the nearest spider, exploiting the creatures' deep vulnerability to flame. She then transformed herself into a giant spider, skittering up to the ceiling to navigate the battlefield on the Rift Weavers' own terms. Valphine unleashed a tide of inky, magical darkness that flooded the chamber and assailed the minds of the creatures within, while the massive Rift Weaver Prime used Legendary Resistance to succeed on its saving throw, still taking half the psychic damage. Brewbarry raged and swung his halberd from the hallway entrance, cutting down one spider and badly wounding another, while a Rift Weaver attempted to ensnare him in a dry, glimmering web — only for Brewbarry to escape it through Valphine's Freedom of Movement.

Vukradin cast Spray of Cards, sending a cone of spectral cards into two spiders; the force damage killed one and wounded the other. He then retreated to safer ground. But the Rift Weaver Prime had other ideas — it vanished from its perch and reappeared directly in front of Vukradin, seemingly fixated on him above all others, and launched a sudden, vicious assault. Vukradin used his reaction to force the critical hit to be rerolled into a miss, as the rest of the party braced themselves for what was still to come.

## NPCs
### The Bartender
A staff member at the House of a Thousand Faces who uses coded language and rehearsed lines to vet visitors and gauge their political and moral leanings.

### Harper Seat
An elderly human woman and high-ranking member of the Harpers who manages shipping routes and intelligence. She is guarded with information and skilled at extracting what others know, but she tasks the party with clearing the sewer junction of Rift Weavers to reopen the Harper food supply route.

### Lim
A long-time Harper agent and former acquaintance of Soma. She previously suspected Soma of being a radical naturalist and was gathering intelligence on her, but the Harper Seat vouches for her loyalty.

### Bimbo
A gnome mechanic who was supposed to repair the Manifold but has since disappeared into the sewers. The party suspects the ninth crate in the sewers is intended for him.

### Rsolk
The leader of the Dead Rats sewer gang. He is pleased to see the party return well-armed and agrees to pay them two hundred and fifty gold pieces to clear out the monstrous spiders disrupting his smuggling routes.

### Rift Weavers
Extra-planar spiders that blink in and out of existence. They lack traditional heads, possessing instead a mouthful of teeth where a neck should be, and spin shimmering, dry, multi-colored webs to restrain their prey. They move in packs and seem to have a particular fixation on Vukradin.

### Rift Weaver Prime
The massive leader of the Rift Weaver pack, possessing Legendary Resistance and the ability to teleport across the battlefield. It clings to webs high above the ground and teleports directly to Vukradin during the battle.

## Locations
### House of a Thousand Faces
A tavern that serves as a secret Harper hangout, hiding in plain sight. It features mirrors that 'flatter selectively,' and displays of high-quality clothing on mannequins.

### The Sewers
A dark, subterranean network of tunnels beneath the city, serving as the territory of the Dead Rats gang. A specific junction is partially flooded, features a water control system, and is heavily choked with thick, shimmering spider webs spun by the Rift Weavers.

## Items
### Soft-lined Coat
A garment displayed on a mannequin within the House of a Thousand Faces, noted for its exceptionally soft lining, which catches Brewbarry's professional interest. The staff inform him that such fabric could be supplied to him through non-Margaster routes.

### Harper Pin
A small pin worn by the elderly human who comes up from downstairs in the tavern, signifying membership or high status within the Harper organization.

### Ninth Crate
A specific shipment located somewhere in the sewers, suspected by the party to be intended for the missing gnome mechanic, Bimbo. The Harper Seat more or less confirms this suspicion.

## Spells
### Freedom of Movement
A protective ward cast by Valphine upon Brewbarry before combat began, granting him the ability to move through the flooded, web-filled cavern without being slowed or restrained by the thick arachnid webbing. It lasts for one hour and allowed Brewbarry to automatically escape a Rift Weaver's restraining web.

### Flaming Sphere
A ball of fire conjured and controlled by Soma that rolled through the sewer tunnels, burning away spider webs and scorching the Rift Weavers, exploiting their vulnerability to fire damage.

### Doom Sphere
A powerful spell cast by Valphine that created a large radius of inky, magical darkness that slowly wafted eastward each turn. The cloud inflicted psychic damage on those caught within it and weakened the saving throws of those who failed to resist it. The Rift Weaver Prime used Legendary Resistance to succeed on the save, taking half the psychic damage.

### Bless
A divine enchantment active during the battle that granted increased accuracy to the party, helping Brewbarry land two strikes on a Rift Weaver that would otherwise have missed.
