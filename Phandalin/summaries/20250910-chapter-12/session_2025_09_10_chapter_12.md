# Chapter 12

Date: 2025-09-10

## Summary
The party arrived at a roughly sixty-foot clearing deep in the woods, where something had gone terribly wrong. The trees surrounding the grove gleamed like obsidian, their bark weeping iridescent sap that shimmered and shifted in the faint, broken light filtering through the canopy above. At the heart of the clearing stood an immense, tree-like figure whose limbs bent at impossible angles, its movements less like a living plant and more like a glitch in reality — a corrupted treant, twisted beyond recognition by some unseen planar force. Scattered across the blackened, thorny underbrush were pulsing tears in reality, whispering promises of power and madness, and magical wells that radiated dangerous, otherworldly energy. Tangled within thick, thorny vines were members of the Emerald Enclave, trapped and helpless, among them their leader — a druid named Lyra — who cried out to the party for aid, explaining that the grove's guardian had been poisoned by a planar infection and had turned on them while they were attempting to seal it away.

The battle that followed was chaotic and punishing. Bukradin attempted to compel the blighted vines to release their captives through sheer force of will, but the magical brambles resisted and held firm. Soma, still wearing the form of a great bear, charged into the thorns and took several vicious lashes before managing to tear through a cluster of vines with her powerful jaws, freeing Lyra and one of the other elven druids. Brewberry grew to an imposing size and waded into the fray, while Valfine cast a shimmering green light over the treant and the remaining vines, making them easier for the party to strike. The corrupted treant, meanwhile, bellowed that it was a feature of the natural world and demanded to be left in peace — but when the Enclave members continued to struggle, it slammed into one of the warriors with devastating force, leaving him clinging to life by a thread. Through coordinated effort, the party eventually tore through the remaining vines, freeing the last of the captives, while Vakradin rushed across the battlefield to pull the fallen warrior back from the brink of death.

With the Enclave members freed, the fight against the treant intensified. Soma shed her bear form and, alongside Lyra, conjured twin pillars of radiant moonlight that seared the corrupted guardian's obsidian bark in waves of brilliant, punishing light. The treant raged, striking down one of the Enclave warriors and trapping another in place with its vines, but the party pressed on relentlessly. Balthina called down radiant flames and a blazing bolt of holy light, while Brewberry delivered a thunderous blow that left the creature reeling. Finally, Soma unleashed a caustic cloud of noxious gas that tore through the treant's remaining strength, and the massive, twisted abomination collapsed and was still.

With the treant defeated, the party turned to the ritual Lyra had described — a series of precise, demanding actions performed at the ritual stones to seal the planar wells and close the rift for good. Despite the combined efforts of the party and the surviving Enclave members, the ritual proved brutally unforgiving. Failures mounted alongside successes, and a surge of psychic feedback from the failing ritual blasted the party with mental agony. When the third failure came, the wells did not close — they grew. The Emerald Withering Grove was fully and permanently claimed by the planar rift, and there was nothing left to do but retreat. Lyra, deeply distraught, admitted under Balthina's pointed questioning that she had not thought her plan through carefully enough, and confessed the ideological fracture running through the heart of the Emerald Enclave: the Interventionists, who believed planar rifts were abominations to be destroyed, and the Naturalists, who saw them as part of the natural order — a schism that had its roots in the War of the Dragons and the debate over whether Tiamat's survival had been the world's rightful fate.

As the party made their way out of the ruined grove, a deeply unsettling sight stopped them cold. A distorted, puppet-like creature with hollow, unfocused eyes emerged from one of the planar wells, moving with eerie purpose through the corrupted clearing. It paid the party no mind, instead silently harvesting ingredients from the fallen treant's remains before diving back into the well and vanishing. Vakradin activated a magical staff capable of detecting unnatural corruption, and it responded with an intense, unmistakable signal — every blackened, planar-scarred element of the grove was deeply, deliberately unnatural. Soma studied the scene carefully and came to a grim conclusion: the planar scars spreading across the region were not random accidents or quirks of nature. Someone — or something — was cultivating them on purpose. The party fled the grove with more questions than answers, and the weight of their failure hanging heavy over them as they turned their steps back toward Phandalin and the counsel of Adabra.

## Locations
### Emerald Withering Grove
A roughly 60-foot clearing surrounded by dense woods with obsidian-like bark and pools of shifting, iridescent sap. The area is marked by blackened thorny underbrush, flickering tears in reality, and magical wells that pulse with dangerous planar energy. The site of a failed ritual that left the grove permanently possessed by a planar rift.

## NPCs
### Lyra
The leader of an Emerald Enclave party and a druid of the Interventionist faction. She was trapped by blighted vines while attempting to seal a planar rift. After being freed, she guides the party in fighting the corrupted treant and attempting the ritual. She is deeply distraught after the ritual fails and reveals the ideological schism within the Emerald Enclave between Interventionists and Naturalists, tracing the split back to the War of the Dragons and the debate over Tiamat's place in the natural order.

### Corrupted Treant
The former guardian of the grove, now twisted by planar energies into a towering figure with glossy obsidian bark, limbs bent at impossible angles, and a voice that echoes with overlapping whispers. It claimed to be a feature of the natural world and viewed the Emerald Enclave as betrayers. It was ultimately slain by the party.

### Emerald Enclave Warriors
A group of elven members of the Emerald Enclave who accompanied Lyra. They were ensnared by blighted vines during the battle. One warrior was struck down by the treant and left clinging to life, another perished during the struggle, and a third was freed by the party's efforts. One warrior demonstrated significant skill in sleight of hand during the ritual attempt.

### Phased Creature
A distorted, puppet-like entity with distorted eyes that emerged from the planar rift after the battle. It appeared to be under the control of an unseen force and was observed collecting ingredients from the corrupted treant before diving back into one of the planar wells.

## Items
### Ritual Stones
Four stones summoned by Lyra after being freed from the blighted vines. They served as the focal points for a ritual intended to seal the planar rift affecting the grove, though the ritual ultimately failed.

### Magical Staff
A staff used by Vakradin that possesses the ability to detect unnatural blights and corruptions. When activated in the grove, it pinged intensely on all the blackened, planar-scarred elements, confirming their extra-planar and unnatural origin and lending support to the Interventionist position.

## Spells
### Command
Attempted by Bukradin in an effort to force the blighted vines to release the trapped Emerald Enclave druids. The vines resisted the mental compulsion and maintained their grip on the captives.

### Faerie Fire
Cast by Valfine, outlining the treant and the blighted vines in a shimmering green glow. This granted the party advantage on their attacks against the affected creatures for the duration of the combat.

### Guiding Bolt
Used multiple times during the session — first to strike the blighted vines with radiant energy, leaving them glowing and easier to hit, and later by Lyra to sear the corrupted treant and leave it momentarily vulnerable to further attacks.

### Moonbeam
Conjured by both Soma and Lyra, creating twin pillars of radiant lunar light that dealt consistent and significant damage to the corrupted treant. The stacking of two moonbeams proved to be one of the most effective tactics of the encounter.

### Sacred Flame
Called down by Balthina as radiant flame-like energy descending on the corrupted treant, bypassing its physical defenses to deal damage directly.

### Poison Spray
A puff of noxious, caustic gas used by Soma to deliver the finishing blow to the corrupted treant, finally bringing the planar abomination down.
