# The Notary of House Margaster

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party visits a notary house controlled by House Margaster to verify Perrin's lineage and the necklace's ownership.
- The party enters a notary house that, while appearing clean and legitimate, is known to be controlled by House Margaster and used to legitimize their business ventures through paperwork.
- Valphine investigates the premises and finds it to be a well-run operation — no signs of forgery, deception, or the usual Margaster demonic trappings on-site. The party's conclusion: "So all the forgeries happen off-site, and they're just notarized here." — "They notarize forgeries. Got it."
- Vukradin raises the identity problem: "How do we know that this is the gentleman who says he is? Anybody can impersonate anybody. He could be a polymorph, for all we know."
- The notary presents the 'Genealogy of the Alagondars,' claiming Perrin is a rightful descendant of Lady Alagondar, verified against forgeries, doppelgangers and miscellaneous magic through the 'threefold rule' — coordinating between three different sources of conflicting evidence with different provenance.
- House Margaster's agent offers to hold the necklace in 'layaway' at their warehouses as a form of 'title insurance,' which the party views as a scam — while the notary, initially confused by the term, visibly files it away: "hmm, this might be an interesting scam we could pull in the future." Soma asks whether an extended warranty on the necklace is also available; the notary allows that "all these flavors of insurance … could definitely be products that could be offered in the future."
- Brewbarry demands that Perrin produce three independent witnesses not affiliated with House Margaster to prove his identity before the necklace is surrendered — "then, you know, you can take a walk and forget about the necklace."
