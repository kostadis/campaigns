# The Shut Down Shipping Hub

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### While heading toward House Margaster, the party discovers a shuttered building near the docks that previously housed the city's magical shipping device.
- The party passes a building near the docks bearing a civic notice declaring it shut down by order of Lord Neverember.
- The notice states that the services for instantaneous shipping have been closed until further notice.
- Perrin explains that the magical manifold device simply stopped working, and nobody knows quite why.
- Perrin launches into a tirade suggesting the Neverwinter Commission sabotaged the device to exert more control over trade — that the Commission "has its hands in everything," and shut the device down so that cheap, easy shipping "would be turned into a mechanism of control."
- Brewbarry connects the dots with a start: "Haven't we been invited by Lord Neverwinter to have dinner?" — Vukradin: "That paid us for the mine?" — Brewbarry: "I'm starting to see your point."
- The party realizes that the disruption of this cheap shipping method has directly benefited House Margaster's traditional caravan business — "How convenient for House Margaster, isn't it?"
- Valphine argues that "undermining power structures is best done behind the scenes," and Soma warns that the powerful "don't need evidence or a reason to disappear anyone" — but Brewbarry lays out his own doctrine of open leverage: "You let them know … then you sabotage, then they know where it's coming from, and then, you know, they're forced to sit at the table." Soma, won over despite herself: "I truly love you, man, this is fantastic."
