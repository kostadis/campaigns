# Arrival at the Counting House

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party arrives at the Counting House in Neverwinter to address supply chain issues for Brewbarry's bathrobe business and collect a letter of credit for Vukradin.
- Brewbarry enters the Counting House wearing his bathrobe and delivers a rehearsed speech about fixing supply chain problems to an empty room, then waits for someone to appear.
- Brewbarry laments the wasted performance — "Damn! I had prepared that speech in my bathrobe last night" — and Vukradin offers persuasion coaching: "If you want, I can give you some tips on how to be more persuasive. I've got a silvery tongue." Brewbarry's rebuttal: "My axe is my tongue" (truncated).
- The party meets Aurelan Vance, a banker who initially mistakes Brewbarry's authentic barbarian attire for a costume — "I think your costume is wonderful, and I really admire the cosplaying of it" — earning the warning, "Now, slippery slope here, Aurelan. Use your next words very carefully."
- Brewbarry announces himself in full: "This is your lucky day, Aurelan. My halberd and my friends here are here to sort out your supply chain problems."
- Brewbarry presents a letter of introduction from Linene to secure funding and shipping for his new bathrobe business venture.
- Vukradin introduces the party's band name, 'Lathander's Death,' to a confused Aurelan — "It's our band, Lathander's Death" — and begins his running interrogation: "What do you count?"
- Aurelan attempts to balance the needs of both Brewbarry's business loan and Vukradin's pending paperwork, directing Vukradin to a different department.
