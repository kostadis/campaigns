# Confrontation at Margaster Logistics

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party visits the Margaster Logistics Operations at the Eastern Quay to confront House Margaster about their monopoly on shipping and to learn about a missing gnome.
- The party arrives at the Margaster Logistics Operations at the Eastern Quay, noting the facility is impressively clean and well-organized — that being about all Soma's rolls turn up.
- Valphine observes that House Margaster is now handling Lionshield Coster goods, charging high rates for traditional caravan transport as the only game in town — Lionshield had no transport arm of its own, having relied entirely on the displacement device, and is now forced to pay Margaster to move its boxes by armed caravan. The party's read: a legitimate business front — "They are legitimately moving goods; how they're getting all the business is less legitimate."
- Cullen Sharpe — the man in the green suit who extorted Lord Cassian — greets the party warmly and attempts to charm them with talk of business arrangements and solving their problems: "Mr. Vukradin, I'm so delighted to see you." He also greets Perrin, hoping "the letter of introduction we provided was sufficient," and reveals in passing that Ser Kaelen reached out to House Margaster through one of their agents about connecting the party's finds with "the real proprietaires" via the house's "patented system."
- The GM reminds Valphine of her ability to sense lies; Brewbarry needs no such gift: "Of course he's lying, he's a thug."
- Brewbarry attempts to intimidate Cullen Sharpe regarding the broken shipping device — rolling a 6, pat on the back and firm grip on the shoulder notwithstanding ("You know, Cullen, I've been told that you're quite sharp, no pun intended") — but the smooth-talking agent remains unfazed and amused. Soma diagnoses the failure: "The robe, man, the robe just makes you look so soft." Vukradin backs the play with a 15.
- Cullen Sharpe denies House Margaster's involvement in the device's failure — "We were not involved with the operation of that thing, and perhaps if we had been, it would not have gotten damaged" — but reveals that a gnome was hired to fix it, "or was it to improve its operation," and that it stopped working as a result of those attempts.
- Vukradin successfully persuades Cullen to look for the name of the gnome, with Cullen agreeing to provide the information the following day. *(Dave, out of character: "I have a feeling… know who the gnome is." **Player knowledge, not Vukradin's** — see the verbatim note below.)*
- Brewbarry floats the repaired device as a future business opportunity for House Margaster — a deception (rolled with two ones) that Cullen entirely fails to register as a lie, taking Brewbarry for "a barbarian who doesn't know what he's talking about." Brewbarry: "Exactly. Perfect cover."
- The party invites Cullen Sharpe to their upcoming benefit concert at the Spire of the Morninglord — "We really want lots of people to come and donate to this important cause" — which he agrees to attend as a representative of his house, though he cannot promise the whole house will come.
- With Perrin and the fixer still in tow — the notary stayed behind at his own premises — the necklace standoff resumes here. The party refuses to hand the necklace over for safekeeping in a Margaster warehouse — "It's much safer with us."
- Brewbarry crumples the Margaster paperwork into a ball and tosses it over his shoulder — "Is that making it crystal clear for you, Perry? Whoever you are." — causing the fixer to momentarily reach for his weapon before a look from Cullen Sharpe settles him. Brewbarry twists the knife: "Wise decision. Now, don't make me… You eat that piece of paper, and, you know, chop, chop, move along."
- Valphine examines the paperwork closely (a 24 on perception) and finds it authentic — Perrin was not lying about the documentation. Vukradin draws the darker conclusion: "So he's not a cheap fraud. Alagondar has allied with Margaster … Could all be totally legitimate, and they're still evil."
- Vukradin accepts the binding logic of their own principles: if Perrin's identity is established and he is the rightful owner, the necklace must be returned — "If he then takes the necklace, puts it on, and shoots a fireball at us, well, that sucks, but that's what we have to do anyway."
- A meeting is arranged at the Spire of the Morninglord following the party's performance for Perrin to present his three independent witnesses and settle the necklace dispute — Valphine's condition: "I definitely want to see him set foot within the temple." Perrin professes himself, though not an exclusive worshipper, a big believer in the Morninglord as a god of good; Valphine receives the speech with complete blaséness.
- The party closes the evening with a resolution from Brewbarry: "We need to find out who is not with House Margaster, and we need to align with those people, just in case."
