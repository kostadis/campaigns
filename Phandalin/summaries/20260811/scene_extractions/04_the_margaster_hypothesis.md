# The Margaster Hypothesis

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party discusses the suspicious connection between the failure of the Commission's displacement manifold — the Lionshield Coster's cheap shipping route — and the rise of House Margaster, and Brewbarry resolves to confront them directly.
- The party reviews their history with House Margaster, noting previous encounters involving extortion and forgery — they personally witnessed the house extort the rightful owner of the lighthouse statue, and Vukradin adds that "Ser Kaelen told me to stay away."
- Brewbarry expresses loyalty to Linene and the Lionshield Coster despite the banker's suggestion to switch to House Margaster for better margins — "Sorry for being loyal. … They're only loyal to money. I'm not."
- The party hypothesizes that House Margaster sabotaged the Commission's displacement manifold — the Lionshield Coster's cheap shipping route — to eliminate competition and monopolize shipping. Vukradin spells it out step by step; Brewbarry's response: "No shit, Sherlock. The question is, what are we gonna do about it?"
- Brewbarry rejects the idea of subtle sabotage or working in the shadows, preferring to confront House Margaster directly and let them know they will suffer his wrath — "I'm a barbarian, not a thief. I don't work in the shadows. I let my enemy know that I am there." And bluntly: "They continue to fuck with my business, then they will be fucked."
- The party debates the risks of confronting the powerful house, including potential expulsion from Neverwinter and seizure of their business assets, but Brewbarry is undeterred. Vukradin notes they're only level 7 with the city guard likely in Margaster's pocket and Lord Neverember probably on the house's side; Soma's deadpan: "We're about to be level dead, that's fine" — and, resigned: "He's a barbarian, guys."
- Out of character, the GM confirms House Margaster is a recurring villain of these campaigns; Brewbarry's player sums up: "They are Moriarty, you mean?" — to which Vukradin's player objects, "Moriarty had class, though."
