# Chapter 4 The Bard, the Kings, and the Carver

Date: 02-01 to 03-01 of Taraskh 1495

## Summary

Fresh from the Gnomengarde mimic fight, the party returns to Phandalin and overhears a tavern tale about a legendary Triboar play featuring an actor named Bob — a man who always performed in a demon costume. Vukradin becomes obsessed with finding the script and organizing a revival. The party departs for Umbrage Hill to save Adabra Gwynn, the midwife.

On the road, the party is ambushed by bandits. Soma discovers her new Wild Shape ability and becomes a spider for the first time — an overwhelming sensory experience of eight eyes, taste-smelling, and alien perception. She kills one bandit with a spider bite. Brewbarry beheads another with a critical crit kill, covering himself in blood. The remaining bandits flee in terror. One surrenders: Corbin, a broken man whose village was destroyed by Uthgardt raids. Brewbarry, recognizing a fellow victim of trauma, drops his halberd and apologizes for his people's atrocities. Corbin breaks down and reveals the Carver is the real figure behind the bandit network. Vukradin invites Corbin to join the party.

Soma finds a druidic scroll on one of the bandits and faces a moral dilemma: keep it (valuable and useful) or return it to a rightful owner she has no way to identify.

Before the ambush, Vukradin's political dismantling of Gnomengarde's dual monarchy continues to echo as the party discusses governance reform with the inventors — the seeds of political chaos fully planted.

## Scenes

### Aftermath of the Mimic Fight
- Brewbarry struggles to free his halberd from the mimic's adhesion. Soma's poison has no effect. The mimic misses its pseudopod attack.
- Mid-battle debate: Soma declares constructs and extraplanars are "abominations." Vukradin takes personal offense as an aasimar.
- Brewbarry adapts: pulls out handaxes when his main weapon is stuck. Soma finishes the mimic.
- **(DM tactical note):** The party again failed to identify enemy resistances before committing resources. Soma's Poison Spray against the poison-immune mimic was a repeat of inefficient resource use from chapter 2's orc fight. The party needs better scouting or identification protocols.
- Post-mimic: Vukradin questions Soma's desire to take mimic treasure. She pointedly ignores him.

### Gnomengarde Political Chaos
- Vukradin confronts the dual monarchy: "So how do you make decisions?" King Gnercli: "We work together."
- Vukradin: "And when you disagree? There is no decision?"
- Valphine whispers to the party about "clear approver structures" — drow wisdom from watching similar communes self-destruct.
- Vukradin suggests three kings to make things "more difficult" — Gnercli is furious.
- King Korboz seizes on the "senior king" argument.
- The inventors overhear and propose rival governance models: Fibblestib wants a bicameral legislature with unitary executive; Dabbledob wants more kings for voting.
- Vukradin suggests one of the inventors should be king. Chaos follows.
- Vukradin collects the Hat of Wizardry and 50 gp from Harbin.

### Return to Phandalin — The Tale of Bob
- Toblen tells Vukradin about a legendary play from Triboar featuring an actor named Bob who wore a demon costume.
- Vukradin becomes fixated: "Perhaps we can do a revival!"
- Quest board still shows three options. The party discusses which to pursue next.

### Road to Umbrage Hill
- The party debates payment ethics. Vukradin wants to ask for more than 25 gp for the Umbrage Hill job.
- Soma argues: "Sometimes the reward is knowing you did the right thing."
- Vukradin is unmoved. Brewbarry is indifferent.
- Valphine senses something on the road but dismisses her drow instincts — "I am not in the Dark Realms."

### Bandit Ambush — Combat
- **Opening conditions:** Party traveling a narrow mountain trail through rocky hills. Ambushers are poorly hidden — Soma hears them before they spring. Bandits are between the party and a steep slope. Five bandits visible.
- **Round-by-round highlights:** Soma uses Spider Wild Shape for the first time — overwhelming sensory overload. Climbs up and strikes a bandit with a spider bite. Brewbarry climbs a hill and beheads a bandit with a critical halberd hit — the head flies off, body falls on him, covering him in blood. Bandit shoots crossbow at Valphine, hits. Spider-Soma kills another bandit with poison bite. Remaining two bandits flee in terror.
- **(DM tactical note):** Soma's first Wild Shape into a spider was handled well mechanically, but the overwhelming sensory description (eight eyes, taste-smelling, alien perception) was excellent flavor. The GM might want to decide in advance what Soma can communicate in spider form — the chapter notes she could only communicate with other spiders, creating an interesting limitation.
- **Key tactical moments:** Soma's climb-and-bite strategy was effective positioning. Brewbarry's critical hit created the blood-covered, terrifying image that made the bandits rout. Valphine's spider-phobia callout ("Spiders!") from a fleeing bandit was a nice character beat that didn't land with the spider being Soma.
- **Enemy behavior:** Bandits break and flee when outmatched — consistent with low-morale bandits. They do not fight to the death. The fleeing bandits represent a moral test: Vukradin and Valphine kill them anyway.
- **Spell/ability impact:** Spider Wild Shape — highly effective, created mobility and surprise. No other spells used this fight.
- **PC resource decisions:** Soma used her one Wild Shape on a spider — a tactical choice that limited her spellcasting but gave her mobility. No other resources were needed for this easy encounter.
- **Post-combat:** Vukradin interrogates Corbin. Brewbarry's unexpected empathy disarms the situation. Corbin reveals "the Carver" as the bandit leader and joins the party.

### Corbin's Recruitment
- Brewbarry's profound empathy: drops his halberd, apologizes for Uthgardt atrocities.
- Corbin breaks down: "It's the Carver's fault. He's the big boss. He's big. I've never met him. I just worked with Jax."
- Vukradin invites Corbin to join them for dinner. Corbin accepts like a lost puppy.

### Soma's Druidic Scroll
- Soma finds a scroll with a druidic spell on one of the dead bandits.
- Vukradin's ethics lecture begins: "This really should be returned to its rightful owner."
- Soma is conflicted: Am I any better than a bandit if I keep this?
- The moral dilemma hangs unresolved.

## NPCs
- **Corbin** — Gaunt, late-20s human. Haunted, hollowed-out look. Matted hair, patchy beard, pale blue eyes. Wears ill-fitting leather armor scavenged from a dead man. Former farmer from Greenmeadow whose family was killed by Uthgardt raids. Turned to banditry to survive. Deeply traumatized and desperate for belonging. Hired through a crew led by "Jax." Has never met the Carver personally. **(DM note):** Corbin is a potential source of intel on the bandit network. His trauma response to Brewbarry's Uthgardt tattoos is realistic — the party should be careful how they handle him.
- **The Carver** (mentioned) — The bandit leader operating in the Phandalin area. Corbin has never met him, only worked through an intermediary named Jax. Described as "big." **(DM note):** The Carver's identity and location are unresolved hooks.
- **Jax** (mentioned) — Intermediate figure in the bandit network. Corbin's direct contact. Possibly knows more than Corbin about the Carver.

## Items
- **Druidic Scroll** — Found on a dead bandit. Contains a druidic spell. Soma is conflicted about keeping it (ethics vs. utility). Vukradin argues it should be returned to its rightful owner. **(DM note):** The scroll's contents and ownership history are hooks for future encounters.
- **Hat of Wizardry** (carried from chapter 3) — Collectible for Harbin's 50 gp bounty. Vukradin unimpressed with its dragon-fighting utility.
- **Sending Stones** (carried from chapter 2) — Vukradin discovers they only send one short message, not the musical application he imagined. Disappointed.

## Spells
- **Wild Shape (Spider)** — Soma's first use of this ability. Overwhelming sensory experience. Effective in combat (climb, bite, poison). Creates communication limitations (can only speak with other spiders).
- **Poison Spray** — Soma. Wasted again on an immune target (mimic). Pattern: Soma needs to vary her spell selection.
- **Vicious Mockery** — Vukradin. Reliable psychic damage against the mimic.
- **Healing spells** — Not used this chapter in combat.
