## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party travels south along the High Road, turns east onto the Triboar Trail, and arrives at the frontier mining town of Phandalin.
- The party arrives in Phandalin, a town of 40 to 50 simple log buildings nestled in the foothills of the Sword Mountains, approaching from the west along the Triboar Trail after turning east off the High Road.
- Crumbling stone ruins surround the newer buildings, hinting at a much larger settlement in centuries past.
- The party notes the town has no walls and no garrison, with most adults keeping weapons within easy reach. Soma finds it "actually kind of weird. There's like no natural defense. There's no [river], nothing like that." (The ASR reads “water riffer.”)
- The DM's reading adds: the residents are "quiet, hard-working folk who came from distant cities to eke out a life amid the harsh wilderness... farmers, stonecutters, blacksmiths, traders, prospectors, and children," and "visitors are welcome here, especially if they have coin to spend or news to share."
- The group spots the Stonehill Inn and the Townmaster's Hall as they enter the village.
- A Lionshield Coster is visible along the road — "the famous interdimensional store where one used to be able to buy everything" — though rumored to have supply chain disruptions. (Table banter blames "Damn COVID" and tariffs.)
