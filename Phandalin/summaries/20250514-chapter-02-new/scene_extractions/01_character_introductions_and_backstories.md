# Character Introductions and Backstories

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The players introduce their new characters and discuss their backstories while the DM prepares the virtual tabletop.
- Vukradin is introduced as a serious musician from Neverwinter who is struggling to make ends meet at a piano bar, playing music that patrons don't seem to appreciate.
- Vukradin's player elaborates: Vukradin "plays in a piano bar seven days a week and somehow never manages to make any money," dreams of opening a music studio to play serious music with serious people, and is trying adventuring only "because he's heard that there's a lot of money in it." He doesn't want to be an adventurer — "he wants to be a musician... But sometimes you got to do the things you have to do."
- A Tortle Druid named Soma is introduced as a guide in her sunset years, leading travelers through the wilderness. Soma's player clarifies she is "a lady turtle," and that "In her sunset year, she's just leading tours around the forest, guiding adventurers to other places."
- Brewbarry is introduced as a Goliath barbarian from the Uthgardt tribes who was kicked out for being a drunk — Brewbarry's player pitches him as "a little bit of a... dopey giant sort of character" who "just wanted some friends" and answered the flyer because it "offered, you know, like someone to hang out with."
- Valphine Sotorra is introduced as a Drow cleric of Lathander, drawn to the light after being driven out of the Underdark. Valphine's player notes bluntly: "She's masochistic. She likes pain." Her backstory is negotiated at the table — the main Drow city "was completely pulverized by demons that ran wild," her first exposure to the sun was a religious experience, and the clerics of Lathander in Neverwinter were ecstatic to have a Drow convert.
- Valphine's player struggles to find her hook — "I don't think she sees herself as a hero" — until the DM lands on it: Neverwinter is too civilized ("There's no assassinations... no torture racks... no open-air slave markets... It's all very boring"), so "when she sees the flyer, she decides that this would be more painful." Valphine's player: "Yeah, okay. There we go. That's a motivation."
- The group establishes their initial connections: Brewbarry became Vukradin's biggest fan at the piano bar, Valphine traveled out of Neverwinter with them, and Soma met the trio outside the city and offered to guide them toward Phandalin.
- The DM proposes that for Brewbarry, who grew up with Uthgardt music "which is just drums," Vukradin's pitches and tones are "the music of the ages" — he is "not just your biggest fan, but he also gets really offended when other people don't share in the enthusiastic appraisal of your music." Vukradin's player: "Me too."
- Vukradin explains his desperate need for money to open a music studio, leading him to take up adventuring after seeing a flyer posted in Neverwinter — and recalls that Brewbarry was "the only patron who ever gave me a 10 out of 10 on the survey" the piano bar used to run.
- Vukradin, lacking a piano on the road, resigns himself to flute and clarinet in camp; the group discusses drum lessons, but percussion is dismissed as "really boring" while Soma insists "It makes the song."
- Table meta: the group reminisces about the prior campaign — Wallace is not only around but has kids now, Orsik is helping take care of them, and Valphine's player once famously played a cleric and a paladin "at the same time... they kept possessing each other's bodies."
