# Chapter 37 — A Gem of a Problem, a Rat of a Solution

**Date:** Feb 20th, 2026

---

## Summary

The party returned from Icespire Hold carrying the cold remains of Cryovain, the white dragon that had terrorized the region for months. Soma, still feeling the strange quiet deepening in her after the rooftop battle, led the harvesting — extracting the dragon's breath pouch and carefully working its scales free from the hide. Brewbarry, struggling with the weight of killing something that had a name, helped haul and brace the enormous carcass, finding a strange comfort in the physical work. The breath pouch pulsed with a cold older than temperature; the scales caught the light like polished stone. They carried the dragon down the mountain, raw material for something they had not yet decided how to build.

At the Mountain's Toe Gold Mine, the silence was wrong. No picks, no carts, no sound of work — just were-rats arranged outside in postures that suggested they had been doing nothing for some considerable time. Dwolin the Grout, the dwarven foreman, explained with theologicalgravity that the gold vein was "shy" and would not produce until treated with the proper respect — specifically, until an Earthstone Gem was acquired from the orc-infested forest. Horia Stonetallow, caught between a devoutly superstitious expert and an increasingly sceptical management, watched the confrontation unfold.

Valphine would have none of it. She summoned the full, searing light of Lathander into the dark mine, illuminating every idle pick and empty cart, and delivered a dressing-down that would echo through were-rat legend. "Pull your fingers out of your rat's ass," she told Zeleen, and sent her gang into the forest to find the gem — threatened with being remembered as "a waste of cheese." Brewbarry, watching with his halberd tapping against his palm, found the visual communication effective.

Once Vukradin had stepped away, Horia proposed a quieter solution: fifty gold from the mine's own accounts to buy a fake Earthstone Gem, should the were-rats fail. Brewbarry negotiated the addendum by threatening to sign it in blood, eventually settling on a thumbprint arrangement kept discreetly from Vukradin — who, it was agreed, would not be open to the nuance of using gold-mine gold to buy a fake gem to make the gold mine produce gold. Brewbarry found the logic unimpeachable.

Phandalin greeted them as heroes. Children ran out to meet them, pressing small hands into Brewbarry's large ones. Harbin Wester claimed his share of the credit with practiced eloquence, praising Vukradin's "undefatigable support." The party let him have it. A festival materialized around them — warm bread, a fiddle, a ribbon from an insistent child. The innkeeper brought out his best barrel, the one he had been saving, and gave Brewbarry his best cup. Brewbarry held the cup for an extra second before drinking, and thought: this town gave me its best barrel. That is an exchange that makes sense.

The pleasant thing ended when Ser Kaelen arrived with the posture of a man who needed to ruin something. Adabra had discovered the truth about the Whispering Grove and had convened a druid council to argue that the party's "reckless interventionism" had brought harm to the forests. She was bringing her war out here. Worse, the druids might seek to evaluate whether Soma should retain Meril's staff — the last physical connection to her teacher, the thing she carries with leaves growing on it, the staff that has chosen her back. Kaelen, with his hand on his sword, offered the only argument that mattered: some things are yours not because you earned them, but because they chose you and you chose them back. "They're not taking it from me," Soma said. She is old, she has seen much, and she is not giving anyone her staff.

## Scenes

**1. Harvesting the Dragon** — On the roof of Icespire Hold, with Cryovain's body still warm, Soma extracts the breath pouch and scales while Brewbarry helps brace and haul. The pouch carries a cold that is more than temperature; the scales shimmer pale and beautiful. Talk turns to what to make from them — dragon scale mail, perhaps something from the breath pouch.

**2. Inspection of the Mountain's Toe Gold Mine** — The party arrives to silence. No mining sounds. Were-rats stand idle. Dwolin the Grout explains the shy gold vein and the need for an Earthstone Gem from the orc-claimed forest.

**3. Valphine's Confrontation with Zeleen** — Valphine calls Daylight into the mine, illuminates the empty carts, and delivers a verbal evisceration. Zeleen's will crumbles. The were-rats are dispatched to the forest to retrieve the gem.

**4. Brewbarry's Fake Gem Negotiation** — With Vukradin absent, Horia proposes buying a fake gem from the mine's own accounts. Brewbarry negotiates the addendum, blood jokes are exchanged, and the deal is quietly struck — to remain between them.

**5. The Hero's Welcome in Phandalin** — Children, cheers, warm bread, a fiddle, and Harbin Wester taking credit with the style of a natural politician. The party lets him.

**6. The Innkeeper's Gift** — The innkeeper presents Brewbarry with his best barrel — the very good stuff — with the warning that he only has two. Brewbarry holds the cup an extra second before drinking.

**7. Ser Kaelen's Urgent News** — Kaelen arrives through the festival. Adabra has convened a druid council over the Whispering Grove. The Lords Alliance won't intervene; the Order of the Gauntlet is stretched too thin. It's all on the party.

**8. The Staff** — Kaelen warns that the druids may evaluate whether Soma should keep Meril's staff. Soma goes still. "They're not taking it from me." Kaelen agrees: some things are yours because they chose you.

## NPCs

- **Zeleen Varnaster** — Leader of the Whiskered Gang (were-rats), tasked with protecting the mine. Barely competent, easily cowed by Valphine's authority. Dispatched on a fetch quest for the Earthstone Gem.
- **Dwolin the Grout** — Dwarven gold-seeker, the best Horia has ever known. Believes gold veins are shy and require ritual respect. Refuses to mine without the Earthstone Gem.
- **Horia Stonetallow** — Dwarven administrator of the mine. Pragmatic, document-minded, willing to work around problems quietly. Proposed and negotiated the fake gem addendum.
- **Harbin Wester** — Townmaster of Phandalin. Arrived at the hero's welcome ready to claim credit for the dragon's defeat, praising Vukradin's management. The party tolerated it.
- **Innkeeper of Phandalin** — Gave Brewbarry his best barrel. "I only have two barrels. So you're just gonna have to, like, remember that."
- **Ser Kaelen** — Knight of the Order of the Gauntlet, ally to the party. Brought urgent news of Adabra's druid council. Showed genuine respect for Soma's claim to the staff.
- **Adabra** — Druid who has called a conclave. Believes the party's actions at the Whispering Grove brought harm to the forests. Does not see the planar incursions she never experienced.

## Items

- **Cryovain's Breath Pouch** — Extracted from the white dragon's throat by Soma. Pulses with ancient cold, heavier than it looks, contains a sound waiting to be released. Potential for a crafted item.
- **Dragon Scales (Cryovain)** — Pale, supple, shimmering scales harvested from the dragon's hide. Candidate material for dragon scale mail armor.
- **Meril's Staff** — Soma's staff, left to her by her teacher Meril. Holds symbolic standing among druids. Has grown more leaves since Meril departed. At risk of being evaluated for removal by the druid council. Soma will not give it up.
- **Earthstone Gem** — A mystical gem Dwolin insists is necessary to coax the shy gold vein at Mountain's Toe Mine. Location: orc-infested forest. The were-rats have been sent to retrieve it; Horia has arranged a fake gem backup plan.

## Spells & Abilities

- **Valphine — Daylight** — Used to illuminate the interior of the mine with the full searing brilliance of midday sun, causing visible discomfort to the were-rat guards.
- **Soma — Abjuration Musings** — Soma reflected on abjuration magic during the journey, noting that these spells are not naturally available to her as a druid — a recognition of limitations rather than a new ability.
- **Valphine — Blessing & Bonds** — Valphine is exploring a new divine ability to thread her presence between allies so courage can travel along the connection. She discussed whether she could have blessed the party before combat (but chose not to, citing purification through pain).
