## ISSUE 1 — Mermaid Statue Owner's Location: Internal Contradiction

**Location:** Scene Summary ("The Forgotten Mermaid Statue") vs. Verbatim Scenes

**Issue:** The Scene Summary correctly states the statue owner is in Neverwinter. However, in the verbatim transcript the GM initially says *"the owner is in Phandalin"* before correcting to Neverwinter. The Scene Summary resolves this in prose, but labels the entire scene "owner is in Neverwinter" without flagging the mid-scene correction. A future reader of the verbatim section alone could be confused about which city was stated.

**Evidence:** Campaign State (Party Current Situation): "Return mermaid statue to Lord Cassian Meliamne" — Neverwinter. NPC table: Lord Cassian Meliamne — Neverwinter. The verbatim GM line reads: *"You do have that statue, which, the owner is in Phandalin, and you still haven't brought it there"* — then later corrected at table.

**Suggested Fix:** Add a parenthetical note in the verbatim section flagging the in-session slip: *(GM self-corrected: owner is in Neverwinter, not Phandalin — see NPC table, Lord Cassian Meliamne).* The Scene Summary prose is already correct; only the verbatim section needs annotation.

## ISSUE 2 — Mermaid Statue Owner: Name Not Established

**Location:** Scene Summary ("The Forgotten Mermaid Statue") and Verbatim Scenes

**Issue:** The recap describes the owner as *"Sir Arielle, Elyara / Eliara / Ellara"* — an unresolved name the GM was fumbling for at the table. The campaign context gives the definitive answer: the contact for the statue is **Lord Cassian Meliamne** (Elara Seasong Meliamne's cousin, who is already dead). The fumbled name at the table does not match either "Cassian" or "Elara." The GM then confirms *"She's a famous… she's an important person of Neverwinter"* — but campaign state specifies the contact is Cassian (male), not a woman, and Elara (female) is dead.

**Evidence:** Campaign State NPC table: *"Lord Cassian Meliamne | Alive | Neverwinter | Elara's cousin; carries her anti-Marcaster case; active contact for mermaid statue and necklace provenance."* Entity registry: *"Elara Seasong Meliamne — Killed by House Margaster approximately 8 days before the party arrives in Neverwinter."* The recap's reference to a living female owner named something like "Eliara" conflates the dead Elara with the living Cassian.

**Suggested Fix:** Replace the fumbled in-session name with: *"Ser Kaelen indicates the statue belongs to a contact in Neverwinter — Lord Cassian Meliamne, cousin of the late Elara Seasong Meliamne."* Note that Elara is dead and Cassian is the living point of contact. The GM's in-session pronoun "she" was an error; Cassian is male.

## ISSUE 3 — "Orcan Game" / "Orc Campaign" Terminology

**Location:** Scene Summary and Verbatim Scenes ("The Forgotten Mermaid Statue")

**Issue:** Vukradin's apology references *"stop an orcan game"* (Scene Summary) / *"stop an orc campaign"* (verbatim). The campaign record contains no encounter described as an "orc campaign" as a discrete named event. The closest candidates are the Circle of Thunder Talosian orc patrols, the Woodland Manse orc occupation, or the Falcon's Lodge anchorite/orc siege — none of which are labelled this way in any context document.

**Evidence:** Campaign State Completed Encounters: the relevant orc-heavy events are "Circle of Thunder — Yargath's Patrol Encounter," "Falcon's Hunting Lodge — Siege and Relief," and "Woodland Manse — Cleared." None are titled "orc campaign."

**Suggested Fix:** Clarify in a GM note which completed event Vukradin is referencing (most likely the Falcon's Lodge siege or Woodland Manse clearance), or leave as flavour dialogue with a note that *"orc campaign"* is Vukradin's informal summary, not a discrete quest name.

## ISSUE 4 — Ser Kaelen Called the Statue's Owner "Rightful" vs. Vukradin Asking About Treasure Owners

**Location:** Scene Summary ("Ser Kaelen and the Treasure Owners" and "The Forgotten Mermaid Statue")

**Issue:** The recap conflates two distinct items: (a) rightful owners of the *recovered treasure* (Necklace of Fireballs, other loot) and (b) the *mermaid statue* specifically. The Scene Summary treats them as one report from Ser Kaelen. The campaign context keeps them separate: the Necklace of Fireballs has an unresolved owner; the mermaid statue's contact is Lord Cassian Meliamne. Presenting them as a single "more owners found in Neverwinter" report may obscure that the statue has a specific, urgent deadline (House Margaster is intercepting it).

**Evidence:** Campaign State Active Quests: *"Return mermaid statue to Lord Cassian Meliamne… Urgency: House Margaster agents have been intercepting the statue."* Objects of Note: Necklace of Fireballs listed separately as *"Rightful owner not yet found."*

**Suggested Fix:** Split the two items in the Scene Summary. Note that Ser Kaelen reports new treasure owners (unspecified) are in Neverwinter, and separately raises the long-outstanding mermaid statue with its specific urgency. Do not merge them into a single "additional owners" update.

## ISSUE 5 — Brother Aldric: Inconsistent Name Spelling

**Location:** Scene Summary ("The Golden Eyes") and DM Note ("Valphine's real feelings about Brother Aldrich")

**Issue:** The Scene Summary spells the priest's name **"Brother Aldric Sunmantle"** (correct per campaign context). The DM Note scene tag header and some verbatim references spell it **"Brother Aldrich"** (with an *h*). These are inconsistent within the same scene.

**Evidence:** Entity registry (vtt_transcription_corrections.md): *"Aldrich, Adric, Adrik, Aldrick, Audric → **Aldric**."* The canonical spelling is **Aldric**, not Aldrich.

**Suggested Fix:** Standardize all instances to **Aldric Sunmantle**. Remove the *h* from every occurrence of "Aldrich" in the recap.

## ISSUE 6 — Ser Kaelen Described as "High Priest" of the Spire

**Location:** Scene Summary ("The Golden Eyes") and Verbatim Scenes

**Issue:** The recap's verbatim GM narration states *"she mentions the high priest at the temple"* and *"the Spire of the Morning Lord"* in a way that is ambiguous about who the high priest is. The Scene Summary then clarifies it is **Brother Aldric Sunmantle** — but describes him as *"the priest who first received her conversion."* The campaign context does not assign Aldric the title "high priest"; he is simply the priest at the Spire of the Morninglord. Calling him "high priest" may inadvertently elevate his institutional rank.

**Evidence:** Neverwinter locations cheatsheet: *"Brother Aldric Sunmantle — Orthodox Lathander priest, 30 yrs."* No "high priest" title assigned in any context document.

**Suggested Fix:** Replace "high priest" with "presiding priest" or simply "the priest" to match his documented role. If the GM intends him to be the high priest, that should be noted explicitly in the world_state or NPC table.

## ISSUE 8 — Toblen's Son Named "Tuck": Unverified Name

**Location:** Scene Summary ("Arrival at Stonehill Inn") and Verbatim Moments

**Issue:** The recap names Toblen's son as **"Tuck."** No campaign context document names Toblen's son. The entity registry lists Toblen Stonehill as proprietor but does not mention a son named Tuck. This may be a GM invention, but it is unrecorded and could conflict with future canon.

**Evidence:** Entity registry: *"Toblen Stonehill — Proprietor of the Stonehill Inn; native of Triboar."* No son named in any document. The published *Dragon of Icespire Peak* module gives Toblen a wife (Trilena) and son (Pip), not "Tuck."

**Suggested Fix:** Either confirm "Tuck" as a deliberate campaign rename of the module's "Pip" and add it to the entity registry, or correct to "Pip" if the module name is being used. Do not leave an unnamed child with a floating name that may create a continuity collision.

## ISSUE 9 — "Jenna Sitting at a Table" in the Inn

**Location:** Verbatim Moments ("Arrival at the Stonehill Inn")

**Issue:** The verbatim section mentions *"Jenna sitting at a table"* as part of the Stonehill Inn arrival scene. The NPC table lists **Jenna Roscoe** as having departed Phandalin ahead of the party for Neverwinter (per `20260623_jenna_roscoe_head_start.md`). If this scene is set in Phandalin before the party travels to Neverwinter, Jenna should not be present at the Stonehill Inn — she has already left.

**Evidence:** `20260623_jenna_roscoe_head_start.md`: *"After Vukradin's Universal Basic Treasure proclamation… Jenna Roscoe departed Phandalin in visible disgust and headed back to Neverwinter ahead of the party."* NPC table: *"Jenna Roscoe | Alive | Lord's Alliance / Neverwinter."*

**Suggested Fix:** Clarify whether this scene takes place before or after Jenna's departure. If after, remove Jenna from the inn description. If before, note this creates a timeline conflict with her established early departure and the GM should adjudicate which is canon.

## ISSUE 10 — Ser Kaelen's Concern About "A Drow Lathanderite Being So Blessed"

**Location:** Scene Summary ("The Golden Eyes")

**Issue:** The recap frames Ser Kaelen's concern as discomfort that *"a Drow Lathanderite could be so blessed."* This is plausible in-world, but the campaign's established two-stage NPC reaction to Valphine (canonical per `valphine_drow_visibility.md`) is: first read as evil drow, second register Lathander iconography and become confused. The recap skips the first stage entirely, presenting Ser Kaelen as already past the drow-hostility beat and concerned only about the theological implications of divine blessing. This is not necessarily wrong for Kaelen specifically (he knows Valphine), but the recap should make clear this is a Kaelen-specific reaction, not a general template.

**Evidence:** `valphine_drow_visibility.md`: *"Any NPC seeing Valphine reads her as 'evil drow' first… then register her holy symbol and vestments of Lathander and become confused."* Ser Kaelen has prior relationship with the party, so his reaction may reasonably differ.

**Suggested Fix:** Add a brief qualifier: *"Ser Kaelen, who knows Valphine and is past the initial drow-hostility read, is nonetheless concerned that a Lathander follower of her heritage could manifest a divine physical blessing."* This preserves the canon NPC-reaction rule while explaining why Kaelen's reaction differs.

## ISSUE 11 — Dragon Described as "Defeated" in Casual GM Narration

**Location:** Verbatim Moments (GM contextualizing heroism)

**Issue:** The GM says *"you had that moment, you were the heroes, you saved the world, defeated the dragon"* and *"saved everybody from Adabra's madness, you know, got the council, that was last week."* While "defeated the dragon" is accurate for Cryovain (dead), *"saved everybody from Adabra's madness"* is potentially misleading. The Emerald Enclave schism is resolved at council level, but Adabra is not dead, not neutralized, not cured of any madness — she left *"choosing a different battlefield"* and is flagged as an active hostile threat.

**Evidence:** Campaign State Active Threads: *"Adabra Gwynn — Different Battlefield… Will act."* Resolved Plot Threads: *"Emerald Enclave internal schism: Druid Council ruled in favor of interventionism; Adabra's faction politically defeated. Adabra is not convinced."*

**Suggested Fix:** This is in-character bard-hyperbole from the GM voicing the celebration, not a factual recap claim. No correction needed to the recap prose itself, but add a GM note: *(Note: "Adabra's madness" here refers to the council vote outcome; Adabra remains an active hostile NPC and is not neutralized.)* This prevents a future reader from treating this line as a closure of the Adabra thread.
