# Chapter 10 Phandalin: Old Debts and New Suspicions

Date: 09-01 Taraskh 1495 — 10-01 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party returns to Phandalin after the Tower of Storms, but the homecoming is anything but peaceful. Soma immediately presses for a detour to the Whispering Wood — a mission given to her by Adabra, the midwife-naturalist. Vukradin, obsessed with his music studio fund, demands to know "how much money" the wood will pay. Soma rebukes him as a "pebble-counter." The exchange reveals a widening rift: Adabra helped Vukradin secure funding before he joined the Order of the Gauntlet, but now that he's aligned himself with an interventionist faction, she has stopped speaking to him entirely.

Adabra approaches Soma in the town square, disappointed they haven't visited the Whispering Wood yet. When Vukradin tries to engage her, she delivers a philosophical broadside: the Order of the Gauntlet's "interventionist" philosophy — striving to make the world better — actually makes things worse by forcing evil to respond. She frames her naturalist worldview as a unified multiverse where good and evil are inseparable forces. Vukradin is shaken; he thought Adabra respected his art, but she accuses him of choosing sides over beauty.

At the Lionshield Coster, Linene Graywind explains that the War of the Giants disrupted the merchant network — she can no longer offer the same services. At Townmaster Harbin Wester's house, Valphine tries to leverage the lighthouse cleansing for recognition, but Harbin is dismissive and warns against traveling to Neverwinter prematurely.

At Stonehill Tavern, Ser Kaelen congratulates the party but grows concerned when they describe Moesko's green-heart beacon: a typical Talos shaman couldn't do such things. He warns of a "hidden force." Vukradin confronts Ser Kaelen about the ambush; the knight points out that *everyone* knew the party was going to the lighthouse — there was a betting pool at the tavern. The party's lack of operational security is laid bare.

Ser Kaelen warns of a bandit leader called the Carver — elusive, magically hidden from divination, running a network from an unknown location. He asks the party to investigate. Soma presses him about the Whispering Wood; Kaelen dismisses it as "tree-hugger business" but concedes the townsfolk would be grateful if the party looked into it. He can't pay — "We do not hire; we find true believers" — frustrating Vukradin.

The party performs at Stonehill that evening. Vukradin plays solo initially (gaining 2 silver), then Valphine and Brewbarry join in for a richer ensemble sound (10 more silver). In the morning, they find a mysterious letter slipped under the door: an anonymous warning about Ser Kaelen and the Order of the Gauntlet, claiming their funding may come from House Margaster — a Waterdhavian noble house with rumored demonic ties and "unsavory business." The letter was delivered from Neverwinter but left with the innkeeper the night before. Vukradin is unnerved — he wanted a fan letter, not conspiracy.

Soma's staff, given by Merill, awakens as the party prepares to head for the Whispering Wood.

## Memorable Moments

> "Pebble-counter! Not everything is about money, it's about keeping the forests in check!"
> — Soma, exasperated at Vukradin's mercenary priorities

**Pebble-Counter.** Soma's dismissive nickname for Vukradin crystallizes their ideological divide: nature vs. commerce, purpose vs. profit. It becomes her shorthand for his narrow worldview.

> "You dry-shell heart. Adabra saw your need and reached out to Harbin to get your money."
> — Soma, revealing Adabra's past kindness to Vukradin

**The Debt Revealed.** Vukradin learns that Adabra — the woman he now distrusts — was the one who secured his initial funding. The revelation complicates his black-and-white moral framework.

> "No, I believe we can each do good and evil. But to change the nature of the world, to strive to change its very nature, forces the powers of evil and good to respond. And those responses hurt everyone."
> — Adabra, laying out naturalist philosophy

**The Philosophy of Non-Intervention.** Adabra's argument cuts to the campaign's thematic core: does trying to do good create backlash that makes things worse? Her words echo Miral's warning about shadows moving in the deep.



> "Raise your hand if you knew these four were going to the Lighthouse? Everyone raises their hand. And Spiderman shouts, 'We had a betting pool!'"
> — Ser Kaelen, demonstrating the party's poor OPSEC

**The Betting Pool.** The party's attempt to identify a leak backfires spectacularly: they were never secret. The tavern had a betting pool on which of them would die. Vukradin demands his cut of the winnings.

## Scenes

### Return to Phandalin — Philosophical Divisions
- Soma wants to visit the Whispering Wood per Adabra's request; Vukradin demands payment details.
- Vukradin refuses to go without a clear monetary reward; Soma calls him a "pebble-counter."
- Adabra approaches Soma in the town square — disappointed the party hasn't investigated the Wood.
- Adabra ignores Vukradin (because of his Gauntlet pin), delivers naturalist philosophy: the Order of the Gauntlet's "good" deeds create backlash that worsens the world.
- Vukradin is shaken — he thought Adabra respected his art; she accuses him of choosing sides over beauty.

**(DM note):** This conversation establishes the naturalist vs. interventionist debate that will define chapters 11-13. Adabra's arguments are not obviously wrong — she challenges the party's assumptions about what "good" means in a multiverse context.

### Lionshield Coster — Disrupted Trade
- Linene Graywind explains the Lionshield Coster's network is disrupted by the War of the Giants.
- She can no longer offer scrolls or rare goods quickly.
- Vukradin's attempt to repatriate shipwreck treasure hits bureaucratic wall: "it could take a while."
- **(DM note, not yet known to party):** Linene's mention of planar magic in the shipping network is a hint that connects to the planar rift in the Whispering Wood. The Lionshield Coster may have ties to forces beyond simple commerce.

### Harbin Wester — Dismissive Politics
- Valphine presents the lighthouse cleansing as a major victory.
- Harbin is uninterested: "I think Lord Neverember will be delighted."
- Offers to send a note — Vukradin offers to deliver it personally.
- Harbin warns against leaving for Neverwinter while the town is still unsafe.
- Valphine's political instincts recognize the implicit warning: unfinished business = no reward.

### Stonehill Tavern — Ser Kaelen and the Carver
- Ser Kaelen congratulates the party but grows concerned at descriptions of Moesko's heart-beacon.
- "A typical shaman of Talos could not do such things" — hidden force behind the scenes.
- Vukradin confronts him about the ambush; Kaelen reveals the "betting pool" — everyone knew.
- Kaelen briefs them on the Carver: elusive bandit leader, hidden from divination magic.
- Asks the party to investigate; offers no payment ("we find true believers").
- Dismisses the Whispering Wood as "tree-hugger business" — no one has been hurt.
- Vukradin reports the shipwreck treasure; Kaelen offers to investigate rightful ownership.

**(DM note):** Ser Kaelen's denial of payment ("we do not hire") contrasts with his claim that the Order of the Gauntlet supports "true believers." Vukradin's mercenary approach doesn't fit this model. The mysterious letter will further erode Kaelen's credibility.

### Evening Performance
- Vukradin plays solo: earns 2 silver.
- Valphine joins (Lathander-enhanced tones); Brewbarry adds percussive halberd rhythm.
- Ensemble earns 10 more silver — crowd far more engaged.
- Vukradin begins to realize that playing popular music has more value than artistic purity.

### The Mysterious Letter
- Morning finds a letter under the door — slid under the previous night by the innkeeper.
- Sent from Neverwinter, addressed to Vukradin.
- Anonymous writer claims to be a "concerned admirer of your noble spirit."
- Accuses Ser Kaelen and the Order of the Gauntlet of accepting tainted funding from House Margaster.
- House Margaster: Waterdhavian noble house with rumored demonic ties and "unsavory business."
- Claims reclaimed artifacts were "forcibly appropriated from minor, neutral parties."
- Writer signs as "A Friend to True Justice."
- Vukradin is disturbed — he wanted a fan letter, not an assassination of his faction's credibility.

**(DM note):** The letter's source is deliberately ambiguous. Possible senders: a rival faction (Zhentarim, Kraken Society), a disillusioned former Gauntlet member, or an agent of House Margaster trying to discredit an investigation. The letter contains half-truths designed to sow distrust.

### Soma's Staff Awakens
- As the party prepares to head for the Whispering Wood, Soma's staff (gift from Merill) suddenly feels alive.
- Magic is unlocked within it. **(DM note):** The staff's awakening is linked to planar disturbance — it may detect unnatural magic or serve as a key to understanding the rift.

## NPCs

- **Adabra the Midwife (Naturalist Druid)** — Member of the Emerald Enclave, naturalist philosophy. Previously helped Vukradin secure funding. Now cold toward him since he joined the Order of the Gauntlet. Believes intervention (good or evil) creates backlash that worsens the world. Her philosophy is internally consistent and challenges the party's assumptions. **(DM note):** Adabra is not villainous — she is sincere in her beliefs. Her role is to embody a worldview the party may not agree with but should not dismiss outright.

- **Linene Graywind** — Proprietor of the Lionshield Coster, 35. Network disrupted by the War of the Giants. Excited about planar-origin items (dire wolf pelts foreshadowing). **(DM note):** Her reaction to planar items is suspicious — she may have orders from higher-ups who specifically seek such materials.

- **Harbin Wester** — Townmaster of Phandalin. Frazzled, bureaucratic, dismissive. Warns the party against leaving for Neverwinter before the area is secure. His veiled threat carries political weight.

- **Ser Kaelen Thorne** — Knight of the Order of the Gauntlet. Charismatic, well-liked. Genuinely concerned about Moesko's abilities. Searching for the Carver but hampered by divination-blocking. His funding source is now questioned. **(DM note):** The letter's accusations may or may not be true. Kaelen's reaction to the treasure repatriation request will reveal more about his character.

- **The Carver** (mentioned) — Elusive bandit leader operating in the region. Hidden from divination magic. His network is connected to the Kraken Society. Unknown location and identity.

- **House Margaster** (mentioned) — Waterdhavian noble house with rumored demonic ties. Allegedly provides funding to the Order of the Gauntlet. If true, this undermines Vukradin's "conflict-free gold" philosophy.
