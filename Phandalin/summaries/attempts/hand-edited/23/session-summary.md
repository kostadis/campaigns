# Chapter 23 From Retreat to Redemption (and Cheese)

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The party stands at the Woodland Manse, battered and bloodied from the Anchorite's lightning assault. As more cultists emerge from the manor and a second Anchorite in bull form unleashes crackling electricity, the party realizes they are dangerously outnumbered. Vukradin vigorously advocates for retreat, and the party makes the tactical decision to withdraw rather than fight a losing battle. Brewbarry, in giant form, carries Vukradin to safety; Soma carries Valphine on her back. The party successfully disengages, leaving disappointed boars who had hoped they would deal with the "impostor" cultist boars.

Collecting Corbin and Shrimpy — and overhearing Shrimpy's cowardly complaints — the party picks up the Emerald Enclave fighters from Falcon Hunter and travels 30 miles south to Phandalin. Upon arrival, Vukradin calls a council meeting. Shrimpy is delivered to Harbin. The Cult of Talos at the Woodland Manse is reported — shapeshifting orcs wielding lightning magic. The council is divided. Ser Kaelen expresses concern about the Kraken Society, mentioning suspicions of psionic creatures. Jenna Roscoe dismisses the Kraken Society as a harmless elite social club, though Valphine senses she knows more than she reveals.

Adabra explodes with fury, denouncing the party's intervention against the Kraken cult as a temporal violation causing planar distortions across the multiverse. She storms out. The party agrees she is dangerously wrong.

Ser Kaelen delivers welcome news: Candlekeep research has identified the original owner of one of the party's ship-found magical items. The process of returning it will be complex but potentially rewarding.

Valphine delivers a sermon at the partially reconstructed church of Lathander. Congregants misinterpret her words about the glorious pain of Lathander's light — they stare at the sun until blinded. Valphine heals them with divine magic, inadvertently creating a new "sacrament" of self-blinding followed by healing. She places a cultist's severed head on a pike outside the church. Teega the Smith watches, disturbed and conflicted.

Don-Jon Raskin, the new overseer of Mountain's Toe Gold Mine, boasts in the tavern. He tells tall tales about attending a barbarian party — the party quickly realizes he was seen as an idiot. His employers in Neverwinter include at least one Kraken Society associate, as Vukradin notes.

The party escorts Don-Jon to the mine. They discover six dead orcs — killed by extreme cold, evidence of the white dragon's presence. At the mine entrance, two wererats in hybrid form claim ownership. Don-Jon's friendly demeanor defuses initial hostility (the wererats react aggressively to Valphine for being a drow). They are invited deeper to meet Zeleen Varnaster, leader of the Whiskered Gang.

Zeleen explains her group was displaced from the Shrine of Savras by approximately sixteen orcs and several ogres — some from a tribe the party has previously angered. She proposes a deal: clear the orcs from the shrine, and the wererats will vacate the mine. She refuses to help reclaim her own home, offering only two warriors. When Valphine jokes about cheese-for-labor, Zeleen is initially offended but admits interest in Petunia's cheese from Butterskull Ranch.

Vukradin persuades Don-Jon that wererats can nest in one section while working another. A deal is struck: the wererats work the mine in exchange for regular cheese shipments. The party leaves without fighting sixteen orcs and several ogres, having turned a potential mine war into a creative arrangement.

## Memorable Moments

**Tactical Retreat.** Facing a second lightning-wielding Anchorite and more emerging cultists, Vukradin's insistence on withdrawal saves the party from a likely TPK. The party demonstrates tactical discipline.

**Boar Culture.** The party's boar allies are deeply disappointed the party didn't resolve their cultural conflict with the "impostor" cultist boars. The quest for proper boar etiquette remains unfulfilled.

**Revisionist History.** Vukradin retroactively claims the retreat was his idea all along, as though the party's survival was part of his grand strategic vision rather than a desperate scramble.

**Adabra's Explosion.** The party's mild-mannered patron erupts with fury about temporal violations, denounces the intervention against the Kraken cult, and storms out of the council meeting. The divide between naturalists and interventionists widens.

**Accidental Sacrament.** Valphine's sermon about Lathander's light inspires worshippers to literally stare at the sun. She heals the newly blinded, inadvertently creating a church tradition. A severed cultist head on a pike outside the church adds an aesthetic touch.

**The Idiocy of Don-Jon.** The party realizes the boastful mine overseer was considered a complete fool by the barbarians he claims as friends. His tall tales betray more than he knows about his own incompetence.

**Cheese for Labor.** The wererats agree to work the mine for cheese shipments. Despite Zeleen taking offense at the stereotype, the deal holds. Petunia's cheese becomes the foundation of inter-species labor relations.

- A second Anchorite in bull form emerges, striking Soma and Vukradin with electricity.
- More cultists emerge from the manor and surrounding area.
- **COMBAT (Skirmish/Retreat):**

  - Valphine calls on Lathander to heal her companions, restoring their strength.
  - Vukradin advocates forcefully for withdrawal — "We are leaving! Now!" — pointing to the gold mine as a reason to survive.
  - Brewbarry in giant form grabs Vukradin and carries him to safety.
  - Soma puts Valphine on her back and carries her out.
  - Party successfully disengages. No pursuers beyond the manse perimeter.
  - The talking boars are disappointed the impostors weren't dealt with.

### Negotiations with the Whiskered Gang
- Zeleen Varnaster, leader: "This is our mine now."
- Her story: displaced from Shrine of Savras by ~16 orcs + ogres.
- Some orcs are from a tribe the party previously angered.
- Zeleen's proposal: clear the shrine → wererats vacate the mine.
- She refuses to fight for her own home. Offers only 2 warriors if pressed.

- Valphine senses the wererats are lying — they'll likely try to keep the mine regardless.
- Vukradin persuades Don-Jon that shared use is viable.
- **The deal:** Wererats work the mine + live in part of it. Party provides regular cheese shipments from Butterskull Ranch.
- Party avoids fighting 16+ orcs and ogres. Profit.

**(DM note):** The party has established a wererat labor force dependent on cheese supply. Any interruption to the cheese chain may have consequences. Don-Jon is a liability — easily manipulated by Vukradin and likely unreliable as a business partner.
