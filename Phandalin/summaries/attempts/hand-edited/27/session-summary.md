# Chapter 27 Sisters Against the Machine

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The party finishes surveying the first floor of Axeholm. They find a storage room with serviceable armor and weaponsmith tools, privies, barracks with 120-ft rubble-choked chimneys, and an empty armory — the dwarves took all weapons before departing. Ascending to the upper floor, the spatial and temporal distortions grow stronger. Scratching sounds echo from the north hallway.

Six out-of-phase dwarves scream about "efficiency" and "processes." Vukradin attempts to reason with them — offering to "improve their efficiency" — which only makes them more agitated. Combat erupts. Brewbarry charges and cuts through. Vukradin slows the remaining enemies. Valphine's radiant guardians destroy two instantly and wound another. Soma (bear) finishes them.

The **Chief Accountant** appears — a luminous, glitching dwarven figure with ink leaking from her eyes. She speaks of ledgers that won't close and regulators dividing by zero. She calls Valphine "sister of the sun" and accuses her of carrying "the scent of bleach and betrayal." The party engages. Vukradin summons a giant adding machine illusion. The Chief Accountant unleashes a TERRIBLE WAIL — Valphine and Vukradin collapse unconscious. Brewbarry and Soma survive but are wounded.

As Soma stabilizes the fallen, the Chief Accountant's demeanor changes — she panics. She frantically tries to revive Valphine with static energy, terrified "the auditor" will liquidate her for closing Valphine's account. Then she becomes incorporeal and flees.

After the party rests and heals, the Chief Accountant re-emerges and explains:
- Valphine's sister **Aletra** (4th daughter of House Satora/Sortorra) made a deal with the dwarven king
- The king was jealous of the Lionshield Coster's multi-dimensional trade
- Aletra installed a machine to improve efficiency and profit margins
- The machine **broke**, unleashing planar chaos
- The Chief Accountant offers the override code in exchange for her freedom

The party descends to the **Machine Room**. It's a nightmarish contraption of brass, orange organ pipes, spinning copper centrifuges, and purple steam. A magical voice booms about non-Euclidean geometry and purging protocols. Four spectral static spiders cling to the ceiling.

Aletra stands beside the machine — a drow woman in high-collared uniform, typing on a glowing keypad, holding a rapier. Valphine recognizes her by scent: lavender and bleach from the Satora poison labs. Aletra explains she needs to extract her cargo (drow poison smuggled through the multiverse). She offers a deal: party kills the guardian spiders, she extracts her cargo, then they can disable the device. Refusal means she tries anyway — which could cause an explosion.

## Memorable Moments

**Scent of Betrayal.** The Chief Accountant calls Valphine "sister of the sun" and speaks of "the scent of bleach and betrayal" — referencing the Satora poison labs. Valphine's family history echoes through the corrupted fortress.

**The Wrong Words.** Vukradin attempts diplomacy by offering to "improve efficiency" — the exact trigger that enrages the out-of-phase dwarves further. Sometimes talking makes things catastrophically worse.

**Accountant's Fear.** The Chief Accountant is more terrified of "the auditor" than she is of the party. She frantically tries to revive Valphine, panicking about accounts being closed — revealing a hierarchy of fear that extends beyond Axeholm.

**Sibling Rivalry, Interdimensional Edition.** Aletra dismisses Valphine with practiced condescension, telling her to leave the surface-dwellers and let the adults handle things. This is not their first fight.

**The Wrong Chapter.** The Chief Accountant's final "The book is closed" line and Aletra's "USE ONLY IF KP ISN'T WATCHING" note appear in later chapters, not here.

## Scenes

### First Floor Survey
- Storage room: serviceable armor and weaponsmith tools — useful for Phandalin.
- Privies (standard dwarven engineering).
- Barracks: dozens of meager cots, two cold fireplaces.
- Chimneys rise 120 ft through the mountain — choked with rubble.
- Empty armory: stone weapon racks, headless mannequins, iron hooks.
- All weapons were removed when the dwarves departed.

### Upper Floor — The Screaming Dwarves
- Spatial/temporal distortions strengthen noticeably.
- Scratching sound from north hallway.
- Six dwarves screaming about "efficiency" and "processes."
- Vukradin attempts reason — offering to "improve efficiency" backfires.
- **COMBAT:**
  - **Opening conditions:** Six dwarves in tight chamber. Party has the initiative.
  - Brewbarry charges in rage, halberd cutting through dwarves.
  - Vukradin casts Slow — remaining enemies are sluggish and vulnerable.
  - Valphine summons Radiant Guardians — destroys 2 instantly, wounds another.
  - Soma (bear form) finishes the wounded.
  - Duration: brief. Party overwhelms with AoE and crowd control.
  - **(DM tactical note):** Slow + Radiant Guardians was an excellent combo. Slowing the dwarves prevented them from reaching Valphine while her guardians did work.

### BOSS — The Chief Accountant
- **Opening conditions:** Single boss enemy. Luminous, glitching dwarven figure. Ink leaking from eyes. Geometric face mask. Static/scratching voice.
- Speaks of ledgers, regulators dividing by zero.
- Calls Valphine "sister of the sun" — "scent of bleach and betrayal."
- Vukradin summons a giant adding machine illusion — psychic damage.
- Valphine's Radiant Guardians continue their work.
- **SPECIAL ABILITY:** TERRIBLE WAIL — both Valphine and Vukradin collapse unconscious.
- Brewbarry and Soma survive but are wounded.
- Soma moves to stabilize Valphine and Vukradin.
- **TURNING POINT:** Chief Accountant panics — tries to revive Valphine with static energy.
- Terrified of "the auditor" liquidating her for closing Valphine's account.
- Becomes incorporeal and flees through a closed door.
- **(DM tactical note):** The Chief Accountant's wail appears to be a psychic/sonic AoE that targets WIS or CON saves. Valphine and Vukradin having lower CON saves explains why they dropped while Brewbarry (rage resistance) and Soma (bear form HP pool) survived.
- **(DM tactical note):** The Chief Accountant's panic suggests she operates under some kind of interdimensional corporate hierarchy. "The auditor" is above her and may be a greater threat than the machine itself.

### The Chief Accountant's Confession
- Party rests and heals.
- Chief Accountant cautiously re-emerges.
- She explains:
  - Aletra (Valphine's sister, 4th daughter of House Satora) made a deal with the king
  - King was jealous of Lionshield Coster's multi-dimensional trade revenue
  - Aletra installed a machine to improve efficiency/profit margins
  - Machine BROKE — unleashed planar chaos throughout the fortress
  - When the Chief Accountant complained about unbalanced books, Aletra laughed and likely killed her
- She offers: override code in exchange for freedom. And a promise not to tell Aletra what transpired.
- Party debates trusting her. Ultimately accepts.

### The Machine Room
- Party descends through a tunnel to the lower level.
- **The Machine:** Massive brass contraption, orange organ pipes, spinning copper centrifuges, twisting mechanical arms bolted to the floor.
- Vibrates violently, vents purple steam.
- Booming magical voice: non-Euclidean geometry, purging protocols.
- Static electricity makes hair stand on end.
- Four spectral spiders of flickering static on the ceiling — hissing like white noise.
- **Aletra:** Drow woman in high-collared uniform. Typing on glowing keypad. Holding rapier.
- Valphine recognizes her by scent: lavender + bleach (Satora poison labs).
- Sharp cheekbones, cold violet eyes. Exposed traitors for profit.
- Aletra speaks condescendingly in Undercommon: "Go play in the mud."
- She explains: she's in equilibrium with the machine and the guardian constructs.
- She needs to extract her cargo (drow poison smuggled multiversally) before the machine can be shut down.
- **HER DEAL:** Party kills the 4 spectral spiders. She extracts cargo. Party disables device.
- **REFUSAL CONSEQUENCE:** She tries anyway → could cause an explosion.
- **(DM note):** Aletra is willing to risk a planar explosion to get her cargo. This tells the party how valuable the Midnight Tears poison cargo is to her operation.
