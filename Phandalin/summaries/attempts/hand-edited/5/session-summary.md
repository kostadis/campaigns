# Chapter 5 Saving Adabra

**Date:** 04-01 of Taraskh 1495

---

## Summary

The party arrives at Umbrage Hill with Corbin in tow, triggering Vukradin's most aggressive ethical confrontation yet: Corbin's armor is "stolen from a dead man," and Vukradin orders him to remove it. Corbin pleads — he needs protection — and Brewbarry's complete lack of armor becomes Vukradin's rhetorical weapon ("I don't wear any"). A compromise is reached: Corbin keeps the armor temporarily until "fair trade armor" can be obtained. Soma, just emerged from spider form after the Wild Shape expired, freaks Corbin out — Aasimar, drow, and now a tortle who was just a spider.

Atop the hill, a manticore perches on the windmill, holding Adabra Gwynn captive inside. The manticore can speak and is willing to negotiate — it offers to leave in exchange for "the succulent taste of human meat," specifically pointing at Corbin. Soma conjures a familiar and orders it into the manticore's mouth as a "tasty morsel," but the creature snaps it up unsatisfied. The battle is fierce: Brewbarry engages in melee, taking claws and frost. Valphine is struck by a tail spike volley. Soma's Ice Knife misses the manticore. Vukradin's Command spell fails. The manticore tries to flee toward Corbin when wounded, but Brewbarry kills it mid-flight.

Post-combat, Adabra Gwynn emerges — a hard-bitten, fanatical acolyte of Chauntea with a piercing gaze, calloused hands that smell of herbs, and an obsession with the Naturalist/Interventionist schism within the Emerald Enclave. She tests Soma's allegiance ("Naturalist or interventionist?") and, upon hearing "naturalist," adopts her completely. She refuses to leave the windmill, insisting she must stay to craft potions. Vukradin plays music — Adabra is delighted by the "blessed music" of an aasimar. She still refuses to leave, giving Vukradin a note for Harbin explaining her decision.

Brewbarry asks to spend the night. Adabra agrees and prepares a meal of fresh-baked bread and vegetable stew. Over dinner, Corbin tells his full backstory: his village Greenmeadow was destroyed by Uthgardt Black Lion raiders during the Ordning's collapse. He lost his wife Elara and daughter Lily. He wandered south for months, stealing to survive, each crime chipping away at who he used to be, until he drifted into banditry. A tragic, broken man desperate for belonging.

The next morning, Adabra has a revelation: Vukradin's principles — no killing for treasure, no plunder — align with naturalism. She launches into a passionate lecture about how "barbaric slaughtering of monsters to seize their treasures is an act of barbarism and vandalism." She reframes Brewbarry from "barbarian" to "nomad" (to his confusion) and declares she will accompany the party to "support" them as fellow naturalists. What began as a quest to retrieve a midwife ends with the party gaining a fanatical Emerald Enclave ally.

## Scenes

### The Armor Debate
- Corbin arrives with the party. Vukradin immediately spots the armor as stolen: "Is that stolen armor?"
- Corbin: "I had no choice. The owner was already dead."
- Vukradin orders him to remove it. Brewbarry's lack of armor is used as leverage.
- Corbin: "Mr Brewbarry, can I please keep my armor?" Brewbarry: "I don't wear any."
- A compromise: Corbin keeps the armor until "fair trade armor" can be obtained. Vukradin relents when he sees the despair in Corbin's eyes.
- Soma-as-spider re-emerges and gestures frantically at Corbin — she's trying to communicate but can only speak with spiders. Corbin freaks out.
- Vukradin: "That's Soma, our tortle." Corbin's worldview, already shattered, takes another blow.

### Journey to Umbrage Hill
- Soma's spider form expires; she returns to tortle form.
- Corbin is in shock: "It is true I am tortle. And why do you need armor?"
- Corbin: "Because I am human." Soma eyes his squishy form, musing on how this race dominates Faerûn.
- Party arrives at the hilltop windmill with a manticore perched on it. Adabra's voice from inside: "Help me!"
- Valphine asks surface-world ethics questions: "We were told to bring her back." Soma: "Yes, but it was implied alive."
- Vukradin suggests killing the manticore, returning to Phandalin, demanding more money, then recovering Adabra. Soma shuts that down.
- Corbin, trying to be helpful, suggests Vukradin leave his instrument behind to avoid damage. Vukradin: "No." Soma offers Mending. Vukradin: "I am not the kind of musician who thinks that hands are better than magical tools."

### Manticore — Combat

**Opening conditions:** Windmill atop Umbrage Hill. Adabra is inside the first floor. The manticore is perched on the roof/second floor. The party approaches quietly but Soma's failed stealth alerts it. Manticore can speak Common. Adabra is a fragile NPC inside. Corbin is at the rear, exposed.

- Valphine fires a crossbow bolt — the manticore is unaffected but speaks: "That hurt?"
- Vukradin attempts Command — manticore saves.
- Manticore offers a deal: give it Corbin (human meat) and it leaves. It specifically wants the succulent taste of human flesh.
- Soma conjures a familiar and orders it into the manticore's mouth as a "tasty morsel." Unsatisfying crunch — "That was not satisfying!"
- Brewbarry throws handaxes, hits a wing. Manticore flies down, claws Brewbarry. Brewbarry hits with halberd as it closes.
- Manticore retreats to second floor, howling in pain. Adabra: "Please do something!"
- Soma tries to assure Adabra — "Stay calm!" Adabra shrieks: "You could run out while it's distracted? It could attack me!"
- Vukradin casts Command again at Soma's encouragement: "I believe in you." The manticore saves again. Vukradin: "Well, I didn't study maths or chance! I studied the arts! I trusted you, Soma!"
- Soma looks visibly hurt. Valphine misses with her crossbow.
- Brewbarry climbs to the second floor, hits the manticore twice. His rage protects him from its return claws.
- Manticore tries to flee toward Corbin (easier prey). Vukradin and Soma strike with spells. Valphine's crossbow misses again.
- Brewbarry kills it mid-flight. "YES!"
- **(DM tactical note):** The manticore's willingness to negotiate before fighting is important — it's intelligent and has preferences. The party could have talked their way out of this encounter, which is worth noting for future intelligent monster encounters. Its focus on obtaining Corbin (the weakest, most exposed party member) as food rather than fighting to the death shows calculated predator behavior.
- **(DM tactical note):** The party's anti-air options remain limited. Brewbarry climbing to the second floor to engage in melee was a good solution but put him in the danger zone. Valphine's hand crossbow is unreliable at range. The party needs at least one reliable ranged damage dealer.
- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's frustration with Soma after the failed Command spell is a notable character moment. He blames Soma for encouraging him to try again — a deflection that shows his own tactical frustration. Soma's visible hurt suggests this stung more than Vukradin realized.
- **Enemy behavior:** Manticore negotiates first, offers bargain, uses hit-and-run tactics (fly in, attack, fly out). Focuses on the downed/weak targets. Tries to flee toward easier prey (Corbin) when badly wounded. Intelligent and tactical.
- **Spell/ability impact:** Command — failed twice; Conjured Familiar (Soma) — creative but minimal mechanical impact; Handaxes (Brewbarry) — surprisingly effective at range; Starry Wisp / spells — used on the fleeing manticore.
- **PC resource decisions:** Vukradin used Command twice despite the manticore saving against it the first time — learned nothing from the first failure. Soma used Wild Shape earlier (in spider form for travel) and a familiar conjuration — creative but less efficient than direct damage.
- **Post-combat:** Vukradin cuts off the manticore's head and marches to the windmill with it. Brewbarry waves the dead tail.

### Meeting Adabra
- Adabra emerges cautiously. She is described in detail: weathered, lean, past her prime, with calloused hands that smell of herbs and antiseptic. A piercing, cold gaze that misses nothing. Hair pulled back simply, plain functional clothing stained with remnants of her work.
- She ensures the manticore is dead, then offers a "special potion of healing with extra scent and taste."
- Soma mutters: "That sounds like a bath water potion."
- Adabra refuses to leave the windmill. She reveals the Naturalist/Interventionist schism within the Emerald Enclave.
- She tests Soma: "Naturalist or interventionist?" Soma answers naturalist — and Adabra's entire demeanor shifts to warm approval.
- Adabra's philosophy: "The naturalists, like us, think that the meta-verse is part of the natural order. We should accept the divine interventions as part of the natural order."
- Vukradin plays music. Adabra is delighted: "How wonderful! This is truly blessed music! Aasimars are truly touched by the Celestial planes."
- She still refuses to leave. Gives Vukradin a note for Harbin explaining her refusal. The note also contains medical instructions for Harbin — Adabra has been treating him.

### Corbin's Backstory (Dinner Scene)
- Corbin tells his full story over Adabra's meal of hearty bread, vegetable stew with local fungi, foraged vegetables, and wild greens.
- **Greenmeadow:** A farming village east of the High Forest. Corbin was a farmer, married to Elara, with a daughter named Lily who loved wildflowers.
- **The Uthgardt attack:** The Black Lion tribe descended during the Ordning's collapse. Corbin fought back with a wood-axe. Watched his home burn, his family fall. Survived by being knocked unconscious by a charging shielder's shield and left for dead in a ditch, buried under his neighbor's body.
- **The aftermath:** Walked south for months, drifting, stealing to survive. Each petty crime — a half-eaten apple, a discarded blanket, a few coins — eroded who he had been.
- Eventually joined a bandit crew when he had nothing left.
- **(DM note):** This backstory is important for several reasons: (1) it establishes the human cost of the Ordning's collapse beyond abstract lore; (2) it gives the party a tangible connection to the Uthgardt raiders and their victims; (3) Corbin's slide from farmer to bandit through loss and desperation is a microcosm of the campaign's themes about how the world's chaos breaks ordinary people.
- Soma listens and thinks of her own training, of Meril, of the time she has left — she empathizes with Corbin's journey even as she stays silent about her own.

### Adabra's Conversion
- Morning: Adabra realizes Vukradin's principles — no killing for treasure, no plunder — align with naturalism.
- She explodes: "This barbaric slaughtering of monsters to seize their treasures is an act of barbarism and vandalism!"
- Brewbarry: "I am a barbarian." Soma, protective: "We don't use the B word in front of Brewbarry."
- Adabra reframes Brewbarry: "You are a nomad. I will not permit such language in this house."
- Her lecture: "The word barbarian implies that there are people who are civilized and those who are uncivilized... The interventionists want to draw lines, and when you draw lines, those that fall out become prey and can be destroyed. You, Brewbarry, are part of the natural world. You are not opposed to it."
- Brewbarry seems uncomfortable but doesn't press the point.
- Vukradin tentatively asks: "I heard you were going to come with us?" Adabra: "Well, yes. You are fellow naturalists!"
- **(DM note):** Adabra's reframing of Brewbarry as a "nomad" is more than wordplay — it's a deliberate narrative device from the naturalist philosophy to deconstruct the language of civilization vs. savagery. This foreshadows the deeper naturalist/interventionist conflict that will recur throughout the campaign. Adabra's sudden decision to join the party is not just character warmth — she sees the party as a way to build a naturalist network in the Phandalin area.

## NPCs
- **Adabra Gwynn** — Acolyte of Chauntea, midwife, and windmill-dweller on Umbrage Hill. Hard-bitten, fanatical, with a piercing gaze that misses nothing. Weathered hands that smell of herbs and antiseptic. Deeply entrenched in the Naturalist/Interventionist schism of the Emerald Enclave. Views divine interventions as "part of the natural order." Reframes Brewbarry from "barbarian" to "nomad" as a philosophical act. Decides to travel with the party after learning Vukradin shares her anti-plunder principles. **(DM note):** Adabra is more than a simple quest-giver. She is a faction representative with a hidden agenda: building a naturalist network in the Phandalin area. Her offer to travel with the party is also surveillance — she's vetting Vukradin and Soma as potential Emerald Enclave members. She has been treating Harbin Wester with some kind of medicine.
- **Corbin** (full backstory revealed) — Former farmer of Greenmeadow, destroyed by the Black Lion tribe of the Uthgardt during the Ordning's collapse. Lost his wife Elara and daughter Lily. Survived by being left for dead under his neighbor's body. Became a drifter, then a bandit. Joins the party as a camp caretaker. A tragic figure whose trauma response to Uthgardt symbols will require careful handling. Wears armor taken from a dead man — Vukradin has ordered him to obtain "fair trade armor."
- **The Manticore** — Intelligent, speaks Common. Has a strong preference for human flesh ("succulent taste"). Offers negotiation before attacking — a sentient creature who treated Corbin as food rather than a person. Uses hit-and-run tactics. Focus-fires weak targets. Tries to flee to easier prey when wounded. Killed by Brewbarry mid-flight. Its preference for human meat was confirmed by Adabra — manticores genuinely do prefer human flesh over other humanoids.
