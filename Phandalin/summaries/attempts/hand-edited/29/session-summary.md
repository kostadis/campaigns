# Chapter 29 Drones, Dread, and Dangerous Deliveries

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

With the machine destroyed and Aletra escaped, the party searches the room where the device imploded. Nothing remains but interdimensional spider silk strands. Valphine, a former devotee of Lolth, considers collecting them for her temple. The party debates whether the Rift Weavers were natural phenomena, defensive mechanisms, or summoned servants.

The party enters Aletra's makeshift quarters in a former barracks. A high-quality silk bedroll and a folding desk of black Zurkhwood reveal where she worked. The lingering scent of lavender and bleach — the signature of House Xorlarrin's poison labs — lingers. Soma finds Aletra's ledger: forged entries, "iron for 500 pounds" crossed out, "Midnight Tears — crate 4 — do not drop" written in. Vukradin leaves the room; Valphine finds a hidden bag with a platinum coin (7 gp), malachite (8 gp), fine wine, and 4 vials of Midnight Tears poison (2000 gp total). The party also finds the Operator's Guide to the Model 1489 Spatial Rectification Calliope — with Aletra's note: "use only if KP isn't watching."

The party returns to the Chief Accountant. She's calm and relaxed now that the spatial distortions have vanished. She's relieved the machine is gone — until they present her with the interdimensional bill for 42,000 gp. She screams for ten minutes about the outrageous charges. Then she sighs, declares "The book is closed," and disappears at peace.

In the accountant's old room, Valphine discovers a secret door hidden behind the soot-stained fireplace. Four **audit drones** — steampunk mechanical constructs — emerge and attack. One sticks a needle into Valphine, filling a vial with her blood. Vukradin kills two. Another attacks him. Valphine captures one by pulling it off her body. Behind the secret door: an 80-lb iron chest with clawed feet and a circular lock. Brewbarry smashes it open — inside: a Dread Helm and Gauntlets of Ogre Power. Brewbarry dons the helm — his eyes glow red.

After a long rest, the party returns to Phandalin. Harbin Wester is ecstatic and pays 250 gp for securing Axeholm. He nervously asks if Brewbarry — still wearing the dread helm with glowing red eyes — is possessed. Vukradin intimidates him with a reminder of his Privy Council position.

Harbin sends the party on a **cheese delivery** to the gold mine. The party travels 15 miles to Mountain's Toe. At the west entrance: 2 ogres and 8 orcs demanding the mine from the wererats. The ogres recognize the party — they killed their tribesmen. Combat erupts.

Javelins fly. An ogre charges Brewbarry. Vukradin slows both ogres and 2 orcs. Soma's Flaming Sphere kills 2 orcs, then she transforms into a bear. Valphine's spectral guardians kill 3 orcs and severely wound an ogre. Brewbarry kills the wounded ogre. The remaining ogre retreats, firing a ranged weapon. Battle continues.

## Memorable Moments

**Closing the Books.** The Chief Accountant's final moments — screaming about a 42,000 gp bill before calmly accepting "the book is closed" — are both tragic and hilarious.

**Corporate Espionage.** Aletra's personal notes on a crate read "use only if KP isn't watching." She's skimming from someone named "KP" whose authority reaches across planar boundaries.

**Demon-Brewbarry.** Harbin's reaction to Brewbarry's glowing red eyes under the dread helm is one of pure, unguarded terror.

**High-Stakes Cheese Delivery.** Harbin sends the party on a cheese delivery mission while warning that the white dragon threatens the farming communities. The tonal whiplash is intentional.

**Privy Politics.** Vukradin reminds Harbin of his council position. The townmaster is effectively cowed.

### Secret Room — Audit Drones
- Valphine discovers a secret door in the soot-stained fireplace.
- **COMBAT (Audit Drones):**
  - **Opening conditions:** Party in tight quarters. Secret door opens. Four steampunk mechanical constructs emerge.
  - One drone sticks a needle into Valphine — fills a vial halfway with her blood. (Sampling.)
  - Vukradin kills 2 drones with powerful strikes.
  - A third drone attacks Vukradin.
  - Valphine captures one by pulling it off her body — retains it for study.
  - **(DM tactical note):** The drones' blood-sampling ability suggests they serve a similar function to the mosquito construct from the smithy. They were auditing the fortress's biological inhabitants.
- Behind the secret door: 10x10 ft room, 80-lb iron chest with clawed feet.
- Chest has a unique circular lock — multiple failed pick attempts.
- Brewbarry smashes the chest open — loud echo through the fortress.
- **LOOT:** Dread Helm, Gauntlets of Ogre Power.
- Brewbarry puts on the dread helm — eyes glow red.
- **(DM note):** The dread helm is likely the Chief Accountant's former property. Brewbarry's immediate claim of it may have alignment/perception consequences.

### Cheese Delivery — Mine Combat
- **Opening conditions:** Party arrives at Mountain's Toe west entrance. 2 ogres, 8 orcs. Wererats inside the mine. Open terrain outside.
- Orcs throw javelins. One hits Brewbarry.
- Valphine attempts Shield (or similar deflection magic).
- Ogre charges Brewbarry — Brewbarry lands a powerful strike as it approaches (readied attack or reaction).
- Vukradin casts Slow (or similar) — both ogres + 2 orcs become sluggish.
- Soma summons Flaming Sphere — kills 2 orcs instantly.
- Soma transforms into bear form.
- Valphine summons Spectral Guardians (Spirit Guardians) — radiant aura around her. Kills 3 orcs, severely wounds an ogre.
- Brewbarry (raging) strikes down the wounded ogre with devastating blow.
- Remaining ogre fires a ranged weapon at Valphine, then backs away.
- Another orc throws a javelin — misses.
- Battle continues with remaining enemies.
- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's Slow + Soma's Flaming Sphere + Valphine's Spirit Guardians is a devastating AoE combo. The orcs had no answer for taking damage every round while being slowed.
- **(DM tactical note):** The ogres recognize the party — this is vendetta, not random encounter. The party's reputation is spreading among orc tribes.
