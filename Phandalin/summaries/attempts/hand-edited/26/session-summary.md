# Chapter 26 From Out-of-Phase Dwarves to Mechanical Mysteries

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The party continues their exploration of Axeholm. They discover a locked room with nothing inside — no treasure, no monsters, no explanation. Pushing deeper, they find a small mechanical construct: a tiny mosquito-like drone designed to sample blood. The presence of advanced technology in an ancient dwarven fortress suggests that external forces have been operating here.

Descending further, the party encounters the Dwarven King — now a three-headed monstrosity that moves with impossible speed. The creature's multi-attack sequence paralyzes Soma and drops her in a single devastating round. The battle is brutal and desperate.

Valphine faces the King's full fury — every claw, every bite, aimed at her — and by sheer fortune, every single attack misses. The party capitalizes and brings down the corrupted king.

## Memorable Moments

**The Locked Nothing.** An empty, locked room with no treasure, no monsters, no explanation. Sometimes the mystery is the point.

**Steampunk Mosquito.** A tiny mechanical blood-sampling drone in an ancient dwarven fortress — clear evidence that someone external and technologically advanced has been operating in Axeholm.

**Triple the Trouble.** The Dwarven King's three-headed form moves with impossible speed. Soma is paralyzed and downed in a single multi-attack sequence.

**Divine Luck.** The King unleashes everything on Valphine — and every single attack misses. She walks away unscathed by sheer fortune.
