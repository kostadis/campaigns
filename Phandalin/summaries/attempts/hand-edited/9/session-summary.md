# Chapter 9 The Beating Heart of the Storm and Whispers from the Deep

Date: Taraskh 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

With Moesko defeated, the party searches his chamber and discovers a concealed compartment containing a dark talisman. Brewbarry's perception identifies a unique symbol on its reverse: an inverted eye with coiling tentacle-like lines. Valphine's divine and psionic senses detect a strange energy — alien, not conventional magic — and a faint rhythmic thrumming like a distant heartbeat. This is the party's first direct contact with the Kraken Society.

The party ascends the lighthouse to confront the beacon. Valphine, obsessed with Lathander's light, stares directly into it and is temporarily blinded — but before her vision fails, she sees the truth: the green light emanates from a human heart several sizes too large. It's Moesko's own heart, still beating. Vukradin insists it must be destroyed; Valphine argues it's "useful light." Vukradin smashes the lightning rod below, returns, and destroys the heart with two weapon swings. He then casts Light to serve as a temporary beacon.

Miral the Sea Elf manifests, thanks the party, and warns them that Moesko was manipulated by "something foreign" — not Talos, but darker forces. She warns of "true storm" gathering in the depths below Leilon. She vanishes. A giant crab appears, grateful for the dispatched sharks.

Soma, in giant toad form, attempts to salvage dry wood from shipwrecks but finds everything waterlogged. She discovers several shipwrecks: the Star Crossed Lover (where she finds a +1 halberd in a dead captain's skeletal grip), the Sea Urchin (locked chest), and the Vainglory (locked chest with a magical cloak). While underwater, she is attacked by a hunter shark, then two more. A brutal underwater fight ensues. Vukradin attempts Vicious Mockery on a shark (1 damage) before putting both remaining sharks to sleep. Valphine casts Faerie Fire on the sleeping sharks, then coordinates an attack with Brewbarry for heavy damage.

Chests retrieved: a blue quartz mermaid statue, a spellbook, and a Wand of Secrets. Vukradin objects to opening "other people's chests." Tensions over loot philosophy continue to simmer.

## Memorable Moments

> "I can actually become, like, an octopus." … "Let's go with giant toad form, that's probably more appropriate."
> — Soma, considering aquatic forms

**Amphibious Assault.** Soma's improvisational approach to underwater navigation — rejecting octopus for giant toad — proves tactically sound, though she takes heavy damage from the shark pack.

> "Can I try to sleep both of them?"
> — Vukradin, on the remaining sharks

**Sharknap.** Vukradin puts two hunter sharks to magical sleep with a single spell, ending the underwater threat without further bloodshed. The tactical pivot from mockery to sleep is inspired.

> "Why are we opening other people's chests? Those aren't yours."
> — Vukradin, as Valphine picks locks

**Ethical Looting.** Vukradin's moral philosophy faces its first major test: recovered treasure from shipwrecks whose owners are dead. His insistence on repatriation over plunder isolates him from the pragmatic rest of the party.

> "What the hell is that! He just said it was a treasure that he was gonna go get for you. Where'd you get it?"
> — Vukradin, realizing Brewbarry's new halberd was the crab's intended gift

**Crab-Baiting the Party.** The giant crab's dry delivery — that the +1 halberd was "the treasure I was going to get for you" — creates a comedy-of-errors moment as Vukradin realizes the party accidentally stole from a crab.

> "Beware, surface dweller, the true dwellers. The true storm gathers not in the sky, but in the depths below Leilon."
> — Miral, before vanishing

**The Deep Warning.** Miral's final words plant the first seed of a deeper conspiracy: Moesko was a pawn, and the real threat lies in the depths. The Kraken Society thread begins.

## Scenes

### Post-Moesko Search
- Soma notices a strange hole in Moesko's chest (the source of the green heart).
- Valphine finds a concealed compartment between loose floorboards — a dark talisman is recovered.
- Brewbarry identifies an inverted eye with tentacle coiling lines on the reverse.
- Valphine detects divine resonance plus a dominant psionic energy — alien magic.
- The talisman's rhythmic thrumming pulses like a distant heartbeat.

**(DM note, not yet known to party):** The tentacle-eye symbol is the mark of the Kraken Society. The talisman is a psionic focus or communication device. Its pulse may be a beacon or a link to a deeper entity.

### Ascending the Lighthouse
- Valphine stares into the green beacon — is temporarily blinded by Lathander's light.
- Before blindness, she glimpses a human heart, several sizes too large, beating within the light.
- Vukradin identifies it as Moesko's heart, removed and magically sustained as the corrupted beacon.
- Morality debate: Vukradin insists on destruction; Valphine argues it serves a useful purpose.
- Vukradin descends, smashes the lightning rod, returns, and destroys the heart with two swings (first misses, offhand hits, second swing kills it).
- The light dies. The air feels cleansed.
- Vukradin casts Light as a temporary beacon: "Shall we set a fire?"

**(DM tactical note):** The heart had no HP indicated in the narrative but required two hits. Vukradin's decision to destroy the lightning rod first suggests creative problem-solving — he suspected the heart and rod were linked.

### Miral's Manifestation and Warning
- Miral the Sea Elf manifests as the party presents the conch.
- She thanks the party: "My spirit can finally rest."
- Reveals Moesko was manipulated — not entirely of his own will.
- "Shadows" moved within him, twisting his Talos devotion into "something colder, more calculating."
- Warns: "The true storm gathers not in the sky, but in the depths below Leilon."
- Vanishes. The tower's curse is broken.
- A giant crab appears, grateful for the dispatched sharks.
- The crab claims the +1 halberd was "the treasure I was going to get for you" — Brewbarry already has it.

**(DM note):** Miral's spirit can now rest. The "depths below Leilon" hint connects to the Kraken Society's underwater operations. This is the first explicit thread pointing toward a larger conspiracy.

### Underwater Salvage and Shark Combat
- Soma takes giant toad form (39 HP) and plunges into murky waters (20-ft visibility).
- **COMBAT (Hunter Sharks):**
  - **Opening conditions:** Soma alone underwater in toad form. Murky visibility. Three shipwrecks as terrain. Party on shore.
  - **Opening maneuver:** Soma swims to the Star Crossed Lover, finds a +1 halberd in the dead captain's skeletal grip.
  - A hunter shark attacks. Soma lands 18 damage with her bite but fails to grapple.
  - Two more sharks join the fray. One hits Soma for 16 damage, dropping her to 22 HP.
  - Soma misses one attack, then lands 12 damage on the second shark — it goes limp and dies.
  - Vukradin contributes 1 damage via Vicious Mockery (hearing the shark).
  - Vukradin casts Sleep — both remaining sharks fall unconscious.
  - Valphine casts Faerie Fire on the sleeping sharks, granting advantage to attackers.
  - Valphine and Brewbarry coordinate an attack: 22 total damage to one shark.
  - **Post-combat:** Three sharks dead or subdued. Shark meat recovered (lawfully earned, per Vukradin).
- Soma retrieves three locked chests from wrecks.
- The wood from shipwrecks is too waterlogged for immediate use as beacon fuel.

**(DM tactical note):** Soma went underwater alone with no support. If the sharks had focused fire (Pack Tactics), she could have been overwhelmed before the party could intervene. The party had no underwater combat capabilities or water breathing prepared (despite having a potion). The Sleep + Faerie Fire combo was efficient, but it required the sharks to clump.

**(DM tactical note):** The potion of water breathing found in the harpy nest was not used here. If Soma hadn't had Wild Shape available, the party would have had no underwater option at all.

### Locked Chests and Loot Philosophy
- Valphine picks all three locks despite Vukradin's objections.
- **Chest 1 (Sea Urchin):** 9-inch blue quartz statue of a mermaid.
- **Chest 2 (Vainglory):** Spellbook (no wizard in party to use it).
- **Chest 3 (Orca):** Wand of Secrets (secret door detection).
- Vukradin insists on returning all items to "rightful owners."
- Soma tries to give Vukradin a magical cloak of many fashions — he refuses.
- The mermaid statue, spellbook, and wand are added to the party's inventory under protest.
- Tensions over clean vs. practical treasure remain unresolved.

## NPCs

Her spirit can finally rest. **(DM note):** Miral knows more than she shared. She felt "shadows" within Moesko — this is likely the Kraken Society's influence or a psionic entity.

- **Giant Crab** — A grateful crustacean who benefited from the party's shark-slaying. Attempts to claim credit for the +1 halberd as a gift. Comic relief.

- **Vicious Mockery** — Vukradin. Dealt 1 damage to a shark underwater. Minimal effect but audacious.
