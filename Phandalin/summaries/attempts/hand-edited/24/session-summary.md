# Chapter 24 The Ale, the Ex, and Axeholm

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

Big Al breaks down and confesses he abandoned Qelline Alderleaf the night before their wedding. The party insists on the truth, forcing a painful but necessary reckoning. Vukradin also discovers, to his embarrassment, that he's been so absorbed in his musical studies that he missed decades of major events — including the destruction of Neverwinter.

The party returns to Phandalin and reports to Harbin. Lyra from the Emerald Enclave is recognized for her role in the rescue — Vukradin inflates her contributions to rebuild her confidence after the Woodland Manse failure.

Valphine struggles with performing at the tavern. Her performance falls flat while the rest of the party thrives. She seems to be wrestling with something deeper than stage fright.

Qelline Alderleaf offers a deliberately high grazing fee for Big Al's cattle — a "hurt price" at one gold piece per week. The economics of petty revenge are surprisingly steep.

Brewbarry creates a new tavern spectacle with impressive physical displays and dance moves that the audience begins imitating. A new Phandalin dance craze is born.

The party makes the decision to investigate Axeholm — the ancient dwarven fortress mentioned in their quest log. They gather supplies and prepare for the journey into the mountains.

## Memorable Moments

**The Wedding Night Confession.** Big Al breaks down and admits he abandoned Qelline the night before their wedding. The party insists on the truth — a painful but necessary reckoning.

**Priorities.** Vukradin discovers that Neverwinter's destruction slipped his notice entirely. Music studies take precedence over geopolitical awareness.

**Leadership Gaslighting.** Vukradin inflates Lyra's role in the rescue to rebuild her confidence — a small deception for a good cause.

**Crisis of Faith.** Valphine's performance falls flat while the rest of the party thrives. Something deeper than stage fright is bothering her.

**Hurt Prices.** Qelline's deliberately high grazing fee — a "hurt price" of one gold piece per week — reveals the depth of her unresolved feelings toward Big Al.

**Brewbarry the Entertainer.** The barbarian's tavern performance starts a crowd-wide imitation trend. Phandalin's hottest new dance is born.
