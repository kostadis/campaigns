# Chapter 26 From Out-of-Phase Dwarves to Mechanical Mysteries

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The battle in the mustering hall of Axeholm continues. Valphine fires her crossbow at a wounded out-of-phase dwarf, bringing it to the brink. Soma shakes off the paralysis that held her bear form, her constitution proving strong enough to resist the unnatural effect. The party systematically eliminates the remaining dwarves — Brewbarry delivers crushing blows, Vukradin supports with inspiring words. Valphine channels healing energy to restore the party's strength.

The fortress's spatial distortions remain — distances shift unnaturally, blinking lights flicker above, the pervasive stench of death hangs in the air. A narrow tunnel leads upward. Exploring the upper level, the party finds ransacked rooms, a blackened fireplace chimney that connects to the mountain face, and a dwarven shrine. In the shrine, they discover a platinum amulet of Moradin and a steel coffer containing a potion of superior healing, protected by packed silt. They debate whether to surrender these to Harbin or keep them for the Privy Council.

A locked door yields an empty room — puzzling. Why lock nothing? The mystery deepens.

In the grand pillared hall, two more out-of-phase dwarves are spotted. Soma in bear form ambushes one, mauling it to death instantly. The party dispatches the second — but their victory is short-lived.

**BOSS FIGHT — The Dwarven King:** A three-headed monstrosity emerges from behind a limestone throne. The former king moves with terrifying speed. He paralyzes Soma, then all three heads descend in a flurry of bites — Soma drops unconscious, reverting from bear form. Valphine turns a lesser dwarf that flees up the chimney. Brewbarry rages, squeezing through the doorway to engage the King. Vukradin's first attempt at an illusion of Moradin fails — the King's will is too strong. The King advances on Brewbarry, taking heavy damage from defensive strikes but pressing forward.

Valphine revives Soma. Vukradin tries again — this time successfully conjuring a giant bear illusion in the King's mind, dealing psychic damage. The confused King turns on Valphine but miraculously misses every claw and bite. The party wears down the three-headed horror until it collapses.

Loot: a signet ring with the Axeholm crest (hand in dwarven gauntlet) — both artifact and key. A grand dining room with melted candles. A 60-ft trash chute (wisely not investigated). A functioning kitchen sink with hot spring water. And in the smithy: a tiny mechanical mosquito-like construct with a glass jar of blood — a sampling device, unnatural and unsettling.

## Memorable Moments

> "Why would someone lock an EMPTY room?"
> — Valphine, after picking the lock

**The Locked Nothing.** An empty, locked room. No treasure, no monsters, no explanation. Sometimes the mystery is the point.

> "That's... offensive. Who would build something so unnatural?"
> — Vukradin, examining the mosquito construct

**Steampunk Mosquito.** A tiny mechanical blood-sampling drone in an ancient dwarven fortress. The party's first clear evidence that someone external — and technologically advanced — has been operating in Axeholm.

> "THREE HEADS?!"
> — Brewbarry, upon seeing the King

**Triple the Trouble.** The Dwarven King's three-headed form moves with impossible speed. Soma is paralyzed and downed in a single multi-attack sequence.

> "All three attacks miss. Every single one."
> — The dice gods

**Divine Luck.** The three-headed King unleashes everything on Valphine — claws, bites, everything — and misses every attack. Valphine walks away unscathed by sheer fortune.

- **Opening conditions:** Party in pillared hall. Three-headed monstrosity behind limestone throne. Companion corrupted dwarf. Terrain: pillars for cover, throne as partial obstacle.

- **(DM tactical note):** The King's all-miss round on Valphine was statistically improbable but fortunate. If even one attack had landed, she could have gone down — the party had no backup healer.

## NPCs

- **The Dwarven King (Three-Headed Monstrosity)** — Former ruler of Axeholm, twisted into a three-headed horror by the planar anomaly. Terrifying speed, paralysis touch, multi-attack. Carried the signet ring key. **(GM note):** His corruption was directly caused by the machine Aletra installed. His deal with Aletra doomed his people.

## Items

- **Platinum Amulet of Moradin** — From the dwarven shrine. Religious artifact of the dwarven god. Party debates ownership.
- **Potion of Superior Healing** — Steel coffer in the shrine, protected by silt. Valuable healing consumable.
- **Signet Ring (Axeholm Crest)** — From the King's body. Hand-in-gauntlet crest. Both artifact and key to further areas of the fortress. **(GM note):** This ring may unlock deeper sections or provide authority to command residual dwarven constructs.

## Spells

- **Turn Undead** — Valphine. Causes the lesser dwarf to flee up the chimney. Strategic removal of an enemy from the boss fight.
- **Healing (Valphine)** — Revives Soma from unconsciousness. Critical resource management during the King fight.
- **Illusory Reality / Major Image** — Vukradin. Failed first attempt (Moradin), succeeded second attempt (giant bear). Psychic damage against the King. The second attempt's success suggests adaptability.
- **Wild Shape (Bear)** — Soma. Frontline combat but repeatedly targeted for paralysis. Reverting on unconsciousness is a significant tactical cost.
- **Rage** — Brewbarry. Reduces incoming damage from the King's multi-attack. Essential for survivability.
