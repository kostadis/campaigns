# Chapter 2 Arrival in Phandalin

Date: 8/1–10/1 of Taraskh 1495

## Summary

Vukradin, an avant-garde aasimar bard whose experimental music finds no audience in Neverwinter, quits his gig at the First Flophouse in Heapside and accepts a Wintershield-sanctioned adventuring contract — but only after a pointed debate about the ethics of plunder. The guard's explanation (tax-free treasure from monsters and evildoers under Lord Neverember's authorization) leaves him deeply uncomfortable, but his empty coin purse leaves him little choice.

He's followed out of the flophouse by Brewbarry, an Uthgardt barbarian who is his only paying fan, and joined on the road by Valphine Sortorra, a drow cleric of Lathander whose conversion from Lolth-worship is as pragmatic as it is sincere. A tortle druid named Soma, a guide and follower of Mielikki, leads the group to Phandalin — a frontier town of 40–50 log buildings nestled in the snow-capped Sword Mountains, with no walls and no garrison.

In Phandalin, Vukradin's music earns them a meal at the Stonehill Inn, where proprietor Toblen Stonehill convinces the party to check the quest board. Three jobs await: warning dwarven prospectors about a white dragon (50 gp), retrieving a magic device from Gnomengarde (50 gp), and convincing midwife Adabra Gwynn to return to town (25 gp). Vukradin picks the dwarven quest for its "honest wages."

The party travels to an ancient dwarven temple of Abbathor in a canyon southwest of Phandalin. Dwarven prospectors Dazlyn and Norbus offer sending stones as payment to investigate a disturbance in the temple depths. Inside, the party triggers a fierce orc ambush that nearly kills Vukradin twice and results in Soma's Ice Knife friendly-firing Valphine unconscious — the party's first taste of combat together, which they barely survive.

On the return journey, the party encounters a road ogre. Vukradin, attempting to befriend it as a "fellow musician," discovers ogres hate music and are attacked. Brewbarry tanks with rage. Valphine uses a magical bullet to knock it prone. The ogre breaks morale and flees, but Vukradin kills it with a Starry Wisp from hiding — prompting Soma's first protest against killing a fleeing enemy.

Back in Phandalin, Vukradin collects 50 gp from Townmaster Harbin Wester, argues with him about the ethics of dwarven treasure-hunting (which Vukradin calls grave-robbing), and meets Linene Graywind of the Lionshield Coster, who hints at supply chain disruption. The party sells orc loot and rests at the Stonehill Inn before heading to Gnomengarde.

## Scenes

### Vukradin's Last Gig
- Vukradin plays to a sparse crowd at the First Flophouse in Heapside, Neverwinter; Brewbarry is his only listener.
- Flashback to his Wintershield recruitment: Vukradin argues ethics about stolen treasure; the guard sighs and explains the 60-day holding period.
- Vukradin quits mid-set, declaring he'll make "honest coin" through adventure.

### The Road Party Assembles
- Valphine, a drow Lathander cleric, follows Vukradin out of the flophouse and volunteers to join.
- **(DM note):** Valphine's internal monologue reveals she is simultaneously drawn to Vukradin's celestial nature and viewing his naivety as something to exploit — a tension that defines her early character.
- Soma the tortle druid meets them outside the city limits and offers to guide them to Phandalin.
- The journey is uneventful. Soma notes the Triboar Trail's dangers but keeps rumors of a white dragon to herself.

### Arrival in Phandalin
- The party enters Phandalin: 40–50 log buildings, crumbling ruins, no walls, no garrison.
- At the Stonehill Inn, Toblen Stonehill offers food in exchange for music. Vukradin plays popular songs reluctantly.
- Toblen hints at orc raids as a past threat. Vukradin realizes uncomfortably that Brewbarry's tattoos match the Uthgardt tribes.
- Toblen: "Well, my friend, the wars of the past are the past."
- Harbin's locked-door routine: his first of many dragon-related panics.

### The Quest Board
- Three quests: Dwarven warning (50 gp), Gnomengarde device (50 gp), Umbrage Hill midwife (25 gp).
- Vukradin picks the dwarven quest for honest wages; Valphine wants plunder but hides her disappointment.
- The party learns of a local white dragon rumor — a running threat that colours every future decision.

### Temple of Abbathor — Combat 1 (Orc Ambush)
- **Opening conditions:** The party enters the dwarven temple of Abbathor, an evil dwarven god of greed. Two dwarven prospectors (Dazlyn, Norbus) have claimed part of the ruins. The dungeon is dark (10-ft ceilings, no light source other than what the party brings). Unknown orcs are hidden behind collapsed walls and on bluffs.
- **Round-by-round highlights:** Vukradin attempts parley — walks up to orcs playing music. An orc rushes from the cliff and knocks him unconscious. Soma uses Thorn Whip to drag a cliff-top orc 20 feet down, prone. Valphine is tempted to let Vukradin die but heals him. Brewbarry charges with halberd, cleaving through orcs. Vukradin goes down a second time. Soma's Ice Knife misses the orcs and friendly-fires Valphine, knocking her unconscious. Brewbarry rages and finishes the last two orcs.
- **Key tactical moments:** Vukradin's decision to parley orcs instead of fighting nearly gets him killed twice — his most reckless tactical choice so far. Valphine's hesitation to heal him (she was tempted to let him die) shows her pragmatic drow instincts warring with her new Lathander faith. Soma's Ice Knife friendly-fire establishes a pattern: area-effect spells are dangerous with this party's positioning. Brewbarry is the party's only reliable damage dealer.
- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's orc parley attempt was tactically catastrophic. He had no leverage, no read on the enemies' disposition, and no escape plan. Orcs in this scenario should have a clear motivation (guarding territory) — the GM might telegraph this more clearly in future encounters to distinguish between parley-able and non-parley-able enemies.
- **Enemy behavior:** Orcs attacked from ambush positions (cliffs, collapsed walls). They focus-fired Vukradin (the weakest-looking target), which was tactically sound but nearly ended the fight too quickly. They did not pursue fleeing party members.
- **Spell/ability impact:** Ice Knife (Soma) — effective aoe potential but dangerous with clumped allies; Thorn Whip — excellent repositioning, key moment; Healing spells (Valphine) — essential to keeping the party alive.
- **PC resource decisions:** Valphine chose to heal Vukradin despite temptation to let him die (faith over pragmatism). Soma cast Ice Knife with allies in the blast radius — questionable positioning awareness.
- **Post-combat:** Dwarves cook a fine meal for the party. Brewbarry carries orc axes as loot.

### Road Ogre — Combat 2
- **Opening conditions:** Single ogre encountered on the road back to Phandalin. Clear day, open terrain. The ogre is whistling cheerfully.
- **Round-by-round highlights:** Vukradin asks if it's a "fellow musician." Ogre hates music, charges. Brewbarry tanks with rage. Valphine uses a "magic bullet" (stolen from the first ogre they killed) to knock it prone. Ogre takes damage from Soma's Poison Spray. Brewbarry's halberd lands a devastating blow. Ogre breaks and flees.
- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's second non-combat approach in as many encounters — nearly as disastrous as the orc parley, but more justifiable since he was ignorant of ogre ecology. The ogre's hatred of music is a good character beat but the GM should ensure the party learns this lesson or Vukradin will repeat it.
- **Key tactical moments:** Valphine's use of the ogre-knocking bullet was excellent resource use. Vukradin hides and uses Starry Wisp from stealth to kill the fleeing ogre — effective but controversial.
- **Enemy behavior:** Ogre breaks morale when significantly wounded and tries to flee. This is the party's first encounter with a fleeing enemy, setting up the moral debate about killing retreating foes.
- **PC resource decisions:** Vukradin expended a spell slot on Starry Wisp to kill the fleeing ogre rather than let it escape — driven by roleplay (the ogre hated music) rather than tactical necessity.
- **Post-combat:** Soma protests killing the fleeing ogre. First significant moral disagreement in the party.

### Return to Phandalin
- Vukradin collects 50 gp from Harbin and argues about dwarven grave-robbing ethics.
- At the Lionshield Coster, Linene Graywind notes supply chain disruption — a plot hook about interdimensional trade.
- Vukradin plays at the Stonehill Inn for the evening; party rests before heading to Gnomengarde.

## NPCs
- **Toblen Stonehill** — Human, proprietor of the Stonehill Inn. Native of Triboar. Runs a modest two-story roadhouse. Friendly but cautious. Accepts Vukradin's music as payment for meals. Hinted at orc/white dragon dangers.
- **Harbin Wester** — Townmaster of Phandalin. Tremulous, thin-skinned, hides behind locked doors when frightened. Writes all job board notices in the same hand. Pragmatic about Neverember's adventurer policy. Pays promptly but is easily pressured.
- **Dazlyn Grayshard** — Shield dwarf prospector and business partner of Norbus. Excavating the Abbathor temple ruins for treasure. Offers sending stones as payment. Claims intent to return treasures to "rightful owners."
- **Norbus Ironrune** — Shield dwarf prospector, Dazlyn's partner. Echoes Dazlyn's promises. Both dwarves are more treasure-hungry than they admit.
- **Linene Graywind** — Master of the Phandalin Lionshield Coster outpost. Businesslike. Hints at interdimensional supply chain disruption.
- **Brewbarry** — Uthgardt barbarian. Vukradin's only paying fan. Wears distinct tribal tattoos (Black Lion tribe). Deeply loyal. Covers himself in orc blood during combat without concern. Emerges as the party's primary damage dealer.
- **Valphine Sortorra** — Drow cleric of Lathander. Recent convert from Lolth. Pragmatic, calculating, but genuinely drawn to Lathander's radiant power. Views Vukradin as an experiment in "good" behavior. Cruelty and kindness war within her.
- **Soma** — Tortle druid of Mielikki. Guide from Neverwinter. Old, patient, wise. Troubled by unnecessary killing. Becomes the party's moral anchor.

## Items
- **Sending Stones** — Offered by Dazlyn/Norbus as payment. Valphine convinces Vukradin they can be used for music. Capable of one short message between paired stones.
- **Orc Axes (broken)** — Loot from the Temple of Abbathor. Sold to Lionshield Coster for coin.
- **Ogre-knocking Bullet** — A magical bullet found on the first ogre's body. Used by Valphine to prone the second ogre. GM note: consider what type of magic item this is.

## Spells
- **Thorn Whip** — Soma uses to drag a cliff-top orc 20 feet down to the ground, prone. Excellent repositioning.
- **Ice Knife** — Soma's area-of-effect spell. Knocks Valphine unconscious in friendly fire. Pattern alert: Soma needs better ally-positioning awareness.
- **Starry Wisp** — Vukradin's ranged attack. Used to finish the fleeing ogre from hiding.
- **Poison Spray** — Soma's reliable single-target damage. Effective against ogres.
- **Healing Word/Light** — Valphine. Used to keep Vukradin alive despite temptation to let him die.
