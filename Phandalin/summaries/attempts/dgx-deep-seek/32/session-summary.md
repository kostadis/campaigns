# Chapter 32 — A Grave New Friend and a Glimmering Blade

**Date:** Feb 18th, 2026

---

## Summary

The party pressed deeper into the Dragonbarrow, following a narrow secret passageway in pursuit of the legendary dragon slayer sword. Vukradin, caught up in excitement, rushed ahead through the two-foot-wide tunnel, only to trigger a hidden pressure plate that collapsed the walls inward—burying the entire party except for Soma in dirt and stone. Drawing upon her connection to the earth, Soma carefully extracted her companions and painstakingly excavated the entire blocked passage using the Mould Earth cantrip, clearing the way forward.

As the party emerged from the debris, Soma discovered the bones and rotting saddle of a horse tucked in a southern niche. When she approached, the skeletal remains writhed together and rose as an undead steed. Through magical communication, Soma learned the creature believed it had been reanimated to serve a figure called Lady Alagondar on an important quest. The party cleverly convinced the horse that Soma was Lady Alagondar reincarnated—a claim the loyal creature accepted without question. After some debate, the skeletal horse embraced the name **Boney**, though it lamented the loss of its mane and other features. Boney revealed that it had been brought to the Dragonbarrow to guard something for Lady Alagondar but was slain by malevolent Will-o'-wisps. The horse vaguely recalled that the sword might be buried "across the way" from where Lady Alagondar's remains rested, pointing the party northward.

Sensing magical auras to avoid traps, the party moved cautiously. Brewbarry took the lead but fell into a non-magical pit trap, taking heavy damage, and was immediately set upon by an invisible Will-o'-wisp that lashed him with crackling energy. Vukradin conjured a zone of absolute silence to protect against the wisps while Soma tended to Brewbarry's injuries. Valphine readied herself to turn undead, and when a wisp reappeared, she succeeded in driving it off. In the chaos, Vukradin misinterpreted Boney's directions and charged west instead of north, splitting the party and falling into yet another trap that shattered his concentration—and the silence with it. A second Will-o'-wisp emerged from the shadows.

**(DM note, not yet known to party):** The Will-o'-wisps are not random monsters—they were drawn to the Dragonbarrow by the concentration of death magic and the lingering aura of the dragon's demise. Their presence may foreshadow a deeper corruption in the barrow that has yet to be fully uncovered.

The battle became a chaotic two-front skirmish. In the northern corridor, Brewbarry entered a battle rage, deliberately triggered a pressure plate with his halberd, then leaped over the pit to destroy a wisp with brutal efficiency. Meanwhile, in the sepulcher chamber to the west, Vukradin and Soma cornered their wisp—Soma called down a beam of pale moonlight that incinerated the creature while it was already caught in Vukradin's Cloud of Daggers. Vukradin also recovered a **Necklace of Fireballs** from one sarcophagus and a **Flute of Illusions** from the tomb of a dead bard.

With the Will-o'-wisps vanquished, Valphine carefully navigated the remaining traps and reached the northern chamber. There, among the ancient bones of a dragon, the dragon's skull rested on the floor. Atop this grim pedestal lay their prize: the **Dragon Slayer Sword**, exactly where Boney had suggested it would be—across from where Lady Alagondar had been laid to rest. The party had successfully completed their quest, though questions linger about the sword's history, its connection to Lady Alagondar, and whether it will satisfy their requirements for fair trade.

## Scenes

### Tunnel Collapse and Mould Earth Rescue

*The party enters a narrow secret passageway. Vukradin triggers a hidden pressure plate, collapsing the tunnel walls. Soma uses Mould Earth to extract the trapped party and excavates the entire passage.*

- The party decides to take a secret passageway to the west, with Vukradin eagerly leading the way toward the sword.
- Vukradin, running ahead, steps on a five-foot-long pressure plate hidden in the two-foot-wide tunnel.
- The tunnel walls collapse inward, burying everyone but Soma in dirt and stone.
- Soma uses the Mould Earth cantrip to carefully extract the party members from the debris without harming them.
- Once everyone is free, the party faces a completely blocked tunnel with no forward progress.
- Soma continues using Mould Earth over several minutes, painstakingly excavating the entire collapsed passage.
- The tunnel is cleared, allowing the party to proceed deeper into the Dragonbarrow.

### The Reanimated Skeletal Horse

*After clearing the tunnel, Soma discovers the bones of a horse in a southern niche. They reanimate, and through magical communication the party befriends the skeletal steed, naming him Boney.*

- Soma, leading the way through the excavated tunnel, notices the bones and rotting saddle of a horse in a southern niche.
- As Soma approaches to investigate, the bones writhe together and rise, forming a fully skeletal horse.
- Soma casts Speak with Animals to communicate with the undead creature.
- The horse initially mistakes Soma for "Lady Alagondar," whom it was brought back from death to help on a quest.
- The party quickly works together to convince the horse that Soma is Lady Alagondar reincarnated—a deception the horse accepts without hesitation.
- The horse expresses confusion about missing body parts, particularly its mane and tail, lamenting their loss despite being fully skeletal.
- Boney reveals that it was brought to the dungeon to guard something for Lady Alagondar but was killed by Will-o'-wisps.
- The party debates names for the horse: "Mr. Ed," "Butterscotch," and "Boney" are the top contenders.
- The horse states its original name was "Barney," but the party convinces it to embrace "Boney," which it accepts happily.
- Boney vaguely indicates the sword might be buried "across the way" from its current chamber, pointing north.
- The party discusses having Boney as a potential sidekick, though his combat utility is limited—only 13 hit points.
- **(DM note):** Boney's memories are fragmented. His recollection of Lady Alagondar and the sword's location is reliable as far as it goes, but he does not recall who reanimated him or why he was left to rot in the niche. Someone raised him for a purpose, then abandoned him.

### First Will-o'-Wisp Encounter

*Brewbarry triggers a pit trap and is ambushed by an invisible Will-o'-wisp. The party scrambles to react as Valphine prepares Turn Undead and Vukradin casts Silence.*

- Soma casts Detect Magic as a ritual to help locate the sword and avoid magical traps.
- Brewbarry, taking the lead, falls into a non-magical pit trap, taking 12 points of piercing damage.
- A Will-o'-wisp, previously invisible, attacks Brewbarry with a shock, dealing 9 points of lightning damage, then becomes visible before phasing through the rock wall.
- Boney warns the party that Will-o'-wisps attacked and killed him in his previous life, describing them as evil and not talkative.
- Vukradin casts Silence in the corridor as a prophylactic measure, creating a 24-foot radius zone where no sound can pass.
- Valphine heals Brewbarry for 25 hit points, bringing him back from the brink.
- Valphine readies an action to use Turn Undead when a Will-o'-wisp reappears.
- When the wisp reappears in the chamber with Boney, Valphine successfully uses Turn Undead, causing it to fail its Wisdom save and flee in terror.
- **(DM note):** Turn Undead causes the wisp to flee for up to 1 minute, but it can still move through walls and may return once the effect ends.
- With the wisp fled, Vukradin sees the path open and sprints toward where he believes the sword lies.
- Soma dashes after Vukradin to provide backup.

### The Party Splits

*Vukradin misinterpret's Boney's directions and charges west instead of north. Brewbarry sighs and moves north. The party splits into two groups.*

- With the Will-o'-wisp turned, Vukradin runs to get the sword—but turns west at the intersection.
- Brewbarry shouts "North!" but the Silence spell prevents any sound from reaching Vukradin.
- Brewbarry sighs in frustration and moves north toward the actual location.
- Valphine follows Brewbarry north.
- Soma hears the commotion and realizes the bard is in danger, racing after him westward.
- **Party split:** Vukradin and Soma (west/southwest sepulcher), Brewbarry and Valphine (north/dragon chamber).

### Vukradin and Soma: Sepulcher Chamber Battle

*Vukradin rushes into a tomb chamber, falls into a pit trap, and is attacked by a Will-o'-wisp. Soma arrives to help, and they destroy the creature together while looting magical treasures.*

- Vukradin rushes past the entrance of the room where Soma's rat died earlier in the dungeon.
- Vukradin falls into a pit trap, taking 19 points of damage—losing concentration on the Silence spell.
- A Will-o'-wisp appears from the shadows and attacks Vukradin, dealing 9 points of lightning damage.
- Vukradin uses a reaction to attack the wisp as it flees, hitting with a 21 and dealing 6 damage.
- Soma hears the sounds of combat and realizes Vukradin is in danger, racing after him.
- The Will-o'-wisp phases into a sarcophagus to hide.
- Vukradin opens the sarcophagus, discovers the wisp inside, and the creature attacks—rolling a natural 1 and missing.
- Vukradin casts Cloud of Daggers on the wisp, rolling four ones on 4d4 and dealing a meager 4 slashing damage.
- Vukradin retrieves a Necklace of Fireballs (seven beads) from the southwestern sarcophagus.
- Vukradin then runs to the northwest corner and picks up a magical Flute of Illusions from the sarcophagus of a dead bard.
- Soma casts Moonbeam on the same Will-o'-wisp caught in Cloud of Daggers, dealing 12 radiant damage and destroying it.
- **(DM note):** The wisp's destruction was a near thing—had Soma's Moonbeam not finished it, the creature would have escaped through the walls to regroup with any remaining wisps.

### Brewbarry and Valphine: Northern Corridor Battle

*Brewbarry moves north, triggers a trap intentionally, and destroys a Will-o'-wisp in a rage. Valphine follows, heading for the dragon chamber.*

- Brewbarry moves north past the intersection and arrives at an archway leading to a tomb.
- Brewbarry notices an odd light source in the archway—the same magical light that killed Soma's rat earlier.
- Brewbarry uses Thaumaturgy to brighten the light, discovering it is a magical spell effect, not a Will-o'-wisp.
- Brewbarry intentionally triggers a pressure plate trap using his halberd, safely springing the trap.
- When Valphine notes Brewbarry's increased intelligence, he shouts, "It's the RED EYES!" (referencing his rage state).
- Brewbarry approaches the pit, sees the Will-o'-wisp, enters a rage, and attacks.
- Brewbarry hits the wisp with a 23, dealing 10 points of damage.
- Brewbarry makes a second attack while raging, destroying the Will-o'-wisp outright.
- Valphine rushes toward Brewbarry, ready to proceed to the northern chamber.

### Discovery of the Dragon Slayer Sword

*Valphine reaches the northern area and discovers the Dragon Slayer Sword resting atop an ancient dragon's skull, completing the party's quest.*

- Valphine moves to the northern area, passing Brewbarry who has just killed the final Will-o'-wisp.
- Valphine jumps over the pit trap and enters a chamber containing a pair of sarcophagi.
- **(DM note):** The sarcophagi in this chamber remain unopened. They may contain additional treasure—or threats—that the party has not yet explored.
- The area contains the scattered bones of an ancient dragon, with its massive skull resting on the floor like a pedestal.
- A long sword gleams atop the dragon's skull—the Dragon Slayer Sword, their quarry.
- The session ends with the party having successfully located the Dragon Slayer Sword, along with the Flute of Illusions and Necklace of Fireballs.
- **(DM note):** The sword's placement atop the dragon skull is not merely decorative. Whoever interred it here intended it as a symbolic statement—the slayer laid to rest above the slain. Whether this was done in reverence or as a warning remains unclear.

## NPCs

- **Boney** — A skeletal horse that rises from bones found in a tunnel niche. Once a living horse named Barney, it was reanimated to serve Lady Alagondar on a quest. Sentient and able to communicate via Speak with Animals, Boney describes being killed by Will-o'-wisps after being brought to the Dragonbarrow. The horse happily accepts the name "Boney" and believes Soma to be Lady Alagondar reincarnated. Boney has only 13 hit points and limited combat utility, but serves as a loyal companion and source of information about the dungeon. **(DM note):** Boney's reanimation raises questions—who raised him, and why was he left in the niche instead of stationed as a proper guardian? Someone with necromantic power passed through the Dragonbarrow before the party.

- **Lady Alagondar** — A figure from the past whom the skeletal horse Boney was reanimated to serve. Boney believes Lady Alagondar was buried "across the way" from where the sword was left. Her identity, her connection to the Dragon Slayer Sword, and the nature of her "quest" remain mysterious. **(DM note, not yet known to party):** Lady Alagondar may be a historical figure from the region's past—possibly a dragon hunter, noble, or paladin. The party may encounter her name again if they research local history in Phandalin or Neverwinter.

## Items

- **Dragon Slayer Sword** — A long sword found resting atop a dragon skull in the northern chamber. It is the object of the party's quest and was buried "across" from Lady Alagondar's resting place. **(DM note):** As a Dragon Slayer weapon, this sword likely deals bonus damage against dragons and may have additional properties revealed upon identification. Its history with Lady Alagondar and how it ended up in the Dragonbarrow are questions the party may pursue.

- **Necklace of Fireballs** — A magical necklace found in a southwestern sarcophagus. It contains seven beads that can be thrown individually or all at once to cast the Fireball spell. All beads together would deal 8d6 plus 1d6 per additional bead beyond the first. Found by Vukradin. **(DM note):** The necklace was sealed in a sarcophagus with a dead wizard—the wizard may have died in a fire-related accident, or the necklace may have been a prized possession buried with them.

- **Flute of Illusions** — A magical flute found with the remains of a dead bard in a northwestern sarcophagus. Its properties are not yet fully identified. Found by Vukradin. **(DM note):** The Flute of Illusions likely allows the user to create illusory effects when played. The dead bard may have been the flute's original owner, buried with their instrument as a mark of respect—or the flute may be cursed.

## Spells

- **Mould Earth** (Cantrip) — Used by Soma to clear collapsed tunnel walls after the pressure plate trap. Soma extracted each trapped party member and then excavated the entire blocked passage to allow the party to proceed. *Effectiveness note:* Excellent in this utility context—a perfect cantrip choice for the situation, though it required several minutes of repeated casting.

- **Speak with Animals** (1st-level) — Cast by Soma as a ritual to communicate with Boney. Enabled the party to learn about Lady Alagondar, the Will-o'-wisps, and the sword's location. *Effectiveness note:* Critical for the encounter—without it, the party would have had no way to gain Boney's trust or knowledge.

- **Detect Magic** (1st-level ritual) — Cast by Soma as a ritual to detect magical auras within 30 feet for 10 minutes. Used to help locate the sword and avoid magical traps. *Effectiveness note:* Required concentration, which was a limiting factor during combat. The non-magical pit traps evaded its detection entirely.

- **Silence** (2nd-level) — Cast by Vukradin in the corridor as a prophylactic measure against the Will-o'-wisps. Created a 24-foot radius sphere of absolute silence. *Effectiveness note:* Strategically sound but fragile—lost when Vukradin fell into a pit trap and failed the concentration save. Had the added drawback of preventing party members from calling out to each other, contributing to the party split.

- **Turn Undead** (Channel Divinity) — Used by Valphine as a readied action against a Will-o'-wisp. The wisp failed its Wisdom save and fled as far away as possible. *Effectiveness note:* Highly effective at creating a window of safety, but the wisp's ability to phase through walls meant it could potentially return once the effect ended. Turned undead are not destroyed—only delayed.

- **Thaumaturgy** (Cantrip) — Used by Brewbarry to manipulate a magical light source, making it brighter to determine if it was a Will-o'-wisp or a magical effect. *Effectiveness note:* Revealed the light was a magical spell effect rather than a creature—a smart use of a utility cantrip to gather information.

- **Cloud of Daggers** (2nd-level) — Cast by Vukradin on a Will-o'-wisp hiding in a sarcophagus. Created a swirling cloud of magical daggers in a 5-foot cube. *Effectiveness note:* Devastatingly unlucky—rolled four ones on 4d4, dealing only 4 slashing damage. Thematically appropriate but mechanically underwhelming in this instance.

- **Moonbeam** (2nd-level) — Cast by Soma on a Will-o'-wisp already caught in Cloud of Daggers. Created a beam of pale moonlight that dealt 12 radiant damage and destroyed the wisp. The spell can be moved as a bonus action on subsequent turns. *Effectiveness note:* Perfect layering of effects with Cloud of Daggers—the radiant damage was particularly effective against the undead wisp.
