# Chapter 33 Silencing the Siren's Warning

**Date:** Feb 18th, 2026

## Summary

Deep within the northern chamber of the crypt, surrounded by the ancient bones of a dragon, Valphine claimed the Dragon Slayer Sword from its grim resting place atop a dragon skull — exactly where Boney had said it would be. The party gathered around the glowing blade, its presence humming with latent power, debating who among them should carry it into the battles ahead. Boney, the sentient bone horse bonded to Valphine, watched with quiet amusement and remarked that irregularity was no longer a problem for him in his partially-alive state. Brewbarry seemed the natural choice to wield the legendary weapon, and the decision was nearly made. But the crypt had not yet surrendered its guardian.

From the shadows, an invisible stalker erupted with brutal force, slamming into Valphine as she held the blade. The creature was a summoned elemental — the kind wizards dispatch to guard precious treasures or eliminate those who dare steal them. The battle that followed was chaotic and blind. Vukradin rushed from the adjacent chamber at the sound of combat, while Soma called down a beam of pale moonlight that tore through the stalker's shimmering form, revealing it to the party. Valphine dropped the sword in a desperate gambit, hoping the stalker would cease its aggression — and to her surprise, it did. The elemental hovered directly over the blade, daring the party to try and take it. It was a fatal mistake. Between Soma's relentless moonbeam and Vukradin's cloud of magical daggers, the stalker was carved from existence, and Brewbarry reached down to claim the Dragon Slayer Sword once more.

As the party caught their breath and admired their prize, a ghost materialized in the chamber — the sorrowful specter of Lady Alagondar. She did not speak. She simply stared with hollow, mournful eyes toward the Icespire Mountains before fading into nothing. The omen was clear: the dragon was their destination, and time was running short. But before the party could plan their next move, the campaign took a sudden and desperate turn.

Outside, Sister Kaella stumbled into the clearing — cloak gone, armor slashed, gasping for breath. She fell to her knees and raised her empty hands toward the glowing sword, delivering a dire warning: the Carver was no longer a man but a hollow shell, speaking to his blade in a tongue that made ears bleed. He was marching on Icespire Hold that very night to bind the white worm. If they did not stop him before he completed his dark chant, he would wear the dragon like a suit of armor — and then burn Phandalin to the ground just to test his terrible new power.

Vukradin, who had long viewed Sister Kaella as a threat and an agent of the Carver, moved with quiet intent. Before she could say another word, he paralyzed her with a powerful enchantment, silencing her mid-warning. Soma and Valphine persuaded him to hold back from killing her and struck her with non-lethal blows until she crumpled unconscious. Only then did the party realize their mistake: Sister Kaella was the only one among them who knew the location of Icespire Hold.

They had little time to dwell on the error. From the tree line emerged Jax — the last of the Carver's agents — flanked by thugs and a hulking Rot-Tusk Ogre. A net pinned Vukradin in place, and Valphine was blinded by a flash of dark magic. But even restrained, Vukradin was far from helpless. He reached out with his magic and seized both Jax and the ogre in a paralyzing hold, leaving them frozen and exposed. Soma shifted into a great bear and tore into Jax, while Valphine summoned a radiant aura of spectral guardians that burned through their enemies like holy fire. Brewbarry raged through the battlefield, delivering crushing blows to the paralyzed ogre. Surrounded by thugs who attacked him to break his concentration, Vukradin unleashed a devastating cone of magical force that dropped several of them in a single blazing instant. When the dust settled, Jax and the ogre lay defeated, the remaining thugs scattered or slain, and Sister Kaella lay unconscious — her warning half-spoken, her knowledge of Icespire Hold locked away behind closed eyes.

## Scenes

### Aftermath of the Dragon Slayer Sword Discovery

The party stands in the crypt's northern chamber, the Dragon Slayer Sword in hand, debating who should wield it. Boney's humor lightens the mood despite the solemn setting of ancient dragon bones.

### Valphine Claims the Sword

Valphine steps forward and retrieves the Dragon Slayer Sword from atop a dragon skull, the blade humming with power as it leaves its resting place.

### Invisible Stalker Ambush

As the party prepares to leave, an invisible stalker launches a surprise attack on Valphine. The creature was summoned to guard the sword and attacks with vicious intent. Valphine is struck twice before the party can react.

### Moonbeam Reveals the Stalker

Soma calls down Moonbeam onto the stalker's position, dealing severe radiant damage and outlining its shimmering form. Valphine follows up with Faerie Fire to outline the stalker in light, making it easier to target. The stalker is now visible.

### Cloud of Daggers Finishes It

Vukradin moves Cloud of Daggers onto the stalker and, combined with Soma's Moonbeam, grinds the elemental down. The stalker dissolves into nothing. Brewbarry claims the Dragon Slayer Sword.

### Lady Alagondar's Ghost

After the party takes a short rest, Lady Alagondar's ghost materializes. She silently stares toward the Icespire Mountains — a clear sign pointing toward the white dragon's lair — then fades away.

### Sister Kaella's Warning

Wounded and desperate, Sister Kaella stumbles into camp. She warns that the Carver is marching on Icespire Hold to bind the white worm, that if he succeeds he will wear the dragon like armor and burn Phandalin. Her plea is urgent and sincere.

### Vukradin Betrays Sister Kaella

Vukradin, suspicious of Kaella all along, casts Hold Person on her before she can finish her warning. The party subdues her with non-lethal blows, knocking her unconscious — and trapping her vital knowledge of Icespire Hold's location.

### Jax and the Rot-Tusk Ogre Ambush

Jax, flanked by thugs and a Rot-Tusk Ogre, ambushes the party immediately after Kaella falls. A net restrains Vukradin; Valphine is blinded by dark magic. But Vukradin holds Jax and the ogre with a single Hold Person.

### Party Defeats Jax's Crew

Soma (as a bear) mauls Jax while Valphine's Spirit Guardians and Brewbarry's attacks finish the ogre. Vukradin, surrounded by thugs, unleashes Array of Cards in a cone to drop them. One thug flees; the party stands victorious but uncertain of their next move.

## NPCs

- **Boney** — A sentient bone horse bonded to Valphine. Partially alive ("unalived"), he provides comic relief and is now able to join the party in combat.
- **Invisible Stalker** — A summoned elemental guardian, deployed by a wizard to protect the Dragon Slayer Sword. It ambushed the party but was revealed by Soma's Moonbeam and destroyed by Cloud of Daggers.
- **Lady Alagondar's Ghost** — A spectral figure that appeared after the stalker's defeat. She silently pointed toward the Icespire Mountains, confirming the dragon's location, then vanished.
- **Sister Kaella** — An agent of the Carver who arrived wounded and desperate to warn the party. She was paralyzed and knocked unconscious by the party before she could reveal Icespire Hold's location.
- **Xanth** — A wounded, bloodied centaur present outside the chamber. Had previously offered to guide the party to half-orc spellcasters.
- **The Carver** — The primary antagonist. Now a hollow shell speaking in a blasphemous tongue to his sword, he is marching on Icespire Hold to bind the white dragon.
- **Jax** — The last of the Carver's agents. Ambushed the party after Kaella fell and was defeated alongside his crew.
- **Rot-Tusk Ogre** — A large, brutish ogre from the clan the party previously clashed with in the forest. Paralyzed by Vukradin and slain by the party.
- **Thugs** — Several henchmen under Jax's command. One survived and fled after the party's victory.

## Items

- **Dragon Slayer Sword** — A +1 sword found atop a dragon skull in the crypt. Possesses the Dragon Slayer property, dealing bonus damage to dragons. Now wielded by Brewbarry.

## Spells

- **Cloud of Daggers** — Vukradin's concentration spell, a swirling mass of slashing blades. Moved onto the Invisible Stalker to deal consistent damage.
- **Moonbeam** — Soma's concentration spell creating a beam of pale moonlight. Revealed the Invisible Stalker and dealt radiant damage throughout the fight.
- **Faerie Fire** — Valphine cast this to outline the Invisible Stalker in light, making it easier for the party to target the creature.
- **Hold Person** — Vukradin cast this to paralyze Sister Kaella mid-warning, and again to simultaneously immobilize Jax and the Rot-Tusk Ogre, enabling automatic critical hits.
- **Spirit Guardians** — Valphine's concentration spell summoning spectral guardians in a 15-foot radius. Incapacitated Jax and the ogre while dealing radiant damage.
- **Silvery Barbs** — Vukradin used this reaction spell to force a thug to re-roll a successful attack.
- **Array of Cards** — Vukradin's signature area spell, unleashed in a 15-foot cone from his magical deck while restrained by a net, dropping multiple thugs instantly.
