# Chapter 28 When the Machine Screams: A Family Affair

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The standoff with Aletra continues in the machine room. Interplanar spider webs cover every surface. Six Rift Weaver spiders — bound to the ceiling, crackling with static electricity — guard the malfunctioning device. Aletra warns the party that the machine is in "spatial equilibrium" and that any disruption could trigger catastrophic failure.

As the party prepares to engage, Aletra manipulates the situation: she points her wand at Brewbarry, causing him to glow red. The machine detects him as a "spatial anomaly" — all six Rift Weavers immediately target him. Aletra has perfectly positioned the party as bait while she works the machine.

The Rift Weavers web Brewbarry, restraining him, and bite. Valphine casts protective magic. But Soma's **Flaming Sphere** proves crucial — the Rift Weavers are deeply disturbed by magical fire. It burns through webs, damages the creatures, and prevents re-webbing. Valphine's radiant energy also causes recoil — the spiders are vulnerable to both fire and radiant.

Aletra works the machine, exchanging barbs with Valphine throughout. She references childhood traps they set together, their mother's opinions, and cutting remarks about Valphine's intelligence. The sisters' relationship is revealed as deeply dysfunctional — competitive, condescending, and scarred.

Valphine attempts Hold Person on Aletra — resisted. She attempts Counterspell — it may or may not succeed. The magic of the machine room interferes with conventional spellcasting.

The party destroys the spiders one by one. The creatures become confused, torn between attacking Brewbarry and the machine itself, which begins warning of catastrophic failure. Aletra completes her extraction: a box containing vials of interdimensional poison — part of House Xorlarrin's illegal drug trade.

Aletra opens a shimmering portal to escape. Valphine strikes her with a blinding curse — Aletra steps through despite the blindness, but fumbles and drops several vials. Her final words acknowledge Valphine "at least tried" to stop her — better than "simpering and crying" like the last time.

With Aletra gone and the machine screaming, Valphine enters the shutdown code. The machine collapses in on itself, shrinking and disappearing into the void. It leaves behind a single piece of paper: a bill from "Interplanar Trade Consultants Incorporated." The remaining Rift Weaver, having nothing left to guard, winks out of existence.

## Memorable Moments

> "It'll be just like old times! Remember that trap we set with the stuffed creature as bait? Just keep him alive and I'll do the rest!"
> — Aletra, to Valphine, as Brewbarry glows red

**Family Bonding.** Aletra's nostalgic reference to childhood traps — she and Valphine setting stuffed-creature snares together — adds a layer of tragic familiarity to their current conflict.

> "Mother always said you weren't the sharpest tool in the shed. Though you DID always enjoy being the target of pointy tools."
> — Aletra, to Valphine

**Sibling Banter.** Even mid-combat, Aletra finds time for cutting remarks about their mother's opinion of Valphine. The Sartorra family dynamics are brutal.

> "I can't leave any evidence behind, unfortunately."
> — Aletra, considering whether to collect Valphine's body parts

**Body Parts Policy.** Aletra's offhand admission that she would "hate to bring Valphine's body parts back to mother" is delivered with casual menace. Family reunions must be interesting.

> "You at least TRIED to stop me. That's better than simpering and crying like the last time. Though I can't remember if that was pain or pleasure..."
> — Aletra, escaping through the portal

**Parting Shot.** Aletra's final words are calculated to wound: acknowledging Valphine's effort while implying past victimization. The ambiguity of "pain or pleasure" is deliberately cruel.

> "INTERPLANAR TRADE CONSULTANTS INCORPORATED — payment for services rendered."
> — The bill left behind

**The Invoice.** When the machine collapses, all that remains is a bill. Capitalism survives planar collapse.

## Scenes

### COMBAT — Rift Weaver Spiders
- **Opening conditions:** Room covered in interplanar webs. Six Rift Weavers on ceiling. Aletra at the machine. Brewbarry marked as spatial anomaly (glowing red). Terrain: webs difficult terrain, machine as central obstacle.
- **Opening maneuver:** Aletra points wand at Brewbarry — he glows red. Machine designates him "spatial anomaly." All Rift Weavers target him.
- Aletra has manipulated the party into being her decoy while she works safely.
- Rift Weavers web Brewbarry — he becomes restrained.
- Rift Weavers bite Brewbarry — he takes damage.
- Valphine casts protective magic on Brewbarry — boosts his resilience.
- **Soma's Flaming Sphere:** Summoned and rolled through enemy ranks. BURNS WEBS — frees Brewbarry. Damages Rift Weavers. CRUCIAL — the spiders are DEEPLY DISTURBED by magical fire. Prevents further re-webbing.
- Valphine strikes a Rift Weaver with radiant energy — it recoils violently. Vulnerability to both fire and radiant confirmed.
- Aletra works the machine, cursing dwarven engineers.
- Sibling banter continues throughout combat.
- Brewbarry and Vukradin, freed from webs, cut through the spiders with brutal precision.
- Spiders become confused — torn between attacking Brewbarry and the screaming machine.
- One by one, the Rift Weavers dissolve into crackling sparkles.
- **Post-combat:** Aletra completes extraction — box of Midnights Tears vials retrieved.
- **(DM tactical note):** Flaming Sphere was the MVP of this fight. Without it, the Rift Weavers could have endlessly re-webbed Brewbarry, keeping him locked down while they focused fire. Soma's choice to maintain concentration on Flaming Sphere instead of Wild Shape was tactically correct.
- **(DM tactical note):** The Rift Weavers' dual vulnerability (fire + radiant) suggests they are extraplanar creatures with specific weaknesses. Soma and Valphine covered both types between them.
- **(DM tactical note):** Aletra's use of Brewbarry as bait was clever tactical manipulation — she redirected the party's "tank" into becoming the enemies' focus without warning him first.

### Aletra's Escape
- Aletra presses a device — shimmering portal opens before her.
- Valphine casts a blinding curse — Aletra is blinded but steps through the portal anyway.
- Blinded, Aletra fumbles and drops several poison vials.
- Aletra's final words: acknowledgment that Valphine at least tried.
- She disappears through the portal.
- **(DM note):** Valphine's spell was partially successful — Aletra escaped but dropped cargo. The "boss" may not be happy.

### Machine Shutdown
- Machine screams warnings of catastrophic failure.
- Valphine rushes to the keypad, enters the shutdown code.
- Machine ceases countdown, folds in on itself, shrinks.
- Disappears into the void.
- Only trace: a bill from "Interplanar Trade Consultants Incorporated."
- Remaining Rift Weaver — nothing left to guard — winks out of existence.
- Room falls silent. Spatial anomalies resolved.
- Dropped vials of poison remain.

**(DM note):** The machine's destruction resolves the planar corruption of Axeholm. The "Interplanar Trade Consultants" bill suggests this was a formal business arrangement, not a rogue operation. The larger organization may or may not follow up.

## NPCs

- **Aletra (Xorlarrin/Satora)** — Valphine's sister. Drow poison smuggler. 4th daughter of noble house. Manipulative, condescending, ruthless. Escaped through portal with majority of her cargo. References "the boss" who may be displeased. **(GM note):** Aletra escapes as a recurring villain/antagonist. Her "boss" remains unnamed but clearly oversees interdimensional smuggling. Her dropped vials weaken her position with them.

- **Rift Weaver Spiders** — Six guardian constructs of flickering static. Bound to the machine. Vulnerable to fire and radiant damage. Dissolve into crackling sparkles on death. Web attack restrains targets. Disturbed by magical fire. **(GM note):** These appear to be summoned guardians tied to the planar device, not natural creatures.

## Items

- **Midnight Tears Vials (4 recovered, several more dropped)** — Drow poison from House Xorlarrin's operations. Each vial worth ~500 gp. Part of Aletra's interdimensional smuggling cargo. Dropped vials are now party inventory. **(GM note):** This is highly illegal contraband. Selling it could attract attention from the wrong people.

- **Interplanar Trade Consultants Bill** — Left behind after machine imploded. Payment for services rendered. **(GM note):** A breadcrumb to a larger interdimensional organization. May be traceable.

## Spells

- **Flaming Sphere** — Soma. MVP of the fight. Burned webs, damaged Rift Weavers, prevented re-webbing. Exploited fire vulnerability.
- **Protective Magic (Shield of Faith or similar)** — Valphine. Boosted Brewbarry's AC/resilience against focused spider attacks.
- **Radiant Energy Attack** — Valphine. Revealed Rift Weaver radiant vulnerability.
- **Hold Person** — Valphine. Attempted on Aletra — resisted. Possible magic resistance from the machine room.
- **Counterspell** — Valphine (attempted). May have been used during escape sequence.
- **Blinding Curse** — Valphine. Partially successful — blinded Aletra but didn't prevent portal escape. Dropped vials are a consolation prize.
