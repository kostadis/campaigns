# Chapter 14 The Butterskull Quest

Date: 1-02 Taraskh 1495 — 4-02 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

After the philosophical turmoil of the Whispering Wood, the party turns to a more straightforward problem: a quest posted by Harbin Wester seeking aid at Butterskull Ranch, five miles east of Conyberry along the Triboar Trail. Orcs have attacked the ranch. The owner, Alfonse "Big Al" Kalazorn, a retired sheriff, may be dead or alive. Either way, the reward is 100 GP — proof of death or rescue.

The party departs Phandalin and heads east. On the road, they encounter a merchant traveling from Conyberry. He describes the town as "ransacked and a wreck," destroyed years ago, now "utterly looted." Vukradin immediately sees a new quest: "We must go there and quickly secure the property." The merchant stares in disbelief: "That's very hard." Vukradin, with theatrical flourish: "Just because something is hard doesn't mean we don't do it!" The merchant is baffled.

The party passes through Conyberry's ghost town. The skeletal remains of homes, collapsed roofs, blackened beams, and overgrown streets confirm the merchant's account. Valphine, accustomed to Underdark devastation, finds the scene unremarkable.

They turn a corner and spot four brigands — members of Carver's gang, according to Corbin. Vukradin wants to parley. Corbin warns that ex-gang members don't get warm reunions. Vukradin insists Corbin approach.

The chapter ends on this cliffhanger — the parley has not yet begun — setting up chapter 15's combat encounter.

## Memorable Moments

> "We must go there and quickly secure the property."
> — Vukradin, on Conyberry

**The Vukradin Expansion.** Vukradin sees every abandoned settlement as a real estate opportunity. His impulse to "secure" Conyberry is pure frontier capitalist energy mixed with his obsessive need for "clean" acquisitions.

> "The place was destroyed years ago, and the town is utterly looted. There is nothing to secure."
> — The merchant, trying to burst Vukradin's bubble

**The Voice of Reason.** The merchant's incredulous response is every sensible person reacting to Vukradin's ambition. It doesn't work — Vukradin simply doubles down.

> "That's very hard."
> — The merchant, on repatriating looted goods

**A Masterpiece of Understatement.** The merchant's deadpan assessment of Vukradin's "find the rightful owners" plan is the chapter's funniest line. It perfectly captures how absurd Vukradin's moral project looks to normal people.

> "Just because something is hard, doesn't mean we don't do it!"
> — Vukradin

**Unshakeable Conviction.** Vukradin's theatrical declaration is genuinely admirable — even if the merchant thinks he's mad. This is the same man who argued about "blood money" from a harpy nest, and he's not backing down.

> "You don't exactly quit a gang without there being consequences."
> — Corbin, on why approaching former gang members is a bad idea

**Hard Truths.** Corbin's practical warning about gang dynamics shows he's no fool — he knows exactly what kind of trouble the party is walking into. Vukradin overrules him anyway.

## Scenes

### Departure and Quest Review
- Party reviews the Butterskull Ranch quest: 100 GP reward, orcs, missing owner.
- Valphine notes the loophole: if Big Al is dead, they just need proof of death.
- The party resolves to avoid operational security failures this time — no public announcements.
- Depart Phandalin heading east on the Triboar Trail.

**(DM note):** The party's decision to keep this mission quiet is a direct consequence of learning about the "betting pool" on the lighthouse mission. They're learning operational security, slowly.

### The Merchant Encounter
- Party encounters a merchant on the road, traveling from Conyberry.
- He describes the town's destruction years ago by barbarian raids.
- Lord Neverember promised to rebuild but hasn't.
- Vukradin's quest-pivot: "We must go there and quickly secure the property."
- The merchant tries and fails to explain why this is impractical.
- Vukradin declares his intent to repatriate all looted items to next of kin.
- The merchant: "That's very hard." Vukradin: "Just because something is hard..."
- The merchant gives up and moves on.

**(DM note):** This encounter establishes Conyberry as a location of potential side quests if Vukradin follows through on his repatriation plans. The merchant is a one-off NPC; his information about Conyberry's destruction is accurate.

### Crossing Conyberry
- The party passes through the ghost town. Three days later (4-02 Taraskh).
- Skeletal remains of homes, collapsed roofs, blackened beams.
- Thorny vines and tenacious weeds overgrow the streets.
- An unsettling silence broken only by wind through hollowed-out homes.
- Valphine notes: this is nothing compared to what happens to noble houses marked for extinction in Menzoberranzan.
- They spot four brigands — Carver's gang members, per Corbin's identification.

**(DM note):** Conyberry's destruction was part of the same Uthgardt barbarian raids mentioned in the campaign's prologue lore. The town was destroyed during the War of the Giants aftermath.

### Approaching the Brigands
- Vukradin wants to parley. Corbin warns it's dangerous.
- Corbin: "You don't exactly quit a gang without there being consequences."
- Vukradin overrules him and insists Corbin approach his former gang mates.
- The chapter ends on a cliffhanger — the parley attempt begins.

**(DM note):** Vukradin's insistence on parley rather than combat is consistent with his character — he wants to avoid killing if possible, especially when information is more valuable than corpses. Corbin's fear suggests the Carver's gang is not forgiving of deserters.

## NPCs

- **The Merchant (unnamed)** — Traveling from Conyberry. Friendly, annoyed by the journey. Provides information about the town's destruction. Baffled by Vukradin's moral crusade.

- **Corbin** — Former Carver gang member, now party aide. Warns the party that approaching his former gang mates is dangerous. Correctly identifies the four brigands as Carver's crew.

- **Carver's Brigands (x4)** — Members of the Carver's gang, spotted in Conyberry. Presumably hostile. Their presence in the ghost town suggests Carver uses it as a waypoint or lookout point.
