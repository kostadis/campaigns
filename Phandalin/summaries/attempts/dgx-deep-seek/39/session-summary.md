# Chapter 39 — The Charge of the Light Brigade

**Date:** Feb 21st, 2026

## Summary

The party returns to Phandalin fresh from the Druid Enclave, where Vukradin's strategic withdrawal turned a hostile hearing into a victory. Before they can even settle into the Stonehill Inn, Lyra bursts in and celebrates Vukradin's tactics in front of the whole common room: by walking out at precisely the right moment, he focused all of Adabra's rage on himself, rendering her arguments irrelevant and enabling Soma to step in and win the day. The inn erupts in warmth, and Lyra orders two beers for Brewbarry by name — a small gesture that lands heavily on the big barbarian, who has never been bought a drink by his own tribe.

That evening, the party weighs their next quest. With Corbin's death fresh in their minds, Soma renames their purpose from "revenge" to "avenging Corbin," and the party agrees. Among the options — the Woodland Manse, Falcon's Hunting Lodge, the Shrine of Savras, and the lair of the half-orc Talosian spellcasters — they choose the Circle of Thunder, guided by Xanth the centaur. Jenna Roscoe of the Lord's Alliance makes a recruitment pitch to Vukradin, who smoothly redirects her toward Valphine's church. Brewbarry punctuates the moment with a legendary burp that ends the conversation entirely.

The journey into the forest brings unexpected encounters. They meet young druids confronting a wildcat logging operation run by Tibor Wester, Harbin's brother. Vukradin delivers an impassioned speech about sanctity, balance, and honest work that leaves the loggers weeping and the druids silent. Brewbarry is moved to tears himself, thinking about every tree he has ever pulled from the ground. The loggers set down their axes and promise to find honest work.

Xanth shares lore around the campfire: Talos is a god of destruction, not a being in a specific place but a condition in the hearts of those who seek to destroy. The cult is like a disease — it explodes, destroys everything, and then consumes itself. He warns that where there is a cult, there is always a Chosen — a cleric who believes Talos speaks through them. The party files this carefully.

They come upon a cursed clearing centered on a forty-foot blackened pine. At its base hang fetish dolls of twigs and black hair, each tied to a still-beating pig's heart. Despite Brewbarry's warnings, Vukradin destroys them one by one. The clearing erupts: roots seize Soma and Boney, forcing Soma to burn a wild shape into a giant elk to break free. Vukradin presses on, and when the last heart is destroyed, the wrongness drains from the clearing like pressure off a wound — ten people, he estimates, saved from whatever curse those dolls were feeding.

The ridge encounter that follows is the party's first direct engagement with the Talosian cult. Across a gully, ten orcs advance behind a Talosian cleric with elongated claws. Vukradin opens with Universal Speech, proselytizing Lathander to the enemy ranks. The cleric is enraged, but three of the orcs slow — an idea has entered minds that weren't expecting one. Valphine casts Daylight from her holy symbol, and sixty feet of Lathander's radiance floods the ridge. The cleric shrieks, screaming for his forces to kill the "drow cleric of Lathander, the abomination." Soma calmly corrects him: "She's not false, she's the real deal." The party resolves to focus attacks on the leader alone and attempt to convert the rest.

## Scenes

### Vukradin mails Adabra 6 gold
Vukradin sits down after the Enclave hearing, counts out six gold pieces (five plus one interest), addresses an envelope, and drops it in the mail. When Brewbarry asks why six, Vukradin explains: "Now that she's lost, she can have her money back." It is a petty, precise, and undeniably effective closing of accounts — the kind of ledger-keeping Valphine quietly recognizes as more drow than Vukradin would ever admit.

### Return to Stonehill Inn
The party arrives back in Phandalin with Corbin's name hanging unspoken between them. Ser Kaelen greets them, noting Soma's staff. Lyra bursts in, incandescent, and publicly credits Vukradin's strategy at the Enclave. She orders two beers for Brewbarry. The evening includes a modest performance, Vukradin raising the quest options, and Soma redefining their mission from "revenge" to "avenging Corbin."

### Quest planning — Choosing the Circle of Thunder
The party weighs three orc-related quests. Vukradin notes the Woodland Manse (burned, occupied), Falcon's Hunting Lodge (overrun), and the half-orc Talosian spellcaster lair (Xanth's offer). Despite Falcon's lodge having personal significance (the studio wood), Vukradin points out Falcon has not yet apologized to him personally. The party chooses the Circle of Thunder. They resupply, discuss lightning resistance strategies, and Vukradin uses Sending to contact Xanth.

### Brewbarry's burp ends Jenna's pitch
Jenna Roscoe approaches Vukradin with the Lord's Alliance pitch — resources, prestige, help finding Sister Kaella. Vukradin smoothly redirects her toward Valphine's church. When Jenna calls it a "torture cult," Brewbarry interjects that it is more of a masochism cult. Jenna turns to Brewbarry and begins a pitch about civilizing Uthgardt barbarians. Brewbarry lets out a legendary burp. She walks away.

### Meeting Xanth on the Triboar Trail
Xanthopoulos is exactly where Vukradin's Sending found him. He praises the party, especially Soma, and mentions Meril was "very interested." The thirty-mile journey into the forest begins. Xanth moves like a creature who belongs there. The party camps when the light goes thin, and Xanth has already scouted a site.

### Campfire with centaur stories
Xanth shares lore around the fire: Talos is a god of destruction present wherever people seek power to destroy. The cult is like a disease — it explodes, destroys everything, then consumes itself. Vukradin asks the incisive question: what changed recently to give Talos power? Xanth acknowledges the question's wisdom. He explains the cult is traditionally small and incoherent, but there is always a Chosen — a cleric who believes Talos speaks through them. Xanth jokes that if he knew where the Chosen was, he would lead the party there; they would probably die, but at least they would learn something.

### Wildcat Loggers
Young druids intercept the party, frantic about illegal logging by Tibor Wester (Harbin's brother). Soma takes charge, telling the druids to teach the loggers proper techniques instead of fighting them. She asks Vukradin to help persuade them. Vukradin delivers a speech about sanctity, balance, honest work, and the difference between taking and receiving. The loggers weep. They set down their axes. Brewbarry is moved to tears, thinking about every tree he has pulled from the ground. The loggers promise to report Tibor Wester to the city.

### The cursed Talosian clearing
The party finds a forty-foot blackened pine in a clearing that feels fundamentally wrong. At its base: fetish dolls of twigs and black hair, each tied to a still-beating pig's heart. Brewbarry warns against touching them — "black magic, voodoo, no touch." Vukradin ignores this and destroys them one by one. Roots erupt from the ground, seizing Soma and Boney. Soma burns a wild shape into a giant elk to break free. Valphine helps. Vukradin finishes destroying all the hearts, and the wrongness drains from the clearing. He estimates ten people were saved.

### Ridge encounter with Talosian cleric and orcs
The party crests a ridge to find ten orcs advancing behind a Talosian cleric with elongated claws. Vukradin opens with Universal Speech, targeting the cleric and the three nearest orcs. The cleric is enraged, but three orcs slow. Valphine casts Daylight from her holy symbol, flooding the ridge with Lathander's radiance. The cleric shrieks, ordering his forces to kill the "drow cleric of Lathander, the abomination." Soma corrects him. The party resolves to focus all attacks on the leader and attempt to convert the rest.
