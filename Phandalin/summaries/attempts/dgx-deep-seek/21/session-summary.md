# Chapter 21 The Spiral's Grasp

Date: 10-02 Taraskh 1495 (continuing)

## Summary

The party gathers at the deserted Logger's Camp. Vukradin learns he has inadvertently gained a reputation as a "noble adventurer" — saving the town from a dragon and advocating for fair wages has made him locally famous, though he protests he just wants to open his music studio. The party recalls a missing Emerald Enclave patrol from earlier briefings and plans a rescue, intending to credit Lyra (a low-ranking member they previously saved) to boost her standing.

Brewbarry consumes fermentable mushrooms and enters an altered state. They consult Joe's notebook and navigate through the wilderness, arriving at a riverbank shrouded in unnatural fog. Standing rigidly by the water is Teega the Smith — glassy eyes, monotone voice, speaking cryptically about "metal bending," "waves," and "minds being cleansed." She draws a spiral symbol in the mud before her eyes briefly clear when Vukradin persuades her, then glaze over again as she wanders into a nearby glade.

The party crosses the river into a planar anomaly — tingling minds, echoing sounds, flickering light. In the glade, they encounter a grim man wielding a black coral dagger, flanked by three mind-controlled Emerald Enclave warriors. Combat erupts. The Cult Fanatic attempts to control Brewbarry's mind, forcing him to flee, while the controlled warriors attack with devastating precision. Soma (bear) charges, Valphine heals, Vukradin struggles to hit. Brewbarry breaks free and delivers a crushing non-lethal blow, knocking the fanatic unconscious.

The mind-controlled warriors collapse screaming — but even unconscious, the fanatic still exerts control, making the warriors move robotically toward him. The party subdues Teega the Smith and secures all prisoners. They discover Kraken Society affiliation (spiral emblems). On the second day, Talosian orcs ambush, demanding the Cult Fanatic. After negotiation, the party hands him over; Valphine executes him. The restored Enclave members reveal they were forced to steal an inscription about a "drowned gate beneath Yllyn" in the Underdark.

## Scenes

### Reconnaissance and Teega the Smith

- Brewbarry consumes mushrooms and enters an altered state. Party follows Joe's notebook directions through wilderness to a riverbank shrouded in unnatural fog.
- Teega the Smith stands rigid by the water: glassy eyes, monotone voice. She speaks of "metal bending," "waves seeking uniformity," "minds being cleansed." Draws a spiral in the mud.
- Vukradin persuades her and her eyes briefly clear — she seems confused — then glazes over again. She wanders into the glade. **(DM note):** Teega's brief moment of clarity suggests the mind control has limits or vulnerabilities. Persuasion checks or *lesser restoration* might be effective against this form of manipulation.

### The Trickle and the Planar Anomaly

- Crossing the river, the party feels a tingling in their minds, echoing sounds, flickering shadows. They recognize the planar anomaly signature from previous encounters.
- **(GM note):** The planar anomaly description (tingling, echoing, flickering) should be consistent with planar breach phenomena from earlier chapters. This tells the party — and the players — that Kraken Society operations involve breaking or weakening planar boundaries, not just standard mind control.

### Combat with Cult Fanatic and Mind-Controlled Warriors — COMBAT

**Opening conditions:** The party enters a wooded glade. A grim man with a black coral dagger stands with three Emerald Enclave warriors in green cloaks with spiral badges. The warriors move with unnaturally precise, robotic movements — clearly mind-controlled. Terrain: glade with trees around the perimeter, open ground in the center, fog clinging to the ground level. The Cult Fanatic is back from the warriors, positioning himself as a commander.

- Round 1: The Cult Fanatic gestures at Brewbarry, attempting mind control. Brewbarry's eyes go blank and he begins fleeing the glade, compelled by the mental command. Soma transforms into a massive bear and charges the Cult Fanatic head-on. Valphine begins channeling healing energy.
- **(DM tactical note):** The Cult Fanatic targeting Brewbarry first was tactically smart — removing the party's tank and frontline damage dealer with a single action. However, the fanatic's position (behind the warriors rather than protected by them) left him exposed to a druid charge.

- The three mind-controlled warriors attack with "devastating precision." Their movements are described as unnaturally coordinated — they seem to anticipate each other's attacks. This suggests the Cult Fanatic is controlling them simultaneously rather than individually.
- **(DM tactical note):** If the warriors all act on the same initiative count and coordinate attacks, this is effectively a higher-CR encounter than three individual warriors. Consider whether they have the "pack tactics" feature or something unique to the mind control — possibly the fanatic shares his own combat instincts through the link.

- Vukradin attempts to strike the Cult Fanatic but struggles to hit. Melee is not his strength, and the fanatic seems to have reasonable AC or defensive capabilities.

- Brewbarry, mid-flee, succeeds on a will save and breaks free of the mental compulsion. He turns, charges back, and delivers a powerful non-lethal strike that knocks the Cult Fanatic unconscious.
- **(DM tactical note):** Brewbarry's will save to break the compulsion was the decisive moment of the fight. The fanatic's control likely requires concentration; if so, it should have broken when he went unconscious. But the next bullet suggests it didn't — ruling inconsistent or deliberate design. Clarify: does the mind control have its own independent duration regardless of the caster's state?

- The moment the fanatic falls unconscious, the mind-controlled warriors collapse to the ground, screaming and clutching their heads. The control appears to be disrupted but not broken — when the party wakes the fanatic, the warriors begin moving toward him robotically again.
- **(DM tactical note):** The "persistent control through unconsciousness" is an unusual mechanic. Standard D&D concentration breaks on unconsciousness. If this is intentional (e.g., the control is a psychic tether that persists until the controller is dead or the tether is broken through other means), it's an important tactical consideration for future encounters with Kraken Society mind controllers. This makes killing the controller the only reliable solution — non-lethal capture doesn't free the victims.

### Securing Prisoners and Kraken Society Discovery

- Soma (bear form) tracks and subdues Teega the Smith, who had wandered away during the combat. The party now has 6 prisoners: Cult Fanatic, 3 Emerald Enclave warriors (mind-controlled), Teega, and Shrimpy (from previous chapter).
- Searching the prisoners: spiral emblems on their gear. This is the first clear Kraken Society identification for the party. The spiral symbol is distinctive.
- Attempting to wake the Cult Fanatic: he mutters about "tides rising" and "waves coming." Refuses to betray his cause.

### Ambush by Talosian Orcs

- Day 2 of journey back. A large raiding party of Talosian orcs surrounds the party, demanding the Cult Fanatic. They identify him as their enemy.
- **(GM note):** The Talosian orcs and Kraken Society are NOT allied. The orcs want the fanatic dead — a rival faction taking out competition. This reinforces that the party is caught between multiple threats in the valley, each with their own agendas.
- The party negotiates. The orcs are wary — they note the party's strength and the presence of a drow. A deal is struck: hand over the Cult Fanatic in exchange for safe passage.
- The fanatic, given one final chance to explain himself, speaks of "petty squabbles" that "mean nothing to what is coming." Valphine executes him swiftly. The orcs take his head. The anchorite half-orc cleric of Talos declares the cultist "one more obstacle crushed by Talos" and warns the party to leave the forest.

### Rescued Enclave Members Awaken

- With the Cult Fanatic dead, the mind control fully breaks. The Emerald Enclave warriors wake, disoriented and horrified. One recognizes Lyra's name and is relieved she sent help.
- They describe the experience: thoughts invaded, orders "dripping like poisoned water into their skulls." They were forced to steal an inscription about a "drowned gate beneath Yllyn" — located somewhere in the Underdark.
- **(GM note - critical lore drop):** The "drowned gate beneath Yllyn" is a major campaign hook. Yllyn is a location in the Underdark. A drowned gate suggests either a sunken portal or a gate associated with water/deep creatures. Kraken Society interest in this site connects to their larger plan. The party now knows: (1) Kraken Society is active, (2) they use psionic/supernatural mind control, (3) they're investigating/accessing a gate in the Underdark, (4) Talosian orcs oppose them (for their own reasons).

### Return to Falcon's Lodge

- Brewbarry, having run out of ale, insists on returning to Falcon's Lodge for more alcohol. The rescued Enclave members need rest. The party decides on a short detour.

## NPCs

- **Three Emerald Enclave Warriors (rescued)** — Freed from mind control. Suffering from psychic trauma. Their intel about the "drowned gate beneath Yllyn" is the chapter's most significant revelation. One knows Lyra personally.
- **Anchorite Half-Orc (Talos)** — Leader of the Talosian orc raiding party. Took the Cult Fanatic's head. Warned the party to leave the forest. Considers Kraken Society an enemy. **(GM note): The Talosian half-orcs are not allies of the party, but they're also not aligned with Kraken Society. They're a third faction with their own agenda. Their cleric's power (divine magic) suggests this isn't just a random orc band.**
- **Lyra (mentioned)** — Low-ranking Emerald Enclave member the party previously saved. The party's plan to credit her for the rescue is intended to boost her standing. It worked — the rescued warriors know her name and are grateful.
