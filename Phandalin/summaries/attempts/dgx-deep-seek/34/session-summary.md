# Chapter 34 — The One Hit Point Principle

**Date:** Feb 18th, 2026

## Summary

After the battle at Dragonbarrow, the party takes stock of their new acquisitions and confronts the emotional weight of what lies ahead. Brewbarry reveals the truth of his exile for the first time — he was sober when the Uthgartian relic was stolen, framed for a crime he didn't commit, and only became the drunkard his tribe accused him of being afterward. The revelation reshapes how the party sees their goliath companion.

Xanthopoulos the centaur awakens, healed by Soma, and immediately recognizes Meril's Staff as a powerful artifact — one that used to have many more leaves. He speaks of the Emerald Enclave's factional infighting between interventionists and naturalists, and offers his aid to Soma, whom Meril apparently knew and trusted with his legacy.

Valphine interrogates the captured Sister Kaella, who reveals she was already turning against the Carver before the party captured her. She confirms the Carver is marching on Icespire Hold to possess the white dragon Cryovain, and reveals she had an arrangement with Valphine — one built on the understanding that Valphine could handle moral ambiguity. Corbin is assigned to guard the prisoner, a task he accepts with unsettling eagerness and a history with Kaella that goes unspoken.

Vukradin plans the route: fifty-five miles of mountain terrain, cutting directly across country instead of detouring through Phandalin. The party secures Xanth as transport, and begins the journey. Along the way, Soma detects the Carver's trail — a straight-line psychic wound through the landscape, warping the rocks where his feet have touched. They encounter Stone Giants who are troubled by the disturbance but refuse to intervene, bound by Hekaton's decree that giants mind giant concerns. They cross the Glacier of Whispers, a field of violet ice containing screaming faces, where Valphine discovers Lathander's light can ward the psychic pressure. And they find a dead young drake, killed by the Carver without hesitation, whose grieving mother circles above — soothed only when Vukradin plays Moonlight Sonata and Brewbarry joins the vigil. Finally, Soma carves a path through a boulder blockage with Mold Earth, refusing to let the mountain stop them.

**(DM note):** The Carver is not a possessed person — the obsidian sword has consumed the host entirely. The "little one with the obsidian sword" that the giants warned about is already functionally a construct of the Carver's will.

- **"This is not a simple staff"** — "Oh, no, this is not Meryl, this is not a simple staff, this is a powerful staff… It used to have more leaves than…" — Xanthopoulos, *recognizing Meril's Staff in Soma's hands and hinting at its true power*

- **"We have an arrangement"** — "And Valphine, Valphine, we had an arrangement." — Sister Kaella, *acknowledging the secret alliance Valphine had built with the Carver's spymaster*

- **"I've been teaching him some of our sacraments"** — "I've been teaching him some of our sacraments." — Valphine, *answering what she taught Corbin without explaining which sacraments, as Kaella's screaming begins behind them*

## Scenes

### Aftermath at Dragonbarrow — Boney and Valphine

- Boney the skeletal horse stands in the aftermath, head cocked at Valphine
- Valphine approaches cautiously, addressing the horse as though it might answer
- She asks whether it's truly a horse, a former horse, or something that has merely decided to be horse-shaped
- **(DM note):** Boney was a real horse when he died in the Dragonbarrow crypts — the circumstances of his reanimation are unknown at this time
- A tentative bond forms — Valphine, despite herself, seems to find the creature's presence grounding

### Brewbarry's Goodberry Confession

- Someone mentions goodberries in passing; Brewbarry stops, the word catching in him
- He tells Vukradin the truth he has never told anyone: he was sober when the Uthgartian relic was stolen
- A goodberry had left him settled and still — a state the tribe interpreted as negligent
- Framed for dereliction of duty, he accepted exile without fighting the accusation
- He only became a drunkard *after* the exile, becoming what they'd called him because the name was already there
- Vukradin is genuinely quiet — a rare moment for him
- The confession ends with them laughing, but the weight of it sits under the laughter

### Xanth Awakens — Meril's Staff

- Xanthopoulos rises healed and immediately announces "this is an important day!"
- He greets Vukradin in full Centaurian: "Vucravinios"
- When he sees Soma, something shifts — gratitude, reverence
- "Soma, that is, I could kiss you"
- He spots Meril's Staff and his demeanor changes entirely
- The staff used to have more leaves — it's "a powerful staff"
- Xanth reveals he and Meril were old friends
- "Meryl must know you. I will help you, Soma. What do you want?"
- Soma answers: "I don't have big aspirations. We're just trying to keep the world a safer place."
- Xanth nods as if the answer was correct

### Emerald Enclave Infighting

- Xanth explains the Enclave's internal conflict: interventionists vs. naturalists
- "There's idiots who are saying one thing, idiots who are saying the other thing"
- They can't even agree on whether orcs are a "natural" or "intervention" problem
- Soma's weariness mirrors his — she's been in these arguments before
- Xanth's conclusion: "You must go deal with a dragon. That is the most important thing."

### Valphine Interrogates Sister Kaella

- Valphine uses Underdark-taught pressure techniques to wake Kaella carefully
- Kaella's first words: "Thank God you're alive!" — spoken with genuine charm
- Kaella explains Jax's crew would have tortured her; the party's intervention was her preference
- Valphine presses the point: "We can still do that. If you'd like."
- **(DM note):** This is the first time Valphine has openly allowed the party to see the edge she carries
- Kaella admits she was already turning on the Carver after the party killed his lieutenants
- She explains her arrangement with Valphine — built on the understanding that Valphine operates in moral gray space
- The Carver is heading to Icespire Hold to possess the white dragon
- She requests Axholm prison if captured — a reasonable case for it
- Corbin is assigned as her guard; he asks "Can I beat her?" as a procedural question
- Vukradin answers: "Until she has one hit point"
- Valphine adds: "I've been teaching him some of our sacraments"
- Kaella begins screaming as they walk away

### Route Planning

- Vukradin calculates two paths: 55 miles direct (one exhaustion level) vs. detour through Phandalin (two levels)
- He chooses direct — partly tactical, partly political (avoids giving rivals on the Privy Council ammunition)
- Xanth agrees to carry Vukradin and Valphine to the foothills
- Soma will become a bear to carry another rider
- Boney the tireless skeleton horse handles the rest of the transport

### The Unnatural Trail

- Soma detects the wrongness before she sees the footprints
- The Carver's path runs in a straight line — over rocks, through obstacles
- Vukradin identifies it: a construct, not a person
- Soma corrects: "A guy under some sort of demonic spell. Possessed."
- Meril's Staff pressed against displaced rocks reveals psychic residue
- The rocks shimmer and phase — still uncertain in their existence

### Stone Giants Encounter

- Stone giants block the pass, troubled by the "wrong rocks"
- They describe the trail-maker: "The little one with the obsidian sword"
- Asked if they tried to stop him: "He is a little one. Is of no concern of ours."
- They explain Hekaton's doctrine: giants mind giant things, the dragon war is over
- Soma: "He will soon" — they both understand the Carver will become their concern
- The giants give the party two potions (greater healing, hill giant strength) before they leave — wordless gifts

### Glacier of Whispers

- Violet ice with screaming faces — dozens trapped within
- **(DM note):** The glacier has been accumulating psychic trauma for centuries; the Carver's passage activated it
- Whispers arrive unbidden: "He walks without rest, without breath, without soul"
- Valphine discovers Lathander's light can act as a ward against the psychic pressure (10-minute window)
- The party crosses with the blessing held in reserve; no one breaks

### The Dead Drake and the Grieving Mother

- They find a young drake dead in the snow — killed by the Carver, eye sockets hollow
- The mother circles above, grief-stricken
- "Mothers are resilient," Vukradin says. "We are," Soma answers
- Vukradin decides: "We don't need to fight her. Save it for the real guy."
- He raises his instrument and plays Moonlight Sonata
- Brewbarry joins in without being asked
- The mother descends, lands beside her young, and lets them pass

### Soma Carves a Path

- A wall of boulders blocks the pass completely
- Brewbarry sizes it up as something to be hit; Vukradin composes; Valphine regards it with annoyance
- Soma presses her hands to the stone and uses Mold Earth
- Vukradin offers bardic inspiration; Soma refuses: "I don't need it. I got it on its own."
- The boulder shifts. Inch by inch, the mountain gives
- **(DM note):** This is a significant moment for Soma's character — asserting her capability independent of the party's support systems

## NPCs

- **Sister Kaella** — The Carver's spymaster, captured at Dragonbarrow. Pragmatic survivor who was already turning against the Carver. Admits to an arrangement with Valphine built on shared comfort with moral ambiguity. Requested Axholm prison. Her relationship with Corbin is fraught with history (**GM note:** she trained him, brutally, and he has never forgotten). Vukradin's instructions for her guard: "Until she has one hit point."

- **Xanthopoulos (Xanth)** — Centaur, old friend of Meril. Enormous, enthusiastic, speaks in connected stories. Recognizes Meril's Staff as powerful and knows Soma was chosen for a reason. Opposed to the Emerald Enclave's naturalist faction. Agrees to carry the party to the Icespire foothills. Views Soma with something approaching reverence.

- **Stone Giants** — A gathering in the mountain pass, troubled by the Carver's passage but bound by Hekaton's non-intervention doctrine. Gift two potions despite refusing direct aid. Their philosophy is ironclad: giants mind giant things, and the Carver is too small to concern them.

- **Boney** — The reanimated skeletal horse from Dragonbarrow. Does not tire. Handles party transport. Developing a tentative bond with Valphine, who seems to find his presence grounding despite (or because of) his unnatural nature.

- **Corbin** — Assigned to guard Kaella. Accepts with unsettling eagerness. Has an unspoken history with her that the party chooses not to examine too closely. Valphine has been teaching him her "sacraments."

- **Mother Drake** — Grieving white drake whose young was killed by the Carver. Allowed the party to pass after Vukradin and Brewbarry played Moonlight Sonata as a vigil. Her grief is raw and genuine, and the party treated it with respect.

- **The Carver** — Seen only through his trail: a straight-line passage through every obstacle, psychic residue warping the rocks, level-draining obsidian sword. "He walks without rest, without breath, without soul." Traveling alone toward Icespire Hold to possess Cryovain.

## Items

- **Meril's Staff** — Carried by Soma; left in her house by Meril. Used to have many more leaves. Xanth recognizes it as "a powerful staff" and hints that Meril chose Soma deliberately. Soma used it to detect psychic residue on the Carver's trail.

- **Necklace of Fireballs** — Held by Vukradin ("absolutely not his necklace, he is merely holding it"). Debated for use against Cryovain until Vukradin noted dragons are immune to fire. The argument collapsed.

- **Dragon Slayer Sword** — Claimed by Brewbarry from Dragonbarrow. His primary weapon for the coming dragon fight.

- **Obsidian Sword (Carver's)** — Level-draining weapon. Warps the fabric of reality around it. The Carver's true identity may be tied to this blade. The entire party agrees: do not come near it.

- **Gauntlets of Ogre Power** — Brewbarry's. Used to aid the mountain ascent.

- **Two Potions** — Greater healing and hill giant strength. Gifts from the stone giants. Pressed into Brewbarry's hands without ceremony.

## Spells & Abilities

- **Mold Earth (Soma)** — Used to carve a path through a boulder blockage. Soma refused bardic inspiration and succeeded on her own. A quiet assertion of her capabilities independent of the party.

- **Wild Shape (Soma)** — Used to become a bear for transport carrying another rider.

- **Detect Magic / Psychic Sensing (Soma via Meril's Staff)** — Used to identify the psychic residue on the Carver's trail. Revealed the Carver's nature as something beyond mere possession.

- **Lathander's Ward (Valphine)** — Newly discovered ability. Lathander's light (specifically directed) can ward psychic pressure. Used to protect the crossing of the Glacier of Whispers. Duration: 10 minutes.

- **Moonlight Sonata (Vukradin)** — Performance used to soothe a grieving mother drake. Brewbarry joined in. No magic involved — just art as genuine offering. The mother let them pass.

- **Underdark Pressure Techniques (Valphine)** — Non-magical interrogation methods. Used to wake Kaella carefully and assess her state. A reminder of Valphine's origins.
