# Chapter 5 Saving Adabra

Date: 04-01 of Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party arrives at Umbrage Hill with Corbin in tow, triggering Vukradin's most aggressive ethical confrontation yet: Corbin's armor is "stolen from a dead man," and Vukradin orders him to remove it. Corbin pleads — he needs protection — and Brewbarry's lack of armor becomes Vukradin's rhetorical weapon ("I don't wear any"). A compromise is reached: Corbin keeps the armor temporarily until "fair trade armor" can be obtained. Soma, just emerged from spider form after the Wild Shape expired, freaks Corbin out.

Atop the windmill, a manticore perches, holding Adabra Gwynn captive inside the mill. The manticore can speak and negotiate — it offers to leave in exchange for the "succulent taste of human meat." Soma conjures a familiar and orders it into the manticore's mouth as a "tasty morsel," but the creature snaps it up unsatisfied. Brewbarry engages in melee, taking a clawing. Valphine is struck by a tail spike volley. She uses area healing and radiant attacks. Brewbarry kills the manticore after it tries to flee toward Corbin.

Post-combat, Adabra Gwynn emerges — a hard-bitten, fanatical acolyte of Chauntea with a piercing gaze and an obsession with the Naturalist/Interventionist schism within the Emerald Enclave. She tests Soma's allegiance ("Naturalist or interventionist?") and, upon hearing "naturalist," adopts her. She refuses to leave the windmill, insisting she must stay to craft potions. Vukradin plays music to convince her — Brewbarry is delighted (he's the only one who ever paid full price for Vukradin's music). Adabra appreciates the "blessed music" but stays put.

The party learns Corbin's backstory in full: his village Greenmeadow was destroyed by Uthgardt raiders during the Ordning's collapse. He lost his wife Elara and daughter Lily. He wandered, starved, stole, and eventually joined a bandit crew. A tragic, broken man.

Adabra changes course the next morning when she realizes Vukradin's principles align with naturalism — no killing for treasure, no plundering — and decides to come to Phandalin to "support" him. She lecture-reframes Brewbarry from "barbarian" to "nomad" (to Brewbarry's confusion) and insists she will accompany the party. She is accompanying Adabra as a landmark decision.

## Scenes

### The Armor Debate
- Corbin arrives with the party. Vukradin immediately spots the armor as stolen (from a dead man).
- Corbin: "I had no choice. The owner was already dead."
- Vukradin orders him to remove it. Brewbarry's lack of armor is used as leverage.
- A compromise: Corbin keeps the armor until "fair trade armor" can be obtained.
- Soma-as-spider re-emerges and gestures frantically at Corbin. Corbin freaks out. Vukradin: "That's Soma, our tortle."

### Journey to Umbrage Hill
- Soma's spider form expires; she returns to tortle form.
- Corbin in shock: "It is true I am tortle. And why do you need armor?"
- Corbin: "Because I am human." Soma eyes his squishy form, musing on human dominance.
- Party arrives at the hilltop windmill with a manticore perched on it. Adabra's voice: "Help me!"
- Valphine asks surface-world ethics questions: "We were told to bring her back." Soma: "Yes, but it was implied alive."
- Vukradin suggests killing the manticore, returning to Phandalin, demanding more money, then recovering Adabra. Soma shuts that down.

### Manticore — Combat
- **Opening conditions:** Windmill at Umbrage Hill on a hilltop. Adabra is inside the first floor. The manticore is perched on the roof/second floor. The party approaches quietly but Soma's failed stealth alert it. Manticore can speak Common. Adabra is a fragile NPC they must protect.
- **Round-by-round highlights:** Valphine shoots a crossbow bolt — the manticore is unaffected but speaks: "That hurt?" Vukradin attempts Command — manticore saves. Manticore offers a deal: give it Corbin (human meat) and it leaves. Soma conjures a familiar (goodberry? The narrative says "conjured a familiar") and orders it into the manticore's mouth — unsatisfying crunch. Brewbarry throws handaxes, hits a wing. Manticore flies down, claws Brewbarry. Brewbarry hits with halberd as it closes. Manticore retreats to second floor. Valphine is struck by the tail spike volley. She heals herself. Brewbarry climbs to second floor, hits twice. Manticore tries to flee toward Corbin. Vukradin and Soma cast at it. Brewbarry kills it mid-flight.
- **Key tactical moments:** The manticore's negotiation attempt (offering to leave if it gets Corbin) created an interesting moral dilemma that the party wisely refused. Soma's familiar-in-the-mouth gambit was creative but mechanically ineffective. Vukradin seeing Valphine go down from tail spikes shows the danger of ranged enemies against a party without reliable cover.
- **(DM tactical note):** The manticore's tail spike volley took Valphine down in one round. This is an important lesson: Vukradin, as a squishy bard, will die quickly if he doesn't seek cover or stay behind the front line. The manticore's willingness to negotiate shows it's intelligent — the party could have talked their way through this encounter, which is worth noting for future manticore/red dragon wyrmling encounters.
- **(DM tactical note):** The party had no anti-air options beyond ranged attacks. Brewbarry climbing to the second floor to engage in melee was a good solution but put him in the danger zone. Valphine's hand crossbow is unreliable at range. The party needs at least one reliable ranged damage dealer.
- **Enemy behavior:** Manticore negotiates first, offers bargain, uses hit-and-run tactics (fly in, attack, fly away). Focuses fire on the downed Vukradin. Tries to flee toward easier prey (Corbin) when badly wounded. Intelligent and tactical.
- **Spell/ability impact:** Command (Vukradin) — manticore saved; Starry Wisp — used on the fleeing manticore; Healing spells (Valphine) — essential; Handaxes (Brewbarry) — surprisingly effective at range; Conjured familiar (Soma) — creative but minimal mechanical impact.
- **PC resource decisions:** Vukradin used Command despite having seen the manticore save against similar magic earlier. Soma used Wild Shape charges on a spider and familiar conjuration rather than direct damage spells — creative but less efficient. Brewbarry wisely saved rage until engaged in melee.
- **Post-combat:** Vukradin cuts off the manticore's head and marches to the windmill with it. Adabra emerges cautiously.

### Meeting Adabra
- Adabra is a weathered, lean woman with calloused hands, piercing eyes, and a fanatical aura. She smells of herbs and antiseptic.
- She ensures the manticore is dead, then offers a "special potion of healing with extra scent."
- Soma: "That sounds like a bath water potion." (Mutters about rich people scenting their bath water.)
- Adabra refuses to leave the windmill. She reveals the Naturalist/Interventionist schism in the Emerald Enclave.
- She tests Soma: "Naturalist or interventionist?" Soma answers naturalist. Adabra's demeanor shifts to approval.
- Adabra's philosophy: "The naturalists, like us, think that the meta-verse is part of the natural order. We should accept the divine interventions as part of the natural order."
- Vukradin plays music. Adabra is delighted by the "blessed music" of an aasimar.
- She still refuses to leave. Gives Vukradin a note for Harbin stating her refusal.

### Corbin's Backstory (Dinner Scene)
- Corbin tells his full story over dinner at the windmill.
- **Greenmeadow:** A farming village east of the High Forest. Corbin was a farmer, married to Elara, with a daughter named Lily.
- **The Uthgardt attack:** The Black Lion tribe descended during the Ordning's collapse. Corbin fought back with a wood axe. Watched his family fall. Survived by being knocked unconscious and left for dead in a ditch.
- **The aftermath:** Walked south for months, drifting, stealing to survive. Each petty crime eroded his former self.
- Eventually joined a bandit crew when he had nothing left.

### Adabra's Conversion
- Morning: Adabra realizes Vukradin's principles — no killing for treasure, no plunder — align with naturalism.
- She explodes: "This barbaric slaughtering of monsters to seize their treasures is an act of barbarism and vandalism!"
- Brewbarry: "I am a barbarian." Soma: "We don't use the B word in front of Brewbarry."
- Adabra reframes Brewbarry: "You are a nomad. I will not permit such language in this house."
- Her lecture: "The word barbarian implies that there are people who are civilized and those who are uncivilized... The interventionists want to draw lines, and when you draw lines, those that fall out become prey and can be destroyed."
- **(DM note):** Adabra's reframing of Brewbarry as a "nomad" is more than wordplay — it's a deliberate narrative device from the naturalist philosophy to deconstruct the language of civilization vs. savagery. This is important foreshadowing for the naturalist/interventionist conflict.
- Vukradin tentatively asks: "I heard you were going to come with us?" Adabra: "Well, yes. You are fellow naturalists!"

## NPCs
- **Adabra Gwynn** — Acolyte of Chauntea, midwife, and windmill-dweller on Umbrage Hill. Hard-bitten, fanatical, with a piercing gaze and weathered hands that smell of herbs. Deeply entrenched in the Naturalist/Interventionist schism of the Emerald Enclave. Views divine interventions as "part of the natural order." Reframes Brewbarry from barbarian to nomad. Decides to travel with the party after learning Vukradin shares her anti-plunder principles. **(DM note):** Adabra is more than a simple quest-giver. She is a faction representative with a hidden agenda: building a naturalist network in the Phandalin area. Her offer to travel with the party is also surveillance — she's vetting Vukradin and Soma as potential members.
- **Corbin** (backstory revealed) — Former farmer of Greenmeadow, destroyed by the Black Lion tribe of the Uthgardt during the Ordning's collapse. Lost his wife Elara and daughter Lily. Survived by being left for dead. Became a drifter and then a bandit. A tragic figure whose trauma response to Uthgardt symbols will require careful handling.
- **The Manticore** — Intelligent, speaks Common. Has a preference for human flesh ("succulent taste"). Offers negotiation before attacking. Uses hit-and-run tactics. Focus-fires weak targets. Tries to flee to easier prey when wounded. Killed by Brewbarry mid-flight.
