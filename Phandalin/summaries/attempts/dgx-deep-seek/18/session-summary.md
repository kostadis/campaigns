# Chapter 18 Falcon's Hoard and Hidden Truths

Date: 9-02 Taraskh 1495 (late night — early investigation)

## Summary

This chapter follows Vukradin's solo covert mission to uncover the truth about Falcon the Hunter's dragon slayer past. While his companions sleep — Brewbarry's thunderous snoring rattling the timbers, Soma safely encased in her shell, Valphine pretending to sleep — Vukradin infiltrates the main lodge and ascends to Falcon's private quarters.

Casting *Silence* as a ritual to avoid detection, Vukradin pries open a locked drawer and discovers several items: a pair of wooden elven ears on a chain inscribed "my dearly beloved," a carved wooden square depicting a labyrinthine maze (which Vukradin recognizes from "The Undercity Chorale," an obscure Waterdeep opera), and a small iron box shaped like an anatomically correct dragon's head. Inside the box is a brass ring inscribed with the Elvish word for "lightning." Vukradin connects this to Iymrith, the legendary blue dragon.

The next morning, Falcon confronts the party about the disturbed drawer. Assuming the intrusion was a Cult of Talos theft attempt, he declares he almost lost a treasure to the cultists. Vukradin plays innocent convincingly enough that Falcon accepts the cover story.

Pressed, Falcon confesses the truth: he is not a dragon slayer. He was the only survivor of his party's encounter with Iymrith, and he arrived at the hoard only after it had been plundered. He found residual gold and the ring — "seed capital" for the lodge. He maintains the lie because "the fantasy of dining with a dragon slayer is good for business." Vukradin secures permission to document the ring. The party now has intelligence linking Iymrith, Talos, and lightning, and is ready to proceed with the supply delivery.

## Scenes

### The Solo Infiltration

- Vukradin stalks through the darkened lodge while his companions sleep. He casts *Silence* as a ritual to cloak his movements, covering both Falcon's bedroom and the adjacent hallway.
- **(DM note):** This is Vukradin's first extended solo covert operation. The *Silence* ritual was a clever choice — it prevents sound-based perception checks but also means Vukradin can't communicate with the party if discovered. The risk was entirely on him. If he'd been caught, there was no backup within earshot.

### The Locked Drawer Discovery

- Inside the drawer: wooden elven ears on a chain ("my dearly beloved"), a labyrinth-carved wooden square, and an iron dragon-head box containing a brass ring inscribed "lightning" in Elvish.
- Vukradin connects the maze carving to "The Undercity Chorale" — an obscure Waterdeep opera about the city's deepest subterranean levels. This is an impressively esoteric knowledge check.
- The dragon-head box is described as "anatomically correct, as if a dragon's head had been expertly shrunk." The ring's "lightning" inscription points to Iymrith, the blue dragon from Storm King's Thunder lore.

### Confrontation at Breakfast

- Falcon discovers the disturbed drawer and immediately blames the Cult of Talos. Vukradin plays innocent with practiced composure. Falcon buys the cover story.
- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's deception check here was critical. If Falcon had seen through it, the party's relationship with their host and ally would have been severely damaged, potentially losing access to the lodge as a safe haven and information source.

### Falcon's Confession

- Cornered by the "cult theft" cover story, Falcon admits the truth: he isn't a dragon slayer. He's the sole survivor of Iymrith's attack. The hoard was already plundered when he arrived. The ring and residual gold funded the lodge. The lie is a business strategy.
- Vukradin views this as confirmation of his worldview — Falcon is a fraud, an artist, and a thief. He secures permission to document the ring.

### Party Reactions

- Valphine approves: the lie is "perfectly normal" and demonstrates genius. Soma sees it as a harmless fantasy that keeps guests feeling safe. The party now has intelligence linking blue dragon (Iymrith), lightning energy, and Cult of Talos iconography.
- **(GM note):** The party now knows: (1) Falcon's ring came from Iymrith's hoard, (2) Iymrith is a blue dragon associated with lightning, (3) the Cult of Talos has interest in this artifact, (4) Falcon's claim to dragon-slaying fame is entirely fabricated. This is actionable intel about the broader campaign threats.

## NPCs

- **Falcon the Hunter** — Lodge proprietor. Revealed to be a fraud — not a dragon slayer but the sole survivor of Iymrith's attack. His hoard was pre-plundered; the ring was scrap he found among the remains. Maintains the lie for business. A sympathetic figure whose deception is more marketing than malice. **(GM note): Falcon is a potential ally who values the party's discretion now that they know his secret. His connection to Iymrith's hoard — even a scrap — makes him a target if Iymrith or her servants come looking.**
- **Vukradin (party member)** — Successful infiltrator. Deception held up under pressure. Secured permission to document the ring. Privately satisfied that his suspicion was confirmed. Gathers intelligence about Iymrith, Talos, and lightning connections.

- **Brewbarry (party member)** — Snored through the entire operation. Unaware of events until breakfast confrontation.
