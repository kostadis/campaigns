# Chapter 12 Naturalists and Interventionists

Date: Early Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party reaches the heart of the Whispering Wood — a 60-foot clearing where reality is fractured. At its center stands a corrupted treant with obsidian bark, limbs bent in impossible geometries, dripping iridescent sap. Pools of shimmering planar energy pulse at its base. Tears in reality flicker and whisper.

Nearby, members of the Emerald Enclave are trapped in blighted brambles. Their leader, Lyra, shouts for help: they came to cleanse the grove's guardian of a "planar infection," but it turned on them. The treant speaks with overlapping whispers: arrogant, claiming ownership of the grove, declaring itself "happy to be powerful and dangerous."

The fight begins. Soma (bear) attacks the brambles but accidentally hits herself — the treant finds this amusing. Brewbarry grows to 15-foot reach. Valphine's Guiding Bolt scorches a vine. Vukradin's Starry Whip connects with the treant.

Lyra produces four crude stones — the ritual to seal the planar rift requires 6 successes, with only 3 failures allowed. The party must fight the treant while simultaneously performing a delicate ritual. The attempts swing back and forth: Vukradin fails (1/3), Soma succeeds, Vukradin succeeds, Brewbarry succeeds (3/1), then Soma fails twice (3/3). The tide swings back and forth.

Soma's staff glows — confirming the unnatural planar origin of the corruption. She hesitates: is helping Lyra the right thing? Is Adabra's naturalism correct? The staff provides the answer: the treant, the pools, the brambles — all are unnatural. This planar rift is not part of the natural order. Lyra explains the schism in the Emerald Enclave: the War of the Dragons split them into naturalists (who see all multiversal existence as natural) and interventionists (who believe planar intrusions must be fought).

On the final attempt, Soma fails the ritual. A psychic shockwave rips through the clearing — everyone clutches their heads in pain. The planar wells expand, pulsing with sickly light. The rift remains open. Worse, a shadowy puppet creature emerges from the rift, collects ingredients from the treant's corpse with unsettling grace, and vanishes back into the tear. Brewbarry identifies it: "He's a puppet that's being controlled."

Lyra is devastated. The party retreats. Soma realizes she must confront Adabra — the naturalist philosophy she trusted allowed this corruption to fester.

## Memorable Moments

> "My name is Lyra. Help us! We're out of the Emerald Enclave! This thing! It was the Grove's Guardian, but it has been corrupted. Please, fight it! Don't let the corruption spread!"
> — Lyra, trapped in the brambles

**Plea from the Brambles.** Lyra's desperate cry for help reveals the stakes: this was a rescue mission gone wrong. The Emerald Enclave came to help and got consumed.

> "You think I'm wrong! You think there is something wrong with me! That I must be changed back! I am… happy to be powerful and dangerous!"
> — The Corrupted Treant

**Happy to Be Dangerous.** The treant's declaration of joyful corruption is chilling. It doesn't want to be saved. The planar infection has overwritten its identity so completely that it considers the corruption an upgrade.

> "It's still there? No, that would provoke too many follow-ups. I will tell her the truth: 'I'm not really sure what happened. There was a massive planar tear, and it was right in the middle of that tear in the fabric.'"
> — Vukradin, planning his half-truths about the treant

**Diplomatic Obfuscation.** Vukradin's rapid calculation of how much to tell Adabra shows a previously unseen talent for political misdirection. His "stable condition" quip becomes one of the campaign's great deceptions.

> "Failure. I love it. One failure down."
> — Vukradin, after failing the ritual

**Owning the Failure.** Vukradin's response to his ritual failure is pure bardic bravado — but the psychic backlash proves there's a cost.

> "He's a puppet that's being controlled."
> — Brewbarry, observing the rift creature

**Brewbarry Sees the Strings.** Amid the chaos, Brewbarry is the one who correctly identifies the shadowy creature. His simple observation reframes the entire planar crisis: it's not random — someone is pulling strings.

## Scenes

### Entering the Clearing
- 60-foot clearing at the forest's heart. Reality is fractured.
- Corrupted treant: obsidian bark, impossible geometries, iridescent sap.
- Pools of shimmering planar energy. Flickering tears in reality.
- Emerald Enclave members (Lyra + others) trapped in blighted brambles.
- Lyra shouts for help: "This thing was the Grove's Guardian, but it has been corrupted."

**(DM note):** The clearing is designed as an encounter area with a time pressure (ritual) and a combat threat (treant + brambles). The party must split attention between fighting and ritual-casting.

### COMBAT: Corrupted Treant + Brambles + Ritual
- **Opening conditions:** 60-ft clearing. Brambles holding Enclave members. Treant at center. Four crude ritual stones on the ground. Lyra explains: 6 successes needed, 3 failures = ritual fails.
- **Round-by-round highlights:**
  - Soma (bear) attacks brambles — first attack misses, second hits herself. Treant finds this amusing.
  - Brewbarry grows to 15-ft reach, attacks a bramble but misses. Steps into the treant's "doom zone" (psychic aura) — shrugs off the madness ("You mean more than I am already?").
  - Valphine casts Guiding Bolt on a bramble vine — hits, weakens it, leaves it glowing for advantage.
  - Vukradin casts Starry Whip at the treant — hits for damage.
  - **Ritual attempt 1 (Vukradin):** FAILURE (1/3). Psychic backlash.
  - Lyra casts Moonbeam on the treant but is re-ensnared by brambles.
  - Brewbarry cleaves a vine, hurts the treant. Valphine casts Faerie Fire on the treant.
  - **Ritual attempt 2 (Soma):** SUCCESS (1 success, 1 failure). Planar backlash damage.
  - Soma casts Moonbeam on the treant — takes damage, reels.
  - Vukradin heals a dying Enclave warrior.
  - **Ritual attempt 3 (Vukradin):** SUCCESS (2 successes, 1 failure).
  - **Ritual attempt 4 (Brewbarry):** SUCCESS (3 successes, 1 failure).
  - Soma and Enclave warrior finish off the treant.
  - **Ritual attempt 5 (Soma):** FAILURE (3 successes, 2 failures).
  - **Ritual attempt 6 (Soma — final):** FAILURE (3 successes, 3 failures).
- **Key tactical moments:**
  - Soma hitting herself as a bear is comedic but mechanically costly — lost an attack.
  - Brewbarry's 15-ft reach changed the battlefield geometry, allowing him to threaten from outside the treant's melee range.
  - Vukradin's decision to heal the Enclave warrior instead of attempting the ritual again was a resource trade-off that saved an NPC but delayed progress.
  - Valphine's Guiding Bolt on a bramble (rather than the treant) was a support choice that aided subsequent attackers.
- **Enemy behavior:**  Treant fought defensively at first (arrogant speeches), then targeted the Enclave warriors when they were freed. Its "doom zone" aura inflicted psychic damage on anyone nearby. It did not pursue fleeing targets — it seemed content to hold territory.
- **Spell/ability impact:**
  - Guiding Bolt (Valphine): Effective against brambles, provided tactical advantage.
  - Moonbeam (Lyra): Damaged treant but required concentration and positioning.
  - Starry Whip (Vukradin): Connected reliably against the treant.
  - Faerie Fire (Valphine): Advantage for attackers.
  - Soma's staff glow: Confirmed unnatural planar origin — decisive for her character decision.
- **PC resource decisions:**
  - Soma spent multiple Wild Shapes on this encounter (bear form + potential backup).
  - Brewbarry's rage was burned. His growth ability (15-ft reach) may be limited-use.
  - Vukradin used healing on an NPC rather than conserving resources for the party.
- **Post-combat:** Treant dead. Ritual failed. Psychic shockwave. Planar wells expand. Shadowy creature emerges, collects treant ingredients, vanishes.

**(DM tactical note):** The party split their focus between combat and ritual, which cost them. If they had dedicated one PC solely to the ritual (e.g., Soma with full concentration) while others fought, they might have succeeded. Soma's hesitation — philosophical uncertainty about whether to help Lyra — cost a critical action at a crucial moment.

**(DM tactical note):** No one attempted Counterspell or Dispel Magic on the treant's buffs or the planar effects. The brambles holding the Enclave members were treated as obstacles to chop through rather than magical effects that could be dispelled. If dispel was available and used, the Enclave members could have been freed faster, joining the fight earlier.

### The Puppet Creature
- A shadowy, phased-out entity emerges from the planar rift.
- Moves with marionette-like grace — "a puppet being controlled."
- Collects ingredients from the treant's corpse.
- Disappears back into the planar tear.
- Brewbarry identifies the controlled nature.
- The planar wells remain, expanded and pulsing.

**(DM note, not yet known to party):** The creature is likely a planar servant of whoever created the rift. It was harvesting treant essence — either to create new corrupted guardians or to fuel the rift's expansion. The "puppet" description suggests it is not the master but a tool.

### Lyra's Explanation — The Emerald Enclave Schism
- Lyra explains the split: naturalists vs. interventionists.
- The War of the Dragons (Tiamat's attempted summoning) created the schism.
- Some Enclave members believed Tiamat was "part of the multiverse" and should not be stopped.
- Delaan Winterhound was convinced by Malfire Morazana to join the war — Treants fought.
- Those who opposed intervention believe Tiamat's defeat caused the War of the Giants (shattered Ordning).
- Naturalists see the planar rift as natural; interventionists see it as a corruption.
- Soma's realization: "This tree ate people. Trees do not eat people."

**(DM note):** This is the campaign's thematic core. The naturalist vs. interventionist debate has no easy answer. Both sides have coherent philosophies and evidence for their positions. The party must decide for themselves — and the answer will shape their relationship with Adabra, Lyra, and the Emerald Enclave.

### Soma's Choice
- Soma hesitates: is helping Lyra interventionist meddling or natural healing?
- Her staff confirms the unnatural origin of the corruption.
- She chooses to help Lyra — the rift is a motivated blight, not a natural phenomenon.
- She completes the ritual attempts but fails on the last try.
- The failure and the puppet creature convince her: "Sickness must be healed."
- She decides to confront Adabra.

## NPCs

- **Lyra** — Emerald Enclave interventionist leader. Came to save the grove's guardian from planar corruption. Overconfident — her solo operation was unauthorized by Enclave leadership. The ritual was beyond her team's capacity. Now devastated by the failure. **(DM note):** Lyra acted without authorization because the Enclave was deadlocked in debate. Her failure may have consequences within the organization.

- **The Corrupted Treant** — Former grove guardian, now twisted by planar infection. Obsidian bark, impossible geometry, dripping iridescent sap. Claims to be "happy to be powerful and dangerous." Killed by the party and Enclave warriors. Its essence was harvested by the puppet creature.

- **Emerald Enclave Warriors (Thomas, Marian, unnamed)** — Lyra's team, trapped in brambles. One nearly died; Vukradin healed him. They survive but are demoralized by the ritual's failure.

- **The Puppet Creature** — Shadowy, phased-out entity from the planar rift. Collects treant ingredients. Controlled by an unseen master. Brewbarry identifies the puppet nature. **(DM note, not yet known to party):** The creature's master is likely connected to the Kraken Society or another planar-power group. The ingredient collection may be part of a larger project.
