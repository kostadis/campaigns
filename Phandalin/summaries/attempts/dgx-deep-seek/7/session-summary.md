# Chapter 7 Conflict-Free Gold, a Curious Side Quest, and a Delightful Ambush

Date: 05-01 to 08-01 of Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party departs for the Tower of Storms, but the journey is interrupted by a series of encounters that deepen the campaign's mysteries. First, they discover an overturned trade cart with a shaken merchant named Elara and her groom Jarek, who were attacked by orcs of unusual discipline. Both describe eerie details — "odd markings like a swirl, or an eye," a humming sound "in my head" before the attack, and ground-shaking tremors — suggesting something more than ordinary banditry. Vukradin insists on returning them safely to Phandalin for "conflict-free gold," over the party's grumbling.

In Phandalin, Vukradin reports the encounter to Ser Kaelen, who is impressed with his commitment to doing good deeds — "Bringing you into the fold was the right thing to do." Vukradin asks Ser Kaelen for the Gauntlet's cheer, getting a reluctant and embarrassing chant that he promises to improve. Ser Kaelen gratefully commissions better music from Vukradin.

Adabra pulls Soma aside with news of a corrupted grove past Whisper Woods — a once-vibrant Chauntea-blessed grove now twisting unnaturally: "Trees twist in impossible shapes, colors bleeding wrong, and the creatures... they're not right. Too many eyes, jerky movements, lost and broken." Adabra hints at interventionist involvement. Soma agrees to investigate.

The party resumes travel, staying at the Wayside Inn (great stews) before pushing toward the coast. Valphine observes the terrain's unnatural gullies and deduces magical alteration — not natural river erosion. Brewbarry's survival instincts detect an ambush: the party is being set up for a trap in a narrow gully where defenders would drop rocks and rain missiles on trapped targets.

The party outmaneuvers the ambushers, climbing out of the gully to attack from the rear. The engagement reveals orc raiders with unusual discipline and an orc shaman who can shapeshift into a boar — adding tactical depth. Vukradin's Sleep spell neutralizes the shaman and one orc. Soma becomes a brown bear. Brewbarry rages. Valphine calls on Lathander's Fairy Fire. The battle is efficient and brutal, though the shaman's boar transformation is a moment of surprising complexity. Valphine finds the entire experience "delightful."

## Scenes

### Elara and Jarek's Rescue
- The party rounds a bend and finds an overturned trade cart with Elara (merchant) and Jarek (groom) behind it.
- They are terrified of the party's appearance: an aasimar, an Uthgardt, a drow, a giant tortle, and a bandit-looking human in dead-man's armor.
- Elara describes the orc attackers: unnerving discipline, odd markings like a swirl or an eye that she can't quite remember.
- Jarek describes a humming sound in his head before the attack.
- Valphine: "It's mindflayers." Vukradin: "She always says it's mindflayers."
- Brewbarry retrieves the spooked horse. Soma mends the cart with magic.
- Vukradin insists on returning them to Phandalin for "conflict-free gold." The party grumbles but complies.
- **(DM note):** The swirl/eye markings and psychic hum are significant plot hooks. Elara's memory being "gone now" suggests either natural forgetfulness or magical suppression. The "mindflayers" joke may be foreshadowing — or a red herring. The GM should decide which.

### Ser Kaelen's Approval
- In Phandalin, Vukradin reports to Ser Kaelen, who is pleased.
- Vukradin asks for the Gauntlet's cheer. Ser Kaelen reluctantly recites: "Steal and shield, Heart and band. We stand as one. A steadfast band."
- Ser Kaelen commissions Vukradin to write better material — the first paying commission from the Order.

### The Corrupted Grove
- Adabra pulls Soma aside privately.
- She describes a grove past Whisper Woods: once a Chauntea-blessed vibrant area, now twisting unnaturally.
- Trees in impossible shapes, colors bleeding wrong, creatures behaving strangely (too many eyes, jerky movements).
- Adabra hints at interventionist involvement.
- Soma agrees to investigate. Vukradin hopes she asked for money.
- **(DM note):** This is a significant faction quest hook. The corrupted grove connects to the naturalist/interventionist conflict. The unnatural changes (wrong colors, impossible shapes, jerky creatures) suggest either a corrupted fey presence, a planar breach, or an interventionist experiment gone wrong.

### The Wayside Inn
- The party stays at the Wayside Inn on the High Road.
- Martisha Vinetalker is cold and calculating. Backes Dunfield the bartender has a scarred face and crooked smile.
- Cooragh Struckt the cook serves incredibly tasty, well-spiced stews and meat pies — a bright spot.
- Valphine enjoys the food.

### Road to the Coast — Unnatural Terrain
- The trail snakes through broken, hilly terrain with unnatural gullies splitting the ground.
- Vukradin notes they're too dry for river erosion — impossible naturally.
- Valphine deduces magical alteration: "Magic transforms the land to help the attackers and defenders. Whatever made these cracks is a force of magic."
- She reflects on Lathander's gifts: by giving overbrighters so much light and land, he makes them "sloppy" and enables war and suffering — and she finds this pleasant.

### Orc/Shaman Ambush — Combat
- **Opening conditions:** The party is traveling through a narrow gully with steep walls. Brewbarry detects signs of an ambush (subtle tracks, concealed armor glint). The party climbs out of the gully to higher ground and attacks from the rear, turning the ambush around. Enemy: several orc raiders with unusual discipline and an orc shaman/cleric.
- **Round-by-round highlights:** Vukradin opens with Sleep, incapacitating one orc and the shaman. Soma Wild Shapes into a brown bear and tears into two orcs. Brewbarry rages, engages head-on, delivers powerful blows, tanks incoming damage. Valphine uses her hand crossbow and Fairy Fire to illuminate enemies. The shaman recovers and reveals a surprising ability: shapeshifting into a boar. Valphine finds the tactical depth "delightful." The party wears the orcs down through combined damage.
- **Key tactical moments:** The party counter-ambushing the ambushers was excellent tactical play — they recognized the terrain trap and used it against the enemy. Vukradin's Sleep incapacitating the shaman first was smart resource prioritization. Soma's bear form absorbed hits and inflicted heavy damage. Fairy Fire giving advantage was decisive.
- **(DM tactical note):** The orcs' ambush plan was well-designed: drop a rock to block the gully exit, then rain missiles from above into a confined killing zone. The party only avoided this because Brewbarry detected the ambush. If they had walked into the trap, it would have been a deadly encounter — a good lesson in the value of perception and investigation.
- **(DM tactical note):** The shaman's boar shapeshift was an effective surprise — the party didn't expect a caster to have a melee transformation. This kept the fight interesting after Sleep neutralized the shaman's magic. Valphine's observation that this was "delightful" suggests she enjoys tactical complexity over brute force.
- **Enemy behavior:** The orcs were unusually disciplined compared to typical orc raiders (consistent with Elara and Jarek's descriptions). They set up a proper ambush with terrain advantage. The shaman used a non-standard ability (shapeshift into boar), suggesting either a specific deity's gift or training beyond typical orc shamans.
- **Spell/ability impact:** Sleep (Vukradin) — excellent, neutralized the primary threat (shaman) and one secondary threat; Wild Shape (Bear) (Soma) — excellent, provided tanking and damage; Fairy Fire (Valphine) — excellent, gave advantage to the whole party; Rage (Brewbarry) — standard but effective.
- **PC resource decisions:** Vukradin opened with Sleep instead of damage — smart control prioritization. Soma used one Wild Shape charge on bear form, committing to melee. Brewbarry raged as soon as engagement began — standard but correct.
- **Post-combat:** No note of loot or interrogation in the narrative. The party pressed on toward the Tower of Storms.

- **Backes Dunfield** — Human bartender at Wayside Inn. Scarred face, crooked smile. Minor role this chapter.
- **Cooragh Struckt** — Cook at Wayside Inn. Famous for well-spiced stews and meat pies. Valphine approves.
- **Orc Shaman** — Enemy combatant. Possesses the ability to shapeshift into a boar. Fought alongside unusually disciplined orc raiders. Possible connection to the psychic hum and swirl/eye markings that Elara and Jarek described. Killed in the ambush.
