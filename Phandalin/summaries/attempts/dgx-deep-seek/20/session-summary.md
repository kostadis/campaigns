# Chapter 20 The Orc Ambush

Date: 10-02 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party travels through dense forest toward the Logger's Camp. Brewbarry and Valphine spot an orc scout attempting to hide. Soma recognizes the scout as a lookout for a larger ambush and transforms into a bear to approach directly. The scout panics, scrambles up a tree, and signals to companions hidden in the surrounding woods. Soma climbs after him and bites savagely into his foot.

The main orc force erupts from cover — raiders and brigands who immediately begin arguing among themselves about the scout's incompetence and his family connections. One raider hurls a javelin at Brewbarry, who catches it mid-air and snaps it in two with a menacing growl. The battle descends into chaos as Vukradin's enchantments turn orcs against each other — one raider attacks his own scout, screaming about the scout's mother. Soma (bear) shoves a brigand and calls down Moonbeam on three orcs. The brigand flees. Orcs panic. Vukradin's phantasmal force makes one raider attack phantoms until he dies. The remaining raider flees but is put into magical slumber and captured.

The captured orc (Shrimpy) reveals under interrogation: his band is an independent raiding party displaced from Ice Spire by a white dragon. Their chief went mad, factions formed — some joined the Cult of Talos, others became independent brigands. The party continues to the Logger's Camp, finding it eerily deserted.

## Scenes

### The Orc Ambush — COMBAT

**Opening conditions:** The party is traveling through dense forest. Brewbarry and Valphine spot an orc scout attempting to hide. Soma transforms into bear and approaches; the scout panics, climbs a tree, and signals to hidden companions to the north. The party is strung out along a forest path, not in formation. Terrain is wooded with trees suitable for climbing, underbrush providing partial cover. Visible enemies: 1 scout + 3-4 raiders/brigands initially, with more emerging.

- The scout climbs a tree and signals to three directions while fleeing upward. Soma (bear) climbs after him and lands a bite on his foot. The scout disengages and fires an arrow into Soma from 10 feet.

- An orc raider throws a javelin at Brewbarry. Brewbarry catches it mid-air, snaps it in two, and snarls at the attacker. This is a psychologically devastating opening — the orcs expected to intimidate, not be intimidated.

- **(DM tactical note):** The javelin catch was a legendary moment but mechanically unusual. Monks have the Deflect Missiles feature for this; for a goliath barbarian/fighter, this was a dramatic flourish. Future rulings on improvised weapon catches should be consistent — if Brewbarry can do this with javelins, he should be able to attempt it with other thrown weapons (at appropriate DCs).

- Vukradin casts an enchantment on one raider, whose eyes glow. The charmed raider turns and attacks his own scout, screaming insults about the scout's mother. The other raiders immediately accuse him of treachery. The orc formation fragments — they're now fighting each other as much as the party.

- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's use of enchantment spells to trigger orc infighting was tactically optimal. The orcs' pre-existing tensions (visible from their arguing before combat) made them vulnerable to this approach. By exploiting the orcs' social dynamics rather than just their HP, Vukradin effectively removed 1-2 orcs from the fight each round through psychological disruption.

- Soma (bear) shoves an orc brigand backward, then calls down Moonbeam on three orcs clustered together. The radiant damage and area control forces the orcs to either take recurring damage or scatter.

- Valphine fires crossbow bolts from range, landing some hits but mostly missing. Her effectiveness is limited by the cover and chaos of melee engagement.

- The brigand under Moonbeam and surrounded by infighting orcs screams that "this was the worst idea he'd ever had" and flees the battlefield.

- Vukradin casts *phantasmal force* on a raider, making him believe he's surrounded by duplicates of his necklace-wearing companion. The raider attacks phantoms until the illusory damage kills him — he never lands a single blow on a real party member.

- **(DM tactical note):** *Phantasmal force* deals psychic damage each round the target believes the illusion is real. The raider spent multiple rounds attacking a nonexistent threat while taking escalating psychic damage. This is a devastating use of a 2nd-level spell against low-INT enemies who have no reason to believe their companion would betray them. The spell's effectiveness depends heavily on target intelligence — orcs are a good target; wizards would be much harder to fool.

- The remaining raider, seeing the chaos and realizing he's next, tries to flee. Vukradin puts him into magical slumber. He is captured. The scout escapes into the woods.

### Prisoner Interrogation

- The captured orc is mockingly named Shrimpy by Brewbarry. Under intimidation, he reveals: independent raiding party, displaced from Ice Spire by white dragon. Chief went mad after displacement. Faction split: some joined Cult of Talos (after shaman was killed), others became brigands, Shrimpy's group tried to stay independent.
- **(GM note):** The white dragon displacement is consistent with the dragon the party saw dive-bomb Phandalin (Chapter 16). This creates a clear ecological chain: dragon drives orcs from mountain → orcs flood valley → orcs form factions → factions menace travelers. The party is seeing the downstream effects of a single apex predator's territorial expansion.

### Arrival at the Deserted Logger's Camp

- The party arrives at the Logger's Camp with Shrimpy bound to the cart. The camp is empty — tents on a sandy beach, cabin, stacked logs, cold fire pits. Morning hour but no one present.
- **(DM note):** The deserted camp raises questions: (1) Were the loggers taken by the orcs? (2) Did they flee? (3) Is there a connection to the missing Emerald Enclave patrol? The delivery cannot be completed without a signature.

## NPCs

- **Shrimpy (captured orc)** — Independent raider. Bone necklace originally worn by the scout's ally; now the party's prisoner. Provides information under duress. Displaced by white dragon from Ice Spire. Chief went mad. Faction knowledge: Cult of Talos absorbed some orcs, others brigands, some independent. Bound to the cart to pull supplies. **(GM note): Shrimpy is a potential source of ongoing orc intelligence. His treatment by the party will determine whether he becomes a useful informant or escapes at a critical moment.**
- **Escaped Orc Scout** — Fled the battlefield during the chaos. Very likely to report to the Cult of Talos or other orc bands about the party's composition and tactics. Particularly notable: he witnessed a bear casting Moonbeam and a drow fighting alongside surface-dwellers, which will make for a confusing report.

## Items

- **Supply Cart** — Two heavy crates (dried meats, cheese, bread, ale, oil) plus Falcon's wine still on board. No one to sign for delivery. Added complication.

## Spells

- **Moonbeam** — Soma. Cast in bear form on three clustered orcs. Forced orcs to take damage or reposition. Area denial against low-HP enemies. Effective despite Valphine's disdain for its "pale imitation of sunlight."
- **Charm Person / Suggestion** — Vukradin. Turned a raider against his scout. Chaos cascaded into infighting that persisted after the charm ended — the psychological damage was self-sustaining. Excellent force multiplier against emotionally volatile enemies.
- **Phantasmal Force** — Vukradin. Created illusion of a companion attacking. Target attacked phantoms for multiple rounds while taking psychic damage until death. Flawless execution against low-INT opponents. **(DM note):** The spell requires the caster to maintain concentration and the target to believe the illusion is real. The target gets an initial save and an investigation check if they interact with the illusion. Against orcs who expect treachery, this is very effective — they have no reason to question it.
- **Sleep** — Vukradin. Used to capture the fleeing raider non-lethally. A 1st-level spell that puts creatures to sleep based on HP total. Requires the target to be low enough HP to be affected. Effective for capture scenarios.
