# Chapter 17 Blood Money, Clean Gold, and Fine Wine

Date: 9-02 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The day after the dragon incident, the party gathers at the tavern to process the fallout. Valphine reveals Sister Kayla's offer — information on the Carver's lieutenants in exchange for cooperation. Vukradin reacts with characteristic fury, refusing outright to work with anyone evil or accept what he considers stolen goods. Valphine argues strategic pragmatism (enemy of my enemy), but Vukradin storms out for air and a cigarette. Jenna Roscoe, observing from the sidelines, explains to the others that Vukradin is a man of principle who believes property belongs to its original owners, not to whoever found it.

While Vukradin is outside, two sunburned villagers — Brin Bundlewine and Giles Slipper-Shine — approach Valphine with excitement. They've heard of a drow who worships Lathander and the purifying pain of the sun, and they want to establish a chapel. Valphine agrees, and the group decides to convert Tresendar Manor into the Temple of Lathander's Searing Pain of Justice. Harbin conveniently materializes to handle the paperwork for a 50 gp "private arrangement," framing it as standard procedure. Brin and Giles will renovate for 25 gp (materials only; labor is free). When Vukradin returns, Harbin claims the temple funds were a "community donation," which Vukradin accepts without question.

The next morning, Ser Kaelen reports he's still waiting for ownership records from Neverwinter. He suggests the party investigate Mountain Toe goldmine or deliver supplies to the Logger's Camp. They choose the delivery. At Barthen's Provisions, they load two heavy crates onto a cart pulled by an ox named Vincent. Brewbarry notes the absence of wine; Barthen suggests stopping at the Falcon Hunting Lodge, known for its fine wine.

After two days traveling through Neverwinter Wood, they reach Falcon's Lodge — a fortified compound with a 10-foot palisade, two-story main building, stone tower, stables, and guest quarters. They're greeted by Corwin, the chef and retainer, and then by Falcon the Hunter himself, a man with impressive mutton chops who is delighted to have guests. He offers hospitality in exchange for musical entertainment from the party's bard.

During dinner, Falcon casually reveals he built the lodge with his share of a dragon hoard, split four ways after his adventuring party killed the beast. Vukradin explodes, accusing Falcon of stealing from the dragon's victims. Falcon is genuinely baffled — adventurers keep treasure from monsters; this is simply how the world works. Vukradin storms out again but is eventually called back to apologize (insincerely). Falcon accepts graciously.

Another guest joins dinner: Sridar, a drow artifact collector. She offers 30 gp for information about planar events the party has witnessed, and 100 gp to purchase the Talisman of Moesko, which she identifies as having a definite planar connection. The party accepts. Sridar also arranges three bottles of wine for Brewbarry as part of the deal. The conversation turns to theories about planar incursions. Sridar speculates it could be magic users, planar entities, or fools meddling with forces they don't understand. Brewbarry suggests the events might be intentional rather than coincidental, which Sridar finds intriguing.

Falcon mentions the Woodland Manse quest — offering boots of elvenkind for clearing out half-orcs — but suggests the party needs more experience first. As the party settles in for the night, Vukradin privately resolves to investigate Falcon's dragon hoard story and find the true owners of the treasure.

## Scenes

### Valphine's Revelation and Vukradin's Walkout

- Valphine reports Sister Kayla's offer: information on the Carver's lieutenants, weakening him until he emerges. Vukradin refuses to work with "evil people" or accept Kayla's "stolen" gold. He storms out for air and a cigarette.
- **(DM note):** Vukradin's absolutism is creating party friction. Valphine's pragmatic "enemy of my enemy" argument is tactically sound. The party needs a mechanism to resolve these philosophical disagreements or they'll fragment under pressure.

### Founding the Temple of Lathander's Searing Pain of Justice

- Brin Bundlewine and Giles Slipper-Shine approach Valphine with a proposal: establish a chapel dedicated to Lathander's purifying pain. Valphine agrees. The site: Tresendar Manor. Renovation budget: 25 gp (materials). Harbin handles "paperwork" for 50 gp, calling it a "private arrangement."
- **(GM note):** Harbin's 50 gp fee is almost certainly a bribe rather than legitimate filing costs. His willingness to bypass the full council suggests either personal enrichment or a desire to keep the temple's founding off official records. Worth tracking what Harbin does with that money.

### The Delivery Quest

- Ser Kaelen suggests Mountain Toe or the Logger's Camp. The party chooses the delivery. Supplies: dried meats, cheese, bread, ale, oil — two heavy crates on Vincent the ox's cart. Brewbarry notes the wine gap; Barthen recommends Falcon's Lodge.

### Arrival at Falcon's Lodge

- Two days through Neverwinter Wood brings them to a fortified hunting lodge. Corwin the chef greets them; Falcon the Hunter welcomes them enthusiastically. Hospitality for music. Soma asks about the large fence; Falcon explains orcs displaced by a dragon have descended into the valley, working with evil half-orcs from a ruined manse.

### Dinner and the Dragon Hoard Admission

- Falcon reveals he built the lodge with his share of a dragon hoard, split four ways. Vukradin accuses him of theft. Falcon is confused — to him, monster treasure is legitimate loot. Vukradin storms out, returns, delivers an insincere apology. Falcon accepts it.
- **(DM note):** This scene is Vukradin's character thesis in miniature. His absolutist ethics clash with the adventuring economy that funds the entire campaign. Falcon is not uniquely evil — he's operating on the same assumptions as every other adventurer in Faerûn. Vukradin's position is internally consistent but socially alienating.

### Sridar the Artifact Collector

- Sridar, a drow staying at the lodge, joins dinner. She collects artifacts related to planar events — her interest began after the "blink" in the Underdark released demons. She pays 30 gp for information about the party's planar encounters and 100 gp for the Talisman of Moesko.
- Valphine shares detailed information about events involving a creature collecting strange items. Sridar is extremely interested, paying the full 30 gp. This is the first time the party has been compensated for sharing their planar observations — a precedent that may encourage future information-sharing.
- The Talisman of Moesko transaction: Sridar identifies it as having a "definite planar connection." She buys it without haggling, suggesting it's worth considerably more to her than 100 gp. The party now knows the talisman was more than just a mummer's trinket.
- Sridar arranges three bottles of wine for Brewbarry as part of the deal, demonstrating diplomatic instincts — keeping the goliath happy maintains good relations with the whole party.
- Sridar speculates about planar incursion causes: magic users, planar entities, or fools meddling with forces they don't understand. Brewbarry suggests the events might be intentional rather than coincidental. Sridar finds this compelling and worth investigating.
- Both Sridar and Falcon identify as "interventionists" — they believe in actively stopping planar disruptions. This contrasts with the Emerald Enclave's naturalists who would let the world burn rather than interfere. The party now has a philosophical framework for faction divides.
- **(GM note):** Sridar's collection and knowledge make her a valuable ongoing contact. Her drow heritage and Underdark planar interest (the "blink") connects to the larger Kraken Society thread. The Talisman of Moesko's planar connection may become significant later.

### Boots of Elvenkind Quest Teaser

- Falcon offers boots of elvenkind for clearing the Woodland Manse of half-orcs. He suggests the party needs more XP first. This frames the Woodland Manse as an appropriate challenge for a slightly higher level.

### Vukradin's Private Resolution

- Vukradin secretly decides to investigate Falcon's dragon hoard story and find the original owners of the treasure. This sets up the covert mission in the next chapter.
