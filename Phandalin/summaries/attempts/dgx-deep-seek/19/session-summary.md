# Chapter 19 To the Logger's Camp

Date: 9-02 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party departs Falcon's Lodge, heading deeper into Neverwinter Wood toward the Logger's Camp. Brewbarry insists on personally dragging the cart loaded with supplies — the addition of Falcon's wine has made him fiercely possessive of the cargo. Valphine keeps a watchful eye on their surroundings.

Scouting ahead, Soma transforms into a bear to approach what appears to be a poorly concealed orc scout. The ruse fails — the scout panics, climbs a tree, and signals to companions in three directions. An ambush is underway. Soma pursues, climbing after the scout and biting his foot, though the orc manages to disengage and fire an arrow into her. The main orc force emerges, and Vukradin begins weaving enchantments: charming one raider into attacking his own scout, then conjuring a phantasmal force duplicate of another. The orcs dissolve into chaos, accusing each other of treachery while Brewbarry rages, Soma crushes with bear strength, and Valphine fires crossbow bolts.

One orc falls to the confusion; others flee. Brewbarry and Vukradin capture one survivor, whom Brewbarry mockingly names Shrimpy. Under interrogation, Shrimpy reveals his group is an independent raiding party displaced from the Ice Spire by a white dragon. Their chief went mad; some orcs joined the Cult of Talos, others became brigands, and Shrimpy's group tried to stay independent. The party binds him to the cart to pull it.

They arrive at the Logger's Camp to find it eerily silent and deserted — tents on the beach, cabin standing, but no one to sign the delivery notice.

## Scenes

### Departure from Falcon's Lodge

- The party treks into Neverwinter Wood. Brewbarry drags the supply cart, now laden with Falcon's wine. Valphine positions herself near him — keeping watch. Vukradin muses about Falcon's fraudulence and his own mission to return the ring to its "rightful owner."

### The Orc Scout Encounter

- Valphine spots a poorly hidden orc scout ahead. Soma transforms into bear and approaches, hoping to pass as natural wildlife. The ruse fails — the scout panics, climbs a tree, and signals to the north, east, and west.
- **(DM tactical note):** Soma's bear approach was a creative attempt at stealth reconnaissance. However, a bear approaching directly during an active watch isn't natural behavior — the scout's perception check was justified. The three-directional hand signals suggest a planned ambush formation, not a random encounter.

### The Ambush Springs

- Orcs emerge from cover: raiders and brigands. They immediately begin arguing among themselves — one raider berating the scout for incompetence, complaining he only got the assignment because of family connections. This internal conflict becomes a recurring theme.
- Brewbarry catches a thrown javelin mid-air and snaps it in two, establishing immediate dominance.

### Vukradin's Enchantment Cascade

- Vukradin charms one orc raider, whose eyes glow before he turns to attack the scout. The other raider screams about family betrayal. The tactical chaos this causes is significant — the orcs spend as much energy accusing each other as fighting the party.
- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's enchantment spells were the decisive factor here. By turning orcs against each other, he disrupted their formation and morale without expending party HP. The orcs' pre-existing tensions (family nepotism, incompetence resentment) made them particularly vulnerable to this approach. This is good tactical play — exploiting enemy psychology rather than just their stat blocks.
- Vukradin follows up with *phantasmal force*, creating an illusion of the necklace-wearing orc attacking his ally. The target spends his turn attacking a phantom, entirely removed from the fight.

### Combat Resolution

- Soma (bear form) climbs after the scout, bites his foot, then drops and shoves a brigand, following up with Moonbeam on three orcs. The druid is operating effectively as a frontline controller.
- Valphine fires repeatedly with her crossbow, mostly missing. Her Moonbeam craters the orcs. The orc scout flees after seeing "a drow who worships Lathander and a bear that casts Moonbeam" — the cognitive dissonance is too much.
- Brewbarry rages, swings his halberd in a 20-foot arc (misses), but stays in the fight as the party's tank.
- **(DM tactical note):** Brewbarry's rage and enlarged halberd is a good tanking strategy, but his miss rate in this fight deserves attention. Consider whether positioning or enemy AC is the issue. The orcs' tactic of hit-and-run (javelin throw, scimitar strike, then disengage) is solid for their CR — they're not standing and trading.

### Prisoner Interrogation

- Shrimpy (the captured orc with the bone necklace) provides useful intelligence under Brewbarry's intimidation: displaced from Ice Spire by a white dragon, chief went mad and joined Cult of Talos with some followers, others became brigands, his group tried to remain independent.
- **(GM note):** The white dragon's displacement of orcs from Ice Spire connects to the broader campaign ecology — the same white dragon that attacked Phandalin (Chapter 16) is driving orc migration into the valley. This creates a cascading refugee/crisis situation the party is now experiencing from the ground level.

### Arrival at the Deserted Logger's Camp

- The camp is silent. Tents on a sandy beach along the river, cabin with stacked logs, old stone foundations where older cabins were torn down. No one to sign the delivery notice. The party must investigate what happened to Tribar Wester and the loggers.

## NPCs

- **Shrimpy** — Captured orc. Bone necklace, independent raider displaced from Ice Spire. Chief went mad. Factions fractured between Cult of Talos and brigands. Offers information under threat of death. Brewbarry names him Shrimpy and binds him to the cart to pull supplies. **(GM note): Shrimpy is a potential recurring NPC. His knowledge of orc factions, Cult of Talos movements, and the Ice Spire situation is valuable. Whether he becomes a loyal retainer or escapes at the worst moment depends on how the party treats him.**
- **Orc Scout** — Escaped. Witnessed the party's capabilities, including a Lathander-worshipping drow and a Moonbeam-casting bear. May report to the Cult of Talos or remaining independent orc bands.
- **Orc Raider (charmed, deceased)** — Fell to the confusion caused by Vukradin's enchantments. His death was caused as much by his companions' distrust as by party damage.
- **Brin Bundlewine and Giles Slipper-Shine** — Mentioned off-screen. The temple renovation is proceeding without the party present.

## Items

- **Supply Cart** — Two heavy crates (dried meats, cheese, bread, ale, oil) plus Falcon's wine. Destination: Logger's Camp. Currently parked at deserted camp.
- **Javelin (snapped)** — Caught mid-air by Brewbarry and snapped. Theatrically destroyed rather than salvaged. Pure dominance display.

## Spells

- **Moonbeam** — Soma. Cast in bear form on three clustered orcs. Valphine dismisses it as "a pale imitation of the searing pain of Lathander" despite its effectiveness. Damages multiple enemies over time and forces them to reposition. Good battlefield control spell.
- **Charm Person / Suggestion (enchantment)** — Vukradin. Used to turn an orc raider against his own scout. The charm broke after the initial attack, but the psychological damage to orc morale persisted. Effective use of low-level control.
- **Phantasmal Force** — Vukradin. Created illusion of the necklace-wearing orc attacking a different orc. Target attacked the phantom, losing his combat turn. Combined with the earlier charm, Vukradin controlled the orc action economy through multiple rounds. High-value use of a 2nd-level slot.
