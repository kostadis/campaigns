# Chapter 22 When Boars Are More Than Just Boars

## Summary

The party returned to Falcon Hunting Lodge with rescued companions, including the disoriented Teega the Smith and two freed Emerald Enclave guards. Teega suffered memory gaps from the Wayside Inn and described being called by "the Kraken," which promised peace. The party determined her mind had been manipulated through psionic means. Falcon then revealed that Mountain Toe's Goldmine, fifteen miles northeast of Phandalin, suffered from poisoned waters. He offered the party a share of the mine if they investigated and resolved the poisoning. After debating priorities, the party chose to pursue the Woodland Manse quest instead, which promised magical boots.

The party traveled ten miles through the forest to reach the Woodland Manse. At the foggy clearing, Soma communicated with wild boars and learned that "new boars" had arrived recently that did not speak proper boar language. The party suspected an Anchorite of Talos in disguise and tested their theory. When Brewbarry summoned radiant moonlight to force the creature to drop its disguise, combat erupted. The Anchorite shouted to alert reinforcements, then retaliated with a lightning bolt that badly wounded Brewbarry as the battle continued.

## NPCs

### Teega the Smith
A woman rescued from capture, Teega worked at the Wayside Inn before her kidnapping. She suffered memory gaps and described being called by "the Kraken," which promised peace. The party determined her mind had been rewired through psionic means. Valphine attempted to intimidate her about following the Kraken cult, while Vukradin offered her work as a blacksmith for the Church of Lathander. She declined the immediate offer but expressed interest in returning to the Wayside Inn first.

### Falcon
A wealthy merchant and friend to Vukradin, Falcon held a share in Mountain Toe's Goldmine as part of a Neverwinter mining consortium. Poisoned waters had strained his finances considerably. He offered the party a share of the gold mine if they investigated and resolved the poisoning. He provided clear directions to the Woodland Manse and advised the party to stick to the trail.

### Anchorite of Talos
A cultist disguised as a wild boar near the Woodland Manse, this creature was revealed by Brewbarry's radiant moonlight beam. Upon revelation, it retaliated with a devastating lightning bolt that struck Valphine, Soma, and Brewbarry, leaving Brewbarry badly wounded. It shouted to alert other inhabitants of the manor to the party's presence.
