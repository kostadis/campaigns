# Chapter 25 Where Reality Unravels: Breaching Axeholm

## Summary

The party arrived at Axeholm to secure it as an evacuation site. Soma's rat familiar found a winch to raise the portcullis while strange sounds—ticking and scratching—emanated from within. The main hall revealed grotesque out-of-phase dwarves warped by planar anomaly. Valphine called upon Lathander and Soma unleashed radiant and force magic in combat. The dwarves' paralysis ability twice trapped Soma, nearly killing her as the session ended.

## Scenes

### Entering Axeholm
The party reaches the fortress.
- Soma summoned a rat familiar to scout
- The rat discovered a winch mechanism inside the entrance
- The party heard a constant low-frequency ticking and scratching of quills on parchment
- They raised the portcullis using a rope
- Brewbarry smashed through an inner portcullis door

### The Corrupted Hall
The party confronts the anomaly.
- The main hall had a forty-foot-high ceiling with thick stone pillars
- Strange spatial distortions existed where distances seemed curiously out of phase
- The light appeared mathematically cold and unnatural, refusing to flicker as normal flames would
- Creatures emerged with parchment-like skin, gold coins embedded in their flesh, and mouths sewn shut
- Soma's staff revealed a planar anomaly had warped reality and driven inhabitants mad

### Combat
The party fights the dwarves.
- Valphine called upon the glory of Lathander, causing creatures to flee
- Soma unleashed beams of radiant moonlight and waves of force
- Vukradin struck with his rapier, causing one dwarf to blink out of existence
- Brewbarry transformed into Big Brewbarry and cleaved through creatures

### The Paralysis Horror
The dwarves' devastating ability emerges.
- One dwarf paralyzed Soma, who experienced being packaged in a box, sealed, and delivered
- Valphine spoke words of divine healing that broke the paralysis
- Soma transformed into a bear and tore through attackers
- Brewbarry resisted paralysis, hearing a voice say "incorrect packaging"
- Another dwarf paralyzed Soma in bear form as the session ended

## NPCs

### Soma
Soma scouted with her rat familiar and detected planar corruption with her staff. She fought with radiant and force magic, was paralyzed twice, and transformed into a bear.

### Valphine
Valphine called upon Lathander to scatter creatures. She spoke words of divine healing that broke Soma's paralysis.

### Brewbarry
Brewbarry smashed through the portcullis door and transformed into Big Brewbarry, cleaving through creatures.

### Vukradin
Vukradin struck with his rapier, causing one dwarf to blink out of existence.
