# Chapter 12 Naturalists and Interventionists

## Summary

The party arrives at a corrupted grove where a twisted Treant, once the Guardian of the Grove, has become infected by planar corruption. Lyra of the Emerald Enclave calls for help, explaining that the Treant was a good tree until planar intrusion poisoned it. The party engages in combat against both the corrupted Treant and its blighted vines while Enclave members remain trapped. Through the battle, character motivations begin to reveal themselves: Valphine celebrates the pain inflicted by divine magic, Vukradin employs theatrical flourishes mixed with actual combat, and Brewbarry demonstrates surprising resilience against the Treant's corrupting aura.

The party attempts a dangerous ritual on four stones to seal the planar rift. The ritual requires six successes with a tolerance of three failures; as the battle rages and the ritual progresses, they accumulate successes and failures. Soma, confronted by the moral paradox Lyra's story presents—that naturalists view this corruption as natural while interventionists see it as a blight—struggles with her philosophy. Lyra recounts the history of this conflict, dating back to disagreement over whether the Emerald Enclave should have intervened in the War of the Dragons to prevent Tiamat's summoning. That intervention, she explains, led to Tiamat's defeat but also to consequences including the War of the Giants.

Despite the party's efforts, their final ritual attempts fail. The planar wells begin to expand violently, a screaming pain rips through everyone's heads, and the rift grows stronger. As they retreat from the grove, a shadowy, phased creature emerges from the chaos—a puppet being controlled by something unseen. It collects ingredients from the Treant's corpse and vanishes back into the planar tear. Soma realizes the stakes: this corruption is not random or natural, but calculated and purposeful. Conflicted between her mentor Adabra's naturalist philosophy and what her own senses and staff reveal about unnatural intrusion, she determines she must confront Adabra for answers.

## Scenes

### The Corrupted Grove
#### The party enters a twisted clearing where an unnatural Treant holds Emerald Enclave members captive.
- Soma arrives in bear form and detects something fundamentally wrong with the air, trees, pools of iridescent sap, and the Treant itself.
- The Treant stands at the grove's heart, limbs bent in impossible geometries, dripping unnatural sap into a dark pool.
- Lyra calls out: "My name is Lyra. Help us! We're out of the Emerald Enclave! This thing! It was the Grove's Guardian, but it has been corrupted. We are trying to cleanse her of a planar infection when it turned on us. Please, fight it! Don't let the corruption spread!"
- The party realizes blighted vines hold the Enclave members and prevent spellcasting.
- The Treant speaks, claiming ownership: "Stay out of my grove. If you stay out of my grove and let me be, then I will not attack you."

### The Battle Against the Treant
#### Combat erupts as the party fights the corrupted guardian and its thorny vines.
- Vukradin attempts to command a vine with no effect.
- Soma rushes toward the blighted brambles but is struck for four points of damage; her counterattack against the vine misses, then she hits herself.
- Lyra, witnessing Valphine reveal herself as a drow cleric of Lathander, expresses shock: "A cleric of a drow that is a cleric of Lathander? Can this be?"
- Valphine responds simply: "Glorious lightbringing pain."
- Soma lands a solid blow, freeing Lyra and another Enclave member.
- Lyra announces: "We need to complete the ritual on the stones to clear… to seal the rift."
- Brewbarry grows in size and swings at a vine but misses.
- Brewbarry steps into the Treant's aura of corrupting energy and successfully resists its madness influence.
- Valphine casts Guiding Bolt at the vines, striking them and weakening them significantly.
- Vukradin's Starry Whip strikes the corrupted Treant.

### The Ritual Begins
#### The party attempts to perform Lyra's ritual while continuing combat.
- Lyra explains the ritual requires six successful actions with a tolerance of three failures.
- Vukradin attempts the ritual and fails immediately; the planar energies exact a toll, inflicting pain on him.
- The Treant declares: "You think I'm wrong! You think there is something wrong with me! That I must be changed back! I am… happy to be powerful and dangerous!"
- The Treant attacks an Enclave Warrior, nearly killing him with a single blow.
- Brewbarry cleaves another vine and damages the Treant while shrugging off the madness aura.
- Soma drops her bear form and successfully completes a ritual action.
- Vukradin heals the dying Enclave Warrior, pulling him back from death's door, then attempts the ritual again with success.
- Brewbarry makes a successful ritual attempt.
- Soma attempts the ritual and fails; the planar energies inflict more pain.

### The Interrogation and Revelation
#### Soma questions Lyra about the nature of the conflict between naturalists and interventionists.
- Vukradin interrogates Lyra about her plan, and she admits: "I don't think I thought this through very carefully."
- Vukradin responds: "Understatement of the year."
- Lyra explains divisions within the Enclave: "There was talk amongst the emerald enclave that we should do something. Some said we should do nothing, saying it was part of the natural world, and others said it was not."
- Soma uses her staff, which glows and reveals that the Treant, pools, and bushes are all unnatural.
- Soma asks: "What is an interventionist? And what will happen here if we fail?"
- Lyra sits and begins a history: she describes a longstanding debate about the boundary of the natural world, dating back to disagreement over whether the Emerald Enclave should intervene in the War of the Dragons.
- Delaan Winterhound, the wisest of the Enclave, was persuaded by Malfire, a hero of the War, to join the fight against Tiamat's summoning.
- Lyra explains: "The naturalists see what you see here and see nothing wrong. I see what I see here and see an alien force corrupting our world."
- Soma declares: "This tree ate people. Trees do not eat people."
- Valphine quips: "Sunlight is not natural," prompting glares from both Soma and Vukradin.
- Lyra explains the tree was poisoned by the planar scar and no longer sustained by sunlight but by the planar pools.

### The Ritual Fails
#### The final ritual attempts collapse, and the planar rift expands violently.
- Soma recalls the tormented deer and makes her decision to complete the ritual successfully.
- The party succeeds with one ritual action, then experiences another failure.
- Soma's final ritual attempt fails.
- Screaming, searing pain rips through everyone's heads.
- The earth shudders and the iridescent pools expand in size, pulsing with violent, sickly light.
- Lyra whispers: "This did not work the way I had hoped."
- The party retreats from the grove as it descends further into planar corruption.

### The Puppet Emerges
#### A shadowy creature appears from the expanding rift, collecting artifacts from the battle.
- A phased, shimmering creature emerges from the chaos of the rift, its eyes distorted, moving with unsettling grace like a marionette.
- Brewbarry observes: "He's a puppet that's being controlled."
- The creature collects ingredients from the Treant's corpse with calculated, purposeful movements.
- The creature vanishes back into the planar tear.
- Soma realizes the corruption is not random but calculated and controlled by an unseen force.
- Soma resolves to confront her mentor Adabra for answers about the naturalist philosophy she has been taught.

## NPCs

### Lyra
Lyra is a leader of the Emerald Enclave who was trapped in the corrupted grove with other Enclave members while attempting to perform a cleansing ritual on the poisoned Treant. She calls for the party's help, explaining that the Treant was once the Grove's Guardian but has been infected by planar corruption. She participates in combat, attempting to cast Moonbeam and providing the four stones necessary for the ritual. Lyra carries the burden of the Enclave's internal schism between naturalists and interventionists, a conflict that dates back to the Enclave's controversial decision to aid in the War of the Dragons. She recounts this history to Soma in detail, explaining how disagreement over whether to intervene against Tiamat's summoning created a rift that persists through this current crisis. Despite her conviction that the planar corruption represents a blight requiring intervention, her ritual fails, and the rift expands.

### The Corrupted Treant
The Treant was once the Guardian of the Grove, a noble protector of the forest, but has been poisoned by planar infection seeping from a rift. It now displays a twisted consciousness, speaking in overlapping whispers and claiming ownership of the grove. It insists it is happy to be powerful and dangerous and refuses intervention, attacking the party and the Enclave members. The Treant sustains itself not on sunlight as it once did but on the unnatural planar pools infecting the grove. It demonstrates awareness and arrogance, finding amusement in the party's missteps, particularly when Soma accidentally strikes herself. Its voice and presence emanate corruption and madness, influencing those who draw near.
