# Chapter 9 The Beating Heart of the Storm and Whispers from the Deep

## Summary

After descending deeper into the Tower of Storms, the party finds Moesko defeated but his secrets still hold power. Among his belongings lies an opalescent conch sought by Miral, and a concealed compartment yields an iridescent talisman marked with the symbol of Talos. When the party reaches the lighthouse beacon, Valphine is temporarily blinded by its pulsating green light, but not before discovering the horrifying truth: the light comes from Moesko's own heart, still beating.

Vukradin, determined to end the curse, destroys the heart and the lightning rod. With the beacon extinguished, the party needs to replace the failed light with a proper fire. Soma volunteers to retrieve dry wood from the shipwrecks below, wildshaping into a giant toad with 39 hit points to brave the murky twenty-foot visibility. The underwater journey yields both treasures and danger: a +1 halberd from the Star Crossed Lover, but also encounters with Hunter Sharks that test Soma's endurance with eighteen, sixteen, and twelve-point damage exchanges.

As Soma surfaces with multiple locked chests recovered from two wrecks, moral tension emerges within the party. Vukradin insists the recovered treasures—a blue quartz mermaid statue, a spellbook, and a Wand of Secrets—belong to others and must be found or returned. Soma and Valphine are more pragmatic about their finds. When Miral's ghost finally manifests to receive her conch, she reveals a darker truth: Moesko was not entirely his own agent but was manipulated by something foreign that twisted his devotion to Talos into something colder and more calculating. Her final warning cuts deep: "Beware, surface dweller, the true dwellers. The true storm gathers not in the sky, but in the depths below Leilon." A giant crab appears to claim the halberd as treasure it intended to retrieve for the party.

## Scenes

### Moesko's Defeat and Discovery
#### The party searches the defeated cleric's body and chambers for clues and treasures.
- Soma notices a peculiar hole in Moesko's chest and finds the opalescent conch
- Valphine locates a concealed compartment between loose floorboards
- The compartment yields a dark, iridescent talisman etched with Talos's stylized lightning bolt
- Brewbarry's sharp vision perceives the talisman's reverse side: an inverted eye with coiling tentacles
- Valphine senses both divine and alien psionic energy emanating from the talisman

### The Corrupted Beacon
#### The party ascends to the lighthouse beacon and confronts its dark power source.
- The beacon pulses with an eerie green light
- Valphine stares directly at it and is temporarily blinded
- Before losing her sight, Valphine glimpses the truth: the light emanates from a human heart, several sizes too large
- Vukradin recognizes it as Moesko's heart and declares they must destroy it and replace the beacon with fire
- Vukradin smashes the lightning rod, then destroys the beating heart
- The light extinguishes; the air feels lighter and cleansed

### Soma's Underwater Quest
#### Soma wildshapes into giant toad form to retrieve dry wood from sunken ships.
- Transformation grants Soma 39 hit points in amphibious form
- Visibility in the murky water extends only twenty feet
- At the Star Crossed Lover wreck, Soma finds a +1 halberd clutched in a ship captain's skeletal grip
- A Hunter Shark approaches; Soma's powerful bite deals eighteen points of damage
- Additional sharks join the fray; one strike lands sixteen points of damage against Soma, reducing her to 22 hit points
- Soma defeats the second shark with a twelve-point bite
- Waterlogged timber proves unsuitable for immediate use as firewood

### Treasure Recovered from the Depths
#### Soma retrieves multiple chests and cargo from the shipwrecks.
- A locked chest clings with starfish from the Sea Urchin wreck
- A third locked chest is found in another wreck
- Soma hauls all recovered chests to shore
- Valphine's nimble fingers open the chests despite Vukradin's moral objections
- First chest: a delicate blue quartz mermaid statue
- Second chest: a spellbook
- Third chest: a Wand of Secrets with ability to detect secret doors

### Combat with the Remaining Sharks
#### The party dispatches the final Hunter Sharks defending the wrecks.
- Valphine casts Sacred Flame on one shark, dealing five points of radiant damage as it fails its save
- Vukradin focuses his lute magic to put both remaining sharks into magical slumber
- Valphine casts Fairy Fire, coating both sleeping sharks in a phosphorescent glow
- Valphine and Brewbarry coordinate an attack dealing a total of 22 points of damage to one shark
- The sleeping sharks provide tactical advantage but cannot be damaged without waking them

### Miral's Manifestation and Warning
#### The ghost of the sea elf appears to receive her conch and deliver a dire warning.
- Miral manifests as the party presents her opalescent conch
- She reveals her spirit can finally rest after receiving the conch
- Moesko was her undoing, but not entirely by his own will
- Shadows within his wine twisted his devotion to Talos into something colder and calculating
- The curse on the tower ends with Moesko's death, but a deeper threat remains
- She warns: "Beware, surface dweller, the true dwellers. The true storm gathers not in the sky, but in the depths below Leilon"
- A giant crab appears and claims the +1 halberd as treasure it had intended to retrieve for the party

### Moral Division Over the Recovered Treasures
#### The party debates the ownership and disposition of found items.
- Vukradin refuses Soma's offer of a magical cloak that changes colors, insisting it is not his to take
- Vukradin questions why they are opening other people's chests and insists they must be returned
- Vukradin searches for any markings to identify the rightful owners
- Vukradin remains frustrated by the party's more pragmatic approach to looting
- Vukradin accepts the shark meat as lawfully earned through combat
- The tension reflects Vukradin's commitment to honor versus the party's practicality

## NPCs

### Moesko
The dark cleric of Talos, Moesko is defeated before this chapter begins. His lifeless form holds the dark secrets of the Tower of Storms. A peculiar hole marks his chest, and his very heart continues to beat within the lighthouse beacon, fueling its corrupted green light. His body is thoroughly searched, yielding the opalescent conch belonging to Miral and the octopus-marked talisman devoted to Talos. His octopus-pattern armor is splint mail. Though his death ends the tower's curse, Miral's ghost reveals he was not entirely responsible for his actions—something foreign manipulated his devotion and twisted it into something colder and more calculating.

### Miral
The sea elf whose opalescent conch the party recovered, Miral appears as a ghost to receive her cherished possession. Her spirit can finally rest now that the conch is returned to her. She reveals crucial information about Moesko's enslavement: he was her undoing, but shadows within his wine twisted his devotion to Talos into something far darker than the Strage of the Stormlord. Before vanishing, she delivers a dire warning to the surface dwellers: "Beware, surface dweller, the true dwellers. The true storm gathers not in the sky, but in the depths below Leilon." Her words hint at a threat far greater than Moesko or the cursed tower.

### Miraal
The giant crab appears as the party finishes their work at the Tower of Storms, offering gratitude for the sharks they dispatched. Upon noticing the +1 halberd Brewbarry recovered, the crab claims it as the treasure it had intended to retrieve for the party as a reward for their service.
