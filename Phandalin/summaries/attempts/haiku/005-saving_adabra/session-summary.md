# Chapter 5 Saving Adabra

## Summary

The party recruits Corbin, a gaunt, haunted former bandit in ill-fitting stolen armor, as a camp worker. Soma briefly tests the sensation of inhabiting a spider's body before the party discovers a windmill on a hill with a manticore perched atop it and a woman calling for help from inside. After a tense negotiation and fierce combat, Brewbarry kills the manticore. The woman inside is Adabra, an older potion crafter with piercing eyes and a weathered face. She initially refuses to leave her windmill and potions, citing mysterious responsibilities. The party discovers that Adabra is a member of the Emerald Enclave and shares their naturalist philosophy. After Vukradin plays music that impresses her, Adabra eventually agrees to spend the night, and Brewbarry requests they lodge at the windmill. Corbin arrives, traumatized, and breaks down recounting his tragic backstory: he was a farmer from Greenmeadow in the plains east of the High Forest, with a wife named Elara and daughter Lily, until the Uthgardt barbarians of the Black Lion tribe destroyed his home, killed his family, and left him for dead. He survived and drifted south for months as a petty thief. The next morning, Adabra has a change of heart. Learning that the party shares her naturalist beliefs, she commits to coming to town and vehemently denounces the slaughter of monsters for treasure as barbarism. She insists that Brewbarry, though a barbarian by class, is a nomad and part of the natural world, not outside it.

## Scenes

### Armoring the Recruit
#### Vukradin questions Corbin's stolen armor and the party debates whether he may keep it.
- Vukradin recognizes Corbin's armor as stolen and demands to know the source.
- Corbin admits the owner was already dead and that he was a bandit facing death without protection.
- Brewbarry demands Corbin remove the armor, but Vukradin relents when Corbin asks, and promises to find him "proper fair trade armor."
- The party encounters Soma, a tortle, who has just returned from inhabiting a spider's body and notes the strangeness of human dominance in Faerun.

### Discovering the Windmill Siege
#### The party climbs toward a windmill where a manticore stands guard and a woman cries for help.
- Valphine and Soma hear the woman shouting "Help me\!" from inside the windmill.
- Vukradin proposes a scheme to kill the manticore, return for payment, and come back for the woman.
- Soma insists on saving her immediately, countering "How about we just kill the manticore and save her?"
- As they climb quietly, Corbin warns Vukradin to leave his instrument behind to protect it from damage.

### The Manticore's Hunger
#### Combat erupts as the manticore attacks, demanding human flesh and offering a deadly bargain.
- The manticore perceives the party and speaks, claiming to want "a tasty meal."
- Vukradin attempts to negotiate payment, but the manticore declares "It would take a lot\!" and "I prefer the succulent taste of human meat to the flesh of other humanoids."
- The creature fires tail spikes, injuring Valphine, then offers to spare the party if they surrender Corbin.
- Soma casts a spell to conjure a familiar to distract the creature, but this enrages it further.
- Brewbarry charges and sustains injuries as the manticore attacks him, but simultaneously strikes it with his halberd.
- The woman inside the windmill pleads "Please do something" and urges "Do something," while refusing to flee.
- Vukradin attempts a spell twice but fails both times, causing tension with Soma when he laments "I didn't study maths or chance\! I studied the arts\!"
- Brewbarry climbs to the second floor and strikes the manticore twice before it attacks his protected form; he kills the creature.

### Meeting Adabra
#### The party discovers that Adabra, the woman they rescued, refuses to leave her windmill despite the danger.
- The woman identified as Adabra is described as being past her prime, with a lean, stooped posture, calloused hands bearing "the faint, persistent scent of herbs and perhaps antiseptic," and piercing eyes burning with intensity.
- Adabra questions whether the manticore is truly dead, uncertain if the impact was the beast or one of the party members.
- She had been under siege for only a short time but was determined not to "tempt fate."
- Adabra refuses to leave, insisting "she must stay here to craft her potions."
- She presents a special potion and describes it as "my special potion of healing with extra scent and taste that makes it go down sweeter\!"
- Soma remarks that the description sounds like "a bath water potion."

### The Emerald Enclave Revealed
#### Adabra reveals her allegiance to the Emerald Enclave and her naturalist philosophy, which aligns the party's goals.
- Adabra asks Soma "In the great schism, are you on the naturalist or interventionist side?"
- Soma responds "Naturalist," and Adabra's aura shifts visibly.
- Adabra speaks with zeal about the Emerald Enclave and declares "The naturalists, like us, think that the meta-verse is part of the natural order."
- She explains that "The natural order requires that they come here" when discussing the villagers.
- Soma notices that Vukradin carries "the look of a gambler" when considering a spell attempt.
- Vukradin admits "I would survey my customers and ask them to give me a score out of ten. I would only accept money from those who gave it a 10/10 rating. Brewbarry was, in fact, the only customer that ever paid me."
- When Vukradin plays music for Adabra, she exclaims "How wonderful\! This is truly blessed music\! Aasamirs are truly touched by the Celestial planes."
- Adabra gives them a note, which Vukradin reads aloud: "Thank you, Townmaster Harbin, but I will not be leaving my home. Thank you for your help. Please reward these heroes."
- The note continues with "a long-winded description of how to take some medicine," revealing Adabra is healing someone.
- Brewbarry asks if the party can spend the night, and Adabra squeals in delight, promising "I will make a fantastic meal\! But I need help."

### Corbin's Tragedy and Trust Questioned
#### Corbin arrives traumatized, and Vukradin continues to interrogate about theft while the party explores Adabra's home.
- Vukradin asks if Corbin robbed Adabra, but she insists "no one has ever robbed her."
- Corbin breaks down, confessing "I didn't want any of this. I woke up with nothing but the clothes on my back after my family was killed. I came to town, and nobody wanted another broken man."
- Adabra shows them "an old dwarven house" and explains she has a deed to the land and has tended "the dwarf cairns for the dead."
- She protests "I am quite human, not a grave robber. That's why the manticore wanted me\!"
- Soma corrects her specist comment, noting the manticore "thought you were just meat. That's why he wanted you."
- Adabra reveals that manticores actually do have a preference for human flesh.
- During dinner, the party is served "hearty, fresh-baked bread, a vegetable and herb stew made with local fungi, foraged vegetables, berries, and wild greens."
- Corbin recounts his full story to Soma: he was "a farmer from a small, forgettable village called Greenmeadow, nestled in the plains east of the High Forest," with a simple life working his father's land, married to "a woman named Elara," and with a "young daughter, Lily, who loved the wildflowers that grew at the edge of our fields."
- When the Ordning broke and giants became emboldened, the "Uthgardt barbarians of the Black Lion tribe descended on Greenmeadow" in an act of "pure destruction, a wildfire in human form."
- Corbin fought with only "a wood-axe and a farmer's grit" but could not save his home or family.
- He "only survived because I was knocked unconscious by a charging barbarian's shield and left for dead in a ditch, buried under the body of my neighbor."
- Awakening "to silence and ash," he drifted south for months as "a ghost haunting the roads," committing petty theft to survive.

### The Naturalist's Change of Heart
#### Adabra reveals her true position and decides to join the party, rejecting the slaughter of monsters for treasure.
- The next morning, Adabra announces "I've got work to do, and you have to go get your reward\!"
- Soma replies "You were the real reward," but Vukradin mutters "I don't think we will."
- Adabra expresses shock and threatens "if he doesn't, come get me and I will give him a piece of my mind\!"
- Soma explains that Vukradin "will only take money from so few sources and he's desperate."
- Vukradin confesses "Because everyone's pressuring me to kill monsters, take their money. I am not going to do that. That's stolen property. Rescuing you is organic, conflict-free, fair-trade gold."
- Adabra has a revelation: "That's such a wonderful change from all the slaughtering of the interventionist faction of the Emerald Enclave. I'm going to come and support you."
- She declares that monster slaughter is "an act of barbarism and vandalism\! It's a CRIME\!"
- When Brewbarry is hurt by the word "barbarian," Adabra insists "He is not a barbarian. That's a word for those who destroy the natural world. He is a nomad."
- She elaborates with passion: "The word barbarian implies that there are people who are civilized and those who are uncivilized and that the uncivilized are outside the natural order of things. There is no such thing."
- She concludes that Brewbarry is "part of the natural world. You are not opposed to it."
- Vukradin asks with the tone of a child receiving good news, "I heard you were going to come with us?"
- Adabra confirms "Well, yes. You are fellow naturalists\!"

## NPCs

### Corbin
Corbin is a gaunt man in his late twenties, haunted and hollow-eyed from staring into the abyss, with matted dirt-brown hair, a patchy beard, and pale blue eyes that shift between distant focus and feral intensity. He was once a farmer from Greenmeadow, a village in the plains east of the High Forest. He had a wife named Elara and a daughter Lily who loved wildflowers. When the Ordning broke and the Uthgardt barbarians of the Black Lion tribe destroyed his village in an act of pure destruction, Corbin fought with only a wood-axe and farmer's grit to protect his family, but survived only by being knocked unconscious and left for dead. He awoke alone to silence and ash, then drifted south for months as a ghost on the roads, committing petty thefts to survive, which gradually eroded his former self. He eventually joined a bandit crew and now works for the party as a camp hand, wearing scavenged leather armor. During the night at Adabra's windmill, he breaks down recounting his trauma to Soma, speaking of his lost life with visible despair about his destroyed family and the impossibility of his former world.

### Adabra
Adabra is a woman past her prime, difficult to age precisely, with a lean and slightly stooped posture as if weighed by endless burdens or years of work. Her calloused hands bear the persistent scent of herbs and antiseptic, suggesting both healing and great precision. Her face is weathered and set in a grim, unyielding line, hard as stone, but her piercing eyes burn with inner intensity hinting at fanatical convictions and ruthless determination. She is a potion crafter who lives in a windmill atop Umbrage Hill, where she has a deed to the land and tends to dwarven cairns. She is a member of the Emerald Enclave's naturalist faction, which aligns with preserving rather than intervening in the natural order. When initially discovered, she was under siege by a manticore but had been trapped only a short time and was determined not to tempt fate. Despite initial reluctance to leave her windmill, where she insists she must stay to craft potions, her commitment to the naturalist cause is deep. She is angered by what she views as the barbaric slaughter of monsters for treasure, viewing such acts as crimes against the natural order. She makes a distinction between the civilized and uncivilized, believing such divisions are false, and insists that even barbarians like Brewbarry are part of the natural world. She serves the party hearty food including fresh-baked bread and vegetable stew with foraged ingredients, and eventually agrees to come to Phandalin when she learns the party shares her naturalist philosophy.

### Manticore
The manticore is a creature perched atop the windmill at Umbrage Hill who speaks clearly and demonstrates preference for human flesh over other humanoids. When confronted, it requests payment in exchange for sparing the party, claiming "It would take a lot\!" but ultimately becomes enraged and attacks. During combat, it fires tail spikes, flies to a second floor of the windmill when injured, and demonstrates both intelligence and predatory ruthlessness before being killed by Brewbarry.
