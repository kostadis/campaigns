# Chapter 23 From Retreat to Redemption (and Cheese)

## Summary

At the Woodland Manse, the party faced a "shapeshifting cultist of Talos" unleashing lightning and a second cultist in "wild bull" form striking with "crackling bolts of electricity." Recognizing they were "dangerously outnumbered," they retreated and traveled thirty miles back to Phandalin.

Vukradin called a council meeting reporting the Talos cultists and their "lightning-wielding" capabilities. Adabra became enraged about their intervention against the Kraken cult, calling it a "temporal violation and planar distortion" that triggered "cascading catastrophic events across the multiverse." She stormed out in "righteous fury."

Valphine's church sermon created unintended consequences. Her message about "experiencing the glorious pain of Lathander's light" was misinterpreted—congregants blinded themselves staring at the sun, then had Valphine cure the blindness as proof of devotion. She placed a severed cultist head on a pike outside the church as warning.

The party escorted Don-Jon Raskin, newly hired overseer for Mountain's Toe Gold Mine. En route they found six orcs "killed by extreme cold," confirming white dragon presence. At the mine, wererats in "hybrid form" claimed ownership. Zeleen Varnaster, leader of the Whiskered Gang, explained they were displaced from Shrine of Savras by "sixteen orcs and several ogres" and proposed the party clear them. She refused to help, claiming she was "too valuable to risk."

Vukradin negotiated settlement. When Valphine suggested a "cheese-for-labor arrangement," Zeleen found it "stereotypical and offensive" but admitted interest in cheese from "the special cow at Butterskull Ranch." Don-Jon initially objected to wererats in the mine, but Vukradin persuaded him. The party struck a deal for "regular cheese shipments from Butterskull Ranch" in exchange for mine occupation, avoiding battle.

## NPCs

### Adabra
Adabra became enraged learning of the party's Kraken cult intervention. She called it a "temporal violation and planar distortion" triggering "cascading catastrophic events across the multiverse," arguing interventionists were wrong. She stormed out in "righteous fury," dividing the council.

### Ser Kaelen
Ser Kaelen of the Order of the Gauntlet reported that Candlekeep research had identified original owners of magical items the party found. He expressed concern about the Cult of the Kraken and psionic creature involvement, emphasizing the research cost the Order considerable time and money.

### Jenna Roscoe
Jenna Roscoe of the Lords' Alliance dismissed the Kraken Society as merely a harmless social club for wealthy elites. Valphine sensed she knew more than she revealed.

### Don-Jon Raskin
Don-Jon, a verbose and boastful man, was hired as Mountain's Toe Gold Mine overseer by a Neverwinter consortium. He regaled listeners with tall tales of past work as a prospector, miner, fur trader, privateer, and whaler, believing he had been accepted by a barbarian tribe when he was actually "seen as an idiot." His friendly demeanor helped defuse tensions with the wererats.

### Zeleen Varnaster
Zeleen Varnaster, leader of the Whiskered Gang, explained displacement from Shrine of Savras by "sixteen orcs and several ogres." She proposed the party clear them but refused to help, claiming "too valuable to risk." She found Valphine's "cheese-for-labor arrangement" "stereotypical and offensive" but admitted interest in cheese from "the special cow at Butterskull Ranch." She agreed to occupy and work the mine in exchange for regular shipments.
