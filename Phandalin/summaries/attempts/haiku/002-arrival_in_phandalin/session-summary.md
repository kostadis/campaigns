# Chapter 2 Arrival in Phandalin

## Summary

Vukradin, a bard struggling to earn coin playing music in Neverwinter's First Flophouse, decides to pursue adventuring after learning from a Wintershield watchman that the town of Phandalin offers treasure and payment for clearing the area of monsters and evildoers. The watchman explains Lord Neverember's strategy to pacify the hinterland using adventurers before establishing formal law and order. Vukradin recruits Brewbarry, an Uthgardian barbarian, and encounters Valphine Sotorra, a drow cleric of Lathander, who joins their venture. They are later guided to Phandalin by Soma, a tortle druid of Mieliki.

Upon arriving in Phandalin on the 8th of Taraskh, the party learns about the modest mining town from Soma: it consists of forty or fifty log buildings surrounded by crumbling stone ruins, with residents who are farmers, stonecutters, blacksmiths, traders, and prospectors. At the Stonehill Inn, Vukradin performs music in exchange for food and learns of three available quests from the townmaster's board. After selecting the Dwarven Excavation Quest for its highest payout, the party travels to ancient dwarven ruins in the mountains, where they meet shield dwarf prospectors Dazlyn Grayshard and Norbus Ironrune. The dwarves reveal a temple of Abbathor hidden behind their dig site and ask the party to investigate something that frightened them, offering to split any treasure found. Tempted by the prospect of sending stones for musical composition, Vukradin agrees despite moral reservations about the excavation.

The party explores the temple ruins but encounters a group of orcs instead of the expected threat. Vukradin attempts to parley peacefully, an approach that nearly gets him killed multiple times. Valphine heals his wounds while Brewbarry rages and eliminates most of the orcs with his halberd. Soma's poison spray proves decisive, and the party narrowly survives. After the battle, the dwarves reward them with a meal, and the party begins their return to Phandalin.

On the road back, they encounter a hungry ogre who attacks when Vukradin offers conversation instead of caution. Despite Vukradin's initial cowardice and several near-fatal strikes against him, the party defeats the creature through coordinated effort. Back in Phandalin, the party collects payment for the warning delivered and then visits the Lionshield Coster to sell orc armor and axes. They learn from Linene Graywind that the store's interdimensional supply chain has been disrupted, though she hints at strange magic disrupting navigation in Neverwinter Wood. The party spends another evening at the Stonehill Inn before departing the next morning for the Gnomengarde Quest.

The journey to Gnomengarde leads south through broken hilly terrain toward the Sword Mountains. The party encounters another ogre blocking a narrow mountain valley path. This ogre expresses hunger specifically for turtle, apparently the brother of the ogre they killed before. Despite Vukradin's failed attempt to intimidate or negotiate, the ogre charges, and the party begins combat. Valphine uses a magical bullet found from the previous ogre to knock it prone as the chapter ends mid-battle.

## NPCs

### Vukradin
Vukradin is an Aasimar bard struggling to earn a living through music. Unable to find appreciation for his artistic work in Neverwinter, he convinces Brewbarry to join him in adventuring as a means to fund his musical pursuits and make "honest coin." Throughout the chapter, Vukradin demonstrates both moral conviction and naiveté, repeatedly attempting to parley with creatures rather than immediately engaging in combat, which nearly costs him his life multiple times. Despite his ineffectiveness in direct combat, his determination and idealism drive the party forward. He is deeply concerned about the ethics of treasure-taking and repeatedly questions whether adventuring involves theft, though he eventually accepts the party's need to survive.

### Brewbarry
Brewbarry is an Uthgardian barbarian with distinctive tribal tattoos who initially meets Vukradin at the First Flophouse. He appreciates Vukradin's music and agrees to join the adventure. In combat, Brewbarry proves devastatingly effective with his halberd, consistently dealing the most damage against enemies. He develops a protective instinct toward Vukradin, rushing to heal him after battles and raging when the bard falls in combat. His simple statement, "I like Vukradin music. My tribe goes boom, boom, boom with a drum. I don't like drums. I like Vukradin," encapsulates his loyalty.

### Valphine Sotorra
Valphine is a drow cleric of Lathander, a paradox of her race and faith. Having fled the madness of Menzoberanzan and the Underdark, she experienced her religious awakening upon seeing the sun and became devoted to the Morning God. She recognizes the duplicity of Lathander's clerics in advancing their god's power while preaching humility, and she struggles to accept their teaching that violence and assassination are wrong—especially when killing monsters is permitted. Throughout the chapter, she remains skeptical of Vukradin's pacifist approach but dutifully heals him after battles. Her moment of ecstatic joy at the pain of being struck by Soma's ice knife hints at her lingering connection to Drow culture's reward of pain and survival. She proves effective in combat, though her divine magic is inconsistent in its results.

### Soma
Soma is a tortle druid of Mieliki who meets the party on the road and serves as their guide to Phandalin. She possesses extensive knowledge of the region's geography and dangers, including the rumored white dragon that discourages bandits. Throughout the chapter, she demonstrates practical wisdom and frustration with Vukradin's naive approach to dangerous creatures, muttering that "the kid is going to get us killed." Her combat contributions are substantial—her Thornwhip, poison spray, and ice knife are often the most effective attacks. She accidentally harms Valphine with an ice knife but immediately heals her. By the chapter's end, she has grown accustomed to the party despite their imperfections, noting they "aren't a well-oiled machine yet."

### Toblen Stonehill
Toblen Stonehill is the proprietor of the Stonehill Inn, a native of Triboar who came to Phandalin intending to prospect but discovered he had greater aptitude for running an inn. He is friendly and flexible, allowing Vukradin to pay for meals with musical performances. He warns the party about orc threats and mentions the rumor of a white dragon in the region. He directly encourages the party to consult the townmaster's quest board for paid opportunities.

### Harbin Wester
Harbin Wester is the townmaster of Phandalin. He initially hides in his stone townmaster's hall, shouting defensive warnings about dragons and his bony, undesirable body. He posts three quests on the job board and awards payment to adventurers who complete their assigned tasks. His conversation with Vukradin about the dwarven prospectors reveals tensions between the adventurer and authority, as he dismisses ethical concerns about grave robbery by invoking Lord Neverember's guarantee of tax-free plunder.

### Dazlyn Grayshard
Dazlyn Grayshard is a shield dwarf prospector and business partner to Norbus Ironrune. He and Norbus discovered evidence of an ancient dwarven settlement while searching for gold. Over several months, they cleared avalanche rubble and found a hidden temple of Abbathor. When the party arrives to deliver the dragon warning, Dazlyn proposes hiring them to investigate a threat deeper in the temple that frightened the dwarves. He offers sending stones as payment, promising to split any treasures found. He appears very pleased when the party returns, victorious over the orcs.

### Norbus Ironrune
Norbus Ironrune is Dazlyn Grayshard's shield dwarf prospector partner. He assists in explaining the temple of Abbathor and responds to Vukradin's ethical concerns about grave robbery by assuring him that found items would be returned to their rightful owners—a claim he makes with apparent sincerity. He appears satisfied with the party's completion of the task.

### Linene Graywind
Linene Graywind is the master of the Lionshield Coster outpost in Phandalin. She greets the party with genuine pleasure and purchases the broken orc axes and hide armor they offer for sale. She explains that the store's supply chain has been disrupted, forcing them to rely on ordinary suppliers rather than their previously superior network. She hints at mysterious magic that has been disrupting navigation and obscuring ancient ruins in Neverwinter Wood, suggesting ongoing complications affecting the region's commerce.
