# Chapter 26 From Out-of-Phase Dwarves to Mechanical Mysteries

## Summary

The party defeated the twisted dwarves in the mustering hall and continued exploring Axeholm's strange, space-warping interior. They discovered a platinum amulet bearing the symbol of Moradin and a potion of superior healing in a dwarven shrine, along with an empty locked storage room that puzzled them.

Their exploration led them to a grand pillared hall where they encountered a horrifying three-headed monstrosity—the corrupted Dwarven King. The creature paralyzed Soma and nearly killed her before the party, using divine intervention, illusions, and coordinated strikes, defeated it. They recovered the King's signet ring, which bore the Axeholm crest and functioned as a key.

The party continued exploring the fortress, discovering a grand dining room, a trash chute, a kitchen with functional hot water from natural springs, and finally a mysterious mechanical mosquito-like construct in the smithy whose purpose remained unknown.

## Scenes

### Battle's End and Early Exploration
#### The party finishes combat and begins exploring Axeholm's interior.
- Valphine heals the party's wounds after defeating the twisted dwarves in the mustering hall
- The group explores upper levels and discovers strange spatial distortions
- They find a ransacked room and investigate a fireplace leading to the mountain exterior
- They discover a shrine with a collapsed eastern section containing skeletal remains, a platinum amulet bearing Moradin's symbol, and a steel coffer with a potion of superior healing

### The Enigma of the Locked Room
#### An empty locked chamber raises unsettling questions.
- Valphine picks a locked door using her thieves' tools
- The room inside is completely empty, puzzling the party

### The Three-Headed King
#### The party encounters Axeholm's corrupted ruler in a devastating battle.
- In a pillared hall with a limestone throne, the party finds two more twisted dwarves
- Soma in bear form strikes one dwarf, killing it instantly
- The second dwarf is quickly dispatched
- A three-headed monstrosity—the Dwarven King—emerges from behind the throne
- The King paralyzes Soma with its claws and bites her unconscious
- Valphine channels divine energy, turning one dwarf who flees up a fireplace in terror
- Brewbarry rages and enlarges to engage the King through the doorway
- Vukradin conjures an illusion of Moradin, then a giant bear in the King's mind
- Valphine revives Soma with healing magic
- The King's attacks miss Valphine entirely; the party wears down the creature
- The party gains strength and recovers the King's signet ring bearing the Axeholm crest and functioning as a key

### Fortress Exploration and Mystery
#### The party searches remaining chambers and discovers an unsettling device.
- They discover a grand dining room with a massive stone table
- An alcove contains a trash chute descending sixty feet
- The kitchen contains dwarven implements and a functional stone sink fed by hot water from a natural spring
- In the smithy, Valphine finds a tiny mechanical mosquito-like construct with a glass jar containing blood
- Valphine subdues the construct; Vukradin identifies markings indicating it is a sampling device

## NPCs

### The Dwarven King
Once the ruler of Axeholm, the Dwarven King has been twisted into a three-headed monstrosity by the interdimensional corruption afflicting the fortress. It demonstrates terrifying speed and unnatural abilities, including the power to paralyze with its touch. Despite suffering grievous wounds from Brewbarry's defensive strikes, the King pursues its attacks relentlessly until the party defeats it. The signet ring recovered from its body bears the Axeholm crest and functions as a key.
