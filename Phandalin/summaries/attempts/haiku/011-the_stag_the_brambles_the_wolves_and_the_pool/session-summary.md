# Chapter 11 The stag, the brambles, the wolves, and the pool.

## Summary

The party ventures into the Whispering Wood and encounters a shimmering stag caught between planes. Soma uses Speak with Animals to communicate with the creature, which has lost its ability to behave naturally. The stag explains that it can no longer simply "walk and prance and eat the vegetables" and instead finds itself able to "blink" and experiencing antlers that are "Different" each time it looks at them. It reveals that "The whole forest has gotten weird. It's almost as if everything has changed." The stag mentions that "other nice people" who "spoke to me, too" claimed they would "fix it, but they haven't," and points them toward the forest's center as the source of the disturbance.

Deeper in the wood, the party encounters a blighted thicket of brambles pulsing with unnatural colors, representing what Soma identifies as "Planar leakage, bleeding." The thorny growth lashes out with supernatural tendrils, striking Soma with piercing and necrotic damage. Brewbarry charges in, raging, while Soma transforms into a bear. The group's coordinated assault subdues the regenerating thicket.

The party stumbles into a clearing where a mysterious pool reflects distorted versions of reality. Vukradin gazes into the pool and experiences overwhelming visions of himself clutching coins alongside cryptic whispers about "the numbers." Compelled to understand, he jumps into the pool and is immediately assailed by fragmented images and chaos, ultimately collapsing unconscious. Brewbarry, disregarding warnings, asks the pool "What side of the bread should I butter?" and receives a vision of himself that says to "butter both sides simultaneously." Terrified and muttering "both sides," he flees screaming. Soma subdues him in bear form and warns the group against further contact with the pool for "our own sanity."

## Scenes

### The Enchanted Stag
A magical creature caught between planes holds crucial information about the forest's corruption.
- A shimmering stag materializes before the party, its form flickering and its antlers perpetually changing
- Soma casts Speak with Animals and asks if the stag is okay
- The stag claims to have been fine "Until you walked into my territory. Why are you in my territory? Get out of my territory"
- Once the party withdraws, the stag confirms "Yes, things have not been normal for some time"
- It reveals the disturbance originated "it all started in the center of the forest"
- The stag indicates other adventurers promised to help but have not delivered

### The Corrupted Brambles
The party battles a thicket twisted by planar forces.
- Dense brambles pulse with unnatural, glowing colors that represent "Planar leakage, bleeding"
- When approached, the thorny growth lashes out with magical tendrils, striking Soma with both piercing and necrotic damage
- Brewbarry enters a rage and attacks with his halberd while Soma transforms into a bear to join the assault
- The brambles appear to regenerate until subdued by the coordinated assault
- The party moves through before the growth can reanimate

### The Wolves and Descent
Vukradin reflects while the group pushes deeper into the corrupted forest.
- The party encounters wolves with unnatural howls and glowing eyes
- Vukradin seizes their pelts as "conflict-free gold" and considers the value of magical corruption
- The supernatural encounter prompts personal reflection on his own motivations and fears
- The group continues toward the forest's center as the disharmony deepens

### The Mysterious Pool
The party discovers a planar scar that reflects distorted realities and truths.
- A pool of water reflects twisted versions of whoever gazes into it
- Valphine asks "Will I see myself happy and content?" but avoids staring directly
- Vukradin looks into the pool and sees himself clutching coins
- Overcome by curiosity, Vukradin jumps into the pool and experiences a overwhelming cascade of "people, scenes, faces looking at me all at the same time"
- He receives fragmented visions containing whispers about "the numbers" before collapsing from exhaustion
- Brewbarry disregards Soma's warnings and asks the pool "What side of the bread should I butter?"
- The pool shows him himself responding that he should "butter both sides simultaneously"
- Brewbarry is terrified by the vision and flees screaming "both sides"
- Soma transforms into bear form to subdue the panicked Brewbarry
- The group decides the pool is too dangerous to investigate further and moves on

## NPCs

### The Stag
A magical creature existing partially between the material plane and another realm, the stag inhabits territory within the Whispering Wood. Its form shimmers constantly and flickers between states, while its antlers perpetually change shape. Through communication with Soma, the stag explains that the forest's corruption has robbed it of its natural existence, forcing it to develop abilities like teleportation. It recalls a simpler time but now experiences constant supernatural changes to its form. The stag provides the critical revelation that the disturbance originated at the forest's center and mentions other adventurers who promised aid but have failed to deliver on that promise.
