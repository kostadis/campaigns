# Chapter 10 Phandalin: Old Debts and New Suspicions.

## Summary

The party returns to Phandalin to handle unfinished business, but tensions emerge over priorities. Soma insists they address the Whispering Wood, a corrupted forest where magical blight is spreading. Vukradin pushes back, focused on building his studio, though the opportunity for payment eventually sways him. The group learns from Adabra, a midwife and member of the Emerald Enclave, that the blight may stem from interventionist meddling. She expresses philosophical concerns about the Order of the Gauntlet and their pursuit of worldly change, believing instead that the multiverse requires balance and acceptance. Adabra also reveals disappointment that Vukradin chose a faction rather than pursuing music and beauty.

In town, the party confronts the limitations of their allies. Linene Graywind at the Lionshield Coster explains that services have diminished since the War of the Giants. Harbin, the Townmaster, expresses relief that the lighthouse has been cleansed of Talos worship but shows little urgency about finding a new keeper. At Stonehill Tavern, Ser Kaelen Thorn reveals that their mission to the lighthouse was no secret—an entire betting pool had formed around their survival, with Stonehill wagering on Brewbarry's devotion to Vukradin's music.

When Vukradin raises concerns about the Carver bandit, Ser Kaelen explains that this bandit hides effectively, unlike boastful criminals. The Whispering Wood lies closer than expected—merely an hour's walk from town—and strange occurrences there may relate to Adabra's interventionist theory. The knight suggests the townspeople would be grateful for investigation. When Vukradin asks about the rightful owners of treasure salvaged from wrecks at the lighthouse, Ser Kaelen promises to investigate. Valphine adds cryptically that they should ask about a secret code in one of the chests.

That evening, the party plays music together. Vukradin performs alone, earning two silver pieces. When Valphine joins him, and Brewbarry adds percussion with his halberd, the crowd's engagement increases, yielding ten more silver. The next morning brings an anonymous letter, slipped under their door. The writer, purporting to be "A Friend to True Justice" from Neverwinter, questions the Order of the Gauntlet's funding sources, hinting at connections to House Margaster and morally questionable artifact acquisitions. The innkeeper later clarifies that he had forgotten to deliver the letter upon their arrival. As the party prepares to investigate the Whispering Grove, Soma notices her staff—a gift from Merill—suddenly feels alive with unlocked magic.

## Scenes

### The Argument Over Priorities
A debate over where the party should focus their efforts—investigating the Whispering Wood or other tasks.
- Soma reports that Adabra told her about the Whispering Wood that needs cleansing
- Vukradin dismisses the quest, claiming to have "no quest or reference to any Whispering Wood"
- Soma describes Adabra's account of a magical blight: "The trees twist in the natural waves, their leaves too bright or too sickly pale, as if… almost as if the colors are wrong."
- Vukradin presses for payment, calling themselves "curse-busters"
- Soma accuses Vukradin of being a "Pebble-counter" focused only on money
- Valphine reminds Vukradin that when he publicly chose the Order of the Gauntlet, Adabra "stopped talking to you and started only talking to Soma"

### Adabra's Philosophy and Warning
The party meets with Adabra, the midwife, who shares her worldview and concerns about interventionist forces.
- Adabra describes the Order of the Gauntlet as committed to "the foolish path of imbalance"
- She explains her philosophy: "there isn't good and there isn't evil, there's just one thing"
- She warns that those who "change the nature of the world" cause "the forces of evil and good to respond" and "hurt everyone"
- Adabra expresses disappointment in Vukradin: "I thought you cared about the music. Because you were devoted to the beauty of being... But instead, you pick a side."
- She clarifies the Whispering Woods threat is not the Order of the Gauntlet but "the result of the actions of the interventionists meddling in affairs they shouldn't be"
- Soma notes that she and Adabra, like the Emerald Enclave, understand "that the multiverse is one thing"

### Lionshield Coster and Townmaster
Brief visits to establish the town's compromised position.
- Linene Graywind explains the coster cannot provide services "like before" due to "events" related to the War of the Giants
- She notes that retrieving goods "could take a while and there's no guarantee of results"
- Harbin reacts to news of the lighthouse cleansing with relief and a sarcastic tone: "You live?"
- When told the altar was sanctified, Harbin responds, "Oh, excellent"
- He deflects the party's concern about finding a new lighthouse keeper, suggesting he will report to Lord Neverember instead
- He warns indirectly: "I, personally, would not got back Lord Neverember while the town is still unsafe and your mission incomplete"

### Ser Kaelen and the Betting Pool
The party learns their mission was far from secret in Stonehill Tavern.
- Ser Kaelen appears delighted to see them and congratulates them on "a great victory"
- Vukradin reports the enemy: "It was Talos"
- He describes its nature: "Talos the god of storms and destruction? The reincarnation of Gruumsh, the Orc God?... And he had a hole in his chest. His heart was a green light that shone over the seas."
- Ser Kaelen's mood shifts dramatically, becoming concerned: "A typical shaman of Talos could not do such things. There's some hidden force behind here."
- Ser Kaelen reveals the ambush's backdrop: everyone in town knew of the mission; Spiderman organized "a betting pool on whether all four of you would come back" with side bets on casualties
- Stonehill admits to winning money: "I bet that your music would protect you. And the big barbarian who seems to adore your tunes. And I won, so here's 10 gold."
- Valphine comments dryly: "If you want to have a private conversation, having it under Lathander's watchful eye and shouting at everyone what your plans are, may not achieve your goals."

### The Carver Investigation and Whispering Woods
Ser Kaelen briefs the party on local threats and the mysterious grove.
- Ser Kaelen requests agents find and "flush out" a bandit named the Carver
- He notes that the Carver "has been hiding from our spells. And hiding from his henchmen"
- He describes the Whispering Wood as "the grove an hour's walk from here"
- He dismisses the threat: "This seemed something that only the tree huggers of the Emerald Enclave would care about. It's a blight. They will manage it."
- Adabra, he reports, "claims there is some kind of interplanar event"
- He notes that some members of the Emerald Enclave now view demons as "part of the natural world"
- Ser Kaelen admits: "We have a dragon flying about, a bandit, and orcs on the move. No, chasing down some trees where, for all I know, there are pixies is not the priority."
- When Vukradin asks about the rightful owners of salvaged treasure, Ser Kaelen promises, "Of course, I will see what I can do."

### The Factions and Funding Discussion
Soma and Vukradin discuss approaches to aid and the Order of the Gauntlet's principles.
- Ser Kaelen explains his organization's approach: "We are not the Harpers. We do not hire. We find true believers."
- When Ser Kaelen suggests the Harpers might hire them, Vukradin expresses interest
- Ser Kaelen jokes, "Next, you will tell me you want to work for the Zhentarim"
- Soma interjects that Vukradin will not do so because "They have blood gold"
- Vukradin mutters plainly: "Won't help me build my studio."

### Music and the Anonymous Letter
Evening entertainment followed by a mysterious warning.
- Vukradin performs alone and earns two silver pieces
- When Valphine joins, adding to the music's power, and Brewbarry provides percussion, the group earns "10 more silver"
- The next morning, a letter arrives directed to Vukradin from "A Friend to True Justice" from Neverwinter
- The letter questions the Order of the Gauntlet's funding, mentioning rumors of "connections to House Margaster, a name long associated with unsavory businesses in Waterdeep"
- The writer also alleges that some "reclaimed" artifacts were "not simply taken from villainous cults, but rather forcibly appropriated from minor, neutral parties"
- Vukradin notes he has heard of House Margaster as being "associated with cults of evil" during his time in Neverwinter
- Valphine appears to recognize the letter's implications, conveying skepticism toward Ser Kaelen
- The innkeeper later explains the letter "came from Neverwinter" but was simply forgotten upon their arrival

### Heading to the Grove
The party prepares to investigate the Whispering Wood.
- As they depart for the whispering grove, Soma's staff from Merill suddenly feels different: "Some magic inside it was unlocked"

## NPCs

### Adabra
The midwife and member of the Emerald Enclave plays a crucial role in the chapter despite her minimal direct appearance. She alerts Soma to the Whispering Wood's blight and later meets with Vukradin to discuss her concerns about the Order of the Gauntlet. Her worldview emphasizes balance and acceptance of natural order over active intervention. She expresses disappointment that Vukradin, whom she once supported, now prioritizes faction loyalty over music and beauty. Her warnings about interventionist meddling shape the party's understanding of the threat ahead.

### Ser Kaelen Thorn
The knight meets the party at Stonehill Tavern in full armor, initially appearing delighted but growing concerned as they describe encountering a divine avatar of Talos rather than a typical shaman. He deflects blame for their ambush by revealing that the entire town knew of their mission and had organized betting pools around their survival. Ser Kaelen describes the Carver as a particularly elusive bandit and mentions the Whispering Wood, dismissing its blight as low priority given other threats including a dragon, orcs, and banditry. He offers to investigate the rightful owners of salvaged treasures.

### Linene Graywind
The proprietor of Lionshield Coster informs the party that the shop's services have deteriorated since the War of the Giants. She indicates that obtaining goods now requires sending out "messages and feelers" with no guarantee of success or timely delivery.

### Harbin
The Townmaster reacts with relief when the party reports cleansing the lighthouse of Talos worship but shows little immediate concern about finding a replacement keeper. He suggests reporting the victory to Lord Neverember instead and warns indirectly against the party abandoning their local mission.

### Stonehill
The proprietor of the tavern/inn wins money from his bet on Vukradin's safety, crediting the bard's musical protection and Brewbarry's devotion to the music. He pays Vukradin ten gold pieces from his winnings and notes that "the only people who didn't know you weren't going were the dead."

### Spiderman
A character who organizes and announces the betting pool regarding the party's mission to the lighthouse, including side wagers on potential casualties.

### Brewbarry
The barbarian, who appears briefly in the tavern scenes, joins the party's musical performance in the evening, creating "a percussive beat hearkens to the tribal music of the Uthgardt tribes" using his halberd.

### Valphine
While a player character, Valphine functions distinctly as an NPC voice in this chapter. She coordinates with Linene and Harbin, provides strategic counsel to Vukradin about discretion in planning, and cryptically asks Ser Kaelen about a "secret code in the chest." She reads the anonymous letter's implications and mutters, "And please make sure to ask them about the secret code in the chest?" Her comment to Vukradin about having conversations "under Lathander's watchful eye" while shouting plans reflects her diplomatic and strategic orientation.

### Merill
Though not appearing directly in this chapter, Merill is referenced as having given Soma a staff whose magical properties awaken as the party heads toward the Whispering Grove.
