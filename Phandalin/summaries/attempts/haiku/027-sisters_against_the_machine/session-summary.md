# Chapter 27 Sisters Against the Machine

## Summary

The party completed their exploration of the fortress's first floor, discovering armor and weaponsmith tools, barracks with cots, and an empty armory stripped by departing dwarves. On the upper floor, they encountered six out-of-phase dwarves screaming about "efficiency and processes" and defeated them quickly.

The Chief Accountant emerged—a glitching luminous figure who spoke of "ledgers that wouldn't close and regulators dividing by zero." She called Valphine "sister of the sun," accusing her of carrying the "scent of bleach and betrayal." Her wail knocked Valphine and Vukradin unconscious, but she panicked and stabilized Valphine with blue-white static, terrified that "the auditor" would liquidate her.

The Chief Accountant revealed that Valphine's sister Aletra had promised to improve the mine's efficiency with a machine, but it broke. When the Chief Accountant complained about unbalanced books, Aletra laughed at her and likely killed her. The Chief Accountant offered the override code if they would release her and stay silent.

At the machine room, the party found a massive contraption venting purple steam. Aletra stood at the machine in a high-collared uniform with a rapier, identifiable by her scent of lavender and chemical bleach from the Sotorra poison labs. Four spectral spiders guarded the ceiling. Aletra revealed she was smuggling drow poison through the multiverse and proposed: if the party killed the spiders, she would extract her cargo and allow them to shut down the device.

## NPCs

### Chief Accountant
A glitching luminous dwarven figure with a face of shifting geometric shapes and ink shadows leaking from her eyes. She spoke of "ledgers that wouldn't close and regulators dividing by zero" and called Valphine "sister of the sun." Her wail knocked Valphine and Vukradin unconscious, but she panicked and stabilized Valphine with blue-white static, fearing "the auditor" would liquidate her. She revealed that Valphine's sister Aletra had installed a machine promising to improve the mine's efficiency, but it broke and caused chaos. When she complained to Aletra about unbalanced books, Aletra laughed and likely killed her. She offered the override code to shut down the machine if they would release her and keep the encounter secret.

### Aletra
A drow woman in a high-collared uniform, identifiable by her scent of lavender and chemical bleach from the Sotorra poison labs. She convinced the king to install her machine by promising to improve the mine's efficiency, exploiting his jealousy of the Lionshield Coster's multi-dimensional operations. She killed the Chief Accountant for complaining. She now smuggles drow poison through the multiverse using the machine and proposes that if the party kills the spectral spiders guarding it, she will extract her cargo and allow them to shut down the device.
