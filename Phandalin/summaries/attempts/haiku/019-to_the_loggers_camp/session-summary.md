# Chapter 19 To the Logger's Camp

## Summary

The party journeys deeper into the Neverwinter Wood forest, transporting a cart of supplies for Tribar Wester. Since the Falcon Hunter filled the cart with booze, Brewbarry has insisted on pulling it himself. Hours into their march, Valphine spots a poorly concealed orc scout in the bushes ahead. When Soma investigates by transforming into a bear, the scout signals to others and flees to a tree, revealing a small clearing used for ambush. This triggers combat with an orc raiding party composed of refugees displaced by a white dragon's conquest of their mountain home. During the fierce battle, Brewbarry becomes enraged and protective of Valphine. Vukradin employs charm magic to sow confusion among the orcs, turning them against each other. Soma deals devastating damage in bear form. Eventually the party defeats most of the raiding party, but one scout escapes. The party captures another orc and forces him to pull their cart. Brewbarry, after threatening the orc with death, gives him the nickname "Shrimpy." Upon reaching the Logger's Camp, the party finds the wooden structures dismantled or destroyed, leaving only stone chimneys and foundations. Tribar Wester is nowhere to be found, and a grim silence hangs over the camp.

## Scenes

### Discovery in the Forest
The party encounters an orc scout while traveling through the Neverwinter Wood toward the Logger's Camp.
- Valphine halts the group and points to a poorly concealed orc scout in the bushes ahead
- Soma transforms into a bear to investigate more closely
- The scout climbs a tree and makes hand signals to the north, east, and west, revealing a small clearing beyond
- Brewbarry stumbles through the woods, alerting the orc commander to their presence
- The orc commander berates the scout for being distracted by the bear

### Ambush and Combat
The party faces an independent raiding party of displaced orcs in the forest.
- The orc scout fires an arrow at Soma from ten feet away, striking her
- Valphine attempts crossbow shots against the orcs
- Vukradin casts charm magic on one orc, causing internal confusion and conflict
- Brewbarry, enraged and enlarged, swings his halberd in a 20-foot arc to protect Valphine
- Vukradin casts phantasmal force, convincing a charmed orc that an ally is actually an enemy
- Soma attacks with her bear form, eventually subduing the orc with the necklace of bones
- One orc scout manages to escape despite Valphine's pursuit

### Interrogation and Capture
The party captures an orc and learns about their displacement and forced servitude.
- The captured orc reveals his raiding party was displaced from their mountain home near the Ice Spire in the Sword Mountains by a white dragon
- He explains their chief fell fighting alongside half-orc cultists of Talos, fracturing the tribe
- He states that he believes orcs should work for orcs rather than for other powers
- Brewbarry threatens the orc and forces him to pull the supply cart
- When Vukradin asks the orc's name, Brewbarry gives him the nickname "Shrimpy"

### Arrival at the Logger's Camp
The party reaches their destination to find it seemingly abandoned.
- The logging camp spreads along the south shore of a river with a dozen tents arranged on a sandy beach
- A cabin stands near a deck with logs stacked under its awning
- Older cabins have been completely torn down, leaving only stone chimneys and foundations
- Vukradin realizes Tribar Wester is absent, jeopardizing his anticipated reward
- A grim silence hangs over the entire camp

## NPCs

### Shrimpy
An orc member of an independent raiding party displaced from their mountain home near the Ice Spire in the Sword Mountains by a white dragon. He was part of a tribe whose chief fell fighting alongside half-orc cultists of Talos, causing many of the tribe to scatter. Shrimpy believes orcs should work for orcs and not for other powers. He was captured during combat in the forest and forced by Brewbarry to pull the party's supply cart, after being threatened with death. Brewbarry assigned him the nickname "Shrimpy."
