# Chapter 4 The Bard, the Kings, and the Carver

## Summary

The party defeats a grotesque unnatural creature despite encountering significant obstacles in combat. Brewbarry's halberd becomes stuck and cannot be freed. Soma's poison gas dissipates harmlessly around the creature, and Vukradin's magical deterrent spell proves ineffective. During the battle, Vukradin and Soma engage in a philosophical debate about constructs and the natural world, with Soma describing them as abominations and disruptions to natural order. The conversation touches on the nature of celestial and infernal beings. Soma ultimately kills the creature with a decisive strike. The party presents the remains to two gnomish inventors, Fiddlestib and Dabblebob, who become excited at the prospect of using the entrails to build a construct.

The party is then brought to meet King Korboz and King Gnercli, the gnomish dual monarchs. Vukradin skillfully interrogates them about how their governance structure functions. Through a series of pointed questions, he exposes logical contradictions in their system—asking how they make decisions when disagreeing, whether seniority matters for kings, and what happens when a new king is elected to join an existing one. King Gnercli explains they argue until the best argument wins, with no inherent seniority. Vukradin's probing creates tension, eventually causing both kings to bicker openly with each other. The inventors, overhearing the debate, become engaged in theoretical discussions about bicameral legislatures and voting systems. King Gnercli grows angry and expels Vukradin, giving the party a magical hat as reward and ordering them to leave.

With fifty gold split among four members, the party travels to Phandalin and stays at the Stonehill Tavern. Vukradin's performance is well-received. Soma mentions a legendary play from Triboar that featured an actor named Bob who played a Demon character. Vukradin, who has attempted to obtain the script for years, expresses enthusiasm about staging a revival. The tavern keeper suggests Triboar would be a suitable place to search for it.

The following day, the party is asked to rescue a woman named Adabra for a reward of 25 gold. Vukradin argues the payment is insufficient and suggests requesting more. Soma counters that knowing they did the right thing is reward enough and expresses kinship with all life. The party departs for Umbrage Hill to complete the rescue.

During their journey on a narrow trail through rocky hills, bandits ambush the party. Soma transforms into a spider and climbs to high ground to engage enemies from above. Brewbarry climbs to an elevated position and decapitates a bandit with his halberd. Several bandits flee in fear or try to escape with stolen treasure. Valphine strikes one fleeing bandit with her crossbow while Vukradin kills another. The final surviving bandit surrenders rather than continue fighting. This bandit reveals he was forced into a life of crime by someone called the Carver and had worked under a man named Jax, though he has never met the Carver himself. Brewbarry, moved by the bandit's story of suffering, drops his weapon and apologizes for the cruelty inflicted by the Uthgardt people. The bandit accepts friendship and joins the party.

The party discovers a druidic spell scroll among the bandits' possessions. Vukradin reminds Soma that the scroll should be returned to its rightful owner rather than kept. Soma recognizes the principle and agrees not to take it.

## NPCs

### King Korboz
King Korboz is one of two gnomish monarchs ruling jointly in a dual monarchy. He complains that King Gnercli does not listen to him or believe his words. When Vukradin questions the governance structure, Korboz becomes agitated and argues that he, as the senior king, should have preferential treatment over newly elected monarchs.

### King Gnercli
King Gnercli is the other gnomish monarch. He initially attempts to defend the dual monarchy system, explaining that the two kings work together and argue until reaching a decision. He insists there is no seniority between them and becomes increasingly angry as Vukradin systematically exposes the logical flaws in their government. Gnercli eventually expels Vukradin from the chamber, giving him a magical hat.

### Fiddlestib
Fiddlestib is a gnomish inventor who becomes excited to see the party. The inventor discusses using the defeated creature's remains to build a construct. After overhearing Vukradin's debate with the kings, Fiddlestib expresses support for a bicameral legislature with a unitary executive and begins discussing ways to implement governmental reforms.

### Dabblebob
Dabblebob is the other gnomish inventor. Like Fiddlestib, Dabblebob is pleased to see the party and interested in constructing something from the creature's remains. After hearing Vukradin's questions about governance, Dabblebob proposes that having multiple kings with a voting system would be preferable. Both inventors become absorbed in discussing whether a tamper-proof voting box could be built.
