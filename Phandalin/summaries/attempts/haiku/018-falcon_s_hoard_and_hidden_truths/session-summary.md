# Chapter 18 Falcon's Hoard and Hidden Truths

## Summary

Vukradin infiltrated Falcon's lodge while his companions slept. He cast Silence and broke into a drawer, discovering wooden elven ears inscribed "my dearly beloved," a maze like "The Undercity Chorale," and a brass ring inscribed "lightning"—identifying it as from Iymrith's hoard.

At breakfast, Falcon blamed the Cult of Talos. Vukradin denied involvement, and Falcon confessed he was "not a dragon slayer." He had found only gold and the ring at the already-plundered hoard and used them to build his lodge. He maintained his false reputation because the "fantasy of dining with a dragon slayer" was "good for business" and attracted clientele who "want to believe the story."

Valphine found the deception "perfectly normal." The party gained intelligence linking the ring to Iymrith, lightning, and the god Talos.
