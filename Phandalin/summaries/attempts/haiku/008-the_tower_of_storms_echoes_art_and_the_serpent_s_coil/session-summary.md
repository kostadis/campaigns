# Chapter 8 The Tower of Storms: Echoes, Art, and the Serpent's Coil

## Summary

The party approaches the Tower of Storms and encounters three harpies whose charming songs initially threaten to incapacitate them. Vukradin swiftly casts Silence, breaking the charm and leveling the battlefield. The ensuing combat proves a "long… slow battle," with the harpies proving surprisingly resilient, but the party's coordinated efforts eventually prevail.

After a short rest, the party explores the tower's interior, discovering a 15-foot-high room with views of shipwrecks through a dirty window, frescoes depicting stormy seas and a "dark and terrible god," a stone altar carved with lightning bolts and a metal lightning rod, and a harpy nest containing a potion of water breathing. Vukradin questions taking "blood money," while the others accept the find.

The party then encounters Moesko, an Orc Anchorite clad in armor made from giant octopus hide, seated in a barnacle-encrusted chair and holding an opalescent conch belonging to Miral. The resulting battle proves fierce, with Moesko casting devastating Lightning Bolt and Thunderwave spells. Vukradin's Cloud of Daggers traps the anchorite in a 5-foot cube, while Soma's brown bear form provides raw damage. Brewbarry's charge brings Moesko to the brink of death, and Soma delivers the killing blow, claiming the conch as their prize.

## Scenes

### Arrival and Harpy Assault
#### The party confronts three harpies at the tower's entrance.
- One male harpy dressed in grey rags and two female harpies descend upon the party with charming songs
- Valphine and Brewbarry become charmed; Vukradin casts Silence to break the enchantment
- The male harpy in grey rags ceases its performance when the Silence sphere takes hold
- A female harpy charms Vukradin, who uses Bardic Inspiration to turn the failure into a success
- Vukradin strikes the female harpy, breaking its concentration and ending its song
- Vukradin engages the male harpy with daggers and finishes it with Vicious Mockery
- Valphine critically wounds the male harpy with her crossbow early in the fight
- Soma fights as a wolf spider, scaling the tower walls and dealing significant damage to a female harpy
- Another female harpy in blue rags attacks Soma; Soma retaliates for much greater damage
- Valphine kills one of the female harpies with her crossbow
- Brewbarry retrieves his handaxes "a few feet away" after the short rest

### Tower Exploration
#### The party explores the tower's interior, discovering rooms, artifacts, and clues to its purpose.
- A 15-foot-high empty room offers a view through a dirty window of shipwrecks below
- Vukradin expresses desire to repatriate the sunken possessions; Soma dismisses the idea as "colonial paradise" or "mercantile"
- The party discovers a room with frescoes depicting stormy seas and a "dark and terrible god"
- A stone altar carved with lightning bolts and a metal lightning rod occupy a prominent space
- The party finds a harpy nest containing a potion of water breathing
- Vukradin questions taking "blood money," but the others accept the potion

### The Orc Anchorite
#### The party encounters and battles Moesko, an Orc Anchorite wielding a powerful artifact.
- Moesko sits in a barnacle-encrusted chair, wearing armor made from giant octopus hide
- He holds an opalescent conch belonging to Miral
- Soma opens with a poison spray attack before transforming into a brown bear
- Vukradin casts Cloud of Daggers, creating a 5-foot cube that deals 9 initial damage
- Moesko retaliates with a devastating Lightning Bolt that burns most of the party, with Brewbarry taking full damage
- Moesko casts Thunderwave, dealing additional damage and pushing Soma back
- Brewbarry charges Moesko and lands a hit that brings the anchorite to death's door
- Soma reverts from bear form to navigate the tight space and delivers the final strike
- The party claims the opalescent conch as their prize

## NPCs

### The Harpies

Three harpies inhabit the Tower of Storms and guard its entrance. One is a male dressed in grey rags whose charming song initially incapacitates Valphine and Brewbarry before Vukradin casts Silence. At least two females accompany him, including one wearing blue rags. The male harpy's meager Intelligence suggests it will attempt charming songs again if given opportunity. All three prove surprisingly resilient in combat. Valphine dispatches at least one female harpy with her crossbow while Soma deals significant damage in wolf spider form. The party eventually defeats all three.

### Moesko

Moesko is an Orc Anchorite who dwells in the upper reaches of the Tower of Storms, seated in a barnacle-encrusted chair and clad in armor made from giant octopus hide. He holds an opalescent conch belonging to Miral. During battle with the party, Moesko proves a formidable opponent, casting a devastating Lightning Bolt that burns most party members, with Brewbarry taking full damage, and a Thunderwave that deals additional harm and pushes Soma back. Vukradin traps him in a Cloud of Daggers within a 5-foot cube, dealing 9 initial damage. Despite Moesko's resilience, Brewbarry's charge brings him to the brink and Soma delivers the final blow.
