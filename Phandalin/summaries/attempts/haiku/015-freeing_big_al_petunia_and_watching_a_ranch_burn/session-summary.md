# Chapter 15 Freeing Big Al, Petunia and watching a ranch burn

## Summary

The party encounters four brigands on the road near Conyberry. When Corbin's attempt at diplomacy fails, Soma transforms into a Brown Bear and kills the first bandit with her claws. Brewbarry dispatches the second instantly, and Vukradin uses magic to sleep the third. The fourth bandit flees. Under interrogation, the captured bandit Skippy reveals that the Carver prepared an ambush at Butterskull Ranch led by Borg the Hammer, and that Sister Kaella serves as the Carver's spymaster running a spy network in Phandalin. Despite the need for speed to prevent the trap from taking hold, Vukradin insists on returning Skippy to Phandalin, but Corbin volunteers to guard the prisoner instead, allowing the party to proceed immediately.

At the ranch, the party discovers already-dead bodies—both orcs and humans—bearing brutal wounds suggesting large, vicious attackers. Vukradin slips into the cellar through an open window and finds Big Al Kalazorn tied to a chair with a burlap sack over his head. Big Al reveals that orcs first raided the farm, but Borg the Hammer and his rot-tusk ogres arrived, killed the orcs, and seized the property. He was kept alive as bait for the party's trap. Big Al insists on recovering his prized cow, Petunia, and offers a suit of mithril chainmail as payment. After verifying the chainmail is legitimate—Big Al is a retired veteran and former Sheriff of Triboar—Vukradin agrees. Soma uses Speak with Animals to locate Petunia among the pigs.

The party settles on a hill roughly 250 feet away to observe the ranch. Borg the Hammer emerges, and immediately executes the bandit who fled the earlier skirmish at Conyberry in fury that the party did not walk into his predictable trap. He then engages in wanton destruction, burning the farmhouse, cornfields, and gardens. After venting his rage on the property, Borg splits his forces, sending the three Rot-Tusk Ogres in one direction while heading in another. The party decides to pursue Borg, seeking to track the villain to his source rather than merely destroy his ambush.

## NPCs

### Skippy
A bandit from the Carver's crew, terrified and intimidated by the party's overwhelming presence. When faced with the blood on Soma's claws, he cracks and reveals critical information: the Carver's ambush at Butterskull Ranch led by Borg the Hammer, and the fact that Sister Kaella serves as the Carver's spymaster running a spy network in Phandalin.

### Big Al Kalazorn
A large man held captive in the ranch cellar, a retired veteran and former Sheriff of Triboar. He was kept alive by Borg as bait to lure adventurers into a trap. Upon rescue, he negotiates with the party to recover his prized cow, Petunia, offering a suit of mithril chainmail as payment for its safe return.

### Borg the Hammer
A half-orc enforcer leading the Carver's ambush at Butterskull Ranch. When the party fails to walk into his predictable trap, Borg's fury erupts. He executes the bandit who fled, then engages in wanton destruction of the farmhouse, cornfields, and gardens to vent his rage. After this destruction, he splits his forces, sending the Rot-Tusk Ogres in one direction while pursuing another course himself.

### Petunia
Big Al's prized cow, marked with the stamp "Big Al's Cow." She is located by Soma through the use of Speak with Animals and is taken to safety by the party.
