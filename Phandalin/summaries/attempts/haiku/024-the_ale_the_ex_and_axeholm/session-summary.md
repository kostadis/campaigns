# Chapter 24 The Ale, the Ex, and Axeholm

## Summary

Harbin Wester presented three new quests: investigating Dragon Barrow, securing Axeholm, and exploring the Woodland Manse. He warned that the dragon and orc incursions threatened ale production and explained Neverwinter's troubled history.

The party negotiated with Qelline Alderleaf for Petunia to graze on her land. Qelline charged a "hurt price" due to Big Al's past competitive pricing damaging her farm. She agreed to reduce it to five silver pieces per week if Big Al apologized. When confronted, Big Al confessed he had been engaged to Qelline but abandoned her the night before their wedding and went to make amends.

Lyra arrived with two rescued Emerald Enclave members, discouraged about her leadership. When the party credited her with planning the rescue, she agreed to let Vukradin help with the Enclave.

Ser Kaelen informed the party that the recovered lighthouse statue belonged to Elara Seasong Meliame, attending a gala in Neverwinter. He warned of House Maragaster agents' plans to intercept it and requested the party escort it to Neverwinter for donations.

The party performed at the tavern. Vukradin energized the crowd and Brewbarry impressed with physical displays. Spiderman rewarded them with free drinks.

The party traveled to Axeholm across grassy lowlands. They found a dark passageway blocked by a rusted iron portcullis that Brewbarry could not force open. Soma summoned a rat familiar to scout through the arrow slits.
