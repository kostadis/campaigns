# Chapter 17 Blood Money, Clean Gold, and Fine Wine

## Summary

At the tavern, Valphine explained that Sister Kaella offered reward money for cooperation against the Carver. Vukradin refused adamantly, unwilling to work with evil or accept stolen goods, and stormed out. In his absence, Brin Bundlewine and Giles Slipper-Shine proposed establishing a temple to Lathander. Harbin Wester arranged paperwork to convert Tresendar Manor into the Temple of Lathander's Searing Pain of Justice for fifty gold pieces. Jena Roscoe explained that investing adventuring money back into the community cleaned blood money into legitimate improvement. Brin and Giles agreed to renovate for twenty-five gold pieces.

Vukradin consulted Ser Kaelen about tracking treasure's rightful owners. Kaelen suggested delivering supplies to the Logger's Camp while awaiting Neverwinter information. The party collected crates from Barthen's Provisions onto a cart pulled by ox named Vincent. After a peaceful journey through Neverwinter Wood, they arrived at the fortified Falcon Hunting Lodge.

Falcon the Hunter welcomed them and offered hospitality for entertainment. He explained orcs displaced by a dragon worked with evil half-orcs in a ruined manse ten miles southwest. During dinner, Falcon revealed he built his lodge from a dragon hoard split among his adventuring group. Vukradin erupted in anger, accusing Falcon of stealing from the dragon's victims. Though confused, Falcon accepted Vukradin's insincere apology.

Also present was Sridar, a drow collector of planar artifacts. She became fascinated with such items after a blink in the Underdark brought demons. When shown the Talisman of Moesko, Sridar identified its planar connection and offered one hundred gold pieces. Sridar arranged for Falcon to provide three bottles of wine for Brewbarry. Both identified as interventionists, opposed to naturalists' non-interference. Falcon offered a quest against half-orcs at the Woodland Mants for boots of elvenkind. Vukradin resolved to find the dragon hoard's rightful owners.

## NPCs

### Jena Roscoe
Observed Vukradin's departure and explained he was a man of principle who believed possessions belonged to their original owners. Later explained that investing adventuring money back into the community cleaned blood money into legitimate improvement.

### Brin Bundlewine
A pale-skinned, sunburned villager who approached Valphine with excitement about establishing a chapel to Lathander. Agreed to renovate the temple for twenty-five gold pieces, providing labor free.

### Giles Slipper-Shine
A pale-skinned, sunburned villager who shared Brin's enthusiasm for founding a temple to Lathander. Agreed to the same renovation terms.

### Harbin Wester
The townmaster and leader of the Privy Council. Arranged paperwork to convert Tresendar Manor into the Temple of Lathander's Searing Pain of Justice for fifty gold pieces as a private transaction. Later offered to buy beer for everyone.

### Ser Kaelen
Spoke with Vukradin about tracking treasure's rightful owners. Waiting for Neverwinter information and suggested the supply delivery quest.

### Corwin
The chef and retainer at Falcon Hunting Lodge who greeted the party and fetched Falcon.

### Falcon the Hunter
Proprietor of Falcon Hunting Lodge with impressive mutton chops. Offered hospitality for entertainment and explained the orc displacement. During dinner revealed building his lodge from a dragon hoard split among his adventuring group. Accepted Vukradin's insincere apology after confusion. Offered a quest against half-orcs at the Woodland Mants for boots of elvenkind, emphasizing he purchased them legitimately.

### Sridar
A drow collector of planar artifacts staying at the lodge. Became fascinated with such items after a blink in the Underdark brought demons. Identified the Talisman of Moesko's planar connection and purchased it for one hundred gold pieces. Arranged wine for Brewbarry. Identified as an interventionist opposed to naturalist non-interference.
