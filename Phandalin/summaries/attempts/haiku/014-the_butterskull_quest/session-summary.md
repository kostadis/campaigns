# Chapter 14 The Butterskull Quest

## Summary

The party plans to investigate Butterskull Ranch following a quest posted by Harbin on the town board, offering 100 gp for assessing the damage to the ranch or returning proof of the owner's death. They travel east along the Triboar Trail and encounter a merchant arriving from Conyberry. The merchant reports that the town is utterly destroyed and ransacked, having been looted years ago with nothing left to secure. Despite his warnings, Vukradin insists they investigate the ruins to find and return stolen property. Upon arrival at Conyberry, the party observes devastated structures: skeletal home frames with collapsed roofs, crumbled wattle-and-daub walls, and streets overgrown with thorny vines and weeds. They spot four brigands identified as members of Carver's gang. When Vukradin urges their aide Corbin to approach his former gang mates, Corbin refuses.

## Scenes

### The Quest for Butterskull Ranch
The party receives a quest from Harbin to investigate an orc attack on a ranch.
- Harbin posts a quest offering 100 gp for assessing Butterskull Ranch's damage or returning proof of the owner's death
- The party departs Phandalin and travels east on the Triboar Trail

### Encounter with the Merchant
The party meets a merchant traveling from Conyberry who warns them about the town's state.
- The merchant reports "The place was ransacked and a wreck. Nothing was there."
- Vukradin proposes investigating to find and return stolen property despite the merchant's assurances that nothing remains

### Conyberry in Ruins
The party arrives at Conyberry and observes the devastation.
- Homes are skeletal remains with collapsed roofs and crumbled wattle-and-daub walls
- Streets are overgrown with thorny vines and weeds
- Four brigands from Carver's gang are spotted

### Corbin's Refusal
Vukradin urges their aide to approach his former gang mates.
- Corbin resists, explaining that "You don't exactly quit a gang without there being consequences."
- Vukradin insists despite Corbin's concerns

## NPCs

### Harbin
Harbin posts the Butterskull Ranch quest on his town board, offering a 100 gp reward for assessing the ranch's damage after an orc attack or for returning proof of the owner's death.

### The merchant
The merchant arrives from Conyberry and reports the town was "ransacked and a wreck" with "nothing" left. He describes the place as utterly looted and destroyed years ago, dismissing Vukradin's proposal to investigate as misguided since there is nothing remaining to secure.

### Corbin
Corbin is the party's aide who has a past with Carver's gang. When Vukradin urges him to approach his former gang mates, Corbin refuses, explaining that "You don't exactly quit a gang without there being consequences."
