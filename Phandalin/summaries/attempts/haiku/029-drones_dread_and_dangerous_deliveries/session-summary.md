# Chapter 29 Drones, Dread, and Dangerous Deliveries

## Summary

After the device collapsed, the party explored Aletra's quarters and found evidence of her tampering. Soma discovered a forged ledger where Aletra crossed out "iron for 500 pounds" and wrote "Midnight Tears - crate 4 - do not drop." Valphine found poison vials, gems, and the Operator's Guide to the Model 1489 Spatial Rectification Calliope with Aletra's note: "use only if KP isn't watching."

The Chief Accountant, relieved the distortions ceased, screamed for ten minutes at a forty-two thousand gold piece bill, then declared "The book is closed" and disappeared at peace. Valphine discovered a secret fireplace door; four audit drones attacked. One drew blood from Valphine before she captured it. Brewbarry smashed open an iron chest and found a dread helm and gauntlets of ogre power. He put on the helm, causing his eyes to glow red. At the Mountain's Toe Gold Mine, two ogres and eight orcs attacked the party in combat.

## NPCs

### Aletra
Aletra forged ledger entries, crossing out "iron for 500 pounds" and writing "Midnight Tears - crate 4 - do not drop." Her quarters contained the Operator's Guide to the Model 1489 Spatial Rectification Calliope with her note "use only if KP isn't watching." She escaped before confrontation.

### Chief Accountant
The Chief Accountant became calm once spatial distortions disappeared. She erupted into a ten-minute outburst upon seeing a forty-two thousand gold piece bill, then declared "The book is closed" and disappeared at peace.

### Brewbarry
Brewbarry smashed open an iron chest and put on the dread helm found inside, causing his eyes to glow red. He persuaded Sir Kaelen that the gold mine was more important than returning a statue.

### Valphine
Valphine discovered a hidden bag with poison vials and gems, found the secret fireplace door, and captured an audit drone that had drawn her blood. At the gold mine, she summoned spectral guardians that killed three orcs and wounded an ogre.

### Vukradin
Vukradin killed two audit drones. At the gold mine, he used magic to slow both ogres and orcs, significantly hindering their movements.

### Soma
Soma discovered Aletra's forged ledger entries crossing out "iron for 500 pounds" and replacing it with "Midnight Tears - crate 4 - do not drop." At the gold mine, Soma summoned flame and transformed into a bear.
