# Chapter 21 The Spiral's Grasp

## Summary

The party gathered at the Loggers Camp to rescue a missing Emerald Enclave patrol, hoping to boost Lyra's standing within the faction. At a fog-shrouded riverbank, they discovered the possessed Teega the Smith, who spoke of minds being cleansed and "waves seeking uniformity." She cryptically mentioned the missing patrol "they were trying to fix a leak with a wooden plug and that the current had found them, reforming their brittle thoughts" before drawing a spiral symbol and wandering into a glade.

In the glade, the party encountered a Cult Fanatic with three mind-controlled Emerald Enclave members. Brewbarry broke free from the fanatic's mind control and delivered a non-lethal blow that knocked him unconscious. The enslaved warriors collapsed, screaming. The party captured six prisoners and discovered the prisoners wore green cloaks with spiral emblems linking them to the Kraken Society.

During transport, Talosian orcs ambushed the party, demanding the Cult Fanatic as an enemy. The party negotiated and agreed to hand him over for safe passage. When given a final chance to explain himself, the fanatic spoke only of petty squabbles and "tides rising and waves coming." Valphine executed him swiftly. An anchorite half-orc cleric of Talos declared him crushed before warning the party to leave the forest.

With the fanatic dead, the enslaved Enclave members awoke. They described "feeling orders that weren't their own dripping like poisoned water into their skulls" and revealed they had been forced to steal an inscription about "a drowned gate beneath Yllyn" deep in the Underdark. The party detoured to the Falcon's Lodge where Brewbarry sought more alcohol.

## NPCs

### Teega the Smith
A possessed blacksmith who speaks in an eerie monotone about minds being cleansed and "waves seeking uniformity." She cryptically references the missing Enclave patrol and draws a spiral symbol in the mud. When Vukradin briefly persuades her, her eyes clear momentarily before glazing over. Later subdued by Soma and captured.

### The Cult Fanatic
A grim-looking man wielding a black coral dagger who commands three mind-controlled Emerald Enclave members. He attempts to control Brewbarry's mind during combat but Brewbarry breaks free. Even while unconscious, he retains control over his minions. He speaks only of petty squabbles and "tides rising and waves coming" before being executed by Valphine.

### The Anchorite Half-Orc
A cleric of Talos appearing with the Talosian orc raiding party. After the Cult Fanatic's execution, he identifies himself and declares the fanatic crushed by Talos before warning the party to leave the forest.
