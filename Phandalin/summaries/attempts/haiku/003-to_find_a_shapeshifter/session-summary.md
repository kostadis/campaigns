# Chapter 3 To find a shapeshifter

## Summary

The party defeats an ogre in the mountain foothills after a grueling battle. Despite early misses from Valphine and Brewbarry, a magical gas weakens the creature enough that when Brewbarry's legendary barbarian resilience lets him shrug off the ogre's final blow, the creature flees in panic. Vukradin delivers the killing stroke while the beast runs, securing a whistle, coins, and a key from its corpse.

The party travels to Gnomengarde, a gnomish settlement hidden behind a waterfall. After discovering mushrooms that Vukradin identifies as useful for flour and fermentation, the party meets two gnome guards, Pog and Ulla, who immediately suspect them of being shapechangers. Through a combination of Vukradin's logical arguments, Soma's pointed questions, and Brewbarry's self-identification as a former Uthgardt barbarian, bureaucratic ambiguity allows them to pass. The guards' uncertainty about whether the standing order to attack Uthgardtians has been renewed ensures safe passage.

Inside the fortress, the party navigates deadly trap corridors including spinning blade turnstiles and a rotating platform with autoloading crossbows operated by a deranged inventor named Facktore. After Vukradin disables the crossbow trap, the party reaches the workshop of the chief inventors, Fibblestib and Dabbledob, who are obsessed with curing their king's madness. Valphine realizes the gnomes will reject magical healing, preferring their own inventions. Vukradin proposes using music to help the king, and Fibblestib eagerly offers to build a piano.

Vukradin negotiates with King Gnercli, who suspects a non-bipedal shapeshifter has been hiding among the barrels in the cellar. In exchange for hunting the creature, the king offers a hat of wizardry—not the device to fight dragons the party had hoped for, but Vukradin accepts the deal for the payment. Valphine, suspicious of the gnomes, interrogates Pog and Ulla and becomes convinced they are the shapechangers. She leads the search to the cellar, where a barrel transforms into a mimic with snapping pseudo-pods and razor-sharp teeth. Battle erupts as Brewbarry charges the creature while Soma prepares a spell and Vukradin uses his bardic magic to make the mimic flinch.

## Scenes

### Ogre Ambush
#### The party encounters and defeats an ogre in a mountain valley.
- Valphine's attack lands a glancing blow that fails to harm the creature.
- Soma jabs at Valphine's poor aim: "A broad side of a barn would have been harder to miss."
- Brewbarry's halberd strikes, only to clang against rock and miss entirely.
- A magical gas cloud weakens the ogre, causing it to cough and rise to its feet.
- The ogre directs its massive axe at Brewbarry, but the barbarian's legendary resilience absorbs the blow without injury.
- Stunned by the failed strike, the ogre turns and runs.
- Valphine shoots it with a crossbow, wounding it, but it continues fleeing.
- Vukradin delivers the final blow while the creature escapes.

### Journey to Gnomengarde
#### The party travels to the hidden gnomish fortress and discovers its location.
- The rest of the journey is uneventful until they find a river leading to the gnomish hold.
- A waterfall with significant erosion has created a natural concavity overlooking a shallow pool with two small mushroom-covered islands.
- Cave openings overlook the pool from rocky ledges twenty to thirty feet above.
- Vukradin examines the mushrooms and identifies them by type: red ones are oily, green ones resemble mushroom flour, and purple ones can be fermented for wine.
- Soma expresses enthusiasm for the mushroom wine.
- Vukradin insists "There is always a secret treasure room behind the waterfall" and leads the search behind the waterfall, finding only solid rock.
- The party climbs a cliff to an entrance 20 feet above, where they must navigate stairs leading deeper into the mountain.

### Negotiating with the Guards
#### Pog and Ulla, gnomish guards, suspect the party of being shapechangers.
- A male gnome shouts in Gnomish, followed by a female voice asking "Who goes there?"
- Vukradin announces himself: "Vukradin, the Bard!"
- The guards ask if the party is shapechangers and demand proof.
- Soma responds with logic: "Would we take these shapes if we were shapechangers?"
- The female gnome counters: "That's what a shapechanger would say!"
- Vukradin argues that their strict orders against shapechangers would logically exclude all visitors.
- Brewbarry declares his identity forcefully: "I am Brewbarry, killer of Ogres! I am no shapeshifter!"
- The female gnome announces they will permit passage because, though he is a Uthgardt Barbarian, the standing order to attack them may no longer be in effect.
- Soma observes wryly: "Bureaucracy saved us."

### Navigating the Deadly Workshop
#### The party encounters and disables two deadly traps while traveling to the inventors' workshop.
- Vukradin spots a room containing two rapidly spinning turnstiles fitted with stacks of sharp blades spaced one foot apart.
- He retreats and learns from Ulla of an alternative path through the northern hallway.
- The first large room contains a rotating platform equipped with four heavy autoloading crossbows.
- The deranged inventor Facktore operates the device and tests it on Vukradin and his companions.
- Vukradin chooses to disable the device rather than kill Facktore.
- After disabling the trap, Vukradin continues through the caves, picking up more mushrooms before reaching the inventors' quarters.

### Meeting the Inventors
#### Fibblestib and Dabbledob clash over their competing approaches to cure King Korboz's madness.
- The two inventors argue intensely about their solutions: Fibblestib proposes a "sanity ray" while Dabbledob favors a straitjacket design.
- Fibblestib critiques Dabbledob's idea: "You want to build a straitjacket instead. A jacket with no holes for the hands! That's not a straitjacket, that's a shirt with no holes for the hands."
- Valphine offers her cleric skills, but both gnomes reject magical solutions: "We are gnomes, we invent our way out of illness."
- Valphine tells Vukradin she doubts she could cure the king anyway.
- Fibblestib asks to explain the situation to get the party's perspective, revealing that two gnomes named Warren and Orrin have gone missing and the king blames a shapechanger.
- Brewbarry proposes a direct approach: "We talk! We cure!"
- Vukradin suggests trying music to help the king, and Fibblestib eagerly agrees to build a piano, promising: "It won't be a normal piano! But it will play music."

### Audience with the Kings
#### Vukradin negotiates with the dual gnomish rulers to hunt the shapeshifter.
- The king screams in hysterics; his co-ruler, King Gnercli, asks if they are adventurers.
- Vukradin explains they are on a diplomatic mission from Townmaster Harbin Wester to acquire a device to fight the dragon.
- King Korboz immediately accuses Vukradin of being a shapeshifter.
- King Gnercli offers a deal: if they kill the shapeshifter, he will give them a device.
- Vukradin responds dryly: "I am a musician."
- Soma adds: "More of a tour guide."
- King Gnercli explains the creature is not bipedal and likely hides among the barrels in the cellar.
- Vukradin asks what treasure they will receive.
- The king offers: "A hat of wizardry!"
- Vukradin protests: "Not exactly going to terrorize a dragon with that."
- King Gnercli admits: "I don't have much else."
- Soma reminds Vukradin they get paid for the task regardless of its usefulness, and Vukradin accepts the deal.

### The Mimic in the Cellar
#### Valphine becomes convinced the gnome guards are shapechangers, leading the party to the cellar where they discover the true threat.
- Valphine, familiar with shapeshifters from the Dark Realms, becomes suspicious of Pog and Ulla.
- Ulla protests: "We are real gnomes!"
- Valphine responds: "That's what a shapeshifter would say!"
- Ulla and Pog turn on each other, arguing about who looks different.
- Valphine enters a room containing twelve forty-gallon barrels secured in wide alcoves, with some tapped and partially drained.
- She points a crossbow at the first barrel.
- The barrel immediately transforms into a mimic, extending pseudo-pods and revealing a mouth full of razor-sharp teeth.
- Valphine shouts: "We found it!"
- Brewbarry charges and strikes with his halberd, wounding the creature but getting his weapon stuck in its body.
- Valphine positions herself outside the range of Soma's Ice Knife spell.
- The mimic advances on Brewbarry, its attacks missing the barbarian.
- Vukradin unleashes bardic invectives that cause the mimic to flinch visibly from the magical enchantment.
- Valphine repeats a rhyme from her youth: "Sticks and stones may break my bones, and words can also hurt me."

## NPCs

### Soma
Soma is a gnomish druid and member of the party. During the journey to Gnomengarde, she discovers and harvests mushrooms with Vukradin, imagining the taste of mushroom wine despite Vukradin's skepticism. She questions the logic of the gnome guards' suspicions with pointed reasoning and eventually leads the charge into the cellar, preparing an Ice Knife spell against the mimic. Throughout the chapter, she provides pragmatic commentary on the party's actions and decisions.

### Brewbarry
Brewbarry is an Uthgardt barbarian and party member. During the ogre battle, his halberd swing misses entirely, striking only rock. His legendary rage and mystical powers allow him to absorb a devastating blow from the ogre's axe. Later, his self-identification as a former Uthgardt helps the party pass through the gnome guards' checkpoint. He advocates for direct action with the king, proposing they talk to and cure him. In the cellar, he charges the mimic with his halberd, wounding it but getting his weapon lodged in its body.

### Vukradin
Vukradin is a bard and party member. He examines the mushrooms near the waterfall, identifying their uses and harvesting them. He demonstrates logical reasoning when negotiating with the gnome guards, pointing out the inconsistency in their orders. He encounters and disables two deadly trap systems built by inventors within Gnomengarde. He meets the dual kings and negotiates a deal to hunt the shapeshifter in exchange for a hat of wizardry. He uses bardic magic in combat against the mimic, unleashing invectives that cause the creature to flinch visibly.

### Valphine
Valphine is a Drow cleric and party member. Early in the chapter, her mace swing against the ogre lands only a glancing blow. She shoots the fleeing ogre with her crossbow but cannot bring it down before Vukradin delivers the final blow. At Gnomengarde, she stands silent while the inventors debate their competing solutions, eventually offering her cleric services only to be rejected. Later, drawing on her knowledge of Dark Realm shapeshifters, she becomes convinced that the gnome guards Pog and Ulla are the true shapeshifters. She leads the search to the cellar where she identifies a barrel as the mimic by pointing her crossbow at it, triggering its transformation. She uses bardic-style language in combat, repeating a rhyme while the mimic is affected by Vukradin's magical invectives.

### Pog
Pog is a male gnome guard stationed at Gnomengarde's entrance. He questions whether the party members are shapechangers and demands proof of their true forms. When the female guard Ulla points out that he has looked different recently, he defensively accuses her of the same. He is ultimately bypassed by the party's reasoning and Brewbarry's declaration of former Uthgardt identity.

### Ulla
Ulla is a female gnome guard stationed at Gnomengarde's entrance. She speaks for both guards and makes the decision to let the party pass based on bureaucratic uncertainty about whether the standing order to attack Uthgardt barbarians remains in effect. She directs Vukradin to the inventor workshop and provides detailed alternative directions when he discovers the death trap of spinning blades. She later suspects Pog of being the shapeshifter when Valphine's interrogation makes her paranoid, but remains convinced of her own true form.

### Fibblestib
Fibblestib is a gnomish inventor and one of the two foremost artificers at Gnomengarde. Obsessed with curing King Korboz's madness, Fibblestib proposes a "sanity ray" as the solution. He argues passionately with his colleague Dabbledob over design details and dismisses Valphine's offer of cleric magic, insisting that gnomes invent their way out of illness. He eagerly accepts Vukradin's suggestion to use music for the king's cure and immediately volunteers to build a piano. He also takes pride in his rotating blade trap, claiming it to be the better invention compared to Dabbledob's crossbow device.

### Dabbledob
Dabbledob is a gnomish inventor and colleague of Fibblestib. She challenges Fibblestib's sanity ray proposal, arguing there is no evidence it would work. She advocates instead for a straitjacket solution, though Fibblestib criticizes her design as impractical. She concedes that music has helped in some circumstances when Vukradin makes his proposal, and she rolls her eyes when Fibblestib boasts about the superiority of his spinning blade trap.

### King Gnercli
King Gnercli is the co-ruler of Gnomengarde, governing alongside the increasingly unstable King Korboz. When the party arrives seeking a device to fight the dragon, he offers them a deal: kill the shapeshifter terrorizing the cave system and receive a device in return. He describes the creature as non-bipedal and likely hiding among the barrels in the cellar. When Vukradin asks about the treasure, Gnercli admits it is only a hat of wizardry and acknowledges he has nothing else more valuable to offer.

### King Korboz
King Korboz is the other co-ruler of Gnomengarde, currently afflicted with madness. He screams and remains in a state of hysterics when the party enters his chamber. Upon hearing Vukradin's name, he immediately accuses the bard of being a shapeshifter, demonstrating his paranoia and mental instability. The two missing gnomes, Warren and Orrin, disappearance has been attributed by Korboz to a shape-changing creature, though the true cause remains unclear.

### Facktore
Facktore is a deranged gnomish inventor encountered in the large workshop room containing the rotating crossbow platform. He tests his invention on Vukradin and the party without apparent regard for their safety. When the party disables his device, Facktore flees the scene rather than face combat.
