# Chapter 13 Confronting Adabra and learning of the Interventionists.

## Summary

The party confronts Adabra in Phandalin's main square after learning of her manipulation. Soma is furious that Adabra deceived them into stopping the Emerald Enclave's ritual to seal a planar rift, believing their actions would help preserve nature when in fact they were working against those Adabra branded as "interventionists." When Soma and Adabra's anger threatens violence, Vukradin uses his charm to defuse the situation, confusing Adabra just enough to prevent a confrontation. The party explains that the ritual failed, the interventionists survived, and the rift remains unsealed—a loss for everyone.

After initially offering to help as a musician rather than engage further with the conflict, the party travels to local merchants to sell the wererat furs. Barthen at Barthen's Provisions offers a low price, but Linene Graywind at the Lionshield Coster is far more interested and offers significantly more gold. That evening, the party performs at the Stonehill Tavern, where Linene watches them perform and praises Valphine for making the ensemble shine.

Later, agents of the Emerald Enclave—Thomas and Marian—sit with the party at their table and explain the situation. Lyra, frustrated by the Enclave's inability to act decisively on the growing planar effects, discovered a powerful ritual and attempted it without proper authorization or the support of her superiors. Thomas and Marian reveal that Lyra saw an opportunity to resolve what others could not, but the ritual was beyond their capacity and nearly killed them all. When asked about the treant in the center of the planar tear, Valphine's observation about a strange creature draws Adabra into ecstasy about its true nature and purpose. Thomas and Marian eventually leave, explaining that their oath to the Emerald Enclave binds them, though they briefly consider joining the party's group.

At the tavern's end, Ser Kaelen approaches Vukradin with questions about his work, plans, and goals. Vukradin, wanting to be liked by everyone, asks Kaelen about the rightful owner of the ship's treasure they had acquired. Kaelen offers limited assistance, though he praises Vukradin's goal of seeking conflict-free treasure and discusses potential work with the Order of the Gauntlet.

## Scenes

### Confrontation in the Main Square
#### The party confronts Adabra about her deception after learning she manipulated them against the Emerald Enclave.
- Soma is enraged and directly accuses Adabra with broad charges.
- Adabra becomes cold and patronizing, claiming she had hope in Soma but that the interventionists filled Soma's head with noise.
- Soma explains they actually interrupted the interventionists and didn't complete the ritual.
- Vukradin smooths over the conflict by confusing Adabra about what truly happened, keeping her from dwelling on any sense of betrayal.
- Adabra asks about the treant at the center of the planar tear.
- Vukradin describes it as being in stable condition, a phrase Soma recognizes as technically true since the treant is dead.
- Lyra becomes furious and shouts at the party's lack of understanding.
- Vukradin defuses the tension by offering beer and suggesting music.
- When Lyra continues ranting, she eventually leaves in disgust, declaring the party fools for not seeing Adabra's evil and fanaticism.

### Music and Philosophy
#### Vukradin discusses the nature of music and conflict with Adabra while Valphine observes Adabra's zealotry.
- Vukradin grows bored with conflict and shouts "Boooring" at the endless dispute over interventionism.
- Adabra pivots to metaphor, asking if all music is good or if some music is bad, using it as an argument about the multiverse's diversity.
- Valphine quips that bad music crosses planar boundaries.
- Vukradin argues there is music that is bad, and Soma agrees that some planes are bad for the material plane.
- Adabra finds hope in Vukradin and expresses doubt about Soma.
- Vukradin learns that among the Emerald Enclave, "interventionist" is the worst insult, as each member believes they preserve rather than intervene in nature.
- Adabra explains she was forced to reach out to allies after being marginalized for her stance against interventionists.
- Valphine reveals her contempt for Adabra's zealotry, seeing it as similar to drow worship of Lolth.
- The party decides to go play music rather than continue the debate.

### Selling the Furs
#### The party attempts to sell wererat pelts at local merchants.
- Vukradin and Corbin discuss finding a fence for the pelts, but Vukradin rejects the idea of selling blood money.
- The party visits Barthen's Provisions, where Barthen offers a paltry sum for the goods.
- At the Lionshield Coster, Linene Graywind, a 35-year-old manager, greets them with enthusiasm and asks detailed questions about the pelts.
- Vukradin answers truthfully about where they found the furs, to Valphine's disdain for his free sharing of valuable information.
- Linene offers more than twice Barthen's price and mentions her bosses will be very interested in the planar effect.
- Vukradin agrees to the price, and Linene asks if they will perform music at a venue that evening.

### Performance at the Stonehill Tavern
#### The party performs music at the tavern with mixed results.
- Valphine notes their performance is off, though she herself is perfect and Vukradin is not getting his vibe right.
- The songs are well-received but not as enthusiastically as their previous performance.
- After a few songs, members of the Emerald Enclave arrive and sit with the party after Vukradin invites them.

### Conversation with Emerald Enclave Agents
#### Thomas and Marian explain why Lyra attempted the dangerous ritual and reveal the internal conflict within the Enclave.
- Vukradin asks if the agents are sticking with Lyra.
- The oldest agent, drinking beer paid for by Spiderman, explains their oath binds them to the Enclave despite Lyra's failure.
- Thomas reveals Lyra discovered the ritual believing it would save the tree and forest, but all that happened was failure.
- Vukradin offers to speak on Lyra's behalf to her superiors, but Thomas warns this would be a bad idea.
- Thomas explains Lyra acted alone because the Enclave was divided over whether to intervene at all.
- Soma notes that Lyra should have centered herself on nature rather than her own needs.
- Marian defends Lyra as a good leader and boss who saw an opportunity and was right in the past, just not this time.
- Thomas asks Valphine about difficult rituals, which provokes a response about blood, death, and torture from the Underdark.
- Thomas learns Valphine is from Menzoberanzan and a cleric of Lathander who came to the surface to escape a demon incursion and now loves the sun's painful brilliance.
- Marian asks if Valphine uses healing spells as a ritual after harming herself, confusing Valphine about whether this is normal in the surface world.
- Soma asks why Lyra chose such a difficult ritual, and Thomas explains the Enclave was paralyzed by disagreement about intervention.

- Kaelen praises Vukradin's goal of seeking conflict-free treasure.

## NPCs

### Adabra
Adabra, the driving force behind the party's involvement in the planar ritual conflict, reveals herself as a zealous advocate for natural diversity and balance across the multiverse. Initially presenting as kind, she becomes cold and contemptuous when confronted about her manipulation. She speaks with patronizing disdain about those she calls interventionists, believing them to be causing violence through their efforts to cleanse what they perceive as planar infections. Through the conversation, it emerges that Adabra has been marginalized within the Emerald Enclave for her stance and was forced to seek outside allies. Though she initially dismisses Soma, she develops hope for Vukradin, finding common ground with him over philosophical matters about the multiverse's inherent value. She rewards the party with five conflict-free gold pieces and leaves in a state of ecstatic rambling about planes, designs, and universalism.

### Lyra
Lyra, an agent of the Emerald Enclave with particular affection for the Whispering Grove, discovered a powerful ritual to seal a planar rift and attempted it without proper authorization or support from her superiors. Frustrated by the Enclave's indecision over whether to intervene in the growing planar effects, she believed the forest and gods would protect her and her companions. The ritual was far more difficult than she anticipated and nearly killed her team. When confronted by the party, Lyra becomes furious, shouting at them in anger before storming away in disgust at their lack of understanding regarding Adabra's danger.

### Thomas
Thomas is an agent of the Emerald Enclave who sits with the party at the Stonehill Tavern to explain Lyra's actions and the internal conflict within the Enclave. He speaks candidly about the Enclave's paralysis over the question of intervention and how Lyra, seeing an opportunity, attempted the ritual despite warnings of its difficulty. He inquires about Valphine's experience with difficult rituals and her background, learning that she is from Menzoberanzan and a cleric of Lathander. Before leaving, he affirms his oath to the Enclave when declining the party's invitation to join them.

### Marian
Marian is another Emerald Enclave agent who joins Thomas at the party's table. She provides a sympathetic defense of Lyra as a good leader and boss who saw an opportunity and had been right in the past, even though this particular venture failed. She asks Valphine a direct and concerned question about whether she harms herself and then uses healing spells as a ritual, revealing confusion or concern about the cleric's practices. Like Thomas, she ultimately affirms her oath to the Enclave over joining the party.

### Barthen
Barthen, the proprietor of Barthen's Provisions in Phandalin, offers a dismissively low sum for the wererat pelts the party attempts to sell him. He does not recognize the value that the party, particularly Vukradin, sees in the goods.

### Linene Graywind
Linene Graywind, a 35-year-old manager at the Lionshield Coster, greets the party with genuine enthusiasm when they bring the wererat pelts to her establishment. She asks numerous detailed questions about where and how the party found the furs, and offers more than twice what Barthen proposed. Linene mentions that her bosses will be very interested in the planar effects represented by the goods. That evening at the Stonehill Tavern, she watches the party perform and specifically praises Valphine for making the ensemble shine, much to Vukradin's slight annoyance.

### Ser Kaelen
Ser Kaelen approaches Vukradin at the tavern to inquire about his work, plans, and goals. He sighs when asked about the rightful owner of the party's ship treasure, claiming it is a difficult matter. Kaelen praises Vukradin's goal of obtaining only conflict-free treasure and discusses the possibility of Vukradin creating music for the Order of the Gauntlet, though Vukradin cautions that such work cannot be rushed and must be better than Kaelen's previous efforts.
