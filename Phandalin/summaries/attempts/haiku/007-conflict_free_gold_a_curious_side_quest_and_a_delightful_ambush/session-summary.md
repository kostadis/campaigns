# Chapter 7 Conflict-free gold, a curious side quest, and a delightful ambush.

## Summary

The party encounters Elara and Jarek, a middle-aged human merchant and her groom, whose cart has been overturned after an orc attack. Elara describes the attackers as moving with unnerving discipline and bearing odd markings like a swirl or an eye, while Jarek recalls hearing an uncanny hum in his mind before the assault. After Brewbarry recovers one of their scattered horses, the party escorts both travelers safely back to Phandalin. Vukradin celebrates this act of kindness as "Conflict Free gold," and Elara offers them a reward directed through Valphine to the Morninglord.

In Phandalin, Vukradin meets Ser Kaelen Thorn of the Order of the Gauntlet, who praises the party's good deed. When Vukradin requests a cheer of the Gauntlet for a musical commission, Ser Kaelen reluctantly shares it, embarrassed by the order's chant: "Steal and shield, Heart and band. We stand as one. A steadfast band." The encounter reinforces Vukradin's conviction that one can earn wealth through genuine assistance rather than violence.

As the party leaves Phandalin, Adabra intercepts them with urgent news. The old grove past Whisper Woods, once a true blessing of Chauntea, is withering unnaturally. Trees twist in impossible shapes, colors bleed wrong, and the creatures inhabiting it are not right, displaying too many eyes and jerky movements. Adabra attributes the corruption to interventionists and the Emerald Enclave. Soma agrees to help investigate.

The party travels for several days along the Triboar Trail to the Wayside Inn, where they meet Martisha Vinetalker, the striking half-elf owner; bartender Backes Dunfield; and cook Cooragh Struckt. Martisha directs them toward the coast the following morning. As they traverse an unnatural coastal gully, Valphine's drow intuition detects danger. Brewbarry confirms hidden attackers positioned above. The party gains high ground for a counter-ambush. Valphine observes orcs of unusual discipline and tactical sophistication—far more calculated than typical surface raiders. They have positioned themselves to drop a blocking rock and attack from above, and an orc shaman or cleric leads them. Overhearing the Orc tongue, Valphine learns they consider the party an "easy kill."

Combat erupts. Vukradin's Sleep spell incapacitates one orc and the shaman. Soma transforms into a brown bear, tearing into two orcs with brutal power. Brewbarry engages directly with his rage. Valphine fires hand crossbow bolts and casts Fairy Fire to illuminate the foes. The orc shaman transforms into a boar—a familiar trick but a surprising tactical development. The battle proves far more engaging than anticipated.

## NPCs

### Elara
A middle-aged human merchant whose trade cart was overturned by orcs. She describes the attackers as moving with unnerving discipline and bearing odd markings like a swirl or an eye. After the party recovers her scattered horses, she offers them a reward directed through Valphine to the Morninglord, and accepts their escort back to Phandalin.

### Jarek
Elara's groom who traveled with her toward Phandalin. He recalls the orc attack and describes hearing an uncanny hum in his mind before the strike, suggesting coordination beyond typical brigandry. He had warned Elara they should have traveled with protection.

### Ser Kaelen Thorn
A member of the Order of the Gauntlet still in Phandalin. He praises Vukradin for balancing large quests with good deeds aligned with the Order's values. When Vukradin asks for a cheer of the Gauntlet to use for commissioned music, Ser Kaelen reluctantly provides the order's chant: "Steal and shield, Heart and band. We stand as one. A steadfast band." He notes that Vukradin's talents would improve anything the Order had done.

### Adabra
A member of the Emerald Enclave who intercepts the party as they leave Phandalin. She warns of corruption in the old grove past Whisper Woods, which was once vibrant but now withers unnaturally, with trees twisting in impossible shapes and creatures displaying abnormal behavior. She attributes the corruption to interventionists and asks Soma to help investigate.

### Martisha Vinetalker
A striking half-elf innkeeper at the Wayside Inn. She is cold, calculating, and charismatic in demeanor. She directs the party toward the coastal trail leading toward the Tower of Storms.

### Backes Dunfield
The bartender at the Wayside Inn, a human with a scarred face and crooked smile.

### Cooragh Struckt
The cook at the Wayside Inn, famous for incredibly tasty and well-spiced stews and meat pies.

### The orc shaman
The leader of the orc ambush party in the coastal gully. This orc speaks the Orc tongue and demonstrates unusual discipline and tactical sophistication, coordinating a carefully planned ambush. The shaman possesses the ability to shape-shift into a boar and calls the party an "easy kill" in the Orc tongue, gravely misjudging the encounter.
