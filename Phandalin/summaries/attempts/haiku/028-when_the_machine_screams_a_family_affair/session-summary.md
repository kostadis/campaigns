# Chapter 28 When the Machine Screams: A Family Affair

## Summary

The party confronted Valphine's sister Aletra, who was extracting poison vials from a malfunctioning planar machine guarded by six Rift Weavers. Aletra claimed they were in "spatial equilibrium" and threatened catastrophic failure if she couldn't complete her extraction. She used her wand to mark Brewbarry, causing the machine to detect him as an anomaly and turn all spiders on him while she worked undisturbed.

Soma's magical flames proved effective, burning away the creatures and their webs. Valphine's radiant energy also wounded them. The spiders fell one by one, dissolving into "crackling sparkles." When Aletra revealed her extracted box of poison vials from House Sotorra's illegal trade, she activated "a shimmering portal" to escape. Valphine's blinding curse caused Aletra to drop several vials before vanishing through the portal. Valphine shut down the machine with the keypad code. The device folded "in on itself" and vanished, leaving only a bill from "Interplanar Trade Consultants Incorporated."

## NPCs

### Aletra
Valphine's drow sister works for House Sotorra's illegal poison trade. She masterfully manipulated the confrontation, using the party and Rift Weavers as tools to extract poison vials from the malfunctioning machine. Through her wand, she marked Brewbarry to draw the spiders' assault, allowing herself to work in safety. She traded cutting remarks with Valphine throughout the battle. Despite Valphine's blinding curse that caused her to drop several vials, Aletra escaped through a portal to serve her mysterious boss.
