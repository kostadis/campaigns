# Chapter 30 A Cheesy Compromise in the Mine

## Summary

Waves of orcs and ogres attacked the mine entrance. Valphine's divine guardians struck with celestial fire while Brewbarry's red-eyed fury devastated enemies. The party recovered alchemist's fire, coins, gems, and a symbol of Talos.

Zeleen Varnaster, a wererat miner, claimed Don-Jon died to a carrion crawler. When pressed, she evasively replied that "kill is such a strong word," revealing Don-Jon had been "conveniently lured to her death." The party received Don-Jon's mine share and gave the wererats Petunia's cheese.

Valphine discovered Don-Jon had died in combat with dwarves, not the carrion crawler. Soma communicated with the creature; it spoke of feasting on "deep stone folk" and "false rats" while waiting for them to fight. Soma persuaded it to seek better hunting grounds and recovered magical goggles and a chain.

Following dwarf tracks, the party encountered five dwarves led by Horia with a prior contract. Horia revealed Don-Jon had boasted of finding "a sucker in some bard fellow" to manipulate wererats with cheap cheese while planning to dismiss the dwarves. This sparked the fatal argument. The party proposed dwarves handle mining while wererats provided menial labor. Valphine's claim that contracts existed only to cheat people offended Horia.

## NPCs

### Zeleen Varnaster
Wererat miner relieved at the party's arrival. She claimed Don-Jon died to a carrion crawler but evasively admitted "kill is such a strong word," revealing Don-Jon had been "conveniently lured to her death." She negotiated to keep Don-Jon's mine share and delighted in Petunia's cheese.

### Horia
Leader of five dwarves with a prior contract to work the mine. Horia revealed Don-Jon boasted of finding "a sucker in some bard fellow" to manipulate wererats while planning to dismiss the dwarves, resulting in his death. He warned wererats would destroy operations through incompetence. Though willing to negotiate a compromise, Horia was offended when Valphine claimed contracts existed only to cheat people.
