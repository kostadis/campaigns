# Chapter 20 The Orc Ambush

## Summary

The party encountered an orc scout in the forest and triggered an ambush by raiders and brigands. Soma transformed into a bear and chased the scout up a tree, while Vukradin wielded enchantments and illusions to turn the orcs against each other. Most combatants were defeated or fled. One raider was captured, and interrogation revealed the orcs had been displaced from the Ice Spire of the Sword Mountains by a white dragon. The party arrived at the Logger's Camp to find it deserted.

## Scenes

### Forest Battle
The party encounters and defeats an orc raiding party.
- Soma transformed into a bear and chased the scout up a tree, biting him savagely.
- Vukradin wove enchantments that turned orcs against each other.
- Soma called down radiant moonlight that engulfed three orcs.
- A brigand screamed that "this was the worst idea he'd ever had" and fled.
- Vukradin conjured an illusion making a raider fight phantoms.
- One raider was captured; the scout escaped.

### Interrogation and Destination
The prisoner reveals his faction's origins; the party reaches the Logger's Camp.
- Brewbarry named the prisoner Shrimpy.
- Shrimpy revealed the raiders were displaced from the Ice Spire of the Sword Mountains by a white dragon.
- Some orcs joined the Cult of Talos after their shaman was killed; others became brigands.
- The party arrived at the Logger's Camp along the river's south shore.
- The camp was eerily silent and completely deserted.

## NPCs

### Shrimpy
An orc scout captured during the ambush. Under interrogation, he revealed the raiders had been displaced from the Ice Spire of the Sword Mountains by a white dragon. Some orc refugees joined the Cult of Talos after their shaman was killed; others became brigands. When he asked if he would be paid for his service, Brewbarry coldly told him his life was payment enough.
