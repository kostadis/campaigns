# Chapter 35 The Carver is not the Carver

**Date:** Feb 19th, 2026

---

## Summary

The party began the long ascent to Icespire Hold, their first approach to the mountain that had loomed on Phandalin's horizon for weeks. The climb was grueling — punishing cold with texture and weight, wind that felt personal, and the particular exhaustion of high-altitude travel. Vukradin composed a marching song in three-four time as they climbed, setting a rhythm to keep the group moving. The party navigated a treacherous rock slide, and during this obstacle, Soma transformed into a giant wolf spider — a form she was quick to clarify with some dignity was a *medium-sized beast*, not merely a "rinky-dink spider." As they crested the last rise, the full scale of Icespire Hold opened before them, and there on the roof, pale against the snow, was Cryovain the white dragon, sleeping.

Rather than take the obvious, exposed gatehouse path, the party opted to bypass the fortress's defenses via a fifty-foot sheer ice cliff. Soma — in her giant wolf spider form — skittered up the ice wall with a rope, while Vukradin briefly proposed using illusion magic to make her *believe* she had seen a spider (a plan that was rightly abandoned). The rope came down, the party climbed, and they gained the high ground. Once atop the bridge, Vukradin immediately envisioned the worst-case tactical scenario — being surrounded from both sides — but Valphine pressed forward, finding a path through earthquake-rubble and smashed corridors that spoke of Cryovain's earlier passage.

Soma scouted ahead to the roof in spider form. What she found was worse than any of them had expected. Cryovain was chained not by physical restraints but by a summoning circle carved into the stone, his scales gone the color of old ice, his eyes hollowed out — something had been taken from him, his nose conspicuously missing, leaving only compressed, seething rage beneath a vacant exterior. The Carver stood over him performing a ritual. Soma relayed her findings and the party debated the nature of the chains — magical or mundane — and whether killing the Carver would release the dragon. Valphine proposed the "enemy of my enemy" theory, suggesting the dragon might be reasoned with. Vukradin imagined the dragon being grateful for rescue. Soma, who had looked into those hollow eyes, was considerably less optimistic.

The confrontation came in the summoning circle chamber. Brewbarry charged in bellowing. Vukradin, recognizing the ritual's magic, cast *Silence* — a dome of complete soundlessness dropped over the circle, choking the Carver's ritual mid-incantation. The human vessel moved with legendary precision, stepping out of the silence zone fluidly. Vukradin cast *Hold Person* and the veteran's body locked solid. For a moment, it seemed controlled. Then the *Silence* spell dropped — and the sword spoke directly into Vukradin's skull. It mocked his "coward's magic" and declared that it would now have what it wanted. The chains binding Cryovain did not break — they *dissolved*, as though they had never been real. The Carver had abandoned the slow ritual and taken direct control. Cryovain was dominated.

The party had three rounds before the domination was complete and Cryovain's mind was gone.

The battle erupted. Brewbarry, wielding the Dragon Slayer Sword he had carried for this exact purpose, landed two clean blows on the dragon — the sword doing exactly what it was forged to do. Cryovain's frost breath hit Brewbarry full in the face, a cone of cold that winter itself would run from, but Brewbarry did not go down. The dragon took to the air, thirty feet above the battlements, out of reach. Soma burned both remaining wild shapes to transform into a bear and close to melee range. Valphine landed a radiant bolt of sacred flame like a small sun. Vukradin, behind cover, considered and declined to cast Fireball — it was his spell, and a private choice he had already made. Instead, he cast *thorn whip* — and the critical hit landed, the vine catching Cryovain and making the dragon yelp in surprise and pain.

## Scenes

### 1. Ascent to Icespire Hold
The party climbs the mountain, seeing Cryovain from the foothills. Vukradin composes a marching song. The cold is relentless. Soma transforms into a giant wolf spider to navigate the rock slide. The fortress and its sleeping dragon come into view.

### 2. Bypassing the Gatehouse
The party decides against the obvious fortified entrance. Soma, in giant wolf spider form, climbs a fifty-foot sheer ice cliff and drops a rope. The party ascends. Valphine finds a path through earthquake-shattered corridors that reveal Cryovain's destructive passage through the fortress.

### 3. Soma Scouts the Roof
Soma climbs the fortress wall in spider form and observes the roof. Cryovain is chained at the center of a summoning circle — not sleeping, but held, hollowed out, his eyes empty and his nose missing. The Carver performs a ritual over him. Soma reports back: the dragon is dominated, not just imprisoned.

### 4. Confrontation at the Summoning Circle
The party breaches the chamber. Brewbarry charges. Vukradin casts *Silence*, dropping a dome of soundlessness over the circle and interrupting the ritual. The Carver's human vessel moves with legendary precision out of the silence zone. Vukradin casts *Hold Person* — the vessel freezes.

### 5. The Reveal: the Sword is the Carver
The *Silence* spell drops. The obsidian sword speaks telepathically to Vukradin. Arcana confirms: the sword **is** the Carver. The human was always just a vessel. The sword declares its victory and dissolves Cryovain's chains. The dragon is dominated — the party has three rounds before the process is complete.

### 6. Battle Against Cryovain
Brewbarry lands two clean blows with the Dragon Slayer Sword. Cryovain breathes frost — Brewbarin does not fall. The dragon takes to the air, thirty feet up. Soma shifts into a bear and closes distance. Valphine strikes with sacred flame. Vukradin, declining to cast Fireball, lands a critical hit with *thorn whip* on the airborne dragon.

## NPCs

- **Cryovain** — The white dragon of Icespire Hold. A young adult white dragon held in a summoning circle by the Carver, his body chained and mind hollowed. His nose has been taken. Beneath the vacant stillness seethes compressed rage. The Carver's sword ultimately dominated him, leaving the party three rounds before his mind was lost entirely.

- **The Carver** — The entity the party has been hunting throughout this arc. Truth revealed: the Carver is not the human veteran they have been tracking. The **obsidian sword** is the Carver; the human form was merely a vessel. The sword spoke telepathically and demonstrated unhurried contempt. It abandoned the slow ritual in favor of direct domination of Cryovain.

- **The Carver's Vessel** — The human veteran whose body the Carver inhabited. Skilled enough to move with legendary footwork out of a silence zone without drawing an attack. Frozen by Vukradin's *Hold Person* and left behind when the sword completed its plan.

## Items

- **Dragon Slayer Sword** — A sword specifically designed to kill dragons, carried by Brewbarry for this fight. Against Cryovain, it functioned as intended — two clean blows landed with devastating effect, the impact traveling through the blade in the way that means you have done something real.

- **The Obsidian Sword (The Carver)** — A black obsidian sword that glowed with purple energy. Revealed to be the true identity of the Carver — a sentient weapon that had been using a human vessel as a host. It spoke telepathically, mocked Vukradin, dissolved Cryovain's chains, and completed the domination ritual. After expending its power to seize the dragon, it sat inert, black and still, emptied out.

## Spells

- ***Silence*** (Vukradin) — Cast at the summoning circle to interrupt the Carver's ritual. Dropped a dome of complete soundlessness that choked the incantation mid-word. The key tactical spell that bought the party the opening they needed.

- ***Hold Person*** (Vukradin) — Cast on the Carver's human vessel after he stepped out of the *Silence* zone. Paralyzed the veteran completely, mid-motion, leaving him frozen like a marionette whose strings had been cut.

- ***Thorn Whip*** (Vukradin) — Cast during the battle against the airborne Cryovain. A critical hit — the whip caught the dragon somewhere vital, producing a sound of pain and surprise rather than contempt. A decisive moment from a spell Brewbarry initially doubted against a dragon.

- ***Sacred Flame*** (Valphine) — A bolt of radiant light that struck Cryovain like a small sun, scattering the dragon's attention and marking the beginning of the party's coordinated assault.

- **Wild Shape** (Soma) — Used twice in this session. First into a giant wolf spider for navigation and scouting (rock slide, ice cliff, roof reconnaissance). Then spent both remaining wild shapes to transform into a bear for melee combat against Cryovain.
