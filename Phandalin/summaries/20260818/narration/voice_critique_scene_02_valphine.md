## Flags

### [1] Cross-narrator convergence / register-wrong vocabulary

> and I stand at the pulpit of Brother Aldric Sunmantle, whose soft, squishy rites I memorized four months ago while feigning devotion

**Why:** `squishy` is Soma's word for surface-dwellers — it appears verbatim in her example file (`I look at his squishy form`) — and Valphine's spec bans modern/casual register without strategic cause; `soft, squishy` is also a doubling where she picks one precise quality.
**Suggested rewrite:** `and I stand at the pulpit of Brother Aldric Sunmantle, whose consolatory rites I memorized four months ago while feigning devotion`

### [2] Voice spec conflict (ornament without landing)

> in the hall where a fool taught me the liturgy of comfort

**Why:** Her spec's rule 2: long sentences must land in a clear verdict, not drift into ornament. This is the third appositive in one sentence saying what the first two already said (Aldric's pulpit, memorized rites, feigned devotion).
**Suggested rewrite:** Cut the clause; the sentence lands harder on `while feigning devotion.`

### [3] Cliché

> The hall holds its breath, and then it does not.

**Why:** `the hall holds its breath` is stock, and the bolted-on reversal is a writer's flourish rather than an observation. `_genre.md`: specific or nothing.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Silence, one beat too long to be politeness. Then the noise.`

### [4] Voice spec conflict

> Vukradin leans in, and his objection is, as ever, sincerity wearing the mask of sarcasm:

**Why:** Her spec is explicit that she reads Vukradin's moral optimism *as expert manipulation, because in her grammar there is no other category for it.* Crediting him with sincerity — and correctly — dissolves the running misread that defines their relationship.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Vukradin leans in, and objects in the register he uses when he wants the room to see him object:`

### [5] Voice spec conflict

> He understands the sacrament perfectly. He simply objects to it.

**Why:** Same problem as [4], compounded: she grants him both accurate comprehension and a principled objection. She would read the objection as positioning and admire the maneuver.
**Suggested rewrite:** `He understands the sacrament perfectly. Objecting to it in front of three hundred people is the better position, and he took it.`

### [6] Generic fantasy prose (decorative simile)

> There is no resistance in the man at all; he opens to it like a door left unlatched, gold flooding in behind his eyes until he beholds only burning.

**Why:** `like a door left unlatched` is decoration on a failed save — her spec bans ornate-without-precision. She catalogues; she does not reach for a household simile. (`beholds` is fine: it deliberately echoes her own sermon text.)
**Suggested rewrite:** `There is no resistance in the man at all. Not one point of it. Gold floods in behind his eyes until he beholds only burning.`

### [7] Tell-not-show emotional commentary

> "Cast him out! Cast out the heathen!" Vukradin cries, and I note with satisfaction that the fervor has caught even him.

**Why:** `I note with satisfaction` names the feeling. Her spec: she does not confess emotion, she analyzes; if she names a feeling it is because the analysis required it, and here it does not.
**Suggested rewrite:** `"Cast him out! Cast out the heathen!" Vukradin cries. The fervor has reached the one man in the hall who came to object.`

### [8] Generic fantasy prose (spec-quoting)

> I concede the point privately, as an archivist concedes a citation.

**Why:** `archivist of motive` is a description *of* her voice in the spec; using it as an in-text simile is the prose citing its own instructions, and it is the third archival figure in 700 words (`catalogue the room`, `a face from our ledger`).
**Suggested rewrite:** `I concede the point privately. Conceding costs nothing when no one hears it.`

### [9] Voice spec conflict (banned tic)

> The faithful stare at them the way one stares at a guest who has insulted the host: the sermon was eloquent and the room knows it, and their accusation dies unheard.

**Why:** `the way one stares at a guest who has insulted the host` is the banned taxonomise construction in its purest form, and the impersonal `one` is authorial rather than Valphine-in-the-room.
**Suggested rewrite:** `The faithful watch them go the way a house watches a guest who has insulted its host. The sermon was eloquent and the room knows it; their accusation dies unheard.`

### [10] Narrator editorializing (wink at the reader)

> And yes, I did just blind the man.

**Why:** A knowing aside to the reader — `_genre.md` bans winking — and the casual `And yes, I did just` is exactly the modern slack her spec's rule 9 forbids.
**Suggested rewrite:** `The man is blind. That is not in dispute.`

### [11] Voice spec conflict (sentimental register)

> But it is not my eloquence that settles the matter in these people's hearts.

**Why:** `hearts` is consolation vocabulary — the surface-dweller register her spec says she has considered and rejected. She settles matters in ledgers and in bodies, not hearts.
**Suggested rewrite:** `But it is not my eloquence that settles it. It never is.`

### [12] Generic fantasy prose (self-repetition)

> Loviatar rations her pain and sells it to the desperate. Mine arrives free, every dawn, inexhaustible.

**Why:** This is the identical doctrinal figure — with the identical verb — as line 31's `Lolth rations her power in the dark and calls the rationing love. My god spends his without counting.` Running it twice in 700 words spends the scene's best idea on a repeat.
**Suggested rewrite:** Keep line 31; recast the close on Loviatar's *price* instead: `Loviatar's pain has a price and a buyer. Mine has neither. Let her church come collect.`

### [13] Tell-not-show / warmth drift

> Brewbarry answers before I can, and what comes out of him is my doctrine, whole, unprompted, better preached than I could have asked:

**Why:** `better preached than I could have asked` is appreciation stated as feeling. Her spec's rule 1 bans the confessional register; she would price the asset, not admire it.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Brewbarry answers before I can, and what comes out of him is my doctrine, whole, unprompted, from a mouth no one in this hall can accuse of subtlety:`

## Verdict

The strongest problem is the pair [4]/[5] — the narration lets Valphine correctly identify Vukradin as sincere, which is the one read her spec says she structurally cannot make, and it costs the set its funniest running misalignment. After that, [12]'s doubled rations/spends figure wastes the sermon's central image. Everything else is spot-editable; this is the most on-spec scene of the five and needs only surgical work, not re-narration.
