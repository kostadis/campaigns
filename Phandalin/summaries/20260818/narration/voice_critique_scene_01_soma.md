## Flags

### [1] Narrator editorializing / recap framing

> Vukradin means to announce the name of his band today, and the impromptu performance he and Valphine put on at the Moonstone Mask has created quite the stir at the Spire of the Morninglord.

**Why:** `_genre.md` bans recap framing — this summarises a prior session's events for the reader's benefit, and `has created quite the stir` is authorial-summary register, not Soma's blunt one.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Vukradin means to name his band today. Whatever he and Valphine did at the Moonstone Mask, the Spire heard about it.`

### [2] Voice spec conflict (banned tic)

> Then the corners. Agents in the shadows, keeping still the way people keep still when they are working.

**Why:** `_genre.md` bans the *taxonomise-the-category* construction, and this is it — the specific stillness never gets rendered, only its class.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Then the corners. Two in the shadows. Nobody stands that still by accident.`

### [3] Cliché / on-the-nose simile

> Valphine reads the room like a tide chart, every eddy accounted for.

**Why:** The tide chart earns its place (coastal life is Soma's actual lexicon), but `every eddy accounted for` extends the figure a second beat purely for polish — Soma's spec says if a sentence is beautiful, blunt it.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Valphine reads the room like a tide chart.`

### [4] Generic fantasy prose (essay register)

> Whose entire trade Valphine's searing sacrament has been conducting without a license.

**Why:** The joke is exactly Soma's — dry, a little mean — but the fronted relative pronoun is polished essay syntax, which the addendum explicitly bans (`Avoid polished, essay-like phrasing`).
**Suggested rewrite:** `That is Loviatar's trade. Valphine has been running it without a license.`

### [5] Voice spec conflict

> The shell sprout spent all night on the white dragon scale, every plate assembled, and now he is fitting it onto her at the temple threshold with his hands still black from the work.

**Why:** `shell sprout` is a signature reserved for naive people — `_genre.md` names Vukradin as the referent — and it is misapplied here to a devout, competent armorsmith who worked through the night; it also reads at first pass as Vukradin, which breaks the sentence.
**Suggested rewrite:** `The armorsmith spent all night on the white dragon scale, every plate assembled, and now he is fitting it onto her at the temple threshold with his hands still black from the work.`

### [6] Tense drift (and redundancy)

> Everybody arrived very early in the morning.

**Why:** Past tense in a present-tense section, and it flatly repeats line 27's `a crowd that got out of bed in the dark to be here`, which already did the job better.
**Suggested rewrite:** Cut it; open the paragraph on `Factions within factions`.

### [7] Cliché

> Factions within factions, and the ones I can name are only the ones wearing their colors where an old tortle can see them.

**Why:** `Factions within factions` is stock phrasing; the rest of the sentence (`where an old tortle can see them`) is genuinely hers and does not need the stock opener propping it up.
**Suggested rewrite:** `The ones I can name are only the ones wearing their colors where an old tortle can see them.`

### [8] Generic fantasy prose

> Then Brewbarry sets his hand on her shoulder and his eyes kindle with light, and a phantom sound rolls out of the doorway as we walk in, low ominous whispers, pure theater.

**Why:** `his eyes kindle with light` is generic fantasy reach — `_genre.md` bans `his eyes blazed with ancient fury` and this is that verb doing that job. The Dread Helm's eyes glow red; Soma would say the colour.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Then Brewbarry sets his hand on her shoulder and the helm's eyes come up red, and a phantom sound rolls out of the doorway as we walk in, low ominous whispers, pure theater.`

### [9] Generic fantasy prose

> Gold, gleaming, plainly visible to every onlooker in the hall.

**Why:** `Her own eyes need no trick` is a perfect Soma verdict; the participial fragment stacked after it is ornament, and `plainly visible to every onlooker in the hall` is essay register.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Her own eyes need no trick. Gold. Everyone sees it.`

### [10] Register-wrong vocabulary

> Then he tilts his head and ponders, and I can see him counting up all the liturgical and ceremonial elements she has forgotten.

**Why:** `liturgical and ceremonial elements` is church vocabulary — Valphine's or Vukradin's register. Soma's wisdom is coastal (weather, hospitality, fishing, mending), not doctrinal; the spec is explicit that she has no Enclave doctrine either.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Then he tilts his head and ponders, and I can see him counting up everything she skipped.`

### [11] Voice spec conflict (authorial caption)

> The goliath as theological witness.

**Why:** A captioned epigram, not a thought — and `theological` is register-wrong for Soma, same problem as [10]. Her spec's rule 1 is `Do not make Soma sound literary for its own sake.`
**Suggested rewrite:** `A goliath vouching for her doctrine. It works.`

### [12] Cliché

> This time her words find their footing.

**Why:** `find their footing` is a stock idiom, and it is the second time the same beat has been narrated in the same terms (`It lands thin` did the setup).
**Suggested rewrite:** `This time it holds.`

### [13] Generic fantasy prose (literary figure for its own sake)

> I have watched her improvise before; this is not that. This is the whole answer arriving whole.

**Why:** `the whole answer arriving whole` is a self-conscious chiasmus, and the semicolon is essay punctuation. The addendum: `Prefer short, blunt sentences... If a sentence sounds too literary, blunt it.`
**Suggested rewrite:** `I have watched her improvise before. This is not that. She had the whole answer before he asked.`

### [14] Narrator editorializing

> And I understand, watching the scarred one's face close up, that he was put in this room to trap her, and she threaded the trap with the needle they handed her themselves.

**Why:** Closing sentence that explains the scene's meaning to the reader — `_genre.md` bans editorializing and says to let the moment land. The metaphor is also mixed: a needle is threaded, a trap is not.
**Suggested rewrite:** `The scarred one's face closes up. He was put in this room to catch her, and he did not.`

### [15] Cross-narrator convergence

> Even with Vukradin's tune riding under her words and a blessing on top of it, the answer comes out flat, and Aldric is not impressed.

**Why:** `tune riding under her words` is musical-layering vocabulary — Vukradin's native organizing metaphor per his spec, not Soma's. She would name the mechanic or the body, not the arrangement.
**Suggested rewrite:** `Vukradin plays under her and she has a blessing on top of it, and the answer still comes out flat. Aldric is not impressed.`

## Verdict

The strongest problem is [5] — `shell sprout` spent on the armorsmith burns a signature phrase on the wrong person and momentarily misdirects the reader to Vukradin; after that, the closing editorial ([14]) and the three literary-polish sentences ([3], [9], [13]) are the ones pulling hardest against Soma's blunt-it rule. The scene's spine (tea, the bale, `Nobody laughs. That is fine. It was accurate, not funny.`, `I watch his hands go quiet`) is genuinely her, so this is spot-edits, not re-narration.
