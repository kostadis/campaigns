## Strongest recurring theme: one writer in five hats

The banned `with the X of someone who Y` construction is nearly gone, but the *habit* it names — one narrator's observational lens leaking into everyone else's — is the set's dominant defect (15 convergence flags, and it drives most of the 16 voice-spec conflicts too). The evidence is verbal, not impressionistic:

1. **`where everyone can see`** — three uses, three scenes, two narrators: `blesses him where everyone can see` (Brewbarry, 03), `I put the question where everyone can see it` (Vukradin, 04), `I lay them out one at a time, where everyone can see them` (Brewbarry, 05).
2. **`lands` as the verb of arrival-and-effect** — six uses across three narrators: `It lands thin` and `Every word lands exactly where she set it` (Soma, 01), `The name lands strange` and `before the sentence lands anywhere` (Brewbarry, 03), `when it lands` / `land on us` (Vukradin, 04).
3. **hands-as-tell** — all four narrators: `I watch his hands go quiet` (Soma), `his hands still black from the work` (Soma), `His hands have stopped obeying him` (Vukradin), `I watch his hands, his mouth, the skin around his eyes` (Brewbarry), `The banker's hands are not easy on his desk anymore` (Brewbarry).
4. **`chew`** — three uses, two scenes: `Soma is still chewing the clerk` (04), `the name gets chewed over twice` and `it is chewing on us` (05).
5. **The `X is not Y` aphorism** — seven instances in scene 04 alone, plus `The doorway is not the room` / `A name is not a rope you can pull` (05) and `To those shadows she is not blessed` (01).

Downstream of that, the two scenes with the same narrator are the clearest test, and Brewbarry fails it in a specific way: his spec forbids sarcasm and interior over-explanation, and both scenes hand him exactly those (the Cullen `same things` gloss in 03; the doubled exile backstory plus the `favors hold a man up better than trust does` maxim in 05).

The second-strongest theme is **signature-as-wallpaper**: a character's specced signature deployed until it stops signifying. Vukradin's music metaphor runs six times in 858 words against a spec that says it should surface only where it earns its place; Valphine's archival figure runs three times in 700 words; Vukradin's own `column closes clean` ledger line is repeated verbatim twenty lines later.
