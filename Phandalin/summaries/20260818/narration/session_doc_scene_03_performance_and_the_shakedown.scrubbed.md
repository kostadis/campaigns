The name is strange in the room. Lathander's Death. Uneasy laughter in the pews. Nobody starts it and nobody stops it. Then somebody stands up and cheers, and the rest of them decide it's allowed. The people are really excited about it. Good name.

Vukradin is bouncing on his toes. "Are we… do we get to play now? Can I play?"

"Yeah, it's time for the band," Valphine says.

Time for death metal, he tells the room. Valphine tells them, or maybe just us, "Common is not my native language, just to be clear." Then, "Undercommon is my native language."

Vukradin starts, and it goes wrong. His fingers tangle. A string buzzes. It comes apart. Scattered applause. Everybody a little uncertain how to handle it. In the back a man hums a hymn that isn't the one being sung.

Vukradin doesn't stop. "Alright, Valphine, pull it off." Then, leaning in, "Valphine, do you want to sing in Undercommon? I speak Undercommon."

"I think that would be appropriate. I think that would be appropriate."

She sings dark words to a bright room. At first the room holds still. Then she gets into it, and the crowd warms, swaying, cheering.

"From abyssal night, the darkness flees beyond…"

"Mighty sword, descendant glory, born in Lord and grave."

"Your sacred lights will save us all."

While the music plays, I go around with a little basket. I hold it out and I look at each person while I hold it. People are generous. Generous is easy when I stand close.

Quite a few of them are dressed like me. Furs, straps, the whole look. Not one bathrobe among them. I check twice. So I go to them between verses and suggest they buy a bathrobe. It would look so much nicer. Soft and comfy. That's what true barbarians do.

Over by the pillar a woman finds Soma. "You are the protector of the Glades, and I was wondering if you would be willing to do a blessing for my garden."

"What, with Meril's staff?" Soma says. "I didn't know the staff could do blessings!" And, "As a druid? I don't know."

The woman doesn't mind. She wants all of them. "There's plenty of gods, why worship only one? You have to have gods for different purposes." Maybe a little of Mielikki's blessing too. She is asking a tortle with a staff.

"I guess I can fake it. I'm not exactly trained in the religious arts, you know? And I'm following — there are these Lathandrians, or Lathandarites, or whatever." Then, "I think we can arrange that. What is it you would like out of your garden?"

"Oh, just that it grows, and it's beautiful and lush, and that nothing…"

"Life to Spring." Soma lifts Meril's staff and says her words, and the blessing takes. From where I stand with the basket I can't tell a faked blessing from a real one. Neither can the woman. She leaves glowing. Soma watches her go. "Yeah, she'll come around, but after Valphine, she's thinking this Lathander's got the right idea."

The concert ends. The service is over. Some folks are still sitting around, and there is the man in the green suit. Cullen Sharpe. House Margaster. He compliments us on the new name, very impressed with it.

"We are Lathander's death! Yeah!" Vukradin says.

Cullen mentions in passing that House Margaster always delivers the goods.

"Ominous, given the trade route shut down, or whatever you want to call it," Soma says.

He sees my basket. I hold it out. While I wait I try to count what's in it. Many coins. I lose count.

"You're taking up donations for the church, right?" Vukradin says.

"Yeah, of course, yeah. It goes to the temple, yeah. Yeah. Absolutely."

"It's normal to make donations while you're at church, that's pretty typical."

"Tithing? Sure," Soma says.

Cullen pulls out some coins. Holds them over the basket. Then he decides he doesn't want to donate. He puts them away. He smiles at us instead. The smile is not coins.

"Who is this guy, again?"

Green suit. House Margaster. Right.

"It's a great smile," Soma says.

"Who stopped the other guy from attacking us," Vukradin says.

Right, right. I tower over him. I make my voice boom with the spell again.

"Why aren't you donating?"

He looks at me awkwardly.

"The better question is, do you fear the light?" Valphine says.

"There you go."

"Well done," Vukradin says.

I flex. One pec, then the other. Boom. Boom.

Cullen notices everybody staring at him. "Well, never let it be said. House Margaster is very happy to donate to Lathander's Temple." And he applauds Valphine's sermon.

"Yes. Hollow flattery is not the…" The word is in my head somewhere. It doesn't come. "…full, brother. Now, spill the coins."

He gives some coins. Some coins. No. My hand closes over the whole pouch. He holds on. It doesn't matter. My hand is bigger. The pouch comes out of his fingers and goes in the basket. "Thank you for your generosity, brother."

I walk away.

"Did you just… Did you just rob him?" Vukradin asks.

"Definitely? I didn't rob him? I give him a nudge to be more generous."

"Of course not, Vukradin. This was a generous donation that I was happy to make," Cullen says, fast.

"Absolutely."

"Thank you for supporting Lathander's death," Vukradin says.

Cullen pauses. "Vukradin, as you know, we all want the same things." The same things. Vukradin gives things back to people. Cullen makes people who need things given back. That is not the same thing.

"I'm Lathander's death? You support Lathander's death. We want the same thing!"

"Exactly! Why would there be any confusion?"

"The world is ugly," Soma says.

Then the whispering. Two of them, heads together, one saying, "I heard Lathander himself walk the surface to mark her personally."

"Sure, yeah, you should go with that," Vukradin tells Valphine.

Valphine is in their faces before the whisper finishes. "And what do you think?"

They go stiff. Mouths half open. Eyes down, then up. "I think that's true. I think that's true."

"Yeah, and doesn't that call for a generous donation?" I put the basket right under them.

"Dude, you're turning it into a shakedown!" Vukradin says.

"Isn't that what's true?"

"You know, properly religious," Soma says.

I lean in. The man doesn't fold. He just beams at me, blessed by Lathander right now, too full of light to be scared of anything my size. Vukradin steps up next to me and glares too. His glare is not a big glare. Then he hums me a little run of notes, and the notes get into my shoulders, and I lean in the rest of the way. Come on, I tell them. Cough up some coins. They cough up. The basket gets heavier.

"We need to not just offer donations, we need to do the whole business model thing. Do the pilot, the business rollout," Valphine says. She is starting to think like Linene. Good.

Which reminds me. "By the way, I meant to ask, is there any magic way to plant a bug onto somebody or someplace?"

They look at me. "So that we could spy on them, listen to their…"

"Yeah, you can totally do that," Vukradin says.

"So, how does that work?"

"Well, we don't have that spell."

"Well, we need to look for that spell, and one of us needs to get it, so that we can listen to Cullen's private conversations and find out how their business works, so that we can do some sabotage."

"I'd be able to buy a scroll, right?" Soma says.

Then Lord Neverember is standing with us, and Aldus is at his elbow, talking to him nervously.

"Welcome to our church," Valphine says.

"Did you call it our church or my church?" Vukradin asks.

"Our church is fine, yeah." And, "They're not branding me as separate from the establishment."

"I thought your intention was to create a schism."

"No, no, I intend to take over the whole religion, not… Not create a schism."

Neverember looks at her and smiles. Then he walks away.

"My lord… We're joining you for dinner tonight, isn't that right?" Vukradin calls.

Another smile. Tight-lipped. Aldus hurries in. "Yes, yes, have no worries, have no fears. Dinner, dinner is going to happen. Lord Neverember is very busy and preoccupied."

Valphine's golden eyes follow the lord. The lord looks at Cullen Sharpe and his face goes sour, like the man is a smell. She says so, quiet.

"Aren't we all?" Soma says.

"My lord, is there anything that we can do to assist you? You may think about that, and if you wish to discuss that tonight at dinner, we'd be very glad to hear of anything we could do to assist you," Valphine says.

"Yes, I think I have many questions, as long as we keep in mind the need to circulate the wealth and not hoard it."

"To circulate the wealth…"

"He wants us — he wants the money," Soma says.

The lord looks past Cullen, who is schmoozing at Vukradin. Looks past me and the pouch I took.

"He gave it willingly," Soma says.

"I don't like anything about Cullen Sharpe. Like, I'm not onto him at all," Vukradin says.

But Neverember is fixed on Valphine. "I truly do admire your focus on eliminating those who are enemies of Lathander."

"Enemies of the light, yes."

"Yes, I think that is an excellent, focused use of power. But I am curious how that message has been received by Lathander's clerics. It seemed to be more about embracing goodness and love for all."

"Well, that is the old way." Then, "Or… dedication to the mission and the glory of the Morninglord, rather than such passive…"

He smiles at her. "Yes, I think, Valphine, your approach to the Morninglord's powers is something that we should keep talking about. I can see how we could work wonders together."

"I look forward to your partnership."

"Yes, I look forward to your partnership as well, Valphine. It's important in these trying times that we have people in our… people who see the need for direct action and…" He walks off in the middle of it, mumbling about power and the need for exercising it.

"We are men of action, lies did not become us," Soma says.

Valphine finds the armorsmith in the crowd and blesses him in front of the whole hall. Then the sacrament.

"Are you not administering the sacrament to everyone?" Vukradin asks.

"Anyone who wants."

"I think you should be calling them up and having them stare into the sun and experience the blinding Light of Lathander."

"This is the way."

Vukradin encourages them forward, and one by one they come up and look into the sun. Most of the congregation has already gone home. The ones who stay, stay all the way. Eyes streaming, faces up. They are happy.

"How many devout followers does Valphine have in this city?" Vukradin asks.

"And how do we invest in the local lens crafters?" Soma says.

Eight.

"8?" Vukradin says. Then, "Oh, those are rookie numbers."

It's a start. Eight here, and the temple back home. The numbers stack.

"This way, each of them convert two people, and those convert two people," Soma says.

While that's going on, a merchant works his way over to Soma. Exile clothes, my look, sewn by somebody who was never exiled from anything. Soma stands off to the side, observing, like she always does. He asks her anyway. "Can I license your look? Your turtle look."

"I was born with it, I don't know that I have any ownership over it."

"Well, I was kind of hoping that we could call it the SOMA line."

"Don't sell out," Vukradin says.

"Don't sell out, but wait a minute, wait a minute, wait a minute. So, my red cloak's not too far off from a bathrobe. Couldn't we sort of combine production lines here?"

"There's an idea," Vukradin says.

They look at me. "Sorry, I was deep into researching how we could spy on Cullen." I pat the basket. "Now that I have his purse, I'm looking for some sort of magic spell or artifact that I can use to plant a bug on him."

"In the meantime, we're trying to figure out if we can share productions of robes and soma cloaks," Soma says.

"Of course."

The merchant looks between us, waiting for a yes or a no.

"That sounds like a yes," Soma says.

"I completely trust my partners."

The basket is heavy on my arm. Best service I have ever attended.
