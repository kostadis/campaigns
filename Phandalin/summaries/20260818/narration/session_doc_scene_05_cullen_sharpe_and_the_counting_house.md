The gnome. That thread is still loose from yesterday. Cullen owes us a name.

"Yeah, we've got a quest. Talk to a gnome at the docks about why the shipping manifold has stopped working," Vukradin says.

The question is simple. I say it out loud so it stays simple. "Yeah. So who's the gnome, Cullen?"

"Why are we asking this guy?"

"Because he knows."

"He's a man who can get things," Soma says.

The name lives in two places. The Counting House keeps the Commission's paper. "We will." And Cullen promised it. A promise from House Margaster is a thing I want to weigh in my own hand. "Now, we're gonna do both."

"That's right," Valphine says.

"First, I ask Cullen, then I go to the counting house and cross-check the info, and see if Cullen…"

"I have want nothing to do with Cullen. If you're talking to him, I'm not there," Vukradin says.

Fair. He can stand in the doorway. The doorway is not the room.

Cullen looks past my shoulder when I come in. He is hunting for Vukradin and does not find him, and something in his face sags. "Oh, I don't care."

"How can I help you?"

"Whoa… Remember, I asked you to find the gnome's name for me."

"Yes, yes, yes, yes. And of course, far be it from me to not deliver on what you've asked for. As I said, the House Margaster always delivers."

He delivers. The name is Bimble Nackle.

From the doorway, where Vukradin is standing so that he is technically not here, the name gets chewed over twice. "Bimble… Bimble Nackle? I like bimbo better."

A name is a start. A name is not a rope you can pull. "Name is great, location is better, and if I could know who he works for as well, that'd be awesome. Do you know?"

"So, what is it that you wanted to know? Like, where is he?"

"Where can I find this gnome, and who does he work for?"

Valphine studies the name like a coin she is testing with her teeth. "So, what we can tell from this gnomish name is that he is of very low class stature, because his name is very short."

"How can we tell that?" Vukradin, from the doorway.

"It has a very small number of syllables. See if…"

A pause. "I just made that up."

"Very convincing!"

"Yeah, you sold me on it." I mean it. That is good spycraft. I file the trick away.

Cullen gives what he has. Bimble was a fastidious gnome. He worked the machinery. Beyond that, nothing.

"He doesn't know who hired him, who he's working for." Where he lives. Where to find him. I count the holes on my fingers. More holes than name.

"Unfortunately, we merely took over the… we were not involved, and perhaps if we had been, the Commission's transportation system would have continued to be working."

"Perhaps," Soma says.

"So who was involved?" He gives me a wall. "I'm asking, who hired this guy?"

"I'm sorry, but the House Margaster was not involved in the hiring of Bimble. We just heard of his name after the machinery stopped working."

"And you didn't recommend him to the people who hired him, or anything like that?"

"No, no, no, no, no, no, no."

Seven nos. I count them. One no does the job. Seven is decoration. I watch his hands, his mouth, the skin around his eyes. Nothing gives. From the doorway Vukradin says, "He's telling the truth."

"No. Certainly doubt it. Okay."

Outside, Vukradin is still wearing his sour face about all of it.

"You know, the word is not always black and white, Vukradin. It's mostly gray."

"Yeah, well, I'm a celestial."

"True," Soma says.

"Alright, so we got the name, not the location. Alright, maybe the counting house will have the location, and who commissioned him."

"Yes. Still need to find a way to spy on Cullen, though. I'll figure…"

Vukradin remembers the pouch. Cullen's pouch, still on my belt. "Do you want to put one of the sending stones in the bag, and then give it back to them?"

"What's the sending stone again?"

Soma explains it. One stone carries a voice to its twin. The pieces click together in my head like a snare being set. "So we disguise the sending stone as a coin, and then we cast Magic Mouth on another coin, and…"

Vukradin can't make a disguise like that hold. Not yet, not with what he carries.

"Yeah, we'll find some magic shop where they can do that for us, for a…"

"I thought we needed to give the bag back to him now."

"No, no, not now, later."

"Yeah, yeah. And so the coin that has Magic Mouth cast on it will just repeat the last 5 minutes of conversation based on the trigger, and then it will tell it to the sending stone that can send the message to us."

"Brewbarry, you've gotten a lot smarter. This is beyond me, man. This is all you."

A fairy gave me goodberries once. They were not goodberries. I woke up, the artifact was gone, and the blame was mine. Since then I check what things are before I trust what they look like.

"I need a headband of intellect."

"It enhances my spycraft." And more than that. "And then we can discover details about their business, and we can act where it will hurt them the most, you know?"

"I'm on a mission, man."

"A mission from Lathander," Vukradin says.

"Mission to sell bathrobes," Soma says.

"Right." Both are true.

Outside the Temple of Lathander the clouds pull apart. The colonnade keeps the wind off and the talk quiet. The city is waking up and it is chewing on us: a new band, Vukradin the legendary musician in it, Lord Neverember speaking privately to Valphine and liking what he heard.

"Yeah, Brewbarry wanted to go to the counting house," Vukradin says.

I do.

Aurelan Vance sees me walk in and for one breath his face is pure pain. Then the smile goes on. The most professional of professional smiles.

"My good friend, Mr. Brewbarry, I am delighted to see you."

"And so am I, because I know you have the information I'm looking for."

"Yes, well, this is not a great secret. I mean, the Commission, as you know, was responsible for trade and revenue growth in the area, and simply said that the manifold experienced a maintenance failure."

"Somebody. Can you be more precise?"

"Not somebody. The commission. The commission."

"Commission, okay? Yes. Who specifically in the commission said this, do you know?"

"Well, it was a public announcement by the… I will get you a name, just there was a commission who announced it, because — that's the word I'm looking for…"

"This was part of the… this, of course, caused great consternation, as — since you are now planning to be a man of wealth, and I'm sure Linene, as a wealth management consultant, has already communicated this to you. But the Commission was involved in managing the trade, and all the major houses had shares in the Commission."

"Right. The majority holder being…"

"Lord Neverember, is my understanding, but the Commission's membership was a tightly guarded secret. So, they're involved in all the businesses around Neverwinter, and have definitely been the method by which Lord Neverember has been using to grow, but this is not secret, Brewbarry, I… So."

I start in. "So my good friend, my good…" He holds up a hand.

"I'm not… before you interrupt me one more time, let me just — so, what happened was that the commission described, says that this is a maintenance failure. They hired a technician who investigated it, but the commission said he found nothing. But they have never proffered his name, or who he worked for, or where he is. For all we know, he never did exist, but I could not imagine why they would say that. After all, the manifold was a critical part of the revenue that the Commission generated for the City of Neverwinter, and this has opened up opportunity for new players in trade."

"Well, that was a very long sentence for very little information. But, if you were me, and trying to find this mysterious gnome, who would you talk to at the commission to find out?"

He goes pale at that. The color drops out of him like a tapped keg.

Valphine leans in with her answer. "I don't know, I feel like the way to find someone is to put a hit out on them, and then it becomes someone else's problem to find them."

"The Assassin's Guild always knows how to find their targets, right?"

Vance goes paler.

"I mean, it's just natural," she says.

"She jests. As you know, she's a cleric of Lathander," Vukradin says.

"They are jokers," Soma says.

I nod along. My friends. The banker's hands are not easy on his desk anymore.

"So we also do have an audience with Lord Neverember. If he was part of this council, we could… ask him," Valphine says.

"I don't know, I can't help you beyond that, unfortunately, Mr. Brewbarry. The information I have and the resources of the Counting House are more in the financial management rather than the investigatory. But I do think I would say that it was an unfortunate malfunction."

"Yeah, sounds to me like you need to rethink your strategy, my friend. I mean, finance is at the root of everything, and so is information. So how can you make more money without good information? I don't know. Anyway…"

"I'll find out who this gnome is, I'll find out who hired him, and I'll get to the bottom of this. Last question, would you have any idea why Lord Neverember's people would tell me that they had nothing to do in the hiring of this gnome technician."

Wrong house. I hear it as it leaves my mouth. "Yeah, yeah, House Margaster, yes."

His confusion looks real. "House Margaster would have nothing to do with the hiring of this individual."

"Aren't they on the commission?"

"Well, to the best of my knowledge, no, this has been the source of much contention in the city."

The pieces sit wrong on the table. I lay them out one at a time, where everyone can see them. "Then there's something I'm missing here, I don't get it. Okay. So, House Margaster is not on the commission. The commission hired the gnome, who sabotaged the device. And the main beneficiaries — benefit from this is House Margaster."

Or there is something else going on. Something I cannot see yet. Soma says a middleman would be easy for them, easy and clean.

"Yeah, yeah, so there must be a connection. Between House Margaster…" Vukradin finishes it for me: and somebody on the Commission. He has heard enough of this.

"He's got the yarn board out already," Soma says.

The board is in my head. The strings are real.

"Yeah, my friend is just kidding. And thank you for doing me this favor and finding this information. Know that if you've done me a favor, I'll do you a favor. Thank you."

"Mr. Brewbarry, as I've said, we are very happy…"

"Doing quid pro quo now?" Vukradin.

"That's right. That's how it works, my friend."

"That is not how it works!"

"That's how it works in Brewbarry's world. You know, you be nice to him, he'd be nice to you."

"You should be nice to everybody!"

"And they'll be nice back," Soma says.

"…trading favors, then I can be Neverwinter's godfather."

"Give him an offer he can't refuse, Brewbarry."

People do things when they are motivated. I am giving this banker motivation. He helped me. I can help him. Simple. The tribe threw me out for a thing a fairy did, and Vukradin's music found me in this city when nothing else did. I know how favors hold a man up better than trust does.

"It's always good to have a banker's friend, you know, or a friend."
