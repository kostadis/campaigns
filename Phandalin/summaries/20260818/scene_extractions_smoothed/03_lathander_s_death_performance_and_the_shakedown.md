# Lathander's Death Performance and the Shakedown

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party's band, Lathander's Death, performs for the congregation while Brewbarry aggressively collects donations and the party mingles with notable attendees.
- The band name draws "uneasy laughter," but as they start playing someone stands and cheers — "the people are really excited about it."
- Vukradin and Valphine perform a musical set, with Valphine singing in Undercommon to a crowd that gradually warms to the performance. Performance rolls: Vukradin a natural 1, Valphine 17. Valphine: "Common is not my native language, just to be clear." Vukradin: "Valphine, do you want to sing in Undercommon? I speak Undercommon."
- The DM narrates scattered applause "as Vukradin's performance leaves everybody a little bit uncertain about how to handle it," and someone starts humming a hymn that isn't the one being sung; once Valphine "starts getting into it, the crowd kind of warms up to it, and there's... swaying and cheering."
- The table plays the song itself: "From abyssal night, the darkness flees beyond... Mighty sword, descendant glory, born in Lord and grave... Your sacred lights will save us all." (truncated)
- Brewbarry works the room with a basket during the music, rolling intimidation "just to make sure that people [are] generous."
- Brewbarry moves through the crowd in his bathrobe, encouraging others dressed similarly to purchase proper bathrobes. He notices "quite a few people dressed like him" — not in bathrobes — and "suggest[s] that they buy a bathrobe, that it would look so much nicer, and it's soft and comfy. That's what true barbarians do."
- Soma is approached by a supplicant who asks for a blessing for her garden: "You are the protector of the Glades, and I was wondering if you would be willing to do a blessing for my garden." Soma: "I didn't know the staff could do blessings!" and "I'm not exactly trained in the religious arts."
- The woman is syncretic — "there's plenty of gods, why worship only one?" — and asks for a bit of Mielikki's blessing. Soma: "What is it you would like out of your garden?" She wants it to grow "beautiful and lush." Soma successfully performs a druidic blessing using the Meril's Staff (religion 15 on a minus 1): "I faked it well enough. Soma's not that religious." DM: "you've got Meril's staff, and rumors are spreading." Soma: "she'll come around, but, you know, after Valphine, she's thinking this Lathander's got the right idea."
- Cullen Sharpe approaches the group to compliment their new band name, Lathander's Death, and makes a pointed remark that House Margaster always delivers the goods. Soma: "Ominous, given the... trade route shut down."
- Brewbarry attempts to collect a donation from Cullen Sharpe, who initially offers only a smile as payment: he "pulls out some coins, puts them over the basket, and decides he doesn't want to donate. And smiles at you, as if, like, the smile is the donation."
- Brewbarry uses a magically booming voice to intimidate Cullen Sharpe, questioning whether he fears the light. Brewbarry towers over him — "Why aren't you donating?" — and Valphine sharpens it: "The better question is, do you fear the light?" Brewbarry flexes, "doing the Rock thing... The pecs are going boom, boom."
- Cullen notices everybody staring at him: "well, never let it be said. House Margaster is very... is very happy to donate to Lathander's Temple," and applauds Valphine's sermon. Brewbarry: "Hollow flattery is not the... full, brother. Now, spill the coins." (truncated)
- Brewbarry successfully grapples Cullen Sharpe's entire coin pouch away from him, claiming it as a generous donation for the church: Cullen offers "some coins"; Brewbarry — "No, I grabbed the whole pouch" — rolls a 20 on strength and stuffs it in the basket. "Thank you for your generosity, brother." Vukradin: "Did you just rob him?" Brewbarry: "I give him a nudge to be more generous."
- Cullen Sharpe attempts to smooth over the interaction, claiming he and the party want the same things regarding returning things to people: "of course not, Vukradin. This was a generous donation that I was happy to make... Vukradin, as you know, we are all... we want the same things." The DM glosses: Vukradin wants to return things to people, Cullen wants to help him return things — "He can provide all the people you need to return." Vukradin: "I'm Lathander's death? You support Lathander's death. We want the same thing!"
- Brewbarry asks how much gold he collected; the DM: "Oh, that's awkward." (Left unresolved.)
- Brewbarry continues to shake down other congregants for donations, filling his collection basket. Valphine overhears whispering — "I heard Lathander himself walk the surface to mark her personally" — and confronts the gossipers ("And what do you think?"), who, caught out, stammer "I think that's true." Brewbarry: "doesn't that call for a generous donation?" and puts the basket under them. Vukradin: "Dude, you're turning it into a shakedown!" Soma: "You know, properly religious."
- Intimidation: Brewbarry 15 plus a D8 Bardic Inspiration (5) for 20, beating the DM's 17 (the gossiper "feels blessed by Lathander right now"); Vukradin joins in with an 11. They cough up coins — "I have to figure out how much they're gonna cough up."
- Brewbarry asks whether there is "any magic way to plant a bug onto somebody" to listen to Cullen's private conversations "so that we can do some sabotage." Vukradin: "we don't have that spell."
- Lord Neverember approaches. Valphine: "Welcome to our church." Vukradin: "Did you call it our church or my church?" Valphine, on her intentions: "I intend to take over the whole... the whole religion, not... a schism." Neverember smiles and walks away.
- Aldus, accompanying Lord Neverember, nervously confirms the party's upcoming dinner invitation. Vukradin: "My lord… We're joining you for dinner tonight, isn't that right?" Neverember smiles "tight-lipped." Aldus: "yes, yes, have no worries, have no fears. Dinner... is going to happen. Lord Neverember is very busy and preoccupied."
- Valphine's insight (21): Neverember "seems particularly appalled with Cullen Sharpe." Soma: "Aren't we all?"
- Valphine offers assistance and proposes discussing it at dinner. Neverember: "yes, I think I have many questions, as long as we keep in mind the need to circulate the wealth and not hoard it." Soma: "He wants the money."
- Lord Neverember approaches Valphine, expressing admiration for her focus on eliminating enemies of the light and suggesting they could work wonders together: "I truly do admire your focus on eliminating those who are enemies of Lathander." Valphine: "Enemies of the light, yes." Neverember: "I think that is an excellent, focused use of power. But I am curious how that message has been received by Lathander's clerics. It seemed to be more about embracing goodness and love for all." Valphine: "Well, that is the old way... dedication to the mission and the glory of the Morninglord, rather than such passive..."
- Neverember: "yes, I think, I think, Valphine, your approach to the Morninglord's powers is something that we should keep talking about. I can see how we could work wonders together." Valphine: "I look forward to your partnership." Neverember: "It's important in these trying times that we have people... who see the need for direct action," then walks off mumbling "about power and the need for exercising it." Soma supplies the Princess Bride line: "We are men of action, lies did not become us."
- Valphine administers the sacrament to willing followers, encouraging them to stare into the sun, establishing a small branch of Lathander's Searing Light in Neverwinter with eight devout followers. Vukradin encourages the crowd forward one by one; the DM clarifies it isn't "half the city" — most people have left, and the small group that remains is "in ecstasy and happy." Vukradin: "Oh, those are rookie numbers." DM: "between the orcs going around preaching conversion, and the eight people here, and then the temple... you're starting to rack up some serious numbers." Soma: "And how do we invest in the local lens crafters?"
- Valphine goes to the armorsmith and visibly blesses him in front of the crowd.
- A merchant approaches Soma about licensing her tortle appearance for a new clothing line, leading to a discussion about combining production with Brewbarry's bathrobe business. The "exile aesthetic merchant" asks to "License your look. Your turtle look" as "the SOBA line." Soma: "I was born with it, I don't know that I have any ownership over it," then: "Greycloak's not too far off from a bathrobe. Couldn't we sort of combine production lines here?" Brewbarry: "I completely trust my partners." DM: "I'm beginning to see why Vukradin and Brewbarry got together so well. There's a certain naivete about them that is charming."
