# Session 2026-08-18

Date: 2026-08-18

## Summary
At the break of dawn, the party arrived at the Spire of the Morninglord in Neverwinter, finding the temple's threshold already crowded with a diverse and fractious gathering. Skeptical Lathanderite clerics eyed the group with suspicion, while agents of Loviatar lurked in the shadows, and Lord Neverember himself stood among the faithful and curious. Adding to the spectacle, the armorsmith Ostren Vayle appeared at the doors, having worked through the entire night to finish the gleaming white dragon scale armor. The commission had been Brewbarry's, but the smith had come to believe - a vision, an insight, a conviction he could not argue himself out of - that the shining white plate was meant for Valphine, and he had built it for her. He fitted it onto her right there at the threshold, seeking the blessing of Lathander rather than payment, causing an immediate stir among the onlookers before the party had even stepped inside.

Valphine entered the temple with her eyes blazing with divine radiance and ominous phantom whispers trailing in her wake, then unleashed a blinding torrent of holy light to announce the start of the morning service, causing half the congregation to squint and shield their eyes. Before the sermon could begin, Brother Aldric pulled the party aside into a private receiving chamber, where Aldric had gathered the clerics knowing what they would do - the trap was his to set, though a scarred sun priest was the one who sprang it - and they attempted to theologically corner Valphine by questioning her emphasis on pain and suffering. She deftly threaded the trap, arguing that healing is painful and that to embrace the fullness of Lathander's light, one must experience its full intensity. Aldric, ultimately persuaded, accepted her theological points and welcomed her to the pulpit.

What followed was a sermon unlike anything the Spire had ever witnessed. Valphine declared Lathander not a god of quiet comfort, but a blinding, unyielding force that destroys darkness without negotiation. She introduced the sacred sacrament of blinding light, urging the faithful to cast off all illusions and stare unflinchingly into pure divinity until every shadow within the soul was utterly incinerated. When a Loviatarite agent rose to denounce her, accusing her of stealing their goddess's sacraments of pain, Valphine responded by flooding him with holy light and robbing him of his sight, calmly insisting she had simply given him the blessing. As the blinded man was cast out, a second Loviatarite warned that there would be consequences, but the congregation met them both with contempt, and Valphine's message carried the room.

With the sermon concluded, Vukradin and Valphine took to performing for the congregation, with Valphine singing in Undercommon as the crowd gradually warmed to the music. Brewbarry moved through the pews in his bathrobe, collecting donations with an enthusiasm that bordered on coercion, pausing to encourage similarly dressed attendees to purchase proper bathrobes. Soma, meanwhile, was approached by a syncretic supplicant who recognised her as a follower of Mielikki and asked for a blessing on her garden, and Soma obliged with a druidic invocation using the Meril's Staff, improvising the form of the rite convincingly enough to satisfy the woman. When Cullen Sharpe of House Margaster appeared to compliment the band's new name, Lathander's Death, Brewbarry used a magically amplified voice to question whether Cullen feared the light, then physically wrestled the man's entire coin pouch away from him and dropped it into the donation basket. Cullen, surrounded by the congregation and unable to protest, was forced to smile and call it a generous donation. Lord Neverember later approached Valphine privately, expressing deep admiration for her aggressive theology and suggesting that the two of them could work wonders together, before walking away muttering about power and the need to exercise it.

The gathering also brought the long-awaited resolution of Perrin's claim to the Alagondar family necklace - the Necklace of Fireballs. He arrived with three witnesses: a passionate revolutionary, a genealogist who had cross-referenced his research with Boney's study of family corpses, and a frightened notary clerk who revealed that many Alagondar records had been purged when Lord Neverember seized power. Despite deep suspicions from some in the party that Perrin was being controlled by darker forces, Vukradin chose to honor the principle of returning what was rightfully owned. He handed over the necklace in the Temple of Lathander; Brewbarry supplied the solemn note, reminding Perrin where the handover had taken place and urging him to use it for good, and Soma called it the start of his redemption arc. By the end of the service, Valphine had established a small but devoted branch of Lathander's Searing Light in Neverwinter, with eight new followers willing to stare into the sun in her name.

Afterward, Brewbarry sought out Cullen Sharpe to collect the name of the gnome technician connected to the shipping manifold failure. Cullen provided the name Bimble Nackle, describing him as a fastidious gnome who had worked on the machinery, but denied any knowledge of his current whereabouts or employer, insisting House Margaster had played no role in his hiring. The party then made their way to the Counting House, where a visibly nervous Aurelan Vance explained that the Commission — a secretive body with Lord Neverember as its majority holder — had publicly declared the manifold failure a maintenance issue and claimed their hired technician found nothing, without ever revealing his name. Brewbarry quickly identified a troubling contradiction: House Margaster was not a member of the Commission, yet stood to benefit most from the trade shutdown, suggesting a hidden connection that had yet to be uncovered. When Valphine casually suggested consulting the Assassin's Guild as the most natural way to locate a missing person, Aurelan Vance turned pale, and Brewbarry smoothed things over by cementing a reciprocal favor-based relationship with the banker, confident that the threads of the conspiracy would eventually lead somewhere they could pull.

## Locations
### Spire of the Morninglord
A grand temple dedicated to Lathander in Neverwinter, featuring high windows that flood the interior with light and a large hall for congregations. It served as the stage for Valphine's controversial sermon, the band Lathander's Death's performance, and the return of the Alagondar necklace.

### Counting House
The financial institution of Neverwinter where Aurelan Vance manages wealth and records. Brewbarry visited to seek information on the Commission and the trade manifold failure, and established a favor-based relationship with the banker.

### Neverwinter
A major city where the party is currently operating, establishing religious influence through a new branch of Lathander's Searing Light and navigating the complex financial and political landscape of its trade houses and commissions.

## NPCs
### Brother Aldric
A high-ranking cleric of Lathander at the Spire who is concerned about Valphine's unorthodox theological leanings regarding pain. He initially challenges her views but is ultimately persuaded by her theological arguments and accepts her approach.

### Ostren Vayle
The owner of 'Dragon Scales R Us' who worked through the night to finish the white dragon scale armor and personally delivered and fitted it at the temple doors, seeking a blessing from Lathander rather than further payment (the armor was paid for out of the party's shared purse, so no debt stands). The commission was Brewbarry's; the smith built it for Valphine instead, on the strength of a vision that the shining white plate was meant for her. Described as "one of the devout" and "very proud of himself." GM ruling: he becomes the first cleric of Lathander's Searing Light.

### Loviatarite Agents
Followers of the goddess of pain who lurked in the shadows of the Spire and attempted to denounce Valphine's sermon as heretical theft of their own rituals. One was blinded by Valphine's magic, and another warned of future consequences before they were cast out.

### Lord Neverember
The Lord Protector of Neverwinter who attended the morning service and privately approached Valphine to express admiration for her aggressive theological direction focused on eliminating enemies of the light, suggesting a future partnership.

### Aldus
An associate of Lord Neverember who nervously accompanied him to the Spire and confirmed the party's upcoming dinner invitation with the Lord.

### Cullen Sharpe
A representative of House Margaster who attended the sermon wearing a green suit. He was intimidated by Brewbarry into donating his entire coin pouch to the church, attempted to claim shared goals with the party, and provided the name of the gnome technician Bimble Nackle while denying that House Margaster was involved in Bimble's hiring.

### Perrin
A claimant to the Alagondar lineage who brought three witnesses to the Spire to verify his claim. He successfully recovers the powerful Alagondar family necklace from the party after his lineage is confirmed.

### Bimble Nackle
A fastidious gnome technician named by Cullen Sharpe as having worked on the machinery of the shipping manifold before its failure. His current location and employer are unknown, and the Commission never publicly identified him.

### Boney
An associate of the party who provided genealogical insights by studying corpses, helping to verify Perrin's Alagondar lineage claim in collaboration with the genealogist witness.

### Aurelan Vance
A representative of the Counting House who provides financial and political context on the Commission and trade houses. He is visibly intimidated by the party's methods and unsettled by Valphine's suggestion of consulting the Assassin's Guild, but agrees to a relationship based on mutual favors with Brewbarry.
