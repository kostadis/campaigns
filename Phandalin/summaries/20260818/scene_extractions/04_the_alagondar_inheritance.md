# The Alagondar Inheritance

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party meets with Perrin and his three witnesses to verify his claim to the Alagondar family necklace, ultimately deciding to honor the principle of returning it to its rightful owner.
- The party approaches Perrin, who is surrounded by three witnesses waiting to verify his lineage. He keeps looking at Brewbarry and "every so often, when you do your terrifying thing, he squirms a little bit."
- A revolutionary enthusiast provides enthusiastic testimony supporting Perrin's claim as the rightful Alagondar heir: "I'm really glad that Perrin's gonna get that necklace, it's a great symbol of the rightful rulers of Neverwinter." Valphine's insight (29) reads him as genuinely "very enthusiastic about the revolution." Brewbarry rolls 11.
- A genealogist confirms that Perrin is a descendant based on research cross-referenced with Boney's study of corpses and family records. "I had a wonderful conversation with Boney." Soma: "Oh, you found my horse." Genealogist: "his interest in Alagondar genealogy is almost as encyclopedic as mine." Soma: "Yeah, he had a lot of free time." Boney "studied the corpses and the dead and the relationships between the dead."
- The genealogist: Perrin is "a descendant, perhaps one of the primary descendants of the Alagondar family." No other living claimants known; "Boney more or less confirmed that with me."
- Brewbarry pounces on the hedge: "Why do you say, perhaps, the primary descendant? I don't like this, perhaps." When the man protests "I am a scientist... Sorry, I'm a historian," Brewbarry: "You're not a scientist, you're a historian. Or are you?... Maybe you're a pole dancer, I don't know." Vukradin: "Dude, dude, go count the gold." Soma: "You're so good at intimidating everyone." The genealogist clarifies there can never be 100% certainty, but he "would stake his professional reputation that Perrin is, in fact, the true and only claimant."
- Vukradin recognises the setup: "I see what you've done here. The moral dilemma of giving the powerful magical item" — Soma: "to an asshole" — "owner who is evil. But I am gonna stick with my principles. And hand it over."
- A frightened notary clerk reveals that many Alagondar records were purged when Lord Neverember took power, but confirms the records he recalls support Perrin's claim. Vukradin's insight (19): "visibly frightened." The clerk: "when the new administration took over, there was a bit of a purging of records... when the Alagondars were removed from power and Lord Neverember became the first Lord of Neverwinter, well, the removal was a part of a purge."
- Brewbarry: "The guy is clearly being intimidated... We're being manipulated here in thinking that Perrin is the right heir." Vukradin: "So he is the rightful heir. He gets the necklace." Brewbarry: "he's evil."
- Valphine: "I don't know that Perrin is evil. He is certainly intimidated and controlled by people who are evil, but maybe if we can give him a protection racket... An alternative protection racket."
- Soma asks why Perrin would need to pay off or threaten "this wormy guy" if his lineage is sound, and rolls investigation (18): the clerk "is not afraid of Perrin." Vukradin: "I don't think he's intimidated... he's just evil."
- Despite suspicions that some witnesses may be intimidated or that Perrin may be influenced by evil forces, Vukradin decides to honor the principle of returning property to its rightful owner. Brewbarry: "I'm not saying we don't give it back, I'm saying we need to tie it to some sort of" — Soma: "reasonable concession?" — and then: "I'll let it go, but mock my words, this is gonna bite us in the ass... we're just handing him over the knife that he's gonna use to stab us in the back." Soma: "He'll probably stab our face." Vukradin: "No, it'll be more of a fireball to the face." DM: "9, 12D6 fireball."
- Brewbarry: "Maybe we could have put a spell on the necklace so that we can disarm it, or... get it back instantly if he uses it." Vukradin: "I don't support that."
- Vukradin hands over the Alagondar necklace to Perrin, who thanks the party greatly. Vukradin: "Yeah, go away, get out of my... get out of my sight. I don't want to talk to you." DM: "This is childish Vukradin." Soma: "I love it!" Vukradin: "Got your necklace! Goodbye."
- Brewbarry tells Perrin: "remember that this was handed over to you in the Temple of Lathander, you know, this is your chance at redemption. Use it for good." Soma: "The start of your redemption arc."
- Soma: "We did the right thing, right guys?" Brewbarry: "No." DM: "There was no right thing. It was just a thing. The plot would have moved in a wide variety of actions... you did exactly what your characters would do."
