# The Sermon of Searing Light

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Valphine delivers a powerful and aggressive sermon to the gathered faithful of Lathander, introducing a radical new theology centered on the sacrament of blinding light.
- Before the sermon, Valphine spots Perrin — "the guy... claims that we owe him the thing" — sitting in the congregation; the party had told him to be there for the Alagondar handover.
- Valphine addresses the packed congregation, describing Lathander as a fierce, unyielding force of blinding radiance that destroys darkness rather than negotiating with it: "Lathander is not a soft god of quiet comfort or gentle reprieve. He is the blinding radiance that cuts through the stagnant dark and shatters the slumber of the world. Darkness does not yield to meekness, nor does the night retreat before trembling hands. Light is an absolute force, unyielding, fierce, and searing."
- "When the first rays of morning pierce the sky, they do not negotiate with the shadows. They destroy them, exposing every hidden corruption and sweeping away the decay of the night with uncompromising glory."
- The sermon introduces the 'sacrament of blinding light,' urging the faithful to stare into pure divinity until every shadow within the soul is utterly incinerated: "In this holy rite, the faithful cast off all comforting illusions and look unflinchingly into the incandescent glare of pure divinity, until the eye beholds only burning gold, and every shadow within the soul is utterly incinerated. It is in a baptism of fire and brilliance that hardens the spirit into a conduit of divine radiance."
- "Through this sacrament, we are remade not as passive observers, but as living blazes. Forged to carry the intolerable heat of Lathander's glory into every dark corner of this realm."
- Valphine calls for the purging of the wicked and the destruction of those who harbor darkness, framing it as a holy duty: "We do not offer pity to those who cling to the dark, or quarter for the meek and weak, for to harbor shadow is to invite destruction."
- The closing doubles as the band announcement: "Rise, warriors of the morning. Lift high your glowing crests. And let the untamed power of Lathander incinerate every enemy of the dawn. Through light alone we dominate. Through light alone we conquer. Now, behold, Lathander's death."
- Table reaction: "Well done." (Vukradin) "That was extremely well done." (DM) "I think I'm changing religions." (Soma) Valphine adds, "and stop by the gift shop on the way out"; Soma: "Don't forget to buy our merch"; Brewbarry: "Get your bathrobe." Vukradin: "Do you want everyone to stare at the sun until they go blind? Is that the plan?"
- A follower of Loviatar stands and denounces Valphine, accusing her of stealing their goddess's sacraments of pain: "Pain is the maidens! The drow has dressed our sacramite in sacrament and called it Holy."
- Brewbarry answers first: "No, my brothers, pain is simply the darkness you break through on your way to the dawn."
- Valphine responds by magically blinding the heckler with holy light, claiming it is a blessing rather than a curse: "DC-15 Constitution save... I cast Blindness at him." The DM rolls a 1 on the save. Vukradin: "You're burning your spells, man!" then "Cast him out! Cast out the heathen!" Brewbarry: "If you're not a zealot, you get off the boat." Duration: one minute.
- A second Loviatarite warns that there will be consequences for stealing their sacraments: "you have stolen the sacraments of Loviatar! There will be consequences!"
- The party litigates the theft charge: Vukradin — "I don't think staring at the sun is a sacrament of Loviatar." DM — "Inflicting pain, though, is…" Valphine — "The goal is the light, the pain is just a nice benefit, side benefit." Brewbarry — "you call it blinding, I call it blessing. He blessed him with the holy light. You saw the light." Soma — "It's an alternate way of taking the sacrament."
- The congregation reacts with awe and contempt for the Loviatarite agents as they are cast out, while the crowd warms to Valphine's message. DM: "your speech was eloquent, which is why, as they leave, you know, people just sort of stare at them with, like, contempt, so they... didn't make their point, but... The fact that you blinded this guy did."
