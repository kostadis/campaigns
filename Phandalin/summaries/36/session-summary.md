# Chapter 36 A Dragon Defeated, a Bard Tempted, a Barbarian's Vengeance

**Date:** Feb 19th, 2026

## Summary

The party ascended Icespire Hold to face Cryovain the white dragon, only to find the beast already ensnared. The Carver had set up a summoning circle on the rooftop battlements — a ring of burning geometric light — using an obsidian sword as a mental amplifier to dominate the dragon's mind. Cryovain fought the influence, but the ritual was nearly complete. The party had three rounds before the domination was absolute.

What followed was the most concentrated violence the party had yet delivered. Vukradin's bardic inspiration threaded through the battle, landing on Cryovain at a critical moment. Soma's moonbeam burned from an impossible angle. Brewbarry's rage unlocked in full force — his halberd finding dragon-scale gaps through two waves of frost breath that pushed him past his limits. And Valphine — after a string of maddening misses — landed a sacred flame of only 3 damage, which proved exactly enough to end the dragon. The Carver's essence dissipated with it, unmade by the same small, sufficient stroke.

With Cryovain dead, the party faced the spoils and the moral fallout. A block of fused planar ice held roughly 3,000 gold, gems, and trade bars — entirely unspendable without a planar theorist to separate the residium-fused components. The obsidian sword carried a mental amplifier gem that could project music directly into the minds of an audience; Vukradin recognized it immediately as the perfect instrument for his music studio ambitions — and, just as immediately, declared it must be returned to its rightful owner before he could use it. "Save me," he said, and meant it. The blue quartz mermaid statue, meant for someone the party had chosen not to prioritize when they chose the dragon, had no available recipient. Every victory came with a cost.

**The Fate of the Veteran:** A paralyzed veteran lay on the stone, still alive. Soma argued against killing him — he was just a victim. She used bear claws and moonbeam to weaken rather than finish him. Brewbarry overrode her objection with the halberd. "I win, you lose — tradition upheld." Soma accepted the record. "I'm on the record as not being pro-killing, but that's okay."

The aftermath brought complications. Sylvine Wintermoon and her sister Runa appeared with a well-rehearsed con, claiming the dragon's hoard belonged to them and invoking Vukradin's reputation as a treasure-returning saint — "Didn't I tell you, Runa, that the saint Vukradin would come and return our treasure?" Vukradin saw through it with exhausted clarity and dismissed them flatly. Sylvine's second plan — "Second plan! We kill them all!" — triggered a skirmish in the fortress. Valphine's spirit guardians thinned the bandits. Soma went bear. Vukradin paralyzed two veterans with Hold Person; Brewbarry cut them down without hesitation. One bandit, looking at his fallen colleagues, made the wise decision to flee. Zee fortress went quiet.

A Sending from Ser Kaelen arrived, praising their "conflict-free triumph" and summoning them to Phandalin — not Neverwinter — with hints of trouble brewing with the Emerald Enclave. Brewbarry heard "celebrate" and was ready to go. The party turned toward Phandalin.

## Memorable Moments

1. **Valphine's sacred flame of 3** — After a fight full of misses, the smallest possible divine spark drops a dragon. "It's 3, so he died." Lathander's timing is impeccable and hilarious.

2. **Brewbarry's rage** — He grew into something beyond himself on the battlements, putting halberd strikes into Cryovain through two waves of dragon breath that left him running on momentum alone. "The rage is math."

3. **Soma's plea for the veteran** — "Don't kill, don't kill! He was just a victim!" She put herself on record, moved the moonbeam over him to weaken rather than kill, and watched Brewbarry end him anyway.

4. **"I win, you lose — tradition upheld"** — Brewbarry's verdict over the paralyzed veteran. Not cruel, just confirmed, like a fact of nature.

5. **Vukradin's "Bluetooth sword"** — The obsidian weapon's mental amplifier gem could project music directly into minds. He named it instantly. "Save me."

6. **The treasure block** — 3,000 gold fused in planar ice. Entirely real, entirely unspendable. A fortune that exists only to be looked at.

7. **"I just wanted my goddamn music studio"** — Vukradin's refrain throughout, a perfect distillation of every adventurer who wanted a quiet life and got a world-saving quest instead.

8. **Sylvine Wintermoon's pivot** — From "you're a saint who returns treasure" to "second plan, we kill them all" in under a second. The smoothest con artist pivot the party has seen.

9. **Ser Kaelen's "conflict-free triumph"** — The Gauntlet knight returns Vukradin's vocabulary in a sending, redirecting the party to Phandalin with hints of Emerald Enclave trouble.

10. **"He's about as close as someone we can actually trust as anybody"** — Vukradin's honest assessment of Ser Kaelen, corrected immediately to "No, I don't trust him. He's as close as anybody."

11. **"Root Smasher stands, you don't. That's the story."** — Brewbarry's post-skirmish verdict, delivered with the finality of a historian.

12. **"I heard 'celebrate,' say no more. Let's go."** — Brewbarry's entire response to Ser Kaelen's sending. Dragon blood still drying, zero hesitation.

## Scenes

### The Dragon's Mountain Fortress
The party found Cryovain inside a summoning circle on the rooftop battlements — a great ring of burning, geometric light. The Carver's obsidian weapon worked as a mental amplifier, pressing into the dragon's mind. Cryovain was enraged but diminished, fighting an influence that had been wearing him down.

### The Fall of Cryovain
Cryovain launched thirty feet into the air. Valphine's divine light missed. Soma's moonbeam found its mark and the dragon screamed. Brewbarry grew into his rage, meeting the dragon in the air with halberd strikes through two waves of freezing breath that pushed him past his limits. Valphine's sacred flame — a 3 — delivered the killing blow. The Carver's essence dissipated with the dragon. Two deaths for one small, sufficient flame.

### The Fate of the Veteran
A paralyzed veteran lay on the stone, still alive, eyes moving. Soma argued against killing him — he was just a victim. She used bear claws and moonbeam to weaken rather than finish him. Brewbarry overrode her objection with the halberd. "I win, you lose — tradition upheld." Soma accepted the record. "I'm on the record as not being pro-killing, but that's okay."

### The Spoils of Victory
The treasure was a fused block of ice and residium holding roughly 3,000 gold — unspendable. The obsidian sword carried a mental amplifier gem that Vukradin recognized as perfect for projecting music into minds. He declared it must be returned to its rightful owner before he could use it. The blue quartz mermaid statue's intended recipient was unavailable. Every prize came qualified.

### Unwelcome Claimants
Sylvine Wintermoon and her sister Runa appeared, having heard of the treasure-returning bard of Phandalin. Their performance was polished but transparent — Vukradin dismissed them flatly. "You're probably gonna try to steal this, like everybody else steals." Sylvine dropped the act instantly and called for violence.

### Skirmish in the Fortress
Valphine's spirit guardians thinned the bandits. Soma went bear. Vukradin paralyzed two veterans with Hold Person. Brewbarry cut them down without hesitation. One bandit fled mid-fight, deciding there was better money elsewhere. The fortress went quiet.

### A Message from the Gauntlet
Vukradin sent a sending to Ser Kaelen: "The dragon is dead, so is the Carver. We're heading to Neverwinter." Kaelen's reply redirected them to Phandalin with praise, celebration, and a buried warning about trouble with the Emerald Enclave. Brewbarry heard "celebrate" and was ready to go. The party turned toward Phandalin.

## NPCs

- **Cryovain** — The white dragon of Icespire Hold, found inside the Carver's summoning circle, fighting mental domination. Killed by Valphine's sacred flame. Free in death.
- **The Carver** — The parasitic entity from Obsidian Isle using an obsidian mental amplifier to dominate Cryovain. Destroyed when the dragon fell.
- **Sylvine Wintermoon** — Smooth-voiced con artist with a well-researched hook about Vukradin's treasure-returning reputation. Pivoted from sainthood to murder in one beat when the con failed.
- **Runa** — Sylvine's sister and accomplice, providing enthusiastic affirmations ("Blessed be! Blessed be!") on cue. Less sharp than Sylvine.
- **Ser Kaelen** — The Gauntlet knight who sent a carefully worded message redirecting the party to Phandalin. Used Vukradin's own vocabulary ("conflict-free triumph"). Hints at trouble with the Emerald Enclave. Vukradin's trust: "as close as anybody."
- **The Veteran** — A paralyzed soldier killed by Brewbarry while held by Vukradin's magic. His death crystallized the party's differing ethics around mercy and combat tradition.

## Items

- **Obsidian Sword (The Carver's Weapon)** — An obsidian blade with a mental amplifier gem in the pommel. The gem can project music directly into the minds of an audience. Vukradin identified it as perfect for his music studio ambitions but insisted it must be returned to its rightful owner before use. Nicknamed "the Bluetooth sword."
- **The Treasure Block** — Roughly 3,000 gold, gems, and trade bars fused into a single mass of planar ice mixed with residium. Unspendable without a planar theorist to separate the components. Vukradin declared it stolen goods requiring proper documentation before division.
- **Blue Quartz Mermaid Statue** — A treasure item whose intended owner is no longer available for return. A reminder of the cost of priorities.

## Spells & Abilities

- **Sacred Flame (Cantrip)** — Valphine's killing blow against Cryovain. Dealt 3 damage. Objectively the smallest roll possible, subjectively the most perfectly timed divine intervention in the campaign.
- **Moonbeam** — Soma's sustained silver column that burned Cryovain through the fight and was later moved over the paralyzed veteran as a non-lethal pressure tactic.
- **Hold Person** — Vukradin's spell that paralyzed two veterans during the fortress skirmish, leaving them helpless for Brewbarry's halberd.
- **Spirit Guardians** — Valphine's defensive aura that thinned the bandits during the skirmish until a successful attack broke her concentration.
- **Rage (Goliath Barbarian)** — Brewbarry's defining combat state, granting extended reach and enough raw force to trade blows with a white dragon. "The rage is math."
- **Sending** — Vukradin used this to contact Ser Kaelen (25 words). The material component (copper wire) prompted an aside about the world having invented the telephone.
- **Bardic Inspiration** — Vukradin's support ability, threaded through the fight to land on Cryovain at a critical moment.
- **Wild Shape (Bear)** — Soma's combat form during the fortress skirmish.
