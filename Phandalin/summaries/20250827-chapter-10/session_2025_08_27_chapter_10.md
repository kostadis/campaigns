# Chapter 10

Date: 2025-08-27

## Summary
The party made their way back toward Phandalin, their minds turning to unfinished business and forgotten obligations. Along the road, talk turned to a quest they had nearly let slip through their fingers — a plea from Adabra Boda, the midwife and herbalist they had once rescued from Umbrage Hill. She had spoken of a strange magical blight creeping through the Whispering Woods, a grove just outside of town where trees twisted unnaturally and leaves turned sickly pale, their colors all wrong. The blight, she believed, was no natural thing — it was the consequence of meddling forces upsetting the balance of the world. With the woods sitting practically on the doorstep of Phandalin, the party pressed on into town.

They had barely crossed into Phandalin before Adabra herself stepped out to meet them, her eyes finding Soma with quiet urgency. She implored Soma directly, warning that if the changes in the Whispering Woods were left unchecked, the natural balance would be disturbed beyond repair. When Felkur pressed her about the Order of the Gauntlet, Adabra launched into a passionate defense of her Emerald Enclave philosophy — that striving to force the world toward good only provoked an equal and opposite surge of evil in response. She had little patience for organizations that slaughtered monsters and moved on without taking their treasure, donating their spoils to charity as if that made the world a better place. Felkur, growing increasingly irritated by her patronizing tone, eventually cut the conversation short, and Adabra departed with a final offer to sell the party potions of healing, which they declined.

The party then turned their attention to the matter of their accumulated loot, inquiring at the Lionshield Coster about magical assistance to identify the origins and properties of the armor and goods they had recovered from the coast. The merchant informed them that while identification scrolls were not currently in stock, a magic user might be located within a few days. It was only then that Felkur recalled he possessed the means to identify magical items himself — and that the party had recovered six pearls worth one hundred gold pieces each from the lighthouse, more than enough to serve as the necessary components.

The party paid a visit to Harbin Wester, the Town Master of Phandalin, to report their success at the Tower of Storms. Harbin expressed grave concern upon hearing that Talos worshippers had taken root at the lighthouse, though he was relieved to learn the altar had been sanctified and the threat neutralized. The party warned him that Talos worship might still be active in Leilon, and that the lighthouse now stood without a keeper — its light destined to go dark without someone to tend it. Harbin promised to send word to Lord Neverwinter and gently discouraged the party from making the journey north themselves while Phandalin remained in such a state of disrepair.

Their next stop was the tavern, where Sir Kaelen and the keeper known as Spider-Man welcomed them back warmly. The party recounted the most troubling detail from the lighthouse — that the Talos cleric had illuminated his own heart to power the light, something Sir Kaelen said no ordinary shaman of Talos could ever accomplish, suggesting a deeper and more dangerous force lurking behind the scenes. When the party raised the matter of the ambush they had suffered on the road to the tower, Sir Kaelen denied any knowledge of it — and to the party's chagrin, Spider-Man cheerfully revealed that the entire tavern had known about their mission, even running a betting pool on whether all four of them would return. Spider-Man had bet they would all make it back, and he pressed ten gold pieces into Felkur's hand as his share of the winnings.

Sir Kaelen went on to share troubling rumors of a new bandit leader operating in the region — a figure known only as the Carver. Unlike most bandits, who boasted and strutted and made themselves easy to find, the Carver had been unusually careful, hiding behind a name and a trail of organized violence while keeping his true identity carefully concealed. Sir Kaelen admitted he had been hoping the party might serve as his agents in the field, gathering intelligence so the Order of the Gauntlet could move in force. He also addressed the Whispering Woods, confessing he had not investigated it himself, having dismissed Adabra's warnings as an internal matter of the Emerald Enclave — though he expressed open skepticism about her claims of interplanar interference in the grove. Felkur attempted to throw Sir Kaelen off balance by casually mentioning a fake secret code supposedly hidden inside one of the recovered treasure chests, though the ruse was transparent to everyone present.

That evening, Felkur took to the stage at the tavern, and with Valfine dancing alongside him, the performance drew in the crowd and earned the party a modest collection of silver pieces. The party retired to their shared room for the night, and when morning came, Felkur found a letter that had been slid beneath their door while they slept. Spider-Man, when questioned, admitted he had forgotten to deliver it the night before — it had arrived by post from Neverwinter. The letter was addressed to Felkur and signed simply as being from a friend to True Justice, its contents hinting unmistakably at contact from the Harpers. With the mysterious letter in hand and the Whispering Woods still waiting just outside of town, Soma made clear she was ready to investigate the blight — and the rest of the party, curious and cautious in equal measure, prepared to follow.

## Locations
### Phandalin
A small frontier town that serves as a central hub for the party's activities. The party returns here to rest, report their findings, and gather information from local contacts.

### Whispering Woods
A grove just outside Phandalin, once vibrant and described as a testament to Chauntea's blessing, now suffering from a strange magical blight that twists the trees unnaturally and causes leaves to take on sickly, unnatural colors.

### Leilon
A nearby town mentioned as a site where Talos worship may still be active, despite the party having cleansed the lighthouse.

### Lionshield Coster
A merchant outpost in Phandalin where the party inquires about purchasing magical identification scrolls and selling their recovered armor.

### Tower of Storms
A coastal lighthouse recently cleared by the party of harpies and a strange cleric of Talos who had used his own heart as a light source.

### Neverwinter
A large city to the north; the party considers visiting Lord Neverwinter there but is advised against it by Harbin Wester while the town remains in disrepair.

## NPCs
### Adabra Boda
A midwife, herbalist, and member of the Emerald Enclave. She is a firm believer in natural balance and neutrality, warning the party about a magical blight in the Whispering Woods. She stopped engaging with Felkur after he began wearing the Order of the Gauntlet pin, preferring to speak only with Soma. She offered to sell the party potions of healing before departing.

### Harbin Wester
The Town Master of Phandalin, known for his cautious nature. He is relieved the lighthouse has been cleared of Talos worshippers and promises to send word to Lord Neverwinter, but discourages the party from visiting Neverwinter personally while the town remains in disrepair.

### Sir Kaelen
A member of the Order of the Gauntlet who values heroism and striving for universal good. He is troubled by the unusual power displayed by the Talos cleric at the lighthouse, concerned about the mysterious bandit leader known as the Carver, and skeptical of claims of interplanar interference in the Whispering Woods.

### Spider-Man
The tavern keeper in Phandalin who runs betting pools on adventuring parties. He won the pool betting that all four party members would return from the Tower of Storms, and paid out Felkur's share of 10 gold pieces. He also acted as a middleman, delivering a letter from Neverwinter to the party's room.

### The Carver
A mysterious and secretive new bandit leader rumored to be operating in the area around Phandalin, known for organizing raids while keeping his true identity carefully hidden — unusual behavior compared to typical boastful bandits.

### Valfine
A drow who accompanies the party. She performed a dance during Felkur's musical set at the tavern, helping to broaden the appeal of the performance and increase their earnings.
