# Chapter 22 When Boars Are More Than Just Boars

Date: 11-02 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The party arrives at Falcon's Lodge with their collection of prisoners: a disoriented Teega the Smith, two freed Emerald Enclave guards, and the captured orc Shrimpy. Falcon greets them with surprise and is impressed by their success.

Teega the Smith, suffering from memory gaps, asks who the party is and why she's there. She last remembers working at the Wayside Inn. She reveals she's been experiencing blackouts for some time, called by something known as "the Kraken," which promised peace after the endless wars along the Sword Coast. Through observation, the party determines her mind was manipulated through psionic means — not traditional magic, and not Mind Flayers. Brewbarry's instincts confirm it. Valphine attempts intimidation; Vukradin offers her a job at the Church of Lathander. She declines but says she might visit later.

Falcon shares news: Mountain Toe Gold Mine has poisoned waters threatening to shut down operations. He's part of a Neverwinter mining consortium, having received a discounted share in exchange for scouting. The investment has strained his finances — he can't offer cash but proposes giving the party a share of the mine itself if they investigate. The party debates priorities. The Woodland Manse quest (boots of elvenkind) wins. They'll do that first.

Early the next morning, the party sets out for the Woodland Manse. They note the mysterious drow Sridar departed during the night. After a 10-mile journey through the forest, they arrive at a foggy clearing. A crumbling two-story manor stands, covered in thorny ivy, atop a six-foot stone foundation. Wild pumpkins grow everywhere. Several wild boars feed among them.

Soma speaks with animals. One boar ignores her; another complains about "new boars" that don't speak proper boar language or respect pumpkin patch etiquette. It identifies three boars as "orc wild boars" and squints suspiciously at a third that appeared recently. The party recalls Anchorites of Talos can shapeshift into animals.

Brewbarry casts *Moonbeam* on the suspicious boar. The disguise fails — it reveals itself as an Anchorite of Talos. The talking boar flees, offended by the breach of protocol. Combat erupts. The Anchorite takes heavy damage but calls lightning in a devastating line before summoning reinforcements from the manor. The chapter ends on a cliffhanger.

## Scenes

### Arrival at Falcon's Lodge with Prisoners

- Falcon is surprised by the party's return with a full prisoner manifest. He's impressed. Teega the Smith is confused and disoriented.
- **(DM note):** The party is accumulating prisoners faster than they can process them. Logistical strain is building — food, security, transport speed. This is a good pressure point for the GM to exploit.

### Teega's Condition and Interrogation

- Teega last remembers the Wayside Inn (High Road and Tribor Trail corner). Experiencing blackouts. Called by "the Kraken," which promised peace after endless war and chaos.
- The party determines psionic manipulation — mind rewired, not magically enchanted. Brewbarry specifically rules out Mind Flayers.
- **(DM tactical note):** The distinction between psionic manipulation and magical mind control matters for future encounters. Spells like *dispel magic* might not work against psionic effects. Spells like *lesser restoration* or *greater restoration* that cure conditions might be more effective. The party should learn what works and what doesn't.
- Valphine intimidates Teega about her "poor life choices." Vukradin offers a church job. Teega declines but leaves the door open — she'll return to the Wayside Inn first, then consider visiting the temple.

### Falcon's Gold Mine Proposal

- Mountain Toe Gold Mine: 15 miles northeast of Phandalin. Water poisoned. Shutdown imminent. Falcon's consortium investment has strained his finances — no cash reward possible.
- Offer: a share of the mine itself in exchange for investigating and resolving the poisoning.
- Party debates: Woodland Manse or Gold Mine first? They choose Woodland Manse (boots of elvenkind as immediate reward). The Emerald Enclave guards will rest at Falcon's Lodge for two nights then report to their order.

### Travel to Woodland Manse

- 5:00 AM departure. 10 miles through forest. Sridar departed during the night — not present at breakfast.
- The party arrives: crumbling two-story manor, thorny ivy, foggy clearing, pumpkins growing everywhere. Boars feeding. Six-foot stone foundation. Balcony overlooking the entrance.
- Vukradin observes: boars evenly distributed around the property. Suspiciously tactical positioning.

### Speaking with Animals — The Boar Network

- Soma speaks with animals. One boar ignores her completely. A second boar is more talkative — complains about "new boars" that don't speak proper boar, don't respect pumpkin patch etiquette, and are generally rude.
- The boar identifies three categories: real wild boars (him and one to the north who they haven't spoken in years but respect), orc wild boars (obviously fake), and a suspicious third boar to the south that appeared recently and acts strangely.
- **(DM note):** This scene is delightful worldbuilding. The Anchorites' animal disguises are apparently convincing to humans but not to actual animals of the same species. The boars notice subtle behavioral differences — eating without etiquette, ignoring pumpkin patch norms, not speaking the language. The party now knows how to identify shapeshifted Anchorites: compare their behavior against actual animals.

### The Shapeshifter Reveal — COMBAT

**Opening conditions:** The party is in a foggy clearing in front of a crumbling two-story manor. Boars are scattered around feeding on pumpkins. The party knows at least one boar is a shapeshifted Anchorite of Talos. Terrain: open ground, pumpkins, six-foot stone foundation of manor providing high ground, balcony overlooking entrance. Fog provides light concealment. Manor interior is unexplored.

- Valphine objects briefly to attacking without parley. The party overrules her — given the previous encounters with Talos cultists and the mind-controlled villagers, they opt for preemptive action.

- Brewbarry casts *Moonbeam* on the suspicious boar (identified by the talking boar). The Anchorite fails to resist the magical effect — the disguise drops, revealing a half-orc cultist.

- **(DM tactical note):** *Moonbeam* is an excellent choice for shapeshifter detection. The spell forces shapechangers to revert to their true form if they fail their save. This is a textbook use of the spell and should be encouraged.

- The talking boar flees in protest, offended by the violation of protocol and worried about his pumpkin patch.

- Valphine opens the fight with a radiant energy bolt that strikes the Anchorite with devastating accuracy. The description suggests *guiding bolt* or a similar radiant damage spell — good tactical choice, as Radiant damage is rarely resisted and disadvantage on the next attack roll helps the party.

- **(DM tactical note):** Valphine's opening salvo was the right call — focus fire on the revealed caster before they can act. The Anchorite is clearly a spellcaster (shapeshifting, likely lightning/storm domain given Talos affiliation). Taking down casters first is standard tactical doctrine.

- Brewbarry rages and charges with advantage, but his strikes go wide. The Anchorite's AC may be boosted by natural armor or a defensive spell.

- Soma follows up with a successful attack, wounding the creature further. The party is converging on the Anchorite, trying to end this before reinforcements arrive.

- Vukradin attempts to cast *silence* on the Anchorite to prevent spellcasting and calling for reinforcements. The Anchorite resists the magical effect.

- **(DM tactical note):** Vukradin's *silence* attempt was the correct play — shutting down a caster's verbal components is fight-winning. That the Anchorite saved is unfortunate but not a tactical error. The attempt itself was sound. Vukradin should try again if he has the spell slot.

- The Anchorite steps out of the *Moonbeam* radius (taking damage but gaining freedom of action) and shouts to alert those inside the manor. Reinforcements are coming.

- The Anchorite unleashes a *lightning bolt* (or similar line-effect lightning spell) that catches Valphine, Soma, and Brewbarry in a devastating electrical line.

- **(DM tactical note - critical):** Three party members in a line is a positioning error against a storm-themed caster. The party clustered during melee engagement, presenting a perfect line target. In future encounters with Talos cultists, the party should spread out when engaging — storm clerics/druids have area lightning attacks. This is a lesson they may learn the hard way.

- Brewbarry uses his Stone's Endurance (goliath racial) to absorb significant damage from the lightning. Valphine partially dodges (Dex save success). Brewbarry takes the full force and is left badly wounded.

- **(DM tactical note):** Brewbarry's Stone's Endurance reaction was perfectly timed. This goliath racial feature reduces damage by 1d12 + CON mod. Against a high-damage spell like *lightning bolt* (8d6), this can mean the difference between consciousness and death saves. Brewbarry is using his racial tools effectively.

- The session ends with the party wounded, the Anchorite still alive, and reinforcements pouring from the manor. Cliffhanger.

This one seems to have access to lightning-based spells consistent with Talos storm domain. His spell list likely includes: *thunderwave*, *call lightning*, *lightning bolt*, and utility spells like *pass without trace*.**

## Spells

- **Moonbeam** — Brewbarry. Used to detect the shapeshifted Anchorite. Forced the boar to revert to true form. Also provides ongoing radiant damage — good area denial against shapechangers. Effective for both detection and combat.
- **Radiant Bolt (likely *guiding bolt*)** — Valphine. First strike against the Anchorite. High accuracy, radiant damage. Sets up advantage for next attack. Good opener.
- **Lightning Bolt (or *call lightning* line version)** — Anchorite of Talos. 8d6 lightning damage in a line. Caught three party members. Brewbarry minimized with Stone's Endurance; Valphine partially dodged. Devastating spell that could have downed multiple party members.
- **Silence (attempted)** — Vukradin. Attempted to shut down the Anchorite's spellcasting. Resisted. The attempt was tactically correct — preventing the lightning bolt would have changed the fight significantly.
