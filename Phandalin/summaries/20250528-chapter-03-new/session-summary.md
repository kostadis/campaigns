# New Chapter 3

Date: 2025-05-28

## Summary
The party stood at a crossroads in the ancient dwarven ruins, debating which path to take before ultimately pressing forward into a narrow hallway. At its end, they discovered a blood-stained altar, its stone surface dark with the remnants of old rituals. Before they could examine it further, a massive ochre jelly — a mindless, mustard-colored blob of acidic slime — lurched out of the shadows toward them. Brewbarry swung his halberd at the creature, but the slashing blow only caused it to split apart into two smaller, equally hungry entities. In the opening moments of the fight the ochre jellies won initiative — rolling a 19 on the die, reduced to 17 by a penalty but still ahead of the entire party — and immediately pressed their advantage. One pseudopod strike left Brewbarry clinging to a single hit point; Brewbarry's Stone's Endurance soaked eleven points of that blow, the only thing keeping him on his feet. After Brewbarry's halberd split the first jelly, a quick Arcana check by Vukradin confirmed that piercing weapons would not cause the same problem, and the party switched tactics.

The battle that followed was chaotic and punishing. The two jellies lashed out with their pseudopods, one of them striking the barbarian Brewbarry with a blow so powerful it nearly dropped him on the spot. Valphine called down radiant light from above, searing one of the creatures, while Vukradin unleashed a thunderous shockwave that sent both jellies skidding backward across the stone floor — one pushed diagonally into a corner. From the safety of the corridor, the dwarf prospectors Norbus and Dazlyn shouted their concerns — not about the party's wellbeing, but about the potential damage being done to the archaeological site around them.

As if the situation were not dire enough, a third ochre jelly dropped from the ceiling directly into the middle of the fray. Soma looked at the newcomer and said flatly, "Guys, we're done here." The DM declared the creature had been stunned by the concussive sound of Thunderwave, buying the party a moment to breathe. Soma struck the ground with trembling force, cracking the stone floor and turning the surrounding area into treacherous, uneven terrain that slowed the jellies' advance. Brewbarry drew on a deep well of fury and delivered a crushing blow — a critical hit with his bare fists — that obliterated one of the creatures entirely, his fists doing what blades could not.

The fight ground on, with Valphine weaving healing magic to keep her companions standing even as the jellies continued their relentless assault. Vukradin whispered discordant, maddening sounds at one of the creatures, causing it to recoil in psychic agony and flee as far from him as it could manage — triggering a mace swipe from Valphine as a reaction while Vukradin retreated to the corner to play his music from relative safety. Soma, battered but resolute, withdrew into her shell and planted herself as a living barrier, her hardened carapace raising her armor class to 21. The remaining jellies slammed into her, one missing entirely when it rolled only a 14, but two consecutive strikes later in the fight finally dropped her unconscious, leaving the party scrambling. It was at this moment that Norbus called out from the corridor: *"Did somebody, did somebody get hurt?"* Dazlyn followed immediately: *"Again, to the extent possible, please don't damage any of the artwork. This is an important archeological find."* Valphine channeled a surge of healing energy that snapped Soma back to consciousness, and the fight continued.

Then, in a moment that stunned everyone present, Vukradin drew his daggers and struck twice in rapid succession — killing two separate jellies in a single fluid motion and bringing the chaotic battle to an abrupt end. "Like a fucking barbarian, I love it," Vukradin said. Soma replied without missing a beat: "Bard-barian."

In the aftermath, Norbus and Dazlyn crept cautiously into the room, visibly relieved that the oozes had been dealt with. Norbus peered in and asked, *"A- are they dead? Is it safe to go in there?"* Vukradin's response: *"It's absolutely dead. Stick your head in and find out."* The party demanded their sending stones, but the dwarves asked them to finish clearing the rest of the ruins first, sweetening the offer with a hot meal and a place to rest for the night. Vukradin agreed — but only on the condition that there would be no stories. Dazlyn corrected Norbus before he could object: *"We have a bard, and we're going to tell stories. The bard will tell us stories."*

The group agreed, and after a much-needed long rest, they pressed deeper into the complex. To the north, they found a room with three stone bed frames — bare stone frames with no mattresses, built to last an eternity — and beyond a hidden door, a small chamber containing an empty stone font and a wardrobe holding the rotted remains of red leather vestments once worn by priests of Abbathor, the dwarven god of greed. Vukradin's passive perception (15) spotted the hidden door.

Deeper still, in a section of the ruins where collapsed tunnels had buried entire passageways, the party came upon a grim discovery. Half-buried in the rubble was the skeleton of a dwarf priest, still clad in the same rotted red leather armor, a holy symbol hanging around its neck — a tiny jeweled dagger on a silver chain, inscribed in dwarvish with the words "greed is good," worth 50 gold pieces. The DM looked directly at Valphine's player: *"Do you palm it or announce to the world that you found it?"* The answer was a sleight of hand check against a DC of five, which Valphine made. True to his nature, Vukradin sprinted back through the ruins to inform Norbus and Dazlyn about the body, leaving his companions alone with the skeleton and the glittering holy symbol for three full rounds. When he returned, the party moved on.

The area behind the blood-stained altar concealed a hidden passage — revealed by yet another secret door — leading to a cramped channel. No sooner had Valphine stepped inside than another ochre jelly slithered out of the darkness to meet her.

The second major battle was swift but brutal. Soma hurled a shard of ice that struck the jelly and exploded into freezing fragments (dealing nine points total between the piercing hit and the cold blast), while Valphine called down radiant light that the creature narrowly avoided. Brewbarry waded into the tight space with his fists, and Vukradin followed with his daggers. The jelly retaliated with a devastating pseudopod strike against Brewbarry — seventeen points of acid damage. Rage halved the physical component, but acid damage cuts through a barbarian's resistance; Brewbarry used Stone's Endurance to absorb a further portion, gritting his teeth through the rest. Soma launched another volley of ice (eleven points), Valphine struck with her mace, and when the creature finally split into two smaller oozes at under twenty hit points, the party redoubled their efforts. Brewbarry delivered another critical hit that destroyed one half instantly; Soma's poison spray whittled the last ooze down by one point ("Hey, that's twenty percent of its hit points"), and Vukradin finished it with his daggers for eight final points of damage.

Beyond the altar channel, the party discovered what the dwarves had been hoping to find all along — the Hall of Greed. Valphine rolled a natural 20 on her perception check entering the area. Soma used her Mold Earth cantrip to shift the loose rubble aside, revealing a sacred site once devoted to Abbathor. In a northern alcove stood an intact statue of a horned dwarf, its stone face twisted into an expression of naked avarice, its hands cradling a glowing green gem. Valphine sensed a faint magical aura radiating from the gem; a subsequent investigation roll of 1 meant the party could not agree on whether it was a priceless emerald or an elaborate piece of glass — and the DM privately noted it was likely the latter. The rubble to the south proved to be the shattered remains of a matching statue, its gem long since gone. Vukradin noted the statue bore a resemblance to classic D&D artwork he had seen before: *"I think I've seen that picture in the Dungeon Master's Guide. Is that, like, a classic D&D picture?"*

The party returned to Norbus and Dazlyn and reported their findings. The two dwarves erupted with excitement at the mention of the Hall of Greed — but when pressed on the historical significance, they visibly improvised. Vukradin rolled a nine on his insight check and could tell they were spewing nonsense. When he pressed — "Why don't you know? What? Why don't you know? Aren't you guys archaeologists?" — Norbus admitted: *"Oh, we're, we're, we're amateur archaeologists. We've heard that this might be an important archeological find."* The DM added quietly: *"I would say several days ago."* Despite the revelation, they held up their end of the bargain and handed over the sending stones.

Earlier in the expedition, the party had also stumbled upon a secret cavity hidden within one of the stone pillars near the altar. When the door swung open, a cascade of dwarf skulls tumbled out onto the floor, and at the bottom of the cavity sat an unlocked stone coffer containing fifteen small gemstones. Vukradin immediately announced the find to the entire group and insisted on informing the archaeologists, to the visible frustration of his companions. Soma quietly suggested they had found only ten. Valphine sharpened her dagger pointedly while Norbus asked the room whether the party was really okay with the split. The DM noted the dwarves were visibly nervous — weapon-carrying party members plus one drow cleric plus one Uthgard barbarian made the room feel very small. Dazlyn broke the tension by suggesting the museum only needed half, and after negotiation the party settled on seven gems for themselves and eight for the dwarves — a compromise that satisfied no one entirely but allowed everyone to walk away.

With the ruins cleared, their reward in hand, and their pockets modestly heavier, the party set off down the road toward town. Then four orcs crested the hill ahead, weapons drawn. Vukradin offered to talk to them; the DM asked if he spoke Orcish. He did not — Undercommon and Goblin, but not Orc. Valphine stepped forward instead. "We had servants," Soma noted. Valphine prepared to address the attackers in their own tongue as the session closed, spell pouches nearly empty and bodies still aching from the battles below.

The site of a chaotic battle against multiple ochre jellies, during which the stone floor was cracked by Soma's Earth Tremor and made difficult terrain. One ochre jelly dropped from the ceiling mid-fight. A room to the north containing three stone bed frames built against the east wall — solid stone construction with no mattresses — and a secret door leading to a small antechamber with an empty stone font and a wardrobe holding rotted red leather vestments of Abbathor, now worthless.

### Collapsed Tunnels
A series of passages that have suffered significant cave-ins. The DM clarified these are not mining tunnels but collapsed corridors; digging through them is possible but time-consuming. Soma's Mold Earth cantrip can move loose earth; some sections were cleared to access the Hall of Greed.

A sacred religious site dedicated to Abbathor, accessible through a passage cleared of rubble. Features two alcoves: the northern alcove holds an intact horned dwarf statue clutching a magically aura'd green gem of uncertain value (emerald or glass); the southern alcove contains only the rubble of a shattered statue, its gem long gone.

A group of four warriors who appeared over a hill and blocked the party's path back to town, preparing for assault as the session ended. Valphine is the only party member who speaks their language.

## Items

### Halberd
Brewbarry's polearm. Its slashing edge inadvertently caused the first ochre jelly to split into two entities rather than taking damage. Brewbarry switched to bare fists for the remainder of the encounter once the split mechanic became apparent.

### Stone Coffer
An unlocked stone container found at the bottom of a skull-packed secret cavity in a pillar. Held fifteen gemstones worth ten gold pieces each. Vukradin announced its contents immediately and in full.

### Gemstones
Fifteen small jewels worth ten gold pieces each. Soma attempted to underreport the count as ten. Final division: eight to the dwarves, seven to the party.

### Rotted Red Leather Armor / Vestments
Ancient priestly garb found in two locations: a wardrobe in the stone bedroom antechamber, and on the skeleton of a dwarf priest buried in the collapsed section. Identified by religion check (Valphine, 17) as vestments of Abbathor's clergy. Worthless due to decay.

### Holy Symbol of Abbathor
A tiny jeweled dagger on a silver chain, hung around the neck of the skeletal dwarf priest. Dwarvish runes on the symbol read *"Greed is good."* Worth 50 gold pieces. Valphine made a sleight of hand check against DC 5 to pocket it while the party's attention was elsewhere. The DM explicitly asked whether she would palm it or announce it; she palmed it.

### Glowing Green Gem
Held in the hands of the intact horned dwarf statue in the Hall of Greed's northern alcove. Radiates a magical aura (confirmed by Valphine's Detect Magic). Investigation roll of 1 left the party unable to determine whether it was a true emerald or ornamental glass; the DM's private assessment favored glass. Left in place.

### Sending Stones
Magical paired stones handed over by Norbus and Dazlyn after the ruins were cleared. The party's contracted reward for completing the job. Allow long-distance communication between holders.

## Spells

### Sacred Flame
Radiant cantrip (DC 13 dexterity save). Cast multiple times across both combats. In one notable instance the jelly failed its save but Valphine rolled a single point of radiant damage — the table's reaction was palpable.

### Thunderwave
Cast by Vukradin. DC 13 constitution save; both jellies rolled a 6 and a 10 respectively and failed, taking 8 damage each and being pushed back 10 feet (one shoved diagonally into a corner). The concussive sound was ruled by the DM to have stunned the third jelly that dropped from the ceiling immediately after.

### Earth Tremor
Cast by Soma in the first combat. DC 13 dexterity save; one jelly made the save, one failed and took 5 damage. The surrounding stone floor became difficult terrain. Soma positioned herself carefully to avoid hitting Brewbarry. Norbus's prior request to preserve the interior was recalled immediately after.

### Healing Word
Bonus-action healing used twice by Valphine in the first combat: once to restore 8 HP to Soma after a pseudopod strike, and once to revive Soma from 0 HP after she was knocked unconscious by two consecutive jelly attacks. The cantrip slot allowed pairing with a damage cantrip on the same turn.

### Dissonant Whispers
Cast by Vukradin in the first combat. DC 13 wisdom save; the jelly failed and took 10 psychic damage, then was forced to use its reaction to flee as far as possible. This triggered a free mace reaction strike by Valphine and removed the jelly from the immediate fight. Ochre jellies are immune to the frightened condition but not to the forced movement or psychic damage.

### Starry Wisp
Radiant cantrip used by Vukradin. One cast dealt 8 damage to the large ochre jelly in the first combat, contributing to the total that caused it to split. A second cast dealt 4 damage in an earlier exchange. Confirmed as radiant damage type, which ochre jellies are not immune to.

### Poison Spray
Used by Soma twice in the first combat — once dealing 7 damage to kill a jelly, and once dealing 1 point against the final split ooze. *"Hey, that's twenty percent of its hit points, guys."*

### Ice Knife
Cast twice by Soma in the second combat (behind the altar). Mechanics: the shard hits as a ranged attack dealing 1d10 piercing; on hit or miss, it explodes for 2d6 cold damage, with a DC 13 dexterity save for half. First cast: hit, 9 total points of damage, jelly to 43 HP. Second cast: hit, 11 total points of damage including half cold from a made save, jelly to 26 HP. After both casts Soma was out of spell slots.

### Mold Earth
Cantrip used by Soma to clear loose rubble in the Hall of Greed, exposing the floor around the statues and confirming the southern pile was the remains of a shattered statue rather than living rubble. Wade confirmed on inquiry that Soma does have the cantrip. *"It's there. I have it as a cantrip for me."*

### Detect Magic
Cast by Valphine on the glowing green gem in the northern alcove. Confirmed a magical aura on the object. A subsequent investigation roll of 1 meant the party could not determine the gem's nature or value beyond *"probably an emerald, but could be glass."*
