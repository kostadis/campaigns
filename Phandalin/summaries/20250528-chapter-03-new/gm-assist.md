# New Chapter 3

Date: 2025-05-28

## Summary
The party stood at a crossroads in the ancient dwarven ruins, debating which path to take before ultimately pressing forward into a narrow hallway. At its end, they discovered a blood-stained altar, its stone surface dark with the remnants of old rituals. Before they could examine it further, a massive ochre jelly — a mindless, mustard-colored blob of acidic slime — lurched out of the shadows toward them. Brewbarry swung his halberd at the creature, but the slashing blow only caused it to split apart into two smaller, equally hungry entities.

The battle that followed was chaotic and punishing. The two jellies lashed out with their pseudopods, one of them striking the barbarian Brewbarry with a blow so powerful it nearly dropped him on the spot. Valphine called down radiant light from above, searing one of the creatures, while Vukradin unleashed a thunderous shockwave that sent both jellies skidding backward across the stone floor. From the safety of the corridor, the dwarf prospectors Norbus and Dazlyn shouted their concerns — not about the party's wellbeing, but about the potential damage being done to the archaeological site around them.

As if the situation were not dire enough, a third ochre jelly dropped from the ceiling directly into the middle of the fray, prompting more than one party member to seriously consider fleeing. Soma struck the ground with trembling force, cracking the stone floor and turning the surrounding area into treacherous, uneven terrain that slowed the jellies' advance. Brewbarry drew on a deep well of fury and delivered a crushing blow that obliterated one of the creatures entirely, his fists doing what blades could not.

The fight ground on, with Valphine weaving healing magic to keep her companions standing even as the jellies continued their relentless assault. Vukradin whispered discordant, maddening sounds at one of the creatures, causing it to recoil in psychic agony and flee as far from him as it could manage. Soma, battered but resolute, withdrew into her shell and planted herself as a living barrier, her hardened carapace deflecting blow after blow while her companions maneuvered around her. Then, in a moment that stunned everyone present, Vukradin drew his daggers and struck twice in rapid succession — killing two separate jellies in a single fluid motion and bringing the chaotic battle to an abrupt end.

In the aftermath, Norbus and Dazlyn crept cautiously into the room, visibly relieved that the oozes had been dealt with. The party demanded their sending stones, but the dwarves asked them to finish clearing the rest of the ruins first, sweetening the offer with a hot meal and a place to rest for the night. The group agreed, and after a much-needed long rest, they pressed deeper into the complex. To the north, they found a room with three stone bed frames — bare frames with no mattresses, built to last an eternity — and beyond a hidden door, a small chamber containing an empty stone font and a wardrobe holding the rotted remains of red leather vestments once worn by priests of Abbathor, the dwarven god of greed.

Deeper still, in a section of the ruins where collapsed tunnels had buried entire passageways, the party came upon a grim discovery. Half-buried in the rubble was the skeleton of a dwarf priest, still clad in the same rotted red leather armor, a holy symbol hanging around his neck — a tiny jeweled dagger on a silver chain, inscribed in dwarvish with the words "greed is good." Valphine's eyes lingered on the symbol a moment too long before her hand moved toward it, but the rest of the party had already seen exactly what she was doing. She said nothing, and neither did they, though the silence spoke volumes.

True to his nature, Vukradin sprinted back through the ruins to inform Norbus and Dazlyn about the body, leaving his companions alone with the skeleton and the glittering holy symbol. When he returned, the party moved on to investigate the area behind the blood-stained altar, where a hidden passage led to a cramped channel. No sooner had Valphine stepped inside than another ochre jelly slithered out of the darkness to meet her.

The second major battle was swift but brutal. Soma hurled a shard of ice that struck the jelly and exploded into freezing fragments, while Valphine called down radiant light that the creature narrowly avoided. Brewbarry waded into the tight space with his fists, and Vukradin followed with his daggers, the two of them hammering the creature from close range. The jelly retaliated with a devastating pseudopod strike against Brewbarry, the acidic blow landing with tremendous force, though Brewbarry gritted his teeth and absorbed much of the impact through sheer toughness. Soma launched another volley of ice, and Valphine struck with her mace, and when the creature finally split into two smaller oozes under the sustained assault, the party redoubled their efforts and cut both halves down before they could cause further harm.

Beyond the altar channel, the party discovered what the dwarves had been hoping to find all along — the Hall of Greed. Soma used magic to shift the loose earth and rubble aside, revealing a sacred site once devoted to Abbathor. In a northern alcove stood an intact statue of a horned dwarf, its stone face twisted into an expression of naked avarice, its hands cradling a glowing green gem. Valphine sensed a faint magical aura radiating from the gem, though when the party examined it more closely, they could not agree on whether it was a priceless emerald or an elaborate piece of glass. A second alcove to the south held only rubble — the shattered remains of what had once been a matching statue, its gem long since taken or destroyed.

The party returned to Norbus and Dazlyn and reported their findings. The two dwarves erupted with excitement at the mention of the Hall of Greed, launching into an enthusiastic explanation of its historical significance — though it quickly became apparent that they were making most of it up as they went. When pressed, they sheepishly admitted they were amateur archaeologists who had only recently taken up the hobby. Despite the revelation, they held up their end of the bargain and handed over the sending stones, the party's hard-earned reward for clearing the ruins.

Earlier in the expedition, the party had also stumbled upon a secret cavity hidden within one of the stone pillars near the altar. When the door swung open, a cascade of dwarf skulls tumbled out onto the floor, and at the bottom of the cavity sat an unlocked stone coffer containing fifteen small gemstones. Vukradin immediately announced the find to the entire group and insisted on informing the archaeologists, to the visible frustration of his companions. Valphine quietly suggested they had found only ten. In the end, after a tense and morally charged negotiation, the party agreed to split the gems with Norbus and Dazlyn — seven for the party, eight for the dwarves — a compromise that satisfied no one entirely but allowed everyone to walk away with their conscience more or less intact.

With the ruins cleared, their reward in hand, and their pockets modestly heavier, the party set off down the road toward town. The hills around them were quiet, the sky wide and open, and for a brief moment it seemed as though the worst was behind them. Then four orcs crested the hill ahead, weapons drawn and eyes fixed on the party with unmistakable intent. Valphine stepped forward, drawing on her knowledge of the orcish tongue, as the rest of the group readied themselves for yet another fight — their spell pouches nearly empty, their bodies still aching from the battles below.

## Locations
### Blood-Stained Altar Room
A chamber within the ruins featuring a central altar stained with old blood and several hidden secret doors concealed in the masonry. The site of a chaotic battle against multiple ochre jellies, during which the stone floor was cracked and made difficult to traverse.

### Stone Bedrooms
A room to the north containing three stone bed frames with no mattresses, and a secret door leading to a small chamber with an empty stone font and a wardrobe holding rotted red leather vestments of Abbathor.

### Collapsed Tunnels
A series of mining tunnels that have suffered significant cave-ins, requiring physical labor or magic to navigate past the rubble.

### Hall of Greed
A sacred religious site dedicated to the dwarven god Abbathor, featuring alcoves with statues of horned dwarves. One intact statue clutches a glowing green gem of uncertain value, while a second statue lies shattered in rubble to the south.

### The Road to Town
The hilly path leading away from the dwarven ruins where the party was ambushed by four orcs at the end of the session.

## NPCs
### Ochre Jelly
Large, mindless mustard-colored blobs that lash out with pseudopods and split into smaller versions of themselves when struck with slashing weapons. Multiple jellies were encountered throughout the ruins, including one that dropped from the ceiling and one lurking behind the altar.

### Dazlyn
One of two dwarf prospectors who hired the party to clear the ruins. He shouted warnings during the battle, urging the party not to damage the interior of the archaeological site. He is an amateur archaeologist who admitted he and Norbus only recently took up the hobby. He opened the negotiation over splitting the found gemstones with the party.

### Norbus
Dazlyn's partner and fellow amateur dwarf archaeologist. He urged the party to avoid damaging the ruins with powerful spells, offered a meal and stories in exchange for clearing the rest of the complex, and was excited by the discovery of the Hall of Greed.

### Orcs
A group of four warriors who appeared over a hill and intercepted the party on the road back to town, preparing for an assault as the session ended.
