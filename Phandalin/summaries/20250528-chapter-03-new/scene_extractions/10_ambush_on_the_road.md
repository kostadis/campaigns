# Ambush on the Road

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### As the party begins their journey back to town, they are intercepted by a group of orcs.
- The party departs the ruins at approximately 10:31 PM (real time); the DM notes this and proposes rolling initiative and ending there.
- Four orcs crest a hill ahead, weapons drawn.
- Vukradin offers to talk. The DM: *"I'm sure they would love to have that conversation with you. Do you speak Orcanese?"*
- Vukradin speaks Undercommon and Goblin, not Orc.
- Valphine speaks Orc. Wade explains why: *"We had servants."*
- Valphine prepares to address the attackers as the session closes. Spell slots are nearly exhausted across the party.
