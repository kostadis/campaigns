# Amateur Archaeologists

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party returns to their employers to report their findings and claim their reward.
- The party reports the Hall of Greed to Norbus and Dazlyn, who immediately erupt with enthusiasm. *"You found the Hall of Greed?"*
- Vukradin presses them on the historical significance. Their explanation is fluent but hollow. Vukradin rolls insight: 9. He can tell they are making it up as they go. *"Why can't they just be honest with me?"*
- When pressed directly — *"Why don't you know? What? Why don't you know? Aren't you guys archaeologists?"* — Norbus admits: *"Oh, we're, we're amateur archaeologists. We've heard that this might be an important archeological find."*
- DM adds quietly: *"I would say several days ago"* on how long they have practiced the hobby. Soma: *"Do we still see, like, an Archeology for Dummies book under their arm or something?"*
- Norbus eventually concedes: *"To be quite honest, I, I don't know whether it is or is not a matter of great historical significance."*
- Norbus and Dazlyn hand over the sending stones. The party considers the ruins cleared and their obligation fulfilled.
