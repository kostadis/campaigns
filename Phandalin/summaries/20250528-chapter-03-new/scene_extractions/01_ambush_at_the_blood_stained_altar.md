# Ambush at the Blood-Stained Altar

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party explores a hallway containing a blood-stained altar and is immediately confronted by a large ochre jelly.
- The party debates between a secret door and the narrow hallway, ultimately choosing the hallway.
- A blood-stained altar is discovered at the end of the passage.
- An ochre jelly appears right next to the party — beside Vukradin on the initiative tracker.
- Brewbarry attacks the jelly with his halberd. The halberd is a slashing weapon; the jelly is immune to slashing damage, and the blow splits the creature into two smaller entities rather than destroying it.
- The ochre jellies win initiative (rolled 19, reduced to 17 by a −2 penalty) before anyone in the party can act.
- In the opening exchange, one pseudopod strike leaves Brewbarry at a single hit point; his Stone's Endurance reaction (1d12+2) absorbs eleven points of that blow.
