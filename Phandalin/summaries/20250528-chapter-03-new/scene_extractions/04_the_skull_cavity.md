# The Skull Cavity

## Scene summary (expanded from gm-assist against the transcript)

#### The party discovers a secret compartment filled with dwarf skulls and a hidden treasure coffer.
- Soma cannot wait to see behind the small secret door: "I just peek... I just have to know what's going on." Vukradin, meanwhile, protests finding his token parked by the other secret door — "Why am I moving? I'm not moving over there, I am over here" — before jokily accepting it: "that's gonna, like, open up, Scooby-Doo style and, like, pull me in and close behind."
- The party opens a secret door in a pillar that conceals a cavity packed with dwarf skulls, which tumble out onto the floor. "Well, that's disgusting."
- At the bottom of the cavity, they find an unlocked stone coffer containing fifteen gemstones worth ten gold pieces each.
- Vukradin insists on informing the archaeologists — announcing "We found 15 gemstones" out loud — sparking a moral debate about whether to keep the gems or hand them over. Soma tries "We found 10," then sighs "Goddamn your moral streak" and sets the coffer back on the altar; Vukradin declares himself "here for the sending stones and for the hard-earned 50 gold pieces for delivering the message" (this line is split across a transcript speaker change — Soma's caption ends "I'm here for the," Vukradin's picks it up; it may belong wholly to Soma).
- Norbus and Dazlyn rush in, excited by the discovery — "Oh, it is as dazzling as we thought it would be" — though Vukradin warns them the items might be cursed: "These are cursed archeological artifacts... you will be handling them with the utmost care, I assume, and taking them to a museum and to a wizard to be uncursed."
- The bewildered dwarves — eyeing the armed drow and the Uthgardt barbarian, half-convinced the whole thing is a trap — ask the rest of the party, "Is he serious?" Soma answers, "Don't really know him"; "Think so" follows in the same GM-labeled span and is plausibly Soma's continuation, though unlabeled on the tape.
- The group eventually agrees to split the gemstones with the archaeologists — Norbus floating that "the museum only needs half of it" — with the GM's "actually make it eight" setting the party's share at eight and the dwarves at seven. Soma's math: "There's four of us, two of them. We all get two."
- Vukradin voices his suspicion that the dwarves have no intention of visiting a museum — "Guys, I'm getting the impression that they're not going to take these to a museum" — then fails the insight check that would have confirmed it, while Valphine (on her own failed insight roll) concludes that splitting loot "is just the normal way of these surface dwellers. Overbrighters."
- Departing, the dwarves ask to be told of "any other archeological finds" — "We found some skulls... lots of skulls. I'll let you know."
