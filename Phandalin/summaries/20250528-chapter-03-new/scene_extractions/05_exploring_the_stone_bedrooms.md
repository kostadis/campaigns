# Exploring the Stone Bedrooms

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### After a long rest, the party explores the northern reaches of the complex, finding ancient vestments.
- The party takes a long rest and heads north into a rubble-flanked hallway. Loose rubble blocks what were once functional tunnels; the DM notes they could be dug through but it would be time-consuming.
- They discover a room with three stone bed frames against the east wall — solid stone, no mattresses, built to outlast civilizations.
- Vukradin's passive perception (15) locates another secret door.
- Beyond the secret door: a small chamber with an empty stone font jutting from the southwest wall and a stone wardrobe on the east wall holding the rotted remains of two suits of red leather armor.
- A religion check (Valphine rolls 17) identifies the garments as vestments once worn by priests of Abbathor — ruined and now worthless.
- Vukradin debates whether to make a note or sprint back to update the archaeologists. He sprints back.
