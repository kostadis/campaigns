# Archaeologists and Altars

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### After the battle, the party interacts with two dwarf prospectors and discovers hidden passages within the temple.
- Norbus and Dazlyn emerge from hiding once the jellies are defeated — "Ah, you dealt with the oozes... Are they dead? Is it safe to go in there?" — and the party invites them to stick their heads in and find out.
- The party grumbles that the dwarves could have helped; the prospectors protest that with four hit points apiece they would have been useless. Vukradin: "It's a good thing I'm such a moral individual. You take those sending stones now." Soma counters: "Give us one sending stone now and we'll talk."
- The dwarves express concern for the temple's artwork and archaeological value while the party demands their reward; Norbus asks them to "just finish clearing everything out." The dwarves deflect both demands — no sending stone changes hands here; per GM ruling they're withheld until the ruins are fully cleared, and handed over only at the end of the session.
- Norbus offers the party a meal and a place to rest in exchange for clearing the rest of the ruins: "We can all chill out. I can make an excellent meal. I can tell you all sorts of wonderful stories, and you could go next morning." Vukradin agrees "if there are no stories" — so Dazlyn thumps Norbus and rules that the bard will tell the stories.
- The GM awards the find of a series of secret doors near the blood-stained altar to Valphine (a 16 on perception, speaker uncertain in the captions), assuming the Morninglord's cleric would share the knowledge rather than hoard it drow-style, "information is power" jokes notwithstanding.
