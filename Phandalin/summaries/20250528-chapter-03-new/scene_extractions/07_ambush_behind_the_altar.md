# Ambush Behind the Altar

## Scene summary (expanded from gm-assist against the transcript)

#### A hidden chamber behind the altar leads to a dangerous encounter with a resilient ochre jelly.
- The party discovers a secret door leading to a channel behind the altar.
- Valphine volunteers to enter first — "I can go in. I'm eager... I've got nine hit points" — and an ochre jelly, a full-sized 52-hit-point specimen, slithers out from the shadows to attack.
- Soma launches a shard of ice that strikes the jelly (a 22 to hit) and explodes into freezing fragments, prompting a full table review of Ice Knife's two-part mechanics — nine damage after the dust settles.
- Valphine calls down sacred radiance, but the jelly avoids the light entirely, making its DC 13 save.
- Brewbarry moves into the cramped space to strike the jelly with his fists — and rolls "my second natural one tonight."
- Vukradin strikes the jelly with his daggers for six damage.
- The ochre jelly lashes out with a pseudopod — target chosen by the DM calling odds-or-evens (Vukradin picks odds) — striking Brewbarry with a seventeen-point acidic blow, close to maximum damage.
- Brewbarry uses his natural endurance to shrug off nine points of the impact — his rage no help, since resistance to weapon damage does nothing against acid.
- Soma hurls another ice shard — eleven more damage, dropping the jelly to 26 — and announces, "And now I'm out of spell slots."
- Valphine strikes the jelly with her mace for six bludgeoning, and Vukradin follows up with two dagger hits for ten.
- The massive jelly splits into two smaller oozes of five hit points each under the assault.
- Brewbarry delivers a crushing critical blow that destroys one of the smaller jellies — Soma: "He's dead, Jim" — and the survivor's counterattack misses.
- Soma sprays a noxious cloud of poison at the remaining ooze for a single point — "Hey, that's 20% of its hit points, guys" — Valphine's mace finds Gary's third natural one of the night, and Vukradin finally finishes it off with eight points across two dagger strikes. "I'm kind of done with those daggers. That's nice."
- The DM wonders aloud whether Valphine's dice luck reflects "moral guilt about the stealing of the treasure" — the answer: "Moral guilt? No. In a drow? Nah."
- A final sweep of the chamber (18 perception) turns up nothing else of value; Vukradin frames it all as duty: "We're doing our job in clearing out these ogres for the dwarves so we can earn our sending stones."

**[Scene Wrap — Ambush Behind the Altar]**
- The party found the secret door and channel behind the blood-stained altar and Valphine's player volunteered to enter first at nine hit points.
- The full-sized ochre jelly (52 hit points) attacked; it rolled a minus-one initiative and went last, amid banter including a joke roll-off the GM "won" at 20 (which settled nothing mechanically).
- Soma's two Ice Knives (nine, then eleven damage) bracketed a full-table review of the spell's hit-roll-plus-save mechanics, ending with Soma out of spell slots.
- The jelly saved against Valphine's Sacred Flame, shrugged off Brewbarry's second natural one, and took six from Vukradin's daggers.
- Its odds-or-evens pseudopod dealt 17 acid damage to Brewbarry, who Stone's-Enduranced away nine after the GM ruled rage resistance useless against acid.
- Vukradin's ten-damage round split the jelly into two five-point oozes; Brewbarry critted one dead, Soma poison-sprayed the other for one, Gary rolled his third natural one of the night (his first on Valphine's dice), and Vukradin's daggers ended it for eight.
- An 18 perception sweep found nothing else of value; for once, Vukradin declines to run back and report — "no, it's okay... He can find out later" — and the party presses on past the channel instead.
