# Ambush Behind the Altar

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### A hidden chamber behind the altar leads to a dangerous encounter with a resilient ochre jelly.
- The party discovers a secret door leading to a channel behind the blood-stained altar.
- Valphine steps in first; an ochre jelly slithers out of the shadows. Initiative is rolled. The jelly gets a −1; Valphine rolls an 18. The DM calls a roll-off — and rolls a 20, taking first initiative. *"Like full on minus one,"* Soma notes drily.
- Soma casts Ice Knife from the back rank. The shard hits (attack roll 22), dealing piercing damage; the shard then explodes for cold damage regardless of the hit. Total: nine points of damage. Jelly drops to 43 HP.
- Valphine calls Sacred Flame. The jelly makes its dexterity save. No damage.
- Brewbarry moves into the cramped space to punch. Natural one.
- Vukradin strikes with a dagger: six points of damage. Jelly at 37 HP.
- The ochre jelly retaliates — pseudopod hits Brewbarry for seventeen points of acid damage. Brewbarry's rage grants resistance to the physical component; acid damage is not reduced by rage resistance. He uses Stone's Endurance to absorb additional damage.
- Soma casts Ice Knife again: eleven total points of damage (piercing hit plus half cold from a made saving throw). Jelly drops to 26 HP.
- Valphine strikes with her mace: six bludgeoning damage. Jelly under 20 HP.
- Vukradin hits twice with daggers: four plus six, ten total damage. The jelly splits into two oozes at five hit points each. The DM rules the split occurs at the end of Vukradin's action.
- Brewbarry delivers a critical punch to one split jelly — dead.
- The surviving split jelly attacks; it misses.
- Soma sprays Poison Spray: one point of damage. *"Hey, that's twenty percent of its hit points, guys."*
- Valphine swings her mace — natural one. Third natural one of the session for Gary.
- Vukradin finishes the last ooze with his daggers for eight damage. Dead.
