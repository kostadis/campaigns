# Amateur Archaeologists

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party returns to their employers to report their findings and claim their reward.
- Before heading back, the party notes that at least "a dragon didn't find us." They then inform Dazlyn and Norbus about the Hall of Greed and the green gem — "You found the Hall of Greed?"
- The dwarves launch into an enthusiastic account of the site's historical significance, but Vukradin's insight (even at a nine) catches them "spewing words hoping you won't notice that they have no idea" — prompting an indignant "Are you guys cheating?... Well, why can't they just be honest with me?"
- Dazlyn and Norbus admit they are merely amateur archaeologists who only recently took up the hobby: "To be quite honest, I don't know whether it is or is not a matter of great historical significance... we're amateur archeologists." A 17 insight pegs the hobby's age at "several days ago" — "Yeah, moments ago. Hours, even." Soma: "Do we still see, like, an Archeology for Dummies book under their arm or something?"
- The dwarves express excitement over the discovery of the intact gem and the party's success in clearing the ruins — "we were looking for the Hall of Greed... This is awesome."
- Dazlyn and Norbus reward the party with a set of sending stones for their services, as Vukradin closes the account: "We warned you, and we cleared out the ochre jellies, so I think job well done, gentlemen."
