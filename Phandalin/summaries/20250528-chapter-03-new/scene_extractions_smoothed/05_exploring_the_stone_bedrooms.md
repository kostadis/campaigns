## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### After a long rest, the party explores the northern reaches of the complex, finding ancient vestments.
- The party takes a long rest before heading north into a hallway with rubble blocking one end (west, per the GM's on-the-fly correction) and a door to the east.
- They discover a room containing three stone bed frames and another secret door, spotted on a passive perception of 15. Soma: "Do they not believe in just having normal doors in this place?" Vukradin: "There was a normal door. We just walked through one."
- The bed frames hold no mattresses — bare stone. Soma: "They'll last forever. That's great."
- Beyond the secret door, they find an empty stone font jutting from the southwest wall and a stone wardrobe containing the rotted remains of two suits of red leather armor.
- Valphine is credited with identifying the armor via a religion check (17; the roll itself lands on Vukradin's caption and the GM audibly hesitates over whose result counts, but rules it to the cleric) as ruined vestments once worn by the priests of Abbathor — now worthless.
- Vukradin runs back to report the vestments to the dwarf prospectors — the first of what becomes a running gag this session — while Valphine (attribution inferred; her audio merges into the GM's caption in this segment) sweeps the room for more treasure with a 22 perception and finds nothing.
