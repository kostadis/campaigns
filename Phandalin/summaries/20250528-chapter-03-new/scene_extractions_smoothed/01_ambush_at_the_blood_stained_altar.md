# Ambush at the Blood-Stained Altar

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, augmented with transcript detail)

#### The party explores a hallway containing a blood-stained altar and is immediately confronted by a large ochre jelly.
- The party decides to investigate a hallway rather than a secret door, after debating both options; Vukradin's "Sure" settled the route.
- A blood-stained altar is discovered at the end of the passage.
- An ochre jelly appears right next to the party — Soma greets it at the table as a "big old pile of mustard"; Vukradin, eyeing its stat block: "There's an AC you don't see every day," noting its armor class of 8.
- The jelly rolled a 19 on initiative (17 after its penalty), beating the entire party. Vukradin: "We got outranked by a fricking ochre jelly." Soma consoles him — "You get used to these things with time... it's been like—" — and Vukradin finishes her sentence: "eight years. I'm not used to it yet."
- Brewbarry attacks the jelly with a halberd, but the slashing damage causes the creature to split into two smaller entities. Soma, reacting to the split: "Well, that's crap."
