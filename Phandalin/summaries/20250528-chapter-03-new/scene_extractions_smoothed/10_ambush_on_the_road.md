# Ambush on the Road

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### As the party begins their journey back to town, they are intercepted by a group of orcs.
- The party departs the ruins, heading back toward town to collect their final reward — the fifty gold pieces owed for delivering the message. "Did you hear something? I don't know. Whatever. Message delivered. Wasn't a dragon."
- Four orcs appear over a hill, blocking the party's path and preparing for an assault. Nobody in the party is surprised; separately, they enter the encounter with spell slots nearly spent.
- Vukradin protests — "Why are they attacking us?... I'd like to talk to them" — and the DM assures him the orcs "would love to have that conversation."
- Valphine prepares to communicate with the attackers in Orcish as the party readies for combat — her fluency explained with chilling drow economy: "I mean, we had servants." (The line is labeled Soma on the transcript though it answers for Valphine — and the immediate reaction, "Oh, you just made me feel bad," is also labeled Soma, which can't both be right on a single-speaker track; the ASR-based diarization on this session's transcript is known-unreliable, so treat the attribution as unconfirmed pending GM/tape review.) Vukradin speaks Goblin, but not Orc.
- Vukradin proposes he and Valphine hold "our side conversations in Undercommon so the other players can't understand us" — to which Soma, the self-described "hired tour guide," objects: "What are you even trying to hide from me?"
- Initiative is rolled and the session ends on the brink of combat, with Soma resigned to a long-term project: getting "what's-his-face, Vukradin, wealthy enough to pay me for my services... It's gonna take the entire campaign."
