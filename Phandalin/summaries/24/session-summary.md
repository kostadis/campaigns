# Chapter 24 The Ale, the Ex, and Axeholm

Date: Eleasis 1495 (late summer)

## Summary

The party returns to Phandalin after securing the gold mine peace agreement. Harbin Wester greets them warmly but warns of a dragon threatening the mountains and orc incursions preventing farmers from tending crops — putting ale production at risk. He presents new quests: investigating Dragon Barrow, securing Axeholm fortress, and exploring the Woodland Manse. Vukradin is particularly interested in the rewards to fund his music studio.

The party addresses the cheese supply problem for the wererats. Big Al's cow Petunia misses the alfalfa fields of Butterskull Ranch. Qelline Alderleaf offers grazing for 1 gp/week — a deliberately high "hurt price" because Big Al wronged her. The party learns the truth: Big Al and Qelline were once engaged, and he abandoned her the night before their wedding. The party insists he confess the full truth. Big Al, in tears, picks up Petunia and heads to Qelline's farm to make amends.

Lyra arrives, discouraged about her leadership after the treant incident and her men's capture. The party claims it was her plan that rescued the Enclave members, boosting her standing with the Emerald Enclave. Vukradin offers to help convince them of her competence.

Ser Kaelen reveals the statue from the lighthouse belongs to Elara Seasong Meliame, a wealthy art collector attending an extravagant gala in Neverwinter. House Margaster agents — a family notorious for demonic alliances — intend to intercept it. The party is asked to escort the statue to Neverwinter personally.

The party performs at the tavern. Vukradin energizes the crowd with music. Brewbarry creates a spectacle with physical displays that the audience imitates. Spider-Man rewards them with free drinks. After rest, the party sets out for Axeholm.

Travel is miserable — cool winds and rain through the grassy lowlands. At the fortress, a dark passageway leads to a rusted iron portcullis. Arrow slits show no signs of occupation. Brewbarry cannot force it open with raw strength. Soma summons a rat familiar to scout through the arrow slits — it finds a winch mechanism inside.

## Memorable Moments

> "Tell her the truth or lose my respect forever."
> — Soma, to Big Al

**The Wedding Night Confession.** Big Al breaks down and admits he abandoned Qelline the night before their wedding. The party's insistence on the truth forces a painful but necessary reckoning.

> "I can't believe you told me about your music studio and not about the destruction of Neverwinter."
> — Vukradin, to himself, upon learning of Neverwinter's catastrophic history

**Priorities.** Vukradin discovers he's been so absorbed in his musical studies that he missed decades of major events in the region. His embarrassment is palpable.

> "It was YOUR plan. I just helped execute it. You're the leader we need."
> — Vukradin, lying to Lyra for morale

**Leadership Gaslighting.** Vukradin inflates Lyra's role in the rescue to rebuild her confidence. The rescued Enclave member backs the story. A small deception for a good cause.

> "I can't, I'm busy having an existential crisis."
> — Valphine, declining to perform

**Crisis of Faith.** Valphine's performance falls flat while the rest of the party thrives. She's clearly struggling with something deeper than stage fright.

> "A SINGLE GOLD PIECE? Per week? For grass? What is this, a HURT PRICE?"
> — Vukradin, on Qelline's offer

**Hurt Prices.** Qelline's deliberately high grazing fee exposes old wounds. The economics of petty revenge are surprisingly steep.

> "Don't worry, I've got this. I'm very good at physical displays."
> — Brewbarry, before creating a new dance craze

**Brewbarry the Entertainer.** The barbarian's spectacular tavern performance starts a crowd-wide imitation trend. A new Phandalin dance is born.

## Scenes

### Return to Phandalin
- Harbin greets the party warmly.
- Brewbarry asks about ale supply — Harbin warns the dragon + orcs threaten the farming season.
- Harbin presents quests: Dragon Barrow, Axeholm fortress, Woodland Manse.
- Vukradin questions Neverwinter gold transport safety — learns the city's history of catastrophe, corruption, and crime.
- Vukradin realizes he missed major regional events due to musical devotion.
- **(DM note):** Harbin is clearly worried. The dragon and orc problems are escalating. Axeholm is meant as a last-resort evacuation point.

### The Cheese Problem — Big Al and Qelline
- Party contacts Big Al about Petunia's cheese supply for wererats.
- Petunia (via magic communication) misses the alfalfa fields at Butterskull Ranch.
- Big Al mentions Qelline offered grazing — 1 gp/week. He can't afford it.
- Party approaches Qelline, senses tension immediately.
- Qelline recognizes Big Al sent the party instead of coming himself.
- She reveals: Big Al's competitive pricing damaged her farm's income.
- The 1 gp price is a "hurt price" — meant to make Big Al suffer.
- She offers 5 sp if Big Al comes to apologize personally.
- Party presses Big Al — he breaks down crying.
- **The confession:** He and Qelline were engaged. He abandoned her the night before the wedding.
- He always loved her but wasn't ready to settle down.
- Party insists he tell the full truth. Big Al takes Petunia and goes to make amends.
- Party wisely does not interfere in the private reconciliation.

### Lyra's Crisis
- Lyra arrives with the two rescued Emerald Enclave members.
- She's deeply discouraged — cites treant incident and men's capture as failures.
- Vukradin offers encouragement.
- One rescued man reveals the party claimed it was Lyra's plan.
- Lyra is heartened. Agrees to let Vukradin help convince the Enclave.
- Meeting set for next day.

**(DM note):** The party's investment in Lyra's leadership is paying off. She's becoming a valuable ally. The "it was your plan" ploy was a selfless move by Vukradin.

### Ser Kaelen's News
- The statue from the lighthouse is identified: Elara Seasong Meliame.
- She's attending a Neverwinter gala hosted by her eccentric cousin Lord Cassian Meliame.
- House Margaster agents will try to intercept the statue to embarrass the Meliames.
- Ser Kaelen requests the party escort the statue to Neverwinter.
- Must mention the Order of the Gauntlet's assistance.
- Vukradin considers thanking multiple organizations for maximum favors.

### Tavern Performance
- Valphine struggles with her performance — existential crisis.
- Vukradin's music energizes the crowd.
- Brewbarry creates a new spectacle: physical displays and dance moves.
- The crowd begins imitating Brewbarry's moves.
- Spider-Man rewards the party with free drinks.
- Crowd showers them with applause and coin.

### Journey to Axeholm
- Party travels across grassy lowlands skirting the Sword Mountains foothills.
- Cool winds and rain from the Sword Coast make the trek unpleasant.
- Arrival: dark passageway carved into a gray mountain.
- Flanked by towering bulwarks of seamless stone.
- Rusted iron portcullis blocks the entrance.
- Arrow slits show no signs of occupation.
- **Portcullis attempt:** Brewbarry attempts to force it open — incredible strength, but even he can't overcome the massive stone block jamming the mechanism.
- Soma summons a rat familiar, sends it through the arrow slit to scout.
- Rat discovers a winch mechanism inside.
- Party attaches rope to the winch. They prepare to raise the gate.

## NPCs

- **Harbin Wester** — Town Master. Grateful for mine resolution. Worried about dragon + orc threats to ale production. Offers quests for Dragon Barrow, Axeholm, Woodland Manse. Prefers Axeholm as evacuation point, not permanent relocation.

- **Qelline Alderleaf** — Local farmer. Formerly engaged to Big Al, abandoned night before wedding. Sets deliberately high "hurt price" for Petunia's grazing. Eventually agrees to 5 sp if Big Al apologizes. **(GM note):** Qelline's grudge is decades old. The private reconciliation may or may not heal this wound.

- **Big Al** — Owner of Butterskull Ranch and Petunia the cow. Boastful exterior hides deep regret about abandoning Qelline. Breaks down when pressed for the truth. Finally goes to make amends.

- **Lyra (Emerald Enclave)** — Returned with rescued members. Deeply insecure about leadership. The party's deception about "her plan" bolsters her confidence. **(GM note):** Lyra is being groomed as a party ally within the Emerald Enclave. Her confidence is fragile.

- **Ser Kaelen (Order of the Gauntlet)** — Identified the statue owner via Candlekeep. Warns of House Margaster interference. Wants party to escort statue to Neverwinter gala.

- **Spider-Man** — Tavern owner. Rewards the party's performance with free drinks. Appreciates good entertainment.

- **Petunia** — Big Al's cow. Can communicate magically. Misses home. Key to the cheese supply chain.

## Items

- **Elara's Statue** — Recovered from the lighthouse. Belongs to Elara Seasong Meliame (wealthy Neverwinter art collector). House Margaster wants to intercept it. Party must escort it to Neverwinter gala. **(GM note):** House Margaster is notorious for demonic alliances. This is a high-risk escort mission.

## Spells

- **Wild Shape (Rat)** — Soma. Summons rat familiar to scout through arrow slits. Crucial for discovering the winch mechanism inside Axeholm.
- **Magic Communication (with Petunia)** — Soma or party. Learned Petunia misses her alfalfa fields.
