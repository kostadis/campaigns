# Chapter 16 Deals with Harbin, and Sister Kayla, and no deal with Jenna

Date: 7-02 Taraskh 1495

## Summary

The chapter opens with Vukradin fuming over Harbin Wester's baseless accusations, venting to Ser Kaelen of the Order of the Gauntlet. Ser Kaelen, in his well-meaning but paternalistic way, counsels patience — suggesting a privy council that leaves Harbin in place as a figurehead rather than overthrowing the Townmaster outright. His reasoning is pragmatic: Lord Neverember will send the army if his appointed representative is removed. Vukradin, burning with revolutionary fervor, refuses to back down and marches onto the town green to convene his own assembly, rallying the townsfolk through music.

The impromptu town meeting is disrupted when a white dragon dives toward the square. Vukradin, rather than flee, casts *phantasmal force* to create the illusion of a peasant in the dragon's claws. The ruse works — the dragon, convinced it has its prey, veers away. This display of quick thinking cements Vukradin's standing in the community. Harbin, reading the room, hastily announces the formation of a new privy council that includes Vukradin alongside the existing town notables. The revolution is traded for a seat at the table.

Meanwhile, Valphine conducts her own covert operation. Assuming the appearance of a drow of the Underdark, she meets Sister Kayla in a decrepit building on the edge of town, escorted by Locutus the orc. Kayla immediately kills Locutus for Borg's failure, then reveals she knows Valphine's true identity. She offers an alliance: Kayla will provide information to eliminate the Carver's lieutenants, weakening him until he emerges from hiding. Her motive is self-preservation — the Carver punishes failure. She assumes Valphine is a Lolth-worshipping schemer running a long con on the party, a misreading Valphine allows to stand. Kayla leaves a bag of gold as a down payment.

At the tavern, Brewbarry intercepts a mysterious woman, later identified as Jenna Roscoe of the Lord's Alliance, before she can approach Vukradin. He disarms her — literally, collecting four weapons from her person — before allowing the introduction. Jenna offers to be Vukradin's agent, promising access to the finest venues in Neverwinter and Waterdeep. The catch: she wants him to abandon his "fair-trade gold" philosophy. Vukradin refuses, unwilling to compromise his principles even for the career opportunity of a lifetime. Jenna admires his conviction even as she dismisses his idealism.

The session ends with the party holding three threads: Sister Kayla's offer of underworld partnership, Jenna Roscoe's tempting but morally compromised patronage, and Vukradin's newly established position on Harbin's privy council — a compromise between revolution and submission.

## Scenes

### Confrontation with Harbin and Ser Kaelen

- Vukradin explodes at Harbin's accusations, calling the Townmaster's fines "blood money." Ser Kaelen arrives to defend Vukradin's honor as a member of the Order of the Gauntlet, but his insistence on a "proper trial" reveals the order's institutional mindset — slow, procedural, deferential to authority.
- **(DM note):** Vukradin's revolutionary zeal is genuine but unfocused. He wants democracy without understanding how to build it. Ser Kaelen's counsel is politically smart but reveals the Order of the Gauntlet's institutional conservatism — they'll defend their own but won't challenge the power structure itself.

### The Town Meeting and the Dragon Attack

- Vukradin takes to the green, performs, and draws a crowd including farmers, miners, and town notables. A mysterious woman (Jenna Roscoe) watches with a calculating smirk.
- A white dragon dive-bombs the square. Vukradin's *phantasmal force* illusion of a peasant in the dragon's claws is a clutch save that avoids what would have been a devastating encounter at low HP.
- **(DM tactical note):** The *phantasmal force* gambit worked perfectly, but it relied on the dragon failing its investigation check. This was a high-risk/high-reward play that could have ended very differently. The party was not positioned for combat — several PCs were scattered through town, no weapons drawn, and a white dragon has a CR that would have been lethal at this level. Vukradin's choice to engage with illusion rather than fight was tactically correct but narratively driven rather than mechanics-based.

### Harbin's Privy Council Announcement

- Capitalizing on Vukradin's heroism, Harbin announces a privy council: himself, Vukradin, Tolbin, Elmer, Halia, Linene, and Adabra. Ser Kaelen advises Vukradin to accept — removal would bring Neverwinter's army.
- Vukradin accepts the compromise, securing a voice in governance without the purging he wanted.

### Locutus Laid Low

- Locutus the orc guides Valphine to the meeting point after dark. Sister Kayla immediately kills him — two rapier strikes miss, but her offhand dagger finishes him. Valphine is "ecstatic" at the display of cutthroat practicality.
- **(DM note):** Kayla's dual-wielding attack pattern (primary miss, offhand kill) suggests she's a rogue with two-weapon fighting capabilities. Her willingness to kill an ally for failure signals she operates with absolute pragmatism.

### Sister Kayla's Proposal

- Kayla reveals she knows Valphine's identity. She offers intel on the Carver's lieutenants as part of a plan to weaken him until he emerges from hiding. Her endgame: kill the Carver and take over his operation.
- Kayla kills Locutus on arrival — two rapier misses followed by an offhand dagger that finishes him. Valphine is "ecstatic" at this display of cutthroat pragmatism, which reminds her of the games she played with her sisters in Menzoberranzan.
- Kayla assumes Valphine is a Lolth-worshipping drow running a front with the Lathander church. This misreading lets Valphine accept the alliance without compromising her true allegiance — she allows Kayla to believe the stereotype.
- Kayla explains her motive: the Carver doesn't tolerate failure, and she failed to deliver on her framing plot against the party. She needs allies to eliminate him before he eliminates her.
- **(DM note):** Kayla mentioned the Carver has a "unique ability" to communicate telepathically with his people. This is a significant clue about his nature — potentially a mind flayer thrall, an aboleth servant, or something stranger. It also explains why Kayla can't simply betray him openly; he'd know.
- Kayla leaves a bag of gold as a down payment — the money Locutus was supposed to receive for killing Borg. Valphine accepts it without committing to anything, maintaining plausible deniability.

### Brewbarry Intercepts Jenna Roscoe

- Brewbarry spots Jenna at the tavern and disarms her (four weapons surrendered) before allowing her to meet Vukradin. She's a member of the Lord's Alliance, representing Neverwinter's intelligence arm.
- **(DM note):** Jenna carrying four concealed weapons in a tavern is notable. She came prepared for violence despite her diplomatic cover. Her willingness to surrender them suggests either extraordinary discipline or a hidden fifth weapon.

### Jenna's Offer and Vukradin's Refusal

- Jenna offers to be Vukradin's agent, leveraging Lord's Alliance connections for grand performances in Neverwinter and Waterdeep. The price: abandon his "fair-trade gold" philosophy. She argues that treasure was stolen from "the people" and redistributing it through commerce is ethically equivalent to returning it.
- Vukradin refuses categorically. Jenna, impressed despite herself, softens briefly before the mask goes back on.

## NPCs

- **Harbin Wester** — Townmaster of Phandalin. Cowardly, corrupt, but politically astute. Uses the dragon attack to co-opt Vukradin's popularity rather than fight it. His "blood money" reference from Vukradin clearly struck a nerve. Maintaining a facade of control while his authority is visibly eroding. **(GM note): His knowledge of events he shouldn't know suggests he may be compromised or simply an informant network — worth tracking.**
- **Ser Kaelen** — Order of the Gauntlet. Institutional, well-meaning, cautious. Counsels patience and political compromise. Views Vukradin as a promising recruit whose idealism needs tempering. His concern about Neverwinter sending troops is genuine — he's seen what imperial crackdowns look like.
- **Sister Kayla** — The Carver's spymaster in Phandalin. Cold-eyed, scarred, pragmatic. Kills Locutus without hesitation for Borg's failure. Believes Valphine is a Lolth-worshipping schemer and uses this assumption to propose an alliance. Genuinely afraid of the Carver's telepathic abilities. Her endgame: kill the Carver and take over his operation.
- **Jenna Roscoe** — Lord's Alliance agent. Sharp, calculating, carries an aristocratic contempt beneath a diplomatic veneer. Collects multiple weapons even to a casual tavern meeting. Offers agent services to Vukradin but refuses to accept his ethical boundaries. **(GM note): Her contempt for Brewbarry as "Uthgartian" is a mask slip — she's classist and racist beneath the polish.**
- **Locutus** — Orc intermediary (deceased). Killed by Kayla for Borg's earlier failure. Served as Valphine's entry point to the underworld meeting. Unceremoniously disposed of.
- **Borg** — Deceased orc. Failed to kill Big Al and leave no witnesses, derailing Kayla's framing plot against the party. His failure set everything in motion.

## Spells

- **Phantasmal Force** — Vukradin. Used to create an illusion of a peasant in the white dragon's claws. Extremely effective — the dragon failed its save and departed with its "prey." This spell creates an image that the target's own mind rationalizes; in this case, the dragon accepted the false prey and retreated.
