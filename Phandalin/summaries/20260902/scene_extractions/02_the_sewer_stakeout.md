# The Sewer Stakeout

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party monitors a suspicious delivery at a sewer grate, leading to a split in their investigation.

- Soma casts *Find Familiar* and creates a rat through whose eyes and ears she can perceive while it remains within 100 feet.
- Vukradin has a +9 Stealth bonus; his Boots of Elvenkind grant advantage when he moves silently, making him the party’s best candidate for personally following a target.
- The stakeout occurs beside a sewer grill reached by aqueduct-like steps.
- Two crate carriers deposit nine crates. Eight bear no official Commission Harbor mark, reinforcing that those goods entered Neverwinter outside normal channels.
- Eight crates are stacked for Lim, while the ninth is placed separately on a ledge.
- The carrier who handles the ninth crate stops, looks down into the dark as if checking for the expected collector, and leaves.
- The two carriers walk away in different directions and conspicuously avoid acknowledging one another.
- Valphine initially rolls a 12 to determine which carrier seems senior, then expends the party’s final Bardic Inspiration to reach 19.
- She identifies the man who looked into the sewer as the one who likely knows more, while the other appeared to be merely stacking boxes.
- A small, quick, cat-sized construct of brass and leather emerges, takes the ninth crate, and returns below.
- Vukradin pursues the construct alone, judging that his chance of a catastrophic stealth failure is “only a 1 in 400.”
- Valphine, Soma, and Brewbarry follow the knowledgeable carrier, later identified as Denvar.
- Vukradin and Soma carry the paired Sending Stones, giving the separated groups a magical “walkie-talkie” connection.
- Denvar stops at one of the few cook shops open around the fourth hour and eats breakfast alone.
- The party declines to confront him because it promised Lim not to spook her connections.
- In the sewers, Vukradin rolls a natural one on a Wisdom save and becomes overwhelmed by the conviction that following his current route is boring and pointless.
- He abandons the construct’s trail without forgetting why he originally entered the tunnels.
