### Brewbarry

I wait with Soma. My legs are stiff.

She casts Find Familiar again. Another rat. More work for her.

Vukradin comes back.

"You've been giving me updates through the Sending Stone, so I should probably join you. It's been a few hours."

"Yeah, yeah, we can have you come along."

"Yeah, I can tell you about Denvar."

"Why don't I tell you about the local wererat mafia?"

I look at him. He is whole. Good.

"Yeah, there's were-rats all over, isn't it?"

"I expect them to be such a big player."

He tells us about Rsolk. Men lost in a wet tunnel. Spiders with wrong colors. Webs we know.

"We just gotta clear out these spiders, and it's more money for the studio. This is great. Finally a business opportunity."

"Fair Trade Gold."

I can help with spiders.

Denvar wakes. We follow him to a stone wall. He looks around before touching it.

"It's a dead drop," Vukradin says.

A stone opens. Denvar takes a bag. The stone shuts.

"Just a bare stone wall, like, on the edge of the…"

The wall joins a building.

"Is it like a two-way doctor's-office window, where you put it in on one side and close the door, and then they…"

Vukradin studies it.

"We'd have to go inspect it."

"Alright. It's effectively a dead drop, so we should let him go and inspect it."

"Yeah."

"Excellent."

Soma watches Denvar.

"Barring coming back the next evening before he leaves his house, but we don't know exactly how early he has to leave to make these drops, so…"

"Denvar might be a dead end."

He has his bag. We have his wall.

"eyeball the wall."

"Valphine, you wanna do your…"

"Infinite perception."

"You mean, like, on him, or on the…"

"on the wall."

"on the wall."

"I'm gonna look at him just before he goes."

Denvar looks at his chit. He smiles.

"Payday! I'm going to the casino!"

I keep still until he goes.

"Could take a little look, then, at the rock."

The hole can work from either side. I cannot tell which side holds the hand that pays him.

"Oh, okay. Well, let's loop around."

"Or is it Earth, or…"

Vukradin looks closer.

"The stone dwelling."

"I'm trying to think whether we have something that can help us without going into the house."

"I mean, we have mold earth, but I know you won't."

"It's not earth, it's stone."

"Let's just quickly look around the house and see what it is."

"The outside of the house."

"Yeah, it's gotta be a Margaster residence, or something like that."

"It's not a residence, it's not a shop."

"You don't think?"

"That's what I'm questioning. Is it a residence or a shop?"

I go around with them.

A tavern. The House of a Thousand Faces.

"Great," Valphine says.

I know this place. I know the name. I know being outside it without enough coin.

"So many taverns. Well, maybe we can just walk right in. Are they open?"

"Yeah."

The door is open.

"Somebody's obviously in there paying off shady debts."

"Okay. Sure, we can hit the tavern. It's early, but why not?"

I go in.

There is room for me. Deep couches. A fire that draws smoke up the chimney. Mirrors on the walls. Figures in old clothes stand without moving.

I touch my robe at the cuff. Soft cloth. I can pay to be here now.

At the entrance, the others keep talking.

"I have an idea for investigating without going inside: the *Knock* spell."

"So we could open our side, and we can cast Knock on the other side."

"Do you want to try to do that, or do you want to just go inside the tavern?"

I already choose.

"Sorry, how does that work? You use *Knock* to open the two-way dead drop?"

"Right. We can't physically open the other side of the two-way hole, but I think we could open it magically."

"If you propose it, I think it's worth a shot."

"And then we could look in and see, like, what room are we looking at, try to find the right room, or we could even send one of your rats in there to look around."

"I like that combo."

"I have a different strategy, which is, we go in there and offer them a business proposal."

"Yeah, Vukradin could play."

"your booking agency."

"Getting backstage would give you access to staff-only areas where you could do your music preparation—and by music preparation…"

"There you go."

I look at the couches. I would listen to him here.

"We could cast two spells, or we could have an…"

"Or we can get paid!"

"extended contract process, and several performance roles, and hours of time."

"I do like to perform, though."

"Yeah, see, I mean… These are tough choices."

"Let's do both!"

"Porque no los dos."

"Exactly. Why not?"

Vukradin settles the work outside.

"The idea is that we open our side, cast *Knock* on the other side, and then send the familiar rat through to look around the room on the other side."

"It's a simple strategy."

"Yeah."

"Before we go inside. We're all together."

"Yeah, we want to at least not walk in completely blind."

I turn back toward them from inside.

"Yeah, he was born in the tavern," Soma says.

Vukradin looks at the room beyond me.

"Oh, there you go."

"Right. It's more of a high-end establishment."

"He was more of a Common Chord slum, and this was a high-end establishment."

I remember the price. I remember leaving.

"He's a man of class now, he can buy his way into high society."

"Isn't it obvious? It's the quality of your talent."

"Yeah."

"Not."

Vukradin's voice changes. I listen.

"It's totally not, you sure."

"It definitely is not."

"There is a force behind our fame. Some groups are maneuvering…"

"We're being…"

"manipulated."

"to their advantage for some reason."

Someone is pushing his name. We do not know who. I do not like hands I cannot see.

The others go to work at the stone. I stay inside. Knock sounds through the wall.

I watch the door until they gather back at the tavern. Soma brings word from the rat. A room below us. No windows. A desk and papers. The Harpers' sign.

"Harp?"

"Oh, wow!"

"Okay, so is it, like, a storage room, or, like, what other…"

"desk, papers, supplies?"

That is what Soma finds. An office. Supplies. A place for the local Harpers.

"No, no, it's just a rat."

"No, they're right, I can't carry anything."

"No."

"Definitely wouldn't steal anything."

Vukradin makes that clear. I have no wish to steal their papers. I want to know whose money sits in the wall.

"Harper, so that's… Weird."

"The Harpers are… smuggling?"

Soma has an answer before anyone can settle it.

"Clearly that ninth crate involves some smuggling because it's being directly delivered. So yes, the Harpers are smuggling in some capacity."

The Harpers have the drop. That does not tell us who wants the crate. It does not tell us who gets it.

I know the Harpers are not good people. Their soft couches do not help.

The talk turns to the Order of the Gauntlet.

"Debatable."

"Are there?"

"Reformed. They're reformed."

I hold my halberd still.

"Well, they had that whole thing with the barbarians, you know."

I know.

"Exactly, that's what I'm saying. They've been past that, they're reformed now."

The Order rejects the masters who use it. That is what we know now.

"Basically, they're the Reformed order of the Gauntlet."

I look at the fire. Smoke goes where it should. Outside, the Bluelake District has small rooms and men worried about rent. Inside, these couches take up more room than a bed needs.

The mirrors show me from several sides. I keep my back clear of the dressed figures.

"level."

"Basement level."

"Went down, alright."

Soma's rat is below these good floors.

"Yep, it's a good place, I need to leave it."

I stay just inside. My friends are close. We know how to find Rsolk again. We know where the money comes through the wall.

I keep the door in sight.
