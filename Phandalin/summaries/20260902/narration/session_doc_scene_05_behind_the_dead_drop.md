### Brewbarry

I wait while Denvar sleeps. My legs get stiff. I stand.

Soma makes another rat. The first does not last through the wait. She calls on Find Familiar again and the small body returns.

Vukradin finds us. I am glad to see him.

“Yeah, yeah, we can have you come along,” Soma says.

“Yeah, I can tell you about Denvar.”

“Why don't I tell you about, the local were-rat mafia.”

“Yeah, there's were-rats all over, isn't it?”

“I expect them to be such a big player.”

I listen. Vukradin meets Rsolk under the streets. Men go missing in a tunnel. There are strange spiders. We can help.

“We just gotta clear out these spiders, and it's more money for the studio. This is great. Finally a business opportunity.”

“Fair Trade Gold.”

I know what to do with spiders. This is good.

Denvar wakes. We follow.

He stops at a stone wall. He looks around before touching it.

“It's a dead drop,” Vukradin says.

Denvar pulls a stone open. There is a bag inside. He takes it and shuts the hiding place.

“Just a bare stone wall, like, on the edge of the…” Soma says.

The wall belongs to a building. I look along it. No one reaches through from the other side.

“Is it like a two-way… it's like…”

Vukradin gestures.

“like a doctor's office window, right? Like, where, like, you put it in on one side and close the door, and then they…”

He looks again.

“We'd have to go inspect it.”

Denvar has his bag. His work is done.

“Alright, well, it's… yeah. I mean, it's effectively a dead drop, so, like, we should let him go and just inspect at this point.”

“Yeah,” Soma says.

“Excellent.”

She watches him leave.

“Barring coming back the next evening before he leaves his house, but we don't know exactly how early he has to leave to make these drops, so…”

“It's probably a… Denvar might be a dead end.”

“eyeball the wall.”

We approach the stone.

“Valphine, you wanna do your…”

“Infinite perception.”

“You mean, like, on him, or on the…”

“on the wall.”

“on the wall,” Soma repeats.

“I'm gonna look at him just before he goes.”

Denvar looks at his pay. He smiles.

“Payday! I'm going to the casino!”

I watch him walk away. A bag makes his shoulders easier. I know that.

“Could take a little look, then, at the rock.”

Valphine studies the hiding place. There is a closure on the far side. Someone might fill it from the street. Someone might fill it from inside. The stone does not tell us which.

“Oh, okay. Well, let's loop around.”

“Or is it Earth, or…”

Vukradin touches the wall.

“the stone dwelling.”

“Trying to think if we have a… somewhere… something that can help us without going into the house.”

“I mean, we have mold earth, but I know you won't.”

“It's not earth, it's stone.”

“Let's just quickly look around the house and see what it is,” Valphine says.

“The outside of the house.”

“Yeah, it's gotta be a Margaster residence, or something like that.”

“It's not a residence, it's not a shop.”

“You don't think?”

“That's what I'm questioning. Is it a residence or a shop?”

I walk around with them.

It is a tavern.

The House of a Thousand Faces.

I know the name. I hear it in Neverwinter when I have no money to spend there. Other people go. I do not.

“Great,” Valphine says.

“So many taverns. Well, maybe we can just walk right in. Are they open?”

“Yeah.”

The door is open. That is enough for me.

“I mean, somebody's obviously in there paying off, shady debts.”

“Okay. Yeah, sure, we can hit the tavern. I mean, it's early, but why not?”

I step inside.

The couches are thick. The fire draws well. Smoke goes up the chimney instead of into my eyes. Mirrors show me from more than one side. There are dressed figures that do not move. Their clothes look old and costly.

I stop near the entrance. The others are still talking outside.

“Oh, so I… okay, so I do have an idea of how we can investigate this without going inside, which is the knock spell.”

Vukradin has a plan for the stone.

“So we could open our side, and we can cast Knock on the other side.”

“Do you want to try to do that, or do you want to just go inside the tavern?”

I am already inside. I look at a couch again.

“Sorry, how does… you use Knock to open up the two-way, the dead drop stuff, or what?” Soma asks.

“Yeah, like… Right, we can't open the other side of the…”

“We can't physically open the other side of the… of the two-way…”

“hole, but we could… I think we could magically open it if we wanted to do that.”

“I mean, if you… if you propose that, yeah, I think that's, worth a shot.”

“And then we could look in and see, like, what room are we looking at, try to find the right room, or we could even send one of your rats in there to look around.”

“I like that combo.”

“I have a different strategy, which is, we go in there and offer them a business proposal.”

“Yeah, Vukradin could play.”

“your booking agency.”

“Actually, you know, getting backstage in there would,”

Soma keeps going.

“You know, you'd have access to staff-only areas where you could, you know, do your music prep, and by music prep.”

“There you go,” Valphine says.

“We could cast two spells, or we could have an…”

“Or we can get paid!”

“I do like to perform, though.”

“Yeah, see, I mean… These are tough choices.”

“Let's do both!”

“Porque no los dos.”

“Exactly. Why not?”

I stay by the door while the plan settles. The fire reaches my hands. This room is warmer than the street.

“Send the rat, the familiar rat, through to look around on what… the room on the other side.”

“It's a simple strategy,” Valphine says.

“Yeah.”

Vukradin wants to know what is inside before everyone enters.

“Yeah, we want to at least not walk in completely blind.”

Soma notices where I stand.

“Yeah, he was born in the tavern.”

I look at the room. I do not know what a couch like that costs. I can afford to be here now.

“Right. It's more of a high-end establishment,” Vukradin says.

“He's a man of class now, he can buy his way into high society.”

Then Vukradin asks about our fame.

“I do have one question about our fame, though. Sorry, this is a little bit off-topic, but, like, do we know how we became so famous? Or is that a mystery for us to be solving?”

We do not know. I look at my reflection. People dress like me in the streets. I do not tell them to.

“Yeah, I thought so, okay.”

“Isn't it obvious? It's the… it's the quality of your… your… your talent.”

“Yeah,” Soma says.

“not.”

“It's totally not, you sure.”

“It definitely is not.”

Vukradin is firm.

“There is a force behind the fame that is.”

“some… Groups are maneuvering.”

“We're being…”

“manipulated.”

“advantage for some reason.”

I take my hand off the doorframe. I like people hearing his music. I do not like someone using him.

The others go back to the wall. I stay inside. I cannot see through stone. I wait for them to finish.

When they gather at the entrance again, I listen to what they find. Knock opens the inner closure. Soma’s rat passes through. The room below has no windows. A desk. Papers. Supplies.

A Harper symbol.

“Harp?” Vukradin says.

“Oh, wow!”

“Okay, so is it, like, a storage room, or, like, what other…”

“desk, papers, supplies?”

“No, no, it's just a rat.”

“No, they're right, I can't carry anything,” Soma says.

“No.”

“Definitely wouldn't steal anything.”

I know Vukradin would not. He wants to know who pays Denvar.

The rat finds a local Harper headquarters. That tells us who uses the room. It does not tell us who owns the crate.

“Harper, so that's… Weird.”

“The Harpers are… smuggling?”

Soma works through it aloud.

“Well, I mean, clearly that 9th crate is some amount of smuggling, because it's being, you know…”

“directly delivered, so yes, the Harpers are smuggling in some capacity.”

I keep the wall in mind. A bag passes through it. A crate goes below the streets. There is still space between those things.

The Harpers are no friends of ours. Talk turns to the Order of the Gauntlet.

“Debatable,” Soma says.

“Are there?” Valphine asks.

“Reformed. They're reformed.”

I look at Vukradin.

“Well, they had that whole thing with the barbarians, you know.”

I know.

Their political masters use them. The Order rejects those masters now. I listen without speaking.

“Exactly, that's what I'm saying. They've been past that, they're reformed now.”

“Basically, they're the Reformed order of the Gauntlet.”

I shift the halberd so its blade clears the door. There is room for all of us.

Vukradin returns to the office’s position.

“Basement level.”

“Went down, alright.”

Below my feet. I look at the floorboards.

Then I look at the mirrors, the old clothes on the still figures, the deep seats. Outside, the Bluelake District has narrow rooms and people who need their pay before evening. Inside, a fire burns clean and the cushions hold their fullness.

The place has more money than the street around it.

“And I guess… and we have the information so that at any time we can go face”

“This spider infestation.”

“In the sewers, and try to clear it out.”

“For the… the were-rat Mafia?”

We have the directions. Rsolk can be found again. The spiders can wait until we go together.

I stand inside the House of a Thousand Faces. My friends are at the door. The fire warms my hands.
