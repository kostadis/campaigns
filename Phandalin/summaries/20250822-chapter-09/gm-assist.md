# Chapter 9

Date: 2025-08-22

## Summary
With Moesko finally defeated, the party turned their attention to the fallen anchorite's body and the crude quarters he had called home. Resting on the fallen anchorite's lap they found an opalescent conch that had once belonged to Miraal, the sea elf whose spirit had been trapped in restless torment. More unsettling still was what lay beneath his armor — crafted from the hide of a giant octopus: a gaping, hollow void where Moesko's heart should have been, a wound that spoke of something far darker than a simple devotion to a storm god. Tucked beneath a loose floorboard, Valphine uncovered a concealed compartment cradling a strange talisman — dark and iridescent, cool to the touch, pulsing with a faint violet glow. One face bore the jagged lightning bolt of Talos, while the reverse concealed a more sinister mark: an inverted eye wreathed in coiling, tentacle-like lines. The talisman radiated a faint divine energy, but beneath it churned something far more alien — a psionic force that had no place in conventional magic.

The party pressed upward through the tower, pausing only for Valphine to call upon her faith to mend their wounds before they climbed further. From the roof, they could see the shattered masts of five shipwrecks jutting from the western waters like broken fingers. A towering wrought iron lightning rod reached toward the sky, and Soma quickly surmised it had been used to channel the storm's fury down into the altar below. Vukradin wasted no time and set about smashing it to pieces, determined to break whatever curse still clung to the tower. At the very pinnacle of the lighthouse, Valphine peered into the pulsating green beacon — and was struck blind. But in the moment before darkness took her, she saw the truth: the beacon was not powered by flame or magic crystal, but by a massive, still-beating heart, grotesquely oversized, continuing to pulse long after its owner's death. After some debate about whether a heart could truly be evil — Valphine argued it was serving a useful purpose and should be left alone — Vukradin struck it down himself, plunging the lighthouse into darkness. Vukradin conjured a soft light to hold back the black while the others searched for something to replace the beacon's flame.

Soma shifted into the form of a giant toad and plunged into the murky, shark-infested waters below alongside Brewbarry, the two of them picking through the sunken wrecks in search of firewood and whatever else the sea had swallowed. It was Brewbarry who found the first prize — a finely crafted magical halberd still clutched in the skeletal grip of a long-dead ship's captain aboard the Star-Crossed Lover. But the sea was not willing to give up its treasures without a fight. A massive hunter shark emerged from the gloom and lunged at Brewbarry with savage force, and before long, two more circled in from the darkness. From the surface, Vukradin hurled biting magical insults at the churning water while Valphine called down pillars of radiant fire upon the predators below. The battle was desperate and close, with Soma absorbing brutal bites in her toad form and Brewbarry trading blows with the circling beasts. It was Vukradin who turned the tide, weaving a haunting melody that lulled two of the sharks into a deep, unnatural slumber. Valphine then outlined the sleeping predators in shimmering violet light, and the party struck with precision, dispatching all three sharks and harvesting their meat as a hard-won reward.

With the sharks dealt with, Soma and Brewbarry continued their salvage of the remaining wrecks, hauling three locked chests to the surface along with a remarkable cloak recovered from the Vainglory — a garment that had somehow remained pristine despite its time beneath the waves, shimmering and shifting color as though alive. Soma presented it to Vukradin with a knowing look, and the musician was visibly tempted, the cloak's pulsing colors perfectly suited to a performer of his sensibilities. He hesitated, however, unwilling to claim something that might belong to another. Valphine, ever practical, pried open the recovered chests over Vukradin's moral objections, revealing a delicate blue quartz mermaid statuette, a spellbook, and a wand capable of detecting hidden secrets. The party gathered their finds and made their way back to shore, shark meat in hand.

They brought the opalescent conch to Miraal at last, and the moment it passed into her hands, her spirit stirred with relief. She thanked them, but her parting words carried a weight that lingered long after she faded from sight. She revealed that Moesko had not acted entirely of his own will — something cold and calculating had moved within him, twisting his devotion to Talos into a tool for a far darker purpose. Her final warning was a whisper that settled over the party like a coming storm: the true danger did not gather in the sky above, but in the depths below the town of Leilon. And then she was gone. The giant crab, overjoyed at the news of Miraal's peace, welcomed the party warmly and confirmed that the magical halberd Brewbarry had pulled from the deep was the very treasure he had intended to retrieve for them. He accepted a share of the fresh shark meat with great enthusiasm, and for a brief moment, the weight of what lay ahead felt a little lighter. But Miraal's words echoed still — something ancient and alien was stirring beneath Leilon, and the party knew their journey was far from over.

## Locations
### Tower of Storms
A lighthouse tower where the party recently defeated Moesko. The interior contains crude living spaces, prayer skulls dedicated to Talos, storm-weathered gear, and a concealed compartment hiding a dark talisman. The roof holds a wrought iron lightning rod, and the pinnacle houses a magical beacon powered by a monstrous beating heart.

### Shipwreck Waters
The murky, shark-infested waters west of the lighthouse containing the remains of five ships: the Star-Crossed Lover (T10), the Sea Urchin (T11), the Vainglory (T12), the Golden Gull (T13), and the Orca (T14). Visibility underwater is limited to twenty feet, and the wrecks contain salvageable treasure.

### Leilon
A town to the south, mentioned by the spirit Miraal as the site of a gathering storm brewing in the depths below it — the party's next destination after completing their quests near Phandalin.

## NPCs
### Moesko
A deceased anchorite of Talos whose body was found with a strange hole in his chest. He wore armor crafted from giant octopus hide. His oversized, still-beating heart was discovered powering the lighthouse beacon even after his death. Miraal later revealed he was being manipulated by a calculating foreign presence from the deep rather than acting purely of his own will.

### Miraal
A sea elf spirit whose opalescent conch was stolen by Moesko. After the party returned the conch, her spirit found peace. Before departing, she warned that Moesko was influenced by a foreign shadow from the deep and delivered a cryptic prophecy about a storm gathering in the depths below Leilon.

### Hunter Sharks
Aggressive aquatic predators that ambushed Soma and Brewbarry near the shipwrecks. Three sharks attacked in total — the meanest of them recorded in campaign canon as Daggermaw — biting with vicious force and attempting to grapple their prey before being dispatched by the party.

### Crabby (Giant Crab)
A friendly, sentient crustacean who was pleased to hear of Miraal's peace. He confirmed that the magical halberd found in the shipwreck was the treasure he had intended to retrieve for the party, and happily shared in the fresh shark meat the party brought back.

## Items
### Opalescent Conch
A beautiful, shimmering shell belonging to Miraal the sea elf. It was found resting on Moesko's lap and was the key to bringing her spirit peace. Upon receiving it, Miraal's spirit was able to depart, but not before delivering a final cryptic warning.

### Iridescent Talisman
A palm-sized object made of a dark material that seems to drink the light, similar to polished obsidian or a fragment of solidified night sky. It pulsates with a faint violet glow and is cool to the touch. One side bears the jagged lightning bolt of Talos, while the reverse features a subtly interwoven geometric symbol of an inverted eye with coiling tentacles. It radiates a faint divine resonance and a dominant, unsettling psionic energy alien to conventional magic.

### Octopus Hide Armor
A set of medium armor crafted from the hide of a giant octopus, worn by Moesko. The party debated taking it and left it behind.

### Moesko's Heart
A heart several sizes too large that continued to beat and emanate a pulsating green light after its owner's death, serving as the power source for the lighthouse beacon. The party destroyed it to break the tower's curse, extinguishing the beacon.

### Plus One Halberd
A magical polearm found in the wreckage of the Star-Crossed Lover, still clutched in the skeletal grip of the ship's captain. The giant crab later confirmed it was the treasure he had intended to retrieve for the party.

### Cloak of Many Fashions
A fashionable, magical garment found in the Vainglory shipwreck that shows no signs of decay despite being submerged underwater. It shifts and pulses with colors on command. Soma presented it to Vukradin, who was tempted but hesitant to take it.

### Blue Quartz Mermaid Statuette
A delicate, nine-inch-tall carving of a mermaid made from blue quartz, recovered from a locked chest salvaged from the underwater shipwrecks.

### Wand of Secrets
A magical wand recovered, with a wizard's spellbook, from the locked chest aboard the Orca (T14). Used for the detection of secret doors and hidden compartments.

### Shark Meat
Fresh meat harvested from the hunter sharks killed by the party during their underwater excursion. The giant crab was delighted to share in the meal.

## Spells
### Prayer of Healing
Cast by Valphine to mend the party's wounds before they ascended the upper levels of the tower, restoring a significant amount of hit points to multiple party members.

### Light
Cast by Vukradin to provide a temporary light source for the lighthouse after Moesko's heart was destroyed and the beacon went dark, buying the party time to search for firewood.

### Wild Shape
Used by Soma to transform into a giant toad, providing the swimming ability and resilience needed to explore the underwater shipwrecks and engage the attacking sharks.

### Vicious Mockery
Employed by Vukradin to hurl magical insults at the attacking sharks. The underwater environment made it difficult to perceive the targets, though the spell was eventually used to impose disadvantage on a shark's attacks.

### Sacred Flame
Cast by Valphine from the surface, calling down pillars of radiant energy to strike the sharks churning in the water below, dealing radiant damage to the predators.

### Sleep
Cast by Vukradin during the shark battle, successfully plunging two of the attacking sharks into a magical slumber and temporarily removing them from the fight, allowing the party to strike with precision.

### Faerie Fire
Cast by Valphine to outline the sleeping sharks in a shimmering violet glow, preventing them from benefiting from the murky water and granting her allies advantage on their attacks against the predators.

### Healing Word
Held in reserve by Valphine during the shark battle, prepared to quickly revive Soma should the druid fall unconscious from the sharks' attacks.
