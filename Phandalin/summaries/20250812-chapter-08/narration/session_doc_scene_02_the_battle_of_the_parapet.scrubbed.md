The silence presses into my head until I can hear my own legs touch stone.

Eight legs. Better legs for this work.

I climb the outside of the tower, my spider body close against the wall. Salt has worked into the mortar. The stone crumbles beneath two of my feet, but six remain, and six are sufficient. The upper parapet waits ten feet above the plateau. The harpies wait above that.

Below me, Brewbarry swings his halberd and finds air. The blade carries him half a step before he catches his weight. A long weapon is useful when your enemy agrees to be near it.

One harpy does not. It tears away from the melee and circles beyond the silence, calling us after it. The song cannot enter Vukradin’s spell, so the creature tries to pull my bale out instead.

Vukradin takes the invitation halfway. He darts beyond the quiet, strikes the distant harpy with magic, and retreats before its song can settle into him. The blast is not much, but the creature stops howling.

Valphine fires at the other singer. Her bolt lands. The harpy jerks in the air, catches itself, and continues singing.

Of course it does.

I crest the parapet. The stone levels beneath my feet, and I turn toward the nearest harpy.

It folds its wings and drops at me. Claws scrape across my spider body, a shallow hit that shakes the borrowed flesh without threatening it. Then the harpy beats upward again.

It should not have turned its back.

I bite as it passes. My fangs sink deep enough to make the creature lurch in flight.

"It's 12 damage. Dude, Circle of the Moon druids start early. They don't really get better, but they start early."

The spider body is still strong. The hit costs me little. I can hold this form and keep climbing, biting, and occupying the attention of anything foolish enough to come close.

"Anyway, I'm just going to go whale on it anyway."

The harpy retreats along the parapet. Fine.

"I'm going to chase after it. I mean, it wants to play this game with me. I'll play with it."

My legs carry me over the stone, faster than my tortle joints would permit and with none of the complaint from my shell. I snap at the harpy and catch nothing.

"All right. That was a worthy effort."

The creature claws at me as I pass. I offer it the opening.

"Take the attack of opportunity. Come at me, bro."

Its talons cut through empty air.

"I see a miss."

It is not close. Not remotely.

"I see a hell of a miss."

Below, Brewbarry abandons the halberd and searches himself for something throwable.

"Well, I may have a throwing axe or something. Yeah, hand axe."

He studies the harpies, chooses the one I have already bitten, and moves until the distance suits his arm.

"All right. I guess hand axes are cheap, right?"

The axe turns once, twice, and passes wide.

"I heard that. Brewbarry's whiffing tonight. It's two whiffs."

Brewbarry finds the second axe.

"Okay. Sure. I guess I can throw the second one then."

"Hey, consistency, man. You can do it."

He throws. The second axe follows the first almost exactly.

"It's the exact same one."

Consistency achieved.

The second harpy draws breath and begins its own song, apparently convinced the first singer was the problem rather than the silence surrounding most of us.

"Good job."

Vukradin has reached his own conclusion about the pair of them.

"Oh, they really are dumb."

I give him the number.

"Intelligence 7."

Vukradin glances toward the boundary of his spell.

"So that'll be an issue if somebody wants to leave the sphere. Okay."

Valphine raises her crossbow toward the active singer.

"Two, you say. All right. Sure. I'll do number two."

The bolt punches into the harpy.

"Seven damage on two. I'll apply the damage."

The song wavers. Then it steadies.

"Ah, fiddlesticks."

The third harpy screeches and dives at Vukradin. Its claws miss, and he answers with magic. The blast catches it and breaks its song.

Vukradin does not wait for me.

"I'll do my attack."

"I hit — and another six damage on three. Okay, down to 22."

"Oh, that one stopped singing. It was singing, and it's no longer singing."

"Okay, but two is still singing."

The remaining voice carries across the open air with no sign of weakening.

"Oh. So it really doesn't matter if three stops singing while two is doing it."

Vukradin has already reached the same conclusion. The third harpy closes on him, expecting another spell.

It gets steel.

"So Vukradin is going to do his double daggers then."

I watch both blades come free.

"Thought you were a spell slinger."

"He's going to get two attacks. Let's see how that goes. That's one hit."

The first dagger lands hard.

"That's a hit and a half. Seven on three. That's down to 15."

The second does not connect.

"And then second attack. That's not a miss. All right. I'll take that."

It is a miss. Vukradin is allowed his own account of events.

The singing harpy drops toward Valphine next. Its claws scrape across her armor and find no purchase. Valphine already has her mace in hand. The harpy sees it too late.

As the creature climbs away, Valphine strikes it across the side. The blow is light, but it lands.

"All right. Yeah, and two."

I keep my eight feet planted on the upper stone and turn toward the harpy I chased. Below, my bale remains inside the silence, armed with daggers, a mace, an empty axe hand, and several increasingly firm opinions about flying creatures.
