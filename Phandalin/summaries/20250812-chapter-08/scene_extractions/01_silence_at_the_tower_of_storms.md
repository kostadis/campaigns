# Silence at the Tower of Storms

## Scene summary (from session-summary.md, verbatim)

#### The party engages in a tactical battle against harpies at the Tower of Storms, using magical silence to counter their charming songs.
- Vukradin casts a sphere of magical silence, deafening everyone inside it and breaking the charm effect on his companions; the airborne harpies remain outside its radius.
- Vukradin sizes up the situation with confidence: *"She can sing all she wants. We can't hear her."*
- The DM clarifies the stakes: characters who made their original saving throw are immune to *that* harpy's song for 24 hours, but remain vulnerable to the other two — and the songs carry to anyone within 300 feet.
- One of the harpies, frustrated by the silence, retreats over the parapet.
- Soma transforms into a giant wolf spider to scale the walls and engage the flying harpies, deliberately limiting herself to familiar shapes.
- Soma, in spider form, climbs the exterior wall and strikes one of the harpies with a powerful bite for 11 damage — the table is dismayed to discover how many hit points the harpies have.
- Brewbarry enters a rage and attempts to strike a harpy with his halberd but fails to connect, rolling a 10 against the creatures' AC of 11.
- The harpies attempt to maintain their distance, flying and disengaging to avoid the party's melee attacks; the DM explains that harpies like to charm their enemies, and since they literally cannot do it here, *"silent spells are very effective at dissuading harpies from attacking you."*
- Valphine puts a critical crossbow bolt into the harpy that had tried to charm her — the charm saves are called only after the shot resolves; the party realizes ranged attacks will decide this fight.
- Vukradin darts out of the silence sphere, blasts Harpy 3 with a 60-foot-range spell for 4 damage (the harpy was only 10 feet away), and runs back inside — establishing the peek-and-shoot rhythm of the battle.
