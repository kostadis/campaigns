# The Harpy's End

## Scene summary (from session-summary.md, verbatim)

#### The party concludes their grueling battle against the harpies on the rocky plateau, finally silencing their enchanting songs.
- Soma's 12-damage bite on Harpy 1 breaks the last singer's concentration, ending its song and freeing Brewbarry from the charm — *"Brewbarry, you're free. Hooray."*
- Vukradin drops the zone of magical silence to free up spell options, at the party's urging that a spell attack would serve better than another whiffed melee swing.
- Soma, in the form of a giant spider, takes heavy damage from a harpy's strike but remains in the fight — a 16 just clears her AC 16, stripping her temporary hit points and dropping her to 24.
- Valphine lands a critical blow on Harpy 3 for 4 hit points. (Harpy 2's death — the 3-damage mace attack on the way out and the crossbow bolt that finished it, *"One third of the way down"* — happens in the two preceding scenes, where its quotes are.)
- Brewbarry delivers a final, lethal blow with his halberd to one of the harpies — a hit and a kill in one stroke.
- Vukradin uses Vicious Mockery to taunt the final harpy to death — 4 psychic damage against its last 4 hit points; *"He is mocked to death."*
- David eulogizes the fallen: *"Their music is just they're not joining the academy, that's for sure."*
- The party takes a short rest — ruled at 30 minutes — to recover from their wounds and retrieve thrown weapons; the DM also lays down an ammunition house rule: no infinite hand axes, and arrows cap at 40 with 50% retrievable after a fight.
