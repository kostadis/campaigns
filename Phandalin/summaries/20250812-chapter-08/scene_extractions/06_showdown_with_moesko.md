# Showdown with Moesko

## Scene summary (from session-summary.md, verbatim)

#### The party confronts the half-orc anchorite Moesko in his chamber at the top of the tower, leading to a desperate and nearly lethal battle.
- The party enters a 50-foot room with two north-facing windows, where Moesko — one of *"those weird clerics,"* a half-orc anchorite like those the party has faced before — sits in a barnacle-encrusted chair against the south wall, clad in armor made from giant octopus hide and holding an opalescent conch stolen from a sea elf.
- The DM reads off his numbers at the table: AC 13, 58 hit points.
- Soma opens with a whip of thorns — after checking whether it could knock the seated orc prone (it only pulls) — and follows on a later turn with a spray of poison for 2 damage; separately, Moesko makes a DC 13 Wisdom save against an effect of Vukradin's; she deliberately holds off on Wild Shape: *"I didn't feel like burning a wild shape in here. One lonely guy."*
- Brewbarry enters a rage — his second of the day — and attacks Moesko with his halberd, though his strikes fail to connect on a 9; Vukradin consoles him: *"You know what? I'm sorry. It was the silence. The fact that you couldn't hear yourself scream."*
- Valphine fires her crossbow at the half-orc, scoring a hit for 6 damage.
- Moesko retaliates by unleashing a powerful bolt of lightning — 8d6 rolled by hand for 25 damage against a DC 12 Dexterity save — that strikes nearly the entire party, leaving several members near death and the table muttering about a TPK.
- Soma, down to two, burns her Wild Shape and transforms into a massive brown bear — 11 hit points in the new form (the DM had pulled a spider icon onto the Roll20 screen by mistake; Wade corrected him to the bear) — too Large for her original position until Brewbarry offers to shift aside — and mauls Moesko with a flurry of attacks: both multi-attack strikes land for 20 damage. *"Yay, bear. Rar."*
- Vukradin conjures a cloud of spinning daggers in a five-foot cube positioned on the half-orc, dealing 9 damage immediately and threatening the same on every round he stays trapped between the blades and the bear.
- The DM's internet dies mid-round, vanishing the bear token and the DM alike (*"Losing you, Kostadis. The tubes are clogged. It's a series of tubes"*); he returns citing *"an internet flux"* and the industrial-grade router his house on Santorini needs to serve fifty-plus DHCP connections from visiting relatives.
- Valphine fires a crossbow bolt at Moesko for 5 damage before retreating from the room to avoid further magical attacks — the party having realized *"another lightning bolt will not go well. There will be three characters down."*
- Moesko, out of lightning bolts, releases a burst of thunderous force — 2d8 with a 10-foot push — that damages the party and pushes them back; he flees the dagger cube rather than end his turn inside it; Soma is pushed back by the blast. Wade, unbothered: *"Everyone's just abandoning the bear. The bear will be fine."*
- Brewbarry charges in and hits Moesko, his rage bonus leaving the anchorite on a single hit point.
- Soma reverts from bear form to manoeuvre in the cramped chamber and delivers the killing blow — *"Can I attack to end the night? Please."* — *"Dead."*
- The party prepares to loot the room and investigate the mysterious opalescent conch: *"we'll figure out what that conch is and everything next week."*
