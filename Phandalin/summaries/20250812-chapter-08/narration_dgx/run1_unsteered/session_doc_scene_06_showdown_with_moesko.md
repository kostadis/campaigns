We go through the door.

The room is fifty feet, two north-facing windows, and a barnacle-encrusted chair against the south wall. Sitting in it is Moesko. One of those weird clerics. And resting in his lap is an opalescent conch, the one he took from Miraal the Sea Elf. The armor is giant octopus hide. The whole picture is very "I live here and I have strong opinions about the decor."

"Oh, yeah."

I guess that doesn't look like a friendly, does it?

"Nope."

The DM reads off his numbers. Initiative 20, then 11, then 10. Armor class 13. Hit points 58. I stop caring about the arithmetic and start caring about the fact that he's sitting. Seated target. Might that matter?

"Is he sitting? Is he, you know, any sort of..."

"Yeah, he's sitting. He packed me sitting. He sees you. He's going to get up and attack you. Usual."

"Does he have any disadvantage for sitting?"

"No."

Figures. "All right. Then I'm just going to try to thorn whip him, knock him to his feet. Oh, no, that just pulls him. That doesn't knock him over, does it?"

"No, I don't think it does."

"Hardly seems worth doing anything serious to him. I'm still going to thorn whip him. Or if we're going to poison spray, let's just do that."

Roll the dice. Two damage. Not poison condition, just poison damage. Two damage against fifty-eight hit points. Off to a roaring start.

"I didn't feel like burning a wild shape in here."

One lonely guy.

Vukradin tries something, calls for a DC 13 wisdom save. "He's a cleric, right? He's probably going to make that."

"Oh, well, then he made it. Okay."

Brewbarry runs in. Second rage of the day. Halberd swings. Misses on a nine. You can hear the frustration in the silence.

Vukradin, ever the consoler: "You know what? I'm sorry. It was the silence. The fact that you couldn't hear yourself scream, that was."

"That's right. That's definitely the problem."

And then Vukradin turns it into doctrine: "Now, Brewbarry is going to have a permanent affectation that silence is bad for it makes him weak."

"Silence makes me weak."

I remember martial arts training. Call out with every punch. It makes you stronger. There's truth in it, even if the mechanism is more about breath than magic.

Valphine takes the first attack with a crossbow. Six points of damage. We're chipping at him. Slow, steady, this is manageable.

Then the orc is up. Anchorite. That's the word. And he casts lightning bolt.

"That's probably not going to be good. That seems bad. Yeah. TPK here."

Dex saves for everybody. DC 12. The bolt catches nearly all of us. Brewbarry, somehow, is spared — the line just misses him. The rest of us take it. Twenty-five damage rolled by hand, halved to twelve for those who made the save.

Vukradin tracks the damage. "Ooh, I'm down to four. Soma, you're down to two."

"Yep. Still standing."

Two hit points. One solid sneeze and I'm done. "Yeah, that's that. Now I do want to burn the wild shape charge coming out as a spider."

"So I'm now at 11 hit points. No bear this time. I can't fit bear in here. Bear is large. Should we move? No, we're committed now, right?"

Brewbarry offers his square. "Want me to move here and then you can get the bear in there?"

"Well, I mean, it was my turn earlier, but I mean, if you could, yeah, a bear would be a little more appropriate. Yes. Give me a bear icon quickly."

"I'll just say I'm bear in this two by two area."

Brown bear. Important thing is brown bear gets multi-attack. Both strikes land. Twenty damage.

"Yay, bear. Rar."

"The best bear. Best kind of bear."

Vukradin conjures a cloud of spinning daggers over the half-orc. Nine damage immediately, and more every round he stays trapped between the blades and the bear. The math starts looking better.

Then the DM's token vanishes. "Where'd my bear go?"

"Oh, you did? Yeah. I mean, you never gave me control, but now it's gone."

"I see a whisper of a bear and cannot get a bear. I should need to figure out how to do this without you."

Then the DM vanishes too. Internet flux. Something about a house in Santorini, eight rooms, nieces and nephews, and an industrial-strength router for all the DHCP connections. Fifty-plus addresses. I offer the Linux server solution. He pays someone in Santorini to maintain the commercial router instead. Different priorities.

While the DM is gone, Vukradin assesses the situation. "Because another lightning bolt is going to be deadly. Lightning will be an issue. Another lightning bolt will not go well. There will be three characters down."

"And that will be painful."

Valphine takes her shot. Crossbow bolt, five damage, then she steps out of the room. Smart. The rest of us are still in here with the anchorite.

"Everyone's just abandoning the bear. The bear will be fine. Mama Bear go down. Brewbarry is fine."

Vukradin asks the important question. "Do you have another lightning bolt?"

"No, I don't."

Good. "That's what I was kind of smiling. Oh, geez."

What he has instead is thunderwave. Two d8s, a ten-foot push, constitution save. "I can survive this. Yeah. Maybe. Maybe. And it's pushed 10 feet. Ooh."

I make the save. Pushed back, but alive, still in the fight.

He moves out of the dagger cloud rather than end his turn inside it. Brewbarry gets the opportunity attack. "He moved out of the cloud, so Brewbarry, you're up. Do your attack. Maybe you can finish him. He's down to three hit points."

I remember the rage bonus. That's a plus two. Two more damage on a hit.

"So he's down to one hit point."

One. One hit point left on this anchorite who nearly killed us all with a single spell. The DM is smiling again, but it's a different smile now. Desperate.

"All right. Can I attack to end the night? Please. Let's see if I can hit once on one of these two tries."

"Dead."

"Dead?"

"Yes."

"I mean, he needed one hit point. I think I did 13."

The reverted form hits. One hit point was all he needed, and I gave him thirteen. The lightning bolt that nearly ended us, answered with a bear and a cloud of daggers and one final, decisive strike. The bear was fine. The bear ended the night.

"All right, guys. We will stop here."

"Pick up the loot and the, yeah, we'll figure out what that conch is and everything next week."
