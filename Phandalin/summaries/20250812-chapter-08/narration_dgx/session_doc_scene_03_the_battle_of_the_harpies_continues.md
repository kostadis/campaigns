The wall is just half my movement. I can climb it. So I climb.

Hands find holds in the rock, old mortar, the worn edge of a battlement. I pull myself up and the song hits me in the chest.

*Sing with me.*

Not words. A pull. Like something warm wrapped around my spine and tugging. I know what it is. I knew before I got up here. There is no way to stand on this wall and not risk it. I climbed anyway, because the axes are not going to throw themselves and the harpies are not going to come down to where I stand useless.

And the pull takes me.

"Charmed one. Got it. Okay. Good times."

I say it out loud because saying it makes it smaller. Harpy One. The singer. The one that has been filling this fight with its terrible music. I can see it, perched on the battlement above me, and I cannot stop looking at it.

My legs want to move. They want to walk toward it. That is all I can do now — walk toward it. No weapons. No rage. Just my legs, wanting to carry me to the thing that called.

I try to take a step and the wall is in the way. The harpy picked its perch well. I cannot walk toward it because there is stone between us. So I do nothing.

The next moment comes and I am still caught. I feel for the rage — the familiar heat, the thing that has carried me through every fight since I can remember. The thing that lets my ancestors speak through my arms. I reach for it and there is nothing there. Just the song. Just the pull.

*The entire day.* Vukradin says it. "Valphine's hit with a couple of crossbow bolts, but a universal whiff. Brewbarry has whiffed everything."

He is not being cruel. He is being true. I have swung and missed, thrown and missed, and now I cannot even swing because I am standing on a wall listening to a bird sing at me. The rage left me while I was not using it. It does not wait forever. I know this. I have always known this. It is not a fire I keep banked; it is a fire that burns when I need it to burn, and somewhere between the whiffed axes and the failed saves it went out.

"We're no longer raging, by the way."

No. We are not.

The spider bites Harpy One and the song holds anyway. Soma is immune to it. Of course she is. She has the patience of stone and the wisdom of the old world. She does not get stopped by a voice. She has heard better.

"From Vukradin, no. From me, probably."

I want to laugh at that and I cannot find it. The pull is still on my spine. I stand on the battlement with my dead hands and my dead rage and my legs that want to walk somewhere I cannot go.

Somewhere below me, Valphine's crossbow sings its own small song and Harpy Two stops singing forever. "Dead." One third of the way down. Good. We are winning. I am the only one who is not winning.

A claw hits Vukradin. I see it happen — the harpy drops out of the air and rakes him across the shoulder and he takes it, standing there in his silence like a man holding up a roof. "I'm feeling weak here, guys." He makes the save. The silence holds. He is at three hit points and he is still holding the silence, still standing between the song and me.

"I'm not feeling so well here, guys."

I am standing on a wall I climbed to throw axes at a bird, and instead I got charmed, and my rage lapsed, and Vukradin is bleeding for me. Soma is fighting a spider-fearer who cannot even commit to its own attack. Valphine is shooting them down one by one with a crossbow. Everyone is doing something. I am the one doing nothing.

The pull is still there. I can feel it, patient and warm, holding my legs in place. I cannot walk toward Harpy One because of the wall. I cannot fight it because I cannot act. I can only stand here and wait for the song to loosen — or for the fight to end without me.

I make the save. Maybe I make it like the spider makes it, patient and unbothered. Maybe the music breaks and my legs are mine again.

Or maybe I stand here until someone wins this fight without me.
