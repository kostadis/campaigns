# The Battle of the Parapet

## Scene summary (from session-summary.md, verbatim)

#### The party continues their grueling skirmish against three harpies atop the Tower of Storms, utilizing a zone of silence to combat the creatures' charming songs.
- Soma, in the form of a giant wolf spider, scales the exterior wall to engage a harpy on the upper level, ten feet above the plateau.
- Brewbarry attempts to strike a harpy with his halberd but fails to connect.
- A harpy disengages from the melee, attempting to lure the party away from the protective silence.
- Vukradin darts out of the silence to blast a harpy with magic before retreating to safety — his 6-damage hit on Harpy 3 stops that harpy howling. (Valphine's own 7-damage shot on Harpy 2 lands here too, but the harpy makes its concentration save and keeps singing.)
- A harpy swoops down to strike Soma's spider form, hitting on a 22 for 6 damage, but the druid retaliates with a powerful 12-damage bite as the creature tries to fly away — *"Dude, Circle of the Moon Druids start early. They don't really get better, but they start early."*
- Brewbarry attempts to use ranged attacks, throwing both his hand axes at the flying foes as action and bonus action, but his aim is off — two whiffs, prompting Vukradin's verdict: *"Brewbarry's whiffing tonight."*
- Caught in melee, Vukradin surprises the harpy by drawing double daggers — the DM notes the dim creature *"thought you could only cast spells,"* or as Soma put it, *"Thought you were a spell slinger."* One dagger lands for 7.
- Valphine defends herself with a mace when a harpy screeches down to attack her in melee.
