# The Battle of the Harpies Continues

## Scene summary (from session-summary.md, verbatim)

#### The party continues their struggle against the trio of harpies on the rocky outcropping, dealing with the creatures' enchanting songs and hit-and-run tactics.
- Vukradin uses his bardic inspiration to resist a harpy's charming song and strikes back with magic, breaking the creature's concentration — the DC was 11, his raw roll failed, and the inspiration die pushed him over.
- Soma, in the form of a giant spider, scales the walls to engage the harpies in melee, successfully resisting their songs on a +5 Wisdom save; the DM rules that *"Soma's heard better songs."* Soma: *"From Vukradin, no. From me, probably."*
- Brewbarry attempts to climb the battlements to reach the flying foes — the DM waives the athletics check, ruling the wall merely difficult terrain — but falls under the charm of a harpy's song on an effective 1 (a rolled 2, minus 1), becoming momentarily incapacitated: no actions, no reactions, compelled to move toward the singer.
- Brewbarry fails his follow-up save on a 4 as well (the die was re-read at the table as a 5; either value fails the DC 11), and his rage lapses from disuse — a universal whiff of a night.
- Harpy 1 attacks the spider but flinches at the last moment; the DM improvises that the creature *"has a certain degree of arachnophobia... It had this whole incident with spiders."*
- Valphine provides ranged support with her crossbow, eventually finishing off one of the wounded harpies.
- Harpy 3 claws Vukradin for 12 damage, dropping him to nine hit points; he grits through the concentration save and keeps the silence intact.
- Vukradin maintains a zone of silence to protect the party from the harpies' songs, even while being physically assaulted by the creatures — at one point standing at three hit points while still holding concentration, muttering *"I'm not feeling so well here, guys."*
- The harpies repeatedly swoop down to strike at the party members before flying back out of reach, leading to a grueling battle of attrition.
