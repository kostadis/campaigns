# Exploring the Tower of Storms

## Scene summary (from session-summary.md, verbatim)

#### After the battle, the party enters the tower to investigate its interior and discovers clues about the source of the local shipwrecks.
- Rough-hewn stairs climb the eastern face of the rocky outcropping to a plateau some 80 feet above the water; wooden doors with rusty iron hinges bear decorative lightning bolts carved into them.
- The party enters an empty 15-foot-high room with windows overlooking the wrecks of ships broken on the rocks below — Vukradin immediately spies *"so many possessions that must be returned to their rightful owners,"* to which the DM points out those owners are dead underwater.
- They discover a room adorned with frescoes depicting a dark god looming over stormy seas, prompting a failed religion check — in fact every attempted religion check at the table fails (*"Our cleric's not very religious, is she?"* — *"We got nothing tonight."*), leaving the party with only *"good art"* to go on.
- Soma admires the frescoes and muses about extracting a section for the academy, possibly via Mold Earth; Vukradin is skeptical: *"I don't think mold earth is going to be sufficient here."* Soma: *"Ye have little faith."*
- The party finds a stone altar carved with lightning bolts against the south wall, beneath a metal rod that descends from the ceiling; a dirty, salt-encrusted window is set separately into the west wall. The rod splitting in two before it embeds itself into the stone — a lightning rod.
- Valphine searches a foul-smelling harpy nest — a ledge enclosed by a three-foot-high stone retaining wall — blowing every search roll, yet *"the potion of water breathing was quite obvious"*; she searches and finds it; Vukradin proposes and commits to torching the nest (*"I'll torch it right now. I'm not even going to search it."*), an idea the DM endorses and Soma flees (*"I mean, it smells like napalm"*).
- The party discusses the morality of keeping the loot found in the tower versus returning it to the heirs of the deceased sailors; Vukradin proposes a Legend Lore spell to identify the potion's owner before filing it under *"blood money. Consumables."*
- The DM observes the party's confusion is well-earned — this is not a libertarian paradise but *"a colonial paradise... There are people who are considered part of civilized society and people who are not"* — and predicts *"The Order of the Gauntlet is going to be so pleased to have you on their side."* Vukradin: *"I don't trust those guys."*
- Debate breaks out over whether the harpies were truly what lured the ships to their doom; the DM nearly tips his hand about the tower itself before conceding the party doesn't actually know — *"I don't think Crabby McGee told us that."*
