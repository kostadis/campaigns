## Features & Traits

### Druid Features
- **Core Druid Traits / Spellcasting (PHB-2024 79):** Standard druid spellcasting.
- **Druidic (PHB-2024 80):** You know Druidic and always have Speak with Animals prepared. You can use Druidic to leave hidden messages; others with Druidic automatically spot them, others need a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check to spot (but not decipher) them.
- **Primal Order — Warden (PHB-2024 80):** Trained for battle, you gain proficiency with Martial weapons and training with Medium armor.
- **Wild Shape (PHB-2024 80):** Bonus Action shape-shift into a learned Beast form; lasts 3 hours or until you use Wild Shape again, are Incapacitated, or die; can leave early as a Bonus Action. 3 uses/Long Rest, regain one on a Short Rest.
  - Level 2: 4 known Beast Forms, max CR 1/4, no Fly Speed.
  - Level 4: 6 known Beast Forms, max CR 1/2, no Fly Speed.
  - Level 8: 8 known Beast Forms, max CR 1, can include Fly Speed.
  - While shape-shifted: retain personality, memories, speech; gain 7 Temp HP; stats replaced by Beast's stat block but retain creature type, HP, HP Dice, Int/Wis/Cha scores, class features, languages, feats; retain skill/save proficiencies using own Proficiency Bonus, gain Beast's proficiencies if better.
- **Wild Companion (PHB-2024 81):** As a Magic action, expend a spell slot or Wild Shape use to cast Find Familiar without material components; familiar is Fey and disappears at end of Long Rest.
- **Druid Subclass (PHB-2024 81):** Circle of the Moon.
- **Circle Forms (PHB-2024 86):** When you assume Wild Shape: max CR for form is 2; AC becomes 17 while in form if higher than Beast's AC; gain 21 Temp HP. (Bonus Action)
- **Circle of the Moon Spells (PHB-2024 86):** Always-prepared spells by level; can be cast while in Wild Shape form.
- **Ability Score Improvement (PHB-2024 81).**
- **Wild Resurgence (PHB-2024 81):** Once per turn, if no Wild Shape uses left, expend a spell slot (no action) to regain one use. Also, once per Long Rest, expend one Wild Shape use (no action) to gain a level 1 spell slot.
- **Improved Circle Forms (PHB-2024 87):** While in Wild Shape form: attacks can deal normal or Radiant damage (choose on hit); add Wis. modifier (+4) to Con. saving throws.
- **Elemental Fury — Primal Strike (PHB-2024 81):** Once per turn, when you hit with a weapon or Beast form attack in Wild Shape, cause an extra 1d8 Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage (choose on hit). 1 Action.

### Tortle Species Traits
- **Languages (MotM 5):** Speak, read, write Common and one other language.
- **Creature Type (MotM 34):** Humanoid.
- **Size (MotM 34):** Medium or Small (chosen at character creation).
- **Speed (MotM 34):** 30 ft. walking speed.
- **Claws (MotM 34):** Unarmed strikes deal 1d6+1 slashing damage. 1 Action.
- **Hold Breath (MotM 34):** Can hold breath for up to 1 hour.
- **Natural Armor (MotM 34):** Base AC 17 (Dex modifier doesn't apply). Cannot wear light/medium/heavy armor, but can use a shield normally.
- **Nature's Intuition (MotM 34):** Proficient in one of: Animal Handling, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival (chosen).
- **Shell Defense (MotM 34):** As an action, withdraw into shell: +4 AC, advantage on Str and Con saves. While in shell: prone, speed 0 (can't increase), disadvantage on Dex saves, can't take reactions; only action available is Bonus Action to emerge.
