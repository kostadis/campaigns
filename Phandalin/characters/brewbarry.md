### Homebrew Feature: The Subconscious Rage (Barbarian Physiological Mutation, Path of the Giant)
The lingering trauma of Brewbarry's Uthgardt exile has violently mutated his biology. His subconscious realized he was not merely careless in the past — he was surgically neutralized by unnatural trickery. His body has built a permanent biological firewall against "Seelie condescension" and any magic that attempts to bypass his physical might.

- **The Phantom Wings (Narrative Manifestation):** Paranoia bleeds into auditory processing — whenever magic is cast nearby, he hears phantom, high-pitched fairy-wing buzzing in his skull.
- **Agentic Resonance (The Primal Radar):** Permanent +4 bonus to Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Insight) checks made specifically to spot unseen or flying threats, or to inspect food/drink for magical tampering. +4 bonus to Wisdom saving throws to resist Enchantment magic or the Charmed condition.
- **Cosmic Static (The Dietary Rejection):** Mutated physiology completely rejects magically conjured sustenance — spells like Goodberry or Create Food and Water provide zero nourishment/healing; tastes of ash and triggers exile memories. Must rely on hunted or mundane rations.
- **Cosmic Static (The Biological Audit):** Body treats all druidic and fey magic as a disguised sedative. To willingly accept a beneficial spell (e.g., Cure Wounds, Haste) from a druid or fey, must expend Reaction to suppress the anti-magic firewall. If Reaction already spent, body instinctively rejects the magic; caster must succeed on a Spellcasting Ability Check against passive Constitution score (10 + Con modifier) to force the magic through.
- **The Uthgardt Rejection (Threshold Override):** Permanently immune to magical sleep, whether raging or not.

Four primal instincts that strengthen the mutation when acted upon:
1. **Trust the Mud, Reject the Spark** — distrust fey/nature magic; rely on mundane resilience.
2. **Perfection is a Predator** — flawless synchronization, perfect manners, or emotionless logic signal a masked predator; trust gut over words.
3. **The Sky is for Cowards** — the sky is the domain of tricksters and snipers; forcing flying/elusive enemies to fight on the ground asserts primal dominance.
4. **The Hammer is the Only Truth** — bypassing performative diplomacy/bureaucracy with physical presence reminds the world that raw strength cannot be lied to.

## Notes

### Character Backstory
Brewbarry Rootsmasher Ogolakanu was born on a windscoured cliffside where most children learned to walk by clinging to boulders. Brewbarry learned by headbutting them. From the start, he treated every moment as a contest — not because he wanted to win, but because he couldn't imagine life any other way. If someone climbed a ridge, he climbed two. If someone carried a goat, he carried the goat and the herder. If someone drank a mug of ale, he drank the keg.

His clan admired his drive… at first. But Brewbarry's competitiveness didn't stop at trials or hunts. It seeped into everything. Eating became a race. Sleeping became a challenge ("I bet I can fall asleep faster than you"). Even meditation — a sacred Goliath practice — became a competition he loudly declared himself champion of.

And then there was the debauchery.

Brewbarry believed that victory meant nothing without celebration, and celebration meant nothing without excess. He threw feasts so wild that the echoes startled mountain goats for days. He invented drinking games that left even seasoned warriors crawling. He once convinced the entire hunting party to skinnydip in a freezing glacial lake "to prove who had the strongest spirit." Half the tribe caught colds. Brewbarry declared himself the winner.

The elders eventually realized that Brewbarry's spirit was too big, too loud, and far too intoxicated for the quiet discipline of the peaks. They didn't exile him — they simply suggested, very firmly, that the world below offered "greater challenges and sturdier taverns."

Brewbarry took this as a blessing.

Now he roams the lowlands with a simple creed:
- Life is a competition.
- Glory is earned through effort.
- And every victory deserves a celebration so wild it becomes legend.

He measures friendships in shared battles and shared hangovers. He respects anyone who can outfight him, outdrink him, or outparty him. And he seeks challenges not for pride, but because... *(text cuts off on sheet)*
