- **Channel Divinity: Balm of Peace** (TCoE, 1 Action): Move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks; when you move within 5 ft. of another creature during this action, restore 2d6+4 hit points to that creature (once per creature per use).

- **Protective Bond** (TCoE, 1 Reaction): When a creature affected by your Emboldening Bond is about to take damage, a second bonded creature within 30 ft. can use its reaction to teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 ft. of the first creature and take all the damage instead.
- **Blessed Strikes — Divine Strike** (PHB-2024 p.71, 1 Action): Once on each of your turns when you hit with a weapon attack, you can cause it to deal an extra 1d8 Necrotic or Radiant damage (your choice).
