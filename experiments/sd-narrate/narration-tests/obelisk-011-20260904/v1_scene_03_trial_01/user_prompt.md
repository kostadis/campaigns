## Party Overview

**Current location:** **Phandalin**, long-rested — a free night at the Stonehill Inn from Toblen after the Redbrands fell. First proper rest since the crypts. Business in town is done: Sildar paid, Garaele commissioned, Halia rebuffed.

**What they just did:** Freed the Dendrars and walked them home; robbed the crevasse without looking for the body they'd promised to find; **lost their first fight** to four bugbears; and escaped by pouring seven pounds of oil across a hallway and lighting it. On the way out a nothic named itself and told Veyra her missing teacher is alive. Then Zenvon, four sessions into lying professionally, told the truth about the black stone in his pocket.

**Active quests / threads:**
- **Sildar Hallwinter's contract** — **delivered.** The Spider's letter was presented in Ch. 8, Iarno's treason proved, and the full **250 gp** paid. New standing bounty on Iarno: **200 gp alive, 100 dead, 50 if he cannot be raised.** Sildar has also offered **500 gp to locate Cragmaw Castle** — a number the party has not yet heard, because nobody asked.
- **Cragmaw Castle** — **named** by the bugbear Nosk in Ch. 8 as the Black Spider's location. The party believes it. They have no route there and nobody in Phandalin knows the way.

- **The three loose bugbears** — wounded, at large, and the only creatures within reach who know the way to Cragmaw Castle. The party thinks they are still behind a locked door.
- **Wyvern Tor** — 120 gp from Harbin Wester, negotiated up by Pip. Accepted, not started.
- **Old Owl Well** — Boots of Striding and Springing from Daran Edermath. Accepted, not started.
- **Return the Lionshield Coster goods** to Linene Graywind. Carried since Ch. 3, still undelivered.
- **Gundren Rockseeker** — still missing, presumed at Cragmaw Castle, location unknown.
- **Halia Thornton** — visited in Ch. 8. She offered **100 gp** for Glasstaff's correspondence and Zenvon refused, telling her the information was worth far more. The party left convinced she knows more than she says. *(They are right, and wrong about why.)*
- **Mirna Dendrar's heirloom** — an emerald necklace behind the storage shelves of the family shop in the **southeast parcel of Thundertree**. Offered freely. Pip wants it for armour.

## Characters

### Zenvon Forepot
- **Class/Level:** **Rogue 3 — Arcane Trickster** | **Species:** Halfling | **Player:** Nikhil Reddy
- **New in Ch. 8:** the Arcane Trickster archetype — *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, and *Thunderwave* (save DC 12). All three carried the session: *Minor Illusion* faked Glasstaff at work and drew the nothic off the crevasse; *Mage Hand* opened the chest at range, eased the barracks door, and lit the oil; *Thunderwave* opened the bugbear fight while deliberately sparing Droop.
- **Traits & motivation:** Started as the scout. Has become the party's face. Two of the last three sessions were won by his mouth rather than his blades — talking down a Redbrand ambush he'd walked into alone, mimicking a bandit's voice through a door to send a bugbear away, and, in Ch. 7, striding into a room of hostile bandits in a red cloak and convincing them their own man was the traitor. He is also the party's executioner: he read Wick as a man who'd take gold and vanish, and killed him rather than let him go.
- **Relationships:** Leads. Pip backs his plays with violence when the talking stops (the knife to Rondar's throat); Maela pushes back when he'd rather not stop for the helpless, and he relents. Pulled Pip out of a twenty-foot pit on his whip.
- **Items of significance:** **Ornate short sword** from the Redbrand armory (semi-precious stones — he traded up on looks, and Maela rolled her eyes); Scimitar (Nick), Dagger, Javelin, **handaxe**; thieves' tools, used to free the captives; a **whip**, pressed into service as a rope and used to haul Pip out of the pit trap (the GM offered rope; Zenvon's player corrected him — Zenvon carries no rope). **The black stone he pocketed at the cart, where they first saw the black obelisk — which in Ch. 8, after four sessions of silence, he finally produced and showed to Veyra.**
- **Recent notable actions:** Negotiated Sildar up to 100 gp upfront after refusing an appeal to loyalty; tailed the Redbrand spotter into the ambush and opened with *"Are you not going to introduce me to your friends?"*; wounded Iarno in the escape passage; dropped to **4 HP** against a skeleton and **was never healed** — he withdrew and finished the crypt fight there; ran the barracks deception; executed Wick. **In Ch. 8:** talked the barricaded Dendrars out from behind their own door; failed a Deception roll to Mirna about Thel and handed the telling to Maela rather than lie again; rage-baited Nosk into naming Cragmaw Castle; took 14 from a morning star and dropped to 6; drank a potion mid-fight; and, when the party was beaten, poured seven pounds of oil across a hallway and lit it with *Mage Hand*. **Told Veyra about the stone.**

## Party Dynamics

The division of labor from Ch. 3 still holds — Maela and Veyra carry light and firepower, Zenvon scouts and finishes, Pip sets up and absorbs — but the last four sessions rewired how the party actually wins. **They now win by lying.** The red cloaks, the mimicked voice, the false accusation in the barracks: the fights they chose were short because the setup was good, and Zenvon has grown into a face rather than just a knife.

**Chapter 8 is where that stopped working.** The same red cloaks that fooled a room of bandits got laughed at by a bugbear — *"leave it to a Redbrand to think he's in charge"* — and the con that followed worked *too* well, extracting Cragmaw Castle and a fight the party could not win in the same breath. They ran. They got out on improvisation rather than deception: a door held shut, seven pounds of oil, and a cantrip used as a match. It is the first evidence this party has that talking is not a universal solvent, and nobody has said that out loud either.

The moral center has moved too, and not in one direction. Maela is the one who insists on saving captives *and* the one who executed a prisoner. Zenvon freed a boy from a cage with his own tools in the same hour he killed a man who had surrendered. Pip made the tactical case for feeding Rondar to a monster. This is a party that has decided it is good and has started doing things that don't match, and nobody has said so.

Both secrets came out in Ch. 8. Veyra asked Pip what his shard felt like to hold, and Zenvon — unprompted, and after four sessions of saying nothing — produced his own stone and let her point the crystal at it. It flared brighter.
