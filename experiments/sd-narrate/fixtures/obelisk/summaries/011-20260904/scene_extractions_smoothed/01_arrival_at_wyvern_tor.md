# Arrival at Wyvern Tor

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party arrives at the rugged ridge of Wyvern Tor and spots a lone bugbear sentry guarding a cave entrance.
- The party ascends a rugged ridge on the hill’s lower slopes, following the faint smell of smoke.
- The cave mouth lies about fifty yards away at the bottom of a ravine; the sentry watches from beside a boulder approximately twenty yards outside it.
- Zenvon confirms that the guard is within shortbow range, then retreats to discuss an approach with Pip, Sister Maela, and Veyra.
- Pip recommends finding out what sort of guard they face before shooting.
- Zenvon rolls 25 on Stealth and approaches unseen. The bugbear has a milky, blind eye, leans on a salvaged pike, and looks bored with his assignment.
- Zenvon cannot see into the cave from his scouting position, but smells cookfire smoke.
- The companions attempt to move closer. Their group stealth fails, and the sentry begins investigating southward, advancing roughly ten feet with his pike ready.
- Zenvon uses Mage Hand to throw stones from another direction and divert the guard’s attention.
- Zenvon initially hopes the disturbance will bring out one or two more enemies. Pip warns that an unknown number could respond and recommends killing the sentry quietly.
- The DM explains the tactical importance of the roughly sixty feet separating Pip from the guard: without a closer approach, Pip cannot contribute an attack in the first round.
- An Insight attempt reveals nothing further about the sentry’s behavior.
