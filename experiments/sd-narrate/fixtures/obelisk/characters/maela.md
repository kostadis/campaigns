# Sister Maela Dawnforge

**Sidekick** — dwarf cleric, lawful good. Healer, protector, practical advisor for Zenvon Forepot.
*Extracted from `notes/open.md` (2026-06-12). Campaign-original character, not from the module. Lineage confirmed dwarf by Kostadis, 2026-06-12 (open.md's block omitted it; Dwarven Resilience, darkvision, and Dwarvish already implied it).*

## Why She's Going to Phandalin

Maela is traveling to Phandalin after receiving a troubling letter from an old dwarven contact who works claims near the Triboar Trail. The letter mentioned miners finding "black stone not born from any mountain" and hearing voices underground.

Maela believes something ancient is stirring beneath the frontier town. She also knows that if Gundren Rockseeker is involved in any rediscovery of old dwarven sites, trouble is probably close behind.

## First Impressions

- **Protector's Enclave glimpse:** speaking with a priest or city official about strange reports from miners near Phandalin. "If miners are hearing voices underground, you don't file that under superstition. You send someone with a lantern and sense."
- **Anchorhook Yard:** has paid for passage as far as Phandalin.
- **On the road:** sits beside the driver, mending a torn cloak and quietly judging everyone's posture.

## Table Voice

- "Ignore him when needed. Feed him when useful. Same as most road companions." (re: Pip)
- "If Gundren and Sildar live, time matters."
- (Stonehill Inn prep) "Fear has settled here. Not passing fear. Lived-in fear."

## Bonds to Zenvon

Maela respects the rogue if the rogue:
- Protects civilians
- Takes practical precautions
- Shows courage without being reckless
- Treats wounds and burials seriously

## Obelisk Hook

- **First Road Omen:** when the black stone cracked under the wagon wheel, Maela felt a brief chill in her holy symbol.
- **After the ambush:** of the goblin's black stone charm, she says the stone feels "wrong in the hand, like it remembers being part of something larger."
- **Obelisk Unease** (trait, below) is the mechanical face of this connection.

## Combat Role

Casts bless if the fight looks dangerous, keeps cure wounds for emergencies, and tries to keep the rogue standing. Opens with bless if the fight looks serious; uses sacred flame or warhammer; saves cure wounds for when someone drops low. She is brave, but not reckless.

## Stat Block (3rd-level sidekick)

*Medium humanoid (dwarf), lawful good*

**Armor Class** 16 (scale mail, shield)
**Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
**Speed** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 12 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +4
**Skills** Insight +4, Medicine +4, Religion +2
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
**Languages** Common, Dwarvish
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

***Dwarven Resilience.*** Maela has advantage on saving throws against poison, and she has resistance to poison damage.

***Sacred Medic.*** When Maela restores hit points to a creature with a spell, that creature also gains 1 temporary hit point.

***Obelisk Unease.*** Maela can feel when stonework or relics have been corrupted by aberrant or psionic influence. This does not identify the magic, but it tells her something is spiritually wrong.

***Consecrating Touch.*** When Maela restores hit points to a creature with a spell, she can also end one charm, fear, or psionic-domination effect on that creature imposed by an Aberration. Once she uses this trait, she cannot use it again until she finishes a short or long rest.

***Spellcasting.*** Maela's spellcasting ability is Wisdom. Spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks.

- Cantrips: guidance, sacred flame, spare the dying
- 1st level (3 slots): bless, cure wounds, detect evil and good, sanctuary

### Actions

***Warhammer.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage, or 7 (1d10 + 2) bludgeoning damage if used with two hands.

***Sacred Flame.*** One creature Maela can see within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 4 (1d8) radiant damage.

## Session History

*Sources: `summaries/00N/`. Sessions 2–3 are Zoom summaries with no recorded play date.*

**Session 1 (2026-05-22, "The Threshold Awaits"):** Badly wounded in the Cragmaw ambush but survived. Forthright — unwilling to let Elmina Barthen remain ignorant of Gundren and Sildar's capture, and told her the truth.

**Session 2 (date unrecorded — into the Cragmaw cave):** Her stealth roll of 3 gave the party away and started the goblin fight. Tried Sacred Flame on the giant snake; it made the save. Kept Pip standing with Cure Wounds for 6 while the goblins concentrated on him, then finished the goblin boss Yimek with her mace for 6 bludgeoning.

**Session 3 (date unrecorded — clearing the hideout):** Carried the light that finally cancelled the party's darkness disadvantage. Caught Zenvon at the edge of death after the wolves mauled him and healed him to a full 16 including temp HP. Took serious damage from Klarg. When the surviving wolves were beaten, she used Animal Handling to subdue them rather than kill them.

**Session 4 (2026-06-17, "Ambush in Phandalin: The Redbrand Reckoning"):** Rolled her eyes at Veyra's flaring crystal over breakfast. Took first blood in the Redbrand street ambush — a shortsword to the ribs — and was badly wounded as her own strike went wide. The session ended mid-fight with her still bleeding.

**Session 5 (2026-07-03, "A Nothic to Worry About"):** Opened the street fight with a punishing warhammer blow, then cornered the last Redbrand, who surrendered rather than face her. During the interrogation her Insight caught Rondar holding something back — the read that led Pip to draw a knife.

**Session 6 (2026-07-16, "It's Nothing Personal, You See"):** Translated the Nothic's demands for the party — her Undercommon made the negotiation possible at all. Then delivered the killing blow to Rondar herself, looking him in the eye: "It's nothing personal, you see." Pulled Urman's journal off a shelf in the wizard's workshop and read it in Dwarvish — the Lost Mine of Phandelver, the Forge of Spells, and Lightbringer, the mace the priests of Lathander commissioned and lost with Wave Echo Cave. Called down Sacred Flame on the fleeing Iarno; he made the save. Named the crypt skeletons as undead, and rolled her eyes at Zenvon and Pip shopping the armory for prettier weapons.

**Session 7 (2026-07-18, "Chapter 07 Deception"):** Raised her holy symbol and turned the crypt skeletons, scattering several of them out of the fight — "Of course it's good work. That's who I am." Healed Zenvon after a skeleton left him on 4 hit points. Heard the crying through the northeastern door and made the call the party's rogue wasn't going to make on his own: "Boss, I think we should help the helpless. We're the good guys, remember?" In the pens she hammered one ruffian and incinerated another with Sacred Flame, then freed the captive women while Zenvon worked the locks. Healed Pip after his fall into the pit.
