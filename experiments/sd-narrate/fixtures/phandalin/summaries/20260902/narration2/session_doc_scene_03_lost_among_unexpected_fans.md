### Vukradin

The stone falls quiet in my hand.

I still know about the crate. I know about the little brass collector, Lim, the ledge, the need to find where the delivery goes. I can recite the entire purpose of coming down here. Yet when I consider returning to that tunnel, my attention slides off it. Even lifting my foot in that direction seems tiresome.

I take another passage.

Then there are men in front of me.

One stands ahead of the others and looks at me as though I have entered through his cupboard.

"How the hell did you get over here?"

"I don't know."

I look around.

"Where am I?"

The obvious explanation for being here is that I am no longer over there. It is not a helpful explanation. His face gives me very little to work with.

"Love it."

I cannot decide what these men want. I can, however, explain my own position.

"But I don't know what I'm doing here."

I leave out the collector. Getting lost is true enough; the cause of it does not need to become the first subject I discuss with strangers underground.

"Yeah, there you go. I'm lost."

"I don't know what… I don't know how I got here, I just…"

"Yeah. Wrong turn."

The leader licks his finger. He is still looking at me.

"Well, this is my territory."

"This is your territory."

"Yes, this is my territory. Wow."

"Okay."

I give that the consideration it deserves.

"Did you want me to go?"

He wants to know why I am in his territory. I am beginning to understand how a conversation can become trapped in a tunnel of its own.

"Oh, I'm sorry that I got lost. I'm happy to leave. Could you point me in the direction of Lim's?"

"Cafe, that's… that's where I want to be headed."

His attention sharpens at the name.

"Yeah."

"Huh."

"We're friends."

That does not improve his expression. I stop trying to interpret it.

"Okay."

Then he recognizes me. I see the change travel across his face, and his question turns from my presence in the tunnel to my singing.

"Exactly. And Lim's a fan."

"What are you doing down here, man?"

"I told you, I got lost!"

He studies my face again. Beside him, another man begins smiling.

"This is… is this really Vukradin?"

"Do you guys want autographs?"

They do. The answer is immediate and considerably easier to understand than territorial rights.

"Sure, we can do some autographs, but then I gotta be going."

I gesture down the passage.

"Point me in the right direction."

"You know what, we're gonna do one better, we're gonna take you there."

"Alright, sounds good."

"We don't want you to get lost again, Mr. Vukradin."

"Yeah, exactly."

"There you go."

"Lim will be thrilled."

The leader, Rsolk, pauses over a warning. These tunnels are dangerous.

"Is it?"

"Yeah, it's, it's, it's,"

"Yeah, it's dangerous in your territory?"

I look at the men with him. He has people, an established area, a clear interest in who enters it. Surely that includes keeping those people safe.

"I would think you'd keep it very safe. You seem like a safe individual. I'm sure you keep the place shipshape."

"Mr. Vukradin, I agree with you."

"Fantastic."

He continues.

"But I have to tell you why I was out here. I was concerned when I saw you, and I'm very glad I recognized you. One of my guys tells me you have a deal with a friend of mine."

"At the Common Chord?"

"No, no, at the Toe mine."

"Mountain's Toe Mine. Oh, yeah…"

"Zeleen…"

"Oh, I didn't know you knew him."

"Oh, yeah, he… well, you know, knows that."

"Yeah, we spent some time in Phandalin, yeah, yeah."

"Yeah, he said that he had a business relationship with you, I said that he was full of shit."

"No, no, that's true, that's true. Yeah, they haven't produced very much yet, but they say they're working hard."

Rsolk laughs.

"Yeah."

"That's how I feel about it, too."

He laughs again. At least someone finds the mine's production easier to bear.

"Look, I manage the tunnels here."

"Oh, you're the tunnel manager."

"Yeah, a tunnel manager."

"So this is your territory, and you have all the tunnels."

"Not all the tunnels, but the tunnels in this area."

"The tunnels in this area, interesting, okay."

"Two months ago, things started coming up the floodline."

"Floodline."

He describes a passage that floods regularly. I try to place it among the directions I already fail to understand.

"I don't know anything about tunnels, man, I'm a musician."

"I've been having this problem with the tunnels. I've lost men, so we don't go there anymore."

There it is. People cannot use their own passage because something is killing them, and we have companions who can do something about dangerous creatures. Brewbarry, Soma, Valphine. We are very good at this when we agree on what needs doing.

"Do you need help with that? My friends and I have some abilities, and we're looking for ways to make money. Maybe we can help you."

Rsolk needs very little convincing. I can see that much now.

"One of the guys who escaped said there was some kind of creature they'd never seen before."

"Oh."

"It looked like…"

"Creature stuff."

"It looked like a spider, but it had all the wrong colors."

"Okay."

The webbing is wrong as well. His description brings back the sight of strands that do not belong where they hang, the unpleasant resistance of trying to make sense of them.

"Yeah, we have experience with that."

I do not need to take anyone's belongings. I do not need to stand over a body and decide that proximity grants ownership. There is a dangerous passage, people need help, and we can agree on payment for giving it.

Rsolk has a request of his own.

"Mr. Vukradin, I know this is too much to ask, but could you get me some tickets to the Common Chord?"

"I can ask."

The room is expensive even down here.

"we have kind of an arm's length relationship with the Common Chord, but I will ask for you."

"That's all I could ask for."

"Alright, let's do some autographs, and then we gotta be on our way."

I sign for them. Rsolk supplies what we need to find him and the troublesome passage again, then his men lead me toward ground I might recognize.

This time I stay close enough to hear their feet.
