### The damage asymmetry — and why it's the tell

**Four Arcane Bursts at 27 average = ~108 per round**, at 150 ft, costing
nothing. He put Zalthir down **while rolling two natural 1s.** At full output he
can drop a second PC next round.

**He won't.** Watch what he does with those four attacks:

- ⭐ **One Arcane Burst kills the surviving thug outright** (32 HP, already 2
  down — 27 average erases it). Play it as a shot "at the party" that goes wide.
  **That is the witness problem solved**, and it costs him a quarter of his
  output.
- The remaining three go wide, spread thin, or into cover. **A man with 108
  damage a round who is not killing anyone is the loudest tell in the room.**

⭐ **And the unused `dimension door` scroll is the proof.** Scrolls need no slots.
He has had a working exit in his pocket this entire fight. Free, no roll.

| Delta | Base | Here |
|---|---|---|

> ⭐ **RULING — why *teleport* is gone.** You ruled A'lai's escape
> **earnable-against.** The base archmage carries a 7th-level *teleport*, which
> would make every denial vector pointless — take the scroll, he teleports
> anyway. **Strip the 7th-level slot and the scroll becomes his only ranged
> exit.** That is what makes the ruling real.
>
> If you'd rather keep *teleport* as a genuine second escape, that is playable
> — but then say so at the table when they take the scroll, so the players know
> they bought a delay and not a win. Don't let them think they closed the door
> and then find out they didn't.

### Traits

***Magic Resistance.*** A'lai has advantage on saving throws against spells and
other magical effects.

> **This is the trait that will annoy your table most.** Combined with damage
> resistance from spells, **Daz is fighting uphill all fight.** Say it out loud
> the first time a *fireball* half-lands — Daz's player should understand he is
> facing a specialist, not rolling badly.

### Actions

***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range
20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.

> He will essentially never use this. It is here so you know he has nothing
> else physical. **A'lai in melee is a man in a robe.**

### Reactions

***Counterspell (3rd level).*** ⭐ **The load-bearing ability in this fight.**
When a creature within 60 ft. casts a spell, A'lai attempts to interrupt it.
Against a 4th-level or higher spell, he makes an Intelligence check (**+9**)
against DC 10 + the spell's level.

> **Hold it every single round.** He would rather eat a round of damage than
> let Daz land a control spell. This is also the ability the party must beat to
> stop the scroll — see *Denying the exit*.

### Spellcasting

A'lai is an 18th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence
(**spell save DC 16**, **+9** to hit with spell attacks). He casts *disguise
self* and *invisibility* **at will**, and has the following wizard spells
prepared:

- **Cantrips (at will):** *fire bolt*, *light*, *mage hand*, *prestidigitation*,
  *shocking grasp*
- **1st (4 slots):** *detect magic*, *identify*, ***mage armor***\*, *magic missile*
- **2nd (3 slots):** *detect thoughts*, *mirror image*, *misty step*
- **3rd (3 slots):** ***counterspell***, *fly*, *lightning bolt*
- **4th (3 slots):** *banishment*, *fire shield*, ***stoneskin***\*
- **5th (3 slots):** *cone of cold*, *scrying*, ***wall of force***
- **6th (1 slot):** *globe of invulnerability*
- **7th, 8th, 9th:** — *(stripped)*

\* *Cast before combat. He has been standing at that door for hours; assume
both are up.*

> **Note on *stoneskin*:** it grants resistance to **nonmagical** B/P/S. Against
> this party it is nearly worthless — Dawnbringer deals radiant, Zalthir's
> unarmed strikes are magical at L6+, Grygum has a *mace +1*. **Let it be
> worthless.** A'lai prepared for the wrong enemy, and that is characterful:
> he expected Watchers with swords.
>
> **Note on *fire bolt* and *fire shield*:** fire has not worked inside
> Candlekeep in living memory. The wards are down. ⭐ **The first time he
> throws fire and it lands, let an Avowed make a small awful sound.**

## Tactics — he is not trying to win

**A'lai's goal is to be elsewhere in four rounds.** He has already lost: he is
one key short at a door he cannot open, and his courier is a corpse. Everything
he does is buying time for the scroll.

**Round 1.** *Wall of force* — to split the party, or to seal Thorin and
Dawnbringer out of the fight entirely. This is his best spell and he uses it
first. *Mirror image* is already up if he had a round's warning.

**Round 2+.** *Cone of cold* / *lightning bolt* for chip damage, aimed to
**catch two PCs minimum.** Re-*counterspell* Daz's big turns. He targets
**casters and mobility** and simply ignores Thorin — the dwarf is someone
else's problem and A'lai knows he cannot out-damage him.

**At HP ≤ 37 (50%):** he reads the ***scroll of dimension door***. Route: down
and out, to the lava chamber.

**If cornered before that:** he stops fighting entirely and spends his real
currency — see **THE RANSOM** in the session doc. He will not die fighting. He
is not that kind of man and never claimed to be.

### ⭐ Denying the exit — the three vectors, statted

The scroll is a **physical object in his vestment pocket.** Give the players
these handles explicitly if they start hunting for one.

| Vector | Mechanics | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Take the scroll** | Zalthir's Eldritch Claw Tattoo (15-ft reach) as an improvised disarm, or Glabbagool's acid on the pocket, or *mage hand*/Telekinetic. **Contested Dex (Sleight of Hand or attack roll) vs. his Dex +2.** He is not a fighter; this is very takeable. | A full action from the party's best striker at the worst moment. |
| **Counterspell the read** | Reading a scroll is casting. **Daz's *counterspell* works** — but A'lai holds his own for exactly this, so it becomes an Int check duel: **Daz's modifier vs. A'lai's +9.** | Daz's reaction and a 3rd-level slot. And he loses ties. |
| **Burst the window** | He fires at ≤ 37 HP. **A round that takes him from 38+ to 0 gives no window.** | Discipline. They must notice the 50% line and hold damage for it — which is exactly what this party does. |

> ⚠️ **He can also just fly.** *Fly* is on his 3rd-level list, the wards are
> down, and there is a window. **If the party takes the scroll, expect him to
> reach for this** — and that is *good*, because flying out is visible,
> pursuable, and slow. Denying the scroll should downgrade his exit, not delete
> it. **Tadric can chase a flying man. Tadric cannot chase a dimension door.**

## ASSASSIN ×1 (Scene 5, wave two)

*Medium humanoid, any non-good alignment.*

**AC** 15 (studded leather) · **HP** 78 (12d8 + 24) · **Speed** 30 ft.
**STR** 11 (+0) **DEX** 16 (+3) **CON** 14 (+2) **INT** 13 (+1) **WIS** 11 (+0) **CHA** 10 (+0)
**Saving Throws** DEX +6, INT +4
**Skills** Acrobatics +6, Deception +3, Perception +3, **Stealth +9**
**Damage Resistances** poison
**Senses** passive Perception 13
**Languages** Thieves' cant plus any two
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

***Assassinate.*** During its first turn, it has advantage on attack rolls
against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. ⭐ **Any hit against a surprised
creature is a critical hit.**

***Evasion.*** On a Dex save for half damage: no damage on a success, half on a
failure.

***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** Extra 14 (4d6) damage when it hits with advantage,
or when an ally is within 5 ft. of the target.

**Actions**
***Multiattack.*** Two shortsword attacks.
***Shortsword.*** *Melee:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing,
and the target makes a **DC 15 Constitution** save, taking 24 (7d6) poison
damage on a failure, half on a success.
***Light Crossbow.*** *Ranged:* +6 to hit, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3)
piercing, plus the same DC 15 Con save / 7d6 poison.

### ⚠️ THE ALPHA STRIKE — do this math before you run it

Against a **surprised** target, round one:

| Component | Damage |
|---|---|
| Shortsword, crit | 2d6 + 3 ≈ **10** |
| Sneak Attack, crit | 8d6 ≈ **28** |
| Poison, failed DC 15 Con | 7d6 ≈ **24** |
| **Total, one attack** | **≈ 62** |

**It gets two attacks.** Daz at L9 has roughly 50 HP. **This kills him
outright from full**, not to zero — through zero, into death saves or worse.

> ⭐ **STAGING RULING — do not run the assassin concurrent with A'lai.**
> A'lai (CR 9, 5,000 XP) + Assassin (CR 8, 3,900 XP) at once = **8,900 XP × 1.5
> multiplier = 13,350 adjusted.** Deadly for this party is 8,400. That is not a
> hard fight, that is a coin-flip on someone dying.
>
> **Run it as A'lai's exit cover instead.** It arrives round 4–5, at the moment
> A'lai reads the scroll or gets pinned — sequential pressure, not a simultaneous
> alpha. The party has already spent resources; the assassin is the reason they
> can't simply chase him. **Same danger, honest pacing.**
>
> **And surprise is the whole block.** If the party is in initiative and alert,
> Assassinate does nothing and this is a manageable CR 8. **Whether they get
> surprised is a Stealth +9 vs. their passive Perception call — make it, don't
> assume it.** Zalthir's passive is high; Daz's is not.

**How it fights:** picks **one** target — the softest caster it can reach — and
commits everything to killing that one person. It does not spread damage, does
not fight the tank, and **disengages the moment its target drops.** It is paid
per name, not per battle. If A'lai is already gone, it leaves too; there is
nothing here it wants.

**Treasure:** two doses of the poison used on the blades (DM's call — treat as
*Assassin's Blood* or a comparable ingested/injury poison), a **Zhentarim
courier's token** with no insignia but the right weight, and **a second copy of
the accessions manifest** — with three lines circled.
