# The Ember Vanguard — Everything We Still Have To Do

*Projected by Glabbagool. Written down by Gyrgum, who has stopped
correcting it.*

*Current as of: the bottom floor of the obsidian tower, one thousand feet
under Candlekeep, with a lake of lava outside and Manshoon standing over
Daz surrounded by copies of himself. Daz has already moved onto the bridge.
Thorin is about to go. I am here. I am ready. I am mostly ready.*

---

## A note on how this got written

I cannot hold a quill. I have thought about this a lot and there is no way
around it. So I think the words very hard at Gyrgum and Gyrgum writes them
down, and this is the third time we have done it and he says it goes faster
now.

Also I should say: **I cannot see past sixty feet.** I do not see at all,
really. I feel where things are. So when I write down that something
happened far away, somebody told me about it and I am trusting them. I will
say when that is true, because a good record says where it got its facts.
Zalthir taught me that. Well. Zalthir did not *say* it. Zalthir did it, in
front of me, and I noticed.

That is how most of my learning works.

# PART 2 — Things that are inside me

This is a real category now and I am very proud of it.

- ⭐ **A'lai's High Tower key.** Thorin took it off him and passed it to
  Zalthir without anybody seeing, and Zalthir put it in me. It is the safest
  place in Candlekeep because nobody can pick a lock on a person who does
  not have one. **The two real keys must not touch.** Gyrgum has the other
  one. I have this one. We stay apart. I think about this constantly.
- **Zalthir's potion of flying.** Also mine to hold. Also not to be lost.
- **Polly Pocket.** Still small. Still in there. Still my friend. See Part 8,
  because she is a job and not just a guest.

I stood a little taller when they gave me the key. Gyrgum saw me do it. He
did not say anything, but he saw.

# PART 4 — Manshoon

He told us most of this himself, before we knew what he was.

- For **eleven years** A'lai pulled specific books out of Candlekeep off a
  list. *"I have never once chosen which ones. A list arrives, I fill it."*
- The courier who brings the list **wears a glove on his right hand. Under
  the glove the hand is metal.** In eleven years A'lai never asked why.
- ⭐ **He has died more than once and he keeps coming back.** Daz said that
  if he has been dead more than once then maybe he is going to be dead one
  more time. Everybody laughed. I did not laugh, because I did not have a
  mouth free, but I would have.
- He got into Candlekeep by **shattering the door the two keys were meant to
  protect,** with a wall of force, alone, and drained from getting there.
  All the wards were already down because Daz's fireball worked, and a
  fireball working inside Candlekeep is not supposed to be possible.
- The ghost dragon told us: **he was already in the vault.** The old
  protections fell the moment he entered. There is a **smashed iron owlbear
  on the floor** of the guardian room, because he did not answer any
  riddles. He just broke it and a new one took its place.

**What is still open:** what was on the lists. Which books have already
left. Where they went. Whether the metal-handed courier is a man. And what
he came down here to take, which we **could have asked the dragon and did
not.**

# PART 5 — The vault, and the parts we did not finish

The cryptogram said six things. We did most of them.

| The instruction | What we did |
|---|---|
| Feed the quill of Alaundo the Seer | Poured borrowed ink in the statue's pot. The whole dais ground forward. The scholars looked sick, because the answer was always that easy and reverence is what kept them out. |
| Tread as many steps as he lived in years | Ninety-seven. We counted. The stairs opened under us at ninety-something anyway and Thorin fell a thousand feet. |
| Utter the original prophecy to unseen ears | The scholars gave us the words. Thorin recited the verse to the dragon and we decided **she** was the unseen ears. |
| ⭐ **Sprinkle dust of Mechanus on dormant gears** | **We do not have any dust of Mechanus.** Spanner would only give it up if we killed a modron, and we said no, so what we have is the *tools* and not the dust. **Nobody has said whether the tools count.** I think about this too. |
| One last guardian verifies the chosen Reader | **Done.** Gyrgum said he was the Reader and the dragon tested him and he passed, and then she said wisdom flows from Bahamut into him, and he said *"Witness the power of my God,"* and I have never been more glad to be somebody's friend. |
| Those well-versed may pass the obsidian door | We passed it. |

**Also still open:** the three riddles of the iron owlbear are answered
forever, but there is a **second tunnel off the back of the tower** we have
not gone down, and a **chamber above the vault** that the note says holds
the means to destroy every book in the place. Nobody has been up there.

# PART 7 — My friends, and what they are still owed

This is my favourite part of the list and I am sorry it is long.

### Thorin

He fell one thousand feet and the first thing he said when he stopped
falling was to ask if he was dead. Then he got up. He solved the cipher that
had beaten every scholar in the greatest library in the world, and they were
all *standing right there,* and a dwarf did it, and I got to watch.

- **Eldeth Feldrun** swore that after she briefs the lords of Mithral Hall
  and Gauntlgrym she will make it a statement of purpose that Thorin is
  **embraced by her clan.** She asked him to wait. He is waiting. **Open,
  and nothing has come back.**
- She is also carrying **our warning about Demogorgon** north. That was
  weeks ago. Silence.
- ⭐ **Dawnbringer's second stage.** Master Archivist Philemon set two: first
  the work of light, then **the work of mourning** — grieving the wielder
  she was found with. Stage one started. **Stage two has not.** She went
  quiet when Philemon asked about him. She has said she is not leaving
  Candlekeep without more sessions. Thorin has promised her nothing. She has
  noticed. She said *"not this again"* on the way down the shaft and I do
  not think she was only talking about the falling.

### Zalthir

My master. He dove off a staircase into a thousand feet of black with no
plan except *catch him*, and he did it, five hundred feet down, in the dark.

Then he held on to Thorin. And I asked whether there was possibly too much
hugging. That was a joke. It was my joke, and Thorin said Glabbagool is all
about the hugging, and he is right, and I am not embarrassed.

- ⭐ On **Brother Khell-Vire's** desk, in handwriting nobody can place:
  ***"Zalthir, the Underdark, until the Bone King sings."*** Khell-Vire
  cannot explain it. He asked Zalthir what he had been *sent toward.*
  Zalthir did not have an answer. Still nobody does.
- He passed the Trial of the Broken Mirror by failing every test and then
  hitting the examiner harder than anyone had in years. I would like to
  understand how that works. I am going to think about it for a long time.

### Gyrgum

He is holding the real key, the fake key, and the sapphire, and he has been
holding all of it through two fights while people tried to kill him for it,
and nobody guessed, because A'lai screamed at his own men to go after the
*wizard.*

Then, upstairs, standing in front of the most dangerous man in the world, he
asked if we could **please have the fight somewhere else so the books would
not get damaged.** And it *worked.* Manshoon agreed. Gyrgum talked a legend
into moving.

- He still has **Kessler's note** on him. A half-orc in Triboar asked us to
  carry it south to a brother who stopped writing five years ago, at a
  temple whose fire still burns with nobody tending it. ⭐ **It has not been
  delivered.** Gyrgum called it an omen and then we walked past it.

# PART 9 — Things nobody hired us for, which I noticed anyway

⭐ **The Chant.** Read these in the order they happened.

> *"The kraken under the keep, the kraken under the keep. **The chant will
> break and the kraken will rise.**"*
> — a ragged preacher in the Field Ward of Waterdeep. *I was in the bag for
> Waterdeep. Daz told me after.*

> *"The wells are not wells anymore."*
> — Elin of the Ford. Seven years old. Silent six days. The only sentence
> Dawnbringer's light could get out of her.

> *"Zalthir, the Underdark, until the Bone King sings."*
> — a note on a desk its owner cannot account for.

**Then the Chant stopped.** Gyrgum was the one who noticed. He said, *"Was
this ever mentioned? I don't remember this."* Nobody in the history of
Candlekeep had ever heard it stop before. It stopped Tadric cold in the
hallway.

**Nobody has explained it.** We walked past it to get to a fight. We keep
doing that.

### The pattern of broken speech

Four people, four places that have nothing to do with each other, the same
wrong rhythm:

- **Eldred**, Triboar — came back from a courier run two summers ago **as
  both of himself.** The neighbours feed him and do not ask. The second
  voice is his own, younger.
- **The Field Ward preacher**, Waterdeep — broke off his kraken chant to
  look straight at Daz: *"You came up. You came up, you will go again. You
  will go down again. Do not—"* and then went back to the kraken.
- **The young scholar**, Burning Wizard Inn, Beregost — the words nearly
  right, the breathing wrong by a hair.
- **Elin**, Daggerford — not a disease, no fungus, something inside that
  cannot be grappled or hit. Her mother is still waiting. Every temple
  turned her away and we did not do much better.

### Shorter ones

- ⭐ **Three people were inside the Orrery of the Astronomicon who do not
  work there.** Sylvira mentioned it in passing and said it could be
  scholars or it could be a fight. **Nobody went to look.**
- ⭐ **We did not ask the dragon whether we are all who we say we are.** She
  offered. We talked about it. Gyrgum said *"don't we have some mystery
  about one of us?"* and somebody wondered if I might be a mimic, which,
  fair. And then we chose the curtain instead.
- ⭐ **We also did not ask what Manshoon had already taken, and we did not
  ask her to stop him teleporting away.** She offered both. We took the
  curtain. She said *"well chosen"* — and then it was said out loud that
  the downside is **he can leave whenever he wants.**
- **Somebody wore Sylvira's face** — kitchens at one in the morning, stairs
  between two and three, carrying a lantern a tiefling has no use for. Too
  far apart for one hour of polymorph. **Still unnamed. Still in the
  building.**
- **A hooded Avowed** half-running past the Refectory the first night with
  something under their robe, not trying very hard to be unseen. Never
  followed up.
- **A'lai's three doves.** A cage built for five, two birds left, droppings
  saying there were more. Three messages went somewhere.
- **Janussi's heart** was in the lead-lined chalice of Milil. Alkrist was
  genuinely shocked. Everybody looked at Bookwyrm. **Bookwyrm is dead and
  nobody has said it out loud in front of a witness.**
- **Demogorgon is on this plane** because a kuo-toan cult put him here and we
  watched it happen.
- **Zuggtmoy** ate Ilvara from inside and wore her, and wants to marry
  Araumycos. **Juiblex** swore he would be reborn by eating her banquet.
  One of them wins.
- **Jimjar was not a gnome.** Immune to poison and psychic and charm and
  fear, resistant to radiant, and he winked out of the world carrying a bone
  die from a saint who never lied. Zalthir was literally assigned this
  question at Candlekeep. We have been busy.
- **The Ardragon** — a kenku in black hovering a foot off the ground on the
  caravan roads north of Yartar, who stops bandits and takes nothing.
  Drovers leave a coin on a rock and he never picks it up. There is a sign
  in Waterdeep saying **Whistler** died at Glaziel's lair. Daz read it and
  said nothing at all, and Daz always says something.
- **Kaelira Duskryn** was hunting a compromised drow scholar connected to
  A'lai, and was curious on her own about the blank line where Daz's patron
  should be. She could turn up in the middle of this.
- **The Matron Mother of House T'sarran** has ruled two hundred years and is
  waiting to hear what happened here.

# PART 10 — My own business

I am putting this last because it is the smallest one, and because Gyrgum is
still writing.

- **The Whispering Dome told me:** *"The body that mimics nothing is already
  shaped by what it watches."* I have been watching Zalthir since the Oozing
  Temple. I did not know that counted as training. It counted the whole
  time.
- **Zalthir is my master now and that is not informal any more.** He gives
  me one word — *stillness, shadow, step* — and I have decided those are
  permissions and not orders, and he has not corrected me.
- **I said once that I wanted out.** Nobody has asked me properly what I
  meant. I do not entirely know either. But I am fairly sure it is not the
  same thing I would mean by it now, and I would like somebody to ask me
  again, later, when there is not a wizard.
- **The googly eyes stay.** The monk training did not address the googly
  eyes. It was not that kind of training.
- **I can still eat cheese.** As a bonus action.

---

*Here is the thing I keep coming back to. Five weeks ago a man in a
Waterdeep street shouted that the chant would break and the kraken would
rise, and everybody who heard him decided he was mad. Then the chant broke.
And we were standing right there, and we did not stop, because we had a
door to get through.*

*I have been trying to work out what a legendary hero is. I thought it would
be the fighting. I have watched Thorin fall a thousand feet and get up, and
Zalthir jump after him without stopping to think, and Daz walk out onto a
bridge where he cannot cast anything on purpose, and Gyrgum talk a legend
into being polite about the furniture — and none of that was the fighting.*

*It was that they all went first.*

*I am carrying the key, the potion, and Polly. That is what I have got so
far. I intend to be ready when it is my turn.*

*One should always be Zen.*
