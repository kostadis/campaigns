# The Ember Vanguard — Open Business

*Kept by Grygum. I took notes. It's what I do.*

*Current as of: the High Tower lobby, mid-fight, A'lai Aivenmore still
standing. One raider dead, one soaked in acid. Zalthir is down. I am
holding the real key and the sapphire, which is a great deal to be holding
during a fight.*

*This is the ledger of what we still owe, what is still owed to us, and
what I have written down because nobody paid us to notice it. It is not the
murder file — that lives in the Janussi tracker, and everything in it
still stands. This is everything else.*

# PART 2 — The job we were actually hired to do

### Find out who killed Janussi

**Who asked:** Bookwyrm, First Reader. Conscripted us before dawn on Day 2
of Deadwinter, before the rest of the Avowed were awake.

**Terms, as agreed at the dodecagonal table:**
- **One book each**, copied at the **House of the Binder**, on successful conclusion.
- **Carte blanche** access — "an all-access library card." Thorin asked. They hated it. They agreed.
- Confidentiality papers signed first.
- Report back to Bookwyrm. Remove nothing from the room.

**Status: delivered. Unpaid.**

We gave them Alkrist, confessed under a zone of truth. Kalan took the credit
and his post back. Bookwyrm said we would be given a full opportunity to
speak before the naming ceremony.

Then Bookwyrm was murdered.

> *Our creditor is dead. The contract was never written down anywhere
> except in the confidentiality papers, which bind us and not them. Kalan
> said he would produce a book for us before we leave, and that it would
> come from the new head of Candlekeep. There is not currently a head of
> Candlekeep.*

**Also outstanding:** Kalan deputized us **Watchers of Candlekeep** and
promised **a small honorarium.** His words: *"it's truly an honorarium, I
can't promise you you'll be — why are we discussing honorariums with
Bookwyrm dead?"* Also unpaid. Also owed by a man we last saw out of breath
on a staircase.

**What we said we wanted instead of coin:** Daz floated leaning on A'lai for
Underdark intelligence rather than gold. A'lai is currently ten feet above
us. That option is open in a way it has never been before.

### The people we owe an answer to

- **Sylvira Savikas** does not know her son is dead. We killed him. Tadric
  said we must tell her and we all found reasons why it should be someone
  else. Daz said "we'll take care of it later." I said it might best be
  someone who knows her. It was passing the buck and everyone at the table
  knew it was passing the buck. **We still owe her that conversation.**
  She is bedridden and dying of the plague she caught from him.

- **Daral Yashenti** — cured, survived his midnight, and last seen being
  nearly killed in the halls. He now intends to found the academic study of
  sentient magical swords, which Dawnbringer considers the finest outcome of
  the entire affair.

- **Fembris Lancer** — our food taster, kept close because he was the only
  witness to the key changing hands. He also broke and gave us A'lai, which
  is the single fact that cracked this open. We have not thanked him or
  released him.

- **Polly Pocket** — the House T'sarran spy, still shrunk, still in the bag,

  handed to Candlekeep's guards. Kalan told us plainly that Candlekeep
  *holds* prisoners but does not *keep* them — they would eventually walk
  her to the gate and let her go with a warning. **We have deferred this
  decision every single time it has come up.** Glabbagool considers guarding
  her pre-monk training and reports that they have become great friends.

# PART 4 — Manshoon *(new, and larger than the murder)*

A'lai told us this himself, mid-fight, unprompted, after he stopped to
compliment our thoroughness.

- For **eleven years** he has been removing **specific books** from
  Candlekeep. *"I have never once chosen which ones. A list arrives, I fill it."*
- The courier who brings the list **wears a glove on his right hand, and
  under the glove the hand is metal.** In eleven years A'lai never worked up
  the nerve to ask why.
- The name: ⭐ **Manshoon.** *"You have not heard of it. That is not an
  accident. It is a career."*
- Daz and I both know the name from history. Zhentarim. Zhentil Keep. A
  wizard who **has died more than once and keeps coming back.**
- The raiders in this lobby are **bagging books off a written manifest right
  now.** The theft did not stop when A'lai got cornered. It is still running.

**What this means, as far as I can write it down:** the murder of Janussi
was an internal academic squabble over a chair. The book theft is not. The
book theft is an outside power that has had a hand inside the world's
greatest library for over a decade, and the murder investigation was the
thing that finally made the hand visible.

> *Daz, on hearing the man had died more than once: "Well, if he's been dead
> more than once, then maybe he's gonna be dead one more time." I did not
> correct him. I would prefer not to be the one to test it.*

**Open:** what was on the lists. Which books have left. Where they went.
Whether the metal-handed courier is a man or a thing. And whether A'lai's
three doves flew to the same address.

**Also Daz's:** the **headache**, worse the closer we got to Candlekeep, and
worse still since. My medicine check says there is a real if tenuous

stopped.

### Thorin — the clan, and the sword

- **Eldeth Feldrun** swore on her honour: after she has briefed the lords of
  **Mithral Hall and Gauntlgrym** on the Underdark, she will make it a
  statement of purpose that Thorin is **embraced by her clan.** She asked him
  not to follow until she has spoken. He told her he was taking it
  seriously and not as politeness. She nodded and shook his hand. **Open,
  and waiting on her.**
- She also carries our warning about **Demogorgon** north. That warning is
  now several weeks old and we have heard nothing back.
- **Dawnbringer's therapy.** Master Archivist Philemon set two stages:
  **first the work of light** — remembering daylight without needing to
  fight — **then the work of mourning**, grieving the wielder she was found
  with. Stage one is underway. **Stage two has not begun.** She fell silent
  when Philemon asked her about him. She has said plainly she is not
  leaving Candlekeep without more sessions, and that the first one was
  productive. Thorin has promised her nothing and delivered nothing, which
  she has mentioned.
- Thorin also wanted to find a **"sword whisperer."** Philemon may be it.
  Or may not.

### Zalthir — the note on the desk

- ⭐ On **Brother Khell-Vire's** desk, in a hand neither of them could
  place: ***"Zalthir, the Underdark, until the Bone King sings."***
  Khell-Vire cannot explain it. He asked Zalthir what he had been *sent
  toward*, not why he was sent. Zalthir had no answer. **Nobody has one.**
- He passed the Trial of the Broken Mirror by failing every formal test and
  then hitting the examiner harder than anyone had in years. Khell-Vire
  granted him the specialised technique regardless.
- **Glabbagool** answered the shadow monk at the Whispering Dome —
  *"The body that mimics nothing is already shaped by what it watches"* —
  and became something more than a passenger. He is now fused to Zalthir's
  arm by choice. What he is becoming, and what he meant when he said he
  wanted *out*, remain his own business and nobody has asked him properly.

### Grygum — mine

- **Kessler's note.** A half-orc at the Mountain's Mouth in Triboar, who
  keeps a small Bahamut shrine, asked us to carry a written note south to a
  brother at a temple. The brother stopped writing five years ago. Kessler
  has not seen him in three. **The temple fire still burns untended.** I
  called it an omen and said I would certainly visit. ⭐ **I have not
  delivered it. We walked past it to get here.** The note is still on me.
- I owe **Brother Vareth** a marked-up copy of the Stroudite papers he
  pressed on me. He has since published the fourth corner — the *Grygumite
  school*, the strong-weak hypothesis — with my name on the back of it. The
  entire keep emptied into the Oval Theater to argue about it, which is the
  only reason we got the hour that found the heart. I have not returned the
  papers.
- **Pizwog's tract** is still in my satchel, unread. Syncretic analysis of
  Sword Coast religions, internally inconsistent in ways that felt
  deliberate. I said I would read it in due time.
- **Fembris** wants acknowledgement in the monograph. So does **Elian**.
  Both were talked down to the acknowledgements section. Both will remember.

# PART 7 — Things nobody has hired us for, which I note anyway

⭐ **The chant.** Read this in order.

> *"The kraken under the keep, the kraken under the keep. **The chant will
> break and the kraken will rise.**"*
> — the ragged preacher, Field Ward, Waterdeep

> *"The wells are not wells anymore."*
> — Elin of the Ford, seven years old, silent six days, the only sentence
> Dawnbringer's light could pull out of her

> *"Zalthir, the Underdark, until the Bone King sings."*
> — a note on a desk that its owner cannot account for

**Today the chant broke.**

I do not know what that means. I am writing it down in the order it
happened so that when it matters, we will not have to remember.

**The pattern.** Four people, in four unconnected places, with the same
broken syncopation in their speech:

- **Eldred**, Triboar. Ran courier routes during the war, came back from his
  last run two summers ago **as both of himself.** The neighbours feed him
  and do not ask. Zalthir and I both heard it: the second voice is his own,
  younger.
- **The Field Ward preacher**, Waterdeep. Same rhythm. Broke his chant to
  look at Daz and say: *"You came up. You came up, you will go again. You
  will go down again. Do not—"* and then went back to the kraken.
- **The young scholar**, Burning Wizard Inn, Beregost. Words almost right,
  breathing wrong by a hair. Daz wrote the cadence down.
- **Elin**, Daggerford. Not a disease. No fungus. Something interior, that
  cannot be slapped or grappled. Her mother is still waiting. Every temple
  turned her away before us and we did not do much better.

The madness from below is on the surface. It arrived before we did.

# PART 8 — What I would do next, if anyone asked

1. **Do not let A'lai die.** He is the only living person who has said he
   knows who paid for Daz. He also just told us about Manshoon without
   being asked, which suggests he would rather be useful than dead.
2. **Do not let the second raider leave with the manifest.** The list is
   worth more than the man.
3. **Find Kalan.** Alive he is the succession, our honorarium, and a witness.
   Dead he is a message.
4. **Tell Sylvira.** Sooner is worse and later is worse still.
5. **Ask somebody about the chant.**

*Five weeks ago a man in Waterdeep told a street full of people that the
chant would break and the kraken would rise, and I filed him under the same
heading as the two-voiced courier and the girl who could not speak. Today
the chant broke. I do not think we were sent here to solve a murder. I think
the murder is how we were got in the door.*
