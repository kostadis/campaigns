# Out of the Abyss — module proper-noun inventory

Complete inventory of NPCs, locations, items, factions, planes, deities,
and concepts mined from the module bible:
`docs/background/Out of the Abyss.md` (9,357 lines, 17 chapters +
Appendices A–D).

This is the **source-material list**, not the homebrew. The user's OOTA
adaptation has diverged significantly from published canon — adapted
NPC roster, restructured Gauntlgrym chapter, additional Underdark
factions, and chapter-by-chapter homebrew live in:

- `docs/chapters/` — adapted bible (narrative wing)
- `docs/campaign_state.md`, `docs/world_state.md`, `docs/planning.md`
- `docs/npcs/` — adapted NPC dossiers
- `docs/gauntlgrym/` — faction-asset packets and scene scripts

For canon-as-played, search the `abyss` / `narrative` wings of the
mempalace. This file exists to answer "what was in the published book?"

## Demon Lords (Appendix D + Ch 17)

The eight demon lords drawn into the Underdark by the Rage of Demons.
Appendix D lists their stat blocks; per-realm and per-cult detail is
scattered through the chapters.

- **Demogorgon** — Prince of Demons; two-headed baboon-tentacle lord of madness; final foe of the campaign; lord of the 88th layer of the Abyss, the Gaping Maw; cult presence among Sloobludop ixitxachitl
- **Orcus** — Demon Prince of Undeath; ram-skull wand-wielder; lord of Thanatos; killed in the demon showdown by Demogorgon, drops the *Wand of Orcus*
- **Graz'zt** — the Dark Prince; six-fingered, ebon-skinned seducer; lord of Azzagrat (the Triple Realm — 45th, 46th, 47th layers of the Abyss); most likely demon lord to survive into a continuing campaign
- **Yeenoghu** — Beast of Butchery / Demon Prince of Gnolls; lord of the Death Dells; rampages through the Labyrinth with his Hunt; slays a goristro mid-chapter (the heart-component for the *dark heart*)
- **Baphomet** — Horned King / Prince of Beasts; minotaur demon lord of the Endless Maze (600th layer); spiral-shrine in the Labyrinth; cult of minotaurs
- **Juiblex** — the Faceless Lord; sentient ooze demon lord (no formal layer); ooze and slime cult; sends the "Flesh for the Faceless Lord!" voice at Velkynvelve and Sloobludop
- **Zuggtmoy** — Demon Queen of Fungi; lord of Shedaklah (the Slime Pits, 222nd layer, co-ruled with Juiblex); corrupts Neverlight Grove and weds Araumycos in the Fetid Wedding
- **Fraz-Urb'luu** — Demon Prince of Deception; trapped in a gem in Mantol-Derith; released in chapter 9; lord of Hollow's Heart; the source of Prince Derendil's false memories
