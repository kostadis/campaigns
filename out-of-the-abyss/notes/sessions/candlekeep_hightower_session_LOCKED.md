## ⭐ NEW EMOTIONAL CORE — the party killed Sylvira's son, eyes open

The prep assumed Moziqodo turns on A'lai (freed by Sylvira) or flees. Instead he's
**dead by the party's hand — and they knew he was Sylvira's abyss-born son when they
did it.** This wasn't mistaken identity; it was a **choice** (self-defense / to stop
the killings / to secure the key). **Sylvira is your default Vault-session ally.**
That alliance now sits on top of a killing the party owns and can't take back.

**What the party still does NOT know:** that **A'lai freed Moziqodo from Sylvira's
sanctuary and bound him** with the Iron Bands. So the son they killed was a **leashed
weapon**, not a free agent — and neither the party nor Sylvira knows it yet. That's
the reveal still in your pocket, and it now cuts **two ways:**
- **For the party** — they killed a *victim*, not just a monster. Recontextualizes
  the kill as tragedy; guilt goes live.
- **For Sylvira** — it hands her grief a target other than the party: **A'lai**, the
  architect. That's the lever that keeps her an ally instead of turning her on them.
- **The Iron Bands / hex-rune scar on the corpse are the evidence that unlocks it.**
  If the party didn't recover them, the leash stays hidden and they carry the kill
  raw — and Sylvira has only the party to blame.

**GM's-call fork — decide before the Vault session:**
- **When and how does Sylvira learn the party's hand did it** — and does the
  **A'lai-leash reveal land *before* she does** (softening it, shared enemy) or
  ***after*** (so the alliance nearly breaks before it's saved)?
- **Aim the grief at A'lai** (leash revealed → shared enemy → ally preserved, hatred
  sharpened) **vs. let it crack** (she blames the party first; they have to earn her
  back — higher-cost, higher-drama).
- **Don't skip the party's own reckoning:** the moment they learn he was leashed,
  *they* have to sit with having killed a bound victim. That's a scene, not a footnote.

## GM-only — why raiders can run around Candlekeep once the wards flex

Candlekeep's wards block **teleportation, flight, and fire damage** — that's the
entire security model; nobody imagined a Keeper of Secrets disabling it from the
inside. When they flex, the arcane glow over the High Tower visibly dissipates and
every Avowed knows instantly — but that same instant, flight/teleport/fire start
working for **everyone**, so Manshoon's strike team gets a violent head start while
Watchers scramble on foot. *Nice free beat:* someone casts a fire spell for the
first time in the chaos and is visibly startled it connects.

# BEAT 2 — A'lai On Screen + The Fight (~35–40 min combat)

> **A'lai is banked, not re-proven.** Alkrist named him the brains under *zone of
> truth* in Ch.57. This is the first time the party **sees him act** — the payoff,
> not a deduction. Calm, courteous, unbothered to be caught. Don't re-litigate whodunit.

He stands holding a **sapphire** (`instant summons`, the Manshoon signal). He meets
their eyes and **smashes it.**

> **A'lai:** "That was the signal. He is on his way. You have three minutes, or four,
> depending on traffic. I would not have chosen to meet you here, but you have been
> so *thorough.*"

> **Optional dagger — A'lai and the dead son.** If you want the leash reveal to
> *start* here rather than in the Vault, A'lai can be the one to plant it, coldly:
> *"You put down her son for me. I had rather hoped he'd put down more of you first
> — but the Iron Bands only buy so much loyalty."* Devastating, and it aims the party
> at him. Use only if you want the reckoning to begin under fire.

### A'lai — scaled CR 9 (Archmage chassis)
- **From MM Archmage**, with: **no 8th/9th-level slots · HP −25% · save DCs −1**.
  Pull the base block from 5etools (`get_monster`), then apply deltas — **do not run
  canonical numbers raw** (A'lai swings toward a TPK at this L8–9 table).
- Keeps the **`dimension door` scroll** exit (route: the lava chamber).
- **Tactics — he wants to delay 3–4 rounds, not win:** R1 `wall of force` to split
  the party / wall off a melee threat; `counterspell` held; `mirror image` up. R2+
  `cone of cold` / `lightning bolt` chip; re-`counterspell` Daz's big turns. **At
  HP ≤ 50% he reads the scroll and vanishes — empty-handed** (unless he took key #2
  off a downed/grappled PC).

> ### ⚠️ REBALANCE — A'lai is now SOLO (the Beast is dead)
> Moziqodo was the second body in this room; he's gone. A'lai alone is built to
> **delay and escape, not to win**, so the fight may run thin. Pick your lever:
> - **Let the clock carry it** — the 3-minute Manshoon countdown (smashed sapphire)
>   is the real pressure; A'lai just has to survive it. Cleanest.
> - **Add teeth** — seed 1–2 Manshoon raiders arriving early, or pull Manshoon's
>   Beat-4 arrival forward so the walls start closing during the fight.

### Trophy / evidence already in hand or on the field
- **Iron Bands of Bilarro** — were on Moziqodo's neck; **if the party looted the
  corpse, they hold them now.** The **hex-rune scar** is physical proof A'lai bound
  and aimed the Beast — **the evidence that both recontextualizes the party's kill
  and aims Sylvira's grief at A'lai.** (If they *didn't* loot it, the leash stays
  hidden — see the ⭐ core.)
- **A'lai's vestment** if he takes a heavy hit before escaping.
- ⛔ **No Gatewarden pin** — Kalan is alive; the pin stays on his chest.
- **Track: Abyssal Plague exposure.** If a PC took Moziqodo's bite, that plague is
  the same one killing Sylvira — a live thread straight into the Vault session.
