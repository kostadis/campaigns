# ISSUE — "O.K. Corral" is an out-of-world reference in the Ch65 standoff

**Filed:** 2026-08-27
**Severity:** Low — flavour, not canon. But it sits on the session's biggest
cliffhanger beat, and if it reaches narration unchanged the Manshoon duel opens
with a Wild West simile in a Faerûnian vault.
**Scope:** `summaries/20260824/scene_extractions/05_the_vault_of_dangerous_secrets_and_the_confrontation_with_manshoon.md`,
`summaries/20260824/session_summary.md`
**Raised by:** GM, during the stage-2 `/staged-consistency` review of Chapter 65
(finding #9, verdict *discuss*).

---

## The GM's note, verbatim

> It's an OK corral moment is a reference to the movie, we need to flag this to
> come up with some in-faerun equivalent.

## What was actually said at the table

The GM, framing the standoff as Manshoon comes down the stairs to the bridge:

> "So, he looks at you, and there's kind of like that, you know, **okay corral
> sort of**…"

and, after Daz finished the image ("to draw"):

> "Yeah, **okay, chorale moment**, right?"

— the second being the transcript's garble of the same phrase. The extraction
had compressed both into a clean quoted *"OK Corral moment"* that appears in
neither, which is what surfaced it.

## What was done

The false quote was replaced with the real one, and an inline ⚠️ flag added in
both files:

> ⚠️ **out-of-world reference; needs an in-Faerûn equivalent before this reaches
> narration.**

**That inline flag is not durable.** The scene extractions are regenerated by
`extract`, so the next pipeline run overwrites it. This file is the copy that
survives.

## What is still needed

A Faerûnian standoff image to use in narration, in place of Tombstone. The beat
it has to carry: two parties who have just agreed *not* to fight where they are,
walking down to a place where they will, both knowing it.

Candidates to consider (none chosen — this is the GM's call):

- A duelling convention from Waterdeep or Baldur's Gate the party would know.
- Something out of Candlekeep's own history — the Avowed have six centuries of
  scholarly grudges and at least one of them ended badly.
- A dragon-court formality, given Grygum is a cleric of Bahamut and the wyrm who
  granted the boon is watching the whole thing as a bound witness.

Whatever is chosen, the *table's* line stays as spoken in the verbatim record —
this is about the narrated prose only.
