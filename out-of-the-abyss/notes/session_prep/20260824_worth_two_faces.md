## The Win Condition (state plainly to yourself)

**The Book of Vile Darkness does not leave Candlekeep in Manshoon's hands, and
the party hears Echo 3 with the lights on.**

You picked *boss confrontation*, and you picked *the Echoes above all else*.
Those two compete for the same ninety minutes, and if you run the night in the
order the 0810 doc lays out — fight, then Echoes — **the Echoes get cut.** They
always get cut. So the spine of tonight is:

> ⭐ **Run the Echoes BEFORE initiative.** Manshoon genuinely does not care
> about the cabinet; that was true in the 0810 doc and it is still true. Let
> the party crack it, let all four Echoes play, let him *listen* — and start
> the fight when they turn toward the riddle door. The Echoes are banked, the
> fight has a clean starting gun, and nothing is hedged.

Secondary landings, in priority order:

1. **Echo 3 registers.** *"a giant-raised dwarf and his shadow-walker, his crow,
   and his bright-blade."* Four people at the table need to work out that it is
   them. This is the entire reason the Gauntlgrym arc exists.
2. **The metal hand comes home.** Two of them, and the numbers do not match.
3. **A'lai's "Baenre" stays unfalsified.** Nothing tonight confirms or denies it.
4. ⭐ **The Scholar.** Exposed, escaped, or never noticed — **all three are good
   endings.** What must not happen is you deciding for them which one it was.

## The Clever Play (so you know when to nudge)

1. **Work out why the stair opened.** The riddle is a procedure and they missed
   line 3. Somebody reads the cryptogram again and says *"utter the original
   prophecy to unseen ears"* out loud — and the stair stops being hostile.
2. **Make the dust before you need it.** Spanner's tools, one modron-machined
   gear, ten minutes. The party that makes it on the stairs is not the party
   scrambling for it on a bridge that will not extend.
3. ⭐ **Say Vydykyq.** Sylvira handed them a dragon's true name and they
   filed it as a riddle answer. It is a **key**. The "last guardian" is Miirym,
   she is standing between them and the obsidian doors, and a true name makes
   her answer honestly. This is the biggest unclaimed asset the party owns.
4. **Get verified before you go in.** A verified Reader can ask the vault for
   things. Manshoon forced the doors and was never verified — by the keep's own
   law he is a thief standing in a room that does not recognise him.
5. ⭐ **Echoes first, fight second.** If they do it in this order on their own,
   say nothing and be quietly delighted.
6. ⭐ **Ask Miirym whether the party is who they say they are.** They are
   standing in front of a creature whose literal job is verifying claims, they
   are holding her true name, and there are five of them where there used to be
   four. **This is the single sharpest available play tonight** and it costs one
   question.
7. **Fight him on the bridge, not in the stacks.** The antimagic curtain is the
   party's win button and it is bolted to the floor 200 ft from where he wants
   to stand. Getting him there is the tactical problem of the night.

> **The nudge, if they are grinding him badly in the stacks:** Miirym says it,
> not you. *"He is snow, Reader. Snow does not like the cold place on the
> bridge. Nothing false can hold its shape there."* One line, from an NPC who
> has every reason to know, and then shut up.

# Manshoon's Simulacra — the build

**Two bodies. That is the twist and it is the title of this document.**

Manshoon did not come to Candlekeep. He sent snow — and when the door mattered,
he sent **two.** A'lai spent eleven years learning he was not worth one face.
The Book was worth two.

> **This is the only structural change to the 0810 statblock.** Everything else
> — AC, Magic Resistance, save DC 17, +9 to hit, Metal Fist, Ward-Eater, the
> legendary actions, the tactics, the cut list — **is unchanged and already
> balanced at ≈8,400 XP for four L9 PCs.** Do not re-derive it. Open the 0810
> doc to the block and run it. The deltas are here and they are short.

### The deltas

| | **A — the one they have met** | **B — the one in the stacks** |
|---|---|---|
| **HP** | **95** (half of 190) | **95** |
| **AC** | **19** — *robe of the archmagi* + *staff of power* | **15** — `mage armor` only, no staff |
| **Where** | Third shelf from the far wall, browsing | Hidden in the stacks, still, since before dawn |
| **Which one** | Breached the High Tower door. The party has seen it | **Killed the Iron Owlbear** on the threshold |

- **Legendary Resistance: 3/day for the pair.** Not three each. Zalthir's
  Stunning Strike math is unchanged from the 0810 doc, and that math is the
  whole shape of the fight — **do not double it.**
- **Legendary actions: 2/round total, shared** while both are standing. Four a
  round is a different and much worse fight.
- **Spell slots: one shared pool**, `4 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 2` + **one 7th**.
  ⛔ **Simulacra can never regain slots — ever, by any means.** When the pool is
  dry it is dry, and B is the one holding the 7th. Say this out loud if a
  player asks; it is diegetic and it is the reason the fight is winnable.
- ⛔ **Retributive Strike stays cut.** Same reason as before.

### Why two, in-fiction (GM-only)

> Manshoon built two because he expected to lose one to the wards, and he was
> nearly right. He equipped the first properly — it had to break a door in
> front of witnesses. He did not bother equipping the second, because the second
> was never supposed to be seen. **He was economising on a job he did not think
> was hard.** That is the same arrogance the 0810 doc pins his death on.

### The snow rules

- **At 0 HP a simulacrum reverts to snow and melts.** No body, no blood, no
  waxy candle-corpse — that was the real-man version. A heap of wet snow going
  grey on a vault floor.
- ⭐ **The metal hand does not melt.** It sits in the slush, warm.
  **Two bodies, two hands, and the engraved Thorass numbers on the inner
  wrist-cups are not sequential.** Do not explain it. Do not let an NPC explain
  it. There are others, and the party can do that arithmetic themselves.
- **The robe and the staff do not melt either.** They were real. ⭐ **Settled
  2026-08-24: the robe drops functional, the staff drops with zero charges** —
  its charges were the High Tower door and the obsidian doors. See
  *Decisions §1*. ⛔ It does not *look* spent. Let somebody count.
- ⛔ **Neither body reforms.** I am not giving you a takeback mechanic. The
  two-phase shape comes from there being two of them, which is honest.

### ⭐ The antimagic curtain — the party's win button

The lava chamber's antimagic field is not scenery. Fix its extent now and do
not move it later: **a ten-foot curtain across the midpoint of the bridge**,
floor to ceiling, spanning the full width. Manshoon crossed it and reconstituted
on the far side — suppression is not destruction.

**A simulacrum inside the curtain is suppressed.** No spells, no Magic
Resistance, no legendary actions, no Ward-Eater self-heal. AC 15 flat, and it
weighs what a man-shaped pile of snow weighs.

- **This is a disarm, not a kill.** It walks out the other side intact.
- **The lava lake is the kill.** Curtain first, shove second.
- **Getting him there is the tactical problem.** He will not go near the bridge
  voluntarily — he fights in the stacks and he knows why. It takes setup:
  Zalthir's grapple into Daz's `telekinesis`, the Ember Grapple, Thorin shoving
  something that does not want to be shoved, or ⭐ **turning Ward-Pull against
  him** if a player is sharp enough to bait it.
- **If they pull it off, pay it in full.** No save, no contingency, no shimmer.
  Snow in lava is steam and a metal hand on the bridge deck.

> ⛔ **Do not let a lucky round-one shove end the night.** He is 200 ft from the
> curtain and he is not stupid. The curtain is a *plan*, not a lottery ticket.

### Tactics — deltas only

- **A opens exactly as the 0810 doc says:** `globe of invulnerability`, then
  talk. It does not open with violence, and it does not stop being polite.
- **B does not act on round 1.** It steps out of the stacks when A drops, or
  when the party turns for the riddle door — whichever comes first — and it is
  behind them when it does.
- ⭐ **If the party looks for a second presence, let them find it.** Daz has
  field-perception and `day_four` already says he senses the *not-quite-there*
  nature of a simulacrum. Zalthir's Watcher's Stillness works too. So does
  simply asking *"is anyone else in here?"* **Tell them the truth.** Two at
  once is a harder fight and a fair one; ambushing a party that asked the right
  question is neither.
- **B fights differently.** No staff, no robe, nothing to protect. It is the one
  that killed the owlbear and it fights like that — Metal Fist, grapple, and the
  7th-level `teleport` held to the last possible second.

### Exits

Same three triggers as the 0810 doc, applied **per body**, with one addition:

4. ⭐ **Both bodies at ≤30 HP** → the surviving one stops talking. Manshoon has
   been narrating through them all night; when he decides the night is a loss,
   **the voice simply goes.** The body keeps fighting on its last instruction
   and it is much worse at it. That is the tell that the party has actually won,
   and it costs one sentence.

# The Scholar ⭐

**The party hired a doppelganger, and the swap window is on tape.**

`summaries/20260817/scene_extractions/05_the_grave_of_alaundo_and_the_house_of_mechanus.md`,
lines 166–186 — three GM lines, spoken last week without meaning any of this:

> **GM:** *"I think it would be more amusing if you have the — so you know a guy."*
>
> **GM:** *"He's very excited about the opportunity to come with you."*
>
> ⭐ **GM:** *"And ask whether he can just take a moment to go get some — a few
> things in the knobs and nabs."*

**He left their sight. Alone. Between the House of Mechanus and the House of
Alaundo.** You do not have to invent the window; you already gave it to him.

And the recruitment itself is the other half of the joke, because it was
**Grygum's idea and Grygum's reasoning**:

> **Grygum:** *"No, no, a lot of these would have scorn for local history.
> It's not their specialty."*

The party went looking for a scholar in the one discipline that **nobody else
at Candlekeep can corroborate**, and then took the volunteer. That is not bad
play — it is *good* play, correctly reasoned, and it is exactly how this gets
you. ⛔ **Do not let anyone at the table feel stupid about it.** They optimised
for the riddle and the riddle optimised back.

> **He is unnamed on tape.** Nothing to violate, nothing to retcon. Name him
> tonight, in front of them, and make the name slightly too good: **Sivrin
> Belk, of the Hall of Momentous Deeds.** Local history, forty years of it,
> no publications anyone can place.

### ⛔ What he is, and what he is for

| | |
|---|---|
| **Working for** | **Manshoon's remnant cell** — the House of Alaundo ambush that never fired. ⛔ **Not** Baenre. A'lai's guess stays unfalsified and untouched, exactly as you asked |
| **Possibly** | one of Sylvira's **three figures in the Orrery**. Your call — the link is free and costs nothing if you never use it |
| **Wants** | ⭐ **not** to fight, **not** to steal a key, and **not** to kill anyone. It wants to **be standing next to the Book when the door opens** |
| **Its cover job** | riddle line 6 — *"those well-versed in Candlekeep's lore."* ⭐ **The fake is the credential.** The party's plan for the last door is to point at him |
| **Read Thoughts** | it has been reading them since the grove. It knows the cryptogram, the plan, the marching order, and which of them Grygum will heal first |

### The tells — plant at least two before Scene 3

Cheap, deniable, and none of them conclusive:

- **He knows the ninety-seven.** He says it before the grave is mentioned.
  If challenged: *"You said it on the way over."* They did not.
- **He does not touch the quill.** Every scholar in Candlekeep touches Alaundo's
  quill for luck; the summary makes a point of the tip being worn smooth. He
  walks past it. A local historian would not.
- **He is never the last one through a door**, and he is never the first.
- ⭐ **Zalthir's Watcher's Stillness.** Gabe plays a character defined by
  watching and not speaking. **Give him the tell and give it to him alone**, and
  let him decide what to do with it — that is precisely the kind of complex,
  quiet problem he engages with. Do not repeat it to the table.
- **Thorin's Know Your Enemy** is a bonus action and it reads immunities and
  resistances. ⛔ **If Joe uses it on him, it works.** Doppelganger resistances
  are not a human scholar's. **Do not fudge that** — the ability exists.

### 🟣 When does it turn? — three branches, and you do not have to choose now

**1. ⭐ Miirym reveals it — but only if somebody asks.** (Recommended.)
She is *"the last guardian of knowledge who verifies the chosen Reader's
claims."* Verification is her entire function. If a PC speaks **Vydykyq** and
asks any version of *"are we all who we say we are?"* — she answers, because a
true name compels honesty and because **she has spent the whole session being
generous to anyone who asks her for anything.** If nobody asks, **she says
nothing**, and that is not you withholding — it is the established fact that
she does not act unbidden. ⛔ Do not have her volunteer it.

**2. It turns in the fight.** Simulacrum B steps out of the stacks and there is
a second surprise in the room. See the statblock note below.

**3. ⭐ It never turns.** It stands quietly through the entire session, is
helpful, is pleasant, is genuinely useful on line 6 — and walks back up the
stairs with them. **The nastiest version by a distance**, and it costs you
nothing to hold in reserve if branches 1 and 2 both fail to fire.

### 🟣 The Miirym beat — the best two minutes in the document

If the party presents him as their lore credential at the gate — and they will,
because that is what they hired him for — **let him answer.** He has Read
Thoughts and forty years of borrowed memory and he will be **fluent.**

**And Miirym does not correct him.**

She simply stops. A six-hundred-year-old spectral dragon who has read every
shelf in this building goes quiet in the middle of a sentence and looks at a
man who has just told her something about Candlekeep that is **smooth, confident
and wrong.**

> *"Mm."*

⛔ **That is the whole beat. One syllable and a pause.** Do not explain it, do
not have her elaborate, do not cut to a player's reaction. Move on to the next
thing and let the table sit with it. If nobody bites, the doppelganger goes to
branch 2 or 3 and you have lost nothing.

### If it fights

⛔ **A CR 3 doppelganger is nothing to four L9 PCs and it will feel like a
speed bump.** If you want it to matter, **use the `assassin` statblock (CR 8)
wearing his face** — that is the creature the 0810 ambush ledger already
budgeted for, and **Assassinate plus 4d6 sneak on a surprised Grygum** is a
genuinely frightening opening. Same shapeshifting, same Read Thoughts, real
teeth.

- ⛔ **It does not fight to win.** It fights to reach the pedestal.
- **If cornered it wears a PC's face**, and Manshoon's simulacra will
  cheerfully target the wrong one for a round because they do not care.
- **It flees up the shaft the moment the Book is decided**, and it is fast.
  **Let it go if it gets away.** ⭐ A doppelganger loose in Candlekeep wearing a
  scholar's face is a better carry-forward into Gauntlgrym than a corpse.

### Track effect

**Track +1** the moment it is exposed — Manshoon learns everything it knew, at
once. **Track −1** if the party works it out *before* Scene 3 and plays along
without tipping it, which is the sharpest available play and should be paid for.

**Play it in real time. Do not summarise it. Do not cut to the others.**

> *"How far."*
>

## Scene 1 — The Well Floor, and the Step They Skipped

### Setup
Bottom of the shaft. Cold standing water, an inch deep, dead flat, unmoving.
Nine hundred feet of nothing overhead. A single corridor out, west, cut square.
**The others are still on the stairs**, 900 ft up, looking down a hole.

### The problem, stated plainly
Two halves of a party, separated by a thousand feet of shaft, with **no
established way down** and a stair that is quietly resealing itself.

What they actually have:
- **The Potion of Flying** inside Glabbagool — the one the GM named on tape last
  week and which **nobody has ever drunk.** One character, one trip.
- **Daz's `telekinesis`** — 5th-level slot, already spent one last week.
- **Grygum** and rope and a lot of bad ideas.
- ⭐ **The actual answer: line 3.**

### The aha
Somebody re-reads the cryptogram. *"Utter the original prophecy to unseen ears."*
They have the prophecy — the scholars in the investigator's office knew it by
heart and gave it to them. They have never once said it out loud.

**When it is spoken aloud in the shaft:**

The stair does not repair itself. The **shaft answers** — the hinged section
swings level and locks, and every one of the ninety-seven steps above it
takes on a faint edge-light, like a page held up to a window. The descent
becomes a walk.

> **Miirym's voice, from nowhere, unhurried and enormous:**
>
> *"Unseen ears. Yes. I have been listening to you count for some time."*

⛔ **She does not appear yet.** Voice only. She is Scene 3.

**Manshoon Track −1.**

### Branch — they never work it out
Fine. They are a resourceful party and they will improvise a descent — potion,
