## ⭐ The question you actually asked: does the adventure end here?

**No — and the reason is structural, not a GM veto.** Write this on your hand:

> **The keys gate the ward-core. The cryptogram gates the Vault.**
> **They are two different doors, and Manshoon only ever wanted the second one.**

Unpack it:

1. **The two High Tower keys open the lightning orb / ward-core** — the thing
   that commands Candlekeep's protective lattice (anti-teleport, anti-flight,
   anti-fire). That ruling is already made in `hightower_session.md` and it holds.
2. **The Book of Vile Darkness is in the lava-chamber Vault,** which is reached
   by the **cryptogram → House of Alaundo → 97 steps.** No key opens it. The keys
   are irrelevant to the prize.
3. **Manshoon wants exactly one book.** A'lai and the keys were the *convenient*
   route: drop the wards, teleport straight in, leave in an hour. The back door
   was always there. Denying him the wards makes him **slower and louder**, not
   stopped.
4. Therefore **killing or capturing A'lai removes Manshoon's inside man, not
   Manshoon.** The party can win this session completely and still be behind.

### So what *is* the backup plan?

**A'lai was never the plan. A'lai was the shortcut.** There are three layers of
fallback, and you should know all three even though the party will only see two.

> **Layer 1 — A'lai's personal exit.** The **sapphire** he stole from Janussi's
> safe is an `instant summons` component: **the Manshoon signal.** Smashing it
> is not a threat, it's a resignation letter — it says *I have failed the quiet
> version, come do the loud one.* Plus the `dimension door` scroll, routed to the
> lava chamber. He does not intend to win this fight. He intends to survive it
> and be somewhere else.
>
> **Layer 2 — Manshoon's route change.** Denied the front door, he runs the
> **same cryptogram/lava-chamber path the party does.** This is what "forced to
> the back door" means. He is not improvising; he had a second route costed out
> the whole time, because a man who plants an agent inside Candlekeep for years
> is not a man who bets everything on one agent.
>
> **Layer 3 — the head start you should plant tonight.** The cryptogram book
> carries **A'lai's thumbprint smudge on two clue-lines** (DC 15 Investigation,
> Beat 5). He has been reading it for a while. Manshoon already has the route.
> The party is not racing to *discover* the back door. They are racing down a
> corridor someone else surveyed months ago.

### And A'lai's backup plan against *the party* isn't magic

It's **information.** He knows who pays to keep Daz alive. That is the one
currency the party cannot obtain anywhere else at Candlekeep, and he will spend
it the instant steel is at his throat. See **Scene 4 — The Ransom.**

## The Win Condition (state plainly to yourself)

The session lands if the party **wins the tower and understands they haven't
won the war** — and if they leave knowing the name **Manshoon**.

Concretely: the breach A'lai opened is **closed by Kalan**, the wards stabilise
at ~50%, the party holds real key #2, the cryptogram is in Daz's hand, and a
calm voice from below names itself. The emotional beat is the turn from *we
caught him* to *he was the small one.*

**If they leave without hearing "Manshoon," this session failed** — not because
they lost, but because the next two sessions are built on a villain they've
never met, and Beat 4 becomes a stranger walking on stage in Act 3.

They won't do nothing — Thorin has already declared *"The High Tower was ahead
of us. We'd go stand in it."* But if they stall in the rotunda, tend Tadric,

- **A'lai waits until dawn, then leaves on his own terms.** He is one key short;

- **The breach stays open.** Kalan cannot reroute the lattice without the tower

- **Kalan dies of being right.** The methodical man who saved the keep, with no

## The Clever Play (so you know when to nudge)

1. **Take the exit before the man.** A'lai's escape is a *scroll* — a physical
   object in a pocket. Zalthir's Eldritch Claw Tattoo (15-ft reach), Glabbagool's
   acid, a grapple, a `counterspell` held for the read. Disarm beats damage.
2. **Feed him the decoy.** Grygum already said it: *"Anybody who doesn't know we
   have a fake key, we can fake them out with it."* A'lai has never seen either
   key. Offering the fake to open the orb buys a full round of his attention and
   costs nothing.
3. **Don't chase the escape — hold the position.** Kalan needs ten minutes and a
   defended tower. The victory condition is the reroute, not the kill.
4. **Ask him who he works for while he still thinks he's leaving.** He's most
   talkative *before* the fight, when he believes he's already won.
5. **Send someone to Sylvira.** She is an archmage, she is three rooms away, and
   her son is dead in the rotunda. Telling her is the ally unlock *and* the
   decent thing, and it costs one PC one scene.

# SCENE 1 — The Rotunda, Before They Move (~10 min)

### Setup
Domed rotunda, top of the Watcher's Stair. Moziqodo's corpse. Tadric alive,
sitting up, real key #2 in his fist. Kalan is somewhere below, planted and
breathing hard. The dome carries sound — **Thorin clocked the whisper-spot on
the way in; give it back to him.**

### The three things this scene exists to do

**1. The corpse search comes up empty.** They will search Moziqodo. Key #1 is
not on him. That is not a dead end — it's a confirmation: *the handoff already
happened, and A'lai is definitively in the tower with it.*

**2. Tadric hands over the key — and asks to come.** He's nineteen, he's been
opened up and closed again by Grygum's hands, and he has just been told the
whole story by Thorin. He will not stay behind.

> **Tadric:** "I held it all night and I didn't know what it was. Now I know
> what it is. I'm not giving it to you and going to bed."

> **⭐ GM-only — ruled: he comes, and his death is genuinely available.** He's a
> Watcher with `fly` and he is literally how bodies get up the tower. **The party
> says yes, so the tab is theirs.** Do not protect him and do not soften it — if
> the dice take him, take him. If Grygum reaches for Revivify, let him, and let
> it cost the slot.

**3. The dome tells them something.** As they stand there, the acoustics deliver
a sound from far off in the keep: **the Endless Chant has stopped.** Not paused
— stopped. It has run without interruption for centuries. Every Avowed in
earshot goes still.

> **Stage direction:** don't announce this as a portent. Describe the silence,

### Branch — the party splits
Encourage it. Siege sessions want two fronts. Daz + Zalthir up the tower,
Thorin + Grygum to Sylvira, is a clean cut.

> **GM-only — how raiders are inside a warded keep, using what the party already

> hacked the firewall and opened another port."* **That breach is still open.**
> It is how the helmed horrors came in and it is how everything else comes in

### Running the approach
Don't make this a corridor. Make it a **fighting withdrawal in reverse** — the
party moving *up* while Avowed and Watchers move *down* past them, carrying
wounded, shouting contradictory orders. Two or three obstacles, escalating:

1. **The dead Watcher** on the walkway. Crossbow bolt, unfamiliar fletching.
   Not a Candlekeep quarrel. First physical evidence of outsiders.
2. **Two raiders** looting the Skyscraping Lobby, unhurried, bagging books from a
   written **manifest.** ⭐ **Recover it — the detail matters more than the
   fight.** Neat scribe's hand, shelf marks, cross-references, and printed at the

   > **CANDLEKEEP — ACCESSIONS, THE PAST XL YEARS**
   > *Available on application. Copying fee: 2 gp.*

   >
   > **Daz:** "He didn't need a spy for the list. He needed a spy for the door."

   > **GM-only — why this is the right answer.** It adds **zero** new plot and
   > tells you everything about Manshoon: patient, unromantic, uses the front
   > counter. It also retroactively shrinks A'lai — he was never the
   > intelligence, he was **labour.** A man who spent eleven years being a hand.
3. **⭐ The free beat: fire works.** A raider — or Daz — throws fire, and it
   *connects.* Fire has not worked inside Candlekeep in living memory. Let an
   Avowed NPC see it happen and make a small, awful sound. That's how you show

# SCENE 3 — A'lai at the Sealed Door (~15 min, no combat yet)

### Setup
Skyscraping Lobby, top landing. The library door **sealed and holding** —
arcane-locked at 9th, `mordenkainen's private sanctum`, alarm keyed to a dead
Keeper. A'lai is **outside** it, and has been for hours.

He has key #1 in his hand. He has been standing at a door he cannot open,
waiting for a courier who is a corpse. **Play the humiliation of that.** He is
immaculate and he has been beaten by arithmetic.

> **Voice:** composed, precise, courteous. Chooses words with visible care —
> he distinguishes between things for a living. **Tic: peppermint.** He smells
> of it; he used it to distract Leuwin at the apothecary and it's become a nervous
> habit. When he's lying, he's *more* fluent, not less. DC 16 Insight on any
> condolence reads **rehearsed.**

He turns when they arrive. He is not surprised.

> **A'lai:** "Ah. All four. And the boy — Tadric, isn't it? I'm genuinely glad.
> I did not ask for him specifically."
>
> *(beat)*
>
> "You've been so *thorough.* I want that on the record before anything else
> happens, because I don't expect either of us will have time for compliments in
> a moment."

### ⭐ What he says if they ask why (GM: let them ask — it's the best material)

His motive is small and human and you should not upgrade it. **He is a thief who
got caught.** Janussi gave him **ten days** to return the tomes he'd been
pilfering, or face exile. Exile from Candlekeep, for a Great Reader, is not a
punishment — it's an erasure. He would cease to be a person who exists.

> **A'lai:** "Janussi gave me ten days. Do you understand what he was offering
> me? Mercy. Genuinely. He thought ten days was *kind.*"
>
> "I have been at Candlekeep for thirty-one years. I do not have a house, or a
> family, or a country. I have a chair. He proposed to take the chair, and I
> found — I was surprised by this — that I would do a great deal to keep it."

He did not set out to be a murderer. He set out to not be exiled. Alkrist was
motivatable; the frame on Daral was housekeeping; Bookwyrm's death was **not his
order** and he is quietly appalled by it, in the way a man is appalled by
weather.

### ⭐⭐ NAMING MANSHOON — do this here, deliberately

You chose to land it hard, and this is the place. The party is about to ask the
question that opens the door themselves: *why steal books at all?*

**Run it in this exact order: name → silence → sapphire.** The name must land in
a quiet room, not under a countdown.

> **A'lai:** "You keep saying *I.* I want to correct that, not out of modesty."
>
> "I have been removing books from this library for eleven years, and I have
> never once chosen which ones. A list arrives. I fill it. The courier who
> brings the list wears a glove on his right hand, and under the glove the hand
> is *metal*, and I have never in eleven years worked up the nerve to ask why."
>
> "His name is **Manshoon.** You've not heard it. That is not an accident; it is
> a career."

> **⭐ Stage direction — STOP TALKING HERE.** Count three. Do not narrate, do not

> correctly and the whole reason for landing it now instead of in Scene 6 has
> just paid off.
>
> **Let an NPC answer, never A'lai.** Kalan or Daral goes grey: the Zhentarim.
> The Moonsea. The man who owned Zhentil Keep. A wizard who has been dead more
> than once. Keep it *institutional* — dread, not a dossier. If both are
> offstage, let a passing Watcher stop walking.

*Only then* does he reach into his vestment.

> **A'lai:** *(producing the sapphire, holding it up)* "He wants one book out of
> this keep, and he was always going to have it. I was simply the version where
> nobody had to die." *(a small, precise correction)* "Fewer people had to die."

**Ruled at the table: Daz lifted the sapphire out of A'lai's open hand with

**The reveal ordering held.** A'lai named Manshoon *before* he ever produced the

> **A'lai:** *(hand still open, still shaped around a stone that isn't there)*

>
> *(He looks at his own palm for slightly too long.)*
>

> **Play it as relief and terror at once.** He did not want to make that call.

>
> **He has *mage hand* on his own cantrip list and could contest it.** Have him

### ⭐ What the party is now holding — three things at once

| It is | Worth |
|---|---|
| **A 1,000 gp sapphire** | The material component for `instant summons`. |
| ⭐ **The third pillar of the Threefold Proof** | It is the **single stone missing from Janussi's blasted safe** — the Ch.57 loose end that was never explained. Taken from A'lai's hand in front of **four deputised Watchers** and a Keeper of Tomes. Kalan said they had *two* pillars at the Sea Warden's Tower. **This is the third.** |
| **A trigger for Manshoon** | Break it and he comes — at a time and place the party chooses. |

> ⭐ **This is the arc's thematic engine paying off.** Monday's whole emotional
> spine was *"by my own paper, she walks"* — the party learning that proof is

> **RAW caveat, so nobody gets caught at the table:** `instant summons` (2nd-level

> not the printed spell. Clean reading: *Manshoon* cast it, attuning the stone to
> a token he carries; destroying it breaks his end and he knows.
>
> **Can the party trigger it? Ruling: yes.** RAW says no, but yes turns a prop
> into a decision they own — and that decision is now your cliffhanger.

### The escalation trigger
With the stone gone, **A'lai's behaviour barely changes** — he was always
leaving, and Manshoon comes eventually regardless. He fights the same delaying
fight (Scene 4). What changed is that he is now leaving as a man who *failed*,
not a man who escalated. **Let that show once, in round three, and never again.**

> **Branch — if the stone does get smashed** (a different table, a different
> night): the fuse is ~3–4 minutes, the A'lai fight is 4–5 rounds (~30 seconds),
> and **Kalan's reroute is ten minutes.** So Manshoon's voice lands **in the
> middle of the reroute, not after it** — Kalan hears the man name himself with
> his hands in the ward-core and cannot stop working. Run Scene 6's magic mouth

# SCENE 4 — The Fight, and The Ransom (~40 min)

### A'lai — scaled CR 9
**MM Archmage chassis**, with: **no 8th/9th-level slots · HP −25% · save DCs −1.**
Pull the base from 5etools (`get_monster`) and apply the deltas. **Do not run the
canonical block raw** — it swings toward a TPK at this table.

**He is not trying to win. He is trying to be elsewhere in four rounds.**
- **R1:** `wall of force` to split the party or cut off the melee threat;
  `mirror image` up; `counterspell` held for Daz.
- **R2+:** `cone of cold` / `lightning bolt` chip damage; re-`counterspell` Daz's
  big turns. He targets **casters and mobility**, never the tank — Thorin is
  someone else's problem.
- **At HP ≤ 50%:** he reads the `dimension door` scroll. Route: the lava chamber.

### ⭐ Escape is earnable-against — three concrete denial vectors

You ruled it contestable. Make the contest legible so the players can *see* the
handle. Any of these takes his exit away:

| Vector | How | Cost to the party |
|---|---|---|
| **Take the scroll** | It's an object in a vestment pocket. Zalthir's Claw Tattoo (15-ft reach) as an improvised disarm, Glabbagool's acid on the pocket, or a `mage hand`/Telekinetic pull. Call it a contested check against his Dex. | A full action from the party's best striker at the exact moment the fight needs him. |
| **Counterspell the read** | Reading a scroll is casting. Daz can `counterspell` it — **but A'lai holds his own `counterspell` for precisely this.** It becomes a two-mage duel with one winner. | Daz's reaction, and probably a 3rd-level slot he wanted for something else. |
| **Burst the window** | He fires at ≤50%. If a round takes him from >50% to 0, there is no window. Requires the party to *hold* damage and then dump it. | Discipline. They have to notice the 50% line and play around it — which is exactly the kind of thing this party does. |

> **GM-only — if he gets away anyway:** that's fine and the back half assumes it.
> But say out loud what it cost him: **he leaves empty-handed.** The party keeps
> key #2, the orb stays split, the wards hold at ~50%. Name that as a win,
> because it is one.
>
> **⭐ GM-only — if they take him: he becomes a terrified informant.** This is
> ruled, not optional. His loyalty to Manshoon was always the loyalty of a debtor,
> and the debt just came due in public. **He gives up, unprompted:**
> - the cryptogram head start — *which lines* he studied
> - the lava chamber route
> - the raider count, roughly, and their entry point (his own breach)
>
> He is not brave and never claimed to be. Eleven years filling a list for a man
> he never dared question.
>
> > **A'lai:** "Lock it. No — *properly.* Is there a deeper one?"
>
> **The cost the party carries:** a cooperative murderer on the books, fed and
> housed by a keep that has no one in charge. Thorin will not be quiet about this.

### ⭐⭐ THE RANSOM — the real scene inside the fight

The instant A'lai is cornered — scroll gone, HP low, grappled, whatever — he
stops fighting and **spends the only currency he has.**

> **A'lai:** "Wait. *Wait.* Drow." *(to Daz, and only to Daz)*
>
> "Someone in Menzoberranzan has been paying to keep you breathing for most of
> your life. You know this. What you do not know is **which seat they sit in** —
> and I do, because I was asked to check, eleven years ago, by a man with a metal
> hand who wanted to know why an extinct house was still spending money."
>
> "That is what I have. It is worth more to you than my corpse is. Name your
> terms quickly, I'm being shot at."

> **⚠️ CANON GUARDRAIL — read before you run this.** The name **DeVir is never
> spoken at Candlekeep.** (`post_candlekeep_open_threads.md`, explicit; Vizeran
> speaks it into place at Stage 4 in Menzoberranzan.) A'lai **cannot** say it and
> **must not.**
>
> What he *can* give — and this is genuinely valuable — is the **third erasure
> piece**, normally gated behind 4+ Yvenne disclosure ticks: **the survivor is
> alive, and someone with current institutional reach is still actively erasing
> the trail.** Recent ink. Years, not decades.
>
> That plus the two pieces Daz already has (**the Fourth Seat, struck** + **the
> razor-cut *Daz'issin* footnote**) completes the set for the **DC 20
> synthesis.** A'lai's ransom is therefore a **live alternate route to the
> Candlekeep payoff Mike may otherwise miss** — and it costs the party something
> real to take.

**This is the moral engine of the session.** Daz wants it more than he wants
A'lai punished. Thorin will hate it in his bones — *"the only thing that doesn't
lie is terrain,"* and a bought confession is not terrain. Grygum will write it
down and let them choose (he withholds Lesser Restoration for the same reason).
Zalthir will say nothing and watch which of them blinks.

**Do not resolve this for them. Let it be an argument with a clock on it.**

### Branch — they take the deal
A'lai gives the third piece, cleanly and accurately (he has no reason to lie; a
lie gets him killed later). Then he goes — released, or he reads the scroll
while they're arguing about it, which is funnier and equally valid. **Daz gets
the synthesis handle; the party gets to feel dirty.** Thorin does not forgive
this quickly.

### Branch — they refuse
He tells them anyway, on the way out, as a parting cruelty — *"I'll give it to
you free. You'll enjoy it less."* Same information, worse taste. **Never make
refusing cost them the clue**; make it cost them the feeling of having chosen.

### Branch — they kill him
Fine. The piece is not lost — Yvenne's fourth tick still delivers it, and
Vizeran's Stage 4 is the failsafe. But **let the loss be visible**: Daz should
watch the answer leave the room and know he chose it. Grygum, quietly:
*"I have done it for a mushroom."*

# SCENE 5 — Kalan Holds His Post (~15 min)

> **The thematic payoff of "The Gatewarden Lives."** The man they could not use
> to win the case is the man who keeps the keep standing. Give it to him whole.

### The reroute
Ten minutes, **DC 18 Arcana**, Kalan leading, Tadric and any surviving Watchers
assisting. **The party's job is to hold the landing while he works** — this is
where the siege pays off. Raiders coming up through the breach; two waves.

> **Kalan:** "They took me off the murder. They did not take me off my wards.
> Stand where I tell you and do not touch the orb."

- **Success → wards restore to ~50%,** and — the part that matters — **the breach
  closes.** No more teleporting in. Manshoon's people already inside are now
  *trapped* inside, which is a different and better problem.
- **Failure → they hold at ~30%** and the breach stays open into the Vault
  session. Don't kill him for it. Let him be furious at himself.

> **Kalan, on success:** "He will have to come the hard way now."

### The cryptogram
**Cryptogram book** in indestructible glass — Kalan's Gatewarden authority opens
the reading room in either branch (the *keys* gate the orb, not the books). Daz
transcribes: **DC 10 Arcana.**

**DC 15 Investigation:** two clue-lines carry a **faint thumbprint smudge.**
A'lai has been studying this for a long time. ⭐ **Manshoon already has the
route.** Land this — it's the line that tells the players the race started
without them.

### ⭐⭐ THE THIRD PILLAR — do not skip this

**Show Kalan the sapphire.** This is the payoff of the entire Monday session and
it takes ninety seconds.

He said it himself at the Sea Warden's Tower: *"You've captured two of the
pillars of the thrice-fold proof."* Monday's emotional spine was the wall —
confession is one spell, the heart is a cover-up, nobody places her at the
scene, **by my own paper she walks.** The party learned that proof is harder
than truth and went to bed having lost the case.

**They just got the proof.** The stone missing from Janussi's blasted safe,
taken from A'lai's own hand, witnessed by four deputised Watchers and a Keeper
of Tomes.

> **Kalan:** *(turning it over, and his hands are not steady)* "This was in the
> safe. This was **in the safe.** I wrote the inventory myself, I wrote *one
> sapphire, star-cut, unaccounted*—"
>
> *(He stops. Looks at the four of them.)*
>
> "Three. That's three."

> **Stage direction:** let him have it, and then let him be too tired to enjoy
> it. Vindication that arrives exhausted lands harder than triumph. **Do not let
> anyone crack a joke over this line** — Grygum will want to, and the beat is
> better if he visibly decides not to.

**What it does not fix:** A'lai is gone or in a cell, Bookwyrm is dead before
conviction, and there is no one in charge to convict anyone. **The methodology
survived. The case is still lost.** That is the arc's actual thesis and this is
where it lands.

### ⛔ Orphaned clue — handle consciously
The forged *"Cursed Tower, midnight. Bring no one. — S"* note lived on Kalan's
corpse in the old script. **Kalan is alive; that scene never happens.** If you
still want the Sylvira-framing on the table, plant it here as an optional find
among A'lai's papers — a half-finished forgery in her hand. **Low priority.**
The whodunit is banked; this is flavour now.

# SCENE 6 — The Stone on the Table (~10 min, no combat)

> ⭐ **This replaces "The Voice Below."** Daz took the sapphire, so Manshoon was
> never signalled and never arrives tonight. **The magic mouth does not fire** —

The ward-chamber, after. Wards holding at ~50%. The breach closed. Raider bodies
being carried out by Watchers who have never carried a body before. Kalan
sitting down, finally, because his legs have stopped negotiating.

**The Endless Chant has not resumed.** Nobody mentions it. Everyone hears it.

### The beat
Somebody puts the sapphire on the table. It is a very small object and everyone
in the room knows exactly what it is, because A'lai explained it before he lost
it.

**Let the party argue. Do not adjudicate. Do not have an NPC weigh in.**

> **What each of them wants — for your ear only, don't announce it:**
> - **Thorin** wants to break it *now.* The wards are up, Kalan's at his post,
>   Sylvira is here, and the ground has never been better. *"The only thing that
>   doesn't lie is terrain. This is our terrain. It won't be tomorrow."*
> - **Daz** will never break it. He treats information as finite currency and he
>   has just acquired the only one that summons an archmage to a place of his
>   choosing. He will want to *keep* it, indefinitely, which is its own answer.
> - **Grygum** will write down that they had the choice. He won't push. He will
>   note who pushed.
> - **Zalthir** says nothing and watches which of them blinks. As ever.

🟡 **CLIFFHANGER.** Cut the session on the argument, not on a resolution.

**The status board they're holding:**
- ⭐ **A sapphire that calls Manshoon whenever they decide.**
- Real key #2 — and key #1 too, if A'lai didn't get out with it.
- The cryptogram, transcribed, with **a stranger's thumbprint on two lines.**
- **The third pillar**, eight hours after being told they'd lost the case.
- Wards at ~50%. Breach closed. Nobody in charge of Candlekeep.
- A'lai below, or in a cell. **Manshoon coming on his own schedule, unhurried.**

> **Why this is the better ending.** The magic mouth is a villain announcing
> himself. This is the party holding the trigger and having to decide. Same
> fight, on terrain they picked — and it hands session +1 an opening question
> instead of an opening threat.

### ⛔ Branch not taken — if the sapphire had been smashed
Preserved for reference, or for a table where it plays out differently.
Teleportation pulse from the Sea Warden's Tower below: crossbow fire, unfamiliar
accents, someone screaming in a language that isn't Common. Then a `magic mouth`

> **Manshoon:** "Citizens of Candlekeep. My name is Manshoon. I am here for one

**Timing, if you ever run it:** fuse ~3–4 min · A'lai fight 4–5 rounds (~30 sec)
· Kalan's reroute 10 min. **The voice lands mid-reroute, not after** — Kalan

## Anticipated player questions

- **"Can we just not give him the key and walk away?"** → Yes, and it works. He

  reroute.
- **"Can we use the fake key on him?"** → **Absolutely yes, reward it hard.**
  A'lai has never seen either #2. Give them a full round of his divided
  attention, and let him realise it a beat too late.
- **"What do the keys actually open?"** → The lightning orb / ward-core. Daral or
  Kalan can say this plainly now. Both keys are needed; split keys = nobody
  commands it = the wards can't be cut from the core. That's *why* they're safe.
- **"Then can't we just open it ourselves and fix everything?"** → No. One key
  each. **This is the good news and they should feel it as good news.**
- **"Who is Manshoon?"** → Let Kalan or Daral answer, not A'lai. Zhentarim.
  Moonsea. Owned Zhentil Keep. A wizard who has been dead more than once. **Do
  not give a stat block's worth of detail** — institutional dread beats a
  dossier.
- **"Is A'lai working alone, or is Bookwyrm's killer his too?"** → Not his.
  Moziqodo tore her throat out; A'lai orchestrated the *escape* that put him in

  murders, two hands — as Daz already sorted in Ch.62.
- **"Can we save Sylvira's plague?"** → Only if they actually spend a curative
  resource. Becoming an ally doesn't cure her. Default stays **untreated.**
- **"What about the Naming ceremony at dawn?"** → **Suspended.** No bell, no
