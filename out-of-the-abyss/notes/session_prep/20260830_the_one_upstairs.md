## ⭐ THE CRYSTAL CLOCK — the engine of the night

**Read this section twice. Everything else hangs off it.**

Last week's Echo cabinet did not work as a treasure chest, so it is not one any
more. **It is a lock, and Manshoon is picking it, and you can hear him doing
it.**

### The setup

Ten prismatic gemstones on a velvet tray in the vault, brass plaque:
*"The Final Voices of the Seer."* Activation phrase: ***"Speak, seer."*** Each
stone plays **in full, once**; after that, fragments only.

**The last stone is Alaundo's instruction for the last door.** `[OVERLAY]`
Nobody knows which one it is. You find out by playing all of them.

Simulacrum B has been in that room since before dawn doing exactly that.

### The stones

| # | What it is | When it plays |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Three atmospheric, plus **Echo 1** (surface contamination) and **Echo 2** (the wedding) | ✅ **Already played.** Before the party ever reached the tower. Fragments only from here |
| **6** | Atmospheric — a prophecy about a drowned Sembian trade-house, of no use to anyone | 🔊 **Playing right now**, faint and indistinct, as initiative resumes |
| ⭐ **7** | **Echo 3 — the Keeper Prophecy.** Eldeth's call to Gauntlgrym | 🔊 **Top of round 4 of the downstairs fight.** Audible. See Act 1 |
| **8** | Atmospheric — a list of names, most of them meaningless now | End of Act 1 |
| **9** | **Echo 4 — the Witness Prophecy.** Jimjar / Callarduran | As the party climbs the stairs, or end of Act 2 |
| ⭐ **10** | **THE DOOR.** Alaundo's instruction for the last threshold | **The moment the party enters the vault** — or the end of the next scene if they never come |

### How to advance it

**One stone per scene the party spends outside the vault**, plus the two hard
cues above (round 4, and the moment they walk in). If they short rest, that is
two stones. If they go out to talk to the dragon, that is one. If they climb to
the attic first, that is one.

⛔ **Do not advance it during a scene.** It ticks between scenes and on the two
named cues, and nowhere else. A clock you can feel is a clock; a clock you can
argue about is homework.

### What the party hears, from round one

**A voice, through the floor, in a language they mostly understand, saying
something they cannot quite make out. Then silence. Then, some minutes later,
another voice.** Nobody in this room is speaking it. It is not a spell. It is a
recording, and it is being played by somebody who is not in a hurry.

⭐ **This is the honest signpost, and it runs continuously from the first round
to the last.** They cannot say they were not told. They can absolutely say they
were busy.

### ⭐⭐ The thing Manshoon worked out at about the fourth stone

`[OVERLAY]` — **this is the spine of the session and I want you to see it
before you read another line.**

The last door has no lock. Alaundo did not build one, because locks are for
things that can be stolen. **He gave it a question, and he gave it a condition:
the answer has to be spoken by a verified Reader, and it has to be offered
rather than demanded.**

Manshoon forced the High Tower door. He forced the obsidian doors. He killed the
owlbear rather than answer three questions. **By the keep's own law he is a
thief standing in a room that does not recognise him, and he has spent six hours
finding out that the last door does not care how strong he is.**

So he does not just need the stones.

> ⭐ **He needs a Reader. And a verified Reader just walked into his building
> and introduced himself to a dragon.**

**Everything follows from this:**

- It is why B stayed upstairs instead of running.
- It is why A engaged instead of fleeing — **A is not trying to kill them. A is
  trying to find out which one is the Reader, and how much fight is in him.**
- It is why B negotiates in Act 3 instead of ambushing.
- It is why, if the party wins downstairs and walks away, **B follows them** —
  and why "he eventually gets what he wants" is not him sneaking off with a
  book, it is him coming down the stairs and asking Grygum, politely, in front
  of everybody.

⛔ **Nobody says any of this out loud until Act 3, and then only B says it.**

## The Clever Play (so you know when to nudge)

1. ⭐ **Ask the dragon whether everyone is who they say they are.** Ruled free
   tonight — it is a question, not a favour, and a true name compels her. It
   costs one player's held action and it ends the doppelganger. **They talked
   about this exact option last week and chose the curtain.** Do not remind them.
2. ⭐ **Count the voices.** Somebody upstairs is playing recordings on a
   schedule. That is not a trap and it is not weather. It is a person.
3. **Fire the banked curtain at the right moment.** 30 ft, ten minutes, on
   command. Not round one — when he is close enough to the threshold to be
   caught by it. **The ten minutes start when they call it, and it is the only
   copy they get.**
4. **Do not put a `fireball` in a room with a hundred warded books.** If they
   work this out on their own before Act 3, they have won something real.
5. ⭐ **Send Grygum up last, or not at all.** The one thing B needs is standing
   in the middle of the marching order wearing a holy symbol. A party that
   realises the Reader is the asset plays Act 3 completely differently.
6. **Take the deal and then break it.** B's offer in Act 3 is honest and it is
   still a disaster. The clever version is accepting it, letting the stone play,
   and having already decided who says the word.

# ⭐ Edvaldo Sedanur — the Borrowed Scholar

**Greater doppelganger. CR 8 (3,900 XP).** `[OVERLAY]`

*Medium monstrosity (shapechanger), neutral evil*

**Armour Class** 15
**Hit Points** 104 (16d8 + 32)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Int +6, Cha +7
**Skills** Deception +10, History +6, Insight +4, Perception +4, Stealth +7
**Condition Immunities** charmed
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Deep Speech, Undercommon, plus any language of a creature
whose thoughts it has read
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP) · **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

***Shapechanger.*** As an action, it polymorphs into a Small or Medium humanoid
it has seen, or back to its true form. Its statistics are the same in each form,
except size and speed. Equipment is not transformed. **It reverts on death.**

***Read Thoughts.*** As a **bonus action**, it magically reads the surface
thoughts of one creature within 60 ft. that it can see. No save, no
concentration, lasts 1 minute. While reading, it has advantage on Wisdom
(Insight) and Charisma checks against that creature.

> ⛔ **It has already read all four of them.** Since the grove. It knows the
> cryptogram, the marching order, and which of them Grygum will heal first.

***Borrowed Expertise.*** While wearing the form of a creature whose thoughts it
has read, it can make Intelligence checks about that creature's field of
expertise with its own proficiency bonus, doubled. **This is how a fake
historian was fluent about the High Tower's height.** It was fluent. It was also
guessing, and the number is *his claim, not confirmed fact*.

***Evasion.*** On a Dex save for half damage, it takes none on a success and
half on a failure. ⚠️ **This matters.** Daz's `fireball` will not solve it.

***Ambusher.*** Advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.

***Assassinate.*** Advantage on attacks against creatures that have not yet
taken a turn in this combat. Any hit against a **surprised** creature is a
critical hit.

> ⚠️ **Assassinate is mostly dead tonight** and that is deliberate — combat is
> already live and nobody is surprised. It comes alive only if it walks into
> Act 3 as a fresh encounter. **Do not hand it a surprise round in the library
> without thinking hard about it first.**

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** It makes two Borrowed Blade attacks.

***Borrowed Blade.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* **+7 to hit**, reach 5 ft., one
target. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage, plus **14 (4d6) poison damage** —
the target makes a **DC 15 Constitution save**, taking half the poison on a
success.

***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* **+7 to hit**, range 30/120, one
target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing plus 14 (4d6) poison, DC 15 Con save for
half poison.

***Sneak Attack (1/turn).*** +17 (5d6) damage when it has advantage on the
attack, or when an ally is within 5 ft. of the target.

### Bonus Actions

***Cunning Escape.*** Disengage or Hide.

### Reactions

***Wear the Face (1/round).*** When it takes damage from a creature it can see
within 30 ft. whose thoughts it has read, it can use its reaction to assume that
creature's form.

> ⚠️ **Use this exactly once, at the single most expensive moment, and then
> never again tonight.** It is a nightmare on a VTT and a masterpiece once.
> ⭐ Manshoon's simulacra will happily spend a round attacking the wrong one,
> because they genuinely do not care which drow they hit.

**B holds the pair's only 7th-level slot.** `[OVERLAY]`

### The kill windows — both real

1. **Straight fight.** 82 HP, three shared Legendary Resistances, a pool that

2. ⭐ **The curtain.** Somebody calls Miirym's banked boon; the band goes to

   The lava kills.** Getting him there takes real setup — grapple into

# Manshoon B — the one upstairs

**HP 95. AC 15** (`mage armor` only — no robe, no staff, he was never meant to
be seen). Same block otherwise. **Holds the pair's only 7th-level slot** and
whatever is left of the shared pool when A is done with it.

**Table rule for a fight in B2** `[OVERLAY]`: **any area spell, or any fire

> should make it challenging. It does. **The design contract from the 0810 doc

### The room, before anyone speaks

Plain circular chamber, continual flame, windows sealed. Shelves floor to

> ⭐ **And then let Manshoon say the quiet part, once, without any threat in it:**

He does not draw. He does not raise his voice. He has an entire wall of bombs

> *"I would rather not do this the expensive way. I have been polite for six

**It is not a trick.** He will keep every word of it. He does not want a fight,
he does not want the gemstones, and he does not care about them personally.

**And if they take it, the door opens on a room containing the Book of Vile
Darkness, a simulacrum of Manshoon, and — if it is still with them and still
unexposed — a doppelganger.** Everyone walks in together. ⛔ **Do not warn them.
Do not have an NPC warn them.**

### 🟣 The four branches — all real, none preferred

**1. They take the deal.** Grygum says the word. Door opens. ⭐ **No initiative
until somebody moves**, and everybody in that corridor knows it. Go to Act 4

**2. They take the deal and break it.** The sharpest play available and it
should work exactly as well as they build it. The door is open, Manshoon is

**3. They fight in the library.** ⛔ **He is not afraid of this and they should

**4. They refuse and walk out.** He lets them. ⭐ **He does not chase, he does

> *"That is a reasonable decision and I would probably make it. I will be here.

> ⭐ **And then it lands next arc, not tonight.** He does not need this Reader.

## Act 4 — The Word, and the Choice

### The door

⭐ **One attempt. One speaker. Said as a gift, in the dark, by a verified

### Beyond

Cold, rancid air that has not moved in six hundred years. **The Book of Vile

⭐ **Sixty crystal lights.** `[MODULE]` — **the lever launches this tower into
the Sea of Swords.** ⛔ **Do not tell them what it does.** The sign is the
warning and it is a fair one.

> ⭐ **This is a real, available, terrifying option and the party may well take

### 🟣 The choice — theirs, entirely

1. **Take it.** A permanent Manshoon-pursuit magnet and a thing they have to
   keep deciding about, forever.
2. **Leave it.** The Avowed reseal the vault and Candlekeep owes them a debt it
   cannot discharge.
3. ⭐ **Destroy it.** **Grygum's arc:** `dispel evil and good` → `holy aura` →
   holy fire. A Life cleric of Bahamut whose entire theology is *choice over
   determinism*, burning the book that argues the opposite. **If Ben goes for
   it, give him the whole table and do not interrupt.**
4. **Give it to Manshoon**, because they said they would. ⭐ **A completely
   legitimate ending** — they made a deal with a monster, the monster kept it,

> 🟣 **If the doppelganger is still unexposed, this decision has a fifth person

> go, and the Book of Vile Darkness walks out of Candlekeep in a scholar's

⛔ **Do not push. Do not have an NPC advocate. Do not put a clock on it.** This
is the last beat of a four-day arc and the only wrong version is the one you
steered.

**Cut on the choice.** Not the walk back, not the climb, not the Avowed's
paperwork, not Eldeth's letter. **The choice, and out. Then level to 10.**
Decision §4 — the reward lands on the decision, not on the fight.
