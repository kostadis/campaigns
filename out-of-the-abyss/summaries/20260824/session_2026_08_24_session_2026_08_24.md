# Session 2026-08-24

Date: 2026-08-24
Party level: 9

## Summary
Zalthir immediately dove after Thorin, plunging into the darkness at tremendous speed to catch up with his falling companion. Some five hundred feet below, Zalthir finally reached Thorin and caught him, the two clinging together in the dark while Glabbagool, who had accompanied the fall with characteristic calm, wondered philosophically aloud whether there was perhaps too much hugging involved. The rest of the party, hearing shouts from below that a feather-fall zone lay further down, leaped after them one by one — including the scholar Edvaldo Sedanur, who muttered something about intending to write a very long monograph about the experience. The entire party landed safely some fifteen hundred feet beneath the surface of Candlekeep.

The tunnel led them into a massive cylindrical cavern, two hundred feet high and a hundred feet across, thick with acrid, sweltering air. A steaming lake of molten lava churned a hundred feet below the ledge where the party emerged, and rising from the lava on a thin pedestal of glassy black obsidian stood a three-story stone tower built in an archaic style that Edvaldo immediately recognized as a classic example of early Candlekeep architecture — a fact he was only too eager to explain at length. Mechanical components were embedded in the stone ledge, and Daz quickly determined that they formed a system to extend a bridge across the lava to the tower. Using the tools borrowed from Spanner, the party activated the mechanism, and the bridge ground outward across the molten lake with a deep, resonant groan.

Inside the tower's first chamber, a tiny iron statue of a winged owlbear sat atop an eight-foot stone pedestal. The moment the party entered, its large eyelids opened to reveal white glowing eyes, its wings unfolded, and it flew up to hover twelve feet above the ground, declaring that three questions must be answered before it would permit them to pass — and promising to tear them to shreds should they fail. Daz correctly identified the hearth as the only refuge for flames within Candlekeep, Grygum named the holy triumvirate that patroned the great library — Oghma, Deneir, and Milil — and together Thorin and Daz determined the height of the High Tower of Exaltation above the sea. The iron owlbear, satisfied, permitted them to pass, and the party noticed as they moved on that a previous owlbear statue lay destroyed on the floor — clear evidence that Manshoon had not been so patient.

Before the party could ascend further, a spectral silver dragon materialized in the lava chamber, her ethereal form so insubstantial that the molten rock flowed straight through her. She was grouchy and imperious, and she wasted no time in revealing that Manshoon had already breached the vault — that the ancient protections had fallen the moment he entered, and that he was still inside. Grygum identified himself as the Reader and proved his knowledge of Candlekeep to her satisfaction — satisfying riddle line 5, the verification of the chosen Reader's claims — and his devotion to Bahamut impressed her besides, prompting her to remark that wisdom truly flowed from the platinum god into his cleric. She offered the party a boon, and after deliberation they asked her to extend the anti-magic field surrounding the bridge to thirty feet for ten minutes — a choice she approved of with a quiet, measured "Well chosen."

The party ascended to the vault, a plain circular room lit with continual light, its windows sealed shut and its eastern wall lined with a single bookcase. A note on the desk, written in archaic Common, warned that the vault contained Candlekeep's most dangerous secrets and that the means to destroy the contents were stored in the chamber above, should they ever fall into foul hands. Around one hundred of the most powerful and dangerous magical tomes imaginable lined the shelves — and seated calmly at the desk, reading one of them without looking up, was Manshoon. He acknowledged the party's arrival with cool composure, remarking that they were welcome to browse, but that the trick was leaving with what they came for.

Daz, with complete earnestness, proposed that it would be a shame to damage any of the priceless books in the coming fight, and suggested they move their inevitable duel to a lower chamber where the tomes would be safer. Manshoon paused, considered the point, and agreed — noting that clearly one of them would leave with the book they wanted, and that the books themselves deserved better than to be caught in the crossfire. He descended the stairs ahead of the party, and as he went he quietly wove a protective enchantment around himself, surrounding his form with shimmering mirror images. The party took up positions near the bridge and the anti-magic zone, preparing to use the field to strip the legendary wizard of his greatest advantage.

Combat erupted with Daz launching a devastating psychic assault at Manshoon, weaving a terrifying mental attack designed to manifest the wizard's deepest fears. Manshoon weighed burning a Counterspell and chose not to, then made the save through sheer force of will — an 8 on the die carried to 17 by his Wisdom — but took 13 points of psychic damage all the same. The party's strategy was already taking shape — strike hard, then retreat into the anti-magic zone where Manshoon's formidable spellcasting would be rendered useless. With Thorin preparing to act and the battle only just beginning, the fate of Candlekeep's most dangerous secrets — and the legendary wizard who had come to claim them — hung in the balance.

## Locations
### The Great Shaft
A massive vertical drop beneath the statue of Alaundo, descending at least 1,500 feet into the depths beneath Candlekeep, with a feather-fall zone near the bottom.

### The Lava Cavern
A sweltering, cylindrical chamber 200 feet high and 100 feet across, featuring a lake of molten lava at the bottom and a central obsidian tower. The spectral silver dragon guardian resides here.

### The Obsidian Tower
A three-story stone structure standing on a thin obsidian pedestal rising from the lava lake, built in an archaic architectural style. It contains a guardian chamber, a vault of dangerous tomes, and is connected to the lava cavern by a stone bridge with an anti-magic field.

### The Vault of Dangerous Secrets
A plain, circular room on an upper floor of the Obsidian Tower, lit with continual light and featuring sealed windows and shelves packed with approximately one hundred of Candlekeep's most hazardous magical tomes. A note warns that the means to destroy the contents are stored in the chamber above.

### The Obsidian Bridge
A stone walkway leading from the tower to the far tunnel, notable for a ten-foot anti-magic field that suppresses all spellcasting. The party plans to use this zone to neutralize Manshoon during their duel.

## NPCs
### Glabbagool
An intelligent gelatinous cube who accompanied the party. He was promised as collateral at the House of Mechanus — the study deferred until after the descent — remained calm and philosophical even while falling down the great shaft, and wondered aloud whether the mid-air rescue involved too much hugging.

### Edvaldo Sedanur
A scholar the party hired for expertise in the history of Candlekeep, who accompanied them into the depths. He *presents* as Avowed and is prone to lengthy academic monologues; none of his credentials have been verified. He supplied the figure the party used for the height of the High Tower of Exaltation — over 500 feet — which is his claim, not confirmed fact.

### Iron Owlbear Guardian
A winged iron owlbear statue that serves as a sentinel in the first chamber of the Obsidian Tower. It activated upon the party's entry, hovered twelve feet above the ground, and challenged them with three riddles, threatening destruction for failure. After the party answered correctly, it permitted them to pass. A previous iron owlbear had been destroyed by Manshoon.

### Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm — the Spectral Silver Dragon
The translucent ghost-dragon guardian of Candlekeep, whose true name **Vydykyq** was the answer to the cryptogram's first riddle — the name Sylvira Savikas handed the party in Chapter 64. Grouchy and imperious, she resides in the lava chamber beneath Candlekeep. She confirmed that Manshoon had breached the vault and that the protections fell when he entered. She tested the party's knowledge and, impressed by Grygum's claim as Reader and his devotion to Bahamut, offered to extend the anti-magic field to thirty feet to hinder Manshoon.

### Manshoon (simulacrum)
Not the man himself but **a simulacrum** — the party has not been told, and nothing on tape reveals it. Found calmly reading at a desk among the forbidden tomes, displaying a surprisingly civilized demeanor and agreeing to move the duel to a lower chamber to protect the priceless books. He buffed himself with mirror images before the fight began, declined to spend his Counterspell on Daz's *Phantasmal Killer*, and made the Wisdom save against it — taking 13 psychic damage regardless.

**A second simulacrum is present and concealed.** GM's stated shape for the fight: the first goes down to the lower chamber to soften the party; the second wants into the vault and will stay and fight there.

## Items
### Mechanist Tools
Specialized mechanical tools borrowed from Spanner at the House of Mechanus. The party used them to activate the bridge mechanism in the lava cavern, extending a stone bridge across the molten lake to the Obsidian Tower.

### Dust of Mechanus — NOT obtained
A perfect lubricant from the plane of Mechanus, required by the cryptogram's instructions to sprinkle on dormant gears as part of the sequence to open the vault. **The party never obtained the dust.** Spanner would only yield it by the death of a modron, which the party refused; what they came away with was the mechanism — the modron tools — which the GM ruled an acceptable substitute for riddle line 4. This is what the party is carrying.

### Dawnbringer
A sentient weapon carried by Thorin that vocally expressed displeasure at the prospect of falling down another dark shaft, crying out 'not this again!' as Thorin plummeted into the darkness.

### Dangerous Tomes
A collection of approximately one hundred books containing Candlekeep's most restricted and hazardous magical knowledge, stored in the vault of the Obsidian Tower. A note in the vault warns that the means to destroy the contents are stored in the chamber above, should they fall into foul hands. Manshoon was found calmly reading one when the party arrived.
