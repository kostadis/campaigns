# Riddles of the Iron Guardian

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### An iron owlbear statue comes to life and challenges the party with three riddles to prove their worth.
- A winged iron owlbear statue sits atop an eight-foot pedestal; "iron statue's large eyelids open to reveal white, glowing eyes, while its wing unfolds, and it flies directly up and hovers 12 feet above the ground."
- The guardian declares: "Halt Seekers! Before you consult the mysteries stored herein, you must prove yourselves a worthy keeper of secrets. Three questions you must answer before I let you pass."
- Thorin reacts to the trope: "Tip-top A-plus DnD right here." The GM plays it straight: "three questions you must answer. Not two, not one, not zero. If thou shalt answer zero, one, or two, bad things shall happen."
- **Question 1** — "Where is the only refuge for flames in Candlekeep?" Daz, prompted that it was the one place he wasn't cold, remembers the hearth. Grygum rolled a 13. The guardian: "Well done! Well done! Well done!" Edvaldo volunteered that he had known the answer — then removed himself from answering: "if you're going to answer the questions, I'm going to just… over here."
- **Question 2** — "Which holy, triumvirate patron, the great library!" Grygum rolls a 20 on the religion check: "Of course I remember that." The answer: Oghma, Deneir, and Milil. The GM: "you barely resist the temptation to explain exactly how they founded Candlekeep, etc, etc."
- The guardian gives a menacing preamble to the third question: "if you fail, I will tear you to shreds and destroy you. I've been waiting a long time for this... This is something I have trained for in my head... I was built for this!" Grygum: "That would be no less than your duty."
- **Question 3** — "How many feet does the High Tower of Exaltation reach above the sea?" The party turns to Edvaldo. The GM notes the practice of memorizing such dry details "has long fallen out of fashion in Candlekeep," but Edvaldo offers: "It's over 500 feet?"
- Because the party brought Edvaldo, the GM reduced the DC: "normally the DC is an insane 23, I have reduced it to 16 nature check." Grygum questions why it's a nature check: "Nature?" Zalthir: "reasons." The GM: "Yes, yes."
- Thorin and Daz both succeed. The guardian: "You have answered all three questions correctly. You may pass."
- The party briefly considers attacking the guardian anyway. Zalthir: "I was tempted to just try anyway... We want to, Thorin? Could be fun." Thorin declines, citing the classic dilemma: "This is the... this is the problem with DnD. You're always like, should I use this spell, or should I save it for later? And you never know."
- **Later, returning from the dragon (after scene 04):** the party notices a destroyed owlbear statue on the floor — evidence Manshoon "destroyed the owlbear, and a new owlbear... Took his spot."
