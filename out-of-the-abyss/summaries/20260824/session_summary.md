# Session 2026-08-24

Date: 2026-08-24
Party level: 9

## Summary
Zalthir immediately dove after Thorin, plunging into the darkness at tremendous speed to catch up with his falling companion. Some five hundred feet below, Zalthir finally reached Thorin and caught him, the two clinging together in the dark while Glabbagool, who had accompanied the fall with characteristic calm, wondered philosophically aloud whether there was perhaps too much hugging involved. The rest of the party, hearing shouts from below that a feather-fall zone lay further down, leaped after them one by one — including the scholar Edvaldo Sedanur, who muttered something about intending to write a very long monograph about the experience. The entire party landed safely some thousand feet beneath the surface of Candlekeep.

The tunnel led them into a massive cylindrical cavern, two hundred feet high and a hundred feet across, thick with acrid, sweltering air. A steaming lake of molten lava churned a hundred feet below the ledge where the party emerged, and rising from the lava on a thin pedestal of glassy black obsidian stood a three-story stone tower built in an archaic style that Edvaldo immediately recognized as a classic example of early Candlekeep architecture — a fact he was only too eager to explain at length. Mechanical components were embedded in the stone ledge, and Daz quickly determined that they formed a system to extend a bridge across the lava to the tower. Using the tools borrowed from Spanner, the party activated the mechanism, and the bridge ground outward across the molten lake with a deep, resonant groan.

Inside the tower's first chamber, a tiny iron statue of a winged owlbear sat atop an eight-foot stone pedestal. The moment the party entered, its large eyelids opened to reveal white glowing eyes, its wings unfolded, and it flew up to hover twelve feet above the ground, declaring that three questions must be answered before it would permit them to pass — and promising to tear them to shreds should they fail. Daz correctly identified the hearth as the only refuge for flames within Candlekeep, Grygum named the holy triumvirate that patroned the great library — Oghma, Deneir, and Milil — and together Thorin and Daz determined the height of the High Tower of Exaltation above the sea. The iron owlbear, satisfied, permitted them to pass, and the party noticed as they moved on that a previous owlbear statue lay destroyed on the floor — clear evidence that Manshoon had not been so patient.

Before the party could ascend further, a spectral silver dragon materialized in the lava chamber, her ethereal form so insubstantial that the molten rock flowed straight through her. She was grouchy and imperious, and she wasted no time in revealing that Manshoon had already breached the vault — that the ancient protections had fallen the moment he entered, and that he was still inside. Grygum identified himself as the Reader and proved his knowledge of Candlekeep to her satisfaction — satisfying riddle line 5, the verification of the chosen Reader's claims — and his devotion to Bahamut impressed her besides, prompting her to remark that wisdom truly flowed from the platinum god into his cleric. She offered the party a boon, and after deliberation they asked her to widen the anti-magic field surrounding the bridge to thirty feet for ten minutes — a choice she approved of with a quiet, measured "Well chosen." **The boon is granted but unspent:** Zalthir asked whether it could be held back for surprise and the GM agreed, so the ten minutes has not started.

The party ascended to the vault, a plain circular room lit with continual light, its windows sealed shut and its eastern wall lined with a single bookcase. A note on the desk, written in archaic Common, warned that the vault contained Candlekeep's most dangerous secrets and that the means to destroy the contents were stored in the chamber above, should they ever fall into foul hands. Around one hundred of the most powerful and dangerous magical tomes imaginable lined the shelves — and seated calmly at the desk, reading one of them without looking up, was Manshoon. He acknowledged the party's arrival with cool composure, remarking that they were welcome to browse, but that the trick was leaving with what they came for.

Grygum, with complete earnestness, proposed that it would be a shame to damage any of the priceless books in the coming fight, and suggested they move their inevitable duel to a lower chamber where the tomes would be safer — Daz, hearing it, immediately called for an inspiration point. Manshoon paused, considered the point, and agreed — noting that clearly one of them would leave with the book they wanted, and that the books themselves deserved better than to be caught in the crossfire. He sent the party down the stairs ahead of him and followed last, weaving a protective enchantment around himself on the way — shimmering mirror images surrounding his form. The party took up positions near the bridge and the anti-magic zone before he arrived, preparing to use the field to strip the legendary wizard of his greatest advantage.

Combat erupted with Daz launching a devastating psychic assault at Manshoon, weaving a terrifying mental attack designed to manifest the wizard's deepest fears. Manshoon weighed burning a Counterspell and chose not to, then made the save through sheer force of will — an 8 on the die carried to 17 by his Wisdom. The damage was briefly called and then taken back at the table; the save held clean and the spell did nothing. The party's strategy was already taking shape — strike hard, then retreat into the anti-magic zone where Manshoon's formidable spellcasting would be rendered useless. With Thorin preparing to act and the battle only just beginning, the fate of Candlekeep's most dangerous secrets — and the legendary wizard who had come to claim them — hung in the balance.

## Locations
### The Great Shaft
A massive vertical drop beneath the statue of Alaundo, descending roughly 1,000 feet into the depths beneath Candlekeep, with a feather-fall zone near the bottom. The fall dropped the party in two stages — roughly 500 feet, then another 500 feet to the featherfall zone — with the tunnel opening at the bottom leading straight ahead into the lava cavern.

### The Lava Cavern
A sweltering, cylindrical chamber 200 feet high and 100 feet across, featuring a lake of molten lava at the bottom and a central obsidian tower. The party emerged onto a five-foot ledge jutting from the passage, halfway up the chamber. The spectral silver dragon guardian resides here.

### The Obsidian Tower
A three-story stone structure standing on a thin obsidian pedestal rising from the lava lake, built in an archaic architectural style that Edvaldo identified as an early Candlekeep design. Its base is surrounded by a narrow walkway; a double door of obsidian stands opposite the party's arrival point (30 feet away, with no obvious way across), and a flat stone bridge leads from the back of the tower to a second tunnel. It contains a guardian chamber, a vault of dangerous tomes, and is connected to the lava cavern by a stone bridge with an anti-magic field. Its walls are unadorned but for continual-flame sconces and four mounted crossbows.

### The Vault of Dangerous Secrets
A plain, circular room on an upper floor of the Obsidian Tower, lit with continual light and featuring sealed windows and shelves packed with approximately one hundred of Candlekeep's most hazardous magical tomes. Unlike the anti-magic zone on the bridge, magic works inside the tower and vault — the GM noted "obviously magic has to work, otherwise all these books would be destroyed." A note warns that the means to destroy the contents are stored in the chamber above.

### The Obsidian Bridge
A stone walkway leading from the tower to the far tunnel, notable for an anti-magic field that suppresses all spellcasting. The GM initially described the field as 30 feet, then corrected it to **10 feet** — "the first, the first time closest to the bridge are not [magical]." The dragon's boon can widen it to 30 feet for 10 minutes. The party plans to use this zone to neutralize Manshoon during their duel.

Cast by Daz at 4th level at the start of combat against Manshoon, weaving a terrifying mental assault designed to manifest the wizard's deepest fears and impose disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls. Manshoon made the Wisdom save (17). Under **PHB 2024 (p.304)** a successful save still deals **half damage and ends the spell** — so **13 psychic damage stands**, Manshoon avoids the disadvantage rider, and Daz's concentration is free. Manshoon briefly considered using this to nullify Daz's *Phantasmal Killer* but chose not to burn it, relying on his own saving throw instead. The slot is unspent — and a simulacrum can never regain slots.
