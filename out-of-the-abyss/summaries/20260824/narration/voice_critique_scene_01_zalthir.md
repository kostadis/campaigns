## Flags

### [1] Em-dash overuse (scan A) — line 31

> I stepped through the dark and came out beside Thorin, and he grabbed me — held on, both arms, an actual hug — and we spread out into a controlled fall together.

**Why:** The genre spec licenses em-dash for *interrupted* speech or thought; this pair is appositive, and it stacks three beats into one breath. Zalthir's spec is monk economy — terse, clipped, one thing at a time.
**Suggested rewrite:** I stepped through the dark and came out beside Thorin, and he grabbed me. Held on, both arms, an actual hug. We spread out into a controlled fall together.

### [2] Em-dash overuse (scan A) — line 17

> I have wings. I almost never use them — I position, then I walk the last distance.

**Why:** Appositive em-dash where the second clause explains the first. A colon does that job and keeps the clipped register.
**Suggested rewrite:** I have wings. I almost never use them: I position, then I walk the last distance.

## Verdict
Two appositive em-dashes are the only structural issue; the scene otherwise holds Zalthir's clipped economy cleanly, and the sailor-grounded rope-walk simile is exactly the kind of specific image the spec asks for. Spot-edit, do not re-narrate.
