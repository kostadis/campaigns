# Voice Critique — Daz, scene 02: The Lava Chamber and the Obsidian Tower

**Narration:** `session_doc_scene_02_the_lava_chamber_and_the_obsidian_tower.scrubbed.md`
**Voice spec:** `voice/daz_voice.md`
**Per-char examples:** `examples/daz.md`

## Flags

### [1] Register-wrong vocabulary (scan B) — line 31

> The rest of the geometry, while he talked: a double door of the same glassy obsidian stood opposite us, across a gap too wide to jump, with no obvious way of getting there.

**Why:** `geometry` is the analytical/mathematical default the scan exists to catch. Daz records the world as a Menzoberranzan audit — inventory, account, column — not as shapes on a plane.
**Suggested rewrite:** The rest of the inventory, while he talked: a double door of the same glassy obsidian stood opposite us, across a gap too wide to jump, with no obvious way of getting there.

### [2] Em-dash overuse (scan A) — line 9

> The passage exhaled it at us — thick, acrid, sweltering — and then the walls opened and we were standing on a ledge halfway up a cylindrical chamber

**Why:** Paired appositive em-dashes around an adjective run. Daz's cadence is declarative and audited; commas carry this without the theatrical pause.
**Suggested rewrite:** The passage exhaled it at us, thick and acrid and sweltering, and then the walls opened and we were standing on a ledge halfway up a cylindrical chamber

## Verdict
`geometry` is the one genuine register break — Daz audits, he does not survey — and nine narration em-dashes make this the densest scene in the session. Both are spot-edits; the audit passage and "the only way to die in it. Efficient." are among the strongest Daz prose in the run.
