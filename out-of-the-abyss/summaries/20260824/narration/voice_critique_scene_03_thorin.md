# Voice Critique — Thorin Giantfriend, scene 03: Riddles of the Iron Guardian

**Narration:** `session_doc_scene_03_riddles_of_the_iron_guardian.scrubbed.md`
**Voice spec:** `voice/thorin_voice.md`
**Per-char examples:** `examples/thorin.md`

## Flags

### [1] Gesture-instead-of-naming — line 113

> "Although this was all very entertaining," the guardian said, and let the rest of the sentence go where sentences go when an iron owlbear is done with you.

**Why:** Same family as the banned "the shape of X" — it gestures at what happened instead of naming it. Thorin's register is stone-weight concrete: he says the plain thing.
**Suggested rewrite:** "Although this was all very entertaining," the guardian said, and didn't finish. Iron owlbears don't have to.

### [2] Register-wrong / flip modern — line 85

> Nobody could say why a tower's height counted as a question about nature, either. Zalthir shrugged. Reasons.

**Why:** "Reasons." is flip internet shorthand. The genre spec allows modern plain English *in the character's vocabulary*; Thorin's is mountain-plain, not ironic-online.
**Suggested rewrite:** Nobody could say why a tower's height counted as a question about nature, either. Zalthir shrugged. Nobody pushed it.

## Verdict
The guardian's exit line gestures where Thorin would name, and "Reasons." borrows a register he does not own. Everything else — "two roads to the same rock," the giants' saying about rocks and walls, "I kept that" — is correct stone-weight Thorin and uses his licensed bookkeeping verb. Spot-edit.
