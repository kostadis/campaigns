# The Long Descent

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Thorin plummets down a massive shaft after the staircase trap triggers, leading to a rescue by Zalthir.
- Thorin falls into the darkness after the stairs hinge open beneath him; the GM recapped the prior session's trap: "a 10-foot section of the staircase hinged open beneath their feet, catching everyone standing between the 93rd and 102nd steps."
- Daz, safely above, had cast Telekinesis to arrest the fall; "Everyone but Thorin caught hold of the stairs and scrambled clear."
- Thorin, checking for outs, is confirmed to have exhausted his options: "I'm gonna assume that I used all the, like, potential outs that I could have had." The GM confirms the luck point was already spent.
- Daz recalls the dice: "It was only, like, ones and twos and stuff... For all of us." The GM: "I was just like... It's, like, pretty impressive."
- Zalthir dives after Thorin. The GM's AI systems flagged that Zalthir shouldn't be able to fly: "my AI systems were all screaming, he can't fly. I'm like, yes. Canonically, Zalthir's characters fly." The GM ruled: "canonically, Zalthir's characters fly, and if they can't, they immediately find an artifact that allows them to fly."
- The GM looked up fall speed: "In Dungeons & Dragons, an optional rule from Xanathar's Guide to Everything states that a character falls 500 feet in a 6-second run." — meaning Zalthir couldn't catch Thorin at the top, but could once both were ~500 feet down.
- Daz riffed on the ruling: "Is it, like... the same speed as the European or the African?"
- Zalthir successfully catches Thorin mid-air about 500 feet down: "he's, like, holding onto you, giving you a hug, and you're, like... and Glabbagool is, like, saying one should always be Zen."
- Glabbagool offers philosophical commentary, wondering aloud if there is too much hugging. Thorin: "I mean, Glabbagool's all about the hugging, let's be clear."
- Thorin checks his fate: "Chris, am I dead?" The GM confirms he is not.
- The rest of the party jumps down after Thorin and Zalthir shout up that there is a feather-fall zone further below. Grygum is nervous: "Oh, God, that's gonna be hard for me... I'll just say a quick prayer... And hope that the featherfall works for me, too."
- Grygum invokes the peer-pressure trope: "If all your friends jumped off the stairs, would you?" Daz: "Yeah." Grygum: "Just to be..." The GM: "That... isn't that the whole point of adventure?"
- Daz's reasoning for jumping: "I mean, I don't want to get split from the party. My whole, like, trying to hold the thing up and save us all didn't work, so..."
- Edvaldo the scholar jumps too, declaring: "I intend to write a very long monograph." The GM: "I'm beginning to sense that monographs are the thing that everybody likes to write here."
- The fall drops another 500 feet before hitting a featherfall zone — Zalthir: "So there was no danger here." The GM: "Nope."
- The entire party safely lands in a single tunnel approximately 1,000 feet below the surface.
