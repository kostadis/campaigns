# The Shortcut to the North Gallery

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Kalan provides the party with a shortcut key to reach Tadric before the enemy does. Kalan, overweight and out of shape, tries to run after them but quickly falls behind, huffing and puffing.
- Kalan reveals Tadric is "a loyal, and he's alone, and he does not know what he carries or what's coming for it," posted "away from the barracks on purpose" — through the Echoing Hall, then the North Galleries.
- Kalan gives the party a Watcher's stair key to access a barred shortcut leading directly to the North Gallery, and shouts "Run!"
- The party questions Kalan's honesty about this new key ("is this key for real this time, or are you just fucking with us again?"). Kalan swears "On my honor," to which Thorin retorts, "And the other one wasn't?"
- Kalan, struggling to keep pace and out of breath, explains that giving the party the fake key was meant to protect them by making them a visible but safe target — "I figured that if they saw you with the key, it would be fake, the fake key would be safer. I couldn't trust you with the key."
- The party arrives at a domed rotunda at the top of the North Galleries to find Tadric under attack.
- Thorin requested the room feature acoustic reflections like a whispering gallery, which the DM agreed to write in ("It's officially in").
