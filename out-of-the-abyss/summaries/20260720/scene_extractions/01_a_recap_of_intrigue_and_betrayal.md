# A Recap of Intrigue and Betrayal

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The Dungeon Master provides a detailed summary of the party's recent investigations into the murder of Janussi and their confrontation with Bookwyrm.
- The party secured a confession from Alkrist regarding the death of Janussi.
- Kalan Strongbranch convinced the party to use patience rather than immediate confrontation to bring Bookwyrm to justice, arguing "that a single confession, however dramatic, was not enough to bring Bookwyrm to justice under the rigorous standards of his own investigative framework," and shoving his paper, the "thrice-fold proof," into their faces.
- Thorin added a cunning wrinkle: use the reward to acquire a resource or tome that might definitively prove Bookwyrm's role as an accomplice after the fact, striking when she least suspected.
- During a tense meeting in Bookwyrm's office, Bookwyrm received the party with regal composure, pouring tea and gesturing for them to sit — an offer they declined. Zalthir, unwilling to be rude but equally unwilling to trust the cup, quietly directed Glabbagool to absorb the tea through his arm, leaving only the faint singe of acid.
- Kalan was reinstated to his position after presenting evidence of the crime.
- Daz noticed that Bookwyrm appeared relieved beneath her performance of grief and outrage.
- The party was escorted to Deneir's sanctum for 'protection' while the investigation continued — though Daz sensed it was equally about keeping them from poking their noses further into Candlekeep's affairs.
- A brief detour to Alkrist's room yielded little: gold, a pair of potions, and, as Thorin put it, "Jimmy Hoffa's chest" — a gold coin engraved with a red herring.
- Zalthir used Glabbagool to discreetly steal a potion of flying from Alkrist's safe while the Avowed were distracted.
- The DM clarified a detail the summary missed: while Grygum was away giving his talk, Kalan's key had passed from Grygum to Daz, and Fembris was still present and noticed the handoff.
