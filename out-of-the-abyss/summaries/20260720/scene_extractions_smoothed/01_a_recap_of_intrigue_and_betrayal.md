# A Recap of Intrigue and Betrayal

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The Dungeon Master provides a detailed summary of the party's recent investigations into the murder of Janussi and their confrontation with Bookwyrm.
- The party secured a confession from Alkrist regarding the death of Janussi.
- Kalan Strongbranch convinced the party to use patience rather than immediate confrontation to bring Bookwyrm to justice, arguing "that a single confession, however dramatic, was not enough to bring Bookwyrm to justice under the rigorous standards of his own investigative framework," and shoving his paper, the "thrice-fold proof," into their faces.
- Thorin added a cunning wrinkle: use the reward to acquire a resource or tome that might definitively prove Bookwyrm's role as an accomplice after the fact, striking when she least suspected.
- During a tense meeting in Bookwyrm's office, Bookwyrm received the party with regal composure, pouring tea and gesturing for them to sit — an offer they declined. Zalthir, unwilling to be rude but equally unwilling to trust the cup, quietly directed Glabbagool to absorb the tea through his arm, leaving only the faint singe of acid.
- Kalan was reinstated to his position after presenting evidence of the crime.
- Daz noticed that Bookwyrm appeared relieved beneath her performance of grief and outrage.
- The party was escorted to Deneir's sanctum for 'protection' while the investigation continued — though Daz sensed it was equally about keeping them from poking their noses further into Candlekeep's affairs.
- A brief detour to Alkrist's room yielded little: gold, a pair of potions, and, as Thorin put it, "Jimmy Hoffa's chest" — a gold coin engraved with a red herring.
- Zalthir used Glabbagool to discreetly steal a potion of flying from Alkrist's safe while the Avowed were distracted.
- The DM clarified a detail the summary missed: while Grygum was away giving his talk, Kalan's key had passed from Grygum to Daz, and Fembris was still present and noticed the handoff.

## Verbatim moments

**[GM — the recap begins]**
- The GM offers a summary of the last session-and-a-half and shares the murder-investigation notes doc.

**[GM]** — *recapping the confession and the debate over strategy*
> You'd secured Alkrist's confession — physical evidence in hand — and started debating what to do next. Naturally, that was the moment Dawnbringer decided it was the opportune time to bring up Candlekeep's promised therapy sessions, which — as he'd literally pointed out — had never materialized.

**[GM]** — *Thorin's sympathy, and the settled strategy*
> Thorin was sympathetic — "I know, this is why we came here after all" — and after some deliberation, the group settled on a patient strategy: hand the credit to Kalan Strongbranch, let him save face after his humiliating dismissal, and collect the reward. You figured that with all the evidence you had, you could march in and everyone would just bow before you.

**[GM]** — *Kalan's argument for patience*
> Kalan Strongbranch tempered your expectations — arguing, with academic precision, that a single confession, however dramatic, wasn't enough to bring Bookwyrm to justice under the rigorous standards of his own investigative framework. He shoved his paper — the "thrice-fold proof" — into your faces and insisted the clever play was patience.

**[GM]** — *the naming ceremony leverage and Thorin's wrinkle*
> With the naming ceremony approaching, the stench of suspicion alone would be enough to deny Bookwyrm the position of First Keeper, and the new head of the Avowed would be far more motivated to see her undone. The party agreed — and then Thorin added a wrinkle of his own: use the reward to acquire whatever resource or tome might definitively prove Bookwyrm's role as an accomplice after the fact, and strike when she least suspected it.

**[GM]** — *the confrontation in Bookwyrm's office and the tea*
> The confrontation in Bookwyrm's office was a master class in controlled tension. She received you with regal composure, pouring tea and gesturing for you to sit — an offer you declined, with varying degrees of visible suspicion. Zalthir, unwilling to be rude but equally unwilling to trust the cup, quietly had Glabbagool absorb the tea through his arm. The only sign anything unusual had happened was the faint singe of acid.

**[GM]** — *Bookwyrm's cracked composure and Daz's observation*
> When Kalan laid out the evidence against Alkrist — the stolen poison, the book, the confession — Bookwyrm's composure cracked and she looked heartbroken. But Daz, watching closely, caught something the others missed: beneath the grief, she looked curiously relieved. The party held back what they knew about the possible accomplices, hinting only that the investigation wasn't finished and that others might be involved.

**[GM]** — *Bookwyrm's performed outrage, Kalan's reinstatement, the escort to the sanctum*
> Bookwyrm performed outrage beautifully, declaring it unthinkable that anyone would have conspired in the death of her beloved Janussi. Kalan was reinstated, his methodology vindicated at last, and Bookwyrm graciously announced the party would get a full hearing before the naming ceremony. She then arranged for them to be escorted to Deneir's sanctum — framing it as protection.

**[GM]** — *Daz's suspicion about the 'protection' and the detour to Alkrist's room*
> Daz suspected it was just as much about keeping them from poking their noses any further into Candlekeep's affairs. A brief detour to Alkrist's room yielded little of consequence — the safe held gold, a pair of potions, and, as Thorin put it, "Jimmy Hoffa's chest."

**[GM]** — *the red herring coin and Zalthir's theft*
> A gold coin engraved with a red herring — a fitting symbol for an investigation full of misdirection. The Avowed declared the contents unremarkable and moved to close the matter. But Zalthir, never one to miss an opportunity, had Glabbagool quietly pocket the potion of flying while everyone else was distracted. No one noticed a thing.

**[GM]** — *the clarification the summary missed*
> What the summary didn't capture: you'd already worked out that the one person who saw the key pass from Grygum to Daz was Fembris Lancer.

So Daz had the key — but Fembris Lancer was apparently still around when you handed it over, and he noticed."
