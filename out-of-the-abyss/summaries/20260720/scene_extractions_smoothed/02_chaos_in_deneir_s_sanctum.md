# Chaos in Deneir's Sanctum

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### While resting in the sanctum, the party's peace is shattered by a sudden and violent mechanical assault.
- The party shared a meal in Deneir's sanctum, using Fembris as a food tester due to their deep suspicions, allowing him to eat first before touching anything themselves.
- Dawnbringer cured Daral of his affliction, leading to an enthusiastic academic monologue from the grateful man about making sentient magical swords an area of serious study, going on at considerable length about "the semiotics of the subject" while Dawnbringer absorbed every word with evident delight.
- Daral attempted to leave the room, only to find it was locked.
- Three helmed horrors smashed through the windows and doors, initiating a chaotic battle within the sanctum.
- The helmed horrors ignored most of the party and converged almost entirely on Daz, who realized mid-battle they were after the mysterious key he carried.
- Daz passed the key to Grygum and threw himself clear as his companions surged into the fight.
- Zalthir drove his fist into the animated armor while Glabbagool, fused to his arm, unleashed corrosive acid; Grygum radiated holy light that seared two horrors; Daz hurled magical fire and force; Thorin struck with Dawnbringer, sapping one horror before cutting it down.
- One by one the constructs collapsed into heaps of rust and broken metal, leaving the party deeply unsettled that the mindless horrors had somehow known exactly who held the key.

## Verbatim moments

**[GM]** — *recapping the prior session's events leading into the sanctum*
> The peace shattered without warning. The evening in Deneir's sanctum had passed quietly at first — food brought in, which the party eyed with deep suspicion, letting Fembris Lancer eat first before anyone else touched a bite.

**[GM]** — *Dawnbringer cures Daral, sparking his monologue*
> Dawnbringer was finally called on to cure Daral Yashenti's lingering affliction, and Daral was so overcome with gratitude that he launched into an enthusiastic monologue — sentient magical swords deserved to be a serious academic study, he insisted, holding forth at length on "the semiotics of the subject" — while Dawnbringer absorbed every word with evident delight.

**[GM]** — *the locked door and the assault begins*
> Daral then tried to leave the room, only to find it locked. The peace shattered without warning — three helmed horrors, towering animated suits of plate armor with cold, glowing eyes,

**[Grygum]** — *reacting to the reveal of the helmed horrors*
> "Well, that's a classic."

**[GM]** — *the horrors converge on Daz for the key*
> smashed through the windows and burst through the door, sending the room into chaos. The constructs moved with terrifying purpose, ignoring most of the party and converging almost entirely on Daz. Only mid-battle, as the horrors relentlessly bypassed every other target to reach him, did Daz realize with a chill what they were after: the mysterious key he carried, the one that had come up completely inert to magical detection.

**[GM]** — *Daz passes the key and the battle joins*
> He passed it to Grygum and threw himself clear as his companions surged into the fight. The battle was fierce and disorienting — Zalthir drove his fist into the animated armor while Glabbagool, fused to his arm, unleashed torrents of corrosive acid that began dissolving the construct's plating.

**[GM]** — *the party's combined counterattack*
> Grygum radiated a burst of holy light that seared two horrors within reach, while Daz hurled volleys of magical fire and force into the fray. Thorin struck with Dawnbringer, saddling one horror with disadvantage on its next attack before cutting it down entirely. One by one, the constructs collapsed into heaps of rust and broken metal. When the last one fell, the party stood in the wreckage of the sanctum, breathing hard and deeply unsettled.

**[GM]** — *the unsettling mystery left in the aftermath*
> The horrors had been mindless, incapable of thought or speech — and yet they'd somehow known exactly who was holding a key that no spell in Candlekeep should have been able to detect. The session ended there, the party out of time, the mystery of the assassin's knowledge left open.
