## Flags

### [1] Mechanical scan A — em-dash overuse

Six narration-level lines (four matches — lines 17, 37, 39, 41 — sit entirely inside quoted dialogue and are correctly excluded as VTT-captured speech):

| Line | Text | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| 27 | "...the orc just cracked open — 'Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha!' — and I'll give myself this much..." | Two narration-level dashes bracketing a quote; convert to commas or a period: "...the orc just cracked open. 'Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha!' I'll give myself this much..." |
| 33 | "...the gap between us opened the way a gap opens when a rock starts down a slope — slow at first, then not." | Colon: "...starts down a slope: slow at first, then not." |
| 47 | "He just — stopped." | Period, or leave — this one is doing real work as an enacted hesitation (the dash mimics the stopping it describes). Weakest candidate for change in this scene. |
| 55 | "A big one — a rotunda, curved stone overhead..." | Colon: "A big one: a rotunda, curved stone overhead..." |
| 59 | "The question hung there a second, and then came back — would I like that." | Colon: "...and then came back: would I like that." |
| 63 | "Grygum liked it too — 'I like the idea of having something special in a rotunda...'" | Comma or period: "Grygum liked it too. 'I like the idea of having something special in a rotunda...'" |

**Why:** Same systemic pattern as the other scenes. Line 47 is the one instance in this batch where the em-dash is arguably load-bearing (it enacts the stopping motion mid-sentence) rather than a generic connective tic — worth a second look before flattening it.

## Verdict

This is the cleanest section in the batch. The "rock in the right place is a wall" callback to Thorin's own giant-taught aphorism, "Later is a real place," and "the terrain doesn't wait for a laugh" are all sharp, spec-grounded Thorin lines, and there's no bookkeeping-verb or register-vocabulary violation anywhere in the section. Only the em-dash pattern needs a pass, and even there line 47 is worth keeping as-is.
