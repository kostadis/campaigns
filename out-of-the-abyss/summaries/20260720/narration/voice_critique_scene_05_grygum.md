## Flags

### [1] Mechanical scan A — em-dash overuse

Eight narration-level lines (one match, line 45, sits entirely inside Kalan's quoted dialogue — "I told her — I told Bookwyrm..." — and is correctly excluded as VTT-captured speech):

| Line | Text | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | "...like a breadcrumb — *if I don't see you tomorrow...*" | Colon, or leave (introduces an italicized remembered line — genre-sanctioned use). |
| 31 | "...I said — though I said *fake* out of habit by then..." | Comma: "...I said, though I said *fake* out of habit by then..." |
| 35 | "...struggling to hold — the walls smearing, the floor pulling long." | Colon: "...struggling to hold: the walls smearing, the floor pulling long." |
| 43 | "...we'd seen the body, and no — 'Oh, we did not find a key.'" | Comma or colon: "...we'd seen the body, and no: 'Oh, we did not find a key.'" |
| 51 | "He looked at the four of us — adventurers, he called us — and deputized us..." | Two dashes in one sentence; convert at least one to commas: "He looked at the four of us, adventurers, he called us, and deputized us..." |
| 55 | "...the whole knot came loose at once — the key he'd handed me..." | Colon: "...the whole knot came loose at once: the key he'd handed me..." |
| 57 | "...because don't people think that one of them was given to us?' And when it all lined up — 'I mean, does Kalan know...'" | Period: "...And when it all lined up: 'I mean, does Kalan know...'" or simply "Then, when it all lined up: ..." |
| 65 | "Thorin built the case fast — one hand behind both attacks, both keys..." | Colon: "Thorin built the case fast: one hand behind both attacks, both keys..." |

**Why:** Same systemic pattern as the other five scenes — connective asides default to em-dash where a colon or comma carries the same beat.

### [2] Generic prose — "Something clicked behind his eyes"

> "Daz worked it out. He does that. Something clicked behind his eyes and he came up with the line Kalan had left us like a breadcrumb..."

**Why:** This is Grygum *observing* Daz's realization, and Grygum's whole spec is built on specific, fast people-reading ("he reads them fast, with the quiet confidence of someone who's seen a lot of bad situations"). "Something clicked behind his eyes" is a stock way to gesture at realization that could describe anyone noticing anything — it doesn't carry Grygum's particular noticing signature the way "I turned that over" and "I noted that for the column marked..." do two lines later in this same scene.
**Suggested rewrite:** "Daz worked it out. He does that. I watched him go quiet for half a second, and then he had the line Kalan had left us like a breadcrumb..."

## Verdict

This is a strong, well-calibrated Grygum section — "the column marked *things nobody tells you about the wise*," "My job is beach... because sometimes that is all the theology a situation can bear," and the "So far we've done badly" self-deprecation are all exactly on-spec, and the bookkeeping-verb use stays within the one-per-section cap. Only the opening beat leans on a generic tell; everything else is a mechanical (em-dash) cleanup rather than a voice problem.
