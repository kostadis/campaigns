# Chapter 59 The Key is secured.

Date: 2026-07-20

## Summary
The peace of Deneir's Sanctum shattered without warning when three towering helmed horrors — animated suits of plate armor with cold, glowing eyes — smashed through the windows and burst through the door. The constructs moved with terrifying, mechanical purpose, ignoring Thorin, Zalthir, and Grygum almost entirely and converging with singular focus on Daz. It was only mid-battle, as the horrors relentlessly bypassed every other target, that Daz realized with a chill what they were truly after: the mysterious key he carried. He passed it to Grygum and threw himself clear as his companions surged into the fight, and one by one the constructs collapsed into heaps of rust and broken metal — leaving the party standing in the wreckage, breathing hard and deeply unsettled.

What disturbed them most was not the violence itself, but the implication behind it. The helmed horrors were mindless, incapable of thought or speech, and yet they had known exactly who held a key that no detection spell in Candlekeep should have been able to find. The only person who had witnessed the key change hands from Grygum to Daz was Fembris, the acolyte who had been quietly present throughout their stay. Suspicion settled over the group like a cold fog, and Daral — the grateful scholar Dawnbringer had recently cured of his affliction — pressed himself close to Thorin's side, unwilling to be far from the radiant sword's protection.

Daral produced a scroll of Detect Magic and read it aloud to examine the key. Every piece of magical equipment in the room blazed with light — Dawnbringer most brilliantly of all, much to her evident satisfaction — but the key itself remained stubbornly, completely dark. It was not the real key. The party finally pieced together the true timeline of events: Janussi had been killed by Alkrist, and Bookwyrm had subsequently gone into Janussi's chambers to protect her nephew and secure the Hightower key. The party realized that Bookwyrm had possessed one of the keys, and that someone else had stolen the sapphire. Whoever stole the sapphire was now after the keys. Before the party could fully process this revelation, an institutional alarm bell began tolling through Candlekeep in a pattern that was not the hour — and a breathless Avowed messenger rushed in with devastating news: Bookwyrm was dead.

The party raced to her office and found a gruesome scene. Her throat had been torn apart with savage, animalistic force — the work of something with claws and teeth, not the surgical precision that had claimed Janussi's life. On her desk lay a half-finished note in her own hand, reading simply: *He is using the beast too...* Daz, reaching out with his arcane senses, felt something deeply wrong with the magical wards that protected Candlekeep — as though a firewall had been breached, a hidden port quietly forced open. The connection clicked into place: Sylvira's abyssal spawn son, the creature he had struggled to keep contained, was the beast the note referred to.

Daral confirmed what the party already suspected about the wards. The great shield that prevented teleportation and suppressed fire throughout Candlekeep was maintained within the room locked by the two high tower keys. To test whether it still held, Daz attempted to conjure a small flame — and watched it erupt briefly before evaporating into nothing, the ward consuming it instantly. The protection was still active, but the situation was deteriorating fast. Bookwyrm had almost certainly possessed one of the real keys, and whoever had torn out her throat had taken it.

The party realized they needed to find the missing key, and that Kalan was the one who knew its location. Unsure of where he was, they first thought to head to the Gatehouse. However, Daz remembered Kalan mentioning he would not be where he normally would be, and Fembris realized he must be in the Sea Warden's Tower. The party rushed there, finding Kalan Strongbranch frantically buckling on his armor. When they told him Bookwyrm was dead and the key was gone, he went pale — and then, under the pressure of their questioning, admitted the full truth. The key he had given them had always been a fake, a decoy meant to make them a visible but survivable target while the real key remained hidden. He had entrusted the genuine artifact to his loyal lieutenant Tadric, posted quietly away from the barracks in the North Galleries. Kalan then grandly deputized the party as Watchers of Candlekeep. When they asked about a stipend, Kalan was dismissive, but when they pressed for an honorarium, he assured them it would be paid, though it was merely symbolic in value. It was then that Fembris, cornered and frightened, broke down and admitted that when he had reported the key's location to Bookwyrm, A'lai Aivenmore had been present in the room. This crucial revelation explained exactly why Tadric and the party had been targeted while Kalan himself remained untouched. Suspicion now turned heavily toward A'lai Aivenmore as the architect behind it all.

Kalan pressed a Watcher's stair key into their hands — a shortcut through a barred door leading directly to the North Gallery — and urged them to run. He tried to keep pace but, being overweight and clearly out of shape, fell behind quickly, huffing and puffing and breathing heavily as he waved them on with a breathless gesture before they disappeared ahead of him. The party arrived at the top of a domed rotunda to find Tadric already in the grip of a massive, terrifying creature: Moziqodo, Sylvira's abyssal spawn son, who had grappled the young man and was tearing him apart with savage claws. There was no time for hesitation.

Thorin charged in first, striking hard with Dawnbringer and planting himself squarely between the demon and the rest of the room, forcing Moziqodo's attention onto him. Zalthir followed with a ferocious assault, driving his magically enhanced fists into the creature and unleashing torrents of corrosive acid through Glabbagool — then fixing the pit fiend with such a terrifying display of focused violence that the demon recoiled in fear, unable to advance. Before Moziqodo could recover, Zalthir pressed the advantage further, hammering the stunned and reeling creature with blow after devastating blow until it could barely stand. Grygum hurled a javelin of crackling lightning at the fiend, though it narrowly missed its mark.

Daz struck the helpless demon with a volley of unerring magical force, and then Thorin raised Dawnbringer and delivered a final, critical blow that brought the pit fiend crashing to the floor. Moziqodo lay dead in the wreckage of the rotunda, and the party stood over him, breathing hard. Grygum knelt beside Tadric and channeled divine healing into the young man's wounds, closing the lacerations and stopping the bleeding before they could claim his life. Tadric, pale and shaking, looked up at the creature that had nearly killed him — Sylvira's own son — and could only sit in stunned silence.

With one real key now secured and Moziqodo slain, the party took stock of what they knew. A'lai Aivenmore held the key stolen from Bookwyrm, and he was almost certainly already moving toward the high tower, where he and a figure known as Moziqodo planned to meet. The party still possessed Kalan's fake decoy key alongside the real one — a potential advantage, since anyone who didn't know the truth might be fooled into thinking they held both. Yet, as they prepared to move, they realized they still did not know who was truly pulling the strings behind this conspiracy, and their suspicions turned heavily toward A'lai. The high tower loomed ahead of them, and with it, the final confrontation that would determine the fate of Candlekeep itself.

## Locations
### Candlekeep
A legendary fortified library-fortress where a series of murders and the theft of magical high tower keys have thrown the academic community into chaos. Its primary defense is a magical ward that prevents teleportation and suppresses fire within its walls.

### Bookwyrm's Office
The site of two significant events: first, a tense confrontation where Kalan was reinstated after evidence was presented; later, the scene of Bookwyrm's gruesome murder, where her throat was torn out and a half-finished note reading 'He is using the beast too...' was found on her desk.

### Deneir's Sanctum
A supposedly secure room within Candlekeep where the party was escorted for protection, only to be ambushed by three helmed horrors that smashed through the windows and door targeting Daz for the key he carried.

### Alkrist's Room
A room containing a safe with gold, potions, and a gold coin engraved with a red herring — a fitting symbol for an investigation full of misdirection.

### Great Library
A central hub of knowledge within Candlekeep where the party began their frantic run to find Kalan Strongbranch after Bookwyrm's murder was announced.

### Sea Warden's Tower
A defensive structure within Candlekeep where Kalan Strongbranch was found frantically arming himself after hearing the alarm bell. It was here that the party learned the truth about the fake key and were deputized as Watchers.

### North Galleries
A section of Candlekeep away from the main barracks where Kalan had hidden Tadric to protect the real key. Accessed via a shortcut through a barred door, it features a domed rotunda at the top that became the site of a bloody confrontation with Moziqodo.

### High Tower
The ultimate destination of the two keys, currently suspected to be where Ilvara and Moziqodo plan to meet. The room within it contains the wards that protect all of Candlekeep.

## NPCs
### Kalan Strongbranch
The reinstated Head of the Avowed and Gatewarden of Candlekeep, whose 'Threefold Proof' methodology was recently vindicated. He admitted to giving the party a fake key as a decoy to protect the real one, which he entrusted to his lieutenant Tadric. He deputized the party as Watchers of Candlekeep and directed them to the North Galleries, though he was overweight, out of shape, and too out of breath to keep up with them, huffing and puffing as he fell behind.

### Bookwyrm
A high-ranking magic user of the Avowed who was suspected of covering up Janussi's murder. She was found dead with her throat torn apart in her office, likely murdered for the high tower key she possessed. A half-finished note on her desk read 'He is using the beast too...'

### Daral
An Avowed scholar who was cured of a lingering affliction by Dawnbringer and launched into an enthusiastic monologue about making sentient magical swords an area of serious academic study. He assisted the party by using a scroll of Detect Magic to confirm the key they held was not magical, and explained the nature of Candlekeep's protective wards.

### Fembris Lancer
An acolyte adjutant who served as a food tester for the suspicious party in the sanctum. He was suspected of leaking information about the key's location, and under pressure broke down and admitted that A'lai Aivenmore was present in the room when he reported the key transfer to Bookwyrm, explaining why Tadric and the party were targeted instead of Kalan.

### Alkrist
The individual whose confession and physical evidence linked him to the murder of Janussi. His room contained a safe with gold, potions, and a gold coin engraved with a red herring.

### Janussi
The deceased member of the Avowed whose murder is the central mystery of the investigation. His key was stolen from his locket after his death, and his room was visited by Bookwyrm shortly after the murder.

### Helmed Horrors
Three towering animated suits of plate armor with cold, glowing eyes that smashed through the windows and door of Deneir's Sanctum. They ignored most of the party and converged almost entirely on Daz, seeking the key he carried, before being destroyed.

### Tadric
Kalan's loyal lieutenant, recognizable by his bowl-cut hairstyle, who was entrusted with the genuine key to the high tower and hidden in the North Galleries. He was nearly killed by Moziqodo before the party arrived and saved him.

### Moziqodo
A massive and powerful pit fiend revealed to be the abyssal spawn son of Sylvira. He attacked Tadric in the domed rotunda in an attempt to seize the real key to the high tower before being slain by the party.

### A'lai Aivenmore
A powerful male drow antagonist revealed to be the associate of the abyssal spawn Moziqodo. He was present when Fembris reported the key's location to Bookwyrm, and is now believed to hold the key stolen from Bookwyrm and to be heading toward the high tower.
