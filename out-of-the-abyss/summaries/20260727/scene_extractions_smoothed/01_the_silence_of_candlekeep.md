# The Silence of Candlekeep

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

*Standing over a healed Tadric, the party notices an ominous change in the atmosphere of the keep as the eternal prophecies suddenly fall silent.*

- The party stands over a healed Tadric, having just rescued him from Moziqodo.
- Grygum notices that the endless chant of prophecies — a constant sound throughout Candlekeep — has suddenly and unnervingly stopped. He asks if this was ever mentioned before; the DM clarifies it's a continuous reading of prophecies that get deleted when fulfilled.
- Tadric notices that Moziqodo is dead and urges that they must tell Sylvira.
- The party deflects — Grygum suggests someone who knows Sylvira should do it, Daz says "We'll take care of it later" — and prioritizes heading to the High Tower to stop A'lai Aivenmore.
- Tadric insists on accompanying the party: "I faced Moziqodo! I faced the full… I… no, I, I, I will…" — he's "suffering from a desperate desire to be heroic."
- Grygum offers reassurance: "I do have resurrection magic if worst comes to worst."
