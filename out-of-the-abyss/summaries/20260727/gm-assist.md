# Chapter 60

Date: 2026-07-27

## Summary
The party stood over a healed Tadric when something else changed — the endless chant of prophecies that had filled Candlekeep without interruption fell suddenly and completely silent, a phenomenon Grygum was the first to notice. It was, apparently, the first time in history that the chanting had ever stopped, stopping Tadric cold in his tracks. Looking around, Tadric realized that Moziqodo was dead. He urgently insisted that they must tell Sylvira about her son's death, but the party deflected, suggesting that telling her they had killed her son was a problem for another moment, and pressed on toward the High Tower with Tadric insisting on accompanying them. Grygum cast a protective blessing over his companions as they moved, and along the way they discovered a dead Watcher on an indoor walkway, killed by a crossbow bolt whose fletching Daz immediately recognized as foreign to Candlekeep — proof that outside attackers had already passed through, and recently.

They arrived at the High Tower lobby to find two Zhentarim raiders looting books from a written manifest, their weapons bloodied, one of them carrying the bow that had killed the guard on the walkway. On a second-floor landing above them, waiting near a sealed door that required the High Tower keys to open, stood A'lai Aivenmore himself. Grygum launched a bolt of radiant energy at the wizard in an attempt to catch him off guard, but A'lai Aivenmore's magical defenses deflected it entirely, and combat erupted in earnest. Zalthir and Glabbagool surged into the fray, delivering punishing blows to the wizard, while Daz conjured a massive hand of compacted soil that erupted from the ground and seized A'lai Aivenmore, crushing him and holding him in place. The wizard burned through his magical reserves trying to resist and escape, ultimately teleporting across the room to free himself, but not before taking significant punishment.

Enraged and cornered, A'lai Aivenmore unleashed a relentless barrage of raw arcane energy, severely wounding Daz and hammering Zalthir hard enough to stagger him. Then, unexpectedly, the wizard paused. He looked around at the carnage — Bookwyrm dead, Daral nearly killed, Janussi gone, guards slain — and his voice, when it came, was not the voice of a megalomaniac. "I have been at Candlekeep for 31 years," he said. "I do not have a house or a family or a country. I have a chair. He proposed to take the chair." He had not set out to be a murderer, he insisted, and the party could almost believe him. He revealed that he had been secretly removing specific books from the library for eleven years on behalf of a mysterious figure whose courier always arrived wearing a glove over a metal hand. "His name is Manshoon," he said quietly. "You have not heard of it. That is not an accident. It is a career." The party recognized the name immediately — a legendary and feared wizard tied to the Zhentarim, a man who had died more than once and kept returning.

As if to punctuate his confession, A'lai Aivenmore produced the stolen sapphire and made clear he intended to smash it. But Daz was faster, reaching out with an invisible force of will and wrenching the gem directly from the wizard's grasp before he could act. Moving quickly and with practiced sleight of hand, Daz feigned a retreat into the hallway while secretly pressing the sapphire into Grygum's keeping — so that Grygum now held both the real High Tower key and the artifact, while A'lai Aivenmore seethed with fury at having been outmaneuvered. That fury found a target. A'lai Aivenmore turned on Zalthir with a flurry of arcane bursts and beat the monk down until he collapsed unconscious, with Glabbagool standing protectively over his fallen body. Thorin engaged the Zhentarim thugs directly, striking one hard and rattling the other with an intimidating display, while Grygum surged forward and unleashed a torrent of caustic acid that washed over both raiders. One died instantly as the corrosive brew melted through him, and the other stood soaked in acid, scarcely marked yet but with the brew still eating at him, the battle still raging around him, with the confrontation against A'lai Aivenmore far from over.

## Locations
### Candlekeep
The great library fortress where the party is investigating a conspiracy involving stolen keys, murdered scholars, and ancient protective wards. The endless chant of prophecies that normally fills the keep falls silent during this session — an event that has never happened before.

### Bookwyrm's Office
The site of a gruesome murder where Bookwyrm was found with her throat torn apart by claws and teeth. A half-finished note on her desk read: 'He's using the beast to...'

### Sea Warden's Tower
The location where the party found Kalan Strongbranch frantically buckling on his armor. It is here that he confessed to the fake key scheme and deputized the party as Watchers of Candlekeep.

### North Gallery Rotunda
A domed chamber in the northern galleries where the party fought and killed Moziqodo to rescue Tadric, who had been posted there quietly to guard the real High Tower key.

### North Gallery Walkway
An indoor passage leading toward the High Tower where the party discovered a Watcher killed by a crossbow bolt with foreign fletching, indicating outside attackers had already passed through.

### High Tower Lobby
The entrance to the High Tower of Candlekeep, featuring bookshelves and a second-floor landing. It served as the site of the session's climactic confrontation, with Zhentarim raiders looting books from a manifest while A'lai Aivenmore waited above near a sealed door requiring the High Tower keys.

## NPCs
### Fembris
An acolyte who was quietly present throughout the party's stay at Candlekeep. Under pressure, he broke down and confessed that A'lai Aivenmore was present in the room when the key's location was reported to Bookwyrm, making him the crucial link that exposed the conspiracy's architect.

### Bookwyrm
A high-ranking member of Candlekeep who had possessed one of the real High Tower keys. She was brutally murdered — her throat torn apart by Moziqodo's claws — and left a half-finished note reading 'He's using the beast to...' on her desk.

### Kalan Strongbranch
A Watcher of Candlekeep who admitted to giving the party a fake decoy key to make them a visible but survivable target. He entrusted the real key to his lieutenant Tadric, deputized the party as Watchers, and provided them with a Watcher's stair key — though he quickly fell behind when trying to keep pace with them, huffing and puffing.

### Tadric
Kalan's loyal lieutenant who unknowingly held the real High Tower key and was nearly killed by Moziqodo before being rescued and healed by the party. Shaken but emboldened by his survival, he insisted on accompanying the party to the High Tower.

### Moziqodo
The terrifying abyssal spawn son of Sylvira, responsible for the murder of Bookwyrm and the near-death of Tadric. He was slain by the party in the north gallery rotunda, with Thorin delivering the killing blow using Dawnbringer.

### A'lai Aivenmore
A powerful drow wizard and long-time scholar of Candlekeep who orchestrated the conspiracy to protect his academic position. He revealed he has been secretly funneling specific books out of the library for 11 years on behalf of Manshoon and the Zhentarim. He is self-preserving, desperate, and willing to use lethal magic, but also capable of a surprisingly sympathetic monologue about losing his only home.

### Manshoon
A legendary and feared wizard associated with the Zhentarim and Zhentil Keep, identified by A'lai Aivenmore as the true architect behind the book thefts. His courier always wears a glove over a metal hand. The party recognized the name and noted he has died more than once.

### Glabbagool
An intelligent gelatinous ooze and loyal companion to the party. During the battle at the High Tower, Glabbagool assisted in combat alongside Zalthir and stood over the fallen monk to protect him after he was knocked unconscious.

### Zhentarim Raiders
Mercenaries working for the Zhentarim, found looting the High Tower's lobby and bagging specific books from a written manifest. Their weapons were bloodied, and one carried the bow used to kill the Watcher on the walkway. One was killed instantly by Grygum's caustic brew.

## Items
### Scroll of Detect Magic
A magical scroll used by Daral to examine the High Tower key. It caused every piece of magical equipment in the room to blaze with light — Dawnbringer most brilliantly of all — but the key remained completely dark, revealing it to be a non-magical decoy.

### Dawnbringer
Thorin's sentient radiant sword, which glowed with evident satisfaction when illuminated by the detect magic scroll. It was used to draw Moziqodo's attention in the rotunda battle and delivered the killing blow that slew the abyssal spawn.

### Decoy High Tower Key
A non-magical fake key given to the party by Kalan Strongbranch to make them a visible but survivable target for the conspirators, while the real key remained hidden with Tadric.

### Real High Tower Key
The genuine artifact required to manage the wards of Candlekeep, recovered from Tadric after the battle with Moziqodo. It is now held by Grygum.

### Watcher's Stair Key
A key provided by Kalan Strongbranch that serves as a shortcut through a barred door leading directly to the north gallery, used by the party to reach Tadric quickly.

### Foreign Crossbow Bolt
A crossbow bolt found in the body of a dead Watcher on an indoor walkway. Daz identified its fletching as foreign to Candlekeep and inconsistent with the bolts used by the Watchers, confirming the attackers came from outside the keep.

### Written Manifest
A list used by the Zhentarim raiders to identify specific books to be looted from the High Tower's collection, suggesting the thefts were carefully directed by an outside party.

### Sapphire Artifact
A magical gem held by A'lai Aivenmore, which he produced and threatened to smash to trigger a specific effect. Before he could destroy it, Daz used telekinesis to wrench it from his grasp. It was then secretly passed to Grygum via sleight of hand, so that Grygum now holds both the real key and the sapphire.
