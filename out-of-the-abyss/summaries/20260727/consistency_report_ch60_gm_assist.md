## 3. Spell Mechanic: "A bolt of radiant energy" deflected by "mage armor"

**Location:** Summary (paragraph 2); Scenes → "Confrontation at the High Tower" (bullet 1)

**Issue:** The Summary says Grygum's "bolt of radiant energy" was deflected by "A'lai Avainmore's magical defenses." The Scenes section specifies that "Grygum's mage armor deflects the attack." Mage Armor is an AC buff — it does not deflect attacks, it raises the attack roll threshold needed to hit. More importantly, **Mage Armor is a wizard spell that A'lai would cast on himself**, not a defense that "deflects" a radiant bolt. The description implies a magical shield/ward, which is not what Mage Armor does mechanically.

**Evidence:** Daz is the party's wizard (Evoker). Grygum is a Cleric of Bahamut (Life Domain). Neither has Mage Armor on their sheet. A'lai Aivenmore is a drow wizard, and Mage Armor on himself is plausible — but the framing "deflects" misrepresents the mechanic. The more likely ruling at the table was a missed attack roll against high AC.

**Suggested fix:** Change "Grygum's mage armor deflects the attack" to "A'lai Aivenmore's high AC (likely from Mage Armor) caused the attack to miss." In the Summary, replace "magical defenses deflected it entirely" with "the bolt missed" or "A'lai's defenses held."

## 5. Counterspell Sequence: "A'lai uses a counterspell to negate Daz's first attempt … then burns another spell slot to resist the second casting"

**Location:** Scenes → "Confrontation at the High Tower" (bullet 4)

**Issue:** The Scenes section states A'lai "uses a counterspell to negate Daz's first attempt at the earthen grasp, then **burns another spell slot to resist the second casting** — but fails the saving throw and is restrained." *Maximilian's Earthen Grasp* requires a Strength saving throw to avoid being restrained — it does not have a "resist" mechanism that costs a spell slot. "Burns another spell slot to resist" is not a D&D 5e mechanic for a saving throw. A'lai may have used Counterspell on the second attempt as well (which would have cost a slot) and failed the counter, or he may have simply failed the save. The phrasing as written implies a non-existent mechanic.

**Evidence:** Party doc and world state confirm Daz has *Maximilian's Earthen Grasp*. No rule allows spending a spell slot to resist a saving throw.

**Suggested fix:** Clarify to either "A'lai attempted to counterspell the second casting but failed" or "A'lai failed his Strength saving throw against the second casting and was restrained." Remove "burns another spell slot to resist."

## 8. Glabbagool's Bond: "Glabbagool surged into the fray"

**Location:** Summary (paragraph 2); Scenes → "Confrontation at the High Tower" (bullet 2 and bullet 17)

**Issue:** The recap describes Glabbagool as a free-moving combat participant ("Zalthir and Glabbagool surged into the fray"). The party doc and world state both establish that Glabbagool is **bonded to Zalthir's left forearm** — he functions "as a semi-autonomous extension of his body." This bond means Glabbagool's combat participation happens as part of Zalthir's action economy, not as an independent surge across the room.

**Evidence:** World state: "Bonded to Zalthir's **left forearm**, functioning as a semi-autonomous extension of his body." Party doc: "Bonded to **Glabbagool** (fused to his left forearm)." The resume doc describes Glabbagool as "positioned to stabilise" Zalthir after he went unconscious — consistent with the arm-bond.

**Suggested fix:** Reframe Glabbagool's combat participation as happening through/with Zalthir rather than as a separate charging entity. E.g., "Zalthir charged in, Glabbagool's acid lashing from his forearm."

## 10. NPC Status: "Bookworm dead" mentioned in A'lai's monologue

**Location:** Summary (paragraph 2): "he looked around at the carnage — **Bookworm dead**, Darrl nearly killed, Janussi gone, guards slain"

**Issue (two sub-issues):**

**10a. Spelling.** The campaign's canonical spelling is **Bookwyrm** (with a Y), not "Bookworm." This is documented extensively in the VTT corrections glossary: "Bookworm, Bookform, Bookworn…" → **Bookwyrm**. The NPC section of this same recap correctly spells it "Bookwyrm."

**10b. Name in monologue.** The recap attributes "Bookworm dead" to A'lai's apparent survey of carnage. However, the campaign's established canon is that Bookwyrm was killed by **Moziqodo** (her throat torn out), and A'lai's reaction to her death per the prep doc is that he is "quietly appalled by it" because "the killing was not ordered." It is plausible A'lai would reference her death, but the framing ("he looked around at the carnage") is ambiguous about whether this was something he saw in the room or knew about.

**10c. "Darrl" vs. "Daral."** The Summary spells the name "Darrl" in A'lai's monologue context ("Darrl nearly killed"). The canonical spelling throughout all documents is **Daral** (Daral Yashenti). The VTT corrections list: "Daryl, Jarl, Droll, Darrell…" → **Daral**. "Darrl" is a new variant not in the glossary.

**Suggested fix:** Change "Bookworm" to "Bookwyrm" and "Darrl" to "Daral" in the Summary paragraph. Clarify the framing of A'lai's survey if needed.
