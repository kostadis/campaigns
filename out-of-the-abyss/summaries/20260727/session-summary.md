# Chapter 60

**Date:** 2026-07-27

## Summary

The party stood over a healed Tadric when something else changed — the endless chant of prophecies that had filled Candlekeep without interruption fell suddenly and completely silent, a phenomenon Grygum was the first to notice. ("Was this ever mentioned? I don't remember this.") It was, apparently, the first time in history that the chanting had ever stopped, stopping Tadric cold in his tracks. Looking around, Tadric realized that Moziqodo was dead. He urgently insisted that they must tell Sylvira about her son's death, but the party deflected — Daz said "We'll take care of it later," Grygum demurred "It might best be someone who knows Sylvira" — and pressed on toward the High Tower with Tadric insisting on accompanying them. ("I faced Moziqodo!") Grygum cast a protective blessing over his companions as they moved, and along the way they discovered a dead Watcher on an indoor walkway, killed by a crossbow bolt whose fletching Daz immediately recognized as foreign to Candlekeep — proof that outside attackers had already passed through, and recently. Grygum's medical estimate placed the death within the last half hour.

They arrived at the High Tower lobby to find two Zhentarim raiders looting books from a written manifest, their weapons bloodied, one of them carrying the bow that had killed the guard on the walkway. On a second-floor landing above them, waiting near a sealed door that required the High Tower keys to open, stood A'lai Aivenmore himself. Grygum launched a bolt of radiant energy at the wizard in an attempt to catch him off guard ("I'm assuming that I can get a spell off before everybody realizes that we're there"), but A'lai Aivenmore's magical defenses deflected it entirely, and combat erupted in earnest. Zalthir and Glabbagool surged into the fray, delivering punishing blows to the wizard, while Daz conjured a massive hand of compacted soil that erupted from the ground and seized A'lai Aivenmore, crushing him and holding him in place. The wizard burned through his magical reserves trying to resist and escape, ultimately teleporting across the room to free himself, but not before taking significant punishment.

Enraged and cornered, A'lai Aivenmore unleashed a relentless barrage of raw arcane energy, severely wounding Daz and hammering Zalthir hard enough to stagger him, though the monk stayed on his feet. Then, unexpectedly, the wizard paused. He looked around at the carnage — Bookwyrm dead, Daral nearly killed, Janussi gone, guards slain — and his voice, when it came, was not the voice of a megalomaniac. "I have been at Candlekeep for 31 years," he said. "I do not have a house, or a family, or a country. I have a chair! He proposed to take the chair! And I found, I was surprised at this, that I would do a great deal to keep it!" He had not set out to be a murderer, he insisted, and Grygum quipped "I understand now, you didn't… you were going to lose your, you know, your academic honor and privilege." A'lai responded sincerely: "Right! Exactly! Thank you!"

He revealed that he had been secretly removing specific books from the library for eleven years on behalf of a mysterious figure whose courier always arrived wearing a glove over a metal hand. "His name is Manshoon," he said quietly. "You have not heard of it. That is not an accident. It is a career." The party's history checks (Grygum 22, Daz 21) confirmed the name immediately — a legendary and feared wizard tied to the Zhentarim, a man who had died more than once and kept returning. Daz remarked, "Well, if he's been dead more than once, then maybe he's gonna be dead one more time."

As if to punctuate his confession, A'lai Aivenmore produced the stolen sapphire and made clear he intended to smash it. But Daz was faster, reaching out with telekinesis and wrenching the gem directly from the wizard's grasp before he could act. ("You have the useful spell. I wish I'd had something like that. Good job, though.") Moving quickly and with practiced sleight of hand (rolled 19), Daz feigned a retreat into the hallway while secretly pressing the sapphire into Grygum's keeping — so that Grygum now held both the real High Tower key and the artifact, while A'lai Aivenmore seethed with fury at having been outmaneuvered. That fury found a target: A'lai Aivenmore turned on Zalthir with a flurry of arcane bursts and beat the monk down until he collapsed unconscious, with Glabbagool standing protectively over his fallen body. Thorin engaged the Zhentarim thugs directly, striking one with Dawnbringer and using Menacing Attack, though the hardened thug resisted the psychological pressure. Grygum surged forward and unleashed Tasha's Caustic Brew, coating both thugs in acid; one died instantly as the corrosive brew melted through him (8 damage on top of Thorin's previous blows), and the other stood soaked in acid, the session ending with the battle still raging.

## Memorable Moments

- **The Silence Falls:** Grygum notices the endless chant has stopped. "Was this ever mentioned? I don't remember this." The DM explains the prophecies are continuously read and deleted when fulfilled. Then: "So, if Grygum is anything like me, then, I haven't noticed it for, like, the last 3 days anyway. Once it stops, I notice that it stopped."

- **The Buck-Passing:** After Tadric insists Sylvira must be told her son is dead, Grygum says "It might best be someone who knows Sylvira." The DM notes the awkward silence. Daz: "They want to be empathetic, and, like, they would have the best understanding of how to be the empathetic… to her, as they explain this unfortunate news. And it's passing the buck." The DM: "And it's passing the buck, right?"

- **Sherlock Daz:** The DM rules that with Passive Investigation 24, Daz is "basically Sherlock Holmes at this point." Daz explains the deduction: "He sees that the cabbie didn't shave the back of his neck, and so he knows that his wife is dead. Because his wife would have told him that the back of his neck needed a shave." The DM confirms: "We're basically at that level of investigation at 24."

- **The Wizard's Defense:** As Grygum's Guiding Bolt misses, the DM notes A'lai has mage armor. Grygum's subsequent roll with Bless yields a 1 on the d4 and a failed attack. "Like, that is, like, a 1 in 56 chance."

- **The Counterspell Gambit:** Daz casts Maximilian's Earthen Grasp. The DM: "He's gonna try to counterspell it." Daz is surprised: "He can counterspell?" The spell gets countered, but Daz recasts it next turn, forcing A'lai to either eat the spell or burn another counterslot. "So he has to decide whether or not he wants to be entangled and 15 points of damage." A'lai fails the save and is restrained, then uses Misty Step to escape — but the DM concedes: "You did force him to burn one of his spells, so he can't cast a fireball, for example."

- **"I Have a Chair":** A'lai's monologue: "I have been at Candlekeep for 31 years. I do not have a house, or a family, or a country. I have a chair! He proposed to take the chair! And I found, I was surprised at this, that I would do a great deal to keep it!" Daz: "So he… this is just… this is the dude with the red stapler in office space, and he snapped."

- **Constructive Criticism Mid-Combat:** After A'lai compliments the party ("You've been so thorough. I want that on the record before anything else happens, because I don't expect either of us will have time for compliments."), Grygum replies: "I mean, I also accept constructive criticism."

- **The Gloved Courier:** A'lai reveals the mysterious courier: "I have never once chosen which ones. A list arrives, I fill it. The courier who brings the list wears a glove on his right hand, and under the glove the hand is metal, and I have never in 11 years worked up the nerve to ask why." Daz: "So, there's, like…" Grygum: "Who… who do you work for, then?" A'lai: "His name is Manshoon. You have not heard of it. That is not an accident. It is a career." Daz's history check confirms: "But that's not possible."

- **Dead More Than Once:** Daz on Manshoon: "Well, if he's been dead more than once, then maybe he's gonna be dead one more time." The DM: "After we're on." Grygum: "with them, sure."

- **The Telekinetic Snatch:** Daz uses telekinesis to grab the sapphire before A'lai can smash it. The DM asks what the save DC is — Daz's Spell DC is 17. A'lai fails his strength save. "Alright, so you managed to wrench the artifact out of his hands." Grygum: "You have the useful spell. I wish I'd had something like that. Good job, though."

- **The Key-to-Grygum Shell Game:** Daz rolls Sleight of Hand 19 to secretly pass the sapphire to Grygum while pretending to retreat. The DM notes: "I'm laughing at the fact that Grygum gave Daz the fake key earlier, and now Daz is faking the sapphire to Grygum." Grygum: "I now have one key and one sapphire."

- **Zalthir Goes Down:** The DM narrates Zalthir taking multiple arcane bursts and being knocked unconscious. "Zalthir just got knocked out." Daz blames himself: "That's my bad. I was a little bitch and grabbed the sapphire and then ran away." The DM: "Fortunately, Zalthir is not here to correct me with all of the caveats… Zalthir would have figured out how to sleazebag himself out of this."

- Grygum notices that the endless chant of prophecies — a constant sound throughout Candlekeep — has suddenly and unnervingly stopped. He asks if this was ever mentioned before; the DM clarifies it's a continuous reading of prophecies that get deleted when fulfilled.

## Locations

### Candlekeep
The great library fortress where the party is investigating a conspiracy involving stolen keys, murdered scholars, and ancient protective wards. The endless chant of prophecies that normally fills the keep falls silent during this session — an event that has never happened before. The DM describes it as "one of the most important things about Candlekeep" — the prophecies are continuously read, and every time a prophecy comes true, it is deleted from the chant.

### Bookwyrm's Office
The site of a gruesome murder where Bookwyrm was found with her throat torn apart by claws and teeth. A half-finished note on her desk read: 'He's using the beast to...'

### Sea Warden's Tower
The location where the party found Kalan Strongbranch frantically buckling on his armor. It is here that he confessed to the fake key scheme and deputized the party as Watchers of Candlekeep.

### North Gallery Rotunda
A domed chamber in the northern galleries where the party fought and killed Moziqodo to rescue Tadric, who had been posted there quietly to guard the real High Tower key.

### North Gallery Walkway
An indoor passage leading toward the High Tower where the party discovered a Watcher killed by a crossbow bolt with foreign fletching, indicating outside attackers had already passed through within the last half hour. The DM drew a quick map: a narrow walkway with stairs leading up, the body found ahead of the party.

### High Tower Lobby (The Skyscraping Lobby)
The entrance to the High Tower of Candlekeep. The DM described it as a large room with bookshelves, stairs, and a second-floor landing 7-15 feet above the main floor. It features a sealed door that requires the High Tower keys to open. The lobby held two Zhentarim raiders looting books from a written manifest while A'lai Aivenmore waited on the landing above. The room includes bookshelves and a table where A'lai was seated, and the DM hinted there "may be other people in the room you can't currently see."

## NPCs

### Fembris
An acolyte who was quietly present throughout the party's stay at Candlekeep. Under pressure, he broke down and confessed that A'lai Aivenmore was present in the room when the key's location was reported to Bookwyrm, making him the crucial link that exposed the conspiracy's architect.

### Bookwyrm
A high-ranking member of Candlekeep who had possessed one of the real High Tower keys. She was brutally murdered — her throat torn apart by Moziqodo's claws — and left a half-finished note reading 'He's using the beast to...' on her desk.

### Kalan Strongbranch
A Watcher of Candlekeep who admitted to giving the party a fake decoy key to make them a visible but survivable target. He entrusted the real key to his lieutenant Tadric, deputized the party as Watchers, and provided them with a Watcher's stair key — though he quickly fell behind when trying to keep pace with them, huffing and puffing.

### Tadric
Kalan's loyal lieutenant who unknowingly held the real High Tower key and was nearly killed by Moziqodo before being rescued and healed by the party. Shaken but emboldened by his survival, he insisted on accompanying the party: "I faced Moziqodo! I faced the full… no, I, I, I will…" Grygum's offer of "resurrection magic if worst comes to worst" convinced him. He is the one who first suggests Sylvira must be told her son is dead.

### Moziqodo
The terrifying abyssal spawn son of Sylvira, responsible for the murder of Bookwyrm and the near-death of Tadric. He was slain by the party in the north gallery rotunda.

### A'lai Aivenmore
A powerful drow wizard and long-time scholar of Candlekeep who orchestrated the conspiracy to protect his academic position. He revealed he has been secretly funneling specific books out of the library for 11 years on behalf of Manshoon and the Zhentarim — his courier always wears a glove over a metal hand on his right. He is self-preserving, desperate, and willing to use lethal magic: he Counterspelled Daz's first spell, used Misty Step to escape restraint, and unleashed a flurry of arcane bursts. But he also delivered a surprisingly sympathetic monologue: "I have been at Candlekeep for 31 years. I do not have a house, or a family, or a country. I have a chair!" He expressed gratitude when Grygum seemed to understand his position: "Thank you! Thank you!" He carried the stolen sapphire, which Daz telekinetically snatched from him. The party's history checks confirmed the name Manshoon — a legendary Zhentarim wizard who has died more than once.

### Manshoon
A legendary and feared wizard associated with the Zhentarim and Zhentil Keep, identified by A'lai Aivenmore as the true architect behind the book thefts. A'lai described the name as a career: "You have not heard of it. That is not an accident." The party's history checks confirmed the name and noted he has died more than once. Daz: "Well, if he's been dead more than once, then maybe he's gonna be dead one more time."

### Glabbagool
An intelligent gelatinous ooze and loyal companion to the party. During the battle at the High Tower, Glabbagool assisted in combat alongside Zalthir and, after Zalthir was knocked unconscious by A'lai's arcane bursts, stood protectively over the fallen monk.

### Zhentarim Raiders
Mercenaries working for the Zhentarim, found looting the High Tower's lobby and bagging specific books from a written manifest. Their weapons were bloodied, and one carried the bow used to kill the Watcher on the walkway. During combat, Thorin struck one with Dawnbringer, and Grygum's Tasha's Caustic Brew coated both: one died instantly when the acid dealt 8 damage at the start of his turn (on top of Thorin's prior damage), the other took 2 damage and was still standing, acid continuing to burn him.

## Items

### Scroll of Detect Magic
A magical scroll used by Daral to examine the High Tower key. It caused every piece of magical equipment in the room to blaze with light — Dawnbringer most brilliantly of all — but the key remained completely dark, revealing it to be a non-magical decoy.

### Dawnbringer
Thorin's sentient radiant sword, which glowed with evident satisfaction when illuminated by the detect magic scroll. The party discussed Dawnbringer's gender: the DM noted she is a "she," and Thorin said "I had a girlfriend in high school named Dawn. God help me." Dawnbringer was used in the battle against the Zhentarim thugs, dealing 13 points of damage on one strike.

### Decoy High Tower Key
A non-magical fake key given to the party by Kalan Strongbranch to make them a visible but survivable target for the conspirators, while the real key remained hidden with Tadric.

### Real High Tower Key
The genuine artifact required to manage the wards of Candlekeep, recovered from Tadric after the battle with Moziqodo. It is now held by Grygum. The party has one of two keys; A'lai was waiting for Moziqodo — who had the other — to show up at the High Tower.

### Watcher's Stair Key
A key provided by Kalan Strongbranch that serves as a shortcut through a barred door leading directly to the north gallery, used by the party to reach Tadric quickly.

### Foreign Crossbow Bolt
A crossbow bolt found in the body of a dead Watcher on an indoor walkway. Daz identified its fletching as foreign to Candlekeep and inconsistent with the bolts used by the Watchers, confirming the attackers came from outside the keep. The DM initially forgot the bow but corrected himself. Grygum noted the oddity of an arrow kill indoors.

### Written Manifest
A list used by the Zhentarim raiders to identify specific books to be looted from the High Tower's collection, suggesting the thefts were carefully directed by an outside party.

### Sapphire Artifact
A magical gem held by A'lai Aivenmore, which he produced and threatened to smash to trigger a specific effect. Before he could destroy it, Daz used Telekinesis (5th level) to wrench it from his grasp — A'lai failed a strength save against DC 17. Daz then used Sleight of Hand (19) to secretly pass the gem to Grygum while feigning a retreat, so that Grygum now holds both the real key and the sapphire. The DM notes they don't know what smashing it would do — "For all we know, it summons an elemental to kill us all."
