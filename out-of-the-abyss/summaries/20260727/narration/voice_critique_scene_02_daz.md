# Voice Critique — Daz, scene 02: The Path to the High Tower

**Narration:** `session_doc_scene_02_the_path_to_the_high_tower.md` (re-run 17:48 from `scene_extractions_smoothed/`)
**Voice spec:** `voice/daz_voice.md`
**Per-char examples:** `examples/daz.md`
**Genre spec:** `voice/_genre.md`
**Supersedes:** the 17:03 critique of the pre-smoothed draft

## Flags

### [1] Two bookkeeping nouns stacked in a single sentence — the failure mode the spec names explicitly

> Half an hour. I added it to the **running tally** without enjoying the **arithmetic**: outside actors, foreign fletching, a route identical to ours, a lead of thirty minutes at most.

**Why:** `_genre.md` permits Daz a second recording metaphor *"only if the two use different nouns and sit paragraphs apart — **never stacked in one sentence**."* This puts both in one clause. `daz_voice.md` adds: *"rotate the bookkeeping vocabulary… rather than letting one noun dominate a scene."* The stacking is what makes it read as writerly rather than habitual.

**Suggested rewrite:** "Half an hour. I added it to the running tally and did not enjoy the total: outside actors, foreign fletching, a route identical to ours, a lead of thirty minutes at most."

### [2] "geometry" is still Zalthir's word *(recurrence — flagged in the previous run, survived the re-run)*

> Grygum studied the ceiling, then the walls, working the same **geometry** I was.

**Why:** `zalthir_voice.md` uses *geometry* twice as his own lexicon (*"battlefield geometry"*, *"ensuring the geometry is wrong for whoever's swinging"*). Daz's register is Menzoberranzan bookkeeping — leverage, debt, audit, account. This is the same flag as the pre-smoothing draft, in the same scene, on the same beat, which means it is not a source problem and will not be fixed by another re-run. Scene 03 then uses *geometry* again for the same referent, so the word appears twice in the document for the identical thing.

**Suggested rewrite:** "Grygum studied the ceiling, then the walls, arriving where I had already arrived."

### [3] Grygum's "+5 medicine" is dropped again *(recurrence)*

> "Got it. So, I'm not a medical expert or anything, but… oh, well, actually, I am." He touched the Watcher's jaw, then his hands… "How long ago do I think he was shot?" A pause. Not very long. Within the last half hour.

**Why:** The smoothed source ends that joke `"…oh, well, actually, I am. **I have plus 5 medicine.**"` The number is the punchline, and `grygum_voice.md` names `"I have a + [thing]"` as one of his three verbal tics — *"capability surfaced as quiet information."* `_genre.md` also wants mechanics in the prose, not sanded off it. The narration sets the joke up and cuts the landing for the second run running.

**Suggested rewrite:** `"…oh, well, actually, I am. I have plus five in medicine."` — then keep the examination beat as written.

### [4] Em-dash count went up, 5 → 9 — the only regression in the document

Nine narration-level em-dashes in a 30-line section, three of them (L29, L37, L49) doing plain appositive work:

- L11 `kept an eye on him — two halves of the same decision` → comma
- L15 `Sound allocation — cheap, broad, works on everything` → colon
- L19 `some distance ahead — arranged the way bodies arrange themselves` → comma
- L29 `The noticing does itself now — that is what the education was for` → period
- L37 `Not from the area — not the pattern, not the cut` → colon
- L49 `from the direction we were approaching — which meant whoever loosed it` → comma
- L53 `the way he makes everything — thoroughly, and while narrating` → colon
- L59 `We went on — slower now, spread out` → comma

**Leave:** L29's second (`the man's wife was dead — because his wife would have told him…`), which carries the reveal.

### [5] Milo Goodbarrel has been replaced by generic Menzoberranzan training

> In Menzoberranzan they teach it with stories. There was one about a House servant who stopped shaving the back of his neck, and the instructor who knew from that alone that the man's wife was dead…

**Why:** Not an error — Menzoberranzan training is one of Daz's two canonical thought-sources, and the section correctly uses only one intrusion. But the previous draft routed the table's out-of-world Sherlock Holmes joke through **Milo Goodbarrel**, the halfling memoirist Daz has demonstrably read (`docs/daz_backstory.md`: his journal habit comes from Milo). Milo is the more distinctive marker, he is now absent from the entire document, and he is load-bearing for the Manshoon handout you just built. Menzoberranzan is the safer choice and the flatter one.

**Suggested rewrite:** Restore Milo — `Milo Goodbarrel has a chapter on it: a constable who glances at the unshaved back of a coachman's neck and knows the man's wife is dead, because a wife would have told him the back of his neck needed a shave. I used to think Milo was embellishing.`

### [6] Generalizing portrait construction — minor

> …and made his assessment the way he makes everything — thoroughly, and while narrating.

**Why:** Same family as the banned `with the particular [noun] of [someone who…]` — it attributes behaviour to a *manner* rather than naming the action. Mild here, because "and while narrating" is genuinely funny and genuinely Grygum.

**Suggested rewrite:** "…and made his assessment out loud, the whole way through, because he does."

## Held correctly (no action)

- `"A man who hands you a key and then insists on climbing the stairs after it is not going to be talked out of the stairs."` — clean Daz logic, no bookkeeping noun required.
- `"because I notice armaments the way other people notice weather"` — his eye rendered as temperament rather than as an audit.
- `*At least we have that.*` — canonical signature, italicised, correctly placed on the Bless.
- `"The lesson was that everything testifies, whether or not it is asked to."` — Menzoberranzan curriculum as method, exactly as the spec describes.
- **Verbatim residue cleared** — `let's, let's`, `Okay, so, and knowing what we know`, and the tripled `who are` are all gone.

## Verdict

The stacked `running tally` / `arithmetic` sentence is the one hard spec violation, and the recurring `geometry` proves that flag needs a hand-edit rather than another run. The em-dash climb from 5 to 9 makes this the only section that got noisier in the re-run.
