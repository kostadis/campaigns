## Flags

### [1] Meaning change — the Dawnbringer referent is still lost *(persists across the re-run)*

> There was an awkward silence while the four of us looked at each other. The news had several complicating features. We were most of them.

**Why:** The smoothed source preserves `"There's this awkward silence as the four of you look at her, right?"` byte-identical, because you ruled that **"her" is Dawnbringer** and both prior passes protected the line. The narration still renders it as the four PCs looking at *each other*, so the beat — the party silently nominating a sword to go tell a grieving mother — is absent for the second run in a row. The source was correct this time; the rendering dropped it anyway.

**Suggested rewrite:** "There was an awkward silence, and then, one at a time, the four of us looked at Dawnbringer. The news had several complicating features. We were most of them."

*(The last two sentences are the best writing in the scene. Keep them exactly.)*

### [2] The voice spec's illustrative example reproduced verbatim

> My old teacher had a habit of asking, at exactly the wrong moment, *what are you optimizing for?*

**Why:** `grygum_voice.md` offers this as an *illustration* of the device — *"someone who had a habit of asking 'what are you optimizing for?' at exactly the wrong moment."* The narration lifts the phrasing word for word, including "at exactly the wrong moment." The spec describes a *habit*; it is not a stock line to be quoted. Scene 03 has the same problem with Brother Quellin, so this is a document-level pattern rather than a one-off.

**Suggested rewrite:** Keep the teacher, change the question to one this scene earns: `My old teacher used to interrupt at the worst possible moment to ask what, exactly, I thought I was measuring. Standing in a keep that had gone silent for the first time in centuries, I could have told him: the tower. We were measuring the tower.`

### [3] Bookkeeping metaphors at two; Grygum's cap is one

> I put it in my notes anyway. *(L43)*

> Everything else went in the margin. *(L69)*

**Why:** `_genre.md` — *"Hard cap: at most ONE bookkeeping/recording metaphor per section… Daz is the only POV allowed a second."* Both are canonical Grygum vocabulary (*notes*, *the margin* are on his approved list), which is why neither reads wrong alone. Together they exceed the cap, and they sit at the two most weighted positions in the section: the chant realisation and the closing turn.

**Suggested rewrite:** Cut the margin. L69 ends stronger on the plain statement: `…I could have answered him: we were optimizing for the tower.` Full stop.

### [4] Unattributed dialogue in a first-person section

> "We'll take care of it later," Daz said.
>
> "Yeah, it's a later… it's a later—"

**Why:** The second line has no speaker. In a Grygum-POV section an untagged quote defaults to reading as someone *other* than the narrator, but the following paragraph (`It was a later kind of problem`) implies Grygum is the one trailing off — so the reader briefly misassigns it and then has to reread.

**Suggested rewrite:** `"Yeah," I said. "It's a later… it's a later—"` — or cut the line entirely; the paragraph after it does the same work better.

### [5] Em-dash — 4 narration-level, the cleanest section in the document

Down from 10. None are egregious; two are worth converting:

- L33 `carry sound a long way — stone corridors, high vaults` → colon
- L71 `he took the lead — walking a little too fast` → comma

**Leave:** L59 (`Daz picked it up smoothly — whoever told her…`) and L67 (`now in our possession — that was a now kind of problem`), both of which are doing genuine pivot work.

## Held correctly (no action)

- **`"It usually does when I'm the one applying it, and I don't advertise why."`** — Disciple of Life rendered exactly as the spec asks: *"He never mentions this."* Mechanics as character, no number, no boast. Best line in the section.
- `"Fair. Wrong, but fair."` / `"I said them in the order that was kindest."` / `"Theologically airtight. Judging by his face, not the comfort he was after."` — three consecutive beats of the dry, self-aware working-priest register.
- `"Bahamut may or may not have been listening. The chant certainly wasn't."` — the late-arc temperature, correct, with a joke on the end.
- **Verbatim residue cleared.** No `you know,` filler, no tripled `I, I, I` in Tadric's line beyond what the smoothed layer carries.
- Zero portable tics (`the shape of X`, `with the particular…`, `cusp of something`).

## Verdict

The Dawnbringer line is the only flag that changes what happened at the table, and it has now survived two runs from two different source layers — it needs a hand-edit, not another re-narration. Everything else is one bookkeeping noun over cap and a spec example quoted too literally; the voice itself is the strongest of the four sections.
