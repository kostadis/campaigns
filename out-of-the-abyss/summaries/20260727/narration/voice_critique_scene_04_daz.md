## Flags

### [1] The section still opens on scene 03's closing beats *(recurrence — but the cause is upstream)*

> Grygum wanted a blessing before we did anything irreversible. "I'll cast Bless on… the three of us who are here." Then, sorting his priorities out loud: "I'm more worried about the wizard than about these raiders…"
>
> …He loosed it before anyone answered. The bolt splashed against nothing at all, a hand's breadth from A'lai's chest — mage armor, laid down long before we arrived.

**Why:** Lines 9–17 re-narrate the Bless, the wizard-vs-raiders assessment, Thorin's kiting offer, and the Guiding Bolt / mage armor — all four of which the reader has just finished in Zalthir's voice at the end of scene 03. `_genre.md` bans recap framing: *"Let the next scene reveal what carried over."*

**Important difference from the last run:** the two source extractions genuinely overlap on these beats, so this is a **scene-boundary problem, not a narration problem.** No amount of re-running fixes it. Either trim scene 04's opening, or move the boundary upstream.

**Suggested rewrite:** Open at line 19 — `I ran the audit while the room broke into motion.` Everything before it is already on the page.

### [2] Cross-narrator convergence — the same idiom, on the same beat, in two voices

> **Right on both counts.** Everything we had heard made A'lai Aivenmore a powerful wizard. And powerful wizards are more obsessed with staying alive than with fighting to the death. *(scene 03, Zalthir)*

> **Correct on both counts.** A'lai Aivenmore was a powerful wizard — and powerful wizards share one trait with Menzoberranzan matrons: they are far more invested in staying alive than in dying well. *(scene 04, Daz)*

**Why:** Two different narrators respond to the identical Grygum line with the identical construction, three sentences apart in structure. This is exactly the convergence `_genre.md` warns about — the sentence is portable, which is why both reached for it. Daz's version is the better one (the matron comparison is his and only his); Zalthir's is the one to change.

**Suggested rewrite:** Keep scene 04 as written. Change scene 03's to `He was right twice.`

### [3] Three bookkeeping metaphors; Daz's cap is two

> I ran the **audit** while the room broke into motion. *(L19)*

> He did the **arithmetic** and chose to take it. *(L27)*

> The item was **sorted into the correct drawer**, and the drawer was an orc. *(L83)*

**Why:** The nouns are correctly rotated and well spaced — this is a count problem, not a register problem. `_genre.md` allows Daz two. Note also that *arithmetic* appears in scene 02 as well (`without enjoying the arithmetic`), so the same narrator uses it in two sections.

**Suggested rewrite:** Cut the middle one, which is the weakest and the one that duplicates scene 02. `He weighed it and chose to take it.` Keep the audit; keep the drawer — *"the drawer was an orc"* is the best sentence in the document.

### [4] Em-dash — 13 narration-level, still the highest in the document

Down from 18, but L91 alone carries three:

> …the raiders resolved into what they were — Zhentarim men, hired hands of the same career we'd just been introduced to… He snarled something meant to break the man's nerve; the man's nerve held — he was Zhentarim, after all — so Thorin simply hit him again.

**Convert:** L11, L19, L33, L75 (×2), L83, L91 (×3), L93. Break L91 into separate sentences regardless — it is currently five clauses.

**Leave:** L17 (`a hand's breadth from A'lai's chest — mage armor`), L29 (`I said — and was wrong`, a genuine self-interruption), L79 (`Telekinesis — the heaviest thing I carry`).

### [5] "*At least we have that.*" appears in two sections of one document

`_genre.md` says to use the ensemble's stock phrases verbatim because they are signatures, so this is sanctioned — but scene 02 already spent it on the Bless, and here it lands on the much stronger beat (A'lai out of good spells). The earlier use dilutes the later one.

**Suggested rewrite:** Drop it from scene 02, where the line before it (`Sound allocation — cheap, broad, works on everything`) already carries the beat.

### [6] The red stapler — improved, with a residual note

> "So he… this is just… this is the dude with the red stapler, and he snapped," I said. No one asked me to elaborate.

**Why:** The previous draft named *office space* outright; dropping the film title is the right call, and `"No one asked me to elaborate"` is a clean save that hangs a lampshade on Daz saying something nobody in the room can parse. Flagging only so the choice is deliberate: the stapler is still an out-of-world reference, just an oblique one. If it plays at the table, keep it.

## Held correctly (no action)

- **`*I have an alternative proposal.*` placed immediately before Telekinesis.** Italicised, unspoken, and landing on the single most decisive action of the session. This is the signature phrase used better than anywhere else in the document.
- **`"Being a Drow, you pick some stuff up."`** — lifted correctly from the spec's *Things he'd say*, and dropped on the Manshoon recognition where it does double duty as deflection.
- `"The item was sorted into the correct drawer, and the drawer was an orc."`
- `"Thirty-one years of lifting books."` — the entire tactical read in five words.
- `"hired hands of the same career we'd just been introduced to"` — callback to *"It is a career"* without restating it.
- `"I felt my margin shrink by a third in the space of a breath."` — mechanics rendered as sensation, no number.
- `"a reaction is a resource, and he had just spent one"` — Menzoberranzan framing applied to the action economy.
- A'lai's monologues intact: the chair, the eleven years, the metal hand, `"His name is Manshoon… It is a career."`, and the `"Well, fewer people had to die"` walk-back.
- **Verbatim residue cleared** — the tripled `who are`, `than, about these raiders`, and `I, sort of jumped the gun` are all gone.

## Verdict

The recap opening is the one structural flag, and it is an extraction-boundary problem that re-running will never fix — trim it by hand. Voice fidelity is the highest in the document: two signature deployments land perfectly, and the Menzoberranzan register carries the fight without a single borrowed word.
