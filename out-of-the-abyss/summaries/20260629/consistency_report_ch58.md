## Issue 1: Bookworm's name/species never stated in the recap, but an inconsistency is present

**Location:** NPCs → Bookworm; Summary; Scenes → Confrontation in Bookworm's Office

**Issue:** The recap describes Bookworm as "the current acting head of Candlekeep." The campaign documents consistently name her **Bookwyrm** (not "Bookworm") and identify her as a **green dragonborn** (world_state §5: "Bookwyrm (Skoda Vanaster, green dragonborn First Reader)"). The recap uses "Bookworm" throughout. Additionally, Bookwyrm is not merely "acting head" — she holds the title **First Reader**, which is her formal role. Janussi as Keeper of Tomes was the executive head; Bookwyrm is First Reader, a distinct senior office.

**Evidence:** campaign_state NPC table: "Bookwyrm (First Reader) — Alive — Candlekeep"; world_state §5: "Bookwyrm (Skoda Vanaster, green dragonborn First Reader)"; player_npc_tracker: "Bookwyrm — First Reader of Candlekeep. Dragonborn sage."

**Suggested fix:** Replace every instance of "Bookworm" with "Bookwyrm." Correct her title to "First Reader" rather than "acting head" (or, if "acting head" is meant to reflect that Janussi's death left the Keeper of Tomes role vacant, clarify that she is First Reader who is now effectively running Candlekeep in the absence of a Keeper of Tomes).

## Issue 4: Dawnbringer described as being used to "cure Daral of his affliction" — this is not a standard sunblade ability

**Location:** Items → Dawnbringer; Scenes → Waiting in Deneir's Sanctum; Summary

**Issue:** The recap states "Dawnbringer was finally called upon to cure Daral of his lingering affliction" and the Items entry lists this as one of Dawnbringer's functions. Dawnbringer's documented ability is **Lesser Restoration** (party.md: "Dawnbringer's lesser restoration"; campaign_state key resources: "Dawnbringer (sentient sunblade, radiant, locking Vaultmaster scabbard)"). Lesser Restoration can cure certain conditions (paralysis, poison, disease, blindness, deafness) but cannot cure all magical ailments. The recap does not specify what Daral's affliction was, which matters because Lesser Restoration has defined limits. The phrasing "cure Daral of his affliction" is ambiguous and may overstate the ability.

**Evidence:** party.md lists "Dawnbringer's lesser restoration" as a resource. The campaign_state active quest §6: "Dawnbringer's therapy — Two-stage process at Candlekeep (light therapy, then grief work). In progress." player_npc_tracker: Daral Yashenti is listed as "Prime suspect; under watch until midnight" — not as someone with an established affliction requiring magical cure. No prior session note establishes Daral's specific affliction.

**Suggested fix:** Specify what Daral's affliction was and confirm it falls within the scope of Lesser Restoration. If the cure used a different mechanism, name it. Add a note clarifying that Dawnbringer cast Lesser Restoration (not a full Cure Disease or Remove Curse, which would require a cleric spell slot from Grygum or a different item).

## Issue 5: "Kalan Strongbranch was reinstated to his position" — this contradicts the session design document

**Location:** Summary; Scenes → Confrontation in Bookworm's Office; NPCs → Kalan Strongbranch

**Issue:** The recap states Kalan "was reinstated to his position, his methodology vindicated at last." The GM planning document (20260629_candlekeep_the_gatewarden_lives.md) states explicitly that Bookwyrm "wins the procedure" in this session, shreds the confession using Kalan's own paper, and that Kalan remains sidelined. The design document's Scene 3 has Bookwyrm keeping the party under watch and the case not landing on her — Kalan being fully reinstated would contradict the "you can't win the case" outcome the session was designed to produce. If Kalan was genuinely reinstated to full authority this session, that is significant new canon that changes the trajectory of subsequent sessions (Bookwyrm was supposed to die before the case could be properly concluded).

**Evidence:** 20260629 doc, Scene 3: "She makes them stay. Night falls. The Beast kills on schedule." Scene 1: Kalan says "she walks." The design intent is that Bookwyrm wins procedurally and Kalan's reinstatement is not the session's outcome — survival of Kalan and his methodology is, not his official restoration. The campaign_state still lists Kalan as "Alive (fearful) — Candlekeep — Cooperative."

**Suggested fix:** Clarify whether Kalan was formally reinstated (which would be a major departure from the session design) or whether Bookwyrm made a more limited gesture — such as publicly acknowledging his methodology was not wrong — while still maintaining practical control. If he was genuinely reinstated to full Gatewarden authority, flag this as significant new canon affecting downstream sessions.

## Issue 8: The helmed horrors' targeting logic — recap implies they tracked the key magically, but the design document specifies they were sent to the party's known location via the bugged sanctum

**Location:** Summary; Scenes → Ambush of the Helmed Horrors; Memorable Moments

**Issue:** The recap frames the mystery as "somehow they had known exactly who was holding a key that no spell in Candlekeep should have been able to detect" — presenting the constructs' knowledge of the key's location as the central unanswered question. However, the GM planning document is explicit that the constructs were sent to Deneir's Sanctum because **A'lai knew the party was there via the bugged sanctum**, not because they were tracking the key magically. The constructs' objective was to seize the decoy key from whoever held it (Daz) and extract. The mystery should be *why mindless constructs had a specific objective* (i.e., who sent them and programmed them), not *how they detected an inert key*. The recap's framing could mislead future sessions into treating "how did they find the key" as the puzzle rather than "who sent constructs programmed to retrieve it."

**Evidence:** 20260629 doc, A1: "The guardians are programmed to seize the decoy key off Daz and extract." A1: "The party's bugged base — the fight; the bugged office is why A'lai's contingency knows where they are." The constructs were aimed at the party's location, not tracking the key by magic.

**Suggested fix:** Revise the closing observation to note that the horrors targeted the known location of the party (the sanctum) and were specifically programmed to seize the key Daz carried — the unsettling question is not how they detected an inert key but who knew to send constructs to this room with orders to take it, and how they knew Daz was the keyholder.

## Issue 9: "Grygum called down a burst of holy radiance" — Word of Radiance is mischaracterized as a burst, and its range is inconsistent with the scenario described

**Location:** Spells → Word of Radiance; Scenes → Ambush of the Helmed Horrors

**Issue:** The Spells section describes Word of Radiance as "a burst of holy light, damaging two helmed horrors simultaneously that were standing near him." Word of Radiance (as a cantrip) affects all creatures of the caster's choice within 5 feet. The recap's description of this as a "burst" hitting two horrors is mechanically consistent only if two horrors were within 5 feet of Grygum simultaneously. The Summary describes Grygum releasing "a burst of holy radiance that scorched two of the horrors at once" which is plausible but worth noting — more critically, the Spells section calls it "a burst of holy light" which is accurate enough, but the scene description should confirm the horrors were adjacent to Grygum, not at range.

**Evidence:** Word of Radiance (PHB): each creature of the caster's choice within 5 feet. Grygum's sheet (party.md) lists "Spirit Guardians" as a known spell but does not list Word of Radiance explicitly; it is a standard cleric cantrip consistent with his class.

**Suggested fix:** Minor — confirm in the scene that the two horrors struck were within melee range of Grygum. No change needed if this is accurate; flag for future reference that Word of Radiance is a 5-foot-radius cantrip, not a ranged blast.

## Issue 11: "Corrosive Embrace" listed as a spell — it is a sidekick ability, not a spell

**Location:** Spells → Corrosive Embrace

**Issue:** The recap's Spells section lists "Corrosive Embrace" as a spell cast by Glabbagool through Zalthir's arm. Per the campaign documents, Corrosive Embrace is a **ki-powered ability** of Glabbagool as Zalthir's Shadow Apprentice sidekick (20260629 doc, A1: "Corrosive Embrace (2 Ki): DC 13 Dex save, 14 (4d6) acid"). It is not a spell — it is an ability that costs Ki points. Categorizing it as a spell in the Spells section is inaccurate and could cause confusion about whether it is subject to the helmed horrors' Magic Resistance.

**Evidence:** 20260629 doc, A1: "Corrosive Embrace (2 Ki)" — explicitly a Ki ability. The document also notes: "treat Glabba's ooze acid as a physical corrosion, not a spell → the Horror's Magic Resistance gives no advantage on the DC-13 save."

**Suggested fix:** Move Corrosive Embrace out of the Spells section and into a separate "Abilities" or "Combat Techniques" section, or add a parenthetical clarifying it is a ki-powered physical ability, not a spell, and therefore not subject to the horrors' Magic Resistance.
