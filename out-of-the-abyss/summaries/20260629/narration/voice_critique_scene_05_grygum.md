## Flags

### [1] Generic-lyric drift (register)

> So the sword did the work I'd been ready to do, and that suited me fine. The blade woke warm — that clean restoring light it carries, the one that burns twice as bright against rot — and it poured into Daral and took the sickness with it.

**Why:** "the blade woke warm… that clean restoring light… burns twice as bright against rot" is prettier and softer than Grygum's register for magic, which is dry, procedural, and faintly accountant-like. His own voice frames healing as resource management (this same scene: *"Lesser restoration is cheap and I had it to spend"*, `:368`) and the examples show him narrating a divine artifact as *"a great museum piece"* and gold as *"a blessing from Bahamut"* — appraisal, not lyricism. The candle-simile cadence reads as house-style lyric prose bleeding in, not Grygum. Note the *content* is his (he'd been ready to cast it himself, the sword beat him to it) — only the register drifts.
**Suggested rewrite:** "So the sword did the work I'd been ready to do, and that suited me fine — one lesser restoration I now got to keep in the bank. It lit, it poured into Daral, and it took the sickness with it. Adequate. Better than adequate: free." (Keeps the professional ledger frame; "Adequate" echoes his own dry verdicts elsewhere in the scene, e.g. "Fembris did not die. Adequate.")

### [2] Cross-scene repetition — Bahamut closer

> Bahamut may or may not have been watching. I took notes either way.

**Why:** This is Grygum's most load-bearing signature line, and scene 02 (`gm-assist-doc.md:184`) *also* closes on it: *"Bahamut may or may not have been reading over my shoulder. I wrote it down either way."* Using the same refrain to end both of his scenes spends a singular line twice and dilutes it — the second landing reads as a formula rather than the note-taking-as-theology beat it is (grounded in the examples' *"as his mentor, Gorg'Bahamut, said…"* and the chess-story note-taker persona).
**Suggested rewrite:** This scene's phrasing is the cleaner of the two, so if you keep the closer anywhere, keep it *here* and re-end scene 02 on its margin-note image instead. Whichever scene loses the refrain should close on a different Grygum beat (dry appraisal, a filed observation) rather than the Bahamut line.

## Verdict

Strong and warm without going soft anywhere else — "The Underdark teaches you to spend the cheap risk before the dear one" and the taster-promotion joke ("blessedly unaware that he had just been promoted") are excellent dry-cleric voice, and the Dawnbringer/Daral "two theatrical creatures finding each other" observation is genuinely in-character noticing. Two touches: warm the healing description back down into his professional register, and decide which scene keeps the Bahamut closer.
