## Flags

### [1] Convergence with self / cross-scene repetition

> I only took notes and marked, in the margin, that we had come all this way to convict a woman not of murder but of embarrassment, and that everyone in the room seemed content with the trade. Bahamut may or may not have been reading over my shoulder. I wrote it down either way.

**Why:** "Bahamut may or may not have been reading over my shoulder / I wrote it down either way" is Grygum's single most load-bearing signature line — but scene 05 *also* closes on it ("Bahamut may or may not have been watching. I took notes either way."). Using the same refrain to end both his scenes spends a line that should feel singular. The spec calls it "the whole transition compressed into one sentence"; it can't do that job twice in one document.
**Suggested rewrite:** Keep the Bahamut closer in scene 05 (its phrasing is the cleaner of the two) and end this scene on the margin-note image the sentence already has: "…everyone in the room seemed content with the trade. I marked it in the margin and left it there." (Note-taking as ontology, without the refrain.)

### [2] Borderline (low-confidence — likely keep)

> "If things go sideways," Daz offered, "we can always start hacking and slashing." A contingency he keeps in his back pocket like a spare component.

**Why:** flagged only for completeness. "like a spare component" is spell-component vocabulary a cleric would reach for, so it reads in-voice; noting it so the mechanical pass is transparent, not because it's wrong.
**Suggested rewrite:** No change recommended. If trimming for rhythm, "A contingency he keeps in his back pocket" carries it alone.

## Verdict

Genuinely excellent — the Stroud/chess intrusion ("a gold dragon who studied one chess position for four hundred years and lost it to a man who couldn't play") and "I know the arithmetic of that. I have done it for a mushroom" are the strongest voice-landings in the document. Only the cross-scene Bahamut closer is worth touching.
