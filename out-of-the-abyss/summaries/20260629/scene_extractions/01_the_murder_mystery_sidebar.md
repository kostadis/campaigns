# The Murder Mystery Sidebar

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party discusses their next steps after obtaining a confession from Alkrist regarding the murder of Janussi.
- The party sidebars to discuss whether to hand the investigation over to Kalan Strongbranch or confront Bookwyrm directly. The GM laid out three options: "turn it over to Kalan," go to "A'lai" and say "we know things," or "go straight to Bookwyrm and demand justice and satisfaction."
- Dawnbringer expresses a desire for more therapy sessions, feeling they were making progress with the residents of Candlekeep. The GM noted Dawnbringer makes noises directly in Thorin's head, and confirmed the sword is referred to as "she."
- The group decides to let Kalan Strongbranch take the credit for solving the case to help him save face after being fired. Thorin: "I think we should just hand it to him, let him save face, because he got fired, and then... with the understanding that they'll make good on the promise that we get our reward for helping crack the case."
- The party considers the benefits of staying infamous versus becoming famous for solving the high-profile murder. Zalthir: "We're already infamous underground. It didn't seem to serve us well." The GM and Thorin then digressed into the Three Amigos joke about the word "infamous" meaning "extra famous."
