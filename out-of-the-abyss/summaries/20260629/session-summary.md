# Chapter 58 Academic vindication and an assault.

Date: 2026-06-29

## Summary
With Alkrist's confession secured and the physical evidence in hand, the party gathered to debate their next move. Dawnbringer lamented, with genuine feeling, that the promised therapy sessions with Candlekeep's scholars had never materialized, and Thorin could only offer a sympathetic shrug in response. After some deliberation, the group settled on a patient strategy: hand the credit to Kalan Strongbranch, let him save face after his humiliating dismissal, and collect their reward — all while keeping their suspicions about Bookwyrm close to the chest.

Kalan, however, was quick to temper their expectations. He argued with academic precision that a single confession, however dramatic, was not enough to bring Bookwyrm to justice under the rigorous standards of his own investigative framework. He shoved his paper — the thrice-fold proof — into their faces and insisted that the clever play was patience. With the naming ceremony approaching, the stench of suspicion alone would be enough to deny Bookwyrm the position of First Keeper, and the new head of the Avowed would be far more motivated to see her undone. The party agreed, and added a cunning wrinkle of their own: they would use their reward to acquire whatever resource or tome might definitively prove Bookwyrm's role as an accomplice after the fact, striking when she least expected it.

The confrontation in Bookwyrm's office was a masterclass in controlled tension. She received them with regal composure, pouring tea and gesturing for them to sit — an offer the party declined with varying degrees of suspicion. Zalthir, unwilling to be rude but equally unwilling to trust the cup, quietly directed Glabbagool to absorb the tea through his arm, the faint singe of acid the only sign anything unusual had occurred. When Kalan laid out the evidence against Alkrist — the stolen poison, the book, the confession — Bookwyrm's composure cracked, and she appeared heartbroken. But Daz, watching her carefully, noticed something the others missed: beneath the grief, she looked curiously relieved.

The party chose to withhold what they knew about potential accomplices, hinting only that the investigation was not yet complete and that others may have been involved. Bookwyrm performed outrage beautifully, declaring it unthinkable that anyone else could have conspired in the death of the beloved Janussi. Kalan was reinstated to his position, his methodology vindicated at last, and Bookwyrm graciously announced that the party would be given a full opportunity to speak before the naming ceremony. She then arranged for them to be escorted to Deneir's Sanctum — framing it as protection, though Daz sensed it was equally about keeping them from poking their noses any further into Candlekeep's affairs.

A brief detour to Alkrist's room yielded little of obvious consequence. The safe contained gold, a pair of potions, and a gold coin with a red herring engraved upon it — a fitting symbol for an investigation full of misdirection. The Avowed declared the contents unremarkable and moved to close the matter, but Zalthir, never one to miss an opportunity, used Glabbagool to quietly pocket the potion of flying while everyone else was distracted. No one noticed a thing.

The evening in Deneir's Sanctum passed quietly at first. Food was brought in, which the party eyed with deep suspicion, allowing Fembris Lancer to eat first before touching anything themselves. Dawnbringer was finally called upon to cure Daral of his lingering affliction, and the man was so overcome with gratitude that he launched into an enthusiastic monologue about his plans to make sentient magical swords an area of serious academic study — going on at considerable length about the semiotics of the subject while Dawnbringer absorbed every word with evident delight. Daral then attempted to leave the room, only to find it was still locked.

The peace shattered without warning. Three helmed horrors — towering animated suits of plate armor with cold, glowing eyes — smashed through the windows and burst through the door of the sanctum, sending the room into chaos. The constructs moved with terrifying purpose, ignoring most of the party and converging almost entirely on Daz. It was only mid-battle, as the horrors relentlessly bypassed every other target to reach him, that Daz realized with a chill what they were after: the mysterious key he carried, the one that had appeared completely inert to magical detection. He passed it to Grygum and threw himself clear as his companions surged into the fight.

The battle was fierce and disorienting. Zalthir drove his fists into the animated armor while Glabbagool, fused to his arm, unleashed torrents of corrosive acid that began dissolving the constructs' plating. Grygum radiated a burst of holy light that seared two horrors within reach, while Daz hurled volleys of magical fire and force into the fray. Thorin struck with Dawnbringer, imposing disadvantage on one horror's next attack with a sapping blow before cutting it down entirely. One by one, the constructs collapsed into heaps of rust and broken metal. When the last one fell, the party stood in the wreckage of the sanctum, breathing hard and deeply unsettled — because the horrors had been mindless, incapable of thought or speech, and yet somehow they had known exactly who was holding a key that no spell in Candlekeep should have been able to detect. The session ended here, with the party out of time and the mystery of the assassins' knowledge left open.

## Scenes
### The Murder Mystery Sidebar
#### The party discusses their next steps after obtaining a confession from Alkrist regarding the murder of Janussi.
- The party sidebars to discuss whether to hand the investigation over to Kalan Strongbranch or confront Bookwyrm directly. Kostadis laid out three options: "turn it over to Kalan," go to "LI" and say "we know things," or "go straight to Bookwyrm and demand justice and satisfaction."
- Dawnbringer expresses a desire for more therapy sessions, feeling they were making progress with the residents of Candlekeep. Kostadis noted Dawnbringer makes noises directly in Thorin's head, and confirmed the sword is referred to as "she."
- The group decides to let Kalan Strongbranch take the credit for solving the case to help him save face after being fired. Thorin: "I think we should just hand it to him, let him save face, because he got fired, and then... with the understanding that they'll make good on the promise that we get our reward for helping crack the case."
- The party considers the benefits of staying infamous versus becoming famous for solving the high-profile murder. Zalthir: "We're already infamous underground. It didn't seem to serve us well." Kostadis and Thorin then digressed into the Three Amigos joke about the word "infamous" meaning "extra famous."

### The Academic's Dilemma
#### The party confers with Kalan Strongbranch about the implications of Alkrist's confession and how to proceed against Bookwyrm.
- Kalan Strongbranch argues that Alkrist's confession and the physical evidence are insufficient to convict Bookwyrm under his academic standards. His paper "basically says you can't rely on one source of truth, you have to have three different kinds of sources of truth."
- Zalthir pushed back that Kalan's protocol "was never approved" and "was declined," noting the confession was compelled with a legitimate spell.
- The party discusses using a Zone of Truth to confront Bookwyrm, but Kalan expresses concern about her potential political power and refuses to act without her permission. Kalan: "I will not sneak this time. We will walk in, and we will boldly demand that she submit to a zone of truth." (The prior confession worked because Kalan had cast it outside the room while Alkrist didn't realize what was happening.)
- Kalan suggests a patient approach, predicting that the upcoming naming ceremony will naturally result in Bookwyrm's removal from power due to the stench of suspicion. Kalan: "Sometimes the clever play is to be patient... the stench of the suspicion will be all over him." He predicted Fembris Lancer would likely be elected the new avowed and would want to get rid of Bookwyrm.
- Kalan made the cynical academic point that removal from her position "would be far more damning than any crime we could convict her of."
- The party devises a plan to accept their reward first and then use it to acquire a book or resource that can definitively prove Bookwyrm's culpability after the fact. Thorin: "we use our reward to access the thing that will do her in." Kalan promised, "I will come up with such a book before you leave," noting the book would come from the new head of Candlekeep.

### Confrontation in Bookwyrm's Office
#### The party meets with Bookwyrm to present their evidence against Alkrist while navigating the political tensions of Candlekeep.
- The party enters Bookwyrm's office, where she offers them tea which they suspiciously decline. Zalthir pretends to drink by directing Glabbagool (fused to his arm) to absorb the tea, which the ooze burns with acid. Bookwyrm remarked, "With all this talk of murder in the house, I think that a little bit of tea is good for soothing the nerves," and drank some herself.
- Bookwyrm opened by dismissing the confession: "You compelled the grieving man with a borrowed spell, and you call it proof... He wrote that a single working proves nothing."
- Kalan Strongbranch presents the evidence against Alkrist, including the stolen poison, the book, and his confession, cutting in with, "but they have," and enumerating the evidence.
- Bookwyrm appears heartbroken but is observed to be curiously relieved, as Daz picks up on through a keen investigation (a 21). She first cried, "Alkrist, I could never have imagined it," before recovering.
- The party decides to withhold details about potential accomplices to use as leverage later, hinting only that they believe there are others involved. Daz argued for the interview-room tactic he'd learned from "48-hour shows": "give enough details to make it look like... they could tell a story," while withholding critical facts. The one fact the party held back was that Alkrist had fingered someone else as the mastermind.
- Bookwyrm performed outrage over the idea of accomplices: "this is just awful, I can't believe that there would be accomplices in the death of such a great man as Janussi, who is beloved by all."

Bookwyrm: "Kalan, I think that it was unwise of me to not keep you in charge, and so with my sincerest apologies, would you be so willing as to take your position once more." - Bookwyrm instructs Kalan to escort the party to Deneir's Sanctum, framing it as protection but effectively placing them under house arrest until the naming ceremony. Zalthir asked directly, "Are we under house arrest?" Bookwyrm insisted it was "simply doing this to protect you," citing the "other criminal elements within the society."
- Daz's insight roll of 24 revealed Bookwyrm's true state: she was "sincerely worried about your safety" but also "delighted by the idea of keeping you wrapped up and not poking your heads anymore anywhere."

### Ambush of the Helmed Horrors
#### While waiting in Deneir's Sanctum, the party is suddenly attacked by three helmed horrors that smash through the windows and door.
- Three helmed horrors burst through the windows and door of the sanctum, initiating a violent ambush. They were described as animated armor with glowing eyes, capable of walking and hovering.
- The constructs appear to specifically target Daz, ignoring other targets to reach him.
- A helmed horror attempts to grapple Daz but fails as he maneuvers out of its reach — the contested roll ended in a tie (both 15), so Daz was not grappled.
- Daz retaliates by unleashing multiple rays of magical fire (Scorching Ray) at one of the horrors, dealing 24 points of damage. He initially considered Phantasmal Killer before learning from Kostadis that the horrors are constructs with no minds to attack ("They don't have feelings... They have objectives.").
- A second horror engages Zalthir, striking him with a combination of slashing and force damage; Zalthir mitigated the slashing to take 9 total.
- A third horror focuses its attacks on Daz, landing a heavy blow (32 points of damage) that leaves the wizard severely wounded with only three hit points remaining.
- Grygum reassured Daz mid-fight: "don't worry, I've got plenty of heal spells. If you get knocked out, I'll just heal you," adding, "And I also have Revivify, so we're covered, either way." Kostadis added the encouragement, "Now is not the time to worry about being knocked unconscious. Now is the time to cast magic."
- Zalthir strikes back at his attacker, and Glabbagool, fused to his arm, unleashes a corrosive acid (Corrosive Embrace, DC 13 Dexterity save) that begins to dissolve the horror's plate armor, reducing its defenses by 1 AC. The constructs have an armor class of 20.
- Thorin strikes a horror with Dawnbringer, slowing the construct's movements with a sapping blow (disadvantage on its next attack), dealing 14 damage, and used a superiority die for a precision attack on a follow-up.
- Grygum moves into position and releases a burst of holy radiance (Word of Radiance), searing two of the constructs simultaneously for 10 points each.
- Daz, realizing the constructs seem to be tracking the mysterious key he carries (revealed via an insight roll), passes it to Grygum, then cast Magic Missile at a horror for 15 damage. The realization: the horrors were "very determined to get a key... that read inert under detect magic."
- Zalthir grapples one of the remaining horrors while Glabbagool delivers another corrosive embrace (14 damage). The party clarified Glabbagool's Corrosive Embrace has its own pool of "uses" tied to hit points.
- Thorin, using the Sentinel feat, got attacks of opportunity when the horrors struck Grygum instead of him. He delivered the killing blow on one horror (before its second attack could land), and then struck down the last with 22 and 17 points of damage, causing them both to collapse into piles of rust and armor.
- The party is left wondering how the constructs knew who held the inert, magically undetectable key. Zalthir noted only Kalan knew Daz held it, but the horrors went straight for Daz even after Grygum had briefly held it — "implying somehow they could... detect or sense the key." Fembris Lancer was present when the key was exchanged, making him a person of interest.
- The session ended as the party ran out of time before they could interrogate Fembris or otherwise pursue the mystery.

## NPCs
### Kalan Strongbranch
An academic investigator and stickler for his own 'thrice-fold proof' methodology. He argues that Alkrist's confession alone is insufficient to convict Bookwyrm, is cautious about directly confronting her without overwhelming evidence, and ultimately is reinstated to his position after his investigative methods are vindicated. He had cast the Zone of Truth from outside the room during the original confession, catching Alkrist unaware. Upon reinstatement he became self-congratulatory, praising his own methods and joining the chorus mourning "what a great man" Janussi was — despite being another of the people happy to see Janussi dead.

### Bookwyrm
The current First Reader (acting head of Candlekeep; real name Skoda Vanaster, a green dragonborn) and Alkrist's aunt. She appears regal and in control during the confrontation, seeming heartbroken over her nephew's guilt but observed to be curiously relieved. She offered the party tea to "soothe the nerves," reinstated Kalan Strongbranch, and keeps the party under close watch in the sanctum, framing it as protection. She is suspected of extracting Janussi's heart and hiding it in the statue's chalice to cover up her nephew's crime — a conspiracy after the fact, though Kalan notes she "didn't commit a crime," only hid one.

### Alkrist
Bookwyrm's nephew who has confessed to the murder of Janussi. He is currently being held in the prisons of Candlekeep. He provided the diversion at the apothecary that allowed the poison to be stolen. During his confession he named someone else as the mastermind — a fact the party is holding in reserve. The party suspects he had accomplices, including possibly Bookwyrm herself.

### Janussi
The deceased whose murder is the central mystery of the investigation. Multiple characters refer to him with exaggerated reverence as 'beloved by all,' which the party finds darkly ironic. In life he was known for issuing one bad review after another to people with access to powerful magic.

### Fembris Lancer
An adjutant to the First Reader who remains with the party while they are confined to the sanctum, serving as an unwitting food taster and a witness to the exchange of the mysterious key. Kalan predicted a Fembris Lancer would likely be elected as the new head of the Avowed. As a witness to the key exchange, Fembris becomes a person of interest after the ambush. The party's distrust of him predates the ambush — the food-tasting was an early tell.

### Daral
A man suffering from a magical ailment who is cured by Dawnbringer. At this point in the investigation he remained a prime suspect, under watch until midnight to test his innocence. He is present in the sanctum during the ambush and becomes enthusiastically obsessed with studying the properties of sentient magical swords, launching into an extended monologue on their semiotics. When the horrors attacked, he cried out that it was impossible for "these creatures" to be there.

### Helmed Horrors
Three mindless animated suits of plate armor with glowing eyes capable of flight and hover, though awkward in the enclosed room. They smash into Deneir's Sanctum and relentlessly target Daz, apparently homing in on the mysterious key he carries. They have an armor class of 20, general magic resistance (advantage on saves against spells), and immunity to three unknown specific spells chosen by their creator. Being constructs, they have "no intelligence or thought of their own" — only objectives — and cannot speak. They are destroyed in combat, collapsing into piles of rust and armor.

## Locations
### Bookwyrm's Office
A regal space where the First Reader receives guests and conducts official business. Bookwyrm offered the party tea here during a tense confrontation over the murder investigation, drinking some herself while the party declined.

### Alkrist's Room
The living quarters of Bookwyrm's nephew, containing a personal safe that held 750 gold pieces, potions, and a coin with a red herring engraved on it. The safe was placed under guard by Kalan Strongbranch's order and ultimately closed with its contents left inside, declared of no interest.

### Deneir's Sanctum
A chamber filled with bookshelves and tables within Candlekeep where the party was held for their safety before the naming ceremony. It has a door and two windows. The room remained locked (even Daral could not leave). Its peace was shattered when three helmed horrors crashed through the windows and door.

## Items
### Dawnbringer
A sentient magical sword wielded by Thorin that communicates telepathically with Thorin (and can make sounds in his head). Referred to as "she." She expressed a desire for more therapy sessions with the Candlekeep scholars, was used to strike the helmed horrors in combat (with a sapping property that imposes disadvantage on a target's next attack), and was used to cure Daral of his affliction.

### The Thrice-Fold Proof
An academic paper written by Kalan Strongbranch detailing the rigorous standards required for a criminal conviction, emphasizing that a single source of truth is insufficient and that three different kinds of sources of truth are required. Kalan shoves it in the party's faces to make his point about the limits of Alkrist's confession, pointing at the lines. The paper was never approved — it had been declined by the (now deceased) Janussi, who "took his paper and just dumped it." That denial was engineered by Bookwyrm, who privately urged Janussi to deny the paper, log it as incomplete research, restrict its circulation, and keep Kalan away from sensitive inquiries — a motive detail, given she is a suspect.

### Potion of Flying
A magical elixir found in Alkrist's safe and surreptitiously pocketed by Zalthir using his Glabbagool arm during the confusion of the investigation, with no one noticing.

### Potion of Superior Healing
A potent restorative draught discovered among Alkrist's belongings in his private safe.

### Red Herring Coin
A gold coin with a literal red herring engraved on it, found among the gold in Alkrist's safe — a fitting symbol of misdirection.

### The Inert Key
A mysterious key that appears non-magical under detection spells but was the primary target of the helmed horror assassins. Originally held by Daz, it was passed to Grygum mid-combat when the party realized the constructs seemed to be tracking it. Notably, the horrors went straight for Daz even though the party thought Grygum held the key, suggesting the constructs could sense the key itself rather than acting on secondhand knowledge. This is the party's working theory only; as of session end it is unresolved whether the horrors tracked the key or Daz, the inert detect-magic result is noted but uninterpreted, and the key's genuineness is unconfirmed.

## Spells
### Zone of Truth
Previously used to compel Alkrist's confession — cast by Kalan from outside the room so Alkrist didn't realize it until too late. The party and Kalan debate its use against Bookwyrm, noting that a target can resist it with a saving throw, and that observers would know if the target succeeded on the save (and could therefore lie), though they wouldn't know if the target was telling the truth. Kalan refused to cast it on Bookwyrm without her permission.

### Locate Object
Cast by Zalthir in an attempt to find a specific sapphire on Bookwyrm's person during the confrontation in her office. The spell failed to locate the object, indicating she did not have it.

### Scorching Ray
Cast by Daz to fire three rays of magical flame at a helmed horror, dealing 24 points of fire damage. Attacking the horror right next to him would have been at disadvantage due to its ranged nature, so he fired at the more distant horror.

### Word of Radiance
Cast by Grygum to emit a burst of holy light, damaging two helmed horrors simultaneously that were standing near him for 10 points each.

### Magic Missile
Cast by Daz to launch unerring darts of magical energy at a helmed horror, dealing 15 damage despite the constructs' general magic resistance (the spell always hits).

## Abilities

### Corrosive Embrace
A ki-powered ability (not a spell) delivered by Glabbagool, fused to Zalthir's arm. Because the acid is physical corrosion rather than magic, it bypasses the helmed horrors' Magic Resistance. It triggers on a hit and forces the target to make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw (with disadvantage if the target is restrained/grappled). Used twice during the battle for 14 damage each, it melted portions of the horrors' plate armor, reducing one construct's armor class by 1. The ability draws on Glabbagool's own ki — he is a monk sidekick with his own ki pool — limiting its uses.

### Sentinel (feat)
Thorin's Sentinel feat: immediately after a creature within 5 feet takes a disengage action or hits a target other than Thorin with an attack, he can make an opportunity attack against that creature. This let Thorin strike the helmed horrors when they attacked Grygum instead of him, and he used it to kill a horror before its second attack could land.
