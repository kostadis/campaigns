## ISSUE 1 — Irony acting alone vs. Irony and Orrin Glass both present

**Location:** Scene summary (bullet on burning the label) vs. Verbatim Moments [scene tag — The poisoning, dramatized]

**Issue:** The Scene Summary states only *"Irony"* arrived carrying the basin of water. The Verbatim Moments section of the same recap has the GM say: *"Irony and Orrin Glass arrived to lay the table for supper."* The summary drops Orrin Glass entirely.

**Evidence:** The GM's own verbatim transcript names both Irony and Orrin Glass. The campaign_state and world_state (entity_registry) list both Irony and Orrin Glass as Deadwinter Supper waiters at the Dead Winter tree.

**Suggested fix:** Amend the summary bullet to read: "…when **Irony and Orrin Glass** arrived to lay the table for supper."

## ISSUE 2 — Alkrist described as "washing his hands" vs. the water belonging to Irony

**Location:** Scene summary (bullet on burning the label) vs. Verbatim Moments [scene tag — The poisoning, dramatized]

**Issue:** The scene summary says *"Alkrist washed his hands in it 'in case I got any midnight tears about them,'"* attributing the hand-washing decision to Alkrist. The verbatim GM transcript says *"Irony was carrying a basin of water and decided to wash her… decided to wash his hands"* — the GM corrects mid-sentence but the phrasing is ambiguous: it is Alkrist who washes his hands in Irony's basin, not Irony washing hers. The summary correctly captures this, but the verbatim text's mid-sentence stumble ("decided to wash her…") could create future confusion about *whose* hands were washed.

**Evidence:** GM transcript, same scene tag.

**Suggested fix:** Add a clarifying note in the verbatim section: "[GM corrects — it is Alkrist who washes his own hands in the basin Irony was carrying, not Irony washing hers.]"

## ISSUE 3 — Kalan described as casting Zone of Truth while "hidden outside the room" — potential contradiction with Alkrist's awareness

**Location:** Scene summary (opening paragraph) and Verbatim Moments [Zalthir confirms truth spell]

**Issue:** The recap correctly states Kalan was concealed outside the room. However, the recap also states Alkrist is "under the influence of a zone of truth cast by Kalan Strongbranch" without noting whether Alkrist was told the spell was active or deduced it. The verbatim section shows Zalthir asking *"did we have Kalan start his truth spell thing?"* in front of Alkrist, and the GM confirms it. This means Alkrist *heard* the party confirm the spell was running — he was not kept ignorant of it. The summary's framing ("under the influence") is accurate but could mislead future readers into thinking Alkrist didn't know, which would affect how his resistance and frustration are interpreted.

**Evidence:** Verbatim: Zalthir's question and GM confirmation are spoken aloud in the room.

**Suggested fix:** Add to the summary: "Alkrist was aware the spell was active (Zalthir confirmed it aloud in front of him), making his attempts to resist and his visible frustration deliberate rather than oblivious."

## ISSUE 4 — Alkrist said to have "learned from A'lai that A'lai was more likely to be exiled than named Keeper of the Tomes"

**Location:** Scene summary (bullet on the baths meeting)

**Issue:** This is accurate per the verbatim, but the summary conflates two distinct beats: (a) Alkrist went to A'lai *expecting* to confirm A'lai's succession, and (b) he *learned* instead that A'lai was facing exile. The summary bullet compresses this, cutting the reversal of expectation that drove the conspiracy. This is not a factual error but is a meaningful narrative compression that could affect future session continuity — the motive logic (Alkrist expected a new, friendlier Keeper and discovered there would be no such thing) is partly lost.

**Evidence:** Verbatim GM: *"I went to find him to confirm that he was to be chosen as Janussi's successor, and instead I learned that A'lai was more likely to be exiled than named Keeper of Tomes."*

**Suggested fix:** Expand the bullet: "Alkrist had gone to A'lai expecting to confirm his succession — and instead learned A'lai was more likely to be exiled. This reversal, combined with Bookwyrm's distress, appears to have been the trigger for the conspiracy."

## ISSUE 6 — Grygum is absent from the interrogation room but is listed in the opening setup without clarification

**Location:** Scene summary (opening paragraph): "Kalan Strongbranch, hidden outside the room, Thorin and Zalthir alone interview Alkrist."

**Issue:** The recap correctly states only Thorin and Zalthir were in the room. However, Grygum is named in the Verbatim Moments as an active speaker ("Grygum" tags appear throughout the interrogation). This is consistent with Grygum being in the room — which contradicts the "Thorin and Zalthir alone" statement in the summary.

**Evidence:** Verbatim section contains multiple [Grygum] dialogue tags during the interrogation (e.g., *"Don't leave anything out,"* *"And what's going to happen to the succession now that Janussi's dead?"* *"What did you do?"* *"Accessory before the fact,"* *"Well, if they're true, then that's no problem, is it?"* etc.). Campaign_state §47 does not explicitly restrict who was in the room for the confession, saying only that "Thorin and Zalthir" conducted it — but the verbatim record clearly includes Grygum as an active participant.

Correct to: "Thorin, Zalthir, and Grygum interview Alkrist" — or, if the design intent was that Grygum was meant to be outside with Kalan, flag this as a discrepancy requiring GM resolution before it is canonized in the campaign_state.

## ISSUE 12 — The recap does not note that the Zone of Truth confession is NOT legal proof per Kalan's Threefold Proof

**Location:** Scene summary (closing reflection) and Julius Caesar reflection section

**Issue:** The recap ends on a triumphant note ("the whodunit is largely answered"). It does not note — even in passing — that the confession is not proof by Candlekeep's own evidentiary standard (The Threefold Proof). This is the single most important forward-hook from Ch. 57 per the campaign_state and the dedicated thread file. A future GM reading only this recap would miss the "false confidence trap."

**Evidence:** candlekeep_false_confidence_bookwyrm_lever.md (explicit): "the confession is strong testimony, not proof." campaign_state Active Thread #1: "FALSE-CONFIDENCE TRAP (GM lever)." threefold_proof.md thesis throughout.

**Suggested fix:** Add a closing note to the scene summary: "**Continuity flag:** By the standard of Kalan's own Threefold Proof (no single spell is proof), Alkrist's confession is strong testimony but not independently corroborated proof. Bookwyrm has read this paper and can weaponize the party's reliance on a single *zone of truth* result. The case is broken open, not closed."
