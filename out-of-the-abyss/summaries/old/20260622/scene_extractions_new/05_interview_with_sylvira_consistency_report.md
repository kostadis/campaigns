Low-severity ambiguity.

**Evidence:** NPC table entry: "Jezebel | Alive | Sylvira's quarters | Sylvira's familiar (opened the door via mage hand)." The verbatim shows the door opened before Jezebel was addressed.

**Suggested fix:** Clarify in the NPC entry that the door was opened by a mage hand (caster unspecified in session — likely Sylvira's cantrip or Jezebel acting on standing orders) and that Jezebel separately reorganized the seats. Keep the two actions distinct.
