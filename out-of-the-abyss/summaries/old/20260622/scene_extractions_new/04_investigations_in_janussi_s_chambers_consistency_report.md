### Issue 2: The note is described as revealing Janussi blocked "Kalan's proposal for new criminal investigation methodologies"

**Location:** Scene Summary — "A note is found…"
**Issue:** The recap's framing is accurate in substance but slightly imprecise in one detail. Per the campaign documents, Janussi dismissed Kalan's proposal as "solipsism" and blocked it — this matches the note's content as described. However, the recap describes the motive as Janussi "actively blocked Kalan Strongbranch's **proposal**," which is correct. The recap also says Janussi dismissed the work as "solipsism and a waste of time" — this matches both the GM's verbatim quote and Kalan's Second Petition letter ("You described my earlier proposal as 'solipsism by lesser minds'"). **No factual error here.**

### Issue 3: Motive framing attributes Kalan's thwarted "career ambitions" — potential overstatement

**Location:** Scene Summary — "The discovery establishes a strong motive for Kalan, as his career ambitions were directly thwarted by the victim."
**Issue:** This is an interpretive overstatement that could mislead future sessions. Per the campaign documents, Kalan has already been **cleared as an ally** (campaign_state NPC table: "Ally"; §5: "Kalan Strongbranch — cleared, ally"). The recap presents the note as pointing to Kalan as a murder suspect without acknowledging the campaign's established conclusion that Kalan is not the killer. Framing his "career ambitions" as the motive implies he remains a live suspect, which contradicts the post-confession canon.
**Evidence:** campaign_state §5: "Suspects, updated: Kalan Strongbranch — cleared, ally; the methodology that cracks (and could un-crack) the case." world_state §5 likewise lists him as cleared.
**Suggested fix:** Revise to: "The discovery establishes why Janussi had sidelined Kalan and why Kalan had strong institutional grievances — context that clarifies the political landscape, even though Kalan has since been cleared of involvement in the murder itself."

### Issue 4: GM quote misattributes who "wanted Kalan fired" — direction of the firing is inverted

**Location:** Verbatim Moments — GM paraphrase: "the reason Janussi wanted Kalan fired is because Janussi was pushing for his new theory…"
**Issue:** This is a significant factual inversion. Per all campaign documents, it was **Kalan** who was pushing the new investigative methodology (The Threefold Proof / the Threefold Practice), and **Janussi** who rejected and blocked it. The GM's paraphrase as rendered in the recap reads as though Janussi was pushing a theory and wanted Kalan fired for opposing it — the opposite of what occurred. The Second Petition letter and Threefold Proof handout confirm Kalan authored the methodology; Janussi called it "solipsism by lesser minds" and refused it.
**Evidence:** Kalan's Second Petition: "You described my earlier proposal as 'solipsism by lesser minds.'" campaign_state §5: "dismissed Kalan's investigative methodology as 'solipsism.'" threefold_proof.md: authored by Kalan, delivered by Elian.
**Suggested fix:** Correct to: "The note reveals that Janussi blocked **Kalan's** proposal for new criminal investigation methodologies, dismissing it as solipsism — and that Janussi had been manoeuvring to force Kalan out. It was Kalan's methodology, not Janussi's."

### Issue 5: The recap does not note that Grygum also searched for additional Kalan-related evidence (the gift wrapping paper thread)

**Location:** Scene Summary; Verbatim Moments (GM setup quote)
**Issue:** The GM's verbatim setup quote explicitly states the purpose of returning to Janussi's chambers was partly to look for "further evidence related to Kalan that you may have not found" — specifically the wrapping paper context. The recap summarises the scene as finding only the note and concludes with Grygum's motive inference. It does not confirm whether the additional wrapping-paper evidence was found or not found in this specific scene. Per campaign_state §9, the *mending* of the wrapping paper is a separate, confirmed completed action. If the recap is meant to cover only the upper-floor note discovery, this is not an error — but the scene's framing is **ambiguous** about whether the wrapping paper was also located here or elsewhere.
**Evidence:** campaign_state §9: "Grygum *mends* the wrapping paper (premeditation)." GM quote in recap: "the paper that wrapped the gift was introduced to these chambers, which you didn't pick up, and there might be some further evidence."
**Suggested fix:** Add a clarifying note: "Note: the wrapping paper evidence and the *mending* spell that confirmed premeditation occurred as a separate action (confirmed in campaign record); this scene covers only the third-floor note discovery."
