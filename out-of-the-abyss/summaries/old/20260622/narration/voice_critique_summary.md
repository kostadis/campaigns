## Strongest recurring issue: em-dash overuse

Every one of the 8 scenes leads with narration-level em-dash overuse as its top or co-top mechanical issue — roughly 90 of the 121 total flags are em-dash conversions. Each narrator's spec calls for a different declarative cadence (Grygum's "and"-chained coordinate clauses, Zalthir's "short and stacked" cadence, Thorin's plain-spoken directness, Daz's audit-log terseness), and the em-dash habit works against all four in the same way: it builds essayistic parenthetical asides the spec doesn't license for any of them. This is a single systemic pattern, not four separate narrator problems — worth fixing as one pass across all 8 files rather than per-scene.

## Second recurring issue: cross-narrator vocabulary bleed

An abstract/analytical register — "architecture," "angle," "data," "shape," "alignment," "filed," "structure" — turns up in multiple narrators' scenes where their own spec doesn't sanction it. Notably this reads like Daz's audit/analyst vocabulary leaking into Zalthir's and Grygum's sections, and in Grygum's case (scene 04) rises to a full extended passage of literary criticism that contradicts his spec'd low-INT, puzzle-blind characterization. Thorin's scene has a parallel but distinct issue: a Star Trek pop-culture reference that the spec sanctions only for out-of-scene table banter, not in-fiction dialogue.
