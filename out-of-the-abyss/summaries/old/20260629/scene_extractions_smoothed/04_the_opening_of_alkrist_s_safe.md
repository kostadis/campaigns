# The Opening of Alkrist's Safe

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party and the Avowed open Alkrist's safe to search for further incriminating evidence.
- The group opens Alkrist's safe, discovering gold coins (750 gold pieces worth), a potion of flying, a potion of superior healing, and a gold coin with a red herring engraved on it, "and nothing else to implicate him."
- Zalthir uses Glabbagool to surreptitiously steal the potion of flying from the safe during the confusion, with no one noticing. The GM confirmed "a sufficiently high sleight of hand, and everybody's so frantically looking at everything else that nobody pays any attention."
- Kalan Strongbranch interjected with the procedural steps: "We will absolutely guard the safe tonight. Alkrist will spend the day in the prisons, where he will be kept secure and unable to escape."
- Zalthir cast Locate Object to look for a sapphire on Bookwyrm's person; the spell failed to find it, confirming she did not have it.
- The safe is declared of no further interest and closed by the Avowed, with everything left inside.
- Daral, still suffering from his affliction, pleads with Dawnbringer for a cure, at one point complimenting the party as he'd "given up on bragging at this point in time."
- The party is escorted to Deneir's Sanctum to wait.
