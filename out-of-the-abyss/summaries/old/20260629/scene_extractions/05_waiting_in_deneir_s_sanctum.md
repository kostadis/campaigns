# Waiting in Deneir's Sanctum

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party waits under guard in Deneir's Sanctum, where Daral is cured and the group debates their next moves.
- Food is brought to the party in the evening, which they distrust and use Fembris Lancer as an unwitting taster. Thorin: "I don't trust any of this food anymore, man." GM: "Yeah, and that's a new best practice."
- The room is described as filled with bookshelves and tables, with Fembris Lancer still confined there alongside them.
- The party debated whether to bother extracting anything more from Daral before deciding he had nothing left to tell. Thorin: "I don't think he had anything to do with it, and I don't think he has anything to tell us anymore. I think we probably just let him... let him go."
- Dawnbringer is used to cure Daral of his ailment, much to his delight.
- Daral enthusiastically announces his intention to make sentient magical swords an area of active academic research, going on at length about the semiotics of the subject — "semiotic spewage for about 5 minutes" per the GM — which Dawnbringer "completely eats up."
- Daral attempts to leave the room but finds it is still locked.
