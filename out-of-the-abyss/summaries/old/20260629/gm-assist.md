# Chapter 58 Academic vindication and an assault.

Date: 2026-06-29

## Summary
With Alkrist's confession secured and the physical evidence in hand, the party gathered to debate their next move. Dawnbringer lamented, with genuine feeling, that the promised therapy sessions with Candlekeep's scholars had never materialized, and Thorin could only offer a sympathetic shrug in response. After some deliberation, the group settled on a patient strategy: hand the credit to Kalan Strongbranch, let him save face after his humiliating dismissal, and collect their reward — all while keeping their suspicions about Bookwyrm close to the chest.

Kalan, however, was quick to temper their expectations. He argued with academic precision that a single confession, however dramatic, was not enough to bring Bookwyrm to justice under the rigorous standards of his own investigative framework. He shoved his paper — the thrice-fold proof — into their faces and insisted that the clever play was patience. With the naming ceremony approaching, the stench of suspicion alone would be enough to deny Bookwyrm the position of First Keeper, and the new head of the Avowed would be far more motivated to see her undone. The party agreed, and added a cunning wrinkle of their own: they would use their reward to acquire whatever resource or tome might definitively prove Bookwyrm's role as an accomplice after the fact, striking when she least expected it.

The confrontation in Bookwyrm's office was a masterclass in controlled tension. She received them with regal composure, pouring tea and gesturing for them to sit — an offer the party declined with varying degrees of suspicion. Zalthir, unwilling to be rude but equally unwilling to trust the cup, quietly directed Glabbagool to absorb the tea through his arm, the faint singe of acid the only sign anything unusual had occurred. When Kalan laid out the evidence against Alkrist — the stolen poison, the book, the confession — Bookwyrm's composure cracked, and she appeared heartbroken. But Daz, watching her carefully, noticed something the others missed: beneath the grief, she looked curiously relieved.

The party chose to withhold what they knew about potential accomplices, hinting only that the investigation was not yet complete and that others may have been involved. Bookwyrm performed outrage beautifully, declaring it unthinkable that anyone else could have conspired in the death of the beloved Janussi. Kalan was reinstated to his position, his methodology vindicated at last, and Bookwyrm graciously announced that the party would be given a full opportunity to speak before the naming ceremony. She then arranged for them to be escorted to Deneir's Sanctum — framing it as protection, though Daz sensed it was equally about keeping them from poking their noses any further into Candlekeep's affairs.

A brief detour to Alkrist's room yielded little of obvious consequence. The safe contained gold, a pair of potions, and a gold coin with a red herring engraved upon it — a fitting symbol for an investigation full of misdirection. The Avowed declared the contents unremarkable and moved to close the matter, but Zalthir, never one to miss an opportunity, used Glabbagool to quietly pocket the potion of flying while everyone else was distracted. No one noticed a thing.

The evening in Deneir's Sanctum passed quietly at first. Food was brought in, which the party eyed with deep suspicion, allowing Fembris Lancer to eat first before touching anything themselves. Dawnbringer was finally called upon to cure Daral of his lingering affliction, and the man was so overcome with gratitude that he launched into an enthusiastic monologue about his plans to make sentient magical swords an area of serious academic study — going on at considerable length about the semiotics of the subject while Dawnbringer absorbed every word with evident delight. Daral then attempted to leave the room, only to find it was still locked.

The peace shattered without warning. Three helmed horrors — towering animated suits of plate armor with cold, glowing eyes — smashed through the windows and burst through the door of the sanctum, sending the room into chaos. The constructs moved with terrifying purpose, ignoring most of the party and converging almost entirely on Daz. It was only mid-battle, as the horrors relentlessly bypassed every other target to reach him, that Daz realized with a chill what they were after: the mysterious key he carried, the one that had appeared completely inert to magical detection. He passed it to Grygum and threw himself clear as his companions surged into the fight.

The battle was fierce and disorienting. Zalthir drove his fists into the animated armor while Glabbagool, fused to his arm, unleashed torrents of corrosive acid that began dissolving the constructs' plating. Grygum radiated a burst of holy light that seared two horrors within reach, while Daz hurled volleys of magical fire and force into the fray. Thorin struck with Dawnbringer, imposing disadvantage on one horror's next attack with a sapping blow before cutting it down entirely. One by one, the constructs collapsed into heaps of rust and broken metal. When the last one fell, the party stood in the wreckage of the sanctum, breathing hard and deeply unsettled — because the horrors had been mindless, incapable of thought or speech, and yet somehow they had known exactly who was holding a key that no spell in Candlekeep should have been able to detect.

## NPCs
### Kalan Strongbranch
An academic investigator and stickler for his own 'thrice-fold proof' methodology. He argues that Alkrist's confession alone is insufficient to convict Bookwyrm, is cautious about directly confronting her without overwhelming evidence, and ultimately is reinstated to his position after his investigative methods are vindicated.

### Bookwyrm
The current First Reader (real name Skoda Vanaster, a green dragonborn) and Alkrist's aunt. She appears regal and in control during the confrontation, seeming heartbroken over her nephew's guilt but observed to be curiously relieved. She reinstates Kalan Strongbranch and keeps the party under close watch in the sanctum.

### Alkrist
Bookwyrm's nephew who has confessed to the murder of Janussi. He is currently being held in the prisons of Candlekeep. The party suspects he had accomplices, including possibly Bookwyrm herself.

### Janussi
The deceased whose murder is the central mystery of the investigation. Multiple characters refer to him with exaggerated reverence as 'beloved by all,' which the party finds darkly ironic.

### Fembris Lancer
An adjutant to the First Reader who remains with the party while they are confined to the sanctum, serving as an unwitting food taster and a witness to the exchange of the mysterious key. The party's distrust of him predates the ambush — the food-tasting was an early tell — and as the key-exchange witness he becomes a person of interest afterward.

### Daral
A man suffering from a magical ailment who is cured by Dawnbringer. At this point in the investigation he remained a prime suspect, under watch until midnight to test his innocence. He is present in the sanctum during the ambush and becomes enthusiastically obsessed with studying the properties of sentient magical swords.

### Helmed Horrors
Three mindless animated suits of plate armor with glowing eyes capable of flight and hover. They smash into Deneir's Sanctum and relentlessly target Daz, apparently homing in on the mysterious key he carries. They are destroyed in combat, collapsing into piles of rust and armor.

## Locations
### Bookwyrm's Office
A regal space where the First Reader receives guests and conducts official business. Bookwyrm offered the party tea here during a tense confrontation over the murder investigation.

### Alkrist's Room
The living quarters of Bookwyrm's nephew, containing a personal safe that held gold, potions, and a coin with a red herring engraved on it.

### Deneir's Sanctum
A chamber filled with bookshelves and tables within Candlekeep where the party was held for their safety before the naming ceremony. The room's peace was shattered when three helmed horrors crashed through the windows and door.

## Items
### Dawnbringer
A sentient magical sword wielded by Thorin that communicates with the party. It expressed a desire for more therapy sessions with the Candlekeep scholars, was used to strike the helmed horrors in combat, and was used to cure Daral of his affliction.

### The Thrice-Fold Proof
An academic paper written by Kalan Strongbranch detailing the rigorous standards required for a criminal conviction, emphasizing that a single source of truth is insufficient. Kalan shoves it in the party's faces to make his point about the limits of Alkrist's confession. The paper had been denied and administratively buried at Bookwyrm's urging: she privately advised Janussi to deny it, log it as incomplete research, restrict its circulation, and keep Kalan away from sensitive inquiries — a motive detail, given she is a suspect.

### Potion of Flying
A magical elixir found in Alkrist's safe and surreptitiously pocketed by Zalthir using his Glabbagool arm during the confusion of the investigation, with no one noticing.

### Potion of Superior Healing
A potent restorative draught discovered among Alkrist's belongings in his private safe.

### Red Herring Coin
A gold coin with a literal red herring engraved on it, found among the gold in Alkrist's safe.

### The Inert Key
A mysterious key that appears non-magical under detection spells but was the primary target of the helmed horror assassins. Originally held by Daz, it was passed to Grygum mid-combat when the party realized the constructs seemed to be tracking it. As of session end this is an open thread: the party noted the key reads inert to detection but has not interpreted why, has not confirmed it is genuine, and it remains unresolved whether the horrors were tracking the key itself or Daz personally.

## Spells
### Zone of Truth
Previously used to compel Alkrist's confession. The party and Kalan debate its use against Bookwyrm, noting that a target can resist it with a saving throw, and that observers would know if the spell failed to take hold.

### Locate Object
Cast in an attempt to find a specific sapphire on Bookwyrm's person during the confrontation in her office. The spell failed to locate the object, indicating she did not have it.

### Scorching Ray
Cast by Daz to fire three rays of magical flame at a helmed horror, dealing significant fire damage to the construct.

### Word of Radiance
Cast by Grygum to emit a burst of holy light, damaging two helmed horrors simultaneously that were standing near him.

### Magic Missile
Cast by Daz to launch unerring darts of magical energy at a helmed horror, dealing reliable damage despite the constructs' general magic resistance.

## Abilities

### Corrosive Embrace
A ki-powered ability (not a spell) delivered by Glabbagool — a monk sidekick drawing on his own ki pool — fused to Zalthir's arm. Because the acid is physical corrosion rather than magic, it bypasses the helmed horrors' Magic Resistance. Used twice during the battle, it melted portions of the horrors' plate armor, reducing their defenses.
