## Flags

### [1] Generic prose (mild)

> Alkrist had said it out loud, in the room, with the truth-magic still humming in the air — he'd killed Janussi.

**Why:** "still humming in the air" is stock atmospheric filler. Thorin renders the supernatural through concrete or mundane frames ("I've had chicken pox, I'm gonna get shingles"), not ambient shimmer — and the sentence's own better instinct is to treat the confession as a physical object ("pried loose at last, waiting for someone to decide where to set it down"). The magic should be a lever, not a mood.
**Suggested rewrite:** "Alkrist had said it out loud, in the room, with Kalan's spell still holding him honest — he'd killed Janussi." (The Zone of Truth as a thing that *holds* him; stone-weight verb, no ambience.)

## Verdict

Near-clean and strongly in-voice — the pun-announcement beat ("Bad joke. That's the reaction I want."), the terrain framing ("That's the terrain — a man told he was done… you don't take a win off a man like that"), and the Dawnbringer thread all land grounded. One optional spot-edit; no re-narration warranted. (Em-dashes handled systemically — see `voice_critique_summary.md`.)
