## Flags

### [1] Voice-spec conflict / cross-scene repetition — the document's strongest flag

> The Garden Shadow had a saying — or possibly Brother Quellin did, I'm not certain, I don't remember which — that the most dangerous person in a room isn't the one with the blade drawn. It's the one who has already decided they've won.

**Why:** The device itself is deployed correctly *here* — source uncertainty named, the read used to set up an action, the aphorism landing as a cold assessment of a room he's about to move in. In isolation this is a good Zalthir beat. The problem is that scene 04 (`gm-assist-doc.md:308`) reuses the **same aphorism with the same "Garden Shadow / Brother Quellin" attribution**, and the spec is explicit that this cannot happen: *"IMPORTANT: Use a different monk name every time this surfaces… The uncertainty about which monk taught what is the point"* (`zalthir_voice.md:64`). Note the aphorism is verbatim from the spec's own worked example (`zalthir_voice.md:62`) — which is exactly why it must not be recycled: repeating the canonical example twice, unchanged, flattens the one device the character is built around into a catchphrase.
**Suggested rewrite:** This scene is the right one to *keep* the aphorism intact (it introduces the read of Bookwyrm and pays off two sentences later in "Her hands were too relaxed"). Leave this occurrence; re-key or cut the scene-04 twin instead (see that report). If you would rather this be the varied one, swap the attribution to a fresh pair — e.g. *"— Brother Tharusk, I think, or the tall one who always smelled of incense —"* — per the spec's naming latitude.

### [2] Tell-not-show (mild, low-confidence)

> She looked regal, in control, and she asked us to sit down.

**Why:** "regal, in control" names the read as adjectives where Zalthir usually renders it through a physical detail and lets the reader draw the conclusion — which the scene itself does correctly moments later: *"The tears were correct and the eyes behind them were doing something else"* (`:216`) and *"Her hands were too relaxed"* (`:218`). The spec: *"The warmth lives in what he chose to mark… The voice never climbs into feeling. It just stops"* (`zalthir_voice.md:106`). Minor because it's a first-glance impression the scene deliberately undercuts.
**Suggested rewrite:** "She had poured the tea already. She asked us to sit." (Let the arranged room and the pre-poured cup carry "in control"; he marks the object, not the mood — matching "The tea was already poured" one line above.)

## Verdict

Strong scene — the Glabbagool tea-into-the-arm beat and its quiet marking ("He stayed still because I asked him to. That is discipline, or the beginning of it.") are exactly the spec's love-language (*"I am going to teach you the discipline I respect"*). The one real fix is the cross-scene aphorism collision; this scene should keep the line and scene 04 should give it up. (Em-dashes handled systemically — see `voice_critique_summary.md`.)
