# Session 2026-08-17

Date: 2026-08-17

## Summary
With the battle in the High Tower finally over, the party secured the captured A'lai Aivenmore and stripped him of his key to the inner sanctum, quietly passing it to Zalthir before tucking it safely away inside Glabbagool for safekeeping. Before they could act on their advantage, Manshoon's simulacrum arrived — alone and visibly drained from the enormous magical effort it had taken to reach Candlekeep. He conjured a wall of shimmering force and shattered the magically protected door with overwhelming power, breaching the inner sanctum as though its ancient wards were nothing. A'lai watched in trembling horror as the truth settled over him like a shroud: Manshoon had never come for him. After eleven years of service, he had not even been worth the effort of a single spell.

Broken and desperate, A'lai offered the party everything he had left. He revealed the existence of a secret room beneath Candlekeep holding artifacts of extraordinary power, guarded by a cryptogram he had spent years quietly researching. He knew the answers to two of its riddles — "Fustilugs," the base insult carved into the black marble knight in the Philosopher's Court, and "Bow," the reply of the legendary halfling bard Batbayar's statue when applauded in the School of the Drama Library. In exchange for this information, A'lai begged not for freedom but for a cell in Candlekeep's null magic prison, the one place he believed no wizard — not even Manshoon — could reach him. When asked about the forces surrounding Daz, A'lai admitted he could not trace the payments, only the patience behind them, and concluded with quiet certainty that only one house in Menzoberranzan could afford to wait eleven years for nothing: House Baenre. Grygum appealed to the Watcher Tadric, invoking the party's record of service and their deputization by Kalan Strongbranch, and Tadric agreed to take A'lai into custody and march him off toward the prison.

The party regrouped in the investigator's office, where A'lai Aivenmore — whose photographic memory allowed him to reproduce the cryptogram in full — spread the riddles across the floor for every sharp mind in the room to examine. Sylvira Savikas arrived shortly after, grateful for the party's efforts and eager to help Candlekeep in its moment of crisis. She provided the true name of Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm and dragon guardian of Candlekeep, as the answer to the first riddle, and identified the Eastern Light of Mystra's Mantle as an obscure star recorded in the charts of the Orrery of the Astronomicon — though she noted with quiet unease that three unfamiliar figures had been inside the Orrery when she passed it. Working together, the party decoded the letter substitution cipher of the cryptogram, with Thorin leading the effort to the stunned admiration of every scholar in the room. The full text revealed a sequence of instructions: feed the quill of Alaundo the Seer, tread as many steps as he lived in years, utter the original prophecy to unseen ears, sprinkle dust of Mechanus on dormant gears, one last guardian of knowledge remains to verify the chosen Reader's claims, and that those well-versed in Candlekeep's lore may fearlessly pass the obsidian door. Kalan Strongbranch arrived huffing and puffing moments later, eager to contribute a clue he had solved — only to find the party had already cracked the entire cipher, leaving him to recover his composure and insist on being useful anyway.

The party made their way to Founders Court and the House of Alaundo, where a great brass statue of the ancient seer stood upon a dais, bearing a quill and an empty ink pot worn smooth by centuries of superstitious scholars touching it for luck. After a brief debate over whether ink or blood was the appropriate offering, the party borrowed fresh ink from the assembled scholars and poured it into the statue's ink pot. The dais ground forward with a deep mechanical groan, revealing a long, unlit staircase descending into darkness below. To know how many steps to count, the party needed to know how long Alaundo had lived, and so they traveled to the grove where Candlekeep's keepers of tomes were buried, finding his grave and learning he had died at the age of ninety-seven.

With the prophecy provided by the scholars who knew it by heart, the party still needed one final ingredient: dust of Mechanus, required to sprinkle on the dormant gears described in the riddle. They made their way to the House of Mechanus, a rectangular building with a blue roof and a clock tower, where the gnome librarian Spanner oversaw thirteen modrons — strange, box-like mechanical creatures from the plane of Mechanus — in their precise and orderly work. Spanner explained that the dust was the most perfect lubricant in existence, and that the only ways to obtain it were to kill one of his modrons — absolutely unacceptable — or to use specialized tools his modrons had crafted. The party persuaded Spanner to lend them the tools, and he agreed on the condition that Zalthir and Glabbagool remain behind for a study of their mechanical symmetry. The modrons then performed a strange, synchronized dance as they deliberated among themselves in a language no one else in the room could begin to understand.

Armed at last with everything they needed, the party descended the hidden staircase beneath Alaundo's statue, counting each step carefully toward the ninety-seventh. Daz positioned himself higher on the stairs for a clear line of sight, while the others spread out below, Grygum offering what healing he could to prepare them for whatever lay ahead. Then, without warning, a ten-foot section of the staircase hinged open beneath their feet. Everyone but Thorin caught hold of the stairs and scrambled clear, but Thorin lost his footing entirely and plummeted downward into the vast, dark circular well below — his fate uncertain as the darkness swallowed him whole.

## Locations
### High Tower
The site of a brutal battle earlier in the session, and the location where Manshoon's simulacrum breached Candlekeep's inner sanctum using a wall of force to shatter the magically protected door.

### Null Magic Prison
A specialized containment area within Candlekeep designed to negate all magical abilities. A'lai Aivenmore desperately requests to be placed here, believing it is the only place he can survive Manshoon's reach.

### Vault of Secrets
A hidden chamber beneath Candlekeep, unknown to most scholars, containing artifacts of extraordinary power and protected by a cryptogram and a series of riddles.

### Investigator's Office
A room near the prison where the party, Kalan Strongbranch, and Sylvira Savikas gather to analyze the transcribed cryptogram and solve the riddles.

### Philosopher's Court
An area within Candlekeep containing oversized black marble chess pieces. The base of the black knight piece has the word 'Fustilugs' magically etched into it — one of the answers to the vault's riddles.

### School of the Drama Library
A library within Candlekeep featuring a statue of the legendary halfling bard Batbayar, which comes to life and bows when applauded.

### Orrery of the Astronomicon
A chamber within Candlekeep containing star charts. Sylvira noticed three suspicious individuals inside who did not appear to work there.

### House of Alaundo
A building in Founders Court whose ground floor stretches to the top with a gallery mezzanine. It features a large brass statue of Alaundo the Seer on a dais, which conceals a hidden staircase triggered by filling the statue's ink pot.

### Founders Court
A central area in Candlekeep where the House of Alaundo is located, also near the House of Mechanus and the Immortal Chambers.

### The Grove
A burial ground within Candlekeep where the keepers of tomes are laid to rest. The party visits to find Alaundo's grave and learn he died at the age of 97.

### House of Mechanus
A large rectangular building with a blue roof and a clock tower, situated between the Immortal Chambers and Founders Court. It serves as a library dedicated to construction, engineering, and technology, and is home to Spanner the gnome librarian and thirteen modrons.

### The Hidden Staircase
A long, unlit passage revealed beneath the statue of Alaundo the Seer, leading down into a vast circular well. A section of the stairs collapses during the party's descent, sending Thorin plummeting into the darkness.

## NPCs

A high-ranking scholar who turned traitor, only to realize he was a disposable pawn for Manshoon. Devastated by this revelation, he trades secrets about the Vault of Secrets, and his own deduction about which drow house has been funding the operations around Daz, in exchange for placement in Candlekeep's null magic prison.

### Manshoon
A legendary and immensely powerful wizard. What reached Candlekeep is his **simulacrum**, arriving alone and visibly weakened, having expended enormous magical resources. It conjures a wall of force and shatters the magically protected door to breach the inner sanctum, seeking something of extraordinary value.

### Tadric
A Watcher and lieutenant to Kalan Strongbranch who is present during the aftermath of the High Tower battle. He agrees to take A'lai Aivenmore into custody after the party negotiates a deal for information.

### Glabbagool
An intelligent gelatinous cube serving as a companion to the party. It stores the High Tower key inside itself for safekeeping and later agrees to be studied by the modrons in exchange for the party receiving the tools needed to obtain Dust of Mechanus.

### Kalan Strongbranch
The head of Candlekeep's guard who has deputized the party. He arrives late to the investigator's office hoping to contribute a clue to the riddles, only to find the party has already solved the cipher. He helps by recalling details about Candlekeep's landmarks from memory.

A powerful and knowledgeable scholar at Candlekeep who arrives at the investigator's office and assists the party in solving the vault's riddles. ### Batbayar
A legendary halfling bard whose statue stands in the School of the Drama Library. The statue comes to life and bows deeply when applauded — the answer to one of the vault's riddles.

### Miirym
The Sentinel Wyrm of Candlekeep, described as a dragon. Her true name is the answer to the first riddle guarding the Vault of Secrets.

### Spanner
A gnome librarian who oversees the House of Mechanus and directs thirteen modrons in their mechanical tasks. He negotiates with the party to provide tools for obtaining Dust of Mechanus in exchange for studying Zalthir and Glabbagool's mechanical symmetry.

### Modrons
Thirteen box-like mechanical creatures from the plane of Mechanus who work under Spanner in the House of Mechanus. They communicate in a strange mechanistic language and perform a synchronized, mechanized dance when deliberating among themselves.
