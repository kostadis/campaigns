# The Statue of Alaundo

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party locates the brass statue of Alaundo the Seer in Founders Court and triggers a hidden mechanism to reveal a secret passage beneath it.
- The party gathers at the House of Alaundo in Founders Court, observing the large brass statue of the seer bearing a quill and an empty ink pot on a large dais. Alaundo is "the great seer whose prophecies were no longer being repeated" — the Nostradamus of his age, "except his prophecies came out."
- Common superstition holds that touching the quill guarantees "a well-critiqued piece of academia, and the brass on the tip is certainly well worn." GM: "I swear to God, that's what it says."
- The party debates whether to use ink or blood to fill the ink pot. Thorin: "I'm guessing blood." Zalthir: "We did take a sample of the poisoned ink, we could try that." Grygum, on sourcing blood: "Just tell them that the first one to solve the riddle gets their paper published." GM: "Then you might have too much blood."
- Grygum decides: "I'm not actually evil, so I'm gonna start with ink" — ultimately choosing fresh ink borrowed from the assembled scholars ("whatever looks freshest").
- Upon pouring ink into the statue's ink pot, the statue grinds forward on its dais, revealing a long set of stairs leading down an unlit passage. Grygum: "This is cool."
- Thorin flags the plot hole: "nobody over all that time has put ink in the ink well. I'm just saying. It's a little on the nose." The scholars look at him in horror — "why would we deface the thing?" GM: "good plot hole discovery."
- The party has by now acquired "quite a retinue" of scholars scribbling notes; this "will be studied for years," particularly "the part where Thorin solved the puzzle" — the cryptogram he led the decode on. Grygum makes a little speech "so that at least some of it is likely to be written down correctly." *(GM ruling 2026-08-24: Thorin genuinely solved it first; Grygum solved it seconds later, or simultaneously, but by then the Avowed's hysteria meant nobody noticed. That is what Grygum's speech is correcting.)*
- The party realizes they need to know the exact age at which Alaundo died to determine how many steps to descend — and nobody knows. It isn't on the plaque, either ("Pretty deficient plaque").
- Options: the biography *Alaundo, the Wonder Years* in the Hall of Momentous Deeds, or "start going down the stairs and take your luck." Thorin: "I'm afraid if we go one step too far... statues come alive and shit." Grygum: "It'll kill us with a quill."
- Scholars suggest checking the grove where the keepers of tomes are buried to find the answer. Thorin: "Are we gonna dig him up and ask him?"

**[The Statue Slides Aside]**
- The GM describes the House of Alaundo in Founders Court: a brass statue of the seer with a quill and empty ink pot on a dais, its quill tip worn by superstitious scholars.
- The party works out that feeding the quill means filling the empty ink pot; Thorin guesses blood, Zalthir offers the sample of poisoned ink.
- Grygum jokes about sourcing blood by offering publication to whoever solves the riddle; the GM warns that would produce too much blood.
- Grygum, not actually evil, borrows the freshest ink from the assembled scholars and pours it in; the statue grinds forward to reveal stairs into an unlit passage.
- Thorin flags the plot hole that nobody in all those years poured ink in; the scholars are horrified at the idea of defacing the statue.
- Nobody knows how old Alaundo was when he died, and it isn't on the plaque; options are his biography in the Hall of Momentous Deeds or trusting luck on the stairs.
- The party has gathered a retinue of note-taking scholars; Grygum makes a speech so the record is at least partly correct.
- A scholar suggests the grove where keepers of tomes are buried, where his age is written down.
