# Consistency Report — "The Methodology of Murder"

---

## Issue 1: Elian described as sharing a paper "Kalan Strongbranch and I have read"

- **Location:** Scene Summary (bullet 2); Verbatim Moments — GM as Elian offering the paper
- **Issue:** The GM's in-character dialogue has Elian say "a paper that Kalan Strongbranch and I have read about the proper methodology of murder investigations." The campaign documents are consistent that the paper (*The Threefold Proof*) was **championed by Kalan** — he wrote the Second Petition to Janussi around it and is identified as its author-champion throughout the NPC table, campaign_state, world_state, and the handout's own provenance note. Elian's role is as Kalan's clerk who *delivered* it. Calling it something he and Kalan merely "read" obscures authorship.
- **Evidence:** `campaign_state.md` NPC table: "Kalan Strongbranch — author-champion of The Threefold Proof." `threefold_proof.md` provenance: "the investigation-methodology paper championed by Kalan Strongbranch." `Kalan to Janussi - Second Petition.md` is signed by Kalan as its author. `entity_registry.yaml`: "Kalan Strongbranch — Gatewarden; archmage."
- **Suggested fix:** Clarify in the recap that Kalan is the paper's author (or at minimum its principal champion), not merely a co-reader. The GM's transcript wording was probably loose in-the-moment delivery; the summary should not canonize it as the paper's provenance. Suggested phrasing: "Elian delivers a research paper authored and championed by Kalan Strongbranch on criminal investigation methodology."

## Issue 2: The paper is described as a "scholarly paper" and "research paper"; the campaign documents call it a memorandum

- **Location:** Scene Summary (bullets 2 and 3); Verbatim Moments — GM as Elian
- **Issue:** The recap calls the document a "scholarly paper" and "research paper." The campaign documents consistently call it a *memorandum* ("The Threefold Proof — a memorandum for Readers, Watchers, magistrates…"). This is a minor but potentially consequential continuity point: the document's formal status (a petition/memo to the Keeper of Tomes, not a published scholarly work) is part of the plot — it was a rejected internal proposal, which is why Kalan was in conflict with Janussi.
- **Evidence:** `threefold_proof.md` subtitle: "A memorandum for Readers, Watchers, magistrates…" `campaign_state.md`: "The Threefold Proof — Kalan's investigation-methodology memorandum." `party.md`: "The Threefold Proof — Kalan's investigation-methodology memorandum."
- **Suggested fix:** Replace "scholarly paper" / "research paper" with "memorandum" (or "methodology memorandum") throughout the recap.

## Issue 3: Grygum's Holmes quotation — recap says he "claims he made it up myself"

- **Location:** Scene Summary (bullet 5); Verbatim Moments — Grygum quip
- **Issue:** This is a real-world Sherlock Holmes quote ("Once you have eliminated the impossible, whatever remains, however unlikely, must be the truth" — Arthur Conan Doyle). The recap records Grygum claiming authorship as an in-character gag. This is not a factual error about campaign canon per se, but it is an **ambiguous claim** that could confuse future sessions: if the quote is logged without context, a future editor might read it as Grygum's in-world original aphorism (which Elian then wanted to use). The recap should flag it as an out-of-universe reference played as in-character humor.
- **Evidence:** No campaign document attributes this quote to Grygum or any in-world figure. It is a well-known real-world literary quotation.
- **Suggested fix:** Add a bracketed editor note: *[OOC: this is the Sherlock Holmes aphorism; played as Grygum's in-character joke claiming original authorship. Not canon as Grygum's coinage in-world unless the table adopts it as such.]*

## Issue 4: Kalan's role is described only as a "boss" who "was fired" — his title is omitted and "fired" is imprecise

- **Location:** Scene Summary (bullets 3–4); Verbatim Moments — Thorin's dialogue ("the guy that they, like, fired")
- **Issue:** The recap describes Kalan only as Elian's "boss" and says he was "fired." The campaign documents establish that Kalan holds the title **Gatewarden** (and Keeper of Secrets), was *sidelined from the murder case* by Bookwyrm, and was asked by Janussi to *resign* the day before Janussi's death — but has not been formally terminated. The distinction matters: Kalan retains his position and key, is an active ally, and "fired" is specifically inaccurate.
- **Evidence:** `campaign_state.md` NPC table: "Kalan Strongbranch — Alive; Ally; Gate Warden, sidelined from the case by Bookwyrm." `world_state.md`: "Kalan Strongbranch — cleared, ally." `bookwyrm_first_reader.new_notes.057.md`: Bookwyrm "ordered him to secure the defenses" — he was removed from the investigation, not dismissed from his post. `kalan_strongbranch.new_notes.057.md`: "Janussi asked Kalan to tender his resignation" — a request that preceded Janussi's death; there is no record it was acted on.
- **Suggested fix:** Replace "fired" with "sidelined from the investigation by Bookwyrm" (and note the earlier resignation request from Janussi). Identify Kalan as Gatewarden.

## Issue 5: The recap states Elian "implies this controversy may be connected to why Kalan was fired"

- **Location:** Scene Summary (bullet 4)
- **Issue:** Same as Issue 4 above. The summary presents the in-scene implication ("there is something rotten here") as pointing toward Kalan being *fired* due to the methodology controversy. Per campaign documents, Kalan was not fired; the controversy is about why Janussi rejected the methodology proposal and potentially why Janussi wanted Kalan gone. Logging "why Kalan was fired" as a plot thread would be factually wrong.
- **Evidence:** Same as Issue 4. Additionally, `candlekeep_false_confidence_bookwyrm_lever.md` frames the methodology paper as a lever *against the party*, not simply as a backstory for Kalan's dismissal.
- **Suggested fix:** Reword to: "Elian implies the pushback against Kalan's methodology may be connected to why Janussi sought Kalan's resignation — and possibly to the murder itself."

## Issue 7: "Thorin asks" about Speak to the Dead — Thorin is a Fighter (Battle Master) with no spells

- **Location:** Scene Summary (bullet 5): "Thorin asks, 'if we were able to actually make Speak to the dead…'"
- **Issue:** This is not a factual error — Thorin is asking a question, not casting a spell, so there is no mechanical contradiction. However, for future continuity: the recap's phrasing "if we were able to actually make Speak to the dead" is Thorin speaking hypothetically, which is consistent with his class. No issue with the recap as written, but it is worth noting that the party **does not have Speak with Dead** — that rite was performed by Kalan and cannot be repeated.
- **Evidence:** `world_state.md` / `party.md`: Thorin is Fighter 8 (Battle Master), no spells. `kalan_strongbranch.new_notes.057.md`: "He had also performed the Speak with Dead rite on the corpse during this inspection, which meant the rite could not be repeated for some time."
- **Suggested fix:** No change to the recap required; this is confirmatory. Consider adding an editor's note that the Speak with Dead opportunity has already been consumed by Kalan.
