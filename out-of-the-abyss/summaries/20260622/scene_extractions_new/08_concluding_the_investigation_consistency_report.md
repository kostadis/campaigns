### Issue 1: Kalan Strongbranch's title

- **Location:** Scene Summary / Verbatim Moments (implied throughout)
- **Issue:** The recap refers to Kalan as the person "who was fired" and whom the party proposes to hand the evidence to. Thorin's line is "we go to the guy that was fired." Kalan was not fired — he was removed from the murder investigation by Bookwyrm and asked by Janussi to tender his resignation the day before Janussi died. He was not dismissed from his post as Gatewarden. His role and standing at Candlekeep remain intact.
- **Evidence:** Campaign state NPC table: "Kalan Strongbranch — Alive, Candlekeep, **Ally**; Gate Warden, *sidelined* from the case by Bookwyrm." The bookwyrm_first_reader notes confirm Bookwyrm ordered him to "secure the defenses," not that he was terminated. The kalan_strongbranch notes confirm he was "removed from direct involvement in the investigation," not fired.
- **Suggested fix:** Replace "the guy that was fired" with "the guy who was pulled off the case" or "the guy Bookwyrm sidelined." Note that Janussi *asked* him to resign the morning before the murder, but Kalan had not done so and retains his title of Gatewarden.

### Issue 4: Janussi described as having "fired" multiple people

- **Location:** Scene Summary, fourth bullet; Verbatim Moments (GM lines)
- **Issue:** The GM line says Janussi "fires and insults multiple powerful magic users all in a single day." The recap summary also uses "firing." Per campaign state, Janussi did not fire all of them — he: cancelled Alkrist's research, denied Bookwyrm succession (naming A'lai instead), threatened A'lai with **exile** (not a firing), asked Kalan to resign, and trashed Daral's research. "Exile" and "cancelling research" are distinct from termination/firing.
- **Evidence:** World state §5 "The motive day": "cancelled **Alkrist's** germ-warfare research, denied **Bookwyrm** the succession (naming **A'lai** instead), threatened **A'lai** with exile rather than elevation, and dismissed **Kalan's** investigative methodology as 'solipsism.'" The GM's own verbatim line says "tell somebody he's getting fired, tell somebody he has to cancel his research" — only one person is described as getting fired in the campaign record (Alkrist's research cancellation; Kalan was asked to resign).
- **Suggested fix:** The recap's "layoff" framing is a player joke and not a factual claim, so this is low severity. However, for future session continuity, the precise actions should be preserved: research cancelled (Alkrist), succession denied (Bookwyrm), exile threatened (A'lai), resignation requested (Kalan). "Fired" is an imprecise shorthand that could blur motivations later.

### Issue 5: The recap does not note the Zone of Truth's evidentiary limitation (potential false-confidence trap)

- **Location:** Scene Summary overall framing
- **Issue:** The recap frames the investigation as concluded ("a web of conspiracy...uncovering") and the confession as the endpoint, with the only remaining question being "what do you do with the evidence." This framing buries the critical open thread that the confession is **not proof** under Kalan's own Threefold Proof standard, and that Bookwyrm can weaponize this.
- **Evidence:** Campaign state Active Thread #1 explicitly flags: "**FALSE-CONFIDENCE TRAP (GM lever):** the party treats Alkrist's *zone of truth* confession as proof. By the standard of **Kalan's own methodology paper (The Threefold Proof)** — a single spell is *not* proof — it isn't." The candlekeep_false_confidence_bookwyrm_lever.md document is dedicated to this issue. Party.md: "nobody applied Kalan's 'no single spell is proof' standard to their own conclusion."
- **Suggested fix:** Add a continuity note to the recap: "The confession is strong testimony but is not proof under the Threefold Proof standard Kalan himself authored. Several holes remain uncorroborated independently: A'lai's role rests solely on Alkrist's word; Bookwyrm's culpability rests solely on Alkrist's word; the heart, locket, and Sylvira impersonator are unresolved." This prevents the session recap from canonizing a false conclusion.
