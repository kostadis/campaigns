### Issue 1: Grygum described as being in "Janussi's office"

**Location:** Scene summary, opening line
**Issue:** The summary states Grygum is "alone in Janussi's office." The campaign documents consistently refer to the relevant space as Janussi's **chambers** (or specifically his third-floor apartment / "love nest"), not an office. The crime scene is in the **Keeper's Tower**; the separate investigation space the party uses is **Deneir's Sanctum**. "Office" is not a term used for any of these locations in the grounding documents.
**Evidence:** campaign_state.md: "Grygum (alone) finds the damning note in Janussi's chambers"; world_state.md §6: "Janussi's Chambers (third-floor 'love nest')"; party.md Ch. 57 beats: "Explored Janussi's third-floor chambers (alone)."
**Suggested fix:** Replace "Janussi's office" with "Janussi's chambers" (or "Janussi's third-floor apartment").

### Issue 2: The wrapping paper is said to confirm the book was wrapped for Daral's gift — but the scene summary frames this as new information when it is already established canon

**Location:** Scene summary, third bullet point; Verbatim moments, final Grygum/GM exchange
**Issue:** The summary presents "the paper had been wrapped around the book" as a discovery made in this scene. The campaign documents record this as already established prior to the mending: the book is identified as Daral's gift to Janussi, intercepted and re-wrapped by Alkrist. The mending's evidential value per the canon is **premeditation** (proof of a deliberate cut showing re-wrapping), not the identity of what the paper was wrapped around.
**Evidence:** campaign_state.md §46 and §47: "coated Daral's gift book, re-wrapped it"; "Grygum's *mending* on the discarded wrapping paper proved a deliberate re-wrap (premeditation)"; world_state.md §5: "Grygum's **mending** on the discarded wrapping paper revealed a deliberate cut → **premeditation confirmed**."
**Suggested fix:** Clarify that the mending confirmed **premeditation** (a deliberate cut distinct from tearing), not the identity of the wrapped object (already known). The final exchange — "gift wrap for the deadly book / Yes" — is accurate in substance but could mislead future readers into thinking object-identification was the scene's forensic contribution. Add a note that the key finding is the cut proving re-wrapping, not that the paper surrounded the book.

### Issue 5: GM's verbatim quote references "the apartment" in a potentially confusing way

**Location:** Verbatim moments, first GM quote
**Issue:** The GM says *"canonically was not supposed to be an apartment, but that I put it in the apartment."* This is an out-of-character GM correction about placing a prop. The scene summary does not flag this as a GM retcon or staging note, which could cause a future reader to believe the wrapping paper's location in the dining-hall trash was always established canon, when the GM is explicitly acknowledging it was a mid-session placement decision.
**Evidence:** The GM's own words: "there was a piece of paper that, canonically was not supposed to be an apartment, but that I put it in the apartment."
**Suggested fix:** Add an editorial note that the wrapping paper's placement in the dining-hall trash bucket was a **GM-introduced prop** (not pre-planned), confirmed retroactively as canon in this scene. This prevents future confusion if anyone cross-references earlier session notes that do not mention the paper.
