**Suggested Fix:** Flag this as an in-session rule establishment (not pre-existing lore) and add a note that the "cannot be stolen from a living possessor" mechanic was stated by the GM in this scene. Verify whether this is a known property of the specific locket/key item before treating it as a general rule.
