# Splitting the Leads

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The investigators divide their efforts to interview key suspects and search for physical evidence.
- Fembris Lancer urges the party to move quickly: "My friends, time is a wasting."
- The party decides to split up: Grygum will investigate Janussi's chambers (specifically a third-story apartment with an open door), Daz will interview Sylvira, and others will prepare to interview Alkrist — with Zalthir noting the value of a "Dragonborn to Dragonborn" approach.
- Fembris departs to locate Kalan Strongbranch so he can assist with a truth-seeking spell for the upcoming interrogation of Alkrist; Kalan had apparently "disappeared somewhere," forcing Fembris to hunt him down.
- Grygum reminds the group to ask Alkrist about "what Alkrist burned at the feast," flagged as an open thread.

## Verbatim moments (voice-smoothed)

**[GM]** — *as Fembris Lancer, urging haste*
> "My friends, time is a-wasting. We will— first of all, persons—"

**[Grygum]** — *proposing the split and announcing his target*
> Somebody go talk to Alkrist. There's something listed as a third-story apartment with an open door we haven't gone into. I'm gonna go look into that.

**[GM]** — *placing the apartment*
> That's in Janussi's Chambers. We learned that the paper that wrapped the gift was brought to these chambers — which you didn't pick up — and there might be further evidence related to Kalan you haven't found. Might be worth going back there to look.

**[Grygum]** — *committing to head up*
> "Yeah, I'll head up and check that out."

**[GM]** — *confirming*
> "Yo, go check that."

**[GM]** — *moving to Alkrist's interview*
> "All right, so he's gonna go check that. What about — does he want to go talk to Alkrist?"

**[GM]** — *trying to recall the name*
> "Daral?"
> "Hello, Kalan Strongbranch?"

**[Zalthir]** — *identifying who can cast the truth spell*
> "Who can cast the truth spell? Yeah — Kalan Strongbranch."

**[GM]** — *Fembris sets off to fetch Kalan*
> Alright — [inaudible; VTT: "if I'm in our size"] — I'm gonna have to hunt Kalan down. We'll have to do that in a bit. Let me go, let me go.
