## Party Accomplishments & Acquisitions

**Janussi's heart and a blood-stained meat cleaver** — Discovered by Thorin inside the lead-lined brass chalice held by the Milil statue in the Southern Dining Hall. Confirmed the heart was removed post-mortem to prevent resurrection questioning.

**Key confirmed clean** — Daz determined via divination that Kalan Strongbranch's key carries no enchantment and is not linked to the sapphire's summoning magic. The sapphire is linked to a *different* key (not this one).

**Squirrel testimony obtained** — Daz bribed Nibbles (Leuwin's squirrel) with nuts and used speak-with-animals to establish the following timeline at the Oak Tree Apothecary:
- Fheminor (gnome, "unpleasant girl") visited first and departed.
- A'lai Aivenmore ("big, clumsy, dark-skinned elf") arrived and occupied Leuwin in the greenhouse below.
- While Leuwin was distracted, a dragonborn ("huge lizard skin") entered alone, broke a vial from the *Dangerous Toxins* shelf, and departed. A peppermint-soaked rag and broken vial were left under the top shelf, partially concealed.

**Room searches completed by Zalthir and Glabbagool:**
- *A'lai Aivenmore's chambers*: Dove cage built for five birds, only two remaining — three messages sent recently via carrier dove.
- *Alkrist's chambers*: Sealed glass jars containing dead rats with blistered, pustule-covered skin (poison research). Bookmarked copy of *1001 Tashalian Nights* open to a tale of a military commander poisoned by a gift — a magic horn with Midnight Tears on the mouthpiece. A locked safe that could not be opened.
- *Bookwyrm's chambers*: Clean, no poison detected.

**Witness interviews completed:**
- *Irony* (junior avowed, compulsive truth-teller): Confirmed Deadwinter supper timeline (5 PM bell, twelve dishes, shared platters); Sylvira Savikas stormed out during dinner; Kazryn Nyantani distributed gifts under the tree; Janussi received his unwrapped, dropped the wrapping paper in a wicker basket by the door; no one fell ill from leftovers. Also reported: at 1:00 AM, she encountered a figure presenting as Sylvira Savikas searching the kitchens by candlelight — notable because Sylvira is a tiefling and does not need light. The figure pointed at her throat (too hoarse to speak) and accepted honey.
- *Sprig Summerfoot* (junior avowed, poison taster): Volunteered to taste all twelve dishes; none were poisoned. Also corroborated the 1:00 AM kitchen encounter with the figure presenting as Sylvira.
- *Orrin Glass* (junior avowed, deaf in one ear): Observed Alkrist burning a scrap of paper over a candle during dinner, dropping it when two junior avowed entered. **Key observation: the string tied around Janussi's Deadwinter gift had clearly been cut and retied** — tampering occurred under the tree, in the room, by someone present.

**Poison profile established:**
- The poison is *Midnight Tears*. Activates at midnight (defined astronomically as when the sun is directly opposite the observer's location on the globe, not by a clock). Countered by: continuous westward travel to stay ahead of midnight; planar shift; or healing that draws poison from the blood. Inert and colorless before activation; only turns black after death.

**Theory of the crime (current working hypothesis):**
- A'lai Aivenmore staged the diversion (occupied Leuwin in the greenhouse).
- An unidentified dragonborn stole the Midnight Tears toxin from the apothecary.
- The book was tampered with under the evergreen tree (string cut and retied, confirmed by Orrin).
- The heart was removed and hidden in the lead-lined chalice to prevent resurrection.
- The likely motive (per Daral's analysis): clear the council of Janussi and frame Daral simultaneously, leaving A'lai's path to ascension unblocked.

**Grygum's theological debate** — Grygum's "fourth corner" synthesis (built on Stroud's Gambit, the triangle of three options) was announced to the Avowed community by Brother Vareth, drawing the bulk of the Avowed to the Oval Theatre and providing the party with an unsupervised window for investigation.
