### Zalthir

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Deneir’s Sanctum, under guard  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Former Ember Vanguard; now an independent investigator and de facto leader of the group; affiliated with Candlekeep as an avowed scholar assigned to the Jimjar thread; bound to Glabbagool as a sentient companion  

**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Eldritch Claw Tattoo (active, granting 15-foot reach via tentacles)  
- Stone Guard passes (from Gracklstugh)  
- Drow spider-silk cloak (taken from Asha Vandree)  
- Potion of Flying (stolen from Alkrist’s safe, concealed beneath skin via Glabbagool)  
- Glabbagool (gelatinous cube bonded to his left forearm; functions as an extension of his body, capable of acid secretion, squeezing through narrow spaces, and sensing environmental anomalies)  
- Minor Illusion spellbook (used for tactical deception)  
- Collection of fungi (including Ormu and Nilhogg’s nose mushrooms)  
- List of names (retrieved from earlier captivity)  
- Ice mirror (used to deflect Medusa’s gaze)  

**Notable Spells / Feats / Abilities:**  
- **Darkness** (cast with surgical precision; used to control battlefield geometry, conceal allies, and disrupt vision)  
- **Shadow Step** (teleports through shadows; used for repositioning and surprise attacks)  
- **Stunning Strike** (monastic ability used to immobilize enemies)  
- **Eldritch Maul** (transformed hands into 15-foot tentacles; used to grapple and control targets)  
- **Minor Illusion** (used to fabricate structures and deceive targets, e.g., posing as an architect)  
- **Street Justice** (feat formalizing grappling and binding mechanics; enables two-turn setup to pin enemies for Daz’s Cloud of Daggers)  
- **Shadow Monk Training** (from The Garden Shadow; includes precise lichen harvesting, redirection of magical energy through shadow space, and silent movement)  
- **Monastic Discipline** (emphasizes discretion, avoidance of frontal conflict, emotional detachment, and self-administered discipline under fear or trauma)  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **Role:** Primary investigator, tactical strategist, and moral anchor of the group. Acts as the group’s silent architect, analyzing patterns, exploiting weaknesses, and executing high-precision maneuvers.  
- **Reports to:** None; operates independently but aligns with Daz’s leadership in tactical matters.  
- **Key Relationships:**  
  - **Glabbagool:** Bonded companion; Zalthir considers him a student of monastic discipline and an extension of his own tactical capacity.  
  - **Daz:** Tactical partner; Zalthir has developed the “Ember Grapple” synergy with Daz’s area-of-effect spells.  
  - **Grygum:** Mutual respect; Zalthir recognizes Grygum’s insight and moral clarity, silently approving his decisions.  
  - **Kalan:** Under investigation; Zalthir suspects Kalan is acting out of self-preservation, not principle.  
  - **Jimjar:** Subject of ongoing investigation; Zalthir confirmed Jimjar’s divine-level immunities and resistances, recognizing him as non-human.  
  - **Bookwyrm:** Officially under her authority at Candlekeep; Zalthir is skeptical of her impartiality.  
  - **Asha Vandree:** Former adversary; Zalthir killed her decisively and took her cloak, viewing her as a symbol of structural erasure.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Successfully stole the Potion of Flying from Alkrist’s safe during the chaos of the Sanctum confrontation, concealing it beneath his skin via Glabbagool.  
- Grappled and neutralized a helmed horror using Glabbagool’s acid to dissolve its armor, deducing the constructs were tracking the key’s magical resonance, not its bearer.  
- Analyzed and exposed Kalan’s flawed logic regarding the Zone of Truth spell, questioning the morality of his academic principles over justice.  
- Identified Sylvira Savikas’s appearances as spanning a two-hour window, proving the use of multiple or complex magical effects beyond standard polymorph.  
- Confirmed the murder of Janussi was a double-strike: poisoning via “Midnight Tears” concealed in a book, followed by heart removal to prevent resurrection, with Daral framed.  
- Accepted the frightened condition during the fight with Ilvara Mizzrym as self-administered monastic discipline, refusing to exploit angles or resist the rule — a defining moment of internal conviction.  
- Killed Asha Vandree with the Eldritch Claw Tattoo, acting when others hesitated, and refused to justify the act — “I made it. That’s all.”  
- Offered Glabbagool monastic training, recognizing his instinct to reach toward problems as a higher form of discipline than Zalthir himself mastered.  

**Revealed Motivations or Secrets:**  
- Zalthir’s monastic training emphasized control through proximity, silence, and discipline — not violence. He believes the true purpose of the monastery was learning to hold something without letting go, even when it humiliates or incapacitates you.  
- He distrusts convenient explanations, but does not dismiss them — he believes the world is built on debts, hidden motives, and structural silences.  
- He is haunted by the erasure he experienced as a Drow — treated as furniture — and sees it mirrored in Candlekeep’s culture of performative civility and hidden grudges.  
- He has been maintaining a secret column since Kaelira revealed someone powerful was paying to protect a man he couldn’t understand — the patron entry has remained blank for months.  
- He believes the most dangerous person in any room is the one who has already decided they’ve won.  
- He is no longer seeking escape or freedom — he is seeking to understand the architecture of power, deception, and silence that governs the world.  

## Uncertainty  
- Contradictory dragonborn lineage: Some facts label Zalthir as a bronze dragonborn, others as gold — later fact (ch28) confirms he is bronze, and Duergar mistakenly perceive him as red due to magical misperception.  
- Unclear origin of the “Ember Vanguard” identity: Zalthir is repeatedly mistaken for an Ember Vanguard member due to racial prejudice and magical distortion, but it is never confirmed whether he ever formally belonged to the group.  
- Ambiguous timeline of Glabbagool’s bonding: While Glabbagool is confirmed as bonded to Zalthir’s arm (ch58, ch61), the exact moment and mechanics of the bond are not described — only its effects.  
- Unclear attribution of “The Garden Shadow” teachings: Multiple instructors (Brother Heslin, Brother Quellin, Brother Tharusk) are cited as sources of Zalthir’s wisdom, but he consistently admits he does not remember who taught him what — suggesting unreliable memory or deliberate obfuscation.  
- Uncertain whether Zalthir is still physically carrying the list of names from ch01 — no later mention confirms its current location or status.  
- Unclear if Zalthir’s vow of silence (from ch03) remains active — he speaks frequently in later chapters, suggesting it was temporary or abandoned.  
- Unclear whether Zalthir’s “maybe list” for killing Daz is still active or merely a theoretical construct — no later confirmation or revision is given.  
- Uncertain if Zalthir’s perception of the Overbright as “violence” is a permanent psychological shift or situational trauma — no follow-up explores its long-term impact.  
- Unclear if Zalthir’s internal monologue about “Leemooggoogoon, Demogorgon, two-headed baby dragons” (ch23) reflects a belief, a joke, or a mental breakdown — no resolution is provided.  
- Ambiguous status of the “Jimjar thread” at Candlekeep: Zalthir is assigned to investigate it, but it is never confirmed whether he has completed it or what he discovered.
