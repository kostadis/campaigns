### The Blind Monk

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed at Zalthir’s training site)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Senior evaluator of the Shadow Monk Order, bound by canonical doctrine  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Evaluating Zalthir’s mastery of shadow monk techniques; reports to no named superior but enforces institutional orthodoxy. Last interacted with Zalthir, whom he has challenged to demonstrate unorthodox techniques.  
**Defining recent actions:** Observed Zalthir perform a shadow step that violated canonical form — skipping the third breath, misdirecting ki into the fingertips — yet acknowledged its superior speed (3 seconds faster than the Grandmaster). Declared Zalthir’s technique “technically wrong in every scroll” but demanded he show “what else you haven’t learned.” Previously judged Zalthir’s failed demonstration with the cold pronouncement, “You have learned nothing.”  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Upholds institutional dogma but is internally conflicted — recognizes the efficacy of adaptive mastery even as he cannot reconcile it with tradition. His blindness may symbolize or enable his detachment from form, allowing him to perceive function over ritual.  

## Uncertainty

- Whether he remains actively evaluating Zalthir or has moved on to another task is unclear.  
- The phrase “You have learned nothing” was said twice in close succession — it is ambiguous whether this was a repeated judgment or a single utterance reported twice with slightly different context.
