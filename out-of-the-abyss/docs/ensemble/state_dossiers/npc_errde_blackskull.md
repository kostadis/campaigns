### Errde Blackskull

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Within the Deepking’s city, likely in the Stone Guard headquarters (last referenced as receiving warnings about the party’s presence)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Captain of the Stone Guards; nominally loyal to the Deepking, but operating with autonomous, self-serving ambition  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Leading the Stone Guards in a covert, obsessive hunt for Droki, the Duergar rogue, whom she believes is tied to the Council of Savants and the Gray Ghosts.  
- Actively seeking physical proof of the Council of Savants’ corruption and their link to the “Empty Scabbard Killers” (assassins with no entrance or exit wounds).  
- Manipulating external parties (including the party) to investigate on her behalf, offering payment (350 gold worth of equipment), protection from Drow slave claims, and promises of safe passage in exchange for evidence.  
- Publicly denies the existence of psychic assassins, but privately directs investigations into the unexplained deaths of two of her own clan members.  
- Engaged in political deception, using layered analogies and emotional manipulation to control narratives and avoid direct confrontation with the Derro or the Council.  
- Her paranoia and megalomania have led her to interpret all unrest as part of a grand conspiracy centered on the Council of Savants, which she seeks to destroy to seize power.  
- Her inaction in sending troops into the Derro slums reveals strategic cowardice masked as prudence.  
- Unwittingly aiding the Cult’s destabilization efforts by focusing her resources on phantom threats.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Continues to pressure the party for evidence linking Droki, the Council of Savants, and the Empty Scabbard Killers.  
- Has not sent Stone Guard forces into the West Cleft District, despite knowing Droki’s location, to avoid provoking war with the Derro.  
- Is being alerted to the party’s movements by her own guards, indicating she remains active and alert to potential threats or opportunities.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Her stated goal of “protecting the city from corruption” is a cover for her personal ambition to rise in power by destroying the Council of Savants.  
- She is consumed by paranoia and megalomania, selectively interpreting evidence to fit her narrative.  
- She is emotionally manipulative, using promises of freedom and protection to exploit others.  
- She is easily swayed by narrative control—Daz’s feigned ignorance and Grygum’s ambiguity have successfully shaped her perceptions.  
- She is not acting in the city’s true interest, but in her own, and her actions are accelerating the city’s collapse.
