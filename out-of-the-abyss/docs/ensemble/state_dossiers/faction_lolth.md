### Lolth

**Current status:** Active and reasserting influence.  
**Current location:** Not physically present; influence manifests through devotees in the ruins of Ilvara’s fall.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Central deity and ideological anchor of the Lolthite cult, now operating through reinterpretation of catastrophe rather than overt dominance.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (as a deity; influence is ideological, not material).  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Ideological foundation for Asha Vandree’s worldview and actions.  
- Reinterpreted as the source of spiritual compensation by Asha Vandree, who claims direct, non-conventional access to Lolth’s gratitude.  
- Cult activity persists through prayers rising from ruin, ensuring continuity despite apparent defeat.  
- Functions as the fabricated identity basis for Daz, used as a social tool by Asha to navigate and manipulate social structures.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Reasserted presence not through direct intervention, but through the transformation of Ilvara’s fall into a devotional narrative.  
- Maintained cult activity via Asha Vandree’s personal, non-traditional devotion and the strategic deployment of Lolth-aligned identity by Daz.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks survival and continuity through reinterpretation rather than conquest — her power now resides in narrative control and psychological adaptation, not physical dominion.  
- Does not require orthodox worship; her influence thrives even when worshippers claim direct, unmediated access to her will.

## Uncertainty
- Unclear whether Asha Vandree’s claim of feeling Lolth’s gratitude is genuine belief, delusion, or manipulation — the text does not confirm Lolth’s agency in this exchange.  
- No evidence confirms whether Daz’s fabricated identity as a "Lolth-aligned drow" is known to others as false, or if it has successfully deceived them — the function is described, but the social outcome is not.  
- No information on whether other Lolthite cults or followers beyond Asha Vandree and Daz still exist or operate.
