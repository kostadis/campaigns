### Lolth

- **Current status:** Active divine entity, central to drow religious doctrine.
- **Faction alignment:** Supreme deity of the drow; worshipped as the Spider Queen.
- **Current role:** Spiritual and ideological foundation of drow society; her name and doctrine are actively exploited as tools of manipulation and social control.
- **Recent actions:** Her invoked authority is weaponized by Daz to deceive Asha Vandree, who herself claims direct, non-ritualistic reception of Lolth’s gratitude — suggesting a fractured or personalized interpretation of her divinity.
- **Significance:** Worship of Lolth is not merely religious but a structural pillar of power, identity, and deception in the Underdark.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between Asha Vandree’s claim of direct, personal reception of Lolth’s gratitude (ch49) and the institutionalized, dogmatic worship described in ch51 — is Asha a heretic, a true mystic, or lying? The nature of her connection to Lolth is unresolved.
- It is unclear whether Lolth herself actively communicates with individuals like Asha, or if such claims are entirely self-generated or manipulated by others (like Daz). The entity’s current direct involvement in mortal affairs is unconfirmed.
