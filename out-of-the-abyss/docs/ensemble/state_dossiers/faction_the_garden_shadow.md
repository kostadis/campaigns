### The Garden Shadow

- **Current status**: Active philosophical/ideological group.
- **Current location**: Unknown; no current location specified.
- **Allegiance/faction**: Independent; no known affiliation with other factions.
- **Core tenets / defining sayings**: 
  - “The most dangerous person in a room isn't the one with the blade drawn. It's the one who has already decided they've won.” (attributed possibly to Brother Tervin)
  - “Count what should be present before you count what is.” (attributed to The Garden Shadow or Brother Tharusk)
- **Notable beliefs**: Emphasizes perception of absence over presence; prioritizes psychological dominance and unspoken certainty in conflict.
- **Current assignment / role**: Functions as a training institution, though its curriculum is noted to be deficient in preparing graduates for the extreme conditions of the Underdark.
- **Defining recent actions**: Graduates of the institution have been deployed into Underdark operations, revealing a mismatch between training and real-world demands.

## Uncertainty
- The attribution of “Count what should be present before you count what is.” is unclear — is it a core saying of The Garden Shadow or solely Brother Tharusk’s personal belief?
- The attribution of “the most dangerous person...” saying is ambiguous — is it a formal doctrine of The Garden Shadow or merely a phrase used by Brother Tervin?
- It is unclear whether The Garden Shadow still actively trains recruits, or if its role as a training institution is historical or defunct.
- No current leadership structure or geographic base is confirmed.
