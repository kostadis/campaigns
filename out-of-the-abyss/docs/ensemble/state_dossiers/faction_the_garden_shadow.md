### The Garden Shadow

- **Current status:** Active philosophical/ideological group.
- **Core tenets:** 
  - A room reveals itself most through what is missing — emphasize absence before presence.
  - The most dangerous person is the one who has already decided they’ve won.
- **Key attributed sayings:** 
  - “Count what should be present before you count what is.” (attributed variably to Brother Tharusk)
  - “The most dangerous person in a room isn't the one with the blade drawn. It's the one who has already decided they've won.” (attributed variably to Brother Tervin)
- **Current standing:** Known and referenced as a source of aphoristic wisdom, not as a militant or operational faction.
- **Recent actions:** No direct actions reported; influence persists through transmitted sayings and conceptual framing.
