### Daral

**Current status:** Alive and fully restored.  
**Current location:** Unspecified, but last seen engaging with the party after being cured.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former head and founder of the Grygumite School; now independent, no longer affiliated with the Council in any official capacity.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- No longer poisoned or ill; fully healed by Dawnbringer.  
- No longer in possession of *The Golden Ass* or any known poisoned item.  
- Carries no weapons or magical artifacts beyond scholarly tools.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Newly appointed as a researcher into magical swords with sentience, specifically focusing on Dawnbringer’s nature and aesthetics.  
- Has declared the study of sword semiotics his life’s new scholarly pursuit.  
- Reports to no one; operates autonomously.  
- Has established a rapport with the party, particularly expressing gratitude toward Dawnbringer and accepting the role of food-taster as a new best practice.  
- Last seen with the party; no known companions.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Reclaimed the poisoned book (*The Golden Ass*) and deliberately ingested its poison to prove he was being framed — licking his finger twice after touching the book, mirroring his original method of delivery.  
- Was cured of the poisoning by Dawnbringer.  
- Immediately shifted from fear and desperation to scholarly euphoria, announcing his intent to research sentient swords as his life’s work.  
- Voluntarily accepted the role of food-taster for the party’s safety, framing it as a “new best practice.”  
- Ceased bragging and began complimenting the party, signaling emotional surrender and trust.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Was framed for Janussi’s murder by A’lai Aivenmore, who perceived Daral’s love of poetry and fiction as a threat to his own ascension.  
- Believed he was being set up to take the fall, and his self-poisoning was an act of desperate truth-telling, not suicide.  
- His earlier celebration of Janussi’s death stemmed from relief at being merely fired — not executed — after assaulting Janussi in the past.  
- His focus on Sylvira Savikas’s absence was not random; he was aware of her impersonation, though he never confirmed it.  
- His emotional volatility (laughter, fear, euphoria) was tied to unresolved trauma from his dismissal and the near-execution he expected.  

## Uncertainty  
- Was Daral’s self-poisoning an act of self-sacrifice, self-punishment, or both? The text implies both but does not clarify intent.  
- Did Daral retain any knowledge of Sylvira Savikas’s impersonation beyond suspicion? He noticed her absence, but no later statement confirms he knew she was a fraud.  
- Is Daral’s new research on sentient swords being formally supported by any institution, or is it purely personal? The text says he intends to make it his life’s work, but not whether he has funding, access, or a lab.  
- The exact nature of the “curse or transformation” referenced in ch61 is unspecified — was it purely physical poisoning, or something metaphysical? Only the poisoning is confirmed.  
- Did Daral ever formally resign from the Grygumite School, or is he still its founder in title only? His status as “former head” is stated, but not whether he relinquished the title.
