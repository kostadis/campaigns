### Buppido

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Corpse found in derro hideout beneath the West Cleft  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly affiliated with the Council of Savants; self-proclaimed divine prophet of the “Divine Plan”  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (corpse); previously carried a shortsword stained with blood (denied responsibility for Yukyuk’s death); no evidence of current magical items or active spells  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** None (deceased); last known role was as a self-styled messianic figure seeking to “save his people” from the Council of Savants. Formerly partnered with Pelek (whom he murdered) and had ties to Grygum (whom he dismembered). Last seen interacting with the party as a delusional, manipulative informant before his death.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Claimed to be on a divine mission to return to Gracklstugh and save his people from the Council of Savants.  
- Constructed a macabre shrine of humanoid remains in the caverns beneath the West Cleft.  
- Murdered Pelek, a former associate and now spectral companion of the party, to eliminate competition and secure smuggling routes.  
- Attacked the party with manic violence in his hideout, invoking the “Divine Plan” as justification.  
- Was slain by Daz’s magic missiles after being confronted with the futility of his delusions: *“Your divine mission is now over.”*  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believed himself to be the incarnate god Diinkarazan, or a divine agent thereof.  
- His descent into madness began after the Faerzress glowed and dimmed, triggering a delusional conviction of divinity.  
- Was a former merchant and courier for the Council of Savants, specializing in smuggling goods to evade Duergar taxes.  
- Killed Pelek and dismembered Grygum to eliminate rivals and consolidate control over underground networks.  
- After consuming the Tongue of Madness, revealed contempt for the party members (e.g., “Daz has foul-smelling breath,” “Eldeth is -not- interested in [Thorin]”), exposing hidden tensions.  
- Repeatedly evaded moral responsibility for murders by blaming “the agent of the divine plan.”  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch18] (Buppido was a Flumph who went mad) and all other facts (Buppido is consistently described as a Derro). The later and more numerous facts (ch01–ch37) identify him as a Derro; ch18 may be misattribution or metaphorical.  
- Unclear whether Buppido’s claim of being “invisible” in ch14 was literal (magical) or delusional — no evidence of actual invisibility magic, but his mental state makes interpretation ambiguous.  
- Unclear if Buppido’s “teleportation or rapid displacement” mentioned in ch17 was actual magic or perceptual illusion — no direct evidence of spellcasting or movement beyond narrative description.  
- [ch17] claims he was “dead” and his corpse found; [ch37] confirms he killed Pelek before the party sought to bury him — consistent with death occurring prior to ch37, but no exact timing given.  
- The phrase “Buppido had killed Pelek” in ch37 is stated as a fact, but the narrative context implies Pelek was a ghost traveling with the party — how and when Buppido killed him (before or after his spectral state?) is unclear.  
- [ch14] says Buppido was a “repentant Duergar,” but all other facts label him a Derro — Duergar and Derro are distinct subraces; this is likely a misstatement or confusion by the narrator.
