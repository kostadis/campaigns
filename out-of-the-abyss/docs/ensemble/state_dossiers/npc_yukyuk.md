### Yukyuk

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Within the Silken Paths, where his corpse was found with a throat wound from a shortsword  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a freelance goblin guide; was promised 20 gold by Jimjar to accompany the group into the tunnels  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None — described as possessing no material valuables  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Former guide and companion to Spiderbait and the party; last seen with Zalthir, Spiderbait, Jimjar, and the group. Was impressed by Zalthir’s spiderweb maneuver and praised it as “next-level.”  
**Defining recent actions:** Critiqued the group’s web-tactics, suggesting sliding under spiders and cutting webs from the opposite side; expressed admiration for Zalthir’s skill; was murdered with a shortsword to the throat.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Motivated by payment (20 gold); driven by curiosity; known for dramatic web-surfing flair including a twist-and-flip maneuver; spoke goblin words written in draconic script; wore an orange color motif, baggy clothing, and a beanie; body covered in incorrectly spelled draconic tattoos.

- Unclear attribution of death: The corpse is found with a throat cut by a shortsword, but no actor is named or identified as responsible.
- The tattoos are described as “goblin words using a draconic script” and “the spelling is wrong,” but their content or meaning is not revealed — their significance remains unknown.
