### 1001 Tashalian Nights

A black leather-bound volume with stars and gold filigree, currently in Alkrist’s possession. Found on a shelf in his chambers, bookmarked precisely at the passage detailing the poison “Midnight Tears” applied to a magic horn. The passage is annotated in Alkrist’s hand with instructions for the poison’s use. The book is not the murder weapon but served as the source and methodological guide for its deployment. Its presence and annotations indicate premeditation and studied intent.
