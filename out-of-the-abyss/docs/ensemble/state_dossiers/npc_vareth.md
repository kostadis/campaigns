## Uncertainty
- The name in Chapter 60 is spelled “Varath,” while all prior chapters use “Vareth.” This may be a typo or an intentional variant — attribution is ambiguous.  
- It is unclear whether Vareth or Heslin taught the cohort the “art of the urgent errand”; the speaker is genuinely uncertain.  
- The phrase “a living half-orc cleric of Bahamut had walked through his door” (ch54) conflicts with the description of Vareth as a half-elf scholar — it is unclear whether this refers to Vareth himself (contradicting his half-elf identity) or to the speaker. Given later context, it likely refers to the speaker, but the phrasing is ambiguous.  
- The term “Stroud’s Gambit” is attributed to Vareth as a newly coined term he embraced — but it is unclear whether he coined it or merely adopted it from the speaker.  
- Whether Vareth retains any personal connection to Bahamut beyond scholarly interest is unstated — his clerical status (if any) is never confirmed.
