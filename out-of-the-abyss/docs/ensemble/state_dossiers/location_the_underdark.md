### The Underdark

- **Current status**: Active, oppressive, and sentient-seeming subterranean realm.  
- **Current location**: Beneath the surface world; the primary environment of recent events.  
- **Current control/occupation**: No single faction controls it; dominated by fungal corruption (fungal cult, Demon Queen of the Fungi), Duergar, Drow, Derro, and native horrors.  
- **Current possessions / notable features**:  
  - Tunnels that force crawling, drop-offs, ledges over voids, and uneven rubble.  
  - Walls and terrain that function as dynamic architecture — rocks in the right place become walls; terrain is the only reliable evidence.  
  - Corrupted Myconid colonies spreading and altering the natural order.  
  - A pervasive fungal presence tied to the Demon Queen of the Fungi.  
  - The ability to force corpses to erupt rather than fall.  
  - An environment that induces psychological imprinting — survival instincts, moral ambiguity, and erasure of surface-world values persist after exit.  
- **Current assignment / role**: The defining environment shaping the party’s identity, tactics, and perception of value. It functions as an antagonist, teacher, and ecosystem.  
- **Key relationships**:  
  - Hostile to surface-world norms and dignity.  
  - Aligned with fungal corruption and the Demon Queen of the Fungi.  
  - Exploited and adapted to by Duergar, Drow, Derro, and survivalist travelers.  
- **Defining recent actions**:  
  - Shaped the party’s journey from the fungal cult’s heart to Blingdenstone.  
  - Enforced brutal adaptation through spatial mastery, lethal terrain, and psychological erosion.  
  - Enabled the Demon Queen of the Fungi’s expansion and dominance over native ecosystems.  
  - Retained its legacy in survivors’ minds — habits, perceptions, and trauma persist beyond exit.  

## Uncertainty
- The extent of the Demon Queen of the Fungi’s direct influence vs. the Underdark’s innate corruption is implied but not explicitly distinguished — is the fungal spread a result of the Underdark’s nature or her active dominion?  
- Whether the Underdark’s sentient qualities (e.g., “remembers who fell — and who rose”) are metaphorical or literal is never confirmed — it is described as a “character,” but no mechanism for agency is given.  
- The current presence or activity of Duergar, Drow, or Derro factions is referenced historically but not confirmed as active or dominant *now* — their presence may be residual or diminished by fungal takeover.  
- The exact geographical boundaries or depth layers of the Underdark are never specified — it is consistently described as vast, but no landmarks or zones are named as currently relevant.
