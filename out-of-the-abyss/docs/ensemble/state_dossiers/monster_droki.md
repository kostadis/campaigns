### Droki

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Traveling through the Whorlstone Caverns; last observed following a trail from Buppido’s lair, through the fungal thicket, past the Black Pool, and into unknown tunnels.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Council of Savants  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Derro courier and messenger for the Council of Savants; follows habitual, ritualistic walking patterns through the slums and caverns. His route is the only known safe path to the Whorlstone Caverns. Likely aware of the Faerzress anomaly and acting on direct orders.  
**Defining recent actions:** Leaves a clear, unmistakable trail through caverns and fungal thickets; mutters repetitive phrases at varying volumes; wanders seemingly without purpose among derro settlements; does not attempt to evade followers.
