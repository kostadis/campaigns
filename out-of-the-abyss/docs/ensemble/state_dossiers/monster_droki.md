### Droki

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Travelling through the Whorlstone Caverns; last observed following a trail from Buppido’s lair, through the fungal thicket, past the Black Pool, and into unknown tunnels.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Council of Savants  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Derro courier and messenger for the Council of Savants; trusted conduit between factions due to his unnaturally fast pace and ritualistic muttering. His path through the slums is the only known safe route to the Whorlstone Caverns. Likely aware of the Faerzress anomaly and acting on direct orders.  
**Defining recent actions:** Leaves a clear, habitual trail through caverns and fungal thickets; mutters repetitive phrases at varying volumes; wanders unpredictably among derro settlements; does not evade pursuit; appears to be on a scheduled routing between key locations.
