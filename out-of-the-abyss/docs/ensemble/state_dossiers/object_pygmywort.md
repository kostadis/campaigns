### Pygmywort

- **Type:** Magical mushroom  
- **Current appearance:** One-inch-long stem, stubby blue cap with white dots  
- **Current effect:** When consumed, triggers a *reduce* spell effect, shrinking the consumer  
- **Current location:** Not specified (last mentioned in context of consumption by Grygum)  
- **Significance:** One of two known magical mushrooms in the region; paired with Bigwig (which causes *enlarge*)

## Uncertainty
- The current physical location of any remaining Pygmywort specimens is unknown.  
- It is unclear whether Pygmywort is still intact after Grygum consumed it, or if more exist.  
- No information is given about growth conditions, rarity, or whether it regenerates.
