### Ormu (Silken Paths)

- **Type:** Object (biological substance)  
- **Current location:** In the possession of Sethir, used to draw a map in Chapter 6.  
- **Current condition:** Harvested, dried, and applied as pigment in a map.  
- **Notable properties:** Emits dim light for 5 feet in its living form; can be dried into phosphorescent powder or pigment.  
- **Origin:** Collected by Zalthir from a narrow tunnel in the Silken Paths.  
- **Recent use:** Applied by Sethir to draw a map.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the Ormu used by Sethir is still in its powdered form or has fully dried into a permanent pigment on the map.  
- No fact confirms whether the original fungal source still exists in the tunnel or if only the harvested residue remains.  
- The exact physical state of the Ormu *now* (e.g., still on the map, scraped off, stored) is not specified beyond its use in drawing.
