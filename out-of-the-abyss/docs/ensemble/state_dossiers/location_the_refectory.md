### The Refectory

- **Type:** Location (dining hall within Candlekeep)  
- **Current state:** Operational, actively used for scholarly meals and prophecy recitation  
- **Key architectural features:**  
  - Vast, high-ceilinged hall, designed to dwarf human scale — high enough for a hill giant to sit comfortably at one end  
  - A long table in the center of the hall where scholars and visitors dine  
  - A high table at one end where the eight Great Readers sit  
  - A gallery overhead from which prophecies are silently recited  
- **Function:**  
  - Primary dining hall for scholars of Candlekeep  
  - Stage for social observation, intellectual tension, and the silent recitation of prophecies  
  - Architecture engineered to induce awe and reflect the tension between order and chaos  
- **Power dynamics:** Authority is displayed through posture, presence, and seating — not through thrones or overt symbols

None.
