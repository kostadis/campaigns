### The Refectory

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current state:** Intact and operational  
- **Location:** Unspecified, but associated with an academic institution housing the Great Readers  
- **Description:** A vast dining hall originally built for scholars, featuring an extraordinarily high ceiling — sufficient to allow a hill giant to sit comfortably at one end without embarrassment.  
- **Atmosphere:** Characterized by loud, intellectual debate among scholars who are acutely aware of their institutional importance.
