### Myconid

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed speaking with Zalthir)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid circle (associated with Rump-a-dump)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Advocate for Rump-a-dump; seeks nonviolent resolution for dissatisfied members of her circle. Last known interaction: telepathic and spore-based communication with Zalthir.  
**Defining recent actions:** Persistently urged Zalthir to assist Rump-a-dump, emphasized nonviolence, claimed her kind can convert dead enemies into zombies. Became emotionally distressed when Zalthir expressed fear of becoming undead. Fell asleep after Zalthir invoked parental advice.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believes her kind are akin to zombies and that converting enemies into undead is a valid or necessary act. Views killing as morally wrong because targets are “like her.” Demonstrates childlike emotional intensity and a strong sense of communal loyalty.
