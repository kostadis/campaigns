### Ront

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** In the possession of a vrock, at the site of the battle in Chapter 3 (last seen being consumed by the demon)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly under Drow command; now deceased, no allegiance  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (body and head destroyed; no items mentioned as retained)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** None (deceased); last known to be acting independently after killing a Drow guard, driven by guilt and a need to prove himself  
**Defining recent actions:** After killing a Drow guard in Chapter 2, Ront, consumed by guilt and a need for redemption, attacked an injured vrock in Chapter 3. His first blow cut through the creature’s hide, causing gangrenous blood to ooze — giving him hope he had wounded it fatally. The vrock retaliated with startling speed, killing him with one taloned hand, seizing his lifeless body, and snapping off his head before swallowing it. His headless corpse fell behind Daz as he escaped.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Ront believed he must prove himself — likely to atone for perceived cowardice — and saw killing the vrock as his path to redemption.

## Uncertainty
- The phrase “Ront and Prince Derendil, the deluded Quaggoth” in Chapter 1 ambiguously implies Ront *is* Prince Derendil or that they are two separate entities — but no other fact distinguishes them. All subsequent actions (fighting, killing the guard, attacking the vrock) are attributed solely to “Ront.” Thus, it is unresolved whether “Prince Derendil” is Ront’s delusional title or a separate individual mistakenly grouped with him.  
- No other entity is confirmed to have interacted with Ront after his death; the vrock’s fate after consuming him is unknown, but this is not relevant to Ront’s current state.  
- The nature of Ront’s “delusion” is stated but never explained — whether it was a false belief in royal status, a magical effect, or a psychological break — remains unclarified.
