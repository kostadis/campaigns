### High Tower

The High Tower is a heavily monitored structure serving as the entrance to a repository of super-magical artifacts sought by all evil entities in the world. It is protected by both magical and physical surveillance, and is watched at all times by unseen sentinels. Unauthorized access is impossible without detection — entry is visibly observed regardless of whether a key is in possession.
