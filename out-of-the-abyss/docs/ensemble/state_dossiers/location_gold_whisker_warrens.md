### Gold Whisker Warrens

- **Type:** Location (subterranean warren complex)  
- **Current state:** Active, occupied, and controlled by the wererat gnome clan.  
- **Location:** Underground, accessed via massive double doors.  
- **Control/occupancy:** Ruled by Chief Chipgrin, who sits on a stone chair draped with a cave badger pelt.  
- **Key features:**  
  - Leadership resides on a moss-covered plateau, symbolizing their connection to nature and rejection of gnome bureaucracy.  
  - Patrolled by guards with increasingly apparent rat-like features: stringy mustaches, pronounced noses, and other subtle wererat characteristics.  
  - Flanked by two giant rats near Chief Chipgrin’s seat.  
- **Inhabitants:** Clan of wererat gnomes, including Chief Chipgrin and his guard contingent.

None.
