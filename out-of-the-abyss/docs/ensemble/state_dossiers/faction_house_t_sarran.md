### House T'sarran

**Current status:** Active and intact.  
**Current location:** Undisclosed, but operates from an ancient drow stronghold in the Underdark.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent drow noble house; no known formal alliances, but engages in covert operations across the surface and Underdark.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Deploys miniaturized drow operatives (likely via spell or magical artifact) for espionage.  
- Possesses knowledge of secure prison escapes and counter-surveillance techniques.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Primary objective: Identify the entity that hired the Duskryn sisters to protect Daz.  
- Conducts long-term surveillance on Daz and his associates via surface agents.  
- Maintains a network of intermediaries and professional mercenaries for high-stakes contracts.  
- Has issued binding contracts to extract Daz alive and unharmed.  
- Considered a threat by the Duskryn sisters, who were contracted to neutralize House T'sarran and House Mizzrym.  
- Viewed by Candlekeep as a powerful external authority whose demands must not be met — but whose presence is not to be ignored.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Sent a miniaturized spy to surveil Daz and the party on the surface; the spy was captured and miniaturized further.  
- Threatened vengeance for the capture of its agent, indicating readiness for retaliation.  
- Demonstrated resilience: despite agent loss, it has the capacity and intent to send replacements.  
- Underwent no recent internal crisis — its two-century rule remains unchallenged and stable.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Motive for targeting Daz is tied to uncovering who hired his protectors — suggesting Daz’s past (possibly linked to Jimjar’s disappearance) holds critical value.  
- Operates with patience, prioritizing intelligence gathering over direct confrontation.  
- May have intentionally allowed the spy’s capture to provoke internal discord within the party or test their loyalties.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact location of House T'sarran’s stronghold remains unknown.  
- The nature of the magic enabling miniaturization is unspecified.  
- Whether House T'sarran is directly responsible for Jimjar’s disappearance or merely connected through intermediaries is unconfirmed.  
- The identity of the entity that hired the Duskryns to protect Daz — the core mystery driving House T'sarran’s actions — is still unknown.  
- Contradiction: Ch51 suggests House T'sarran’s forces were "annihilated" in the cavern, but later chapters (ch53–55) confirm the house remains active, sending agents and issuing contracts — later facts override earlier, so the annihilation is not current state.  
- Attribution ambiguity: Ch51 and ch53 refer to “the matron mother” as the decision-maker, but ch54 reveals the spy’s matron ruled for two centuries — no name is given, and it’s unclear if this is the same matron referenced earlier.
