### Derro Territory

**Current status:** Fragmented, oppressed, and on the brink of violent uprising.  
**Current location:** Subterranean burrows beneath Gracklstugh, with presence in slums and city edges.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Cultic devotees of Demogorgon, under the ritual leadership of Narrak; victims of the Ember Vanguard and oppressors of the Duergar; targeted by the Gray Ghosts for vengeance.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- None explicitly stated as currently held by the faction as a whole.  
- Individual Derro are observed clutching body parts (obsessive behavior), but no specific items are named as faction-owned.  
- Psionic sensitivity is a racial trait, amplified by trauma; no new spells or feats are documented as newly acquired.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serve as ritualists, laborers, and necromancers in Demogorgon’s cult, performing chants and transformations to empower their city’s doom.  
- Obey Narrak, but with half-hearted, undisciplined compliance.  
- Oppressed and exploited by the Duergar.  
- Targeted for extermination by the Ember Vanguard, who killed several members of the Derro High Council.  
- Now under threat of retaliation from the Gray Ghosts, who seek vengeance for the Council’s murder.  
- No central leadership remains after the Council’s destruction; fractured into panicked, disorganized groups.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Chanted “die, die, die” in a ritual tied to Demogorgon’s triumph.  
- Underwent a synchronized psychic collapse after the theft of the “egg,” resulting in total loss of cohesion.  
- Systematically killed in large numbers by the party during a ritual site assault — their perfidy and support for Demogorgon now documented as evidence.  
- Exhibiting signs of pre-revolutionary violence, watching and waiting in slums, preparing for rebellion against Duergar rule.  
- Misidentify key details (e.g., scale color, Ember Vanguard appearance), indicating deep psychological instability.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Once peaceful, pre-Faerzress beings who shared space with Duergar; their madness stems from mind flayer corruption and the Faerzress’s disruption.  
- Believe their elevation and the destruction of the Duergar are the only purpose, as dictated by Demogorgon.  
- Their collective insanity is not merely madness — it is a symptom of deeper magical corruption spreading from the Underdark, possibly tied to the stolen egg.  
- The theft of the egg triggered a psychic tsunami that shattered their remaining unity.
