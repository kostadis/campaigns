### White Feather (Buppido’s Lair)

- **What it is**: A ritual focus used to cast fear magic.
- **Current holder/location**: In Buppido’s lair (last seen in Narrak’s possession during the fear spell cast on the Ember Vanguard).
- **Current condition**: Intact and functional as a spell focus.
- **Significance**: Symbolic tool of control, linking the caster’s will to the emotional state of targets; used to induce terror in Thorin, Grygum, and Zalthir.
- **Notable use**: Casts fear spell on three members of the Ember Vanguard (Thorin, Grygum, Zalthir) when pointed at them by Narrak.

## Uncertainty
- The feather’s current physical location after Narrak’s use is unconfirmed — it is not stated whether it was retained, dropped, or taken by another after the spell.
- It is unclear whether the feather remains in Buppido’s lair or was removed by Narrak after the encounter.
- No information is given about its origin, material composition, or whether it is unique or one of many.
