### Voucher of the Varmint (Hall of Melting Mirrors)

A braided rat's foot token, currently held by Daz, presented to him by Chief Chipgrin upon the party’s departure from the Hall of Melting Mirrors. Recognized by wererats and outcasts throughout the Underdark, from Blingdenstone to Menzoberranzan. Serves as a symbol of alliance and friendship with the wererats.

## Uncertainty
None.
