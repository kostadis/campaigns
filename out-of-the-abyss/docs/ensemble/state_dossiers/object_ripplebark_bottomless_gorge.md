### Ripplebark (bottomless gorge)

**Type:** Object (fungus)  
**Current location:** Bottomless Gorge (Underdark)  
**Current condition:** Naturally occurring, edible fungus with a rotten-fresh appearance; scorched or burnt specimens turn black but remain edible.  
**Current possessions / notable properties:**  
- Neutralizes disease germs.  
- Edible raw.  
- Roasting enhances its nutty flavor.  
- When scorched or burnt, turns black but remains edible.  

**Significance:** A resilient, multipurpose fungal organism native to the Underdark, valued for its medicinal and culinary properties.

None.
