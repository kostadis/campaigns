### The Ember Vanguard

**Current status:** Active, but no longer directly controlled by its original members.  
**Current location:** Not physically present; operates as a legendary rumor across the Underdark, particularly in Gracklstugh and surrounding caverns.  
**Allegiance/faction:** None — it is a fabricated identity with no formal hierarchy, structure, or true allegiance. Originally self-styled by Thorin, Grygum, Daz, and Zalthir, it has since detached from its creators and become an independent myth.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None. No physical artifacts, spells, or feats are attributed to the faction itself — only to individuals previously associated with it.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- The Ember Vanguard no longer functions as an operational unit.  
- Its name and reputation are now exploited by others — including cultists, Duergar, and Drow — as a bogeyman to justify fear, control, or scapegoating.  
- The original members (Daz, Thorin, Grygum, Zalthir) are no longer acting under the banner; they are now haunted by the legend they created.  
- The faction is falsely blamed by the Duergar for the assassination of their High Council and the theft of The Egg — a misattribution that persists despite the party’s innocence.  
- The group is now referenced as a rival entity in rumor, with exaggerated traits (e.g., “lies about our height”) that make their infamy a burden to the original members.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- The Ember Vanguard last acted as a cohesive unit in Chapter 52, charging into combat with fire and flash — a tactical identity distinct from “The Ember Grapple.”  
- Since then, the group has disbanded operationally. The name now exists only as a myth propagated by terrified Derro and Duergar, amplified by wanted posters, distorted descriptions, and whispered tales.  
- In Chapter 61, the original members observe that the legend of the Ember Vanguard now walks ahead of them in the tunnels — a phantom of their own making, larger, more fearsome, and entirely false.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The name was invented by Grygum as a performative tool to intimidate Derro and unify the group — not as a claim to real power, but as psychological warfare.  
- The group never had ties to Menzoberranzan, nor did they commit the crimes they are blamed for. Their entire reputation is a case of mistaken identity amplified by fear.  
- The original members now carry the burden of a reputation they never earned — the legend has outgrown them, and they are powerless to correct it.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Ch23 and ch24 suggest the group was founded by Thorin and Grygum as a unified mercenary faction, while ch24 and ch25 imply it was always a fabricated cover — which is it? Later chapters (ch61) confirm it was never real, so earlier descriptions of it as “a disciplined group” reflect its *perceived* nature, not its actual structure.  
- Ambiguous attribution: Ch25 says the Ember Vanguard is “bound by magical fear and ritual alignment with Narrak’s cult” — but this conflicts with all other evidence that the party is resisting cult influence. This may be a misperception by enemies or a psychological effect on the members, not a true allegiance.  
- Unclear chronology: Ch49 states “I am the feared mage of the Ember Vanguard” — but it is unclear if this mage is one of the original members or a new actor co-opting the name.  
- Ambiguous identity: Ch52 contrasts “The Ember Vanguard” with “The Ember Grapple” — are these two units, or is it the same group using different tactical labels? No evidence confirms a split.  
- Unclear current status of members: While the faction is now a myth, the current whereabouts and status of Daz, Thorin, Grygum, and Zalthir are not stated — only that they are now victims of their own legend.
