### The Ember Vanguard

**Current status:** Active and infamous.  
**Current location:** Operating throughout the Underdark, with rumors and wanted posters spreading through Gracklstugh and beyond.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent mercenary/assassin organization; no known higher command.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Known to wield soul-rending blades (per Duergar belief).  
- Members include a feared mage specializing in precision destructive magic (Daz).  
- Capable of casting meteor swarm (as demonstrated by the Drow Archmage under the banner).  
- Use silent, nonverbal communication as a trained operational language.  
- Associated with Shape Water usage for environmental detection.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Operate as shadowy assassins and infiltrators, specializing in stealth, psychological intimidation, and high-value strikes.  
- Daz is recognized as “the feared mage of the Ember Vanguard” and remains central to its identity.  
- The group is falsely blamed by Duergar and Derro for the assassination of the Derro High Council and theft of The Egg.  
- Their reputation is actively exploited by the Duergar as a tool of fear; posters misrepresent their appearance and deeds.  
- The party is mistaken for them, and their own actions are increasingly attributed to the Ember Vanguard in underground lore.  
- Zalthir’s monastic training within the group enables advanced environmental threat detection and silent coordination.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully used reputation to evade capture by Duergar and Drow by being mistaken for the feared group.  
- Charged into combat with fire and flash, announcing their presence — a tactic contrasted with the “Ember Grapple” stealth model.  
- Maintained operational cohesion through nonverbal communication during surveillance detection.  
- Their name and legend now precede the party in rumors, with exaggerated posters and false attributions of crimes.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Originally formed as a mercenary unit led by Thorin, later adopted and redefined by Daz as a mythic identity.  
- The Drow Archmage co-opted the name to intimidate Derro, cementing its fearsome reputation.  
- The group’s true composition is fragmented — members hold divergent beliefs (Grygum sees it as divine; Daz as a weaponized identity).  
- The Ember Vanguard is now more a legend than a coherent unit; its identity is sustained by perception, rumor, and the actions of those who impersonate or are mistaken for it.  
- Daz embraces the role of “feared mage” as a source of personal purpose, even as the group’s original members may no longer all be active together.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction over leadership: Thorin is named founder in ch23, Grygum and Daz are named leaders in ch24, but Daz is the only consistent figure linked to the group through ch51–ch61. Who leads now?  
- Attribution of the meteor swarm: Was it cast by the Drow Archmage *as* the Ember Vanguard, or did he merely borrow the name? Is the Drow Archmage a member or an imposter?  
- Are Thorin, Grygum, Zalthir, and JimJar still active members? Ch51–ch61 focus only on Daz and Zalthir; others are never mentioned again.  
- Is the “Ember Vanguard” in ch52 the same entity as the “Ember Grapple,” or are they two distinct groups? The text implies a contrast, not identity.  
- The group is described as both a “monastic order” (ch54) and a “mercenary band” (ch28, ch29) — are these two facets of the same organization, or conflicting reports?  
- The posters in ch61 bear “the party’s name” — does this mean the posters falsely label the party as the Ember Vanguard, or that the Ember Vanguard’s posters now falsely bear the party’s physical description? Ambiguous phrasing.  
- The “three members” terrified in ch25 — are these Daz, Zalthir, and Grygum? Or others? Unclear if this is the same group as later chapters.
