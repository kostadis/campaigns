### Bloppblippodd

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** At the profane altar in the Darklake, where the ritual was completed  
**Allegiance/faction:** Archpriest of Leemooggoogoon the Deep Father (cult)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified beyond ritual implements (ritual knife used in final act); no items listed as retained post-death  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Orchestrated the final sacrificial ritual to summon Demogorgon; was the high priestess and daughter of Ploopploopeen; relationship with father was strained and contested; last seen commanding sacrifice procession and delivering the fatal blow to Ploop  
**Defining recent actions:** Led the ritual sacrifice of Ploop; shouted "Bring me the knife!" and completed the incantation "Leemooggoogoon," which summoned Demogorgon; died immediately after the ritual’s completion.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction in identity: [ch11] refers to “Blopp” as the leader of the Sea Mother’s followers slain by the archpriest of the Deep Father — but then immediately identifies the archpriest as “she” who killed Blopp and uttered “Leemooggoogoon.” This implies “Blopp” and “Bloppblippodd” are distinct entities. However, all prior facts refer only to “Bloppblippodd,” and no other character named “Blopp” is introduced elsewhere. The name “Blopp” may be a shorthand or typo for “Bloppblippodd,” but this is not confirmed. If they are separate, then the final death and ritual act are misattributed in the text.  
- Unclear whether “Bloppblippodd” is the same person as the “archpriest” who killed “Blopp” — the text says “the archpriest of the Deep Father” killed Blopp, and then says “she” (referring to Bloppblippodd) delivered the blow. This is internally consistent only if Bloppblippodd *is* the archpriest — which all prior facts confirm — but the naming shift (“Blopp” vs. “Bloppblippodd”) creates ambiguity.  
- The phrase “she struck the final, fatal blow” and “she died moments after completing the ritual” are unambiguous, but the introduction of “Blopp” as a separate victim/actor creates uncertainty about whether “Blopp” was an independent NPC or a misstatement of “Bloppblippodd.”
