### Miners' Guild

- **Current status:** Active  
- **Current location:** Operating in underground regions tied to the Stoneheart Enclave (implied by association in ch38)  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Independent gnome organization; participating in inter-clan coordination (evidenced by proposed inclusion in clan meeting)  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** Weapons and armor susceptible to acidic damage (per Kazook Pickshine’s concern)  
- **Current assignment / role:** Engaged in underground mining operations; seeking external labor (expressed interest in hiring Glabbagool)  
- **Key relationships:** Represented by Kazook Pickshine; in communication with the Stoneheart Enclave; open to contractual work with outsiders like Glabbagool  
- **Defining recent actions:** Kazook Pickshine raised concerns about acidic damage to equipment during a coordination effort; members have proactively sought to hire Glabbagool for future operations
