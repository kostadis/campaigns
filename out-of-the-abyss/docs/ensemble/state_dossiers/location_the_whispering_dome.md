### The Whispering Dome

A sacred, low-light chamber within the Hall of Momentous Deeds, characterized by gold-veined architecture and enforced silence. It is a place of judgment and silent revelation, where supplicants undergo profound stillness to confront truth through presence rather than speech. Voices from the past may be heard by those who stand in the right place, though the primary function now is introspective revelation. Glabbagool was summoned here for a “little quest,” indicating its use as a site for solemn judgment or trials.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the phenomenon of hearing past voices is still active or merely residual — ch58 emphasizes silence and revelation, not auditory phenomena, but does not contradict ch56.
- The exact physical layout (e.g., whether the “right place” for hearing voices is a specific spot or a general condition) is not specified in ch58.
- It is ambiguous whether the Dome is currently under the control of a specific authority or faction, though its use for judgment implies institutional oversight.
