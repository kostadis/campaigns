### The Shattered Spire

The Shattered Spire is a tavern built on a broken stalagmite over Darklake, accessible only by a wool bridge. It functions as neutral ground where merchants, spies, and assassins meet. Its isolation makes it a focal point for secrets — every conversation is a gamble, every gemstone a bribe, every silence a threat. Werz Saltbaron uses it as a meeting place to reveal information about the cracks in the Duergar system. It is not a place of safety, but the eye of the storm.
