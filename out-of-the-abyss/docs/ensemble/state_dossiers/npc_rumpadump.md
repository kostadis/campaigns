### Rumpadump

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove  
**Allegiance/faction:** Follower of Zalthir, member of the Neverlight Grove fungal community  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None noted  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as a quiet, precise observer and advisor within the Neverlight Grove community.  
- Maintains a close, if unconventional, relationship with Zalthir, whom he critiques with detailed, unsolicited self-improvement suggestions.  
- Last seen with Stool, whom he respects and encourages Zalthir to listen to.  
- Deeply familiar with and emotionally attached to Neverlight Grove, which he regards as sacred and inviolable.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Upon entering Neverlight Grove, shouted “We’re home!” with visible joy and belonging.  
- Presented Zalthir with a list of fifty specific behavioral and dietary improvements, including: “Listen to Stool, he knows what he is doing” and “Eat more Zurkhwood, it's good for your digestion.”  
- Bid farewell to the party with quiet sadness, indicating emotional investment in their departure.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes in terse, restrained communication and rejects Stool’s verbose style.  
- Holds a naive but firm conviction that the Neverlight Grove cannot be corrupted because Zalthir (their sovereign) would not permit it.  
- Rejects anthropomorphic assumptions about myconid biology; insists mushrooms are not myconids and questions whether other races practice cannibalism.  
- Was saved by Zalthir in the past, which may underpin his loyalty and critical concern for Zalthir’s behavior.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch29] describing Rumpadump as “childlike” and [ch27]/[ch33] portraying him as a thoughtful, precise, and critically observant adult — though this may reflect differing perspectives rather than factual inconsistency.  
- Unclear whether Rumpadump is still physically with Zalthir after the farewell in ch33; he is in Neverlight Grove, but his current proximity to Zalthir is unspecified.  
- Ambiguity over whether “Zalthir” is his sovereign or if “our sovereign” refers to a higher entity — the text never clarifies Zalthir’s exact role beyond being the one who saved Rumpadump.  
- The phrase “myconid or humanoid follower” in ch31 conflicts with all other references identifying him as a myconid — later facts override this, but the source of the contradiction remains unexplained.
