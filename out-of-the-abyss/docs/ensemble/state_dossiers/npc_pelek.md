### Pelek

**Current status:** Deceased (ghost), spirit laid to rest  
**Current location:** No longer present — spirit faded after burial rites completed  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly associated with the Council of Savants; no current allegiance  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Obsidian family ring (on a lost hand; remains with remains); no longer carrying physical items  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** No longer traveling or active; last seen with the party, specifically Grygum (who carried his bones) and Dawnbringer (the cleric who performed burial rites). Last known interaction: spirit materialized to compel Glyphic Shroomlight to inter his remains.  
**Defining recent actions:** Spirit compelled a cleric to perform burial rites for his scattered bones; after the rites were completed, his spirit faded with profound relief, having feared eternal torment.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Sought a proper burial in Blingdenstone to end his spectral unrest; driven by familial shame over his name (given by his mother after an actor, opposed by his father); aware of Buppido’s betrayal, the Council of Savants’ tax evasion schemes, the Faerzress anomaly, and the dangers of Lolth and Menzoberranzan. Had attempted psychic intrusion into a thief-derro’s mind, which failed violently. Previously possessed a Derro body, but no longer does so.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch23] (Pelek claims to have occupied a Derro body) and [ch33] (Pelek denies possessing Zalthir). The latter occurs later and may imply the possession ended before ch33, but it is unclear if Zalthir was the same Derro body or if the possession was temporary and resolved.  
- Unclear whether Pelek’s ghostly state persisted *after* ch33’s denial of possession — did he abandon the Derro body permanently, or was the denial only about Zalthir specifically?  
- [ch37] states Pelek “had been traveling with the party” and was killed by Buppido — but [ch17] already established he was killed and dismembered by Buppido. This is consistent, but the phrasing in ch37 implies the party was still traveling with his ghost *after* his death, which is plausible, yet the transition from possession (ch23) to pure ghost (ch25, ch33, ch37) lacks explicit chronology.  
- The obsidian ring is mentioned in ch17 as being on a “lost hand,” but it is never confirmed whether the ring was recovered with the bones or remains lost.  
- The identity of “Glyphic Shroomlight” is not established elsewhere — whether they are a named NPC, a title, or a fungal entity — is unknown.
