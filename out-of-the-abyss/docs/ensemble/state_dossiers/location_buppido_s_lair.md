### Buppido’s Lair

Buppido’s Lair is an abandoned, grim site filled with dead bones and an altar to a demonic power. It was formerly the stronghold of the cult and the base of operations for the Ember Vanguard, but is now deserted. It is remembered as the site of a past betrayal in which Buppido killed a member of the group. The Ember Vanguard has departed from this location to return to the cultist cavern.

## Uncertainty
None.
