### Buppido's Lair

Buppido’s Lair is an abandoned, grim site filled with dead bones and an altar to a demonic power. It was formerly the stronghold of the cult and the base of operations for the Ember Vanguard, but is now deserted. The location is tied to past trauma — specifically, the betrayal and murder of a party member by Buppido. The Ember Vanguard recently departed from this location to return to the cultist cavern.

- The current condition of the altar (active, inert, damaged) is not specified beyond its existence.
- Whether Buppido’s remains or influence still linger at the site is unconfirmed.
- The exact nature of the demonic power worshipped at the altar is not named or described.
