### bronze forearm monument (The Burning Wizard Inn)

A bronze-cast forearm, severed at the elbow and not holding anything, is mounted on a stone pillar as a public memorial to Tarvis Ulain at The Burning Wizard Inn. It bears a Dwarven inscription: *“The move is yours.”* It also bears a Common inscription signed by Milo Goodbarrel, a Bahamutian epitaph, and a Stroudite inscription. The monument is associated with the Order of the Gauntlet and the Metalworkers Guild, and serves as a physical artifact embodying the collision of orthodoxy and heresy, and the unspoken acceptance of ambiguity in post-war faith.

## Uncertainty
- Contradictory attribution of inscriptions: One fact cites a Bahamutian epitaph and Stroudite inscription; another cites a Dwarven inscription and Common inscription by Milo Goodbarrel. It is unclear whether these are distinct inscriptions or overlapping descriptions of the same texts. No resolution is provided on whether Bahamutian/Stroudite texts are separate from or part of the Dwarven/Common ones.
