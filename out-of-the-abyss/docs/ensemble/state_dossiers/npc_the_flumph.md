### The Flumph

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed after the battle described in ch22)  
**Allegiance/faction:** None; self-identified as non-parasitic, non-evil, distinct from Mind Flayers  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None (previously had a gem stolen by Grygum in ch19; no new possession noted since)  
**Spells / feats:** None specified  
**Wounds:** None specified; appears psychologically exhausted  
**Current assignment / role:** None formally assigned; functions as an empathic psychic sponge, absorbing and processing emotional/psychic emissions from others  
**Key relationships:** Last known to be exploited by Daz (as a prop to feed Errde’s paranoia) and Grygum (who stole its gem); no current companions confirmed  
**Defining recent actions:** After being overwhelmed by the psychic intensity of a battle, it engaged in a trauma-dump that brought it temporary relief, causing its bioluminescent glow to settle into a soft teal — a sign of exhausted catharsis. It has since expressed that its usual trauma-dumps are “no longer as satisfying,” suggesting a growing need for stronger psychic stimuli.
