### vrock (Darklake)

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Cavern of Darklake (where it was killed)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demonic (part of the invading force alongside chasme and drow)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (corpse remains); previously had abnormally long, raptor-like clawed arms dense with lean muscle, and feathered wings.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Engaged in combat against drow and allies; last seen fighting alongside four chasme. One vrock was injured and landed on the ground before killing Ront by swallowing his head, then was killed in retaliation.  
**Defining recent actions:** One vrock landed injured, extended its feathered wings, and used its elongated raptor-like claws in combat. It fatally bit off and swallowed Ront’s head before being killed.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the injured vrock that landed and killed Ront is the same as one of the two vrock identified by Daz, or if there were three vrock total (Daz says two, but the injured one may be one of them).  
- The fate of the second vrock is unconfirmed — it was present in the cavern but no further action or death is described for it.  
- Attribution of the kill: Was the injured vrock killed by Ront (implied by “not before fatally retaliating”) or by another combatant? The text is ambiguous.
