### Circle of Masters of Neverlight Grove

- **What it is:** A secretive subgroup of myconids within Neverlight Grove, closest to Phylo.
- **Current state:** Active and operational within Neverlight Grove.
- **Allegiance/faction:** Loyal to Phylo; operates independently of other myconid circles.
- **Current role and key relationships:** 
  - Receives and reanimates dead creatures brought in by the Circle of Hunters.
  - Sends groups of myconids outside the grove without disclosing purpose or destination.
  - Maintains direct proximity and likely direct guidance from Phylo.
- **Defining recent actions:** 
  - Systematically reanimating corpses from Hunter hauls.
  - Deploying myconid agents beyond the grove’s boundaries under opaque directives.

## Uncertainty
- The exact number of members in the Circle of Masters is unknown.
- The specific destinations or purposes of the myconids sent outside the grove are unreported.
- Whether the reanimated creatures are controlled, enslaved, or autonomous is not specified.
- No details are given on the methods or limits of reanimation.
- It is unclear if the Circle of Masters reports to Phylo directly or through intermediaries.
