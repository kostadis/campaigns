### Traders' Grotto

- **Current status:** Active defensive position under Gnomish control, recently secured but scarred by conflict.  
- **Current location:** Blingdenstone, within the city’s core.  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Gnomish defense forces of Blingdenstone.  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** None (location, not entity).  
- **Current condition:** Breached wall sealed after battle; littered with residual ooze traces and patches of accelerated fungal growth. Petrified fungal stone structures from a centuries-old medusa spell still serve as market stalls, though now damaged and partially buried under ooze residue and rubble.  
- **Current assignment / role:** Primary defensive chokepoint against uncontrolled oozes; now a fortified barrier after the horde was pacified by Jimjar’s bone die via Glabbagool.  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Breached by a massive swarm of uncontrolled oozes, jellies, and black puddings no longer bound by the Pudding King’s will.  
  - Gnomish army was overwhelmed until Jimjar’s bone die, channeled through Glabbagool, pacified the ooze horde.  
  - Breach was sealed; the area remains under military guard with lingering ooze contamination and abnormal fungal proliferation.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact structural condition of the petrified fungal stalls — whether they are fully intact, partially collapsed, or repurposed as barricades — is not specified beyond "littered with rubble" and "residual ooze traces."  
- Whether the gelatinous cube battle from Chapter 38 is entirely resolved or if remnants of those creatures still linger is ambiguous; later facts focus solely on the ooze breach, suggesting the cube conflict is past, but no explicit closure is given.  
- The current presence or status of Glabbagool and Jimjar at the site is not confirmed beyond their role in pacifying the horde — they may have departed.  
- The extent of merchant activity — whether trade has resumed, been suspended, or relocated — is not stated after the ooze breach.
