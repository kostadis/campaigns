### Tongue of Madness (Blade Bazaar)

A magical mushroom that, when consumed, compels the eater to speak their true thoughts, revealing hidden animosities, resentments, delusions, and secrets. It was used by Zalthir on Buppido to extract unfiltered confessions and expose the party’s internal tensions. The mushroom exerts absolute compulsion — the consumer cannot refuse or withhold when ingested.

**Current possession/location:** Last known to be in Zalthir’s possession during the interrogation of Buppido. No evidence it has been moved, consumed, or destroyed since.

**Significance:** A potent psychological tool for extracting truth; used as an instrument of interrogation in the Blade Bazaar. Its effect is immediate, unavoidable, and psychologically devastating.

## Uncertainty
- Whether the mushroom is singular or if more exist.  
- Whether Zalthir still holds it or has transferred it to another party since the event.  
- Whether the mushroom is consumed upon use or remains physically intact after ingestion (source does not specify).
