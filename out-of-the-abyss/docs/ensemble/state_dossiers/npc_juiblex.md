### Juiblex

**Current status:** Alive (demonic presence enduring)  
**Current location:** Not explicitly stated; influence lingers in the region where the Pudding King fell  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demon lord of oozes and shapeless things  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None directly held; power is currently fragmented, but sustained by a latent demonic will  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Seeking rebirth by consuming the Queen of Fungi; previously channeled through the Pudding King, now acting through a disembodied voice  
**Defining recent actions:** After the defeat of the Pudding King, Juiblex’s physical influence receded from the area, but a demonic voice—likely his own—proclaimed his intent to be reborn by consuming the Queen of Fungi.  

## Uncertainty
- The voice declaring rebirth is “possibly Juiblex himself” — not definitively confirmed as his direct speech or consciousness.
- It is unclear whether Juiblex retains any physical form, is a disembodied spirit, or is merely a psychic echo.
- The location of the Queen of Fungi and whether she is currently accessible or even alive is unknown and not stated.
