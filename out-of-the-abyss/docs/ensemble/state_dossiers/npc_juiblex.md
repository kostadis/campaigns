### Juiblex

**Current status:** Alive (demonic presence enduring)  
**Current location:** Not explicitly stated; influence lingers in the region of the Pudding King’s defeat  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demon lord of oozes and shapeless things  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None directly held; power is non-corporeal and tied to infernal influence  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Seeking rebirth by consuming the Queen of Fungi; previously channeled through the Pudding King (now defeated)  
**Defining recent actions:** After the Pudding King’s defeat, Juiblex’s infernal influence receded from the area, but a demonic voice — likely Juiblex himself — declared intent to be reborn by consuming the Queen of Fungi.

## Uncertainty
- The demonic voice declaring rebirth is described as “possibly Juiblex himself” — attribution is not confirmed.
- It is unclear whether Juiblex currently possesses a physical form, a vessel, or exists purely as a disembodied will.
- The location of the Queen of Fungi and whether she is currently accessible or even alive is not stated.
