### Candlekeep

**Current status:** Active institution, undergoing leadership transition.  
**Current location:** Unspecified, but operates as a centralized monolithic archive and scholarly authority.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent scholarly order; no external overlord named.  

**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- Access to a vast archive of spells, including high-level magic such as *polymorph*, available to any avowed with sufficient standing.  
- A system of monographs and scholarly attributions as primary currency of status and influence.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Functions as a guardian and cataloguer of knowledge, prioritizing observation, silence, and administrative order over punishment.  
- Exiles individuals for major offenses rather than imprisoning them; temporary detention is permitted only as a procedural delay.  
- Operates under a new head who now holds the key to forbidden knowledge and wields the ultimate authority to condemn or absolve — institutional power has shifted from the prior leadership.  
- Leadership is implicated in systemic corruption, including RIFs, budget cuts, and research purges that created conditions for Janussi’s murder, with possible cover-up.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- The new head has assumed control of forbidden knowledge, altering the locus of institutional justice.  
- Scholarly credit and monograph attribution are actively used to measure value and prestige, incentivizing collaboration for status over truth-seeking.  
- Internal administrative cruelty — masked as efficiency — has led to lethal consequences and potential institutional complicity in murder.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Knowledge is controlled not by secrecy alone, but by bureaucratic access thresholds: five books grant “facetime,” the real currency of influence.  
- The institution’s silence is not neutrality — it is a tool of control.  
- Justice is not administered by law, but by who holds the key during institutional transition.  

## Uncertainty
- The identity of the “new head” is not named; only their role and power are confirmed.  
- It is unclear whether the prior head is still alive, deceased, or removed — only that authority has shifted.  
- The nature of “forbidden knowledge” and what specific knowledge the new head now controls is unspecified.  
- While Candlekeep’s policy against imprisonment is stated twice, it is unclear whether this applies universally or has exceptions not documented.  
- No confirmation whether Janussi’s murder was directly ordered by leadership or merely enabled by systemic neglect — attribution of complicity remains ambiguous.
