### Candlekeep

**Current status:** Active and operational.  
**Current location:** Unspecified, but universally recognized as the world’s foremost library institution.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent scholarly order; officially neutral, non-interventionist, and self-governing.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Holds access to powerful arcane texts, including polymorph spells, available to avowed scholars with sufficient standing.  
- Maintains a vast archive of rare and dangerous texts, including the *Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal*, *Ilvara’s prayer book with Zuggtmoy marginalia*, and *Asha Vandree’s theological treatise* (recently submitted and archived).  
- Possesses institutional authority to grant or withhold access to magical knowledge based on scholarly reputation and contribution.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Functions as a repository and sanctuary of knowledge, enforcing strict protocols: one book per group for entry, with increased access granted per additional submission.  
- Treats all guests as part of its scholarly order, regardless of origin; refuses to imprison, execute, or surrender individuals to external powers (e.g., House T'sarran).  
- Operates under a policy of temporary detention followed by exile.  
- Internal power dynamics are defined by scholarly prestige: monographs, authorship, and endorsements are the primary currency of influence.  
- Recent internal strife includes budget cuts, RIFs, and power struggles — culminating in the assassination of Janussi, masked as administrative efficiency.  
- Leadership transition is imminent: the new head of Candlekeep is positioned to reward allies with access to incriminating or powerful magical texts.  
- Key figures in current internal competition: Fembris and Elian, both vying for monograph authorship.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Accepted five rare books from an adventuring party in exchange for extended scholarly access.  
- Refused to surrender a prisoner to House T'sarran, upholding its policy of non-intervention and exile.  
- Concealed the assassination of Janussi as bureaucratic streamlining, revealing institutional decay.  
- Positioned the incoming head to leverage institutional authority as a political tool by offering restricted magical texts to those who have earned goodwill.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The institution’s culture is poisoned by silent grudges, rivalries, and the accumulation of scholarly resentments — a quiet rot beneath its pristine facade.  
- Knowledge is not shared as a right but controlled as a resource; prestige, not truth, drives advancement.  
- The library’s neutrality is a performance — its power is wielded selectively, through access, silence, and the strategic release of dangerous knowledge.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact identity of the incoming head of Candlekeep is unknown.  
- Whether Janussi’s assassination was ordered by Fembris, Elian, or another internal actor remains unattributed.  
- It is unclear if the “budget cuts and RIFs” mentioned are ongoing or resolved.  
- The current location of the *Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal*, *Ilvara’s prayer book*, and *Asha Vandree’s treatise* within Candlekeep’s archives is unspecified.  
- Whether the new head has already assumed power or is still in transition is ambiguous.
