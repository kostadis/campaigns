### Obelisk

- **Type:** Object  
- **Current location:** Second tier of the mesa, standing sentinel on the adjacent topmost tier.  
- **Physical description:** A fifty-foot-tall structure crafted from smooth, jet-black metal. Its surface bears noticeable imperfections, appearing as though small fragments have been chipped away over time.  
- **Current condition:** Emits pulses of light that react to the presence of faerzress.  
- **Significance:** Revered by Plinki, who pats it reverently. Reacts visibly to faerzress glow, as demonstrated by a single flash when touched by a derro.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the obelisk’s reaction to the derro’s touch (ch20) was a one-time event or part of its normal behavior — the ch21 description implies ongoing faerzress-reactive pulses, but does not confirm if non-faerzress contact (like the derro’s touch) also triggers responses.  
- The phrases “second tier” and “adjacent topmost tier” are ambiguous in spatial relation — whether they refer to the same location or two distinct but nearby tiers is not resolved.
