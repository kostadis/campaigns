### Obelisk

- **Type:** Object  
- **Current location:** Second tier of the mesa, standing sentinel on the adjacent topmost tier.  
- **Physical description:** A fifty-foot-tall structure crafted from smooth, jet-black metal. Its surface bears noticeable imperfections, appearing as though small fragments have been chipped away over time.  
- **Current condition:** Emits pulses of light that react to the presence of faerzress.  
- **Notable behavior:** Reacts visibly to faerzress glow — flashes or flares in response to its presence or touch.  
- **Cultural significance:** Revered by Plinki, who pats it reverently.  

## Uncertainty
- Unclear whether the obelisk’s imperfections are the result of natural erosion, deliberate damage, or magical degradation — the source of the chipping is unspecified.  
- Contradiction in spatial description: One fact places it on the "second tier," another on the "adjacent topmost tier" — these may refer to the same location or may be inconsistent; no clarification is given.  
- No confirmation whether the obelisk is sentient, magically active beyond light pulses, or connected to any other artifacts or entities beyond its reaction to faerzress and Plinki’s reverence.
