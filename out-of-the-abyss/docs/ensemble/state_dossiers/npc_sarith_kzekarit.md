### Sarith Kzekarit

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove (site of death)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a Drow warrior of the Cult of Lolth; now a corrupted spore servant of Zuggtmoy (before death)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Studded leather armor, shield, hand crossbow (equipped at time of escape; no evidence of loss or replacement)  
**Current assignment / role:** Former guide of the party through the Underdark; last known role was as a corrupted spore servant under Zuggtmoy’s influence, leading the group toward the Lost Tomb of Khaem and Neverlight Grove  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Led the party through the Silken Paths, Darklake, and into the Neverlight Grove, confidently asserting knowledge of the route.  
- Confirmed the direction of the trapped voice led to the Lost Tomb of Khaem.  
- Was visibly serene but unhappy near Myconids, exhibiting signs of psychic corruption.  
- Exploded into a Drow spore servant under Zuggtmoy’s influence, then was killed by Jimjar.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believed the “Dark Lady” (Zuggtmoy) offered liberation, revenge, and transformation through spore-induced ascension.  
- Claimed to have been unfairly imprisoned after being a member of Lolth’s warrior caste.  
- Experienced recurring bouts of madness since Derendil’s death, later amplified by Faerzress and Zuggtmoy’s spores.  
- Held a fanatical belief that “revenge is so tasty,” and viewed the destruction of his former oppressors as divine purpose.
