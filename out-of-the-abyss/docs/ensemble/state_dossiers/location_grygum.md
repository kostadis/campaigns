### Grygum

Grygum is a cavernous derro hideout located beneath the Underdark, formerly occupied by the derro Buppido. The site is littered with bones, including the scattered remains of Buppido and his dismembered partner Pelek. The cavern contains a variety of magical fungi: Pygmywort and Bigwig mushrooms flank a narrow crack in the wall too small for passage; Nightlights, torchstalk, timmask, and barrelstalk also grow throughout. To the northeast lies a thermal lake with unnervingly black water, stretching nearly one hundred feet across. To the east, a dense fungal thicket of hissing fungi forms a wall of growth five feet tall, blocking the tunnel. The area is haunted by Pelek’s ghost and exhibits recent planar instability.

- The nature or origin of the planar instability is not specified.
