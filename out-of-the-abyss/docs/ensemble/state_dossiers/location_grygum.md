### Grygum

Grygum is a cavernous derro hideout located beneath the Underdark, currently occupied by the remains of Buppido and the scattered bones of his dismembered partner Pelek. The cavern is filled with bones, fungi, and magical mushrooms including Pygmywort, Bigwig, Nightlights, torchstalk, timmask, and barrelstalk. A thermal lake with unnervingly black water stretches nearly one hundred feet to the opposite shore, located to the northeast. A dense fungal thicket of hissing fungi blocks the eastern tunnel, forming a wall of growth up to five feet tall. A narrow crack in the wall, flanked by Pygmywort and Bigwig mushrooms, is too small for passage and leads to unknown regions. The site is haunted by Pelek’s ghost and exhibits recent planar instability.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between “a narrow tunnel behind a crack in the wall leads to unknown regions” and “a crack in the wall... too small for anyone to fit into” — one implies passable access, the other denies it. The latter is more specific and appears later in the same chapter, but both are from ch17 with no clear temporal ordering between them.  
- Unclear if the “narrow tunnel” and the “crack” are the same feature or distinct.  
- No confirmation whether Buppido’s corpse is still present as a physical remains or has been removed/absorbed by planar instability.  
- No clarification on whether the “planar instability” is ongoing or a one-time event.
