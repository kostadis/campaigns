### Demogorgon

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Active in the Prime Material Plane, specifically in the region surrounding the former town of Sloobludop and under the influence of the Derro and Narrak.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Prince of Demons; sovereign entity of the Abyss, not bound to any mortal faction but served by Narrak, the Derro, and the Council of Savants (who seek to manipulate him).  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Wields no conventional items, but is the intended recipient of a sacrificial egg and an Obelisk, destined to be fused with him as a mount.  
- Embodies primal chaos and corruption; his presence radiates bloodlust, madness, and psychic decay.  
- Possesses twin baboon-like heads, each with distinct functions: one whispers insidious lies, the other roars with primal fury.  
- Has thirty-foot-tall, reptilian-demonic form with writhing tentacles replacing hands, crimson blazing eyes, and wickedly curved tusks.  
- Immune to conventional destruction; his manifestation defies mortal understanding and is tied to the Abyss’s hunger.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as the ultimate target of the Council of Savants and Plinki, who seek to forcibly bind him to a psionic dragon form to serve as a mount.  
- Is the divine patron and source of inspiration for Narrak and the Derro, whose genocidal campaign is framed as “The Triumph of Demogorgon.”  
- His influence has breached planar barriers, enabling unprecedented demonic incursions.  
- Last known action: Destroyed the town of Sloobludop and is actively advancing through the region.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Manifested from the Darklake after a ritual’s completion.  
- Destroyed Sloobludop, triggering widespread panic and confirming planar breach.  
- Is the focal point of a blasphemous ritual to transform him into a two-headed dragon mount via the sacrificial offering of an egg and Obelisk.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Not merely a destroyer—he is a *target* for forced ascension into a new, hybrid form, suggesting his power is being weaponized by mortal cultists.  
- His nature as the antithesis of Bahamut (order, justice, compassion) is not just thematic—it is cosmically significant, making his corruption of the egg and Obelisk a direct assault on divine balance.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the Council of Savants and Plinki’s attempt to transform Demogorgon into a dragon mount has succeeded, failed, or is still in progress—no outcome is stated.  
- The exact current location beyond “advancing through the region” is unspecified; no coordinates or named landmarks are given after Sloobludop.  
- It is ambiguous whether Narrak and the Derro are physically accompanying Demogorgon or merely serving his will from afar—no direct interaction is described.  
- The egg and Obelisk’s current location or whether they have been delivered to Demogorgon is not confirmed—only their intended purpose is stated.  
- The nature of his “triumph” in Chapter 24 is symbolic or literal? Is he physically present at the site of the Triumph, or is it a cultic banner? Not specified.
