### The Overbright

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current state:** Active, accessible surface phenomenon  
- **Nature:** A self-sustaining, non-sourced radiant light that fills everything from every direction simultaneously — not emanating from a point source, but existing as an ambient, overwhelming condition of perception.  
- **Effects:**  
  - Fundamentally alters perception, inducing a profound and violent sensory shift.  
  - Triggers spiritual awakening in those exposed.  
  - Causes material vulnerabilities — specifically, Drow cloaks disintegrate upon exposure.  
- **Significance:** A phenomenon previously believed by some to be myth or metaphor, now confirmed as real and physically manifest.  

## Uncertainty
None.
