### The Overbright

- **Type:** Location (surface region)  
- **Current state:** Accessible surface area characterized by unsourceable, all-encompassing sunlight — a phenomenon distinct from artificial, bioluminescent, or magical illumination.  
- **Current control/occupation:** Not specified; inhabited by surface dwellers who hold prejudiced beliefs toward tieflings.  
- **Current notable condition:** The light here is absolute and directionless — filling everything from every angle at once — and is perceived as alien or divine by those emerging from the Underdark.  
- **Key recent significance:**  
  - Served as the first point of emergence from the Underdark for Zalthir, Glabbagool, and others, triggering profound perceptual and spiritual shifts.  
  - Associated with systemic prejudice against tieflings, who are routinely assumed to be abyssal spawns — Sylvira, a tiefling researcher, is from this region and endures this bias.
