### Neverlight Grove

**Current status:** Corrupted, contested, and actively dangerous.  
**Current location:** Deep Underdark, accessible via a dried underground stream tunnel; connected to the Northern Terraces, central basin, and fungal forests.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Dominated by Sovereign Phylo and the Circle of Welcome; influenced by Zuggtmoy’s corruption via the fungal artifact.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Hosts the **mushroom artifact** (over 20 feet tall, branching stalks, carved trunk windows), radiating decay and unnatural luminescence — the mechanism of corruption.  
- Fungal forests cover ceiling, walls, and ground, emitting bioluminescent light in blues, greens, and purples.  
- Central basin contains a clear pool.  
- Terraces along northern walls are saturated with **faerzress**.  
- **Zurkhwoods** move erratically throughout the grove.  
- **Glowing myconids** are present, some under Phylo’s control, others corrupted.  
- **Acid-resistant fungal ingredients** are still present and harvestable.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as the corrupted heart of myconid civilization, now a site of ideological and spiritual fracture between Phylo’s regime and surviving loyalists.  
- Functions as a nexus of demonic and fungal corruption, channeling Zuggtmoy’s influence through the artifact.  
- Acts as a waypoint and obstacle on the party’s path to the surface — negotiated, fought through, and lied through.  
- Draws beings through compulsion, as with Sarith and Thorin.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- The mushroom artifact has fully activated, causing fungal mutations, shifting tunnels, hostile light, and spore-infused madness.  
- Myconid leadership is fractured; Phylo enforces the Circle of Welcome, while remnants of the original colony (e.g., Grygum, Rump-a-dump) are displaced or silenced.  
- The party collected acid-resistant ingredients from the grove for potion crafting.  
- The grove was traversed and survived by the party as part of their ascent to the surface, marked by violence, deception, and negotiation.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The grove was once a serene, bioluminescent sanctuary for myconids — now a cathedral of corrupted life.  
- The corruption is not merely ecological but spiritual: the artifact channels Zuggtmoy’s will, turning the grove into a living engine of transformation and madness.  
- Faerzress and fungal growths are intertwined, enabling hidden transmissions and unnatural control.
