### Waterfall

- **Current state**: Active natural feature, functional as an escape route.
- **Current location**: Above the prison, accessible via a slippery path leading to its head.
- **Current condition**: Flowing; creates pools below and is interwoven with spiderwebs.
- **Notable features**: Slippery path at its head; downstream pools and spiderwebs form a hazardous but viable descent route.
- **Current assignment / role**: One of two designated escape routes from the prison, used for sliding down or jumping into spiderwebs and water pools.
- **Defining recent action**: Proposed and acknowledged as a viable escape method by Jorlan.
