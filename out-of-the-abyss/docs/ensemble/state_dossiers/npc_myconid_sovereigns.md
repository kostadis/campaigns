### Myconid Sovereigns

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed in presence of Zalthir)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid circle (led by or associated with Rump-a-dump)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Advocate for Rump-a-dump; seeks nonviolent resolution to perceived mistreatment of her circle member. Communicates telepathically and via spores. Reports to no known superior — acts autonomously as a representative of her circle’s values.  
**Defining recent actions:** Engaged Zalthir in a prolonged telepathic dialogue urging nonviolence toward her kind, asserting that myconids can convert dead enemies into zombies. Became emotionally distressed when Zalthir expressed fear of becoming undead, accusing him of abandoning her friends. Ultimately fell asleep after Zalthir invoked parental advice, ending the interaction.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believes myconids are akin to zombies; holds that killing enemies is morally wrong because they are “like her.” Possesses the ability to convert slain enemies into undead, though the mechanism is unexplained. Views Rump-a-dump as a wronged member of her circle deserving of aid.
