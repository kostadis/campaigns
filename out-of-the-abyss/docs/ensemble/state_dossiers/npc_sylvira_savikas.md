### Sylvira Savikas

**Current status:** Alive, but feverish and dying slowly from an abyssal plague.  
**Current location:** Her chambers in Candlekeep, bedridden.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Curator of the Infernal Fortress, tiefling abyssal spawn researcher; formerly associated with Janussi, now isolated and framed.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Suffering from advanced abyssal plague (poisoned, feverish, shuddering).  
- Capable of casting *mage hand* and controlling a familiar from across the room despite physical debilitation.  
- Previously carried a lantern at night (now inactive); no current evidence of possession.  
- Owns a Mordenkainen’s Mansion, constructed to protect her son.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Central figure in the investigation into Janussi’s murder; accused but claims to be framed.  
- Last seen speaking with investigators at her bedside; cooperating fully, offering detailed knowledge of infernal domains and rivalries.  
- Relationships:  
  - Deceased: Janussi (former lover; last argument was emotionally devastating).  
  - Son: Abyssal-born, currently absent; she has been trying to coax him back into her Mordenkainen’s Mansion; last argument with him is a source of grief.  
  - Enemy: Teles Ahvoste (named as a long-term hostile party who may have framed her).  
  - Dismissed: Kalan Strongbranch (considered his research incompetent; denied ever calling him a “genus traitor”).  
  - Assisted by: Sprig (who gave her honey at 1:00 a.m., though she later denied ever consuming it).  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Stormed out of the Deadwinter supper before gift-giving, creating a window of opportunity.  
- Was seen searching the kitchens by candlelight at 1:00 a.m., hoarse and unable to speak — a behavior inconsistent with her tiefling darkvision.  
- Insisted she was with her son after 12:30 a.m., returning alone to her chambers before succumbing to the plague.  
- Denied ever drinking honey, despite Sprig’s testimony — a contradiction suggesting deception or impersonation.  
- Anticipated being asked if she called Kalan a “genus traitor,” indicating foreknowledge of the accusation.  
- Named Teles Ahvoste as a likely framer and dismissed all claims of seeing her after 12:30 a.m. as lies.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes jealousy is a powerful motivator for murder — possibly reflecting her own emotional state.  
- Suspects she was impersonated; the “Sylvira lookalike” was sighted in two locations within a timeframe that defies normal polymorph duration.  
- Her infernal research and relationship with Janussi made her a target of prejudice.  
- She is not merely a suspect — she is a victim of a sophisticated frame-up, possibly involving her own likeness.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Sprig reports Sylvira was hoarse and accepted honey at 1:00 a.m.; Sylvira later insists she “would never drink honey.” Is this a lie, a delusion, or proof of impersonation?  
- Unclear attribution: Was the lantern-carrying figure seen at 2–3 a.m. and the hoarse figure in the kitchens the same impersonator, or two different entities?  
- Ambiguous chronology: Did Sylvira return to her chambers alone after 12:30 a.m., or was she replaced before then? Her claim is uncorroborated.  
- Unclear: Is the fever and plague natural, or induced by her son or an external agent?  
- Contradiction: She claims to have never called Kalan a “genus traitor,” yet her dismissal of him was extreme — is this denial genuine or strategic?  
- Unclear: Who exactly impersonated her? The text implies the impersonator knew her habits (lantern use, avoidance of honey) but not her biology — how?
