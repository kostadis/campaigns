### Waterorb

- **Type:** Object (fungus)  
- **Current status:** Collected and in possession of Daz  
- **Current location:** With Daz  
- **Physical description:** Mature specimen, bulbous in shape, larger than a human head  
- **Current possession:** Used as a utility item for storing water  
- **Function:** When squeezed and wrung out, yields approximately one gallon (four liters) of potable water; leaves behind a bland, chewy, edible husk  
- **Origin:** Grows naturally in shallow water in the Underdark  

## Uncertainty
None.
