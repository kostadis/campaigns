### Senni Diggermattock

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone  
**Allegiance/faction:** Blingdenstone leadership (labor groups); member of the Gold Whisker clan  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Wife of Chief Dorbo Diggermattock  
- Assistant to Chief Dorbo Diggermattock  
- Leader of Blingdenstone’s labor groups  
- Primary advocate for unity with the Gold Whisker clan and reconciliation with the wererats  
- Instrumental in shifting Blingdenstone policy against enslaving summoned Earth Elementals  
- Tasked the party with securing the Gold Whisker clan’s alliance and facilitating the grand moot  
- Supported Daz’s plan to confront the Pudding King and helped secure Dorbo’s agreement to it  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Silenced Chief Dorbo during a council meeting and demanded a better plan than his resistance to inclusion.  
- Declared “Gnome Labor, not Summoned Labor!” to halt the exploitation of summoned creatures, leading to policy change.  
- Advocated for and secured Dorbo’s compromise on alliance with the wererats, citing community safety over profit.  
- Expressed deep concern for civilian safety in Blingdenstone, warning that failure to stop the ooze threat could lead to total overrun.  
- Facilitated the grand moot by appointing Daz to lead it and backed his strategy against the Pudding King.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes in communal welfare over mining profits; prioritizes civilian safety and ethical labor practices.  
- Secretly a member of the Gold Whisker clan, which explains her persistent advocacy for alliance with the wererats — a stance she maintained even when Dorbo opposed it.  
- Her influence was decisive in shifting Dorbo’s position during the final council meeting, indicating her hidden authority and moral leverage within the clan.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Chapter 35–38 consistently describe Senni as Dorbo’s *assistant*, while Chapter 48 reveals she is his *wife* and a member of the Gold Whisker clan — this is not a contradiction, but the earlier facts omitted her familial tie. The later fact (ch48) supersedes and adds context; no conflict remains.  
- Ambiguity: Chapter 48 states “Senni Diggermattock is Dorbo's wife and a member of the Gold Whisker clan who advocated for a functional alliance... and whose support was noted as helpful to Dorbo's decision-making.” This implies her advocacy preceded and aided his compromise — but it does not clarify whether she publicly opposed him before this point, or if her role as wife was previously unknown to the party. No other fact contradicts this, so it is accepted as current.  
- Unclear: Whether Senni still holds the title “Quartermaster” — this title appears in ch35–36 but is never used again. Later chapters refer to her as “leader of labor groups” or “assistant to Dorbo.” The later roles override, but it is unclear if “Quartermaster” is an outdated title or still in use.  
- Ambiguity: Chapter 45 describes her as “a leader of the labor groups, not the business leaders,” which implies a factional divide — but it is unclear if she holds formal authority over labor groups or merely represents them.  
- No fact confirms whether she remains in Blingdenstone after the council meeting in ch48 — but since no departure or relocation is mentioned, and her concerns are tied to Blingdenstone’s survival, it is assumed she remains.
