### Bookwyrm (First Reader)

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Candlekeep, occupying the top floor chambers  
**Allegiance/faction:** Acting Head of Candlekeep; leader of the Avowed; formerly under Janussi’s authority, now de facto head following his death  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated beyond personal quarters (comfortable, neatly arranged, no extravagance); previously had access to wine and glassware; last seen drinking tea from a chalice; suspected of possessing Janussi’s heart (now hidden in the statue’s chalice)  
**Current assignment / role:** Acting Head of Candlekeep; overseeing the investigation into Janussi’s murder; directing investigators’ access to resources and suspects; managing institutional narrative and political fallout  
**Key relationships:**  
- Supervises Fembris Lancer and Vooshadi Moonriver as assistants  
- Formerly worked under Janussi; now in his place  
- Opposed Kalan Strongbranch’s investigative safeguards; engineered his removal  
- Had a heated confrontation with Alkrist after his confession; now appears to be protected by his admission  
- Suspected by Kalan, Thorin, and Daral Yashenti; trusted implicitly by no one  
- Maintains a tense, manipulative rapport with the party, offering assistance while steering investigation toward Sylvira Savikas  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Orchestrated the party’s immediate, secret summons to Janussi’s murder scene, bypassing all institutional protocols  
- Directed investigators to Deneir’s Sanctum as their office and assigned Fembris Lancer to fetch suspects  
- Deliberately steered the investigation toward Sylvira Savikas through unstated preference, without naming her  
- Opposed Kalan Strongbranch’s security proposals as “absurd,” contributing to the environment that enabled the murder  
- Confronted Alkrist after he revealed Janussi was poisoned; reacted with appalled outrage and stormed off  
- Later placed Janussi’s heart inside the chalice of a statue to conceal evidence and shield Alkrist  
- Hosted the investigators in her chambers, calmly drinking tea while accusing them of relying on weak proof — a performance of composure and innocence  
- Declared the safe containing incriminating evidence “of no further interest,” sealed it, and allowed the group to be escorted away  
- Publicly expressed shock at Alkrist’s confession (“I could never have imagined it”), while privately relieved — positioning herself as a victim of deception  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Secretly hid the crime of moving Janussi’s heart to protect Alkrist — not committing murder, but concealing it  
- Seeks to secure permanent leadership of Candlekeep by appearing cooperative, grief-stricken, and above suspicion  
- Uses theological language, bureaucratic control, and emotional manipulation to maintain authority  
- Views the cat as an unproven supernatural entity and avoids contemplating its actions — suggesting prior, unacknowledged encounters  
- May have been involved in Janussi’s death indirectly by removing safeguards; whether she ordered it remains unproven  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Described as both a *dragonborn* (ch55, ch56, ch58) and a *drow* (ch56). Later facts (ch56 onward) consistently refer to her as a green dragonborn — drow reference is likely erroneous or misattributed.  
- Ambiguous attribution: Was Bookwyrm the one who ripped out Janussi’s heart, or did she only move it after Alkrist killed him? The text says she “ripped out Janussi’s heart” (ch60) but also says she “hid a crime by placing a heart in the chalice” (ch61) — implying she moved it, not that she killed him. Conflict: who killed Janussi? Alkrist confessed, but Bookwyrm’s emotional reaction and access to powerful magic leave room for doubt.  
- Unclear chronology: Did Bookwyrm’s confrontation with Alkrist occur before or after she moved the heart? The text implies the heart was moved after the confrontation, but the sequence is not explicit.  
- Ambiguous current state: Is she still under threat of removal? The text says her election as permanent head is under threat — but does not confirm whether the threat was resolved, deferred, or escalated after the safe was sealed.  
- Unclear possession: Was the heart moved by Bookwyrm alone? Was it still in the statue’s chalice at the end of ch61? The last line (“She walks.”) implies she remains in position, but does not confirm the heart’s location.  
- Contradictory role: Is she “the leader of the Avowed” (ch61) or “the First Reader” who assigns handlers to avowed? The terms are used interchangeably, but the Avowed are a separate order — are they under her command, or is she one of them?
