### Leemooggoogoon

- **Current status:** Existence is metaphysically contingent — brought into being by collective imagination.  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Worshipped by Bloppblippodd; not recognized as real by Zalthir.  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** None (non-corporeal entity).  
- **Current assignment / role:** Revered as the Deep Father by adherents; symbolic focal point of belief.  
- **Defining recent actions:** Invoked by Shuushar as a product of collective imagination following the demon lord’s appearance.  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:** Does not act independently — existence is sustained solely by belief. The manta-ray hide idol stained with blood is a physical anchor for worship, not a vessel or conduit.
