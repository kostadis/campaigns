### Inner Circle

The Inner Circle is the sacred heart of the Myconid society within the Neverlight Grove, serving as the fungal hierarchy’s core. It is the location where Phylo and Basidia confronted each other, triggering sentient Zurkhwoods to stir and the air to thrum with psychic resonance of fungal unity and madness. It is the site of Phylo’s betrayal and the party’s escape. Phylo is currently present there.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch31 descriptions: one states the Inner Circle consists of all circle leaders and is separate from the Circle of Masters, while another implies the Inner Circle *and* Circle of Masters together form “a circle of those who are closest to Phylo.” The latter phrasing is ambiguous and may be a misstatement or overlapping definition — no later chapter clarifies the structural relationship.  
- The current status of the Circle of Masters and its members is not updated in ch33 — their presence, allegiance, or location in relation to the Inner Circle is unknown.  
- Whether “the heart of the fungal hierarchy” implies physical centrality, spiritual authority, or both is not explicitly defined — the description is metaphorical without spatial confirmation.
