### Daz

**Current Status:** Alive.  
**Current Location:** On the path to Candlekeep, actively investigating the murder of Janussi and the mystery of his own origins.  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Independent; formerly associated with the Ember Vanguard, now operating as a pragmatic investigator with no formal allegiance. Has been the target of a high-value protective contract from House T'sarran and is suspected of being a living key to a hidden patron in Menzoberranzan.  

**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- **Stonespeaker Crystal** — Used to communicate with beasts and interpret vibrational patterns.  
- **Dawnbringer** — Intelligent sunblade, carried and occasionally negotiated with.  
- **Collection of Duergar and Drow faction badges** — Used strategically for access and deception.  
- **Copy of Milo Goodbarrel’s book** — Referenced for psychological tactics, narrative theory, and historical parallels.  
- **Inert High Tower Key** — Invisible to magical detection; the true target of helmed horrors.  
- **Journal** — Kept daily since escaping Velkynvelve; records observations, faction dynamics, and personal reflections.  
- **Glabbagool** — Gelatinous ooze familiar, bound through performance and psychological manipulation.  

**Notable Spells / Feats / Abilities:**  
- **Evoker specialization** — Trained in Menzoberranzan; excels in precise, sculpted area-of-effect fire magic (Fireball, Scorching Ray).  
- **Fey Touched** — Grants two additional prepared spells and free castings; synergizes with the Ebonmire spellbook.  
- **Warcaster** — Maintains concentration under duress.  
- **Misty Step** — Used for tactical repositioning and escape from grapples.  
- **Sculpt Spells** — Enables precise control over area effects to spare allies.  
- **Telekinetic** — Used as a geometry tool to reposition allies, not for offense.  
- **Shape Water** — Deployed for environmental manipulation, detection of hidden threats, and survival (e.g., freezing water into ice blocks).  
- **Magic Missile** — Primary offensive cantrip; used with clinical precision.  
- **Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp** — Used to immobilize or crush targets.  
- **Toll the Dead** — Employed against undead and resistant foes.  
- **Hypnotic Pattern** — Used to incapacitate enemies; requires concentration.  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **Lead Investigator** — Managing the murder investigation of Janussi at Candlekeep, piecing together connections between the stolen key, the sapphire summoning spell, the missing book fragment, and the Menzoberranzan-linked conspiracy.  
- **De Facto Tactical Leader** — Guides the group’s strategy with analytical precision, often framing decisions as group consensus while steering them toward his conclusions.  
- **Key Relationships:**  
  - **Zalthir** — Trusted tactical partner; shares awareness of environmental anomalies.  
  - **Grygum** — Lieutenant and ally; respects Daz’s judgment, confirmed his status as “the feared mage of the Ember Vanguard.”  
  - **Thorin** — Relies on Daz’s strategic insight; shares history with Dawnbringer.  
  - **Asha Vandree** — Believes Daz is a vessel of Lolth; his fabricated identity remains load-bearing.  
  - **House T’sarran** — Actively hunting him; believes he is bait for a hidden patron.  
  - **A’lai** — A drow with Underdark knowledge; Daz requires information from him.  
  - **The Unnamed Patron** — Someone in Menzoberranzan paid to protect Daz; identity unknown, but Daz suspects his value lies not in lineage, but in being a “living indicator.”  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Successfully interrogated Sylvira Savikas, extracting her testimony implicating Kazryn Nyantani and Daral Yashenti, while recognizing her emotional truth and the Menzoberranzan-style framing of her as a target.  
- Identified the inert key in his pocket as the true target of helmed horrors, surviving their attack and casting Scorching Ray to defeat them.  
- Filed Daral Yashenti under “desperate,” recognizing his honesty born of impending ruin.  
- Concluded the Janussi murder investigation, documenting the confession and recognizing Kalan Strongbranch as the sole witness holding the complete record.  
- Rejected the notion of noble blood as an explanation for his value, preferring to believe his significance stems from something more complex — possibly tied to the erasure of the name “Daz’issin” from heraldic records.  
- Maintained his persona as a Lolth-follower with “consistent characterization,” not deception, to manipulate Asha’s perception.  
- Refused to cure Daral, still weighing the decision.  
- Uses “118” as cryptic shorthand for a taster protocol, understood only by the group.  
- Continues journaling daily, analyzing every interaction as data, and treats information as finite, valuable currency.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- **Motivation:** To understand his own identity and the reason powerful forces are invested in his survival. Rejects inherited status; seeks truth over narrative.  
- **Secret:** He is likely the last known bearer of the name “Daz’issin” — a diminutive form used by drow mothers for sons they hoped would survive. His name was deliberately erased from Menzoberranzan’s records, and his survival may be tied to a forbidden act or hidden lineage.  
- **Secret:** He has been the subject of a high-stakes, multi-faction game since his escape from Velkynvelve — not because he is powerful, but because he is a “bait” to reveal the identity of the patron who paid to protect him.  
- **Secret:** His persistent headache is not from sunlight, but from proximity to forbidden knowledge — possibly linked to the same corruption that drove Eldred and the Field Ward Preacher mad.  
- **Secret:** He is not merely a survivor — he is a living anomaly in a system designed to erase people like him. His honesty, a radical act in Menzoberranzan, is the key to unraveling the city’s hidden history.  

## Uncertainty  
- **Contradictory Origin Claims:** Multiple facts describe Daz as a Drow from Menzoberranzan, yet one fact (ch55) refers to him as a dwarf. Later facts consistently treat him as Drow and reference his drow heritage, so the “dwarf” reference is likely an error or misattribution.  
- **Identity of the Patron:** While Daz suspects someone in Menzoberranzan paid to protect him, the patron’s identity, motive, and affiliation remain entirely unknown.  
- **Nature of the “Struck” Record:** Daz suspects his name was erased from heraldic records, but the exact event, the responsible party, and the consequences are unconfirmed.  
- **True Nature of the Headache:** Is it caused by proximity to forbidden knowledge, the spreading madness of the Underdark, or something else? No definitive cause is established.  
- **Status of Glabbagool:** Is the ooze familiar a product of manipulation, genuine bond, or something else? Daz treats it as a tool, but its sentience and loyalty are unconfirmed.  
- **Daz’s Name:** Is “Daz” a shortened form of “Daz’issin,” or is it a name he adopted? The connection is strongly implied but not confirmed.  
- **Daz’s Alignment with Lolth:** Is his Lolth-follower persona entirely fabricated, or has he developed genuine, if warped, belief? He claims it’s “consistent characterization,” but his internal monologues suggest ambiguity.  
- **Fate of the Patron’s Contract:** Is the contract still active? Has House T’sarran given up? Is A’lai connected to the patron? Unresolved.
