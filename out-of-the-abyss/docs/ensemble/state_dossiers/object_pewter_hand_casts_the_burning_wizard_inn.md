### Pewter Hand Casts (The Burning Wizard Inn)

- **What it is:** Miniature, charm-sized pewter reproductions of Tarvis Ulain’s severed forearm.  
- **Current holder/location:** Being sold by a village priest at the River Shining Tavern.  
- **Current condition:** Small, intact, mass-produced as devotional or donation objects.  
- **Significance:** Sold to raise donations for the Order of the Gauntlet; commodify Tarvis Ulain’s martyrdom and reflect institutional efforts to control narrative despite ideological or theological ambiguity in the monument’s inscription.
