### Deepking Tarngardt

**Current status:** Alive, ruling sovereign of Gracklstugh.  
**Current location:** Gracklstugh (unspecified, but actively governing from within).  
**Allegiance/faction:** Ruling authority of Gracklstugh; ostensibly independent, but actively manipulated by Pliinki.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Clenched-fist pendant (symbol of authority).  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Enforces control through Grogluk and a network of Duergar guards, spies, and propaganda.  
- Maintains strict identity verification to suppress the Ember Vanguard.  
- Relies on bribery, psychological manipulation, and fear to maintain order.  
- Subject to manipulation by Pliinki, who keeps him ignorant of the true purpose of the Obelisk and the Red Dragon Egg.  
- Feared by Duergar and Derro alike.  
- Directly concerned with the Ember Vanguard’s presence in Gracklstugh.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Raised exorbitant trade prices on Deep Gnomes to consolidate economic dominance.  
- Authorized surveillance, identity checks, and hunts against the Ember Vanguard.  
- Issued erratic edicts under Pliinki’s influence, deepening paranoia in Gracklstugh.  
- The Stone Guard invokes his name in enforcement actions.  
- The party carries the Red Dragon Egg as a secret tied to his hidden plots.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- His rule is maintained not by strength alone, but by engineered fear and ignorance.  
- He is unaware of the true nature of the Obelisk and the Red Dragon Egg — Pliinki is the true architect behind the city’s instability.  
- His obsession with suppressing the Ember Vanguard is a vulnerability exploited by Pliinki.
