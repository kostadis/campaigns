### Blade Bazaar

**Type:** Location  
**Current Status:** Operational, but deeply corrupted  
**Current Location:** Gracklstugh, the Duergar stronghold  
**Control/Occupancy:** Controlled by Duergar merchants and enforcers; no external authority visibly in charge  
**Current State:** A marketplace where commerce is governed by rigid, arbitrary laws that are systematically broken. Reality is unstable: merchants give inconsistent prices, deny previous transactions, vanish mid-deal, and speak to non-existent companions. The din of Dwarvish arguments drowns out the forge hammering. It is the symbolic heart of Gracklstugh’s decay — a microcosm of systemic corruption where chaos is disguised as order.  

**Current Possessions / Notable Features:**  
- Stalls and shops selling nearly everything available in Gracklstugh, including goods from visiting merchants  
- Abundance of weapons and tools (origin of its name)  
- Persistent auditory chaos: overlapping Dwarvish arguments and forge noise  

**Current Assignment / Role:**  
- Primary marketplace of Gracklstugh  
- Center of economic activity and social deception  
- Site where systemic corruption is most visibly manifested — behaviors observed here by Daz were later repeated by him as evidence of institutional rot  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Merchants routinely renege on deals and deny their own statements  
- Daz documented and replicated the marketplace’s corrupt behaviors as proof of systemic failure  
- The party first recognized the city’s laws as broken here  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- The marketplace’s instability is not random — it reflects the city’s broader unraveling, where rules exist only to be violated by those in power  
- The corruption is self-perpetuating and normalized; deception is the currency
