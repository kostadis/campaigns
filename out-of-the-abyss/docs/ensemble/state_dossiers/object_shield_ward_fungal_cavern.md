### shield ward (Fungal Cavern)

- **What it is**: A reaction-based magical defense effect tied to Daz’s innate combat training, manifesting as a protective barrier that activates autonomously.
- **Current holder/location**: Actively sustained by Daz; located in proximity to him within the Fungal Cavern.
- **Current condition**: Functional and responsive; activated reflexively in response to an incoming Drow bolt, successfully deflecting it.
- **Significance**: Represents ingrained, subconscious combat reflexes rather than deliberate spellcasting; functions as a mechanical advantage in chaotic environments, effectively acting as a life-saving autoprotect mechanism.
