### Diggermattock Miners Patch (Blingdenstone)

A commemorative miner's patch awarded to Thorin by Diggermattock, declaring him an honorary member of the mining crew. The patch was initially sewn onto Thorin’s gear by Diggermattock himself, then magically reattached with divine precision by Grygum’s cantrip. It now remains firmly affixed to Thorin’s gear, its magical reinforcement ensuring permanent adherence.

None.
