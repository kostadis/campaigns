### Sloobludop

Sloobludop is a destroyed Kuo-toan village, utterly razed by Demogorgon. Once situated between two concentric walls of netting, a cave wall to the south, and the Darklake to the north, it housed over a hundred mud-walled homes with ceilings made of unidentified dried vegetable matter. The village featured docks along the Darklake, some with boats, and was centered around two altars: one dedicated to the Sea Mother (marked by a humanoid female statue carved from wood, adorned with the severed head and claws of a giant albino crayfish, and piled with shells, stones, mushrooms, and rotting fish) and one to the Deep Father (marked by a hide stretched on poles, bearing a pinned dead manta ray, two dead octopuses, and stained dark with blood). The settlement was inhabited by approximately 500 Kuo-toans, led by arch-cleric Ploopploopeen, a follower of the Sea Mother who could cast *zone of truth*. The village had been a site of ideological tension between cults but was otherwise a long-standing community in harmonious service to the Sea Mother.

Now, Sloobludop is a charred ruin. Its population has been slaughtered. The screams of its dead inhabitants echo in the darkness.
