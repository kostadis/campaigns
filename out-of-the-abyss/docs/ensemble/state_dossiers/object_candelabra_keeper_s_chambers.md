### Candelabra (Keeper's Chambers)

- **Type:** Object  
- **Current location:** Keeper’s Chambers  
- **Current condition:** Seven-armed bronze candelabra with all seven candles entirely burned down to wax puddles.  
- **Significance:** The complete consumption of all candles confirms the room remained sealed and unattended for many hours after the death of its occupant, contradicting the housekeeper Hollypocket’s known standards of maintenance.
