### Juiblex

**Current status:** Alive, active, and regenerating.  
**Current location:** No fixed physical form; presence is latent but resonant across the Abyss, with residual influence centered on the former site of the Pudding King’s corruption in Blingdenstone.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demon lord of the Abyss; sovereign over oozes and shapeless entities.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None physically held; manifests through corrupted oozes and telepathic echoes. Recently demonstrated the ability to project a sentient, telepathic voice across dimensions.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Seeking rebirth and expansion by consuming the power of Zuggtmoy, the Queen of Fungi. Last known to have spoken directly through the dissolved Pudding King’s residual ooze, vowing rebirth. Has no known direct agents currently active, but his influence lingers in the corrupted ooze residue of Blingdenstone.  
**Defining recent actions:** After the dissolution of the Pudding King — his primary earthly manifestation — Juiblex’s consciousness echoed a vow of rebirth and consumption of Zuggtmoy’s power. His presence receded from direct corporeal interference but remains a latent, predatory force.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of Juiblex’s current “presence” — whether he is fully dormant, subtly influencing ooze mutations, or actively shaping a new avatar — is not confirmed beyond his vow.
- Contradiction in tone: Ch35 and ch39 portray Juiblex as a being of “unrivaled obscenity” and moral corruption, while ch48 (Jimjar) reduces him to a “loud, messy neighbor” in contrast to Zuggtmoy’s stealth. This may reflect Jimjar’s personal trauma distorting perception, but it creates ambiguity about Juiblex’s true demeanor or public reputation among other demons.
- Attribution of the telepathic voice in ch47: Is it Juiblex himself, or a residual echo of his will? The text says “possibly Juiblex himself,” leaving uncertainty.
- Whether Juiblex’s influence is still actively corrupting gnomes in Blingdenstone post-Pudding King’s dissolution is implied but not confirmed — only his vow of rebirth is stated.
