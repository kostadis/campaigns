### Juiblex

**Current status:** Alive (demon lord, not physically present but actively influencing events)  
**Current location:** No fixed physical form; influence emanates from the Abyss, with residual psychic presence tied to the dissolved Pudding King’s remains in Blingdenstone  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demon lord of the Abyss, ruler of oozes and shapeless horrors  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (as a demon lord, he has no tangible possessions; his power is intrinsic and manifest through corruption)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Primary agent of corruption: The Pudding King (now dissolved, but was his embodied vessel)  
- Active rival: Zuggtmoy, the Queen of Fungi — Juiblex has vowed to consume her power to rebirth and grow stronger  
- Recently referenced by Jimjar as a “loud, messy neighbor” in contrast to Zuggtmoy’s insidious nature  
- Recognized by Grygum, Chipgrin, and the party as the source of the ooze corruption in Blingdenstone  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- His demonic power was fully embodied in the Pudding King, which corrupted Blingdenstone’s gnome population with greed, aggression, and paranoia.  
- After the Pudding King’s dissolution, Juiblex’s consciousness echoed telepathically from the ooze remnants, declaring his intent to be reborn by consuming Zuggtmoy’s power.  
- His influence is receding from Blingdenstone but not extinguished — he remains an active, evolving threat in the Abyss, poised to exploit Zuggtmoy’s vulnerability.

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks to transcend his current form by consuming Zuggtmoy’s fungal dominion, implying a hierarchy or rivalry among demon lords.  
- His corruption is not merely destructive — it is transformative, turning order into formless chaos, and he takes pleasure in obscenity and entropy.  
- Jimjar’s trauma suggests Juiblex may have personally inflicted past harm upon him, though the nature of that trauma is not specified.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of Jimjar’s trauma from Juiblex is unexplained — was it a direct encounter, a loss, or an experiment?  
- Whether Juiblex’s voice in ch47 was his true consciousness or a residual echo is ambiguous.  
- The phrase “loud, messy neighbor” (ch48) is metaphorical or literal? If literal, does Juiblex physically reside near Zuggtmoy in the Abyss? No context confirms this.  
- Contradiction: Ch37–39 attribute the Blingdenstone corruption solely to Juiblex via the Pudding King, while ch43 lists him alongside Zuggtmoy as a separate threat — implying possible co-influence or confusion in attribution.  
- Is Juiblex’s “rebirth” a physical return, a new avatar, or a metaphysical expansion? The text does not clarify.
