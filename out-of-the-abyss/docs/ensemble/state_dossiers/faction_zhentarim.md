### Zhentarim

- **Current status:** Active, influential faction.
- **Current goals:** Survive and exert power by employing morally uncompromising, ruthlessly efficient tactics that others refuse to use.
- **Current standing:** Recognized as a paradigm of pragmatic, brutal effectiveness — now seen by the Narrator as philosophically aligned with his own evolved survival instincts.
- **Key philosophy:** Prioritizes effectiveness over decency; survives by doing what others won’t.
- **Recent actions:** Their tactical framework — “Zhentarim Tactics” — has been observed, analyzed, and internally adopted as a model by the Narrator during his transformation in the Underdark.
- **Key relationships:** Not explicitly allied or opposed to the party, but their ideology is now a mirror the Narrator acknowledges within himself.
