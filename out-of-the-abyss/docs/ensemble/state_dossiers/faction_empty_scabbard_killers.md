### Empty-Scabbard Killers

**Current status:** Active  
**Current location:** South of Area 5 (the former mesa war zone)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent assassin guild; currently at war with the Cult of Demogorgon  
**Current assignment / role:** Engaged in open conflict with the Cult of Demogorgon in Area 5; maintain a base or stronghold to the south  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully targeted and killed Duergar Werz in the Underdark  
- Transformed Area 5 from a neutral social hub into a war zone through sustained hostilities against the Cult of Demogorgon  
- Relocated or reorganized their primary base to the south, as confirmed by Thorin’s decision to avoid that direction  

**Notable items / traits:**  
- Use of magical light blades (confirmed in attack on Duergar Werz)  

**Key relationships:**  
- Hostile: Cult of Demogorgon  
- Unknown: Council of Savants, Grey Ghosts (connection remains unclear)  

## Uncertainty
