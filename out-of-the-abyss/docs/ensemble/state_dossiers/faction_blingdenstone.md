### Blingdenstone

Blingdenstone is a deep gnome city in the Underdark, currently under gnome control after multiple historical shifts of power between gnomes and drow. It maintains formidable defenses, including a maze of trap-laden tunnels. The city is facing existential threats from elemental and demonic forces: Zuggtmoy, Juiblex, the Pudding King, and the absence of Entémoch, alongside a severe ooze infestation. Its leadership was recently in disarray over conflicting priorities until the party intervened.

In a major policy shift following the party’s moral intervention, Blingdenstone has banned the use of summoned Earth Elementals for mining or construction, enshrining the principle: *“We serve the stone, not enslave it.”* Elementals are now permitted only for defense and warfare. This reversal has disrupted the city’s economic model but earned widespread gratitude from the gnome workforce.

The city commissioned the quest to hallow a temple and obtain Entémoch’s boon — a mission now critical to its survival.

## Uncertainty

- Unclear if the “ooze problem” is resolved or still active; only its existence is confirmed, not its current severity.
- Unclear whether the “temporal distortions” mentioned in ch43 are ongoing or connected to Zuggtmoy’s influence.
- The phrase “changed hands between gnomes and drow multiple times” in ch35 implies possible drow presence or influence — but no current drow affiliation, leadership, or occupation is confirmed in later chapters.
