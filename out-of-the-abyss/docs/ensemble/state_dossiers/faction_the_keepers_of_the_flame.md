### The Keepers of the Flame

**Current status:** Active faction  
**Current location:** Gracklstugh (base of operations)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Psionic sect; opposed to the Gray Ghosts  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Recovered dragon egg (taken from Narrak’s chest)  
**Current assignment / role:** Seeking to replace Themberchaud with a hatched dragon; one of four factions that dispatched the party into the caves  
**Defining recent actions:** Recovered a red dragon egg from Narrak’s chest to use as a replacement for Themberchaud; engaged in cold war with the Gray Ghosts over dragon egg sovereignty  
**Revealed motivations:** To supplant Themberchaud with a new dragon they control, using psionic influence to manipulate agents and maintain covert dominance; view Themberchaud as a tool, not a master  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch16 (egg stolen from Keepers by Gray Ghosts) and ch26 (Keepers recovered egg from Narrak’s chest): It is unclear whether the egg recovered from Narrak is the same one stolen earlier, or a different egg.  
- Unclear if Themberchaud still believes he commands the Keepers — ch16 implies ongoing deception, but ch26 gives no update on his awareness or current status.  
- Unclear if the Keepers still hold control over Themberchaud; ch16 asserts it, but ch26 focuses on the recovered egg and does not reaffirm his subjugation.  
- Ambiguity: “Red Dragon” in ch26 — is this Themberchaud, or another entity? The term is not clarified.
