### The High Tower

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current State:** Active and perpetually monitored  
- **Location:** Unspecified, but universally recognized as the entrance to a cache of super-magical artifacts  
- **Control/Occupation:** Unspecified; under constant, unseen surveillance  
- **Key Features:**  
  - Serves as the sole entrance to a cache of super-magical artifacts coveted by all evil entities in the world  
  - Contains the High Tower Library, where critical research on the murder was conducted  
  - Entry is perpetually monitored by unseen sentinels; unauthorized access is detectable regardless of key possession  
- **Recent Significance:** Site of critical research into the murder; target of interest to all evil factions due to the artifacts it guards
