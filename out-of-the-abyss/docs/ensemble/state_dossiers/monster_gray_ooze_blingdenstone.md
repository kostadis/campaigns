### gray ooze (Blingdenstone)

**Current status:** All encountered gray oozes are dead.  
**Current location:** None remain; all were destroyed within the square-shaped chamber of the stone fountain in Blingdenstone.  
**Allegiance/faction:** None; non-sentient, spontaneously generated monsters.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** None; each ooze was an ephemeral entity summoned by Glabbagool’s touch or rising water, then immediately destroyed.  
**Defining recent actions:** Multiple gray oozes emerged — one from rising waters, others from formless sculptures activated by Glabbagool’s touch and by the party’s deliberate activation of statues. Each was swiftly dispatched: Zalthir destroyed all oozes emerging from the seven niches via bare-fisted strikes, Daz obliterated one with a devastating spell, and Glabbagool consumed at least one after it materialized.

## Uncertainty
- Exact number of gray oozes encountered is ambiguous: “at least three” are named, but Zalthir dispatched “each ooze that emerged from the seven niches,” implying up to seven — conflicting with the count of described activations.  
- Attribution of destruction for oozes beyond the one killed by Daz and the one consumed by Glabbagool is unclear: Zalthir is said to have dispatched “each” ooze from the niches, but earlier facts also credit Daz and Glabbagool with killing oozes — it is unclear whether these were among the seven or additional ones.  
- It is unclear whether the ooze consumed by Glabbagool was one of the seven from the niches or a separate instance generated by his initial touch.
