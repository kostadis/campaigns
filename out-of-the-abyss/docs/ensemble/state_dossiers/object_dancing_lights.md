### Dancing Lights

- **Current holder/location**: Held and manipulated by Daz.
- **Current condition**: Actively shaped into a towering, feminine silhouette with eight eyes and infinite patience.
- **Current use**: Used by Daz to simulate the presence of the Spider Queen.
- **Notable transformation**: No longer manifested as floating globes or crude illusions; now takes a deliberate, awe-inspiring form mimicking divine presence.

## Uncertainty
- The earlier description of the Dancing Lights being "absorbed into the familiar" and "blinking out" in chapter 49 conflicts with their current active, shaped form in chapter 50. Since chapter 50 is later, the absorbed/blinking-out state is overridden — but the mechanism of how they were reacquired or reconfigured is unexplained.  
- It is unclear whether Daz created this new form independently or inherited/modified it from the earlier version used by the narrator.  
- The entity controlling or influencing the form (e.g., Spider Queen’s direct involvement) is not confirmed — only that Daz uses it to simulate her presence.
