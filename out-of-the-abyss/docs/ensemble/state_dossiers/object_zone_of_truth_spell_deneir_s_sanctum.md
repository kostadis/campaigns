### Zone of Truth spell (Deneir's Sanctum)

- **Type:** Magical effect (spell)  
- **Current location:** Enchanted within Deneir’s Sanctum  
- **Current condition:** Active and in regular ceremonial use  
- **Mechanism:** Does not compel honesty; reveals whether a subject can resist its effect.  
- **Function:** Serves as a tool of political pressure — not to extract truth, but to expose the subject’s capacity to lie.  
- **Significance:** Its use has evolved from covert casting to public demand; its value lies in observing resistance, not in guaranteeing truth.  
- **Key quote:** “It isn’t a lock, it’s a pressure.”  

## Uncertainty  
None.
