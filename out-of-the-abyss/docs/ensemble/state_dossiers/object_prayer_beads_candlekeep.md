### prayer beads (Candlekeep)

- **Current holder/location:** Worn around the neck of the captured drow spy, following being handed to her by Grygum.
- **Current condition:** Shrunken in size but operating at full power; possibly more potent than in their original form.
- **Notable properties:** An artifact of an alien faith; functions not as a weapon but as an instrument of psychological disruption. Its symbolic weight and unknown origin bypassed the drow spy’s mental defenses without explanation or threat.
- **Current use:** Employed by Grygum as a non-lethal tool of psychological coercion and subjugation; its presence serves as a visible sign of the drow spy’s broken resistance and ongoing containment.
- **Significance:** Demonstrated efficacy in neutralizing a trained spy’s defenses through psychological means alone; its amplified potency despite diminutive size suggests unique magical properties tied to form or origin.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of the “alien faith” associated with the beads is unspecified.
- Whether the beads are actively monitoring or magically constraining the drow spy beyond psychological impact is implied but not confirmed.
- The origin or original form of the beads is unknown.
- It is unclear whether Grygum retains any direct magical control over the beads now that they are worn by the drow spy.
