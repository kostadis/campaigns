### West Cleft District

**Current status:** Heavily fortified entrance to Gracklstugh, under Duergar control.  
**Current location:** Entrance to Gracklstugh, formerly a Derro slum district.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Controlled by Duergar forces (likely under Deepking Tarngardt’s regime or its successors).  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- A guardhouse now occupied by Duergar sentries.  
- Wanted posters displaying the Ember Vanguard (with misspelling: “aliave”).  
- Invisible and visible Duergar patrols.  

**Current assignment / role:** Chokepoint checkpoint enforcing identity verification, collecting bribes, and hunting the Ember Vanguard.  
**Key relationships:**  
- Oversees access to Gracklstugh.  
- Patrols former Derro territory now repurposed as a military gate.  
- Formerly abandoned Duergar guardhouses now actively manned.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Reclaimed and militarized after decades of Derro occupation.  
- Transitioned from squalid slum to high-security checkpoint under Duergar authority.  
- Actively intercepts and interrogates travelers; displays public wanted notices targeting the Ember Vanguard.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The district’s transformation signals a shift in Duergar strategy — from neglect to active surveillance and suppression.  
- The presence of wanted posters implies the Ember Vanguard are a current, high-priority threat to the regime.  
- The abandoned guardhouses, once used to monitor enslaved Derro, now serve as tools of state control — repurposed to enforce loyalty and eliminate dissent.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact chain of command for the Duergar guards (e.g., whether they answer to Tarngardt directly or a subordinate) is not specified.  
- The current status of the Derro population — whether they were expelled, exterminated, or remain hidden beneath the new regime — is unconfirmed despite prior descriptions of them as the district’s inhabitants.  
- The phrase “aliave” on the poster is likely a typo, but its intentional meaning (e.g., “alive” as a target status) is not clarified by context.  
- Whether the “hidden fissure used by Droki” (mentioned in ch17) still exists or is blocked/monitored is unknown.  
- It is unclear if the Derro still occupy the deeper tunnels beneath the guardhouse, or if the district is now fully Duergar-controlled surface-to-subterranean.
