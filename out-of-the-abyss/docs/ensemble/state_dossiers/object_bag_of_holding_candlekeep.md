### bag of holding (Candlekeep)

- **Current holder/location:** Carried by Glabbagool; last observed shifting when Glabbagool spoke in Candlekeep (ch59).
- **Current condition:** Extradimensional container with ventilation holes for breathing; shielded from sunlight.
- **Current contents:** Miniaturized drow spy and Glabbagool (the ooze).
- **Notable features:** Interior is shielded from sunlight as a mercy; contains no books or assassin as previously stated — those items are no longer present.
- **Current assignment/role:** Serves as a mobile prison and transport for the drow spy, under Glabbagool’s direct custody; used as a conduit for Glabbagool’s emergence and communication.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch54 (contents: miniaturized assassin, Glabbagool, five books) and ch59 (contents: only drow spy and Glabbagool); ch59 overrides ch54 for current state, but the fate of the assassin and books is unaccounted for.
- Unclear whether the bag was ever physically at Candlekeep or merely referenced there — "referenced by Thorin" does not confirm location.
- Ambiguity: ch54 states the bag was used as a “secret passage between the Underdark and the surface”; ch59 shows it shifting when Glabbagool spoke, but no confirmation this function is currently active.
