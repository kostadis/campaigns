### Voucher of the Varmint (Blingdenstone)

- **What it is:** A braided rat's foot token.  
- **Current holder/location:** In Daz’s possession.  
- **Current condition:** Intact, ceremonially presented.  
- **Significance:** A recognized symbol of friendship and alliance with wererats and outcasts throughout the Underdark, from Blingdenstone to Menzoberranzan.
