### Dust of Suleiman

- **Type:** Magical spell (4th-level evocation)  
- **Current holder/location:** Held by Grygum (last referenced as uncast, but already deployed)  
- **Current condition:** Already cast and active — forms a persistent 20-foot sphere on the cavern floor where the Big Heart Fungus resides  
- **Effect:** Deals 20 points of damage per turn to all creatures within the 20-foot sphere; originates from a 120-foot range casting point  
- **Current purpose:** Used as an infrastructure-demolition tool — actively destroying the Big Heart Fungus and severing Ilvara’s control over the area; no longer intended for direct use against Ilvara  
- **Significance:** A non-replaceable, high-cost resource deployed decisively; its deployment marks a tactical pivot from targeting Ilvara directly to neutralizing her anchoring infrastructure  

## Uncertainty
- The spell is referred to both as a "resource" and a "spell" — it is unclear if "Dust of Suleiman" is the name of the spell, the material component, or the resulting phenomenon.  
- While the spell is described as "cast directly onto the fungus" and "positioned on the cavern floor," it is unclear whether the spell effect is now stationary (fixed to a location) or mobile (still under the caster’s control).  
- Grygum is named as the user, but no confirmation exists that they still control or can recall/redirect the spell after casting.  
- The exact physical form of the spell’s manifestation (e.g., visible dust, energy field, etc.) is not described beyond its damage effect and area.
