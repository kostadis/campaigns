### Janussi

**Current status:** Deceased  
**Current location:** Remains secured in Candlekeep’s mortuary (last known location: Hightower Library chambers)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former Keeper of Tomes, Candlekeep; no active allegiance posthumously  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None (locket, one Hightower key, and heart removed postmortem; book *The Golden Ass* stolen; poisoned volume delivered as Deadwinter gift recovered)  
**Spells / feats / wounds:** None (deceased); fatal injuries: caved-in rib cage, heart removed, broken nose, chipped front tooth, blackened fingertip and lip (poison ingestion), tongue blackened — all prior to death  
**Current assignment / role:** Deceased; replaced as Keeper of Tomes by Daral Yashenti  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- On the day of his death, refused Kalan Strongbranch’s request for increased security; dismissed Kalan’s research as solipsistic in written critique; terminated multiple projects and staff; publicly criticized Teles Ahvoste.  
- Accepted a Deadwinter gift (a wrapped book) during dinner; later unsealed and read it, licking his finger to turn pages — ingesting poison.  
- Wrote annotations in midnight blue ink on *The Discoveries* and *The Golden Ass* after being struck in the face; then died at his desk.  
- His heart and locket were surgically removed postmortem; the heart later found in the lead-lined Milil chalice.  

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
- Secretly annotated a comedic text (*The Golden Ass*) with hidden spellwork, suggesting dual-layered scholarly subterfuge.  
- Was in a romantic relationship with Sylvira Savikas; previously involved with Kazryn Nyantani.  
- Had been compiling a scathing critique of Kalan Strongbranch’s methodology, later found wrapped in the same paper as the poisoned book.  
- His death was the intended outcome of a two-part scheme: poisoning to appear natural, followed by heart removal to prevent resurrection magic — designed to discredit Daral Yashenti.  
- His refusal to support Kalan’s research proposal and his public denunciations made him a target for multiple individuals (Bookwyrm, Alkrist, A'lai, Sylvira, Daral).

- Ambiguous attribution: Was the locket removed by the same hand that took the heart? Or by Sylvira, as implied by “Just to please his horned tart”? No direct witness or forensic link established.  
- Unclear chronology: Was the book wrapped when Janussi picked it up at dinner, or was it wrapped *before* the gift was delivered? The fact says “wrapped at the time,” but does not specify if tampering occurred before or after delivery.  
- Unresolved detail: Who physically removed the heart? The text confirms it was done to prevent resurrection, but does not identify the actor — only that it occurred after death.  
- Contradictory motive: One source says Janussi was poisoned *because* the recipient failed to appear “sufficiently grateful,” implying Daral expected gratitude — yet another source says Janussi was enraged upon reading the book. Can both be true? No resolution provided.
