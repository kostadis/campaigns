### Empty Shell Gem (Lost Tomb of Khaem)

- **Current holder/location:** In Jimjar’s possession, wagered as part of a bet with Grygum.
- **Current condition:** Intact, inert, and used as a magical wager token — no visible damage or active enchantment observed.
- **Significance:** A symbolic object tied to a binding magical bet concerning the Neverlight Grove; represents a foreshadowed ritual of unwilling vows.
- **Current assignment / role:** Wagered item in a supernatural contract between Jimjar and Grygum; its outcome is tied to the party’s experience in the Neverlight Grove.

## Uncertainty
- The gem’s physical origin (whether from the Lost Tomb of Khaem or elsewhere) is never confirmed — only its name and use in the bet are stated.
- Whether the gem has latent magical properties beyond its role as a wager token is unconfirmed.
- It is unclear if the gem is still in Jimjar’s possession after the bet was accepted, or if it was transferred to Grygum or a third party as part of the wager’s terms.
