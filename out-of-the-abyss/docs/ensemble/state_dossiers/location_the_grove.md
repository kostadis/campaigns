### The Grove

The Grove is a fungal ecosystem in the Underdark, currently deteriorating rapidly and undergoing unpredictable, hostile changes. It is the site of the Circle of Welcome’s rituals and is cultivated and bonded to by Myconids. Basidia has observed its decline.

None.
