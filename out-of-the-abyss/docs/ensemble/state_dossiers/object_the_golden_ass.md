### The Golden Ass

- **Type:** Object (book)  
- **Current location:** In Daral’s possession  
- **Current condition:** Dull and unremarkable in appearance; annotated with dried midnight blue ink containing jokes, matching the ink used in Janussi’s spell notes  
- **Notable properties:** When opened, renders a person motionless and uninterested — specifically prevents tickling (referred to by Queenie as a "hypnotic block")  
- **Current holder:** Daral  
- **Recent action:** Taken from the Hightower Library’s restricted section by Daral, who carried it out of the room  
- **Significance:** Linked to the deletion of content from the restricted Hightower Library book; originally annotated by Janussi with private humor  

## Uncertainty
- The exact mechanism by which the book renders someone motionless and uninterested is not described — only its observed effect.  
- Whether the book’s properties are inherent or magically activated is unconfirmed.  
- It is unclear if the book’s ability to prevent tickling is its only effect or if it has broader hypnotic or enchantment properties beyond what was observed.  
- The book’s original origin or creation is not stated — only its recent annotation by Janussi and removal by Daral.
