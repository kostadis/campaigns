### The Golden Ass

**Current status:** In possession of Daral.  
**Current location:** With Daral (last seen being carried away by him).  
**Current condition:** Physical book, intact; annotated in midnight blue ink with jokes that had dried before Janussi turned to spell research.  
**Notable property:** When opened, renders a person motionless and uninterested in external stimuli — not through magic or enchantment, but through sheer, overwhelming dullness (e.g., induces stillness so profound that a person fails to react to scratching a cat).  
**Significance:** Originally a comedy book; its contents were later used as a hypnotic block to disable observers. Its removal from the Hightower Library and Janussi’s chamber was deliberate. The book’s disappearance is directly linked to the deletion of its contents and Janussi’s final research.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of how the book induces stillness is described as non-magical, but no mechanism is given — whether it is psychological, linguistic, or some unknown property of the text remains unexplained.  
- Whether the blue ink annotations (jokes and spell research) are still legible or have been altered/erased since removal is unclear.  
- It is not confirmed whether Daral is currently using the book or merely holding it; its present function or activation state is unknown.  
- The phrase “the book had walked out of the room” is metaphorical in context, but the ambiguity of whether the book moved on its own (e.g., animated) or was simply carried is unresolved — though all other evidence confirms Daral carried it.
