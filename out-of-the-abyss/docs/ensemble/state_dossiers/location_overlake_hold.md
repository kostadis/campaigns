### Overlake Hold

Overlake Hold is the austere, stone-built administrative center and fortress of the Stone Guards, serving as the seat of Duergar authority in the undercity. Constructed entirely of grey stone with no color and dim, cold lighting, it reflects the brutal efficiency of Duergar governance. It is the personal stronghold of Errde Blackskull, where official business is conducted, covert agents are recruited, trauma dumps occur, and political manipulation is orchestrated. Its thick walls, shadowed corridors, and oppressive atmosphere make it a pressure cooker of fear and whispered plots — the center of Duergar power, yet paradoxically its most vulnerable point. It is here that Daz plants seeds of rebellion and Zalthir begins to doubt his allegiance.
