### The Ghohlbrorn’s Lair

The Ghohlbrorn’s Lair is a cold, austere Duergar stronghold serving as a temporary refuge for escapees. It contains a large, damp dining room and communal sleeping chambers. Accommodations are unpleasant, reflecting typical Duergar design.
