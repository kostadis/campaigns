### A'lai

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last known at Oak Tree Apothecary; last interaction occurred during/after the dismantling of the Janussi conspiracy)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent drow scholar with suspected Menzoberranzan ties; formerly embedded in Candlekeep’s corruption network; now compromised but still operational  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Formerly orchestrated the conspiracy leading to Janussi’s death, framing it as administrative restructuring.  
- Now a compromised but valuable asset due to his knowledge of the patron funding his survival and the hidden power structures behind Candlekeep’s corruption.  
- Identified as a candidate for the head of the Avowed, though not formally appointed.  
- Last known to be in contact with Leuwin (who facilitated his diversion to the greenhouse).  
- The party considered informing him of their findings but chose to bypass him in favor of Kalan’s strategy.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Initiated the conspiracy against Janussi, likely via a performance review or budget decision that gave Janussi motive to be targeted.  
- Used a staged request for fennel (at 2:30 PM on the day of the murder) to create a diversion, enabling the apothecary’s absence during the intrusion.  
- Maintained a narrative of administrative neutrality, treating blackmail as a career and masking his role as bureaucratic.  
- Remains the only credible lead on the patron behind Kaelira’s original mystery and the source of Menzoberranzan-level funding sustaining his existence.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- His silence is his only asset; he trades in knowledge, not gold or books.  
- He knows who is paying for his survival and the full scope of the corruption network tied to Candlekeep.  
- He has chosen not to weaponize this knowledge — yet — making him uniquely exploitable through discretion.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact current location of A'lai after the conspiracy’s dismantling is unconfirmed.  
- Whether he is still actively working within Candlekeep’s hierarchy or has gone into hiding is unclear.  
- His formal status as a candidate for head of the Avowed is noted, but it is unknown if the appointment was finalized, rejected, or suspended.  
- It is ambiguous whether he is aware the party uncovered the full truth about his role — he was not approached, but he may have inferred it.  
- Contradiction: Ch59 says he arrived at 3:00 PM; Ch60 says 2:30 PM. Later chapter (Ch60) overrides, so 2:30 PM is current.  
- Attribution ambiguity: Ch60 states “A'lai as the one who set it in motion” — but does not specify if this was direct action or indirect influence via administrative pressure; the exact mechanism remains inferred.
