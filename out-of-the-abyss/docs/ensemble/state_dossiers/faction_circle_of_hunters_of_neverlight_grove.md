### Circle of Hunters of Neverlight Grove

**Current status:** Functional faction  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid sub-group under Phylo’s doctrine; indirectly influenced by Basidia, but loyalty is divided  
**Current assignment / role:** Retrieve and deliver corpses of slain creatures (not killed by themselves) to the Circle of Masters for reanimation; operate under the doctrine of non-killing  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Key relationships:** Reports to or cooperates with the Circle of Masters (who now intercept and exploit most of their collected carcasses); led by Loobamub  
**Defining recent actions:** Collecting carcasses from the wild; carcasses increasingly intercepted by the Circle of Masters; Loobamub revealed knowledge of a concealed Shambling Mound nearby  

## Uncertainty
- The nature and extent of Basidia’s “indirect influence” is not specified — is it coercive, manipulative, or advisory?  
- “Loyalty is now divided” — among whom? Between Phylo and Basidia? Between the Hunters and the Masters? No clear attribution.  
- It is unclear whether the Circle of Hunters is aware that the Circle of Masters is exploiting them, or if they still believe their role is purely ceremonial or spiritual.  
- The Shambling Mound’s current status (active, dormant, controlled?) is unknown beyond Loobamub’s statement.
