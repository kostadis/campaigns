### Drow Poison

- **Current holder/location:** Attached to Grygum’s armor, alongside poisoned bolts (per ch03).  
- **Current condition:** Intact, contained in a vial (per ch03).  
- **Significance:** Used to coat weapons or bolts; confirmed lethal (implied by use against Prince Derendil).  
- **Notable attribution:** Identified as Drow poison by Sarith (ch03); previously used by Imbros to strike Prince Derendil (ch02).  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the vial found on Grygum is the same one used by Imbros — no direct link is established.  
- The method of application (e.g., on bolts vs. blades) is not consistently specified; Imbros used it in an unspecified manner, while Grygum’s is attached to bolts.
