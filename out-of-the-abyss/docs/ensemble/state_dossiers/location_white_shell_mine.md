### White Shell Mine

- **Current status:** Accessible, partially breached.  
- **Current location:** Underdark, deepest tunnels.  
- **Current state:** The previously impassable tunnel, characterized by blinding crystallite walls and absolute, suffocating silence, has been breached by a magically redirected explosive blast. Shadows within the mine still appear painted on the walls rather than flickering, indicating temporal stagnation persists in surrounding areas.  
- **Notable features:** Beyond the breached wall lies a large cavern containing a summoning circle inscribed with giant runes. Two basilisks guard the circle, moving with mechanical precision. The summoning circle requires three sacrifices (wealth, a weapon, blood) to activate; one sacrifice (a dart) has already been accepted and consumed.  
- **Current control/occupation:** The party (Thorin, Zalthir, Daz, Grygum, and the gnomes) currently occupies the breached tunnel and the cavern with the summoning circle. The basilisks remain active and hostile.  
- **Defining recent action:** The party successfully breached the crystallite barrier using a magically redirected explosive detonation, enabled by Zalthir’s Shadow Monk abilities and gnomish explosives. Thorin completed the first sacrifice on the summoning circle, triggering its activation phase.  
- **Significance:** The mine is the largest salt mine in the Underdark and serves as the final locus of the quest to free Entémoch. The temporal stagnation and crystallite formation were metaphysical barriers now overcome at one point, but the deeper cavern remains under ritualized magical protection.

## Uncertainty
- The current status of the *entire* mine beyond the breached tunnel and cavern is unknown — whether temporal stagnation persists elsewhere in the mine is not confirmed.  
- It is unclear whether the basilisks are bound to the summoning circle permanently or if they are merely guardians activated by its proximity; their origin or creation is not specified.  
- The exact nature of the “metaphysical blockage” and whether it was fully resolved or merely circumvented is ambiguous — the breach allowed passage, but the temporal stillness may remain intact in unexplored regions.  
- The current whereabouts or status of Perigrog Scrapedust after the breach is not stated — he was the source of prior information but has not been observed since.
