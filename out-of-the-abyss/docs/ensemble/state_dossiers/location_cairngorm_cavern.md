### Cairngorm Cavern

- **Current status:** Active, occupied  
- **Current location:** Beneath Gracklstugh, beyond Themberchaud’s cave, in the eastern wall south of Laduguer’s Furrow  
- **Control/occupancy:** Inhabited and tended by Hgraam, the venerable Stone Giant  
- **Physical state:** A vast, ancient, polished cavern with deeply resonant earth-magic; marked by colossal stone structures and serene, deliberate atmosphere  
- **Connections:**  
  - Connected to the Duergar city via a gigantic tunnel originating south of Laduguer’s Furrow  
  - Serves as the origin point for journeys to Laduguer’s Furrow and the Derro slums  
  - Functions as the entrance to the Deep King’s domain  
- **Significance:**  
  - Sacred site tied to Stone Giant lore and the legacy of Stonespeaker Hgraam  
  - The only place in the Underdark where truth is spoken slowly and deliberately  
  - A sanctuary of ancient wisdom and geological resonance, where stones “speak” and Hgraam tends to them  
- **Current role:** Spiritual and historical anchor of Stone Giant heritage in Gracklstugh; refuge and waypoint for those seeking deep truth or passage to the Deep King’s realm  

## Uncertainty
- Unclear whether Hgraam is the sole resident or if other Stone Giants also dwell there — only Hgraam is confirmed present.  
- Ambiguous whether “the Deep King’s domain” refers to a separate layer beyond Cairngorm Cavern or is synonymous with it — no clarification on spatial hierarchy.  
- No confirmation whether the tunnel from Themberchaud’s cave to Cairngorm Cavern is currently passable or guarded.  
- No evidence of current Duergar presence or control within the cavern — only its connection to the Duergar district is noted.
