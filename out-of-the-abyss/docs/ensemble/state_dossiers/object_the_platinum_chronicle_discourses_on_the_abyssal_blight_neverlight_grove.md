### The Platinum Chronicle: Discourses on the Abyssal Blight (Neverlight Grove)

A sacred holy text that details Zuggtmoy’s nature as the Demon Queen of Fungi, Lady of Rot and Decay, her opposition to Bahamut, and the corrupting, mind-destroying nature of her fungal dominion. It serves as theological evidence identifying Zuggtmoy as the Demon Queen and describes her spore-based corruption as a perversion of life and a weapon against free will.
