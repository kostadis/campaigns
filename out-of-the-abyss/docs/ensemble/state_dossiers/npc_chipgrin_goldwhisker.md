### Chipgrin Goldwhisker

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone (upper boroughs, under wererat control)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of the wererats; allied with Blingdenstone’s peace faction  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly held, but granted the *Voucher of the Varmint* to Daz (no longer in possession)  
**Current assignment / role:** Leader of the wererat pack; negotiator and territorial claimant for upper boroughs of Blingdenstone; advisor on psychic noise mitigation  
**Key relationships:**  
- Allies with the party (especially Daz and Thorin, whom he respects)  
- Previously disputed territory with Senni Diggermattock; now in negotiated coexistence  
- Reports to no one — leads his own pack, but participates in Blingdenstone Council  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully negotiated wererat control of Blingdenstone’s upper boroughs during the Council meeting  
- Led wererat scouts in the Hall of Melting Mirrors, offering to sacrifice his pack to neutralize psychic slime effects  
- Collaborated with the party to locate the harmonic center of the psychic noise; praised Daz’s insight  
- Gifted Daz the *Voucher of the Varmint* as a token of lasting friendship  

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
- Values stealth and honor among his pack, taking offense at being called noisy  
- Seeks territorial legitimacy and autonomy for wererats within Blingdenstone  
- Respects competence over race, as shown by his apology to Zalthir and admiration for Daz

- The exact current location within the upper boroughs is unspecified — only that control is established.

- The nature of the "Voucher of the Varmint" is described but not defined — its magical or legal function is unknown.  
- No confirmation whether Senni Diggermattock is currently alive or present in Blingdenstone — only that territorial disputes were resolved.
