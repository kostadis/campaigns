### Daral Yashenti

**Current status:** Alive, physically restored by Dawnbringer.  
**Current location:** Traveling with the investigative party, recently escaped an ambush in Candlekeep’s lower halls.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former Council member of Candlekeep; now a suspect-turned-survivor with no formal allegiance.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly held at this moment, but previously carried *The Golden Ass* (the poisoned book); no longer in possession of it.  
**Notable spells/feats/wounds:** Recovered from poison ingestion via Lesser Restoration; no lingering physical wounds reported, but mentally fragile.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Formerly Janussi’s rival and former Keeper of the Tomes; now a desperate survivor seeking exoneration.  
- Last seen traveling with the investigative party (Zalthir, Thorin, Sprig, Dawnbringer); is not trusted but tolerated.  
- Key relationships:  
  - **Dawnbringer:** Viewed with fearful reverence; begged for healing, now visibly shaken by their presence.  
  - **Sylvira Savikas:** Dismissed by her as incompetent and incapable of orchestration; remains a target of her scorn.  
  - **Zalthir:** Feared as a potential conduit for exposure of his drunken admissions about dragonborn.  
  - **A'lai Aivenmore:** Believes A'lai framed him to eliminate ideological opposition; sees himself as a pawn in A'lai’s rise.  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Inadvertently poisoned himself by licking his finger after touching the poisoned book *The Golden Ass* while reclaiming it.  
  - Was deliberately framed for Janussi’s murder; the book was re-wrapped to appear as his gift.  
  - Openly pleaded for healing after being left untreated, fearing he was deemed “not worth the spell.”  
  - Panicked during the helmed horror ambush, screaming that such creatures “can’t be here,” revealing severe psychological trauma.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes he was framed by A'lai Aivenmore, who views Daral’s love of poetry and fiction as a threat to ideological control of Candlekeep.  
- Admits to giving Janussi the book as a symbolic lesson — not to sell books — but denies any intent to kill.  
- Claims he was drugged and confused during the incident, but later evidence suggests he was sober enough to act with intent — and then to be manipulated.  
- Secretly fears his own incompetence and believes his survival hinges on proving his value — hence his offer to install magical wards on all future gifts.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Was Daral the *intended victim* (the book was re-wrapped to look like his gift) or the *actual killer* who poisoned himself? The text asserts both that he was framed and that he licked the book accidentally — but does not clarify whether the re-wrapping was done *by him* or *by the true killer*.  
- Attribution ambiguity: Who exactly re-wrapped the book to make it appear as Daral’s gift? The facts state it was done, but do not name the actor.  
- Chronological uncertainty: Did Daral’s panic during the ambush occur *after* or *during* his restoration? The text says he was restored, then panicked — but does not specify timing relative to the ambush.  
- Motive vs. capability: Sylvira dismissed him as incapable of orchestration, yet he was the one who delivered the book and licked the poison — suggesting he may have been manipulated, not merely incompetent. Is his “lack of execution capability” a genuine trait or a cover for being used?  
- Status of *The Golden Ass*: After Daral reclaimed it, who took possession of it? Not stated.
