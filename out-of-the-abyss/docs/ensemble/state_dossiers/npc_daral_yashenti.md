### Daral Yashenti

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last seen in context of the candle keep’s library, but not confirmed present)  
**Allegiance/faction:** None explicitly stated; previously associated with the candle keep’s library as a rival to Janussi

**Current assignment / role:** Drunken poet; sells worthless poetry to fund a new library  
**Key relationships:** Long-standing bitter rival of Janussi; dismissed by Sylvira as incompetent and incapable of orchestrating the murder; his poisoned book was the framing device Alkrist intended to use

- Sylvira explicitly dismissed him as too drunk and too inept to have planned the murder, despite his plausible motive  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**
