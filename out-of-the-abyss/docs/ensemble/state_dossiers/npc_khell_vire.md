### Khell-Vire

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Candlekeep  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former member of the Garden of Shadow (exiled 80 years ago); currently operates as a blind monk and lineage keeper at Candlekeep, unaffiliated with any active order  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Faded robe of the Garden of Shadow (worn beneath Candlekeep gray robes)  
**Current assignment / role:** Assesses shadow monk initiates through existential, experience-based questioning; serves as a witness to their transformation, not a doctrinal instructor  
**Key relationships:** Last seen with Zalthir, whom he has begun to guide; no other companions named  
**Defining recent actions:** Observed Zalthir’s unorthodox combat technique (shadow step + strike), recognized it as a rediscovery of lost shadow monk principles, and chose to offer him a specialized technique rather than correct his form. Refused to impose doctrinal training, instead asking: “What were you sent toward?”  
**Revealed motivations / secrets:** His own silence during Zalthir’s trial suggests he is answering the same existential question he poses — his exile and continued adherence to the Way of the Shadow, despite rejection by his former order, may be his own path to remembrance.
