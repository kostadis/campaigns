### Khell-Vire

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Inner ward of the monastery (implied by context of testing Zalthir)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Silent archivist and lineage keeper of forgotten shadow monk lineages; operates outside traditional monastic hierarchy  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified; implied mastery of shadow monk techniques, including advanced shadow step and perception-based assessment abilities  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Evaluates students (like Zalthir) not on technical skill, but on the emergent truth of their lived experience; recognizes and honors unorthodox expressions of the shadow path that align with forgotten lineages. Last seen instructing Zalthir, with whom he has a mentor-student dynamic emerging. Declined direct involvement in Zalthir’s upcoming trials: “I will not be there. This is your work, not mine.”  
**Defining recent actions:** Recognized Zalthir’s unorthodox fusion of shadow step and strike as a rediscovery of a lost shadow monk lineage; awarded him a specialized shadow technique in acknowledgment, despite Zalthir’s initial lack of formal training. Framed the evaluation as an inquiry into what Zalthir had become through experience, not what he was taught.
