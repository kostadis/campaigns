### Zuggtmoy

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Actively influencing the Festering Fissure and surrounding caverns, with fungal growth spreading from the Garden of Stone and Ilvara’s ritual chamber.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent demonic deity; self-proclaimed Lady of Rot and Decay.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None listed. Manifests through fungal biomass and mycelial networks.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Expanding her dominion by exploiting the power vacuum left by the Pudding King’s defeat. Uses oozes to “soften the shell” to take root in flesh beneath. Tended by mycelium-clad servants who weave her bridal gown from lichen and mycelium. Revered as a goddess by the infected (Sarith, Zalthir, Phylo).  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Moved to fill the power vacuum after the Pudding King’s defeat, accelerating fungal growth throughout the caverns.  
- Actively corrupting the Festering Fissure and Garden of Stone via oozes and spores.  
- Infiltrating Ilvara’s ritual chamber with pulsing fungal growth.  
- Operates covertly, preferring to spread while others sleep — a quiet, creeping threat contrasting with Juiblex’s overt chaos.  
- Previously destroyed Neverlight Grove and nearly possessed Thorin.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Seeks to replace order with chaos through fungal domination — transforming life into mindless hosts. Her corruption is slow, rhythmic, and insidious, designed to take root beneath the surface before being noticed. She is perceived as a comprehensible evil compared to newer, unknown afflictions, yet her legacy of destruction lingers in the minds of survivors.
