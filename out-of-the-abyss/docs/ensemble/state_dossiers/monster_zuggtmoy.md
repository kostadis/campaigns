### Zuggtmoy

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Influencing the Festering Fissure and surrounding caverns, with active fungal growth spreading from the Garden of Stone and through the ruins of Neverlight Grove.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent demonic entity; Demon Queen of Fungi, Lady of Rot and Decay.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None physically carried; manifests through fungal biomass, spore clouds, and psychic corruption. Employs mycelium-clad servants and oozes as extensions of her will.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Actively exploiting the power vacuum left by the Pudding King’s defeat. Uses oozes to “soften the shell” to take root in flesh beneath. Serves as the demonic patron to Ilvara Mizzrym (now manifested as a spore cloud). Corrupts minds to induce delusions of divine purpose — notably influencing the wizard and Glabbagool.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Expanded fungal infestation throughout the caverns, with unnatural, rapid growth of mushrooms and lichen.  
- Subtly infiltrated the minds of victims (e.g., Ilvara, the wizard, Glabbagool), rewriting their thoughts and texts to inscribe her will.  
- Manipulated Ilvara Mizzrym as her instrument, leading to Ilvara’s transformation into a spore cloud.  
- Operates quietly while targets sleep, contrasting with Juiblex’s overt chaos.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks to replace overt chaos with insidious, slow corruption — transforming faith, language, and self-perception to make victims believe they are conduits of her divine will.  
- Her ultimate goal is to root her garden in living flesh, using oozes as vectors to break down biological defenses.  
- Her influence is detectable only through gradual distortion — not by violence, but by the silent rewriting of identity.

## Uncertainty
- The exact physical location of Zuggtmoy’s core form remains unspecified — whether she resides in the Festering Fissure, the Garden of Stone, or a hidden fungal tower is implied but not confirmed.  
- It is unclear whether the “writing in the prayer book” was done by Ilvara under Zuggtmoy’s direct control, or if Zuggtmoy herself inscribed it psychically; the source does not distinguish between possession and direct intervention.  
- While Ilvara is confirmed as her former instrument, it is not stated whether Ilvara’s spore cloud form is still active or has been dispersed.  
- The nature of Zuggtmoy’s “bridal gown” from ch33 — whether it is a literal artifact, a vision, or a symbolic manifestation — is not resolved in later chapters.
