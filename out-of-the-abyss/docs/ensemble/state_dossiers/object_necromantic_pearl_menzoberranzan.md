### necromantic pearl (Menzoberranzan)

- **What it is:** A fist-sized, smooth, dense object of unnatural origin, resembling a worked stone rather than a natural growth.  
- **Current condition:** Radiating strong necromantic energy, evoking a sensation akin to standing before a door to an unwanted room.  
- **Current location:** Buried at the core of the fungal mass in the chamber once used by Ilvara as a ritual anchor.  
- **Origin:** Either deliberately placed there by an unknown agent, or grew under uniquely corrupted conditions that mimicked intentional crafting.  
- **Significance:** Serves as the focal point of the corrupted fungal mass; its unnatural finish and placement suggest deliberate necromantic engineering, though its creator or purpose remains unconfirmed.

## Uncertainty
- Whether the pearl was placed by an external agent or spontaneously grew under ideal conditions — both possibilities are presented as equally plausible with no definitive evidence favoring one.  
- The identity of the entity responsible for its creation or placement is not stated.  
- Whether the pearl is still active, dormant, or has been disturbed since discovery is not specified.
