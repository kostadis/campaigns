### Poisoned Book (Oak Tree Apothecary)

**Current holder/location:** Reclaimed and held by investigators (specifically, in custody as key evidence).  
**Current condition:** Laced with a slow-acting toxin; physically intact, bearing traces of saliva from Daral’s licking.  
**Significance:** A weapon of premeditated murder, originally from Janussi’s research, used to kill or incapacitate him under false pretenses. Served as the central piece of evidence in the murder plot, designed to frame a colleague and obscure the true perpetrator. Symbolizes intellectual betrayal.  
**Notable physical interaction:** Licked by Daral’s finger during its reclamation, transferring toxin and saliva to his skin.  

## Uncertainty
- The current physical location (e.g., evidence locker, investigator’s possession) is implied but not named.  
- Whether the book is still toxic to touch (post-reclamation) is not stated.  
- The exact identity of the person who laced the book is not confirmed — only that it originated from Janussi’s research and was delivered under false pretenses.  
- It is unclear if Daral’s licking was the *only* human contact after poisoning, or if others handled it.
