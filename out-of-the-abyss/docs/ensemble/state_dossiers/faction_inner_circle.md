### Inner Circle

The Inner Circle is the elite tier of Phylo’s hierarchy, composed of the leaders of all other myconid circles and the sovereigns. They enforce Phylo’s doctrine, maintain the reanimation pipeline, and serve as the core of ideological control. Phylo has restricted their interactions exclusively to other circle leaders and sovereigns, severing traditional ties to the broader myconid community.

## Uncertainty
- Ambiguity in whether “Circle of Masters” is a subset of, synonymous with, or distinct from the Inner Circle — the quote suggests they are separate (“the Inner Circle of all of the circle leaders and the Circle of Masters is a circle of those who are closest to Phylo”), but no clarification is given on membership overlap or hierarchy between them.
