### Entémoch

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed in proximity to the Earth Elemental’s throne room)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Prince of Good Elementals  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Ruler and embodiment of Good Elementals; feels favorably toward the party following their assistance to the Earth Elemental in the throne room. Subtle approval was sensed as warmth in the stone and stilling of residual elemental chaos.  
**Defining recent actions:** Recognized and responded favorably to the party’s aid to the Earth Elemental, manifesting his approval through environmental cues in the throne room.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** His power remains locked behind a ritual requiring three sacrifices — wealth, a weapon, and blood — with two sacrifices still unfulfilled. His favor toward the party may imply potential willingness to waive or guide them toward completing the ritual.

## Uncertainty

- It is unclear whether the two remaining sacrifices (wealth, weapon, blood) are still required for his boon, or if his newfound favor alters or nullifies the ritual requirement.  
- The attribution of “Entémoch is the Prince of Good Elementals” is stated as a fact in ch48, but no prior chapter confirms his title — this is the first and only source of his formal designation.  
- It is unknown whether the warmth and stilling in the stone were direct manifestations of Entémoch, or ambient effects of the Earth Elemental’s resolution.
