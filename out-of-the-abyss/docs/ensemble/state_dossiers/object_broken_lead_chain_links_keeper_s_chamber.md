### broken lead chain links (Keeper's Chamber)

Current status: Recovered and catalogued as evidence.  
Current location: In possession of Grygum (physically embedded in his neck at time of discovery).  
Current condition: Two broken lead links, snapped at the ends; show wear patterns consistent with long-term use as a pendant or locket chain. Material and craftsmanship suggest non-common, possibly sacred or magical origin.  
Current holder: Embedded in Grygum’s neck at time of discovery; no indication of removal since.  
Notable recent action: Found post-mortem on Grygum’s body, indicating the chain and its attached object were forcibly removed after he was struck but before death — same pattern observed in Janussi’s case, suggesting a shared modus operandi.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradictory attribution: One fact places the links on Janussi’s neck; another places them beneath the Keeper’s neck; a third places them embedded in Grygum’s neck. The Keeper and Janussi are likely the same person (context suggests “Keeper” = Janussi), but Grygum is a separate individual — so the links cannot be simultaneously on all three. The final fact (Grygum) is chronologically last and thus overrides earlier placements — but this creates a contradiction: how could the same two links be found on both Janussi and Grygum? Either the facts conflate two separate incidents/objects, or one is misattributed.  
- Unclear whether the links were removed from Grygum’s neck after discovery — no later fact confirms their current physical location beyond the discovery scene.  
- Ambiguous whether “the Keeper” and “Janussi” are the same person — if not, then the two sets of links may belong to different objects, making the description of “two broken lead links” refer to two distinct artifacts.
