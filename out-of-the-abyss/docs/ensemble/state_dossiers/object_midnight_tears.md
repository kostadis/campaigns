### Midnight Tears

**Type:** Object  
**Current status:** Active poison, recently applied and transferred  
**Current location:** Traces present on the margins of *Tashalian Nights* and on Alkrist’s hands  
**Current condition:** Activated — has been ingested via contact with saliva (darkened residue observed), and is now lethally active in the system of Janussi  
**Notable properties:**  
- Pale ochre dust, derived from Feywild pollen; inert until contact with saliva  
- Upon ingestion (via food, liquid, or licked fingers), it darkens and becomes lethal, causing death precisely at midnight unless neutralized  
- Neutralized only by specific Feywild sap — otherwise, fatal  
- Extremely rare; accessible only through Feywild connections  

**Recent actions:**  
- Applied to a book (*Tashalian Nights*)  
- Transferred to Janussi’s body through handling, leaving visible residue on the book’s margins and Alkrist’s hands  
- Now active in Janussi’s system, with death scheduled for midnight unless neutralized  

## Uncertainty  
- Contradiction between sources: One fact states the poison is lethal *upon contact with saliva*, another says it remains inert *until midnight* — both cannot be simultaneously true without clarification on whether “contact with saliva” triggers activation or merely marks ingestion (later fact implies ingestion triggers countdown).  
- Unclear if the poison darkens *only* upon ingestion or upon any saliva contact (e.g., external touch).  
- Attribution of application: Was the poison applied to the book by Alkrist, Janussi, or another? The facts state it was on the book and transferred to Janussi, but do not specify who applied it initially.  
- “Black stuff” quote in ch58 may refer to Midnight Tears, but is ambiguous — could be a different substance.
