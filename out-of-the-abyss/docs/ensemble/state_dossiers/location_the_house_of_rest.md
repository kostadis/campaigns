### The House of Rest

The House of Rest is the party’s quarters at Candlekeep, serving as a private space where mundane and metaphysical elements converge. Glabbagool is confined there, and Dawnbringer is placed against a wall within immediate reach. Above the quarters, in an unseen gallery, someone is still reciting prophecies, underscoring the institution’s pervasive influence even in personal spaces.
