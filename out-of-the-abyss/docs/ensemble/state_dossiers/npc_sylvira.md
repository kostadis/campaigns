### Sylvira

**Current status:** Alive, but dying slowly under the effect of a poison. Bedridden and shuddering from fever, yet cognitively intact and capable of arcane activity (manipulating a familiar and mage hand remotely).  

**Current location:** Her chambers, likely within the Institute or a secured academic residence.  

**Allegiance/faction:** Tiefling researcher of infernal domains; affiliated with the Institute (implied by context of Janussi’s murder investigation and academic rivalries). Not aligned with the Avowed — in fact, she identifies them as hostile.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Maintains an active **Mordenkainen’s Mansion** in the Abyss, constructed specifically to protect her son.  
- Has a **familiar** in active service (operating remotely).  
- Capable of **mage hand** and other minor arcane control despite physical debilitation.  
- Suffering from an **abyssal plague** transmitted by her son.  
- Under the slow-acting **poison** that is killing her.  
- No wristwatch; does not track time precisely.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acting as a cooperating witness in the investigation into Janussi’s murder.  
- **Relationship with Janussi:** Formerly in a volatile romantic/academic relationship; grieving their final argument.  
- **Relationship with her son:** A tiefling born in the Abyss; she is trying to coax him back into her Mordenkainen’s Mansion.  
- **Relationship with Teles Ahvoste:** Openly hostile; names him as the primary source of long-standing animosity and the likely source of false sightings after 12:30 AM.  
- **Relationship with Kalan Strongbranch:** Promised to intercede with Janussi on his behalf — never did.  
- **Relationship with Bookwyrm:** Bookwyrm opposed Kalan’s proposal most strongly; Sylvira agreed with Bookwyrm’s stance.  
- **Authority status:** Respected enough that Grygum references her as the arbiter of Drow fates (“Sylvira might decide his fate should be precisely like Serith’s”).  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Sneaked out after the feast to visit her son in the Abyss.  
- Returned to her chambers around 12:30 AM, suffering from the onset of fever and plague.  
- Refused to acknowledge any sighting of herself after 12:30 AM, attributing such claims to the Avowed — specifically naming Teles Ahvoste.  
- Denied ever calling Janussi a “genus traitor,” insisted she never drank honey, and dismissed Kalan’s proposal as insane.  
- Maintained diplomatic silence regarding Alkrist’s actions at the feast.  
- Declared the feast “the same drab affair it always was” and left early due to illness.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes jealousy is a powerful motivator for murder — specifically citing Teles’s resentment toward Janussi.  
- Believes Janussi was right to want Kalan gone, calling him a “clown, a boar,” and criticizing his research.  
- Constructed a Mordenkainen’s Mansion in the Abyss not for herself, but solely to protect her son — indicating deep, hidden maternal investment.  
- Aware she is being impersonated; anticipates being accused of things she didn’t say (e.g., calling Janussi a traitor) and has prepared rebuttals.  
- Has no motive to lie now — she is dying, framed, and has nothing left to lose.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Is Sylvira’s son currently inside the Mordenkainen’s Mansion or outside? She was “trying to coax him back into it,” but it’s unclear if he has returned.  
- Ambiguity: Was the “torn demon lady” seen in ch57 a physical manifestation, an illusion, or an impersonation? No confirmation whether this was Sylvira herself or someone mimicking her.  
- Attribution: Grygum says Sylvira “might decide” the fate of failed Drow — is this a formal authority, a rumor, or a personal reputation? No evidence of institutional power granted.  
- Chronology: Did Sylvira’s fever begin *before* or *after* returning from visiting her son? The text links the fever to her return, but the timing of infection is unclear.  
- Possession: Does she still possess the Mordenkainen’s Mansion? No indication it was destroyed or lost — assumed active, but not confirmed post-ch60.
