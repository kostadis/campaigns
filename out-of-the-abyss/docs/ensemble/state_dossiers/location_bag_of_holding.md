### Bag of Holding

- **Current status:** Active and in use; contains Glabbagool and a miniaturized drow spy.
- **Current location:** Carried by an unknown party (last observed in proximity to Daz).
- **Current condition:** Emits a shifting grey ooze; surface is intact with ventilation holes punched through to sustain the drow.
- **Current possessions / notable items:** Contains Glabbagool (a sentient, ooze-like entity) and a miniaturized drow spy.
- **Current assignment / role:** Functions as a mobile containment vessel for Glabbagool and the drow; serves as an observational platform — Glabbagool’s eye can protrude from its ooze to survey external objects.
- **Defining recent actions:** Glabbagool’s eye emerged from the bag’s grey ooze to observe a bright object in Daz’s hand.
- **Key relationships:** Glabbagool is the dominant occupant and active agent within the bag; the drow is a captive, kept alive via ventilation holes and under Glabbagool’s instinctual surveillance.

## Uncertainty
- The current carrier of the Bag of Holding is not named; only that it was near Daz in ch59.
- It is unclear whether the drow is still alive or merely being kept alive by the holes — no confirmation of her current state beyond needing watching.
- It is ambiguous whether Glabbagool is physically inside the bag or partially merged with it, given the ooze-emergence behavior.
- The origin or nature of the “shifting grey ooze” is not explained beyond its function as Glabbagool’s medium.
