### Sapphire summoning spell (Deneir's Sanctum)

An ancient arcane enchantment that allows the caster to summon a single hidden object into their hand, requiring a very expensive sapphire as a focus. The spell functions regardless of whether the object is concealed, buried, or discarded (e.g., thrown into a river), but does not work if the object is worn. It is specifically designed to enable secure concealment and instant retrieval of critical items — such as the High Tower key — by storing them in inaccessible locations and recalling them only when needed.

None.
