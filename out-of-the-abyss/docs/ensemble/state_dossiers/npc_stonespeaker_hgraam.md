### Stonespeaker Hgraam

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Cairngorm Cavern  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent Stone Giant oracle; referenced as a divine agent of the Ember Vanguard with the ability to reverse grafting rituals, though not confirmed as an active member  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Stonespeaker Crystal (19-inch shard of quartz; enables *Speak with Animals*, *Speak with the Dead*, *Speak with Plants*, and enhanced divinations) — last confirmed gifted in ch17; no evidence of loss or transfer  
**Current assignment / role:** Reclusive oracle of stone wisdom; feared and distrusted by surface-world travelers; believed by Grygum and Daz to possess divine magic capable of reversing the cult’s grafting rituals on Stone Giants  
**Defining recent actions:** Closed his cavern to outsiders (ch28), refused to acknowledge surface routes or their existence, offered coins to the party, and withdrew into isolation. Previously revealed the corruption of the Deep King by Shal and gifted the Stonespeaker Crystal (ch17).  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Deeply attuned to the suffering of the Underdark; believes the stones cry out against corruption and madness. Fears outside interference and views the surface world as alien and threatening. His isolation stems from trauma and distrust, possibly linked to the events surrounding his former apprentices’ fate and the Deep King’s fall.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch15 (implied death of Hgraam) and all later chapters (ch17–28, where he is alive, speaking, and active). Later chapters override earlier — he is alive now.
- Unclear whether Hgraam is an official member of the Ember Vanguard or merely believed by others (Grygum/Daz) to have their divine power. No direct confirmation of affiliation.
- Uncertain if Hgraam still physically possesses the Stonespeaker Crystal — it was gifted in ch17, but no later fact confirms whether he retained it or if it remains with the party.
- Ambiguous whether the “tragedy” referenced in ch17 (Hgraam being driven mad) refers to Hgraam himself or his apprentices. The quote “He was driven mad” is ambiguous in referent.
- Unclear if the “divine agent” status from ch25 is a belief held by others or an objective truth — no direct statement from Hgraam confirms it.
