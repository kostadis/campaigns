### Stonespeaker Hgraam

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Cairngorm Cavern  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent Stone Giant oracle; referenced as a divine agent of the Ember Vanguard (per ch25), though not confirmed as an active member  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Stonespeaker Crystal (19-inch quartz shard; enables *Speak with Animals*, *Speak with the Dead*, *Speak with Plants*, and enhanced divinations) — last confirmed gifted in ch17; no indication it was taken or lost  
**Current assignment / role:** Reclusive keeper of rock wisdom; reluctant oracle who interprets portents from the stones; believed by Grygum and Daz to possess the divine ability to reverse the grafting magic inflicted on Stone Giants by the cult’s ritual  
**Key relationships:** Last known to have mentored apprentices (ch15); last interacted with the party in ch28; no current companions named; Deep King and Shal are referenced as corrupted entities he has witnessed but no longer engages with  
**Defining recent actions:** In ch28, Hgraam closed his cavern to outsiders, refused to acknowledge surface worlds or routes, and offered coins to the party before withdrawing into isolation. He embodies deep distrust of surface interference and the ancient insularity of his kind.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believes the stones cry out in pain due to corruption spreading from the Underdark; aware of Shal’s influence over the Deep King; fears outside interference and the erosion of stone-based truth; his isolation suggests trauma or disillusionment from past revelations.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch17 (Hgraam actively gifts the Stonespeaker Crystal and reveals Shal’s corruption) and ch28 (Hgraam refuses to discuss surface routes and denies existence of surface world) — whether his withdrawal is due to trauma, fear, or a shift in allegiance is unconfirmed.
- ch25 repeatedly refers to Hgraam as a “divine agent of the Ember Vanguard” capable of reversing grafting magic, but no direct interaction or confirmation from Hgraam himself occurs in ch25 or ch28 — is this belief accurate, or merely assumed by Grygum/Daz?
- Is Hgraam still the same individual who mentored apprentices in ch15, or is this a title passed down? No evidence of succession or death of prior Hgraam.
- The Stonespeaker Crystal was gifted in ch17 — is it still in Hgraam’s possession, or did he retain a copy? Unclear if he still uses it or if it remains with the party.
- No confirmation whether Hgraam is aware of the cult’s recent activities in ch25–28; his isolation may mean he is unaware, or he may be deliberately ignoring them.
