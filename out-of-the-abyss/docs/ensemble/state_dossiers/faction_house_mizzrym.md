### House Mizzrym

**Current status:** Active but disrupted.  
**Current location:** Unknown primary seat; operational presence confirmed in the chamber where its agents died (location tied to Daz’s protection zone).  
**Allegiance/faction:** Noble drow house; formerly bound to Zuggtmoy through Ilvara Mizzrym, now operating without its high priestess.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Last confirmed assets include short swords, long swords, and steel whistles carried by slain agents. One agent bore a signet ring of House T'sarran, suggesting possible covert alliance or compromised identity.  
**Current assignment / role:** Actively seeking Ilvara Mizzrym’s whereabouts via frantic sealed orders; tasked with neutralizing Daz per a protection contract known to the Duskryn sisters.  
**Defining recent actions:** Sent a contingent of guards and a mage (wearing T'sarran sigil) on a mission that ended in total fatalities; the mission was compromised or sacrificed. Ilvara’s disappearance has triggered internal panic and unanswered communications.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Ilvara’s corruption and death have destabilized the house’s core operations. The house remains unaware that Ilvara was consumed by Zuggtmoy and believes her silence to be disobedience or betrayal. House Mizzrym is a direct threat to Daz’s life, alongside House T'sarran.

## Uncertainty
- The current leadership of House Mizzrym is unknown — no successor to Ilvara is named or confirmed.
- Whether House Mizzrym still actively collaborates with House T'sarran is unclear; the presence of a T'sarran signet ring on a Mizzrym agent could indicate espionage, a double agent, or a shared operation — the intent is unconfirmed.
- The current physical location or stronghold of House Mizzrym is not stated.
- Whether the house has initiated retaliation, reorganization, or new operations since Ilvara’s death is not documented.
