### The Oak Tree Apothecary

A treehouse grown into the roots of an evergreen oak, located between the Sea Warden's Tower and the House of Mechanus. It is currently known as the original source of the stolen vial of midnight tears and the site where peppermint was spilled on the counter floor during a cover-up. It was also the suspected source of the poison used in Janussi’s murder, and a location where Daz could communicate with a pet squirrel. Leuwin is expected to identify the poison here.

## Uncertainty
- The current operational status of the apothecary (open, closed, abandoned) is not stated.
- The identity or current presence of the apothecary’s owner or staff is unknown.
- Whether the pet squirrel is still present or active is not confirmed beyond its prior existence.
- It is unclear if the peppermint spill is still visible or has been cleaned.
