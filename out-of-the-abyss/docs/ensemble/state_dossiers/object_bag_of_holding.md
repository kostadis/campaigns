### Bag of Holding

- **Current holder/location**: Carried by the party; currently in use as a containment vessel for drow and Glabbagool.
- **Current condition**: Intact; has survived months of Underdark travel, the ooze crisis, and Blingdenstone. No signs of damage or degradation.
- **Current contents**: 
  - Glabbagool (a sentient grey ooze), who resides inside and frequently peers out from the opening.
  - Drow prisoners, requiring active monitoring; Glabbagool has been tasked with watching them.
- **Notable modifications**: Holes have been poked into the bag to allow ventilation for contained creatures.
- **Current assignment/role**: Used as a mobile, semi-secure containment unit for sentient beings (Glabbagool and drow), leveraging Glabbagool’s presence for passive surveillance.
- **Defining recent actions**: 
  - Used to transport Glabbagool safely through the Underdark.
  - Accidentally opened by Grygum in ch53, exposing Glabbagool to sunlight and triggering transformation.
  - In ch54, repurposed to contain a shrunken spy alongside Glabbagool.
  - In ch56, Glabbagool was confined inside for extended periods, though he repeatedly peered out, indicating ongoing autonomy-seeking behavior.
  - In ch58, Glabbagool reminded the party that the drow inside still required monitoring, confirming their continued presence.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the drow are alive or merely incapacitated; the facts state they “require monitoring” but do not confirm their current biological state.
- The exact number of drow contained is unspecified.
- It is ambiguous whether Glabbagool is voluntarily confined or physically restrained; the bag is described as “inadequate as a long-term prison,” but no restraints or locks are mentioned.
- The origin or full contents of the bag prior to carrying Glabbagool remain unexplained — what, if anything, was inside before ch53?
