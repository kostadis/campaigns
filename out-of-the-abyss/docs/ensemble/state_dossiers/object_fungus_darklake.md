### fungus (Darklake)

- **What it is:** A bioluminescent fungal growth found in the Darklake, known by the name "Tongue of Madness."
- **Current location:** Darklake (as contextually implied by all references).
- **Current condition:** Intact and observable; physically described as resembling a giant’s tongue.
- **Notable properties:** 
  - Consuming it is believed to establish a connection with the gods.
  - Described by Sarith as the "tastiest thing in the world."
- **Significance:** Revered or feared for its divine-connective properties; its appearance and effects suggest it is a sentient or spiritually potent entity.

None.
