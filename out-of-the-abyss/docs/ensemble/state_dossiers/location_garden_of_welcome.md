### Garden of Welcome

**Current status:** Active, corrupted ritual site  
**Current location:** Eastern plateau of Neverlight Grove  
**Control/occupation:** Controlled by the Circle of Masters; epicenter of the Great Seeder’s wedding ritual  
**Current state:** A necrotic fungal field where dozens of underdark creatures (including Drow and myconids) are embedded head-first into the earth, their bodies fused with pulsing mold and fungi. The ground continuously emits spores, and corpses explode upon death. The planted heads emit a cacophony of croaking, groaning, and hissing, forming a discordant fungal choir.  
**Notable items/phenomena:**  
- Pulsing fungal spore network  
- Exploding corpses upon death  
- Glowing mushrooms as the only light sources  
- Living, vocalized heads embedded in the soil, singing in unison  
**Current assignment/role:** Ritual site for the Great Seeder’s wedding; used to transform captives into fungal vessels for the demonic spore network.  
**Defining recent action:** The Circle of Masters completed the wedding ritual, activating the Garden as the epicenter of their fungal dominion, with dozens of living heads now contributing to the fungal choir.

## Uncertainty
- The exact identity of the "Great Seeder" is not specified, only referenced as the subject of the wedding.
- It is unclear whether the Circle of Masters is physically present in the Garden or directing it remotely.
- The total number of embedded creatures is described as "dozens" but no exact count is given.
- It is unknown whether any of the planted heads retain consciousness or are fully transformed.
- The fate of Phylo in relation to the Garden’s current state is not confirmed in ch32, despite earlier association.
