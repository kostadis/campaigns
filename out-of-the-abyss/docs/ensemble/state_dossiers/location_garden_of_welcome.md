### Garden of Welcome

**Current status:** Active ritual site, corrupted and necrotic  
**Current location:** Eastern plateau of Neverlight Grove  
**Allegiance/faction:** Controlled by the Circle of Masters, serving the Great Seeder  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (location, not entity)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Ritual epicenter for the Great Seeder’s wedding; processes and transforms captured myconids, Drow, and other underdark creatures into fungal hosts. Directly serves the Circle of Masters.  
**Defining recent actions:** Hosted the wedding ceremony of the Great Seeder; dozens of captured creatures (including myconids and Drow) have been planted alive into the earth, their bodies fused with fungal growth. Corpses explode upon death; living captives emit moans and croaks, contributing to a discordant fungal choir. The ground pulses with spores.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact number of planted heads is not quantified beyond "dozens" and "remaining heads," leaving total count ambiguous.  
- It is unclear whether all planted creatures are still alive or if some have already exploded; the text implies both ongoing croaking and explosive death, but does not specify current state per individual.  
- The identity or current presence of the Circle of Masters members at the site is not confirmed — only that they control it.  
- Whether Phylo is physically present or merely the source of the corruption is not stated in ch32.  
- The origin of the "wondrous and glorious surprise" beyond the wedding ritual is not elaborated — its full purpose remains unexplained.
