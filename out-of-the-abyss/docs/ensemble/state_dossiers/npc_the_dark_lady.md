### The Dark Lady

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unspecified, but manifesting in a realm where mycelium warps reality (last observed in ch27)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demon Queen of Fungi, the Lady of Rot and Decay — a singular demonic power, no higher authority named  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Crown: A halo of glowing spores  
- Eyes: Twin lanterns of hollow radiance  
- Ability: Wields dominion over mycelium, spores, and decay; bends air and light with her presence  
- Voice: Haunting melody that promises transcendence through corruption; can induce visions of entwinement with the mycelium  
- Spell-like effect: Promotes “eternal entwinement” — a metaphysical corruption that dissolves selfhood into the endless mycelial network  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Role: Sovereign of fungal decay and transcendence through corruption; lures mortals to surrender their identity to the mycelial dream  
- Key relationship: Engages directly with mortal wanderers who encounter her domain; no companions, servants, or subordinates named  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Manifested before mortal travelers, offering them transcendence through entwinement with the mycelium, framing decay as a sacred, eternal dance.  
- Spoke in poetic, hypnotic cadence, framing her existence as the “pulse beneath the stone” and “breath within the spore.”  
- Implied prior attempts to claim souls, suggesting a pattern of seduction and corruption.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks to dissolve individuality into the collective mycelial consciousness, offering eternal existence at the cost of self.  
- Views decay not as destruction, but as sacred transformation — a “song” to be understood, not feared.  
- Her “dream” is a metaphysical realm where life and decay are inseparable; she is its heart and its voice.
