### Great Readers

- **Current status**: Active scholarly order within Candlekeep.  
- **Current location**: Based in Candlekeep.  
- **Allegiance/faction**: Elite scholarly order operating internally within Candlekeep; not externally aligned but subject to internal power dynamics.  
- **Current members (confirmed)**: Teles Ahvoste, Janussi, Sylvira (implied still active). Alkrist is a newly admitted but poorly understood member.  
- **Current assignment / role**: Engaged in scholarly pursuits; suspected of wielding influence over institutional narratives and academic rivalries.  
- **Defining recent actions**:  
  - Involved in a power struggle, with Teles Ahvoste making advances toward Irony.  
  - Alkrist has recently joined, with little known about their role or motives.  
  - Sylvira suggests the group may have been used as a tool to discredit her, implying internal rivalries and institutional manipulation.  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets**:  
  - The group is suspected of involvement in a murder; Grygum believes one of them is the killer and that Bookwyrm is protecting them.  
  - Internal grudges and rivalries are common; the group may function as a vehicle for political maneuvering under scholarly pretenses.

## Uncertainty

- Ambiguity: Sylvira’s implication that the group “was used to discredit her” suggests past manipulation, but it is unclear whether she is still a member or an outsider now.  
- Unclear attribution: It is unknown whether Teles Ahvoste’s advances toward Irony are romantic, political, or coercive — and whether Irony is a member, associate, or external party.  
- Unclear status: Alkrist’s role, allegiance, or influence within the group remains entirely undefined.  
- Unclear scope: Whether “Bookwyrm is protecting them” refers to protection from external investigation, internal purge, or something else is unspecified.
