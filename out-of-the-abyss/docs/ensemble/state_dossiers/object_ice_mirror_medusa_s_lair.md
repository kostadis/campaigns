### Ice Mirror (Medusa's Lair)

An ice mirror, crafted by Daz using magic to shape and freeze water, combined with gems and ice, was constructed to reflect Medusa’s petrifying gaze. When activated, it caused Medusa to recoil in horror at her own reflection, successfully resisting her own petrification effect. The mirror remains in Medusa’s lair, positioned to reflect her gaze. It is currently intact and functional.
