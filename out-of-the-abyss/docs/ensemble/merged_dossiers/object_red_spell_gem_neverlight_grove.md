### Red Spell Gem (Neverlight Grove)

- **What it is:** A rare magical gem containing a powerful sanctification spell.  
- **Current condition:** Intact and functional; remains the key component for the cleansing ritual.  
- **Current holder/location:** Not specified; its physical location is unrecorded in the provided facts.  
- **Significance:** Must be placed into a menhir at the Temple of the Steadfast Stone to:  
  - Cleansing the temple,  
  - Awaken its three guardians,  
  - Summon Earth Elementals,  
  - Provoke the hostile response of Ogrémoch's Bane and its servants.  
- **Notable properties:** Its activation triggers dangerous supernatural retaliation, making its use a high-stakes ritual.
