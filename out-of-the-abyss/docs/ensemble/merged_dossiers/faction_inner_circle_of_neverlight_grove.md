### Inner Circle of Neverlight Grove

The Inner Circle is the sacred heart of the Neverlight Grove’s fungal hierarchy and the seat of Phylo’s authority, composed exclusively of the leaders of each myconid circle. It is the site where Phylo’s betrayal was revealed, culminating in a violent confrontation between Phylo and Basidia. The space is psychically charged, thrumming with the resonance of fungal unity and madness, and triggered a sentient response from the Zurkhwoods during the conflict. The party escaped from this location during the chaos.

## Uncertainty
- The current status of Phylo and Basidia after the confrontation is not stated — whether they are alive, dead, or displaced is unknown.
- It is unclear whether the Circle of Masters still exists as a distinct entity within or alongside the Inner Circle after the events of ch33.
- The physical structure or current occupancy of the Inner Circle post-escape is not described — whether it is abandoned, guarded, or altered remains unspecified.
