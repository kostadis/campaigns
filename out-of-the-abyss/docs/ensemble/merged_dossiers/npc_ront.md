### Ront

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Remains consumed by a vrock in the underground caverns (headless corpse left behind near Daz)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly under Drow command; now deceased, no allegiance  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (body destroyed; no items mentioned in possession at time of death)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Formerly a prisoner laborer under Drow supervision; last seen acting independently in combat. Last known relationship: none alive (all companions escaped or deceased).  
**Defining recent actions:** Attacked and wounded an injured vrock, believing it to be an opportunity for redemption; was instantly killed by the vrock, which severed and swallowed his head, leaving his body behind.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Consumed by guilt over past cowardice; believed killing the vrock would prove his worth and redeem himself.

## Uncertainty
- The phrase “Ront and Prince Derendil, the deluded Quaggoth” in ch01 ambiguously links Ront and Derendil — it is unclear whether “Prince Derendil” is a title Ront holds, a separate individual, or a misattribution. However, later chapters refer to Ront alone as the actor, and no other Quaggoth is mentioned, so Ront is treated as the deluded prince.  
- No other possessions, weapons, or gear are ever described as carried by Ront — it is unknown what weapon he used to kill the Drow guard or strike the vrock.  
- The exact sequence of Ront’s death (whether he was killed before or simultaneously with the head being snapped off) is described in one sentence with multiple actions — but the outcome (death and decapitation) is consistent.
