### Chamber of Lost Lore

- **Current state:** Active and secured under emergency protocol.
- **Current location:** Part of the High Tower Library complex.
- **Current control/occupation:** Under emergency guard detail (doubled security); accessed only for official inspection or authorized research.
- **Recent notable events:** Kalan conducted private research here following his visit to Sylvira Savikas. On the morning of the murder, he performed a formal inspection at approximately 8:00 AM, after which emergency guards were posted and security was heightened.
- **Significance:** Site of Kalan’s final known research activity prior to his death; central to the murder investigation timeline.

- The exact physical condition of the Chamber (e.g., whether it was ransacked, sealed, or left undisturbed since the murder) is not specified.
