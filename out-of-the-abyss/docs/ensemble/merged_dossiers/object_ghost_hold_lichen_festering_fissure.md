### Ghost Hold Lichen (Festering Fissure)

- **What it is:** A rare alchemical ingredient used to craft anti-acid armor.  
- **Current location:** Harvested and in the party’s possession (last known after completion of harvesting in the Festering Fissure).  
- **Current condition:** Harvested successfully; no indication of damage or contamination during collection, though it was previously noted to be contaminated by Zuggtmoy’s influence — this contamination status is unconfirmed post-harvest.  
- **Significance:** Requires extreme care to harvest — must avoid rupturing poisonous sacs growing beside it, and demands a warding prayer to repel demonic spirits during collection.  
- **Origin:** Grows from the corpse of a giant within the Festering Fissure.  
- **Current holder:** The party (last confirmed during successful harvesting; specific individual not named).  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between “found... in the Garden of Stone” and “harvested from the corpse of a giant in the Festering Fissure” — location of growth is inconsistently attributed.  
- Contamination by Zuggtmoy’s influence was noted before harvesting; it is unclear whether this contamination persists in the harvested specimen.  
- No explicit confirmation of who currently holds the harvested lichen — only that the party finished harvesting it.
