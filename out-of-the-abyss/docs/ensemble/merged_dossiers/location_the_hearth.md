### The Hearth

The Hearth is a tavern in Menzoberranzan, functioning as a hub for informal intelligence-gathering and rumor circulation. It is a noisy, firelit venue saturated with the scent of alcohol and sweat, deliberately chosen by suspect Daral Yashenti as the site of his interrogation to assert control over the environment — forcing investigators to come to him rather than submitting to standard protocol. The setting contrasts sharply with the scholarly calm of the library, reinforcing its role as a place of raw, unregulated social power.
