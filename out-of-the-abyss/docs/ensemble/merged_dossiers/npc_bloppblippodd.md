### Bloppblippodd

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** At the profane altar in the Darklake, where the ritual was completed  
**Allegiance/faction:** Archpriest of Leemooggoogoon the Deep Father (cult leader)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None explicitly stated beyond ritual implements (ritual knife used in final act); no ongoing wounds noted as she is deceased  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Orchestrated the sacrificial ritual to summon Demogorgon; was the final actor in the ritual that killed Ploop and invoked the entity. Daughter of Ploopploopeen; formerly high priestess and leader of the Deep Father’s cult. Relationship with father was strained and contested. Sister of Gloog.  
**Defining recent actions:** Led the sacrifice procession, demanded the ritual knife, delivered the fatal blow to Ploop, and uttered the final incantation “Leemooggoogoon,” successfully summoning Demogorgon. Died immediately after completing the ritual.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction in identity: One fact refers to “Blopp” as the leader of the Sea Mother’s followers who was slain by the archpriest of the Deep Father — but the name “Blopp” is likely a shorthand or typo for “Bloppblippodd,” as no other character named Blopp is mentioned elsewhere. If “Blopp” is distinct, then the fatal blow and incantation are attributed to two different entities — but the context and naming pattern strongly suggest identity.  
- Ambiguity in cause of death: The fact states she “died moments after completing the ritual,” but does not specify whether this was due to the ritual’s backlash, the blow to Ploop, or another cause.  
- Unclear if “Ploop” is Ploopploopeen: The ritual victim is named “Ploop”; Ploopploopeen is her father. Are they the same? The text does not confirm. If not, then the identity of the sacrificed victim is ambiguous.  
- The term “false god” used by others in ch10 conflicts with the ritual’s success in ch11 — but since the ritual worked and she died after summoning Demogorgon, the *reality* of the deity’s power is now confirmed, even if her legitimacy as a cleric was questioned.
