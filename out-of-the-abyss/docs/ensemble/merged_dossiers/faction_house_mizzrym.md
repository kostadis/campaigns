### House Mizzrym

House Mizzrym is a drow noble house in Menzoberranzan, historically aligned with Zuggtmoy, the Demon Lord of Fungal Nightmare. Its legacy is defined by demonic pacts, fungal corruption, and the subversion of its high priestess, Ilvara Mizzrym — whose transformation and disappearance have triggered internal collapse. The house remains unaware of the full extent of Ilvara’s subversion, and its leadership is now in a state of urgent confusion, having dispatched sealed orders demanding her whereabouts and response.

House Mizzrym has actively targeted Daz for elimination, identified as one of two primary threats to him in a mercenary contract (the other being House T’sarran). The house deployed a contingent of soldiers to the chamber where Ilvara conducted her rituals; their corpses, bearing gold, short swords, a long sword, and steel whistles, were found dead — their pay never collected.

The house’s political structure appears blind to theological decay, and its dependency on Ilvara’s role as high priestess has left it vulnerable to sudden operational collapse.

- The house’s current stance toward Zuggtmoy after Ilvara’s collapse is unreported — whether it still venerates the demon lord or is in denial remains ambiguous.
