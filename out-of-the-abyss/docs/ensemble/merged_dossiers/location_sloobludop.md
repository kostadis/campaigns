### Sloobludop

Sloobludop is a destroyed Kuo-toan village, now a charred ruin echoing with the screams of its slaughtered inhabitants. It was once situated between two concentric walls of nEttin g netting, a cave wall to the south, and the Darklake to the north. The village contained over a hundred mud-walled homes with ceilings made of unidentified dried vegetable matter, docks lining the Darklake (some with boats, some empty), and was dominated by two altars: one dedicated to the Sea Mother, marked by a humanoid female statue carved from wood with a giant albino crayfish head and claws, piled with shells, stones, mushrooms, and rotting fish; and one dedicated to Leemooggoogoon the Deep Father, marked by a manta ray hide stretched on poles with a pinned dead manta ray, two dead octopuses draped across it, and a blood-stained stone altar.

Sloobludop was the seat of the arch-cleric Ploopploopeen, a follower of the Sea Mother, and had long been a settlement of Kuo-toans living in harmonious service to the Sea Mother — though tensions existed between her cult and the Deep Father worshippers. It served as a waypoint between the Silken Paths and Gracklstugh, accessible via the Underdark web network.

It was destroyed by Demogorgon, who slaughtered its entire population. The screams of the dead Kuotoans still echo in the darkness of the ruins.
