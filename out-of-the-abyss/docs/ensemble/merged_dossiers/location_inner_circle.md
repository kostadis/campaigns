### Inner Circle

The Inner Circle is the sacred heart of the Myconid society and the central node of the Neverlight Grove’s fungal hierarchy. It is the location where Phylo and Basidia confronted each other, triggering sentient Zurkhwoods to stir and the air to thrum with psychic resonance of fungal unity and madness. It is the site of Phylo’s betrayal and the party’s escape. The Inner Circle consists of all circle leaders, distinct from the Circle of Masters, though both are part of Phylo’s new social order.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch31 statements: one says the Inner Circle and Circle of Masters are both circles of those closest to Phylo, while another implies the Circle of Masters is separate and composed of those closest to Phylo — unclear if they are overlapping, hierarchical, or mutually exclusive.  
- No current information on who physically occupies or controls the Inner Circle after Phylo’s betrayal; Phylo was present at the time of confrontation, but current status or location of Phylo post-confrontation is unconfirmed.  
- The exact physical form or structure of the Inner Circle (e.g., chamber, grove, network) is not described beyond its symbolic and functional role.

### Inner Circle

The Inner Circle is the elite tier of Phylo’s hierarchy, composed of the leaders of all other myconid circles and the sovereigns. They enforce Phylo’s doctrine, maintain the reanimation pipeline, and serve as the core of ideological control. Phylo has restricted their interactions exclusively to other circle leaders and sovereigns, severing traditional ties to the broader myconid community.

## Uncertainty
- Ambiguity in whether “Circle of Masters” is a subset of, synonymous with, or distinct from the Inner Circle — the quote suggests they are separate (“the Inner Circle of all of the circle leaders and the Circle of Masters is a circle of those who are closest to Phylo”), but no clarification is given on membership overlap or hierarchy between them.
