### Stroud

- **Current status:** Alive  
- **Current location:** Unknown (last referenced in context of philosophical and theological discourse, not tied to a physical location)  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Associated with the alliance formed after Protanther’s defection; no formal faction listed  
- **Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
- **Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
  - Revered as a philosopher and accidental icon of divine intervention; the figure behind “Stroud’s Gambit”  
  - Cited as the agent of a decisive, non-expert move that caused Protanther (ancient gold dragon) to defect to the alliance  
  - Linked to the inscription “The move is yours,” found on the Castle Ward statue in Waterdeep  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Rolled an unintended, non-strategic move in a chess game against Protanther — a move he could not have made intentionally, as he cannot play chess — which landed perfectly and definitively, shattering four centuries of dragon-level analysis and triggering Protanther’s defection  
  - This act became a theological and scholarly phenomenon, redefining understanding of divine intervention, constrained choice, and the limits of deterministic prediction

- Contradiction in attribution: One fact says Stroud “sat down across from Protanther” (ch53), another says he “rolled” a move (ch54); no clarification on whether these are simultaneous or sequential, nor whether Stroud was physically present during the move or if it was metaphorical.
