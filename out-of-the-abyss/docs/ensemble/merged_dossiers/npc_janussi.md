**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Body remains in Candlekeep, likely still in his chamber or the morgue (no evidence of removal)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Keeper of Tomes, Candlekeep (deceased)

- Wounds: Rib cage caved in; heart removed; broken nose with settled bruising; blackened left forefinger and lower lip (poison residue); chipped front tooth; signs of ingestion of Midnight Tears poison via book-lapping

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**

- Licked his finger while turning pages of the poisoned book, ingesting Midnight Tears  
- Was struck in the face (broken nose), returned to his desk, wrote final notes in midnight blue ink, then was murdered — heart removed and hidden in the lead-lined Milil chalice  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Sought to sell priceless poetry to fund a worthless tome on demonic planes  
- Conducted research on celestial and forbidden magical workings, annotated in Celestial, midnight blue, and purple ink — final notes vanished  
- Used a comedy book for both jokes and spell research, indicating unorthodox scholarly methods  
- Viewed himself as gatekeeper of knowledge, believing it was his privilege to decide which research deserved advancement — a stance that made him a target  
- His death was not random but a strategic elimination to erase dangerous insights and destabilize Candlekeep’s council  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Was Janussi poisoned *by* the book he licked (ch58, ch59, ch60) — or was he poisoned by food/liquid (ch58)? The facts state both possibilities, and the book-lapping is the only direct physical evidence.  
- Contradiction: Was Janussi the *intended* poison victim (ch59: “the poison taster” implies he was meant to die, but also “collateral”) — or was he a misdirect, with Sprig being the true target? The text says he was “the only mark” and “collateral,” which are mutually exclusive.  
- Attribution uncertainty: Was Daral Yashenti the one who gave him the poisoned book? (ch59 says “Daral had given him,” but no direct witness or evidence confirms this — only implication)  
- Ambiguity: Was the locket and key taken by the same person who killed him? Or by a separate thief? No clear attribution.  
- Chronology uncertainty: Did the assault (broken nose) occur before or after the poisoning? The bruising formed before death, and the poison was ingested during the meal — but exact sequence is unclear.  
- Ambiguity: Was Janussi’s research truly “third-rate” (Sylvira’s opinion) or was he a brilliant but reckless scholar whose insights were dangerous? The facts present both views.  
- Unclear: Who removed the heart? The text says it was hidden in the Milil chalice — but no actor is named.  
- Unclear: Was the Deadwinter gift string cut and retied *before* or *after* his death? The fact states it was, but no context is given.
