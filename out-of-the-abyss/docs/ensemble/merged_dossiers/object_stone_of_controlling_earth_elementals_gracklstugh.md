### Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals (Gracklstugh)

- **What it is:** A powerful magical artifact capable of commanding Earth Elementals.
- **Current holder/location:** Unclaimed; still held by Chief Dorbo as an offered reward. Not yet transferred to the party.
- **Current condition:** Intact and functional, as described.
- **Significance:** A reward offered by Chief Dorbo for cleansing the Rock Blight and dealing with Ogrémoch's Bane at the Temple of the Steadfast Stone. The party has expressed a philosophical preference for befriending Earth Elementals over controlling them, but this has not altered the stone’s status or availability.
