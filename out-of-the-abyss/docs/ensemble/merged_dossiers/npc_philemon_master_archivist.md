### Philemon (Master Archivist)

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unspecified, but actively engaged with Thorin and Dawnbringer  
**Allegiance/faction:** Master Archivist of the Archive of Sentient Artifacts (implied by title and role)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Therapist and counselor to Thorin and Dawnbringer, guiding their emotional reconnection.  
- Recognizes Dawnbringer as a sentient being with trauma, not a specimen.  
- Directly addresses Dawnbringer as “she,” treating her as an equal participant in therapy.  
- Prescribes and facilitates a two-stage therapeutic process: *light* (reconnecting with peaceful, non-combative memories of daylight) and *mourning* (processing trauma associated with violence and loss).  
- Reports to no named superior; operates with autonomous authority in therapeutic matters.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Shifted from academic classification of Dawnbringer to prioritizing her emotional healing.  
- Successfully facilitated a demonstrable improvement in the bond between Thorin and Dawnbringer.  
- Deliberately structured and guided a therapeutic session centered on memory, safety, and grief, addressing both Thorin and Dawnbringer as co-participants in healing.

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Motivated by genuine empathy and recognition of sentient artifacts’ inner lives.  
- Believes healing requires honoring both joy (light) and sorrow (mourning) — not suppressing either.  
- Secretly views Dawnbringer as a *friend*, not merely a case study.
