### The Shattered Spire

A tavern built on a broken stalagmite over Darklake, accessible only by a wool bridge. It serves as neutral ground where merchants, spies, and assassins meet — its isolation making it ideal for secrets. It is not a place of safety, but the eye of the storm: every conversation is a gamble, every gemstone a bribe, every silence a threat. Werz Saltbaron uses it as his base to reveal cracks in the Duergar system. It functions as a front or safehouse for Gray Ghost operations.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of Werz Saltbaron’s formal affiliation with Gray Ghosts is implied but not confirmed — “rumored meeting place” (ch16) conflicts with his active role in ch19, but no direct statement confirms operational control.
- Whether the tavern is owned by Werz, Gray Ghosts, or an independent operator is unspecified.
- The current status of the wool bridge (intact, guarded, or damaged) is not stated beyond its existence.
