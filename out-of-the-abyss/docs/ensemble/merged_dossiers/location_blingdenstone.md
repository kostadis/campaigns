### Blingdenstone

Blingdenstone is a subterranean deep gnome city in the Underdark, currently under gnome control and restored to stability after narrowly surviving a catastrophic ooze infestation led by the Pudding King and fueled by Juiblex’s influence. The city’s massive gates, constructed of steel, mithral, and adamantine, are guarded by deep gnome sentinels, and its internal structure includes Diggermattock Hall (administrative center), the Trader’s Grotto (commercial hub), and a reception hall with a cart. The city features extensive cavernous tunnels and staircases designed for gnome-sized physiology.

Following the defeat of the Pudding King and mediation by the Ember Vanguard, Blingdenstone transitioned from fractured factional conflict to a fragile but unified community. A peace accord was reached between miners, wererats, and spiritual leaders, ending internal strife. Economic plans were restructured: the use of Earth Elementals for mining was abandoned in favor of defense-focused elemental applications, earning the gratitude of the working class. The city has reopened for trade with the surface world.

Blingdenstone now serves as a center of a new alliance between gnomes, wererats, and miners. Legends of the Ember Vanguard’s deeds are spreading through the tunnels, and the city sent the party off with ceremonial fanfare: working-class gnomes chanted a new rallying cry, Burrow Warden ghosts saluted with spectral banners, and Chief Chipgrin gifted Daz the Voucher of the Varmint — a token recognized by wererats from Blingdenstone to Menzoberranzan.

The city is no longer under ooze threat, and its infrastructure has been stabilized. It remains a known exit route from the Underdark and a destination for those seeking alchemical services (e.g., restoring spell gems) or safe passage to the Overbrite.

### Blingdenstone

Blingdenstone is a deep gnome city in the Underdark, currently under gnome control after multiple historical shifts in power between gnomes and drow. It maintains formidable defenses, including a maze of trap-laden tunnels. The city is now facing severe threats from elemental and demonic forces: Zuggtmoy, Juiblex, the Pudding King, and the absence of Entémoch, alongside a serious ooze infestation. Its leadership was recently in disarray over conflicting priorities until the party’s intervention restored cohesion.

Blingdenstone commissioned the quest to hallow a temple and obtain Entémoch’s boon to counter these threats. In a major policy shift following the party’s moral arguments, the city has banned the use of summoned Earth Elementals for mining or construction, permitting their use only in defense and warfare. This reversal, encapsulated in the slogan “We serve the stone, not enslave it,” has disrupted the city’s economic model but earned widespread gratitude from the working gnome population.
