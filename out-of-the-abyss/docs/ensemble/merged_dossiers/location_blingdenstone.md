### Blingdenstone

Blingdenstone is a subterranean deep gnome city beneath the Underdark, currently under gnome control and functioning as a unified, thriving community following the defeat of the Pudding King and the resolution of a devastating ooze infestation tied to Juiblex. The city has transitioned from near-collapse to a fragile but stable peace, mediated by the Ember Vanguard, and has reopened for surface trade. Its infrastructure includes a massive steel, mithral, and adamantine gate, a long gnome-sized staircase approach, a reception hall with a cart, the bustling Trader’s Grotto, and Diggermattock Hall — the administrative center of Clan Diggermattock.

The city is no longer plagued by oozes; the Pudding King’s biomass conversion scheme was thwarted, and its leadership abandoned plans to exploit Earth Elementals for mining, instead redirecting them toward defense — earning the gratitude of the working class. Political disputes between miners, wererats, and spiritual factions have been resolved through negotiated accord. Blingdenstone now serves as a center of a new alliance between gnomes, wererats, and miners, and is recognized as a safe haven and trade hub.

The city is known for its unusual norms: no spiders, coexistence of elves and drow, and the keeping of dogs as pets — a contrast to typical Underdark culture. It remains a known exit route to the Overbrite and a destination for those seeking alchemical services (e.g., restoring spell gems) and safe passage from the Underdark.

Legends of the Ember Vanguard’s deeds are spreading through its tunnels. The Voucher of the Varmint — a braided rat’s foot token — is now a recognized symbol of alliance and safe passage, extendable from Blingdenstone to Menzoberranzan.

- Whether the Burrow Warden ghosts are still actively present or merely commemorated in the city’s new culture is ambiguous.

### Blingdenstone

Blingdenstone is a deep gnome city in the Underdark, currently under gnome control after multiple historical shifts of power between gnomes and drow. It maintains formidable defenses, including a maze of trap-laden tunnels. The city is facing existential threats from elemental and demonic forces: Zuggtmoy, Juiblex, the Pudding King, and the absence of Entémoch, alongside a severe ooze infestation. Its leadership was recently in disarray over conflicting priorities until the party intervened.

In a major policy shift following the party’s moral intervention, Blingdenstone has banned the use of summoned Earth Elementals for mining or construction, enshrining the principle: *“We serve the stone, not enslave it.”* Elementals are now permitted only for defense and warfare. This reversal has disrupted the city’s economic model but earned widespread gratitude from the gnome workforce.

The city commissioned the quest to hallow a temple and obtain Entémoch’s boon — a mission now critical to its survival.

- Unclear if the “ooze problem” is resolved or still active; only its existence is confirmed, not its current severity.
- Unclear whether the “temporal distortions” mentioned in ch43 are ongoing or connected to Zuggtmoy’s influence.
- The phrase “changed hands between gnomes and drow multiple times” in ch35 implies possible drow presence or influence — but no current drow affiliation, leadership, or occupation is confirmed in later chapters.
