### Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals (Neverlight Grove)

- **What it is:** A powerful magical artifact capable of commanding Earth Elementals.
- **Current holder/location:** Unclaimed; still held by Chief Dorbo as an unawarded reward in Neverlight Grove.
- **Current condition:** Intact and functional, as described.
- **Significance:** A reward offered by Chief Dorbo for cleansing the Rock Blight and defeating Ogrémoch's Bane in the Temple of the Steadfast Stone. The party has expressed interest in befriending Earth Elementals rather than controlling them, but this does not alter the artifact’s nature or current status.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the Stone has been physically transferred to the party’s possession — no fact confirms it was handed over after the quest’s completion. All references treat it as an offered reward, not a claimed one.
