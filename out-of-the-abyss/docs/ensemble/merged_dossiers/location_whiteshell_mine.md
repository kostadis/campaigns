### Whiteshell Mine

The Whiteshell Mine is the largest salt mine in the Underdark. A tunnel within it has become impassable due to blinding crystallite walls, absolute silence, and temporal stagnation—shadows cast by torches appear frozen and painted on the walls, as if time itself has stopped. This metaphysical blockage was previously unbreakable without a Shadow Monk to safely navigate the temporal barrier. However, in Chapter 44, the party successfully breached the barrier using specialized explosives and a shadow-space redirection maneuver executed by Zalthir, with Grygum’s blessing. Beyond the breached wall lies a large cavern containing a summoning circle inscribed with giant runes, guarded by two basilisks that move with mechanical precision. The party has activated the ritual, requiring three sacrifices: wealth, a weapon, and blood. Thorin has already sacrificed a dart as the weapon offering; the circle has transformed, the air has grown hot, and one basilisk has become frantic as the ritual progresses. Two sacrifices remain to be completed.
