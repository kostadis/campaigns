### Cult of Demogorgon

**Current status:** Active and entrenched in the Underdark.  
**Current location:** Primarily operating among Derro communities, with ritual sites tied to Stone Giant grafting operations near Gracklstugh.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Loyal to Demogorgon; no longer allied with the Council of Savants or Gray Ghosts (per ch25’s singular leadership structure).  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Possesses forbidden ritual texts used to perform head-grafting and fear magic.  
- Uses chanted incantations and ritual precision as core magical tools.  
- Derro acolytes serve as ritual conduits; no known individual items specified beyond ritual implements.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Led by **Narrak**, who functions as an authoritarian choir director overseeing the ritual hierarchy.  
- Actively grafting second heads onto Stone Giants to create monstrous avatars for Demogorgon.  
- Goal: Destroy Gracklstugh by empowering Demogorgon through these abominations.  
- Adherents include **Skiit** and **Ulnara**, who uphold the cult’s ideology as divine elevation.  
- Derro followers view the Duergar as enemies to be destroyed.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Conducting ritual grafting ceremonies on Stone Giants using Derro acolytes.  
- Maintaining morale among Derro through dogma that frames madness and atrocities as divine acts.  
- Replacing Plinki’s earlier goal (dragon egg transformation) with a larger-scale, hierarchical ritual program under Narrak.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The cult’s true purpose is not merely chaos but the creation of divine avatars to directly empower Demogorgon.  
- Plinki’s influence is obsolete; Narrak now commands the cult’s operations.  
- The cult is not a smokescreen — it is real, deeply structured, and actively escalating its threat.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch18 (Plinki as leader) and ch25 (Narrak as leader): Later chapter (ch25) overrides earlier, but the transition from Plinki to Narrak is unexplained.  
- ch20 claims the cult is a "misdirected threat" and "fabrication" used by assassins — contradicted by ch24 and ch25, which confirm its real, active, hierarchical structure. Later chapters override this.  
- Unclear if the egg from ch18 is still relevant; no mention of it after ch18, and ch25 introduces entirely new goals.  
- Unclear whether the assassins referenced in ch20 are still operating under the cult’s name or if the cult has fully separated from them.  
- "Pliinki" (ch24) may be a misspelling of "Plinki" — assumed to be the same person, but not confirmed.
