### Kalan Strongbranch's Key (Candlekeep)

- **Current location:** In transit; not currently possessed by any known individual.  
- **Current condition:** Physical key intact; not enchanted.  
- **Notable property:** Bound to a summoning spell via a separate, now-missing enchanted key stored in the sapphire.  
- **Significance:** Can be summoned by the sapphire’s magic only when unpossessed — proven when retrieved from Kalan’s corpse after death rendered it unclaimed.  
- **Recent history:** Removed from Kalan Strongbranch’s neck after his death; stolen and passed to Daz, who no longer holds it.  

## Uncertainty
- The current possessor of the key is unknown — it was last known to be with Daz, but its status since then is unconfirmed.  
- The location of the enchanted key stored in the sapphire is unspecified; its current state and accessibility are unclear.  
- It is ambiguous whether “Strongbranch handed his off rather than wear it” refers to a prior act of transfer before death or is a misstatement — the prior quote says the key was removed from his corpse, implying it was still on him at death.
