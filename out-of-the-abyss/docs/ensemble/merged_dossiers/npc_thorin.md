### Thorin

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Surface, near Candlekeep or en route south (last seen preparing to use a reward to obtain a magical book to prove Bookwyrm’s guilt; last physical setting: Deneir’s Sanctum, following ambush of helmed horrors)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Independent; self-identified as “The Ember Vanguard” (coined the name); no longer serving any faction, though formerly entangled with Stone Guards, Duergar authorities, and Zhentarim-aligned pragmatism  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- **Dawnbringer** — sentient, radiant sunblade; referred to as “she,” with whom Thorin has a deep, negotiated bond; used for combat, healing, and philosophical dialogue; recently carried in a Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard as a diplomatic tool  
- **Recovered tooth** — taken as a trophy by Ilvara at Velkynvelve; reclaimed after her defeat; held as a sentimental object  
- **Necromantic pearl** — recovered from the heart of Ilvara’s corrupted fungal ritual site; considered valuable and dangerous; kept in pocket  
- **Diggermattock Miners’ patch** — magically mended onto his gear, earned for identifying the psychic center in the Hall of Melting Mirrors  
- **Light crossbow** — used temporarily in combat; not his preferred weapon  
- **Darts** — primary ranged weapon; he has acquired a Sharpshooter’s instinct for thrown weapons  
- **Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard** — used to negotiate with Dawnbringer; a tool of diplomacy and control  

**Current Spells / Feats / Abilities:**  
- **Hill Strike** — battle technique learned from a hill giant battle master  
- **Sharpshooter’s instinct** — acquired proficiency with thrown weapons (javelins, darts); prefers reach and positioning  
- **Tremor Sense** — feat chosen for Underdark travel; now considered useless on surface terrain; source of frustration  
- **Healing ability** — demonstrated self-healing during combat (ch26)  
- **Combat pragmatism** — excels in direct melee, battlefield control, opportunity attacks, and exploiting terrain  
- **Contract negotiation** — trained by giants; understands legal language, seals, and enforceability  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **De facto leader** of the Ember Vanguard — orchestrates strategy, demands clarity, and pushes for tangible outcomes  
- **Primary wielder and companion of Dawnbringer** — treats the blade as a sentient friend, not a weapon; seeks to restore her connection to light; considers her gender “she”  
- **Trusted by group** — despite his blunt, chaotic style, he is relied upon for physical strength, tactical aggression, and moral grounding  
- **Last seen with:** Zalthir, Grygum, Daz, Glabbagool, Eldeth (last interaction: Eldeth promised clan embrace, but Thorin accepted her departure)  
- **Key relationship:** With **Kalan** — he chose to hand over the Candlekeep case to him to preserve his dignity after being fired; sees this as terrain-based justice  
- **Relationship with Dawnbringer:** Central to his identity; they have developed a ritual of negotiation (“Do I need to show you the scabbard again?”); he believes she needs to remember daylight and grieve  

**Defining Recent Actions (Collapsed):**  
- Led the ambush on helmed horrors in Deneir’s Sanctum, using Dawnbringer to land a “sap” effect and stabilize the party  
- Orchestrated the plan to expose Bookwyrm by using the investigation reward to obtain a magical book that retroactively proves her guilt — a calculated bureaucratic grift  
- Reclaimed his stolen tooth and the necromantic pearl from Ilvara’s ritual site, marking his transition from captive to agent of agency  
- Declined to kill Asha Vandree, recognizing her as a Lolth cleric requiring lethal neutralization — later corrected by Drow mercenaries  
- Chose not to intervene as Jorlan confronted Ilvara, prioritizing emotional distance over tactical control  
- Destroyed the Heart Fungus in one round with Dawnbringer, accepting the moral weight of the act — “I felt a little bad, in the way you feel bad about something you’d do exactly the same way again”  
- Negotiated the group’s escape from Gracklstugh by advocating to ride Themberchaud — a recurring, absurdly pragmatic solution  
- Advocated for radical transparency, but chooses silence when speech adds nothing to the next ten seconds  
- Accepted his identity as a dwarf raised by hill giants, seeking integration with dwarven roots — now desires to be “an integrated person”  

**Revealed Motivations or Secrets:**  
- **Core drive:** To walk up to the problem and hit it with Dawnbringer until it stops — a philosophy forged in the Underdark and hardened by trauma  
- **Hidden trauma:** Survived the War of the Giants when the ordning fell apart; carries the lessons of giants — direct, brutal, unyielding — even as he rejects their moral ambiguity  
- **Moral evolution:** From hopeful adventurer to hardened pragmatist who understands the Underdark doesn’t care who you want to be; now embraces Zhentarim Tactics — “willing to do what others won’t” — as survival, not ideology  
- **Secret:** His tooth was taken as a symbol of ownership by Ilvara; reclaiming it was a symbolic act of reclaiming self  
- **Secret:** He has crossed a threshold he didn’t notice — “I was bigger somehow. More there.” — a quiet transformation from survivor to force of will  
- **Secret belief:** The only thing that doesn’t lie is terrain — the ground under your feet, the stone, the physics of the world — a lesson from giants and the Underdark
