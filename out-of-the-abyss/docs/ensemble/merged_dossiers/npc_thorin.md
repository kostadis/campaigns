### Thorin

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Likely within or near Candlekeep, having recently concluded an investigation into the murder of Janussi and the exposure of Bookwyrm’s guilt. Last seen orchestrating a plan to use the investigation’s reward to obtain a magical book proving Bookwyrm’s culpability.  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Independent; self-identified as “my faction.” Formerly a core member of The Ember Vanguard, now operates as a free agent with no formal ties.  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- **Dawnbringer** — a sentient, radiant sunblade; now consistently referred to with female pronouns (“she”). Thorin treats it as an extension of his identity and has developed a ritualized relationship with it, including negotiation, scabbard maintenance (Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard), and emotional attunement.  
- **Diggermattock Miners’ patch** — magically mended onto his gear, awarded for identifying the psychic center of the Hall of Melting Mirrors.  
- **Reclaimed tooth** — taken from Ilvara’s confiscated belongings, held as a sentimental artifact.  
- **Crossbow and javelins** — acquired for ranged utility; demonstrates Sharpshooter-level proficiency.  
- **Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard** — custom device for managing Dawnbringer’s objections to darkness.  
- **Pocket Drow** — a nickname for the shrunken drow spy, kept in a ventilated bag of holding.  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- Acting as a pragmatic investigator and tactical leader, focused on leveraging institutional systems (Candlekeep’s reward structure) to achieve justice through indirect means.  
- Has developed a deep, non-verbal bond with Dawnbringer, whom he consults regularly and defends as a person.  
- Maintains a working relationship with Glabbagool, whom he invited into the group as a companion and knowledge-source.  
- Respected by the group for his blunt honesty, combat effectiveness, and moral clarity; Zalthir defers to him on strategic and ethical decisions.  
- Recently proposed handing the solved case to Kalan to “let him save face,” in exchange for the group receiving their promised reward — a move of calculated mercy and institutional pragmatism.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Led the investigation into Janussi’s murder, identifying the poisoned book, the Milil chalice’s hidden cleaver, and the role of the shapeshifter.  
- Discovered the red herring coin in the safe, interpreting it as a sign of the killer’s confidence.  
- Deduced the murder required two actors due to the physical impossibility of stealing a locket from a living man.  
- Orchestrated the ambush against helmed horrors, using Dawnbringer’s sap ability and tactical positioning to disable them.  
- Proposed using the investigation’s reward to obtain a magical book that retroactively proves Bookwyrm’s guilt — a plan he is now building “the stage” for, treating the resolution as theatrical drama.  
- Declined to extort Daral, instead advocating for his release: “I don’t think that would be nice.”  
- Accepted that Dawnbringer is female, and fondly acknowledges her enjoyment of being called “beautiful and worthy of study.”  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Seeks integration of his dual heritage: raised by giants, now actively pursuing his dwarven roots.  
- Believes in radical transparency and direct action, rejecting factional subterfuge.  
- Views the Underdark’s truth as terrain — “the one thing that didn’t lie.”  
- Operates on a code of blunt pragmatism: “I’m a dwarf with a glowing sword in a sprawling cavern fight and at some point you stop pretending you’ve got angles on all of it and just pick a direction and go.”  
- Secretly believes therapy is a process, not a product — and is seeking a therapist, though he won’t admit it outright.  
- Holds a quiet, enduring grief over Buppido’s death: “He was a crazy one, but he was our crazy one.”  
- Wants to be remembered not as a pawn of factions, but as someone who chose his own path: “I want to be my faction.”
