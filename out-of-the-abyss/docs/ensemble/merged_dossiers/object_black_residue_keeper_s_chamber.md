### Black Residue (Keeper's Chamber)

- **What it is:** A black, toxic substance found on Janussi’s fingers and mouth, definitively not blue ink.
- **Current location:** On the remains of Janussi (last observed adhering to his fingers and mouth).
- **Current condition:** Present as a physical residue, chemically distinct from the blue ink used in the book’s annotations.
- **Significance:** Associated with poison or a toxic chemical agent; potentially the key to identifying the murder weapon.

## Uncertainty
- The exact chemical or magical composition of the residue is unknown.
- Whether the residue is still present on Janussi’s remains after post-mortem handling is not stated.
- The source or origin of the residue (e.g., weapon, substance ingested, environmental contaminant) is not identified.
