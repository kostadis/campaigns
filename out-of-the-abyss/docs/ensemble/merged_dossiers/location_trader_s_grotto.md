### Trader's Grotto

Trader's Grotto is a Gnomish defensive position in Blingdenstone, formerly the city’s bustling central market. It is built among petrified fungi turned to stone by a medusa’s spell centuries ago. The area was the site of an earlier battle against gelatinous cubes, leaving rubble and lingering damage. It was later breached by a massive, uncontrolled horde of oozes, jellies, and black puddings no longer bound by the Pudding King’s will, making it the focal point of the ooze crisis. A large Gnomish army gathered at the breached wall to hold the line. The breach was sealed after Jimjar’s bone die pacified the horde through Glabbagool. The area is now littered with residual ooze traces and patches of accelerated fungal growth.
