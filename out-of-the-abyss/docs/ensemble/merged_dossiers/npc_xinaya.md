### Xinaya

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Corpse in the Neverlight Grove (last seen where party fled)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly agent of Ilvara (Menzoberranzan); later corrupted into fungal slave of the Great Seeder and the Lady of Decay  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None (all items collected by Zalthir post-death)  
**Spells, feats, wounds:** None (body fully consumed by fungal transformation; no active abilities)  
**Current assignment / role:** None (deceased)  
**Key relationships:** Formerly sent by Ilvara to capture the party; corrupted by Phylo’s spores; last spoke to the party before death  
**Defining recent actions:** Begged the party to kill her after being fully transformed by fungal infection; identified the Great Seeder and the Lady of Decay as present in the Underdark; commanded the party to warn Menzoberranzan of the Demon Queen of the Fungi; died after consenting to death at Thorin’s request.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of her transformation (whether fully fungal entity or still partially Drow) is described inconsistently as “the thing that was once a drow” and “female Drow” — but since she is now a corpse, this distinction is irrelevant to current state.  
- Whether she retained any agency in her final moments beyond begging for death is ambiguous — her final speech may have been coerced by the fungal influence.  
- The specific items collected by Zalthir from her corpse are not listed — only that he took “everything.”
