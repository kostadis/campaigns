### Cult of the Deep Father

- **Current location**: Operating alongside the Sea Mother’s cult, though exact base is unconfirmed.  
- **Allegiance/faction**: Independent but allied with the Sea Mother’s cult; operates under the authority of an Archpriest.  
- **Current possessions / notable items**: None specified beyond ritual significance of sacrifices.
