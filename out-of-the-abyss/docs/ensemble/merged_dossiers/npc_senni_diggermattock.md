### Senni Diggermattock

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone  
**Allegiance/faction:** Blingdenstone leadership (labor groups), member of the Gold Whisker clan  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Leading voice for Blingdenstone’s labor groups; key decision-maker in clan governance; instrumental in policy shifts regarding summoned labor and inter-clan alliances. Acts as a bridge between mining and non-mining populations.  
**Key relationships:** Wife of Chief Dorbo Diggermattock; ally of the Gold Whisker clan; supporter of the adventurers; previously clashed with Dorbo over ooze response, now collaborates with him on compromise.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Seized control of a clan council meeting and declared “Gnome Labor, not Summoned Labor!”, successfully shifting Blingdenstone’s policy against enslaving Earth Elementals.  
- Advocated for and facilitated inclusion of the Gold Whisker clan in clan-wide decisions, including the grand moot.  
- Supported the adventurers’ plan to confront the Pudding King and helped secure Dorbo’s agreement to it.  
- Expressed deep concern for civilian safety, warning that failure to act could lead to Blingdenstone’s overrun.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Deeply committed to the welfare of Blingdenstone’s civilian population and the integrity of gnome labor.  
- Believes in unity between the mining and non-mining populations and sees the Gold Whisker clan as essential allies against ooze threats.  
- Her advocacy for the Gold Whisker clan and her marriage to Dorbo were pivotal in overcoming his resistance and achieving compromise.
