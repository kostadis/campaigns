### Missing Red Dragon Egg

- **Current status**: Physically present, in the possession of the party.
- **Current location**: Carried by the party (last confirmed in ch28).
- **Nature**: A mysterious, occult-marked magical artifact of unknown origin, originally recovered from a ritual.
- **Current possessions / notable items**: Bears strange occult markings; radiates intense psychic anxiety in proximity; induces panic in derro and fear in duergar artisans.
- **Current condition**: Undergoing transformation — no longer in its original form; previously described as mutating into a two-headed wyrmling destined to serve Demogorgon (ch23). Its current state of mutation is unconfirmed but implied to be active.
- **Significance**: 
  - Feared by Duergar artisans.
  - Desired by the Gray Ghosts, who believe it was stolen from them and have issued a vendetta to recover it.
  - Previously sought by the Keepers of the Flame as insurance against Themberchaud and by the Red Dragon.
  - Triggered a psychic collapse among derro when removed from its location.
  - Was to be handed over to Gartokkar as part of an escape plan (ch27), though this plan is now overshadowed by the spore crisis.
- **Recent actions**: Recovered from a ritual by the party; transported and held by the party since ch28; previously seized by Daz the wizard (ch22), then lost to mutation (ch23), then recovered again.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch23 (“There is no egg.” — implying it mutated and ceased to exist as an egg) and ch28 (the egg is physically present and carried by the party). Is the current object the same entity, a transformed version, or a different artifact altogether?
- Unclear if the “egg” recovered in ch28 is the same one seized by Daz in ch22, or a different one (ch26 mentions an egg recovered from Narrak’s chest — was this the same object?).
- Attribution of ownership: Gray Ghosts claim it belongs to them (ch28), but it was delivered by them (ch23) — was it stolen from them, or did they lose it during the ritual?
- The nature of its transformation: Is it still an egg? Is it now a wyrmling? Is the mutation complete? The facts conflict on whether it “is” or “was” an egg.
- The egg’s connection to Themberchaud (ch16) and the Red Dragon (ch26) remains unexplained and unverified in later chapters.
