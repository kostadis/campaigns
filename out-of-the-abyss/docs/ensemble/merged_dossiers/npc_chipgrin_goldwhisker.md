### Chipgrin Goldwhisker

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone (upper boroughs, under wererat control)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of the Gold Whisker clan of wererat gnomes; allied with the party and supportive of Blingdenstone’s peace efforts  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Possesses the Voucher of the Varmint (gifted to Daz upon departure; not in his possession) — *no current personal items listed as held by him*  
**Current assignment / role:** Negotiated and secured wererat control of the upper boroughs of Blingdenstone; leads scouting operations and tactical support for allied forces; maintains vigil to suppress psychic static in the Hall of Melting Mirrors  
**Key relationships:**  
- Reports to no superior; operates as autonomous clan chief  
- Allies with Daz (personal respect established)  
- Formerly in dispute with Chief Dorbo and Senni Diggermattock; now engaged in territorial negotiation with Diggermattock  
- Respects Thorin’s insight; acknowledged his own oversight in not consulting him first  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Led wererat scouts in stealth operations ahead of allied forces in area twenty-two  
- Offered to sacrifice his pack by howling to drown out psychic slime effects, drawing oozes to themselves  
- Successfully negotiated wererat territorial control in the Blingdenstone Council meeting  
- Contributed to the disruption of Blingdenstone’s elemental labor policy by advocating for ethical treatment of allies  
- Maintained a chanting vigil to suppress psychic noise in the Hall of Melting Mirrors  
- Gifted Daz the Voucher of the Varmint as a token of lasting friendship  

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
- Deeply values autonomy and accountability; refused subordination to Chief Dorbo  
- Holds strong moral opposition to the exploitation of allies (“A people that chains its allies cannot stand long”)  
- Recognizes the Pudding King’s corruption as rooted in vengeance, pride, madness, and demonic influence — a perspective shaped by personal observation  
- Respects competence over status; was impressed by Thorin’s insight and acknowledged his own oversight
