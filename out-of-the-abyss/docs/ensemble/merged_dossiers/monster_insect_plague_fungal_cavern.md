### Insect Plague (Fungal Cavern)

**Current status:** Active  
**Current location:** Pervasive throughout the Fungal Cavern  
**Type:** Magical swarm (conjuration by Ilvara)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (environmental effect)  
**Current assignment / role:** Primary environmental hazard; persists as a lingering effect of Ilvara’s divine magic, inflicting damage and impairing movement on all creatures within its range, regardless of allegiance  
**Defining recent actions:** Continues to blanket the cavern in a dense, chittering swarm that bypasses rational thought and induces primal flight response. Its presence was mitigated in effect — not removed — by the casting of a Fireball, which silenced the Drow warriors’ irritation but did not disperse the swarm.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact current density or range of the swarm post-Fireball is unspecified — was it diminished, dispersed, or merely no longer a source of complaint?  
- Whether the Fireball destroyed part of the swarm or merely suppressed its perceived nuisance (e.g., by killing insects or stunning creatures) is unclear.  
- Attribution of the swarm’s persistence: While originally conjured by Ilvara, it is not confirmed whether her ongoing magic sustains it, or if it has become self-sustaining.
