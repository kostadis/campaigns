### Blade Bazaar

- **Type**: Location (marketplace)  
- **Current status**: Active and occupied  
- **Location**: Central marketplace of Gracklstugh  
- **Control/Occupation**: Operated by Duergar merchants and visiting traders; no external controller named  
- **Notable goods**: Weapons, magic items, illicit goods; sells almost everything available in Gracklstugh, including goods from visiting merchants  
- **Atmosphere**: Deafening din of people arguing, mainly in Dwarvish, nearly drowning out the hammering of the city’s forges; dense crowds that provide cover to evade pursuers  
- **Key anomaly**: Merchants exhibit signs of collective delusion — altering prices based on unspoken rules, denying prior conversations, and speaking to invisible siblings; Duergar merchants specifically behave strangely, offering inconsistent prices for identical items depending on the buyer  
- **Recent activity**: Daz and his group arrived at the Blade Bazaar immediately prior to proceeding to the Darklake Brewery  

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of the “collective delusion” is described but not explained — no cause (e.g., spell, artifact, plague) is named.  
- It is unclear whether the “invisible siblings” are hallucinations experienced by merchants only, or if others perceive them too.  
- The phrase “most abundant goods the Duergar offer” is referenced but not specified — weapons? Tools? Slaves? The content is implied but not confirmed.  
- While Daz arrived at the Blade Bazaar before going to the Darklake Brewery, it is unknown whether he or his group is still present there now.  
- No information confirms whether the delusional behavior is worsening, stable, or receding since the last observation.
