### Rapport Spores

- **Type:** Object (magical fungal communication spores)  
- **Current location:** Not specified; last observed in use by Zalthir  
- **Current condition:** Active and functional  
- **Current holder/primary user:** Zalthir  
- **Notable function:** Enable telepathic communication across distances; allow the user to perceive private thoughts of a bonded Myconid (specifically Grygum)  
- **Recent action:** Zalthir is actively using them to eavesdrop on Grygum’s private thoughts  

## Uncertainty
- The physical location of the spores is unknown.  
- It is unclear whether the spores are a physical object Zalthir carries, a symbiotic colony, or a persistent magical effect tied to Grygum.  
- No information exists on whether Grygum is aware of the eavesdropping or if the spores require mutual consent/bonding to function.  
- No other users or instances of the spores beyond Zalthir and Grygum are mentioned.
