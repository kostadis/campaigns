### Cairngorm Cavern

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current status:** Active, occupied, and controlled by Hgraam, the venerable stone giant.  
- **Location:** Beyond Themberchaud’s cave, within the Duergar district of Gracklstugh; accessible via a gigantic, well-polished tunnel in the eastern wall south of Laduguer’s Furrow.  
- **Physical state:** A vast, ancient cavern with polished rock surfaces, deeply resonant with earth magic. Serene, silent, and imbued with ancient memory. Contains colossal stone structures.  
- **Significance:**  
  - The origin point for journeys to Laduguer’s Furrow and the Derro slums.  
  - The entrance to the Deep King’s domain.  
  - The last known sanctuary of Stonespeaker Hgraam and the last living repository of the stone giant apprenticeship tradition.  
  - A place where truth is spoken slowly, and the stones themselves are said to "speak."  
- **Current occupants:** Hgraam resides here permanently, tending to the rocks and observing visitors with deliberate patience.  
- **Key relationships:**  
  - Hgraam is the sole known guardian and inhabitant.  
  - Connected to the Deep King’s domain; may hold secrets of his madness and the giants’ immunity.  
  - Dorhun (a stone giant) previously invited the party here, indicating prior knowledge of its importance.  
- **Recent actions:**  
  - Served as the final destination for Thorin and his party after traversing from Themberchaud’s cave through Gracklstugh.  
  - Hgraam remains seated on a rock, speaking slowly, refusing haste, and tending to the cavern’s stones.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Dorhun still has any active presence or authority in the cavern — he invited the party there in ch15, but ch17 shows only Hgraam present.  
- The nature of the "connection" to the Deep King’s domain is described but not explained — whether it is physical, magical, or symbolic is unclarified.  
- It is not stated whether the cavern is accessible to others besides Hgraam and those who find the tunnel — no indication of guards, wards, or restrictions beyond its isolation.  
- The exact relationship between Hgraam and the "legacy of Stonespeaker Hgraam" is ambiguous — is this the same Hgraam, or a successor? The text treats the name as both a title and a person, but does not resolve this.
