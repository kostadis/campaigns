### Zalthir

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Unknown (last seen in the Readers’ Chambers of Candlekeep, engaged in covert investigation)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Former member of the Ember Vanguard; currently operates as an independent agent under the nominal oversight of the First Reader, assigned to the Jimjar thread and the Khell-Vire case. Bound by self-imposed monastic discipline and loyalty to Glabbagool.  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Eldritch Claw Tattoo (active, granting 15-foot tentacle reach)  
- Drow cloak of spider-silk that drinks shadow (taken from Asha Vandree)  
- Glabbagool (gelatinous ooze fused to his left forearm; capable of acid secretion, wall-eating, stealth movement, and potion theft)  
- Stone Guard passes (Duergar-issued, likely still valid)  
- Minor Illusion-capable focus item (used to create architectural illusions)  
- Potion of flying (stolen during the Candlekeep investigation)  
- Collection of fungal specimens (Ormu, Nilhogg’s nose, Ghost Hold lichen)  
- List of names from the winch outpost (still in possession)  
- Journal entries and marginalia documenting events, suspects, and metaphysical puzzles  
**Spells/Feats:**  
- Shadow Step (teleportation through darkness)  
- Stunning Strike  
- Grappling and control geometry (specialized tactical synergy)  
- Street Justice (Rope Bind feat applied to grappled targets)  
- Darkness (area control and tactical concealment)  
- Minor Illusion  
- Locate Object (used to detect the sapphire)  
- Eldritch Maul (tentacle manifestation)  
- Monastic training in silence, stealth, and environmental perception  
**Current Assignment / Role:**  
- Investigative analyst for the First Reader, pursuing the Jimjar thread and the mystery of Khell-Vire.  
- Silent enforcer and tactical coordinator for the group, specializing in neutralizing threats with precision, exploiting contradictions in magic and testimony, and manipulating social dynamics through observation.  
- Primary handler and trainer of Glabbagool, guiding its development toward disciplined control and tactical utility.  
- Maintains a detached, observational role in group decisions, often stabilizing consensus through quiet confirmation.  
**Key Relationships:**  
- Glabbagool: Fused companion and tactical extension; Zalthir considers him a living lesson in silent communication and discipline.  
- Daz: Tactical superior and de facto leader; Zalthir follows orders but maintains independent judgment.  
- Thorin: Respected for raw effectiveness but viewed as reckless; Zalthir tolerates his theatrics as “the work.”  
- Jimjar: Subject of prolonged scholarly interest; Zalthir confirmed his divine-level immunities and believes he is not what he appears.  
- Bookwyrm: Recognized as having won the confrontation; Zalthir notes her relaxed hands as evidence of victory.  
- Kalan Strongbranch: Seen as a flawed proceduralist; Zalthir has exposed his protocol loopholes and moral blind spots.  
**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Stole the Potion of Flying from the table during a distraction, using Glabbagool’s arm with perfect stealth.  
- Confirmed the Sylvira lookalike sightings violated polymorph spell duration, concluding magic was *creating* contradictions rather than resolving them.  
- Identified the true crime as embarrassment, not murder, and documented this in his margins.  
- Used Glabbagool to consume Bookwyrm’s tea as an act of quiet resistance and control.  
- Grappled Glabbagool to grant advantage to allies during combat with helmed horrors.  
- Used Locate Object to determine if Bookwyrm possessed a sapphire.  
- Declined to correct Thorin’s theatrical performance, affirming that “theater is the work.”  
- Maintained silence during the resolution of the Janussi case, recording events with precision and letting the system turn against itself.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between Zalthir being described as a *bronze* dragonborn (ch16) and a *golden* dragonborn (ch28, ch52). Later references consistently call him “golden,” but ch16 explicitly corrects “gold” to “bronze.” The most recent usage (ch28, ch52, ch61) favors “golden,” but the origin of the discrepancy is unresolved.  
- Zalthir is referred to as a *drow* in ch50 and ch54, but all prior and subsequent physical descriptions identify him as a dragonborn. This may be a misattribution by another character or a magical disguise, but no evidence confirms his drow identity.  
- Whether Zalthir is still under the influence of the Heart Fungus spores is unclear. He caught spores during the aftermath (ch51) and was poisoned (ch49), but no cure or resolution is stated.  
- The status of his vow of silence (ch03) is unconfirmed — he speaks frequently after this point, suggesting it was abandoned or only applied to specific contexts.  
- Whether Zalthir’s “monk training” refers to a specific order (e.g., Shadow Monks, Ember Vanguard, Garden Shadow) or is a generalized term for his discipline is ambiguous.  
- The nature of Zalthir’s “internal argument” on the Candlekeep roof (ch55) and its resolution is described but not fully explained.  
- Whether Glabbagool’s fusion with Zalthir is permanent or reversible is not stated.  
- The identity of the “person they are actually interested in” (ch54) who is observing Zalthir remains unknown.  
- The exact source of his “Eldritch Claw Tattoo” is never specified — whether it was acquired from a merchant, created, or granted.  
- Whether Zalthir’s “maybe list” (ch54) includes Daz as a potential target remains speculative, with no further context.  
- The fate of the Demogorgon statue (ch23) is unreported after its retrieval.
