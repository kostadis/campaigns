### Circle of Masters of Neverlight Grove

**Current status:** Active, rogue faction within Neverlight Grove.  
**Current location:** Controls the Garden of Welcome; operates covertly throughout the grove and beyond.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Officially aligned with Phylo as the innermost circle of trusted leaders, but acts autonomously and in defiance of the grove’s communal norms.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** Not specified.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Oversees reanimation of dead creatures; harvests carcasses from the Circle of Hunters; dispatches myconids secretly beyond the grove; conducts rituals in the Garden of Welcome. Reports directly to Phylo (“the circle of those closest to them”), but is suspected of corrupting Phylo via demonic spore infection. Includes specialized producers like Hepbobe under its structure.  
**Defining recent actions:** Secretly hoards and transports corpses; initiates unauthorized reanimation rituals; is the suspected source of the demonic spore infection spreading through Neverlight Grove; has subverted the grove’s traditional consensus-based governance in favor of task-specific, top-down productivity.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: [ch31] describes the Circle of Masters as a rogue faction acting against the grove’s interests, while [ch32] describes them as legitimate specialists operating under Phylo’s “new structure” — is this a reformation or a cover?  
- Ambiguity: The quote from [ch32] — “The circle of those closest to them.” — is incomplete and lacks context; does this refer to the Circle of Masters or another group?  
- Unclear attribution: Is Hepbobe a member of the Circle of Masters, or merely a producer working under their oversight? The fact doesn’t specify direct membership.  
- Unclear chronology: Did the Circle of Masters become “rogue” after aligning with Phylo’s new structure, or was it always rogue under the guise of alignment?

### Circle of Masters of Neverlight Grove

- **What it is:** A secretive subgroup of myconids within Neverlight Grove, closest to Phylo.
- **Current state:** Active and operational within Neverlight Grove.
- **Allegiance/faction:** Loyal to Phylo; operates independently of other myconid circles.
- **Current role and key relationships:** 
  - Receives and reanimates dead creatures brought in by the Circle of Hunters.
  - Sends groups of myconids outside the grove without disclosing purpose or destination.
  - Maintains direct proximity and likely direct guidance from Phylo.
- **Defining recent actions:** 
  - Systematically reanimating corpses from Hunter hauls.
  - Deploying myconid agents beyond the grove’s boundaries under opaque directives.

## Uncertainty
- The exact number of members in the Circle of Masters is unknown.
- The specific destinations or purposes of the myconids sent outside the grove are unreported.
- Whether the reanimated creatures are controlled, enslaved, or autonomous is not specified.
- No details are given on the methods or limits of reanimation.
- It is unclear if the Circle of Masters reports to Phylo directly or through intermediaries.
