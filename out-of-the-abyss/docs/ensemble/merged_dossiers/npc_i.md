### I

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** En route to or near Candlekeep (last known context)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Ember Vanguard (as the feared mage)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** The Overbright (carried as a conceptual anchor, like a stone in a pocket)  
**Spells/feats:** Evoker (Menzoberranzan-trained); knows *Lesser Restoration*; employs Zhentarim Tactics; applies giants’ lesson: “You don’t chase, you position, and the ground guides your actions”  
**Current assignment / role:** Feared mage of the Ember Vanguard; acts as strategic observer and silent operator; serves as bait/indicator in operations  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Chose not to cast *Lesser Restoration* on Daral, trusting the group’s movement toward mercy.  
- Did not correct Zalthir’s assessment that Daz might extort Daral — agreed with the assessment.  
- Recognized helmed horrors as assassins selected for their silence, and considered using one as a divining rod to trace key-holders.  
- Maintained neutral expression during theoretical murder planning of Daz.  
- Noted and internalized Glabbagool’s earnest attention to the miniaturized drow prisoner.  
- Reflected on being used as bait — understood self as “an indicator on the board.”  

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
- Privately rejects Menzoberranzan’s dogma of destruction as the only valid magic; seeks purpose beyond inherited ideology.  
- Believes in the moral weight of mercy and collective moral motion — trusts groups to move toward it.  
- Holds deep, unspoken empathy for the oppressed (drow prisoner, Daral), yet remains emotionally restrained.  
- Carries the Overbright as a personal talisman against the crushing weight of the Underdark.  
- No longer sees her Lolth-follower persona as a lie — it has become consistent characterization.  
- Values efficiency and pragmatism (Zhentarim Tactics), yet is drawn to small, truthful acts of goodness (Dawnbringer and Daral).
