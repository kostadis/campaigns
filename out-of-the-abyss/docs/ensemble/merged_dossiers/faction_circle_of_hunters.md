### Circle of Hunters

**Current status:** Active faction.  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid sub-group under Phylo’s doctrine of non-killing; nominally aligned with the Circle of Masters, but loyalty is divided.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified.  
**Current assignment / role:** Retrieve and deliver slain creatures’ remains to the Circle of Masters for reanimation; do not kill, only recover the dead.  
**Key relationships:** Operates under Basidia’s indirect influence; reports to and supplies the Circle of Masters, who now intercept and exploit most of their carcasses. Led by Loobamub.  
**Defining recent actions:** Successfully recovered and delivered carcasses; recently revealed knowledge of a concealed Shambling Mound near their territory.  

## Uncertainty
- The extent of Basidia’s influence vs. Phylo’s doctrine is unclear — are they acting under Basidia’s orders, Phylo’s teachings, or both?  
- It is unclear whether the Circle of Hunters is aware that the Circle of Masters is exploiting them, or if they believe their role is still purely ceremonial or dutiful.  
- Loobamub’s personal allegiance (to Phylo, Basidia, or the Circle of Masters) is not stated — only that the faction’s loyalty is divided.
