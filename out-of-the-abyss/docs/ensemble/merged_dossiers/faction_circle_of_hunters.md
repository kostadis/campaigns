### Circle of Hunters

**Current status:** Active  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid sub-group under Phylo’s doctrine; nominally aligned with the Circle of Masters, but loyalty is divided  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Retrieve and deliver corpses of slain creatures from the wild for reanimation by the Circle of Masters; strictly adhere to the doctrine of non-killing  
**Key relationships:** Operate under indirect influence of Basidia; led by Loobamub; interact with and are being exploited by the Circle of Masters  
**Defining recent actions:** Consistently recover carcasses from the wild; recently discovered that the Circle of Masters is intercepting and exploiting most of their collected remains; Loobamub revealed the concealment of a Shambling Mound near their operations  

## Uncertainty
- The extent of Basidia’s indirect influence and whether it is active or merely historical is unclear.  
- It is unconfirmed whether the Circle of Hunters is aware that the Circle of Masters is exploiting their carcasses, or if they are complicit.  
- The current status of Loobamub’s leadership (alive, present, or still in command) is not explicitly confirmed beyond the single utterance in ch31.  
- Whether the Shambling Mound mentioned by Loobamub is under Circle of Hunters’ control, a threat, or an unrelated entity is not clarified.
