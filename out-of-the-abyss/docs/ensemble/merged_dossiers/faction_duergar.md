### Duergar

**Current status:** Active, fragmented, and in crisis.  
**Current location:** Primarily centered in Gracklstugh, with influence extending across key trade routes in the Underdark.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Ruled by the Deepking, though his madness has destabilized the hierarchy. Noble houses compete for power amid internal decay, demonic incursions, and the rise of the Ember Vanguard.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Maintain control over a network of watercraft locks and widened passages in the Darklake.  
- Enforce identity checks at city gates, demanding bribes (gold pieces) and proof of innocence.  
- Utilize Themberchaud, a red dragon, as a living forge-lighter — not for magical efficiency, but as a tool of psychological domination.  
- Employ innate invisibility as a systemic tool of control, breeding paranoia and suppressing civil discourse.  
- Enforce strict segregation between their geometric, clean dwellings and Derro slums.  
- Maintain militarized Stone Guard units on high alert.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Enforce taxation and tolls on all trade and movement through their territories.  
- Manipulate and suppress Themberchaud through pampering and false authority, denying his sovereignty.  
- Fear and actively hunt the Ember Vanguard, reacting with panic upon any perceived resemblance (e.g., Daz).  
- Are complicit in Gartokkar’s genocidal campaign against the Derro.  
- Report to the Deepking — though his authority is unraveling, and noble houses now operate with increasing autonomy.  
- Maintain historical enmity with the Derro, who now seek their total destruction under Demogorgon’s influence.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Panic-stricken mobilization upon sighting Daz, triggering mass warnings to guard posts and command centers.  
- Continued exploitation of Themberchaud despite his immense power, revealing a cultural preference for psychological control over practicality.  
- Intensified surveillance and identity enforcement at Gracklstugh’s gates, reflecting deepening paranoia.  
- Ongoing suppression of internal dissent, with failure met by extermination and prolonged factional infighting.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Their rigid hierarchy and innate invisibility have made governance impossible without fear; they are not inherently evil, but terrified — and fear has made them cruel.  
- They believe dragon scale color determines morality, a delusion used to justify control and hierarchy.  
- Their greatest weakness is not external enemies, but their inability to confront the truth: the Deepking is not the source of their collapse — their own fear of power is.  
- They are aware of their own decay, yet remain outwardly rigid, using oppression to mask internal fragility.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch18] (Duergar and Derro were once peaceful companions) and [ch17]/[ch29] (Duergar enslaved Derro, then were overthrown) — unclear if “peaceful companions” refers to a pre-Faerzress era or a myth.  
- Unclear if the Deepking is still alive or merely a symbolic figurehead — his madness is noted, but no chapter confirms his death or deposition.  
- Ambiguous whether “innate invisibility” is a racial trait all Duergar possess, or only certain castes/agents — source descriptions are inconsistent in scope.  
- Unclear if the Ember Vanguard is a known faction to all Duergar or only Gracklstugh’s military — [ch24] suggests broader awareness, [ch29] suggests localized terror.  
- No current confirmation of whether Buppido’s network or Grygum’s smuggling routes are still active or relevant to Duergar economic control.  
- Unclear if the guardhouses in the West Cleft are still abandoned or have been reactivated — [ch17] and [ch17] state they were abandoned, but no later chapter confirms their current status.
