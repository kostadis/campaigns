### Dust of Suleiman

A 4th-level magical spell that creates a 20-foot sphere of damaging dust at a range of 120 feet, dealing 20 points of damage per turn to all creatures within the area. Originally intended for direct use against Ilvara, it was reclassified by Asha as a superior tool for infrastructure demolition. It has been cast directly onto the Big Heart Fungus, where it now functions as a persistent, lethal environmental hazard targeting the fungus’s control infrastructure. The resource is non-replaceable and was expended despite its high cost, deemed necessary due to the fungus’s demonstrated threat. Its deployment was a deliberate strategic choice to neutralize the fungus’s anchoring power, not to achieve emotional satisfaction through Ilvara’s demise.
