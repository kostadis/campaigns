### Caves of Clatter

The Caves of Clatter are a cave system that formerly housed Buppido’s shrine of bones and skeletons. Following a recent battle, the caves are now littered with the remains of derro and their undead servants, including the scattered, blood-spattered remains of Buppido himself.

None.
