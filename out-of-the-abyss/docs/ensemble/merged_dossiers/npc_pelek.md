### Pelek

**Current status:** Deceased (ghostly spirit, at rest)  
**Current location:** At rest, interred (spiritual resolution achieved)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a Svirfneblin merchant; later a ghost bound to the quest for burial; no current allegiance  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Obsidian family ring (worn on a lost hand; last known location: with remains); no items currently held  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** No active role. Last known companion: the party (travelled with them until burial). Last known adversary: Buppido (killed him). His spirit was last seen compelling Glyphic Shroomlight, a priest, to inter his bones.  
**Defining recent actions:** His spirit materialized to compel a cleric to inter his bones, fulfilling his lifelong quest. After the burial, his spirit faded with profound relief, no longer tormented.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Sought burial in Blingdenstone to end his spectral torment. His name, given by his mother after an actor, caused familial shame and contributed to his isolation. He was murdered and dismembered by Buppido, who also betrayed his trust as a merchant partner in the Council of Savants’ tax-evasion scheme. He feared eternal wandering and was deeply unsettled by his ghostly state and failed psychic attempts (e.g., invading the thief-derro’s mind). He denied possessing Zalthir and had no expertise in fungi, despite earlier knowledge claims.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Ch17 describes Pelek as a *Svirfneblin* ghost, but ch23 has him occupying a *Derro* body. Later chapters (ch25, ch33, ch37) refer to him only as a ghost without specifying original race — the Derro body occupation is presented as a temporary event, but his original race remains ambiguous in later state.  
- Unclear attribution: Ch17 implies Pelek knew about Pygmywort and fungal properties; ch33 has him deny being a fungi expert. The later statement overrides the earlier, but it’s unclear whether this is due to memory loss, possession, or deliberate deception.  
- Ambiguous chronology: The timeline of his possession of the Derro body (ch23) and his later ghostly appearances (ch25, ch33, ch37) is unclear — was the possession temporary and abandoned? Did the ghost return to his original spectral state? The dossier assumes the latter, but the transition is not explicitly stated.  
- Unclear status of the obsidian ring: Last mentioned as worn on a lost hand in ch17; its current location is unknown — assumed with the bones, but never confirmed.
