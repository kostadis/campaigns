### Oozes (Blingdenstone)

**Current status:** Active but dispersing  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone, primarily threatening Trader’s Grotto  
**Allegiance/faction:** No longer under the Pudding King’s command  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (as a monster type)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** No coordinated role; currently engaged in uncontrolled predation and mutual consumption. Primary threat to Gnomish defenses at Trader’s Grotto.  
**Defining recent actions:** A massive swarm of oozes, previously under the Pudding King’s control, launched an uncontrolled assault on Gnomish defenses at Trader’s Grotto. The swarm has since begun dispersing naturally: gelatinous cubes are consuming other oozes, black puddings are slipping through underground cracks, and gray oozes have gone inert.
