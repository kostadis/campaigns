### Circle of Explorers of Neverlight Grove

- **What it is:** A Myconid gathering point and safe haven located near an exit hole in Neverlight Grove.  
- **Current state:** Occupied and active as a refuge for Myconids who have fled the Inner Circle.  
- **Who controls/occupies it now:** Rasharoo and other Myconid survivors; Basidia, Loobamub, and hunters were last known to be present there during a deep mind meld prior to fleeing.  
- **Notable feature:** Contains an exit hole used for escape.  
- **Recent actions:** Served as the destination where Zalthir dragged Basidia to safety after their confrontation with Phylo; site of a deep mind meld involving Basidia, Rasharoo, Loobamub, and hunters before the group fled the Grove.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Basidia, Loobamub, or the hunters are still present at the Circle — they were last seen there during the mind meld, but it is not confirmed if they remain or have since departed.  
- The exact location of the exit hole relative to the Grove’s layout is not specified.  
- Whether the Circle is under active defense or has any other inhabitants beyond Rasharoo and the fleeing Myconids is unconfirmed.
