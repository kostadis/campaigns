### The Stone Guard

**Current status:** Active faction, operational in Gracklstugh.  
**Current location:** Based in Overlake Hold, patrolling and enforcing order throughout Gracklstugh.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Official law enforcement of Gracklstugh, nominally loyal to the Duergar regime; however, now corrupted and under covert influence by the Keepers of the Flame.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** Not specified.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Primary role: Law enforcement and internal security in Gracklstugh.  
- Actively investigating corruption tied to Droki, the Council of Savants, and Clan Lairds.  
- On high alert due to Ember Vanguard attacks and fear of Menzoberranzan assassins.  
- Demanding proof of identity from strangers; accepting bribes.  
- Led by Captain Errde Blackskull.  
- Previously operated with autonomy from Duergar authority and in alliance with Stone Giants; now fully integrated into Duergar structure, used as a tool of intimidation.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Shifted from pragmatic, covert enforcers to paranoid, corrupt officials demanding bribes and identity verification.  
- Used as a threat against non-Duergar (e.g., Thorin) by Duergar authorities.  
- Actively seeking Droki as a key to uncovering corruption, but may be compromised.  
- Manipulated by the Keepers of the Flame to suppress dissent and control information.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between ch15 (Stone Guards as autonomous enforcers allied with Stone Giants) and ch28 (Stone Guard as a Duergar military force with no mention of Stone Giants). Later chapter (ch28) overrides earlier, but the nature of their original alliance and whether Stone Giants still influence them is unclear.  
- Unclear if Errde Blackskull still commands them — no later chapter confirms his status after ch15.  
- Ambiguous whether “Stone Guards” and “Stone Guard” (singular) refer to the same entity throughout; ch28 uses singular form, possibly indicating reorganization or linguistic shift.  
- Unclear if the Stone Guards still have any operational ties to Stone Giants, or if that alliance was dissolved under Duergar consolidation.  
- Grygum’s claim in ch16 (“We have people who will come looking for us!”) implies external connections — but whether these are to the Stone Guards or another faction is unconfirmed.
