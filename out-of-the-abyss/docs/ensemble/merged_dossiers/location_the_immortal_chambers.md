### The Immortal Chambers

A sacred space within Candlekeep, featuring a tile floor where each tile bears the symbol of a different deity. Stepping on a tile whose symbol does not align with one’s doctrinal alignment is considered a theological error, physically manifesting the library’s underlying doctrinal tensions.
