### Hemeth

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Traveling with the escapee group (last seen in ch14, actively advising on Gracklstugh)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly bound to the Archpriest of the Deep Father; now an uneasy ally of the escapees, with no formal allegiance  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Can turn invisible at will (duration: one hour) — used tactically to evade and mislead  
- Deep knowledge of Gracklstugh’s districts, especially the Darklake District and Blade Bazaar  
- Familiar with duergar customs, trade networks, and the city’s slave economy  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acts as a guide and informant on Gracklstugh’s geography, commerce, and social structure  
- Serves as a pragmatic, if distrusted, advisor to the group; specifically consulted on Kuo-Toan behavior and boat acquisition  
- Deeply skeptical of Brannam’s magical goods business; resents being bypassed in magical transactions  
- Last seen traveling with Zalthir, Brannam, and the escapee group  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Suggested seizing a boat from the Kuo-Toans, citing prior near-sacrifice during a weapons deal  
- Demonstrated invisibility to prove duergar capability and unsettle allies  
- Provided detailed, accurate descriptions of Gracklstugh’s Blade Bazaar, Darklake District, and systemic xenophobia  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Originally believed his sacrifice would be “weapon enough” for the Archpriest — now seeks survival through utility  
- Views Gracklstugh as a xenophobic, slaver-run city where non-Duergars are confined to the Darklake District  
- Trusts no one fully; his skepticism is rooted in lived experience of betrayal and ritualized violence  

## Uncertainty
- The exact timing of his invisibility ability’s activation (ch14 says “at will,” but ch12 implies it’s situational — no contradiction, but no confirmation of frequency or cooldown)  
- Whether he still possesses any weapons or trade goods from his former business — none are mentioned as currently carried  
- Whether he has formally severed ties with the Archpriest or still fears retribution  
- Whether his invisibility is innate or magically augmented — described as a duergar trait, but no source confirms if all duergar have it or if it’s unique to him  
- His current relationship with Grygum — mentioned in ch11 as non-violent, but no further interaction recorded since
