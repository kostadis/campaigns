### potion of flying

**Current holder/location:** Held by Zalthir, concealed within the ooze of his Glabbagool arm.  
**Current condition:** Intact, unmissed by all observers.  
**Significance:** A magical vial granting the drinker the ability to fly; recently stolen from Alkrist’s safe.  
**Recent action:** Swiped from Alkrist’s safe by Zalthir using his Glabbagool arm during the distraction of the safe’s opening.  

> "my Glabbagool arm swipes the potion of flying"  
> "The potion of flying went with us, in the bag, in the ooze, and no one in that room would ever know to miss it."  
> "I had my Glabbagool arm swipe the potion of flying off the table."

## Uncertainty
- The claim that Glabbagool “has internalized its properties and approves of its utility” conflicts with the later, more consistent statements that the potion was stolen and is now physically held by Zalthir within the ooze. Since the later facts explicitly describe physical possession and concealment, the internalization claim is likely metaphorical or misattributed — but it is not definitively contradicted.  
- It is unclear whether “Glabbagool” in the first fact refers to the entity, the arm, or Zalthir’s connection to it — the term is used ambiguously.  
- No fact confirms whether the potion has been consumed or remains in its vial.
