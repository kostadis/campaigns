### Prince Derendil

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Corpse remains in the prison chamber, standing as a silent monument  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former noble of an unknown realm; enslaved by Mistress Ilvara in the drow prison complex  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (corpse); last known to be armed in his Quaggoth form, but no specific items are noted as retained  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** None; deceased. Last known relationship: enslaved by Mistress Ilvara; hostile toward Imbros.  
**Defining recent actions:** Declared himself armed in his Quaggoth form, charged Imbros, was struck by Drow Poison, and was stabbed to death by Imbros. His corpse now serves as a monument to the cost of resistance.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believed no abolitionist movement existed among Lolth-worshippers; viewed aid as inherently self-serving to the drow. Retained memory of his noble past and Ilvara’s cruel rituals, but was deluded into accepting his Quaggoth form. Later revealed to have been a polymorphed elf.

## Uncertainty
- The exact origin realm of Prince Derendil is not specified.  
- It is unclear whether his "deluded Quaggoth" state was caused by magic, curse, or psychological breakdown — only that he was a polymorphed elf.  
- The phrase “deluded Quaggoth” is used by others (ch01), but whether he internally identified as such or only perceived himself as a noble in captivity is ambiguous.  
- While ch02 states he was stabbed to death by Imbros, ch03 and ch18 confirm his death — but no detail is given about whether his body was moved, guarded, or desecrated after death beyond being a monument.  
- The identity of the scarred drow flanking Ilvara is not named or connected to any other character.
