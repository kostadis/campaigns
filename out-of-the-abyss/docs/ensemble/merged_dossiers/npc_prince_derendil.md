### Prince Derendil

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Corpse remains in the prison chamber, standing as a silent monument  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a noble of unknown origin; now a deceased prisoner of the drow under Ilvara’s control  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (corpse); last known to be armed in his Quaggoth form; suffered injury from Drow Poison prior to death  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Formerly enslaved by Mistress Ilvara; last seen charging and attempting to kill Imbros; no current companions or allegiances  
**Defining recent actions:** Declared himself armed in his Quaggoth form, charged Imbros, was struck by Drow Poison, and was stabbed to death by Imbros. His corpse now serves as a grim monument to the cost of resistance.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believed no abolitionist movement existed among Lolth-worshippers; held onto his noble identity despite enslavement; recalled Ilvara’s greeting and her mantra: “Accept your fate, learn to obey, and you may survive.” Later revealed to have been a polymorphed elf, formerly a prince.

## Uncertainty
- The exact origin of his polymorphed form (who cast it, when, or why) is unconfirmed.  
- Whether he retained any elfic magic, memories, or abilities in Quaggoth form is not specified.  
- The nature of his “arming” in Quaggoth form (natural weapons? concealed item?) is ambiguous.  
- The timeline between his death and the monument’s creation is unclear — but his corpse is confirmed present in ch03 and referenced in ch18.  
- The phrase “deluded Quaggoth” is used in ch01 but later recontextualized in ch18 as “mad Quaggoth, once a polymorphed elf” — no contradiction, but the full transformation backstory remains unexplained.
