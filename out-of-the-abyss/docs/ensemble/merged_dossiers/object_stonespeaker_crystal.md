### Stonespeaker Crystal

A 19-inch shard of quartz crystal, originally gifted by Hgraam, that enables the spells *Speak with Animals*, *Speak with the Dead*, and *Speak with Plants*, and enhances divination magic. It is attuned to the voices of the stones and the Underdark’s pain, and may serve as a conduit to deeper truths such as the crying rocks or the whispers of the Faerzress. Currently owned and carried by Daz, who uses it to interpret hidden truths, conduct research into his own history and unresolved group mysteries, and specifically to communicate with beasts — as demonstrated in the interrogation of a cat.
