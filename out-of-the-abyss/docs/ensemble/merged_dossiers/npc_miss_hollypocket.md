### Miss Hollypocket

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Janussi’s apartment (as caretaker and housekeeper)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Unaffiliated; serves as private housekeeper to the late Janussi  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated beyond her role; likely retains standard housekeeping tools, but no items are confirmed as currently in her possession  
**Current assignment / role:** Housekeeper and caretaker of Janussi’s apartment; primary witness to his final days and routines; tasked with maintaining protocol and order in the residence; designated by investigators as the key source for establishing objective baseline facts about the crime scene  
**Key relationships:**  
- Was the sole witness to Janussi’s final confrontations: overheard Teles calling him “hypocrite,” Daral calling him “glorified book peddler”  
- Observed the midnight thud and saw Sylvira Savikas carrying a lantern at night, her face glowing red in candlelight  
- Believes Janussi never removed his locket — it was always with him  
- Expresses fear of Sylvira Savikas: “That devil woman! She gives me the heebie-jeebies!”  
- Maintains rigid adherence to protocol — notably, would never allow candles to burn down to nothing, implying Janussi was dead before the candles extinguished  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Discovered Janussi’s body at approximately 7:30 a.m. alongside A'lai Aivenmore  
- Provided critical testimony on Janussi’s daily habits, timing of confrontations, and the suspicious nocturnal movement of Sylvira Savikas  
- Is the only person who can confirm the baseline state of the apartment and library — investigators rely on her for objective truth  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Her strict adherence to ritual (candle protocol) suggests deep personal discipline and possible guilt or trauma over the event  
- Her fear of Sylvira Savikas may imply more than mere superstition — she witnessed something she cannot explain (Sylvira glowing red without light, carrying an unnecessary lantern)  
- Her insistence that Janussi never removed the locket may indicate she observed it closely — potentially implying she noticed its absence, though she does not say so  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Hollypocket still resides in the apartment or has been displaced since Janussi’s death — she is described as caretaker, but no confirmation of current occupancy is given after the investigation began.  
- The locket’s current location is unknown — Hollypocket believes it was always worn, but it is not confirmed to be present or absent in the apartment.  
- It is ambiguous whether she witnessed Sylvira Savikas’s lantern use *with* or *without* the candlelight — the quote says “glowing red in the candlelight,” but also notes Sylvira “can see in the dark without any light at all,” creating potential contradiction in lighting conditions.  
- No confirmation is given whether Hollypocket has been officially retained by authorities or is acting independently.  
- The phrase “she would never have allowed the Keeper to let candles burn down to nothing” is repeated, but it is unclear if “the Keeper” refers to Janussi or another servant — attribution of responsibility for candle maintenance is ambiguous.
