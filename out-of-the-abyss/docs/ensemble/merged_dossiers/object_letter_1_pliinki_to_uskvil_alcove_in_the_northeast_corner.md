### Letter 1: Pliinki to Uskvil (alcove in the northeast corner)

- **Current holder/location:** Hidden in the alcove in the northeast corner, as originally placed.  
- **Current condition:** Intact, unread since delivery; contains Pliinki’s handwritten message.  
- **Significance:** Contains critical operational intelligence:  
  - The Thrazgad ore is discarded and forgotten beside Pliinki’s workbenches.  
  - The Gray Ghosts successfully delivered the Egg as planned.  
  - The Obelisk must be repaired to conquer Gracklstugh and serve as a sacrificial gift to Demogorgon.  

## Uncertainty
- The physical location of the Thrazgad ore, the Egg, and the Obelisk is not confirmed to be current — only that Pliinki *says* they exist in those states.  
- It is unclear whether Pliinki still possesses the letter after writing it, or if it was sent and remains in the alcove as a static artifact.  
- The current status of the Gray Ghosts, the Obelisk’s fractures, or Demogorgon’s awareness is not stated beyond Pliinki’s claims.
