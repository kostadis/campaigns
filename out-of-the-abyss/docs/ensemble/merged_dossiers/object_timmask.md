### Timmask

- **Type:** Object (magical fungus)  
- **Current location:** Not specified (last observed in natural growth, likely in wild or enchanted terrain)  
- **Current condition:** Intact when undisturbed; spore-emitting when uprooted or heavily damaged  
- **Physical description:** A two-foot-tall toadstool with a beige cap crossed with orange and red stripes; commonly known as “the devil’s mushroom”  
- **Notable property:** Uprooting or heavily damaging it causes it to release a 15-ft radius cloud of spores that induce madness  
- **Grouping:** Grows in clusters of no more than four  

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of “madness” versus “confusion spell effects” is contradictory: ch07 describes confusion spell effects, ch17 says “madness.” No clarification on whether these are equivalent, overlapping, or distinct.  
- The term “Timmask” is used in ch17 but “Tinmmask” in ch07 — possible typo, but not confirmed.  
- No current holder or location is specified; its present whereabouts are unknown.  
- Whether the spore cloud duration (1 minute) from ch07 still applies under ch17’s “madness” effect is unclear.
