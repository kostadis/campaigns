## Uncertainty
- [ch56] Claims Imbros read Vof Klownits's opus, but no direct evidence or context is provided; no other chapter confirms this. Is this a posthumous attribution, a hallucination, or an error?  
- Contradiction: ch02 states Imbros was “cut down by Sarith’s sword” and “slain by Sarith after being struck by magic missiles and crossbow bolts,” yet also says he was “barely alive but still snarling” moments before — this is consistent with a dying state, not a contradiction.  
- Attribution of “Imbros is an elite Drow male fighter trained in Menzoberranzan” is given by Sarith — is this an accurate identification or a post-mortem assumption? No other source confirms or denies it, but it is repeated in the same passage as his death, so it is treated as factual.  
- No later chapter overrides his death in ch02; thus, death is current state.  
- No evidence he ever recovered from his wounds or was resurrected.
