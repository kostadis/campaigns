### Diggermattock Miners Patch (Hall of Melting Mirrors)

A commemorative miner’s patch awarded to Thorin by Diggermattock in recognition of his assistance reinforcing support beams near the black ooze. The patch, bearing the insignia “Diggermattock Miners,” is currently magically mended and affixed to Thorin’s gear with divine precision via Grygum’s cantrip. It signifies Thorin’s status as an honorary member of the Diggermattock mining crew.

## Uncertainty
None.
