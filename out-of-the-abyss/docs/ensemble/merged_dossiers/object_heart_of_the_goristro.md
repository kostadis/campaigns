### Heart of the Goristro

- **What it is**: The heart of Janussi, formerly part of the goristro entity.
- **Current holder/location**: Inside the chalice of a statue.
- **Current condition**: Removed from the body and concealed; intact and preserved as a deliberate act of misdirection.
- **Significance**: Used to obscure the true nature of Janussi’s death and protect the responsible party; likely a key to uncovering the goristro’s true origin or power source.

## Uncertainty
- The identity of the person who extracted the heart and placed it in the chalice is not named.
- The statue’s location and the chalice’s current accessibility are not specified.
- Whether the heart is still functional, sentient, or magically active is not stated.
