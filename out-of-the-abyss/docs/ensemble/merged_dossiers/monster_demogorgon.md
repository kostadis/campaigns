### Demogorgon

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Active in the Prime Material Plane, operating in proximity to Derro territories and the ruins of Sloobludop.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Prince of Demons; supreme ruler of Abyssal demons. Served by Narrak and the Derro, who revere him as the source of their crusade.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None explicitly possessed or wounded — his form is innate: a thirty-foot-tall, two-headed, baboon-like Abyssal horror with twin necks, writhing tentacles in place of hands, wickedly curved tusks, and red eyes blazing with bloodlust and madness. Radiates chaos, corruption, and madness.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as the divine focus of the Derro’s campaign of elevation and destruction, particularly against the Duergar.  
- Is the target of the Council of Savants and Plinki, who seek to summon and forcibly transform him into a two-headed dragon mount through blasphemous psionic-demonic fusion.  
- Commands legions of demons; his influence has breached planar barriers, enabling unprecedented demonic incursions.  
- Last seen advancing after the destruction of Sloobludop.  
- Directly worshipped and invoked by Narrak, who channels his will.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Erupted from the Darklake after a ritual’s completion.  
- Destroyed the town of Sloobludop, unleashing mass slaughter.  
- Is now the central object of a heretical plan to subjugate and reshape him into a celestial-like mount — a direct affront to his nature.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Embodies pure chaos, the antithesis of Bahamut’s order, justice, and compassion.  
- His dual heads manifest opposing yet complementary forces: one whispers insidious lies to corrupt the weak, the other roars with primal fury to revel in carnage and despair.  
- His presence alone defies belief and shakes the earth — his roar is a weapon of psychological and physical terror.  
- The Derro’s entire campaign is driven by his influence; they believe their “elevation” is his divine will.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch11] describing Demogorgon as a “baboon-like abomination” and [ch18]’s goal to transform him into a “two-headed dragon mount” — the latter implies a potential change in form, but no evidence confirms this transformation has occurred.  
- Unclear if Demogorgon is actively aware of or cooperating with the Council of Savants and Plinki’s plan — he may be their target, not their ally.  
- The exact current location beyond “proximity to Derro territories” is unspecified — no confirmation he remains near Sloobludop or the Darklake.  
- Whether the tentacles are still dripping with “venom of chaos” (ch11) or if this is merely descriptive lore, not a current trait.  
- Narrak’s current physical proximity to Demogorgon is unconfirmed — he serves Demogorgon’s influence, but it is unknown if they are together.
