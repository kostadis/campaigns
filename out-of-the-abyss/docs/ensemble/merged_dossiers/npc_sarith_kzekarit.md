### Sarith Kzekarit

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove (body destroyed after head explosion)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a Drow warrior of the Cult of Lolth; now a corrupted spore servant of Zuggtmoy, briefly before death  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Studded leather armor  
- Shield  
- Hand crossbow (carried since ch03)  
- Knowledge of Underdark routes from Velkynvelve to Gracklstugh via Silken Paths and Darklake  
- Trained in Lolth’s Guard torture techniques  
- Infected with Zuggtmoy’s demonic myconid spores (detected by Basidia in ch31)  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Former guide for the party, leading them through Underdark routes with claimed expertise  
- Last seen following myconids into the Neverlight Grove, under supernatural compulsion  
- Previously trusted by the group for navigation and survival knowledge; later regarded as mentally unstable and corrupted  
- No longer alive to maintain relationships — last seen being killed by Jimjar after transforming into a Drow spore servant  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Confirmed the direction of the trapped voice led to the Lost Tomb of Khaem (ch30)  
- Followed myconids into the Neverlight Grove, appearing serene but infected (ch31)  
- Head exploded into a Drow spore servant; immediately killed by Jimjar (ch32)  
- Previously exhibited escalating madness: cursed Drow guards, killed Imbros, ranted about revenge and the “Dark Lady,” and showed susceptibility to Faerzress and spore influence  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believed the “Dark Lady” (Zuggtmoy) offered liberation, revenge, and transformation through spiritual and physical change  
- Secretly harbored deep trauma from Derendil’s death and his own fall from the Drow warrior caste  
- Was never fully sane after imprisonment and the death of his prince, with his madness intensifying under spore influence  
- Claimed unjust imprisonment and deep knowledge of Underdark geography, but his reliability became increasingly suspect  

## Uncertainty
- The name "Sethir" appears multiple times in the facts — some entries refer to "Sethir" when describing Drow informant in ch01, while others refer to "Sarith" in later chapters. It is unclear if this is a transcription error or two distinct characters. All later actions (ch02–ch32) are attributed to "Sarith" — but the inconsistency in ch01, ch06, ch07, ch09, ch12, ch13, ch14 may indicate conflicting source data.
- In ch31, Sarith is referred to with the pronoun "she" ("Sarith, our drow guide, looks unhappy and serene but follows the myconid’s"). This contradicts all prior references using male pronouns and the name’s traditional Drow male association. Unclear if this is a narrative error or intentional gender ambiguity.
- The exact nature of Sarith’s transformation into a “Drow spore servant” is not defined — whether it is a unique hybrid of Drow and myconid, or simply a reanimation under Zuggtmoy’s control, remains unspecified.
- It is unclear whether Sarith’s belief in the “Dark Lady” was a personal delusion or a genuine psychic resonance with Zuggtmoy — the facts describe both psychological instability and spore-based influence, but do not resolve which was primary.
