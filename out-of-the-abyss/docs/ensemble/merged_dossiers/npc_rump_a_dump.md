### Rump-a-dump

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid community under Zalthir’s leadership  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as a quiet, precise-speaking observer and advisor within the myconid community.  
- Maintains a close, if strained, friendship with Stool, whom he finds overly verbose but ultimately worthy of respect.  
- Reports directly to and offers structured, unsolicited critiques to Zalthir, the sovereign of Neverlight Grove, whom he views as a protector and leader.  
- Deeply familiar with and emotionally attached to Neverlight Grove, which he considers sacred and inviolable.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Upon re-entering Neverlight Grove, declared “We’re home!” with clear joy and belonging.  
- Presented Zalthir with a list of fifty specific behavioral and dietary improvements, including: “Listen to Stool, he knows what he is doing” and “Eat more Zurkhwood, it's good for your digestion.”  
- Bid farewell to the party with visible sadness, indicating emotional investment in their journey despite his reserved nature.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes in terse, precise communication and rejects anthropomorphic assumptions about myconid biology.  
- Holds a firm, childlike conviction that Zalthir’s sovereignty prevents corruption of Neverlight Grove, even in the face of evidence to the contrary.  
- Expresses moral concern about cannibalism among other races, suggesting a strong ethical framework rooted in myconid identity.  
- Was personally saved by Zalthir in the past, fostering loyalty and a sense of indebtedness.
