### House T'sarran

**Current status:** Active and enduring.  
**Current location:** Based in Menzoberranzan, operating covertly across the surface and underground.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Drow noble house of Menzoberranzan; independent, not currently allied or at war with any named faction, but hostile to Daz and his associates.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Deploys miniaturized drow spies (likely using *tiny servant* or similar magic); each spy wears a piwafwi (a magical feathered cloak) that serves as a magical tether to the house — its absence confirms agent loss.  
- Maintains a network of professional drow mercenaries and cutouts for covert operations.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Primary objective: Recover or eliminate its captured miniaturized spy, currently in the party’s custody.  
- Secondary objective: Identify the mysterious patron who paid for Daz’s protection — Daz is being treated as bait.  
- Operates under the rule of a matron mother who has held power for two centuries; her strategy is patient, long-term, and avoids direct confrontation.  
- Monitors Daz and his party continuously; has been tracking him since before he surfaced on the surface.  
- Has commissioned contracts to extract Daz alive and unharmed; directly implicated in the events of the cavern confrontation.  
- Viewed as an external threat by Candlekeep due to its covert operations and ties to Jimjar’s disappearance and Daz’s origins.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Sent a miniaturized drow spy into the party’s custody on the surface during daylight hours.  
- Systematically neutralized by the party through psychological, magical, and informational means — but continues to send agents despite losses.  
- Vowed vengeance against Daz after the capture of its spy, delivering the threat with ceremonial gravity.  
- Previously lost a mage and all guards in a failed operation involving a Fireball in a cavern — a failure that triggered its current long-game strategy.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- House T'sarran is not in crisis; its two-century rule indicates stability and strategic patience.  
- Its actions are not driven by immediate retaliation but by the need to uncover the identity of Daz’s mysterious protector.  
- Jimjar’s disappearance and the activities of hired Duskryns are linked to Daz’s origins — House T'sarran is part of a deeper, hidden conspiracy.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: [ch51] states House T'sarran’s last warrior threatened “We will meet again” and the matron mother will hear about the Fireball — implying hostility. But [ch54] describes the house as patient watchers, not enemies, treating Daz as bait. These are not mutually exclusive, but the shift from reactive aggression to cold surveillance is unexplained.  
- Ambiguous chronology: It is unclear whether the contract to extract Daz alive was issued before or after the cavern confrontation.  
- Unclear attribution: [ch54] says House T'sarran “has vowed vengeance against Daz, a member of the party who is currently in their possession” — but Daz is not in their possession; their spy is. Likely a misstatement; the intended meaning is vengeance for the spy’s capture.  
- Unclear status: Whether the miniaturized spy is still alive or has been destroyed is not confirmed — only that its absence will be noticed.  
- Unclear scope: The extent of House T'sarran’s involvement in Jimjar’s disappearance is implied but not proven — only that it is being researched as a possible link.
