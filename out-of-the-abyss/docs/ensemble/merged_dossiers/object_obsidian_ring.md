### Obsidian Ring

- **Type:** Object (non-magical family ring)  
- **Current Holder/Location:** Attached to the missing hand of Grygum  
- **Current Condition:** Physically intact, still affixed to the severed hand  
- **Significance:** Personal artifact from Grygum’s former life; carries familial or symbolic significance. Not inherently magical, but Grygum implies its perceived value could motivate retrieval of the hand.  
- **Notable Detail:** The ring was originally described as belonging to Pelek, but the later fact reattributes it to Grygum — the ring is now confirmed as Grygum’s family artifact.

## Uncertainty
- Contradictory attribution: One fact states the ring belonged to Pelek, the later fact states it belonged to Grygum. The later fact overrides, but the origin of the discrepancy (e.g., misremembering, deception, or shared family lineage) is unexplained.  
- Unclear whether the hand (and thus the ring) is currently in a specific location or in possession of any character — only that it is “missing.”  
- Ambiguity in whether “Pelek” and “Grygum” are the same person (unlikely, given names) or if there is an error in attribution — no clarification exists.
