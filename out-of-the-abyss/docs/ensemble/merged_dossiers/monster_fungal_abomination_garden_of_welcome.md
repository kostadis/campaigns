### Fungal Abomination (Garden of Welcome)

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Garden of Welcome (site of death)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Physical avatar of the wedding cult, animated by the will of the Great Seeder  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (destroyed)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Served as the physical embodiment of the Great Seeder’s corruption; commanded exploding corpses and emitted acid-laced spore clouds; spoke in dual voices (oral and telepathic)  
**Defining recent actions:** Engaged travelers in the Garden of Welcome, attempting to lure them into assimilation via the Great Seeder’s doctrine; was targeted and killed by Zalthir after being wounded by the orc cleric of Bahamut.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Seeks to assimilate all visitors into the "Great Body" under the guise of wedding rites; its form is a grotesque fusion of Myconid and Drow flesh, indicating forced synthesis by the Great Seeder’s will.
