### Fembris Lancer

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Imprisoned in Deneir's Sanctum  
**Allegiance/faction:** Avowed (Candlekeep); anticipated successor to Bookwyrm as head of the Avowed  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated; previously carried notes for a monograph on the investigation (now in Glabbagool’s possession)  
**Current assignment / role:** Unwilling companion to the escapees; designated food taster due to apparent lack of suspicion  
**Key relationships:**  
- Trusted by no one, but tolerated for his non-threatening appetite  
- Previously served as guide, adjutant, and liaison for investigators  
- Was assigned to monitor the party as an Avowed minder (abandoned post to attend Grygum’s debate)  
- Recognized by Kalan as the likely next head of the Avowed  
- Observed the key transfer to Daz and is the only known witness to its prior state  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Hunted down Kalan Strongbranch with monastic urgency, intending to return with him  
- Witnessed Grygum hand the Hightower Library key to Daz — the only person present who saw it before the ambush  
- Stood in shock during the helmed horrors’ ambush, rendering him inert and treated as terrain  
- Now serves as the group’s food taster in Deneir’s Sanctum, consuming meals without suspicion  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Aspires to author a monograph on the investigation, though he misuses academic terminology and was disoriented when his work was handed to Glabbagool  
- Believes the cat is more than it appears, having revised his initial claim that it was an interdimensional horror created by the Avowed  
- Seeks purpose beyond manuscript copying, attaching himself to the investigation with acolyte-like fervor  
- May hold unacknowledged knowledge about the ambush’s origin, having been the sole witness to the key’s transfer  

## Uncertainty
- Contradictory attribution of role: Was Fembris an “adjutant” assigned by Bookwyrm (ch58) or an “Avowed minder” (ch59)? The terms are used interchangeably, but it is unclear if “adjutant” is an informal title or a distinct position.  
- Unclear chronology: Did Fembris *already* believe the cat was more than it appeared before or after seeing Janussi’s body? The vomit reaction (ch56) and cat revelation (ch56) are described in the same chapter but not explicitly ordered.  
- Ambiguous authority: Was Fembris’s shift from “catch” to “fetch” suspects (ch58) a linguistic slip, deliberate obfuscation, or a change in official designation?  
- Uncertain current status of Kalan: While Fembris intended to return with Kalan (ch60), it is unknown if he succeeded — and if so, whether Kalan is now with him in Deneir’s Sanctum or elsewhere.  
- Unclear if Fembris is aware he is being used as a food taster — his naivety is noted, but whether he suspects his role is tactical is unstated.  
- Contradiction in succession: Is Fembris elected *by default* (ch61) due to Bookwyrm’s collapse, or is he merely a *likely candidate*? The phrasing varies between “will probably get elected” and “elected by default,” leaving his official status ambiguous.
