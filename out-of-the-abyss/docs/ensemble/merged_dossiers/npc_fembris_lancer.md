### Fembris Lancer

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Deneir's Sanctum  
**Allegiance/faction:** Avowed (expected to become its next head)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Notes and theories on the colloquium (handed over to Glabbagool)  
**Current assignment / role:** Serving penance by keeping company with the adventurers; previously adjutant assigned to fetch witnesses and suspects  
**Key relationships:**  
- Reportedly under investigation for complicity or negligence in Janussi’s death and Alkrist’s confession  
- Handed intellectual materials to Glabbagool, unaware of its true nature  
- Expected to succeed Bookwyrm as head of the Avowed  
- Last seen accompanying the adventurers as assigned punishment  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Voluntarily sought to locate Kalan Strongbranch to verify a truth-seeking spell  
- Offered collaborative scholarly work (a “duograph”) to Glabbagool, misjudging its nature  
- Accepted punishment by remaining with the adventurers in Deneir’s Sanctum  
- Unwittingly served as food taster, displaying no suspicion or caution  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Ambitious academic seeking institutional validation through co-authored monographs  
- Misreads scholarly collaboration, conflating bureaucratic procedure with intellectual contribution  
- Unassuming and trusting nature makes him a tool for others’ caution; his lack of suspicion is noted as both a flaw and a vulnerability  
- Gender identity is ambiguously referenced in later sources — referred to as “she” in context of succession, despite prior male-presenting actions and name; this contradiction is unresolved  

## Uncertainty
- Gender: Later facts refer to Fembris Lancer as “she” in the context of succession (“she’ll want to get rid of Bookwyrm”), but all prior descriptions (ch58–ch60) use male pronouns implicitly via name, title, and actions. No explicit clarification of gender identity is given.  
- Attribution of succession: Two ch61 quotes state Fembris Lancer will “probably get elected the new avowed,” but neither confirms the election has occurred or whether Bookwyrm has been formally removed — only that it is anticipated.  
- Current possession of notes: Notes were handed to Glabbagool; it is unclear if Fembris retains copies or any physical artifacts beyond what was transferred.  
- Whether Fembris is still actively serving as adjutant — role shifted to penance; no confirmation of continued official duties.  
- Whether Fembris is aware Glabbagool is a gelatinous cube — fact states he was “unaware,” but no follow-up confirms if he learned the truth.
