### yellow diamond gem (Medusa's lair)

- **What it is:** A yellow diamond gem capable of summoning another Earth Elemental.  
- **Current location:** Medusa’s lair (within the cursed chamber).  
- **Current condition:** Intact and functional, but its use is fraught with peril.  
- **Significance:** Summoning an Earth Elemental with this gem within the cursed chamber will cause the summoned elemental to go mad and attack indiscriminately.  

## Uncertainty
None.
