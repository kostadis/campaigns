### Black Pudding (Blingdenstone)

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** A twenty-foot stretch of passageway in Blingdenstone, formed into a pulsating pit with an acid-dripping ceiling  
**Allegiance/faction:** None (natural monster)
