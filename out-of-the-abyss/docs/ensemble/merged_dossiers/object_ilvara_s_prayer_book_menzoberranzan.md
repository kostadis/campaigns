### Ilvara's prayer book (Menzoberranzan)

A standard Lolth devotional text, originally issued to promising young drow acolytes. Its margins contain a second, increasingly unstable handwriting that documents Ilvara’s gradual corruption by Zuggtmoy. The marginalia began as formal, liturgical annotations but evolved into associative, fragmented, and self-answering script — indicating a shift in consciousness, whether from external influence or internal fragmentation. The handwriting in the margins is distinct from Ilvara’s original script, though it remains ambiguous whether this was written by another entity or by Ilvara herself in a psychologically altered state.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether the marginalia were written by an external agent (e.g., Zuggtmoy) or by Ilvara in a dissociated state — the source text presents both possibilities without resolving them.
