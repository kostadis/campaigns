### Spiderbait

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Traveling with Thorin’s group, en route to Candlekeep  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent goblin guide; no formal faction, but now de facto companion to Thorin’s group  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated; previously carried no valuables beyond Yukyuk’s memory (“Other than the kindest heart, no.”)  
**Spells, feats, wounds:** None mentioned  
**Current assignment / role:** Survivor of the Underdark journey; now seeking safe integration into surface society by attaching to a protective group. Acts as a guide with intimate knowledge of the Silken Paths and Giant Spider behavior.  
**Key relationships:**  
- Last known companion: Thorin’s group (now his primary protective circle)  
- Former close companion: Yukyuk (deceased; killed by unknown party, Spiderbait accused Shuushar)  
- Former employer: Jimjar (promised 20 gold; Spiderbait demanded payment and left after Yukyuk’s death, but later rejoined)  
- Endorsed by: Grygum, who supports his survival plan  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- After Yukyuk’s death, accused Shuushar of murder due to Kuo-toan madness.  
- Carried Shuushar during the demonic escalation in the Underdark, visibly terrified.  
- At one point, raged at Buppido and wanted to kill him on the spot — overruled by the group.  
- Survived the full Underdark journey and emerged with the “moltley crew.”  
- Initially considered venturing alone on the surface, believing goblins were fully integrated — then, upon realizing the danger, chose to attach himself to Thorin’s group for safety.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Deeply traumatized by Yukyuk’s death; views him as the “kindest, gentlest goblin” and mourns his loss.  
- Fears surface prejudice against goblins but clings to hope of peaceful integration.  
- Values skill over magic, considers magical shortcuts in games like cut-the-graph “cheating.”  
- Proud of his route through the spider’s legs — sees complexity as a mark of competence.  
- Motivated by survival and safety, not ambition or treasure — seeks a “nice place” and “something innocuous” to do in Candlekeep.
