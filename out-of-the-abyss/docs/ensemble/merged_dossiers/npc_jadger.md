### Jadger

**Current status:** Alive as a spectral entity  
**Current location:** Ruby and the Rough temple, Blingdenstone  
**Allegiance/faction:** Borough/Burrow Warden (restored lineage, now leading spectral remnants)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Spectral banner (wielded in salute at departure)  
**Current assignment / role:** Training the next generation of Burrow Wardens; spectral leader of Blingdenstone’s ghostly guardians  
**Key relationships:** Coordinates with other Barrow/Burrow Warden ghosts; last seen encouraging Glabbagool, Eldeth, Jimjar, and Spiderbait; tasked party with rescuing tormented ghosts including Udhask  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Awarded the party two truth-telling boons for laying to rest two tormented spirits in the catacombs.  
- Confirmed location of the Temple of the Steadfast Stone in the Rock Blight and offered aid of other ghostly Wardens in exchange for quest completion.  
- Encouraged living gnomes to engage in ghost-hunting missions, affirming their potential to fight as resurrected spirits.  
- Observed the party’s departure from Blingdenstone and saluted them with his spectral banner, acknowledging their role in preserving burial sanctity.  

**Revealed motivations:** Re-establish the legacy of the Borough/Burrow Wardens by training new guardians and resolving spiritual unrest in Blingdenstone’s catacombs.  

## Uncertainty  
- The terms "Borough Warden" and "Burrow Warden" are used interchangeably across chapters — it is unclear if this is a transcription error, regional variation, or evolving title.  
- "Uth-Jadger" in chapter 39 may be a typo or variant of "Burrow Warden Jadger" — no other entity by that name appears, and all actions align with Jadger’s known role.  
- It is unclear whether Jadger is physically present at the Ruby and the Rough temple (as a spirit) or merely stationed there as a guiding presence — no confirmation of his physical or spectral form’s exact state beyond “materialized as a friendly ghost.”  
- The nature of his spectral banner — whether it is a unique artifact, symbolic, or merely a manifestation — is not described beyond its use in the salute.
