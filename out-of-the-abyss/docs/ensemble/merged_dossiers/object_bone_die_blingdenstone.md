### bone die (Blingdenstone)

**Current status:** Spent and inert  
**Current location:** In the possession of the party (handed to them by Jimjar)  
**Current condition:** No longer functional; triggered one irrevocable effect and now holds no residual power  
**Notable items / properties:** Carved from the knucklebone of a saint who never told a lie; possesses a "memory for the truth" (as claimed by Jimjar and the Duergar)  
**Current holder:** The party (specific recipient unnamed, but possession is collective and acknowledged)  
**Recent action:** Used once to pacify the entire Glabbagool ooze army by triggering a "blink" that corrected the "world's math," after which it became inert and was surrendered as a keepsake.  
**Significance:** A one-time-use artifact of divine or metaphysical origin, tied to absolute truth; its activation required direct contact with Glabbagool and resulted in permanent neutralization of all oozes in the cavern.

## Uncertainty
- The exact party member who physically received the die is unnamed; only "the party" is cited as the recipient.  
- The phrase "memory for the truth" is attributed to the Duergar and repeated by Jimjar, but its mechanism or nature is never defined — it remains a claim, not a demonstrated property.  
- The origin of the saint is unverified; the Duergar’s claim is presented as potentially deceptive (“I thought he was just trying to cover his losses”), leaving the saint’s existence or the die’s true provenance ambiguous.
