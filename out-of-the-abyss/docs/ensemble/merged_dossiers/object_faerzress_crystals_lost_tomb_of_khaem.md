### Faerzress Crystals (Lost Tomb of Khaem)

- **What it is:** Fist-sized quartz formations naturally occurring in the Underdark, formed by Faerzress arcane energy.  
- **Current condition:** Destroyed.  
- **Current holder/location:** None — all collected crystals were smashed within the cavern of the Lost Tomb of Khaem.  
- **Notable properties:** Emit a dim ten-foot radiance; their magical properties do not affect shriekers or fungi.  
- **Recent action:** Collected by the group for light and utility during exploration, then deliberately destroyed to disguise their escape as a monster attack.  

## Uncertainty
None.
