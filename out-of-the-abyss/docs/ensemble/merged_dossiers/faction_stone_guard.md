### Stone Guard

- **Current status:** Active, but deeply compromised.  
- **Current location:** Operate throughout the Duergar undercity, particularly in regions where law enforcement and travel control are enforced.  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Officially the enforcers of Duergar law; de facto fractured — some members remain loyal to institutional duty, others serve the Deepking, and many serve personal interests.  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** Official passes (recognized as legitimate but easily forged).  
- **Current assignment / role:** Enforcers of Duergar law; gatekeepers of movement and authority via passes; counterbalance to the Deepking’s autocratic power.  
- **Defining recent actions:** Have become susceptible to bribery since the Deepking’s madness took hold; their authority is still acknowledged but no longer trusted; their erosion reflects the broader collapse of institutional order in the undercity.  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:** Their original incorruptibility is gone; their continued existence as an institution is now a hollow shell — tolerated by the Deepking not out of respect, but because outright dismantling them would undermine the appearance of lawful order.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between "want the destruction of the Derro" (ch19) and no later mention of this goal — is this still an active objective, or was it abandoned with their corruption?  
- Unclear whether the Stone Guard as an organization still officially opposes the Deepking, or if their "counterbalance" role is merely a residual function while most members are now co-opted.  
- Ambiguous whether "some serve the Deepking, others serve their own interests" (ch19) means internal factions still exist, or if the faction has fully splintered into independent actors.  
- No confirmation whether the passes are still issued by a centralized authority or if they are now fabricated by rogue guards.
