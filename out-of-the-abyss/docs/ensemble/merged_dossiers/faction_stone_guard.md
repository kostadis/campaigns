### Stone Guard

**Current status:** Active, but institutionally compromised.  
**Current location:** Operate throughout the Duergar undercity, particularly in zones of law enforcement and travel control.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Officially the enforcers of Duergar law; de facto divided — some members remain loyal to institutional authority, others serve the Deepking or personal interests.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Official passes (recognized as legitimate but easily forged).  
**Current assignment / role:** Enforce Duergar law; regulate movement and authority through issued passes; act as a counterbalance to the Deepking’s autocratic power.  
**Defining recent actions:** Erosion of institutional integrity has led to widespread bribery and corruption; passes are now routinely forged, undermining their authority while still being nominally respected.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Their decline mirrors the collapse of order in the undercity; they once stood as incorruptible guardians, but now embody the fragility of law under tyranny. Their continued existence, despite corruption, suggests they are still perceived as a necessary, if broken, pillar of structure.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Ch19 states they “want the destruction of the Derro,” but ch28 says nothing about the Derro — no confirmation whether this goal is still active or abandoned.  
- Ambiguity: Ch19 claims “some serve the Deepking, others serve their own interests,” but ch28 says they are a “counterbalance to the Deepking’s power” — unclear whether this means they oppose him, merely constrain him, or are split internally.  
- Unclear attribution: No confirmation whether the forged passes are sanctioned by the faction (as a tolerated flaw) or are purely the result of individual corruption.
