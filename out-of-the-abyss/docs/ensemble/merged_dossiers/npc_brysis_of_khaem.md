### Brysis of Khaem

**Current status:** Alive (as a wraith-bound consciousness)  
**Current location:** Inside her floating tomb, Netherese origin  
**Allegiance/faction:** Netherese noble sorceress (self-preserved via necromantic binding)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Tomb contains a stone diorama of herself, murals of floating Netherese cities  
**Spells / feats / wounds:** Bound to the tomb by necromantic magic; maintains control over undead retainers  
**Current assignment / role:** Guardian of her own tomb; seeks to escape her prison through the deaths of intruders  
**Defining recent actions:** Communicates telepathically via her Wraith servant, who declares intent to use intruders’ deaths to break her binding.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Her consciousness persists through necromantic enchantment; she views the deaths of those who enter her tomb as the key to her liberation.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Brysis’s consciousness is fully contained within the Wraith or if she exists as a separate spectral entity controlling it — the Wraith speaks for her, but the diorama and murals imply her preserved identity is distinct.
- The exact nature of her “prison” (whether it is the tomb itself, her physical remains, or a magical ward) is not specified.
- It is ambiguous whether the Spectres and Wraith are her original undead retainers or were summoned later — though they are bound to her will, their origin is not confirmed.
