### Resonant Slime (The Burning Wizard Inn)

- **What it is**: A vial of magical substance.
- **Current holder/location**: Held by Glabbagool (accepted as a symbolic gesture of recognition).
- **Current condition**: Intact, presented as a formal token.
- **Significance**: A symbolic affirmation of the gelatinous cube’s sentience, produced by the xenobiology sage after being satisfied by Thorin’s defense of the cube’s personhood.
- **Producer**: The xenobiology sage.
- **Key relationship**: Presented by the xenobiology sage to Glabbagool as institutional recognition of the gelatinous cube’s sentience.

## Uncertainty
- The exact physical location of the vial within the inn is not specified — only that it was accepted by Glabbagool.
- It is unclear whether the vial is currently on Glabbagool’s person, in their quarters, or stored elsewhere.
- The phrase “produced by Glabbagool” in one fact contradicts the others stating it was produced by the sage; the sage is named as the producer in two sources, and Glabbagool is the recipient — so the claim that Glabbagool produced it is likely erroneous or misattributed. The later, more consistent facts attribute production to the sage.
