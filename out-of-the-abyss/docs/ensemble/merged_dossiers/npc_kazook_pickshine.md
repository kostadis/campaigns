### Kazook Pickshine

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone  
**Allegiance/faction:** Representative of the Miners Guild; local alchemist  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None explicitly stated; known to possess alchemical expertise and access to spell gems and rare lichen (Ghost Hold Lichen).  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Official representative of the Miners Guild, addressing logistical vulnerabilities in Blingdenstone’s armaments.  
- Conducting alchemical research focused on acid-protection solutions and rare lichen for potions.  
- Recently sent scout Bimble to investigate the Garden of Stone in the Rock Blight to procure Ghost Hold Lichen.  
- Bimble is a close personal friend, not merely an assistant; Kazook is deeply distressed by Bimble’s infection and madness.  
- Maintains reputation as a skilled alchemist capable of enhancing spell gems into magically powerful ones.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Sent scout Bimble to the Rock Blight to retrieve Ghost Hold Lichen for alchemical use.  
- Received Bimble back infected and raving about a singing garden and the “Lady of Rot feeding the soil.”  
- Appealed to the party for compassion after they mocked Bimble’s condition, revealing deep personal attachment.  
- Previously proposed two contingency plans to mitigate acidic damage to gnomish weapons and armor: sourcing ingredients from Neverlight Grove or trading salt in Gracklstugh.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Kazook currently possesses any of the Ghost Hold Lichen retrieved by Bimble, or if it was taken from Bimble after his infection.  
- The exact nature of Kazook’s alchemical spells or feats is not specified — only his skill and role are confirmed.  
- While he previously suggested trading salt in Gracklstugh, it is unknown whether any such trade has occurred since, or if Gracklstugh’s destruction (mentioned in ch39) has altered his plans.  
- It is ambiguous whether Kazook still holds authority over other guild members beyond his alchemical role, or if his influence has shifted since Bimble’s return.
