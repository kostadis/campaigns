### Buppido

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Corpse found in the derro hideout beneath the West Cleft  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly affiliated with the Council of Savants; self-proclaimed divine prophet of the “Divine Plan”  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (corpse; last known to carry a shortsword with bloodstains, possibly a hand-held crossbow — both unconfirmed in final state)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** None (deceased); last known to be seeking to return to Gracklstugh to “save his people” from the Council of Savants, with claimed ties to underground contacts there. Formerly traveled with Daz, Thorin, Eldeth, Zalthir, Jimjar, Shuushar, and Grygum — all of whom are now separated or deceased.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Murdered and dismembered Pelek (his former business partner and supplier) to eliminate competition and seize control of smuggling routes.  
- Constructed a macabre shrine of humanoid bones and skeletons in the caverns beneath the West Cleft.  
- Declared himself a divine figure fulfilling a divine mission to save the Derro people, rejecting the Council of Savants as corrupt.  
- Attacked the party with manic violence before being slain by Daz’s magic missiles.  
- After consuming the Tongue of Madness, revealed unfiltered, bitter truths about the party members — criticizing Daz’s breath, Thorin’s vanity, and Eldeth’s disinterest in him — before pleading for mercy.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believed himself divinely chosen after the Faerzress glowed and dimmed, triggering a psychotic break.  
- Was a former merchant and courier for the Council of Savants, smuggling goods to evade Duergar taxes.  
- Murdered Pelek not only for profit, but to assert autonomy and eliminate a rival.  
- His “Divine Plan” was a delusion masking ambition, trauma, and madness — later acknowledged by him in his final moments: *“I was mad. I have learned from Shuushar that I was mad.”*  
- The Flumph in ch18 confirms Buppido was once a rational Derro who complained about the Council’s tyranny — his descent into madness began after exposure to psychic energy from the Council of Savants.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: [ch17] and [ch18] conflict on Buppido’s race — ch17 identifies him as a Derro; ch18 calls him a Flumph. The later and more detailed ch17 account (including murder of Pelek, shrine construction, and death by magic missiles) is more consistent with all other facts and is treated as authoritative; ch18 may be metaphorical, misremembered, or refer to a different entity.  
- Unclear attribution: [ch17] states Buppido killed Pelek; [ch37] confirms this. But [ch17] also says he “dismembered Grygum” — yet earlier chapters (ch08) show Grygum alive and trapped under rocks, and later ch12 shows Grygum alive and traveling with the group. This suggests either a misattribution in ch17 or a second derro named Grygum. Since Grygum is confirmed alive past ch08 and appears in ch12, the claim that Buppido killed Grygum in ch17 is likely erroneous or refers to another individual.  
- Ambiguous timeline: Was Buppido’s death in ch17 or ch37? Ch17 describes the attack and death; ch37 merely confirms the act of killing Pelek. No later chapter contradicts ch17’s account of his death, so ch17 is treated as final.  
- Unclear possession: Whether Buppido still carried the crossbow or shortsword at time of death is not confirmed in final state — only that blood was on the shortsword earlier.  
- Unclear origin of “Diinkarazan”: [ch06] implies Buppido believes himself to be the incarnate god Diinkarazan, but no other chapter confirms this name or its significance — it is not referenced again.
