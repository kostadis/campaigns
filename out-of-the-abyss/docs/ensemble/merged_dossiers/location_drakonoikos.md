### Drakonoikos

Drakonoikos is a monumental structure built from the scales and bones of a dragon of great age and significance. Its walls are composed of enormous, overlapping scales and worked bones, replacing conventional stone. At sunrise, the scales catch and hold the light, gleaming visibly. The building embodies Candlekeep’s philosophy of transforming lethal threats into vessels of knowledge, reflecting a monastic logic — that dangerous things contain the wisdom of their own defeat. It is implied to hold hidden archives or magical resonance relevant to the murder and follows the pattern of other significant Candlekeep structures like the Astronomicon and Whispering Dome.
