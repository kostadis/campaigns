### Darklake

**Current status:** Active, contested, and corrupted subterranean waterway.  
**Current location:** Underdark, spanning over a hundred miles, bordering Sloobludop to the north, adjacent to the Shattered Spire (~40 feet offshore), and lying between Gracklstugh and Blingdenstone.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Controlled by no single faction, but spiritually and tactically central to the Deep Father cult; heavily influenced by Demogorgon’s emergence and presence.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** N/A (location).  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as the primary transit route between Gracklstugh and Blingdenstone.  
- Acts as the ritual and sacrificial heart of the Deep Father cult, where bloody chum is cast into its waters and consumed by an unseen entity.  
- Now infested with demonic manta-rays—predatory, flesh-rending creatures descended from or corrupted by Demogorgon’s emergence.  
- The water’s surface is frequently foamy and oily, with signs of violent subaquatic activity.  
- The Shattered Spire, a key landmark, stands on a stalagmite 40 feet from its shore.  
- Recently crossed by the party en route to Blingdenstone, leaving them drenched and exhausted.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Demogorgon emerged from its depths during a Kuo-toan ritual at the altar, causing the collapse of Kuo-toan societal order and the corruption of its ecosystem.  
- The water now teems with demonic manta-rays that hunt travelers and prey on any living thing near the surface.  
- The Darklake has become a refuge and escape route for fleeing parties, including the group that escaped Gracklstugh after being framed by the Gray Ghosts.  
- The Deep Father cult continues to make blood offerings, though their rituals are now likely disrupted or perverted by the demon’s influence.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The Darklake is not merely a body of water but a living conduit—its depths are unfathomable, its surface lacks a conventional boundary, and it appears to be a vessel or gateway for primordial or divine entities.  
- The "something" that consumes the bloody chum may be Demogorgon himself, or a lesser entity bound to him.  
- The concept of the Overbright was carried through the Darklake, implying it holds metaphysical or symbolic weight beyond its physical form.
