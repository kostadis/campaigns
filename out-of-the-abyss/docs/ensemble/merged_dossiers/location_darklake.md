### Darklake

- **Current status:** Active, accessible, and traversed.  
- **Current location:** Lies beyond the slums of Gracklstugh, on the path to Blingdenstone. The party recently arrived at its shores after crossing its cold waters.  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Controlled by no single faction, but spiritually and tactically contested. Formerly the center of the Deep Father cult’s rituals; now corrupted by Demogorgon’s influence and the collapse of Kuo-toan order.  
- **Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (location).  
- **Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
  - Serves as the primary waterway route between Gracklstugh and Blingdenstone.  
  - Acts as a conduit for the party’s journey; recently crossed by them, leaving them drenched and exhausted.  
  - Borders the Shattered Spire (40 feet offshore), a landmark structure.  
  - Associated with the Gray Ghosts (hunted by their agents) and the Deepking’s forces.  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Recently crossed by the party, who traveled through its cold, murky waters to reach Blingdenstone’s shores.  
  - Infested with demonic manta-ray predators that rend flesh — remnants of Demogorgon’s emergence and the collapse of Kuo-toan society.  
  - Surface remains murky and foamy in places, with echoes of ritual violence and unseen predation.  
  - Once site of Deep Father blood offerings; now haunted by the aftermath of Demogorgon’s rise and the destruction of Sloobludop.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact current state of the Deep Father cult’s presence is unclear — they were active in ch09 and ch11, but no later chapter confirms their continued operation.  
- Whether the demonic manta rays are still actively hunting along the entire stretch or are concentrated near former ritual sites (e.g., Sloobludop) is unspecified.  
- It is unclear if the “unseen entity” consuming bloody chum (ch09) still exists or if it was Demogorgon himself.  
- The phrase “Dark Lake” in ch12 and ch35 is capitalized differently from other entries — whether this denotes a distinct location or is a stylistic inconsistency is ambiguous.  
- The nature of the “cold waters” in ch35 is not clarified — are they unusually frigid due to depth, magic, or recent events?
