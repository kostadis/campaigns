### Nilhogg's Nose

- **Type:** Magical mushroom  
- **Current location:** In Zalthir’s possession (last seen being collected by him)  
- **Current condition:** Multiple specimens gathered; intact and edible  
- **Properties:**  
  - Enhances the sense of smell, making pleasant scents vividly perceptible  
  - Intensifies pain from unpleasant odors to severe levels  
  - Provides no nourishment; does not fill the eater  
- **Notable recent action:** Zalthir collected several specimens

- Whether any have been consumed since collection is unknown
