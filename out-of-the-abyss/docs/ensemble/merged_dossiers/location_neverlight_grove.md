### Neverlight Grove

**Current status:** Corrupted, contested, and actively dangerous.  
**Current location:** Deep Underdark, accessible via a dried underground stream tunnel; connected to the Northern Terraces, central basin, and fungal forests.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Dominated by Sovereign Phylo and the Circle of Welcome; influenced by Zuggtmoy’s corruption via the fungal artifact.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Hosts the **mushroom artifact** (over 20 feet tall, branching stalks, carved trunk windows), radiating decay and unnatural luminescence — the mechanism of corruption.  
- Fungal forests cover ceiling, walls, and ground, emitting bioluminescent light in blues, greens, and purples.  
- Central basin contains a clear pool.  
- Terraces along northern walls are saturated with **faerzress**.  
- **Zurkhwoods** move erratically throughout the grove.  
- **Glowing myconids** are present, some under Phylo’s control, others corrupted.  
- **Acid-resistant fungal ingredients** are still present and harvestable.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as the corrupted heart of myconid civilization, now a site of ideological and spiritual fracture between Phylo’s regime and surviving loyalists.  
- Functions as a nexus of demonic and fungal corruption, channeling Zuggtmoy’s influence through the artifact.  
- Acts as a waypoint and obstacle on the party’s path to the surface — negotiated, fought through, and lied through.  
- Draws beings through compulsion, as with Sarith and Thorin.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- The mushroom artifact has fully activated, causing fungal mutations, shifting tunnels, hostile light, and spore-infused madness.  
- Myconid leadership is fractured; Phylo enforces the Circle of Welcome, while remnants of the original colony (e.g., Grygum, Rump-a-dump) are displaced or silenced.  
- The party collected acid-resistant ingredients from the grove for potion crafting.  
- The grove was traversed and survived by the party as part of their ascent to the surface, marked by violence, deception, and negotiation.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The grove was once a serene, bioluminescent sanctuary for myconids — now a cathedral of corrupted life.  
- The corruption is not merely ecological but spiritual: the artifact channels Zuggtmoy’s will, turning the grove into a living engine of transformation and madness.  
- Faerzress and fungal growths are intertwined, enabling hidden transmissions and unnatural control.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between "demon-haunted" (ch29) and "myconid-dominated fungal ecosystem" (ch31–52): Is the "mighty demon" a separate entity, or is Phylo/Zuggtmoy’s artifact being misidentified as a demon?  
- Unclear if the "Grick corpse" (ch31) is still present or has been removed.  
- Unclear if Dawnbringer is still present in the grove — last seen enjoying it (ch32), but no later confirmation of her location.  
- Unclear if the "Circle of the Builders" or "Gasbide" still operate within the grove — mentioned in ch31 but not referenced again.  
- The nature of the "unwilling vows" (ch29) tied to the grove is never explained — no actor or mechanism identified.  
- Whether the "dancing myconid" (ch32) linked to Zuggtmoy’s melody still exists or has been consumed by the artifact is unconfirmed.

### Neverlight Grove

**Current status:** Active but corrupted faction under spiritual and biological siege.  
**Current location:** Unspecified, but centered within its original myconid colony territory.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly allied with Ilvara; alliance dissolved without notice due to Zuggtmoy’s influence. Now functionally under the dominion of Zuggtmoy’s corrupting power, with no active external alliances.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Sovereign Phylo is corrupted and has restructured the social order into multiple functional Circles.  
- Basidia, the true ruler, remains powerless to act without risking further infection.  
- The colony is internally fractured, with social order replaced by hierarchical Circles serving under Phylo’s corrupted rule.  
- Attempted diplomatic outreach to Ilvara failed; correspondence was unanswered due to Ilvara’s disappearance under Zuggtmoy’s influence.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully severed all formal ties with Ilvara through abandonment of correspondence, indicating Zuggtmoy’s influence overrode prior diplomatic commitments.  
- Maintained internal structure through the Circle system, enforcing biological and spiritual control under the corrupted Phylo.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The colony’s survival is now secondary to the spread of corruption; preservation efforts are subverted by Zuggtmoy’s will.  
- Basidia’s inaction is not indifference but a calculated restraint to prevent wider infection — implying a hidden, desperate attempt at containment.

## Uncertainty
- The current physical location of the Neverlight Grove colony is not specified beyond its original territory.  
- The exact nature of the “Circles” (their functions, leadership, or membership) is not detailed beyond their existence.  
- Whether Basidia is still alive, conscious, or physically present is implied but not confirmed.  
- Whether Phylo is still the de facto leader or merely a puppet of Zuggtmoy is unclear — his corruption is stated, but the degree of autonomy or control is ambiguous.  
- The letter sent to Ilvara is confirmed, but its content and whether it was delivered or intercepted remain unknown.
