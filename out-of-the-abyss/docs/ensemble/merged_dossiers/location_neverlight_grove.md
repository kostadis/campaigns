### Neverlight Grove

**Current status:** Corrupted, active site of fungal and demonic entanglement.  
**Current location:** Subterranean cavern system in the Underdark, accessible via a dried underground stream tunnel.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Dominated by Sovereign Phylo and the Circle of Welcome; under latent influence of Zuggtmoy. Formerly home to a peaceful myconid colony.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Contains the **mushroom artifact** — a 20+ foot tall, branching fungal entity named Yggmorgus — with carved trunk windows radiating unnatural luminescence and decay.  
- The **central basin** holds a clear pool, surrounded by fungal mounds and bioluminescent growths.  
- Walls, ceiling, and ground are covered in **living bioluminescent fungi** emitting a mosaic of blues, greens, and purples.  
- **Faerzress** saturates the Northern Terraces and terraced walls.  
- **Zurkhwoods** move erratically throughout the grove.  
- **Spore-infused madness** and **unnatural fungal growths** are spreading.  
- The grove’s environment is **“singing”** — a perceptual distortion consistent with Zuggtmoy’s influence.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as the **epicenter of fungal corruption** and the source of the mushroom artifact that enabled widespread spiritual and physical decay.  
- Once a cultural and spiritual heart of myconid civilization; now a site of ideological fracture between Phylo’s faction and Basidia’s remnants.  
- Was a node of **political correspondence** with Ilvara — now inactive due to her disappearance under Zuggtmoy’s influence.  
- **Destination of compulsion** for certain individuals (e.g., Sarith, Thorin) drawn by demonic resonance.  
- **Required source** for ingredients to craft acid-protection potions — indicating its fungi retain utilizable, though dangerous, properties.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- The mushroom artifact from Neverlight Grove was the mechanism of corruption across the Underdark.  
- The grove’s environment has begun “singing,” a perceptual disruption now manifesting in others (e.g., Elin).  
- Myconids are experiencing transformation, madness, and fracturing leadership; the Circle of Welcome is expanding.  
- The grove’s light, once cheery and vibrant, is becoming **hostile** — a sign of spiritual decay.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Neverlight Grove was not merely corrupted — it was **engineered** as a nexus for Zuggtmoy’s influence, using the myconids’ natural symbiosis with fungi as a vector.  
- Its bioluminescence and faerzress are not natural phenomena but conduits for demonic resonance.  
- The grove’s “singing” is a manifestation of Zuggtmoy’s will echoing through fungal networks — a signal, not a symptom.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between “serene, glowing cavern” (ch27) and “corrupted, singing, hostile light” (ch32–55): later chapters override earlier, so current state is corrupted — but the *transition point* of when the light turned hostile is unspecified.  
- Attribution of demonic presence: Zalthir (ch29) calls it a “mighty demon,” while later facts point to Zuggtmoy’s spores and artifact as the source — no direct confirmation that a physical demon resides there now, only that Zuggtmoy’s influence is dominant.  
- Ilvara’s exact connection to Neverlight Grove is unclear — was she a resident, envoy, or just a correspondent? Her disappearance is noted, but her prior role in the grove is not.  
- The “dancing myconid” (ch32) is mentioned as linked to Zuggtmoy’s melody, but its current status or location within the grove is unknown.  
- Whether the central pool or Yggmorgus is actively sentient or merely a vessel is not confirmed — only that it radiates decay and luminescence.  
- The “dried underground stream” tunnel is the known entrance, but are there other access points? Unclear.  
- The “Circle of the Builders” and “Northern Terraces” are named, but their current operational status (active, abandoned, or corrupted) is not specified.

### Neverlight Grove

A myconid colony under spiritual and biological siege, currently governed by a corrupted sovereign, Phylo, who has restructured social order into functional Circles. The true ruler, Basidia, remains powerless to act without risking further infection. The colony was originally in peaceful harmony until Voosbur’s spores disrupted it, and the infection continues to threaten its survival. Recent attempts at political correspondence with Ilvara failed due to her being pulled away by Zuggtmoy’s influence.

## Uncertainty
- The current status of Basidia (alive? confined? mentally intact?) is implied but not confirmed beyond “powerless to act.”
- The exact nature of the “infection” (biological, magical, or both) is not specified beyond its link to Voosbur’s spores.
- Whether the Circle of Masters is a formal faction within the colony or merely a descriptor of its new social structure is ambiguous.
- It is unclear if the political correspondence attempt was initiated by Basidia, Phylo, or another myconid — attribution is unspoken.
