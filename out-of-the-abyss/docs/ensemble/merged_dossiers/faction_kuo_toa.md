### Kuo-toa

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Near the Darklake, in proximity to a ritual altar  
**Allegiance/faction:** Servants of the Deep Father, under direct ritual authority of the archpriest (Ploopploopeen)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Spears (used to prod escapees)  
**Current assignment / role:** Ritual enforcers — chant in circles around the altar, wade through the Darklake as part of ceremony, and herd escapees toward a grated depression using spears  
**Defining recent actions:** Participated in a formal escort to Ploopploopeen’s quarters (ch10); now actively enforcing a ritual by prodding escapees (Zalthir and others) toward a grate in the Darklake (ch11)  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Serve as ceremonial instruments of divine discipline; their actions are tied to the worship and ritual demands of the Deep Father, with no indication of independent will.

## Uncertainty
- The exact number of kuo-toa currently active in the ritual is unknown — twelve were present in ch10, but ch11 does not specify if it is the same group or a different contingent.  
- Whether the monitor from ch10 is still leading them in ch11 is unconfirmed.  
- It is unclear if all kuo-toa in the ritual are identical in role or if some have specialized duties beyond prodding and chanting.
