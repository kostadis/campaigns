### Trillimac Books (cage chamber)

- **What it is:** A set of ancient, bound volumes bound in Trillimac, housed in Narrak’s ritual chamber.
- **Current condition:** Intact and present in the cage chamber; contain legible heretical texts.
- **Content:** Treatises on Demogorgon’s influence, including:
  - *The Spiraling Descent: A Treatise on Duality and Madness*
  - *The Prince's Gift: On the Creation of Favored Spawn*
  - *Rituals of the Two-Headed Beast*
- **Significance:** Irrefutable evidence of Demogorgon’s influence on the Derro; sought by Errde Blackstaff for ideological validation.
- **Current location:** Narrak’s ritual chamber (cage chamber).
- **Current holder/possessor:** Unoccupied — not in personal possession of any character; physically located in the chamber.
