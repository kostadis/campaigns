### Wax Pouch Contents (Menzoberranzan)

- **What it is:** A sealed wax pouch containing theological writings.
- **Current holder/location:** Unspecified, but last observed in Menzoberranzan context.
- **Current condition:** Sealed, intact, containing written pages.

The argument is internally consistent but logically contorted, starting from a desired conclusion and working backward.
