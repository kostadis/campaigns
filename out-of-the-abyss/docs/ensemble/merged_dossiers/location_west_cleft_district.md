### West Cleft District

The West Cleft District is a heavily fortified entrance to Gracklstugh, now patrolled by Duergar guards and serving as a high-surveillance chokepoint. Once an abandoned slum inhabited by Derro, it has been reclaimed and militarized: a former Duergar guardhouse is now actively occupied, with visible and invisible sentries, identity checks, and demands for bribes. Wanted posters for the Ember Vanguard are prominently displayed. The district is no longer a chaotic Derro slum — the Derro have been cleared or pushed back, and the area is under strict Duergar control. The hidden fissure used by Droki still exists beneath it, but access is now monitored or blocked by the new guard infrastructure. The atmosphere is one of controlled menace — “a gauntlet where power, politics, and peril converge.”

## Uncertainty
- The current presence or status of Derro within the district is unclear — they are said to have “thinned out,” but it is unknown if any remain hidden, displaced, or exterminated.
- The exact fate of Droki’s hidden fissure is unconfirmed — while it is noted to still exist, it is unknown if it is still usable, guarded, or sealed.
- Whether the Duergar guardhouse was repurposed from the same structure previously used to oversee Derro slaves, or if it is a new or rebuilt facility, is ambiguous.
- The timeline of the transition from Derro slum to Duergar checkpoint is not precisely dated — only that it occurred between ch17 and ch28.
