### Errde Blackskull

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Within the Deepking’s city, likely in her command post or office (last referenced as receiving reports from Stone Guards)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Captain of the Stone Guards, agent of the Overlake Hold, ostensibly loyal to the Deepking’s court  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Head of the Stone Guards, directing investigations into corruption linked to Droki, the Council of Savants, and the Gray Ghosts.  
- Actively seeking physical evidence of the Council of Savants’ involvement in city-wide madness and the “Empty Scabbard Killers.”  
- Publicly denies the existence of psychic assassins; privately directs inquiries into the unexplained deaths of two clan members.  
- Manipulates outsiders (including the party) to gather intelligence, offering freedom and protection in exchange for proof.  
- Politically isolated: avoids direct confrontation with Derro, refuses to send troops into Derro slums, and is easily swayed by narrative manipulation (e.g., by Daz and Grygum).  
- Obsessed with destroying the Council of Savants to reclaim power, driven by paranoia and megalomania.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Continues to demand proof of Council of Savants’ corruption from the party.  
- Has ordered Stone Guards to monitor and report the party’s movements (ch29).  
- Maintains control over access to armory resources and the fate of captured Drow.  
- Uses layered political analogies to deflect, delay, and control narratives around the city’s unrest.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Secretly believes the Deepking is being corrupted by the Council of Savants — a belief used to justify her power grab.  
- Her paranoia is not merely suspicion but a tool of control; she selectively interprets evidence to fit her narrative.  
- Her refusal to act against Derro slums is strategic cowardice, not prudence — she fears losing authority if she fails.  
- Her offer of freedom to the party is a transactional lever — not a promise, but a conditional bargaining chip.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch15] describing her as “emotionally controlled” and [ch19] labeling her as “consumed by paranoia and megalomania” — whether this is progression or conflicting interpretation is unclear.  
- Unclear if her “loss of two clan members” refers to personal relatives or subordinates — no clarification on relationship.  
- [ch16] and [ch18] attribute “Empty Scabbard Killers” to psychic assassins or government agents, but [ch19] does not resolve whether she believes in them — only that she uses the term strategically.  
- [ch29] implies she is still active and receiving reports, but does not confirm her physical location or whether she has moved from her previous command post.  
- No definitive confirmation whether she still holds the authority to grant “free and safe passage out of the city” — this offer was made in ch15 and never rescinded, but context has shifted.
