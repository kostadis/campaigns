### Kalan Strongbranch

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Candlekeep, overseeing the investigation from a position of restored authority  
**Allegiance/faction:** Reinstated Head of the Avowed; operates under Candlekeep’s institutional framework, though personally antagonistic toward Bookwyrm  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated, but no longer holds the second key to the Hightower Library (transferred to an unnamed third party); no longer carries the summoning sapphire key (stolen and linked to his murder attempt)  
**Notable spells/feats:** Sole known caster of the truth-seeking spell that previously anchored the investigation; refuses to cast Zone of Truth without consent, citing fear of political reprisal if Bookwyrm becomes his superior  
**Current assignment / role:** Head of the Avowed, leading the official investigation into Janussi’s death; tasked with ensuring the adventurers’ testimony is included in his final report; reassigned to escort prisoners to Deneir’s Sanctum (prior role)  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully lobbied for reinstatement after being fired for failing to solve Janussi’s murder.  
- Orchestrated a politically cautious campaign to expose Bookwyrm using institutional procedures and Avowed voting dynamics, avoiding direct confrontation.  
- Demanded, publicly and without subterfuge, that Bookwyrm submit to a Zone of Truth spell — a move framed as procedural integrity but rooted in self-preservation.  
- Accepted credit for the investigation’s resolution despite the adventurers solving the case, promising their voices would be heard in his report.  
- Refused to act covertly, insisting on formal channels, even when it hindered progress.  
- Developed and applied his “Threefold Proof” methodology — previously dismissed by Janussi as solipsism — as the foundation of the current investigation.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Motivated by fear of Bookwyrm becoming his superior; his adherence to procedure is a shield against her authority.  
- Believes patience and institutional leverage are the “clever play” — not force or direct confrontation.  
- Seeks to find a book that proves Bookwyrm’s culpability in Janussi’s death, intending to use it after the naming ceremony.  
- Views himself as a scholar and truth-seeker, but his actions are deeply entangled with personal ambition and resentment over being dismissed as a “clown” and “third-rate” by Janussi and Sylvira.  

## Uncertainty

The facts describe him as “Head of the Avowed” and “in charge of the investigation,” but earlier facts call him “Gate Warden.” The later facts override, but the title “Gate Warden” is not explicitly revoked — ambiguity remains whether he holds both roles or was reassigned.  
- Unclear attribution: The fact that he “handed the key to someone else” is repeated, but the recipient is never named — is this the same person who later stole the summoning sapphire? Or a different act?  
- Ambiguous chronology: Was he fired before or after transferring the key? The key transfer and firing are both described in ch58–ch61, but the sequence is not pinned.  
- Unclear current status of the summoning sapphire: It was stolen and linked to his attempted murder, but it is not confirmed whether he still possesses it or if it was recovered.  
- Unclear: Is the “truth-seeking spell” the same as “Zone of Truth”? The text treats them as distinct — one is a prior investigative anchor, the other is a spell he refuses to cast without consent — but their nature and relationship are not clarified.  
- Contradiction: One fact says he was “fired from his position after failing to solve the case,” another says he was “reinstated.” This is consistent if reinstated after firing — but no source confirms the reinstatement was official or permanent.  
- Unclear: The phrase “Kalan Strongbranch was the First Reader” is repeated, but no definition is given of what “First Reader” means — is it a title, a role, or a historical designation?
