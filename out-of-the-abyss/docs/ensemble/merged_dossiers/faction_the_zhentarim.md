### The Zhentarim

**Current status:** Active and enduring faction.  
**Current standing:** Recognized as a ruthless, effective organization whose survival is rooted in pragmatic, unethical adaptability.  
**Current goals:** To persist and exert influence through methods others deem unacceptable — survival and dominance via moral flexibility.  
**Key philosophy:** “Doing what others won’t” is not merely a tactic but a core survival principle, now internalized by Daz and adopted as a legitimate framework for combat and strategy in the Underdark.  
**Recent actions:** Their tactics have transitioned from being viewed as merely unethical to being accepted — however uncomfortably — as a necessary and effective paradigm for survival in the Underdark.  
**Relationships:** Opposed by “decent people,” but their influence is spreading through ideological adoption, particularly among survivors like Daz.
