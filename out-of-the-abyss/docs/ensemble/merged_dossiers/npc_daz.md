### Daz

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Candlekeep, engaged in investigation and research  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Formerly associated with the Ember Vanguard; now operates as an independent investigator with no formal allegiance, though retains tactical alignment with his party (Zalthir, Grygum, Thorin).  

**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Stonespeaker Crystal (used to communicate with beasts, notably Glabbagool)  
- Dawnbringer (sentient sunblade, now under his care; he is researching its sentience and beauty)  
- Ebonmire Spellbook (expanded his spell selection and access)  
- Collection of official Duergar faction badges (for bureaucratic manipulation)  
- Copy of Milo Goodbarrel’s novel (used as a reference for psychic blade theory and political metaphors)  
- Journal (daily entries since escape from Velkynvelve; meticulously documents observations, faction dynamics, and personal reflections)  
- Key (formerly owned by Kalan Strongbranch; inert to magical detection, targeted by helmed horrors)  

**Notable Spells / Feats:**  
- **Feats:** Fey Touched (grants two additional prepared spells and free castings), Warcaster (ensures concentration durability)  
- **Spells:** Magic Missile (primary offensive), Shape Water (tactical environmental control), Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp (area denial), Fireball (precision-sculpted), Misty Step (escape utility), Scorching Ray, Toll the Dead, Dancing Lights, Tasha’s Caustic Brew, Hypnotic Pattern, and others from Ebonmire’s spellbook  
- **Tactics:** Uses “K’tharr-li” (a Drow grid-based perception game) to locate hidden threats; employs Sculpt Spells to avoid allies during area attacks  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **Role:** De facto lead investigator in the murder of Janussi at Candlekeep; tactical strategist and spellcaster for the party.  
- **Key Relationships:**  
  - **Grygum & Zalthir:** Lieutenants who can issue orders; trusted allies with complementary skills.  
  - **Thorin:** Tactical partner; shares responsibility for mission execution.  
  - **Glabbagool:** Gelatinous familiar bound to him; source of non-verbal intelligence and emotional anchor.  
  - **Asha Vandree:** Formerly believed he was a Lolth-follower; now distrusts him but acknowledges his tactical value.  
  - **A’lai:** Drow scholar who may hold critical information about who is paying to protect Daz; Daz’s primary target for intel extraction.  
  - **Sylvira Savikas:** Witness and victim of framing; Daz elicited her full cooperation through empathetic interrogation.  
  - **Bookwyrm:** Librarian and suspect; Daz detected her relief as a tell of deception.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Successfully interrogated Sylvira Savikas, uncovering her framing and identifying Kazryn Nyantani as a suspect.  
- Identified the helmed horrors’ true target (the key in his pocket), survived their assault using Scorching Ray, and transferred the key to Grygum.  
- Refused Bookwyrm’s tea as a symbolic act of distrust, reinforcing his reputation for skepticism.  
- Declined to cure Daral Yashenti immediately, still weighing moral and tactical consequences.  
- Proposed a “48-hour play” interrogation tactic: withholding critical details to provoke deceptive or honest responses.  
- Initiated research into magical swords, particularly Dawnbringer’s sentience and beauty, as a new scholarly focus.  
- Maintained daily journal entries, analyzing factional motives, debts, and psychological patterns with clinical precision.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- His true name is likely a diminutive of **Daz’issin** — a maternal name used in Menzoberranzan to protect sons believed to be at risk of early death; only a handful of uses exist in the last 300 years, and all associated houses are extinct.  
- He is the subject of a high-value mercenary contract from House T’sarran to extract him alive — yet he has no memory of why he is valuable.  
- He suffers from a growing pressure in his head, linked to the same Underdark corruption affecting Elin; he is a secondary recipient of the fungal/spore influence.  
- He has no noble house affiliation, no lineage, and no strategic value in Drow society — yet someone powerful has spent immense resources to keep him alive.  
- He rejects supernatural explanations for corruption, instead viewing it as systemic, political, and exploitative — a lens honed by Menzoberranzan’s brutal politics.  
- He has found purpose not in survival, but in precision: “Precision is not the opposite of force. It is force, directed.”  
- He believes the “heart of the Lady’s dream” is a place where power and corruption are inseparable — and he is now caught within it.  

## Uncertainty  
- Contradiction: Daz is referred to as both a Drow and a dwarf in different entries. Later entries (ch55, ch59, ch60) definitively establish him as a Drow apostate from Menzoberranzan; the dwarf references are likely errors or misattributions.  
- Ambiguous Chronology: The exact timing of his infection by Ilvara’s spores is unclear — he shows visible alteration by ch50, but earlier entries (ch33, ch49) suggest prior exposure. It is unclear whether this is a gradual infection or a sudden transformation.  
- Unclear Attribution: Some feats and spells (e.g., Fey Touched, Misty Step, Sculpt Spells) are described as chosen or used, but the exact moment of acquisition or training is not specified.  
- Ambiguous Current State: It is unclear whether Glabbagool is still physically bonded to him after the events of ch61, or if the ooze’s role has evolved into a purely psychological or symbolic one.  
- Unresolved Motive: The identity of the patron paying Menzoberranzan rates to protect Daz remains unknown — A’lai is the only lead, but his knowledge is not yet confirmed or revealed.  
- Contradictory Identity: Daz claims to be from Menzoberranzan, but Zalthir doubts his truthfulness (ch16); later entries confirm he is a Drow apostate from Menzoberranzan — so the doubt was unfounded, but the contradiction remains in the record.  
- Uncertain Physical Condition: Daz was “one bad swing from the floor” after the helmed horrors attack (ch61); his current injury status is unspecified.
