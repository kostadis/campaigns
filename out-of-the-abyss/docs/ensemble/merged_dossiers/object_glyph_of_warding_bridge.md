### Glyph of Warding (bridge)

- **Type:** Magical trap (object)  
- **Current location:** Inscribed into the stone of the bridge.  
- **Current condition:** Active and untriggered since detonation; remains physically embedded in the bridge stone.  
- **Trigger:** Hostile creature crossing the bridge.  
- **Effect:** Detonates with force and light, dealing 21 damage in a sphere (confirmed active and functional once).  
- **Current holder/location:** Fixed in place on the bridge; not carried or moved.  
- **Creation:** Inscribed into the bridge stone by the party, following Grygum’s idea.  
- **Purpose:** Tactical ambush weapon, designed to exploit chaotic battlefield conditions and overconfidence.  
- **Recent action:** Activated once, destroying one spore servant and crippling Jorlan.  

## Uncertainty
- Whether the Glyph remains *functionally intact* after detonation — the fact that it fired once does not confirm whether it is still active, depleted, or destroyed as a magical construct.  
- Whether “hostile creature crossing” is still the only trigger, or if the detonation altered its parameters (no post-activation description provided).  
- Attribution of the physical act of inscribing: One quote says “He moved to the crossing...” (ambiguous “he”), another says “the placement was all of us” — exact individual responsible for the carving is unclear.
