### Kalan

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Outside the interrogation room, monitoring Alkrist under a truth spell  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former gate warden of the Hightower Library; now acting as an independent moral authority with vested interest in exposing the conspiracy  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Empty locket (formerly held the second key to the Hightower Library); no longer in physical possession of the key  
**Current assignment / role:** Maintaining a truth spell on Alkrist; acting as the moral anchor and strategic advisor to the group; seeking accountability through non-traditional means  
**Key relationships:**  
- Was fired and framed by Janussi  
- Now works in tandem with the investigators, having shifted from suspect to guiding figure  
- Has no current allegiance to the Hightower Library’s new leadership  
- Endorses Thorin’s plan to access the book via the new head of Candlekeep as the true instrument of accountability  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Relinquished his key to an unknown third party, as he claims: “I have given it now to someone who is not me.”  
- Demonstrated the locket-carrying method with an empty locket, confirming the key is no longer in his possession.  
- Actively sustains a truth spell on Alkrist, ensuring truthful testimony without Alkrist’s knowledge.  
- Shifted from skepticism about spell-based evidence to championing Thorin’s plan for accountability via Candlekeep’s new leadership.  
- Posed the pivotal moral question: “What do you do with the evidence you've collected?” — catalyzing the group’s ethical pivot from justice to strategic silence.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes the theft and deletion were carried out by a magic user capable of “talking any lock open,” arcane or mundane.  
- Holds the most complete account of the conspiracy due to being fired and framed.  
- Believes the arrival of the investigators was not accidental — “fate had brought disinterested outsiders to exactly this place, and that this meant something.”  

## Uncertainty
- Who received the second key from Kalan? He says “someone who is not me,” but the identity is not revealed.  
- Whether Kalan is still officially employed or has any formal standing with the Hightower Library after being fired and framed.  
- Whether the locket’s emptiness is due to him giving the key away, losing it, or having it stolen — the cause is unconfirmed.  
- The exact nature of the truth spell he is maintaining — its range, duration, and whether it can be detected or countered — is unspecified.  
- Whether Kalan’s endorsement of Thorin’s plan implies active participation or merely moral support; his role beyond the spell is ambiguous.
