### Overlake Hold

Overlake Hold is a fortress and jail carved from grey stone, serving as the seat of Errde Blackskull’s power and the center of Duergar authority in the undercity. It is cold, silent, devoid of color, and designed to evoke the feeling of being deep underground. Its walls are thick, corridors shadowed, and air thick with fear. It functions as a site of interrogation, control, trauma dumps, and political manipulation — enforcing law without mercy or pretense. It is simultaneously the heart of Duergar control and its most vulnerable point, where rebellion is seeded and loyalty begins to fracture.
