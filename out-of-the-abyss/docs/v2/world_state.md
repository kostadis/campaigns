# 1. THE PARTY — "The Ember Vanguard"

The four player characters, plus their core attached companions, now operate as a self-declared faction: **the Ember Vanguard** (named in ch23 by Thorin). They have emerged from the Underdark, traveled south via Daggerford, and arrived at **Candlekeep**, where they are investigating the murder of Keeper of Tomes **Janussi**.

**Reconciled current roster (Candlekeep, ch56):**
- Daz, Thorin, Grygum, Zalthir (the four PCs)
- Glabbagool (sentient ooze, Daz's de-facto familiar — carried in the Bag of Holding)
- The captured/shrunken House T'sarran drow spy (in the Bag of Holding, watched by Glabbagool)
- Elin (a silent ~7-year-old child afflicted by surface-bleeding Underdark corruption)
- Spiderbait (goblin, attached, seeking innocuous Candlekeep work)

**Departed companions:** Stool & Rump-a-dump (returned to Neverlight Grove), Eldeth (departed for Mithral Hall), Shuushar (joined reformist kuo-toa), Jimjar (vanished from existence), Dawnbringer (held by Thorin — see object dossier).

> **Note on "the Narrator" / "I" dossiers:** Several source dossiers ("Narrator," "I") are first-person aggregations that blend Daz, Grygum, and Zalthir. Treat their content as PC-internal monologue, attributed below to the most likely speaker. Do not treat them as a separate NPC.

### Daz (PC) — Drow Wizard, "The General"
**Status:** Alive. At Candlekeep.
**Role:** Tactical architect and strategist of the Ember Vanguard; their feared mage. Primary diplomat/manipulator. Master of *Sculpt Spells* (carves allies out of his own Fireballs).

**Background:** An exile/wanderer of Menzoberranzan who rejected the Cult of Lolth and, uniquely for a drow, rejects slavery outright — believing "free people are the basis of any society." Has surface experience (recognized Prince Derendil's fictional High Forest kingdom as a lie). Fluent in Machiavellian drow politics, which he weaponizes — adopting a domineering noble persona, enforcing magical NDAs, identifying "two in a box" power-sharing as weakness. Keeps a journal modeled on surface author Milo Goodbarrel.

**Key current facts:**
- Has **no memory of his life before Velkynvelve**. Believes his name may derive from **"Daz'issin"** ("small shadow"), a softening name mothers give sons they hope will survive.
- An **unknown patron paid drow mercenaries (Nym & Kaelira) to keep Daz alive and unharmed.** Two drow houses — **House Mizzrym** and **House T'sarran** — are the named threats. House T'sarran's true goal is not Daz himself but identifying his secret patron.
- Maintained a long con on Asha Vandree that he was Lolth-possessed; the performance has reportedly become internally real to him.
- Killed Ilvara with a Guiding Bolt while shielding allies (per his own account; reconcile with Zalthir/Grygum claims — see Ilvara dossier).
- Suffers a persistent, unexplained head-pressure since arriving at Candlekeep (shared affliction with Elin — see Open Threads: *Underdark corruption reaching the surface*).

**Possessions:** Stonespeaker Crystal, Milo Goodbarrel's book, Gracklstugh badges, Bag of Holding (Glabbagool + spy), Dawnbringer (emotionally bonded but currently wielded by Thorin — see Open Threads), Princess Ebonmire's spellbook (unopened), Voucher of the Varmint.

### Thorin (PC) — Dwarf raised by Hill Giants, "Giantfriend"
**Status:** Alive. Near Daggerford / en route Candlekeep with the party.
**Role:** Frontline combatant; de-facto direct-action leader. Grappler, thrown-weapon specialist.

**Background:** A shield dwarf raised by a hill giant tribe — speaks Dwarvish with a heavy Giant accent, considers the giants his family. Learned the "hill strike" from a giant battlemaster; inherited the giant antipathy toward dragons (which flared during the Themberchaud episode). At odds with traditional dwarves like Eldeth, whom he won over by insisting his tribe were "different giants." Despises Underdark secrecy and NDAs; champions "radical transparency." His drive to "be my own faction" birthed the Ember Vanguard.

**Key current facts:**
- Custodian of **Dawnbringer**, the sentient sun-sword, whom he treats as a friend (pronoun "she") and has arranged therapy for with **Philemon**, Candlekeep's artifact-psychologist.
- Reclaimed **his own tooth**, taken as a trophy by Ilvara at Velkynvelve.
- Toppled the bridge and helped destroy the Heart Fungus that ended Ilvara.
- **Accepted Eldeth's invitation** to join her clan and be presented at **Mithral Hall**, seeking to reconcile his dwarven blood with his giant upbringing.
- Has undergone a "dark transition" — no longer the carefree youth who entered the Underdark; exhausted in the "fire kind" of way.

**Possessions:** Dawnbringer (+ Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard), reclaimed tooth, javelins, borrowed light crossbow, Diggermattock Miners patch.

### Grygum (PC) — Half-Orc "Cleric" of Bahamut, Founder of the "Grygumite School"
**Status:** Alive. At Candlekeep, Gate of Tomes.
**Role:** Lieutenant, philosopher, strategist, theatrical spellcaster.

**Background:** Raised in an orc tribe; learned to navigate brutal hierarchy by invoking superiors' names. Became a disciple of mentor **Gorg'Bahamut**, studying *The Platinum Chronicle: Discourses on the Abyssal Blight* and the legend of **Stroud vs. Protanther** — a chess match Grygum's sect believes Bahamut won by guiding mortal Stroud's hand, proving choice can alter the cosmos. From this he holds his radical doctrine: **dragons are defined by choice, not scale color**; judging by race is the ultimate evil. Disgusted by Underdark bigotry (e.g., Eldeth's). Plays the "Platinum Fool" — disarming theatrics, staged healing rituals.

**Key current facts:**
- **Privately admits his divine magic is self-created "theater"** and that Bahamut is "boring" — yet carries an unresolved crisis: "What does Bahamut actually want from me?" His casting (Guiding Bolt, Spirit Guardians, Revivify, etc.) demonstrably works regardless.
- Executed the Glyph of Warding trap against Jorlan; used Dust of Suleiman to destroy the Heart Fungus; struck Ilvara (Zuggtmoy's vessel) triggering the spore detonation.
- **Shrank and recruited the House T'sarran drow spy** (via *Reduce* + prayer beads), trading survival for intelligence.
- Floated killing Daz with intent to **Revivify** him as a diagnostic to flush out his hidden patron (plan not confirmed executed; Daz is alive).
- Declared himself **founder of the "Grygumite School"** at Candlekeep to gain leverage. Scholar **Brother Vareth** has, against all expectation, taken this seriously, formalizing it as the "strong-weak hypothesis with fixed points" (see Open Threads).

**Note:** Source variously calls him orc and half-orc; **half-orc** is current and consistent with Candlekeep scholarship treating him as a "half-orc cleric of Bahamut."

### Zalthir (PC) — Bronze Dragonborn Shadow Monk
**Status:** Alive. With party near Candlekeep.
**Role:** Scout, enforcer, close-combat controller, battlefield assassin.

**Background:** Raised in a secretive monastic order prizing silence, discretion, and evasion over frontal conflict. Trained under "The Garden Shadow" (delicate handling of deadly flora) and steeped in the legend of **Korrh Erann**, a monk who survived the Underdark by relentless flight from drow — a lesson Zalthir applies constantly (evasion over combat). Holds intense intolerance for noise; views interrogation as pragmatic "motivation," not torture.

**Key current facts:**
- **Bronze dragonborn**, frequently misidentified as red (the Ember Vanguard wanted-poster wrongly depicts him gold/red). His lineage carries a **hidden stigma** — the medusa Neheedra mocked "the circumstances of his birth."
- Reveres **Bahamut** through his discipline (called a "priest of Bahamut" in some logs) but belongs to no named Bahamutite order — treat as a Bahamut-revering monk.
- Wields an **Eldritch Claw Tattoo** (15-ft tentacle reach) combined with Shadow Step; developed a signature "punch through shadows" technique that impressed Candlekeep's **Brother Khell-Vire**, who awarded him a specialized shadow technique.
- **Killed Asha Vandree** with the Eldritch Claw and took her cloak; grappled and dropped Ilvara 40 feet.
- Took a vow of silence after the Tongue of Madness mushroom (now an evolving partial vow — speaks sparingly).
- Mentoring **Glabbagool** in the monastic Way of the Shadow.
- Assigned by Candlekeep's First Reader to investigate **the Jimjar thread** (dovetails with Khell-Vire's personal, unexplained interest in Zalthir).

# 4. MAJOR ANTAGONISTS & DEMON LORDS

### Ilvara Mizzrym — DECEASED
**Status:** Dead. Detonated in a toxic spore bloom after being struck (cavern beneath the old Velkynvelve site).
- Former High Priestess of Lolth and Velkynvelve commander; the reason the party were imprisoned. Became a **corrupted vessel of Zuggtmoy** — in her final moments she *was* the demon lord "walking in a drow priestess's bones." Seduced by the heresy that "Lolth wishes to be married" (the Bride). Controlled the cavern via the **Heart Fungus**, summoned spore-servants, cast *Insect Plague*, fungus-walked to evade grapples. Howled prophecy of "Vengeance. Zuggtmoy. The bride." Her prayer book documents her corruption timeline.
- **Kill credit is contested across PC dossiers** (Daz's Guiding Bolt / Grygum's Guiding Bolt / Zalthir's 40-ft drop / Thorin's Heart Fungus strike). Treat as a combined party kill; the Heart Fungus destruction (Thorin/Dawnbringer + Grygum's Dust of Suleiman + Glyph) collapsed her control, and a final divine bolt detonated her.

### Zuggtmoy — Demon Queen of Fungi (ACTIVE THREAT)
**Status:** Alive, actively influencing the Underdark via fungal colonization and possession.
- Patron of Ilvara, whom she fully possessed and used as her primary Underdark instrument. Seeks to fill the power vacuum left by Juiblex's defeat. Long-term goal: the **Fetid Wedding** to **Araumycos** (status of that plan now unknown). Infected Sarith, the Neverlight Grove myconids (via Voosbur/Phylo), and briefly Daz & Glabbagool. Asha Vandree's theology claims **Lolth and Zuggtmoy are the same entity** — a "Lolth/Zuggtmoy equivalence" now archived at Candlekeep.

### Demogorgon — Prince of Demons (ACTIVE)
**Status:** Alive. Emerged from the Darklake at Sloobludop (destroying the village); presence/influence felt across the Underdark; invoked by the Derro cult of Gracklstugh.
- Two-headed baboon-tentacle lord of chaos and madness. Worshipped by the Sloobludop Deep Father cult (as "Leemooggoogoon") and the Whorlstone Derro cult (Narrak/Plinki). Framed throughout as the campaign's ultimate evil and the antithesis of Bahamut.

### The Pudding King — DECEASED
**Status:** Dead (dissolved, ch47).
- Insane deep gnome enthralled by **Juiblex**; commanded an ooze horde (incl. Princess Ebonmire, Prince Livid) from Blingdenstone's Pudding Court; sought to reduce the Underdark to ooze. Body-swapped with Glabbagool before dissolution. On his death a voice (likely Juiblex) vowed rebirth by "consuming the banquet of the queen of fungi."

### Buppido — DECEASED
**Status:** Dead. Slain by Daz's magic missiles at his bone-altar lair near Laduguer's Furrow.
- Derro serial killer who believed himself the incarnate god Diinkarazan. Murdered and dismembered **Pelek**. His madness reportedly intensified after the Faerzress "blinked."

### Narrak — DECEASED
**Status:** Dead. Slain by Thorin in the Whorlstone ritual cavern.
- Derro cult leader of Demogorgon; conducted the two-head-curse ritual on stone giants and sought to repair the Obelisk to deliver Gracklstugh to Demogorgon as sacrifice. Correspondent of "the Prince" (Demogorgon).

### Plinki — Derro Cult Leader
**Status:** Alive (last seen at the black Obelisk).
- Demogorgon cultist obsessed with "the Big Baboon Demon." Sought to seal a demon-infused dragon egg using a Bahamut cleric's heart; believed Grygum's arrival was divine validation. Daz **teleported the egg away**, collapsing her plan (egg's current location unknown).

### Asha Vandree — DECEASED
**Status:** Dead. Killed by Zalthir's Eldritch Claw; body looted at the drow outpost.
- Junior drow priestess of Lolth who rejected Ilvara's "Bride Heresy," then underwent a theological transformation **synthesizing Lolth and Zuggtmoy as one** before death. Briefly recognized Daz as a "friend of Lolth" (deceived by his con). Pressed her theological document into Grygum's hands — now an artifact at Candlekeep.
> **Disambiguation:** "Asha," "Asha Vandry," and "Asha Vandree" are the same person. Earlier-chapter dossiers showing her alive precede her death (ch53/56 confirm deceased).

### Jorlan Duskryn — Disfigured Drow Elite
**Status:** Alive (last seen on the cavern floor near Ilvara, badly wounded).
- Ilvara's discarded ex-lover, scarred by acid; offered the original "Jorlan's Gambit" escape. His hatred of Ilvara acted as immunity to her spores. Inscribed the Glyph of Warding that Grygum triggered against him; survived it and a Dawnbringer crit, then charged Ilvara on raw emotion. His final fate (reaching her, survival) is unconfirmed.

### Sarith Kzekarit — DECEASED (Spore Servant)

- Former drow guide; succumbed progressively to Zuggtmoy's spores, embracing the "Dark Lady's song" as liberation.

# 5. FACTIONS

### House T'sarran (ACTIVE THREAT)
Drow noble house; matron mother has ruled 200 years. Patient, long-term operators. Their true objective is **not Daz but his hidden patron**. Sent the captured spy to the surface; lost a mage, guards, and a warrior at the ruined bridge (ch51) but will keep sending agents. Researching the Daz/Jimjar connection. One of the two named threats to Daz.

### House Mizzrym
Fourth House of Menzoberranzan; Ilvara's house and the dominant slave-trading lineage. Remains a standing threat to Daz despite Ilvara's death; named alongside House T'sarran in Daz's extraction contract.

### Bahamut (Deity)
Platinum Dragon; patron of life, free will, and moral choice; antithesis of Demogorgon. Served (in their fashion) by Grygum and Zalthir. Opposed to Zuggtmoy. Central to the Stroud/Protanther chess legend and the emerging Candlekeep "Grygumite School" theology.

### Duergar / The Deepkingdom (Gracklstugh)
Ruling faction of Gracklstugh; xenophobic slave-trading industrial state. Non-duergar confined to the Darklake District; movement requires Stone Guard/Keeper badges. The **Deepking's descent into madness/paranoia** has made laws arbitrary and destabilized the city. Innate invisibility makes them paranoid and untrusting even internally.

### Keepers of the Flame
Duergar psionic clerics in Gracklstugh's far-southeast cavern; tend the red dragon **Themberchaud** as forge-engine. The Deepking's prized advisors. Secretly believe they control Themberchaud (he believes the reverse). Panicked over the theft of their **red dragon egg** (Themberchaud's intended replacement) by the Gray Ghosts. At secret war with the Council of Savants and the Gray Ghosts. **Gartokkar Xundorn** is their primary external liaison (see Open Threads re: his contradictory "Deepking" identification).

### Council of Savants
Derro savant ruling-circle (nominally 36). Destabilized after the Faerzress "blink." Some members serve Narrak's Demogorgon cult; the body is fractured. Their **High Council was murdered** and the dragon egg lost, triggering the Gray Ghosts' independence.

### Gray Ghosts
Formerly the Council of Savants' covert retrieval arm; now an **independent vendetta faction** seeking revenge on the (misidentified) "Ember Vanguard" for the murder of their High Council and the theft of the Red Dragon Egg. Use invisibility, illusion, whispered attacks. Believe the egg is rightfully theirs.

### Derro (Gracklstugh)
Fragmented, fanatical descendants of mind-flayer slaves — endemically insane. Splinter cults serve Demogorgon (under the late Narrak / Plinki); others scattered after the egg's loss. The Gray Ghosts faction now pursues the "Ember Vanguard" based on the delusion that they are drow/Duergar-aligned killers.

### Drow (general)
Active across the Underdark (Menzoberranzan, the ruins of Velkynvelve). Society built on competition, deception, and silence-as-weapon. A faction was corrupted by Ilvara's spore-heresy (now largely destroyed in the cavern detonation). Drow mercenaries (Nym, Kaelira) and House agents operate on the surface.

### Myconids (Neverlight Grove)
Fragmented: **Sovereign Phylo** and the corrupted Circle of Masters (incl. Yestabrod-style transformation via the Garden of Welcome) vs. the resisting **Sovereign Basidia** and her loyalists (Loobamub, Rasharoo). The Grove is under Zuggtmoy's influence; myconids dance unnaturally, speak of "the Dark Lady's dream," accumulate surface treasure, and retreat from radiant light (Dawnbringer).

### Blingdenstone Factions
Deep gnomes under **Chief Dorbo Diggermattock** (and Quartermaster Senni). Allied **Goldwhisker wererat clan** under **Chipgrin**. Recovered Earth Elementals for mining; preparing to reopen surface trade after the Pudding King's defeat. Alchemist **Kazook Pickshine** present.

### Mercenary operatives: Nym & Kaelira
Drow mercenaries (Nym alternately "he"/"she" in sources; Kaelira possibly the same individual or a partner — identity ambiguous). Contracted by Daz's **unknown patron** to keep him alive against Houses Mizzrym and T'sarran. Cold, professional; question why a "nobody" warrants such expense. Last seen examining the burned extraction contract after Asha's death.

### Candlekeep (Institution)
Independent scholarly theocracy on the Sea of Swords, governed by the Avowed under the **First Reader (Bookwyrm)**. The **Keeper of Tomes, Janussi, has been murdered** — heart removed (to prevent resurrection), his recent annotation-book *The Golden Ass* missing. Strict reciprocal-knowledge entry policy (one book per group for access). The party donated five books incl. the **Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal**, and has been delegated to investigate Janussi's death. Handlers assigned: Daz→Sister Yvenne, Grygum→Vareth, Thorin→Philemon, Zalthir→Khell-Vire.

# 9. OPEN THREADS & MYSTERIES
*(Clustered; chapter references retained for prep.)*

## A. Daz's Identity — THE CENTRAL MYSTERY
- **Who is Daz, really?** He has no pre-Velkynvelve memory. His name may be **"Daz'issin"** ("small shadow"), a name mothers give sons they hope survive (ch55). The Menzoberranzan Librarian revealed a noble house was **"struck"** (erasure attempt) for failing to kill a child survivor — a "blithering child idiot." **Is Daz that survivor?** (Yvenne: name link "possible but unlikely"; Librarian declined to confirm directly.) An appendix recording his history was cut from the records with a razor — deliberate erasure.
- **Who is Daz's secret patron?** Someone with serious resources paid mercenaries (Nym/Kaelira) to keep Daz alive. House T'sarran's entire operation is to flush out this patron when Daz surfaces. The patron's identity is unknown even to the mercenaries and to House T'sarran. **The party plans to research this at Candlekeep.** (Daz: "I do want to know who protected me.")
- **Daz's headache** — unexplained pressure since Candlekeep; linked by Grygum to Elin's affliction and to Underdark-corruption-reaching-the-surface.

## B. Jimjar's Disappearance & True Nature
- Jimjar vanished from existence with angel/deity-tier stats. **What is he?** Why did he guide the party? Why his trauma at Juiblex/Zuggtmoy's names? **The party intends to research him at Candlekeep**, and the thread dovetails with Khell-Vire's personal interest in Zalthir (the "Jimjar thread").

## C. The Surface-Bleeding Corruption
- Underdark "structural wrongness" (demon-lord-associated madness) is **reaching the surface**, manifesting in **Elin** (7-year-old, silent 6 days, compulsively draws a six-pointed star, says "it is only the shape that holds") and in **Daz's** headache. Cause and cure unknown. Grygum perceives the world "speaking a language Elin and Daz cannot process."

## D. The Janussi Murder (Candlekeep)
- Keeper of Tomes murdered; **heart removed to prevent resurrection**; recent annotation-book ***The Golden Ass* is missing** (deliberate). Janussi had requested **Kalan Strongbranch's** resignation the morning he died (Kalan now relieved of duty). The **Gate Warden (Kalan/Tadric)** left an inspection "looking like a man who had found something" — report unshared. **Sylvira Savikas absent** from the council without explanation. The inexplicable **gray cat** passes all wards. Investigation in progress.

## E. Zuggtmoy & The Fetid Wedding
- Zuggtmoy is **active and unbeaten**, wearing Ilvara's death as theology. Is her **wedding to Araumycos** still planned, thwarted, or pending? Araumycos's current state is silent. The **Lolth/Zuggtmoy equivalence** (Asha's doctrine) — is it heretical confusion or a genuine metaphysical truth?

## F. Demogorgon & The Egg
- Demogorgon emerged and rampaged; his current physical location vs. multi-plane influence is unclear. The **demon-infused red dragon egg** (teleported away by Daz) is loose — coveted by three Gracklstugh factions. Fate of Gracklstugh (the cult's ritual destruction) is **unconfirmed**.

## G. Gracklstugh Identity Contradiction
- Source dossiers contradict whether **Gartokkar Xundorn IS the Deepking** or merely a Keeper-priest serving a separate Deepking (Horgar Steelshadow V). **Treat Gartokkar as the Keeper liaison and the Deepking as a separate, mad ruler** unless play establishes otherwise — but flag this for resolution.

## H. The "Grygumite School" / Fixed-Points Theology
- Grygum's accidental theological school is being taken seriously by Brother Vareth (the "strong-weak hypothesis with fixed points"): a possible fourth theological position holding that the universe is indifferent except near "fixed points," where it grows "conspiratorial" to compel certain outcomes. A live intellectual thread that may matter cosmologically.

## I. Dawnbringer's Past
- Conflicting age (2,000 vs 3,000–4,000 years of darkness). **Who was her previous wielder** (the corpse she was found in)? She went silent when asked — unresolved trauma; possibly significant.

## J. Lesser / Lingering Threads
