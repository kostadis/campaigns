## NPC Dossiers

### Daz
- **Location/Status:** Candlekeep, alive. Persistent headache and memory loss attributed to library proximity.
- **Plans/Goals:** Investigate his own identity and the patron protecting him; proposes splitting any proceeds.
- **Party knows vs. hidden:** Party knows he's "feared mage of the Ember Vanguard," carries a sentient ooze and sword. - **Arc score:** None bound.

### Grygum
- **Location/Status:** Candlekeep, outside the Gate of Tomes, alive. Awaiting the Keeper of Tomes.

## Faction States

### Ember Vanguard
- **Goals/Status:** Self-proclaimed faction formed by Thorin; the party's own identity. Now surfaced, fragmenting toward Candlekeep. "Feared mercenary band" reputation precedes them (reached Gracklstugh rumor mill).
- **Key members:** Thorin (frontline/leader), Daz (mage), Zalthir (scout/enforcer), Grygum (lieutenant), Glabbagool. 
- **Relationship to party:** They *are* the party.
- **Resources/Vulnerabilities:** Strong reputation; but identity makes them a target (Gartokkar's warnings; House interest in Daz).
- **Arc score:** None bound.

### House T'sarran
- **Goals/Status:** Matron Mother (200-year rule) seeks the patron who paid to protect Daz; uses Daz as bait. Spy currently captured by the party.
- **Key members:** Matron Mother; the captured drow spy.
- **Relationship to party:** Surveilling, not yet openly hostile; waiting for the party to surface.
- **Resources/Vulnerabilities:** Intelligence network; vulnerability: their spy is compromised and feeding the party information.
- **Arc score:** None bound.

### House Mizzrym
- **Goals/Status:** Named as a threat to Daz in the extraction contract; Ilvara's former house. Remains a danger even after Ilvara's death.
- **Relationship to party:** Hostile to Daz.
- **Arc score:** None bound.

### Candlekeep (Avowed / First Reader's circle)
- **Goals/Status:** Reeling from Janussi's murder and Kalan's death; lockdown ordered. The party is delegated the investigation. First Reader operates covertly "before the Avowed awake."
- **Key members:** Bookwyrm/First Reader, Sister Yvenne, Vareth, Philemon, Khell-Vire, Fembris, Tadric.
- **Relationship to party:** Employer/host; reciprocal-information policy; payment = one book each.
- **Resources/Vulnerabilities:** Vast forbidden knowledge; vulnerability: internal power struggle (Janussi/Kalan), the unexplained gray cat, an active murderer inside.
- **Arc score:** None bound.

### Keepers of the Flame (Gracklstugh)
- **Goals/Status:** Control Themberchaud secretly; want the stolen red dragon egg and proof of Grey Ghost involvement; Gartokkar seeks genocidal war on the Derro.
- **Relationship to party:** Off-screen/past employer; coercive.
- **Arc score:** None bound.

### Blingdenstone Coalition
- **Goals/Status:** Defeated the Pudding King; rebuilding, reopening surface trade; gnome–wererat–Gold Whisker alliance brokered.
- **Key members:** Chief Dorbo, Senni, Chief Chipgrin, Dasco Pickshine.
- **Relationship to party:** Allied; Daz honored (Voucher of the Varmint).
- **Arc score:** None bound.

### Cult of Zuggtmoy / Fungal Corruption
- **Goals/Status:** Zuggtmoy spreading fungal dominion in the vacuum left by Juiblex; worked through Ilvara (now dead). Corruption may be spreading to the surface (Grygum's fear).
- **Relationship to party:** Primary metaphysical antagonist.
- **Resources/Vulnerabilities:** Spore servants, possession; vulnerability: lost her vessel Ilvara.
- **Arc score:** None bound.

## Active Plots
*Ordered by urgency.*

### 1. The Murder of Keeper Janussi (IMMEDIATE)
- **What:** Janussi found murdered, heart removed, rib cage caved in. Party delegated the investigation; Candlekeep in lockdown.
- **Trigger/Timeline:** Active now; First Reader wants it handled "before the Avowed awake." Kalan (a suspect/resigned warden) is now also dead.
- **Party intersection:** They are the investigators; strict no-removal-of-evidence and confidentiality rules. Grygum noted organ removal as an anti-resurrection safeguard — a clue.
- **DM threads:** The gray cat (multiple NPCs unsettled by it); midnight-blue-ink research notes; Janussi's resignation request to Kalan; who killed Kalan?

### 2. Daz's Identity & the Unknown Patron (HIGH)
- **What:** Daz is likely the surviving heir of a destroyed Drow house (House Daz'issin?). An unknown patron paid for his protection; House T'sarran hunts that patron using Daz as bait.
- **Trigger/Timeline:** Active; the captured spy says the matron expects the patron to reveal themselves once Daz surfaces — which has now happened.
- **Party intersection:** Daz proposes investigating; the Librarian/Yvenne hold sealed-record clues (a "struck" fourth seat; a house that failed to kill a child survivor). Mercenaries Kaelira & Nym are contracted around this.

### 3. The Captured Drow Spy (HIGH)
- **What:** A compliant House T'sarran spy held shrunken in the party's bag of holding under Glabbagool's watch.
- **Trigger/Timeline:** Ongoing; she believes her matron senses her failure. Loyalty/escape risk unresolved.
- **Party intersection:** A live intelligence asset and a liability; her presence ties directly to Plot 2.

### 4. Jimjar's Disappearance & Celestial Nature (MEDIUM)
- **What:** Jimjar vanished from existence after revealing celestial-like traits.
- **Trigger/Timeline:** Open thread; possible return teased. First Reader assigned Zalthir to investigate; Khell-Vire's personal interest is tied to the "Jimjar thread."
- **Party intersection:** Zalthir's investigation; Khell-Vire's mentorship of Zalthir.

### 5. Dawnbringer's Grief & Past (MEDIUM)
- **What:** Sentient sun-blade processing millennia of darkness and the loss of her previous wielder.
- **Trigger/Timeline:** Slow-burn; therapy with Philemon underway.
- **Party intersection:** Thorin's emotional arc; the corpse she was found in is an unanswered question.

### 6. Spreading Fungal Corruption / Zuggtmoy (LOOMING)
- **What:** With Ilvara dead and Juiblex receding, Zuggtmoy seeks to expand. Grygum fears the corruption is reaching the surface; Asha Vandree's chain-mail document equates Lolth and Zuggtmoy.
- **Trigger/Timeline:** Background threat; "wall remembering" phenomenon and Elin's/Daz's "language gap" may be early signs.
- **Party intersection:** The party are the only ones who grasp the connected scope (Zalthir's theory); Voosbur and Araumycos remain unresolved.

### 7. Eldeth's Warning to Mithral Hall (BACKGROUND)
- **What:** Eldeth carries word of Underdark threats to the dwarven lords and a promise to bring Thorin into her clan.
- **Timeline:** She has departed west.
- **Party intersection:** Thorin's heritage arc; a future Mithral Hall hook.

## DM Notes

**Convergence points:**
- **Candlekeep is the hub.** Plots 1, 2, 3, and 4 all converge here. The murder investigation gives the party reason to access sealed knowledge — which is exactly what Daz needs for his identity quest. Let the two investigations cross: a clue in Janussi's research or the restricted stacks could touch House Daz'issin or the Lolth/Zuggtmoy equivalence.
- **The "blithering child idiot" phrase** recurs across Sister Yvenne, the Librarian/Yvenne, and Menzoberranzan record-lore — a deliberate thread
