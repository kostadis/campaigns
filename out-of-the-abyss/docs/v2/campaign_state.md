10. **Blingdenstone / Pudding King Crisis** — Juiblex-driven ooze infestation resolved. *Consequence:* **Pudding King dissolved**; Glabbagool body-swapped and survived as a sentient ooze; Earth Elementals secured; gnome rule stabilized; Juiblex vowed rebirth.
11. **Ogrémoch's Bane / Temple Cleansing (Galeb Duhr)** — Began Entémoch's boon quest. *Consequence:* Weapon sacrifice placed; **quest INCOMPLETE** (see Active).
12. **The Final Cavern (beneath old Velkynvelve)** — Heart Fungus battle vs. Ilvara (Zuggtmoy's vessel). *Consequence:* **Ilvara killed and detonated; Asha Vandree killed by Zalthir; Heart Fungus destroyed; House T'sarran extraction force defeated.** First contact with mercenaries Nym/Kaelira.
13. **Emergence to the Surface ("Overbright")** — Eldeth departed for Mithral Hall; Shuushar departed.
14. **Daggerford** — Discovered a Stroudite-inscribed Bahamutian monument; deepened theology threads.
15. **Capture of the House T'sarran Drow Spy** — Shrunk by Grygum, held in Bag of Holding. *Consequence:* Confirmed Daz has a hidden patron; intelligence gained; spy's fate still pending.
16. **Candlekeep Entry** — Donated five books incl. the Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal. *Consequence:* Granted access; PCs assigned handlers; delegated to investigate Janussi's murder.
