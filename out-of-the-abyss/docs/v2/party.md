# PARTY REFERENCE — The Ember Vanguard

## Party Overview

**Current Location:** Candlekeep, at the Gate of Tomes / outside the Gate of Tomes (Daz and Grygum within the library complex; Zalthir on the surface nearby; Thorin last logged near Daggerford traveling with the group). The party has surfaced from the Underdark and converged on Candlekeep as of Chapter 56.

**Active Quests / Threads:**
- Investigate the murder of Janussi at the behest of the Candlekeep Archivists (payment negotiated: one book each).
- Uncover the identity of Daz's unknown patron and his possible lineage (House Daz'issin); Daz proposed splitting any proceeds.
- Evade or expose House T'sarran, who is observing Daz as bait to flush out the patron.
- Await news from the Keeper of Tomes (scene ongoing).

**Collective Resources:**
- Bag(s) of Holding containing Glabbagool (sentient ooze companion) and the miniaturized "Pocket Drow" spy from House T'sarran.
- Recruited drow spy under Glabbagool's watch (loyalty unverified).
- Sentient sword Dawnbringer (in Thorin's care, in therapy with Philemon).
- Stonespeaker Crystal, Demogorgon statuette, lists of names, assorted enchanted gear and looted valuables.

**Group Reputation:** Self-proclaimed faction "the Ember Vanguard," a name now carrying legend in the Underdark (notably via wanted-style posters depicting Daz as a "feared mage" and misidentifying Zalthir's color). They reject all prior factional allegiances. At Candlekeep they present as investigators-for-hire.

### Daz
- **Class/Level/Player:** Wizard (drow); level and player not specified in source.
- **Personality & Motivations:** Analytical tactician who treats spellcasting as "interlocking geometry"; cynically detached from Menzoberranzan's slave hierarchy, convinced "free people are the basis of any society." Driven now by discovery — of ecosystems, civilizations, and his own buried identity.
- **Relationships:** Bonded to Glabbagool (familiar) and Dawnbringer; tactical partner to Zalthir (Darkness/Cloud of Daggers); titled "General" of the Ember Vanguard. Watched warily by Grygum, who suspects his "madness" stems from Underdark corruption. Observed covertly by House T'sarran; protected by an unknown patron.
- **Items of Significance:** Stonespeaker Crystal, Milo Goodbarrel's book, Bag of Holding (with Glabbagool), Dawnbringer (now in Thorin's care), Princess Ebonmire's spellbook, Voucher of the Varmint, Gracklstugh badges.
- *Current state:* Suffering persistent headache and memory loss since arriving at Candlekeep; increasingly believes his Lolth ruse has become genuine communion.

### Zalthir
- **Class/Level/Player:** Shadow Monk (bronze dragonborn), reveres Bahamut; level and player not specified in source.
- **Personality & Motivations:** Disciplined to near-silence (vow taken after the Tongue of Madness mushroom); speaks only when necessary. Has forged a personal combat philosophy "better than the manual," and feels bound to protect his companions though he refuses to return to the Underdark.
- **Relationships:** Mentoring Glabbagool toward monkhood; suspicious of Jimjar (immune to poison/psychic); tactical partner to Daz; mutual respect with Grygum and Thorin. Killed Asha Vandree and took her cloak. Recognized by Khell-Vire for his unique shadow technique.
- **Items of Significance:** Eldritch Claw Tattoo, crystal spectacles (sight through magical darkness), drow spider-silk cloak, Keoghtom's Ointment, Demogorgon statuette, jug of any liquid, Narrak's/Xinaya's strange texts (withheld from the group).
- *Current state:* Continues low-grade suspicion of hidden forces (e.g., the "ordinary cat"); believes the Underdark's demonic events are one coordinated corruption.

### Thorin Giantfriend
- **Class/Level/Player:** Frontline martial combatant (dwarf raised by hill giants); level and player not specified in source.
- **Personality & Motivations:** Once happy-go-lucky, now hardened by a "dark transition." Believes in radical transparency (when it changes the moment) and seeks to integrate his dwarven heritage with his giant upbringing as a whole, not a contradiction.
- **Relationships:** Devoted to Dawnbringer as a sentient companion (secured her therapy with Philemon); trusted bond with Grygum; accepted Eldeth's invitation to join her clan and travel to Mithral Hall; advocate for Glabbagool's inclusion. De facto leader in direct action.
- **Items of Significance:** Dawnbringer, his reclaimed sentimental tooth, Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard, Bag of Holding (with "Pocket Drow"), Diggermattock Miners patch, javelins, borrowed light crossbow, *The Case of the Missing Book*.
- *Current state:* A deep "fire kind" weariness from survival and loss; restrained (spared Asha after grappling her).

### Grygum
- **Class/Level/Player:** Cleric of Bahamut (orc/half-orc — source conflicts), self-styled founder of the "Grygumite School"; level and player not specified in source.
- **Personality & Motivations:** Pragmatic survivor who openly admits his divine magic is self-created "theater." Believes dragons are defined by choice, not scale color, and that race-based judgment is the ultimate evil. Compulsively documents everything as a coping mechanism for chaos.
- **Relationships:** Emotionally anchored to small/vulnerable creatures (Glabbagool, Stool, Spiderbait, the recruited drow spy); frequent betting rival of Jimjar; lieutenant within the Ember Vanguard; watches Daz closely, suspecting Underdark corruption in him and Elin.
- **Items of Significance:** Enchanted shield and mace, magical short sword, Bag of Holding (with Glabbagool), prayer beads, vial of drow poison, mad theological text, jailer's keys, notes on every deal and event.
- *Current state:* Unresolved spiritual crisis ("What does Bahamut actually want from me?"); senses the Underdark's madness spreading to the surface; braced at the Gate of Tomes, half-expecting to be "caught."

## Party Dynamics

The Ember Vanguard is a self-declared faction bound by shared escape from the Underdark rather than ideology, with overlapping but distinct command roles: **Daz** as the strategic "General," **Thorin** as frontline leader in direct action, **Grygum** as lieutenant/philosopher, and **Zalthir** as silent enforcer and scout. Combat cohesion is strong — the trio of Daz, Zalthir, and Thorin developed the coordinated "Ember Grapple" / Darkness-and-Cloud-of-Daggers tactics.

**Shared goals:** Surfacing into a new life, the Candlekeep murder investigation, and protecting their adopted vulnerable companions (Glabbagool above all).

**Current tensions and watch-points for the GM:**
- **Grygum vs. Daz:** Grygum suspects Daz's headaches and "madness" are Underdark corruption, and previously floated *killing Daz with Revivify* as a diagnostic to flush out his protector. Whether that plan is fully abandoned is unconfirmed — a live source of distrust.
- **The trackless threats they carry:** Two captured drow (the bagged "Pocket Drow" and the recruited House T'sarran spy) travel with them; loyalty and containment are unverified, and T'sarran is actively hunting Daz.
- **Diverging values:** Thorin's radical transparency sits uneasily against Daz's manipulation/NDA style and Zalthir's withholding (e.g., the unshared Xinaya scrolls). These philosophies have coexisted but could fracture under pressure.
- **Pulls away from the group:** Thorin is drawn toward Eldeth's clan and Mithral Hall; Zalthir refuses to return to the Underdark; Daz is fixated on his own lineage mystery. Their long-term goals are beginning to point in different directions.
