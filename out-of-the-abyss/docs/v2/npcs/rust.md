### Rust

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Assassin’s chambers (with Daz, Grim, and others)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Derro assassin cadre (likely serving a higher leadership, possibly tied to Demogorgon)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Lead assassin and liaison; coordinates entry protocols and communicates with leadership. Directly reports to “the boss” and consults leadership on operational plans.  
**Key relationships:** Works alongside Grim (another Derro assassin); reports to an unnamed “boss”; coordinates with Daz, Thorin, Zalthir, and Grygum as the designated entry team.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Identified himself as “Rust,” the unpronounceable Derro assassin.  
- Confirmed his faction’s identity as “the empty scabbard killers.”  
- Asked if the party are enemies of Demogorgon.  
- Instructed non-essential members (Jimjar, Spiderbait, Sarith, Stool, Eldeth) to remain outside while permitting only Daz, Thorin, Zalthir, and Grygum to enter the chamber.  
- Declared intent to confer with his leadership regarding an operational plan.  
- Returned to relay that “the boss wants to see you.”  
- Directed the group to move along.  

## Uncertainty
- The identity of “the boss” is never named or described.  
- The phrase “The Drow not named Grimgrim explains that he can be called — ‘Rust.’” contradicts all other references identifying Rust as a Derro — this may be a misattribution or error in the source text.  
- It is unclear whether Rust is a Derro or a Drow — all other facts label him Derro, but one line calls him “Drow.”  
- Rust’s exact chain of command beyond “the boss” is unspecified.  
- Whether Rust is still standing behind Daz at the end of the chapter is implied but not explicitly confirmed after his final dialogue.
