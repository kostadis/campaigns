### Ront

- **Current status:** Dead  
- **Current location:** Dead body found behind Daz in the cave, head severed and consumed  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Formerly under the Drow Beremil; now deceased, no allegiance  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** None specified at time of death (last seen wielding a blade in combat)  
- **Current assignment / role:** None (deceased); last known role was as a captive Quaggoth assigned to water-carrying duties under Drow supervision  
- **Defining recent actions:** Attacked and wounded a vrock with a blade, then was killed by it — the vrock severed his head with its beak, swallowed the head, and flew off; his headless body fell behind Daz.  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:** Still consumed by anger over perceived cowardice; believed killing a vrock would redeem him.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of Ront’s blade (type, origin, or whether he retained it from earlier) is unspecified.  
- Whether Ront ever consciously broke free of Drow control or remained under coercion until death is ambiguous — his actions suggest autonomy, but no explicit declaration of rebellion is given.  
- The phrase “Prince Derendil, the deluded Quaggoth” is applied to both Ront and Derendil in early chapters — it is unclear if “Prince Derendil” was a title mistakenly assigned to Ront, or if Ront and Derendil are two distinct individuals (later facts only mention Ront).  
- The identity of “the guard” killed in ch02 is unspecified — whether Drow, another captive, or a different foe is not named.
