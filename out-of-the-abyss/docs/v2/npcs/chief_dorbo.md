### Chief Dorbo

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of the mining coalition in Blingdenstone  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Leader of the mining coalition; active participant in Blingdenstone’s leadership council  
**Key relationships:** Works alongside Senni Diggermattock, Chipgrin, Nomi Pathshutter, Gurnik Tapfinger, Kazook Pickshine, and the ghost Barrow Warden Uth-Jadger. Has a history of distrust toward the Gold Whisker clan, whom he suspects of being wererats.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Agreed to support Daz’s plan to confront the Pudding King.  
- Suggested using the Temple of the Steadfast Stone as a staging area to isolate ooze threats from population centers.  
- Attended the grand moot with all major Blingdenstone leaders.  
- Participated in the leadership meeting opposing elemental enslavement.  
- Previously refused to cooperate with the Gold Whisker clan due to suspicion of lycanthropy.  
- Expressed concern over the gnomes’ lack of equipment and resources to combat oozes.  
- Directed the party to eat at the Foaming Bug inn after intruding into his private quarters/kitchen.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Obsessed with money — an obsession noted as seeming unhinged given the scale of the ooze threat and broader crisis in Blingdenstone.
