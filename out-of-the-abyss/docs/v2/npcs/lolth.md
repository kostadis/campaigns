### Lolth

**Current status:** Alive (as a deity)  
**Current location:** Not physically present; worshipped across the Drow realms and cults  
**Allegiance/faction:** Supreme deity of the Drow; head of the Lolthite cult  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (as a deity, not an embodied entity)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Supreme deity worshipped by Asha Vandree, Daz (under cover), and other Drow loyalists  
- Source of power and faith for Drow priests and priestesses; associated with deception, fear, and spider symbolism  
- Opposed to Ilvara’s “bride heresy” — worshippers of Lolth reject Ilvara’s claims as heretical  
- Daz is currently pretending to serve Lolth to maintain cover with Asha Vandree  
- Asha Vandree prays to Lolth post-Ilvara’s death, reaffirming Lolth’s continued dominance in Drow faith  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Maintained religious authority over Drow cults despite the rise and fall of Ilvara’s movement  
- Inspired Asha Vandree’s immunity to Ilvara’s spores through unwavering faith  
- Daz’s deception as a Lolth-follower confirms Lolth’s symbolic power as a cover identity in Drow society  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Originated through betrayal and subterfuge (ch19), suggesting a foundational nature of treachery in her divinity  
- Worship of Lolth is tied to control through fear and deception — priests are known for lying and manipulating (ch25)  
- Lolth’s faith is used as a tool of social dominance, especially by priestesses toward males (ch32)
