### Basidia

**Current status:** Alive, but emotionally devastated.  
**Current location:** Leaving Neverlight Grove, dragged away by Zalthir.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former co-sovereign of Neverlight Grove; now in open opposition to Phylo and the Circle of Masters.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly held at departure, but recently distributed Myconid-acquired treasure and potions of greater healing to the party.  
**Spells/feats:** Capable of releasing spores for telepathic communication; possesses deep knowledge of myconid melding and spore recognition.  
**Wounds:** None physically noted; emotionally traumatized, appears to be sobbing.  
**Current assignment / role:** Former co-sovereign; now a fugitive from Phylo’s regime, actively resisting his rule.  
**Key relationships:**  
- Formerly allied with Phylo; now accuses him of betrayal.  
- Recognizes Sarith’s spores as foreign; suspects Phylo is infected by the same diseased spore.  
- Has followers including Loobamub.  
- Views Hepbode as Phylo’s ally, not their own.  
- Whispered to Rasharoo, who offered a secret passageway to the Garden of Welcome.  
- Was last seen being forcibly removed by Zalthir, who subdued their resistance.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Accused Phylo of betraying the myconid Circle by allying with a monster.  
- Refused to enter the Garden of Welcome out of fear of infection.  
- Offered the party potions of greater healing and Myconid treasure before departing.  
- Insisted on confronting Phylo directly, but was overpowered and dragged away by Zalthir, weeping as they left.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes myconid unity and harmony depend on open, collective melding where all can hear each other — a system Phylo dismantled.  
- Fears Phylo is infected by a diseased spore identical to the one infecting Sarith.  
- Knows the Circle of Masters is hoarding carcasses from the Circle of Hunters and secretly sending myconids out of the grove.  
- Suspects the “Garden of Welcome” is a trap that consumed a prior group of softers.  
- Does not recognize Sarith’s spores — confirming he is not a native myconid sovereign, and thus an outsider influencing Phylo.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of the “monster” Phylo allied with is not named or described.  
- Whether Basidia is still alive after being dragged away is implied but not confirmed beyond their emotional collapse — no death or capture is stated.  
- It is unclear if Basidia’s emotional state (sobbing) is literal or metaphorical — myconids may not cry physically, but the text says “if a Myconid could sob.”  
- The final destination of Basidia after leaving Neverlight Grove is unknown.  
- Whether Basidia retains any authority or followers outside the grove is unconfirmed.  
- The identity of the “monster” is not specified — only that Basidia accuses Phylo of allying with it.  
- It is ambiguous whether the “treasure” given to the party was all that remained, or if Basidia retained some.
