### Eldeth

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** On the surface, preparing to travel west toward Mithral Hall  
**Allegiance/faction:** Clan of Mithral Hall (dwarven lineage); formerly a prisoner of the Drow, now a sworn emissary of her clan  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None explicitly stated; known to carry and use a hand-held crossbow (last seen in ch02); skilled in foraging, track-hiding, and Underdark survival; no current wounds reported  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Assigned to inform the lords of Gauntlgrym and Mithral Hall of the demonic incursions, Duergar corruption, and the threat of demons escaping the Underdark.  
- Sworn to ensure Thorin (her “giant friend”) is embraced by her clan as a matter of clan purpose.  
- Last seen with Thorin; departed from the group after emerging into the Overbright.  
- Maintains a close, meaningful bond with Thorin, evidenced by the extended handhold and use of the term “giant friend” as an endearment.  
- Previously traveled with and protected Zalthir, Jimjar, Sarith, Spiderbait, Daz, Grygum, and others; now separated from them.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully hid the party’s tracks for two days in the Underdark, enabling evasion of Drow pursuers.  
- Identified and warned the group of Tinmmasks spores and their confusion-inducing effects.  
- Destroyed the deep gnome ghost Vazuk alongside Jimjar.  
- Volunteered to confront the ghost problem in the haunted chamber.  
- Shifted from viewing all Drow as evil to recognizing the cult of Lolth as the true enemy, and seeing individual Drow (like Daz) as potentially redeemable.  
- Emerged from the Underdark tunnel into the Overbright and immediately sought out Thorin to bid farewell and pledge her mission.  
- Swore an oath to secure Thorin’s acceptance into her clan after briefing the dwarven lords.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes the Underdark’s dangers (demon lords, Duergar slavery, fungal plagues) pose an existential threat to Faerûn and must be warned to the surface.  
- Feels deep personal obligation to her clan and sees informing the dwarven lords as her sacred duty.  
- Carries trauma from her upbringing (raised to kill Drow on sight) but has undergone profound moral evolution.  
- Secretly values Thorin’s companionship and sees him as an ally beyond duty — her final gesture to him was a meaningful, unspoken vow.  

## Uncertainty  
- Was Eldeth carrying the hand-held crossbow at the time of her departure? It was last used in ch02, but no later mention confirms its current possession.  
- Did she retain any items from the Underdark (e.g., silk rope, mushrooms, artifacts)? Not stated.  
- The exact nature of her “particular expression” toward Zalthir before her promise is ambiguous — its meaning is implied but not explained.  
- Whether she is traveling alone or with others after leaving Thorin is unspecified — only her destination and purpose are confirmed.  
- Her physical condition post-emergence (e.g., injuries from the Underdark) is not described.  
- The phrase “Eldeth was already gathering herself” implies imminent departure, but the precise moment of her leaving the group is not timed — only that she did so after Thorin.
