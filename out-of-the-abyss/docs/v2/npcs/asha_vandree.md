### Asha Vandree

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Drow outpost (body found there)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a cleric of Lolth; later became, in some capacity, a cleric of Zuggtmoy.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Chain mail containing a theological document asserting the equivalence of Lolth and Zuggtmoy.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** None. Formerly declared herself in charge of the group after surviving the extraction attempt, framing her survival as divine favor. Last seen pressing her theological document into Grygum’s hands.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Accepted Daz and his companions as “friends of Lolth” after initially suspecting them of deception.  
- Declared herself in charge of the group following chaotic survival, interpreting near-death events as divine endorsement.  
- Wrote and disseminated a theological argument equating Lolth and Zuggtmoy as the same entity.  
- Was grappled by Thorin and silenced before casting any spell.  
- Killed by the narrator using the Eldritch Claw Tattoo — no last words.  
- Her body was looted at the drow outpost after death.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believed Ilvara’s corruption was a threat that needed elimination, initially planning to use Daz as a weapon.  
- Underwent a theological transformation: from skeptic of Daz’s claims to believer in Zuggtmoy’s power, ultimately synthesizing Lolth and Zuggtmoy into a single divine principle.  
- Her devotion to Lolth was not blind — it was instrumental, adaptive, and ultimately self-redefining under pressure.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch49] stating she disbelieved Daz’s claim of Lolth’s possession and [ch51] accepting him as a “friend of Lolth” — but this is resolved by her later theological shift, so not a true contradiction.  
- Unclear whether her becoming a cleric of Zuggtmoy was voluntary, coerced, or a result of spore infection — no source specifies mechanism.  
- Unclear if the theological document was completed before or after her death — only that it was pressed into Grygum’s hands and later found in her chain mail.  
- No source confirms whether Glabbagool remained bound to her at time of death — only that she accepted it as a familiar earlier.  
- No source specifies whether she retained Ilvara’s contaminated magical items — only that she offered them as payment earlier.
