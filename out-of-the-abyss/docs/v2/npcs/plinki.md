### Plinki

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Near the black obelisk in a chamber with a circular pathway  
**Allegiance/faction:** Cult of Demogorgon (specifically, obsessed with “the Big Baboon Demon”)  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- A magically infused egg (believed to be transformed into a dual-headed dragon via demon spirit infusion)  
- A black obelisk (reverently tended, described as “mighty and tasty”; source of ritual energy)  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Leading a ritual to seal the egg’s transformation using the heart of a cleric of Bahamut  
- Believes Grygum (a cleric of Bahamut) is a divine omen confirming her ritual’s success  
- Commands several derro subordinates (at least five total, including four previously mentioned guards and others summoned to the egg)  
- Reports to no named superior — acts autonomously under the compulsive dictates of her corrupted mind  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully activated the obelisk, causing a flash of magical energy that triggered her glee  
- Declared the ritual complete upon Grygum’s arrival, believing his presence as a Bahamut cleric is the necessary sacrifice  
- Shouted for Grygum to approach and later screamed “Let me go!” — possibly reacting to a sudden shift in control or backlash from the ritual  
- Continuously reverently pats and strokes the obelisk’s surface  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes the egg has been infused with a demon’s spirit in honor of Demogorgon, intending to present it as a mount  
- Her mind is consumed by a fractured, violent devotion to “the Big Baboon Demon” — a personal, distorted name for Demogorgon  
- Sees the sacrifice of a Bahamut cleric not as blasphemy, but as divine validation — a twisted inversion of her cult’s doctrine  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Ch18 states “She has four guards” (female pronoun), but ch21 and ch22 refer to Plinki as female — consistent. No contradiction.  
- Contradiction: Ch21 states Plinki believes Grygum is a member of *her sub-sect of the Cult of Bahamut* — yet she is a devoted follower of Demogorgon. This is clearly a delusion, not a factual allegiance.  
- Ambiguity: The shout “Let me go!” in ch21 occurs without context — is it a plea for help, a reaction to ritual backlash, or a moment of temporary clarity? Unclear.  
- Unclear attribution: Who is “she” in “She has four guards” (ch18)? All other references use “Plinki” and describe her as female — consistent, so no conflict.  
- Unclear: Whether the obelisk’s flash and Plinki’s squeal of glee were caused by the egg’s activation, the obelisk’s power surge, or Grygum’s presence — not specified.  
- Unclear: Whether the four guards from ch18 are still present in ch21–22; only “several other derro” are mentioned later — possible replacement or expansion?  
- Unclear: Is the egg now fully transformed? Plinki believes it is, but no confirmation of actual physical change is given.
