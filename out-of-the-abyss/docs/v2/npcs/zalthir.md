### Zalthir

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** On the surface, near Candlekeep (last known location: Chapter 56)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Formerly a monk of an unknown order; now operates as an independent agent aligned with the remnants of the Ember Vanguard. Affiliated with Bahamut through teachings, though not formally a priest.  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Eldritch Claw Tattoo (manifests as writhing 15-foot tentacles, channels eldritch energy)  
- Spectacles crafted from glowing crystal (allow sight through magical darkness and detection of invisible creatures)  
- Drow spider-silk cloak (absorbs shadow, worn after killing Asha Vandree)  
- Keoghtom’s Ointment  
- Strange texts (from Narrak’s chest)  
- Jug capable of generating any liquid (previously used to produce mayonnaise)  
- List of names (from the Drow outpost, still in possession)  
- Statuette of Demogorgon (2 feet tall, retrieved from the obelisk chamber)  
- Ormu moss and fungi (collected for phosphorescent pigment)  
- Bag of Holding (used tactically against the Pudding King)  
- Small piece of spider silk rope (taken from the Drow outpost)  
- Charcoal drawing of the Duergar merchant (from assassin corpse)  
- Box of candles (pleasant-smelling, given to Thorin)  
- Bloodstained gloves (from assassins)  
- Coins (looted from assassins)  

**Current Spells / Feats / Abilities:**  
- **Shadow Monk** abilities: Shadow Step, Silence, Darkness (cast at will, can move it as a bonus action)  
- **Eldritch Maul** (via tattoo): Tentacle grapples and strikes  
- **Stunning Strike** (monastic feature)  
- **Monastic Deflection** (reduces bludgeoning damage)  
- **Street Justice** (formalizes binding grappled foes with rope)  
- **Fey Touched** (feat: grants *misty step* and one cantrip — likely *minor illusion*)  
- **Punch Through Shadows** (unique Underdark-developed technique: precise strike delivered through darkness)  
- **Darkness + Cloud of Daggers synergy** (tactically coordinated with Daz)  
- **Teleportation** via Shadow Step  
- **Dragonborn Fire Breath** (still available, though rarely used since acquiring eldritch abilities)  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **Role:** Tactical operative, scout, and enforcer for the party. Functions as the group’s primary close-combat controller and battlefield manipulator.  
- **Reports to:** No formal superior. Operates with autonomy but defers to group consensus.  
- **Travels with:** The surviving members of the Ember Vanguard (Daz, Thorin, Grygum, Glabbagool, Stool, Rump-a-dump, and possibly Jimjar).  
- **Key Relationships:**  
  - **Glabbagool:** Actively mentoring as a prospective monk; believes Glabbagool has potential for discipline and stillness.  
  - **Jimjar:** Suspicious of his true nature; confirmed Jimjar is immune to poison and psychic damage.  
  - **Daz:** Tactical partner in Darkness/Cloud of Daggers coordination; has learned battlefield geometry from him.  
  - **Grygum:** Mutual respect; considers him a reliable ally despite his unpredictability.  
  - **Stool & Rump-a-dump:** Maintains wary, protective distance; acknowledges their influence but resists manipulation.  
  - **Asha Vandree:** Killed her after recognizing her as a threat; recalls her erasure of him as a drow tactic.  
  - **Khell-Vire:** Recognized Zalthir’s unique skill; Zalthir chose Option C from a specialized shadow technique offered.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Killed Asha Vandree with precise Eldritch Claw strikes, taking her cloak.  
- Grappled and dropped Ilvara Mizzrym from 40 feet after two critical strikes, ending her reign.  
- Successfully used Shadow Step and Eldritch Claw to teleport, grapple, and eliminate targets with surgical efficiency.  
- Picked up the Demogorgon statue, the list of names, and other key artifacts from the Obelisk Chamber.  
- Survived the Heart Fungus toxin and the frightened condition, choosing to adhere to the rules of the condition despite tactical cost.  
- Accepted Glabbagool as a student in monkhood, offering training despite the cube’s non-human nature.  
- Shrank a drow spy to toy-size using a mushroom, then observed her psychological transformation.  
- Identified and documented the nature of the “tides have turned” moment between Jorlan and Ilvara.  
- Demonstrated “punch through shadows” technique to Khell-Vire, a unique ability developed in the Underdark.  
- Rolled a natural 20 to hit a monk during a test of stealth and precision.  
- Quietly questioned whether a seemingly ordinary cat is truly just a cat — signaling continued suspicion of hidden forces.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Zalthir is a bronze dragonborn, though misidentified as red on Ember Vanguard posters — a detail he does not correct.  
- He took a vow of silence after consuming the Tongue of Madness mushroom; speaks only when there is truly something to say.  
- His monastic training emphasized discretion, shadow, and avoiding frontal conflict — yet he has become a devastating close-range combatant.  
- He believes the demonic events across the Underdark (Valkenvylve, Spidersilk Caves, Demogorgon, Beholder, etc.) are connected — part of a larger, coordinated corruption.  
- He does not intend to return to the Underdark, but feels bound to protect those who have become his companions.  
- He was afflicted by a magical compulsion to hoard items in Neverlight Grove — cured by Lesser Restoration — which left him embarrassed and reflective.  
- He sees himself as having “built something better than the manual” — his own combat philosophy, forged in spite of monastic doctrine.  
- He believes soldiers become weapons through time and circumstance — a thought he files away to avoid emotional distraction.  
- He is deeply affected by silence as a weapon of erasure — having experienced it in drow society — and resents being treated as furniture.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Zalthir is described as a “priest of Bahamut” (ch14) and a “monk” throughout — but no faction or order is named. Is he a Bahamutite monk? A secular monk who reveres Bahamut? The facts are ambiguous.  
- Contradiction: Zalthir is called a “golden dragonborne” on the Ember Vanguard poster (ch28) but later identifies as bronze (ch16). The poster’s depiction is clearly wrong — but was it intentional misdirection?  
- Unclear Attribution: Was Zalthir the one who killed the Drow spy in ch54? The text says “Zalthir had already ended the question...” but does not explicitly say he delivered the final blow — only that he grappled and stunned.  
- Ambiguous Chronology: Zalthir’s vow of silence is declared in ch03 after eating the Tongue of Madness mushroom — but he speaks frequently afterward. Does the vow still hold? The later chapters show him speaking sparingly, but not in total silence — is this a partial or evolving vow?  
- Unclear Current Status of Companions: Are Stool, Rump-a-dump, Glabbagool, and Jimjar still with him? The last chapters imply they are, but no explicit confirmation of current proximity exists beyond Chapter 56.  
- Unclear Motivation: Why did Zalthir take Xinaya’s scrolls and refuse to share them? He says “I will figure it out later” — but what is the purpose? The scrolls’ content is never revealed.  
- Ambiguous Outcome: Zalthir was shrunken in ch54 — but the text says “she shrank” (referring to the spy). Is this a typo? Or did Zalthir consume the mushroom too? The text is inconsistent.  
- Unclear Future Intent: Zalthir was assigned by the First Reader to investigate Jimjar — but no follow-up is given. Is this a future plot thread? Or was it dropped?
