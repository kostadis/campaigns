### Asha Vandry

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Unknown (last observed in negotiation with Daz and the narrator; no indication of departure or relocation)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Devoted follower of Lolth; opposes Ilvara’s “bride heresy”  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:** None explicitly stated; carries knowledge of cavern politics and fungal corruption  
**Spells / Feats:** None explicitly stated  
**Wounds:** None mentioned  
**Current Assignment / Role:** Orchestrator of Ilvara’s elimination; tactical advisor on cavern power structures; tasked with identifying and neutralizing fungal contamination vectors  
**Key Relationships:**  
- Reports to no one explicitly; acts as independent high priestess of Lolth  
- Last seen engaging exclusively with Daz; has severed all direct communication with the narrator  
- Acknowledges Jorlan as the only other uninfected individual besides herself  
- Views Ilvara as a fallen high priestess and abomination; believes Ilvara’s spores have corrupted her former allies  
- Suspects Daz of being either infected by Zuggtmoy’s magic or a charlatan pretending devotion to Lolth  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Demanded Ilvara be killed, butchered, and sent to an unearthly grave to prevent the spread of fungal corruption  
- Identified Ilvara’s spores as the vector of infection (not the Heart Fungus itself)  
- Concluded the Dust of Suleiman is more effective as infrastructure demolition (targeting the Heart Fungus) than as a direct weapon against Ilvara  
- Tested Daz’s sincerity as a follower of Lolth, interpreting his caution as a potential ruse  
- Declared the narrator unworthy of direct communication, refusing to acknowledge them after initial engagement  
- Claimed to have resisted Ilvara’s influence through unwavering faith in Lolth and rejection of the bride heresy  
- Stated that only she and Jorlan remain uninfected among those who interacted with Ilvara  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Was caught in the emotional fallout of Jorlan and Ilvara’s past romantic relationship, forced into proximity with both for months  
- Believes Ilvara’s fungal transformation of allies is a perversion of Lolth’s will  
- Fears contamination from Zuggtmoy’s influence and avoids physical contact entirely  
- Views the Spider Queen’s manifestation as potentially a trick of Ilvara’s  
- Uses suspicion as a form of faith; her devotion manifests as paranoid vigilance
