### Xinaya

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Unknown (last seen at the Circle of Welcome, Underdark)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly of Menzoberranzan, under Ilvara; now deceased fungal host of the Lady of Decay  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Hand fused with a spider medallion covered in fungal growths  
- Half her face rotten and postulated, serving as a bed for mushroom sprouts  
- Infected by Phylo’s invitation to the Circle of Welcome, transformed into a fungal host  
- Formerly carried orders to capture the party (not kill) on Ilvara’s command  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Formerly tasked by Ilvara to capture the party; no longer active  
- Last known to be in contact with the party; pleaded for death and urged them to warn the Drow of the Demon Queen of the Fungi  
- Believed to have been taken by the Lady of Decay; the Great Seeder was present at her transformation  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Begged the party for death, citing possession by the Lady of Decay and the presence of the Great Seeder  
- Urged the party (specifically Daz, from Menzoberranzan) to warn the Drow of the Demon Queen of the Fungi in the Underdark  
- Was killed by Thorin at her own implicit consent, following consultation with Dawnbringer  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Accepted Phylo’s invitation to the Circle of Welcome, resulting in her infection and transformation  
- Her squad was also infected; she was the sole survivor who retained enough sentience to plead for mercy  
- Ilvara intended to kill the party herself — Xinaya’s mission was capture, not execution  

## Uncertainty
- The exact timing of her death relative to her final speech is ambiguous — she was crying and alive immediately before being killed, but the narrative does not clarify whether she spoke the warning *after* agreeing to die or during the plea for death.  
- Attribution of the "Lady of Decay took me" claim: Was this her own perception, or an external fungal influence? The text does not distinguish.  
- Whether the spider medallion was her own item before infection or a fungal growth is unclear.  
- The phrase "So close to liberation and yet so far" suggests a possible internal conflict or hope of redemption — but no evidence confirms whether this was genuine hope or fungal delusion.  
- No confirmation whether the Demon Queen of the Fungi is a known entity in Drow lore or a new name for the Lady of Decay or Great Seeder — this is reported as Xinaya’s belief, not verified fact.
