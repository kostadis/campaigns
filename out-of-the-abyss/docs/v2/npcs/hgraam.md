### Hgraam

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Cairngorm Cavern (entrance closed for a while)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent stone giant; formerly aligned with or closely associated with the Deep King of the Deep Gnomes  
**Current possessions / notable items:** A small pile of coins (offered to the party in ch28)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as a seer and guardian of stone wisdom, interpreting portents from the rock.  
- Last known to have had a close, personal bond with the Deep King, now strained due to the King’s paranoia following his friendship with Shal.  
- Refused access to Shal; Shal refuses to meet him.  
- The party believes Hgraam may be capable of reversing ill effects on Stone Giants (ch25).  
- Tends to rocks and maintains a solitary, meditative presence in his cavern.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Offered the party a small pile of coins after they clarified that the ritual was not the work of all Derro (ch28).  
- Declared the entrance to his cavern closed for a while and urged the party to leave (ch28).  
- Reaffirmed his belief that the surface world does not exist and that a world without stone overhead is incomprehensible (ch28).  
- Continues to perceive and articulate portents of evil stirring in the Underdark: rock crying in pain, madness spreading, tunnels shaking, earth rejecting its wards, and the pebble believing itself flesh (ch17, ch28).  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Deeply connected to the stones and the ancient rhythms of the earth; views the Deep King’s descent into paranoia as a catastrophic rupture in natural order.  
- Believes the tragedy of the Stone Giant’s madness was caused by forces beyond the victim’s control — implying he sees the madness as an external corruption, not a personal failing.  
- Holds a profound, almost spiritual reverence for stone and subterranean stability; the idea of the surface world is not just unknown to him, but ontologically alien.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of the "ill effects on the Stone Giants" that Hgraam may reverse (ch25) is unspecified — is this physical, mental, or magical?  
- Whether Hgraam’s refusal to meet Shal is due to Shal’s refusal alone, or if Hgraam has also withdrawn due to distrust or grief — the cause of the mutual estrangement is ambiguous.  
- Whether Hgraam’s offer of coins is a gesture of gratitude, payment for services rendered, or a symbolic offering with deeper meaning is unstated.  
- The identity of the “he” driven mad (ch17) is never named — though context suggests a Stone Giant, the specific individual remains unnamed.  
- The timeline of the Deep King’s paranoia and its direct link to Shal’s influence is described, but whether Shal is a manipulator, a victim, or something else is unclear.  
- Whether Hgraam still considers the Deep King a friend, an enemy, or a lost cause is not explicitly stated in the latest chapter.
