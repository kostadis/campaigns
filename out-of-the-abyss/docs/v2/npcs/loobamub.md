### Loobamub

- **Current status:** Alive  
- **Current location:** Near one of the exit holes identified by Rasharoo  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Follower of Basidia; Leader of the Circle of Hunters  
- **Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
- **Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
  - Leader of the Circle of Hunters, tasked with guiding and coordinating myconid efforts.  
  - Reports to and follows the doctrine of Basidia, who oversees reanimation practices.  
  - Last seen gathered with Rasharoo and the Circle of Hunters and Explorers near an exit hole.  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Solicited the party’s aid to deal with a grick, citing the need to recover the creature for reanimation.  
  - Upheld the Circle’s doctrine: myconids do not kill; all dead creatures are brought to the Circle of Masters for reanimation, per Phylo’s insistence.  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
  - Believes in the sanctity of reanimation over destruction; sees death as a resource for the Circle’s purpose.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Loobamub still personally leads the Circle of Hunters or if his role has shifted since gathering with Rasharoo — the facts confirm his leadership in ch31 but only his presence in ch33.  
- The relationship between Phylo and Basidia is referenced but not clarified — whether Phylo is superior, equal, or a former leader is unknown.  
- No current possessions, wounds, or spells are mentioned in ch33, so any prior items (if any) cannot be confirmed as still held.
