### The Librarian

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Within the sealed archive chamber of Menzoberranzan’s central record-hall  
**Allegiance/faction:** Serves as the living embodiment of the sealed public record; bound to the institutional memory of Menzoberranzan’s noble houses  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None explicitly stated; possesses authoritative access to ritual archive privileges (fourth seat) and legal authority to alter records  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acts as the custodian and embodiment of the sealed public record — “she is today the public record”  
- Enforces the transactional exchange of information: must receive information to grant information  
- Reports to the structural imperatives of Menzoberranzan’s noble hierarchy; bound by the rules of the ledger and the sealed record  
- Last seen interacting with the drow narrator, whom she found unexpectedly honest — a deviation from cultural norms that impressed her  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Confirmed that “struck” denotes a failed attempt to erase a record, not its elimination  
- Revealed that the fourth seat was struck — a record deletion requiring expensive infrastructure and political access  
- Disclosed that Menzoberranzan trains its people first to track debts and knowledge (the ledger), then to detect linguistic inconsistencies  
- Told the story of a noble house that failed to eliminate a child survivor, resulting in its downfall  
- Declined to name the sealed house or point to alternative access points to the sealed records, citing the seal’s binding nature  
- Expressed visible confusion and then quiet admiration at the drow narrator’s honesty, a trait alien to drow norms  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The sealed record is not merely hidden — it is *embodied* in her; speaking of the sealed house within the chamber is physically or magically impossible  
- Archive privileges (like those of the fourth seat) are not just access — they are the legal apparatus to alter records invisibly  
- Betrayal is rewarded in Menzoberranzan *only* if it strengthens the web of power — loyalty is transactional, not absolute  
- The child survivor of the destroyed house is referred to as a “blithering child idiot” — a phrase she uses, indicating her personal framing of the event  

## Uncertainty
- The exact identity of the sealed house remains unspoken and cannot be inferred from the facts.  
- Whether the librarian is the *only* living embodiment of the record, or if others share this role, is unclear.  
- The nature of the “infrastructure” required to strike the fourth seat is described but not named or located.  
- It is unclear whether her confusion and admiration toward the narrator imply a shift in allegiance or merely cognitive dissonance — no action beyond observation is recorded.  
- The phrase “she is today the public record” is metaphorical or literal? The facts do not clarify if she is magically bound, psychically linked, or simply the last custodian.  
- Attribution of the term “blithering child idiot”: while she used it, it is not confirmed whether she coined it or inherited it from another source.
