### Werz

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Shattered Spire (last seen sitting alone at a table)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent Duergar merchant; no formal allegiance, though his dealings involve Duergar clans and Blingdenstone gnomes  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Merchant facilitating illicit weapons deals between Duergar clans and the gnomes of Blingdenstone; actively seeking to resolve an assassination contract against him  
**Key relationships:**  
- Last seen interacting with Grygum’s group at the Shattered Spire  
- Has spoken to the owner of Gholbrorn’s Inn (claims to have “taken care of everything”)  
- Target of a contract by the Duergar assassin guild; contract is cancellable by Eldgrim for a fee  
- Clan Ironshadow’s leader is willing to wait out the Deepking’s madness, but other members are unhappy — Werz is likely connected to this unrest  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Admitted to facilitating forbidden weapons deals between clans, violating the Deepking’s edicts  
- Claimed to be broke after receiving 1100 gold for silence, and again when asked to pay for assassin removal  
- Attempted to bribe his assassins with a higher rate — they refused, citing professionalism  
- Declared he only cares about trade and survival, not politics  
- Asserted assassins are a “children’s fable” to the Stone Guards, implying he believes deniability is his best defense  
- Actively seeking to have his assassination contract canceled  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- His primary motivation is survival and continuing his trade operations — not loyalty, politics, or vengeance  
- He does not know why he was targeted for assassination, despite his illicit dealings  
- He is aware that misinformation could trigger inter-clan war, suggesting he understands the stakes of his trade  
- His claim that assassins are “professionals” and not personal enemies implies he has direct, non-lethal experience with them  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Werz claims assassins are a “children’s fable” to others, yet simultaneously admits he tried to bribe them and knows they are professionals — this suggests either deception or psychological dissonance, but the truth of his awareness is unclear.  
- Ambiguous chronology: Did Werz speak to the owner of Gholbrorn’s Inn before or after the assassination attempt? The timing of this action relative to his meeting with Grygum’s group is not specified.  
- Unclear attribution: Who paid the assassins? Werz says “somebody,” but no identity is revealed.  
- Uncertain current status of Gholbrorn’s Inn connection: Werz says he “will take care of everything” with the inn owner, but no follow-up confirms whether this was successful or even occurred.  
- Status of the assassination contract: While Daz asks Eldgrim about the hit, it is not confirmed whether the contract is still active, canceled, or suspended.
