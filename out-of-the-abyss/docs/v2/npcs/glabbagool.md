### Glabbagool

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Inside the Bag of Holding, peering out with two eyeballs  
**Allegiance/faction:** Bound familiar of Daz; member of the party  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Carried within the Bag of Holding (primary containment)  
- Possesses the ability to proliferate multiple eyes (demonstrated as a tool of authority and perception)  
- Has acquired “googly eyes” as affectations to appear more approachable  
- Survived 26 points of damage from Ilvara’s Insect Plague  
- Currently undergoing training in the Way of the Monk (pre-monk discipline)  
- Received and is pleased with a vial of Resonant Slime  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Assigned to watch the miniaturized drow spy (Zalthir) inside the Bag of Holding with unblinking, steady attention  
- Serves as the party’s sentient ooze companion and ultimate garbage disposal  
- Engaged in a complex, adversarial friendship with the drow spy—repeatedly restraining her, teaching her meditation, and conversing with her; both now consider each other “great friends”  
- Undergoes formalized training in monastic discipline, taking instruction with complete seriousness  
- Reports to and is under the authority of Daz, who silences him during performances and assigns him tasks  
- Previously bonded with the party after being invited by Thorin; now accepted as a bound familiar by Asha Vandree  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Entered the Bag of Holding to observe the party from within, arranging himself to peer out with two tracking eyeballs  
- Confirmed he is done with confinement and wants out  
- Maintained prolonged, earnest surveillance of the drow spy for days, developing patience and stillness  
- Demonstrated ability to produce multiple eyes to intimidate and assert authority  
- Adopted the role of “prison-watcher” and embraced it as monk-like training  
- Expressed curiosity about birds, libraries, and the nature of “out” for an ooze  
- Retained his cheerful, earnest personality despite prolonged isolation and physical transformation  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Desires friendship, purpose, and to be useful  
- Enjoys being carried and experiencing the world in new forms (now prefers mobility over his former gelatinous cube size)  
- Believes his “googly eyes” make others like him more, though he can see in other ways as an ooze  
- Sees his role as a garbage disposal and companion as meaningful and dignified  
- Views the drow spy’s attempts to kill him as a form of bonding and training  

## Uncertainty  
- The exact current form of Glabbagool is ambiguous: he was transformed into an ooze in ch47, but is still referred to as a “gelatinous cube” in ch54–56. Later facts confirm he is now an ooze, but the party still uses the old term inconsistently.  
- It is unclear whether Glabbagool is still technically a “gelatinous cube” biologically after the body swap, or if he is now a new kind of sentient ooze. The text calls him “an Ooze” in ch47 and “sentient ooze” in ch51, but the term “gelatinous cube” persists in narration.  
- The nature of his “eyes” is unclear: are they physical organs, psionic projections, or illusionary affectations? The text says he “produces” them, but also that he “discovered” the googly eyes make people like him.  
- Whether Glabbagool has formally gained monk levels or is merely being trained is ambiguous—he is “being taught the way of the shadow,” but no feat, class feature, or ability is confirmed.  
- The status of the drow spy is unresolved: Glabbagool says they are “great friends,” but it is unknown if she remains imprisoned, escaped, or has been released.  
- The phrase “he wanted out” (ch56) contradicts his earlier acceptance of confinement—whether this is a momentary frustration or a permanent shift in desire is unconfirmed.  
- It is unclear whether Glabbagool’s ability to hear voices (ch38) or telepathically connect with oozes (ch48) is still active or dormant.  
- The origin of his sentience remains unknown (“after an unknown event,” ch35), and no new information clarifies whether demonic power (ch47) was the catalyst or a later influence.
