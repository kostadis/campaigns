### Dancing Myconid

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed in Chapter 32, context implies proximity to the Myconid collective or a fungal nexus tied to Phylo’s plan)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Loyal to Phylo’s great plan and the Great Seeder  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None explicitly stated  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Serves as a messenger or herald for Phylo’s plan; actively recruits or informs outsiders (e.g., Thorin and Grygum) about the impending deployment of Myconids to the Overbright under Gasbide’s direction. Sings the same melody associated with Zuggtmoy’s dream.  
**Defining recent actions:** Declared the necessity of all Myconids for Phylo’s plan; sang the Zuggtmoy melody; inquired whether Thorin and Grygum had heard of Gasbide’s plan to send Myconids to the Overbright.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact location of the Dancing Myconid is not specified beyond being present during Chapter 32.
- It is unclear whether “Gasbide” is a Myconid, a higher entity, or an external force — no further context is given.
- The phrase “Greet Seeder” in the quote appears to be a typo for “Great Seeder”; this is assumed but not confirmed.
- The connection between the melody and Zuggtmoy is noted by Thorin, but the Dancing Myconid’s awareness or intent in singing it is unexplained.
- Whether the Dancing Myconid is acting independently or under direct command of Gasbide or Phylo is ambiguous.
