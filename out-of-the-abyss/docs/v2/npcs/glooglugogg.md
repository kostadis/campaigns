### Glooglugogg (Gloog)

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Deep Mother’s temple complex, within the Kuo-Toa village (last seen preparing for imminent conflict)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Loyalist faction of the Deep Mother (Sea Mother), aligned with his father Ploopploopeen  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acting as a liaison and motivator for the escapees, urging them to join the fight for the Deep Mother.  
- Reports to and supports his father, Ploopploopeen.  
- Last seen with the escapees and Kuo-Toa loyalists, preparing for battle.  
- Disapproves of his sister Bloppblippodd, whom he accuses of disrespecting their ways and falling prey to madness.  
- Considers the escapees allies who have shown respect to the Deep Mother and understands their ways.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Introduced himself to the escapees as “Gloog” and affirmed his loyalty to his father and the Deep Mother.  
- Condemned his sister Bloppblippodd for undermining Kuo-Toa traditions and fracturing their society.  
- Rejected the use of outsiders in their internal conflict but accepted the escapees as allies due to their respect for the Deep Mother.  
- Declined to let the escapees strike first, insisting the conflict be resolved honorably by their own people.  
- Expressed surprise and approval that the escapees did not execute the captured derro, calling it “both honorable and odd.”  
- Instructed the escapees to rest, as battle is imminent.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes Bloppblippodd’s influence is dangerous but numerically limited; the majority of the village still supports the Deep Mother.  
- Sees himself as the moral heir to his father’s authority, correcting the perceived leniency shown to his sister.  
- Values honor, tradition, and the sanctity of their people’s ways above expediency.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Glooglugogg holds any official title or position beyond being Ploopploopeen’s son — his role as a motivator may be informal.  
- The phrase “our society is torn to pieces by my sister” suggests deep internal conflict, but no concrete evidence confirms whether he leads any faction beyond his personal stance.  
- While he says “we will have to fight soon,” it is unknown whether he will personally engage in combat or remain in a command/support role.  
- The exact nature of the “madness” he accuses his sister of is never defined — is it supernatural, ideological, or psychological?  
- Though he says the escapees “showed respect to the Sea Mother,” it is unclear if this is a genuine belief or a tactical justification for allying with them.  
- His claim that “her hard-core supporters are few” and “her side is unknown” contradicts no other fact, but the uncertainty of village alignment remains unresolved — is this his assessment, or is it truly unknown?
