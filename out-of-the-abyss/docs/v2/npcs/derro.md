### Derro

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Near a tent, likely within or adjacent to Grey Ghosts territory  
**Allegiance/faction:** Grey Ghosts  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated, but carries knowledge of a shimmering obsidian scale pulled from a “dark lake” and familiarity with ritualistic demands of “the prince”  
**Current assignment / role:** Member of the Grey Ghosts, involved in ritual or magical preparation (possibly harvesting or refining materials for the prince’s demands); actively engaged in planning vengeance for the murdered High Council  
**Defining recent actions:** Participated in ritualistic discourse about a shimmering obsidian scale from a deep, voice-filled lake; acknowledged the prince’s insatiable hunger for contributions; publicly declared the Grey Ghosts’ intent to avenge the High Council’s murder  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believes in the necessity of collecting strange, otherworldly materials (e.g., the obsidian scale) to satisfy “the prince”; operates under a hierarchy where mastery demands constant offerings; holds a grudge tied to the death of the High Council, suggesting internal factional trauma or loyalty to a fallen authority  

## Uncertainty
- The identity of “the prince” is never named or clarified — is it a Derro lord, an external entity, or a title?  
- It is unclear whether the Derro who spoke of the obsidian scale is the same one who declared revenge — or if these are two distinct Derro with different roles.  
- The exact nature of the “dark lake” and the origin of the scale remain unexplained.  
- The phrase “more of this” lacks a clear referent — what is “this” being demanded? The scale? Blood? Magic?  
- The tent’s purpose and who (if anyone) controls it are unspecified.  
- It is unknown whether the two Derro seen in ch24 are still together or if one has since died, departed, or been reassigned.
