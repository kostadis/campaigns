### Chief Dorbo Diggermattock

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Blingdenstone, presiding over the council chamber in Diggermattock Hall  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of Clan Diggermattock and de facto ruler of Blingdenstone  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Supreme leader of Blingdenstone; overseeing defense against the Pudding King, economic recovery, labor shortages, and preparation to reopen trade with the surface world  
**Key relationships:**  
- Direct superior to Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock (his assistant, with whom he has had heated disputes)  
- Relies on Daz the dragonborn as the designated military leader for the assault against the Pudding King  
- Views the Gold Whisker clan as squatters and excludes them from clan meetings  
- Answerable to no higher authority; holds absolute command over Blingdenstone’s military and civic affairs  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Declared a final offensive against the Pudding King, publicly naming Daz as the leader of the assault.  
- Expressed urgent concern over acid attacks from oozes threatening troop weapons and lives.  
- Led a council meeting addressing Blingdenstone’s economy, labor shortages, and defense.  
- Actively preparing to reopen Blingdenstone’s trade routes to the surface world following a peace arrangement.  
- Previously authorized the party to handle the ooze crisis, despite calling their initial plan “the dumbest he had ever heard.”  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Prioritizes wealth and stability over inclusivity, as shown by his refusal to recognize the Gold Whisker clan.  
- Publicly projects strength and resolve, but privately reacts with alarm to existential threats (e.g., the Pudding King).  
- Under pressure from Senni and Daz, he has shifted from reluctant bureaucrat to decisive wartime leader.  

## Uncertainty
- The nature of the “peace arrangement” enabling trade reopening is unspecified — who negotiated it, and with whom?  
- Whether Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock is still actively serving as his assistant, or if their earlier conflict has resulted in a change in their working relationship, is unclear.  
- The exact source of the “peace arrangement” is not named — whether it was brokered by the party, another faction, or unilaterally by Dorbo.  
- While Dorbo is called “leader of Blingdenstone” in ch48, earlier facts only name him as leader of Clan Diggermattock — the extent of his political consolidation over the entire city is implied but not explicitly confirmed beyond his authority to declare war and reopen trade.
