### drow prisoners count

The total number of drow prisoners currently accounted for is 12: Daz, Sarith, Stool, Shuushar, Topsy, Turvy, Gyrgum, Zalthir, Buppido, Jimjar, Eldeth, and Xinaya.

Xinaya is one of two drow turned into slaves, implying at least one other drow prisoner shares her status — but the identity of the second is not specified.

## Uncertainty
- The identity of the second drow turned into a slave alongside Xinaya is unknown; no other drow is explicitly stated to share this status.
- It is unclear whether all 11 drow named in Chapter 1 are still prisoners as of Chapter 32 — no later confirmation or contradiction is provided.
- Xinaya’s squad is mentioned as captured, but its size or composition beyond Xinaya is not specified; it is unknown if any of her squadmates are among the 11 named earlier or are additional prisoners.
