### Vareth

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Candlekeep  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Scholar of Candlekeep; affiliated with the Bahamutian scholarly tradition; assigned as Grygum’s handler  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items/Spells/Feats/Wounds:** None specified  
**Current Assignment / Role:** Handler for Grygum at Candlekeep; leading academic researcher on half-orc clerics of Bahamut  
**Key Relationships:**  
- Reports to/works under institutional authority at Candlekeep  
- Assigned to handle Grygum  
- Last seen interacting with the player character (referred to as a “weak Stroudite”)  
- Previously had direct contact with the First Reader (now estranged or superseded in authority)  
- Recognized by peers as the leading authority on Bahamutian half-orc conversions; frequently invited to conferences  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Named and formalized the “strong-weak hypothesis” regarding Bahamutian converts, distinguishing between “strong Stroudites” (who reject fixed points entirely) and “strong Bahamutians” (who operate around fixed points).  
- Declared the player character a “weak Stroudite,” indicating a doctrinal disagreement.  
- Characterized the “fixed points” approach as a “compromise of the Bahamutian school.”  
- Expressed institutional restraint during a prolonged tortle handoff, suggesting ongoing administrative tension.  
- Received funding and conference invitations following the controversy around Stroud’s conversion.  
- Adopted and intends to deploy the term “Stroud’s Gambit” in academic discourse.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Devoted to uncovering the causality and structural pattern behind half-orc conversions to Bahamut — a niche he pursued while others pursued “more fashionable problems.”  
- Sees the player character as a living data point and potential validation of his theories.  
- Motivated by scholarly recognition and the desire to systematize a phenomenon he believes has been ignored by mainstream academia.  
- Believes the “strong-weak hypothesis” is a definitive classification — naming it with the satisfaction of filing a correct taxonomic entry.
