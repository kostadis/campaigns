### Ploopploopeen

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Near the Deep Father’s cult site, preparing to present the party as an offering to Bloppblippodd  
**Allegiance/faction:** Archpriest of the Sea Mother (traditional Kuo-toan faith)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Uses a *Tongues* spell (recently realized it failed to convey intent accurately)  
- Carries no explicitly named items, but is actively orchestrating a ritual deception  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Leading a covert operation to infiltrate the cult of Leemooggoogoon (the Deep Father) by presenting the adventurers as a “token of peace and reconciliation” — though they are in fact infiltrators.  
- Plans to accompany the party when they are presented as an offering to his daughter, Bloppblippodd, the archpriest of the Deep Father.  
- Intends for his followers to launch a surprise attack when the offering is made.  
- Has a strained relationship with his daughter, Bloppblippodd, whom he views as corrupted by the Deep Father.  
- Claims the Sea Mother has answered his prayers and that Leemooggoogoon is the true evil.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Offered the party 500 gold, two potions of healing, two boats, and navigational guides in exchange for distracting Bloppblippodd and disrupting the Deep Father’s rituals.  
- Declared the Sea Mother the true faith of the Kuo-toans and denounced the Deep Father’s cult as heretical.  
- Attempted to use a white flag as a parley gesture — rejected by Bloppblippodd.  
- Now intends to personally deliver the party as an offering to Bloppblippodd, while secretly planning to have his followers strike during the ritual.  
- Believes unwilling sacrifices (like the party) are more potent for the Deep Father, and thus more effective for his sabotage.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks to eliminate his daughter’s cult and reclaim religious authority for the Sea Mother.  
- Views the party not as prisoners, but as weapons — willing pawns in a religious coup.  
- His use of the Tongues spell backfired, revealing he is not as in control of communication as he believed.  

## Uncertainty  
- Pronoun inconsistency: Multiple facts refer to Ploopploopeen as “she” (ch09), others as “he” (ch10). The latest chapter (ch10) uses “he/his,” but the earlier consistent usage was “she.” Which is correct?  
- Gender ambiguity: The entity is referred to as both “she” and “he” — no clear resolution in later text.  
- “His followers” vs “her followers”: Ch09 uses “her true believers,” ch10 uses “his followers.” Contradiction in possessive pronouns.  
- Is Ploopploopeen’s daughter Bloppblippodd the archpriest of the Deep Father? Ch09 implies it; ch10 says “I will present you to her” — but does not explicitly confirm her title.  
- Did Ploopploopeen actually receive the two boats and guides? The reward was promised, but no confirmation of delivery.  
- The “Tongues spell” — is this a spell Ploopploopeen cast, or was it cast on him? Unclear source.  
- “Walks outside while standing” — is this a literal description of Kuo-toan sleep behavior, or a narrative error? No other context clarifies.
