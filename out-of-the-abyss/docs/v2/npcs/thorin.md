### Thorin

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** On the surface, near Daggerford, traveling with the Ember Vanguard  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Member of the Ember Vanguard (self-proclaimed faction); no longer serving any Duergar or surface power structure.  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Dawnbringer (sentient radiant sword, now in her care; Thorin refers to her as “she” and considers her a friend)  
- His reclaimed tooth (taken by Ilvara at Velkynvelve, recovered from the confiscated belongings chest)  
- A steel dart (used as a ritual component)  
- A Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard (negotiated with Dawnbringer as a sign of commitment)  
- A Diggermattock Miners patch (honorary, sewn onto his gear)  
- A bag of holding (containing the miniaturized drow spy, referred to as “Pocket Drow”)  
- Glabbagool (the sentient ooze companion, now a full member of the party)  
- Javelins (acquired proficiency; primary ranged weapon)  
- Light crossbow (borrowed from Grygum, used in combat; now likely retained)  
- A set of giant-sized clothing (worn out of habit)  
- A copy of *The Case of the Missing Book* (received as payment for investigation work)  

**Notable Spells/Feats:**  
- Proficient with thrown weapons (Sharpshooter’s instinct trained)  
- Tremor Sense (acquired for Underdark use, now largely useless on the surface)  
- Hill Strike (battle technique learned from a hill giant battle master)  
- Grappling expertise (demonstrated against Asha Vandree)  
- Expertise in contract law (learned from dealings with giants)  
- Blindsight (acquired during Underdark travels)  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **Role:** Frontline combatant and tactical pragmatist of the Ember Vanguard; de facto leader in direct-action scenarios.  
- **Key Relationships:**  
  - **Dawnbringer:** Sentient sword; partner in combat and emotional support; undergoing therapy with Philemon.  
  - **Grygum:** Trusted ally; shared unspoken understanding (“blood oath” silence); mutual respect.  
  - **Eldeth:** Drow who offered him clan membership; Thorin is seeking to integrate his dwarven heritage with his giant upbringing and has accepted her promise to bring him to Mithral Hall.  
  - **Glabbagool:** Comrade and companion; Thorin considers him a friend and has advocated for his inclusion in the group.  
  - **Zalthir, Daz, Stool, Asha Vandree:** Fellow Ember Vanguard members; Thorin respects their skills, even when he doesn’t understand their motivations.  
  - **Philemon:** Therapist for sentient artifacts; Thorin and Dawnbringer are under his care.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Led the Ember Vanguard out of the Underdark into the surface world.  
- Toppled a bridge and destroyed the Heart Fungus with Dawnbringer in a single decisive round, ending Ilvara’s reign.  
- Successfully negotiated with the Candlekeep Archivists for access and payment (one book each) in exchange for investigating Janussi’s murder.  
- Advocated for and secured therapy for Dawnbringer with Philemon, acknowledging the sword’s sentience and emotional needs.  
- Reclaimed his stolen tooth from Ilvara’s confiscated belongings, calling it “very sentimental.”  
- Proposed and implemented the “Ember Grapple” tactic with Zalthir and Daz.  
- Declared himself and his companions a new faction — the Ember Vanguard — rejecting all prior allegiances.  
- Chose to spare Asha Vandree after grappling her to prevent spellcasting, demonstrating a growing restraint.  
- Suggested and supported the use of non-lethal force, poison containment, and psychological tactics over brute force where possible.  
- Accepted Eldeth’s invitation to join her clan, seeking to reconcile his dual identity as a dwarf raised by giants.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Thorin is no longer the “happy-go-lucky kid” who entered the Underdark; he has undergone a “dark transition,” accepting that the Underdark doesn’t care who you want to be.  
- He seeks to become an integrated person — honoring both his dwarven roots and his giant upbringing — not as a contradiction, but as a whole.  
- He views Dawnbringer not as a tool, but as a sentient companion, and is committed to her well-being, even if it means therapy and emotional labor.  
- He believes in radical transparency — but only when it serves the moment; he withholds profound thoughts when they won’t change the next ten seconds.  
- He carries the tooth as a symbol of unresolved debt; reclaiming it was not just about possession, but closure.  
- He is weary, but not broken; his exhaustion is of the “fire kind,” not the sleep kind — a deep, quiet weariness from survival, loss, and transformation.
