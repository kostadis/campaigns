### Droki

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Inside a narrow fissure behind cloth, canvas, and moldy boxes in the West Cleft District, last seen entering a crack in the wall with mushrooms on either side; trail continues east through a thicket of fungi after crossing a lake.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Agent of the Council of Savants (confirmed by multiple sources including the ghost, Keepers of the Flame, and Gartokkar); suspected by Errde Blackskull of aiding the Council’s corruption.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Floppy hat with two tentacle-like things that bob up and down in time with his pace.  
**Notable spells/feats/wounds:** Appears to move with unnatural speed — “walking too quickly for a Derro, as if there was something magical about his pace.” Consumed mushrooms from the trail near the fissure; possible magical or altered state implied.  
**Current assignment / role:** Derro courier and messenger for the Council of Savants; trusted by all factions to deliver messages safely through the Derro slums to the Whorlstone Caverns and keep secrets.  
**Defining recent actions:** Entered a hidden fissure in the West Cleft District after traversing a well-worn path marked with Derro-dropped junk and patches of Bigwig and Pygmywort mushrooms. Was observed muttering obsessively (“Droki is late! They will be so angry!... No time, no rhyme, not mine! No! Time!”) and showed no concern about being followed.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Deeply fixated on time and delays; muttering suggests paranoia or psychological disturbance. His unusual pace and mushroom consumption imply possible magical influence or transformation. Despite being labeled “peculiar” and “mad,” he is trusted across faction lines — suggesting his role is critical and his reliability is absolute, even if his sanity is not.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Initially described as a Duergar (ch15), but consistently referred to as a Derro from ch17 onward — later sources override earlier, so Derro is current, but origin of initial misidentification is unclear.  
- Ambiguous attribution: Gartokkar identifies Droki as a Gray Ghosts agent (ch16), while Keepers of the Flame and the ghost identify him as a Council of Savants agent (ch17). No resolution given whether he serves both, or if one faction is mistaken.  
- Unclear current state of mushrooms: Consumed mushrooms are noted, but their effect (magical, toxic, enhancing) is not specified — whether they altered his speed, perception, or stability is inferred, not confirmed.  
- Unknown if Droki is still inside the fissure or has moved beyond it — last confirmed action is entry; no follow-up confirms exit or current position beyond the trail leading east.  
- Errde Blackskull suspects Droki of aiding the Council, but no evidence is stated — whether this is suspicion or confirmed fact is ambiguous.
