### Buppido

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** His lair in the caves near Laduguer’s Furrow, now known as the final resting place of Buppido  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly associated with the Council of Savants; later claimed divine mission independent of any faction  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Macabre altar of bones and skeletons (constructed by him), makeshift weapon (hooked spear), shortsword with bloodstains, vest stained with residue from shrine construction  
**Notable spells/feats:** Capable of mind-projection speech; consumed The Tongue of Madness mushroom; cannot perform interplanar or gate travel  
**Current assignment / role:** None — deceased. Last known role: self-proclaimed divine figure leading a cultist shrine; guide to the western gate of Laduguer’s Furrow; informant on Droki and Derro tunnels  
**Key relationships:** Former business partner of Pelek (whom he killed and dismembered); old friend of Daz; former associate of the flumph; led Grygum and Thorin’s group to the western gate; last seen addressing Thorin’s party as potential worshippers  
**Defining recent actions:** Constructed a shrine of bones and skeletons in the caves; claimed divine mission to save Derro from the failing Council of Savants; attacked Thorin’s party with a hooked spear after declaring himself a god; was killed by Daz’s magic missiles; his corpse lies scattered among the bones of his altar.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of Buppido’s "divine plan" is inconsistently framed: sometimes as a personal delusion, sometimes as a mission tied to the Council of Savants, sometimes as a post-fall revelation. No single source defines its origin definitively.  
- Whether Buppido’s madness began with the Faerzress glow or with his disillusionment over the Council of Savants is not clearly ordered chronologically.  
- The phrase “Buppido, before he became mad, as The Flumph” (ch19) is ambiguous — it may be a misstatement or a reference to a separate entity, but no other evidence confirms “The Flumph” is a distinct being.  
- The quote “Buppido is now known as The Flumph” (ch19) directly contradicts all other evidence that Buppido is dead and The Flumph is a separate, surviving entity referencing him. This contradiction cannot be resolved without additional context.  
- It is unclear whether the “faithful” mentioned in ch17 were real followers or delusional projections — no other characters confirm interacting with them.  
- The source of the blood on Buppido’s shortsword is never definitively confirmed: was it Pelek, Yukyuk, or another?  
- The phrase “We are once again at the final resting place of Buppido” (ch25) implies the party returned to his lair, but no chapter describes this return — only the reference.
