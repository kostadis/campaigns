### Dark Lady

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed in a liminal space between waking and dreaming)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demon Queen of Fungi; sovereign of the Rot and Decay  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None explicitly held or worn; embodies the mycelial network as her extension.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Sovereign of a sentient fungal realm; seeks to entwine willing minds into the endless mycelium. Last known interaction was with wanderers of flesh and bone, offering them transcendence through fusion with her essence.  
**Defining recent actions:** Manifested in a liminal threshold, addressing intruders with poetic menace; offered eternal union through fungal transcendence, framing it as both revelation and corruption.
