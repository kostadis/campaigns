### Drow Spy

- **Current status:** Alive, subdued, and under the party’s control.  
- **Current location:** In the possession of the party, following Grygum’s ritual and the exchange of the prayer beads.  
- **Allegiance/faction:** House T’sarran, loyal to her matron mother.  
- **Current possessions / notable items:**  
  - Prayer beads (given to her by Grygum and now worn).  
  - Piwafwi (offered as a trophy; current possession status ambiguous — not confirmed taken or retained).  
- **Notable spells/feats/wounds:**  
  - Affected by Grygum’s *Reduce* spell (stunned and shrunk).  
  - Stunned by Zalthir’s strike.  
  - Professional armor compromised by an unknown religious ritual.  
- **Current assignment / role:** Formerly assigned to track the party on the surface without engagement; now incapacitated and compliant.  
- **Key relationships:**  
  - Reports to Matron Mother of House T’sarran (who has ruled for 200 years).  
  - Matron is waiting for the party to surface and is interested in Daz and the one who paid for his protection.  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Was tracking the party silently on the surface.  
  - Was caught off-guard by the party’s response to her surveillance.  
  - Was subdued by Zalthir and reduced by Grygum.  
  - Accepted the prayer beads and surrendered her piwafwi.  
  - Refused to give her name but revealed strategic intelligence about House T’sarran’s objectives.  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
  - Her loyalty is tied to her matron’s will: “The matron mother cares, and therefore I care.”  
  - House T’sarran’s true target is not the party, but the mysterious patron who paid for the protagonist’s protection.  
  - Matron expects the patron to reveal themselves upon the protagonist’s arrival on the surface.  
  - She believes her matron will sense her failure even without direct confirmation.  

## Uncertainty
- Whether the piwafwi was taken by the party or remains with her — she offered it, but no confirmation of retrieval.  
- Whether she is still under the effects of *Reduce* or has recovered (spell duration not specified).  
- Exact current physical condition beyond “stunned and shrunk” — no indication of healing or movement since.  
- Whether she is still actively reporting to her matron or if the ritual disrupted that connection.  
- Ambiguity in attribution: Grygum’s ritual “slipped past every prepared defense” — but it is unclear if this refers to her armor, magic, or both.  
- Contradiction: She was “defeated, frustrated, annoyed” after receiving the beads, yet later stares blankly at Thorin — emotional state shift unexplained.
