### Rasharoo

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Traveling through the Underdark, en route to Blingdenstone (last seen departing with the party on the 4th day of the first tenday of Kythorn 1495)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of the Circle of Explorers; loyal to Basidia  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Escape routes and nutrient caches (multiple known locations)  
**Current assignment / role:** Acting as a guide and protector for the party; leading them toward Blingdenstone with a designated guide for the first half of the journey  
**Key relationships:**  
- Close ally and subordinate of Basidia (follows her into danger despite caution)  
- Friend and collaborator of the party (provided guidance, escape route, and logistical support)  
- Opposes Phylo’s leadership and the plan to move Myconids to the Overbright  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Urged the party to flee the Inner Circle after Basidia confronted Phylo, shouting a telepathic warning: *“If you want to live, come with me.”*  
- Led the group to a pre-established escape hatch to evade capture or death.  
- Offered to arrange a guide for half the journey to Blingdenstone, after which the party would be on their own.  
- Bid farewell to the party as they continued into the Underdark.  

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
- Believes Myconids are naturally nomadic and belong in the Underdark; rejects the idea of settling in the Overbright as unnatural and dangerous.  
- Holds a spiritual belief that the tunnels and caves of the Underdark are a single, vast Myconid entity, and that individual Myconids are messages or expressions of it.  
- Maintains secret escape routes and nutrient caches as contingency plans for mass flight, indicating foresight and preparation for collapse of the Grove.  
- Deeply concerned by the Grove’s deterioration, observing its decline with Basidia and preparing for its potential collapse.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact identity of the “guide” Rasharoo arranged for the party is not named or described.  
- Whether Rasharoo is still traveling with the party after bidding farewell is ambiguous — he may have continued with them, returned to the Grove, or gone elsewhere.  
- The location of the escape hatch he used is known to exist but not specified.  
- It is unclear whether the “escape routes and nutrient caches” are solely his personal preparations or shared with the Circle of Explorers as a whole.  
- The nature of the telepathic communication (“shouts into my brain”) is attributed to Daz’s perspective — it is not confirmed whether Rasharoo can telepathically communicate with others beyond Daz.
