### Stool

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove, among his myconid kin  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid colony (specifically, the Great Circle of Neverlight Grove)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Possesses and regularly uses rapport spores to establish telepathic group-mind connections with nearby creatures.  
- Has consumed Nilhogg’s nose, enhancing his sense of smell (and sensitivity to unpleasant odors).  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Now fully reintegrated into his myconid community in Neverlight Grove.  
- Maintains a close, affectionate bond with Rumpadump, whom he considers a friend and insists is harmless.  
- Deeply loyal to Grygum, whom he credits for saving him and convincing him to leave the Grove temporarily; still holds gratitude for Thorin, who carried him during travel.  
- Views Zalthir as a “grouchy but really nice softer” who saved Rumpadump.  
- Communicates primarily through spore-based rapport, not speech; expresses emotion through physical proximity (e.g., curling against companions) and enthusiastic, repetitive verbalizations.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully reconnected with his people in Neverlight Grove after being captured by Duergar and later rescued by Zalthir.  
- Insisted the party save Rumpadump, despite evidence of myconid corruption, and ultimately accepted Grygum’s counsel to return to his people.  
- Shouted “We’re home!” upon reentering Neverlight Grove and bid farewell to the party with visible, quiet sadness.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes myconids are inherently harmless and would never attack unless provoked — a belief he holds despite witnessing corrupted, dancing myconids and harvested zombie-like forms.  
- Seeks belonging and emotional connection; his attachment to Grygum and Rumpadump stems from deep loneliness and fear of isolation.  
- Views communication through spores as superior to verbal language — a perspective affirmed even by Grygum, who later compared Elin’s silence to Stool’s profound, non-verbal expressiveness.  

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Stool retains any lingering spore-link with former companions (e.g., Daz, Grygum) after departure — no evidence confirms or denies ongoing telepathic connection.  
- The phrase “really nice softer” (ch31) is likely a misstatement; the intended word is unknown, and its meaning remains ambiguous.  
- Whether Stool has fully accepted the corruption within his colony or still denies it is unresolved — he insists myconids are harmless, but no later statement confirms if he now acknowledges the threat.  
- His current physical condition (e.g., any wounds from Duergar captivity or the Neverlight Grove conflict) is not mentioned post-ch33.  
- The exact nature of his relationship with the two large myconids he was seen chatting with (ch31) is unspecified.
