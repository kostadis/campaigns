### Shuushar the Awakened

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Traveling with the group toward Sloobludop (last known direction as of ch09)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Kuo-Toan; affiliated with the Sea Mother’s faith, reverent of Ploopploopeen  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acts as translator and spiritual guide for the group; refuses to repeat offensive language but translates it verbatim.  
- Advises and supports Thorin, suggesting Ploopploopeen, archpriest of the Sea Mother in Sloobludop, may aid his madness.  
- Maintains a pragmatic, calm demeanor; encourages others to accept daily hardships as routine (“These are daily chores, nothing more”).  
- Previously coordinated with Topsy and Turvy to use Gyrgum’s cooking as a distraction for theft.  
- Has a working rapport with Gyrgum, recognizing his culinary skill as potentially divine to his people.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Affirmed the group’s decision to continue their journey toward Sloobludop (ch09).  
- Repeatedly advocated for seeking Ploopploopeen in Sloobludop to address Thorin’s madness (ch07).  
- Maintained linguistic discipline by refusing to repeat Drow insults while accurately translating their threats and intentions (ch01).  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks spiritual balance by abstaining from repeating offensive speech — a personal discipline tied to coping with his race’s “madness.”  
- Holds deep respect for Ploopploopeen as a potential healer, indicating a faith-based orientation toward the Sea Mother’s clergy.  
- Believes tracker lore (e.g., reading signs from the “circle of life”) is absurd — suggests skepticism toward conventional wilderness tracking.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Shuushar still travels with Topsy and Turvy; they were last mentioned in ch01 as planning food theft, but their current presence is unconfirmed in later chapters.  
- It is unclear whether Shuushar has any magical abilities or divine connection beyond his spiritual beliefs — no spells, feats, or divine powers are explicitly stated.  
- The nature of “the madness of my race” is never defined — whether it refers to Kuo-Toa, Drow, or another group remains ambiguous.  
- It is unclear whether Shuushar’s “smile” or mouth movement is a genuine expression or a physical limitation — no clarification on his species’ facial anatomy beyond ch01.  
- While Ploopploopeen is named as the archpriest of the Sea Mother in Sloobludop, no confirmation exists that this figure is real, reachable, or willing to help — only Shuushar’s belief is stated.
