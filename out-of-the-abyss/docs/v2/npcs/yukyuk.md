### Yukyuk

**Current status:** Deceased  
**Current location:** Inside the dangerous tunnels, where his corpse was found  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a guide goblin allied with Spiderbait; joined the escapee group after being promised 20 gold  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified beyond clothing — relaxed shoes, baggy pants, shirts with buttons above the knees, wide sleeves, and a beanie (last seen tossed on ground in ch05); tattoos written in draconic script spelling goblin words  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Was guiding the escapee group through the Silken Paths; last seen traveling with Spiderbait and the group led by Jimjar. Was impressed by and in awe of Zalthir after witnessing his web-walking combat technique.  
**Defining recent actions:** Joined the escapee group after being promised 20 gold; guided the group through the Silken Paths; expressed admiration for Zalthir’s spiderweb maneuvering; suggested an improved tactical approach during combat (“sliding under the spiders and cutting the web from the other side”).  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believed the web was the safest route through the Silken Paths, claiming all clients made it out alive; held deep knowledge of the web’s dangers and spider behavior; dismissed fire as ineffective due to spiders’ rapid rebuilding.
