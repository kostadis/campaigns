### Gartokkar

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Gracklstugh, within the Keepers of the Flame’s stronghold (likely near Themberchaud’s cavern)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Custodian and high-ranking agent of the Keepers of the Flame; claims to serve Themberchaud but secretly believes Themberchaud serves them. Acts as an agent of the Deepking of Gracklstugh, though the Deepking is unaware of the true nature of the red dragon egg.  

**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Gold badges bearing Themberchaud’s profile (issued to agents)  
- Holy symbols of Laduguer (issued to agents)  
- Knowledge of the red dragon egg’s location and occult markings (now observed and feared)  

**Current Assignment / Role:**  
- Primary liaison between the Keepers of the Flame and external agents (including the party).  
- Tasked with retrieving the stolen red dragon egg and proof of the Gray Ghosts’ involvement.  
- Acting as an intermediary for the Deepking, though the Deepking remains unaware that the egg is not merely a replacement for Themberchaud but carries occult significance.  
- Recently warned the party about the Deepking’s shadowy agents (Menzoberranzan-style assassins) and the volatile state of Gracklstugh.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Demanded the party retrieve the statue of Demogorgon as proof of demonic worship.  
- Offered 2,000 gold as payment for services rendered.  
- Threatened the party with exposure to the Stone Guards if they refused to serve the Keepers.  
- Warned the party that the Deepking’s agents are hunting a fearsome mercenary band resembling the Ember Vanguard, and that the city is on the brink of explosion due to rising tensions.  
- Observed occult markings on the red dragon egg and became visibly worried, suggesting a shift in his understanding of its nature.  
- Expressed fear that the Ember Vanguard’s actions could trigger city-wide catastrophe.  
- Urged caution, noting that “some want something to happen.”  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Believes the Keepers of the Flame control Themberchaud through psionics, Deepking influence, and ritual — not the reverse.  
- Seeks a genocidal war against the Derro.  
- Is paranoid and erratic; his actions are destabilizing Gracklstugh’s authority.  
- Hides his fear of the egg’s true nature — which he now recognizes as dangerous — from the Deepking.  
- Will hunt down any betrayer “no matter the cave, pool, river, or place in the dark.”  

## Uncertainty  
- Gartokkar is referred to as both a “Duergar priest” and the “Deepking of Gracklstugh” in different chapters. These cannot both be true — is he the Deepking, or merely a high priest serving the Deepking? The chapter 23 facts claim he *is* the Deepking, but earlier chapters portray him as a subordinate priest. Later facts (ch28–29) refer to “the Deepking” as a separate entity whom Gartokkar reports to or fears. This is a direct contradiction.  
- Chapter 16: Gartokkar says, “We lost face when the Gray Ghosts stole the red dragon egg.” Chapter 23: “The Deep King will be delighted when he sees it, though he knows nothing yet of its true destiny.” — Is Gartokkar the Deepking, or is the Deepking someone else? If Gartokkar is not the Deepking, who is?  
- Chapter 18: Gartokkar says, “I could give them badges!” — implying he has authority to issue badges, consistent with being a high priest. But chapter 23 explicitly calls him “the Deep King.” No later fact resolves this.  
- Chapter 28: Gartokkar asks Grygum (a cleric of Bahamut) for his opinion on the egg — suggesting he is now in direct contact with the party, not merely issuing orders. But his exact current physical location or whether he still holds the egg is not stated.  
- Chapter 28: Gartokkar is described as afraid the city will explode — but what caused this fear? Is it the egg? The Ember Vanguard? The Deepking’s agents? The text implies it’s the egg’s markings, but does not confirm.  
- Chapter 23: “Gartokkar’s paranoia” is cited, but also “The Deepking’s escalating paranoia and erratic edicts.” Are these the same person? The text does not clarify.  
- The phrase “Gartokkar is the Deepking” in chapter 23 is the only direct assertion — all other references treat “the Deepking” as a separate figure. This contradiction is unresolved.
