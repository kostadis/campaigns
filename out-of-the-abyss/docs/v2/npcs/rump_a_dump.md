### Rump-a-dump

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid circle of Neverlight Grove (loyal to their sovereign)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Member of the Neverlight Grove myconid circle, aligned with Stool.  
- Recently convinced Stool to flee the Grove with the party, then chose to remain with his people.  
- Has a close, if contrasting, friendship with Stool — respects him but finds his verbosity hindering.  
- Expressed concern over Voosbur’s spores and the corruption of myconid nature.  
- Last seen giving Zalthir a list of fifty specific personal improvements, including: “Listen to Stool,” “Don’t assume you know everything,” and “Eat more Zurkhwood.”  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Identified Voosbur as the source of the corrupting spores that changed the myconids.  
- Believed the dancing and “Lady’s dream” speech were unnatural and forced.  
- Worried the spores had reached Neverlight Grove, but later affirmed his circle’s sovereignty would prevent corruption.  
- Chose to stay in Neverlight Grove after helping the party understand the threat, and bid the group farewell with quiet sadness.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes myconids should communicate with precise, restrained speech — opposes excessive verbosity.  
- Rejects the idea that myconids consume their own kind, questioning humanoid cannibalism.  
- Holds deep faith in the sovereignty of his circle, believing it cannot be corrupted.  
- Feels personally responsible for the safety of his home and kin, prompting his urgent return to Neverlight Grove.  

## Uncertainty  
- It is unclear whether Rump-a-dump’s belief that “the circle cannot be corrupted” is factually true or a hopeful assertion — no evidence confirms or denies the sovereignty’s power.  
- The chronology of his decision to flee with the party vs. return is ambiguous: he convinced Stool to flee (ch33), yet then stayed behind — it is unclear if this was a reversal or a coordinated plan.  
- The phrase “they are with their people now” (ch33) implies Rump-a-dump is with other myconids, but it is unknown whether he is now part of a leadership role, a guardian, or simply a returning member.  
- It is unconfirmed whether Voosbur is still alive, active, or contained — Rump-a-dump named him as the spore-source, but no later update on Voosbur’s status exists.  
- The “fifty specific ways to improve” are listed, but their impact or Zalthir’s response is not recorded — whether they were accepted, ignored, or acted upon is unknown.
