### Juiblex

- **Current status:** Alive (in a state of imminent rebirth); physical form likely destroyed or dispersed, but consciousness/presence persists.  
- **Current location:** No physical presence; spiritual/demonic essence lingers in the vicinity of Blingdenstone, specifically tied to the Queen of Fungi’s banquet site.  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Demon lord of oozes and shapeless things; operates independently but has a pact with the Pudding King.  
- **Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None directly held; power is channeled through the Pudding King (now severed) and is being redirected toward the Queen of Fungi’s banquet.  
- **Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
  - Formerly channeled power through the Pudding King to seize Blingdenstone.  
  - Now seeks rebirth by consuming the “banquet of the queen of the fungi” to grow stronger.  
  - Previously promised the kingdom of Blingdenstone to the Pudding King.  
  - Known as the Faceless Lord, a demon who infuses power to create chaos.  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Successfully corrupted the Pudding King into a sentient, telepathic ooze as a vessel.  
  - After the Pudding King’s defeat, his power receded from the area.  
  - Declared imminent rebirth by consuming the Queen of Fungi’s banquet.  

## Uncertainty
- The voice declaring rebirth is stated as “possibly Juiblex himself” — not definitively confirmed as his direct manifestation.  
- It is unclear whether Juiblex’s power receding means he is weakened, banished, or merely shifting focus to the Queen of Fungi.  
- The nature of the “banquet of the queen of the fungi” is not defined — whether it is literal, symbolic, or a magical event — leaving the mechanism of rebirth ambiguous.  
- No confirmation whether Juiblex ever physically manifested in the material plane or remains purely a metaphysical influence.
