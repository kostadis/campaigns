### Dawnbringer

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Traveling with Thorin and the party, recently emerged from the Underdark into sunlight  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent sentient artifact; bonded to Thorin as a companion and friend  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Housed in a proper scabbard; capable of casting *lesser restoration*; radiates warmth like a hearth in sunlight  
**Notable spells/feats:** Can cast *lesser restoration*; reads and writes; possesses self-awareness, emotional memory, and agency  
**Current assignment / role:** Companion and weapon to Thorin; undergoing interpersonal conflict resolution counseling with him; seeking to reconcile with her past and remember daylight without combat  
**Key relationships:**  
- Bonded to Thorin, whom she calls “annoying” but acknowledges mutual understanding with  
- Directed to Philemon (monk and artifact psychologist) for couples counseling  
- Refuses to be treated as a religious symbol or purification tool  
- Avoids discussion of her previous wielder; went silent when asked, indicating unresolved trauma  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Refused to be used in purification rituals, asserting her identity as a weapon that kills  
- Waited longer than anyone in the party to emerge into sunlight after leaving the Underdark, radiating quiet contentment  
- Teased Thorin about his anger issues  
- Acknowledged mutual annoyance with Thorin and warmed slightly in response to his acknowledgment of their bond  
- Claimed to have been discovered in her tomb after two thousand years of dormancy (contradicting earlier claim of 3–4k years of darkness)  
- Requires time and space to grieve her lost past and remember daylight without the need to fight  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Endured 3,000–4,000 years of sustained darkness and disuse, causing deep unease and trauma  
- Needs to process the loss of her previous existence and identity, likely tied to her original Netherese-era sun-worship origins  
- Her silence when asked about her previous wielder suggests the corpse she was found in was not merely a container, but someone significant — possibly her last wielder — whose death she has not processed  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch55] stating Dawnbringer endured 3–4,000 years of darkness and [ch56] claiming she was found after 2,000 years — which is the correct timeline?  
- Pronoun ambiguity: While “she” is used consistently in later chapters, [ch55] notes uncertainty over whether to use “he,” “she,” or “them” — is her gender identity settled or still fluid?  
- The nature of her previous wielder: [ch55] implies she was found in a corpse, and she went silent when asked — was this wielder her creator, her last master, or someone else? No facts clarify.  
- The scabbard’s origin: Is it the same one she was found in, or was it newly acquired? [ch45] says she was “pleased to have a proper scabbard,” but [ch56] implies it’s always been hers — no resolution.  
- Did the 3–4k years of darkness include her time in the tomb, or was the tomb only part of it? [ch56]’s claim of “two thousand years” may refer only to the tomb phase, but this is not stated explicitly.
