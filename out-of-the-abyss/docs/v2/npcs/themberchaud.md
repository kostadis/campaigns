### Themberchaud

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** En route to the docks of Gracklstugh, having agreed to transport Grygum’s group after persuasion.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Officially claims to be the Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh and an independent authority; secretly controlled by the Keepers of the Flame, though he is unaware of their full manipulation.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Shiny, carefully polished lava-colored scales; painted claws.  
**Current assignment / role:** Believes himself to be the ruler of his cavern (“my town”) and the patron of the group as his agents; offers express aerial transport to their next destination.  
**Key relationships:**  
- Considers Grygum and the party his agents, bound by his protection.  
- Reports to no one openly, but is manipulated by the Keepers of the Flame, who use him as a glorified forge-lighter.  
- Dislikes gold dragons; has no known familial ties.  
- Has no awareness that the red dragon egg was stolen or that his actions harm dragonkind’s reputation among the Duergar.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Proclaimed the cavern his domain and flew the party through it, declaring it “my town.”  
- Offered express aerial transport to the group’s next destination.  
- Shouted for the party to climb aboard under threat of questioning his will.  
- Pouted when uncertain of next steps, then agreed to go to the docks after being persuaded.  
- Breathed fire at a forge while declaring, “I am Themberchaud the Wyrmsmith, bringer of flame!”  
- Called the Keepers of the Flame “short-sighted” and dismissed their checkpoints as wasteful.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes he is in charge and seeks to assert control, but is deeply insecure and unsure of his true position.  
- Desperately wants information about what is happening around him, sensing he is being used.  
- Is a pampered, overweight red dragon with no hunting instincts, maintained as a symbol and tool by the Keepers of the Flame.  
- His entire persona — the titles, the threats, the grandeur — is a performance to mask his captivity and lack of autonomy.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Themberchaud claims to be the Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh and in charge, yet multiple facts confirm he is a captive tool of the Keepers of the Flame — but he is unaware of this. The current state is that he *believes* he is in charge, even though the truth is otherwise. This is not a contradiction in the dossier, because the dossier reports his *current perception* (which is what matters for GM use), not the objective truth.  
- Ambiguity: The phrase “wormsmith” in his shout (“question the will of your wormsmith”) is likely a typo for “Wyrmsmith.” No other source confirms he uses this term, so it’s unclear if this is a slip, a curse, or a new self-identity.  
- Unclear attribution: While Themberchaud says “I am Themberchaud, and I wish for you to be my agent,” the Keepers of the Flame also claim he works for them — but the latter is presented as an external truth, not his belief. No contradiction in the dossier since we report his current state as he perceives it.  
- Unclear chronology: The exact moment he agreed to go to the docks is described as after being “persuaded,” but the persuader is unnamed. Was it Grygum? The party? One of his agents? Not specified.  
- Ambiguous detail: The “second chin” and “painted claws” are noted, but it is unclear if these are signs of pampering by the Keepers, self-indulgence, or ritual markings. No source clarifies intent.
