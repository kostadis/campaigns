### Kalan Strongbranch

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Candlekeep (site of death)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Candlekeep (former Gate Warden)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Former Gate Warden; resigned the morning before death  
**Key relationships:**  
- Had a troubled relationship with Keeper of Tomes Janussi  
- Previously sought to imprison Daz upon the party’s arrival  
- Was ordered by Janussi to tender his resignation the morning before his death  
- Was addressed directly by Bookwyrm, who cut him off and ordered him to secure defenses and enforce a lockdown  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Previously asserted Candlekeep’s policy of holding but not keeping prisoners, refusing to deliver a spy to House T'sarran or execute her.  
- Arrived at the Chapter House after a decision was reached, where Bookwyrm issued him a direct, pre-prepared order to focus on securing defenses and forbidding entry or exit to any creature.  
- Bowing in compliance, but with eyes that were not respectful.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- His resignation was demanded by Janussi, suggesting internal conflict or power struggle within Candlekeep’s leadership.  
- His authoritative demeanor and lack of respect during his bow imply he did not accept his removal gracefully.

- It is unclear whether his resignation was formally submitted or accepted before his death.
