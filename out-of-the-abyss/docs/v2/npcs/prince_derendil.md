### Prince Derendil

- **Current status:** Dead  
- **Current location:** His corpse stands as a silent monument in the Underdark (location unspecified, but implied to be near the site of his death)  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Formerly a polymorphed elf, possibly of noble elf origin; no current allegiance  
- **Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (corpse; no items mentioned in possession at time of death)  
- **Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Formerly a chamber slave under Mistress Ilvara; last seen charging and attempting to kill Drow Imbros; no current companions or superiors  
- **Defining recent actions:** Declared himself armed in his Quaggoth form and charged Imbros with intent to kill; was stabbed to death by Imbros and succumbed to Drow Poison; his corpse remains as a monument to the cost of victory  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believed there was no abolitionist movement among Lolth worshippers; suspected Jorlan’s motives were self-serving; remembered Ilvara’s rituals and her phrase: *“Accept your fate, learn to obey, and you may survive.”*; was a polymorphed elf, now remembered as “the mad quagoth”
