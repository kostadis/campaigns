### Galeb Duhr

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** The hallowed temple in the Realm of the Rock  
**Allegiance/faction:** Free guardians of the Realm of the Rock (formerly under Ogrémoch’s control)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- One of three Galeb Duhr now freed from Ogrémoch’s influence, actively acknowledging the party’s success.  
- Functions as a guardian of the temple and custodian of the Realm of the Rock’s true name.  
- Interacts with the party: responded to Thorin’s humor, questioned Thorin’s clothing, and corrected Grygum’s misstatement of the realm’s name.  
- Previously stated it could not act until all its brothers were freed — now, with three emerging together, this condition has been met.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Emerged alongside two other Galeb Duhr after the temple’s hallowing.  
- Corrected Grygum’s reference to the “Realm of the Dragon,” asserting it is the “Realm of the Rock.”  
- Expressed amusement at Thorin’s joke about the temple being both “hollow” and “hallowed.”  
- Questioned Thorin’s choice to wear giant-sized clothing.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Was bound by Ogrémoch’s corruption, unable to act until all its brethren were freed.  
- The temple’s hallowing and the destruction of the Earth Elementals broke Ogrémoch’s hold, restoring their autonomy.
