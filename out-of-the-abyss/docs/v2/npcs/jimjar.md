### Jimjar

**Current Status:** Missing (vanished entirely from existence)  
**Current Location:** Unknown (last seen walking back toward Blingdenstone; vanished mid-conversation)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Likely a celestial being (possibly an angel or divine entity), formerly associated with Blingdenstone; bore a profile resembling a divine figure and exhibited immunities and resistances consistent with a celestial entity.  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Bone die, claimed to be carved from the knucklebone of a saint who never told a lie (won from a blind Duergar in Gracklstugh)  
- Multiple empty spell gems (received from Daz and used in wagers)  
- Knowledge of Underdark flora: Barrelstalk, Ormu, Trillimac mushrooms, and their uses (water, food, pigment, scroll material)  
- Evidence of prior possession: a tinderbox (used for toasting Ripplebark)  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- Former guide and de facto strategist for the escape party; led them through the Underdark toward Blingdenstone.  
- Last known interaction: returned ten platinum pieces to Grygum and warned of a rapidly growing pale white puffball mushroom emitting a sweet smell of rot.  
- Last seen walking back toward Blingdenstone after vanishing from physical existence.  
- Formerly associated with: Grygum (bets and guidance), Daz (advised on heritage), Zalthir (observed and scrutinized), Glabbagool (recognized as intelligent), and the gnomes of Blingdenstone (who showed deference to him).  
- Believed to have destroyed Vazuk, a deep gnome ghost, and to have held a significant, unstated position in Blingdenstone.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Vanished entirely from existence after offering a knowing wink to Zalthir following the revelation of his celestial-like resistances (immunity to poison, psychic damage, charm, fear; resistance to radiant and non-magical physical attacks).  
- Delivered the final ominous warning before departure: identified a rapidly growing, sweet-smelling fungal threat emerging from a wall crack.  
- Previously guided the party through multiple deadly Underdark passages, survived multiple near-fatal encounters (including being trapped under rockfall and surviving a dragon fall), and consistently used bets as a psychological tool — even in moments of grave danger or grief.  
- Killed Sarith after Sarith became a Drow spore servant.  
- Apologized for leading the party into explosive gas caverns and other deadly traps, but also revealed hidden paths and solutions (e.g., discovering a passageway after a collapse).  

**Revealed Motivations or Secrets:**  
- Used betting as a distraction mechanism during guilt, anxiety, or emotional discomfort.  
- Exhibited deep, personal knowledge of Juiblex and Zuggtmoy, suggesting prior traumatic exposure — his demeanor changed noticeably when Juiblex was mentioned.  
- Possibly a celestial being masquerading as a gnome, with motives tied to guiding the party toward Blingdenstone and confronting fungal threats (possibly connected to Zuggtmoy’s influence).  
- His vanishing suggests he was never truly a prisoner or mere companion — his presence was purposeful, and his departure was intentional and divine in nature.  

## Uncertainty

- Unclear chronology of disappearance: Was Jimjar’s vanishing at ch48 his final known state, or did he reappear later? Ch53 and ch54 reference his disappearance and possible return, but no later chapter confirms his physical reappearance — only speculation.  
- Ambiguous nature of “celestial being”: While his resistances and profile strongly suggest divinity, no explicit confirmation (e.g., name, title, domain) is given — only inference by the party.  
- Unclear status of his debt to Yukyuk and Spiderbait: He promised to pay 20 gold when able, but whether he ever settled it before vanishing is unknown.  
- Unclear whether the bone die is truly magical or merely believed to be — its origin story may be a lie (he suspected the Duergar was covering losses).  
- Unclear if his knowledge of Blingdenstone’s inner workings was due to prior position or divine insight.  
- Unclear whether his disappearance was voluntary or forced — no evidence of conflict or attack preceded it.
