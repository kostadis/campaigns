### Brother Vareth

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Candlekeep, West Inner Ward (Immortal Chambers)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Stroudite scholar, institutional fixture of Candlekeep  
**Current possessions / notable items:** A small, full side office in the Immortal Chambers (held for 41 years); a stack of Stroudite papers (recently handed to the narrator)  
**Current assignment / role:** Senior scholar and custodian of Stroudite theological archives; recently proposed and named the "strong-weak hypothesis" as a new theological framework  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Coined and named the "strong-weak hypothesis," defining theological positions as "strong" or "weak" Stroudites and Bahamutians based on their stance toward fixed points.  
- Dismissed the Bahamutians and waved off dragons during theological discourse.  
- Handed the narrator a stack of Stroudite papers, then immediately rushed off to begin his own research.  
- Delivered an unscripted, well-prepared speech on theological architecture, asserting that a god seeking control would shape evidence, polemic, and institutional design — not direct intervention.  
- Exhibited brief but genuine panic at the suggestion of a fourth theological school.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Deeply invested in preserving the integrity of Stroudite orthodoxy against perceived dilution.  
- Views theological innovation not as rebellion but as filing — a precise, institutional act.  
- Possesses intimate, decades-long knowledge of Candlekeep’s institutional gravity and unspoken power structures.  

## Uncertainty  
- The exact current physical location within the Immortal Chambers after he rushed off is unspecified.  
- Whether he still holds the Stroudite papers he handed over (or if they remain with the narrator) is unclear — the act is documented, but not the subsequent custody.  
- The nature of his “own research” is not described beyond the fact that he began it.  
- No confirmation of whether he still considers himself aligned with the Bahamutians or has fully severed ties beyond his dismissals.  
- His current relationship with Pizwog after the “tortle handoff” is not elaborated beyond his expression of restraint.
