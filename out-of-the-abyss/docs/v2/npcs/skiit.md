### Skiit

- **Current status:** Dead (pushed over the cliff by Grygum)  
- **Current location:** At the bottom of the cliff where Grygum pushed him  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Follower of Demogorgon; formerly associated with Pliinki and the Derro of Ulnara’s project  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** Amulet of Demogorgon; tattered, greasy leather and cloth stained with grime  
- **Current assignment / role:** Was tossing body parts into a pit as part of “the great project of Ulnara of the Council of Savants”; believed to be under Pliinki’s command  
- **Defining recent actions:** Accused Grygum’s party of serving the Duergar or Mindflayers; declared allegiance to Demogorgon; requested permission to kill Spiderbait; was actively disposing of body parts into a pit; pleaded with Grygum that Pliinki promised him a better job  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:** Believes following Demogorgon will restore Derro pride and power; deeply loyal to Pliinki’s promises; associates Ulnara’s project with Derro resurgence  

## Uncertainty
- Contradictory attribution: Two facts state Skiit accuses Grygum’s group of working for the Duergar or Mindflayers (one attributed to “Skiit accuses,” one to “I don’t believe you guys...” — the latter may be direct quote from Skiit, but it’s unclear if this is the same utterance or two instances).  
- Unclear chronology of death: The fact that Skiit was tossing body parts and speaking immediately before being pushed over the cliff is consistent, but no fact confirms whether he died instantly, was injured and died later, or if his body was recovered — only that he was pushed.  
- Ambiguous status of Pliinki: No fact confirms whether Pliinki is alive, present, or still in command — only that Skiit believed Pliinki promised him a better job.  
- Unclear if Skiit’s request to kill Spiderbait was granted or acted upon — no follow-up fact confirms the outcome.
