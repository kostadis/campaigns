### Narrator

**Current location:** Unspecified, but actively engaged with the party in the aftermath of events in Voosbur and the chamber of the chanting scholar (ch56).  
**Allegiance/faction:** Agent of Bahamut (under direct command), but operates with independent strategic autonomy. Not a cleric, but deeply familiar with Bahamut’s clerical traditions.
