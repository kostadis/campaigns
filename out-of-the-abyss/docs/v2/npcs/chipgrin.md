### Chipgrin

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Hall of Melting Mirrors, Blingdenstone  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of a wererat contingent allied with Blingdenstone, under Chief Dorbo Diggermattock  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Leads a group of wererats assigned by Chief Dorbo Diggermattock.  
- Maintains a chanting vigil in the Hall of Melting Mirrors to suppress lingering psychic static from the mirrors.  
- Recently sought assistance from the party’s spellcasters to locate the harmonic center of the Hall to permanently silence the psychic noise.  
- Impressed by Thorin’s success in identifying the harmonic center; acknowledged he should have asked Thorin first.  
- Maintains a defensive sensitivity about wererat stealth, taking offense at suggestions they are noisy.  
- Expressed concern that his reference to Thorin as a “surface dweller” might offend Zalthir, and hastily clarified his intent.

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Led wererats in a sustained chanting vigil to suppress psychic residue.  
- Attempted to use his pack’s howling to counteract the psychic static before seeking arcane aid.  
- Recognized Thorin’s expertise in locating the harmonic center and verbally acknowledged his oversight in not consulting the dwarf earlier.  
- Demonstrated social awareness by immediately mitigating potential offense to Zalthir after using the term “surface dweller.”

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Values the effectiveness and reputation of his wererat pack, particularly their stealth.  
- Seeks to resolve the psychic disturbance in the Hall not through force, but through harmonic suppression — indicating an understanding of magical resonance.  
- Shows humility and adaptability in acknowledging his mistake in overlooking Thorin’s knowledge, despite initial assumptions.

## Uncertainty
- It is unclear whether Chipgrin still holds any leadership role beyond the wererat contingent or if he reports directly to Chief Dorbo or Senni Diggermattock — only that he was “offered as allies by Chief Dorbo.”  
- The exact nature of the “chanting vigil” (e.g., whether it is a magical ritual, a cultural practice, or a spell-like effect) is not specified.  
- It is ambiguous whether the wererats under Chipgrin are exclusively his own pack or include others from Blingdenstone.  
- The phrase “he should have asked the dwarf first” is attributed to Chipgrin in multiple near-identical forms — but it is unclear if he said it to Thorin, Zalthir, or internally; the context implies he said it aloud to Thorin, but the quote sources are fragmented.  
- No confirmation whether Chipgrin is still actively leading the vigil or has delegated it since Thorin located the harmonic center.
