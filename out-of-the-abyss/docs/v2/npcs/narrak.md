### Narrak

**Current Status:** Dead  
**Current Location:** Ritual cavern beneath Whorlstone, slain during the battle with the Ember Vanguard  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Cult leader of the Derro cult devoted to Demogorgon; former agent of the Prince (as per correspondence)  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- White feather (used to cast *fear*)  
- Short spear (used in final attack while restrained)  
- Copy of *The Rituals of the Two-Headed Beast* (found on person)  
**Notable Spells / Feats:**  
- Cast *fear* using a white feather (targeting Thorin, Grygum, Zalthir)  
- Demonstrated command over ritual incantations and chaotic divine channeling tied to Demogorgon  
**Current Assignment / Role:**  
- Former orchestrator of rituals to convert giants into two-headed monsters and repair the Obelisk using Thrazgad ore  
- Led Derro cultists in chanting rituals to invoke Demogorgon’s madness and enact the “Triumph of Demogorgon”  
- Previously corresponded with Pliinki regarding the Obelisk and Prince’s plan  
**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Directed Derro cultists in ritual rehearsals and performances, demanding “passion and vigor”  
- Attempted to restart the ritual after disruption, shouting incantations and correcting chants  
- Cast *fear* on members of the Ember Vanguard during the battle  
- Attacked Thorin with a short spear while restrained by Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp  
- Screamed that “the curse spreads” and “Demogorgon’s madness flows through me!” as the ritual failed  
- Declared “No, the ritual cannot be undone!” as his final words before being slain by Thorin  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Believed the Obelisk’s repair would enable ascension and the destruction of the Duergar  
- Saw the genocidal war against the Derro as the “first cleansing” — a twisted inversion of reality  
- Viewed the Deepking as senile and weakened, and the Empty-Scabbard Killers as tools of the Deepking’s folly  
- Considered Errde Blackskull delusional for pursuing “conspiracy theories”  
- Saw himself as the vessel of Demogorgon’s will, claiming “madness flows through me”  
- Believed the ritual’s success was inevitable, even as his conviction waned in death  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Earlier chapters refer to Narrak as “the Prince Awaits, Narrak,” implying allegiance to a “Prince” — later chapters reveal devotion to Demogorgon. No clarification is given on whether the “Prince” is Demogorgon, a separate entity, or a title Narrak adopted.  
- Ambiguity: Narrak’s final screech — “Your sacrifice is but a step towards the glory” — is said twice, once as conviction wanes and once during battle. The exact timing and context of the second instance are unclear.  
- Unclear Attribution: The letter to Pliinki is signed “The Prince Awaits, Narrak,” but no other entity named “the Prince” is identified in later chapters. Is this Demogorgon? A mortal figure? Unknown.  
- Unclear: Whether the Obelisk was ever repaired — no later chapter confirms its status after Thrazgad ore was applied.  
- Unclear: The nature of “the curse” Narrak claims spreads — no definition or mechanism is given.
