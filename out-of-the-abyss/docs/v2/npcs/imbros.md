### Imbros

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Unknown (last seen in ch02)  
**Allegiance/faction:** House Ilvara (Drow elite fighter)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Drow Poison (used in ch02)  
**Current assignment / role:** Enforcer and field commander assigned to suppress slave revolt; previously assigned cooking duties to subordinates (ch01)  
**Defining recent actions:** Fought against a slave revolt in ch02, striking Thorin unconscious and Prince Derendil twice with Drow Poison; dodged Zalthir’s shove; resisted necrotic influence from Grygum’s bell; unaffected by Mind Sliver; survived multiple crossbow bolts and magic missiles before being slain by Sarith’s sword. Realized the revolt was beyond his control and feared Ilvara’s punishment for failure.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Deeply aware of the consequences of failure among Drow; chose to handle the revolt alone rather than risk calling for help. Understood his fate might mirror Sarith’s if he failed. Had read Vof Klownits’ opus on plans and encounters (ch27), suggesting scholarly background beyond combat training.
