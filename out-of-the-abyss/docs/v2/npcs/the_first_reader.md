### The First Reader

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Candlekeep (specifically near the emerald door)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Candlekeep (high-ranking scholar-official; operates independently of institutional norms)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** A whole shelf of drow prison journals, including the Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal (held with particular reverence); recently received five unspecified books (received with barely-contained delight)  
**Current assignment / role:** Acting as an independent investigator or custodian of forbidden knowledge; bypasses standard institutional channels to directly summon the party for urgent, covert consultation  
**Key relationships:** Last summoned Daz, Grygum, and the party directly; operates in opposition to or in secrecy from the Avowed; succeeded the late Keeper of Tomes in active authority over the emerald door and its associated protocols  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Received five long-awaited books with intense, private joy.  
- Lingered reverently over the Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal, declaring it a “masterpiece of interesting value.”  
- Bypassed institutional protocol to personally summon the party through the emerald door immediately after the Keeper of Tomes’ death.  
- Urged immediate summoning “before the Avowed awake,” indicating a need to contain the scene and act covertly.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of the five received books is unspecified.  
- The current whereabouts of the Keeper of Tomes’ body or succession formalities are not stated — only that the First Reader acted after their death.  
- Whether the First Reader is officially appointed or has assumed authority de facto after the Keeper’s death is unclear.  
- The identity of the messenger sent through the emerald door is named as “me” (likely a NPC from ch56), but not specified beyond that.  
- It is unclear whether the First Reader’s reverence for the Velkynvelve journal is purely scholarly or tied to a deeper personal or magical connection.
