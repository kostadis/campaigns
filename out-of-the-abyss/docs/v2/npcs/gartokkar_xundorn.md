### Gartokkar Xundorn

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Gracklstugh (presumed at Keepers of the Flame headquarters)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of the Keepers of the Flame, directly advising the Deepking  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated beyond authority and influence; holds power over badges of passage  
**Spells / feats:** Not specified  
**Wounds:** Four non-self-inflicted notches on his ears (unchanged from ch15)  
**Current assignment / role:** Custodian of the Flame; chief of the Keepers of the Flame; tasked with tending to Themberchaud’s needs  
**Key relationships:** Reports to the Deepking; oversees Duergar under his command; actively manipulates Zalthir’s party for his own ends; views the Derro as enemies to be exterminated  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Demands the statue of Demogorgon as proof of demonic worship to justify a genocidal campaign against the Derro.  
- Offers 2000 gold for the retrieval of the statue, with a 1000 gold confidentiality rider.  
- Threatens lethal retribution against any betrayal, regardless of hiding place.  
- Seeks the dragon egg as insurance against Themberchaud’s potential betrayal.  
- Smiles slyly upon recognizing the party’s mercantile nature, indicating strategic opportunism.  
- Confirmed literal-mindedness and authority by correcting sarcasm about Themberchaud being a “lighter.”  

**Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
- His push for war against the Derro would expose that the Keepers of the Flame have lost control over Themberchaud — a crucial pillar of his power.  
- The egg is not merely ceremonial; it is an insurance policy against the dragon’s defiance.  
- He uses the appearance of loyalty to the Deepking to mask his own vulnerability and ambition.  

## Uncertainty
- Exact current location within Gracklstugh is not confirmed beyond “presumed at headquarters.”  
- Whether he still physically possesses the badges he offered to give (or if they were issued) is unconfirmed.  
- The status of the dragon egg — whether he currently holds it or only seeks it — is not explicitly stated after the demand.  
- The fate of the four ear notches: while noted as non-self-inflicted, their origin (enemy, punishment, ritual) is unknown and not clarified in later chapters.  
- No confirmation of whether he has resumed direct contact with Zalthir since ch18, or if the offer remains active.  
- Contradiction in attribution: ch15 says he “disappears” after making his offer; ch18 shows him actively negotiating again — this implies he reappeared, but the mechanism or timing is unexplained.
