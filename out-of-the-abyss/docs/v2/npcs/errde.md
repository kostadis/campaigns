### Errde

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Gracklstugh (specifically, likely within or near Stone Guards command)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Captain of the Stone Guards, Duergar; affiliated with Clan Ironhead (though restricted by Clan Steelshadow’s monopolies)  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:** Weapons distributed to Jimjar, Spiderbait, Sarith, Eldeth, Stool, and Flumph (no personal items retained or noted; her arsenal is now in the hands of her proxy team)  
**Current Assignment / Role:** Leading a covert investigation into corruption within the Deepking’s court; tasked with proving an alliance between the Council of Savants and the Grey Ghosts; seeking evidence of the Empty Scabbard Killers’ involvement; directing non-Duergar operatives (Daz’s crew) to gather proof in Derro slums; intends to use this proof to eliminate perceived enemies and gain favor with the Deepking  
**Key Relationships:**  
- Reports to or seeks favor from the Deepking  
- Directs Daz’s crew (Jimjar, Spiderbait, Sarith, Eldeth, Stool, Flumph) as operational agents  
- Views Droki as central to the corruption  
- Believes Clan Steelshadow is the Deepking’s favored weapons supplier and a corrupting force  
- Has a strained, distrustful relationship with Daz over insufficient evidence  
- Her own clan (Ironhead) is economically marginalized and upset by weapon trade restrictions  
**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Armed Daz’s crew with weapons from her reserves after refusing to deploy Duergar troops into Derro slums  
- Demanded and pressured Daz for proof of the Grey Ghosts’ alliance with the Council of Savants  
- Insists the Flumph showed her the alliance, though her claim is unstable (“Errde stutters, ‘But the flumph showed me.’”)  
- Wants to kill as many Derro as possible upon proving the conspiracy  
- Is actively hunting phantoms through conspiracy theories that are destabilizing the city  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Believes corruption begins with breaking market rules and escalates to chaos and destruction  
- Sees the Deepking’s shift to Clan Steelshadow as evidence of systemic corruption  
- Thinks her clan’s suffering and deaths are the result of deliberate decisions by unseen powers  
- Her obsession with proving the conspiracy may be irrational — described as “not right,” “consumed by conspiracy theories,” and “hunting phantoms”  
- Her pursuit of proof is not purely civic — it is personal, vengeful, and tied to restoring her clan’s status and her own standing as guardian  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Errde is described as believing Droki is linked to the Council of Savants and Clan Lairds (ch15), but later focuses exclusively on the Council of Savants and Grey Ghosts — no mention of Clan Lairds after ch15. Is the Clan Lairds link abandoned or suppressed?  
- Ambiguity: Is Errde still in direct command of the Stone Guards? Ch29 says “Captain Errde” is to be warned — implying her title and role are still active — but ch23 and ch19 portray her as increasingly unstable. Is her authority intact or under scrutiny?  
- Attribution: Who exactly decided “her clan members must die”? The text says she wants to identify them, but no source identifies the decision-maker.  
- Unclear: The Flumph’s role — is it a real entity, hallucination, or metaphor? Errde’s stuttered claim is presented as fragile, but no other entity confirms or denies it.  
- Chronology: Ch23 labels her theories as contributing to the city’s spiral — is this a recent development or longstanding? The text doesn’t clarify when this perception formed.  
- Possession: Are the weapons still physically in the possession of her agents, or has she reclaimed them? The fact says they are “carried by” them — not that she no longer owns them.
