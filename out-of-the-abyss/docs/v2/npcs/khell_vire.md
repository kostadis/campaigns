### Khell-Vire

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last observed at the Garden of Shadow, but has been absent for eighty years; currently interacting with Zalthir in an unconfirmed location)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former shadow monk of the Garden of Shadow; currently unaffiliated, having refused an unexplained order eighty years ago  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Wears a faded shadow monk under-robe beneath Candlekeep gray robes  
**Spells / feats:** Mastery of specialized shadow techniques; possesses deep knowledge of shadow monk traditions and combat philosophy; recently awarded Zalthir a specialized shadow technique with three options  
**Current assignment / role:** Acting as a mentor and evaluator of Zalthir’s combat style and potential; assessing whether Zalthir has transcended the manual’s teachings  
**Key relationships:** Last known associate: Zalthir (evaluating him as a potential inheritor of unorthodox shadow techniques); former peers: shadow monks of the Garden of Shadow (whom Zalthir is said to be “beginning to remember”)  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Evaluated Zalthir’s combat style, noting his lack of focus and training but identifying a unique, redeeming quality in his combination of shadow step and strike.  
- Recognized that Zalthir had independently developed a superior method beyond the shadow monk manual.  
- Awarded Zalthir a specialized shadow technique with three options.  
- Declined to be present during Zalthir’s trial, stating: “This is your work, not mine.”  
- Asked Zalthir: “What were you sent toward? Not why were you sent here?” — indicating a focus on purpose over origin.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Refused an order from the Garden of Shadow eighty years ago — reason never explained.  
- Believes Zalthir’s unorthodox approach reflects a deeper, forgotten lineage or instinct tied to shadow monks who once frustrated him.  
- His interest in the player character is intensely personal: not about the party, but about *them* specifically — tied to the Jimjar thread, though the exact nature remains unexplained.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of the “Jimjar thread” and how it connects to Khell-Vire’s personal interest in the player character is unexplained.  
- The specific order Khell-Vire refused, and why, remains unknown.  
- Khell-Vire’s current physical location is not confirmed — he was last observed interacting with Zalthir, but whether he remains nearby or has departed is unclear.  
- It is ambiguous whether Khell-Vire is still in contact with the Garden of Shadow or any other faction after his eighty-year exile.  
- The three options for the specialized shadow technique were presented but not listed — their nature is unknown.  
- Whether Khell-Vire’s observation that Zalthir is “beginning to remember” the shadow monks who “drove him nuts” implies a psychic, ancestral, or metaphysical connection is speculative.
