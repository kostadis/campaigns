### Ilvara Mizzrym

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Cavern beneath House Mizzrym, where she detonated after being struck by Grygum’s Guiding Bolt  
**Allegiance/faction:** Corrupted vessel and instrument of Zuggtmoy; former high priestess of House Mizzrym  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Cast *Insect Plague* at the cavern entrance  
- Cast a fear effect (DC-14 Wisdom save) that repelled Zalthir  
- Reshaped the battlefield with fungal growth  
- Radiated the spiritual signature of one promised the Underdark by a demon lord  
- Had been granted demonic power through a pact with a fungal demon lord (Zuggtmoy)  
- No longer merely aided by Zuggtmoy — she *was* Zuggtmoy’s work, walking in a drow priestess’s bones  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Served as Zuggtmoy’s primary instrument in the Underdark  
- Was the reason the party became prisoners in House Mizzrym  
- Last seen muttering prophecy about Zuggtmoy, the bride, and impending mayhem  
- Was grappled and dragged 40 feet by Zalthir before her detonation  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Cleared the drop across the broken bridge with Jorlan  
- Cast *Insect Plague* to seal the cavern entrance  
- Used fear magic to repel Zalthir’s approach  
- Continuously reshaped terrain with fungal growth  
- Howled despite injury  
- Detonated in a massive toxic bloom of poisonous spores after being struck by Grygum’s Guiding Bolt  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Abandoned Jorlan for being scarred, per Drow societal norms  
- Traded herself to Zuggtmoy, the fungal demon lord, in exchange for a promise of dominion over the Underdark  
- No longer a priestess serving a deity — she had become the embodiment of Zuggtmoy’s will  

## Uncertainty
- The exact timing of her detonation relative to being dragged by Zalthir is implied but not explicitly ordered — was the drag immediately before detonation, or was there an intervening action?
- Attribution of the fear effect: the text says “she pushed… against the part of me that the training does not fully reach,” but also says “Roll a DC-14 Wisdom saving throw.” Is this the same effect, or two separate fear manifestations? Unclear if the “push” was a spell, a psychic attack, or a narrative description of the spell’s effect.
- Whether she was still actively speaking prophecy at the moment of detonation — “still muttering” and “still here” are present-tense, but detonation is the final event; was she still conscious when she exploded?  
- The nature of the “fungal demon lord” — explicitly named as Zuggtmoy in other facts, but the phrase “a fungal demon lord” is used separately — is this redundant or a distinction? (All evidence points to Zuggtmoy, so likely redundant, but the phrasing introduces ambiguity.)
