### Zuggtmoy

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown, but actively influencing the Underdark through fungal colonization and possession  
**Allegiance/faction:** Demon Lord of Fungi; patron deity of Ilvara Mizzrym  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Wears the face of Ilvara Mizzrym as a vessel  
- Exerts control over fungal spores that induce madness, possession, and mindless transformation  
- Commands spore servants and twisted fungi as extensions of her will  
- Uses psychic intrusion to speak directly into minds (as demonstrated with the Pudding King and the party)  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acting to seize power in the vacuum left by Juiblex’s defeat, aiming to spread her fungal dominion across the Underdark  
- Promised Ilvara Mizzrym the Underdark in exchange for her service; Ilvara is now a corrupted vessel fully embodying Zuggtmoy’s will  
- Previously intended to marry Araumycos to gain untold power over life and decay  
- Considered a rival by Juiblex (the Pudding King), who viewed her as a threat to his domain  
- Revered as “the Bride” by followers and cultists, including those under Ilvara’s influence  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Took direct control of Ilvara Mizzrym, replacing her identity with her own presence (“the demon lord wearing her face”)  
- Accelerated fungal growth across former Pudding King territories, signaling active expansion  
- Spread spores that infected Daz and Glabbagool, causing possession and behavioral corruption  
- Used psychic communication to manipulate and threaten the party, particularly through the Pudding King  
- Orchestrated the fungal colonization of spore servants and the corruption of drow priestesses  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks to replace or supplant other Abyssal powers (Juiblex, possibly Lolth) through fungal assimilation  
- Views marriage to Araumycos as a ritual of cosmic domination, not merely union  
- Believes chaos and transformation are inevitable — Ilvara’s prophecies reflect Zuggtmoy’s own acceptance of this as divine truth  
- Equated with Lolth in theological texts, suggesting a theological convergence or deliberate equivalence in the Underdark’s belief systems  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Is Zuggtmoy’s intended marriage to Araumycos still active? Ch33 states intent, but no later chapter confirms whether it occurred, was thwarted, or was abandoned after Juiblex’s death.  
- Ambiguous attribution: Ch51 states Ilvara “was Zuggtmoy’s work, walking in a drow priestess’s bones,” and later “the demon lord wearing her face” — is Ilvara’s body now entirely Zuggtmoy’s, or is it a possession that retains some residual identity?  
- Unclear chronology: Ch56 references “the Lolth/Zuggtmoy equivalence” in Asha Vandry’s chain mail — is this a newly discovered theological link, or has it always been known? The timing and origin are unspecified.  
- Unclear current status of Araumycos: No chapter after ch33 confirms whether Araumycos still exists, is controlled, or was consumed — its current state is silent.  
- Ambiguous scope of “the Bride”: Is “the Bride” a title Zuggtmoy claims, or a separate entity she embodies? Ch50 and ch51 use it interchangeably, but no source defines its origin.
