### Jorlan

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Cavern floor, near Ilvara Mizzrym, following the collapse of the bridge  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly Ilvara’s polycule (number one); now independent, driven by personal grudge  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** Armed; bearing a facial disfigurement from acid and missing a finger; wounded from Glyph of Warding detonation and Dawnbringer strike (14 damage); still standing despite injuries  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Engaged in a personal, emotional confrontation with Ilvara Mizzrym; previously attempted to manipulate prisoners into escaping to humiliate Shoor and Ilvara; now acts on raw, unresolved grief and hatred toward Ilvara. Last seen charging toward Ilvara across a swarm-infested terrain, shouting her name.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Inscribed a Glyph of Warding onto the bridge as a trap.  
- Triggered the glyph by charging onto it, taking full detonation force.  
- Survived the glyph’s explosion and a critical strike from Thorin’s Dawnbringer (14 damage), remaining standing.  
- Broke from position and charged across insect-swarm terrain toward Ilvara Mizzrym, shouting “ILVARA” — a cry of wounded emotion, not rage.  
- Cleared the drop from the broken bridge and landed on the cavern floor alongside Ilvara, still breathing.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Hates Ilvara for discarding him after he was scarred by acid and lost a finger — a punishment rooted in Drow society’s obsession with beauty.  
- Their past relationship included an affair, now fractured by betrayal and societal cruelty.  
- His hatred functions as an immunity to Ilvara’s spores — described as “its own kind of immunological theory.”  
- His actions are not strategic but emotionally compulsive: he seeks not victory, but confrontation and reckoning.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Jorlan is described as having “attacked us first” at the beginning of the catastrophe (ch51), yet also offered to help the prisoners escape in ch01 — was this a ruse, or did his allegiance shift? The chronology of his intent is ambiguous.  
- Unclear attribution: Was Jorlan’s Glyph of Warding inscribed as a trap for the party, for Ilvara, or as a general defensive measure? His knowledge of the glyph is stated as “doesn’t know about the glyph” (ch50), yet he inscribed it himself (ch49) — this contradiction is unresolved.  
- Ambiguous wound status: While he is described as wounded and staggering (ch50), no specific wound type or location is named beyond the glyph and Dawnbringer hits — whether these injuries are now healed, infected, or incapacitating is unknown.  
- Unclear current companions: He was last seen charging Ilvara — but whether he reached her, engaged her, or what happened after is not stated. His current proximity to her is inferred, not confirmed.  
- Motivation ambiguity: Is his charge toward Ilvara an act of vengeance, reconciliation, or suicide? The text says “something older than tactics took over,” but the exact nature of that impulse is not defined.
