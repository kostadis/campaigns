### Ilvara

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Cavern beneath Velkynvelve (site of her final battle)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly High Priestess of Lolth and commander of Velkynvelve; later corrupted instrument of Zuggtmoy, demon lord of fungi and decay  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- Scourge (still held at time of death)  
- Prayer book with marginalia documenting Zuggtmoy’s corruption timeline  
- Mushroom token (central to her fungal ritual)  
- Trophies from captives, including Thorin’s tooth  

**Notable spells/feats:**  
- Summoned spore servants and reanimated Velkynvelve guards  
- Cast *Insect Plague* at the cavern entrance  
- Called down divine fire on Daz and Nym Duskryn  
- Manipulated fungal growth to reshape battlefield and reform after physical damage  
- Dissolved into and reemerged from fungal matter (fungus-walking)  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- No active role — deceased.  
- Was the reason the party were imprisoned in the first place.  
- Was pursued by Asha Vandry as a target for assassination due to her fungal corruption.  
- Was the source of the spore illness infecting drow and the Heart Fungus that controlled the cavern.  
- Had been promised the Underdark by Zuggtmoy and refused to surrender that claim.  
- Was inhabited and spoken through by Zuggtmoy in her final moments — her final gaze at Zalthir was not her own, but the demon lord’s.  
- Formerly led House Mizzrym; her house remains a threat to Daz even after her death.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- After the destruction of Velkynvelve, pursued the party to avoid becoming a Drider.  
- Corrupted the cavern with the Heart Fungus and used it as a ritual anchor.  
- Absorbed 24 damage from Daz’s Fireball, remained standing, and continued fighting.  
- Melted into fungal matter to evade grapples and reappear elsewhere.  
- Cast *Insect Plague* after the Heart Fungus was destroyed.  
- Called down divine fire on Daz and Nym Duskryn despite being battered from a 40-foot drop.  
- Howled prophecies of “Vengeance. Zuggtmoy. The bride.” as she fought.  
- Detonated into a massive toxic bloom of poisonous spores upon death.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Once a devout high priestess of Lolth, she became seduced by the idea that Lolth wished to be married — a delusion that masked Zuggtmoy’s corruption.  
- Kept prisoner trophies (e.g., Thorin’s tooth) to reinforce dominance and ownership.  
- Documented Zuggtmoy’s creeping influence in her prayer book, suggesting she was aware of her corruption — and perhaps complicit.  
- Believed the “bride” (a fungal entity) was Lolth’s manifestation, and that chaos and dominion over the Underdark were imminent.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: [ch49] refers to “a drow priestess of Ilvara” — likely a typo or misattribution; all other facts consistently identify Ilvara as the drow priestess, not a follower of someone named Ilvara.  
- Ambiguity: [ch53] states “she was already dead and I had killed her” — but does not specify *who* “I” is. While context implies the narrator, the identity of the killer is not explicitly confirmed in the facts.  
- Unclear attribution: [ch49] says “Grabbing a drow priestess of Ilvara's caliber” — this phrasing is grammatically inconsistent with all other references and may be an error.  
- Unclear chronology: Whether Ilvara was already fully inhabited by Zuggtmoy before the fireball or only during her final moments is implied but not definitively stated.  
- Ambiguity: [ch51] says “Ilvara — Zuggtmoy's instrument, the corrupted high priestess of House Mizzrym” — but no prior fact confirms she was of House Mizzrym; this is the only mention of the house name.
