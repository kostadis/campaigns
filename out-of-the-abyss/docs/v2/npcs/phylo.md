### Phylo

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove, specifically within or near the Inner Circle / Circle of the Masters  
**Allegiance/faction:** Myconid Sovereign of Neverlight Grove (self-proclaimed co-sovereign with Basidia)  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Co-sovereign with Basidia; reports to and claims divine mandate from the Great Seeder.  
- Architect of the new social order in Neverlight Grove: mandates exclusive circle melding, limits cross-circle interaction to circle leaders and sovereigns.  
- Successfully convinced select circle leaders to replace the Great Meld with nested, focused circle meldings (group → leader → sovereign), claiming the Great Seeder endorsed this change.  
- Enforces strict control over information: redirects all inquiries about the Grove and the Circle of the Masters to the Great Seeder.  
- Prohibits access to the eastern plateau, citing a “wondrous and glorious surprise” in the Garden of Welcome.  
- Insists all dead creatures be brought to the Circle of the Masters.  
- Silences dissenting myconids (e.g., Rump-a-dump, Stool) who speak of outsiders like Thorin and Zalthir.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Reorganized the social and melding structure of Neverlight Grove to prioritize narrow-focus, hierarchical melding circles.  
- Presented these changes as divinely inspired by the Great Seeder.  
- Actively seeks Basidia’s approval for the reforms and pleads with her not to leave, asserting the Grove’s future prosperity depends on her presence and the Great Seeder’s promise of universal happiness.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes efficiency and productivity are paramount, and that eliminating cross-functional groups will elevate the myconids.  
- Uses the Great Seeder as a rhetorical and authoritative tool to legitimize his reforms, though it is unclear whether he truly believes in her or manipulates her name.  
- Secretly fears Basidia’s departure will undo his restructuring — his plea to her reveals deep personal investment in maintaining control.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between [ch31] stating Phylo is “genuinely happy to see the party” and [ch33] where he pleads with Basidia not to leave — is his happiness genuine or performative? (No clear resolution.)  
- Unclear whether Phylo’s claim that the Great Seeder endorsed the new melding structure is true or fabricated — no independent verification.  
- Ambiguous whether Phylo’s role as “co-sovereign” with Basidia is mutual and recognized by her, or solely self-declared — Basidia’s stance is not confirmed in these chapters.  
- Unclear if Phylo is physically present with Basidia in [ch33] or separated — their interaction is described but spatial context is missing.  
- No confirmation whether the “Circle of the Masters” is a physical location, a ritual, or a metaphor — described inconsistently as both a place and a preparatory group.
