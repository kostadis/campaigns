### Pliinki

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Whorlstone Caverns (last known activity site)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Devotee and high authority of Demogorgon’s cult among the Derro; operates as a direct agent of the Deepking  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Journal detailing experiments with the dragon egg and the Obelisk; journal documenting the birth of a demonic monster in the Whorlstone Caverns  
**Current assignment / role:** Overseer of Demogorgon-aligned Derro operations; architect of the "Prince’s Gift" — a two-headed wyrmling intended as Demogorgon’s mount; custodian of the Deepking’s hit list, ensuring its custody by the Empty-Scabbard Killers  
**Key relationships:** Reports to the Deepking; referenced as superior by Skiit and Ulnara; author of the Obelisk’s ritual design and dragon egg manipulation experiments  
**Defining recent actions:** Successfully orchestrated the acquisition and incubation of the Red Dragon Egg as a vessel for a two-headed wyrmling; documented and facilitated the birth of a demonic monster in the Whorlstone Caverns; continues to seek power sources to mend the Obelisk, having discarded Thrazgad ore as ineffective; signs communications as “The Stone Hungers, Pliinki”

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: The journal from the Whorlstone Caverns is described as “the one where a man had written carefully about helping birth a demonic monster” — but all other facts identify Pliinki as female and the journal’s author. Is the journal’s author Pliinki, or was it misattributed?  
- Ambiguity: The phrase “Pliinki wrote a journal about helping birth a demonic monster” conflicts with the quote’s phrasing “a man had written” — is this a textual error, a disguise, or an unreliable narrator?  
- Unclear attribution: It is stated that Pliinki “ensures the Deepking’s hit list remains in the hands of the Empty-Scabbard Killers,” but it is not confirmed whether she currently holds the list, delegates it, or has lost control of it since ch23.  
- Unclear status: Whether Pliinki still resides in her former lab (with discarded Thrazgad ore) or has relocated permanently to the Whorlstone Caverns is not explicitly stated.  
- Ambiguous hierarchy: While Ulnara reports to Pliinki and Skiit was promised advancement by her, it is not confirmed whether Pliinki answers directly to Demogorgon, the Deepking, or both — and whether her authority is absolute or contested among other Derro.
