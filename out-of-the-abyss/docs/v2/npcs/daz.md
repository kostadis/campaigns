### Daz

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Candlekeep, likely within the library complex or adjacent quarters  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Independent; formerly associated with the Ember Vanguard (now defunct or dormant); currently operating as a free agent with no formal allegiance, though under covert protection by an unknown patron (likely from Menzoberranzan) and being observed by House T’sarran as bait to reveal that patron.  

**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Stonespeaker Crystal (confirmed possession)  
- Copy of Milo Goodbarrel’s book (carried and referenced regularly)  
- Collection of badges from Gracklstugh (used for navigating authority structures)  
- Bag of Holding (contains Glabbagool, the sentient ooze familiar)  
- Glabbagool (sentient, gentle, luminously good-natured ooze familiar; formally recognized as Daz’s companion)  
- Dawnbringer (sentient sword; emotionally bonded to Daz)  
- Spellbook containing spells from Princess Ebonmire’s collection (unopened but held)  
- Voucher of the Varmint (received from Chief Chipgrin of Blingdenstone)  

**Notable Spells / Feats / Abilities:**  
- **Sculpt Spells** (core ability; used to carve allies out of area effects like Fireball, Cloud of Daggers, and Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp)  
- **Fey Touched** (feat; grants two additional prepared spells and free castings of *Faerie Fire* or *Misty Step*)  
- **Warcaster** (feat; improves concentration on spells like *Hypnotic Pattern* and *Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp*)  
- **Misty Step** (cast without expending a spell slot, used for battlefield mobility and escape from grapples)  
- **Magic Missile** (primary offensive spell; used with precision and reliability)  
- **Scorching Ray** (used for high-damage single-target attacks)  
- **Fireball** (used with Sculpt Spells for area denial and tactical destruction)  
- **Toll the Dead** (used for necrotic damage against undead and resistant foes)  
- **Shape Water** (used creatively for terrain control, detection, and games like K’tharr-li)  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- Acting as a strategic planner and tactical spellcaster for the party; widely recognized as the “feared mage of the Ember Vanguard,” though the group no longer operates as a unit.  
- Primary liaison in diplomatic and intelligence-gathering scenarios; known for meticulous observation, psychological insight, and manipulation of perception.  
- Maintains a performative identity as a follower of Lolth to Asha Vandree, which has become internally convincing due to prolonged roleplay.  
- Deeply bonded to Glabbagool (familiar) and Dawnbringer (sentient weapon); treats both as companions with agency.  
- Traveling with Grygum, Thorin, Zalthir, and others from the original escape group; though companions are not confirmed present, Daz is last known to be with them in Candlekeep.  
- Under observation by House T’sarran (via drow spy) as bait to identify the unknown patron who paid to protect him.  
- Has no memory of his life prior to imprisonment in Velkynvelve; believes his name may be a shortened form of “Daz’issin,” a name mothers use for sons they hope will survive.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Successfully led the party through multiple Underdark crises: neutralizing the Pudding King, dismantling the Derro cult of Demogorgon, defeating the Medusa and Earth Elementals, and orchestrating the fall of the Deepking’s influence in Gracklstugh.  
- Engineered a complex political maneuver in Blingdenstone to unite factions, expose corruption, and prevent a slave labor system by drawing parallels to Drow society.  
- Used a combination of deception, performance, and spellcraft to convince Asha Vandree he was possessed by Lolth, a ruse that later became internally real to him.  
- Survived a direct confrontation with Ilvara, killing her with a Guiding Bolt while shielding allies with Sculpt Spells and Shield.  
- Voluntarily adopted the identity of a “nobody” from Menzoberranzan to deflect attention, though recent events suggest he may be the sole surviving heir of a destroyed noble house.  
- Upon arriving at Candlekeep, developed a persistent headache and memory loss, which he attributes to proximity to arcane knowledge.  
- Declined to wear armor, preferring mobility and spellcasting efficiency.  
- Proposed investigating the mystery of his own identity and the patron behind his protection, suggesting the party split any proceeds from the discovery.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Secretly believes he is the “blithering idiot” survivor of a destroyed Drow noble house, possibly House Daz’issin.  
- Feels a deep, personal conviction that “free people are the basis of any society,” shaped by his escape from Menzoberranzan’s slave hierarchy.  
- Has internalized the role of Lolth’s follower so thoroughly that he now genuinely believes he has communed with her — a transformation from deception to authentic spiritual experience.  
- Views his spellcasting not as mere destruction, but as precise geometry: an “interlocking argument” about positioning, allies, enemies, and which parts of a room he is willing to burn.  
- Feels purpose in standing in the smoke of his own Fireball, surrounded by others fighting because he refused to leave — a profound shift from survival to sacrifice.  
- Secretly keeps a mental journal inspired by Milo Goodbarrel, documenting his journey not as an escape, but as discovery — of ecosystems, civilizations, and his own identity.  

## Uncertainty  
- The identity of the patron who paid to protect Daz remains unknown; conflicting theories suggest a former noble house, a rogue priestess of Lolth, or even a long-dead relative.  
- Whether Daz’s memory loss is due to magical suppression, trauma, or the influence of the Stonespeaker Crystal is unconfirmed.  
- The origin of the “Daz’issin” name and its connection to Daz’s true lineage is speculative; no corroborating records exist.  
- The current status of companions (Grygum, Thorin, Zalthir, etc.) is unclear — they were last seen in Candlekeep, but their presence or survival is not confirmed in the latest chapter.  
- Whether Daz’s claim of communion with Lolth is a permanent spiritual shift or a lingering performance artifact is ambiguous.  
- The nature of the “pressure in his head” and its connection to Candlekeep’s library or the Stonespeaker Crystal is unexplained.  
- The fate of the Zuggtmoy-aligned icon after Daz’s Fireball is uncertain — it was damaged but not destroyed.  
- Whether Daz’s earlier role as the “Ember Vanguard” still holds any weight or is merely a legend is unclear.  
- The source of the “divine gratitude” Daz claims to feel is unverifiable — is it psychological, magical, or literal?
