### Deepking

- **Current status:** Alive  
- **Current location:** Gracklstugh (implied by rulership)  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Ruler of Gracklstugh; commands Clan Steelshadow and Duergar guards (e.g., Grogluk)  
- **Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
- **Current assignment / role:** Sovereign ruler of Gracklstugh; issues all orders to Clan Steelshadow; oversees Duergar guard forces in the West Cleft District  
- **Defining recent actions:** Maintains control over Clan Steelshadow and Duergar personnel; continues to issue directives from within Gracklstugh  
- **Revealed motivations or secrets:** Perceived as mad by subordinates; believed by Errde to be corrupted by the Council of Savants — though this is presented as her delusion, not confirmed fact  

## Uncertainty
- The claim that the Council of Savants is corrupting the Deepking is attributed solely to Errde and labeled as her conviction/delusion; no independent confirmation exists.  
- It is unclear whether Clan Steelshadow is the only entity receiving orders or if others (e.g., Grogluk’s unit) receive direct commands — the facts state orders are sent “exclusively” to Clan Steelshadow, yet Grogluk (a Duergar guard) also works for the Deepking, creating ambiguity in chain of command.  
- The nature of the Deepking’s “madness” is not defined — whether it is psychological, magical, or induced remains unspecified.
