### Ulnara

- **Current status**: Alive (emerged as an Ogre Zombie, but retains sentience and speech).  
- **Current location**: Emerging from the pit in the necromantic operation site.  
- **Allegiance/faction**: Cultist of Demogorgon; claims affiliation with the Ember Vanguard (though this conflicts with Grygum’s Ember Guard).  
- **Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds**:  
  - Currently inhabiting the body of an Ogre Zombie.  
  - Functions as a necromancer with the ability to raise and control undead (evidenced by intent to turn Skiit and others into zombies).  
- **Current assignment / role and key relationships**:  
  - In charge of the pit operation.  
  - Reports to or seeks approval from Pliinki.  
  - Addresses Daz as “Your lordship” after recognizing his Drow heritage, indicating subservience to him.  
  - Identifies herself as “Ulnara of the Council of Savants.”  
  - Claims to have aided the Ember Vanguard and intends to report this to Pliinki.  
- **Defining recent actions**:  
  - Emerged from the pit as an Ogre Zombie while retaining her voice, intellect, and cultist zeal.  
  - Attempted to curry favor with Daz by praising the Ember Vanguard and offering to turn enemies into undead servants of Demogorgon.  
  - Repeatedly referred to the group as the Ember Vanguard, despite Grygum’s claim that his group is the Ember Guard.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between Ulnara’s claim that the group is the “Ember Vanguard” and Grygum’s declaration that his group is the “Ember Guard.” It is unclear whether Ulnara is mistaken, lying, or if two factions exist.  
- It is ambiguous whether Ulnara’s consciousness fully controls the Ogre Zombie body or if it is a puppet — her retained speech and intent suggest sentience, but the nature of her transformation is not fully explained.  
- The relationship between “Ulnara of the Council of Savants” and Pliinki is unspecified — is Pliinki her superior, patron, or peer?  
- The phrase “he will seve the cult” appears to be a typo for “serve,” but it is unclear whether this reflects a deeper linguistic quirk of Ulnara or a transcription error.  
- It is unknown whether Ulnara’s subservience to Daz is genuine or tactical deception.
