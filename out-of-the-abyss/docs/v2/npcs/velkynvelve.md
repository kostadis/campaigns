### Velkynvelve

Velkynvelve is not an NPC — it is a location. It is referenced as a site where demons were previously seen and as a pivotal event in a sequence of recent traumatic occurrences, following the Drow apostate’s survival and preceding months of flight. It is not a person, nor is it currently active or inhabited in any known capacity; it functions only as a named landmark in memory and narrative.
