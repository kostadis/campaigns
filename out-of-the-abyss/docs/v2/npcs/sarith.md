### Sarith

**Current status:** Dead (transformed into a Drow spore servant)  
**Current location:** Neverlight Grove (as a spore servant, fused with the myconid hive)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Zugtomy (corrupted by demonic spores; no longer loyal to the Cult of Lolth or the escape party)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None (physical body destroyed; now a spore servant entity)  
**Spells / feats / wounds:** None (physical form dissolved; now a hive-bound fungal servant)  
**Current assignment / role:** Spore servant of Zugtomy, serving the Dark Lady’s dream; acts as an extension of the myconid hive in Neverlight Grove  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Was compelled by Zugtomy’s spores to abandon the party and enter Neverlight Grove.  
- Confirmed the party’s path aligned with a mysterious voice, reinforcing his corrupted guidance.  
- Ultimately, his head exploded and transformed into a Drow spore servant, fully assimilated into Zugtomy’s hive.  
- Previously claimed to know the Underdark routes, recommended Gracklstugh as a destination, and guided the party through the Darklake and Silken Paths.  
- Repeatedly invoked themes of revenge and transformation, culminating in his embrace of the Dark Lady’s song as liberation.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Former member of the Drow warrior caste of the Cult of Lolth, trained in torture and Underdark navigation.  
- Believed his imprisonment was unjust and sought revenge against Drow oppressors.  
- Gradually succumbed to madness, initially triggered by Prince Derendil’s death, then amplified by Zugtomy’s spores.  
- Ultimately, his desire for revenge merged with the Dark Lady’s promise of transformation — he saw madness and death as the path to true purpose.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact moment of Sarith’s full corruption is ambiguous: was it the spores in Whorlstone Caverns, the compulsion to the Neverlight Grove, or the final transformation? The facts describe a progression, but no single event is marked as the irreversible point of no return.  
- The phrase “Sarith, our drow guide, looks unhappy and serene but follows the myconid’s.” in ch31 uses “myconid’s” — possibly a typo for “myconids.” This does not alter meaning but introduces minor textual uncertainty.  
- The identity of “the other woman, Aliza Argot” is not expanded upon; whether she is connected to Sarith’s past or Zugtomy is unknown.  
- It is unclear whether Sarith’s final muttering of “revenge is so tasty” in ch32 was a residual habit before transformation or a final, corrupted echo of his former self.  
- The source of the “voice” Sarith confirmed in ch30 is never identified — whether it was Zugtomy, the Dark Lady, or a psychic residue — remains unexplained.
