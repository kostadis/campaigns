### Pelek

**Current Status:** Deceased (ghost laid to rest)  
**Current Location:** Interred in a proper resting place in Blingdenstone (after bones were delivered)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** None (former Svirfneblin merchant; no longer bound to any faction)  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:** None (family obsidian ring remains unclaimed; body no longer exists as a physical entity)  
**Spells / Feats / Wounds:** None (ghostly existence ended)  
**Current Assignment / Role:** None (mission completed)  
**Key Relationships:** Last seen with the party; had been traveling with them after his bones were recovered. Formerly friends with Buppido; mother named him after an actor; father resented the name.  
**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Manifested as a ghost bound to his scattered bones in Buppido’s hideout.  
- Advised the party on Blingdenstone customs: avoid references to the number eight or spiders, deny ties to Lolth or Menzoberranzan.  
- Identified Pygmywort and Bigwig mushrooms and their magical effects.  
- Attempted to psychically enter a thief-derro’s mind, resulting in painful backlash.  
- Possessed the body of a Derro after his bones were disturbed, expressing fear of losing control.  
- Was tied up by the Ember Vanguard due to this possession.  
- After his bones were interred in Blingdenstone, his spirit found peace and dissolved, expressing profound relief and gratitude.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- His name, given by his mother after an actor, caused deep family friction and doubts about his lineage.  
- He was fixated on ending his ghostly existence, fearing eternal torment if his bones were not buried.  
- He was a merchant and business partner of Buppido, whom he failed to recognize as a murderer until it was too late.  
- His obsidian ring had sentimental value but was not magical.  
- He was not a fungi expert, despite his knowledge of Pygmywort and Bigwig.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Ch17 describes Pelek as a ghostly Svirfneblin whose bones are scattered in Buppido’s hideout; Ch23 and Ch25 describe him as possessing a Derro body. The later chapter (Ch23/25) overrides the earlier — but Ch37 confirms his ghost was laid to rest after burial, meaning the possession was temporary. No contradiction in timeline if possession occurred *before* burial.  
- Ambiguity: Ch37 says Pelek “had been traveling with the party” — but Ch23–25 imply he was physically present only as a possessed Derro. Was his ghost accompanying them *after* possession ended? Or was the Derro body the only physical manifestation? The text doesn’t clarify if his ghost remained visible after possession.  
- Attribution: Ch33 says “Pelek, the ghost” confirms Zalthir is not possessed — implying the ghost still existed at that point. But Ch37 says his spirit was laid to rest *after* burial. Since burial occurred in Ch37, Ch33 must have occurred before. No conflict, but chronology of possession vs. ghostly presence is implied, not explicitly stated.  
- Unclear: Was the Derro body *originally* a living Derro, or was it a corpse? The text says “occupied this body,” but does not specify if the Derro was alive or dead when occupied.  
- Missing: What happened to the obsidian ring? The party was told to find the hand to claim it — but no record confirms whether it was recovered or kept.
