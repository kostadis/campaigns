### Chief Chipgrin

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Likely within the Gold Whisker clan’s territory (last seen in ch48, no indication of relocation)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Leader of the Gold Whisker clan; allied with the coalition against the Pudding King  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated beyond the item given away — no new possessions noted since ch48  
**Notable spells, feats, wounds:** None mentioned  
**Current assignment / role:** Leading the Gold Whisker clan in the coordinated effort to combat the Pudding King’s corruption; serves as a liaison and local authority in the coalition  
**Key relationships:**  
- Allied with Daz (impressed by his directness; gave him a token)  
- Resists command by Chief Dorbo (insists Gold Whiskers will not be sent to slaughter under him)  
- Has personal history with the Pudding King (knew him before transformation)  
- Interacted with Zalthir (awkwardly clarified term “surface dweller”)  
- Previously assumed the party was sent by the Diggermattocks (now corrected)  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Participated in coalition planning with other clan leaders (ch39)  
- Delivered critical intel on the Pudding King’s origins and corruption (ch39)  
- Gifted Daz a braided rat’s foot as a token of goodwill (ch48)  
- Clarified a culturally insensitive remark to Zalthir (ch48)  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes the Pudding King’s descent was fueled by vengeance, pride, madness, and demonic influence  
- Fiercely protective of Gold Whisker autonomy — refuses to submit to Dorbo’s command  
- Recognizes the pervasive corruption in the community and seeks to act without sacrificing his clan’s integrity
