### Grygum

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Candlekeep, outside the Gate of Tomes (as of ch56)  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Self-proclaimed founder of the Grygumite School; formerly a cleric of Bahamut (now openly admits his divine magic is theatrical performance).  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Magical short sword (claimed from aftermath of battle, ch38)  
- Light crossbow (lent to Thorin, ch49)  
- Shield and mace, both enchanted with protective magic (ch45)  
- Bag of holding (containing Glabbagool, ch53)  
- Prayer beads (shrunken by *Reduce* spell, at full power, possibly more deadly, ch54)  
- Caltrop (used as symbol of mission, ch29)  
- Vial of Drow poison and poisoned hand crossbow bolts (hooked to armor, ch03)  
- Scrap of paper with notes on all deals and events (ch23, ch49)

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **Role:** Lieutenant of the Ember Vanguard (ch46); self-appointed philosopher and strategist; reluctant spiritual guide.  
- **Current Companions (Last Seen With):** Thorin, Zalthir, Daz, Glabbagool, Spiderbait, the shrunken drow spy (now under Glabbagool’s watch), and Elin (the silent child).  
- **Reports to:** None; operates autonomously.  
- **Key Relationships:**  
  - Glabbagool: Comrade and emotional anchor; carried through the Underdark, now protected in the Bag of Holding.  
  - Stool: Formerly a companion; no recent interaction, but Grygum still references his telepathic bond.  
  - Jimjar: Frequent betting partner; Grygum has won and lost gold, platinum, and dignity to him.  
  - Bahamut: Publicly claimed deity; privately admits faith is performative theater.  
  - Stroud: Object of sentimental attachment; Grygum has a scholar’s performative interest in Stroud’s legacy.  
  - Daz: Observed closely; suspects Daz’s madness is linked to Underdark corruption.  
  - Elin: Silent child; Grygum perceives her condition as a linguistic disconnect, not illness.  
  - The drow spy: Recruited after psychological intervention; now under Glabbagool’s supervision.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Claimed a magical short sword after battle (ch38).  
- Proposed and executed a successful Glyph of Warding trap to eliminate Jorlan (ch49).  
- Used Dust of Suleiman to destroy the Heart Fungus, calling it “beautiful” (ch51).  
- Attacked and killed Ilvara Mizzrym (Zuggtmoy’s vessel), triggering a deadly spore cloud (ch51).  
- Shrank and recruited a drow spy from House T’sarran, offering survival in exchange for intelligence (ch54).  
- Used Revivify to justify killing Daz as a diagnostic tool to uncover his protector (ch54).  
- Declared himself founder of the Grygumite School to gain authority at Candlekeep (ch56).  
- Identified the removal of vital organs as a safeguard against resurrection rituals (ch56).  
- Observed and interpreted the gleam of Drakonoikos scales at sunrise as spiritually significant (ch56).  
- Opened the door to the Keeper of Tomes, bracing for news (ch56).  

**Revealed Motivations or Secrets:**  
- Grygum’s divine magic (healing, guiding bolts, etc.) is self-created theater — he admits Bahamut is “boring” and that his rituals are “theater I created for myself” (ch24).  
- He has no direct divine connection; his faith is a constructed identity, possibly rooted in a past admiration for Stroud.  
- He carries a deep, unresolved spiritual crisis: “What does Bahamut actually want from me?” (ch54).  
- He is obsessed with recording everything — notes on events, people, spells, and philosophical contradictions — as a coping mechanism for chaos.  
- He is emotionally attached to small, vulnerable creatures (Stool, Glabbagool, Spiderbait, the drow spy) and endorses plans that protect them.  
- He is haunted by Zuggtmoy and the madness of the Underdark, and suspects it is spreading beyond the Underdark into the surface world (ch55).  
- He believes the world is speaking a language Elin and Daz cannot process — a corruption that transcends magic and touches perception itself.  
- He is preparing for death, acknowledging he “still has plenty left in [him]” (ch56), but also seems resigned to being caught — “We’ve been caught” (ch55).  

## Uncertainty
- **Contradictory Allegiance:** Grygum claims to be a cleric of Bahamut, yet admits his divine magic is theater and Bahamut is “boring.” Does he still believe in Bahamut as a concept, or is he purely a secular actor playing a role?  
- **Origin of Grygumite School:** Is this a genuine philosophical movement he created, or a desperate fabrication to gain leverage at Candlekeep?  
- **Daz’s Protector:** Grygum proposes killing Daz to reveal his protector — but Daz is alive at the end of ch54. Was the plan aborted? Is Daz’s protector still active?  
- **Grygum’s True Faith:** Is his “sentimentality for Stroud” a genuine spiritual attachment, or merely intellectual nostalgia?  
- **Elin’s Condition:** Is the “language gap” Grygum describes metaphorical, metaphysical, or a literal magical corruption? No diagnostic resolution is given.  
- **Fate of the Drow Spy:** She is under Glabbagool’s watch — but is she truly loyal? Will she escape?  
- **Grygum’s Future:** He is at Candlekeep’s gate, awaiting the Keeper of Tomes — but what news is coming? The dossier ends mid-scene.  
- **The “Grygumite School”:** No prior mention exists. Is this a new identity, or a lie?  
- **Grygum’s Half-Orc Status:** Earlier chapters call him an orc; ch29 calls him a half-orc. Which is accurate?  
- **The “Great Bright”:** Grygum claims the party is from the “Great Bright” — is this a real place, or a fabrication?  
- **The “Lady of Rot” / Zuggtmoy Connection:** Grygum immediately recognized her — but how much does he know about her current state? Is she truly gone?
