### Shuushar

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Traveling with or among Kuo-Toans from Sloobludop, having departed from the party  
**Allegiance/faction:** Member of a reformist Kuo-Toan movement based in Sloobludop, seeking to end the madness of god-creation and build a better Kuo-Toan society  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Self-appointed evangelist and teacher of “Kuo-toan-fulness,” a technique to create space between impulse and action  
- Guides and navigates Kuo-Toan communities, teaching openness to all viewpoints before judgment  
- No longer traveling with the original party; has departed to join Kuo-Toans from Sloobludop who are receptive to his message  
- Previously translated Drow speech (omitting offensive words, though inconsistently), assisted in navigation on the Darklake, and claimed to have discovered a cure for Kuo-Toan insanity  
- Key relationships: Formerly traveled with Gyrgum, Topsy, Turvy, Zalthir, and others; now aligned with unnamed Kuo-Toans from Sloobludop, including those who have seen the danger of madness and wish to reform  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Declared he has found his calling and left the party to join Kuo-Toans in Sloobludop who wish to build a better world free from the madness of god-creation  
- Taught Kuo-toan-fulness to Buppido, who later cited Shuushar as the source of his realization that he was mad  
- Advocated for nonviolent engagement with other Kuo-Toans over attack, urging dialogue and understanding  
- Identified the Deep Father (not the Sea Mother) as the true source of the demand for humanoid sacrifices  
- Mutated his spiritual practice from avoiding offensive speech to actively teaching mental discipline and societal reform  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Seeks to end the cycle of Kuo-Toan madness caused by collective delusions of new deities (e.g., Leemooggoogoon)  
- Believes enlightenment and mental discipline, not ritual or sacrifice, are the path to survival  
- Claims to have “figured out the cure” to Kuo-Toan insanity, though the nature of this cure remains undefined beyond his teachings  
- May have been labeled a “visionary” who stirred trouble among traditional Kuo-Toans  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction: Shuushar claims to avoid repeating offensive words for spiritual balance, yet repeatedly translates Drow insults verbatim — later chapters do not resolve this inconsistency.  
- Ambiguity: The phrase “Shuushar the Awakened” is used interchangeably with “Shuushar,” but it is unclear if “Awakened” is a title, a name, or a state of being — and whether it persists after his departure.  
- Unclear attribution: Chapter 12 states Shuushar is an “escaped Kuo-Toan,” but it is unclear from which location or authority he escaped — earlier chapters imply he was assigned to cook with Gyrgum under Drow oversight, but no formal captivity is confirmed.  
- Unclear chronology: Shuushar says “Sloopdopblop” is eight days away (ch03), but later refers to “Sloobludop” as the center of reform — are these the same place? The names are phonetically similar but not identical; the text does not clarify.  
- Ambiguity: Shuushar’s final words are “I have found my people, and we will build a better world!” — but it is not confirmed whether he is physically present with them, or merely speaking to them from afar.  
- Unclear status: While Buppido credits Shuushar with his realization of madness, it is unknown whether Shuushar is actively teaching Buppido now or if this was a past influence.
