### Bookwyrm

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Candlekeep, specifically in the Keeper’s Chambers and surrounding administrative areas  
**Allegiance/faction:** First Reader of Candlekeep  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- As First Reader, oversees the assignment of handlers to visiting party members (e.g., Daz to Sister Yvenne, Grygum to Vareth, Thorin to Master Archivist Philemon, Zalthir to Brother Khell-Vire).  
- Has delegated the investigation of Keeper Janussi’s murder to the adventuring party, referring to them as “seekers who the gods have sent us.”  
- Has relieved Kalan of his duties following Janussi’s request for his resignation the morning of his death.  
- Directs the party to report back upon completion of their investigation and strictly prohibits removal of any items from the crime scene.  
- Requires confidentiality papers before proceeding with the investigation.  
- Interacts regularly with a gray cat in the tower, which passes without resistance — a phenomenon she avoids analyzing.  
- Reports to or coordinates with the Gate Warden and the Candlekeep council; recently convened the council to address Janussi’s death and urged focus amid chaos.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Confirmed existing handler assignments upon returning from the baths at 9 a.m.  
- Declined to elaborate on Janussi’s reasons for requesting Kalan’s resignation, citing investigation integrity.  
- Publicly denied involvement in Janussi’s death, stating, “I had nothing to do with it.”  
- Called for focus during a council meeting with the repeated command: “FOCUS, PEOPLE! Focus! Focus! Focus! Focus! Focus!”  
- Proposed delegating the murder investigation to the party, framing it as divine intervention.  
- Described the library’s policy: “not in the habit of giving out books.”  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Appears weary and shaken, having been awake since Janussi’s death; is trying to maintain composure.  
- Acknowledges the gray cat’s inexplicable behavior but deliberately avoids theorizing about its nature, noting: “nobody had been able to conclusively prove the cat was some kind of power that lived here, and nobody had been able to conclusively prove that it was not.”  
- Values order, protocol, and discretion — assigns handlers without ceremony, insists on confidentiality, and resists speculation.  
- Holds the party in high regard, invoking fate and divine purpose to justify their involvement — suggesting she sees them as uniquely suited for the task.  

## Uncertainty
- Whether Bookwyrm is still actively overseeing the investigation or has fully delegated it — she proposed delegation, but no later fact confirms she has stepped back.  
- The exact nature of her relationship with the gray cat — while she interacts with it routinely, the reason for its non-resistance remains unexplained and she refuses to consider it.  
- Whether “suitable remuneration, upon successful conclusion, dignity and support” has been formally offered or just described — no confirmation of payment terms being agreed upon.  
- Whether Kalan’s relief was ordered by Bookwyrm alone or under council directive — she announced it, but no source confirms the authority behind it.  
- Whether the “confidentiality papers” have been issued or merely required — no evidence they were distributed.  
- Whether Bookwyrm’s statement “I had nothing to do with it” refers solely to Janussi’s death or also to other unspoken actions — context is limited.
