### Master Archivist Philemon

- **Current status:** Alive  
- **Current location:** Candlekeep (assigned as Thorin’s handler)  
- **Allegiance/faction:** Candlekeep Archivists  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** Small clay tablet (in use, actively writing on it)  
- **Current assignment / role:** Handler of Thorin; primary archivist studying Dawnbringer  
- **Key relationships:** Reports to/assigned to Thorin; interacts with Dawnbringer as a subject of study  
- **Defining recent actions:**  
  - Documented Dawnbringer’s origin as a late Netherese-era sunblade with sun-worship origins, exposed to darkness for 3,000–4,000 years.  
  - Concluded Dawnbringer must remember daylight without needing to fight.  
  - Declared that Dawnbringer cannot be polished.  
  - Asserted that Dawnbringer needs to grieve.  
  - Began recording observations on a clay tablet upon first encountering Dawnbringer.  
- **Revealed motivations / secrets:**  
  - Dedicated to studying entities that outlived him; approaches Dawnbringer with scholarly rigor, not as a weapon or tool.  
  - Recognizes Dawnbringer’s sentience and emotional needs, framing her healing as a process of memory and grief, not restoration or repair.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature of his relationship with Thorin beyond “handler” is unspecified — is Thorin under his care, custody, or supervision?  
- Whether he has any other current duties or responsibilities at Candlekeep beyond handling Thorin and studying Dawnbringer.  
- The chronological order of his writing on the tablet relative to his verbal statements — did he write before or after identifying Dawnbringer’s origin? (Both occurred during the same encounter, but the tablet was “already writing” upon seeing her, suggesting prior preparation.)  
- Whether “Dawnbringer” is the name he assigned or if it was used by others first — he uses it without clarification, but origin of the term is not stated.
