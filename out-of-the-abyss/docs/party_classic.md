## Party Overview

**Current location:** Candlekeep — the North Gallery rotunda, immediately after slaying the pit fiend Moziqodo and saving the Watcher Tadric [109]. The party's next move is to confront A'lai Aivenmore in Candlekeep's High Tower [110].

**Group identity:** Self-styled "the Ember Vanguard" — a name now trailing an exaggerated legend through the Underdark and appearing (misspelled) on Gracklstugh wanted posters. Recently deputized as **Watchers of Candlekeep** [99][100].

**Active quests / open threads:**
- Confront A'lai Aivenmore in the High Tower; he is the buyer behind both attacks and the key theft [103], waiting for a second key his dead courier will never deliver.
- Recover/negotiate the deferred reward for solving Janussi's murder — payment set at a book per suspect [66], deliberately left un-negotiated to leverage a larger boon [104].
- Daz's mysterious Menzoberranzan patron — first flagged by Kaelira Duskryn [68]; tied to the struck record "Daz'issin."
- House T'sarran surveillance of Daz (captured surface spy held in the bag of holding).
- Jimjar's disappearance remains an open investigation thread.
- Dawnbringer's ongoing "sword whisperer"/therapy work [78].
- The loose demon lords (Demogorgon, Zuggtmoy, Juiblex) and Underdark madness bleeding to the surface.

**Collective resources & assets:** Genuine second High Tower key plus a decoy [106]; a stolen potion of flying [71][72]; Janussi's murder evidence (poisoned book [56], recovered heart and cleaver [49], reassembled wrapping paper [58]); Dawnbringer; Glabbagool; Watcher's Stair key [91]; the fake high-tower key [92]. Plus (per campaign records) the Alchemy Jug, Stonespeaker Crystal, Necklace of Fireballs, Princess Ebonmire's spellbook, Entémoch's Boon, and recovered gold and documents.

**Group reputation:** At Candlekeep — favorable and now formally deputized, though two murders occurred on their watch [108]. Underground — "infamous" as the Ember Vanguard [86], wanted in Gracklstugh, hunted by House T'sarran. Drow pursuit from Velkynvelve is definitively ended.

## Characters

### Zalthir
- **Monk 8** (Warrior of Shadow), Dragonborn (Brass), Sailor background. Player: Gabe.
- **Personality & motivations:** Monastery-forged discipline built on silence, discretion, and control-through-proximity rather than violence; models his Underdark survival on the monk Korrh Erann's doctrine of flight over needless combat. No longer seeks escape — he seeks to understand the architecture of power, deception, and silence. Draconic heritage and birth circumstances are a genuine sore point.
- **Notable relationships:** Bonded to **Glabbagool**, whom he treats as a monastic student and who is now permanently attached to his arm [13][14], granting him teleport-in-darkness [15], a flurry of strikes and an acidic shadow-key attack [16], and a corrosive embrace usable on bound targets [17]. Tactical partner to **Daz** (the "Ember Grapple" combo). Mutual, wary awareness with **Jimjar**, whom he inspected and confirmed as a divine/celestial being. Killed **Asha Vandree** decisively when the party hesitated. Handler at Candlekeep: **Khell-Vire**.
- **Items of significance:** Eldritch Claw Tattoo (attuned); Glabbagool (bonded companion); Dustsight/glowing-crystal spectacles (see invisible / through magical darkness); the stolen potion of flying [71]; collected fungi and a mysterious list of names.

### Thorin
- **Fighter 8** (Battle Master), Dwarf, Giant Foundling background. Player: Joe Beda.
- **Personality & motivations:** A dwarf raised by hill giants — speaks Dwarvish with a Giant accent, prizes radical transparency, and despises Underdark intrigue. Hardened by the journey from hopeful adventurer into a pragmatist now even weighing "Zhentarim Tactics" as a fighting style. Core drive: walk up to the problem and hit it with Dawnbringer until it stops. Seeks to reconcile his dwarven roots and become "an integrated person."
- **Notable relationships:** Deep, negotiated bond with the sentient sunblade **Dawnbringer**, whom he has hunted a "sword whisperer" for since the road south [78] and continues to seek therapy for [78]. Sworn oath from **Eldeth Feldrun** to advocate his adoption into her clan. Tunnel-forged rapport with **Grygum** — the two repeatedly arrive at the same investigative questions [10]. Chose to hand the solved murder case to the fired **Kalan Strongbranch** to let him save face [74]. Handler at Candlekeep: **Philemon**.
- **Items of significance:** **Dawnbringer** (sentient radiant sunblade, attuned); Mithral Hall Plate + Shield, +3; Stonegnarl Warband Token; Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard (leverage over Dawnbringer); recovered tooth and the necromantic pearl; Diggermattock Miners patch; Watcher's Stair key [91]; the fake high-tower key [92]; a hoarded Potion of Giant Strength.

### Grygum
- **Cleric 8** (Life Domain), Orc, Sage background; faith of Bahamut. Player: Ben Pfaff.
- **Personality & motivations:** A radical, near-heretical Bahamutian who holds that dragons are defined by choice, not scale color, and that judging by race is the ultimate evil. Obsessive note-taker who treats documentation as a sacred act. Pragmatic, transactional, and unafraid to weaponize a harmless exterior; founder of the "Grygumite School" of theology — his honest answer to the Question of the Age was published by Vareth as a celebrated "fourth corner" [40][41][57].
- **Notable relationships:** Genuine, sincere bond with **Glabbagool**. Tunnel-forged partnership with **Thorin** [10]. Collaborator **Vareth** at Candlekeep (research funding now tied to access to Grygum). Was entrusted the second high-tower brass key by **Kalan Strongbranch** — who "chose the orc" [27] — then handed it to Daz [38][46]. Classified **Bookwyrm (First Reader)** as "not above suspicion" and saw through her feigned grief [75].
- **Items of significance:** Mace, +1 (enchanted); enchanted shield; obsidian/bone prayer beads; scrolls (Detect Magic, Guiding Bolt, Identify, Inflict Wounds); spell gems; the fist-sized pearl from the big heart fungus; forgery seal; the fake high-tower key [92]; prepared Revivify, Mending, and Lesser Restoration [54][55].

### Daz
- **Wizard 8** (Evoker), Elf (Drow Lineage), Sage background. Player: Mike Hall.
- **Personality & motivations:** Drow apostate and self-styled "General" — the party's analytical tactical architect. Rejected Menzoberranzan's slavery and fanaticism ("free people are the basis of any society"); highly analytical, journals daily, and treats information as finite currency. Shifted from seeking escape to seeking discovery — and now, above all, seeks the truth of his own erased origins.
- **Notable relationships:** Trusted by **Grygum** and **Thorin**; tactical partner to **Zalthir**. Maintains a load-bearing false "vessel of Lolth" persona with the late **Asha Vandree**. Contract subject of the Duskryn sisters (**Nym** and **Kaelira**), who confirmed a powerful patron pays "Menzoberranzan rates" to protect him [68]. Hunted by **House T'sarran** as bait to expose that patron. Handler at Candlekeep: **Sister Yvenne** (who withholds more than she says).
- **Items of significance:** Shortsword, +1; Stonespeaker Crystal (attuned, beast-speech [1]); Dustsight Spectacles (attuned); Princess Ebonmire's spellbook; his daily journal; the inert decoy High Tower key he carries [39][106] (which the helmed horrors were tracking [87]); Milo Goodbarrel's memoirs.

## Party Dynamics

The Ember Vanguard is a battle-forged unit whose members were strangers thrown together in Ilvara Mizzrym's slave pen and have since become a genuinely interdependent team. **Daz** supplies strategy and analysis; **Zalthir** is the silent precision instrument who pins and binds; **Thorin** holds the melee line and provides blunt moral grounding; **Grygum** is the healer, lore-anchor, and archivist. Their combat doctrine has explicitly converged into a combined-arms approach (Zalthir grapples and binds, Daz applies sustained area damage, Thorin holds the front).

The central relationships are strong and openly acknowledged: Thorin praises Zalthir's specialization to his face, Grygum and Thorin "think along the same line" after months in a tunnel [10], and both Zalthir and Grygum have deep, sincere bonds with Glabbagool. Grygum entrusting his high-tower key to Daz [46] and Thorin handing the solved case to Kalan Strongbranch [74] show a group comfortable delegating trust.

**Current tensions and fault lines** run mostly along moral method. Zalthir is willing to kill unilaterally when he judges it necessary — his killing of Asha Vandree happened without group consensus, and Thorin explicitly prefers not to kill when it can be avoided [76]. Daz's ever-expanding web of deception (his Lolth persona, his manipulation of witnesses) sits uneasily against Thorin's stated craving for radical transparency. Grygum has floated morally compromising options — blackmailing the guilty suspects [61] — that the group has so far tempered toward mercy [76].

**Shared goals:** confront A'lai Aivenmore and close the Candlekeep murder conspiracy; extract their deferred reward; unravel the mystery of Daz's protector and House T'sarran's interest; and carry warning of the loose demon lords to the surface world. Underlying all of it is a collective, unspoken understanding — voiced by both Thorin and Daz — that after everything the Underdark took from them, they are now a faction unto themselves, loyal to each other rather than to any of the powers courting them.
