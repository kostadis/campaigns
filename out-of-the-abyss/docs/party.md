## Party Dynamics

The four operate as a tightly interdependent unit with complementary roles: **Daz** as strategist, **Thorin** as the physical/moral anchor, **Zalthir** as the silent tactical architect and investigator, and **Grygum** as support caster and documentarian. Combat synergy is well-developed — Zalthir's grappling sets up Daz's area-of-effect spells ("Ember Grapple"), while Grygum keeps the line standing and Thorin holds terrain.

Shared goals right now: **beating Manshoon to the Book of Vile Darkness**, deciding what A'lai is worth alive, and, underneath it all, unraveling **Daz's origins** — the mystery that increasingly drives the whole party. Both Zalthir and Daz are attuned to environmental anomalies and suspicious of convenient explanations. Their distrust of Bookwyrm is now moot; she died before anyone could prove anything, which is its own kind of loss.

Points of philosophical friction rather than open conflict: **Thorin's** giant-bred demand for radical transparency sits uneasily against **Daz's** habit of steering the group toward his own conclusions while framing them as consensus, and against **Zalthir's** monastic secrecy. **Grygum's** choice-over-determinism theology and **Daz's** rejection of inherited status align them ideologically against the racial essentialism of the Underdark they've left. Two companions strain the group's patience — **Daral Yashenti** (tolerated, not trusted) and captive **Fembris Lancer** — while **Glabbagool** and **Dawnbringer** are treated less as gear than as members with their own arcs (Glabbagool's monk training; Dawnbringer's insistence on continued "therapy" before she'll leave Candlekeep).
