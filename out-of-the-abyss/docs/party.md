## Party Overview

**Current location:** Candlekeep — confined in **Deneir's Sanctum** (repurposed as the party's holding cell and investigation office) after destroying a pack of helmed horrors; two ruined armor piles and one inert Candlekeep's High Tower key remain there. The active crisis is at the **High Tower**, where **A'lai Aivenmore** has retreated with the first key.

**Active quests / open threads:**
- **The two keys (most urgent):** A'lai Aivenmore holds the first Candlekeep's High Tower key and awaits **Moziqodo**, who is bringing the second. What they unlock — and who Moziqodo is — is unknown. The inert key Daz carries is a helmed-horror beacon, deliberately built and aimed.
- **Kalan Strongbranch's fate:** Last seen running, breathless; survival unknown.
- **Bookwyrm (First Reader)'s fate & the naming ceremony:** Her status after A'lai Aivenmore seized the key is unconfirmed; a ceremony to replace her is imminent; the concealed heart of Janussi may yet surface.
- **The false Sylvira Savikas:** A shapeshifter impersonated her (wrong scent, two locations at once) and is loose in the tower.
- **Daz's origins (central campaign mystery):** The Menzoberranzan patron who paid to protect him, the struck "Daz'issin" name, the sealed "fourth seat" journal.
- **Cosmic:** Zuggtmoy vs. Juiblex; Demogorgon loose on the Prime Material (Eldeth Feldrun carries the warning north); the "wells are not wells" / "Bone King sings" prophecies.

**Collective resources & assets:**
- The party self-identifies as **The Ember Vanguard** (name coined by Thorin) — now more legend than unit, with Duergar wanted posters across Gracklstugh misattributing crimes to them.
- Held items of note: the inert High Tower key, Glabbagool (with a Potion of Flying and a captured House T'sarran spy inside), Dawnbringer + Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard, Asha Vandree's spider-silk cloak and wax pouch, a necromantic pearl, and Grygum's full deal/event records.
- Attached companions: **Glabbagool**, **Dawnbringer**, **Spiderbait** (guide), **Daral Yashenti** (tolerated, not trusted), and captive **Fembris Lancer** (food taster and sole witness to the key handoff).

**Group reputation:** Celebrated in Blingdenstone; hunted/branded as criminals in Gracklstugh; protected-but-detained at Candlekeep, which refuses to surrender them to House T'sarran. Hunted by **House T'sarran** and **House Mizzrym** on Daz's account.

## Characters

### Zalthir — Monk 8 (Warrior of Shadow) · Bronze Dragonborn · Player: Gabe
- **Personality & motivations:** Silent tactical architect and the party's moral anchor, raised in a secretive monastic order that prized silence, discipline, and control-through-proximity over violence. No longer seeks mere escape — he wants to understand the architecture of power, deception, and silence that governs the world. Haunted by having once been treated as "furniture."
- **Notable relationships:** Bonded to **Glabbagool** (fused to his left forearm; offers it monastic training). Tactical partner to **Daz** (the "Ember Grapple" synergy). Mutual respect with **Thorin** and **Grygum**. Killed **Asha Vandree** without justification. Assigned by Candlekeep to investigate the "Jimjar thread." Skeptical of **Bookwyrm (First Reader)**.

## Party Dynamics

The four operate as a tightly interdependent unit with complementary roles: **Daz** as strategist, **Thorin** as the physical/moral anchor, **Zalthir** as the silent tactical architect and investigator, and **Grygum** as support caster and documentarian. Combat synergy is well-developed — Zalthir's grappling sets up Daz's area-of-effect spells ("Ember Grapple"), while Grygum keeps the line standing and Thorin holds terrain.

Shared goals right now: resolving the Candlekeep endgame (the two keys, A'lai Aivenmore's play, Kalan Strongbranch's fate) and, underneath it all, unraveling **Daz's origins** — the mystery that increasingly drives the whole party. Both Zalthir and Daz are attuned to environmental anomalies and suspicious of convenient explanations; both distrust Bookwyrm (First Reader).

Points of philosophical friction rather than open conflict: **Thorin's** giant-bred demand for radical transparency sits uneasily against **Daz's** habit of steering the group toward his own conclusions while framing them as consensus, and against **Zalthir's** monastic secrecy. **Grygum's** choice-over-determinism theology and **Daz's** rejection of inherited status align them ideologically against the racial essentialism of the Underdark they've left. Two companions strain the group's patience — **Daral Yashenti** (tolerated, not trusted) and captive **Fembris Lancer** — while **Glabbagool** and **Dawnbringer** are treated less as gear than as members with their own arcs (Glabbagool's monk training; Dawnbringer's insistence on continued "therapy" before she'll leave Candlekeep).
