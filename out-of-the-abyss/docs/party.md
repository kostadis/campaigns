# Party Reference — Out of the Abyss (Modified)

## Party Overview

**Current location:** Deneir's Sanctum, Candlekeep — a book-lined chamber repurposed as a comfortable but guarded holding cell. The party are detained witnesses following the Chapter 61 helmed horror ambush and Thorin's assault on their minder.

**Active quests / open threads:**
- **The Daz Enigma** — an unknown patron pays Menzoberranzan rates to protect Daz; A'lai Aivenmore is the sole living lead and remains unconfronted.
- **The High Tower key and the assassins** — Grygum holds a key; helmed horrors came for it. Sender unknown. Kalan Strongbranch gave a second key to an unnamed third party.
- **Candlekeep succession** — Bookwyrm guilty but unaccused, ceremony cancelled; Fembris Lancer expected to be elected next head.
- **The shapeshifter** who impersonated Sylvira Savikas is still at large in Candlekeep.
- **Zalthir's Avowed assignment** — the Jimjar thread and the Khell-Vire case.
- **Spore corruption in the party** — undiagnosed and escalating (Daz's head-pressure worst).
- **House T'sarran's vengeance** — matron mother playing a patient long game; the shrunken spy is a magical tether.
- **Jorlan Duskryn's fate** — wounded, missing, sustained by hatred.
- **The demon war** — Demogorgon on the Prime Material; Zuggtmoy seeks Araumycos; Juiblex vows to consume her. Off-screen, escalating.
- **Thorin's clan acceptance** (Eldeth Feldrun carrying his case west), **Dawnbringer's therapy**, **Glabbagool's reserved question**, **the Grygum school of thought**, **Fheminor Scrivenbark's erased name**.

**Collective resources:**
- One **High Tower Library key** (Grygum)
- **Dawnbringer** — sentient sunblade, Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard (Thorin)
- **Stonespeaker Crystal**, Princess Ebonmire Spellbook (Daz)
- **Glabbagool** — fused to Zalthir's left forearm; acid, four eyes, skill absorption
- **Bag of Holding** (air-holed) — contains the shrunken House T'sarran spy
- **Voucher of the Varmint** — safe passage from Chipgrin Goldwhisker, extendable to Menzoberranzan
- Standing alliance with restored **Blingdenstone**; working alliance with **Kalan Strongbranch**
- Party cash on hand is substantial (each PC carries ~1,000+ gp)

**Reputation:** Known as the **Ember Vanguard** (and the Zalthir/Daz pairing as the **Ember Grapple**) — a myth they no longer control. **Wanted in Gracklstugh**, posters bearing distorted names. Allies in Blingdenstone. In Candlekeep: murder-solvers, and therefore inconvenient.

**Known vulnerability:** no meaningful ranged capability. Flying or distant enemies exploit this; the party compensates by controlling space.

## Characters

### Daz — Wizard 8 (Evoker), Elf (Drow Lineage) — Player: Mike Hall

**Personality & motivations.** A cynical drow apostate who rejected Menzoberranzan's slavery and Lolthite fanaticism outright — "free people are the basis of any society." He is the party's tactical architect, styled "General," and prefers to overpromise and under-deliver to control expectations. He journals obsessively (a habit adopted from surface-world author Milo Goodbarrel) to process his past and sharpen his thinking.

**Relationships.** Brother **Jorlan Duskryn** — hostile-obsessive, missing and wounded. **Nym** and **Kaelira Duskryn** — mercenaries paid by an unknown patron to protect him. **Sister Yvenne** — his coercive Candlekeep handler. **A'lai Aivenmore** — the only lead on his patron; unconfronted. **House T'sarran** — killed their mage and guards with a Fireball at Velkynvelve; the matron mother has vowed vengeance. **Chipgrin Goldwhisker** — gave him the Voucher of the Varmint. Killed **Buppido**.

**Items of significance.** Stonespeaker Crystal (attuned), Dustsight Spectacles (attuned), Princess Ebonmire Spellbook, Shortsword +1.

**Current state.** Spore corruption manifesting as worsening head-pressure. Wants to find out who sent the helmed horrors.

---

### Grygum — Cleric 8 (Life Domain), Orc — Player: Ben Pfaff

**Personality & motivations.** A heterodox cleric of Bahamut who holds that dragons are defined by choice, not scale colour, and that judging by race is the ultimate evil — a doctrine traced to the Stroud/Protanther chess match. Raised in an orc tribe, he is pragmatic about brutal hierarchies and weaponises a harmless exterior, playing the "Platinum Fool" with theatrical cooking and staged healing rituals. He takes meticulous notes on deals and lore and believes the Underdark runs on transactional favours.

**Relationships.** Mentor **Gorg Bahamut**. Handler **Vareth** at Candlekeep, who is now researching "the Grygum school" as the question of the age. Traumatised by **Buppido**, who dismembered him. Contempt for **Eldeth Feldrun's** blanket hatred of duergar and orcs.

**Items of significance.** **The High Tower Library key** — the object the assassins came for. Mace +1, two fourth-level spell gems, six 100gp gems, Udhask ghost bones, jailer's ring of keys, forgery seal, obsidian and bone prayer beads, fist-sized pearl from the heart fungus, three spore sacs.

**Current state.** Holds the single most dangerous object in the party's possession and is in a locked room because of it.

### Thorin — Fighter 8 (Battle Master), Dwarf — Player: Joe Beda

**Personality & motivations.** A dwarf raised by hill giants — speaks Dwarvish with a thick Giant accent, fights with hill-giant battle master technique, and inherited the giants' cosmic antipathy toward dragonkind (which nearly boiled over in Themberchaud's service). He despises secrecy, NDAs, and faction politics, advocating "radical transparency," and when overwhelmed by competing agendas his instinct is to simply "be my faction." Trait: *"Every avalanche begins as a single pebble."*

**Relationships.** **Dawnbringer** — his bonded sentient sunblade, currently in therapy with **Philemon** and refusing to leave Candlekeep without more sessions. **Eldeth Feldrun** — rival-turned-ally, travelling west sworn to speak for his clan acceptance at Mithral Hall and Gauntlgrym. Killed **Narrak**. Carries quiet grief over **Buppido**. **Fembris Lancer** — knocked unconscious by Thorin in Ch61; the only witness to the key, and he will wake up.

**Items of significance.** **Dawnbringer** (attuned), Mithral Hall Plate, Shield +3, Longsword +1, Stonegnarl Warband Token (AC 22 on stone), Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard, holy water, wooden stakes.

**Current state.** Has an unrevealed "mission in mind." His single punch is the proximate reason the party is confined.

### Zalthir — Monk 8 (Warrior of Shadow), Dragonborn (Brass) — Player: Gabe

**Personality & motivations.** Trained by a secretive monastic order that prizes silence, discretion, and evasion over frontal conflict; he laments that his masters taught him "how to make people blind, and not how to make them silent." He models his Underdark survival on **Korrh Erann**, who taught that fleeing the drow beats fighting them. He regards brutal interrogation as "motivation," not torture, and dismantles the most dangerous target first.

**Relationships.** **Glabbagool** — permanently fused to his left forearm after sunlight transformation; devoted companion learning monastic silence. The **Avowed** — he is nominally their investigative agent (Jimjar thread, Khell-Vire case). Killed **the Pudding King** and **Asha Vandree**; grappled and dropped **Ilvara Mizzrym**. Paired with Daz as the "Ember Grapple." His draconic heritage carries a hidden stigma — the medusa **Neheedra Duskryn** mocked "the circumstances of his birth."

**Items of significance.** Eldritch Claw Tattoo (attuned), Insignia of Claws, drow shadow-silk cloak (taken from Asha Vandree), Glabbagool, four Greater Healing Potions, Scroll of Remove Curse, Scroll of Spider Climb, two Mysterious Lists of People from Settlement.

**Current state.** Suspected spore corruption. Carries the Jimjar research assignment personally.

## Party Dynamics

**Shared goals.** Get out from under Candlekeep's detention without surrendering the key; find out who sent the helmed horrors; decide what to do about the guilty who walk free (A'lai Aivenmore and Bookwyrm). Everything else — Daz's patron, the demon war, Thorin's clan, Zalthir's Avowed docket — routes through staying in Candlekeep's good graces long enough to use its library.

**Structure.** Daz is the acknowledged General and does the political and drow-cultural manoeuvring. Grygum is the transactional diplomat and the party's disarming face — and, at the moment, its liability, since he holds the key. Zalthir is the executioner and the advocate for evasion over engagement. Thorin is the blunt instrument and the moral floor.

**Current tensions.**
- **Transparency vs. tradecraft.** Thorin openly despises the NDAs, secrecy, and faction politics that Daz treats as basic professional tooling. Candlekeep is exactly the environment that maximises this friction.
- **Thorin and dragonkind.** His giant-taught antipathy sits directly beside a brass dragonborn ally and a bonded sentient sword in therapy. It flared under Themberchaud; it has not been resolved, only postponed.
- **Grygum's anti-racialism vs. Thorin's inherited prejudice.** Grygum's entire theology is that judging by species is the ultimate evil. Thorin's giant upbringing gave him a species-based hatred. They have not argued about it yet.
- **Noise.** Zalthir considers the party's overlapping chatter a tactical liability and says so.
- **The Fembris Lancer problem.** Thorin's punch created the detention; the other three now bear its cost, and Fembris Lancer — soon-to-be head of the Avowed — will wake with a grievance and a memory of the key.
- **Corruption.** Daz's head-pressure worsens and Zalthir and Glabbagool are suspect. No one has named it aloud as a party-level problem.

**Cohesion.** Despite the friction, this is a group forged by Velkynvelve, Buppido, and the Pudding King, and it defaults to acting as one unit. The Ember Vanguard name — Grygum's invention, now beyond their control — is a shared identity all four have accepted.
