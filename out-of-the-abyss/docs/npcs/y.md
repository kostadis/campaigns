# Y (Unidentified Impersonator)

## Identity
- Role: An impersonator — the "false devil-lady" who impersonated Sylvira Savikas. True name/identity unknown (referenced only as "Y").
- Faction affiliation: Unknown.
- First appearance: Not directly encountered by the party; known only through reference. Party awareness comes via Thorin's account of the failed attempt to question Janussi's corpse.

## Personality & Motivations
- Core drive (as understood): Concealment — appears to be actively suppressing information the party seeks. Motivations otherwise unknown.
- Personality traits: Insufficient direct interaction to characterize. Known only through the consequences of their actions (impersonation, corpse-tampering).

## History with the Party
- No confirmed direct interaction. Y is implicated retroactively: when the party attempted to question Janussi's corpse, the questioning failed. Thorin attributed this failure to Y — the imposter is said to have questioned or sealed the corpse first, locking its mouth shut for days.
- Thorin explicitly flagged this as possibly hearsay: no one could verify whether the corpse was ever truly made to answer.

## Current Status
- Last known location: Unknown.
- Active operations: Impersonation of Sylvira Savikas; suspected tampering with / sealing of Janussi's corpse.
- **What the party knows:** An impersonator ("Y") posed as Sylvira Savikas and may have sealed Janussi's corpse against questioning.
- **What remains hidden:** Y's true identity, whereabouts, motive, and whether the corpse-sealing actually occurred (unverified — flagged as possible hearsay).

## Relationships
- **Sylvira Savikas:** Impersonated by Y ("false devil-lady").
- **Janussi (corpse):** Allegedly questioned or sealed by Y prior to the party's attempt.
- **Thorin (party):** Source of the party's information on Y; treats the account with skepticism.
