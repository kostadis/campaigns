# Nibbles

## Identity
- Leuwin's pet squirrel; key witness to the apothecary theft.
- The party encountered Nibbles while investigating the apothecary intrusion; the squirrel fetches implements one at a time for Leuwin.

## Personality & Motivations
- Food-motivated: bribeable one acorn at a time, and stopped talking once its cheeks were stuffed full of nuts.
- Present and observant when Leuwin was absent from the room — a reliable eyewitness, though limited in the detail it can convey.

## History with the Party
- During the apothecary theft investigation, Daz cast a spell to loosen Nibbles's tongue and bribed the squirrel acorn by acorn to extract testimony.
- Nibbles reported witnessing three figures during the intrusion:
  - The unfriendly gnome girl (Fheminor).
  - A big, clumsy, dark-skinned elf (A'lai).
  - A huge lizard-skinned figure — a dragonborn — who made a big mess, made something go *bling*, moved things around, and left.
- Critically, Nibbles saw the intruder because Leuwin was not in the room: Leuwin was in the greenhouse/garden helping A'lai while the dragonborn broke the vial upstairs and took the toxin.
- Nibbles could not identify any distinguishing marks on the culprits. The interview ended when its cheeks were full of nuts.

## Current Status
- Last seen with Leuwin at the apothecary, having concluded its testimony.
- No active plans of its own.
- **Party knows:** the theft occurred while Leuwin was distracted in the greenhouse with A'lai; a dragonborn broke the vial and took the toxin; Fheminor and A'lai were also present.
- **Remains hidden:** any distinguishing marks or further identifying details on the dragonborn or other figures.

## Relationships
- **Leuwin (owner):** pet squirrel; fetches implements for Leuwin. Was separated from Leuwin during the theft (Leuwin in the greenhouse with A'lai).
- **Daz (party):** used a spell and acorn bribes to obtain Nibbles's testimony.
- Witnessed **Fheminor**, **A'lai**, and an unidentified **dragonborn** at the scene.
