# Leuwin

## Identity
- Elderly, absent-minded satyr druid; proprietor of the **Oak Tree Apothecary**.
- The shop is a treehouse grown into the roots of an evergreen oak, located between the Sea Warden's Tower and the House of Mechanus, with a greenhouse below. Keeps a pet squirrel, **Nibbles**.
- Party (Daz) met him while investigating a poison theft/murder — Leuwin can identify poisons definitively.

## Personality & Motivations
- Kindly, helpful herbalist focused on remedies (cold medicine, digestive aids); provides an orderly account of his day when questioned.
- Absent-minded and unobservant of the key event — he missed the intrusion entirely. Genuinely shocked and morally offended ("unacceptable") when shown that a vial from his own "Dangerous Toxins" shelf was broken. Trusts his squirrel Nibbles as a potential witness.

## History with the Party
- **Investigation of the theft/murder:** Daz questioned Leuwin at the apothecary. Leuwin gave a timeline of visitors and confirmed he was in the greenhouse/garden below with A'lai while the theft occurred upstairs — so he witnessed nothing himself.
- Daz pointed out a broken vial on the "Dangerous Toxins" shelf; Leuwin was shocked, called it unacceptable, and suggested Nibbles might know something.
- The intruder's details (dragonborn thief, spilled peppermint) came from **Nibbles the squirrel**, not Leuwin.

> **GM note — source conflict on timeline.** The session notes disagree on who arrived when:
> - **Version A:** Gnome **Fheminor** came at 2:30 for cold medicine (horehound tincture, licorice); **A'lai** arrived at 3:00 asking for fennel for indigestion; Leuwin took A'lai down through the rear exit to the greenhouse; theft occurred at 3:15.
> - **Version B:** **A'lai** arrived at 2:30 asking for fresh fennel; while Leuwin was in the garden with him, the dragonborn **Alkrist** entered and stole the poison vial, spilling peppermint on the counter floor.
>
> Both agree Leuwin was below with A'lai during the theft and saw nothing. Reconcile before relying on exact times.

## Current Status
- **Location:** Oak Tree Apothecary.
- Cooperating with the investigation; directed the party toward Nibbles as a witness.
- **Party knows:** the visitor timeline, that a Dangerous Toxins vial was broken/stolen, that Leuwin has an alibi (in greenhouse with A'lai), and (via Nibbles) that a dragonborn thief spilled peppermint.
- **Hidden/unconfirmed:** Leuwin personally witnessed nothing of the theft; the exact arrival times are unreconciled between sources.

## Relationships
- **Nibbles (pet squirrel):** the actual witness to the theft; key source of intruder details.
- **A'lai:** customer Leuwin was assisting in the greenhouse/garden during the theft — Leuwin's alibi.
- **Fheminor (gnome):** customer for cold medicine (Version A only).
- **Alkrist (dragonborn):** the thief who stole the poison vial (Version B only).
- **Daz (party):** conducted the interview; recipient of the visitor timeline.
