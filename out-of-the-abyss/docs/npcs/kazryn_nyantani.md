# Kazryn Nyantani

## Identity
- Chief cleric; also known as "Tani."
- Named among the readers/council attending to Janussi's death. First surfaces at the shouting council held over Janussi's body.
- Physical description: slender and gray, with wrinkles around the eyes. Sylvira derides her as a "bitter old hag" and "frumpy old boar."

## Personality & Motivations
- Presents as level-headed and procedural — cut through the council's argument by insisting "first you must find the murderer."
- Stepped up to run the Deadwinter gift exchange (Secret Santa style, from under the evergreen tree) after Sylvira stormed out, matching each gift to the correct great reader — suggesting she keeps community rituals intact under stress.
- Per Sylvira, harbors long-standing bitterness over a past romance (see below); whether this is true resentment or Sylvira's spin is unconfirmed.

## History with the Party
- **Council over Janussi's body:** Kazryn intervened in the shouting match, pushing the group toward finding the murderer before anything else.
- **Deadwinter gift exchange:** After Sylvira left, Kazryn took over handing out the gifts to the great readers.
- **Suspect tally:** Named by Sylvira as a prime suspect in Janussi's murder. Zalthir lists her among his eight suspects. Daz entered her on the tally, judging her resentment credible enough to survive scrutiny — long-standing grudge plus apparent capability.

## Current Status
- Last seen actively distributing Deadwinter gifts to the great readers.
- No active operation known beyond her role as chief cleric.
- **Party knows:** she is chief cleric, was present at the council, and is a named murder suspect with a plausible motive.
- **Hidden:** whether she is actually involved in Janussi's death; the truth of Sylvira's claims about her motive remains unverified.

## Relationships
- **Janussi (deceased):** former lover, "on and off," before Sylvira. Central to the alleged motive.
- **Sylvira Savikas:** hostile. Sylvira claims Kazryn "always resented Janussi for ditching her in favor of me" and openly insults her. Note this is Sylvira's characterization, not confirmed fact.
- **The great readers:** administers community ritual (gift exchange) among them.
- **Party — Zalthir:** lists her among his eight suspects.
- **Party — Daz:** logged her on the murder tally as a credible suspect.
