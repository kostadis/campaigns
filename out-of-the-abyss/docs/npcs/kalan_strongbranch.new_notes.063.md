# New notes for Kalan Strongbranch (from dossier_extract_063.md)

Found buckling on his armor at the Sea Warden's Tower, already moving toward the party at a half-run, having heard the alarm bell. His first question on learning of Bookwyrm's death was not who or how but "Does she have the key?" — and he was relieved to learn no key was found on her body, initially framing her death as an accident, "a maddening attack by Sylvira Savikas's son," noting "I told Bookwyrm that creature would be the death of us." When told about the three armor constructs hunting a key in the sanctum, his composure drained: "Gadzooks. Someone is definitely after the keys." He deputized the party as **Watchers of Candlekeep** (offering a "small honorarium" when Daz asked about pay). Critically, he revealed his deception: the key he had earlier pressed on Grygum — with the strange phrasing "*I have given it now to someone who is not me*" — was a **decoy from the first hour**; the genuine key had been posted on **Tadric**, a young Watcher, in the North Galleries, away from the barracks on purpose. He justified this by reasoning that if enemies saw the party with a fake key, both the party and the real key would be safer. He gave Thorin a **Watcher's Stair key** for a shortcut to the North Gallery, swearing "On my honor" that this one was genuine. He tried to run with the party but, carrying extra weight and years ("Little Rotund," per Thorin), fell behind and planted, hands on knees, done. Last seen breathing hard and out of the pursuit; Tadric's anxious repeated question was whether Kalan is still alive — the party's honest answer was that he was alive when they left him.
