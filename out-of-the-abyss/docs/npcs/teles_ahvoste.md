# Teles Ahvoste

## Identity
- Great Reader among the avowed at the library (Blingdenstone/Gauntlgrym context — Keeper of Tomes institution).
- Never appears directly in play; known entirely through report and gossip. One of eight named suspects in a murder investigation.

## Personality & Motivations
- Driven by a long-nursed grievance: passed over for Keeper of Tomes ~20 years ago despite being older and regarded as the more skilled practitioner. Sylvira frames his jealousy as a possible motive "for revenge. Murder."
- Portrayed as bitter, contemptuous, and self-important — Daral dismissed him as "the deluded old git," and he scoffed at Alkrist's supper project. Described as a great reader intellectually. Reportedly made unwanted advances on the junior avowed Irony.

## History with the Party
- The party has not met Teles face-to-face; all knowledge is secondhand.
- Hollypocket reported that around the ninth bell, Teles had a meeting with Janussi that broke into a shouting match — she clearly heard Teles shout **"hypocrite!"** at Janussi before storming out.
- Named among the eight murder suspects; Daral dismissed him at the Hearth as "the deluded old git."
- Thorin proposed that after splitting investigation duties, someone should interview "Teles and Kalan."
- Orrin revealed Teles had been making unwanted advances on the junior avowed Irony (the "too interested in the junior avowed" gossip both Irony and Orrin hinted at).
- Sylvira named him a suspect — one of the Great Readers hostile to her — and detailed his ~20-year hostility toward Janussi over the passed-over Keeper of Tomes appointment. Daz filed this as a live grievance.

## Current Status
- Last known activity: stormed out of a shouting-match meeting with Janussi around the ninth bell.
- **Party knows:** he's a suspect, held a two-decade grudge against Janussi, publicly clashed with Janussi that day, and made unwanted advances on Irony.
- **Hidden / unresolved:** whether he is actually the murderer; his current whereabouts; the substance of the "hypocrite!" accusation.

## Relationships
- **Janussi:** Bitter rival for ~20 years after being passed over for Keeper of Tomes; recent shouting match ("hypocrite!").
- **Irony (junior avowed):** Subject of his unwanted advances.
- **Sylvira:** Hostile toward her; she counts him among the Great Readers opposed to her and flags him as a murder suspect.
- **Orrin:** Source of the gossip exposing his advances on Irony.
- **Daral:** Dismissive of him ("the deluded old git").
- **Alkrist:** Scoffed at Alkrist's supper project, likening it to "some Fheminor Scrivenbark program."
- **Kalan:** Paired with Teles as a joint interview target by Thorin.
- **Party:** Flagged as a live grievance/suspect by Daz and slated for interview by Thorin.
