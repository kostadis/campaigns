# Brother Quellin

## Identity
- Apparently a monastic figure ("Brother"), possibly associated with the Garden Shadow — affiliation unconfirmed.
- Has never appeared in play. Known to the party only through Zalthir's second-hand reference.

## Personality & Motivations
No direct characterization available. The only trace is an aphorism uncertainly attributed to him: "the most dangerous person in a room is the one who has already decided they've won." Nothing else about his goals, drives, or demeanor is established.

## History with the Party
- **Alkrist interrogation:** During his recollection, Zalthir invoked the above maxim to explain Janussi's fatal overconfidence. He credited the saying to the Garden Shadow — or "possibly Brother Quellin" (and in one telling hedged toward "Brother Harren"), admitting he could not remember the true source.

## Current Status
- Location, activities, and current plans: unknown. No on-screen presence.
- **What the party knows:** effectively nothing — only a name surfaced in Zalthir's uncertain attribution.
- **What remains hidden:** everything, including whether Quellin is the actual author of the maxim or even a distinct individual.

## Relationships
- **Zalthir:** the only party-side link — Zalthir may have learned the maxim from him, but is unsure.
- **Garden Shadow / Brother Harren:** named as alternative sources of the same saying; relationship to Quellin undefined.

## Arc Score Events
None recorded.
