# Archibald Roots Senior

## Identity
- Legendary gnome botanist; father of Archibald Roots Jr.
- Subject of much of the book *Fey-mous Flowers & Fungi*.
- Not directly encountered by the party — referenced only, primarily through his son.

## Personality & Motivations
- Driven by a lifelong compulsion to catalog flora and fungi in dangerous, unexplored places ("cataloging things that grow where no one wants to go").
- Characterized as a life of exploration across a long gnomish lifespan. Spoken of by his son in the careful, reverent tone of a man whose father saved lives, suggesting his botanical work had real, life-preserving consequences.

## History with the Party
- No direct interactions on record. Known to the party only secondhand through his son, Archibald Roots Jr., and through the book *Fey-mous Flowers & Fungi*.

## Current Status
- Unknown from the notes. No location, activity, or current operations recorded.
- What the party knows: his reputation as a legendary botanist, his authorship/subject role in *Fey-mous Flowers & Fungi*, and his relationship to Archibald Roots Jr.
- What remains hidden: his present whereabouts, whether he is living, and the specifics of his explorations and life-saving deeds.

## Relationships
- **Archibald Roots Jr.** — son; primary source of information about him, speaks of him with careful reverence.
- **The party** — no direct relationship; known only by reputation and through his son.

## Arc Score Events
- None recorded.
