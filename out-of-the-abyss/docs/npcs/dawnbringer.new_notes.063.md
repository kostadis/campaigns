# New notes for Dawnbringer (from dossier_extract_063.md)

Thorin's radiant sentient sword, active throughout the chapter. It lit up under Daral's Detect Magic in the sanctum. In the rotunda fight, Thorin had to be reminded — and the sword required an apology before the fight, which it got ("You are Radiant Sword!") — that it deals radiant damage, not fire (relevant against the fire-immune pit fiend). It landed three opening strikes on Moziqodo and delivered the killing blows. Still humming in Thorin's grip after the fight.
