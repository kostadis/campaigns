# Bahamut

## Identity
- Deity; god of the paladin/cleric Grygum. Functions in play as a divine framework rather than an acting character.
- Has never appeared. Referenced only through Grygum's internal narration and once by the NPC Fembris.

## Personality & Motivations
- Domain includes the preservation of truth (as invoked by Grygum). No demonstrated personality — Bahamut does not act or speak in any session.
- Characterized indirectly through Grygum's devotion: a distant, silent presence who "has rarely volunteered an opinion on anything."

## History with the Party
Bahamut never manifests at the table. Every appearance is a reference, all clustered around a murder investigation in a keep/library:
- Grygum works out the murder in the library, musing "Bahamut may or may not have been watching me work it out. I took notes either way." Tied to Grygum's habit of thinking in maxims.
- As the keep empties for a theological debate, Grygum repeats the sentiment: "Bahamut may or may not have been watching. I took notes either way."
- Grygum ties Bahamut's truth-preservation domain to his use of the Mending cantrip to restore cut wrapping paper and reveal what it concealed. Fembris invokes his own standing "as a cleric of Bahamut" to flatter Grygum.
- When the party resolves to convict Bookwyrm of embarrassment rather than murder, Grygum notes "Bahamut may or may not have been reading over my shoulder — I wrote it down either way."
- In another beat, Bahamut's silence ("has rarely volunteered an opinion on anything") is invoked to argue it would still not call Daral's behavior innocent.

## Current Status
- No location — a referenced deity, not a present entity.
- No active plans or operations.
- What the party knows: Bahamut is Grygum's god, associated with truth and its preservation. What remains hidden: whether Bahamut observes or responds at all — deliberately left ambiguous by Grygum ("may or may not").

## Relationships
- **Grygum:** Primary devotee. Grygum keeps notes "whether Bahamut reads them or not," frames his investigative work through Bahamut's domain of truth, and repeatedly wonders whether the god is watching.
- **Fembris:** Claims standing "as a cleric of Bahamut," using the affiliation to flatter Grygum.
