## Completed Encounters & Quests

*This content is DONE. Do not replay or re-suggest.*

1. **Escape from Velkynvelve (ch1–3)** — Party imprisoned by Ilvara Mizzrym; slave revolt succeeded. Imbros killed Prince Derendil, then was slain. Ront killed by a vrock. *Consequence:* Velkynvelve destroyed; its guards later found corrupted into fungal spore-servants. Ilvara pursued the party thereafter.
2. **The Silken Paths crossing (ch5)** — Party traversed the webs with goblin guides. *Consequence:* Yuk Yuk murdered en route (killer never confirmed); Spiderbait mourns him and blames Shuushar, unproven.
3. **Bandit ambush at the bottomless gorge (ch7)** — Bandits killed; corpse-disposal debate resolved in transit. *Consequence:* Eldeth's distrust of Thorin's giant ties surfaced.
4. **Sloobludop and the Deep Father schism (ch8–12)** — Kuo-toan cult war between Ploopploopeen and Bloppblippodd; both died in the ritual, which **summoned Demogorgon**. Sloobludop razed. *Consequence:* Demogorgon loose on the Material Plane; Darklake corrupted; Shuushar departed to lead reformed Kuo-toans.
5. **Gracklstugh — Stonespeaker Hgraam's counsel (ch16–17)** — Party learned of the Deepking's madness, the advisor Shal, and the portents. *Consequence:* Daz received the Stonespeaker Crystal; Hgraam has since closed himself off.
6. **Buppido exposed and killed (ch25 area)** — Derro serial killer / false prophet slain by Daz. *Consequence:* Companion threat removed; Pelek's ghost thread opened and later closed.
7. **The Whorlstone Caverns (ch19–29)** — Plinki's dragon-egg ritual and Narrak's grafting cult foiled. Plinki psychically killed by Zalthir (journal recovered); Narrak killed by Thorin. *Consequence:* Party branded "the Ember Vanguard"; wanted posters across Duergar/Drow territory misattribute the Derro High Council assassination and "theft of the Egg" to them.
8. **Lost Tomb of Khaem / Dawnbringer recovered (ch30)** — The trapped voice was Dawnbringer; Thorin bonded with her. Fargas Rumblefoot rescued. *Consequence:* Dawnbringer is now a party member and an ongoing therapy obligation.
9. **Neverlight Grove (ch27–33)** — Colony found corrupted by Zuggtmoy through Sovereign Phylo. Sarith Kzekarit (spore-servant) killed; Xinaya granted the death she begged for. *Consequence:* Sovereign Basidia exiled and now a fugitive; Stool and Rumpadump returned home to a corrupted place; Rasharoo parted from the party.
10. **Blingdenstone peace brokerage (ch34–40)** — Party settled the miners/wererats/spiritual-leaders conflict; abolished the enslaving of summoned earth elementals ("We serve the stone, not enslave it"). *Consequence:* Blingdenstone stabilized and allied; reopened to surface trade; Jadgar's Burrow Wardens order revived; Pelek's ghost laid to rest.
11. **Entémoch's Boon / the Festering Fissure (ch42–44)** — The sealed path to the stone prince's throne found; Ogrémoch's Bane cleared and the Galeb Duhr guardians freed; the three sacrifices (weapon, wealth, blood) rendered. *Consequence:* The mad earth elementals resolved; Entémoch's boon claimed.
12. **The Pudding King defeated (ch46–47)** — Juiblex's avatar dissolved by Zalthir; the ooze infestation of Blingdenstone ended. Jimjar's saint's-bone die pacified the ooze horde. *Consequence:* **Glabbagool bonded to Zalthir's left forearm.** Juiblex vowed rebirth by consuming Zuggtmoy.
13. **Honors at Blingdenstone / Jimjar's departure (ch48)** — Party honored; Voucher of the Varmint gifted to Daz. Zalthir's inspection revealed Jimjar's angelic profile; Jimjar winked and vanished from existence. *Consequence:* "What was Jimjar?" is now a Candlekeep research thread. Eldeth Feldrun departed for Mithral Hall and Gauntlgrym to warn the surface of Demogorgon and to arrange Thorin's clan adoption.
14. **Ilvara Mizzrym's last stand (ch49–54)** — The Heart Fungus destroyed with Dust of Suleiman; Ilvara, fully consumed by Zuggtmoy, detonated into spores. Asha Vandree killed by Zalthir. A House T'sarran spy, miniaturized, was captured and held inside Glabbagool. *Consequence:* House Mizzrym is disrupted and searching blindly for Ilvara; her prayer book (Zuggtmoy marginalia) recovered. Party emerged to the surface.
15. **Surface transit / The Burning Wizard Inn (ch55)** — Ellen restored ("The wells are not wells anymore"). Sister Yvenne interviewed; she deflected from Daz's name toward his patron. *Consequence:* The struck name "Daz'issin" surfaced as likely Daz's true name.
16. **Donation for access at Candlekeep (ch53–56)** — Ilvara's prayer book, Plinki's journal, and Asha Vandree's treatise donated. *Consequence:* Party owed five research answers (Dawnbringer, Janussi, House T'sarran, Jimjar, Daz's history).
17. **Grygum's "fourth corner" thesis (ch56)** — Grygum's reinterpretation of Stroud's Gambit, formalized by Vareth, triggered an Avowed-wide mass debate. *Consequence:* Candlekeep's security emptied — the window that enabled Janussi's murder. The Grygumite School of Theology now exists as an academic faction.
18. **The murder investigation of Janussi (ch56–61)** — Solved to the party's satisfaction: Janussi poisoned via a Deadwinter gift-book coated in **Midnight Tears** (saliva-activated, midnight trigger), then his heart removed post-mortem to block resurrection, with Daral Yashenti framed as gift-giver. Queenie the cat identified both the false Sylvira (wrong scent) and Daral removing the book. Daral, poisoned by the tainted book, was restored by Dawnbringer and exonerated. **Alkrist confessed under Zone of Truth** to the poisoning — unaware the heart had been removed — and is now **dead**. *Consequence:* Case publicly "closed" with Alkrist as scapegoat. Kalan Strongbranch reinstated as Head of the Avowed; his rejected "Threefold Proof" is now the institutional investigative framework. Bookwyrm's concealment of the heart (lead-lined Milil chalice) and A'lai Aivenmore's authorship of the plot remain unpunished.
19. **Helmed horror ambush in Deneir's Sanctum (ch61)** — Two helmed horrors came through the windows targeting the inert High Tower key in Daz's pocket; both destroyed. *Consequence:* Two ruined suits of armor and the inert key remain; the party is now confined under guard in Deneir's Sanctum. Someone built and aimed them knowing exactly which pocket to reach for.

## Active Quests & Open Threads

**Candlekeep conspiracy (immediate).** The case is publicly closed with Alkrist as scapegoat, but Bookwyrm concealed the heart and A'lai Aivenmore architected the plot — both unpunished. Kalan Strongbranch intends a **public Zone of Truth demand** against Bookwyrm at the pending **naming ceremony** (humiliation, not conviction). Who becomes head — Fembris Lancer by default? Still unresolved: who physically removed Janussi's heart; who impersonated Sylvira and how; whether her plague is natural or induced; Teles Ahvoste's exact role; Fheminor Scrivenbark's erased record and whereabouts. Party is currently confined in Deneir's Sanctum.

**The five owed research answers.** Candlekeep still owes the party: Dawnbringer's condition, Janussi's murder, House T'sarran, Jimjar's disappearance, Daz's history. Zalthir has been formally assigned the Jimjar thread.

**Daz's patron (central long arc).** Someone in Menzoberranzan pays Menzoberranzan rates to keep Daz alive; the patron's record has been **blank for months**. A'lai Aivenmore knows the identity and pays only in intelligence. Kaelira Duskryn hunts the same answer via a "compromised drow scholar." House T'sarran hunts Daz to smoke the patron out; House Mizzrym still wants him dead. Tied to the struck name **"Daz'issin"** and the sealed noble house Sister Yvenne will not name.

**The inert High Tower key.** Undetectable, in Daz's pocket, and something built and aimed helmed horrors at it knowing exactly where it was. Origin and purpose unknown. Fembris Lancer is the sole witness to its transfer.

**The captured House T'sarran spy inside Glabbagool.** Held as of ch56 — still there, escaped, or dead? House T'sarran is two centuries stable and will send replacements.

**Zuggtmoy.** The dominant Underdark threat. Spreading from the Festering Fissure, Garden of Stone, and Neverlight Grove into the vacuum the Pudding King left. Manipulating the Myconids as the "Great Seeder"; seeking union with **Araumycos**. Her physical locus unknown; timeline unknown.

**Juiblex's rebirth.** Vowed to return by consuming Zuggtmoy's banquet. Residual influence lingers in Blingdenstone's ooze residue. Implicit demon-lord war brewing.

**Demogorgon on the surface.** Loose on the Material Plane since Sloobludop. Location and trajectory unknown. Eldeth is carrying the warning to Mithral Hall and Gauntlgrym.

**Dawnbringer's therapy.** She refuses to leave without continuing sessions and has begun communicating telepathically beyond her wielder. Open: the "sword whisperer" she's been hunting since the road south; her trauma with her prior, dead wielder.

**"Until the Bone King sings."** An unexplained note anchored to Zalthir and the Shadow Monk lineage; no origin in Candlekeep's records. Resonates with Ellen's "the wells are not wells anymore."

**Loose ends.** Yukyuk's true killer. Whether Gracklstugh still stands and what the mushroom-artifact "marker" on the party does. Fate of the dragon egg and the Obelisk of Diirinka. Zalthir's disputed lineage (bronze dragonborn vs. drow experience) and whether his vow of silence nominally holds. Fuurm's missing sister.
