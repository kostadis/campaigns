# Campaign State Reference — Out of the Abyss (Modified)

**Campaign horizon:** Party has escaped the Underdark, reached the surface, and is currently at Candlekeep resolving the murder of Keeper of Tomes Janussi. **This campaign diverged sharply from the published module** — the party never undertook a second Underdark expedition, never reached Gravenhollow/Menzoberranzan/Mantol-Derith, never met Vizeran, and never performed the demon-lord summoning ritual or fought the demon lords. Do not suggest those beats as active content.

**Current PCs:** Grygum (half-orc cleric of Bahamut), Daz (drow apostate evoker), Thorin (dwarf, wields Dawnbringer), Zalthir (dragonborn shadow monk, vow of silence). Companion: Glabbagool (sentient ooze, Zalthir's Shadow Apprentice sidekick).

## Completed Encounters & Quests

*(Most recent last.)*

- **Velkynvelve captivity & slave-pen intelligence** — Party captured by Ilvara Mizzrym's drow, gathered gear/knives and mapped the outpost. Lasting consequence: escape groundwork laid.
- **Jorlan's Gambit** — Jorlan Duskryn left the prison door unlocked and created a distraction out of spite toward Ilvara/Shoor. Executed successfully.
- **Guard-chamber revolt & armory raid (Velkynvelve)** — Prisoners killed Imbros and two guards; armed themselves. Prince Derendil killed by Imbros.

- **Flight/pursuit through Underdark tunnels** — Evaded Nym Duskryn's scouts via Grygum's Thaumaturgy; survived cave-ins and a giant centipede.

- **Yuk Yuk's murder resolved** — Buppido exposed as the killer; bound and gagged.
- **Kuo-toa ambush & Ploopploopeen alliance** — Defeated a kuo-toa raiding party; agreed to serve as false sacrifices to trap Bloppblippodd.

- **Whorlstone Tunnels campaign** — Killed Buppido at his shrine (recovered Pelek's ghost/bones); recovered the demon-corrupted dragon egg from Plinki; killed Narrak and stopped the two-headed-giant ritual; gathered cult evidence. Adopted the alias "the Ember Vanguard."
- **Dragon egg returned to Gartokkar** — Delivered the corrupted egg; secured safe passage; concealed the full derro conspiracy to avoid genocide.
- **Escape from Gracklstugh** — Fled by Themberchaud dragon-ride and boat, hunted as "the Ember Vanguard." Killed a Grey Ghost ambush.
- **Lost Tomb of Khaem** — Cleared; destroyed the wraith Brysis of Khaem; recovered Dawnbringer (sentient sunblade, now Thorin's), Necklace of Fireballs, and Netherese treasure.
- **Neverlight Grove** — Found the colony corrupted by Zuggtmoy via Sovereign Phylo; allied with Sovereign Basidia; cleared the Garden of Welcome (killed Yestabrod, mercy-killed drow scout Xinaya). Sarith Kzekarit died (spore servant). Witnessed Zuggtmoy's wedding rehearsal and fled. Stool & Rumpadump evacuated with Basidia's myconids.
- **Oozing Temple** — Escaped the flooding dungeon; recruited Glabbagool the gelatinous cube.
- **Reached Blingdenstone** — Admitted despite Daz (drow) and Glabbagool; tasked with the ooze infestation before surface help.
- **Pelek laid to rest & Jadger's tasks** — Interred Pelek's bones; laid Udhask's and Vazuk's spirits to rest; established info-for-ghosts deal with Jadger.
- **Trader's Grotto cube incursions** — Defeated gelatinous-cube attacks.
- **Blingdenstone coalition assembled** — Brokered truce with Chipgrin Goldwhisker's wererats; unified factions under Daz's command.
- **Cleanse the Steadfast Stone / Ogrémoch's Bane** — Hallowed the temple; killed the medusa Neheedra Duskryn; freed the Galeb Duhr.

- **Blingdenstone post-war settlement** — Pacified the ooze horde; unified all factions; city reopening for surface trade. Jimjar revealed as a celestial/divine being and vanished from existence.
- **Fungal Cavern — final confrontation with Ilvara Mizzrym** — Several days from Blingdenstone (NOT Velkynvelve). Destroyed the Heart Fungus; killed the Zuggtmoy-corrupted Ilvara; defeated a House T'sarran extraction team (one survivor fled); killed Asha Vandree; recovered Daz's belongings and evidence of Ilvara's corruption. Drow pursuit definitively ended.
- **Return to the surface** — Emerged from the Underdark; Eldeth departed to Mithral Hall (oath to advocate Thorin's clan adoption); captured a House T'sarran surface spy (shrunk, held in bag of holding).
- **Surface journey to Candlekeep** — Passed through Daggerford (Elin the mute child, Tarvis Ulain monument, Milo Goodbarrel/Order of the Gauntlet links) and other stops; admitted to Candlekeep by donating five books; PCs paired with scholar-handlers.
- **Candlekeep — Janussi murder investigation** — Conscripted by Bookwyrm; solved via forensics and interrogation. Janussi poisoned with midnight tears (poisoned book pages), heart removed post-mortem and hidden in a Milil chalice. Alkrist confessed (poisoner) under zone of truth; A'lai Aivenmore identified as mastermind. Bookwyrm exposed as covering for her nephew Alkrist.

- **Bookwyrm's murder & Moziqodo** — Bookwyrm killed by Moziqodo (Sylvira's abyss-born pit-fiend son). Party killed Moziqodo in the rotunda, saved Tadric, recovered the genuine second High Tower key. Deputized as Watchers of Candlekeep.
