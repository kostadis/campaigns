## Completed Encounters & Quests

**Candlekeep murder investigation (Janussi's murder) — Chapter 57, resolved.** The party solved the murder of Janussi. Through split investigation (Grygum in Janussi's chambers, Daz interviewing Sylvira Savikas, Zalthir/Thorin interrogating Alkrist under a zone of truth cast by Kalan Strongbranch), they extracted a full confession and identified all conspirators. Outcome: success. All named suspects proved guilty of some involvement.

**Alkrist interrogation — Chapter 57, confession obtained.** Interrogated in Janussi's chambers under Kalan Strongbranch's zone of truth (Alkrist unaware). Alkrist fully confessed: he coated Daral Yashenti's gift book (1001 Tashalian Nights method — midnight tears) with poison, re-wrapped it, stole the vial from the Oak Tree Apothecary, and burned the vial's label on the Deadwinter tree (washing his hands in Irony/Orrin Glass's supper basin). Named A'lai Aivenmore as the instigator (baths meeting, 11:45 PM). Motive: Janussi's performance review dismissed him and ordered his germ-warfare research stopped.

**Sylvira Savikas interview — Chapter 57.** Daz interviewed her in her fever-ridden quarters. She was framed via a polymorph/disguise of her likeness. Established a self-reported alibi (visiting her son, returned ~12:30 AM). She named Kazryn Nyantani and Teles Ahvoste as those with grudges against Janussi.

**Grygum's evidence reassembly — Chapter 57.** Grygum used Mending on crumpled wrapping paper from the dining-hall trash, revealing a deliberate straight cut distinct from ordinary tears — proof the poisoned gift-wrapped book was a premeditated act. He also found a note in Janussi's own hand on the third floor showing Janussi had blocked Kalan Strongbranch's investigative-methodology proposal as "solipsism."

## Resolved Plot Threads

- **Who killed Janussi:** A conspiracy, not a single killer. A'lai Aivenmore set it in motion; Alkrist executed the poisoning of the book; multiple others (at least three) were implicated separately. Janussi had, in one day, given Bookwyrm, Alkrist, and A'lai Aivenmore separate reasons to want him dead.
- **The heart removal / chalice:** Established as a separate act from the poisoning. Alkrist had no knowledge of the heart being ripped out; Bookwyrm is implicated in the post-mortem cover-up/mutilation.
- **Two-Sylvira / disguise timeline:** Confirmed the sightings could not be Sylvira (kitchen "honey" figure vs. her son-visit alibi); more than a simple one-hour polymorph was used (kitchen sighting ~1 AM, stairs sighting 2–3 AM — a two-hour gap). Anyone of standing at Candlekeep could access such a spell via the Avowed.

## NPC State Changes

- **Alkrist** — Confessed under zone of truth to poisoning the book; guilty. Currently seated and compliant in Janussi's chambers. Dragonborn, bronze-scaled. Nephew of Bookwyrm.
- **A'lai Aivenmore** — Identified as the instigator of the plot; drow. Now a candidate for blackmail by Daz, who suspects A'lai may have information on Daz's unknown Menzoberranzan-rate patron.
- **Bookwyrm (First Reader)** — Alkrist's aunt; implicated in the cover-up and the ripping-out of Janussi's heart. She had argued with Alkrist over the poisoning (appalled), then stormed off. Also opposed Kalan Strongbranch's investigative reforms.
- **Daral Yashenti** — Alive but poisoned (licked the reclaimed poisoned book); awaiting Grygum's Lesser Restoration, which Grygum has deliberately withheld pending cooperation. Dismissed as a suspect (motive present, execution capability lacking).
- **Sylvira Savikas** — Framed for the murder; bedridden with an abyssal plague/fever caught from her son. Revealed she has a son born in the Abyss, housed in a Mordenkainen's Mansion she built. Grieving Janussi (her lover). Cleared via her son-visit alibi.
- **Janussi** — Dead. Poisoned via the gift book; heart removed post-mortem. On his last day he had fired/dismissed Alkrist, blocked Kalan Strongbranch's proposal, and argued with Sylvira.
- **Kalan Strongbranch** — The scholar Janussi fired; his blocked investigative methodology ("Threefold Proof") was the framework the party used. Cast the zone of truth for the Alkrist interrogation. Party intends to hand him the collected evidence to "clean house."
- **Fembris Lancer** — Attended Grygum's colloquium; dispatched to hunt down Kalan Strongbranch. Angling to co-author with Grygum.
