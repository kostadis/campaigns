# Moziqodo

## Identity
- Abyssal pit fiend; revealed by Tadric to be **Sylvira Savikas's son**.
- The "beast" referenced in Bookwyrm's dying note, and the tool of whoever is "using the beast."
- First encountered by the party in the domed rotunda / North Gallery, mid-kill over a grappled Tadric.

## Personality & Motivations
- Singularly goal-driven: kill Tadric, seize the second key, disengage, and fly into the dark before anyone could cross the floor.
- So absorbed in dismembering its prey that it never registered the party's approach — methodical, predatory, tunnel-visioned to the point of vulnerability.
- Demonstrated (against Thorin's earlier skepticism) that a pit fiend *can* be frightened, and could also be stunned. Immune to fire.

## History with the Party
- Beat the party to the North Gallery, grappled Tadric flat to the floor, and was methodically clawing him open when the party arrived.
- The party interrupted before it could execute any part of its plan and took its entire turn away:
  - Thorin engaged first with Dawnbringer (radiant, not fire), landing three strikes and **frightening** it.
  - Zalthir crossed forty-five feet, struck, and **stunned** it with a precise monk strike ("Even better than frightened").
  - Grygum's saved lightning javelin missed.
  - Daz finished with three darts of plain force (declining fire, to which the fiend is immune).
  - Thorin delivered the killing blows.
- Went down without ever landing an attack.

## Current Status
- **Deceased** on the rotunda floor.
- Never took the second key or executed its escape plan.

## Relationships
- **Sylvira Savikas** — her son (per Tadric's revelation).
- **Tadric** — his intended victim; source of the identity reveal.
- **Bookwyrm** — the "beast" of Bookwyrm's dying note.
- Unnamed party controlling it — the one "using the beast."
- **The party** — killed it before it could act; Thorin (frightened + killing blows), Zalthir (stunned it), Grygum (missed), Daz (force damage).

## Arc Score Events
- None recorded in source notes.
