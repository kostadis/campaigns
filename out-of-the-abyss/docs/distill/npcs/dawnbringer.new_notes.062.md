# New notes for Dawnbringer (from dossier_extract_062.md)

Dawnbringer, the sentient sword carried by Thorin, spoke in his head throughout, insisting "I'm not leaving here without the therapy. That first session was actually very productive," and asking for more sessions with a sword-whisperer — the very thing Thorin has been hunting for her since the road south. She made a click "like a palm slapping stone" inside Thorin's skull. Her gender was briefly debated (Thorin: "I thought Dawnbringer was a she… I'm cool either way"; settled on "she"). She performed the cure of Daral Yashenti, lighting up and drawing the sickness out of him, then basked in Daral's lengthy praise of sentient swords — Grygum watched "the whole demeanor of the thing open up." In the helmed horror combat she was Thorin's weapon: her "sap" ability slowed a horror's movements after he struck it.
