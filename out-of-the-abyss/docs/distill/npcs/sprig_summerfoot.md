# Sprig Summerfoot

## Identity
- Junior avowed; voluntary poison taster.
- No first-appearance details or party-meeting circumstances given in the notes.

## Personality & Motivations
Diligent and proactive: she insisted on tasting all twelve supper dishes even after the chefs told her it wasn't necessary — initiative that Daral praised as a best practice. Comes across as conscientious and eager to prove herself in her junior role.

## History with the Party
- Investigated in connection with the supper poisoning plot. Thorin reasoned that if Janussi were the sole target, Sprig would have been unwitnessed collateral — so whoever spared the food (opting to use the book instead) may have been acting to protect her.
- Provided testimony to the party: confirmed that the gifts under the tree were all books, wands, or magical artifacts.
- Independently corroborated the 1am Sylvira/honey encounter.

## Current Status
- No last-known location or active operations recorded in the notes.
- What the party knows: her role as poison taster, her corroboration of the gift contents and the 1am Sylvira/honey encounter, and the theory that someone may have acted to protect her.
- What remains hidden: whether she was in fact a protected party, and who (if anyone) sought to spare her.

## Relationships
- **Daral** — praised her poison-tasting initiative as best practice.
- **Janussi** — possible co-target/collateral consideration in the poisoning theory.
- **Orrin** — his testimony reframed the "too interested" gossip as being about Irony, not Sprig, effectively clearing her of that suspicion.
- **Party (Thorin)** — Thorin advanced the theory that Sprig may have been someone's protected party in the poisoning plot.
