# New notes for Kalan Strongbranch (from dossier_extract_059.md)

The gate warden and holder of one of the two High Tower keys (Janussi held the other). Officially barred from the case by Bookwyrm, Alkrist having demanded his dismissal because the murder happened on his watch. Kalan stepped out of an unnoticed doorway, checked the corridor both ways, and pressed a small brass key specifically into Grygum's palm — choosing the orc cleric deliberately — warning "whatever Bookwyrm is telling you, listen for what she isn't" and that if he wasn't seen tomorrow, not to look for him at his post, since "it is best that if I am killed, the key not be found." He explained the High Tower is the entrance to all the super-magical artifacts every evil entity would covet, and that it needs both keys turned at once (Thorin's "war-game swapper" / two-officer missile-launch metaphor). He argued the tower could not have been secretly compromised because it is always watched regardless of the key, which Thorin took as clearing him. Zalthir followed him for a private conversation: Kalan probed the party's motives, decided fate had brought disinterested outsiders here for a reason, and shared his own findings — he'd noticed the poison in the left hand, the saliva, the heart cut out after death to spoil resurrection, and deduced the murderer was a magic user who could open any lock, arcane or mundane. Zalthir corrected his hasty assumption that the safe-rifler and the murderer were the same person (given the locket-key and multiple visitors), and Kalan conceded "perhaps it was two different people." Kalan suspected one of the Great Readers was the murderer and that Bookwyrm was either protecting them or manipulated into firing him; he believed the party was hired to bungle the case or be silent. Zalthir persuaded him to collaborate "unofficially," proposing a five-way meeting to merge findings. Kalan confirmed he could question the dead and detect lies ("of course I could"). He gave his timeline: the eight o'clock meeting where Janussi refused more watchers, a quarter-past-ten visit to Sylvira on a cosmological question, research in the Chamber of Lost Lore, then the morning inspection just after eight, the cordon, the posted watcher, and the doubled guard at the High Tower Library. He went pale when Zalthir revealed someone had impersonated Sylvira the night of the murder — "No. That is new information… You are very good at this" — and warned that whoever named him to the wrong people would be disappointed. Zalthir warned him to watch his back and beware shapeshifters. Kalan departed to his research; the party flagged him and his second key as a key thread. Zalthir noted the locket Kalan produced to demonstrate carry-method was empty and proved nothing — the key he held could be Janussi's, handed off by a man building a clean story.
