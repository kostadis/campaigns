# Daral Yashenti

## Identity
- Candlekeep scholar; an "avowed" of the keep. Longtime rival of Janussi (the murdered keeper of the tomes).
- Never appeared in person at first — introduced as the party's emerging prime suspect, named by Fembris Lancer as Janussi's years-long rival. Party first met him in person when Daz and Thorin interviewed him at the Hearth.

## Personality & Motivations
- Driven by resentment of Janussi and a scholar's contempt for what he saw as Janussi's philistinism — especially Janussi's plan to sell "priceless poetry" to fund a tome on demonic planes for Sylvira ("his horned tart"). Passionate about poetry and fiction; later channels this into grandiose research ambitions.
- A heavy drinker, boisterous, and flush with bravado when drunk — freely insulting colleagues and casting suspicion on everyone. Beneath the swagger he is anxious and self-preserving; under pressure his bragging collapses into desperate flattery and pleading. Prone to loud confrontation but read by Thorin as genuinely terrified rather than conniving.

## History with the Party
1. **Named as prime suspect (in absentia).** Fembris Lancer flagged him as Janussi's rival who publicly called the party "sellswords." Hollypocket and Queenie placed him at Janussi's door twice on the fatal day: an ~11th-bell visit (drunk, shouting that Janussi was "not fit to be the candle keep's keeper" / "a glorified book peddler"), and a return at 10:30pm — pounding, arguing, then fleeing down the stairs faster than Hollypocket had ever seen. Queenie ("the stinky breath one") reported he left carrying a "hypnotic block," deduced to be the missing book *The Golden Ass*, taken while Janussi was still alive. Thorin treated the long grudge as a vector to guilt.
2. **The Hearth interview (Daz & Thorin).** Daral refused to come to them (Daz: a suspect picking his own ground). Found drunk and boisterous, greeting them as "Faerûn's most famous detectives! Bookwyrm's brownnosers," buying spiced brandies. Admitted the 11am clash (calling Janussi a "philistine," "uneducated bookseller"), blessed "whoever bumped him off," and admitted the evening visit — reclaiming the book he'd gifted and catching Janussi "lightly on the nose" after Janussi "sat upon him." Throughout, he wet his finger and flicked through the book's pages, laughing — which Daz watched as the suspected poison-delivery mechanism. Named suspects freely: Teles ("deluded old git"), A'lai ("preying cock"), Fheminor ("steely nature"), Bookwyrm ("cold-blooded, they eat their young" — prompting Thorin's "some of my good friends are lizards"). When told Janussi was poisoned via a licked, page-turned book, Daral threw the book down and blurted "you've got to do something!" Party took the book into evidence and resolved to keep Daral close until midnight — reasoning his survival would prove innocence ("a man does not lace the pages he's been licking all night").
3. **Shift to ally / framed victim.** Daral glued himself to Daz and Thorin, concluding they were the only people in Candlekeep who didn't want him dead. He worked out someone wanted him to take the fall. Revealed the book's timeline: dropped under the evergreen tree with the other gifts ~11am Deadwinter's Eve (wrapped, never personally handed over), before rushing to his Keeper's Tower meeting; picked up by Janussi at dinner. Baffled by the idea of a magical seal ("Why would I put a magical seal on the wrapping? It's a gift!"); promised warded gift-wrappings as future best practice if he survived. Anxious that no one tell Zalthir about his drunken anti-dragonborn barroom talk. Pointed the party to the Oak Tree Apothecary and the satyr druid Leuwin; in the Southern Dining Hall recognized the lead-lined Milil chalice (Midsummer Wine), leading to discovery of Janussi's heart. Named A'lai Aivenmore as having motive to both kill Janussi and frame Daral. Thorin crossed him off the suspect list. Last seen accompanying Thorin to the kitchen to interview staff.
4. **The reunion — dying.** Appeared alive "technically" but poisoned by the same book, having licked finger–book–finger while reclaiming it, dosing himself with midnight tears. Poured out excessive flattery (Thorin a "fantastic investigator," Dawnbringer "stunning") — read by Grygum as a dying man spending everything left. Grygum held the Lesser Restoration deliberately, the party wary he'd vanish once healed. Alkrist's confession confirmed Daral's own gift had been unwrapped, coated in midnight tears, and re-wrapped as the murder weapon.
5. **The cure.** Daral begged repeatedly for "the curing of me," asking Dawnbringer directly. Thorin argued he had nothing to do with the murder and nothing left to tell — let him go, don't extort him. Grygum held back to let the room reach mercy on its own; ultimately Dawnbringer cured him (the blade poured light in and took the sickness). Healed and ecstatic, Daral launched into a five-minute rhapsody proposing a new field of research into sentient magical swords — their thematics and semiotics — using Dawnbringer as subject (Dawnbringer visibly drank it in).
6. **Helmed horror / animated armor ambush.** Came apart, insisting "it wasn't possible, these creatures can't be here"; found the door locked and could not leave. After the attack, pounded on the already-smashed-open door until he realized it was open, then turned to the party: "What do we do next, gentlemen?"
7. **Investigation aid (post-ambush).** Confirmed the two High Tower keys are "completely magical." Cast Detect Magic from a scroll — it lit up Dawnbringer, Grygum's armor, and himself, but not the key: "This is not the key," confirming the party held a decoy. Helped bring the party to Bookwyrm's ruined office; could not identify "the beast" from her dying note. Explained Candlekeep's defenses: a great shield preventing teleportation in/out, and that fire does not work in the keep (a "burning bush" effect), the fire-suppressing ward living in the single room locked by the two High Tower keys. Found a teleport-protection failure inconceivable but was pressed by Grygum to check. Named the Sea Warden's Tower as Kalan's likely location.

## Current Status
- Last seen assisting the party's investigation after the animated-armor attack, having been cured of the poison.
- No active plans of his own noted, beyond his newly declared ambition to research sentient magical swords using Dawnbringer. Continues to serve as an expert guide to Candlekeep's magical defenses.
- **Party knows:** He gave Janussi the (later poisoned) gift book, was framed, and is cleared of the murder; his account of the book's timeline; his knowledge of Candlekeep's wards and keys.
- **Hidden / open:** Identity of "the beast" from Bookwyrm's note; whether the teleport ward has actually failed; who unwrapped, poisoned, and re-wrapped his gift (revealed as Alkrist's doing).

## Relationships
- **Janussi (deceased):** Years-long rival; publicly and privately contemptuous ("philistine," "glorified book peddler"). Gave him the gift book that became the murder weapon; admitted striking him on the nose.
- **A'lai Aivenmore:** Named by Daral as the man with motive to kill Janussi and frame Daral, coveting the keep and threatened by Daral's love of poetry/fiction.
- **Sylvira:** Disparaged as Janussi's "horned tart"; Sylvira in turn named Daral a possible but unlikely suspect ("a drunk who is bitter... too much of a drunk to manage the planning").
- **Alkrist:** Whose confession revealed the true tampering of Daral's gift.
- **Other Candlekeep figures:** Freely insulted Teles, Fheminor, and Bookwyrm during interview. Fembris Lancer named him as suspect.
- **Party:**
  - *Daz & Thorin:* His chosen protectors ("the only people who didn't want him dead"). Thorin cleared him and advocated mercy.
  - *Grygum:* Held the cure over him, wary he'd flee once healed.
  - *Dawnbringer:* Cured him; now the flattered subject of his sword-research obsession.
  - *Zalthir (dragonborn):* Daral anxious to conceal his drunken anti-dragonborn remarks.
