# Archibald Roots Junior

## Identity
- Gnome botanist; author of *Fey-mous Flowers & Fungi*. Son of the "legendary gnome botanist" Archibald Roots Sr.
- Not encountered in person. Known to the party only through his book, which Grygum found in the Flora Theca.

## Personality & Motivations
No direct interaction — personality not demonstrated in play. His writing focuses on documenting his father's explorations and botanical collections, suggesting a scholarly, cataloguing sensibility and reverence for his father's legacy.

## History with the Party
- Grygum discovered *Fey-mous Flowers & Fungi* in the Flora Theca. The book's early chapters recount Archibald Sr.'s explorations, but its critical value was the entry on **midnight tears**.
- That entry enabled Grygum to solve a murder: it described midnight tears as a highly toxic pale ochre dust from Feywild pollen — lethal in food or liquid, with no effect until the stroke of midnight, and neutralizable into a pain-relief solution when mixed with the sap of a certain Feywild tree.
- The entry let Grygum identify the poison, gauge its rarity (narrowing the suspect list), and recognize the black-finger/black-lip saliva-reaction mechanism that made the licked book the murder weapon.

## Current Status
- Whereabouts and current activity unknown — has not appeared as a present character.
- **Party knows:** he authored the book and is Archibald Roots Sr.'s son.
- **Hidden/unknown:** his location, whether he is alive, his motivations, and any direct connection to the events of the campaign.

## Relationships
- **Archibald Roots Sr.** — father; a "legendary gnome botanist" whose explorations and collections form the subject of the book's early chapters.
- **Grygum (party)** — indirect; his book was Grygum's key resource in identifying the midnight tears poison and solving the murder.
