# Fheminor Scrivenbark

## Identity
- Great Reader; listed among Zalthir's eight suspects in the death of Janussi and named as one of those who wanted the top (head) job.
- **Description discrepancy in source notes:** referred to both as "a dowdy halfling Great Reader" (she/her) and as "the gnome who had wanted the head job." Gender and race are inconsistent across notes — resolve before play.
- First placed at the scene by Queenie, who saw Fheminor arrive as the first guest of the evening after supper, identified by gait as "the short-legged one" (arriving before Daral). Has not yet appeared directly to the party.

## Personality & Motivations
- Ambitious: wanted Janussi's head job.
- Consistently described as unpleasant — Nibbles the apothecary recalled her as unpleasant and "always unpleasant."
- Daral warned the party: "don't be fooled by her stature, she has a steely nature." A minor figure who "otherwise left no mark."

## History with the Party
- **Council over Janussi's body:** At the shouting council, Fheminor asked whether Janussi had named a successor.
- **Party notes flag:** Grygum noted the party's notes flagged Fheminor as "somebody who seems to have the same priorities that we do" — a potential ally to interview for tips. Oddly, the notes entry had "no name on it," only her name.
- **Interview decision:** Daz chose to interview Fheminor, reasoning that witnesses with the least time often hold the most insight.
- **Zalthir's briefing to Daz** — specific questions to put to her:
  1. Establish *when* Fheminor was beaten up — beaten at eight tells a different story than beaten when Janussi died ("he was beat up before he was dead").
  2. Whether she noticed anything missing.
  3. Whether she might have poisoned Janussi during a visit.
- **Apothecary sighting:** Visited the apothecary at 2:30 for cold medicine (horehound tincture and licorice). Nibbles noted she came and went *before* the theft.
- **Reputation reference:** Teles scoffed at Alkrist's project as being as likely to succeed as "some Fheminor Scrivenbark program" — a remark Irony didn't understand.

## Current Status
- Last concrete placement: at the apothecary at 2:30 (cold medicine), and earlier at the scene as the first after-supper guest.
- No active operations known beyond her stated ambition for the head job.
- **Party knows:** she wanted the top job, was present the evening in question, was at the apothecary before the theft, and is a flagged suspect/potential-ally.
- **Hidden / unresolved:** timing and cause of her being "beaten up," whether anything went missing, whether she poisoned Janussi, the meaning of the unnamed notes entry, and her true race (halfling vs. gnome discrepancy).

## Relationships
- **Janussi:** rival for the head job; subject of the death investigation Fheminor is a suspect in.
- **Daral:** knows Fheminor well enough to warn the party of her "steely nature"; both listed among those who wanted the top job. Fheminor arrived at the scene before Daral.
- **The Party:** flagged by their own notes as a like-minded potential ally; targeted for interview by **Daz**; questions supplied by **Zalthir**; noted by **Grygum**.
- **Nibbles (apothecary):** served her cold medicine; regards her as consistently unpleasant.
- **Queenie:** witness who placed Fheminor at the scene.
- **Teles / Alkrist / Irony:** invoked her name dismissively in reference to Alkrist's doomed project.
