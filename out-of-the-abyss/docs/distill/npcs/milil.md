# Milil

## Identity
- The god of bards, present in the keep as a **statue** in an alcove of the Southern Dining Hall — depicted with open hands holding a brass chalice as an offering.
- First encountered by the party in the Southern Dining Hall.

## Personality & Motivations
- Not an active NPC — appears only as a statue. No demonstrated personality or motivations in the source notes.

## History with the Party
- The party examined the statue's brass chalice (used for the Midsummer Wine). Daral recognized that the chalice was **lead-lined** — lead blinds far-seeing magic. Thorin retrieved the cup and found **Janussi's heart** and a **blood-stained meat cleaver** hidden inside; the lead lining had concealed both items from every scrying working in the keep.

## Current Status
- Statue remains in the alcove of the Southern Dining Hall.
- **What the party knows:** The chalice's lead lining hides its contents from scrying, and it concealed Janussi's heart and the murder weapon (a meat cleaver).
- **Hidden / unresolved:** Who placed the heart and cleaver in the chalice, and why, is not established in these notes.

## Relationships
- None established in the source notes beyond the statue's role as a hiding place tied to Janussi's death.
