# Kelemvor

## Identity
- Deity (god of death); referenced through his followers/faith rather than in person.
- Has not appeared at the table. Known to the party only via discussion of a summoning mechanic and the pronouncements of his followers.

## Personality & Motivations
- Communicates through "cryptic, incomprehensible phrasing" (his usual mode, per his followers).
- No direct goals or drives established in the notes.

## History with the Party
- Kelemvor has not appeared directly. His faith entered play as the theological basis for a summoning mechanic: the followers of Kelemvor have repeatedly asserted that "an undead possesses things while a dead man possesses nothing." This underlies why killing Strongbranch would free his key for summoning.

## Current Status
- Not present. No location or active operation established.
- **What the party knows:** the doctrinal point tying death, possession, and the freeing of Strongbranch's key for summoning.
- **What remains hidden:** everything else — Kelemvor's direct intentions, any personal stake in these events.

## Relationships
- **Followers of Kelemvor:** convey his doctrine to the party through cryptic phrasing.
- **Strongbranch:** relevant via the mechanic — killing him (rendering him a "dead man" who possesses nothing) frees his key for summoning.

## Arc Score Events
- None recorded in the source notes.
