# Sylvira Savikas

## Identity
- Tiefling (technically abyssal spawn); Curator of the Infernal Fortress. Grygum re-filed Hollypocket's "devil woman" epithet as her actual job function.
- Never appears in person during the early investigation; emerges as the prime suspect in Janussi's murder through multiple witness accounts. First interviewed directly by Daz in her quarters.
- Physical: copper-red skin, blue-black hair, magnetic golden eyes.

## Personality & Motivations
Grieving, proud, and sharp-tongued even while bedridden. She reacted with genuine offense to trivial accusations ("I would never drink honey") yet expressed real delight and relief that the investigation into Janussi's death was proceeding. Fiercely protective of her Abyss-born son, whom she shelters in a self-built Mordenkainen's Magnificent Mansion. Guarded and suspicious — briefly assumed Daz's revelations were a drow mind-trick before accepting the truth.

## History with the Party
- **Chapters 1–3 (in absentia):** Named repeatedly as the leading suspect. Witnesses reported: Queenie awoken by Sylvira blasting the armchair with a force spell (a lover's quarrel — matching the force-dart holes found); Hollypocket witnessing a violent morning confrontation where Sylvira screamed Janussi was a "traitor" and stormed out; Hollypocket also seeing Sylvira's face glowing red by candlelight at 2–3 a.m., carrying a lantern a tiefling wouldn't need.
- Queenie noted "the torn demon lady" "didn't smell like the demon lady" — the party seized on this as evidence of a **shapeshifter wearing Sylvira's form** ("that explains the candle").
- She was absent from the Council of Great Readers; Daral pointedly asked "why isn't Sylvira Savikas here?" Bookwyrm seemed to steer the party toward her without saying so.
- Zalthir listed her as the obvious suspect everyone pointed at, and **deliberately skipped her chambers first**, reasoning an obvious name is rarely the answer.
- Irony and Sprig independently reported the impersonator: seen searching by candlelight ~1 a.m., pointing at her throat (too hoarse to speak), helped to find honey. Thorin concluded this was someone wearing her face — the lantern and honey being a cover story.
- Thorin assigned himself to interview her; **Daz ultimately conducted the interview** in her quarters.

## Current Status
- **Location:** Bedridden in her own quarters, attended by her familiar Jezebel.
- **Condition:** Claims "winter flu" — actually an **abyssal plague** contracted from her Abyss-born son. Still functional: directed Jezebel to arrange seating and cast mage hand despite fever (Daz filed her as "not incapacitated").
- **Alibi/timeline she gave:** 11:30 p.m. meeting with Janussi planning a Great Wheel library, ending in a private argument (insists she never called Janussi a "genus/genus traitor"); left the feast early as the flu set in; snuck out to visit her son, returned ~12:30 a.m., alone thereafter. The "honey" sighting is not her — she was being impersonated.
- **Known to party:** She was Janussi's lover, is being framed, and someone wore her likeness the night of the murder. She was shocked to learn of the framing.
- **Hidden/emerging:** Her son **Moziqodo**, Abyss-born, kept locked in a magical sanctuary. Kalan Strongbranch later blames Moziqodo for Bookwyrm's death, having warned "that creature would be the death of us."

## Relationships
- **Janussi:** On-and-off romantic partner and lover; they had a private argument the night of the murder. She grieves their last argument.
- **Moziqodo (son):** Abyss-born; she shelters him in a Mordenkainen's Mansion, either to protect him or to protect others from him. Source of her abyssal plague.
- **Jezebel:** Her familiar.
- **Suspects she named:** Daral Yashenti (dismissed as too drunk to plan it — he in turn disparaged her as Janussi's "horned tart"/"tart" and blamed her for pushing demonic-planes funding); Kazryn Nyantani (Janussi's bitter ex, "that bitter old hag"); Teles Ahvoste (passed over for Keeper of Tomes for 20 years — "jealousy is a powerful motivator").
- **Bookwyrm:** She noted Bookwyrm pushed hardest against Kalan's investigative proposal, calling it insane.
- **Kalan Strongbranch:** Visited her ~10:15 p.m. on a cosmological question, unaware of the impersonation until Zalthir told him; later blames her son for Bookwyrm's death.
- **Party:** Interviewed by Daz; briefly suspected him of a drow mind-trick. Zalthir and Thorin both flagged her as the "too-obvious" suspect.
