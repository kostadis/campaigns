# Orrin Glass

## Identity
- Third waiter in service during the Deadwinter gathering.
- Party encountered him among the serving staff; he surfaced as a witness during the investigation into the tampering of Janussi's Deadwinter gift.

## Personality & Motivations
- Deaf in one ear and regarded as a bit of an oddball, but a man who notices everything — a sharp, observant witness.
- Willing to share what he sees, though at least partly motivated by grievance: he offered up information on Teles Ahvoste specifically because Irony "threw him under the bus."

## History with the Party
- Provided the party several key observations during the investigation:
  - Reported that **Teles Ahvoste** had been making passes at **Irony** (volunteered because Irony had thrown him under the bus).
  - Saw **Alkrist** feed a scrap of paper to a candle and drop it when a junior avowed entered.
  - Most importantly, noticed that the string tied around **Janussi's** Deadwinter gift had clearly been cut and retied — confirming the tampering happened under the tree with everyone present.
- Did not appear directly in a later scene but was named in **Alkrist's confession**: Orrin arrived alongside Irony carrying the basin of water for supper — the water Alkrist used to wash the poison from his hands after burning the vial's label.

## Current Status
- Last active as a waiter during the Deadwinter events; no separate whereabouts noted.
- No active plans or operations attributed to him.
- The party knows him as a reliable, detail-focused witness whose observations helped establish that the gift was tampered with in plain sight and tie Alkrist to the poisoning.

## Relationships
- **Irony:** Antagonistic — Orrin volunteered information against Teles partly out of resentment for Irony throwing him under the bus. Arrived alongside Irony with the water basin.
- **Teles Ahvoste:** Reported Teles's advances toward Irony.
- **Alkrist:** Key witness against him — observed the paper-burning and the retied string; named in Alkrist's confession as delivering the water Alkrist used to wash off the poison.
- **Janussi:** Witness to the tampering of Janussi's gift.
