# Alkrist

## Identity
- Bronze dragonborn Great Reader (Avowed) of Candlekeep; newly appointed, so little known about him. Nephew of the Great Reader Bookwyrm.
- First encountered by the party during the investigation into Janussi's murder — present at the council over Janussi's body and at the supper of Great Readers.

## Personality & Motivations
- Driven by ambition and resentment: he pursued germ-warfare/poison research that Janussi dismissed as dangerous, and felt denied recognition of his leadership potential.
- Cheerful and self-absorbed to the point of obliviousness — the only upbeat one at the supper, pitching a book project (*Tales of War by the Generals That Waged Them*). He missed the edge of Thorin's ironic remark about remuneration, taking it as sincere.
- Performative and self-justifying: deployed rehearsed grief on entering Janussi's chambers, and even after full confession insisted "I didn't actually kill him. I just put some poison on a book."

## History with the Party
- **Council over Janussi's body:** Demanded gate warden Kalan Strongbranch be dismissed, arguing the murder happened on his watch.
- **The supper:** The lone cheerful Great Reader, pitching his war-strategy book. Thorin initially dismissed him as a suspect ("the cheerful one's not usually the killer").
- **Evidence mounts:** Orrin Glass saw him feeding a scrap of paper to a candle flame, dropping it when a junior Avowed entered. Zalthir searched his lavish chambers and found sealed glass jars of dead rats with blistered, pustuled skin (poison study), and a black leather copy of *1001 Tashalian Nights* bookmarked to a tale of a commander poisoned via a Midnight Tears–coated horn. A locked safe could not be opened at the time.
- **Interrogation and confession:** Entered Janussi's chambers with rehearsed grief, unaware Kalan's Zone of Truth was active. Came apart and confessed the full plot:
  - Aunt Bookwyrm was distraught over the succession.
  - Janussi had dismissed his research and denied him leadership.
  - The 11:45 PM baths meeting with A'lai concerned "dealing with Janussi."
  - Mechanics: stole the vial from the Oak Tree Apothecary, coated Daral's gift book with Midnight Tears and re-wrapped it, tore the vial's label and tied it to the Deadwinter tree as decoration, then burned the label when Irony and Orrin Glass arrived with a basin — washing his hands in it just in case.
  - Recounted a midnight visit to Bookwyrm with two bottles of fine wine, announcing the "Janussi problem" was solved; she was appalled, they argued, she stormed off.
  - Absurdly tried to claim he was under mind control while asserting everything he said was true.
  - Was genuinely confused to learn Janussi's heart had been ripped out — an act attributed to Bookwyrm.
- **Aftermath:** His safe was later opened, holding a potion of flying, a potion of superior healing, a purse of gold, and a red herring joke coin (a gold coin stamped with a herring, colored red) — nothing further implicating him.

## Current Status
- Held in the Candlekeep prisons.
- Fully confessed under Zone of Truth to poisoning Janussi; named Bookwyrm as the mastermind.
- Kalan considers him "finished" — the confession, cleaver, and blood damn him.
- Will face trial "after the naming, by the new head of the Avowed."
- **Party knows:** He poisoned the book, the full method, his motive, and that Bookwyrm was mastermind. **Hidden/attributed elsewhere:** The heart-extraction was not his doing (attributed to Bookwyrm).

## Relationships
- **Bookwyrm** (aunt, fellow Great Reader): Co-conspirator and named mastermind; distraught over the succession. Alkrist brought her the news the "problem was solved"; she was appalled and they argued. She has been covering for him.
- **Janussi** (victim): Dismissed Alkrist's germ-warfare research and denied his leadership ambitions — Alkrist's motive.
- **A'lai:** Met Alkrist at 11:45 PM in the baths to discuss "dealing with Janussi."
- **Kalan Strongbranch** (gate warden): Alkrist demanded his dismissal; Kalan's Zone of Truth secured the confession.
- **Orrin Glass / Irony:** Orrin witnessed the paper-burning; both arrived with a basin, prompting Alkrist to burn the label and wash his hands.
- **Party — Zalthir:** Searched his chambers, secretly read his bookmarked *1001 Tashalian Nights*, and noted his self-absorption; attributed the heart-extraction to Bookwyrm. **Thorin:** Initially dismissed him as a suspect; his ironic remark about remuneration went over Alkrist's head.
