# Irony

## Identity
- Junior avowed; served at the Deadwinter Eve supper as one of the serving staff (alongside Orrin Glass and Sprig).
- Compelled to always tell the truth. First surfaced as a witness the party interviewed regarding the supper.

## Personality & Motivations
- Bound by a compulsion to speak only truth, but *not* compelled to answer — she can withhold by simply staying silent or redirecting. Zalthir identified this as her tell.
- Discreet and self-protective: she steered the party toward Sprig rather than speak directly of Teles's advances toward the junior avowed.
- Hates the obvious joke about her name. Otherwise a cooperative, observant witness whom Thorin valued.

## History with the Party
- Interviewed by the party as a witness to the Deadwinter supper. She gave a detailed account:
  - Supper began at the 5PM bell with twelve shared dishes (beetroot soup, fried carp, smoked mackerel, fruit compotes, spiced wines and ales, honey, vodka, green tea), all passed communally, served by her, Orrin, and Sprig.
  - Everyone at table was tense except the cheerful Alkrist.
  - Around 1am she encountered Sylvira Savikas searching by candlelight and helped her find honey.
  - Hinted that an avowed was "too interested in the junior avowed."
- Zalthir noted her truth-but-not-answer tell after she deflected the Teles topic toward Sprig.
- Later named (not present) in Alkrist's confession: she and Orrin Glass brought a basin of water to the supper, in which Alkrist washed his hands while burning the poison-vial label — to remove any "midnight tears."

## Current Status
- Last known context: serving at the Deadwinter supper; no separate current location established.
- The party knows she is a reliable truth-teller and a key witness to the supper's timeline and the honey/Sylvira encounter.
- Hidden/unspoken by her: the specifics of Teles's advances toward the junior avowed (deflected to Sprig). The significance of the water basin — used by Alkrist to wash away "midnight tears" while destroying the poison-vial label — is unknown to her account and surfaced only via Alkrist's confession.

## Relationships
- **Orrin Glass & Sprig** — fellow servers at the supper. She redirected the party to Sprig on the Teles matter.
- **Sylvira Savikas** — assisted her searching by candlelight at ~1am (found honey).
- **Alkrist** — unknowingly provided (with Orrin) the basin Alkrist used to wash his hands while destroying the poison-vial label.
- **Teles** — the avowed implicated in unwanted interest toward the junior avowed; Irony would not speak of this directly.
- **Thorin (party)** — valued her as a witness.
- **Zalthir (party)** — identified her can't-lie-but-can-decline-to-answer tell.
