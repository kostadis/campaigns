# Kei Tigersteel

## Identity
- Lorekeeper of Candlekeep; priest of the Temple of Oghma; described by Bookwyrm as "the library's spiritual leader."
- First appeared in private conference with Bookwyrm behind a closed door when the party arrived. Introduced to the party by Bookwyrm.

## Personality & Motivations
- Concerned with religious/spiritual duties, notably the purification of the dead.
- Demonstrated deference to Bookwyrm's authority — dismissed easily and departed without objection when granted leave.
- Too little shown in play to characterize further.

## History with the Party
- The party arrived to find Kei already in closed-door conference with Bookwyrm. Daz wanted to eavesdrop but his page-reading tools couldn't work through a closed door.
- Bookwyrm dismissed him and granted him permission to take Janussi's body to the Shrine of Oghma for purification. Kei left with the body.

## Current Status
- Last known: departing with Janussi's body, headed to the Shrine of Oghma to perform purification.
- **What the party knows:** His identity, title, and that he has taken Janussi's body.
- **What remains hidden:** The content of his private conference with Bookwyrm (the party never overheard it).

## Relationships
- **Bookwyrm:** In private conference with him; clearly subordinate to Bookwyrm's authority.
- **Janussi:** Took custody of Janussi's body for purification rites.
- **Daz (party):** Attempted (and failed) to overhear Kei's closed-door meeting.

- None recorded in source notes.
