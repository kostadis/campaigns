# Fembris Lancer

## Identity
- Adjutant/assistant at the chapter house; the "avowed minder" formally assigned to keep the four armed strangers from helping themselves to the library's contents. Serves as the party's designated **runner** under Bookwyrm's authority.
- First met in the hall outside the murder chamber, presenting himself as an eager, hovering assistant who had decided the party were the people who could fix things.

## Personality & Motivations
- Driven by eagerness to be useful and to be included — volunteers facts freely, insists on process (returning to give Bookwyrm a summary), and attaches himself to the investigation. Vibrates with energy, "fills the air with urgent noise," and dispatches errands with near-monastic commitment. Craves intellectual recognition (proposes co-authoring a monograph with Grygum) and social belonging (pointedly asks where the rest of the party was during the colloquium). Trusting to a fault — ate untrusted food eagerly and without suspicion.

## History with the Party
- **The murder chamber:** Met the party outside; when asked about enemies/rivals, immediately named **Daral Yashenti** ("at each other's throats for years"), noting Daral called the party "sellswords." Prompted by the missing locket, revealed Janussi held one of two keys to the Hightower Library, the other held by gate warden Kalan.
- **The investigation:** Insisted the party return to report to Bookwyrm, who designated him their runner. Escorted Grygum to research; clarified there was no poison section but a **Flora Theca**, and confirmed lab access if needed. Carried Daral Yashenti's refusal to come to Deneir's Sanctum (redirecting the party to the Hearth). Thorin noted Bookwyrm's slip — "catch… fetch any suspects" — raising suspicion Fembris's helpfulness was a leash.
- **The library:** As avowed minder, asked to leave to attend Grygum's debate in the Oval Theater; Daz let him go, silently noting this handed the party an unsupervised hour.
- **The reunion:** Effusive over Grygum's colloquium; tried to deposit theory notes with "secretary" Glabbagool (who declared them "not food"). Negotiated a proposed monograph down to a promised draft by tomorrow. Asked pointedly about the party's missing members, got no answer, then took on hunting down Kalan Strongbranch for the Alkrist truth-spell interview.
- **Deneir's Sanctum:** Stood silently while the party argued (Thorin respected this), then announced Bookwyrm wished to speak with them. Later served a punishment by keeping the party company under guard — "his penance was us." Used as an unwitting food-taster (Daz rated him "118"; Daral called it "a new best practice"); he did not die. Stood in shock during the helmed horror ambush.
- **Sea Warden's Tower:** Helped bring the party to Bookwyrm's office. Under Thorin's firm questioning, admitted that when he reported the key to Bookwyrm, **A'lai Aivenmore had been in the room** — redirecting suspicion onto A'lai. Kalan counted this as a "pillar of the thrice-fold proof."

## Current Status
- Last active at the Sea Warden's Tower, having given the confession that placed A'lai Aivenmore in the room. **Cleared as an active suspect** by chapter's end.
- Standing errand outstanding: dispatched to find/return with Kalan Strongbranch for the Alkrist interview; promised Grygum a monograph draft "by tomorrow."
- **Party knows:** his role, his access, and that he witnessed the key pass and reported it. **Hidden/uncertain:** whether his helpfulness is genuine or a "leash" for Bookwyrm.

## Relationships
- **Bookwyrm:** His superior; assigned him as the party's runner/minder. Zalthir/Thorin suspect he functions as her leash on the party.
- **Daral Yashenti:** Longstanding mutual rivalry ("at each other's throats for years").
- **A'lai Aivenmore:** Named her as present when he reported the key, redirecting suspicion onto her.
- **Kalan Strongbranch:** Tasked with fetching Kalan; Kalan predicts Fembris will "probably get elected the new avowed" and would remove Bookwyrm as her first act.
- **Grygum:** Admirer of his theology; seeks collaboration/co-authorship. **Daz:** let him leave the library, tracked the unsupervised window; rated him "118" as food-taster. **Thorin:** questioned him, respected his silence. **Zalthir:** admired his frictionless dispatch of errands; deduced he witnessed the key handoff.
