# New notes for Bookwyrm (First Reader) (from dossier_extract_059.md)

Bookwyrm received the party at the chapter house, where they found her already in private conference with Kei Tigersteel behind a closed door. She waved off the moon-elf scribes to usher the party in herself, "all warmth," and introduced Kei, then dismissed him to take Janussi's body to the Shrine of Oghma for purification — leaving herself alone with the investigators, which Daz noted. She pressed them repeatedly for their conclusions and suspects ("Is there any suspects in particular?" — a half-step too fast), and asked pointedly "Was there nobody that you wished to start your investigation with?" The party read this as a question wanting a name back to measure where they were pointed; they gave her only Ms. Hollypocket as a harmless answer. Daz and Zalthir concluded she was steering them toward Sylvira Savikas without ever saying the name — an unstated but strong preference, ambiguous between wanting the case solved and putting a thumb on the scale away from herself. She offered Deneir's Sanctum as their office and Vooshadi Moonriver as their doorkeeper, and warned they should not move through Candlekeep without an escort — generosity the party read as "rarely free" and possibly a bugged room. Thorin wanted her whereabouts before nine that morning to keep her on the suspect list. Kalan Strongbranch suspects Bookwyrm is either protecting one of the Great Readers or was manipulated into firing him to shield the real killer. The party filed her as "not above suspicion" while acknowledging accusing her would require hard facts. She had officially barred Kalan from the case. Last seen at the chapter house after the Council of Great Readers dispersed.
