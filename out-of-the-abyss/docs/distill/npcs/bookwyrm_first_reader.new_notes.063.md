# New notes for Bookwyrm (First Reader) (from dossier_extract_063.md)

Killed during this chapter — the alarm bell that tolled "wrong" (a flat institutional pattern) announced her death. The party had previously proven her guilty (of Janussi's murder / taking his heart and key) but left her unaccused on Kalan's patient schedule, awaiting the completed thrice-fold proof; Daz had been treating her as "an asset to collect from later." An eighteenth-level magic user whose high-level slots ran to scholarly magic (Daz imagines Validate Citation / Identify False Citation rather than combat spells). Her killer struck her at rest in her own tower, exploiting foreknowledge of how relaxed and confident she'd be — information Daz judged had to have been sold to someone. Her **throat was torn, not cut** (marking an animal, not surgical, hand — distinct from Janussi's murder), and her **heart was left present and intact**, unlike Janussi's. Her office was left a ruin. On her desk, in her own hand, was a note stopped mid-sentence: "*He is using the beast to—*". Bookwyrm had been first into Janussi's chamber and had taken Janussi's heart and key; her own key was torn from her by her killer. Deceased.
