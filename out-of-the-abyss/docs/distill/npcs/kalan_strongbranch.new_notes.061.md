# New notes for Kalan Strongbranch (from dossier_extract_061.md)

Kalan Strongbranch, the First Reader, is the author of the blocked proposal for new criminal-investigation methodologies (the Threefold Proof / Threefold framework) that the party had been handed as their operating toolkit. His truth-seeking spell (zone of truth) anchored the investigation and was needed for the Alkrist interview; Fembris went to hunt him down for that purpose, and Kalan stood just outside the door throughout Alkrist's confession, hearing everything. Grygum's discovery of Janussi's note revealed that Janussi had blocked Kalan's proposal as "solipsism" and a vanity project, handing Kalan a clear motive — Grygum noting this "points to Kalan pretty strongly" while acknowledging it doesn't make him a murderer, only a man who built his methodology in the wreckage of the one who told him he was wrong. Sylvira independently confirmed Kalan had begged her to intercede with Janussi to save his job (she agreed but never did), and dismissed his research as "third-rate." At the conclusion, the party resolves to hand Kalan — the wronged party with standing and motivation — the collected evidence to "clean house." Kalan poses the final question: what to do with the evidence collected.
