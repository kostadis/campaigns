# Queenie

## Identity
- Janussi's tower cat; a five-year-old sleet-grey housecat with strong opinions. Resident of Janussi's tower.
- First encountered when Daz interviewed her using beast-speech granted by the Stonespeaker crystal.

## Personality & Motivations
Imperious, fastidious, and transactional — demanded tickles as payment for information and warmed only once Daz found the right lever. Deeply contemptuous of two-leggers' hygiene, delivering several insults on the subject. Categorizes people and events by gait and smell rather than name or appearance. Regards the tower as *her* domain.

## History with the Party
- Daz used the Stonespeaker crystal's beast-speech to interview her about events in the tower.
- After being placated with tickles, she provided a timeline of the night's events (see Current Status / knowledge below).
- Dismissed the party at the interview's end: "You may now leave, and please give yourself a proper licking before returning to my tower."

## Current Status
- Last known location: Janussi's tower.
- No active plans of her own; she serves as a witness/information source.
- **What she reported (party now knows):**
  - Woken from her armchair nap around morning by Sylvira Savikas ("our Sylvira") blasting the chair with a lance of force — characterized as a lover's quarrel.
  - Woke from an afternoon nap around eight, after supper, and identified the evening's guests by gait/smell: first Fheminor ("the short-legged one"), later Daral ("the stinky breath one").
  - Saw Daral run downstairs carrying a "hypnotic block" — deduced to be the missing book *The Golden Ass* — while Janussi was still alive.
  - In the night, saw "the torn demon lady" (Sylvira's shape) descending the steps, but noted "she didn't smell like the demon lady" — suggesting a shapeshifter impersonating Sylvira.
- **What remains hidden/unresolved:** She could not identify the missing locket ("I don't know what it was"). The true identity of the shapeshifter wearing Sylvira's form is unconfirmed.

## Relationships
- **Janussi** — her owner; was still alive when Daral fled with the book.
- **Sylvira Savikas** — "our Sylvira"; Queenie witnessed her force-blast the armchair (lover's quarrel). Note: the nighttime figure in Sylvira's shape "didn't smell like" her.
- **Fheminor** — "the short-legged one," an evening guest.
- **Daral** — "the stinky breath one," an evening guest seen fleeing downstairs with *The Golden Ass*.
- **Daz (party)** — interviewer via beast-speech; earned cooperation with tickles.
