# Miss Hollypocket

## Identity
- Elderly gnome housekeeper to Janussi, the Keeper of the candle keep; tended all his personal needs (morning tea and pastry, chamber pot).
- Lives in a modest, warm apartment below the Keeper's tower. First encountered when the party sought her out during the investigation into Janussi's death.

## Personality & Motivations
Devoted and diligent — she never sleeps before the eleventh bell in case the Master needs her. Warm and hospitable, she offered the party seats and herbal tea and readily "gossiped" once given permission for the investigation. Emotionally tender: she broke into tears when reminded that the "thud" she heard was Janussi's death. Superstitious and wary of outsiders she distrusts, especially Sylvira Savikas.

## History with the Party
- The party sought her out as a witness in the investigation of Janussi's death.
- Given permission to "gossip for the investigation," she delivered a detailed catalogue of the day's events (see Relationships and Current Status).
- Grygum clarified for the party that the "devil woman" she feared is Sylvira Savikas, Curator of the Infernal Fortress.
- Later, Daz named her to Bookwyrm as "the one harmless witness the party had already spoken with" — offered deliberately as "a name that costs nothing" to test what Bookwyrm would do with a name, not as a genuine lead.

## Current Status
- Last known at her apartment below the Keeper's tower, cooperating as a witness.
- Key testimony provided to the party:
  - Passed **Kalan Strongbranch** on the steps with "a face like thunder" while bringing tea.
  - A ninth-bell shouting match in which **Teles Ahvoste** shouted "hypocrite" before storming out.
  - **Daral Yashenti** arrived around the eleventh bell, hungover, calling Janussi "not fit to be the candle keep's keeper" and "a glorified book peddler."
  - A violent confrontation with **Sylvira Savikas**.
  - Daral returned drunk at half past ten, pounded on the door, argued again, then fled down the stairs.
  - Fixed the time of a "thud" upstairs (Janussi's death) to just after the bell tower rang — confirming Janussi was alive until then.
  - Around 2–3 a.m., woke to see **Sylvira Savikas's face glowing red in candlelight** before she vanished downstairs carrying a lantern — which struck Hollypocket as bizarre, since a tiefling needs no light in the dark.
  - Confirmed Janussi never removed his locket.
- What the party knows: her full witness account. What remains hidden: whether Bookwyrm has since acted on her name (Daz offered it as a test).

## Relationships
- **Janussi (the Keeper):** Loyal servant; refers to him as "the Master"; grief-stricken over his death.
- **Sylvira Savikas (Curator of the Infernal Fortress):** Fears and distrusts her — "that devil woman... she gives me the heebie-jeebies." Key witness against her (the 2–3 a.m. lantern sighting).
- **Kalan Strongbranch, Teles Ahvoste, Daral Yashenti:** Observed each in conflict with Janussi on the day of his death.
- **The party:** Cooperative witness; hospitable host.
- **Daz / Bookwyrm:** Named by Daz to Bookwyrm as a "harmless witness" — used as an expendable test name.
