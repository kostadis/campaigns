# Brother Vareth

## Identity
- Member of the Avowed community (a monastic/scholarly order); presents as a scholar-theologian.
- No first-meeting details recorded in these notes.

## Personality & Motivations
- Driven by intellectual ambition — described as having "the look of a man with a paper to publish." His core drive is to make and broadcast a theological breakthrough. He seizes on others' ideas and reframes them as his own contribution, prioritizing the thrill of the debate over practical consequences (including an active murder investigation happening around him).

## History with the Party
- Sought out Grygum and obtained Grygum's answer to the Question of the Age — the triangle framing with three options, built in part out of Stroud's Gambit.
- Took that answer and converted it into a "fourth corner": a new theology, which he then proclaimed to everyone.

## Current Status
- Last known location: at the keep, having roused the entire Avowed community.
- Active operation: instigating a community-wide debate of his new theology in the Grand Hall — which effectively emptied the keep of watchful eyes despite an ongoing murder investigation (a possible opening the party may exploit or a complication to manage).

## Relationships
- **Grygum (party):** Source of the answer Vareth appropriated and reworked into his new theology.
- **The Avowed:** Vareth mobilized the whole community to drop everything and debate in the Grand Hall.

- None recorded in these notes.
