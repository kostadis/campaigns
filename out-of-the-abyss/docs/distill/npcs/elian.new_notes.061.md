# New notes for Elian (from dossier_extract_061.md)

Elian pushes Thorin to co-author a monograph on investigative methodology, praising the value of Kalan's proposal and the pushback it faced. Thorin corrects him that a co-authored work isn't a monograph; Elian retreats to proposing he write the monograph while Thorin contributes a preface — Thorin insists on writing "the introduction" instead, which frames the argument rather than decorates it. Elian argues that even a counterfactual endorsement ("these techniques *would have* helped") would win academic approval, and that someone of Thorin's "stature" would do wonders for criminal investigation across Faerûn. He delivers the investigation's core intellectual content: that magic like Speak with Dead cannot be trusted because the dead are still themselves — the "myth of the person" — and that one must collect physical, testimonial, and procedural facts and find where they converge. He lights up when Grygum offers "Once you have eliminated the impossible..." and asks to use it. He handed the party a written document articulating that magic doesn't close contradictions but creates them — which underpinned Zalthir's realization about the polymorph timeline gap.
