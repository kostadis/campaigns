# Brother Heslin

## Identity
- Monastery brother (monk/instructor) from Zalthir's past — associated with the monastic order where Zalthir was raised/trained.
- Has **not appeared in play**. Exists entirely as a figure in Zalthir's unreliable memory. Referenced under multiple names — "Brother Tharusk," and once with the parenthetical "(Tervin)" — reflecting Zalthir's uncertainty about who taught what.
- Distinguishing detail Zalthir consistently attaches: "the tall one who always smelled of incense."

## Personality & Motivations
No first-hand personality is established — every trait is filtered through Zalthir's half-attention. What survives is a portrait of a teacher of monastic disciplines: silent communication, reading meaning in objects, and "the urgent errand and clean dispatch." Whether these are genuinely Heslin's lessons or another brother's is left open by the source.

## History with the Party
Brother Heslin has never been present at the table. All mentions are Zalthir recalling monastery instruction, always hedged:
- Credited (with "The Garden Shadow") for a saying: *count what should be present before counting what is missing.* Zalthir admits he wasn't listening closely.
- Possible teacher of a class on "communication without sound" — a skill Glabbagool later mastered in the Whispering Dome "while Zalthir was sleeping." Zalthir remembers the light through the shutters, not the lesson.
- Possible teacher of reading "what an object meant rather than what it was" — a skill Zalthir now applies to Alkrist's bookmarked poison-tale.
- Possible teacher (or possibly Brother Varath) of "the art of the urgent errand and clean dispatch," taught over a season; Zalthir "learned poorly, having been looking out the window."

## Current Status
- **Location unknown.** Not encountered; presumed at the monastery of Zalthir's youth. No confirmed activities.
- No active plans known.
- **Party knows:** only Zalthir's vague, self-contradicting recollections. Whether Heslin still lives, and which lessons were truly his, is entirely unverified.

## Relationships
- **Zalthir:** former student. The relationship is defined by Zalthir's chronic inattention — Heslin's teachings are recalled but never mastered at the time.
- **Other brothers (referenced, identity blurred with Heslin):** "The Garden Shadow," "the quiet one with the ink-stained hands," Brother Varath. Zalthir routinely confuses which brother taught which discipline.
- **Glabbagool (thematic contrast only):** mastered the silent-communication lesson Zalthir slept through — no direct connection to Heslin.

## Arc Score Events
None. No arc-affecting events in the source notes.
