# New notes for Glabbagool (from dossier_extract_061.md)

The gelatinous cube travels with the party as Grygum's self-styled "secretary." At the reunion, Fembris's notes are handed to him; he evaluates them for nutritional content and pronounces "This is not nutritious... This is not food," then expresses genuine longing: "I remember when there was cheese. Perhaps if we had some cheese." Grygum reflects that Glabbagool doesn't perform emotions — he has them, and the grief over cheese is real. During the safe discussion, Glabbagool volunteers that he could have eaten through Alkrist's locked safe ("It would have taken a while"), and reminds Zalthir with quiet authority: "Walls don't stop us. I can eat through walls." He accepts Zalthir's point that punching or eating the safe would have ruined the sneaky approach ("Good point, good point, good point"), his dignity intact. He is present in the room during Alkrist's interrogation, having simply chosen to pay attention without being asked.
