# New notes for Glabbagool (from dossier_extract_059.md)

Summoned to the Whispering Dome for his "little quest" / trial. The party was told Glabbagool is now permanently attached to Zalthir, who logged no objection. In the Dome — where supplicants learn something about themselves — a monk posed the question "What is the art of movement." Glabbagool went utterly still for the better part of a minute, then answered "The body that mimics nothing is already shaped by what it watches," which the monk accepted as wisdom. Glabbagool then declared "I've been waiting to do this for a very long time" and flowed up onto Zalthir's forearm, settling as though he'd measured it in advance — "Glabbagool and Zalthir, together now… We ride." His new abilities: teleport in darkness, a flurry of strikes, forcing an acidic shadow-key into a wound, and a costly corrosive embrace that dissolves a target Zalthir has already bound (leaving nothing to hold). He also manifested four eyes on Zalthir's scales, which Zalthir suggested he blend away to look like normal scales; Glabbagool considered it. Before settling in, he reminded Zalthir that the drow in the bag of holding still needed watching — housekeeping at the moment of his own transformation. A former shadow monk observed that Glabbagool had mastered in under a minute the silent-communication lesson Zalthir had slept through. Glabbagool is now traveling with the party permanently, riding on Zalthir's arm.
