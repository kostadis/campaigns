# New notes for A'lai Aivenmore (from dossier_extract_061.md)

A'lai is identified as the one who set the murder in motion. On the day of the murder at 2:30 PM, A'lai appeared at the Oak Tree Apothecary asking Leuwin for fresh fennel (citing indigestion), drawing Leuwin into the garden while a dragonborn (Alkrist) entered and collected the poison. Alkrist's confession under the zone of truth placed a baths meeting at 11:45 PM between A'lai and Alkrist to discuss "their mutual problem" — Janussi — and the plan to deal with him using the midnight-tears method from 1001 Tashalian Nights. A'lai is a drow, which becomes central to Daz's private calculation: Daz sees A'lai as a possible lead on the unknown patron paying "Menzoberranzan rates" to protect him, and proposes that A'lai pay not in gold or books but in information about who is behind that arrangement. A'lai treats blackmail "as a kind of career" and would construct an administrative narrative for the killing. Not physically confronted in this chunk.
