# New notes for Bookwyrm (First Reader) (from dossier_extract_061.md)

Bookwyrm is Alkrist's beloved aunt, distraught that the succession was not going her way. Per Alkrist's confession, he visited her at midnight with two bottles of fine wine and two glasses to announce their Janussi problem was solved; she was appalled by the poisoning, argued loudly with him, and stormed off. Zalthir concluded Bookwyrm was the one who ripped out Janussi's heart and looted the chest/chalice afterward — someone who knew about the chalice, what it hid, and why no one would look there. Sylvira separately identified Bookwyrm as the one who felt Kalan's investigative proposal was absurd and pushed hardest against it. Daz "opened an account" on Bookwyrm as a suspect at the edge of the conspiracy. Not physically present in this chunk.
