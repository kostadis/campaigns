# Bahamut

## Identity
- Deity; god of the paladin/cleric Gyrgum. Functions in play as a divine framework rather than an acting character.
- Has never appeared. Referenced only through Gyrgum's internal narration and once by the NPC Fembris.

## Personality & Motivations
- Domain includes the preservation of truth (as invoked by Gyrgum). No demonstrated personality — Bahamut does not act or speak in any session.
- Characterized indirectly through Gyrgum's devotion: a distant, silent presence who "has rarely volunteered an opinion on anything."

## History with the Party
Bahamut never manifests at the table. Every appearance is a reference, all clustered around a murder investigation in a keep/library:
- Gyrgum works out the murder in the library, musing "Bahamut may or may not have been watching me work it out. I took notes either way." Tied to Gyrgum's habit of thinking in maxims.
- As the keep empties for a theological debate, Gyrgum repeats the sentiment: "Bahamut may or may not have been watching. I took notes either way."
- Gyrgum ties Bahamut's truth-preservation domain to his use of the Mending cantrip to restore cut wrapping paper and reveal what it concealed. Fembris invokes his own standing "as a cleric of Bahamut" to flatter Gyrgum.
- When the party resolves to convict Bookwyrm of embarrassment rather than murder, Gyrgum notes "Bahamut may or may not have been reading over my shoulder — I wrote it down either way."
- In another beat, Bahamut's silence ("has rarely volunteered an opinion on anything") is invoked to argue it would still not call Daral's behavior innocent.

## Current Status
- No location — a referenced deity, not a present entity.
- No active plans or operations.
- What the party knows: Bahamut is Gyrgum's god, associated with truth and its preservation. What remains hidden: whether Bahamut observes or responds at all — deliberately left ambiguous by Gyrgum ("may or may not").

## Relationships
- **Gyrgum:** Primary devotee. Gyrgum keeps notes "whether Bahamut reads them or not," frames his investigative work through Bahamut's domain of truth, and repeatedly wonders whether the god is watching.
- **Fembris:** Claims standing "as a cleric of Bahamut," using the affiliation to flatter Gyrgum.
