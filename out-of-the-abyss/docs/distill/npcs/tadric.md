# Tadric

## Identity
- Young human Watcher; low-ranking guardsman. Secret bearer of the genuine High Tower key.
- Posted by Kalan in the North Galleries, away from the barracks — loyal, alone, and unaware of what he carried or what was coming for it. Had earlier guarded Janussi's door.

## Personality & Motivations
Anxious and impressionable — when Thorin invoked a superstition about "shaking the candle" of a fallen statue, Tadric grew visibly nervous, unable to keep his eyes off it, and asked whether it was "true about candles." Deeply loyal to Kalan; on regaining consciousness his first (and repeated) concern was "Is Kalan alive?" Reflexively protective of his charge — grabbed for the key before his eyes had even focused. Thorin's blunt summary: "Short human, young, Tadric. His role is Tadric."

## History with the Party
- **Crime scene:** Present guarding the body/chamber. Thorin told him to "shake the candle" of a fallen statue (fearing a magical statue might wake); Tadric grew anxious, then asked Thorin if the candle superstition was true. Thorin reassured him to stay close and be protected.
- **The rotunda:** The party raced via the Watcher's Stair shortcut to reach him but arrived to find Moziqodo already tearing him apart on the rotunda floor. After the party killed the fiend, Grygum healed him closed.
- On coming around he grabbed for the key, then identified the corpse: "Moziqodo. Sylvira Savikas's son." He asked twice whether Kalan was alive. Thorin chose radical transparency and told him the whole situation.

## Current Status
- Last seen alive on the rotunda floor, key in fist, color returning after Grygum's healing.
- Still holds the genuine High Tower key.
- **Party knows:** he is the true key-bearer, posted by Kalan; the attacker was Moziqodo, Sylvira Savikas's son. **He now knows** the full situation (Thorin told him everything). What he was originally unaware of — what he carried and what was coming for it — has since been exposed.

## Relationships
- **Kalan:** Posted Tadric with the key; object of Tadric's loyalty and first concern on waking.
- **Moziqodo:** His attacker, nearly killed him; identified by Tadric as Sylvira Savikas's son.
- **Grygum:** Healed him back to consciousness.
- **Thorin:** Reassured him at the crime scene; later gave him the full truth of the situation.
