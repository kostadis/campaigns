## Janussi

Janussi (Keeper of the Tome) does not appear as a living character in this chunk — he is the murder victim whose corpse and chamber the party investigates in Candlekeep. His body was laid out for examination in his tower chamber, found at dawn by the Great Reader A'lai Aivenmore. Forensic findings: his heart had been torn out (via an axe-like implement smashing the ribcage) as a precise, unhurried desecration — the party concluded it was done to prevent him from being raised or questioned after death, since he was already dead before the chest was opened. His left forefinger, lower lip, and tongue-tip were blackened with a dark substance darker than any of his inks — the party deduced he had been poisoned by wetting his finger to turn the pages of a poisoned book (*The Golden Ass*). He had also suffered a broken nose (bruised, therefore inflicted before death, in a struggle between dinner and death) and a chipped front tooth from falling face-first onto stone before being rolled onto his back by his killer. Blue ink stained his right (writing) sleeve — he had been taking notes when interrupted. A chain impression with two broken lead links on his neck showed a locket had been torn from him (Janussi's locket, which per Hollypocket he never removed). His safe stood open with one sapphire and one spell-component parchment taken, but a fortune in diamonds, platinum, potions, a raven figurine, and other sapphires left untouched. Janussi kept his chamber thick with cherry-tobacco pipe smoke to medicate his failing lungs — smoke that would have masked the poison's smell. His last written notes were jokes copied from *The Golden Ass*, then research on unfamiliar spells in *The Discoveries*. He had spent his final morning in one-on-one meetings with the Great Readers, many ending in shouting matches. He held one of the two keys to the Hightower Library. He and Sylvira Savikas had an on-and-off romantic relationship. Hollypocket described him as "a nice man, a little stiff." Daral Yashenti had publicly called the party "sellswords" on his behalf and was his years-long rival.

## Miss Hollypocket

Miss Hollypocket does not appear until the party seeks her out; she is the elderly gnome housekeeper who tended to all of Janussi's needs (morning tea and pastry, chamber pot) and lives in a modest, warm apartment below the Keeper's tower. She offered the party seats and herbal tea and, given permission to "gossip for the investigation," delivered a catalogue of the day's events. She reported passing Kalan Strongbranch on the steps with "a face like thunder" while bringing tea; a ninth-bell shouting match in which Teles Ahvoste shouted "hypocrite" before storming out; Daral Yashenti arriving around the eleventh bell hungover (calling Janussi "not fit to be the candle keep's keeper" and "a glorified book peddler"); and a violent confrontation with Sylvira Savikas ("that devil woman... she gives me the heebie-jeebies"), whom Grygum clarified is Curator of the Infernal Fortress. She recounted Daral returning drunk at half past ten, pounding on the door, another argument, then fleeing down the stairs. She never sleeps before eleven in case the Master needs her — which let her fix the time of a "thud" upstairs (Janussi's death) to just after the bell tower rang, confirming he was alive until then. She broke into tears when reminded what the thud was. Most crucially, she reported waking around 2–3 a.m. to see Sylvira Savikas's face glowing red in candlelight before she vanished going downstairs — carrying a lantern, which struck Hollypocket as bizarre since a tiefling needs no light in the dark. She confirmed Janussi never removed his locket.

## Daral Yashenti

Daral Yashenti does not appear directly but is the party's emerging prime suspect, named by Fembris Lancer as Janussi's years-long rival who had publicly called the party "sellswords." Hollypocket and Queenie corroborate his movements: he arrived around the eleventh bell hungover ("he drinks way too much"), shouted that Janussi was "not fit to be the candle keep's keeper" and "nothing but a glorified book peddler," then returned drunk at half past ten at night, pounded on the door until admitted, argued loudly, and fled down the stairs faster than Hollypocket had ever seen him run. Queenie identified him by smell ("the stinky breath one") and reported he left carrying a "hypnotic block" — deduced to be the missing book *The Golden Ass*, which he took while Janussi was still alive. Thorin regards Daral's long-set grudge as a vector pointing to guilt.

## Sylvira Savikas

Sylvira Savikas does not appear directly but emerges as a major suspect through multiple witnesses. She is a tiefling and Curator of the Infernal Fortress (Grygum re-filed Hollypocket's "devil woman" epithet as a job function). She and Janussi had an on-and-off romantic relationship. Queenie reported that Sylvira woke her that morning by blasting the armchair with "a lance of unerring force" (the three force-dart impacts and two punched-through holes the party found in the chair) — a lover's quarrel opened with a flung force spell. Hollypocket reported a violent morning confrontation in which Sylvira screamed that Janussi was a traitor, nearly coming to blows before storming out. Most damningly, Hollypocket saw Sylvira's face glowing red in candlelight around 2–3 a.m., carrying a lantern she had no use for as a tiefling. Queenie, however, saw "the torn demon lady" coming down the steps that night but noted "she didn't smell like the demon lady" — implying a shapeshifter wearing Sylvira's form, which the party seized on ("that explains the candle").

## Kalan Strongbranch

Kalan Strongbranch does not appear directly. Fembris Lancer identified him as the gate warden who holds the second of the two keys to the Hightower Library (Janussi held the first, now stolen with his locket). Hollypocket reported passing him on the tower steps that morning with "a face like thunder," nearly knocking the tea tray from her hands. Daz connected the two keys to the possibility that someone used a key to access the library and delete/tamper with a book "no one should have touched."

## Teles Ahvoste

Teles Ahvoste does not appear directly. Hollypocket reported that around the ninth bell he had a meeting with Janussi that descended into a shouting match, and that she clearly heard Teles shout "hypocrite!" at Janussi before storming out.

## Fembris Lancer

Fembris Lancer appears in the hall outside the murder chamber as an eager, hovering assistant who had decided the party were the people who could fix things. Prompted by Thorin and Grygum's simultaneous question about enemies/rivals, he immediately named Daral Yashenti — "those two have been at each other's throats for years" — and noted Daral was the one who called the party "sellswords." Once talking, he kept volunteering useful facts: reminded by the missing locket, he revealed ("by golly!") that Janussi held one of the two keys to the Hightower Library, and that the other key is held by the gate warden Kalan. He trailed the party as they left, asking if they were done.

## A'lai Aivenmore

A'lai Aivenmore does not appear on-screen but is referenced as the Great Reader who discovered Janussi's body at dawn. In his shock he knocked over the three-foot marble statue of a hooded, bearded old man holding a candle aloft — the party confirmed this was the one honest accident in an otherwise deliberately staged scene (a scholar reaching blindly for the wall and finding a statue).

## Tadric

Tadric is present at the crime scene, hovering and guarding the body/chamber. When Thorin told him to "shake the candle" of the fallen statue (out of fear a magical statue might wake and attack), Tadric grew visibly anxious — trying not to look at the candle, then unable to stop, finally asking Thorin whether it was "true about candles." Thorin reassured him to stay close and be protected.

## Queenie

Queenie is Janussi's tower cat — a five-year-old sleet-grey housecat with strong opinions — interviewed by Daz using the beast-speech granted by the Stonespeaker crystal. She demanded tickles as payment for information and warmed once Daz found the lever. She reported being woken in her armchair nap around morning by Sylvira Savikas ("our Sylvira") blasting the chair with a lance of force (a lover's quarrel). She woke from an afternoon nap around eight after supper and categorized the evening's guests by gait and smell: first Fheminor ("the short-legged one"), later Daral ("the stinky breath one"). She reported Daral running downstairs with a "hypnotic block" — deduced to be the missing book *The Golden Ass* — while Janussi was still alive. In the night she saw "the torn demon lady" (Sylvira's shape) descending the steps but noted "she didn't smell like the demon lady," suggesting a shapeshifter. She could not identify the missing locket ("I don't know what it was"). She delivered several insults about two-leggers' poor hygiene and finally dismissed the party: "You may now leave, and please give yourself a proper licking before returning to my tower."

## Fheminor Scrivenbark

Fheminor Scrivenbark does not appear directly but is placed at the scene by Queenie, who saw him arrive as the first of the evening's guests after supper, identifying him by gait as "the short-legged one" (arriving before Daral).

## Milo Goodbarrel

Milo Goodbarrel does not appear in this chunk. He is referenced by Grygum as the source of Daz's recent education in forensic reading — "Milo's murder mystery, fresh in his head" — crediting a prior murder-mystery experience for Daz's sharp eye over Janussi's corpse.

## Glabbagool

Glabbagool does not appear physically but is invoked as a resource: Zalthir suggested that Glabbagool, an ooze who has ingested "all sorts of different kinds of materials and poisons and goo in the Underdark," might identify the black poison substance from Janussi's body. The party gave him the substance; despite two attempts (one with help), he could not name it — but even his failure signaled the poison was dangerous and obscure enough to be worth fearing.

## Brother Heslin

Brother Heslin does not appear in this chunk; he is referenced by Zalthir under the name "Brother Tharusk, the tall one who always smelled of incense," half-credited (alongside "The Garden Shadow") with a saying about counting what should be present before counting what is missing. Zalthir admits he wasn't listening closely to either.

## Bahamut

Bahamut does not appear; the deity is referenced twice as Grygum's god in the narration — noting that even Bahamut "has rarely volunteered an opinion on anything" would not call Daral's behavior innocent, characterizing Grygum's clerical devotion during the investigation.
