# Chapter 31 It's profitable to be the BBEG

Date: 2026-06-28

## Summary

When Triton’s signal cut across the water — a desperate plea, and with it the warning that a kraken would be loosed if no rescue came — Zephyr didn’t stop to question it. He took the bait whole and threw himself into the pool. The water answered with a will of its own: an elemental intelligence seized him, locked him in place, and would not let go. Acrid smoke began to curl off the surface, and the truth became clear too late — the water was acid, and Zephyr was caught in it.
Sequoia took command, rallying the party and lashing a rope around his waist to haul Zephyr free. But the pool ran four feet deep and Sequoia stood only three feet tall — a problem Calmer was quick to point out, and one Sequoia did not appreciate hearing. In the end it was Zinnia who took up the rope, tied off, and leapt in. The water made no grab for him. He dragged Zephyr out — alive, but coated head to foot in acid.

Shaken, the group turned to leave. Brother Eelrich named the place unwelcoming and said as much, though he agreed to follow Brother Calmer’s lead; Calmer looked to Zephyr, who told him plainly to get out. As Brother Eelrich and Calmer hurried for the exit, certain it was over, Sequoia jumped in a second time. The acid burned him before he understood his mistake, and he scrambled clear. Only then did Calmer piece it together: the thing in the water could plant suggestions in their minds. The party fell back behind the curtain just outside the Hall of Verdigris to catch their breath. Calmer weighed going back in — but their thoughts drifted instead to the chambers Brother Eelrich had emerged from, and the treasure waiting there, now claimed in the name of the Prophet of Earth.

T
The party wasted no time claiming what was rightfully theirs. On a desk in the priest's chambers, they uncovered a collection of powerful spell scrolls, while Zephyr and Sequoia pried open the locked chests in the bedroom to reveal ritual texts on elemental water, a ceremonial bronze mace, a gold ring set with a pearl, and potions of water breathing. Calmer confronted Brother Eelrich directly, demanding the man address him as "Lord Master Calmer" and reminding him in no uncertain terms that the temple had fallen and a new order had risen in its place. As the party leafed through the ritual texts, an unsettling suspicion began to take root — the writings hinted that Brother Eelrich may have known far more about the living, sentient nature of the water elemental than he had ever let on, casting a long shadow over how much the defeated priest could be trusted going forward. Before leaving, the party made their position crystal clear: the next surprise found in these halls would be meant for him.

Returning to their lair on the first level to rest and recover, the party took stock of their new standing. Calmer formally assigned roles to each of his companions, naming Zinnia as the head of all intelligence operations, Zephyr as chief operating officer responsible for the day-to-day running of the temple, and Sequoia as the one charged with ensuring that valuables found their way into the right hands and stayed there. Calmer himself declared that his concerns lay with the spiritual matters of the earth temple gods, and that petty operational details were beneath him — a declaration he delivered with the full gravity of a man who had just forced a priest to call him "Lord Master."

Their rest was interrupted by a knock at the door. Dren, their ever-watchful subordinate, reported that a soft-looking, gray man who smiled far too much had arrived at the entrance, claiming to have been sent by Barkinar. The man had laughed when Dren patted him down for weapons, which Dren found deeply suspicious, though he admitted the visitor appeared harmless enough. The party allowed him in, and the man introduced himself as Lucius Graeme — a facilitator, a middleman, a carrier of things from clever people to clever people, and by his own account, the new management was the cleverest thing to happen to the temple in a year.

Lucius arrived bearing gifts. He produced a cask of genuinely excellent wine and presented each member of the household with a potent healing potion, all courtesy of Barkinar and the temple's supply chain. He explained that his role was to serve as the conduit between the outside world and the temple's leadership, ensuring that whatever the new heads required, they would receive. When the party asked about magical wares, Lucius snapped his fingers and had Dren — who nervously warned that some chests had a habit of turning into mimics — bring in his chest of goods, revealing an assortment of enchanted items available for purchase.

The negotiations that followed were equal parts exciting and humbling. Sequoia and Romag each walked away with finely enchanted blades, procured by Lucius with a merchant's practiced enthusiasm. But the items that truly captured the party's imagination — a ring that rendered its wearer invisible and another that made the wearer immune to all magical restraint and difficult terrain — carried price tags that dwarfed everything the party had accumulated. Lucius, ever the professional, offered to hold both items in reserve for a deposit, ensuring no other buyer could claim them. The party eagerly paid the deposits to secure the rings. As the meeting drew to a close, the party found themselves staring at the vast gulf between their ambitions and their coin purses, newly and powerfully motivated to plunder every last secret the temple had left to offer.

## NPCs
### Brother Eelrich Vane
A defeated priest of the Water Temple who whines about the loss of his religious materials and personal belongings, but ultimately submits to Calmer's authority after being reminded that the temple was defeated and the party is now in charge.

### Dren
A subordinate who manages security for the party's lair, reporting on visitors with suspicion and checking them for weapons. He bristles when Zephyr gives him orders, noting that Zephyr and he are peers.

### Lucius Graeme
A soft-looking, gray man who smiles frequently. He acts as a conduit between Nulb and the temple leadership, sent by Barkinar to ensure the new management has everything it needs. He provides luxury goods and magical items for purchase, and offers to hold rare items for a deposit.

### Barkinar
A high-ranking figure who sent Lucius to support the party. Referenced as the man who had four problems in the morning that were all gone after the party's victory, and who warned the party about Hartsch.

### Lord Hedrak
A high-ranking authority within the temple hierarchy, referenced by Lucius as having made it clear that magical items are not to be given away for free.

## Items
### Spell Scroll of Resist Poison
A protective spell scroll found on a desk in the Water Temple chambers, added to the party's inventory.

### Spell Scroll of True Seeing
A spell scroll found on a desk in the Water Temple that allows the caster to see the true nature of creatures and detect traps. It can be used once by anyone, but cannot be learned as a wizard spell.

### Spell Scroll of Resist Energy (Fire)
A defensive spell scroll discovered by the party while plundering the Water Temple desk.

### Ritual Texts on Elemental Water
Four books detailing the elemental evil of water, found in the locked chests in Brother Eelrich's bedroom. Each is worth 250 gold pieces. The party took three and left one for Brother Eelrich. Reading them raised suspicions about how much Brother Eelrich knew about the living water elemental.

### Ceremonial Bronze Mace
A decorative ceremonial weapon found among the spoils in the Water Temple bedroom.

### Gold Ring with a Pearl
A piece of valuable jewelry recovered from a locked chest in the priest's quarters.

### Potions of Water Breathing
Two magical elixirs found in the temple that allow the consumer to breathe underwater. The party noted they may not be straightforwardly useful against a living water elemental.

### Cask of Excellent Wine
A gift of genuinely good wine brought by Lucius Graeme for the household to celebrate the party's new positions of power.

### Potion of Superior Healing
Potent restorative tonics gifted to each member of the household by Lucius Graeme as a gesture of goodwill.

### Shortsword +1
Magically enhanced blades purchased for one thousand gold pieces each, procured by Lucius Graeme to improve the wielders' accuracy and effectiveness in combat.

### Ring of Protection
A powerful magical ring available from Lucius that enhances the wearer's armor class and saving throws. Discussed as a strong option particularly for the monk who cannot wear armor.

### Ring of Invisibility
An extremely rare and expensive ring priced at 10,000 gold pieces. Lucius offered to reserve it for Sequoia for a deposit of 4,000 gold pieces.

### Ring of Free Action
A legendary ring priced at 20,000 gold pieces that allows the wearer to move normally through difficult terrain and grants immunity to magical effects that reduce speed, paralyze, or restrain. Lucius offered to hold it for Zephyr for a deposit of 4,000 gold pieces.

## Spells
### Resist Poison
A protective spell found in scroll form on the desk in the Water Temple, added to the party's inventory for future use.

### Resist Energy (Fire)
A defensive spell scroll discovered by the party while plundering the Water Temple, offering protection against fire damage.

### True Seeing
A spell scroll found in the Water Temple that allows the caster to perceive the true nature of creatures and detect hidden things such as traps. It can only be cast once from the scroll.

### Greater Restoration
A powerful healing spell chosen by Calmer upon reaching level nine, intended to protect the party from debilitating conditions in future encounters.
