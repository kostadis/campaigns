## Consistency Report

### 1. Barkinar is incorrectly identified as a former Earth Temple leader

- **Location:** Summary; Scenes — “A Message from the Necromancer”; NPCs — Barkinar and The Necromancer
- **Issue:** The recap claims Barkinar previously led the Earth Temple, was promoted, and was replaced by Romag. It then identifies the necromancer as a former associate passed over for Romag’s position.
- **Evidence:** The **AUTHORITATIVE CANON entity registry** identifies Barkinar as Commander of the Greater Temple’s troops, not a former Earth Temple prophet. It separately identifies **Landers** as the former Earth Temple leader whom Barkinar betrayed and eliminated. The GM working material instead proposes an unnamed predecessor of Romag who was promoted to the Deep Temple, but explicitly says that predecessor’s name is not established.
- **Suggested fix:** Remove Barkinar from this deduction. Say that the necromancer may be connected to Romag’s unnamed predecessor or former Earth Temple leadership, but that the necromancer’s identity remains unconfirmed.

### 2. The recovered shield has the wrong enhancement bonus

- **Location:** Summary; Scenes — “The Slippery Cistern”; Items — “Shield +1”; Memorable Moments
- **Issue:** The recovered item is repeatedly called a **shield +1**.
- **Evidence:** The **AUTHORITATIVE CANON entity registry** identifies the treasure in the **Cistern Chamber, area 236**, as a **shield +2**.
- **Suggested fix:** Replace every instance of “shield +1” with “shield +2.”

### 3. The black-cloaked bandits are unjustifiably identified as undead

- **Location:** Summary, final paragraph; Scenes — “The Slippery Cistern”; NPCs — The Minotaur
- **Issue:** The recap concludes that some of the Minotaur’s “bandits in black cloaks” were undead and presents this as confirmation of the necromancer’s activity.
- **Evidence:** The **AUTHORITATIVE CANON entity registry** identifies black-clad guards and bandits serving the overall Temple as a distinct, living faction aloof from the elemental temples. Nothing in the supplied context establishes that the Minotaur’s attackers were undead. The necromancer is known to animate Earth Temple corpses, but that does not establish that these particular black-cloaked attackers were undead.
- **Suggested fix:** Preserve the Minotaur’s report of black-cloaked bandits, but remove the conclusion that they were undead unless the session explicitly established it.

### 4. The account of who paid to raise Calmer is overstated

- **Location:** Summary; Scenes — “Calmer’s Crisis of Faith”; NPCs — Calmer; Memorable Moments
- **Issue:** The recap states that “a tiefling, a scarred spy, and a rogue paid the price” to raise Calmer, implying that three party members jointly supplied the resurrection payment.
- **Evidence:** Campaign state says **Zinnia tithed a magical trident to Terjon**, after which Terjon raised Calmer. The broader party may have helped arrange his return, but the documented material payment is attributed specifically to Zinnia.
- **Suggested fix:** Use wording such as: “His companions arranged his return, with Zinnia surrendering a magical trident to Terjon as payment.”

### 5. Frostbrand’s remark is given a stronger interpretation than the quotation supports

- **Location:** Summary; Scenes — “Calmer’s Crisis of Faith”; NPCs and Items — Frostbrand; Memorable Moments
- **Issue:** Frostbrand is described as expressing concern over Calmer’s attachment to temple leadership and “openly evil behavior.” The quoted remark only says that someone is forgetting the party’s plan to remain discreet.
- **Evidence:** The quotation addresses Sequoia and references the “sneakity-sneakety” plan. It does not explicitly identify Calmer’s morality or growing attachment to leadership as Frostbrand’s concern.
- **Suggested fix:** Describe Frostbrand as warning that the party—or possibly Calmer—was becoming insufficiently discreet. Present the more specific interpretation as inference rather than fact.

### 6. The necromancer’s history is presented as established rather than speculative

- **Location:** Summary; Scenes — “A Message from the Necromancer”; NPCs — The Necromancer
- **Issue:** The recap turns the party’s theory into a detailed history: the necromancer expected leadership, was passed over for Romag, and now regards Calmer’s appointment as a personal insult.
- **Evidence:** Campaign context confirms only that an unidentified necromancer is raising Earth Temple corpses. The GM working document proposes that Romag’s unnamed predecessor was promoted to the Deep Temple and may be responsible, but labels the identity and motives as decisions still to be made. The **AUTHORITATIVE CANON registry** does not identify that predecessor as the necromancer.
- **Suggested fix:** Clearly frame this as the party’s hypothesis: “They suspected the necromancer might be connected to Romag’s predecessor, but his identity and motives remained unconfirmed.”

### 7. Several item entries falsely claim they were referenced in this recap

- **Location:** Items — Golden Pearl Ring, Ring of Fire Resistance, Magical Wood Totem
- **Issue:** These entries say the items were “referenced in the session recap,” but none appears in the Summary, Scenes, Locations, NPCs, or Memorable Moments outside the item list itself.
- **Evidence:** The supplied recap text contains no earlier reference to the Golden Pearl Ring, Ring of Fire Resistance, or Magical Wood Totem.
- **Suggested fix:** Remove these unrelated item entries, or change their descriptions to say they are prior-session inventory rather than items referenced in Chapter 33.
