# The Juggernaut's Rampage

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party faces off against a massive rolling idol and the remaining gargoyles in a chaotic battle that leaves several members wounded.
- Calmer struggles to maintain concentration on a protective spell while being battered by the gargoyles, at one point dropping to 15 hit points.
- A massive rolling idol, identified as a Juggernaut, enters the fray and launches three pseudopods at Calmer, dealing 36 points of damage.
- Zephyr attempts to strike the Juggernaut with a ranged attack before disengaging to regroup near Calmer (Juggernaut AC 18).
- Sequoia leaps onto the back of a gargoyle; when he asks for a sneak attack, the DM asks him to "tell me something creative, role-play." Sequoia describes riding its back where "it can get to me, but… can't exactly get" a good angle, passes an acrobatics check, and lands a devastating strike, dropping the creature.
- Sequoia notes his Lucky feat when passing the falling-gargoyle acrobatics check — the DM confirming he can only fail on "2 natural ones."
- Calmer unleashes a burst of radiant energy, damaging the nearby gargoyles and the Juggernaut before healing his own wounds; the DM narrates one landing "like the scene from Pirates of the Caribbean."
- Zinnia jumps from the altar to strike a gargoyle, landing a powerful blow while the creature is distracted.
- The Juggernaut attempts to roll over Zephyr, but the rogue makes a Dexterity saving throw and narrowly dodges the crushing weight of the stone idol — "it tries to roll over you and misses completely."
- Zephyr and Sequoia coordinate their attacks against the Juggernaut, though the creature proves immune to Zephyr's attempts to trip it ("it's a rolling creature, so it can't be toppled") or poison it.
- The Juggernaut smashes through the temple altar, reducing the sacred structure to a pile of rubble; the DM notes the altar "did if you were the Water Temple, but not otherwise" have significance.
- The Juggernaut focuses its fury on Zephyr, unleashing a series of brutal strikes (36 damage) that knock the rogue unconscious.
- Sequoia uses Silvery Barbs to force the Juggernaut to reroll, reducing the blow from 36 to 24 — not enough to keep Zephyr conscious, though he survives his first death save.
- Sequoia dashes to grab the unconscious Zephyr and drags him to safety behind a door as the party prepares to make a final stand.
