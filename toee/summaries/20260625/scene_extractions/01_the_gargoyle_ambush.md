# The Gargoyle Ambush

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### A group of gargoyles ambushes the party, focusing their attacks on Zinnia and Calmer.
- A gargoyle hisses and strikes Zinnia with its claws before flying away — a critical hit for 16, then a second claw for 6, dealing 19 points of damage in all.
- Another gargoyle enters a protective magical zone — the "yellow, shiny area" — takes 12 points of damage and is slowed by its effect, but still manages to strike Calmer, who makes his saving throw to keep concentration.
- A third gargoyle attempts to attack Calmer but fails to land a hit and retreats.
- Zinnia, unable to reach the flying creatures, briefly considers Misty Step before opting to Dash as a bonus action and make a running charge; the DM confirms it counts as a charge attack. He rolls a 1 on one attack and lands for 8, then chooses to hold onto the gargoyle rather than fall, avoiding its reaction attack.
- Zephyr moves into position and fires his pistol at one of the flying creatures (gargoyle AC 15).
- Sequoia casts Jump to enhance his jumping ability and leaps toward a gargoyle but misses the attack; a rules discussion follows about whether Silvery Barbs can still be used as a reaction after casting a leveled spell (it can, on a later turn/as a reaction if no other spell is cast).
- Calmer unleashes a powerful magical blast (3d8) that damages multiple gargoyles, dealing 11 points to two of them.
- Zinnia delivers a finishing blow to the gargoyle he is clinging to for 17 points of damage and, with an acrobatics check, uses the falling creature's body to cushion his landing.
- A large, mysterious idol-like creature rolls initiative and begins to move, extending "several pseudopods… projections that come out and are sort of reaching out."
