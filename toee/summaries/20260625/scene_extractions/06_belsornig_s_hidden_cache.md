# Belsornig's Hidden Cache

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The defecting cleric leads the party to a hidden treasury concealed within the temple's basin.
- At the basin, the cleric notes the Juggernaut damaged it and asks the party to move the rubble; wary of a trap, the party makes him do it himself, and he navigates the traps to reveal Belsornig's getaway cash.
- The party recovers a chest containing three thousand gold pieces along with various gems and jewelry — 1,250 gold each.
- The group discusses how to divide the spoils and decides to set aside a ten percent share (about 1,000 gold, 250 each) for their ally, Dren.
- Zinnia proposes evening out the shares since some party members have more gold than others; the DM quips, "I see the Temple of Elemental Evil has already started to take root amongst you."
- Zephyr, as leader, offers to pay the bigger share of Dren's tribute; Calmer, conflicted, ultimately calls it "an excellent idea," and praises Zinnia's suggestion as "a masterful stroke."
