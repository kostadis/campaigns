# The Chamber of the Pool

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party enters a grand, serpentine-walled chamber containing a mysterious pool and a chained prisoner.
- Brother Eelrich leads the party to a domed room of polished serpentine with a ceiling of polished crystals that reflect the room "in a distorted way," concentrating light on a large oval pool lit with blue-green light that also seems to "absorb light, capturing in its heart."
- The cleric describes the pool as holding "a creature of unparalleled power, one that we worshipped, and one that we served, and one that served us."
- Pale green flames shed light but not heat from eight bronze cressets — four on the east wall, four on the west.
- A mosaic on the south wall depicts a great ten-armed octopoid monster of dark purple tile with reddish highlights and green orbs, holding a sacrificial victim in each tentacle.
- The pool is 20 feet across and 30 feet long, set in a basin of lapis lazuli tiles; crystal-clear water reveals a humanoid chained at the 12-foot-deep center.
- A telepathic message from a Triton reaches the party, pleading for release to help defend against an imminent Kraken attack.
- The session ends on a cliffhanger as the party must decide whether to dive in and free the Triton before the Kraken materializes.
