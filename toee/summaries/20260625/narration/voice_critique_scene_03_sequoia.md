# Voice Critique — Sequoia, scene 03: The Fall of the Juggernaut

**Narration:** `session_doc_scene_03_the_fall_of_the_juggernaut.scrubbed.md`
**Voice spec:** `voice/sequoia_voice.md`
**Per-char examples:** none (only global thematic examples exist in `examples/`)

## Flags

### [1] Convergence with another narrator's register (Zephyr)

> You spend a man and you cover the debt in the same breath. That's the whole job.

**Why:** "Spend a man / cover the debt" is Zephyr's ledger-and-expenditure vocabulary — his spec is explicit that *he* is the one who "spends people… resources spent on objectives… the accounting." Sequoia's register is information and positioning ("distance, adjacency, blade angle"), not accounting in human lives. This sentence reads like Zephyr's narrator wandered into Sequoia's scene.
**Suggested rewrite:** "I used him as bait, then I got him out. Same move, both halves. That's the job." *[grounded in spec: practical, forward-calculating, no metaphor]*

### [2] Register-wrong vocabulary (clinical)

> A hit like that doesn't hurt so much as reorganize you — one moment standing, the next a heap of tiefling on brown marble, badge and all.

**Why:** "reorganize you" is an administrative/clinical verb; Sequoia's spec forbids flourish and reaches for plain, physical words ("greased hinges, not ominous portents").
**Suggested rewrite:** "A hit like that doesn't hurt so much as unmake you for a second: one moment standing, the next a heap of tiefling on brown marble." *[grounded in spec: practical vocabulary]*

### [3] Em-dashes (systemic — Scan A)

12 em-dashes in the file; the majority are narration-level (a handful sit inside verbatim dialogue and stay). Representative conversion:

> Serious hit. Not a kill — the thing was still grinding.

**Suggested:** "Serious hit. Not a kill; the thing was still grinding." *(Same pattern recurs ~8× in this scene — a per-scene sweep converting narration `—` to `;`/`:`/`.` is the efficient fix.)*

## Verdict

Sequoia's voice is otherwise spec-accurate (the staccato "That's not good.", "Good thing I am small. Nobody clocks the halfling hauling the body.", and the closing "*By peaceful meat.*" callback are all dead-on). The one real problem is flag [1] — Zephyr's ledger register bleeding in — which is a spot-edit, not a re-narrate.
