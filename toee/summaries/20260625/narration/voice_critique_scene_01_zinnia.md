# Voice Critique — Zinnia, scene 01: The Gargoyle Ambush

**Narration:** `session_doc_scene_01_the_gargoyle_ambush.scrubbed.md`
**Voice spec:** `voice/zinnia_voice.md`
**Per-char examples:** none (only global thematic examples exist in `examples/`)

## Flags

### [1] Register-wrong vocabulary (clinical)

> then a second that opened the first wider, and the impact reorganized how much margin I thought I had left.

**Why:** "reorganized how much margin" is administrative/auditing language. Zinnia's spec is emphatic that he *perceives* rather than computes ("I knew" not "I calculated"); a felt read, not a recalculated balance.
**Suggested rewrite:** "then a second that opened the first wider, and I knew I had less left than I'd thought." *[grounded in spec: "he arrives at conclusions, he doesn't reason to them"]*

### [2] Register-wrong vocabulary (Scan B — low priority / defensible)

> Something large at the edge of the room, something I had filed as furniture, as an idol, as scenery — moved.

**Why:** "filed" is on the clinical/administrative list. It's *defensible* for Zinnia specifically — his spec gives him spy-tradecraft vocabulary ("filing," information-broker cataloguing) — so this is a judgment call, not a clear miss.
**Suggested rewrite (only if converting):** "something I'd taken for furniture, for an idol, for scenery — moved." *[grounded in spec — but "filed" arguably reads as his tradecraft mind; keep if you like it]*

### [3] Em-dashes (systemic — Scan A)

11 em-dashes; most narration-level. Representative conversion:

> The gargoyles came out of the dark ceiling before I could name what they were — stone wings, grey skin, the ambush already sprung…

**Suggested:** "…before I could name what they were: stone wings, grey skin, the ambush already sprung…"

## Verdict

Strongly in-voice — "Every step a decision made in the body before the mind caught up" and "The body knew the target before I did" are spec-perfect (body-before-mind). Only flag [1] is a real off-note; flag [2] is defensible. Spot-edit, don't re-narrate.
