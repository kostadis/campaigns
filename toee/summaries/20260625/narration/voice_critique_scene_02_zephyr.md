# Voice Critique — Zephyr, scene 02: The Juggernaut's Rampage

**Voice spec:** `voice/zephyr_voice.md`

## Flags

### [1] Cliché / on-the-nose simile

> Three times, stone on flesh, and the world went out from under me like a floor giving way.

**Why:** "like a floor giving way" is a workshop-stock simile. Zephyr's spec renders combat in staccato bursts ("They attack. They die.", "Thud. One gone.") and measures the world against the Abyss — not against generic collapsing floors.
**Suggested rewrite:** "Three times, stone on flesh, and then there was no floor." *[grounded in spec: staccato combat, drop the simile]*

### [2] Em-dashes (systemic — Scan A)

12 em-dashes; most narration-level. Representative conversion:

> The Juggernaut was in bad shape — the whole party had been carving at it while I was drooling on the floor.

**Suggested:** "The Juggernaut was in bad shape; the whole party had been carving at it while I was drooling on the floor."

## Verdict

Excellent Zephyr — "The assassin. Cargo now.", the debt/ledger read on his own near-death, and "I've heard of worse in the Abyss, but this is bad." are all spec-true. Only the one simile ([1]) breaks register. Spot-edit.
