## Summary
The air in the Water Temple crackled with danger as a pack of gargoyles descended upon the party, their stone wings beating silently as they hissed and slashed with razor-sharp claws. The creatures focused their fury on Zinnia and Calmer, darting in to strike before retreating into the air where blades could not easily follow. Calmer, ever mindful of his own importance, called out to his companions for protection even as the gargoyles raked at him again and again, insisting that his survival was critical to the group's success.

Zinnia, refusing to be grounded while enemies flew overhead, sprinted across the chamber and launched himself into the air in a breathtaking leap, slamming into one of the gargoyles mid-flight. Rather than fall back to the stone floor, he clung to the creature's back, riding it like a furious storm while it hissed and writhed beneath him. When the moment was right, he drove his weapon home with a killing blow, then used the creature's crumbling body as a springboard to land gracefully on the ground below, drawing an astonished hiss from the surviving gargoyles. Meanwhile, Zephyr took aim with his pistol from a distance, and Sequoia called upon magic to enhance his own leap, bounding upward to trade blows with the flying stone beasts.

The battle grew far more dangerous when a massive stone idol lurched to life in the center of the chamber, extending grotesque pseudopods that lashed out at Calmer with terrifying force. The creature, which the party came to call the Juggernaut, rolled and crashed through the room, reducing the temple's sacred altar to a pile of rubble as it bore down on the group. Calmer, battered but resolute, called upon his divine power to slow the creature's advance and mend his own wounds, buying precious moments for the rest of the party to regroup.

Sequoia proved himself a cunning fighter, clambering onto the back of a gargoyle and delivering a devastating strike before leaping clear, while Zephyr darted in and out of the Juggernaut's reach, trading blows and narrowly avoiding being crushed beneath its rolling bulk. The Juggernaut, immune to poison and impossible to topple, eventually turned its full wrath on Zephyr, hammering the rogue with a relentless series of blows that left him crumpled and unconscious on the temple floor. Sequoia acted swiftly, using a magical reaction to blunt the worst of the assault, but it was not enough to keep Zephyr on his feet, and he dragged him behind a heavy door to safety.

With Zephyr revived by Calmer's healing touch, the party rallied for a final push against the Juggernaut. Sequoia used Zephyr as a deliberate distraction, positioning him nearby so he could strike the creature from an advantageous angle, landing a punishing blow that left the idol teetering on the edge of destruction. Calmer strode forward and delivered the killing strike, and the massive stone construct ground to a halt, its pseudopods going limp as silence finally fell over the shattered chamber.

With the battle won, the party turned to the grim work of looting the fallen. The Juggernaut's body was encrusted with agates, and Canon Belsornig's chambers yielded a breathtaking haul of ceremonial treasures: ivory trays, crystal decanters, silk robes threaded with gold, books of lore, and a trident. More importantly, they recovered powerful magical artifacts from Belsornig's corpse, including a finely enchanted suit of half-plate armor, a mace of smiting, and a ring of free action, along with several rare spell scrolls.

Their inventory of riches was interrupted by a knock at the north door. When Zinnia opened it, he found a soft, perpetually damp man in cowled green robes, water beading at his hairline and cuffs as though he had never once been fully dry. He introduced himself as Brother Eelrich, a surviving cleric of the Water Temple and launched immediately into a torrent of flattery, praising Calmer as the glorious new leader of the upper temple and offering his loyalty in exchange for his life. He dangled two valuable secrets: the location of a hidden treasury Belsornig had been hoarding for his own escape, and knowledge of an unsigned contract with a band of trolls that had not yet been finalized.

The party was skeptical but pragmatic. Zinnia led the negotiations with cool composure, insisting the cleric prove his worth before being granted any formal position, while Zephyr watched with barely concealed distrust. The cleric, eager to demonstrate his value, led them to the Fourth Basin and navigated its hidden traps to reveal a concealed sump containing a chest brimming with gold, gems, and jewelry that Belsornig had been quietly squirreling away for years. The party divided the windfall, setting aside a share for their ally Dren, and Calmer magnanimously offered to contribute the largest portion of that tribute, a gesture that earned him a sharp look from the rest of the group.

Brother Eelrich then led them to the chamber of the high priest, a breathtaking room of polished serpentine walls and a crystal dome ceiling that bent and concentrated the pale blue-green light rising from a great oval pool at its center. He explained that the pool held the powerful elemental entity Belsornig worshiped. Heatless green flames burned in bronze crescents along the walls, and a mosaic of a monstrous ten-armed kraken dominated the south wall, its tentacles wrapped around sacrificial victims rendered in dark purple tile. As the party peered into the crystal-clear water, they could see a humanoid figure chained at the deepest point of the pool, and a moment later a telepathic voice filled their minds, urgent and desperate. It was a triton, pleading to be freed so that it could help them survive what was coming, for the kraken depicted on the wall was not merely a decoration — it was about to materialize, and time was already running out.

- Zinnia and Sequoia attempt to hold a door shut against a pursuing gargoyle (a strength check of 14 and 10), but the creature forces its way through.
- Zinnia delivers a series of strikes to a gargoyle, while Sequoia — asked to be "the sacrificial lamb again" via Zephyr — uses the distraction to land a lethal blow, killing the creature.
- The party focuses their efforts on the Juggernaut; Zephyr acts as a distraction while Sequoia delivers a heavy strike, aided by another Silvery Barbs on the Juggernaut's reaction attack.
- Calmer walks into the guide zone and delivers the final blow, causing the massive stone construct to stop moving forever — "Whoops! That was a bit of a combat."

### Looting the Water Temple
#### Following the battle, the party explores the chambers of the Water Temple, discovering vast riches and the remains of their enemies.
- The party (level 8) takes a short rest, rolling up to 8d8 to recover hit points; Calmer, however, declines to rest.
- The party loots the Juggernaut's remains, finding seventeen agates embedded in its form, each worth 10 gold pieces — the DM pasting the full gem breakdown into the Discord chat for reference.
- In the room of the two clerics killed in the first round, the party finds two gold rings shaped like sea creatures, each worth 500 gold pieces.
- Calmer leads the group into the chambers of the fallen Canon Belsornig to search for hidden treasures.
- The party discovers a wealth of ceremonial items: an ivory tray, six bowls of serpentine inlay, six crystal decanters, six stemmed cups, lanterns with continual flame effects, brass braziers, plate mail, shield, mace, green silk robes with threads of gold set with peridot, a bronze censer covered in verdigris, ten blocks of fine incense, a book of rites of the Temple of Water, six books of lore, a trident, and a coral box holding 100 tiny seed pearls.
- Valuable magical items are recovered from Belsornig's corpse, including a mace of smiting, a ring of free action, and half-plate +1, along with a gold belt worth 500 gold pieces.
- Calmer claims the Scroll of Flame Strike for himself.
- The group finds several spell scrolls, including a scroll of true seeing, a scroll of resist poison, and a scroll of resist energy (found on an investigation check of 24).
- The party debates whether to keep or wear the robe of the Chief Water Temple cleric, deciding to keep it around for a potential new recruit rather than take it as treasure.

### An Unexpected Petitioner
#### A surviving cleric of the Water Temple approaches the party, offering loyalty and secrets in exchange for his life.
- A knock sounds at the north door; when Zinnia opens it, a soft, damp man in cowled green robes — a curled circle stitched front and back, plain mail glinting underneath, water beading at his hairline and cuffs — surrenders and immediately begins flattering Calmer as "the great and now glorious leader of the temple, the Upper Temple."
- The cleric warns the party against deciding too fast: "let me tell you what's in my head, because it is worth more to your love than my blood is worth on that nice floor."
- The cleric reveals that Belsornig is dead and offers to lead the party to a hidden treasury (which drains to a sump only he can find) and to resolve an unsigned contract with trolls — the same trolls the party was tasked by Barkinar to deal with.
- Zinnia takes the lead in negotiations at Calmer's request ("you're much better at these negotiations"), questioning the cleric's trustworthiness — "how do I know I can trust you?" — while Zephyr expresses skepticism.
- The cleric pledges his loyalty "to Calder and the new leadership of the Earth Temple and of the Lower Temple," greets Zinnia as "the masked one, the killer, the seer of all," and hails Zephyr as "one of the Abyssal's creatures."
- The cleric angles to be granted the robes of the chief water cleric, imagining himself "as the head of the Water Cleric underneath the all-powerful Earth Temple Cleric, Calmer"; the party insists he prove himself first and go "through the full hiring process."
- The party agrees to accept the recruit provisionally, keeping a close eye on him.

### Belsornig's Hidden Cache
#### The defecting cleric leads the party to a hidden treasury concealed within the temple's basin.
- At the basin, the cleric notes the Juggernaut damaged it and asks the party to move the rubble; wary of a trap, the party makes him do it himself, and he navigates the traps to reveal Belsornig's getaway cash.
- The party recovers a chest containing three thousand gold pieces along with various gems and jewelry — 1,250 gold each.
- The group discusses how to divide the spoils and decides to set aside a ten percent share (about 1,000 gold, 250 each) for their ally, Dren.
- Zinnia proposes evening out the shares since some party members have more gold than others; the DM quips, "I see the Temple of Elemental Evil has already started to take root amongst you."
- Zephyr, as leader, offers to pay the bigger share of Dren's tribute; Calmer, conflicted, ultimately calls it "an excellent idea," and praises Zinnia's suggestion as "a masterful stroke."

### The Chamber of the Pool
#### The party enters a grand, serpentine-walled chamber containing a mysterious pool and a chained prisoner.
- Brother Eelrich leads the party to a domed room of polished serpentine with a ceiling of polished crystals that reflect the room "in a distorted way," concentrating light on a large oval pool lit with blue-green light that also seems to "absorb light, capturing in its heart."
- The cleric describes the pool as holding "a creature of unparalleled power, one that we worshipped, and one that we served, and one that served us."
- Pale green flames shed light but not heat from eight bronze crescents — four on the east wall, four on the west.
- A mosaic on the north wall depicts a great ten-armed octopoid monster of dark purple tile with reddish highlights and green orbs, holding a sacrificial victim in each tentacle.
- The pool is 20 feet across and 30 feet long, set in a basin of lapis lazuli tiles; crystal-clear water reveals a humanoid chained at the 12-foot-deep center.
- A telepathic message from a Triton reaches the party, pleading for release to help defend against an imminent Kraken attack.
- The session ends on a cliffhanger as the party must decide whether to dive in and free the Triton before the Kraken materializes.

## Locations
### Water Temple
A damp and dangerous complex where the party defeated gargoyles and a Juggernaut, then confronted the remnants of the water priesthood. There remain "a couple more chambers" to explore further south.

### The Protective Zone
A shimmering yellow area of magical protection, roughly sixty feet in range, that both damages (3d8) and slows enemies who enter it.

### The Water Temple Altar
A once-significant religious site that was reduced to a pile of stone rubble when the Juggernaut rolled through it during combat. The DM noted it "did" have significance "if you were the Water Temple, but not otherwise."

### Canon Belsornig's Chambers
The private quarters of the fallen Prophet of Water, filled with opulent ceremonial items, books of lore, and hidden wealth — a room the party had rushed through earlier without fully exploring.

### The Fourth Basin
A location within the Water Temple where Belsornig had hidden a secret treasury in a sump — one "only I can find," per the defecting cleric — accessible only after navigating hidden traps.

### The High Priest's Chamber
A grand room fashioned of great blocks of polished serpentine, with a domed ceiling of polished crystals that reflect and concentrate the pale blue-green light rising from a central oval pool. It features a north-wall mosaic of a ten-armed kraken rendered in dark purple tile and is lit by heatless pale green flames from eight bronze crescents.

## NPCs
### Gargoyles
Flying stone creatures (AC 15) that hiss and attack with sharp claws. They utilized their flight to strike and retreat quickly, focusing their attacks on Zinnia and Calmer, and could only be reached by leaping, jumping magic, or ranged weapons.

### The Juggernaut
A massive, rolling stone idol (AC 18) covered in agates that served as a guardian of the Water Temple. Beginning as a still idol, it animated to extend pseudopods and attempted to crush enemies by rolling in a straight line, proving immune to poison and impossible to topple. It reduced the temple altar to rubble in its rampage.

### Calmer
A cleric of St. Cuthbert and member of the party, now leader of the Earth/Upper Temple, who served as the primary target for the enemies due to his effective protective spells. He healed allies, dealt divine damage with Toll the Dead and his protective zone, revived the unconscious Zephyr, and ultimately delivered the killing blow to the Juggernaut. He proudly reminded the party afterward, "See? I told you gentlemen, that we would get lots of treasure."

### Brother Eelrich Vane
A soft, damp man in cowled green robes with a curled circle stitched front and back and plain mail glinting underneath, with water beads perpetually at his hairline and cuffs and a "deep stench of water about him." The last remaining cleric of the Water Temple. He is a sycophantic opportunist who quickly defects to the party, shamelessly flattering Calmer and angling to secure the top job of chief water cleric for himself — though he protests he "would not presume" to claim it while Calmer leads. To ingratiate himself and prove his worth, he offers up Belsornig's secret treasury and information about the unsigned troll contract. He is admitted only provisionally, with the party resolving to keep an eye on him.

### Belsornig
The deceased Prophet of Water whose corpse and chambers were looted by the party, yielding magical artifacts, ceremonial treasures, and a hidden cache of three thousand gold he had been planning to abscond with.

### The Triton
A humanoid figure chained at the bottom of the temple's central pool who communicates telepathically, warning the party of an imminent Kraken attack and pleading to be released so it can prevent their death.

### Dren
An associate of the party mentioned during the treasure division who is expected to receive a ten percent share of the loot — roughly a thousand gold pieces.

## Spells
### Misty Step
A teleportation spell considered by Zinnia as a potential way to reach the flying gargoyles, though ultimately a Dash-fueled running jump was used instead.

### Jump
A spell cast by Sequoia to dramatically increase their jumping height, allowing them to leap up and engage the flying gargoyles in melee combat.

### Silvery Barbs
A reaction spell used by Sequoia to force enemies to reroll and use the lower result. He used it to blunt the Juggernaut's assault on Zephyr (reducing 36 damage to 24, though not enough to keep him conscious) and again on the Juggernaut's reaction attack later in the fight. Its interaction with the one-leveled-spell-per-turn rule was discussed at length during combat.

### Toll the Dead
A necrotic cantrip Calmer used against the Juggernaut for 19 points of damage after the creature failed its saving throw.

### Cure Wounds
Calmer used a high-level casting of this healing magic to instantly restore his own wounds after being nearly crushed by the Juggernaut, and later used it to revive the unconscious Zephyr, restoring him to 20 hit points.
