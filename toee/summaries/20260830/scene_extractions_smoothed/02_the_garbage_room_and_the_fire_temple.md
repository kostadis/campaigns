# The Garbage Room and the Fire Temple

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party discovers a refuse-filled chamber and enlists the Fire Temple’s help to burn it before the necromancer can use the remains.

- Sequoia insists on exploring the remaining rooms because, as he puts it, “I’m a completionist.”
- The party enters a rotted chamber that was probably once a secluded relaxation area but is now filled with stinking garbage, dung, bones, and skulls.
- Parts of a bronze-bound coffer are visible near the center of the refuse.
- Concerned the necromancer will use the bones, the party decides to burn the entire room.
- Calmer admits that he has no fire spells and calls that omission a mistake.
- When Meteor Swarm is suggested, Calmer laughs: “No, no, we do not have one of those.”
- Zephyr and Sequoia head to the Fire Temple while Zinnia stays with Calmer to guard the room and watch what he does.
- Calmer claims he would have been safe alone because he is “the master of his domain,” but Zinnia elects to remain anyway.
- Zephyr and Sequoia take an unexplored dark corridor, emerge in familiar territory, and — given the choice of going to Vurakhal directly — approach the captain of the guard instead, meeting Captain Varek Solain.
- Varek explains that the refuse chamber is where the Fire Temple traditionally throws its garbage.
- After hearing about the undead, Varek reveals that a necromancer formerly under Barkinar disappeared after Romag received the promotion he wanted.
- Varek does not know where the necromancer is hiding, but says that if undead are appearing, “he’s around.”
- Varek says he warned Alrrem, but Alrrem failed to listen or seek an alliance with the necromancer.
- When ordered to locate the threat, Varek protests, “With what army? I need supplies. I need food, I need troops,” and reminds the party that it took his money and resources.
- He asks Sequoia, as head of logistics, to send someone to Nulb to repair the Fire Temple’s supply chain.
- Varek provides a limited number of fire arrows and explains that they should be used after throwing a flask of oil onto the target.
- Offered the arrows or Vurakhal’s help with the burning, Zephyr takes the arrows — the second time this session the party declines to involve the salamander.
- Zephyr grants the Fire Temple control over the northern food depot.
- Varek is delighted and declares, “He who controls food controls everything.”
- Zephyr corrects him: “We control the food, not you. You’re simply guarding it.”
- Varek calls the appointment a sign of trust and proclaims, “And fire shall burn again!” Zephyr answers, “Okay. Maybe don’t.”
- Zephyr suggests placing an Earth Temple agent near the depot to keep Varek and the Fire Temple in check.
- The party returns and ignites the refuse; a horrific, furious creature emerges and attempts to douse the fire.
- The creature is initially surprised, giving the party a free opportunity to attack or withdraw. They choose to defend their territory.
- Calmer casts Hold Monster and successfully paralyzes the creature, granting nearby attackers automatic critical hits.
- Zinnia deals 27 damage, spends a focus point, and follows with another 19.
- Zephyr’s doubled sneak attack contributes to a 52-point critical strike.
- Sequoia rolls twelve damage dice on a critical but averages poorly, loudly curses the result, and blames Frostbrand for failing at its “one job.”
- Calmer casts Toll the Dead and remarks, “This new spell of mine is very effective.”
- The creature repeatedly fails to escape the paralysis.
- Zephyr lands a final 57-point critical hit, killing it.
- The party steps outside and allows the room to burn for several hours.
