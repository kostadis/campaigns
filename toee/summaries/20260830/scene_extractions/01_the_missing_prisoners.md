# The Missing Prisoners

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party discovers that the prisoners they had left behind are gone, their chains torn apart and their bodies claimed by the necromancer.

- The party checks on the four prisoners it had left without food for several days, with the DM noting that no one knows whether they are still alive.
- Upon arrival, the captives are missing.
- Zephyr rolls a 22 on Investigation and discovers that the chains have been torn apart.
- The prisoners did not escape; their corpses have been taken and apparently added to the necromancer’s undead forces.
- The group concludes that its “friendly neighborhood necromancer” is becoming increasingly troublesome.
- The party discusses administrative roles, with Calmer designating Zephyr as Chief of Staff and Zinnia as Chief of Security.
- Calmer does not hold the titles steady — he later asks Zinnia to approve “my Chief of Operations plan.” The wobble is his, not the transcript’s: he is inventing the org chart as he goes and has not settled what he has appointed anyone to.
- Sequoia is reminded that he oversees supplies and summarizes his role as, “I’m in charge of getting shit done.”
- Zephyr proposes posting notices throughout the temple ordering anyone who finds information about the necromancer to report it.
- Calmer enthusiastically approves and tells Zephyr, “Produce an appropriate note, and I will sign it.”
- Calmer then questions whether hunting the necromancer should properly be Zinnia’s initiative as Chief of Security.
- Zinnia approves the plan, and the party decides to draft the notice after finishing its exploration.
