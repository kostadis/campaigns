# The Supply Room Ambush

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party explores a cluttered storage chamber filled with Greater Temple equipment, only to be ambushed by psychic predatory creatures clinging to the ceiling.

- Before reaching the larger storeroom, the party finds a chamber filled with broken containers and signs of recent traffic, probably from people removing the remaining food.
- The discovery confirms that the temple’s food supplies are running low and that nobody is currently managing them.
- The party enters an oddly shaped chamber crammed with large jars, boxes, barrels, crates, chests, sacks, casks, bales, bags, and hampers.
- Some containers are broken or empty, but many remain full. Bales contain black cloth and finished garments.
- Zinnia rolls a 24 and identifies the materials as equipment of the Greater, or Deep, Temple.
- The party’s Arcana checks fail to reveal an additional feature of the room.
- Sequoia checks the ceiling because “the answer is always on the ceiling,” rolls a 21 on Investigation, and spots four creatures hanging by their prehensile tails.
- The creatures are gaunt, four-armed, ape-like predators with ash-gray hides, hooked black claws, uneven fangs, three eyes, and violet psychic energy pulsing around their skulls.
- Sequoia exclaims, “I was right,” then admits, “I was saying, as a joke, oh my god.”
- His vigilance prevents the party from being surprised.
- Zephyr wins initiative with a 27 but misses both opening pistol shots.
- Zinnia, then Sequoia, attacks the same predator; Sequoia deals 24 damage with one hit and 9 with another before disengaging.
- The creatures unleash psychic blasts requiring DC 17 Wisdom saves.
- Zinnia, Zephyr, and Calmer resist the initial attacks, but Sequoia fails, takes 17 psychic damage, and is stunned.
- Calmer casts Spirit Guardians, creating a fifteen-foot aura that damages and slows several predators.
- Zephyr shoots the most injured predator for 30 total damage, including sneak attack, and kills it.
- The creatures focus their fury on Calmer, attacking with as many as five limbs at once.
- Calmer’s armor class is 21 with the Shield +1. One creature hits him only once, while another misses all five attacks.
- The successful claw deals 8 slashing damage and breaks Calmer’s concentration on Spirit Guardians.
- Zinnia takes 8 damage from another predator.
- At Sequoia’s insistence, Calmer uses Healing Word, then casts Word of Radiance against the creatures surrounding him.
- Zinnia kills the second predator after Zephyr leaves it with only 8 hit points.
- Calmer asks, “Are you okay, Sequoia?” When Sequoia replies, “I guess,” Calmer insists, “My guess is not an answer, yes or no.”
- Sequoia answers, “Maybe,” and warns, “Just don’t blame me when your logistics breaks down.”
- Calmer restores another 14 hit points to Sequoia and follows with a second Word of Radiance, dealing 11 radiant damage to both nearby creatures.
- Zephyr uses Chill Touch on one predator, dealing 9 damage and preventing it from regaining hit points.
- Zinnia is reminded that he can spend focus points on Stunning Strike, which can stun a target or at least halve its speed and grant advantage on the next attack.
- After two of their number are slain, the remaining two creatures disengage and retreat into a dark corner.
- Zephyr intimidates the survivors, then attempts Undercommon; no one in the party speaks Deep Speech, and the creatures only sneer and curl into the shadows.
- The survivors sneer, howl, hiss, and curl into the shadows rather than continuing the battle.
