# A Message from the Shadows

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### An illusory hooded figure appears to the resting party, offering cryptic warnings about powerful artifacts, dangerous enemies, and a hidden sleeper beneath the temple.

- The party retires to its quarters, where Calmer relaxes and admits, “I like being in charge.”
- While the group drinks and rests, an illusory image of a hooded man appears.
- The figure begins, “Forgive the theatrics,” explaining that he cannot be seen sending a runner and the party cannot afford to be seen receiving one.
- The message identifies him through implication as Falrinth, a wizard hiding from the Greater Temple whom Barkinar had previously considered worth discussing.
- Falrinth warns them about Lucius Graeme, “The flattering man” with “golden eyes, soft hands, a great deal of wine.”
- He says Lucius gives presents, asks what his targets lack, and “collects the ones he gives you.”
- Lucius has already visited the party bearing gifts.
- Falrinth reveals that he possesses the Orb of Golden Death and refuses to give it to Hedrack, Senshock, the “holy gentlemen” who want him dead, or the party.
- When Sequoia asks how he acquired it, Falrinth answers, “That is a conversation for another year.”
- Romag had previously described the complete orb as an artifact capable of summoning monsters and destroying virtually anything.
- Falrinth warns Calmer to be careful with his scarab bearing the letters T-Z-G-Y.
- Both Lucius and Senshock will recognize the scarab on sight, use it against the party in different ways, and conceal the fact that they noticed it.
- Falrinth says Lareth was his friend and expresses appreciation that the party handled him “with discretion.”
- He warns that he has other friends who are not as patient as he is.
- Falrinth advises the party not to commit when Lucius returns: “Stall.”
- He tells them that “A candle in the West” will reach him.
- He also says they may report him to Barkinar, who will believe them, but Falrinth will no longer be wherever they direct Barkinar to look.
- Falrinth reveals that a deep, forgotten room on the level beneath the party contains a sleeper in an iron coffin.
- He says “the holy gentleman” keeps the sleeper there, while Falrinth’s own leash is short and “the gates are watched.”
- The party possesses keys Falrinth never had.
- Waking the sleeper would bring Verbobonc and all its banners down upon the temple.
- Falrinth urges them to ask their “golden friend” what he fears they will discover, then signal him.
- The party realizes too late that the illusion is a one-way message and that Falrinth merely anticipated many of their questions.
- The illusion fades, leaving uncertainty over whether Falrinth truly has the orb, why he is not using it, and what lies inside the iron coffin.
