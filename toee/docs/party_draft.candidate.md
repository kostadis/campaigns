# Party Overview

**Current location:** Deep within the Temple of Elemental Evil, occupying the **Air and Water Temple levels** with the Fire Temple fully absorbed. Zephyr, Zinnia, and Sequoia operate together; **Calmer is benched** — severely wounded and psychologically fractured, curtained off in the Upper Temple under Dren Halveth's guard. The party is poised toward the Deep/Greater Temple. Hommlet remains their western base.

**Active quests & open threads:**
- **The Deep / Greater Temple** — a hidden authority orchestrating the elemental war; its forces were revealed in Ch30. Nature, location, and identity unknown; the obvious next push (a west-door breach already failed).
- **Water Temple second level (uncleared)** — occupation ≠ clearance; an active wraith, seven specters, and animated gargoyles still hold the level.
- **Calmer's condition** — fractured, wielding poorly-understood necrotic power, unable to name St. Cuthbert. With him down, the party has **no healer**.
- **Missing artifacts** — the **Orb of Earthly Supremacy** (lost with Romag, needed to open the temple vault); the **Chest of Rao** (held as Hommlet leverage but owed to Veluna).
- **The "Calmer leads the party" deception** — the Greater Temple (via Barkinar) believes Calmer commands the Obsidian Edge; exploitable but fragile.

**Collective resources:** **Frostbrand** (sentient ice longsword, bonded to Sequoia); the **Chimera's control chain** and a **bound salamander** (Zephyr); the **Chest of Rao** (leverage, whereabouts to confirm); the Fire Temple fire-pit treasure hoard; **Dren Halveth** as loyal retainer; **Kelno** as a conditional-ally prisoner and possible informant network. Thorne on retainer (20 GP/mo).

**Group reputation:** Known within the Temple as the **"Obsidian Edge,"** the faction that engineered and won the three-way cult war ("ran a three-way war and let it eat itself"). The Greater Temple believes Calmer is their leader. Sequoia's standing with **Verbobonc** was never formally severed. In Hommlet they are trusted agents of the council (Burne, Rufus, Jaroo), shadowed by the unresolved art-chest cover story.

# Characters

## Zinnia
- **Class/Level:** Monk 6 (Way of the Drunken Master) · **Player:** kostadis1 · Elf, he/him
- **Personality & motivations:** A masked, disfigured spy driven not by ideology but by operational precision and the elimination of chaos. He trusts almost no one, sees through every mask — religious, political, personal — and speaks the unvarnished truth even when it breaks bonds. He believes in "operational hygiene": exit the kill zone before it turns indiscriminate.
- **Relationships:** Zephyr's most trusted knife and strategic anchor. Silent, clinical coordination with Sequoia. Openly distrusts Calmer, whom he judges to be "going too deep" into his Earth Temple cover ("You're not a real cleric"). No longer reports to any external temple.
- **Items of significance:** Cloak of Elvenkind; +1 dagger; three vials of holy water.

## Zephyr "King of Hell" Cross
- **Class/Level:** Rogue 6 (Assassin) · **Player:** kostadis1 · Tiefling, Neutral
- **Personality & motivations:** A cold pragmatist who seeks control rather than wealth — power as a ladder he intends to climb to the top ("killing the boss makes you the boss"). Distrusts loyalty; trusts raw survival instinct. Has begun internalizing the role of Earth Temple prophet, blurring his own identity with the doctrine he wears.
- **Relationships:** De facto leader of the absorbed Earth Temple. Values Kelno for his honest hunger to survive; bound a salamander by blood pact; subjugated the Fire Temple's leadership. Increasingly wary of Calmer as an unpredictable liability, and suspects "something else may be wearing his voice."
- **Items of significance:** Chimera's control chain; +1 dagger; pistol; Dust of Disappearance; smoldering studded leather; salamander blood pact.

## Sequoia
- **Class/Level:** Rogue 6 (Arcane Trickster) · **Player:** kostadis1 · Halfling, Neutral Good
- **Personality & motivations:** The group's quiet decider — final calls in a single word ("Yep"), surgical execution, and a talent for letting enemies destroy themselves. He openly admits his moral compromises (skimming treasure, manipulating loot) yet Frostbrand names him neutral good, a tension he denies.
- **Relationships:** Trusted partner to Zephyr; silent tactical ally to Zinnia; pragmatic alliance with Kelno. Revived Calmer but no longer includes him in strategic decisions. Bonded to the sentient sword Frostbrand, which chose him. Never formally severed from Verbobonc.
- **Items of significance:** **Frostbrand** (sentient ice longsword); two shortswords and a shortbow; Potion of Gaseous Form; thieves' tools.

## Calmer
- **Class/Level:** Cleric 6 (War Domain) · **Player:** kostadis · Human
- **Personality & motivations:** Once a cleric of St. Cuthbert, now psychologically fractured — he can no longer speak his god's name, and his prayers come out sideways as complaint. He clings to titles and hierarchy ("Prophet Calmer, Prophet of the Earth Temple") to avoid the silence of having lost everything he believed in; the cover identity is increasingly writing his objections for him.
- **Relationships:** Self-declared Chief Cleric and Prophet of the Earth Temple, with Dren Halveth installed as his subordinate commander and physical guardian. Still seeks Zinnia's counsel — the last vestige of his former self. Tolerated by Zephyr as a useful figurehead; benched from front-line operations.
- **Items of significance:** Mace of Smiting; +1 plate mail; holy symbol (cudgel); scarab inscribed "TZGY."

# Party Dynamics

The Obsidian Edge is a covert cell built on cold competence rather than affection. **Sequoia and Zephyr** form the decision-making core — Zephyr sets direction, Sequoia executes and quietly initiates the critical moves — while **Zinnia** serves as the group's knife, conscience, and truth-teller, the one member who reliably resists charm and calls out self-deception in the others.

The central tension is **Calmer**. His slide from St. Cuthbert cleric into genuine Earth Temple "prophet" is exactly what Zinnia warned against ("going too deep"), and it now reads as a real fracture rather than a cover story. Zephyr sees him as a liability and suspects something is wearing his voice; Sequoia revived him but has cut him out of strategy. Yet the party deliberately sustains the fiction that **Calmer leads them**, because the Greater Temple believes it — a shared lie that is both a shield and an exposure risk, made sharper by the fact that the party now has no healer while he is down.

Their **shared goal** is unambiguous: having broken all three elemental cults and occupied their levels, the party is consolidating power and turning toward the Deep/Greater Temple — the hidden authority behind the whole war — as reconnaissance-risk, not conquest, until Calmer or another source of healing is restored.
