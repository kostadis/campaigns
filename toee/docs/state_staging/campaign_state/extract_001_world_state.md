*Authoritative canon reference. Current-state as of Chapter 31. Read the Open Threads and Canon Events sections for unresolved hooks and chronology.*

## CORE SITUATION (read first)

The four player characters — **Zephyr, Zinnia, Sequoia, and Calmer** — operate as an infiltration cell known (to their marks) as the **Obsidian Edge**. They have burrowed into the ruined Temple of Elemental Evil and deliberately set its four elemental cults against one another — *"Ran a three-way war and let it eat itself."* They now effectively control the gutted temple complex.

**Critical interpretation for the reader:** The grand titles attached to the party in faction records — Zephyr "leader of the Earth Temple," Calmer "Prophet of the Earth Temple," Dren Halveth "Supreme Commander of Earth Temple Forces" — are **assumed cover identities**, not real institutional appointments. When a temple faction "commands the Obsidian Edge as its vanguard," that is the deceived faction's external view of the party. The Obsidian Edge answers to no one but itself.

**Current party position:** Deep in the Temple complex, having conquered/occupied the **Water Temple** and **Air Temple** levels and absorbed the remnants of the **Fire Temple**. Zephyr, Zinnia, and Sequoia are active together. Calmer is benched — incapacitated and psychologically fractured, under guard.

**The next frontier:** the **Deep Temple / Greater Temple** — a rumored hidden authority behind the entire elemental war (see Open Threads).

## THE PARTY (Player Characters)

### Zephyr — Tiefling, infiltrator/operative

- **Role:** Strategic apex of the cell; poses as de facto Earth Temple leader after eliminating rivals. Rejects the "assassin" label; styles himself a leader.
- **Identity note:** Tiefling, male (canonical). The "human cursed into this form" claim was an in-fiction deception; his horns are consistently referenced.
- **Kit:** Chimera control chain (seized in battle), enchanted dagger, short sword, pistol (treats as 1d10 crossbow), Dust of Disappearance, thaumaturgy. Bound by a struck-through blood pact with a salamander (soul/ownership clauses removed).
- **Skills:** Sneak attack specialist; expert at intimidation, deception, impersonation, and tactical/environmental reading.
- **Drives:** Power as an ever-rising hierarchy he intends to top ("killing the boss makes you the boss"). Distrusts loyalty; values raw survival instinct. Wants to "go home" — origin unnamed. Suspects something else may be "wearing Calmer's voice."

### Zinnia — Elf, masked knife/spymaster
- **Status:** Alive. Active. Air Temple level.
- **PRONOUNS: he/him — authoritative** per player and `voice/zinnia_voice.md`. Later-chapter "she/her" usages are VTT degendering errors; sweep and correct on every recap pass. *Do not treat later usage as an in-fiction change.*
- **Aliases (transcription noise):** Ziinnia, Zinia.
- **Role:** Primary infiltrator, assassin, and the cell's truth-teller/moral compass. Zephyr's most trusted knife.
- **Kit:** Cloak of elvenkind, magical dagger (both acquired ch07; still presumed carried).
- **Background:** Face disfigured by disease and violence — hence the mask. Childhood trauma (hags who ate children). Recruited by the Water Temple, defected to the Fire Temple, then to the Obsidian Edge. Real name never revealed. Motivated by operational precision and "operational hygiene," not ideology. Sees through every mask — and openly confronts Calmer for "going in too deep."
- **Lingering wounds:** Injuries from Gremag (ch14) and Otyugh poison (ch24); later hints of ongoing physical strain.

### Sequoia — Halfling, Arcane Trickster (rogue)
- **Status:** Alive. Active. Air Temple inner sanctum.
- **Identity note:** He/him. Race is Halfling (ch31: "Good thing I am small"). **Casts arcane spells legitimately as an Arcane Trickster** — this is by design, not an error.
- **Role:** Quiet decision-anchor of the active trio; strategist, enforcer, negotiator/liar ("Housekeeping!"). Newly gained Uncanny Dodge.
- **Kit:** **Frostbrand** (sentient ice longsword — chose him after rejecting Zinnia and Calmer; reads him as neutral good and speaks to him), two shortswords, a ranged weapon, Potion of Gaseous Form.
- **Background:** Originally sent by **Verbobonc** to investigate the cultists (ch08); now operates independently (no formal severance recorded). Morally compromised (skims and redistributes treasure) yet Frostbrand insists he is neutral good — an unresolved internal tension.

### Calmer — Cleric of St. Cuthbert (undercover as Earth Temple "Prophet")
- **Status:** Alive, severely wounded, **psychologically fractured. Benched** — behind a curtain in the Upper Temple, guarded by Dren Halveth and two mercenaries to keep him out of combat.
- **Role:** Wears the cover identity of "Prophet Calmer, Prophet of the Earth Temple," and the cover is now writing his objections for him — he is assimilating into the role he faked.
- **Kit:** Mace of Smiting, plate mail (damaged), cudgel holy symbol.
- **Abilities:** Toll the Dead, Guiding Bolt, Sacred Flame, Cure/Healing Word, Guidance, Spectral Guardians, Rite of Turning. Now channels a poorly-understood **necrotic power** (used to kill Romag and the Chimera).
- **Faith crisis:** Slew a wraith with a self-destroying blast of divine radiance, leaving permanent necrotic trauma and an inner void. Can no longer speak St. Cuthbert's name in the temple; his prayers "come out sideways as complaints." His St. Cuthbert faith is **broken, not renounced** — internal collapse, not confirmed conversion.

## FACTIONS

### The Obsidian Edge = The Party
Not an external faction. The name Hartsch pinned on the four PCs. All temple-leadership titles held by the party are cover. This is the single most important consolidation in this document.

### Temple of Elemental Evil
- **Status:** Fractured; its four elemental cults driven into open civil war (largely by the party). Physical temple: a ruined, rune-sealed subterranean complex.
- **Doctrine:** Cosmic truth is chaotic elemental conflict, with Earth destined to reduce all to dust. Once bound by the **Circle of Eight** after the Battle of Emridy Meadows.
- **The Deep Temple:** rumored hidden higher authority manipulating the whole conflict. Nature, location, and identity **unknown** — the campaign's central mystery.

### Earth Temple
- Outwardly the dominant cult (held Fire Temple territory, deployed necromantically-raised ghoul soldiers). Inwardly a **hollow shell** — leadership assassinated, morale broken, base largely abandoned. Its "commander" (Dren) and "Prophet" (Calmer) are the party's people. New "Earth Temple cleric" of ch31 may be a convert, puppet, or infiltrator.

### Fire Temple
- **Defeated and absorbed** into Earth Temple control. Leadership (Alremm) dead; surviving clerics subjugated; salamanders and bound monsters now serve under coercion. The Fire Temple's former commander serves as a retainer under Calmer. Physical hall still stands, occupied, with its treasure (fire-pit hoard) claimed by the party after the salamander's surrender.

### Water Temple
- Former **strongest** cult, led by the late Prophet Belsornig. Orchestrated the Chimera scheme. **Broken** after Hartsch's unauthorized raid (which massacred the Broken Blades). Now occupied by the party but still lethal: **active undead guardians (one wraith + seven specters)** and animated gargoyles defend the second level; trolls were "on contract" (status unconfirmed).

### Air Temple
- Weakest cult; reduced to Kelno + a few hidden agents using its insignia on **forged bribe coins** to frame rivals. Now occupied/controlled by the party. Its leader Kelno is a prisoner. A wraith haunted its halls (Kelno deludedly believed it an air elemental).

### Verbobonc / Hommlet council
- The regional theocratic power. Its Hommlet agents (Burne, Rufus, Elmo) now publicly back direct action against the resurgent Temple. Invested in the **art-gift-to-Celene** cover-up. Council: Burne, Rufus, Jaroo, Ostler Gundigoot.

## LOCATIONS

### The Temple of Elemental Evil (dungeon complex)
- Ruined, rune-sealed multi-level subterranean structure NE of the monastery grounds; walls of leering faces and obscene carvings. Bronze doors chained with glowing silver runes. Divided into the four warring elemental temples over deeper levels of "ancient evil."
- **Earth Temple level:** Largely abandoned by the party; fractured, hidden loyalists/gnolls/undead may remain. Secret doors exploited by both party and gnolls. Site of Romag's assassination and the wraith's death.
- **Fire Temple hall:** Standing, occupied, leaderless. Gold-skull throne, tapestried chamber, barricaded stairway, secret north door, claimed fire-pit treasure.
- **Water Temple sanctum:** Conquered but lethal. Coin/gem/seashell altar ("drowned fortune"), bronze underwater bas-relief, eel-and-octopus stone idol, gargoyle pillars/plinths, luminous azurite/malachite floor. Active undead guardians.
- **Air Temple chamber:** Kelno's nihilistic quarters — skull-lined shelves, scrolls, mead crock, narrow bed. Source of the forged bribe coins. Now under party control.

### Hommlet
- Small village at Verbobonc's edge near the Gnarley Forest. Administrative hub riddled with competing factions (Verbobonc officials, Temple agents, Lolth-cult remnants, bandits). Party's western base — used to report/regroup. The party holds the **Chest of Rao** as leverage here. Contains the Inn of the Welcome Wench, Terjon's Temple of St. Cuthbert, Jaroo's grove, and Burne & Rufus's tower.
  - **Inn of the Welcome Wench:** Ostler Gundigoot's inn; central intelligence/coordination hub. Mandatory three-night stay policy.
  - **Burne and Rufus's tower:** The only completed structure of their castle complex; a fortified seat of judgment, residence, and feast hall (murder holes, drawbridge-door, seating for 40).

### Nulb
- Lawless frontier town NE of the Kron Hills, ruled by **Madame Selentis** amid competing gangs (Crimson Reapers, Iron Lash, River Pirates) and Earth/Water Temple recruiters. The designated infiltration gateway to the Temple. Origin point of the **Chimera's control gear** (River Pirate stamps on its collar/chains — they were paid to haul it to the Fire Temple).
  - **Waterside Hostel** *(canonical; variants: Waterstead, Watersteide):* Dick Rentsch's dilapidated base; neutral ground for covert deals and Broken Blades recruitment.

### Moathouse
- Former Temple outpost east of the main road in boggy marshland. **Cleared** by the party (Lareth's cult, ogre, bugbears, zombies, green slime defeated; treasure looted). Now under Verbobonc observation per Elmo's orders. Retains traps (iron grate, false wall) of unconfirmed status.

### Sheernobb *(variant: Shernobb)*
- Gnome settlement in the **Kron Hills**; burrow homes, central Moot Hall, famed for enchanted honey. Home of Pasmarie Clee. Recent party goodwill from returning stolen honey. Contains the **Honey Haven Tavern** *(variant: Honey Tavern Inn)* — gnome-scaled tavern, firefly-globe fireplace, party base during the Black Earth investigation.

### Kron Hills
- Rugged gnome territory (Verbobonc can't deploy troops there without political fallout). Contains the **Hall of the Scarlet Moon** and a former Fire Temple encampment. River network worked by Captain Tolub's pirates.

### Gnarley Forest
- Vast woodland straddling Verbobonc, Celene, the Kron Hills, and more. Home to woodsmen, Sylvan Elves, treants, unicorns; rumored to harbor a monster (unverified).

### Abbey of Beory
- Home of Abbot Brother Eldin; guardian of Chest of Rao lore; site of the party's reward and the "Path of Righteousness" trials.
