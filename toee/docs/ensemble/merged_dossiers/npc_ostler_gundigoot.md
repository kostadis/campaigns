### Ostler Gundigoot

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Hommlet, operating the Inn of the Welcome Wench  
**Allegiance/faction:** Local council of Hommlet (member alongside Jaroo, Burne, and Rufus)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
**Current assignment / role:** Innkeeper of the Inn of the Welcome Wench; local council member  
**Key relationships:** Reports to and collaborates with council members Jaroo, Burne, and Rufus; directed the party to consult Jaroo (the druid); paid for the party’s room until the 19th; expressed approval of their decision to consult Jaroo before outsiders  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Questioned the party about the sudden departure of the merchants in Hommlet and was successfully bluffed with the story of a magical chest leaving town.  
- Gathered with the council (Burne, Rufus, Jaroo) and accepted the party’s proof of the Temple of Elemental Evil’s resurgence and Lareth’s death, agreeing they must go to Nulb.  
- Previously offered a job to expose Furnoc’s theft of guest belongings, with three free nights as incentive.  
- Enforces a mandatory three-night stay policy for adventurers.  
- Pays for the party’s lodging until the 19th.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Subtly aligned with local power structures, favoring Jaroo and distrustful of “interloping outsiders.”  
- Maintains control over guest goods: sells abandoned items after three days.  
- Uses his position as innkeeper and council member to influence the party’s choices, steering them toward Jaroo and away from external influences.
