### Gnarley Forest

The Gnarley Forest is a sprawling, ecologically rich woodland straddling the borders of Verbobonc, Viscounty, Dyvers, the Domain of Greyhawk, the Wild Coast, Celene, and the Kron Hills. It serves as a critical ecological barrier and cultural crossroads, part of a vast contiguous forest system that includes the Welkwood, Celene, and Suss Forest. The forest features fertile glades and valleys, conceals hidden paths and forgotten lore, and is home to ancient magic, woodsmen, Sylvan Elves, treants, and unicorns. It is rumored to harbor a monster, though its current location or identity remains unconfirmed.
