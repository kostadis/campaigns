### Mari Clee

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Honey Haven Tavern  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent owner of the Honey Haven Tavern  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Enchanted mead (in a small keg), moonbeam pie (served on premises)  
**Current assignment / role:** Barkeep and proprietor of the Honey Haven Tavern; actively leverages Zephyr’s perceived leadership to secure payments for meals and luxuries  
**Key relationships:** Zephyr (treated as the de facto payer for the group); patrons of the tavern  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Offered free lodging for a week and a small keg of enchanted mead as a reward for recovering stolen barrels.  
- Routinely directs meal and drink orders (including moonbeam pie and honey meed) toward Zephyr, asserting he will pay.  
- Publicly and repeatedly asserts, “I assume the leader, here, will pay for everything?” — a recurring tactic to shift financial burden onto Zephyr.  

## Uncertainty  
- It is unclear whether “meed” is a typo for “mead” — the term appears inconsistently (meed/mead), but context confirms it refers to the enchanted honey beverage.  
- The exact status of the stolen barrels is unknown — Mari Clee offered a reward for finding them, but it is not confirmed whether they were recovered.  
- It is not stated whether Zephyr has actually paid any of the charges — only that Mari Clee expects and insists he will.
