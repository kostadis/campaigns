### Water Temple

**Current status:** Conquered ruin, now a battleground.  
**Current location:** Subterranean sanctum within the Obsidian Edge complex.  
**Current control:** Unclaimed; occupied by the party and haunted by undead guardians.  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- A round, waist-high altar with a concave basin containing coins, gems, and seashells (a “drowned fortune”).  
- A great bronze bas-relief on the west wall depicting an underwater vista with seaweed, shells, and a fish-head spewing water into four tiered basins.  
- Four stone gargoyle-crowned pillars (one per corner), each animated when the temple is disturbed.  
- An idol of serpentine stone, taller than a man, composed of knotted eels, octopi, and fish, located in a southern alcove.  
- Seaweed drapery hung behind the idol.  
- Polished azurite and malachite flooring emitting a soft, cloudy greenish light.  
- Bronze doors and marble thresholds.  
- Four triangular plinths, each topped with a bronze gargoyle covered in verdigris.  
- Walls of polished blue-swirled-deep-green stone with ambient luminosity.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- No active leadership or cultists remain; original faction (Belsornig’s cult) is extinguished.  
- Undead guardians remain active: one wraith and seven specters block access to the second level.  
- Trolls are on contract, but their current presence or status is unconfirmed.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Survived and repelled Hartsch’s unauthorized raid, resulting in the massacre of the Broken Blades.  
- Became a death trap after the raid, with undead deployed as guardians.  
- Now a contested site where the party has asserted dominion amid lingering magical defenses and environmental hazards.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The temple was once a sacred site of Belsornig, designed as a sanctum of water magic and ritual.  
- Its architecture and artifacts suggest ancient, possibly pre-Obsidian Edge origins, with magic woven into stone, bronze, and floor.  
- The “drowned fortune” on the altar implies ritual offerings or a hidden reservoir of wealth tied to its original purpose.  
- The ambient luminosity and brine-scented air suggest lingering enchantments tied to deep water or drowned realms.  

## Uncertainty
- Conflict between [ch30] describing the Water Temple as “lightly guarded” and [ch29] describing it as “heavily defended” and “the strongest faction” — the latter (ch29) describes its state *before* the raid; the former (ch30) describes its state *after* the massacre, but it is unclear whether the trolls are still present or active.  
- Unclear whether the four “stone gargoyles” on pillars (ch31) are the same as the four “bronze gargoyles” on plinths (ch30) — possible duplication or confusion in description.  
- The status of the trolls on contract is mentioned in ch30 but not confirmed in ch31 — are they alive, dead, or departed?  
- The phrase “conquered ruin” (ch30) contradicts the continued presence of active undead guardians and animated gargoyles — is “ruin” metaphorical, or has the structure been physically breached?  
- The source of the “soft cloudy greenish light” is attributed to “old magic” in both ch30 and ch31, but its origin or caster is unnamed.

### Water Temple

**Current status:** Active, but in civil conflict.  
**Current location:** Deep levels of the Temple of Elemental Evil; now claimed and occupied by Calmer and his party.  
**Allegiance/faction:** One of the four elemental temples under the Temple of Elemental Evil; currently the strongest remaining faction.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Magical architecture: polished azurite and malachite slabs with sentient, reactive magic that seeps a soft cloudy greenish light from the floor.  
- Sentient defenses: gargoyles, rotating idols, and spiritual retribution mechanisms that activate upon improper touch.  
- Previously held: a drowned fortune (now unclaimed but present).  
- Formerly led by Prophet Belsornig, who orchestrated the Chimera attack and is now likely dead or displaced (no current mention of his presence).  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Now a contested sacred site under the control of Calmer and his party.  
- Previously engaged in factional warfare against Fire, Earth, and Air Temples.  
- Formerly recruited the Broken Blades in Nulb, using propaganda and financial incentives to undermine the Earth Temple.  
- Previously allied with Vesrek and Prophet Belsornig to frame the Fire Temple and orchestrate the Chimera attack.  
- Now internally fractured: cult fanatics guard doors, and a figure (possibly the prophet) is last seen reading scrolls while guards are killed.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Orchestrated the Chimera attack as a win-win scheme to eliminate rivals or bankrupt the Fire Temple.  
- Engaged in sustained elemental warfare using water gargoyles against the Fire Temple.  
- Was the target of Hartsch’s unauthorized raid and the site of the Broken Blades’ massacre.  
- Now reacts autonomously to intrusion: gargoyles detach from pillars, idols rotate, and spiritual retribution activates when improperly touched.  
- Has been seized and claimed by Calmer’s party, who now occupy and presumably control the site.

## Uncertainty
- The current status of Prophet Belsornig: last seen orchestrating events in ch27, but unmentioned since; presumed dead, missing, or displaced — no confirmation.  
- Whether Calmer’s party has fully subjugated the temple or merely occupies it — the temple’s sentient defenses still activate, suggesting it is not fully controlled.  
- The nature of the “cult fanatics” guarding doors in ch30: are they loyal remnants of the old hierarchy, or new occupants using the temple’s structure?  
- Attribution of the Chimera attack: ch26–27 attribute it to Belsornig as the Water Temple’s prophet, but ch27 also says the party falsely accused the Water Temple — implying the truth may be ambiguous or manipulated.  
- Whether the Water Temple still exists as an organized faction beyond its physical structure — ch29 calls it the strongest faction, but ch30–31 suggest internal collapse and occupation, creating contradiction in its operational status.
