### Dick Rentsch

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Waterside Hostel, Nulb (primary base of operations)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Loyal agent and recruiter for the Earth Temple, operating under Romag’s authority but with independent influence that challenges Romag’s hierarchy  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Map of gnoll territories (provided to party), access to secret passages within the Temple of Elemental Evil, authority to deploy and coordinate covert missions  
**Current assignment / role:** Recruiter and liaison for the Earth Temple in Nulb; tasked with replacing unreliable gnolls and bugbears with disciplined mercenaries (specifically the Broken Blades); coordinates covert operations, including recruitment, sabotage, and infiltration; maintains covert alliances with figures from rival factions (e.g., Wat of the Water Temple) for strategic expediency  
**Key relationships:**  
- Reports to Romag, but operates with autonomy that challenges Romag’s hierarchy  
- Recruited Commander Joradin into the Earth Temple  
- Employed Wat (alleged Water Temple loyalist) despite suspicions of dual allegiance  
- Arranged meeting between party and Romag  
- Supervised Alremm’s use of werewolves as a test for new recruits  
- Imprisoned Jorin Pike Kale for misconduct  
- His men captured several merchants  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Recruited the Broken Blades as a new force of disciplined mercenaries for the Earth Temple  
- Maintained Wat on payroll despite his suspected allegiance to the Water Temple, justifying it as necessary until the Earth Temple secures complete power  
- Sent the party into Air Temple territory under false pretenses, depositing them directly into enemy-controlled space  
- Conducted private meetings in the Waterside Hostel’s back room to coordinate missions, including one with Dren Halveth of the Broken Blades  
- Oversaw the use of werewolves as a lethal test for new recruits in the Temple’s secret chamber  
- Imprisoned Jorin Pike Kale for making a pass at Dalia  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Describes the central altar chamber of the Temple of Elemental Evil as his “happy place,” indicating deep personal alignment with its ethos  
- Expresses weariness with making deals with evil powers that want him dead, suggesting internal conflict or moral fatigue masked by pragmatism  
- Believes in consolidating power through ruthless pragmatism: “until they had secured complete power, everyone had to work together”  
- Uses deception, misdirection, and lethal tests to filter and control recruits
