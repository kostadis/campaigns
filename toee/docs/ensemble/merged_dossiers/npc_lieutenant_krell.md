### Lieutenant Krell

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Site of ritual sacrifice in Earth Temple territory (presumed destroyed or entombed by earth elementals)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly Romag’s agent; betrayed to ally with gnolls and bugbears; sought to join the Fire Temple under Alremm  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Peculiar hand crossbow contraption—a pistol of his own devising (last seen in use during ambush; fate unknown)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** None (deceased). Last known role: traitorous operative plotting rebellion against the Earth Temple. Last seen encouraging gnolls in ambush; directly targeted Calmer with his pistol.  
**Defining recent actions:** Betrayed the Earth Temple by aiding gnolls and bugbears; attempted to defect to the Fire Temple; captured by Romag; subjected to ritual sacrifice and killed by earth elementals.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact sequence of capture and sacrifice is implied but not explicitly attributed: Was Krell captured *by* Romag, or by Earth Temple forces acting under Romag’s orders?  
- The final disposition of his pistol is unknown—was it recovered, lost, or buried with him?  
- While Romag declared him a spy and ordered his death, it is unclear whether Romag directly orchestrated the ritual or authorized it after Krell’s capture by another party.  
- The phrase “subjected to a ritual sacrifice by Romag” may conflate Romag as the entity vs. Romag as the faction—was it Romag personally, or agents acting in Romag’s name?
