### Crimson Reapers

**Current status:** Active  
**Current location:** Operating behind the four elemental temples, specifically occupying the room next door to the Broken Blades in Nulb  
**Allegiance/faction:** Serving a deeper, unnamed organization behind the four elemental temples (not directly affiliated with any of the four temples)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Stolen shipment of weapons (contained in a chest claimed as temple property)  
**Current assignment / role:** Seizing temple property, extorting local operatives (specifically Dren), enforcing recruitment through death threats  
**Defining recent actions:** Stole a weapons shipment from Dren, claimed it as temple property, issued a midnight ultimatum to Dren to join their organization or be killed; physically confronted Calmer when he inquired about the chest, asserting authority over temple assets  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Acting under orders from a hidden superior organization that uses the elemental temples as a front; their goal is not mere criminal dominance but controlled appropriation of temple resources and personnel  

## Uncertainty
- The identity of the “deeper organization” behind the elemental temples is unnamed and unrevealed.  
- It is unclear whether Zelt the Rat still leads the Crimson Reapers — ch23 does not mention him, and ch17’s leadership claim is superseded by newer context but not explicitly contradicted.  
- Whether the Crimson Reapers are now directly answering to one of the four temples (despite denying it) or to an external force remains ambiguous.  
- The exact nature of the “temple property” claim (legal, magical, or fabricated) is not clarified.
