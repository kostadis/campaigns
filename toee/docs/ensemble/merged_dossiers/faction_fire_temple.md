### Fire Temple

**Current status:** Defeated and dismantled as an independent entity.  
**Current location:** No longer operates as a sovereign faction; its former sacred spaces and infrastructure are occupied and repurposed under Earth Temple command.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Subjugated under the Earth Temple. Former leadership eliminated; surviving members are absorbed, subjugated, or dead.  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- Symbols of power (robes, amulets, insignia) repurposed under Earth Temple authority.  
- Fire Temple Amulet and other ritual artifacts now in Earth Temple custody.  
- Former leadership’s equipment used to mark former clerics as subordinates.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Former leader Alrrem is dead or deposed; no active leadership remains.  
- Last surviving clerics are now subordinates under Earth Temple command.  
- Former Fire Temple agents (including salamanders) now operate under Earth Temple orders, bound to serve and eliminate Water Temple stragglers.  
- The Fire Temple’s former commander now serves as a retainer under Calmer.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Engaged in a three-way elemental war with the Water and Earth Temples, initiated by manipulation from the Water Temple’s scheme involving the Chimera.  
- Attacked the Water Temple after being fed false evidence of betrayal.  
- Maintained a fortified barricade at the second-level stairway, which was deliberately destroyed to trigger internal collapse.  
- Suffered heavy losses; its forces fractured into chaos during the Earth Temple’s assault.  
- Now fully absorbed: its infrastructure, personnel, and symbols repurposed under Earth Temple control.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact fate of Alrrem is ambiguous: ch31 states leadership was “eliminated” and “the last one. Ours now,” but does not specify if Alrrem was killed, captured, or forced into servitude.  
- Whether any Fire Temple clerics still exist as independent actors (beyond those subjugated under Earth Temple) is unconfirmed — ch31 says “clerics are dead or subjugated,” but does not rule out hidden survivors.  
- The identity of “the last one” referred to in ch31 (“The last one. Ours now.”) is not named — it may be Alrrem, another cleric, or a symbolic figure.  
- The current status of the salamanders and other bound monsters: ch31 says they “now operate under Earth Temple orders,” but whether this is voluntary, magically enforced, or coerced is not specified.  
- The origin of the “broken throne” referenced in ch31 is not explained — whether it was physically destroyed or merely symbolically abandoned.

### Fire Temple

**Current status:** Active but diminished, leaderless, and in a state of tactical retreat.  
**Current location:** Still standing and occupied, located within the greater temple complex.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent cult organization, formerly associated with the warlock, the amulet, and the Chimera weapon; now isolated and caught between rival factions.  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- A throne with a gold skull on the back, located in a chamber lined with red, orange, and yellow tapestries and lit by torchlight on lacquered wood.  
- A fire pit containing guarded treasure, now claimed by the party after the salamander’s surrender.  
- A secret door opening north, recently discovered and activated.  
- Massive brass doors covered in leering faces and horrible scenes.  
- Barricades of pews and rubble at the stairway to the second level, maintained by bugbears.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acting as a defensive stronghold under degraded command.  
- No known leadership; cultists are leaderless.  
- Formerly attacked the Greater Temple and attempted to assassinate Romag’s allies; now under threat of destruction by Romag and the party.  
- In hostile standoff with the Water Temple, following Alrrem’s declaration of war after receiving a note under the door.  
- Recently subdued by the party after a failed incursion; now transitioning from combat to negotiation.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Attempted and failed to force entry into the Greater Temple.  
- Declared war on the Water Temple via note delivered under the brass doors.  
- Maintained barricades and bugbear defenses within its interior.  
- Lost leadership; cultists are now leaderless.  
- Surrendered its final guardian (salamander) and lost control of its fire pit treasure.  
- Party now occupies its inner sanctum and has access to its secret north door.

## Uncertainty
- The exact status of the warlock: whether they are still alive, present, or connected to the current cultists is unconfirmed.  
- Whether the “faking” mentioned in ch07 is still relevant — the later facts confirm physical presence and active occupation, but the origin of the deception (if any) is unresolved.  
- Who delivered the note under the door to Alrrem — unattributed.  
- The current whereabouts or fate of the Fire Temple’s former leader(s) — only known to be gone, not how or by whom.  
- Whether the secret north door leads to another location or is merely an escape route — its destination is unknown.  
- Whether the Fire Temple’s association with the Chimera attack is still operational or merely historical — the entity is leaderless and diminished, but no confirmation that the Chimera weapon is still in use.
