### Rufus

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Hommlet  
**Allegiance/faction:** Verbobonc theocracy (political/military arm); local council member of Hommlet  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated beyond clothing  
**Current assignment / role:** Member of Hommlet’s local council; formerly military commander and captain of the militia; agent of Verbobonc tasked with monitoring the Moathouse and the annual art gift to the Kingdom of Celene  
**Key relationships:** Partner to Burne in Verbobonc’s occupation of Hommlet; reports to or coordinates with Verbobonc authorities; recently worked with the party after they presented proof of the Temple of Elemental Evil’s resurgence  
**Defining recent actions:**

- Urged the party to confront the Moathouse.  
- Attended the local council meeting in Hommlet where the party presented undeniable proof of the Temple of Elemental Evil’s resurgence and Lareth’s death.

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Deeply invested in the political symbolism of the annual art gift to Celene; fears exposure of Verbobonc’s deception if the missing chest is recovered.

- Previously dismissed evidence of the Temple’s resurgence as insufficient for national intervention, but changed stance after receiving undeniable proof.
