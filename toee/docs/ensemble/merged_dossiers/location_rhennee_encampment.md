### Rhennee Encampment

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current state:** Active settlement, now vibrant with music and dancing following Serena’s return.  
- **Location:** Near Nulb.  
- **Current condition:** Ash-laden, but no longer dominated by river rot (the rot has receded since Serena’s return).  
- **Key occupants:** Eryk Velkori and Vethra reside here.  
- **Defining recent action:** The encampment erupted in music and dancing after Serena’s return, transforming it from a site of grief and decay into a place of renewed celebration.  
- **Notable relationships:** Eryk Velkori is a resident and was encountered here by the party during their investigation.  

## Uncertainty
- The current presence of Vethra is inferred from the first fact but not confirmed in later ones; no recent interaction or observation of Vethra is recorded after the transformation.  
- Whether river rot is *completely* gone or merely suppressed is ambiguous — the fact states it “erupted in music and dancing after Serena’s return,” implying a change, but does not explicitly state the rot has vanished.  
- No information on whether the encampment has new inhabitants beyond Eryk and Vethra.
