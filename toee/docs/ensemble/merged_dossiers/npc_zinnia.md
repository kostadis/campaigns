### Zinnia

*Also known as: Ziinnia, Zinia (VTT transcription variants)*

**Current Status:** Alive.  
**Current Location:** Within the Greater Temple complex, likely on the Air Temple level or adjacent zones, following the failed breach of the west door and the revelation of hidden forces.  
**Allegiance/Faction:** Core member of the Obsidian Edge; formerly a defecting agent of the Fire Temple, now operating as Zephyr’s most trusted lieutenant and de facto operational lead.  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Magical cloak of elvenkind (acquired from ogre’s nest in ch07)  
- Magical dagger (won in fair debate after ch07)  
- Black robe symbolizing the Fire Temple (worn for infiltration, ch07)  
- Kept a share of looted treasure, slightly larger than others’ (ch25)  
- Forged letter of assassination (presented as proof in ch30)  
- Possibly the key to the Water Temple’s inner sanctum (implied by attempted breach in ch30)  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- Primary infiltrator, tactical observer, and assassin for Zephyr’s faction.  
- Acts as the group’s moral compass and truth-teller, unafraid to confront deception—even among allies.  
- Recognized by Zephyr as his most reliable knife and strategic anchor.  
- Deeply distrustful of Calmer’s growing immersion in his Earth Temple cover identity; has directly confronted him, stating, *“You’re not a real cleric for the Earth Temple”* and *“I think you’re going in too deep.”*  
- Maintains a silent, clinical bond with Sequoia and Zephyr; the only one who consistently challenges Hartsch’s leadership and Kelno’s motives.  
- No longer reports to any external temple; operates independently under Zephyr’s command, though his loyalties remain ambiguous.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Led the assassination of Romag with precise dagger strikes, cementing the Obsidian Edge’s rise.  
- Successfully infiltrated and eliminated the Fire Temple warlock and key cultists during the temple conflict.  
- Broke the charm on Zephyr, Sequoia, and Calmer by attacking them in ch25, saving the party from harpy domination.  
- Identified hidden forces of the Greater Temple operating in plain sight (ch30).  
- Attempted and failed to breach the west door of the Water Temple (ch30), revealing a critical vulnerability.  
- Delivered the true, unvarnished speech that rallied the Broken Blades in the slaughterhouse chamber (ch29).  
- Continues to observe, catalog, and analyze every behavioral, environmental, and political detail without sentiment.  

**Revealed Motivations or Secrets:**  
- Zinnia is an Elf (not a Tiefling — see `docs/party.md`) whose face is disfigured by disease and violence, which is why he wears a mask; his past is marked by trauma, including childhood exposure to hags who ate children.  
- He is not motivated by ideology, but by operational precision and the elimination of chaos.  
- He understands the art of spying better than anyone in the group and recognizes when others are losing themselves in their roles—especially Calmer.  
- He has lived the life of a silent operator: redistributing treasure, taking unilateral decisions, and acting without consultation.  
- He does not rally people—he observes, waits, moves. His presence alone is the anchor.  
- He believes in *operational hygiene*: exiting kill zones before they become indiscriminate.  
- He sees through all masks—religious, political, personal—and speaks the truth even when it breaks bonds.  

## Uncertainty  
- **Gender Pronouns:** Zinnia is he/him — confirmed by the player and the character's voice notes (`voice/zinnia_voice.md`). "She/her" references that appear in some later-chapter facts (ch28–ch31) are VTT transcription/degendering errors, not an in-fiction change; do not treat later usage as overriding earlier usage on this point. See `notes/vtt_transcription_corrections.md` and the standing correction to sweep this on every recap pass.  
- **Current Wounds:** Sustained severe injuries from Gremag (ch14) and Otyugh poison (ch24). Later mentions of ribs “having opinions” (ch28) and being “slow off his mark” (ch31) imply lingering physical trauma, but no confirmation of full recovery.  
- **Status of Calmer:** Zinnia believes Calmer is “going native” and is not a real cleric—but whether he is still under the influence of charm, deception, or genuine conversion is unresolved.  
- **Exact Location After ch31:** The group is in the Air Temple; Zinnia has just warned against overreaching into unowned rooms. His precise position is not stated.  
- **Ownership of the Cloak and Dagger:** While he acquired them, no later chapter confirms he still carries them—though no evidence suggests they were lost or transferred.  
- **Relationship with the Water Temple:** He was recruited by them, defected to the Fire Temple, then to the Obsidian Edge. Is he still being watched? Are they aware of his betrayal? Unclear.  
- **Fate of the Black Jay:** Mentioned in ch03 as a bandit informant, but never revisited. Is he still active? Unknown.  
- **Zinnia’s Original Name:** Never revealed. He is only known as “Zinnia” or “the masked one.”
