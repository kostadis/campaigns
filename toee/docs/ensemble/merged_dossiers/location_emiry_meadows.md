### Emiry Meadows

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current state:** Site of a historic battle and an active arcane ruin.  
- **Control/Occupation:** Unoccupied; no known current controllers.  
- **Notable features:**  
  - Contains a lost wizard’s laboratory beneath its surface.  
  - A tower located near the site of the Battle of Emiry Meadows houses a permanent summoning circle and a mural on its floor.  
- **Recent significance:**  
  - The Battle of Emiry Meadows ended the expansion of the Temple of Elemental Evil.  
  - Spugnoir has revealed the location of the underground wizard laboratory and offered to guide others there.  
  - The tower with the summoning circle is a distinct structure near, but not at, the exact battle site.  

## Uncertainty
- The exact spatial relationship between the battle site, the laboratory, and the tower is ambiguous: the tower is “near” the battle site, but it is unclear whether the laboratory is beneath the battle site, beneath the tower, or elsewhere under the meadows.  
- It is unclear whether the tower and the laboratory are part of the same complex or separate structures.  
- The current status of the tower (occupied, trapped, active) is not specified — only its physical features are described.  
- Spugnoir’s knowledge is confirmed, but it is unknown whether he has led anyone to the site since ch05.
