### Waterside Hostel

A run-down, smoke-filled tavern in Nulb with a sign reading *"Rooms are cheap, lives cheaper."* It is a dilapidated building with sagging timbers and lopsided walls, serving as Dick Rentsch’s base of operations and a neutral ground for covert factional dealings. The establishment hosts uneasy coexistence between Rentsch (contact for the Earth Temple) and the bandit chief from the forest. It is used for covert recruitment efforts and as a meeting point for clandestine arrangements, including the party’s arrangement to meet Dren Halveth.
