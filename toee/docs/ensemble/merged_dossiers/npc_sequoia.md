### Sequoia

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Within the Air Temple, having infiltrated and secured control of its inner sanctum  
**Allegiance/Faction:** De facto leader of Zephyr’s faction; aligned with Kelno as a pragmatic partner in controlling the aftermath of the temple war; no longer loyal to Verbobonc or any external authority  
**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- **Frostbrand** — a sentient, ice-cold longsword that chose Sequoia as its wielder; it speaks to him and recognizes him as neutral good  
- **Two shortswords** — primary melee weapons, used in close combat  
- **One ranged weapon** — likely a crossbow or shortbow, used for precise shots (evidenced by arrow kills and ranged attacks on harpies and ghouls)

  - **Kelno** — Pragmatic partner; alliance formed not from trust but mutual interest in controlling the power vacuum  
  - **Frostbrand** — Sentient weapon that speaks to him; acknowledges Sequoia’s moral ambiguity but accepts him as its ideal wielder  

**Defining Recent Actions:**
