### Temple of St. Cuthbert

- **Current state:** Active and occupied.
- **Current location:** Hommlet.
- **Current control/occupancy:** Prefect Terjon resides here; it serves as a center of civic authority and religious influence.
- **Current possessions / notable features:** Contains a painted rock (evaluated but rejected) and space for formal presentations and deliberations.
- **Current role:** Functions as both a religious site and seat of local governance; used for official announcements and evaluation of discoveries.
- **Defining recent action:** Prefect Terjon received and rejected the painted rock after Calmer presented it as evidence within the temple.

None.
