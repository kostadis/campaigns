### Captain Vesrek

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Unknown (last associated with the Crimson Traitor)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Ally of Belsornig, the Water Temple prophet; captain of the Crimson Traitor  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated beyond command of the Crimson Traitor  
**Current assignment / role:** Captain of the Crimson Traitor; intermediary who sold the Chimera to the Fire Temple on behalf of the Water Temple  
**Defining recent actions:** Sold the Chimera to the Fire Temple, as documented in a wax-sealed ledger bearing the crest of the Crimson Traitor.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Acting as a conduit between the Water Temple (via Belsornig) and the Fire Temple; facilitated the transfer of a chimera under official-looking documentation, suggesting coordinated, possibly covert, inter-temple dealings.

## Uncertainty
- The exact current location of Captain Vesrek or the Crimson Traitor is not stated.

- The current status of the Crimson Traitor (seized, active, scuttled) is unknown.
