### Kron Hills

The Kron Hills are a rugged region northeast of Nulb, serving as the territory of the gnome population whose political sensitivity prevents Verbobonc from deploying troops without risking conflict. The Hall of the Scarlet Moon is located within these hills, and the party arrived there after traveling from Hommlet and passing Shernobb. The region is also the site of a Fire Temple encampment and was the last known location of the stolen chest near Sheernob. The trail from the caravan attack leads into the hills, and the area is part of the dragon’s journey home.
