### Sheernobb

Sheernobb is a small gnome settlement nestled in the Kron Hills, characterized by burrow homes with intricately carved doors and winding forest roads. The village is dominated by the Moot Hall, a central building where governance and community meetings occur. The main road ends at the Moot Hall and splits into two branches—one north and one south—that encircle the hall and converge again on the road leading out of town. Most stores are located along this circular route.

Sheernobb is culturally significant as a crossroads of trade and tradition, known for its beehives and enchanted honey. It is the home of Palsmarie Clee, the beekeeper druid, who resides there and received the returned stolen honey from the party in Chapter 11. The return of the honey and receipt of reward there established recent local goodwill.
