### Kelno

**Current status:** Alive, prisoner of the Greater Temple.  
**Current location:** Lower chamber beneath the Water Temple, held beside Zinnia under Barkinar’s orders.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former Prophet and leader of the Air Temple; now operates as a self-aligned survivor with no formal allegiance, having abandoned the Air Temple’s symbolic authority.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly held, but previously possessed and transferred the *Dagger of Venom* to Zephyr. Maintains a ledger detailing payments to a dozen named informants and a two-week-old note: “set out the second chair.”  
**Spells / feats / wounds:** None specified.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Prisoner under Barkinar’s surveillance; Barkinar smiles at him, indicating prior knowledge and likely complicity or leverage over Kelno.  
- Formerly the clandestine operator behind the Air Temple’s infiltration of the temple war, using hidden passages to loot corpses, weapons, and coin.  
- Recruited individuals from the Earth Temple by exploiting fear, triggering a three-way war.  
- Offered Zephyr partnership: control of his goblin network, informants, and operational infrastructure in exchange for becoming his “number two.”  
- Has been waiting two weeks for Zephyr’s group to emerge as the “fourth temple,” anticipating their rise and preparing the second chair in advance.  
- Personal grudge against Belsornig, for whom he commissioned the Dagger of Venom’s use.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Orchestrated covert looting during the temple war while presenting as a powerless relic.  
- Actively recruited from the Earth Temple, destabilizing the balance of power.  
- Gifted the Dagger of Venom to Zephyr to kill Belsornig, testing alignment and trust.  
- Admitted his guards are the worst in the dungeon, acknowledging vulnerability.  
- Declared loyalty to chaos and survival, not institutions: “I’m for chaos, but I’m also for living.”  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Survived multiple temple wars by aligning with the strongest faction, never with ideology.  
- Planned for Zephyr’s rise as the “fourth temple” weeks in advance, indicating long-term foresight.  
- Maintains a network of bribery, informants, and goblins — still active despite imprisonment.  
- The “second chair” was not symbolic — it was a physical, prepared seat for Zephyr, confirming deliberate, operational intent.

- Whether the Dagger of Venom is still in Zephyr’s possession or has been used — the fact that Kelno gave it is confirmed, but its current status is not.
