### Earth Temple

**Current status:** Alive, dominant faction within the Temple of Elemental Evil.  
**Current location:** Control of the entire Temple of Elemental Evil complex, including former Fire Temple strongholds and territories.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Unified under Hartsch’s leadership as Supreme Prophet, though internal fractures persist due to betrayal, necromantic interference, and recruitment losses.  

**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Possesses Romag’s Vestments, worn by Hartsch as symbol of legitimate authority.  
- Controls sacred artifacts and personnel from the absorbed Fire Temple.  
- Deploys necromantically raised ghouls (formerly Earth Temple members) as frontline combatants and looters.  
- Commands the Obsidian Edge as its elite vanguard and primary tactical instrument.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Led by Hartsch, Supreme Prophet, who replaced Romag after his assassination.  
- Hartsch’s leadership is militantly aggressive, rejecting Romag’s former philosophy of passive endurance.  
- The Obsidian Edge serves as Hartsch’s personal agents of chaos and strategic enforcers.  
- Has absorbed the leadership, clerics, and territory of the Fire Temple through force and psychological domination.  
- Engages in systematic recruitment and defection operations (e.g., Kelno’s infiltration) and maintains subsidiary holdings (e.g., former ghast and harpy territories).  
- Maintains nominal allegiance from former enemies (e.g., bugbears, gnolls, clerics) through coercion, ritual surrender, and symbolic marking (e.g., red ink oaths).  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Assassinated Romag and seized leadership through symbolic appropriation of his vestments.  
- Rebranded the faction as a militant force, launching full-scale offensives against Fire and Water Temples.  
- Formed and deployed the Obsidian Edge as an elite strike unit.  
- Necromantically raised deceased Earth Temple members as ghouls, weaponizing their corpses.  
- Absorbed the Fire Temple’s leadership, clerics, and sacred sites through conquest and forced surrender.  
- Maintains control through psychological dominance, ritual coercion, and internal purges.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Hartsch seeks total destruction of rival elemental temples and absolute doctrinal supremacy.  
- The Earth Temple’s recent dominance is built on instability: internal betrayal, recruitment erosion, necromantic sabotage, and leadership vulnerability.  
- Many adherents (e.g., ghasts, captured clerics) offer hollow allegiance under duress.  
- The temple’s structure is now a hollow shell of its former self — outwardly dominant, internally rotting.

## Uncertainty
- The exact nature and origin of the necromantic interference raising Earth Temple corpses as ghouls is unattributed — is it internal (e.g., Hartsch’s secret rites) or external (e.g., a third party manipulating both sides)?  
- Whether the “new Earth Temple cleric” mentioned in ch31 is a genuine convert, a coerced puppet, or an infiltrator is ambiguous.  
- The current status of Calmer and Ben is unclear — while they are noted as loyal to the Earth Temple, their present location and role are not confirmed beyond ch29.  
- It is unclear whether the “stalled advance” in ch28 was fully resolved after the barricade fell, or if residual resistance remains.  
- The fate of Hartsch’s leadership is implied stable, but ch29 suggests the temple is “watching its soldiers die while waiting for the Obsidian Edge to decide their fate” — implying Hartsch’s control may be tenuous or contingent on the party’s actions.

### Earth Temple

**Current status:** Abandoned by the main group; partially occupied by remnants and hidden threats.  
**Current location:** Subterranean dungeon complex, likely beneath or adjacent to the Fire and Water Temples.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly the Earth Temple faction; now controlled by Hartsch as Supreme Prophet, but effectively fractured and in collapse.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Contains secret doors (at least one cracked open by gnolls weeks prior).  
- Houses remnants of undead, broken armor, and abandoned supplies.  
- Still holds wine cellars, storage chambers, prison cells, armories, and refuse pits.  
- Site of recent ritual activity: earth elementals were summoned here, and Hartsch conducted speeches to incite cultists.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Formerly the base of operations for Hartsch’s coup against Romag; now a fallback position and refuge for Broken Blades and surviving loyalists.  
- Hartsch is no longer physically present (last seen summoning elementals and rallying troops — his current whereabouts unconfirmed).  
- Calmer was left behind in its aftermath.  
- Dren and the Broken Blades previously settled here but have since moved on.  
- The party used it as a staging ground for espionage, sabotage, and the coup — now it is abandoned by them.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Seized by Hartsch from Romag in a coup.  
- Used as the origin point for assaults on the Fire and Water Temples.  
- Suffered internal betrayal: guards bribed or killed, loyalty dismantled.  
- Survived a Chimera attack and later became a refuge after the failed Air Temple raid.  
- The secret door, previously cracked by gnolls, was later used by the party as an entry point.  
- The wraith was slain within its halls.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- The temple was once a proud bastion, now a symbol of collapsed authority.  
- Its internal decay was accelerated by Hartsch’s manipulation and the party’s sabotage.  
- Secret doors and hidden passages were exploited by both the party and gnolls.  
- Morale is broken; leadership is absent or ineffective.  

## Uncertainty
- Hartsch’s current location: last seen leading rituals in the temple, but no evidence he remains there now.  
- Whether Calmer is still alive or present in the temple after being left behind.  
- Whether Dren and the Broken Blades still have any presence there — they “settled into” the temple but later moved to regroup elsewhere.  
- Exact current control status: is the temple entirely abandoned, or are there still hidden loyalists, gnolls, or undead occupying sections?  
- Contradiction: [ch29] describes it as a “refuge where loyalty can be rekindled,” while [ch30] says it’s “abandoned by the main group” — implying a shift in status between chapters; no clear transition event is documented.  
- Who currently holds authority? Hartsch claimed it, but he may be dead, gone, or overthrown — no confirmation.
