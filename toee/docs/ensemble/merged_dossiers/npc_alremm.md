### Alremm

**Current status:** Deceased  
**Current location:** On his pyre  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former leader of the Fire Temple  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None (deceased)  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Former leader of the Fire Temple; last known action was ordering a full-scale attack on the Water Temple after being manipulated by the party into believing Belsornig of the Water Temple orchestrated the Chimera incident to bankrupt him.
