### Pasmarie’s Beehives

Pasmarie’s Beehives is a well-maintained farmstead located a quarter mile southeast of Sheernobb, surrounded by verdant fields and wildflowers. The hives are arranged in neat rows, with designated paths leading to each hive and small benches for visitors. A small wooden building serves as Pasmarie Clee’s workshop and meeting space, where she tends to her bees and speaks with visitors. The site remains a productive asset and a symbol of Sheernobb’s identity, though recent thefts of honey by raiders who identified themselves as “Black Earth” have disrupted its peace.

## Uncertainty
- The current condition of the hives (whether any were destroyed or abandoned after the theft) is not specified beyond the theft being reported.
- The current status of Pasmarie Clee (alive, present, or still in charge) is implied but not confirmed beyond her speaking to Zephyr’s party — no later chapter overrides this.
- Whether the “small building” is still in active use as a workshop or has been sealed/abandoned since the theft is not stated.
- The identity or current presence of the “Black Earth” raiders at the site is unknown.
