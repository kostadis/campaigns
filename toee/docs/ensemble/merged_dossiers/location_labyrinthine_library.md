### Labyrinthine Library

A magical location filled with floating books, glowing runes, and earth magic. Contains a hidden door that is revealed by knocking three times on a specific bookshelf. Zephyr and Nicholas recently navigated this space to decipher a clue regarding four stones and four passages.
