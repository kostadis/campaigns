### Zephyr

**Current Status:** Alive  
**Current Location:** Within the Greater Temple complex, likely in the newly consolidated Earth Temple leadership quarters  
**Allegiance/Faction:** De facto leader of the Earth Temple; formerly a covert operative of the Obsidian Edge, now independent but aligned with the fractured remnants of the Earth Temple hierarchy after absorbing the Fire Temple  

**Current Possessions / Notable Items:**  
- Control chain of the Chimera (seized from the Fire Temple warlock)  
- Short sword (offered during weapon surrender; still carried)  
- Dagger (magically enchanted)  
- Pistol (treated as an improved crossbow, 1d10 damage)  
- Dust of Disappearance (used multiple times for infiltration and escape)  
- Thaumaturgy spellcast (used to seal Romag’s chamber doors)  
- Signed blood contract with a salamander (clauses on soul and ownership struck out)  

**Notable Spells / Feats / Abilities:**  
- Expert in Sneak Attack (used against Tharok, Rannos, ghouls, and harpies)  
- Skilled in psychological intimidation and interrogation (“good cop, bad cop,” fear-based coercion)  
- Proficient in deception, bluffing, and impersonation (posed as messenger for Kelno, Belsornig; wore Fire Temple robes)  
- Tactical awareness: excels at environmental reading, ambush positioning, and route efficiency  
- Immune to one harpy’s charm song (but not others)  
- Endured and adapted to fire damage from the salamander pact  

**Current Assignment / Role and Key Relationships:**  
- **Role:** Supreme leader of the Earth Temple; has assumed command after eliminating or subjugating rivals (Romag, Thalsor, Fire Temple commander)  
- **Reports to:** No one — now the apex authority. Claims to have “accepted the chain of command” and restructured the temple under his rule  
- **Travels with:** Zinnia, Sequoia; Calmer is no longer with the group (deliberately removed from front-line operations)  
- **Key Relationships:**  
  - **Calmer:** Former ally and tactical asset; now seen as a liability or unpredictable variable. Zephyr has removed him from combat to preserve operational autonomy.  
  - **Kelno:** Trusted ally; valued for his honest hunger to survive, not loyalty. Zephyr has absorbed Kelno’s intelligence network and goblin forces.  
  - **Fire Temple Commander:** Subjugated; forced to surrender via symbolic payment of two gold coins as “respect.”  
  - **Fire Temple Cleric:** Appointed as new Head Cleric under Zephyr’s authority.  
  - **Salamander:** Bound by blood pact; Zephyr endured its fire to gain knowledge of the Greater Temple.  

**Defining Recent Actions:**  
- Seized control of the Chimera’s control chain during battle, turning it against its master.  
- Used Thaumaturgy to seal Romag’s chamber, enabling his assassination and ascension.  
- Orchestrated the downfall of the Fire Temple by bluffing its guards into opening the west door, then eliminating its leadership.  
- Negotiated a blood pact with a salamander, striking out soul-binding clauses and enduring fire to gain insight into the Greater Temple.  
- Forced the Fire Temple commander into submission by offering mercy after feigning intent to kill — demanding two gold coins as symbolic “respect.”  
- Appointed a new Head Cleric and restructured the Earth Temple hierarchy under his sole authority.  
- Declared himself and Calmer “leaders,” rejecting the label of assassin.  

**Revealed Motivations / Secrets:**  
- Zephyr seeks not wealth, but control — he views power as a hierarchy that keeps rising, and he intends to reach the top.  
- He distrusts loyalty and instead trusts raw, honest survival instinct (e.g., Kelno).  
- He is not a true cleric but has begun to internalize the role of prophet, blurring his identity with the Earth Temple’s doctrine.  
- His actions are driven by cold pragmatism, tactical efficiency, and a belief that “killing the boss makes you the boss.”  
- He recognizes the danger in Calmer’s divine outbursts and suspects something else may be wearing his voice.  
- He believes his luck has a “restrained, unkind sense of humor.”  

## Uncertainty  
- Contradiction: Zephyr is described as both a **tiefling** (ch05, ch09, ch19) and a **human cursed into his current form** (ch08). Later chapters consistently refer to him as tiefling and reference his horns, suggesting the “human cursed” claim was a deception. The tiefling identity is the current state.  
- Ambiguous chronology: The exact moment Zephyr assumed formal leadership is unclear — was it after Romag’s death, after the Fire Temple’s fall, or after the salamander pact? All are pivotal, but the last (ch31) is the most definitive.  
- Unclear attribution: In ch19, Zephyr is referred to with female pronouns (“her dagger,” “she delivered a devastating blow”) — all other instances use male pronouns. This is likely an error, but the gender of Zephyr remains ambiguous in one source.  
- Uncertain possession: It is unclear whether Zephyr still carries the painted rock from ch05 — it was handed to Burne and Rufus, and never mentioned again.  
- Uncertain status of companions: Sequoia and Zinnia are last seen with Zephyr in ch30, but their current whereabouts or allegiance are not confirmed beyond that.  
- Unclear origin: Zephyr’s home is mentioned as a desire (“wanted to go home”) but never named or located — current location of origin unknown.
