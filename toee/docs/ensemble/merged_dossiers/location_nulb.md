### Nulb

Nulb is a lawless, desperate frontier town where power is everything, controlled by Madame Selentis and riddled with competing factions including the Crimson Reapers, the Iron Lash, River Pirates, and recruitment drives from the Earth and Water Temples of Elemental Evil. It serves as a hub for mercenary activity, smuggling, and hidden agendas. Key locations include the Boatman’s Tavern, the Gallows Table (site of public executions), Magda’s Trove (housing cursed ink), Garik’s Armory (stocked with rusted weapons), and the Watersteide Hostel — the latter being the meeting point for Dick Rentsch and the recruitment ground for the Broken Blades. Nulb is the origin point of the Chimera’s control equipment, as confirmed by the stamped marks of the River Pirates on its heavy iron collar and dragging chains, indicating the pirates were paid to transport the monster to the Fire Temple. The town is the designated target for infiltrating the Temple’s operations, and the party has returned here to confront Tolub and demand an audience with the temple leadership.

## Uncertainty
- The exact current status of Madame Selentis’s control (whether she still holds power or has been overthrown) is not confirmed beyond her association with the town’s factions.
- Whether the River Pirates still operate openly in Nulb or have been suppressed since transporting the Chimera is unclear.
- The current presence or status of Tolub within Nulb is not confirmed after the party’s plan to confront him.
- “Waterside Hostel” and “Watersteide Hostel” are mentioned in different chapters — whether this is a naming inconsistency, typo, or two distinct locations is unresolved.
- The temporal relationship between the recruitment of the Broken Blades and the Chimera’s transport is ambiguous — it is unknown if these events are concurrent or sequential.
