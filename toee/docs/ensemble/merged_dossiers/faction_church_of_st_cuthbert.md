### Church of St. Cuthbert

**Current status:** Active and influential in the region.  
**Current location:** Operates in the settlement where Tomlyn the tailor resides; presence noted in both civic and religious spheres.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent theocratic faction; opposes the Old Faith.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:** None specified.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** Enforces strict doctrinal purity, treating affiliation with rival cults (including the Temple and the Old Faith) as a capital offense. Known for zealous, brutal punishment to instill terror. Represented locally by Tomlyn the tailor and his son Marek. Calmer, a cleric of St. Cuthbert, serves as an enforcer and is actively involved in punitive missions.  
**Defining recent actions:** Actively pursues and executes suspected members of rival cults; maintains doctrinal dominance through fear. Tomlyn and Marek are publicly known members, suggesting institutional integration into local life despite its violent reputation. Calmer’s ironic, gleeful enforcement of “calm” underscores the faction’s hypocritical and fanatical nature.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:** Motivated by ideological purity and control; uses capital punishment as a tool of social domination. The fact that Tomlyn, a civilian tailor, is a member suggests the Church has deep, normalized roots in the community — not merely a monastic or military order.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between “membership in the cult is punishable by death” — it is unclear whether “the cult” refers to the Church of St. Cuthbert itself (which would imply self-punishment) or rival cults. Later context confirms it refers to rival cults, but the phrasing in ch04 is ambiguous.  
- Unclear if Calmer and Tomlyn are part of the same hierarchical structure or if Calmer is an external enforcer sent to the region.  
- No confirmation whether Marek is an active member or merely a familial associate of the Church.  
- The term “the Temple” is referenced as a rival cult — but it is never defined; it may or may not be the same as the Old Faith.  
- No evidence of current leadership structure beyond Calmer and Tomlyn; their relative authority is unknown.
