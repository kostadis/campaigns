### Air Temple

**Current status:** Occupied and active  
**Current location:** Dungeon level, accessible via northeast passage; connected to Water Temple via stairs descending from its chamber  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly independent temple of the Four Temples; now under de facto control of Zephyr as part of a strategic assault to eliminate rivals and seize dungeon dominance. Kelno remains its nominal leader but is a pawn in Zephyr’s scheme.  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- Two upholstered chairs  
- A crock half-full of mead  
- Dull metal lamps  
- Shelves holding scrolls and foodstuffs  
- Dozens of human and demi-human skulls arranged on shelves  
- A narrow bed in the corner  
- A secret door that hung open after gnolls were killed (now unguarded)  
**Current assignment / role:**  
- Serves as Zephyr’s staging ground for eliminating rival temple factions.  
- Used as a decoy to mask the true source of bribe coins stamped with the Air Temple insignia — a covert operation to destabilize the Obsidian Edge.  
- Kelno’s agents use hidden passages to move undetected.  
- The chamber functions as Kelno’s personal quarters and command post, though he is isolated and delusional.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Hosted a secret door that released gnolls, leading to a violent confrontation in the brown-and-black corridor.  
- Was the origin point of bribe coins used to corrupt Obsidian Edge members, revealing internal sabotage.  
- Is now the target of Zephyr’s direct assault to eliminate its occupants — four starving prophets — and consolidate control.  
- Calmer was last conscious here before being removed.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Kelno believes the wraith haunting the temple is an air elemental — a delusion reflecting his faction’s irrelevance.  
- The Air Temple’s insignia was used to forge bribes, making Kelno a sacrificial decoy.  
- The temple’s treasure was looted; its weakness made it the first offensive target.  
- The gray, lifeless chamber with skulls suggests Kelno has abandoned all spiritual purpose and retreated into nihilistic isolation.

## Uncertainty
- The current whereabouts of Kelno are not confirmed beyond his association with the chamber — is he still present, hiding, or dead?  
- The wraith’s current state is ambiguous: it was stationary in ch28, but no later chapter confirms if it remains, moved, or was destroyed.  
- The identity of the “four starving prophets” occupying the temple in ch30 is not specified — are they new occupants, remnants of the old guard, or Zephyr’s pawns?  
- It is unclear whether the secret door is still functional or actively used after the gnolls’ deaths.  
- Attribution of the “strategic assault” is attributed to Zephyr, but no direct quote confirms Zephyr personally ordered the attack — only that it is Zephyr’s objective.

### Air Temple

**Current status:** Active but covert, politically compromised, and strategically exploited.  
**Current location:** Deep levels of the Temple of Elemental Evil; occupies abandoned corridors and infiltrated prison levels.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly associated with the Obsidian Edge; now a hidden, deniable asset used to mask true loyalties. Actively in conflict with the Deep Temple and the Water Temple.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Forged coins stamped with Air Temple insignia (used in covert operations); access to prison-level archives and strategic knowledge from the Deep Temple.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Led by Prophet Kelno, who remains delusional (believes the wraith is an air elemental) but is being manipulated by unseen operatives within the faction.  
- Functions as a covert proxy for a hidden power within the Obsidian Edge, used to frame rivals (Water Temple, Kelno) and destabilize enemy factions.  
- Agents have infiltrated Deep Temple prison levels and control access points to sacred knowledge.  
- The wraith haunting its halls is an uncontrolled entity, not understood by Kelno, but exploited by operatives as a deterrent and distraction.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Orchestrated the bugbear defector’s betrayal using forged Air Temple coins to implicate Kelno and the Water Temple.  
- Exploited the chaos of the temple war to loot and extract intelligence from Deep Temple prison levels.  
- Abandoned its public identity as a legitimate elemental temple; now operates as a shadow faction using its name to misdirect blame.  
- Survived the collapse of its territorial integrity by retreating into subterfuge, becoming the most vulnerable yet most dangerous of the four elemental factions due to its hidden reach.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between Kelno being described as a delusional leader (ch27) and the faction successfully executing complex covert ops (ch28–29): Is Kelno a puppet, or are his actions being hijacked by unseen handlers?  
- Unclear if the wraith is under Air Temple control or merely an unmanaged hazard they tolerate — no evidence confirms whether it was summoned, bound, or simply left behind.  
- Ambiguous whether the Air Temple still has any members beyond Kelno and a few hidden agents; "powerless remnant" (ch27) conflicts with "hidden manipulator" (ch29) — is the faction a shell, or has it been fully subsumed by an external force?  
- The Obsidian Edge’s current relationship to the Air Temple is implied to be exploitative and temporary — but it is unclear if the Air Temple is still formally allied, entirely abandoned, or secretly controlling the Obsidian Edge from within.  
- No confirmation whether Kelno is still alive or has been replaced; his last known state is delusional, but no death or capture is recorded.
