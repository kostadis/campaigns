### Jaroo

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Hommlet  
**Allegiance/faction:** Druid devoted to Beory; local council member of Hommlet  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Possesses a healing potion (last offered in ch05; no indication it was used or lost)  
- Holds the blessing of Beory (last conferred in ch05; no indication it was revoked)  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Serves as the local druid and council member in Hommlet, tasked with observing post-Temple events and safeguarding natural order.  
- Reports to no formal superior but operates independently under Beory’s mandate.  
- Last seen directing the party toward Nulb to infiltrate the Temple’s operations, suggesting active leadership in the group’s mission.  
- Maintains distrust of Verbobonc’s expansionist agenda, Burne and Rufus’s motives, and Terjon’s silence.  
- Has no known companions currently; last interacted with the party in ch16–17.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- In ch16, asserted that the party must travel to Nulb to infiltrate and disrupt the Temple’s operations and identify its true leadership.  
- In ch17, expressed deep suspicion of the Circle of the Scarlet Moon, condemning their practices as too showy, flashy, and religiously performative rather than natural.  
- Previously in ch07, presented the narrative that brigands were masquerading as Temple agents, not true agents.  
- In ch05, blessed the party with Beory’s favor and a healing potion after they reported interactions with Terjon, Burne, and Rufus.  
- In ch03, instructed the party to investigate the Moathouse and report findings to him alone, citing threats from Temple agents and Verbobonc’s suppression.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes Tieflings are outside the natural order and have no place in the Prime Material Plane.  
- Fears Verbobonc’s Church is absorbing Hommlet’s faithful rather than protecting them.  
- Suspicious of any organization that prioritizes spectacle over natural harmony, including the Circle of the Scarlet Moon.  
- Operates under the conviction that the Temple of Elemental Evil’s influence persists covertly, and that only he can be trusted with sensitive findings.
