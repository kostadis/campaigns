### Hall of Trophies and Elemental Strife

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current state:** A ruined chamber once used for conquest celebrations, now dominated by ongoing elemental warfare.  
- **Control/Occupation:** Unclaimed; no known current occupants, but the space is actively contested by elemental forces.  
- **Notable features:**  
  - Broad steps of dull black stone descending from the north wall into shadow.  
  - Walls lined with mummified heads, their leather-dry mouths twisted in dying expressions.  
  - Torn battle standards and battered shields mounted as trophies.  
  - A broken broadsword displayed as a trophy.  
  - Tapestries depicting raiding figures in the act of taking, burning, and dragging.  
- **Atmosphere:** Heavy silence pressing down, broken only by the hiss of steam and the violence of elemental conflict.  
- **Significance:** A shrine to past conquests, now transformed into an arena of raw, uncontrolled elemental strife — the trophies serve as both relics and warnings.

## Uncertainty
- The nature of the ongoing elemental battle (which elements, their sources, or whether they are sentient or wild) is not specified.  
- Whether any individuals currently enter, control, or observe the hall is unknown.  
- The origin of the broken broadsword or the identity of those whose heads are displayed is not stated.  
- It is unclear whether the tapestries and trophies are actively maintained or simply decayed remnants.
