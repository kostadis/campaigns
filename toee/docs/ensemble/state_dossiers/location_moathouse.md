### Moathouse

**Current status:** Active site of recent evil activity, recently cleared of hostile forces.  
**Current location:** East of the main road, surrounded by a treacherous, boggy marshland thick with sickly vegetation, cattails, tamaracks, and strangling vines; accessible via a narrow, overgrown track (15 feet wide) with crumbling embankments and a high, banked side path leading to the entrance.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former outpost of the Temple of Elemental Evil; now controlled by Lareth the Beautiful and his cultist/ogre/bugbear forces — recently dismantled.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Contains a secret mechanism that drops an iron grate, a false wall triggering a trap linked to the grate, and a chamber guarded by six intelligent Bugbears (now cleared). Treasure looted from Lareth’s inner sanctum. A collapsed tower contains a faint gleam of coins. Lower levels include an abandoned torture chamber, cells, a storeroom, and green slime.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:** No longer a bandit hideout or abandoned ruin. Was the operational base for Lareth the Beautiful; now a cleared site under observation by Verbobonc authorities (per Elmo’s orders). No known current occupants.  
**Defining recent actions:** The party successfully cleared Lareth the Beautiful’s inner sanctum, defeated his guards, and looted the treasure. Prior to this, the Moathouse housed zombies, an ogre, green slime, and a network of secret doors and traps. It was the origin of bandit activity and a focal point for cultist resurgence. Elmo of Verbobonc ordered its immediate investigation and destruction due to suspected evil presence.
