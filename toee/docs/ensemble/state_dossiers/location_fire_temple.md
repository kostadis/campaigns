### Fire Temple

**Current status:** Active but diminished, leaderless, and in a state of tactical retreat.  
**Current location:** Still standing and occupied, located within the greater temple complex.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent cult organization, formerly associated with the warlock, the amulet, and the Chimera weapon; now isolated and caught between rival factions.  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- A throne with a gold skull on the back, located in a chamber lined with red, orange, and yellow tapestries and lit by torchlight on lacquered wood.  
- A fire pit containing guarded treasure, now claimed by the party after the salamander’s surrender.  
- A secret door opening north, recently discovered and activated.  
- Massive brass doors covered in leering faces and horrible scenes.  
- Barricades of pews and rubble at the stairway to the second level, maintained by bugbears.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Acting as a defensive stronghold under degraded command.  
- No known leadership; cultists are leaderless.  
- Formerly attacked the Greater Temple and attempted to assassinate Romag’s allies; now under threat of destruction by Romag and the party.  
- In hostile standoff with the Water Temple, following Alrrem’s declaration of war after receiving a note under the door.  
- Recently subdued by the party after a failed incursion; now transitioning from combat to negotiation.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Attempted and failed to force entry into the Greater Temple.  
- Declared war on the Water Temple via note delivered under the brass doors.  
- Maintained barricades and bugbear defenses within its interior.  
- Lost leadership; cultists are now leaderless.  
- Surrendered its final guardian (salamander) and lost control of its fire pit treasure.  
- Party now occupies its inner sanctum and has access to its secret north door.
