### Moot Hall

- **Type:** Location  
- **Current State:** Active and central to Sheernobb’s civic life  
- **Location:** On the largest hill in the center of Sheernobb  
- **Structure:** Features wide stone steps leading to grand stone doors carved with runes; doors open into the Feasting Hall  
- **Function:** Used for special gatherings and public meetings  
- **Layout:** Two branches of the road split and converge around the Moot Hall, forming a circular path; most stores in Sheernobb are located along this circle  

## Uncertainty
- The phrase “the two branches of the moot hall form a circle” is ambiguous — it likely intends to describe the roads, not the hall itself, but the text incorrectly attributes the branches to the hall. This may be a transcription or wording error; the intended meaning (roads branching around the hall) is clear, but the exact phrasing conflicts with architectural logic.
