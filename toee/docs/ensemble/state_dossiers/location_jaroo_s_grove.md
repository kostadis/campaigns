### Jaroo's Grove

Jaroo's grove is a place of worship with neatly pruned trees and well-tended grass. A line of bluish stones marks the path leading to a rock cairn, where flowers, nuts, berries, and leaf garlands are placed as offerings. A small path extends from the cairn to a low-roofed wooden building situated beneath the central oaks.
