### Temple of Elemental Evil

**Current state:** Ruined, active, and deeply corrupted subterranean complex.  
**Current location:** Northeastern region of the monastery grounds, accessible via main entrance or side entrance.  
**Current condition:** A vast, shattered structure dominated by a ruined northeast tower. Surfaces are covered in leering faces and writhing, obscene carvings. Arched buttresses are grotesque and overgrown with vegetation. The main entrance is sealed by two pairs of 23-foot-high, 20-foot-wide bronze doors, bound by massive iron chains with glowing silvery runes sealing all cracks. A side entrance leads to a vestibule with horrifying murals, pinkish mineral pillars, and a central altar featuring a bloodstained human-shaped depression and a circular pit beneath. A pervasive, tangible cloud of vileness hangs over the entire area, distorting sound and perception, with movements flickering at the edge of vision. The wind moans through hundreds of wall apertures like the wails of doomed souls. Ravens croak and flap among the ruins.  

**Structure:** Multi-level subterranean complex divided into four warring elemental temples (Earth, Fire, Water, Air). Deeper levels contain ancient evil, hidden secrets, and power sources guarded by undead and hostile factions.  

**Control/occupation:** No single authority confirmed. The complex is contested by warring elemental factions and infested with undead. No living overseer or controller is named.  

**Recent actions:** The party has entered the temple through its side entrance and descended into its depths, encountering warring elemental factions and ancient evil.  

**Significance:** The ultimate revelation of the monastery’s true purpose; a nexus of elemental corruption and ancient dark power.

## Uncertainty
- The exact current control or leadership of each elemental temple (Earth, Fire, Water, Air) is unspecified — only that they are “locked in conflict.”
- Whether the glowing runes on the bronze doors are active wards, seals, or something else is implied but not defined.
- The fate of any prior occupants or cultists is unknown; only the presence of ravens, undead, and hostile factions is confirmed.
- The nature of the “ancient evil” in the deeper levels is not named or described beyond its existence.
- It is unclear whether the temple is still actively being used for rituals or if it is merely a ruin inhabited by residual evil.
