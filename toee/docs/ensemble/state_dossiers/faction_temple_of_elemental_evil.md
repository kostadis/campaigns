### Temple of Elemental Evil

**Current status:** Active, but fractured.  
**Current location:** Operations centered in Nulb and the Moathouse; the physical Temple structure is a ruined, leprous gray monument, currently empty but intended for future occupation.  
**Allegiance/faction:** A fractured network of rival elemental cults (Earth, Fire, Water, Air) formerly unified under a single doctrine, now in open civil war.  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- None explicitly held by the faction as a whole.  
- The structure contains obscene carvings and ancient bindings from the Circle of Eight’s spell.  
- The “mysterious Deep Temple” is implied to control or manipulate the factions from behind the scenes.  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- The Temple’s structure is now a three-way war among Earth (led by Romag), Fire (unnamed prophet), and Water (led by Belsornig); Air is unmentioned in ch31, possibly inactive or absorbed.  
- Each cult operates independently with its own secrets and ancient grudges.  
- The factions were once unified under the doctrine that true cosmic nature is chaotic elemental conflict, with Earth destined to reduce all to dirt.  
- The party has infiltrated the Temple, earned Romag’s trust, and established a base of operations within the stronghold.  
- The Deep Temple is the rumored higher authority manipulating the conflict.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Successfully recruited forces, including gnolls from the local gnoll king.  
- Operated through agents Rannos and Gremag in Hommlet until their elimination.  
- Resurged after Lareth’s death, expanding operations into Nulb.  
- Now engaged in a self-sustaining three-way war among elemental cults, deliberately manipulated to weaken itself (“Ran a three-way war and let it eat itself”).  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Founded on the belief that elemental chaos is the true nature of the universe, with Earth as the ultimate victor.  
- Once bound by the Circle of Eight after the Battle of Emiry Meadows.  
- The “Deep Temple” is the hidden power behind the current fragmentation — its nature, location, and identity remain unknown.  
- The physical Temple is not merely a headquarters but a ritual site meant to be filled with supplicants who will kill and bury alive opponents after the Earth Temple eliminates “false prophets.”  

## Uncertainty
- The current status of the Air Prophet’s cult is unmentioned in ch31 — was it destroyed, absorbed, or dormant?  
- The identity, location, and nature of the “mysterious Deep Temple” are entirely unknown.  
- The phrase “Ran a three-way war and let it eat itself” implies external manipulation, but no actor is named — is this the Deep Temple, or another force?  
- Ch16 claims Lareth was the leader in the Moathouse; ch31 implies he is dead and the Temple is now fractured — but who, if anyone, has replaced him as nominal leader?  
- Ch18 states the Temple is ruled by a “mysterious deep temple”; ch31 describes it as a fractured network — is the Deep Temple still active, or has it been overthrown?  
- Ch07 and ch08 suggest brigands may be masquerading as or aligned with the Temple — are they still active proxies, or has the Temple’s internal war rendered them obsolete?
