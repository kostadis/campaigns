### Burne and Rufus's Tower Interior

- **Current status:** Intact and occupied.  
- **Current location:** Standing as the only completed structure of the castle complex.  
- **Control/occupation:** Controlled by Burne and Rufus; used for judgment, feasting, and daily living.  
- **Architecture:**  
  - 55-foot outer tower with a 35-foot inner tower rising within it.  
  - Splay base 6 feet high; machicolated lower and upper battlements; pierced merlons.  
  - Entrance accessible only via a curving stone stairway terminating in a landing 10 feet above ground.  
  - Outer door lowers to form a bridge to the stone landing.  
  - Shuttered arrow slits flank the inner door.  
  - Two murder holes in the ceiling of the entry room.  
- **Interior features:**  
  - Main room contains comfortable chairs along the walls, trestle tables extendable to seat 40, and a curved alcove used for judgment.  
  - Walls hung with tapestries, shields, arms, and hunting trophies.  
  - Two fireplaces flank the inner portal, used for cooking by two to three servants.  
  - Ramps curving upward and downward connect to other levels of the tower.  
- **Current assignment/role:** Functions as a seat of authority (judgment), residence, and feasting hall for Burne and Rufus.  
- **Defining recent actions:** Used for holding judicial sessions and hosting feasts; servants actively cook in the fireplaces; outer door operates as a bridge to the landing.
