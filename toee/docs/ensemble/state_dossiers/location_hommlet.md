### Hommlet

Hommlet is a small village at the edge of Verbobonc’s territory, near the Gnarley Forest, serving as a deceptively quiet administrative hub and nexus of hidden factions. It is currently under the nominal oversight of Verbobonc’s agents, Burne and Rufus, who are stationed there and part of the local council. The town is occupied by competing interests: Verbobonc’s officials, agents of the Temple of Elemental Evil, remnants of the Lolth cult, traders, bandits, and the Fire Temple, each operating with conflicting agendas. 

The party has used Hommlet as a base of operations, returning here multiple times to report, regroup, and plan. Most recently, the party gathered the local council—Osler Goodingoot, Burne, Rufus, and Jaroo—to present evidence of the Temple’s resurgence and received the directive to travel to Nulb. The council meeting followed an elaborate misdirection enacted after the deaths of Rannos and Gremag. 

Hommlet is currently the site of recent violence, including a battle resulting in multiple deaths, and remains a center of political tension and espionage. The Temple of Elemental Evil maintains active agents here, and the party has leveraged the Chest of Rao as leverage against them. A tavern run by Osler Goodingoot serves as a meeting point, and local druid Jaroo, consulted for guidance on the Circle of the Scarlet Moon, remains a key contact. The Inn of the Welcome Wench is still operational as a place of rest and information exchange.

The party last recalled Spugnoir’s story of the Circle of Eight in Hommlet, confirming the town as a place of memory and strategic importance.
