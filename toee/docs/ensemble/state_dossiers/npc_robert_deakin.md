### Robert Deakin

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Ostverk  
**Allegiance/faction:** Independent; gnome community affiliate  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Honey mead from the Honey Tavern Inn  
**Current assignment / role:** Owner of The Cheap Forester tavern in Ostverk; demands payment for information  
**Key relationships:** Related to the Clee family (honey purveyors in Sheernobb)  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Demanded food payment before speaking with visitors.  
- Dismissed rumors of “big evil powers” as bandit cover stories.  
- Affirmed his firsthand participation in the Battle of Emiry Meadows and belief that the Temple was truly destroyed.  
- Expressed clear displeasure and secret relief that the alleged treaty between Verbobonc and Celene (to wage war on gnomes and seize gem mines) failed.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- As a gnome, he opposes any alliance that would target his people; his gladness over the failed treaty reveals deep personal investment in gnome survival.  
- His connection to the Clee family suggests ties to the honey trade network, possibly a cover or resource for intelligence gathering.  

## Uncertainty  
- Contradiction: One fact states he “claimed to have fought in the Battle of Emiry Meadows,” while another implies he *knows* the Temple was destroyed *because* he fought there — but no independent corroboration of his claim exists. His account may be true, exaggerated, or fabricated.  
- Ambiguity: While he is said to be “related to the Clee family,” the nature and closeness of that relation (e.g., cousin, distant kin, in-law) is unspecified.  
- Unclear attribution: The quote “Deakin, being a gnome, was clearly displeased by this...” attributes his emotional reaction to his race — but is this his personal view, or an observer’s assumption? The dossier treats it as fact, but the source may be interpretive.
