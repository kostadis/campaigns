### Air Temple

**Current status:** Active but covert, politically compromised, and strategically exploited.  
**Current location:** Deep levels of the Temple of Elemental Evil; occupies abandoned corridors and infiltrated prison levels.  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly associated with the Obsidian Edge; now a hidden, deniable asset used to mask true loyalties. Actively in conflict with the Deep Temple and the Water Temple.  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Forged coins stamped with Air Temple insignia (used in covert operations); access to prison-level archives and strategic knowledge from the Deep Temple.  
**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Led by Prophet Kelno, who remains delusional (believes the wraith is an air elemental) but is being manipulated by unseen operatives within the faction.  
- Functions as a covert proxy for a hidden power within the Obsidian Edge, used to frame rivals (Water Temple, Kelno) and destabilize enemy factions.  
- Agents have infiltrated Deep Temple prison levels and control access points to sacred knowledge.  
- The wraith haunting its halls is an uncontrolled entity, not understood by Kelno, but exploited by operatives as a deterrent and distraction.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Orchestrated the bugbear defector’s betrayal using forged Air Temple coins to implicate Kelno and the Water Temple.  
- Exploited the chaos of the temple war to loot and extract intelligence from Deep Temple prison levels.  
- Abandoned its public identity as a legitimate elemental temple; now operates as a shadow faction using its name to misdirect blame.  
- Survived the collapse of its territorial integrity by retreating into subterfuge, becoming the most vulnerable yet most dangerous of the four elemental factions due to its hidden reach.  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between Kelno being described as a delusional leader (ch27) and the faction successfully executing complex covert ops (ch28–29): Is Kelno a puppet, or are his actions being hijacked by unseen handlers?  
- Unclear if the wraith is under Air Temple control or merely an unmanaged hazard they tolerate — no evidence confirms whether it was summoned, bound, or simply left behind.  
- Ambiguous whether the Air Temple still has any members beyond Kelno and a few hidden agents; "powerless remnant" (ch27) conflicts with "hidden manipulator" (ch29) — is the faction a shell, or has it been fully subsumed by an external force?  
- The Obsidian Edge’s current relationship to the Air Temple is implied to be exploitative and temporary — but it is unclear if the Air Temple is still formally allied, entirely abandoned, or secretly controlling the Obsidian Edge from within.  
- No confirmation whether Kelno is still alive or has been replaced; his last known state is delusional, but no death or capture is recorded.
