### Air Temple

**Current status:** Occupied and active  
**Current location:** Dungeon level, accessible via northeast passage; connected to Water Temple via stairs descending from its chamber  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formerly independent temple of the Four Temples; now under de facto control of Zephyr as part of a strategic assault to eliminate rivals and seize dungeon dominance. Kelno remains its nominal leader but is a pawn in Zephyr’s scheme.  
**Current possessions / notable items:**  
- Two upholstered chairs  
- A crock half-full of mead  
- Dull metal lamps  
- Shelves holding scrolls and foodstuffs  
- Dozens of human and demi-human skulls arranged on shelves  
- A narrow bed in the corner  
- A secret door that hung open after gnolls were killed (now unguarded)  
**Current assignment / role:**  
- Serves as Zephyr’s staging ground for eliminating rival temple factions.  
- Used as a decoy to mask the true source of bribe coins stamped with the Air Temple insignia — a covert operation to destabilize the Obsidian Edge.  
- Kelno’s agents use hidden passages to move undetected.  
- The chamber functions as Kelno’s personal quarters and command post, though he is isolated and delusional.  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Hosted a secret door that released gnolls, leading to a violent confrontation in the brown-and-black corridor.  
- Was the origin point of bribe coins used to corrupt Obsidian Edge members, revealing internal sabotage.  
- Is now the target of Zephyr’s direct assault to eliminate its occupants — four starving prophets — and consolidate control.  
- Calmer was last conscious here before being removed.  
**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Kelno believes the wraith haunting the temple is an air elemental — a delusion reflecting his faction’s irrelevance.  
- The Air Temple’s insignia was used to forge bribes, making Kelno a sacrificial decoy.  
- The temple’s treasure was looted; its weakness made it the first offensive target.  
- The gray, lifeless chamber with skulls suggests Kelno has abandoned all spiritual purpose and retreated into nihilistic isolation.

## Uncertainty
- The current whereabouts of Kelno are not confirmed beyond his association with the chamber — is he still present, hiding, or dead?  
- The wraith’s current state is ambiguous: it was stationary in ch28, but no later chapter confirms if it remains, moved, or was destroyed.  
- The identity of the “four starving prophets” occupying the temple in ch30 is not specified — are they new occupants, remnants of the old guard, or Zephyr’s pawns?  
- It is unclear whether the secret door is still functional or actively used after the gnolls’ deaths.  
- Attribution of the “strategic assault” is attributed to Zephyr, but no direct quote confirms Zephyr personally ordered the attack — only that it is Zephyr’s objective.
