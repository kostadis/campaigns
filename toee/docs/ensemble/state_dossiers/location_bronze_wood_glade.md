### Bronze Wood Glade

A glade in the Gnarley Forest, surrounded by fully grown bronze wood trees. The trees have slender trunks, reddish-brown hard bark, and branches that naturally grow at 45-degree angles from the trunk, each bearing 8-inch leaves. The trees are nearly 40 feet tall.
