### Romag

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Assassinated in his sleeping chamber within the Earth Temple  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former High Priest of the Earth Temple, Temple of Elemental Evil  
**Current possessions / notable items, spells, feats, wounds:**  
- Wore the ceremonial vestments of the High Priest (seized by Hartsch after death)  
- Possessed the Orb of Earthly Supremacy (key to unlocking the temple’s central power) — current location unknown, but last held by Romag  
- Utilized the Otyugh as a living disposal system for enemies  
- Maintained control over territory cleared by the party, though large sections of the dungeon remained unused  

**Current assignment / role and key relationships:**  
- Former leader of the Earth Temple; commanded the party to destroy rival elemental temples, recruit monsters, and secure the Orb  
- Oversaw ritual sacrifices (including Lieutenant Krell) and enforced loyalty through fear  
- Operated a tribute system: party paid him a portion of treasure, not received payment  
- Was the ideological mentor of Hartsch, whom he lectured on the “slow, erosive power of dust”  
- Replaced by Hartsch as Supreme Prophet after assassination  
- Was known to distrust unproven allies, micromanage operations, and prefer to lead from behind as a “chess king”  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Ordered the party to eliminate Alrrem of the Fire Temple following the Chimera attack  
- Planned a loyalty ritual using prisoners as offerings, postponed until the next day  
- Was assassinated in his sleeping chamber by the party using the necrotic spell *Toll the Dead*, after being isolated from his guards  
- His death triggered Hartsch’s immediate seizure of power and vestments  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believed the universe’s true nature was elemental chaos, with Earth destined to reduce all things to dust  
- Sought to unify the four elemental temples under Earth’s dominance, using the Orb to subdue Fire and Water  
- Viewed dust as the ultimate, inevitable force — passive, patient, and inescapable  
- Was aware of Lieutenant Krell’s espionage and used it as a test of loyalty  
- Lacked the strategic depth of his predecessor, who had been promoted to the Deep Temple — a source of resentment and insecurity  

## Uncertainty  
- The exact location of the Orb of Earthly Supremacy after Romag’s death is unconfirmed — it may have been taken by Hartsch, the party, or hidden elsewhere.  
- Whether Romag personally possessed the badge of Earth (awarded to loyal agents) is not explicitly stated after his death.  
- The precise timing of when the Orb was last physically in Romag’s possession is not pinned to a specific chapter after ch23 — though no later fact contradicts this.  
- Attribution of the assassination: while ch30 says “knifed Romag at his own table,” ch27 specifies the use of *Toll the Dead* and isolation from guards — these may describe different phases of the same act or conflicting accounts.  
- Whether Romag’s “passive strategy” was truly his own philosophy or a facade is implied but not confirmed — ch27 suggests Hartsch used it as a point of shame, implying it may have been performative.
