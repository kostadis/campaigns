### Skole

- **Current status:** Alive  
- **Current location:** Boatman’s Tavern  
- **Allegiance/faction:** None (independent; opposes both Tolub and Lodriss)  
- **Current possessions / notable items:** None specified  
- **Current assignment / role:** Bartender and de facto operator of the Boatman’s Tavern  
- **Key relationships:**

  - Confided in the party about his hatred for Tolub and Lodriss and his desire to be free of them
