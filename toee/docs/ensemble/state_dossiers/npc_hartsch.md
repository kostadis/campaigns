### Hartsch

**Current status:** Dead  
**Current location:** Antechamber of the Earth Temple (site of assassination)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Former Supreme Prophet of the Earth Temple; leader of the Obsidian Edge (now defunct under his command)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None (assassinated; last known possession was Romag’s religious vestments, now likely seized or discarded)  
**Current assignment / role:** None (deceased; last role was nominal commander of the Earth Temple forces and de facto leader of the Obsidian Edge)  
**Key relationships:**  
- Formerly Romag’s sub-general; assassinated Romag and seized power.  
- Declared himself Supreme Prophet after ritualistically covering his hands in Romag’s blood.  
- Rebranded the party as the “Obsidian Edge,” hailed them as elite vanguard.  
- Last known to have falsely ordered the Broken Blades to raid the Water Temple under the Obsidian Edge’s name.  
- Was regarded by the party as a liability and a reckless, unstable leader.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- Seized leadership of the Earth Temple after Romag’s assassination, declaring himself Supreme Prophet and initiating full-scale war against the Fire Temple.  
- Summoned and commanded the Obsidian Edge, treating them as his personal elite force.  
- In a moment of panic and incompetence, unilaterally ordered the Broken Blades to attack the Water Temple, falsely attributing the order to the Obsidian Edge, risking factional trust and lives.  
- Abandoned his post after the failed raid, visibly broken and deferential, asking “What do we do next?”  
- Assassinated by unknown agents one hour before a scheduled meeting with Kelno, Prophet of the Air Temple.

## Uncertainty
- The exact identity of Hartsch’s assassins is not specified — only that they reached his antechamber an hour before the meeting with Kelno.  
- The current status of Romag’s vestments after Hartsch’s death is unrecorded — they may have been taken, destroyed, or left behind.  
- Whether the Obsidian Edge still exists as a functional unit under new command is unknown; Hartsch’s death leaves their allegiance ambiguous.  
- The precise moment Hartsch lost command authority (after the Broken Blades’ failure or after the assassination) is not clarified — only that he was a liability and was killed before any formal transition.  
- There is a minor name discrepancy in the facts: one entry refers to “Harch” (ch25), but all others use “Hartsch.” Given context and chronological progression, “Harch” is presumed a typo for “Hartsch,” but this remains unconfirmed in source material.
