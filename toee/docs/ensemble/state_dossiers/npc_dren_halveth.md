### Dren Halveth

*Also known as: Dren*

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Within the Water Temple, near the captain’s door and Alrrem’s pyre (earlier: Upper Temple, under Earth Temple control)  
**Allegiance/faction:** Formally installed by Calmer as supreme commander of the Earth Temple Forces, delegated authority to enforce rule over the conquered temple hierarchy and placed in command over the former Fire Temple commander — but personally loyal exclusively to Zephyr and the party; no longer affiliated with the Crimson Guard/Broken Blades as an operational identity.  
**Species:** Half-orc, with one ruined white eye (`docs/world_state.md`).  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None specified beyond implied armor/weapons as a mercenary captain.  
**Current assignment / role:** Guarding Calmer with two mercenaries; acting as direct enforcer and courier for Zephyr’s team.  
**Key relationships:**  
- Reports directly to Zephyr and the party; follows only their orders — despite formally reporting to Calmer within the Earth Temple hierarchy.  
- Sees Calmer as the man who once gave him hope, echoing a past figure.  
- Guards Calmer, the only individual in the temple whose loyalty cannot be bought.  
- Views Kelno as a nuisance.  
- Last seen interacting with Belsornig’s people and reporting Alrrem’s death.  
- Formerly captain of the Broken Blades; command of that unit is unmentioned since and likely dissolved or absorbed.  

**Defining recent actions:**  
- After being broken by the loss of his mercenary crew and the theft of their weapons, was recruited by Calmer and elevated to supreme command of the Earth Temple Forces.  
- Successfully carried Calmer up two flights of brown marble stairs after the wraith battle.  
- Dragged the incapacitated cleric Calmer back through the corridor when healing resources were depleted.  
- Delivered urgent battlefield intelligence: “Belsornig’s people are at his door. He’s screaming for you.” and “Alrrem’s on his pyre.”  
- Accepted and executed the order to stay behind and guard Calmer without question.  
- Said “okay, boss” prior to the infiltration of the Water Temple.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Not motivated by coin or faction loyalty; follows truth and direct command.  
- Rejects mercenary deals and external influence; his loyalty is absolute to those who give him clear, honest orders.  
- Views himself not as a commander but as a tool: “You didn’t put me in charge of mercenaries, because I tell you what to do.”  

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between earlier claims that Dren led the “Crimson Guard” and “Broken Blades” versus his current role as a personal enforcer with only two mercenaries — it is unclear whether these were formal factions he commanded or informal labels now obsolete.  
- The fate of the rest of his original mercenary force (Broken Blades/Crimson Guard) is unmentioned after ch27; their current status is unknown.  
- Whether “Crimson Guard” still exists as a named unit or was a temporary designation is ambiguous.  
- The nature of his “two mercenaries” — whether they are the same ones from ch29, remnants of Broken Blades, or new hires — is unspecified.  
- The exact reason for his loyalty (negotiation? truth? personal respect?) is implied but not definitively stated as a single cause.  
- Whether Dren still possesses any personal weapons or armor from his time as captain of the Broken Blades is unclear.  
- The phrase “he was recruited by Sequoia” in [ch29] conflicts with no other mention of Sequoia; no other fact identifies Sequoia’s role or identity, leaving the nature of this recruitment ambiguous.
