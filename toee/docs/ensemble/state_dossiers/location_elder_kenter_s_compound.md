### Elder Kenter’s Compound

Elder Kenter’s compound is a defensible residence enclosed by an eight-foot-high stone wall and a heavy gate. It serves as the political heart of the village and houses five families — Elder Kenter and his wife, his four sons and their wives — along with servants. The interior includes a large seating area furnished with several comfortable chairs, a divan, colorful rugs, and a small table.
