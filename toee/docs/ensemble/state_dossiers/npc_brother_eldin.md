### Brother Eldin

**Current status:** Alive but wounded  
**Current location:** Abbey of Beory  
**Allegiance/faction:** Abbey of Beory (abbot)  
**Current possessions / notable items:** None explicitly stated, but holds authority over the Abbey and its lore  
**Current assignment / role:** Abbot of the Abbey of Beory; guardian of the Chest of Rao’s lore; tasked with ensuring its return to the Archclericy of Veluna  
**Defining recent actions:** Survived the ritual chamber collapse; revealed the Chest of Rao’s origin and divine significance (artifact of great good, built by high priests of Rao); warned the party that the chest attracts both evil claimants and forces of good from the Archclericy of Veluna; urged the party to return it to avoid peril; rewarded the party with wealth and magic items.  

**Key relationships:**  
- Formerly led the “Path of Righteousness” trials with Sister Alina (ch12)  
- Now serves as abbot and spiritual authority at the Abbey of Beory  
- Has no current alliance with the party — they are now recipients of his guidance and reward  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Believes the Chest of Rao must be returned to the Archclericy of Veluna to prevent misuse and divine retribution  
- Understands the chest’s dual nature: a vessel of great good that is also a dangerous magical battery  
- Holds deep knowledge of Rao’s faith and the old ways of Beory’s faith (implied by lore delivery and the stone guardian’s riddle)

## Uncertainty
- Name discrepancy: Earlier chapters refer to “Brother Eldren,” while later chapters refer to “Brother Eldin.” The final state (ch14) confirms “Eldin” as the abbot alive in the Abbey — but it is unclear if “Eldren” and “Eldin” are the same person, a clerical error, or two distinct individuals. The stone guardian is attributed to “Brother Eldrin” (ch14), a third variant.  
- Attribution of the “Path of Righteousness” leadership: Was it Eldren or Eldin? If they are the same, then Eldin led the trials before becoming abbot — but if they are different, then the early events are unrelated to the current Eldin.  
- The stone guardian’s connection: Was it created by Eldin, Eldren, or Eldrin? The text says “Brother Eldrin’s stone guardian,” but no other entity by that name is mentioned.  
- Whether Sister Alina is still present or alive — her status is not mentioned after ch12.
