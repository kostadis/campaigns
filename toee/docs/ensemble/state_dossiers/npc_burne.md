### Burne

**Current status:** Alive  
**Current location:** Hommlet  
**Allegiance/faction:** Local council of Hommlet; formerly an agent of Verbobonc  
**Current possessions / notable items:** Leisure suit (last seen wearing it; mage robe no longer referenced)  
**Current assignment / role:** Local council member of Hommlet; actively involved in addressing the resurgence of the Temple of Elemental Evil  
**Key relationships:** Collaborates with Rufus; reports to and coordinates with other council members (Osler Goodingut, Jaroo); previously worked under Verbobonc authority  
**Defining recent actions:**  
- Present at the council meeting in Hommlet where the party presented undeniable proof of the Temple of Elemental Evil’s resurgence and the death of Lareth.  
- Agreed that the party must travel to Nulb to confront the threat.  
- Previously commissioned the party to investigate sabotage at the construction site and later urged them to deal with the Moathouse.  
- Shifted from covert Verbobonc agent (wearing mage robe, overseeing “Burne’s Folly”) to public local council member, abandoning prior cover identity.  

**Revealed motivations or secrets:**  
- Formerly acted under Verbobonc directives to suppress local dissent and cover up failures (e.g., the missing art festival chest), promoting plausible deniability.  
- Now publicly acknowledges the existential threat of the Temple of Elemental Evil and supports direct action, indicating a shift in allegiance from bureaucratic cover-up to local defense.  
- Maintains a close personal relationship with Rufus, consistent since early encounters.

## Uncertainty
- Contradiction between Burne’s earlier identity as a Verbobonc mage and his current role as Hommlet council member — no explicit statement confirms whether he was fired, defected, or reassigned.  
- The transition from wearing a mage robe to a leisure suit is noted, but the timeline and reason for the change are unexplained.  
- Whether Burne still has any official ties to Verbobonc is unclear — his council role supersedes prior duties, but no source confirms severance.  
- Attribution of “Village Elder” and “militia captain” titles in ch07 conflicts with his later confirmed role as council member; these may be informal descriptors or errors, but cannot be resolved.  
- The nature of his relationship with Rufus is consistently implied but never explicitly defined (romantic, professional, or both) beyond “secretly in a relationship” in ch03 — later chapters do not confirm if this still holds.
