# Jorin Pike Kale

## Identity
- Role: River sailor, former prisoner
- Faction: None
- First appearance: Chapter 21 — discovered as one of seven prisoners in a locked chamber within Earth Temple territory

## Personality & Motivations
- Motivated by survival and gratitude; shared useful intelligence unprompted as a way of repaying the party for his freedom.
- Practical and knowledgeable about river trade and dock operations.

## History with the Party
- **Chapter 21:** Found imprisoned by Dick Rentsch's men as punishment for making a pass at a woman named Dala (recorded at the time as "Dalia"; see `dala.md`). The party freed him and staged his escape to look like the work of the Fire Temple. In gratitude, he volunteered intelligence about Captain Tolub's pirate ship schedule at the docks in Nulb, noting Tolub had already betrayed the party by directing them into a Fire Temple ambush.

## Current Status
- **Last known location:** Unknown — fled following the staged escape.
- **Active plans:** Presumably returning to life as a river sailor.
- **What the party knows:** He was freed and gave them Tolub's dock schedule.
- **What remains hidden:** His current whereabouts; whether he stays quiet about being rescued by the party.

## Relationships
- **Dick Rentsch:** Imprisoned Jorin as punishment — antagonistic.
- **Dala:** The woman whose attention led to his imprisonment. GM-side, she is Dick Rentsch's lover (entity_registry.yaml) — which is why Rentsch's men moved against Jorin. The party does not know this. See `dala.md`.
- **Captain Tolub:** Aware of Tolub's schedule and operations at Nulb docks; no direct relationship noted.
- **The Party:** Grateful; cooperative during single interaction.
