# Captain Tolub

## Identity
- **Role:** Pirate captain, master of the *Iron Dagger*; equal parts pirate and politician
- **Faction (GM truth):** **Water Temple** — Belsornig's man. The Village of Nulb sourcebook repeats it throughout: *"Belsornig, through Tolub and Vesrek, uses piracy to destabilize rivals"*, *"Tolub is Belsornig's river fist"*, and his own entry gives his motivation as *"Dominate Nulb's river trade and amass wealth while strengthening the Water Temple."* Fire's man in Nulb is **Wat**, not Tolub.
- **Faction (what the party believes):** Fire Temple. They have excellent in-character reason — see the ch.17 betrayal below — and the misread is intact and playable. Do not correct it in front of them.
- **How both are true:** Tolub sent them to burn on the Fire Temple's agents. A Belsornig man aiming marks at a rival prophet's den *is* Water Temple work — the sourcebook's "destabilize rivals" in practice. The party read the destination as the employer.
- **Base of operations:** Boatman's Tavern, Nulb; runs a pirate ship out of the Nulb docks on a known schedule
- **First appearance:** The party met him at the Boatman's Tavern in Nulb
- *Also known as: Tolub (short form). NOTE: "Tolubb"/"Captain Tolubb" are transcription garbles, NOT alternate names — GM ruling 2026-09-05. The module spells it Tolub 95 times and Tolubb appears in no source. Moved to the VTT glossary as wrong-forms.*

## Personality & Motivations
- Driven by personal gain and river-trade dominance; his partnership with Belsornig funds his ventures, and his growing ambitions bring him into friction with Selentis
- Boisterous, intimidating, and socially dominant — commands a crew that laughs on cue
- Uses surface-level charm and generosity (buying drinks) to disarm marks while concealing treacherous intent
- Comfortable with deception; frames betrayal as helpful misdirection; willing to sell out allies for personal gain or self-preservation

## History with the Party
1. **Boatman's Tavern, first meeting:** Tolub was holding court at the tavern, trading bad jokes with his laughing crew. After some charm and negotiation from the party, he agreed to buy them drinks.
2. **Information on Serena:** Confirmed that Serena had been handed over to the Iron Lash brothel as payment for something, and mockingly revealed the brothel was located upstairs in the same building.
3. **Deliberate misdirection (ch.17):** When the party sought contact with the Earth Temple and Romag, Tolub directed them instead to the Hall of the Scarlet Moon — a trap set by the Fire Temple. GM-side this is Water work: he pointed Romag's would-be contacts at Alrrem's people and let the two rivals maul each other.
4. **Post-departure smear:** After the party left, Tolub told others in the tavern he had "disposed of them," calling them meddlers who didn't belong.
5. **Treachery confirmed:** Following the party's survival of the Fire Temple trap, Skole told them Tolub had betrayed them *on behalf of the fire prophet*. **Treat that attribution as Skole's claim, not established fact** — Skole hates Tolub and Lodriss, and it is the main reason the party has Tolub's allegiance wrong.
6. **Ship schedule leaked:** Jorin Pike Kale provided the party with Tolub's ship schedule at the Nulb docks — actionable intelligence on his predictable movements that the party flagged as significant.

## Current Status
- **Last known location:** Boatman's Tavern, Nulb; running his pirate ship on a known, predictable schedule
- **Active operations:** Belsornig's river carrier — smuggling, slaving and extortion at the docks, feeding the Temple's covert economy. Tolerated, taxed and watched by Selentis.
- **What the party knows:** Tolub intentionally sent them into a Fire Temple trap; they believe he works for the fire prophet; his dock schedule is known (via Jorin Pike Kale). As of ch.34 he is still boasting they are dead.
- **What remains hidden:** That his actual patron is **Belsornig / the Water Temple**; that Skole's "fire prophet" attribution was wrong or self-serving; what he was paid; and whether he knows they survived or have his schedule.

## Relationships
- **Belsornig (Water Temple):** His actual patron. Belsornig funds his ventures; Tolub is the Water Temple's river fist. *(GM-side — the party does not know this.)*
- **Alrrem / Fire Temple:** NOT his employer, despite the party's belief. He aimed them at Fire's people in ch.17 and let them be ambushed.
- **Selentis:** Friction. She taxes and watches his runs and would see him "lamed, not killed"; his ambitions chafe against her authority.
- **Grud Squinteye:** His mate and enforcer; commands the crew when Tolub is absent.
- **Marad Vesrek (Crimson Trader):** Fellow pirate, also loosely allied to Water through Belsornig.
- **Lodriss:** Boatman's Tavern power broker who cultivates ties with him while guarding her independence.
- **Romag / Earth Temple:** Actively working against them by misdirecting would-be contacts
- **Skole:** Was aware of Tolub's treachery and disclosed it to the party
- **Jorin Pike Kale:** Knows Tolub's schedule well enough to share it — nature of their relationship unclear
- **Albreth & Ingulf:** Fire Temple priests Tolub directed the party toward at the Hall of the Scarlet Moon ambush
- **The Party:** Views them as threats or meddlers; believed (or claimed) he had eliminated them

## Arc Score Events
- **Decrease:** Deliberately misdirected the party into a Fire Temple trap — a clear act of betrayal once Skole confirmed his role.
