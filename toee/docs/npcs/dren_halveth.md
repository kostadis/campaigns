# Dren Halveth

## Identity
- **Role:** Captain of the Broken Blades, a professional mercenary company. *Also known as: Dren.*
- **Faction:** Nominally affiliated with the Earth Temple (recruited Ch.20)
- **First Appearance:** Chapter 20, Watersteide Hostel in Nulb — questioned the party about their purpose, allegiances, and patron gods

*Note: an earlier duplicate dossier (`dren.md`, since merged into this file) mislabeled him "Leader of the Crimson Guard faction" — that title belongs to no one; the actual Crimson Reapers leadership is Zelt the Rat (`zelt_the_rat.md`) and the unnamed hostel spokesperson (`the_crimson_reaper_unnamed_spokesperson.md`), both distinct NPCs. That dossier's other content, once corrected, has been folded in below.*

## Personality & Motivations
- **Core Goals:** Securing profitable, sustainable work for his company; protecting his crew from unnecessary risk
- Pragmatic and transactional — weighs temple affiliations by material benefit and survival odds rather than ideology; motivated by plunder, combat, and the strength of whoever he's aligned with
- Projects controlled authority; his crew mirrors this, relaxed but ready for violence
- Skeptical of propaganda and cannon-fodder arrangements; responds to demonstrated respect and concrete offers
- Visibly impressed by displays of power — agreed to assist the party without hesitation after witnessing their victory over the Chimera

## History with the Party
1. **Ch. 20 — Watersteide Hostel:** Approached the party and interrogated them on their purpose in Nulb. Revealed his company's weapons had been stolen by the Crimson Reapers and tasked the party with recovery.
2. **Ch. 20 — Hostel, continued:** Party returned with intelligence that the Crimson Reapers served a unified organization behind all four elemental temples, delivering a join-or-die ultimatum by midnight. Dren weighed his options, expressing skepticism about the Earth Temple and interest in the Water Temple's money.
3. **Ch. 20 — Recruitment:** Sequoia persuaded him to side with the Earth Temple, arguing the Water Temple relied on propaganda while the Earth Temple demonstrated genuine commitment. The party sweetened the deal by offering shared territory and 100 gold pieces. Dren agreed.
4. **Ch. 20 — Departure:** Water Temple agent Wat whispered something to Dren as the party left; Dren shoved him away, indicating he held to his decision.
5. **Ch. 20 — Earth Temple:** Dren and the Broken Blades arrived successfully. Temple priest Romag assigned them the newly cleared territory under the party's nominal command.
6. **Prisoner Rescue (Ch.21, adjacent territory):** Dren and his men were settled in territory adjacent to the prison chamber during the party's rescue operation there. The party flagged him as a potential complication and posted Calmer at the door as a precaution. Dren took no direct action and had no interaction with the party.
7. **Romag Assassination Setup (Ch.27-28):** The party enlisted Dren to arrange a morning meeting with High Priest Romag, ensuring he would be present and off-guard. Dren agreed readily — impressed by the party's reputation — and returned with confirmation that Romag was looking forward to the meeting.
8. **Temple War (Ch.28):** As Hartsch mobilized his forces, the party encountered Dren preparing his men for battle. After a brief exchange, the party directed him and the Broken Blades into the three-way temple conflict, rallying his men with promises of blood and loot.

## Current Status
- **Last Known Location:** Deployed into the Earth Temple's three-way war as part of the assault force; the war has since concluded (Alrrem and Belsornig both dead; Hartsch also since murdered by Belsornig's assassins).
- **Active:** Occupying Earth Temple territory. With both Romag and Hartsch dead, there is no surviving Earth Temple authority above him except the party — formal installation of his command is an outstanding item (see `notes/phase3_endgame.md`'s "Barkinar's List" and `notes/upper_temple_network.md`).
- **What the party knows:** He is loyal to the deal as struck; he rejected the Water Temple's last-minute approach; he is a mercenary willing to work with them for the right incentives; he arranged the Romag meeting and fought in the temple war on their behalf.
- **What remains hidden:** His long-term intentions once formally established — whether he views the Earth Temple arrangement as permanent or merely convenient; the content of Wat's whisper; his ultimate loyalties once the dust fully settles.

## Relationships
- **The Party:** Professional alliance; owes them for the recruitment deal and the 100 gp offer; arranged the Romag assassination meeting on their behalf; responds to strength and respects demonstrated power.
- **Romag (Earth Temple Priest, deceased):** Received his original assignment from Romag; later helped set up Romag's assassination at the party's request.
- **Hartsch (deceased):** Mobilized his forces against Hartsch's during the temple war; Hartsch has since been murdered by Belsornig's assassins.
- **Wat (Water Temple Agent, Hostel Barkeep):** Attempted last-minute contact as the party recruited him; Dren rejected him physically — suggests active distrust or a clean break.
- **Crimson Reapers / Zelt the Rat:** Stole his company's weapons; adversarial, though the fate of that dispute is unresolved in the notes.
- **Sequoia (Party Member):** Directly persuaded him to side with the Earth Temple; likely holds her in higher regard than other party members.
