# Rannos Davl

> **Naming note (GM ruling, 2026-08-23).** This dossier merges three files that described one
> man: `rannos.md`, `ranos_davl.md` and `ranus_duval.md`. **Rannos Davl** is the canonical
> spelling — T1–4 module canon, and the form used in `docs/entity_registry.yaml` and
> `notes/proper_nouns_adventure.txt`. `Ranos Davl` and `Ranus Duval` are transcription
> garblings and are now glossed in `notes/vtt_transcription_corrections.md`. A prior pass had
> also given "Ranus Duval" the alias *"the Duke"* — that was wrong; **only Thorne** carries
> that name.

## Identity
- **Role:** Rotund trading-post proprietor in Hommlet; covert agent of the Deep Temple of
  Elemental Evil. Chaotic evil, Level 10 Thief.
- **Faction:** Temple of Elemental Evil, alongside his partner Gremag. Named by a captured
  cultist as working for "a larger competing organization" — the Deep Temple, as seen from the
  local Earth Temple's point of view.
- **First Appearance:** Referenced before he was met — introduced as Thorne's employer and the
  man who had promised Zephyr a man-at-arms. First direct contact came when he answered a
  party-sent note requesting a meeting, countering with his own terms: his establishment,
  after dark.
- *Also known as: Rannos, Ranos Davl, Ranus Duval.*

## Personality & Motivations
- **Core goal:** Served the Temple's interests in Hommlet; sought to acquire the Chest of Rao
  on the Temple's behalf.
- Suspicious and calculating — refused the party's proposed meeting location in favour of his
  own controlled territory.
- Contemptuous of the party; he and Gremag mocked their attempt to negotiate a peaceful
  withdrawal from the region.
- Operated as a patron and employer, placing his own people close to others — it was he who
  sent Thorne to the party.

## History with the Party
1. **Before contact:** Promised Zephyr a man-at-arms, and made good on it by dispatching
   **Thorne**, who confirmed directly that Rannos was the one who sent him.
2. **Chapter 10.5 (Gallows Corner):** A captured cultist, broken by Zephyr's intimidation,
   named him as an agent of a larger competing organisation and tied him to a missing chest —
   the one meant for **Romag** — believed held at an abandoned monastery near Gallows Corner.
   The party had not yet met him. (This is the testimony the old `ranus_duval.md` recorded
   under a garbled name.)
3. **The meeting:** The party sent a note requesting a meeting; Rannos answered with suspicion
   and set his own terms — his establishment, after dark.
4. **The confrontation:** The party tried to negotiate for the Temple to abandon the region.
   Rannos and Gremag mocked them and demanded the Chest of Rao.
5. **His death (Chapters 12–13):** With Gremag already dead, Rannos was severely wounded by
   **Sequoia** (26 damage), then finished by **Calmer's** Spiritual Weapon while he stood at
   2 hp.
6. **The cleanup:** **Thorne** dismembered the body to prevent *Speak with Dead*. Bloodied
   clothes were planted outside town, and the party bluffed **Ostler Gundigoot** with a cover
   story that both traders had left urgently after securing a magical chest.

## Current Status
- **Status:** Deceased; body dismembered.
- **Cover story (party-constructed):** Rannos and Gremag left Hommlet in a hurry after
  obtaining a magical chest.
- **What the party knows:** He was a Temple agent working with Gremag; he wanted the Chest of
  Rao; he was Thorne's former employer.
- **What remains hidden:** Whether the Temple is aware of his death or will investigate his
  disappearance. The chest tied to him — the one meant for Romag, at the monastery near
  Gallows Corner — and his exact standing in the wider organisation were never established
  before he died.

## Relationships
- **Gremag:** Co-conspirator and fellow Temple agent; tall, thin, fussy, Level 7 Assassin.
  Killed by the party in the same encounter.
- **Thorne:** Former employer/employee. Thorne was hired through him and held no loyalty to
  him — he gave Rannos up without hesitation, then disposed of his body.
- **Zephyr:** Rannos promised him a man-at-arms and delivered Thorne.
- **Romag:** The missing chest tied to Rannos was originally intended for Romag; the nature of
  their relationship was never established.
- **Ostler Gundigoot:** Local contact who accepted the party's cover story about the two
  traders' departure.
- **Unnamed cultist:** Knew of Rannos's operation and named him under interrogation;
  relationship unclear.
- **The Party:** Killed by Sequoia and Calmer; body disposed of by Thorne.
