# Dala

*Merged 2026-09-05 from `dala.md` + `dalia.md`. The GM ruled during a
`/staged-consistency` pass that Dala and "Dalia" are the same person and that
**Dala** is the canonical adventure character. The registry corroborates it: Dala is
"barmaid at the Waterside Hostel, Level 3 Thief and Dick Rentsch's lover" — which
joins the two former dossiers directly, since Jorin Pike Kale was imprisoned by
Rentsch's men for pursuing her.*

## Identity
- Role / title / faction affiliation: Barmaid at the Waterside Hostel; Level 3 Thief; Dick Rentsch's lover
- Registry aliases: `Dela`. `Dalia` is a transcription garble, not an approved alternate name.
- First appearance: Referenced only, never seen. Named by Wat (bartender, Waterside Hostel) while warning the party, and separately by Jorin Pike Kale.

## Personality & Motivations
- No direct information. She has never appeared on screen; everything known is second-hand.

## History with the Party
- **Wat's warning:** Wat the bartender mentioned Dala by name while warning the party and serving them gin. No further context was given. The party has not met her.
- **Jorin Pike Kale's imprisonment:** Jorin named Dala as the woman whose attention he had been seeking. That pursuit drew the notice of Dick Rentsch's men and led directly to his imprisonment. The registry's note that Dala is Rentsch's lover supplies the motive the party never learned.

## Current Status
- Last known location: Presumably the Waterside Hostel, Nulb
- Active plans or operations: Unknown
- **Party knowledge:** Her name, that Wat warned them in connection with her, and that Jorin's interest in her triggered his capture. The party does NOT know she is Rentsch's lover — that is GM-side, and it is the fact that makes both mentions cohere.

## Relationships
- **Dick Rentsch:** Her lover (registry). Explains why his men moved against Jorin.
- **Jorin Pike Kale:** Sought her attention; imprisoned by Rentsch's men as a result.
- **Wat (bartender, Waterside Hostel):** Colleague; aware of her, and raised her name in a warning to the party.

## Arc Score Events
- None recorded.
