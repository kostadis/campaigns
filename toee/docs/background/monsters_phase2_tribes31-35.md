# Phase 2 — Global Tribes 31–35 (Scan E: Elemental Nodes)

## Tribe 31 — Air Node natives — the Stormwrack Brood
Indigenous stormborn guardians of the Air Node — dragons, giants, and lesser motes churning the endless gales.

### 31.1 The Frostmolt Brood
- **Monster type:** young white dragon
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `3 {@creature young white dragon}`
- **CR (individual):** 6
- **Named as:** the Frostmolt Brood
- **Description:** A single lair family of three young white dragons hoarding the node's heights. Includes Rimefang the Thrice-Molted, a blue-frost-scaled wyrmling with one eye clouded white from an old lightning strike.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.2 The Nine-Winds Skirl
- **Monster type:** air elemental
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `6 {@creature air elemental}s (encountered singly or in pairs)`
- **CR (individual):** 5 (air elemental base)
- **Named as:** the Nine-Winds Skirl
- **Description:** Six shrieking gales of condensed wind roaming the updrafts, met singly or in pairs. Skirl-of-the-Nine-Winds manifests as a corkscrew of sleet that never fully closes into a single shape.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.3 The Hail-Crown Reavers
- **Monster type:** cloud giant
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `5 {@creature cloud giant}s`
- **CR (individual):** 9
- **Named as:** the Hail-Crown Reavers
- **Description:** Five well-defended giants in a lair-cage of wind, with a 50% chance of 1–3 human captives. Cumulos Hail-Crown wears a diadem of frozen hailstones and hoards captives aloft.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.4 The Screeching Shelves
- **Monster type:** hieracosphinx (use manticore as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `8 hieracosphinxes (2 lairs of 4; use {@creature manticore} or {@creature griffon} as base)`
- **CR (individual):** 3 (manticore base)
- **Named as:** the Screeching Shelves
- **Description:** Hawk-headed predators diving from the node's high shelves, kept in two lairs of four. Talonmaw Screech is the lead hunter, missing three tail-feathers, who drops from the shelves.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.5 The Gemward Motes
- **Monster type:** ildriss grue (use will-o'-wisp as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node (lair A18)
- **Verbatim count:** `8 ildriss grues (use {@creature will-o'-wisp} as base, with fog cloud and dispel weather effects)`
- **CR (individual):** 2 (will-o'-wisp base)
- **Named as:** the Gemward Motes
- **Description:** Eight fog-wreathed motes guarding the aquamarine power gem that keys the gate to the Water Node. Gemwarden Ildriss-Prime is the eldest guardian-mote coiled around the gem.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.6 The Hollowbreath Drift
- **Monster type:** mihstu (use water weird as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 mihstu (use {@creature water weird} or {@creature air elemental} as base, Constitution drain: DC 15 Constitution saving throw or lose 1 point of Constitution maximum; regained at 1/day with rest)`
- **CR (individual):** 3 (water weird base)
- **Named as:** the Hollowbreath Drift
- **Description:** Two drifting vapor-knots that drain Constitution from their victims. Hollowbreath is a knot of vapor that leaves prey gasping as it saps their vitality.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.7 The Frostcroak Colony
- **Monster type:** giant ice toad (use giant toad with cold aura)
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `10 giant ice toads (use {@creature giant toad} with cold aura: creatures within 10 ft. at start of their turn make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 3d6 cold damage on a failed save, or half on a success; aura activates every other round)`
- **CR (individual):** 1/4 (giant toad base)
- **Named as:** the Frostcroak Colony
- **Description:** Ten cold-aura toads crusting the shelf-stone with frost, kept near the other ice creatures. Chillcroak the Slow is the largest of the brood, its aura frosting the ledges.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.8 The Gassy Lair-Runners
- **Monster type:** vapor rat (use giant rat with gaseous form)
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `16 vapor rats (2 lairs of 8; use {@creature giant rat} with gaseous form at will; on serious wound or death, creates a stinking cloud effect centred on one creature within 5–8 feet: DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be incapacitated until end of next turn)`
- **CR (individual):** 1/4 (giant rat base)
- **Named as:** the Gassy Lair-Runners
- **Description:** Sixteen rat-things in two lairs of eight near the giants and dragons, shifting to gaseous form and releasing stinking clouds when wounded or slain.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.9 The Cone-Gale Wardens
- **Monster type:** vortex (use air elemental as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `5 vortexes (use {@creature air elemental} as base; cone attack 40 ft. × 10 ft., DC 14 Strength saving throw or take 1d3 bludgeoning damage per round with a 5% cumulative chance per round of being killed outright)`
- **CR (individual):** 5 (air elemental base)
- **Named as:** the Cone-Gale Wardens
- **Description:** Five spinning air-cone predators that fling creatures about with their blasting gales, churning through the node between the greater families.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 31.10 The Ethereal Gale-Walkers
- **Monster type:** wind walker (use air elemental as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Air Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 wind walkers (use {@creature air elemental} as base with ethereal movement, mind-reading, and spell immunities per original source)`
- **CR (individual):** 5 (air elemental base)
- **Named as:** the Ethereal Gale-Walkers
- **Description:** Two eerie, mind-reading entities moving ethereally through the node, immune to spells, among the lesser motes that churn the gales.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

## Tribe 32 — Earth Node inhabitants — the Stone-Choir
Deep-rooted, patient burrowers and ambushers of the Earth Node, measuring time in the settling of stone.

### 32.1 The Petrifier Pair
- **Monster type:** basilisk
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 {@creature basilisk|MM|basilisks}`
- **CR (individual):** 3
- **Named as:** the Petrifier Pair
- **Description:** Two stone-gazing lizards that petrify trespassers in the node's main residents list. Gravelgaze the Petrifier bears a chipped horn-ridge and garnet-like eyes.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.2 The Tumblestone Colony
- **Monster type:** Bowlers (use twig blight stats as proxies)
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `12 Bowlers (use {@creature twig blight} stats as proxies for small lurking creatures)`
- **CR (individual):** 1/8 (twig blight base)
- **Named as:** the Tumblestone Colony
- **Description:** A colony of twelve small lurking stone-spheres surviving by camouflage and cowardice. Tumblestone Rattle is a boulder-mimic whose "shell" bears a fossil spiral no other bowler shows.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.3 The Garnet Wardens
- **Monster type:** chaggrin grue (use shadow as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node (lair E11)
- **Verbatim count:** `5 Chaggrin grues (no 5e equivalent; treat as {@creature shadow} variants)`
- **CR (individual):** 1/2 (shadow base)
- **Named as:** the Garnet Wardens
- **Description:** Five shadow-dark motes guarding the garnet power gem that opens the way to the Fire Node. Chaggrin Gemward is the warden coiled on the gem in lair E11.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.4 The Oldslate Family
- **Monster type:** stone giant
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `6 {@creature stone giant}s`
- **CR (individual):** 7
- **Named as:** the Oldslate Family
- **Description:** A large single family of six in a defensible overhang lair. Cairnfather Oldslate is the grey-bearded patriarch who carved his lair into the rock.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.5 The Ironhide Bulls
- **Monster type:** gorgon
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 {@creature gorgon}s`
- **CR (individual):** 5
- **Named as:** the Ironhide Bulls
- **Description:** Two iron-plated bulls whose petrifying vapor is avoided by nearly everyone in the node. Ironhide Bellows snorts fog that petrifies the cavern.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.6 The Deepschool Rock-Fish
- **Monster type:** Khargra (use xorn as proxy)
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `4 Khargra (no direct 5e equivalent; use {@creature xorn} as a proxy for burrowing stony ambush predators)`
- **CR (individual):** 5 (xorn base)
- **Named as:** the Deepschool Rock-Fish
- **Description:** A wandering school of four burrowing rock-fish ambush predators. Deepschool Khargra-Lead is the lead rock-fish with teeth worn to iron nubs.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.7 The Mica-Stalkers
- **Monster type:** rock reptile (use giant lizard with chameleon ambush)
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `4 Rock reptiles in two lairs of 2 each (no direct 5e equivalent; use {@creature giant lizard} with chameleon ambush traits, or {@creature basilisk} as a proxy for large territorial reptiles)`
- **CR (individual):** 1/4 (giant lizard base)
- **Named as:** the Mica-Stalkers
- **Description:** Four territorial reptiles in two lairs of two that hunt by chameleon ambush. Chameleon Grit is flecked with mica that betrays it only in torchlight.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.8 The Crossroads Strand-Caster
- **Monster type:** storoper (use roper as proxy)
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `1 Storoper (no direct 5e equivalent; use {@creature roper} as the closest proxy — ambush predator with charm and strength-sapping strands)`
- **CR (individual):** 5 (roper base)
- **Named as:** the Crossroads Strand-Caster
- **Description:** A single ambush predator lurking near a major intersection with charm and strength-sapping strands. The Storoper of the Crossroads is the sole of its kind here.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.9 The Friendly Deep-Burrowers
- **Monster type:** xaren (use xorn as proxy)
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 Xaren (no direct 5e equivalent; use {@creature xorn} as a proxy for shifting, fire- and cold-immune burrowing creatures native to the Elemental Plane of Earth)`
- **CR (individual):** 5 (xorn base)
- **Named as:** the Friendly Deep-Burrowers
- **Description:** Two neutral, shifting, fire- and cold-immune burrowers friendly to fellow plane-natives, keeping to themselves in the node.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 32.10 The Gem-Diggers
- **Monster type:** xorn
- **Source:** ToEE Earth Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 {@creature xorn}s`
- **CR (individual):** 5
- **Named as:** the Gem-Diggers
- **Description:** Two neutral xorn friendly to plane-natives, burrowing the earth for treasure; part of the node's quiet burrowing majority.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

## Tribe 33 — Fire Node inhabitants — the Cinder Court
The most predatory ash-born court of ember, smoke, and living flame.

### 33.1 The Ashwing Swarm
- **Monster type:** fire bat (use stirge stats with fire flavouring)
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 lairs of 12 {@creature fire bat|ToB} each (use {@creature stirge} statistics with fire damage flavouring if fire bats are unavailable)`
- **CR (individual):** 1/4 (stirge base)
- **Named as:** the Ashwing Swarm
- **Description:** Two lairs of twelve fire-damage bats that haunt and track the party through the node. Ashwing Flicker is the ragged, scorched-membrane straggler leading the swarm.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.2 The Shadow-Scourge
- **Monster type:** bodak
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `1 {@creature bodak|MTF}`
- **CR (individual):** 6
- **Named as:** the Shadow-Scourge
- **Description:** A single undead horror that hits-and-runs, hides, and tracks the party through the node. (Note: the bodak is a sometime ally of Darley, a named NPC excluded from renaming.)
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.3 The Sundered Pair
- **Monster type:** adult red dragon
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `2 {@creature adult red dragon}s (encountered singly)`
- **CR (individual):** 17
- **Named as:** the Sundered Pair
- **Description:** Two former mates now split and encountered singly, each casting one spell per day. Cinderscourge Vorth (the larger, sulking beast) and Emberscourge Vashti (now ranging apart, scales dulled to matte coal) are the two.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.4 The Smokeless Schemer
- **Monster type:** efreeti
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `1 {@creature efreeti}`
- **CR (individual):** 11
- **Named as:** the Smokeless Schemer
- **Description:** A single brass-skinned efreeti who may masquerade as a mere fire elemental. Qadesh the Smokeless is the schemer who intrigues in disguise.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.5 The Blazewreath Band
- **Monster type:** fire elemental
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `6 {@creature fire elemental}s (encountered singly or in pairs)`
- **CR (individual):** 5
- **Named as:** the Blazewreath Band
- **Description:** Six living flames roaming singly or in pairs with no lair. Blazewreath burns hotter and bluer at its core than its kin.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.6 The Emberchoke Nest
- **Monster type:** fire snake (use giant poisonous snake stats)
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `5 {@creature fire snake|MTF}s (use {@creature giant poisonous snake} statistics with fire damage and a paralysis effect in place of poison)`
- **CR (individual):** 1/4 (giant poisonous snake base)
- **Named as:** the Emberchoke Nest
- **Description:** Five fire-damage, paralysis-venomed serpents lairing wherever the coals glow. Emberchoke Coil is the longest of the nest.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.7 The Exploding Croakers
- **Monster type:** firetoad (use giant toad stats)
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `1–2 firetoads (use {@creature giant toad} statistics; on death, explodes dealing fire damage equal to remaining hp in a 20-foot radius, DC 14 Dexterity saving throw for half)`
- **CR (individual):** 1 (giant toad base)
- **Named as:** the Exploding Croakers
- **Description:** Scattered fire-toads that explode on death in a 20-foot fire burst (DC 14 Dex for half), lurk wherever the coals glow, 1–2 at a time.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.8 The Slagbeard Clan
- **Monster type:** fire giant
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `6 {@creature fire giant}s`
- **CR (individual):** 9
- **Named as:** the Slagbeard Clan
- **Description:** Six fire giants in one or two lairs, probably sharing with a non-Chaotic ally. Forgefather Slagbeard is the soot-plaited beard who shares his lair with an ally.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.9 The Smoky-Quartz Wardens
- **Monster type:** harginn grue (use will-o'-wisp as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node (lair F9)
- **Verbatim count:** `8 harginn grues (use {@creature will-o'-wisp} or {@creature hell hound} as a base, adding shapechange and blink abilities; treat as CR 4 each)`
- **CR (individual):** 2 (will-o'-wisp base)
- **Named as:** the Smoky-Quartz Wardens
- **Description:** Eight shapeshifting flame-motes guarding the smoky quartz power gem that unlocks the Air gate. Harginn Gemward is the flame-warden of the gem, never leaving its post.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 33.10 The Wary Coals
- **Monster type:** salamander
- **Source:** ToEE Fire Node
- **Verbatim count:** `3 {@creature salamander}s`
- **CR (individual):** 5
- **Named as:** the Wary Coals
- **Description:** Three salamanders, recently attacked by humans and giants, who usually flee if damaged and negotiate if cornered. Quench the Wary is scarred and quick to flee or bargain.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

## Tribe 34 — Water Node inhabitants — the Drowned Choir
Cold, crushing, patient natives ruling an endless flooded dark.

### 34.1 The Pool Tyrant
- **Monster type:** dragon turtle
- **Source:** ToEE Water Node
- **Verbatim count:** `1 {@creature dragon turtle}`
- **CR (individual):** 17
- **Named as:** the Pool Tyrant
- **Description:** A single barnacle-crusted dragon turtle that considers the entire node its private pool. Old Shellbreaker is the ancient tyrant lord of the node.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 34.2 The Undertow Column
- **Monster type:** water elemental
- **Source:** ToEE Water Node
- **Verbatim count:** `1–2 {@creature water elemental}s (mix of standard and stronger variants as appropriate)`
- **CR (individual):** 5
- **Named as:** the Undertow Column
- **Description:** One or two water elementals (mix of standard and stronger variants) with no lair. Undertow is the stronger-than-standard variant manifesting as a silent churning column.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 34.3 The Palegaze Shoal
- **Monster type:** floating eye (use will-o'-wisp as base)
- **Source:** ToEE Water Node
- **Verbatim count:** `10 {@creature floating eye}s (treat as {@creature will-o'-wisp}s with a paralysis gaze; DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed until the end of the target's next turn)`
- **CR (individual):** 2 (will-o'-wisp base)
- **Named as:** the Palegaze Shoal
- **Description:** Ten paralysis-gaze eyes that always drift with other water creatures. Palegaze Prime is the boldest of the shoal, milk-white iris rimmed in blue.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 34.4 The Grotto-Kings
- **Monster type:** frost giant
- **Source:** ToEE Water Node
- **Verbatim count:** `6 {@creature frost giant}s`
- **CR (individual):** 8
- **Named as:** the Grotto-Kings
- **Description:** A frost-giant family keeping ice lizards as pets in a grotto lair, encountered in pairs or more if in water. Rimebeard the Grotto-King is the ice-braided patriarch.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

### 34.5 The Sleetscale Pets
- **Monster type:** ice lizard (use giant lizard stats)
- **Source:** ToEE Water Node
- **Verbatim count:** `Ice lizards (2):`
- **CR (individual):** 1/4 (giant lizard base)
- **Named as:** the Sleetscale Pets
- **Description:** Two node-native ice lizards kept as pets by the frost giants at the chill margins. Sleetscale is the larger, marked by a torn dewlap from old handling.
- **Named-leader:** NONE

## Tribe 35 — Wandering humans — the Bargent's Road Brigands
Gate-stranded surface bandits roaming all four nodes, desperate and lost.

### 35.1 The Bargent's Road Brigands
- **Monster type:** bandit
- **Source:** ToEE Nodes-wide (wandering)
- **Verbatim count:** `7 {@creature bandit}s (each armed with shortbows and melee weapons) led by Grank, use {@creature priest} (CR 2) with chain mail and shield.`
- **CR (individual):** 1/8
- **Named as:** the Bargent's Road Brigands
- **Description:** Seven shortbow-and-steel bandits, survivors of a surface band that fled through the gates, who cling to their cleric-captain Grank for leadership and healing. Named members include "Ferret" Dob (lean lookout, broken nose), Hesk Two-Quiver (spare shortbow-quiver), Marra of the Sinking Coin (holed copper luck-charm), Old Cathmor (grey-stubbled veteran), and Wick the Sallow (jaundice-pale, jumpy).
- **Named-leader:** Grank (EXCLUDED)
