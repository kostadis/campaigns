# The Upper Temple Network — GM Reference

The party (the Obsidian Edge) instigated a three-way war among the Earth, Fire, and Water Temples, then assassinated or absorbed every faction leader who mattered. Order of events, reconstructed from `docs/chapters/chapter_30_dungeon_boss.md` plus the user's corrections:

1. **Romag (Earth) assassinated** (ch.28) — Hartsch seized the throne.
2. **Alrrem (Fire) dies on his own pyre** (ch.28-29) — Varek Solain and Maeris Dorn both defected rather than die with him.
3. **Kelno's bridge scene runs** (`chapter_30_dungeon_boss.md`) — Kelno offers to become the party's #2 rather than their enemy. The party takes the deal (Zephyr: *"for chaos, but also for living... that's the genuine article"*). Kelno hands over intel (Belsornig's assassins already inside Hartsch's antechamber; Alrrem dying), a Dagger of Venom meant for Belsornig, and points them at the now-lightly-guarded Water Temple.
4. **Belsornig (Water) assassinated** in his inner sanctum via stealth infiltration and Kelno's dagger — but **before the party can loot him, Barkinar's forces converge and interrupt them.** Hartsch is murdered by Belsornig's own assassins around this same window — his last act kills the party's nominal boss just as the party kills him.
5. **Barkinar's first arrival** — reveals the Greater Temple had bugbear forces stationed on this level the whole time, unseen; formally hands the Upper Temple to the party with a one-week deadline (see Barkinar section below).
6. **The party returns later to finish the Water Temple** — clearing the Hall of Verdigris's guardians (the animated idol/"Juggernaut" and four gargoyle-cloaked zombies, per 5e canon Area 213) that they'd bypassed during the original stealth run, and finally looting Belsornig's sanctum/treasury properly (mace of smiting, ring of free action, half-plate +1, ceremonial haul) — this is the session captured in `summaries/20260625/session-summary.md` (2026-06-22).
7. **The acid-pool "triton" cliffhanger** (chapter 31 raw session, 2026-06-28) and Lucius's furnishing visit follow from there.

So Kelno is not an untouched thread — he's an ally, not a kill. Per `notes/phase3_endgame.md`'s stated win condition ("at least two of the four other faction leaders dead"), that threshold was cleared well before Barkinar ever made contact.

The party thinks they now run the Upper Temple autonomously. They don't, not really — see also `notes/hedrack_strategy.md` for the coup this whole promotion serves, `notes/lucius_strategy.md` and `notes/lucius_network.md` for the operational detail on the visible handler, and `notes/phase3_endgame.md` for the "Barkinar's List" to-do items this network exists to service.

## The Hidden Hand — Hedrack's Coup

### Hedrack — Supreme Commander, Mighty Priest of Iuz (secretly Zuggtmoy's man)

Officially Iuz's enforcer over the Greater Temple, tasked with keeping the elemental cults in line and preparing for the invasion of Furyondy/Veluna. Actually betraying Iuz — secretly orchestrating Zuggtmoy's release in exchange for his own godhood, using Iuz's authority structure as the launch platform for his own ascension.

**What he runs:** the entire cultivation arc the party is currently inside. He needs the Orb of Golden Death restored, Senshock (a possible Iuz informant) neutralized, and Iuz kept blind until the ritual completes — and he needs agents who can do the finding and surviving he can't risk his own troops on. That's the party.

**Personality:** never appears yet. Everything about him is filtered through Barkinar (sincere) and Lucius (knowing).

**What the party knows:** `[GM-ONLY]` — the name "Hedrack" has been mentioned once, by Lucius, as the man who sent Barkinar's list of expectations. The party has no idea who he actually is or what he wants.

### Lucius Graeme, alias "Aldren" — the friendly quartermaster (Tier 1, knows everything)

Runs Hedrack's whisper-web across Verbobonc, Nulb, and Hommlet. Holds the Selentis leash. Communes with Zuggtmoy directly. Delivers all three of the coup's manipulation arcs to the party — Barkinar genuinely doesn't know the coup exists; Lucius does, and he's the one who runs the party-facing lies. Wears an *Amulet of the Shrouded One* — the party cannot detect-evil or scry him. They have to read him with plain tradecraft, which they currently have no reason to try.

**How he introduced himself, verbatim (per `lucius_network.md`'s script):** *"I'm Aldren — well, Lucius, Aldren when I'm working, it's a long story and a dull one."*

**What he's doing right now (ch.31, per `lucius_network.md`):** pure resource play. That's deliberately held for a later visit.

**What he knows about the party** `[GM-ONLY]`: four prophets dead in a day, Barkinar handed the Upper Temple to unknowns he didn't pick, they were in Nulb recently (network flagged it, doesn't know who they saw), they're loot-rich and gear-poor, and Calmer is carrying the TZGY scarab (Senshock's control sigil) — he'd recognize it on sight and per the doc's recommendation, clocked it silently and said nothing.

**What the party knows about him:** `[KNOWN]` a soft, damp— no, wait, that's Eelrich — a soft, gray, perpetually-smiling man, sent by Barkinar, "a facilitator... a carrier of things from clever people to clever people." Gave gifts, is holding a Ring of Invisibility and a Ring of Free Action on deposit (4,000 gp each). `[SUSPECTED]` nothing — per `lucius_network.md`, Falrinth's usual priming warning ("golden eyes, don't commit, stall") has been deliberately deferred to *after* this visit, so the party has no external cue to distrust him yet.

**Quote canon:** *"Civilization is a fragile mask over the face of the elemental chaos. It's a shame when it breaks. Or a delight."*

### Barkinar — Commander of the Greater Temple (Tier 2, sincerely deceived)

An Iuz operative (initiated personally by Hedrack two years ago) who believes Hedrack is loyal to Iuz and that the party is being cultivated for legitimate Iuz-aligned operations. Positions himself as Hedrack's eventual successor. His warmth toward the party is genuine — he admires competence — *and* it is instrumental. Both at once.

**What he runs:** the visible administrative layer. His forces (bugbears in Greater Temple black) converged from three directions the instant Belsornig died — revealing they'd had troops stationed throughout that level the entire time, unseen. He came up the stairs himself rather than summon the party down, brought Kelno along as a witness, and delivered the line almost verbatim to script: *"Romag. Hartsch. Alrrem. Belsornig — problems I had on a list yesterday morning, and four problems gone by sundown. You will forgive me if I'm impressed."* He assumed Calmer (the cleric) was in charge — the party let that wrong read stand. He inventoried what they now owned (leaderless Fire Temple cultists, the Water Temple troll contract, an unaccounted-for treasury, mercenaries needing a chain of command, the Chest of Rao he wants back) and gave them a week to look organized, naming Senshock ("ambitious and paranoid... let him underestimate you") and Falrinth ("hiding from the Greater Temple for reasons of his own") as people to be careful of. Left an ogre behind as visible surveillance.

**What the party knows:** `[KNOWN]` he's the Greater Temple's military commander, he sent Lucius, he expects Fire Temple pacified, the troll contract resolved, the Water Temple treasury audited, Dren formally installed, and a decision made on the Chest of Rao — all within the week. `[GM-ONLY]` he has no idea any of this serves Zuggtmoy rather than Iuz. He is a genuine sleeper-ally candidate for the party post-reveal, once/if they ever learn what Hedrack is actually doing.

## The Party's Visible Command Structure

This is what the party actually built, roughly in order of installation.

### Varek Solain — Commander, Fire Temple ground forces (Fire, retained)
Found alone and exhausted on Alrrem's throne, ch.28. Expected a sword; Zephyr offered him his old job under new management instead. Genuinely pleased, genuinely not going to perform gratitude. Pragmatic, dry, contemptuous of the clerics who died above him. Carries the Flametongue +1 openly. Reports to Zephyr specifically; hasn't yet formed a read on the rest of the party.

### Maeris Dorn — Head Cleric, Fire Temple (Fire, retained)
Alrrem's curate, caught mid-flight with his portable valuables, grappled before she could fight. Chose subordination in real time ("Three options. The first kills me immediately. The second, eventually. I'll take the third.") Now wears Alrrem's ceremonial robe, too big, unaltered. Watching Calmer closely — she's trained to read devotional behavior, and Calmer is the party member most at risk of a cover slip in front of her.

### Vurakhal — bound salamander, Fire Temple altar guardian (Fire, contracted to Zephyr specifically)
Last of three salamanders bound to Alrrem's altar (Area 212); surrendered when Alrrem's death left it masterless. Handed Zephyr a literal *Contract of Bound Service* (`notes/handouts/07_vurakhal_contract.md`) — recognized his infernal blood, rendered itself legible to him specifically. Will not enter standing water or deep cold under any order (Water Temple's raids killed its kin — genuine grudge, genuinely useful against Water Temple remnants). Voids instantly and irreversibly if anyone attempts a ritual re-binding. The contract passes to whoever holds the document if Zephyr dies, with a three-day consent window.

### Dren Halveth — Captain, Broken Blades mercenary company (Earth, not yet formally installed)
Recruited in Nulb (ch.20) over a competing Water Temple bid. Currently occupying Earth Temple territory under an arrangement that traces back to Romag — and both Romag and his successor Hartsch are now dead, so there is no surviving Earth Temple authority above Dren except the party itself. Per `phase3_endgame.md`'s "Barkinar's List," formalizing Dren's command is an explicit open item, not yet done — and with Hartsch's murder confirmed, it's no longer a housekeeping formality, it's the only thing standing between Earth Temple and a leadership vacuum. Pragmatic, transactional, responds to demonstrated strength. `docs/npcs/dren.md` was a duplicate dossier (mislabeled him "Leader of the Crimson Guard faction" — a title that actually belongs to no one; real Crimson Reapers leadership is Zelt the Rat and an unnamed hostel spokesperson, both separately dossiered) — merged into `docs/npcs/dren_halveth.md` and deleted.

### Brother Eelrich Vane — provisional puppet head-cleric, Water Temple (Water, on probation)
The only surviving Water under-priest; flipped smoothly and immediately rather than die with Belsornig. Holds real operational value the party hasn't fully cashed in yet: the location of the Fourth Basin's hidden treasury sump, the half-finished troll-chieftain contract (Areas 218-219, still unpaid, still a live CR 5 problem), and soft intel on which Nulb contacts were Belsornig's. He is not lying when he says his loyalty is "to the current arrangement" — that's genuinely the most reliable kind he has to offer, and the party has already flagged him as a snake they chose to keep rather than kill.

### Kelno — Prophet, Air Temple (allied, the party's #2 in the Air Temple)
Not an unresolved thread — his bridge scene ran (`chapter_30_dungeon_boss.md`) and the party took his deal rather than kill him. Found in his gray "Grim Chamber," skulls labeled with names and dates, a second chair already set out for them two weeks before they arrived — he'd been expecting them specifically. His pitch (per `phase3_endgame.md`, played almost word for word): kill him and walk into the deep temple blind, no goblin eyes, no informants; keep him and inherit his network — "his goblins, mine; his informants, mine." Zephyr read his honesty as the tell — *"for chaos, but also for living"* — and took the deal on the spot. Kelno then handed over: confirmation that Belsornig's assassins were already inside Hartsch's antechamber, that Alrrem was dying on his pyre, a Dagger of Venom "saved... to kill Belsornig," a pointer to the now-lightly-guarded Water Temple, and his bribery ledger (`notes/handouts/02_kelno_ledger.md`) covering a dozen paid agents. `docs/npcs/kelno.md` is stale (still describes him only as Hartsch's dismissive pre-war aside) and needs an update to reflect the alliance.

## Gaps the GM Can Exploit

- **The party fully trusts Lucius and Barkinar right now.** No Insight catches, no external warning fired (Falrinth's priming was deliberately deferred). This is the cleanest read Lucius will ever get of them — and the widest-open door for a believable next visit.
- **Earth Temple has now lost two prophets in a row (Romag, then Hartsch) with no third in line.** The party is the only remaining authority. Barkinar's check-in will be looking for evidence someone (Dren, formally) is actually running that territory — an unstaffed Earth Temple is a worse look than an unstaffed Fire Temple, since Fire already has Varek + Maeris installed.
- **Vurakhal hates Water, Eelrich (probably) will come to hate Fire.** Putting the bound salamander and the provisional water-priest in the same room is a scene that's been sitting ready since both were recruited.
- ~~**Dren's formal installation is now urgent.**~~ **Done (as of 2026-08-22 prep):** Dren has been formally installed as commander of the Earth Temple forces. Barkinar's List is now essentially complete — Dren installed, Fire and Water Temples pacified, troll contract resolved by force. Only the **necromancer** and the **Chest of Rao** remain outstanding, which makes the check-in a commendation rather than a reprimand — and a commendation is how the leash gets shorter.
- **Kelno knows about the Prince (Thrommel) territory before anyone else does — or does he?** He's now embedded with the party as an ally with a two-week head start on reading them. Worth deciding whether his network has picked up anything on the sleeping-prince thread before Lucius ever raises it.
- **The Kelno/Barkinar overlap.** Barkinar met Kelno standing beside the party at the moment of "promotion" and smiled at him — filed by Zinnia as "a man who already knows more than he's saying." Whether that's just Barkinar recognizing a useful defector or something sharper is an open GM call.
