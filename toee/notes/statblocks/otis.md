# Otis, Master Smith

*Medium humanoid (human), neutral good*

**Armor Class** 17 (concealed rune-tempered chain shirt)  
**Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Str +6, Dex +8  
**Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +6, Survival +6  
**Damage Resistances** acid  
**Condition Immunities** frightened  
**Senses** passive Perception 16  
**Languages** Common, Elvish, Sylvan  
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +3

***Forge-Hardened.*** *(reflavored Ayo Jabe's acid resistance and frightened immunity).* Years working caustic flux, white-hot iron, and dangerous covert assignments have left Otis resistant to acid damage and immune to the frightened condition.

***Reed-Breather.*** *(reflavored Ayo Jabe's Amphibious trait).* Otis carries a Gnarley Ranger breathing reed of treated ipp-wood. While it is on his person, he can breathe air and water.

***The Smith's Hidden Harness.*** *(reflavored Ayo Jabe's Special Equipment).* Beneath work-stained clothes, Otis wears rune-tempered armor and carries a longsword whose magic remains dormant until he speaks its secret rune. If Otis rolls a 1 on an attack roll with the sword, he must succeed on a DC 20 Charisma saving throw or gain 1 level of exhaustion as he forces its concealed magic to remain unnoticed.

***Watcher Network (Narrative Feature).*** Otis's two giant owls watch the Temple by night, while four sprites watch by day. Given reasonable time and access to his network, Otis can provide recent signs of troop movement, traveled routes, and conspicuous activity around Nulb and the Temple. This feature grants no automatic combat benefit.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** *(reflavored Ayo Jabe's Multiattack).* Otis makes three Rune-Woken Longsword or Gnarley Longbow attacks in any combination.

***Rune-Woken Longsword.*** *(reflavored Ayo Jabe's Ruidium Harpoon).* *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage. When Otis speaks the hidden rune, pale forge-lines flare along the blade and the blow lands with the accumulated certainty of a knight who has studied the Temple for years.

***Gnarley Longbow.*** *(reflavored Ayo Jabe's Longbow).* *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) piercing damage. Otis's cold-iron-headed arrows bear tiny smith's marks that identify range, wind, and intended quarry by touch.

## Bonus Actions

***Bent-Nail Signal.*** *(reflavored Ayo Jabe's Reposition).* Otis issues a terse signal to one ally he can see within 30 feet. If the target can see or hear him, it can use its reaction to move up to its speed. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

## Reactions

Otis has no special reaction. He reserves his reaction for opportunity attacks and for movement granted by an ally's features.

## Spellcasting

Otis has no spellcasting in this conversion. His ranger identity is expressed through fieldcraft, exceptional archery, prepared equipment, and his watcher network, preserving the Ayo Jabe chassis without adding power.

## Tactics

Otis avoids fair fights. Before violence, his owls and sprites establish routes, numbers, watches, and exits; he then places himself where a longbow can control an approach without exposing his identity. He uses **Bent-Nail Signal** to move an ally out of a closing trap, across a watched opening, or toward a prepared escape.

If forced into melee, Otis speaks the rune and draws the **Rune-Woken Longsword**, concentrating three attacks on the enemy most capable of preventing withdrawal. He does not pursue fleeing enemies into unknown ground. At half hit points—or the moment his cover, followers, or intelligence mission becomes worth more than the immediate victory—he breaks contact and extracts survivors.

Otis should usually resolve action off-screen: a safe route prepared, a watcher intercepted, a pursuit delayed, or a wounded asset delivered alive. If present in a scene with the party, he protects the escape plan rather than becoming their primary damage dealer.

## Treasure

- The rune-woken longsword and concealed rune-tempered armor, both recognizable as Velunan work once cleaned of soot and false maker's marks.
- A Gnarley longbow and 20 cold-iron-headed arrows, each coded by tiny file cuts near the nock.
- A bent iron nail used as a Ranger recognition token.
- A route ledger disguised as unpaid smithing accounts; without Otis's key, its place names read as horse ailments and repair jobs.
- 25 mixed gems worth 100 gp each, his canonical reserve for maintaining the cell and paying emergency couriers.

## CR Calibration

Intended CR 8. Otis is designed as an off-screen NPC ally who does not adventure with the party, so this block prioritizes credible independent survival and extraction capability rather than party encounter balance. Expected outcome: he can defeat ordinary Temple operatives, delay a stronger force, or survive long enough to withdraw, but he is not a solo boss and has no legendary mechanics.

## Source / Chassis

This conversion uses **Ayo Jabe (Tier 3), CR 8** (*Critical Role: Call of the Netherdeep*, p. 192) as its mathematical chassis. Ayo's Amphibious, Special Equipment, Ruidium Harpoon, Longbow, and Reposition features are explicitly reflavored above. The legacy source is Otis's canonical level-10 ranger-knight entry in *The Temple of Elemental Evil*: undercover smith, agent of Furyondy, Knight Bachelor of Veluna, bearer of a secretly activated magic sword, and coordinator of Murfles, two giant owls, and four sprites.
