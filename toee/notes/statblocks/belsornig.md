# Belsornig, Canon and Prophet of the Water Temple

*Tired cult administrator who has outlived three rivals by never being in the room. Cleric-9 caster on a Priest (MM) chassis, reflavored for Olhydra. He does not die in place.*

**AC** 17 (chain shirt + shield of brinewood) | **HP** 90 (16d8 + 16) | **Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
**STR** 12 (+1) **DEX** 10 (+0) **CON** 14 (+2) **INT** 14 (+2) **WIS** 18 (+4) **CHA** 16 (+3)
**Saves:** Wis +7, Cha +6
**Skills:** Deception +6, Insight +7, Persuasion +6, Religion +5
**Damage Resistances:** cold
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages:** Common, Aquan, Abyssal
**CR** 7 (2,900 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +3

***Tide's Custodian*** *(reflavored Tempest Domain — Divine Strike, PHB Tempest Cleric)*. Once per turn, when Belsornig hits with a weapon attack, he can drown a curl of seawater up the haft and deal an extra 1d8 cold damage to the target.

***The Sea Knows His Name*** *(reflavored Sanctuary, cast at-will on himself as a 1st-level slot equivalent — costs his reaction, not a slot)*. The first time each turn a creature targets Belsornig with an attack or harmful spell, that creature must make a DC 15 Wisdom save or its eyes fill briefly with saltwater and the action targets a different valid target within range; on a success, the effect proceeds normally. Belsornig loses this benefit until the start of his next turn if he attacks or casts a harmful spell. (GM note: this is the *Sanctuary* spell built into the chassis; tracking it as a trait avoids spending a slot.)

***Amphibious.*** Belsornig can breathe air and water.

**Actions**

***Multiattack.*** Belsornig makes two melee attacks with his Brine-Mace, or casts a spell and makes one Brine-Mace attack.

***Brine-Mace*** *(reflavored +1 Mace)*. *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) cold damage (Tide's Custodian).

***Drowning Spark*** *(reflavored Sacred Flame cantrip)*. One creature Belsornig can see within 60 ft. must make a DC 15 Dexterity save; on a failure, a fistful of cold seawater erupts inside the creature's lungs for 13 (3d8) radiant damage. *(Note: keep radiant — swapping to cold would be buffed by his own resistance aura tactics.)* No cover benefit.

***Sigil of Olhydra*** *(reflavored Misty Step, granted by the symbol; 3/Day, no slot cost)*. *Bonus action.* Belsornig collapses into a curl of black brine and re-forms in a space he can see within 30 ft.

**Reactions**

***Wrath of the Storm-Below*** *(reflavored Tempest Domain — Wrath of the Storm, 2/day)*. When a creature within 5 ft. hits Belsornig with an attack, he can force it to make a DC 15 Dexterity save. On a failure: 9 (2d8) cold damage from a column of pressurized water hammering up from the floor. On a success: half damage.

**Spellcasting**

Belsornig is a 9th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following cleric spells prepared.

- **Cantrips (at will):** *Drowning Spark* (reflavored *Sacred Flame*, see Actions), *Toll the Drowned* (reflavored *Toll the Dead*), *Guidance*, *Thaumaturgy*
- **1st level (4 slots):** *Mark of the Tide* (reflavored *Bane*), *Tidal Mercy* (reflavored *Cure Wounds*), *Surface Breath* (reflavored *Healing Word*), *Shield of Faith*
- **2nd level (3 slots):** *Undertow Grip* (reflavored *Hold Person*), *Drowning Trident* (reflavored *Spiritual Weapon* — appears as a barbed glaive of black water), *Stillwater* (reflavored *Silence*)
- **3rd level (3 slots):** *Encircling Tide* (reflavored *Spirit Guardians* — wraps him in a 15-ft. aura of grasping kelp-spirits dealing cold damage on a failed Wis save), *Dispel Magic*, *Sleet Storm*
- **4th level (3 slots):** *Pull to the Deep* (reflavored *Banishment* — the target is dragged momentarily into the Elemental Plane of Water), *Currents Yield* (reflavored *Freedom of Movement*), *Control Water*
- **5th level (1 slot):** *Abyssal Stillness* (reflavored *Hold Monster*)

**Tactics — how Belsornig actually plays the fight**

Belsornig has been told by Hartsch and by his own dead lieutenants what dying-in-place looks like; he is not interested. **Round 1: open with *Encircling Tide* (Spirit Guardians) on himself as he steps adjacent to the strongest melee PC.** This locks the frontliner into a slow-damage zone and removes their incentive to chase him.

**Round 2–3:** *Undertow Grip* on the war-priest or any Wis-low PC, or *Mark of the Tide* on three party members if grouped. Bonus action *Drowning Trident*. He does not Melee unless an enemy is already adjacent — he leans on *The Sea Knows His Name* to redirect attacks instead, and uses *Sigil of Olhydra* (Misty Step) to break melee whenever a martial closes.

**Escape trigger: below half HP (45 HP) OR two PCs grappling/restraining him.** He uses *Sigil of Olhydra* to drop behind a barrier or into water if any is present, then drinks the **Pearl of the Black Current** (see Treasure) and dissolves into mist of saltwater that pours through any opening larger than a coin slot. He surfaces a mile away, three hours later. *He has done this before. He has the Pearl because Hartsch's last gambit almost killed him.*

He does not parley mid-fight. He **does** parley before the fight if the party walks in talking — he is willing to give up Vesrek, Snej, or both, in exchange for being allowed to "withdraw to consult his oracle." This is a lie; the consultation is him leaving the temple complex entirely.

**Treasure**

- **Brine-Mace** (+1 mace, sea-glass haft; cold damage rider is a property of *him*, not the weapon — the mace is just a +1 mace in someone else's hands)
- **Shield of Brinewood** (mundane shield, carved with Olhydra's sigil; worth 50 gp to a cultist, 5 gp otherwise)
- **Sigil of Olhydra** (silver amulet; functions as a holy symbol; *Misty Step* charges do not transfer — only Belsornig can use it that way)
- **Pearl of the Black Current** (single-use consumable; functions as a one-shot *Gaseous Form* + 60-ft. teleport through any liquid pathway; if the party recovers it unused, this is genuinely useful — flag it)
- **Belsornig's ledger** (small, leather, salt-stained). Lists payments to Snej, Vesrek, and three names the party does not yet know: *Yarrow* (a Hommlet contact), *the Lake-Hand* (broker), and *Mother Screel* (unknown). This is the real prize.
- 80 gp in mixed coin, in a pouch tied to his sash. He does not value coin and forgets it's there.

**CR calibration**

Intended **CR 7**, sized for the current party of ~level 6–7 (Calmer cleric-6, Sequioa/Zinnia/Zephyr — assassin / monk / rogue). Expected outcome: **tough fight with a planned escape**, not a kill. If the party catches him before he gets to *Sigil of Olhydra* / Pearl — i.e., they have grapples, *Hold Person* counter-cast, or *Counterspell* burned on his *Misty Step* — he can be killed. Otherwise the table-story is "we hurt him, he vanished, we got the ledger." That's the right outcome for an NPC the dossier explicitly describes as never being in the room.

**Source / chassis**

This block uses **Priest (MM p348)** as its conceptual chassis, scaled up to a **9th-level cleric caster** with **Tempest Domain features (Wrath of the Storm + Divine Strike, PHB)** reflavored to water/cold. Spell list rebuilt around water/control/escape rather than Priest's vanilla healer kit. The escape ability (*Sigil of Olhydra* Misty Step + Pearl of the Black Current) is not from the chassis — added as a one-NPC item to honor the dossier line that Belsornig "engineers schemes with no lose condition." Damage type swaps: kept *Sacred Flame* as radiant despite the water flavor, because cold would be redundant with his cold resistance for the rebound case where it's cast on him by a charmed PC; everything else is cold or pure control.
