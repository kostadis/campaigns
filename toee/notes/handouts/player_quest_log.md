## The Job

### Destroy the Temple of Elemental Evil from the inside

**Who's asking:** Nobody had to ask. It is why we came.

**What we know:** We came here to bring it down. We are now its management. Calmer is Prophet of Earth, Zinnia runs intelligence, Sequoia keeps the valuables where they belong, and I run the day-to-day. Barkinar of the Greater Temple looked at four dead prophets in one day and decided we were an asset.

**The operative unknown:** Whether we are still doing the job. Zinnia says Calmer is going in too deep. Zinnia is usually right about this kind of thing.

**Status:** Open. Complicated. Profitable.

---

## Owed to Management

Barkinar handed us the Upper Temple and a list, and said the place should *look organized* by the next check-in. That was a week's grace and the week has been busy.

**Pacify the Fire Temple** — ◐ Salamanders dealt with. Varek Solain submitted and paid two gold for the privilege. Maeris Dorn installed as head cleric and sent off to find the captain of the guard and restore order. We have not heard back from Maeris since.

**Resolve the troll contract** — ◐ Three trolls and a chieftain are dead. Vane says the contract was never signed. The paperwork side of this is not closed.

**Audit and secure the Water Temple treasury** — ▲ We have looted it thoroughly. Auditing it for Barkinar is a different activity and we have not done that one.

**Formalize Dren's command** — ◐ Named Supreme Commander of the Earth Temple Forces, out loud, in front of a Fire Temple officer. Calmer wanted a ceremony. There has been no ceremony.

**Recover the Chest of Rao** — ▲ We know exactly where it went. We handed it to Romag ourselves to buy our way in. Romag is dead, Hartsch is dead, and the chest is somewhere in Earth Temple holdings. This one is on us in both directions.

**Deal with Kelno** — ◐ He is our number two by agreement. His goblins are our goblins, his informants are ours. He has also quietly moved bugbears in Air Temple colors into rooms that used to be Water Temple. We took twenty gold off them and moved on. He is expanding while we work.

**Identify the necromancer** — ▲ Something has been raising the dead in this dungeon. Ghouls came up out of our own fallen. Erith fed on the war and made specters out of the corpses we produced. Erith is dead now and the dead are still standing up. Nobody has a name for who is doing it.

## Debts

### Owed by us

**Dren Halveth — his cut.** ▲ We agreed a ten percent share of the Water Temple haul, about a thousand gold, two hundred and fifty a head. I offered to carry the larger share of it. I have not seen it handed over. Dren has stayed loyal through a meat grinder he was sent into under our name, and he has not asked twice.

**Thorne, the Duke — retainer.** ◯ Twenty gold a month, a hundred per incident, for cleaning up bodies. We paid the first hundred. Whether the monthly has been running since is a question the book cannot answer.

**Lucius Graeme — deposits.** ◯ The book says four thousand down on the ring of invisibility for Sequoia and four thousand on the ring of free action for me, to hold them against other buyers. Sequoia says we looked at the prices and walked away with nothing. Eight thousand gold is not a rounding error. Settle this before anyone spends anything. *(Calmer is already wearing a ring of free action off Belsornig's corpse, which makes the second one a harder line to justify.)*

### Owed to us

**Airgid's mother.** ▲ We escorted a chained silver hatchling out of the Abbey and she flew west into the Lortmil Mountains shouting that her mother would reward us. No one has come to pay. That was a long time ago and several towns back.

**The Glimmerfields.** ▲ One hundred silver, promised for pulling them out of a cell on the level above this one. We framed the Fire Temple for the escape and sent them up the stairs. Nobody has been back to collect.

**Gonnathrad Kevanarial's letter of permit.** ▲ One hundred gold, payable at Caer Ostverk on presenting the letter and what we learned about the chest near the monastery. We learned about the chest. We then gave the chest to an evil high priest. The letter is still good; the conversation would be awkward.

**Glennddarc, the headgnome of Sheernobb.** ⌀ Offered us work on some giants and said the rewards would be greater than chasing bandits for a lovesick guard captain. We never took it. It was never withdrawn.

## Unfinished Business Down Here

**The pool in the Hall of Verdigris.** The water is acid and the water thinks. It put a voice in our heads — a triton, chained at the bottom, begging to be freed before a kraken came through the wall. I went in on that voice. Zinnia went in after me on a rope and pulled me out coated in acid. Then it got Sequoia to jump in too. Calmer worked out what it was doing to us only after the second man was burned. There is still something down there at the bottom of that pool. There was no kraken.

**The minotaur.** It is sitting on a throne pretending to be a statue and Sequoia has just worked out that it is breathing. This is the current problem.

**Brother Eelrich Vane.** He handed us Belsornig's getaway money to buy his life and he has been useful. The ritual texts in his own bedroom say he knew that pool was alive and thinking, and he walked us toward it anyway. We told him the next surprise in these halls would be meant for him. He calls Calmer *Lord Master* now. That is not the same as trust.

**Vurakhal's contract.** Signed in my blood, with the clauses about souls struck out first. He guards the Fire Temple and he burned four trolls for us. He has said he knows things about the Greater Temple and what is really going on down there, and that he will share them if the business relationship improves. It has improved. He has not shared them.

**The four in the chains.** We freed one prisoner because he had something to sell — the location of the door to the Greater Temple. The other four demanded the same and we walked out. That was during the war. Nobody has been back.

**The owlbear.** Chained, starving, mean. Zinnia fed it and calmed it. We left it chained. Sequoia says releasing it would be a gamble and Sequoia is the one who knows about owlbears.

**The watching door.** During the retreat through the Air Temple, Zinnia saw a door to the east open and close like someone was watching us work. We never found out who. Nobody has explained it since.

**Senshock.** The Lord Wizard. Ambitious, paranoid, useful — Barkinar's words. We are to be civil and let him underestimate us. We have not met him.

**Falrinth.** A wizard hiding from the Greater Temple for his own reasons. Barkinar raised the name, said it was a matter for later, and walked off into the dark. Later has not arrived.

## Our Own Business

**Calmer** — He has stopped performing the Prophet of Earth and started enjoying it. He made a priest call him Lord Master. He announced that operational detail is beneath him. Zinnia has already told him he is going in too deep and he agreed, respectfully, and then did it again.

**Zinnia** — Head of intelligence, which he was doing anyway without the title. He is the one keeping score on how far the rest of us have drifted, and he is the only one who went into the acid on purpose, on a rope, to pull a man out.

**Sequoia** — Killed a thing with a hundred and fifty-four hit points with one stab while it lay on the floor. Carries Frostbrand, which talks. Also jumped into the acid pool for no reason he can now defend, which the rest of us have agreed to keep bringing up.

**Frostbrand** — Wants vengeance. It has been a trophy in a box since the Battle of Emridy Meadows with Vurakhal as its jailer, it has picked a fight it cannot physically have, and it now introduces itself as *the evil sword* to help with our cover. It is not helping with our cover.

**Zephyr** — Two rings I cannot afford, and I have decided the world owes me both. There is a captain called Tolub who sent us into a fire-priest ambush and believes we died there. I have his ship's schedule at the Nulb docks. He is not on this list as work. He is on it because I have not forgotten.

## Things Nobody Is Paying Us For, Which We Notice Anyway

- **Lord Hedrak.** Lucius let the name drop — the authority who has made it clear that magical items are not given away free. Nobody has told us who he is or where he sits.
- **The Greater Temple seals the doors when the lesser temples tear each other apart.** Erith said it happens *every so often*. So this has happened before, on a schedule, and someone down there decides when.
- **The voice behind the sealed door.** "Survive the fight, and maybe you'll get a chance." We survived it. Nobody has mentioned the chance.
- **Bugbears came at us through the east and west doors** on a level we thought we had mapped. The Greater Temple had people up here the whole time, in rooms we have never opened.
- **Kelno set out a second chair and waited two weeks.** He knew we were coming from the moment we walked into this temple. Somebody told him, or he worked it out. Either answer is interesting.
- **Belsornig's standing order about us:** withdraw to the steam corridors, do not engage, *they are not to be killed.* The Prophet of Water wanted us alive and we never found out why.
- **Vane's temple is running its own supply chain** — Lucius arrives from Nulb with wine, potions, and a chest of enchanted goods whenever management wants us equipped. Someone outside is funding all of this.

---

*Four dead prophets, four temples, one dungeon, and every single line above is somebody who wants something from us or owes us something we haven't collected. I came here to burn this place down. I appear to be running its accounts payable. I've heard of worse in the Abyss.*
