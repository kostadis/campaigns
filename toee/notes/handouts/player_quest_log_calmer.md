## Our Actual Sacred Duty

### Destroy the Temple of Elemental Evil from within

**Who is asking:** Nobody needed to ask. This is why we entered the place.

**What we know:** We have killed the four former prophets and now administer the Upper Temple. I am Prophet of Earth. Zinnia commands intelligence, Zephyr handles operations, and Sequoia sees that valuables remain in responsible hands. Barkinar believes we are useful. This remains an excellent position from which to destroy the Temple, regardless of what my advisors occasionally imply.

**Status:** Open. Under control. Zinnia has raised professional concerns about excessive integration, and I have heard them.

---

## Obligations to Greater Temple Management

Barkinar gave us the Upper Temple and instructed us to make it look organized within a week. Management has not yet returned to appreciate our progress.

**Pacify the Fire Temple** — ◐ Vurakhal guards it under contract. Varek Solain submitted. Maeris Dorn was appointed head cleric and ordered to restore the remaining forces. We should confirm that she has done what she was told.

**Resolve the troll contract** — ◐ The trolls are dead, including their chieftain. Vurakhal was useful. Brother Eelrich says their contract was never signed, leaving a paperwork problem attached to several dead trolls.

**Audit and secure the Water Temple treasury** — ▲ We secured it extremely thoroughly. Belsornig’s hidden escape fund, ceremonial valuables, and enchanted equipment are no longer at risk of misuse by the Water Temple. Barkinar asked for an audit, however, which is apparently different from securing it.

**Formalize Dren’s command** — ◐ Dren has been publicly named Supreme Commander of the Earth Temple Forces and answers to me. I also proposed a ceremony. The ceremony has not occurred, through no fault in the quality of the proposal.

**Recover the Chest of Rao** — ▲ We delivered it to Romag to secure our infiltration. Romag and Hartsch are dead. The chest should still be somewhere in Earth Temple holdings. It is both our original objective and Barkinar’s property request, which is inconveniently efficient.

**Deal with Kelno** — ◐ Kelno remains head of the Air Temple beneath us by agreement. His goblins and informants are supposedly ours. Air Temple bugbears have also occupied former Water Temple territory while we were busy elsewhere. I recommend an accounting of what, precisely, he believes “beneath us” means.

**Identify and remove the necromancer** — ▲ The dead are still being raised, now in stronger forms. The necromancer sent us a note thanking us for the corpses, called this place his home, and described my ritual practice as incorrect. This is an attack upon the Upper Temple, our lives, and professional standards. He is to be found.

## Accounts Requiring Settlement

### What we may owe

**Dren Halveth — recruitment money.** ◯ Dren joined the Earth Temple after we offered one hundred gold to establish his company here. The record shows the promise and does not show payment. Confirm before paying twice; pay promptly if it remains due.

**Dren Halveth — Water Temple share.** ▲ We set aside ten percent of the Water Temple haul, approximately one thousand gold. Zephyr offered to carry the larger portion. I have not personally witnessed delivery. Dren remained loyal after Hartsch sent his company into a slaughter under our name, so this is not an account to neglect.

**Thorne, called the Duke — retainer.** ◯ Twenty gold each month and one hundred per incident for discreet disposal. The first incident was paid. The ledger does not establish whether monthly payments continued.

**Lucius Graeme — reserved rings.** ◯ One account says we placed four-thousand-gold deposits on a ring of invisibility and a ring of free action. Another recollection says we admired the prices and purchased neither. Eight thousand gold warrants reconciliation before Lucius’s next visit.

### What others owe us

**Airgid’s mother.** ▲ We escorted the silver hatchling out of the Abbey. Airgid flew west toward the Lortmil Mountains shouting that her mother would reward us. A promise delivered at volume remains a promise.

**Derrick and Florian.** ▲ We rescued the two merchants from Lubash’s pantry beneath the Moathouse. They promised a reward upon returning safely to Hommlet. No collection is recorded.

**The Glimmerfields.** ▲ One hundred silver for freeing their household from the cells above. We staged the escape to implicate the Fire Temple and sent them out alive. Nobody has collected.

**Gonnathrad Kevanarial.** ▲ His letter of permit promises one hundred gold at Caer Ostverk for information about the chest near the monastery. We recovered the chest and subsequently handed it to an evil high priest as part of a successful infiltration. The facts satisfy the letter, even if the explanation may require care.

**Glennddarc of Sheernobb.** ⌀ Offered work against giants and promised rewards “greater” than the local bandit business. We did not accept. The offer was never withdrawn.

## Unfinished Business in Our Temple

**The sentient acid pool.** It planted a false triton’s plea in our minds and trapped Zephyr. Zinnia entered on a rope and pulled him out. It then persuaded Sequoia to jump in. The alleged kraken never appeared. The intelligence remains at the bottom of the pool, and Brother Eelrich’s own texts suggest he knew what it was before leading us there.

**Brother Eelrich Vane.** He bought his life with Belsornig’s hidden treasury and information about the trolls. He has been useful, obsequious, damp, and less than honest about the acid pool. We warned him that the next surprise would be intended for him. He now addresses me correctly as *Lord Master Calmer*. Respect is encouraging; it is not proof.

**The Minotaur’s provisions.** We promised it regular food and protection from the black-cloaked “bandits” attacking its reception hall. It agreed to guard the room and threatened to kill and eat us if the attacks continue. Some of the attackers were undead raised by the necromancer. Feeding arrangements remain undecided. Sequoia has objected to one practical proposal.

**Vurakhal’s contract and intelligence.** Zephyr signed the amended contract in blood after removing the clauses concerning souls. Vurakhal guards the Fire Temple and helped us kill the trolls. He said he knows what is truly happening in the Greater Temple and would speak once the business relationship improved. It has improved. He has not spoken.

**The four prisoners.** We freed one captive in exchange for the route to the Greater Temple. Four others remained chained and demanded release. No final decision was made. They are not to be quietly converted into Minotaur provisions merely because an administrative discussion occurred.

**The chained owlbear.** Zinnia fed and calmed it during the troll expedition. We left it chained. Releasing it remains, in Sequoia’s professional judgment, a gamble.

**The watching door.** During our retreat through the Air Temple, Zinnia saw a door open and close as if someone were observing us. We never identified the watcher.

**The ruined stores.** We found excellent supplies beside rotting food and unacceptable disorder. Dren, the Broken Blades, Lucius, or other properly delegated personnel should restore them. Zephyr is Chief of Operations and has correctly concluded that someone else should do it.

**Senshock.** Lord Wizard, ambitious, paranoid, useful. Barkinar told us to remain civil and allow him to underestimate us. We have not met him.

**Falrinth.** A wizard hiding from the Greater Temple for reasons Barkinar did not explain. Barkinar said this would be discussed later. It has not been.

## Matters Nobody Has Commissioned, but Competent Leadership Notices

- **Lord Hedrak.** Lucius named him as the authority who refuses to distribute magical items freely. Nobody has explained his office or location.
- **The Greater Temple lockdown.** The doors seal whenever the lesser temples tear each other apart. Erith said this happens repeatedly, which means the civil wars are anticipated rather than exceptional.
- **The voice behind the sealed door.** It told us to survive the fight and perhaps receive a chance to meet those truly in charge. We survived. The promised chance has not been mentioned.
- **Hidden Greater Temple forces.** Black-clad bugbears reached us through east and west doors on a level we believed mapped. There are occupied rooms we have never opened.
- **Kelno’s preparation.** He placed a second chair and waited two weeks for us. Either somebody informed him or he correctly predicted us. Neither answer is comforting.
- **Belsornig’s order.** His people were told to withdraw from us and not kill us. We killed him without learning why he wanted us alive.
- **The external supply chain.** Lucius moves between Nulb and the Temple with wine, potions, and enchanted goods. Somebody outside supports this operation.
- **The necromancer’s grievance.** He claims this is his home, knows Earth Temple rituals, commands undead that once answered to temple authority, and considers me an unqualified usurper. We have a hypothesis, not a name.
