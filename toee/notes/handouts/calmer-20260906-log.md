# The Prophet’s Ledger of Outstanding Business

*Kept by Calmer, High Lord Master and Prophet of Earth — because authority without records is merely shouting, and I have appointed people to do the shouting.*

*Current as of: our return to Nulb, where the river folk welcomed us as family and Skole reported that Captain Tolub is still telling people he had us killed.*

---

## We Are Here

**Nulb, on the muddy river road.** We stand just inside the village, dressed discreetly, while a Rhennee child runs ahead shouting that family has returned and Sequoia holds Skole's note.

**Why we are here:** The Temple’s supply line runs through this village; we have come to restore provisions and pay, then determine who controls the route.

**What we left behind:** Dren holds our Temple with three days of Minotaur food, four days of pay, and orders to burn corpses before the necromancer can claim them.

**Our present advantage:** Nulb saw us survive the Scarlet Moon and recruit Dren, but people repeat Tolub's later boast that he had us killed because doubting him is dangerous. If we show ourselves and protect the first doubter, everyone carrying a grievance against Tolub receives permission to use it.

## The Original Mandate, Properly Prioritized

### Master the Temple before deciding what must be destroyed

**Who is asking:** Jaroo and the Hommlet council sent us toward Nulb to infiltrate the Temple’s operations, disrupt them, and discover who truly ruled. Nobody needed to explain that the Temple of Elemental Evil was dangerous.

**What we know:** We have killed the former elemental prophets, survived the war that followed, and been installed as rulers of the Upper Temple. I am Prophet of Earth. Zephyr directs operations, Zinnia oversees security and intelligence, Sequoia controls logistics and valuables, and Dren commands the forces. Barkinar considers us useful. Kelno calls himself our subordinate. Fire, Water, and Earth now answer—however imperfectly—to our administration.

**The position:** Destroying a dangerous institution may still prove necessary. Destroying it before we understand its artifacts, supply lines, servants, gates, and sources of power would be wasteful and strategically childish. The Temple contains an orb said to command monsters, a scarab important enough to frighten wizards, hidden levels, old alliances, and offices which have already accepted our authority. We should know what all of that can do before anyone begins swinging a hammer at the foundations.

**Status:** Open. Deliberate. Under competent management, with occasional assistance.

## Immediate Business in Nulb

### Restore what feeds the Temple

Dren’s report was precise: the Minotaur had food for three days, the men had pay for four, and the dead had begun leaving without permission. The Temple’s ordinary supply line runs through Nulb. We came here to restore it.

Varek needs food, troops, money, and equipment. We assigned the northern food depot to the Fire Temple to guard, the armor depot to the Water Temple, and the firewood depot to the Air Temple. These are custodial assignments. We control the stores. Zephyr explained this to Varek after Varek became excited about controlling the food.

**Status:** Urgent. A throne without provisions is a decorative chair.

### Captain Tolub is enforcing our alleged death

Tolub sent us into a Fire Temple ambush. We survived, returned to Nulb, and recruited Dren Halveth in full view of people who possess eyes. Tolub now boasts that he killed us—or had us killed—afterward. Skole’s new note says he is still insisting upon it.

The village is not fooled. It is frightened. Everyone knows that doubting Tolub aloud may cause Tolub to kill the doubter, which has given his fiction the administrative convenience of unanimous agreement.

Once we make ourselves known, those with grievances against Tolub will have an opportunity to settle their scores—provided we first prove that he cannot punish them for taking it. This may be settled by confrontation, leverage, or a sufficiently public demonstration of continued life. “Power and subtlety,” as Zephyr put it. This is why I made him Chief of Staff.

### Our Rhennee family

The river folk greeted us as family the moment we returned. Eryk Velkori previously told us that unknown people were coercing his caravan into ambushes along the forest road. Nobody hired us to solve that problem, and we made no promise, but family is an asset one should not permit others to spend.

## Obligations to Greater Temple Management

Barkinar gave us the Upper Temple and instructed us to make it look organized. It now has delegated officers, guarded depots, subordinate temples, a Minotaur, a fire salamander, and written procedures for corpse disposal. Management may not appreciate how much work this represents.

**Recover the Chest of Rao** — ▲ We delivered it to Romag as the price of entry. Romag and Hartsch are dead. Barkinar has now reminded Dren that he awaits our decision concerning it. The chest is somewhere in Earth Temple possession or storage, unless somebody moved it without filing the appropriate notice. It is also the object Brother Eldin urged us to return to the Archclericy of Veluna. One chest, multiple claimants, considerable leverage.

**Audit and secure the Water Temple treasury** — ▲ We secured the treasury with unusual thoroughness. Belsornig’s escape fund, ceremonial goods, scrolls, enchanted equipment, and private texts are no longer available to him. An audit suitable for Barkinar is a different document, regrettably, and has not been produced.

**Pacify the Fire Temple** — ◐ Maeris Dorn was appointed head cleric. Varek Solain commands its guard, Vurakhal serves under contract, and the Fire Temple guards the northern food depot. Varek’s spirits and ambitions have both recovered. An Earth Temple observer near the depot would be prudent.

**Settle Kelno’s position** — ◐ Kelno agreed to remain head of Air beneath us, with his goblins, informants, and bribery network placed at our disposal. While we worked, Air Temple bugbears occupied former Water Temple territory. We collected tribute and moved on. We require an accounting of what Kelno believes *beneath us* means.

The troll matter is closed in the practical sense. The trolls and their chieftain are dead. If Barkinar requires the unsigned contract countersigned by the corpses, Zephyr may arrange the meeting.

## Threats to My Administration

### The necromancer who wanted my office

The necromancer served under Barkinar and disappeared after Romag received the promotion he expected. He commands undead that once answered to the Earth Temple. He has raised our battle dead, taken the four prisoners we neglected, sent a demented ghoul after us, and written at insulting length about my qualifications and ritual technique.

This is not merely an attempt on our lives. It is an unauthorized rival claim upon the personnel, corpses, and traditions of the Earth Temple. We will find him. His knowledge may be valuable; his undead will not be tolerated. Those two judgments are entirely consistent.

Zephyr proposed posting notices throughout the Temple requesting information. Zinnia approved the security initiative. I am still waiting for the appropriate notice to sign.

### Two psychic creatures in the Greater Temple storeroom

Four creatures were hanging from the ceiling above stores of black cloth and Deep Temple equipment. We killed two. The remaining two withdrew into a dark corner, rejected Zephyr’s warning, and still occupy the room. Nobody speaks their language.

They have now been warned who the boss is. They have not acknowledged it.

### The sentient acid pool

The pool in the Hall of Verdigris planted a false triton’s plea in our minds, trapped Zephyr, and persuaded Sequoia to enter after Zinnia had already pulled Zephyr free. The creature remains in the pool. Brother Eelrich’s own books suggest he knew it was alive and thinking before he led us there.

An intelligence able to place thoughts in people’s minds is dangerous. It is also an intelligence able to place thoughts in people’s minds. We should learn its limits before deciding whether it can only be destroyed.

### Brother Eelrich Vane

Eelrich bought his life with Belsornig’s hidden treasury and information concerning the trolls. He has been useful, damp, flattering, and dishonest about the pool. We warned him that the next surprise would be intended for him. He now addresses me correctly as *Lord Master Calmer*. Respect is the beginning of an employment relationship, not proof of loyalty.

## Sources of Power Not Yet Properly Examined

### Falrinth, the orb, and the sleeper

Falrinth sent us a one-way illusion because he could not risk a runner. He claims to possess the Orb of Golden Death—the artifact Romag described as capable of summoning monsters, forcing the elements to submit, opening the Temple’s deep vaults, and destroying almost anything. Falrinth will not give it to Hedrack, Senshock, the Temple’s holy men, or us.

He says a candle in the West will reach him. We may contact him, report him to Barkinar, or do neither. Any answer declares an allegiance, so we should choose the allegiance that yields the most authority and information.

Falrinth also told us of a sleeper in an iron coffin on the level beneath our feet. Waking it would bring Verbobonc and all its banners down upon the Temple. That is a weapon, certainly. A weapon is not improved merely because one uses it immediately. First we should ask our “golden friend”—presumably Lucius—what he fears we will find.

### Lucius Graeme and the price of presents

Lucius brought excellent wine, healing potions, enchanted weapons, and access to magical goods. Falrinth says Lucius gives people what they lack and later “collects” them. He will recognize my T-Z-G-Y scarab on sight and conceal that he has noticed it.

Falrinth’s instruction was simple: when Lucius returns, do not commit. Stall. We can follow that instruction without surrendering access to Lucius’s inventory, which would be an overreaction.

### The T-Z-G-Y scarab

Falrinth says both Lucius and Senshock will recognize the scarab and use it against us in different ways. We still do not know what it is. I will carry it with greater discretion. I will not discard a thing that powerful people are hoping I fail to understand.

### Vurakhal’s withheld intelligence

Zephyr signed Vurakhal’s amended contract in blood after removing the clauses concerning souls. Vurakhal guards the Fire Temple, helped us kill the trolls, and is now authorized to help Dren burn corpses before the necromancer can claim them. He said he knows what truly happens in the Greater Temple and would speak when the business relationship improved.

It has improved. He should speak.

### The Minotaur

We promised regular food and protection from the black-cloaked “bandits” attacking its reception hall. It agreed to guard the room and promised to kill and eat us if we failed it. Dren has authority to feed it usable corpses and burn the rest. The supply line must provide for it once those corpses become scarce.

A Minotaur who recognizes our authority is an excellent guard. A hungry Minotaur who believes we broke our word is a meeting Zephyr can attend first.

### Senshock and the hidden Greater Temple

Barkinar described Lord Wizard Senshock as ambitious, paranoid, and useful, and advised us to remain civil while allowing him to underestimate us. We have not met him. Falrinth now says Senshock will recognize my scarab.

Black-clad forces have repeatedly emerged from rooms and passages we had not mapped. The Greater Temple stored supplies in our level and sealed its doors during the elemental war as though such wars were expected. We rule the Upper Temple; we do not yet know how much of it the Greater Temple quietly occupies.

## Accounts Requiring Settlement

### What we may owe

**Dren Halveth — recruitment money.** ◯ Dren joined the Earth Temple after we offered one hundred gold to establish the Broken Blades here. The record contains the promise and no clear payment. Confirm before paying twice; pay promptly if it remains due.

**Dren Halveth — Water Temple share.** ▲ We set aside ten percent of the Water Temple haul, approximately one thousand gold. Zephyr offered to carry the larger portion. Delivery has not been recorded. Dren remained loyal after Hartsch sent his company into a slaughter under our name and is presently holding our Temple together. This account should not remain open.

**Thorne, called the Duke — retainer.** ◯ He proposed twenty gold each month and one hundred per incident for discreet disposal. The first hundred was accepted, while a later cleanup is recorded at fifty gold. Confirm what arrangement survived before commissioning further work.

### What others owe us

**Airgid’s mother.** ▲ We escorted the silver hatchling out of the Abbey. Airgid flew west toward the Lortmil Mountains shouting that her mother would reward us. A promise delivered at that volume remains binding.

**Derrick and Florian.** ▲ We rescued the two merchants from Lubash’s pantry beneath the Moathouse and returned them toward Hommlet. They promised a reward for safe return. No collection is recorded.

**The Glimmerfields.** ▲ One hundred silver for freeing their household from the Earth Temple cells. We staged the escape to implicate the Fire Temple and sent them out alive. Nobody has collected.

**Gonnathrad Kevanarial.** ▲ His letter of permit promises one hundred gold at Caer Ostverk for information concerning the chest near the monastery. We recovered the Chest of Rao and then used it to purchase our way into the Earth Temple. The promised sum remains due. The explanation merely requires confidence.

### Offers never accepted

**Glennddarc of Sheernobb.** ⌀ Offered work against giants and promised rewards greater than the local bandit business. We never accepted, and he never withdrew it.

**Jira and Marek.** ⌀ Asked us to speak with their Old Faith and St. Cuthbert parents, who opposed their relationship. Zinnia told them to go away. This was not acceptance, however much they called us heroes.

## Unfinished Business in Our Temple

**The chained owlbear.** Zinnia fed and calmed it during the troll expedition. We left it chained. Releasing it remains, in Sequoia’s professional judgment, an unpredictable gamble. If it remains alive, it also requires food, which returns us to the supply line.

**The watching door.** During our retreat through the Air Temple, Zinnia saw a door open and close as though somebody were observing us. We never identified the watcher.

**The burned coffer.** A bronze-bound coffer was visible in the garbage heap we burned to deny the necromancer its bones. Nobody recovered it first. This is recorded so that the person responsible for valuables can experience the appropriate professional feeling.

**Belsornig’s order.** His agents were told to withdraw from us and not kill us. We killed him without learning why he wanted the Obsidian Edge alive.

**Kelno’s preparation.** He set out a second chair and waited for us for two weeks. Either he was informed or he correctly predicted our ascent. Both possibilities deserve attention from Zinnia’s office.

## Personal Matters Which Affect Operations

**Calmer** — My companions arranged my resurrection when the Church did not. Zinnia surrendered a magical trident to Terjon for it. Loyalty is reciprocal. St. Cuthbert and I retain a working relationship. The Temple, meanwhile, has supplied an office, followers, military authority, access to artifacts, and a practical education in how power is actually administered. There is no doctrinal conflict in acknowledging results. My revised curriculum remains preliminary.

Canoness Y’Dey remains missing from the Church of St. Cuthbert in Hommlet, while Terjon holds her office and has been notably unwilling to discuss the matter. At some point our working relationship may require a fuller accounting.

**Zinnia** — Chief of Security, Intelligence, and Professional Warnings. He believes I am going too deep. I have heard him. He also approved the plan to solicit information about the necromancer, and Zephyr still owes me the notice to sign.

**Sequoia** — Chief of Logistics, Supplies, and Getting Things Done. He bears Frostbrand, which seeks vengeance against the Temple, and is responsible for valuables. The burned bronze coffer is therefore entered under his section for administrative clarity.

**Frostbrand** — Good-aligned, talkative, and devoted to our “sneakety-sneakety” mission. It sometimes introduces itself as an evil sword. This remains unconvincing, but useful effort should be recognized.

**Zephyr** — Chief of Staff, Operations, and whichever security matters have not been assigned to Zinnia. He is responsible for repairing the supply line and producing the necromancer notice. Captain Tolub betrayed us and continues demanding that Nulb agree he had us killed. Zephyr has Tolub’s ship schedule, a village full of suppressed grievances, and an admirable sense of patience where profitable vengeance is concerned.

## Matters Nobody Has Commissioned, but Competent Leadership Notices

- **Lord Hedrack.** Falrinth named him among those who want the Orb. Lucius previously named him as the authority who refuses to distribute magical items freely. We still do not know his office or location.
- **Falrinth’s other friends.** Lareth was his friend. Falrinth appreciated the discretion with which we handled him and warned that his other friends may be less patient.
- **The external network.** Lucius moves people, wine, potions, weapons, and information between Nulb and the Temple. Varek’s shortages prove this network can be interrupted. Whoever controls it controls more than food.
- **The Orb of Golden Death.** Falrinth claims he possesses it but does not appear to be using it. Either he is lying, unable to command it, or waiting. All three possibilities are useful.
- **The iron coffin.** Waking the sleeper may summon an army against the Temple. The more important question is why powerful men are afraid of what is inside.
- **Eryk’s coercion.** Someone has been forcing our Rhennee family into road ambushes. We now need the same roads and river contacts to feed the Temple.

*Gentlemen, we did not survive four prophets, a temple war, a wraith, trolls, a living pool, and several administrative meetings merely to throw away the first position of genuine authority any of us has held. We will restore the supply line, secure every asset, learn what the deeper powers are hiding, and then—once the inventory is complete—make a properly informed decision about what, if anything, must be brought down.*
